# Dine Sight — Video scripts + shot lists

**You already have a 0:46 film and it doesn't play on some browsers** (`04` Fix 6). Fix that before making anything new — a working existing asset beats a new broken one.

The videos below are for a different job than your film: they're for **WhatsApp**, where this business actually happens. Owners forward things to each other in WhatsApp groups. Make videos that survive being forwarded: vertical, short, captioned, under 10MB.

---

## Video A — The 30-second doorstep video 🔴 *(your most useful asset)*

**Use:** WhatsApp follow-up after a visit, Instagram DMs, restaurant owner groups.
**Goal:** the owner who said "send me something" actually watches it and gets it.

| Time | On screen | Voice (Hinglish) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | A real Google listing, scroll to an unanswered 2-star from 11 days ago | **"Ye review gyarah din purana hai. Koi reply nahi."** |
| 0:06–0:11 | Hold on it | **"Jo customer aapka page dekh raha hai booking se pehle, wo yahi dekhta hai."** |
| 0:11–0:20 | The drafted reply appears, typed out | **"Ab ye dekhiye. Yahi review, aur ye reply — aapke hi andaz mein. Naam liya, problem ka zikr kiya, sorry bola, ek solution diya."** |
| 0:20–0:26 | Tap `Approve & post`. It appears under the review. | **"Ek tap. Post ho gaya. Ya pehle edit kar lijiye — aapki marzi. Bina aapke tap ke kuch nahi jaata."** |
| 0:26–0:30 | End card | **"Har naye review pe, kuch minute mein. dinesight.in"** |

### Shot list
1. **Use a real Google Business listing** — yours, a friend's restaurant, or a demo account that looks real. A mockup is visible instantly to someone who looks at their own listing every day.
2. Phone screen recording, vertical, portrait.
3. **Hinglish voiceover.** English halves your reach with this buyer.
4. Burned-in captions — restaurant owners watch on mute, in a noisy kitchen.
5. **Keep the file under 10MB** so it forwards cleanly on WhatsApp. This matters more than quality.
6. Make an English version too, for chains and metro cafés.

## Video C — The 60-second demo (horizontal + vertical cut)

**Use:** the landing page (as a working backup to your film), partner pitches, association meetings.

**0:00–0:12 — The problem**
> "Almost no independent restaurant replies to its reviews. Not because they don't care — because by the time the owner sees a complaint, it's eleven days old and the moment's gone. Meanwhile that review is the first thing a new customer reads."

*Shot: a real listing, scrolling past unanswered reviews with visible dates.*

**0:12–0:25 — Connect**
> "You sign in with Google — the real Google screen. No password is shared with us, and we only get access to your reviews. Nothing else about your account."

*Shot: the actual Google OAuth screen, with the permission scope visible. **Showing the real consent screen is the most trust-building five seconds in the video.***

**0:25–0:45 — The draft**
> "Then every new review gets a reply drafted within minutes, matched to how your restaurant actually talks. Here's one: 'Waited forty minutes, food was cold.' And here's the reply — it names her, names the problem, apologises once, and offers something concrete."

*Shot: the review, then the reply appearing. Read it aloud. Hold long enough to read.*

**0:45–0:55 — Control**
> "You approve it with one tap. Or you edit it first. Or, once you trust it, you let it post on its own. Nothing goes out under your restaurant's name without you deciding."

*Shot: tap approve. Then show the edit view. Then the auto-post toggle, deliberately switched off.*

**0:55–1:00 — Close**
> "Google reviews, live today. Zomato and Swiggy next. We're onboarding restaurants in [city] this month. dinesight.in."

### Shot list
- Real listing, real OAuth screen, real reply.
- Phone-shaped where possible; your buyer will watch on a phone.
- Honest about what's live. Never show a Zomato integration that doesn't exist.

## Video D — The reply quality explainer (0:40, vertical)

Teaches something genuinely useful. The product is incidental. This is the one to post in Facebook and WhatsApp owner groups.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | A one-star review | "Bura review aaya. Kya likhein?" |
| 0:05–0:16 | ❌ *"Dear Guest, We regret the inconvenience caused. Your feedback is valuable to us and has been forwarded to the concerned department."* | "Ye mat likhiye. Ye har jagah likha hota hai. Padhne wale ko lagta hai — dekha toh, par kaha kuch nahi." |
| 0:16–0:30 | ✅ *"Thank you for telling us, Priya. Forty minutes is far too long, and cold food isn't how we want your evening to go. We're sorry — please give us another chance, and ask for the manager on the night."* | "Ye likhiye. Naam liya. Problem ka naam liya — chalis minute, thanda khana. Ek baar sorry. Aur ek solution." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Text: **"Reply customer ke liye nahi hai. Agle sau logon ke liye hai."** | "Aur yaad rakhiye — reply us customer ke liye nahi hai, wo toh kha ke chala gaya. Reply un agle sau logon ke liye hai jo ye padh ke decide karenge." |

**That last line is the single most quotable thing in this kit.** Use it in every conversation.
