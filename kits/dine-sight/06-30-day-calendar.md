# Dine Sight — 30-day execution calendar

**This month is mostly spent on your feet.** Three field days a week, every week. Nothing in this kit matters more than that.

**The weekly rhythm:**
- **Mon, Wed, Fri: field days.** 20 restaurant visits each, 3pm–7pm.
- **Tue, Thu: build, follow up, partners, content.**
- **Sat: owner groups and online.**
- **Sun: review and plan next week's streets.**

## The three things that decide this month

1. **Did you do 11 field days?** ~220 visits is the floor. No amount of online activity substitutes for it with this buyer.
2. **Are the onboarded restaurants actually approving replies?** A restaurant that signed up and never approved anything has churned and doesn't know it yet. **This is the number that matters**, not signups.
3. **Did you get referrals?** Restaurant owners on the same street talk daily. If your first 20 customers produce zero referrals, something about the product or the experience is off — find out what, in person.

---

## The compounding play

**Finish one neighbourhood completely before starting the next.** Twenty restaurants on one street who all know each other, all using it, all mentioning it to each other, is a defensible position and a case study. Forty scattered across a city is a list.

When a neighbourhood is done, your pitch on the next street becomes: *"Every café on [road] is using this."* That sentence is worth more than any feature.

---

## If an association says yes

Clear the calendar. Speaking to 50 restaurant owners in one room is 2.5 field days of value in 90 minutes, and association endorsement is the closest thing to a distribution deal in this market. Prepare properly: Video C, the 81-of-100 survey, and a signup sheet in the room.
