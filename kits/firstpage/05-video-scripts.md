# First Page — Video scripts

Use your own reading opinions and test account. No copied reviews, full book pages, hidden spoilers or unconsented reader profiles.

## A. One book, one exchange — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “I need a reader's reason, not another bestseller list.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Public curated stacks|“First Page lets you browse before taking a quiz.”|
|10–25s|One book you've actually read, no plot reveal|“Here's one original reason I liked this book.”|
|25–40s|Actual tested log/visibility path|“Before posting, I check who can see the activity.”|
|40–52s|Real consented reply or labelled synthetic example|“The useful moment is another reader explaining their taste.”|
|52–60s|URL and optional-quiz note|“Bring one book. The 14-question taste quiz can wait.”|

If logging/social exchange isn't working, show the limitation; don't splice in a fabricated conversation.

## B. No spoilers — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Tell me why you liked it, not how it ends.” Show your own sentence about pacing/style, then a concealed spoiler area only if tested. Say: “A short reading reason can help without giving away the story.” Use no quoted commercial-book passage.

## C. Fourteen questions, optional — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A quiz shouldn't block the books.” Show first quiz step and 1/14 indicator, then public stacks. Say: “Reader DNA is playful taste discovery. If you just want to browse, start here.” No personality/psychometric validity claim.

## D. Local note or shared review? — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “Not every note is a public conversation.” Show the actual privacy explanation and your own synthetic note/review visibility. Say: “Margin notes stay in this browser. Shared activity follows your account settings. The page needs to make that difference obvious.” Only show behavior you tested; no assumed sync.
