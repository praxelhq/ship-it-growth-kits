# First Page — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real accounts, excluding tests and duplicates. Report **accounts logging a first real book** beside signups, then meaningful original reviews/replies and later-day returners. Quiz results, anonymous catalogue visits and Amazon clicks are separate—not substitute accounts.

## Day 0

Record existing accounts, first-book logs, original shared activity and seven-day sessions from your own system; private counts were unavailable during research. The [privacy page](https://first-page.app/privacy) already describes Clarity/Search Console, so confirm the existing connection rather than adding duplicate tracking. Keep private/local notes out of replay and event payloads. Record actual [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/firstpage) reading, period and rank.

Minimum aggregate events: `signup_completed`, `first_book_logged`, `original_review_posted`, `reader_reply_received`, `quiz_completed` and `later_day_returned`. Distinguish founder/test activity and synthetic examples. A local margin note is not a shared social reply. Do not publish readers' identities or private activity to prove engagement.

## Honest funnel

One 12-reader invitation, five organizer notes, four owned-feed posts and six Shorts support a small initial circle. At an assumed 20% visit→account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified visits. At an assumed 50% new-account→first-log rate, 1,000 signups would produce 500 activated readers. Neither is a benchmark; replace both with observed results.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Accounts, first logs and replies from a real second reader|If no exchange happens, build around one existing club/read rather than more empty profiles.|
|Day 14|Quiz completions versus first logs, visibility questions|If quiz dominates but reading activity doesn't follow, demote it to an optional path.|
|Day 21|Later return, original replies without founder prompts|If every conversation needs you, ask what makes the room worth revisiting.|
|Day 30|Accounts, activated readers, genuine exchanges, returners, actual reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000; don't add books, quiz results and people into one number.|

Keep weekly actual rows with those counts, best referrer and hours. Curated catalogue size is supply; a meaningful exchange between consenting readers is the community evidence.
