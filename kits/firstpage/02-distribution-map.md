# First Page — Distribution map

**Lead with one existing small book circle and a useful conversation.** Don't send a generic social-network pitch to every reader online.

|Route|Specific action and boundary|Planning result, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Your existing campus book-club organizer|One permitted 12-reader session in Days 4–7, around a book members already read. No forced public profile or plot spoilers.|12 invited × 60% attend × 50% account = 3–4 accounts.|#1,#4|
|Five book-club organizers with a legitimate public route|Three notes on Day 9, two on Day 16. Offer a small useful discussion, not access to attendee lists.|5 × 20% acceptance = one possible later session; zero until agreed.|#4|
|Your LinkedIn feed|Four posts Days 8/15/22/29 at 09:00 IST, original reading-process observations, not copied reviews.|4 × assumed 200 views × 3% visits × 20% account = about 5 accounts; zero possible.|#2|
|Your own YouTube channel|Six original clips and one demo under [YouTube policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/). No unlicensed book pages or spoilers.|6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits.|#3,videos|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Working book-log/social path; check current slots and don't ask for votes.|0–10 relevant testers, optional upside.|#5|
|[r/books rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/books/about/rules)|Rules endpoint resolved without readable text. Read current sidebar/ask moderators before an app-development/promotional post.|Zero forecast until allowed. No private solicitation of reviewers.|#6|

The [StoryGraph official product/pricing route](https://app.thestorygraph.com/plus) and [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/about/us) help you understand existing reader expectations. Don't post competing links into their discussion rooms without permission. A willing book-club bulletin can carry #1 once; no external newsletter placement or librarian partnership is assumed.

Optional [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) launch comes after real readers complete the first log/exchange. Personal maker account, no upvote solicitation, check existing listings first. A maker launch is not a substitute for a book group returning.

This small plan supports tens of accounts. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→account rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 visits. The calendar doesn't establish that reach. Scale a discussion format that readers voluntarily repeat, not a quiz result they never use again.
