# First Page — Positioning

**Live:** https://first-page.app/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/firstpage

**A reading community needs real readers talking, not only a beautiful empty room.** Your first result should be one book logged and one useful, spoiler-safe exchange. A 14-question quiz can be optional; it is not evidence that your recommendations are scientifically validated.

Actual headline: “A social home for readers who live between pages.” “Discover Your Reader DNA” opens `/quiz` with 14 questions; “Explore Curated Stacks” opens `/discover`, showing 55 curated books and shelves such as Midnight & Psychological and Tender & Bittersweet. These routes were checked in a browser. The catalogue is browseable without sign-in; account creation, book logging and social posting were not submitted.

Your first reader is an adult college book-club member who has finished a book and wants a better next recommendation than the loudest bestseller. Start with a small circle sharing taste, not a promise to replace every book platform.

**Pitch:** Log a book you've read, find readers whose taste you understand, and choose your next book without plot spoilers.

**Bio:** A small reading room built around books and people you trust.

The wedge is **one existing book club bringing its current read into a useful discussion**. The catalog, diary and chosen-reader activity support that. A Reader DNA label is playful taste discovery, not a psychological diagnosis; don't oversell it.

[Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/about/us) already supports shelves, friend activity and reviews. [StoryGraph Plus](https://app.thestorygraph.com/plus) currently displays $49.99/year in USD, with a no-payment-details 30-day trial that cancels automatically. Don't call that $4.17 cancellable monthly billing; it's the annual equivalent. No First Page price appeared in the checked experience, so don't invent a subscription or promise free forever.

Your distinction must be a small, thoughtful, spoiler-safe room rather than more catalogue volume. The [privacy page](https://first-page.app/privacy) says reviews/activity may be public; private mode limits activity to the reader and mutual follows. Margin/spoiler-room notes are local-browser-only. Resolve any implication that every local note is socially shared before advertising that behavior.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**, with first real book logs and later-day meaningful participation beside accounts. Quiz completions and Amazon clicks are separate signals, not a reading community.
