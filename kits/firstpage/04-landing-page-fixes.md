# First Page — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 by rendered fetch/browser: home, quiz step 1/14, public discover and privacy. No answers, signup, log or social post was submitted.

1. **Give the actual headline one clear first job.** Current “A social home for readers who live between pages” can stay, with **“Bring one book you've read. Find your next through readers whose taste you understand.”** Make **“Browse the curated stacks”** → `/discover` primary. Let people see books before the optional quiz.

2. **Disclose quiz effort and scope.** Current “Discover Your Reader DNA” opens 14 questions and calls it psychological profiling. Replace supporting copy with **“A 14-question reading-taste quiz. Playful guidance, not a psychological assessment.”** Test the final recommendation/save behavior before promising it. No validated matching method was established here.

3. **Show a real, useful reader exchange.** The page describes an intimate salon, but a new community needs proof. Add one consented original spoiler-safe review and a meaningful reply; label staged/synthetic examples clearly if used. Never seed fake people or borrowed Goodreads reviews. A 55-book counter is content supply, not reader participation.

4. **Explain social visibility before the first post.** The privacy page says public reviews/activity can be seen by others, and private activity is limited to mutual follows. Show **“Choose who can see this before posting”** with the actual setting/preview. Do not describe every diary interaction as private by default unless tested.

5. **Clarify which notes are local only.** Privacy says margin notes and spoiler-room notes stay in the browser, while landing copy suggests exchange. Reconcile the implemented behavior. Use **“Personal margin notes stay on this browser”** for local notes and reserve **“Shared discussion”** for actual server-backed conversation. Do not promise a local note appears on another reader's device.

6. **Separate books from the app.** Discover links to external Amazon search for a featured book. Label **“Find this book on Amazon”** and say leaving the site uses the retailer's terms. Do not imply First Page provides free full-text access or owns book content. No First Page plan price was observed; disclose any actual cost before signup/purchase.

7. **Phone, preview and signup checks.** Title **“First Page — your small spoiler-safe reading room”**; description **“Browse curated books, log your reading and choose reader activity visibility. The taste quiz is optional.”** Test catalogue search, first book log, auth return and spoiler concealment on a phone. Confirm analytics doesn't expose private notes; a privacy-page claim is not an independent implementation audit.
