# Ledgerly — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **a small permitted monthly khata reconciled by the user and reviewed again later**. Record initial review and independent return separately. Imported messages, flagged subscriptions, cancellation stamps and hypothetical annual savings are not users or realised outcomes.

## Day 0

Record actual email-link account creation, import/parse successes/failures, reviewed khatas, corrected amounts, independent returns, delivered emails and real Premium purchases/refunds. No private baseline was established here. Capture current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/ledgerly) rank, source reading and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Every home amount is explicitly sample data, including ₹2,566 potential monthly savings.

Check actual data/consent terms and existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding tracking. Mask SMS, statement input, balances, merchants, amounts, email and category/source details. Minimal proposed events only if supported: `account_created`, `input_parsed`, `parse_failed`, `record_corrected`, `khata_reviewed`, `independent_return`, `digest_delivered`, `premium_purchase_confirmed`. A sent email isn't read, a delivered reminder isn't bill payment and a stamp isn't provider cancellation.

Use a voluntary workflow response instead of reading users' private khatas to count value. Keep original raw-message handling separate from sorted records and check that the actual implementation matches the published promise. Never add hidden content telemetry to make acquisition attribution easier.

## Scheduled forecast

Assumptions, not personal-finance benchmarks:

- 20 invitations × 40% try × 70% account ≈ five to six accounts.
- Six posts × 100–500 impressions × 2% visits × 25% account ≈ 3–15 accounts.
- One accepted session: ten invited × 60% attend × 50% account ≈ three, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–100 visits × 15% account = 0–15 accounts.

Rounded base **8–39 before overlap**, below 1,000. Half activating means roughly 4–20 reviewed return users. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified visits; the calendar doesn't establish that reach. Recompute after 15 invitations rather than raising the forecast with an unverified “savings” screenshot.
