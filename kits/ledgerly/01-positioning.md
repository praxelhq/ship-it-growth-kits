## Your first person and sentence

An adult who already gets bank debit SMS and wants to understand a few recurring charges before reviewing a month. They don't want to connect a bank, give OTPs or upload a whole financial life merely to inspect a tool.

> Turn permitted debit messages into a monthly record you check, with recurring charges marked for your review.

Bio: **A clearer khata before the next recurring-charge review.**

Use three synthetic messages first, with a duplicate and a non-recurring purchase. Check amount, currency, date and classification. Only after processing/storage/privacy is verified should a user choose to enter redacted real messages. No screenshots of bank statements, card numbers, balances, account identifiers or OTPs in marketing.

## Alternatives

[Rocket Money's official site](https://www.rocketmoney.com/) describes a free plan with spend/subscription tracking and paid Premium cancellation help. A current Premium amount or Indian-bank support wasn't established, so don't invent either or promise its cancellation service is yours. A personal spreadsheet with manually entered amounts has no new specialist-software subscription cost if already available.

Your distinction is user-supplied Indian SMS → reviewed khata with no bank login, if real parsing proves it. The user still decides what a recurring service is worth and cancels through the provider's actual process. Don't claim a cancellation stamp in Ledgerly actually stops a payment.

## The honest weakness

The home says **“Cancel these.”** Four subscriptions and a ₹2,566 saving are explicitly **sample figures**. A duplicate-looking music/gym service may be intentionally used. A price-rise flag may be a parsing error, and a possible saving isn't money kept. Lead with “review,” keep user-reported cancellation distinct from verified avoided charges and don't copy sample provider steps as current universal instructions.

No readable public policy link was visible in the inspected home/login entry. Publish actual processing, sorted-data storage, deletion/support and email handling before private SMS recruitment. Keep **1,000 individual accounts** as the target, with **a reconciled small khata and later independent review** alongside. Paste events, flagged subscriptions and theoretical annual savings aren't users.
