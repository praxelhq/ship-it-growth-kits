# Ledgerly — Landing-page fixes

Home and browser-rendered email-link entry checked 29 October 2026. No sign-in email, SMS/PDF/CSV import, stored result, digest, reminder or payment was tested. Home's raw-message claims are operator statements, not independent verification.

## 1. Put actual data handling before private SMS

**Current:** “Your SMS is read in your browser. Only the sorted result is saved. The raw message is never stored.” No policy route was visible on the inspected home/login entry.

Publish the actual supported formats, processing location/providers, saved fields, retention/deletion and working support route. **Write first-use helper:** **“Try three fictional messages first. Read what is processed and saved before using private records.”** Verify with controlled network/storage tests before advertising no-upload/raw-never-stored as proven. Keep identifiers/OTPs out of input and recordings.

## 2. Make the headline a useful job without blame

**Current:** “Your bank already told you. You just weren't listening.” CTA “Start your khata.”

**Write:** **“Turn debit messages into a monthly khata you can check.”** Helper: **“Start small. Review amounts, dates and recurring labels before relying on the total.”** Button: **“Try a synthetic khata first.”** Implement a real sample path if absent; keep email-link account creation clear for saving. Don't imply every missed debit is personal carelessness.

## 3. Replace the cancellation command with review

**Current:** “Cancel these,” four sample subscriptions, stamp interactions and potential savings.

**Write:** **“Recurring charges worth a second look.”** States: **“Review”**, **“User says cancelled”**, **“Next charge checked”** only where supported/measured. Keep **“All figures on this page are sample data.”** attached to each crop. A stamp isn't a provider action; don't label hypothetical avoided amounts realised savings.

## 4. Show a duplicate and parsing uncertainty

**Current:** monthly khata and category totals appear clean, without independently tested input/output.

**Write a labelled example:** **“Original message → proposed amount/date/category → your correction.”** Test duplicate messages, ambiguous merchants, reversals and one-off charges. Leave unknowns visible rather than forcing every debit into a confident category. Don't infer a true price rise from unmatched amounts without the user's review.

## 5. Qualify email/reminder promises

**Current:** monthly khata/weekly Sunday digest by email; Premium bill/EMI reminders before due dates.

**Write:** **“Check the recorded date and current delivery setting. Email delivery isn't proof a bill was paid.”** Test authorized synthetic delivery, unsubscribe/preferences, wrong/expired email link and failed scheduled job before promoting reminders. A trial debit is flagged only after a charge appears; it isn't preventive cancellation or a universal “day 8” rule.

## 6. Make billing and official cancellation sources precise

**Current:** Free ₹0 forever, Premium ₹149/month; sample cards include provider cancellation steps.

Publish actual recurring charge/tax, renewal, cancellation and refund terms before upgrade. Verify current provider-specific instructions against the provider's own help, or use **“Open your provider's current cancellation instructions.”** No guessed exact refund or cancellation guarantee. Don't borrow Rocket Money's concierge capability.

## 7. Phone and preview

**Current login:** “Open your khata,” email link creates/signs into an account.

Preview: **“Ledgerly — a reviewed monthly khata from debit messages.”** Description: **“No bank login. Start with a fictional example and inspect storage/access terms.”** Test 360px, paste/import errors, amount formatting and email-link return. No measured mobile/load issue is alleged. Keep financial text out of preview images and analytics.
