# Ledgerly — Distribution map

**Synthetic SMS reconciliation first; opt-in personal finance organization second.** Keep this distinct from FamilySubSaver's household coordination and PerkEasy's US benefits. No bank scraping, loan promotion or messages under someone's financial distress.

| Route | Exact action and boundary | Planning assumption / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known adults with recurring-charge questions | Five opt-in invitations on days 4,8,12,16 = **20**. Synthetic/redacted examples, no statements or identifiers sent to you. Explain actual processing and email-link account entry. | 20 × 40% try × 70% account ≈ five to six accounts; half reconcile and return ≈ three activations. #1 |
| 2 · Own LinkedIn/Instagram/X lessons | Six posts on days 5,10,15,20,25,29. Fictional SMS, reviewed totals and potential-versus-real savings, not another person's bills. Follow [LinkedIn policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies). | Six × 100–500 impressions × 2% visit × 25% account ≈ 3–15 accounts. #2, #3 |
| 3 · One known adult practical-finance organizer | Ask day 6 for a ten-person bookkeeping-literacy exercise, accepted session day 11. No personalized cancellation/credit advice or member records. Distinct group from other finance tools. | 10 × 60% attend × 50% account ≈ three if accepted, otherwise zero. #4 |
| 4 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Check actual Monday Launchpad slot/eligibility after privacy/parsing/email entry work. One maker launch, synthetic data, actual free/paid scope; no vote requests. | 0–100 visits × 15% account = 0–15 accounts. #5 |
| 5 · [AlternativeTo FAQ](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) | Optional free factual application submission, email verification and review needed; no review-date guarantee or paid priority. | Zero guaranteed first-month users. #5 adapted |

[Rocket Money's actual product page](https://www.rocketmoney.com/) is a comparison source, not a place to advertise under its support questions. No finance subreddit promotion rule, newsletter placement or bank partnership was verified. If an introduced editor asks for a free review worksheet, use #4 without claiming access to subscriber data.

Base **8–39 accounts before overlap**, from twenty invitations, six posts and optional session/launch. Below 1,000. At day 14 inspect whether messages become accurate records and a later review, not just a dramatic savings number. No signup target justifies collecting a full bank statement or promising users money saved from sample charges.
