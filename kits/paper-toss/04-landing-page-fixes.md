# Paper Toss — Landing-page fixes

Rendered page checked 29 October 2026. Gameplay, reload persistence and cross-device behavior were not exercised; no account system is implied.

1. **Give the real headline a concrete goal.** Current H1: “Throw. Score. Win.” Supporting replacement: **“Ten throws. Make four to earn a star and open the next room.”** Keep the playable bin as the focal element. The first action should be the actual flick, not a newsletter popup.

2. **Show swipe and wind before scoring.** Current “Flick up from the paper to throw” and “Wind: 0.0 m/s” need a clear first-use example. Add **“Swipe angle aims. Swipe speed adds distance. Aim against the wind.”** Offer one practice throw before the scored round only if implemented; don't promise a tutorial that does not exist.

3. **Explain the result without a guessed physics story.** After a miss, keep the wind arrow and attempted direction visible. Use **“Try a little more into the wind”** only when the actual calculation supports it; otherwise say **“Watch the wind arrow and adjust your next flick.”** Test with three fresh players rather than inventing a diagnosis from screenshots.

4. **Make progression factual.** Current stars are earned at 4/6/8 makes and one star unlocks another room. Result copy: **“Four makes earns your first star. Six earns two. Eight earns three.”** Match the actual counter and do not call a local record a global rank.

5. **Bring local-save limits forward.** Current “Scores and stars are saved in this browser only” is important. Repeat by room selection: **“Progress stays on this browser. Clearing its storage or changing device may not carry it over.”** Test reload behavior; do not promise an account backup.

6. **State access accurately.** No price was visible in the checked public interface. Keep the first playable action clear and disclose any actual future payment before it occurs. Do not invent Pro, a subscription or an app-store availability claim to fill a marketing card.

7. **Make phone and preview usable.** Title **“Paper Toss — ten throws against the wind”**; description **“Make four shots to open the next room. Play in your browser; progress stays on this device's browser.”** Test the ball's swipe area with the browser bar and safe-area inset visible. Keep the wind readable while the hand covers the bottom screen. No performance score was measured here.
