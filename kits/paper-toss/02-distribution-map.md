# Paper Toss — Distribution map

**A visible near-miss and a simple ten-shot invitation are your best demonstration.** Ask for play, not support for a student founder.

|Route|Action and boundary|Planning outcome, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Your own friends/classmates|Ten personal permission-first invitations, five each on Days4/11.|10 × 60% start × 70% finish = 4 completed rounds.|#1|
|Your YouTube channel|Six original Shorts plus one complete-round demo, under [YouTube's policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/).|6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits; zero possible.|#2,videos|
|[itch.io](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started)|Free project page with original screenshots and external browser link/your own HTML5 build. Preview before publishing.|0–20 visits; discovery not guaranteed.|#3|
|One existing campus break/game organizer|Ask for an optional ten-person first-room test in Days10–13; use a different organizer from other games this week.|10 × 50% attend × 70% complete = 3–4 rounds.|#4|
|[r/WebGames rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/WebGames/about/rules)|Endpoint resolved but rule text unavailable to extraction. Read sidebar/ask moderators before a promotional link.|Zero until allowed.|#5|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Show the working game; confirm current launch slot. No vote trades or false traction.|0–10 useful testers, optional upside.|#6|

Study the public [FRVR page](https://basketball.frvr.com/) for how it explains swipe power and moving targets. Don't advertise under its work or reuse its assets. A permitted campus bulletin can include your ten-shot invitation; no external newsletter placement is assumed.

This plan supports tens of players. At an assumed 60% visit→start rate, 1,000 starts require about 1,667 relevant visits. At 1% video-view→visit, that is about 167,000 views. Those are scenario inputs, not evidence that six clips will produce the target. A friend choosing a later round matters more than a large view count.
