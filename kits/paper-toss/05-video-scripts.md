# Paper Toss — Video scripts

Use your own gameplay and voice; no borrowed footage or soundtrack. Keep failures and actual scores honest.

## A. Ten throws — 60-second demo

**Hook:** “Four makes. That's the whole first challenge.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Open the first room and show ten-shot counter|“Paper Toss starts with one clear round.”|
|10–25s|Real swipe and wind arrow, hand visible|“Angle aims. Speed changes distance. Wind pushes the arc.”|
|25–42s|Actual attempts, speed-up labelled if needed|“I'll show the misses too; they tell me whether the controls make sense.”|
|42–52s|Actual round result|“Four earns the first star. My score is whatever this run actually produced.”|
|52–60s|URL and local-save disclosure|“Try ten throws. Progress stays in this browser.”|

Post on itch and your own channel. If you don't unlock the next room, don't edit in a false unlock.

## B. Wind miss — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “The bin didn't move. The air did.” Show wind arrow, an honest miss, then an adjusted attempt. Say: “Aim against the wind, then see if your adjustment makes sense.” Don't guarantee the second shot goes in. End with the URL.

## C. Clean swish — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “This sound is why I made it.” Show a real clean swish with the actual score feedback, then replay your own recorded shot once. Say: “One good throw. Now do four in a round.” No manufactured score or borrowed game audio.

## D. Same browser — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Your stars live here, not in a cloud account.” Show your actual local room map, reload and the retained result if it truly persists. Say: “Progress is saved in this browser. Changing devices isn't a synced account.” If persistence fails, fix it before recording a success. Use as a practical player-help clip.
