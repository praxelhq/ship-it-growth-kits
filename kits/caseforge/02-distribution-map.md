# CaseForge — Distribution map

**Read this first:** your advantage is that you are **not** aiming at consulting candidates, which means you are not competing for the four crowded channels every other case-prep tool will crowd into this month. Your audience sits in **function-specific communities** — product, marketing, HR — that nobody else in this cluster has a reason to enter.

That is where your month goes. Reach estimates state their basis; **verify a member count yourself before you plan around it.**

---

### 2. Your own campus, in the first three days

| Where | Reach | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your batch WhatsApp group(s)** | 60–400 people, **30–60% will actually open it** | The highest-converting message you will send all month. You are a classmate, not a marketer. |
| **The product / marketing / HR interest groups on your campus** | 30–150 each | Exactly your four functions, pre-segmented for you. Post in each separately, with that function's name in the message. |
| **Your placement committee (placecom)** | They control the only channel every student reads | **Ask, don't pitch.** Offer the free case to the whole batch and let placecom distribute it. If they forward it, you reach 100% of a campus in one message. |
| **Seniors who got PM/marketing offers last year** | 5–20 people | Ask three of them to try it and tell you where the model answer is wrong. Two of them become your testimonials, which is what `04` needs most. |

### 3. Other campuses, via one person each

The repeatable unit: **one well-chosen student per campus, given something free and exclusive.**

Target the HR and marketing specialisations specifically, because nobody has ever built them anything:

| Campus cluster | Who to contact | Realistic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **XLRI, TISS, MDI, SCMHRD, SIBM** (HR strongholds) | HR club secretary, or the most visible HR-track student on LinkedIn | 5–10 replies from 20 messages; each is worth 20–80 students |
| **Marketing-heavy campuses** — MICA, NMIMS, SIMSREE, Great Lakes | Marketing club secretary | Same arithmetic |
| **New IIMs and tier-2 campuses** | Anyone in the placement committee | **Far more responsive than the old IIMs**, who get pitched constantly and have more resources already |

20–40 DMs over the month. Expect 8–15 replies, 4–8 who actually distribute. **Each one who does is worth more than a good Reddit day.**

## Tier 3 — Don't 🔵

- **Product Hunt, Hacker News.** A first-year at MDI is not there. Peerlist instead.
- **X/Twitter.** This audience is on LinkedIn and WhatsApp.
- **Consulting communities.** You will be the eleventh option. Stay out.
- **Coaching-owned Telegram groups** (iQuanta, Cracku and similar). Those are a competitor's funnel and you'll be removed. Also: wrong audience — those are CAT aspirants, not placement candidates.
- **Paid anything.** Budget is zero.

## Reach summary

| Channel | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own campus (batch + function groups + placecom) | 150 | 500 |
| PM communities (Reddit + WhatsApp/Slack/Discord) | 400 | 3,000 |
| HR / marketing campus outreach (20–40 DMs) | 150 | 900 |
| LinkedIn (posts + comments) | 400 | 4,000 |
| r/MBA + adjacent subs | 200 | 2,500 |
| Peerlist + Unstop + directories | 100 | 600 |
| Quora + YouTube comments | 50 | 500 |
| **Total addressable visitors** | **~1,450** | **~12,000** |

**Read this honestly.** 1,000 signups needs roughly 8,000–10,000 visitors at a realistic student-tool conversion rate — so **the low end of this plan does not get you to 1,000, and the high end does.** Two things close the gap, and neither is "post more":

1. **The conversion fixes in `04`.** A page with no proof converts at 2–3%. The same page with three named testimonials and a visible free case converts at 8–12%. That difference is worth more than every Tier 2 channel combined.
2. **One campus distribution that actually lands** — a placecom forward or a club mailing list — is worth 100–400 signups in an afternoon. Four of those is the month. **This is why `06` front-loads DMs to clubs in week 1.**

Your `07` works the arithmetic properly.
