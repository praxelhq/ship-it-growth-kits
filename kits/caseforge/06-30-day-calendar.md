# CaseForge — 30-day execution calendar

**One or two things a day. Done properly beats done widely.**

The month is front-loaded on purpose: you are inside the Indian B-school summer placement window, which is the one month of the year this audience is desperate. A channel that works on day 4 may be worthless on day 26 because the rounds are over. **Verify your own campus's placement dates this week** and pull everything forward if they're earlier than you think.

Open this file every morning. Reply to every comment and DM every evening.

## The five things that actually decide this month

1. **Three named testimonials by day 7.** Everything downstream converts at double rate with them. Nothing else in this kit has that multiplier.
2. **Club and placecom DMs in week 2, all 30 of them.** One forward to a batch mailing list is 100–400 signups in an afternoon. Four of those *is* your target.
3. **Staying out of consulting communities.** The moment you start competing for the consulting candidate you become the eleventh option instead of the only one.
4. **Front-loading.** The placement window closes. A perfect week-4 plan into a campus that's finished interviewing produces nothing.
5. **Case completion rate above 50%.** If people sign up and never finish a case, you don't have a growth problem, and more traffic won't fix it.

## What to drop if you're short on time

In this order, the lowest-value hours in the plan: **Quora** (day 17 — compounds slowly, won't help this month), **YouTube comments** (day 18), **Unstop** (day 20). The three you cannot drop: **day 2 and 7 (testimonials)**, **days 9 and 11 (club DMs)**, **day 3 (your own batch)**.
