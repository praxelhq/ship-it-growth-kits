# CaseForge — Video scripts and shot lists

Scripts and shot lists only — **you record these on your phone.** A rough, real video from the founder outperforms a polished one with this audience, who can smell a template instantly.

Everything here is vertical (9:16), shot on a phone, with captions burned in. **Most of this audience watches muted**, so if the words aren't on screen the video doesn't exist.

---

## Video 1 — The hook video (your most important asset)

**Length:** 22–30 seconds · **Where:** LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp status, embedded on the landing page

### Script

| Time | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Your face, close, no intro | "Seventy percent of my batch isn't going into consulting." |
| 3–6s | Still your face | "A hundred percent of the prep material is." |
| 6–11s | Screen recording: scrolling a folder of consulting case PDFs | "This is the folder everyone shares. Profitability, market entry, M&A." |
| 11–16s | Cut to your page, the HR prompt filling the screen: *"Attrition has doubled in one engineering pod"* | "This is an actual HR round question. Tell me which framework in that folder helps." |
| 16–24s | Screen recording: the four-phase timer running, then the scorecard appearing | "So I built it. Four phases, a real clock, scored on five things an interviewer actually judges." |
| 24–30s | Your face. URL as a caption, not spoken over music | "Product, HR, sales, marketing. One free case each. caseforge.in" |

### Shot list

1. **Talking head, 0–6s.** Phone at eye level, propped — not handheld. Window light on your face, you facing it. Record in a quiet room; audio quality matters more than video quality and phone mics pick up every echo.
2. **Screen recording of a consulting PDF folder, 6–11s.** iOS/Android screen record, scroll slowly. This shot is the argument — it has to look like a real folder, because it is one.
3. **Your page, HR prompt, 11–16s.** Zoom in so the prompt text fills the frame. Hold it for a full two seconds — long enough to read.
4. **The product working, 16–24s.** Timer counting, phases advancing, scorecard landing. **Use a real attempt with a middling score.** A 94 looks staged; a 71 looks true.
5. **Talking head close, 24–30s.** URL in a caption on screen for the final four seconds.

### Rules for this one

- **The first three seconds are the whole video.** "Seventy percent of my batch isn't going into consulting" is a statistic, not a claim, and that's why it stops the scroll. Don't open with "Hi guys, so I built...".
- **Don't say the URL out loud over music.** Nobody retains a spoken URL. Caption it.
- **No logo intro. No background music under your voice.** Both read as an ad and the algorithm treats it like one.

## Video 2 — The demo (your proof asset)

**Length:** 75–100 seconds · **Where:** landing page (Fix 6 in `04`), Peerlist launch, reply to anyone who asks "how does it work"

### Script

| Section | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–8s** | Your page, the four function cards | "I'll run one real case start to finish. Marketing one — CAC has tripled on the main paid channel. Nothing cut." |
| **8–20s** | Phase 1, Clarify. You typing actual clarifying questions | "Phase one is clarify. Which channel, over what period, did spend change or conversion. Most people skip this in a real round and then answer the wrong question." |
| **20–40s** | Phase 2, Structure. Your structure appearing | "Phase two, structure. I'm splitting CAC into spend and conversions, then conversions into traffic and rate. Say it out loud before you dive in — the interviewer needs to be able to follow you." |
| **40–58s** | Phase 3, Analyse. Numbers being used | "Phase three, the numbers. This is where I usually lose points — I reason well and then never actually touch the data." |
| **58–70s** | Phase 4, Recommend | "Phase four. One clear recommendation, a number attached, and what I'd check next." |
| **70–88s** | The scorecard, all five dimensions, honest score | "And the score. 74. Structure was fine, I lost it on gaps — I never looked at retention, and the model answer goes straight there. That's the bit I'd have been caught on." |
| **88–100s** | Your face | "That's the whole loop, about eighteen minutes. One free case per function at caseforge.in." |

### Shot list

1. **Screen recording of a full, genuine attempt.** Record yourself actually doing it. Don't rehearse to a high score.
2. **Speed up the typing sections 2–3×** — keep real time on the scorecard, which is the payoff.
3. **Zoom in on the scorecard** so the five dimensions are readable on a phone.
4. **Talking-head bookends only**, 8 seconds at each end.

### The rule that makes this video work

**Show a score you're not proud of, and name what you got wrong.** A demo where the founder scores 74 and says "I never looked at retention" is the most persuasive thing you can publish, because it proves the scoring is real. A demo with a 95 proves only that you can game your own product.

## Video 3 — "The HR one nobody built" (your sleeper hit)

**Length:** 35–45 seconds · **Where:** LinkedIn, HR-track WhatsApp groups, DMs to HR clubs

HR-track candidates are the most underserved audience in Indian placement prep and the most likely to share something made for them. This video exists purely for them.

### Script

> **(0–5s, your face)** "If you're HR specialisation, you've probably noticed something: nobody has built you anything."
>
> **(5–12s, screen: consulting folder again)** "Consulting candidates get timed simulators. Product candidates get teardown guides. HR gets 'prepare well'."
>
> **(12–22s, screen: the attrition prompt, held)** "This is an HR case round. Attrition has doubled in one engineering pod. There's a structure to answering that, and a way to lose the room in the first minute."
>
> **(22–35s, the four phases, then the scorecard)** "So it's in here. Timed, same four phases, scored on structure, gaps, whether you used the numbers, business sense, and whether you landed a clear answer."
>
> **(35–45s, your face)** "Free HR case, no card, no email: caseforge.in. And if the case isn't realistic, tell me — I'm not HR-track and I built these off interview reports."

**Why it ends like that:** the ask for criticism is honest, it's the only credible position when you're not from that function, and it's what makes an HR student reply instead of scroll.

## Video 4 — Daily story / status content (30 seconds each, make 8 of them)

One per day during weeks 1–3, for WhatsApp status and LinkedIn. **Format: one case prompt, read aloud, with your own 20-second structure.** No product shot until the last three seconds.

Prompts you already have on the page:

1. *"Design the onboarding for a first-time UPI user aged 60+"*
2. *"Attrition has doubled in one engineering pod"*
3. *"Win rate fell from 28% to 17% after a pricing change"*
4. *"CAC has tripled on the main paid channel"*

And four you should write for the other rounds candidates actually face:

5. A brand on declining market share in a tier-2 market
6. A sales team missing quota in one region only
7. A feature that shipped and nobody used
8. An employee-relations escalation two weeks before appraisals

**Structure, every time:** read the prompt (5s) → "here's how I'd open" (20s) → "full case is free at caseforge.in" (5s).

This is the cheapest content you will make and the most repeatable. It also does something no launch post does: it demonstrates competence over and over, which is what makes someone trust your scoring.

## Production rules for all of them

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Vertical 9:16, captions burned in** | Muted autoplay is the default on every surface you're posting to |
| **Record audio in a quiet room, phone propped** | Bad audio kills more student videos than bad video |
| **Window light, you facing the window** | Free, and the only lighting advice that matters |
| **No logo intro, no music bed under speech** | Reads as an ad, gets suppressed as one |
| **URL as on-screen text in the final 4 seconds** | Spoken URLs are never retained |
| **Under 30 seconds unless it's the demo** | |
| **Reshoot the first 3 seconds, not the whole video** | If a video underperforms, the hook failed. Everything after it was probably fine. |

**Make Video 1 and Video 3 first.** Those two carry the month. Video 2 is for the page and the launch. Video 4 is volume once the others are live.
