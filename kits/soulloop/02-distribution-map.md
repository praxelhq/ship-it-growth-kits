No unverified religious group names, clergy endorsements or newsletter slots are assumed. Ask an organizer privately before any group invitation. A free newsletter inclusion is upside only after acceptance. Don't recruit people by implying a missed counter streak makes them less sincere.

At 50% visit-to-practice-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. The first tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Use the real source-open and return rate before widening reach. Keep your Days 24–27 reading-circle invitation separate from Gita For Me and Kindred's earlier group windows; do not pitch all three to the same people that week.
