# Soulloop — Positioning

**Live:** https://soulloop.live/ · **Category:** Daily practice / spiritual reading · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/soulloop

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“Your Faith. Your Practice. Your SoulLoop.”** The CTA is **“Start Your SoulLoop”**, pointing to `/onboarding`. The page says **“Free. No account needed.”** It describes a story after every 50 chants and currently says **“Begin with Sikh wisdom · More traditions coming soon.”**

**Do not market every listed faith as a complete live experience.** Onboarding labels several traditions coming soon. Your first usable audience must match the current reviewed content, not the largest audience named in the hero.

Your first person is a willing adult who already follows a Sikh remembrance practice and wants a simple counter plus a short contextual story. You are helping an existing practice, not telling someone which faith or ritual to choose.

**Pitch:** Keep a small practice count and open a short story from the currently supported tradition.

**Short version:** A quiet count, a little context, at your own pace.

## The wedge

Complete one voluntary practice session and read the unlocked story. The reward should support the person's interest, not turn devotion into a competition. Fifty taps alone are not evidence of a meaningful practice or spiritual progress.

A free [Tally Count tool](https://tallycount.app/) or a paper tally already solves counting at $0. SoulLoop earns attention through the combination of a calm flow and accurately sourced stories. Don't pretend counting requires an app, and don't claim the story is historically authoritative without identifying its source and review.

## The uncomfortable bit

The public page and onboarding were read, but no practice, counter persistence or story unlock was completed during research. Test the supported route, source labels and recovery before promotion. Other traditions should remain clearly unavailable until their experience is actually ready.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful practice sessions, with completed 50-count sessions and stories opened beside it. Do not measure spiritual merit, promise wellbeing effects or use guilt about missed days. The practice belongs to the user; your product is the small supporting tool.
