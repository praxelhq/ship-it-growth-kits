# Soulloop — Video scripts

Show the app, not someone performing a private ritual for your promotion. Use only sourced text and avoid ranking beliefs or practice.

## A. Supported-route demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“A small counter, not a score for devotion.”**

2–12s, open onboarding and show current/coming-soon labels. “This is the supported starting experience today.”

12–25s, use the counter at a demonstrative pace. “Tap, correct a tap, stop when you choose.” Label any time compression.

25–38s, open the story after the real threshold. “The story needs its own source and context.”

38–50s, show context link and URL. “Free, no account. Begin with the current content, at your own pace.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Short

0–2s: “A missed day isn't a moral failure.”

2–10s: stop/reset a demonstration count. “Use the rhythm that fits your own practice.”

10–15s: current content label and soulloop.live. No claimed wellbeing effect.

## C. Source hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Where did this story come from?”

2–12s: open actual source/context label. “A retelling and an original source are different things.”

12–20s: return to the readable story. “The app should help you see that difference.” If a source is missing, fix it before claiming this flow exists.

## D. Honest scope — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Coming soon should mean coming soon.”

2–12s: show unavailable tradition labels and current supported route. “We're starting here, not pretending every path is finished.”

12–20s: free CTA. “Try the current experience if it fits your practice.” No religious-pressure language.
