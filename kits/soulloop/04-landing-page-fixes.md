# Soulloop — Landing page fixes

Based on [soulloop.live](https://soulloop.live/) and `/onboarding`, checked 29 October. No counter or story unlock was completed during research.

## 1. Match the hero to the live tradition coverage

Current headline: **“Your Faith. Your Practice. Your SoulLoop.”** CTA: **“Start Your SoulLoop.”** The page names many traditions, but says Sikh wisdom is the current starting content and onboarding marks others coming soon.

Suggested heading: **“A quiet practice count, beginning with Sikh wisdom.”** Support: **“Choose your own pace. Complete a count and explore a short, sourced story. More traditions are being prepared.”** CTA: **“Try the current free experience.”** Do not make an unavailable path look selectable and complete.

## 2. Prove the supported path

Fresh browser → supported tradition → counter → 50 → story → source/context → return. Test refresh, accidental taps, decrement/reset and slow network. If a counter or unlock fails, stop promotion and fix the specific step. An onboarding page existing is not proof the practice works.

## 3. Put sources next to the story

Current promise: **“Every 50 Chants, A New Story.”** Each story should distinguish source, retelling and app commentary, with a readable context note. Suggested label: **“Retelling for this app. Read the source/context.”** Never invent a traditional quotation or make an unsourced tale look historically verified.

## 4. Let users control the count

Provide clear undo/reset and explain what is saved. Copy: **“Count at your own pace. You can stop, correct a tap or reset.”** Avoid public streak rankings, guilt messages or spiritual-performance scores. No app knows what a person's practice means to them.

## 5. Explain no-account persistence

The page says **“Free. No account needed.”** Add the actual storage behaviour: whether progress is only on this browser, lost when data is cleared, or retained another way. Do not promise cloud recovery without an account system that supports it.

## 6. Check phone and accessibility

At 390px, test large tap targets, one-handed operation, screen-reader labels, reduced motion and readable scripts. No sound should begin unexpectedly. Preview: **“SoulLoop — a free practice counter, beginning with Sikh wisdom.”** No medical benefit or universal coverage promise in the image.
