# TidyRow —Measurement

**Target:10 real business customers**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Four tests: genuine operation; own permitted spreadsheet/workflow onboarded; independent last-seven-day use; privately known owner name/phone. Free-active firms count. Rows, datasets, cleaned companies, workflows and exports aren't additional businesses.

Signup is a real account. Activation: an owner applies an understood rule to its own authorised file, reviews changes/unresolved fields and compares the export with original/destination needs. A sample download or AI instruction alone isn't activation. Assisted cleanup and independent use are separate. Readiness doesn't prove successful CRM import.

Private baselines unknown: real firms, own files, reviewed runs, changed/unresolved records, wrong merges, actual exports/import outcomes, independent recent use, allowance consumption and real paid subscriptions/refunds. Test-mode transactions excluded. Save rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/tidy-row); unknown isn't zero and rolling readings aren't cumulative.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before tagging. Exclude raw cells, private filenames, selected AI values, instructions containing confidential data, accounts and payments. Aggregate events: own_file_loaded, processing_route_chosen, plan_reviewed, cleanup_completed, unresolved_reviewed, export_completed, independent_return. Follow actual privacy/storage terms; don't add cloud recovery or team sharing by analytics accident.

| Review | Diagnostic |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches. Can owners understand the rule and where data goes? Fix before more real files. |
| Day 14 | Twenty approaches. Compare own setups with reviewed/correct exports; pause destructive errors. |
| Day 21 | Twenty-eight approaches. Independent recent runs, recurring value and unresolved quality issues. |
| Day 28 | Thirty-six approaches. Audit all four tests and distinct businesses. |
| Day 30 | Forty planned. Actual customers against ten, activation, correct exports, errors, revenue/hours and gap. |

Assumed 40 ×50% ×70% ×70%=9.8 firms; at 25% conversation,4.9. Three posts/two introductions are upside only. Replace rates after eight/twenty approaches. Useful one-off cleanup may fail final-week policy; report that without prompting artificial use.

Weekly quality checks: false duplicate merges, changed IDs/leading zeros, date ambiguity, fabricated missing values, export differences and unexpected AI exposure. Any speed claim requires a timed comparable task with real conditions and sample size. No perfect accuracy, universal import acceptance, all-local processing or made-up savings.
