# TidyRow — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.tidyrow.tech/ · **Category:** Business spreadsheet cleanup · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/tidy-row

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Clean messy spreadsheets without doing the cleanup yourself.”** Actual public actions include **“See an example”** and **“Download this sample CSV”**; the entry says **“Free to start · Sign in with Google or email and password.”** No account, spreadsheet, AI request or purchase was submitted.

Your first ten are independent small B2B service firms preparing recurring permitted CRM imports. The operations owner has an original file, destination rules and authority to process it. Start with one weekly company-data CSV and deterministic spacing/case/identical-row review. Personal contacts are unnecessary for the first test.

**Pitch: Review a repeatable cleanup, keep unresolved issues visible and export for your own import check.** Short version: **Cleaner rows without invented missing data.**

Free $0:10,000 rows/file,15 files/month,100 operations/month. Pro $5 USD/month plus applicable checkout tax:100,000 rows/file,500 files,5,000 operations,100 browser-local workflows. Sign-in required; no payment for Free. Started runs count even if failed/cancelled; AI batches up to 100 rows count separately. Reset first calendar day UTC; limits stop actions, no automatic overages. Monthly renewal, no annual plan. Actual checkout can be labelled Test mode in test deployments; simulated payments are not revenue.

Core cleanup/recovery is browser-local, **but selected AI values can go through the server to Google Gemini**; instructions/column names can be sent for planning. Account metadata/usage is stored separately. Browser recovery is not cloud backup; clearing site data removes local workflows/recovery, not the account or previously sent provider data. Don't promise all-local or zero retention.

The free manual alternative is [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) cleanup plus an explicit change log and original copy. TidyRow must earn adoption through reviewed repeatability, not “hours to seconds” without a timed comparable task. Similar rows may be distinct businesses; likely duplicates require review. Incomplete email remains flagged, never guessed. Pro readiness checks do not import or guarantee destination acceptance.

**Target:10 real business customers**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Own permitted file/workflow, independent recent use, real operation and privately known owner name/phone. Rows, cleaned companies and sample downloads are not customers. Keep confidential personal/financial data out until processing, privacy and accuracy are understood.
