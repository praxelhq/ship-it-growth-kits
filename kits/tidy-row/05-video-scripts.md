# TidyRow — Video scripts

Use synthetic company-only rows and an original copy. No real CRM, personal contacts, private finance or account tokens. Screen recording and voice; labels remain visible.

## A. Clean, review, export —65 seconds · owner follow-up

0–3 s: incomplete synthetic field beside a formatting issue. **“Which problem can software actually fix?”**

3–18 s: actual harmless file/basic cleanup route. “Start with an original copy and a clear rule.”

18–32 s: preview trimmed/cased values and identical-row choice. “Inspect what will change before applying it.” Leave a real review action uncut.

32–47 s: unresolved field remains flagged. “Missing facts stay missing. Don't invent a contact detail.”

47–57 s: actual export/change log if supported. “You still check the destination and import yourself.” Label untested paid controls rather than fake them.

57–65 s: free allowance/data boundary and URL. “Basic and AI processing have different data routes. tidyrow.tech.”

## B. Duplicate hook —20 seconds · LinkedIn

0–3 s: two similar fictional company names. **“Similar doesn't always mean duplicate.”**

3–13 s: actual match review or separate labelled example. “Check the distinguishing fields before removing a record.”

13–20 s: original copy and URL. No perfect-accuracy claim.

## C. Recovery hook —25 seconds · onboarding

0–3 s: local recovery label. **“Recovered here isn't backed up everywhere.”**

3–16 s: download and actual local-workflow disclosure. “Keep your own copy. Clearing site data removes local recovery and saved workflows.”

16–25 s: AI/account boundary. “It doesn't undo information already sent to a provider.” Hold URL; never demonstrate deleting a real customer's records.
