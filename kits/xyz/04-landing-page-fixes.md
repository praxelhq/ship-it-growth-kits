# Lumi — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://meetlumi.in/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Lumi is here for you. ✨”**; the action is **“Keep Lumi with you”**. The first five activities need no account. Premium is ₹249 once for twelve months, with no automatic renewal and manual activation within 24 hours. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an adult deciding whether one short activity fits the moment. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Finish the public policy

**Current:** The refund page contains an unfinished phone-contact field.

**Write this instead:**

> For purchase or access help, use the working contact route on this site and include your payment reference privately.

**Verify:** Replace/remove the unfinished field with an actual supported route. Do not invent a phone number or publish a student contact here.

## Fix 2 — Keep the first action concrete

**Current:** “Lumi is here for you” is warm but broad.

**Write this instead:**

> Choose how much time you have. Try one small reset, then get back to your day.

**Verify:** Let a new visitor see the time/step flow without a paid-plan detour.

## Fix 3 — Separate free and paid choices

**Current:** The page says first five activities need no account and Premium costs ₹249.

**Write this instead:**

> Try your first five activities free. Premium is ₹249 once for twelve months; nothing renews automatically.

**Verify:** Check whether a new browser sees exactly that allowance before any payment.

## Fix 4 — Set manual activation expectation

**Current:** The Razorpay section says activation is manual within 24 hours.

**Write this instead:**

> After verified payment, we activate Premium within 24 hours. Use the same login email and keep your receipt.

**Verify:** Do not advertise instant unlock. Test the recovery route with a real internal test payment, not a public claim.

## Fix 5 — Explain local storage

**Current:** Initial mood text is read on-device and not saved; journal material stays on the device.

**Write this instead:**

> Your journal is kept on this device. Check the privacy page before writing anything sensitive.

**Verify:** Confirm actual storage, clearing and restoration behaviour; do not promise cloud backup without evidence.

## Fix 6 — Keep evidence language narrow

**Current:** The interface offers “Why this helps” and a study link.

**Write this instead:**

> Read the source behind this suggested activity. It is general information, not proof of a guaranteed result for you.

**Verify:** Check every actual study link and remove a claim that the linked evidence does not support.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
