# Lumi — Positioning

**Live:** https://meetlumi.in/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/xyz

## The thing to face first

Your paid plan is not an instant automatic unlock. The page says Premium is activated manually within 24 hours of verified payment. Also, the refund page still has an unfinished phone-contact field. Fix that public policy and set the payment expectation before promoting the annual offer.

## What is actually on your page

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Lumi is here for you. ✨”**. The next explanation is a wellness companion that asks how you feel and how much time is possible, then offers one small guided step. The action is **“Keep Lumi with you”**. The first five activities need no account. Premium is ₹249 once for twelve months, with no automatic renewal and manual activation within 24 hours. These are observations from [your public product page](https://meetlumi.in/), not a completed purchase or a promise that every authenticated flow works.

The public flow shows mood/time choices, short activities, a garden and a device-held journal. The payment section names Razorpay and the manual-verification boundary. Reading those sections does not prove payment, restoration or deletion was tested. “Research-backed” is the site’s description; individual study links and personalized effectiveness were not independently validated here.

## Your first person

An adult who wants a small pause between ordinary work tasks, has two to ten minutes and does not want a streak to lose. They should be able to inspect one fictional demonstration without revealing a personal mental-health story.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Choose how much time you have and try one small reset, without a streak to protect.

For a bio or a short reply:

> One small reset at your pace. First five activities free.

## Your opening use case

Show the difference between choosing two minutes and ten minutes, then the end of one activity. The garden is a gentle record, not a score of mental health. Avoid a dramatic before/after claim or any suggestion that the companion replaces professional support.

## The real alternative

[Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) is $0/year and offers journaling/prompts; it is a valid manual-reflection alternative. [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for personal notes and a simple reset checklist. Lumi must earn the next use through the short mood/time-to-step interaction, not a claim that people need to buy wellbeing.

## What you need to prove

Use sample selections and demonstrate one full start-to-finish activity. Test what is stored locally, how a returning browser sees the journal and what happens after a verified payment. Do not publish an identifiable reflection, a treatment claim or an unsupported “research-backed result” as proof.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
