# SpeakCoach — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one new non-test account where the verified account flow exists. If a real no-account mode supports practice, count meaningful first recording-session starts under the existing policy, separately labelled and deduplicated rather than added twice. A homepage visit/name form alone is not an activated user.

**Activation:** one completed recorded scenario with its report opened. Second action: a same-scenario retry, with voluntary report of whether one suggestion was useful. Returning practice matters more than increasing an AI score. Do not treat score changes as validated improvement or job readiness.

## Baseline

Record actual accounts, session starts/completions, reports opened, retries, returns, provider errors, free-limit hits and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/speak-coach. Public sample 84/+11 values are not baseline. Check existing Clarity before adding analytics. Exclude audio, transcripts, scores tied to identity, names, account and payment screens from replay; keep aggregate events only.
