# SpeakCoach — Video scripts

Record a real harmless answer after privately verifying the flow. Show your own actual report; if using the homepage preview instead, keep “Sample” visible and do not claim it is your score.

## A. Say it out loud — 40 seconds, homepage/club invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Scenario prompt. | “Say it out loud once.” |
| 3–13 s | Choose a real prompt and begin your own recording. | “SpeakCoach gives you a scenario. Try an answer without using confidential work details.” |
| 13–25 s | Actual resulting report, not substituted sample. | “Read the feedback and choose one thing you want to change.” |
| 25–34 s | Start same-scenario retry. | “Then try again. The second attempt is the point, not just the number.” |
| 34–40 s | URL/free limit. | “Start free. Three sessions each week.” |

## B. One change — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Highlight actual feedback. | “Pick one thing to change.” |
| 3–14 s | Your own retry, normal speed. | “Use the report as a suggestion, then rehearse again. You decide what makes your answer clearer.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Try a practice scenario at SpeakCoach.” |

## C. Sample is sample — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Homepage “Sample data”. | “This is an example report.” |
| 3–14 s | Sample label then actual entry. | “The homepage shows the format. Your own practice gives you feedback to inspect, not a guaranteed result.” |
| 14–20 s | URL/free plan. | “Three free sessions per week. Paid plans are coming later.” |

Keep hooks to these short spoken lines. No fake applause, changed score, guaranteed confidence claim or borrowed candidate recording.
