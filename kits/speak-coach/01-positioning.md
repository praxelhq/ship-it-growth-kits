# SpeakCoach — Positioning

**Live:** https://speakcoach.live/ · **Category:** Speaking practice · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/speak-coach

## The free offer is live; the paid rates are forthcoming

On 29 October 2026 the actual headline reads **“Speak with confidence. Improve with every conversation.”** The CTA **“Start Practicing Free”** links to `/dashboard`; `/onboarding` begins by asking what to call the user. Dashboard extraction showed the practice introduction, not a completed account, microphone session or report. Test those privately before a broad launch. The homepage's **84** and **+11** are labelled sample data, not real evidence of improvement.

The free plan advertises three AI-scored sessions per week. The ₹99 ten-drill pack, ₹299/month Pro and ₹1,499/six-month option are explicitly **coming soon**. Do not sell them as already purchasable or imply an unlimited free tier.

Start with an adult student who has an interview opener to rehearse this week and can spare one short recording plus a retry. SpeakCoach's wedge is scenario→spoken answer→line-specific report→retry. First Take helps with delivery to camera; this product's first test is responding out loud to a scenario, not reading a teleprompter. Keep those campus invitations separate.

**Pitch:** Rehearse one speaking scenario, inspect the feedback and retry the answer.

**Bio:** Speak. Review. Retry. Three free sessions per week.

[Yoodli](https://yoodli.ai/pricing) offers five lifetime Starter sessions; its public FAQ lists Pro at US$8/month billed annually and Advanced at US$20/month billed annually. Do not compare those annual-effective rates with a supposed live SpeakCoach purchase. A phone voice recorder and a trusted listener are another zero-extra-tool-cost baseline. Your difference must be usable specific feedback and an easy retry, not a guarantee to remove an accent or win an offer.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**. Report first completed recording/report review alongside it, then same-scenario retry. Scores are product feedback, not validated measures of employability, confidence, pronunciation quality across accents or clinical ability. Privacy says recordings, transcripts, scores and profile details are stored; do not call the flow device-only or confidential by default. A public practice prompt should need no employer secrets.
