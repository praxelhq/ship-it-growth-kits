# SpeakCoach — Copy pack

Send https://speakcoach.live/ first. Explain microphone/storage before asking someone to record. Use paid prices only as forthcoming, never a purchase CTA.

## 1. Personal invitation

> Have an interview answer you want to rehearse out loud?
>
> I’m building SpeakCoach: choose a scenario, record an answer, review the feedback and try again. The free plan has three sessions per week. Would you try one harmless practice answer and tell me whether the feedback is specific enough to use? https://speakcoach.live/
>
> It stores practice recordings and transcripts, so please leave confidential details out.

## 2. LinkedIn post

> An answer can sound clear in your head and different out loud.
>
> SpeakCoach is built around the rehearsal: a scenario, a spoken answer, a report and another try.
>
> I want the useful part to be one thing you can actually change in the next attempt. A score alone is not enough.
>
> Try the free plan: three sessions per week. Paid plans are not open yet. https://speakcoach.live/

## 3. Short caption

> One answer. One piece of feedback. Another try.
>
> Rehearse a speaking scenario with SpeakCoach. Three free sessions each week; use a practice answer without confidential details. https://speakcoach.live/

## 4. Organizer request

> Could I offer a short optional rehearsal exercise for adult members preparing interviews or talks? I’m building SpeakCoach and want feedback on its scenario→report→retry flow.
>
> Participants would keep their recordings and scores private. Nobody needs to buy anything or share an actual employer/candidate story. May I send a reviewed invitation at a time that does not clash with other tools?

## 5. Launch listing

**Tagline:** Rehearse a scenario, review the report, retry your answer.

SpeakCoach offers spoken scenario practice with transcript-based and scored feedback, plus another attempt. Start with the free three-session weekly allowance. The homepage's report is sample data and paid plans are forthcoming. Read storage/deletion terms before recording. https://speakcoach.live/

For repeats, demonstrate a different real prompt or a real feedback improvement. Never invent a higher score, fake a before/after result or promise a job offer. Do not present one accent as the only correct way to speak.
