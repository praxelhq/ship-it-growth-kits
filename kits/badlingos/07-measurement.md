## Day 0: write the baseline before changing the page

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, under the [consumer target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). For this product, count **a distinct new person beginning the actual first use, with the exact account/install/session rule to be set only after the product is identified**. Exclude your own tests, bots and repeat identities. This definition is intentionally provisional. It is not permission to count profile views or conversations as signups. Retain 1,000 as the roster target while recording the unresolved product facts.

**Activation:** the real core first action, still unestablished; specify and instrument it after the product demonstration rather than inventing an event from the name. Report the signup and activation totals beside one another every week. A view, a CTA click or a download attempt is not automatically either.

## Product events, with a reason

- Working public URL confirmed: recovery milestone, not signup.
- Five relevant prospect conversations completed: research, not users.
- Five real first-use tests observed: tests excluded from public acquisition totals.
- Exact first-start and completion events defined after product verification.
- Actual new and returning eligible people reported only once instrumentation works.

Keep the source on the first visit and the date of the first eligible completion. Show counts by source and the observed conversion fraction. Record returning people separately; do not add overlapping 24-hour cohort readings to manufacture a monthly total. The leaderboard is context, not your definition of a successful user.
