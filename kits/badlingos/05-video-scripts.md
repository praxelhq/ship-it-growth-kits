## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture only the public profile, a clean-browser release checklist and a blank baseline worksheet. Add actual product footage later only after the URL and job have been verified; do not manufacture it for this kit.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Keep these as private recovery/test recordings until the product facts and first-use route are established. Do not use them to imply that an unidentified product is ready for a launch.
