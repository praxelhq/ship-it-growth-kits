# Kriya — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real accounts. Report **a completed CV scan plus one user-understood truthful revision** alongside it. A score increase, rescan, application or mock is not another acquired user; an Offer Score isn't a hiring probability.

## Day 0

Read latest date/traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/kriya). Establish actual accounts, attempted/completed/failed scans, revisions understood, job checks, mocks, paid orders/refunds and return use. The kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured**, not zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and exclude/mask CV files, report text/scores, identity/contact fields, private role history and voice practice. Use aggregate events; don't publish candidate examples as proof without specific permission.

## Funnel

`account_created → CV_scan_started → report_delivered → suggestion_understood → truthful_revision_made → later_practice_used`.

Track parsing/provider errors and allowance consumed separately. Don't require a second paid rescan to verify that someone made a useful edit. A voluntary report that the revision helped is a learning signal, not causal evidence of an offer.

## Projection and decisions

`02` schedules 20 direct invitations, two circles, three clips and one listing: roughly 14–63 accounts before overlap under guessed rates, with a confirmed session potentially overlapping the same people. At hypothetical 20% qualified-visit-to-account rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 qualified visits. Current reach and activation don't establish that volume.

| Review | Check | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Five testers understand the score and free limit. | If two hear an offer guarantee/unlimited scans, fix copy before posting. |
| Day 14 | Accounts→reports, actual errors and allowance disputes. | Fix delivery/limit surprises before another circle. |
| Day 21 | Suggestions understood and truthful revisions users can explain. | Improve feedback rather than rewarding made-up metrics. |
| Day 28 | Real return use, paid satisfaction and privacy/support issues. | Keep the route yielding useful work, not raw score curiosity. |
| Day 30 | New accounts, scans, revisions and target gap separately. | State actual results without invented placement claims. |

These are chosen product checks, not employment predictions. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings and repeated scans aren't additive users.
