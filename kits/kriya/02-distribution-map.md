# Kriya — Distribution map

**Lead with adult freshers who already have a role and CV.** Campus permission and useful examples beat a generic “AI career platform” launch.

All forecasts are assumptions for the calendar, not placement/conversion benchmarks.

| Route | Action / rule | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Twenty relevant adult freshers | Individually ask whether they'd use their one free scan; start demos with fictional material, not someone's private CV. | 20 × assumed 50–70% signup × 60–80% scan completion = 6–11 scans. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned class/career circles | Ask the actual admin/career representative; one useful example and optional link each. No scraped student emails. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–25% visit × 20–40% signup = 2–8 accounts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned LinkedIn/short clips | Truthful bullet, score limitation, free-plan limits at 18:30 IST. No private resumes or invented employment proof. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. One free peer CV exercise | Ask a known organizer; use fictional text or private individual work. No public ranking or required upload. | If confirmed, 5–15 attendees and 2–8 accounts, likely overlapping circles. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after scan/account tests; no vote exchange. | Assume 5–20 visits and 1–5 accounts. | #5 |

Base scenario is roughly 14–63 accounts before overlap, plus only genuinely new session attendees. It doesn't establish a thousand. Activation after signup and the one-scan allowance need to be measured honestly.

## Communities and accounts

No placement newsletter, job-search subreddit promo permission or employer partnership was verified. Don't post in recruitment threads pretending to be a recruiter or claim a company uses your score.
