# Kriya — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://kriyaonline.in/) and [signup](https://kriyaonline.in/signup) were read 29 October. No personal CV, job check, mock or checkout was submitted; public pricing isn't proof all private features were tested.

## 1. Explain what the score can and cannot mean

Current H1: **“Know your Offer Score before you apply.”** CTA: **“Check my CV free.”**

Replacement subhead: **“Get specific feedback on your CV and a truthful next revision. The score is a practice aid, not an offer probability or employer decision.”** Consider action **“Review my first CV scan”** rather than a hiring promise.

## 2. Put the one-scan free limit before upload

Starter offers one CV scan, five job checks/week, one tailoring and one mock/month. Show these beside the CTA, not only in pricing. A user shouldn't spend the free scan on a test file because the allowance was hidden. State reset rules and whether failed scans consume allowance according to actual behaviour.

## 3. Demonstrate one real, truthful improvement

Use a clearly fictional bullet with vague duty→specific scope/output. Don't add a fabricated result metric. Show the actual feedback explanation, not only a larger score. Label examples so they don't become customer testimonials.

## 4. Explain private document handling before upload

Publish the actual processing provider, storage/retention, deletion and access rules. Don't invent local-only or never-stored claims. Offer a sample CV so a visitor can understand the report before sending personal history. Keep resumes out of session recordings.

## 5. Make the paid boundary precise

Current Pro ₹399/month and Pass ₹1,499 once/90 days need clear inclusions, renewal/end dates and refund/delivery terms before payment. The Pass's “unlimited” claim should disclose genuine fair-use or feature limits if any. Don't present a mock-interview allowance as proof of interview outcomes.

## 6. Test the full first scan and failed cases

Use your own fictional CV to check signup return, accepted file types, parsing errors, report delivery and failure recovery. Verify how the allowance behaves on errors. If the private flow fails, fix that stage before distribution; an auth form alone doesn't establish a working CV product.

## 7. Make mobile/shared cards discreet

Title: **“Kriya — clearer CV evidence for freshers.”** Description: **“One free scan with transparent limits. Practical feedback, not an offer guarantee.”** No private scores or CV details in previews. Test phone upload, text zoom, keyboard and report readability on a slow connection.
