# Kriya — Video scripts

Use a fictional CV/role. No real contact details, employment claims or private candidate score.

## A. One defensible revision — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Don't fix a vague CV with a fake number.”**

2–15: Show fictional duty-only bullet and actual supported feedback. “What did this person really do, produce or investigate?”

15–28: Write a truthful scope/output version. “If impact wasn't measured, say the real scope. Don't invent a percentage.”

28–38: Show current one-free-scan limit. “Use the report thoughtfully; Starter isn't unlimited rescanning.”

38–45: “Kriya. Work you can explain. kriyaonline.in.” Main demo; show live feedback only after the actual scan works.

## B. The missing detail — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Worked on what, exactly?”**

2–14: Change a fictional generic line into a concrete task/output without invented results. “That is something an interviewer can ask about.”

14–20: “Clearer evidence, not louder wording.” URL. First clip.

## C. Not a probability — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Seventy isn't a 70% chance of an offer.”**

2–12: Show a clearly illustrative score/report. “A tool's score is a feedback model. The useful question is what you understand and improve.”

12–20: “Keep the work truthful.” URL. Second clip; don't imply a specific employer uses the score.

## D. One means one — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Read the free allowance before uploading.”**

2–13: Show actual Starter limits. “One CV scan; published limits for job checks, tailoring and mocks.”

13–20: “No surprise unlimited claim. Check the current plan.” URL. Third clip; no fake checkout/offer letter.
