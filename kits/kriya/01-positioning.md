## Your first person

An adult fresher with one real role in mind and a CV describing duties more clearly than evidence. They have a project they can explain but don't know how to express what they actually did. Start with one truthful bullet, not a promise to “beat every ATS.”

**Pitch:** Find one weak part of your CV, improve it honestly, and practise explaining the work.

**Bio:** Better evidence before another application.

The wedge is CV→specific fix→a short practice answer for that same project. No invented impact metrics, employers, skills or interview stories. If a student didn't measure an outcome, help them describe scope honestly instead of creating a number.

[Resume Worded's free checker](https://resumeworded.com/score) is a real $0 entry alternative. A campus career adviser is another route where already available. Kriya must win on a clear fresher-specific improvement loop and transparent free limits, not a false claim that it is the only free score.

Target stays **1,000 signups**: unique real accounts, beside **a completed CV scan and one user-understood truthful revision**. Scores, applications sent and interview offers are different stages. Don't attribute employment to the app from a tiny uncontrolled sample.
