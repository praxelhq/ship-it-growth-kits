# COMMIT&KEEP — Measurement

**Assigned target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Today the public entry is a waitlist, so report **new consented waitlist registrations as a separate prelaunch count**. Do not call them live paid members, verified gym users or completed adoption. When the actual service opens, count genuine new onboarded consumer accounts and report activation beside them. Exclude duplicate emails, queue checks, tests and repeated form attempts.

**Live activation:** a real adult member understands the fee/data terms, registers the actual supported gym and completes a verified visit. A pending/failed location reading is not a verified day. Refund completion is a later separate event after its earning window; no thirty-day growth plan should imply all future refunds have already arrived.
