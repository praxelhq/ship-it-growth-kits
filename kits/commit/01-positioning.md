# COMMIT&KEEP — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.commitkeep.me/ · **Category:** Gym accountability · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/commit

## Today you have a waitlist, not a verified refund service

Actual 29 October 2026 headline: **“Show up. Get paid back.”** CTA **“Join the waitlist”** leads to `/waitlist`, inspected in Chrome. It asks for name/email, with city and desired gym optional, and says rollout is city-by-city/gym-by-gym. No form was submitted, active city confirmed, paid subscription started, gym check-in verified or refund received in this research. Do not market a working paid service merely because its terms describe one.

The advertised model is **US$29/month**, with **$0.50 returned per verified local calendar day during the first 28 days**, maximum **$14 per payment**, issued as one refund after the window. Arithmetic: 16 verified days × $0.50 = $8 back, leaving $21 of the base fee. Even the maximum leaves $15 of the base fee. This is money returned from a fee, not profit for exercising. Refund policy mentions checkout taxes; do not turn the base $15–$29 range into a verified all-inclusive local total.

Start with an adult who already has an independent gym routine and wants to evaluate a paid accountability mechanism. They need to understand the real cost, verification limits, location use and cancellation before a commitment. Do not target someone through body shame or prescribe daily training to chase the maximum refund.

**Pitch:** A planned paid gym-accountability service returning part of its monthly fee for verified visits.

**Bio:** Join the city-by-city waitlist. A partial fee refund, not exercise income.

[Strava's US pricing](https://www.strava.com/pricing) lists US$11.99/month or $79.99/year plus tax for its different activity features; it is not the same verification/refund contract. [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for a personal self-log. COMMIT&KEEP needs to justify its paid commitment and trustworthy verification/refund operation, not claim it is the only way to track consistency.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups** as the assigned target, with live-service activation beside it. Report waitlist registrations as a distinct prelaunch measure, never activated subscribers or verified gym users. Once available, activation is a verified real visit after informed onboarding. Gym partners are an operational dependency, not a replacement target of ten businesses.
