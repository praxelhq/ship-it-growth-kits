# COMMIT&KEEP — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Put prelaunch status beside the headline

Current heading **“Show up. Get paid back.”**, CTA **“Join the waitlist”**. The Chrome-inspected waitlist says rollout is gym-by-gym and does not confirm an active area. Add **“Prelaunch: join the waitlist for your area. No paid service or verified refunds yet confirmed here.”** Update only when the actual operational status changes. Do not imply the waitlist itself checks gym visits.

## 2. State the arithmetic, including its limits

Replacement supporting copy: **“Planned base fee: US$29/month. Earn back $0.50 per verified local day during the first 28 days, maximum $14 per payment. Your base cost remains $15–$29.”** The refund policy mentions checkout taxes while the homepage says nothing beyond the monthly price. Reconcile the actual tax-inclusive/exclusive total before opening payment; do not hide that ambiguity or invent a local rupee total.

## 3. Explain verification before location permission

Terms describe foreground check-in at one registered gym, with an accepted location reading and one count per local day. Provide a plain explanation and failure/review path before any eventual permission prompt. No background tracking is claimed by privacy, but this was not tested. Do not promise every real visit will verify or ask for location as part of waitlist acquisition.

## 4. Separate cancellation from earned refunds

The policy says cancellation stops future monthly charges; already-paid base fees are not automatically refunded, while earned days retain the payment's earning window. Make that distinction readable before commitment. Use the actual policy and final working cancellation flow; no refund or cancellation was executed here.

## 5. Minimise waitlist friction and expose timing honestly

Actual form requires name/email; city/gym are optional. Keep them optional, explain the use and do not collect schedules. Publish launch availability only from real operational readiness, never fake queue urgency. Check mobile form errors and confirmation. Privacy mentions Clarity: exclude location, identity, payment and gym details from replay. A real model explanation beats an invented refund screenshot.
