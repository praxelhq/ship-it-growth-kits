# Proofwall — Landing-page fixes

Read public page 29 October 2026. Headline **“Let your happy customers sell for you.”** CTAs **“Get started”** / **“Create your wall”**, leading to `/login`. No numeric price or authenticated collection/embed test established.

1. **Explain the actual workflow.** Replacement support under current headline: **“Collect written or video feedback, review each submission and display approved testimonials on your own site.”** Do not imply guaranteed sales.
2. **Make permissions visible.** Current accountless-link promise addresses friction, not consent. Replacement contributor note: **“Choose what you permit the business to publish. Review exact attribution and media before submitting.”** Implement actual controls before promising them; link privacy/removal route.
3. **Separate approval from verification.** Replacement wall caption: **“Selected customer testimonials published with permission.”** Don't imply independent audit, representative ratings or authenticity verification merely because the owner approved a card.
4. **Show the exact free/paid offer.** No public numeric amount was verified. Replacement entry block: **“Review current collection, media and embed limits before creating your wall.”** Populate configured amounts/limits before paid promotion; don't invent a free-forever plan.
5. **Show a real embed and removal.** Current one-code promise needs a harmless end-to-end test. Replacement CTA: **“Preview a labelled sample wall.”** Test approved versus unapproved visibility, second device, revoked content, caching and website layout. A copied code snippet is not proof installation succeeded.
6. **Phone/media states.** Test contributor link, consent, slow upload, retry, owner review and responsive embed. Never require a face/video for a written story. Keep unpublished submissions private and check access boundaries. No private content or recording links in analytics.

Gate: genuine permission survives collection, display and removal. If the embed does not work, repair it before asking owners to move real customer stories.
