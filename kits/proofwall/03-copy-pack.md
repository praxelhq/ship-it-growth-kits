# Proofwall — Copy pack

## 1. Owner opener

Hi — after a client project finishes, how do you collect a testimonial and confirm exactly where the client is happy for it to appear?

I build Proofwall. It gives clients a collection link and lets you review submissions before showing them on your own site. Would a harmless sample collection-and-removal walkthrough help? No client list or real testimonial needed for the first conversation.

## 2. Interested-owner email

**Subject: A client story with clear publication permission**

The first test is small: submit a sample, approve the exact words, show it on a test page and remove it again. Before real use, confirm the customer's wording, attribution, photo/video permission and publication channels.

The public page describes written/video collection without a contributor account and an embed for your site. Actual account, upload and embed behavior still need your own check; confirm current pricing before committing. https://proofwall.click/

This is a selected testimonial wall, not a representative all-customer rating. If irrelevant, tell me and I will stop.

## 3. LinkedIn post

A testimonial request should ask for permission, not just praise.

Can we use these exact words? With which name or business attribution? Is the photo or video included? On which page? What happens if the customer wants it removed?

That is the workflow I am testing in Proofwall: collect, review, publish and respect the permission behind the story.

The demonstration is fictional. A real wall should contain real submissions, not invented proof. https://proofwall.click/

## 4. Introduction ask

Do you know an independent web-design business collecting feedback from completed clients? Could you ask whether a sample testimonial workflow would help? Please don't send their client list, private feedback or contact details without permission.

## 5. Agreed owner check

Would you test a harmless submission on a page you control? We can check approval, exact wording, attribution and removal on a second device before asking a customer for real content. You decide the publication request; no one needs to give a positive statement to participate.

Optional owner-to-client request after permission is ready: “Would you describe your actual experience in your own words? Please choose what attribution and media, if any, you permit us to publish. No positive review is required. We will confirm the exact public use with you.”

One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop on no. Never reuse a negative/private message as public proof or strip a disclosure from a sample.
