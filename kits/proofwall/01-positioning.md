# Proofwall — Positioning

**Live:** https://proofwall.click/ · **Category:** Business testimonial collection · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/proofwall

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Let your happy customers sell for you.”** Public **“Get started”** / **“Create your wall”** entries lead to `/login`. The page describes written/video collection through an accountless customer link, owner approval and one-code embedding. No account, submission, approval or embed was created; no public numeric price was established.

Start with a small independent web-design business that has completed real client projects and controls its own website. The owner has customers willing to decide whether to give a public testimonial. Your first job is one permissioned submission, reviewed accurately and displayed where the customer agreed—not a wall full of invented enthusiasm.

**Pitch: Collect a genuine client story, approve its exact public use and show it on your own site.** Short version: **A real customer's words, with permission.**

The free manual alternative is an email request plus an owner-written website section, with a [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) consent/publication log. Proofwall must simplify collection, approval and removal, not imply testimonials cannot be published manually. Never invent names, client photos, metrics, endorsement or a review that was never submitted.

Owner approval is editorial control, not independent verification. A selected testimonial wall is not a representative review average. Do not present only selected praise as an unbiased all-customer rating. Keep permission for wording, attribution, photo/video and channels distinct; no incentive for a positive statement. This is an owned-site workflow, not automatic posting to Google reviews or a claim to meet another platform's rules.

Test a harmless owned submission through approval, embed, revocation/removal and second-device viewing. Check upload privacy, spam/error handling and phone readability before real client material. **Target: 10 real business customers**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each needs own authorized business data, independent recent use and privately known owner name/phone. Testimonials, contributors, wall views and client projects do not become extra software customers.
