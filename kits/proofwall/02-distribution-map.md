# Proofwall — Distribution map

**Owner outreach first.** Start with businesses that have completed work and website control; do not recruit unrelated people to populate demo reviews.

| Route | Action and boundary | Input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing independent web-design businesses | Qualify real completed client work and an editable owned website. Use the business's actual contact honestly. | Ten four-firm blocks = 40;03 #1/#2. |
| Two designer introductions | Ask a known operator to forward a sample invitation, never disclose a client list. | Days 5,16;#4. Upside only. |
| Own LinkedIn | Show exact permission and public-display choices with a fictional submission. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST;#3. Assumed 100–400 impressions/0–2 qualified visits each, excluded from base. |
| One owner-controlled embed check | Owner tests its own harmless record, removal and phone display. | Day 12 conditional;#5. Existing-trial support. |

Blocks Days 4,5,8,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 total 40. Base assumptions 40 ×50% conversation ×70% own-site setup ×70% independent recent use =9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation,4.9. This is a forecast to test, not conversion evidence. Posts and introductions add no guaranteed customers.

Keep the list distinct from FirmQuote's imminent scope-proposal owners. This buyer must have completed projects and a consent/publication job. One organizer gets one primary invitation per week across tools. No newsletter placement, designer-group promotion permission or platform partnership was verified. Ask first, stop on decline, one relevant follow-up after seven days.

Do not contact a designer's clients to acquire the designer. No scraping reviews, copying third-party profiles, buying testimonials, automated DMs or asking students to impersonate customers. Public sample quotes must remain obviously fictional. A directory listing is research, not permission to publish a person's identity.
