# Proofwall — Video scripts

Use an owned fictional submission clearly labelled sample. No real customer face, name, company logo or private feedback without specific permission. Record your screen and voice.

## A. From submitted to permitted display —65 seconds

0–3 s: fictional quote beside permission choices. **“Can you publish this exact story?”**

3–18 s: actual contributor route with harmless text. “A link makes collection simpler. Permission still matters.”

18–32 s: actual owner review and attribution, if verified. “Check wording and where it may appear.” Keep sample label visible.

32–47 s: approved content on your own test page. “This is a selected testimonial, not every customer's rating.” If embed isn't verified, show the missing step honestly.

47–57 s: actual removal and second-device recheck. “Removal needs to work too.” Do not stage success.

57–65 s: URL/current-offer boundary. “Test your own harmless record first. proofwall.click.”

## B. Attribution hook —20 seconds

0–3 s: name/photo choices. **“Permission for words isn't automatically permission for a face.”**

3–13 s: exact approved attribution in a sample. “Confirm what the person agreed to show.”

13–20 s: sample wall and URL. No invented customer identity.

## C. Removal hook —25 seconds

0–3 s: sample testimonial removed. **“Would the old page still show it?”**

3–16 s: recheck your actual embed in a fresh session. “Test the published page, not just the owner dashboard.”

16–25 s: actual outcome and URL. “Respect the permission through the whole workflow.” Label untested behavior rather than fabricate it.
