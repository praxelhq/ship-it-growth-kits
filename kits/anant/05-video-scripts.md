# Anant — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 45 seconds · class demo and homepage

**First two seconds:** “What does it sell?”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–10s: public sample, first card. | Start with what it sells, not a logo you recognise. |
| Middle | 10–30s: show the actual card format and source/date location. | A short explanation still needs a source and a date. |
| Payoff | 30–40s: show note/quiz only if working, otherwise sample next-step. | Now explain it in your own words; reading is not the same as recalling. |
| Close | Hold `https://anant.website/c/eternal` legibly. | Try a sample before an account. |

Do not repeat current leadership or market numbers without a fresh official source check.

## B — the problem hook · 20 seconds · Shorts

**First two seconds:** “Recognise the name?”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–5s: sample company title. | What does it actually sell? |
| Middle | 5–14s: one legible sample card. | Anant begins with the basic explanation. |
| Payoff | 14–18s: sample route. | No account needed to inspect this sample. |
| Close | Hold `https://anant.website/c/eternal` legibly. | Education only. |

The clip teaches the format, not an investing opinion.

## C — the honest comparison · 25 seconds · LinkedIn maker post

**First two seconds:** “Short still needs sources.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–7s: sample source/date area. | Company details move. |
| Middle | 7–18s: show a source check with no private data. | The concise version should let you inspect the original. |
| Payoff | 18–22s: sample link and education label. | Short is not the same as certain. |
| Close | Hold `https://anant.website/c/eternal` legibly. | Tell me which part remains unclear. |

Show a real source route, not a fabricated “verified” indicator.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture the no-account sample link,1/5 label, a legible card, real source/date controls and the actual recall flow if available. Do not invent company facts for an attractive clip.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
