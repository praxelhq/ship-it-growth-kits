# Anant — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://anant.website/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Understand any company in 60 seconds”**; the action is **“Try one now”**. No paid price was displayed on the observed entry or sample. Do not promise an unlimited free plan or invent a subscription. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a student asking what a company actually does before creating another account. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Lead with the sample

**Current:** The public entry asks for email/password but also has “Try one now”.

**Write this instead:**

> Try one company first — no account needed for the sample. Create an account when you want to keep going.

**Verify:** Make /c/eternal the clear first-use option; do not remove an existing no-account route.

## Fix 2 — Narrow the time claim

**Current:** “Understand any company in 60 seconds” can sound exhaustive.

**Write this instead:**

> A first explanation in five short parts. Check what you understood with a note and quiz.

**Verify:** If keeping sixty seconds, show its scope and actual tested reading time rather than a universal guarantee.

## Fix 3 — Date company claims

**Current:** The sample includes current leadership information.

**Write this instead:**

> Sources checked on the date shown. Company details can change; open the official source for the latest.

**Verify:** Add real checked dates and official sources, not an invented verification badge.

## Fix 4 — Show the next step

**Current:** The sample begins at “What it sells”,1/5.

**Write this instead:**

> Part 1 of 5: what it sells. Read the next part, then write your own short explanation.

**Verify:** Test the entire card/note/quiz flow on a phone before claiming it is seamless.

## Fix 5 — Make the account offer explicit

**Current:** No paid plan or precise allowance was visible at entry.

**Write this instead:**

> Before creating an account, see what it saves and whether any limits apply.

**Verify:** Replace with verified current limits; do not invent a free-forever offer.

## Fix 6 — Keep education in the preview

**Current:** The metadata says education only.

**Write this instead:**

> Anant — a first company explanation, not investment advice. Try one sample before signing up.

**Verify:** Use the same boundary in social preview and any shared company card.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
