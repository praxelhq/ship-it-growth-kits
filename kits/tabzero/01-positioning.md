# TabZero — Positioning

**Live:** https://tabzero.online/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/tabzero

**Show why someone should pay for triage when Chrome already groups tabs.** Do not sell native grouping as your invention, and do not treat a grouped tab as safely archived or backed up.

The [actual headline](https://tabzero.online/) is “Close the tabs. Keep the context.” Rendered purchase controls say “Get TabZero Pro—$2.99.” The page describes Gemini-assisted categorization into Action, Read Later, Reference and Done, and native Chrome groups. The footer also claims private/local in-browser processing. Public page/controls checked 29 October 2026; checkout, extension installation, permissions and AI data flow were not tested.

## Your first person

An adult student builder with one live research task and fifteen open public tabs, who cannot tell which need action and which are only reference. They do not need a permanent reading library or another dashboard. They want a reviewable first pass that keeps the right task context in the tab bar.

Use a dedicated demo window with non-sensitive public pages. Do not ask a stranger to expose their real inbox, client portal or private research tabs just to see the product.

## Pitch

> Sort this task's open tabs into a reviewable next-action view, then choose what to keep open.

Short version:

> Open-tab triage, not another bookmark pile.

The wedge is a single current task: inspect suggested categories, correct mistakes and create the actual groups. Activation is one real reviewed grouping in the installed extension. Retention is another independently initiated task/window session on a later day.

## Actual offer and alternatives

The page advertises Pro at $2.99. A billing period, final tax total, exact entitlement and usable installer/store URL were not verified; do not invent “lifetime,” a free plan or a subscription period.

## The trust gap

“Gemini AI” and “100% Private, Local In-Browser Processing” need an exact explanation. Are titles/URLs processed locally, or sent to a provider? The public copy does not establish the architecture. Do not assert a leak or invent an offline guarantee; verify the actual release and explain it before promotion.

Your target remains **1,000 signups**, defined for an extension as valid new installations/first launches, with completed reviewed groupings beside it. Purchase clicks and a landing-page demo are not installations. At a paid-only entry, this is a demanding target; the first small-circle plan does not honestly forecast it.
