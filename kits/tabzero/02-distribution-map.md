## Honest composition

Twenty personal invitations, two sessions, eight clips and one listing model roughly **8–69 valid first launches before overlap**, assuming the paid/install path is usable. That is not sufficient evidence for 1,000. At assumed 10% qualified-visitor-to-install, the target needs 10,000 qualified visitors. A landing click or checkout start does not close the gap.

Use Days 24–27 for your current-tab workflow, distinct from Kept/Boomerang/Minutes'collection/retrieval/reading exercises. Separate contacts and actual dates; leave seven days between primary invitations to the same organizer. No paid acquisition or forced referral loop.
