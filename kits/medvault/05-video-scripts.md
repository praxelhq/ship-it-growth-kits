# MedVault — Video scripts

Use only a clearly marked synthetic document under an authorised test account. No record/upload/share/export flow was executed in this research. Before filming those actions, the owner must verify the permitted access and actual functions. Hide all tokens, identities and account details.

## A. Find one copy — 35 seconds, conditional product walkthrough

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Marked synthetic record. | “This is a sample, not a patient record.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual tested organise/open path. | “MedVault aims to keep your own copies organised, so you can find the file you mean to show.” |
| 14–25 s | Actual selected-record sharing preview, token hidden. | “Check exactly which records a link includes before you send it.” |
| 25–31 s | Actual expiry/revoke controls, only if tested. | “Know when sharing ends and how to stop it.” |
| 31–35 s | URL/invite-only. | “Access is currently invite-only.” |

## B. Entry without records — 20 seconds, usable now

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual invite-only notice. | “You need an invitation first.” |
| 3–14 s | Public entry only, no credentials. | “MedVault is testing personal record organisation. You do not need to send me a real record to ask about it.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Inspect the entry. Keep your information private.” |

## C. A link is access — 20 seconds, conditional Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Synthetic selection, token hidden. | “A live link gives access.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual tested scope/expiry and revoke result. | “Select deliberately, check the recipient and test the end of sharing with sample content first.” |
| 14–20 s | URL/invite-only. | “Do not publish a live record link.” |

Hold A/C until true. No “hack-proof”, lifetime guarantee, clinical outcome or simulated doctor reaction belongs in these clips.
