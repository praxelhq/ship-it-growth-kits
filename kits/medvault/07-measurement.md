# MedVault — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one new non-test account created through the permitted invite process. An access request, code inquiry, loading view or your synthetic test account is not a signup. **Activation:** a person successfully retrieves their own intended record copy; verify sharing scope/expiry/revocation separately with synthetic test data before promoting those claims. No medical outcome is inferred.

## Baseline and privacy

Record actual invited accounts, successful first retrievals, returns, permitted invite capacity, upload/view/share failures and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/medvault. Public research did not establish private totals. Do not copy diagnoses, documents, record names, dates, share URLs or recipient identities into a growth log.

Actual consent notice says Clarity is sign-in-only and never inside the vault. Verify that boundary, input masking and decline behaviour; do not place replay in record/share/account views. Aggregate counts/errors can diagnose conversion without any medical content. Never inspect another person's files merely because a browser or link makes them available.

The forecast assumes 14–24 distinct permitted accounts, far below 1,000; unavailable access means zero acquisition. Server-encryption, export, deletion and link controls remain claims until actually tested. Do not interpret successful organisation as improved care, avoided retesting or clinical safety proof.
