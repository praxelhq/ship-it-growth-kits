# EATME — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** For the functioning anonymous wheel, count unique genuine spin sessions, with **result reached and a specific meal/LET'S EAT choice** beside it. A streak day, repeated spin, sample recording or second person watching one phone is not automatically another signup.

## Day 0

Record latest date/traffic/rank from the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/eatme). In actual analytics record sessions, first spins, results, choices, repeat days and optional accounts. The kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured**, not zero. Exclude the writer's successful anonymous test.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and mask account/personal fields. No private dietary/health data is needed to count a meal choice. Use privacy-consistent aggregate session measurement and state its limits; don't invent unique humans from raw spin events.

## Funnel

`entry_started → spin_started → result_displayed → meal_chosen → later_day_used`.

Also record alternative choice and spin-again count. Ask a small consented sample whether they actually ate the chosen meal; don't equate clicking a button with eating, ordering or a nutritional benefit.

## Honest forecast

`02` budgets ten pairs, two circles, three clips, one small demo and one listing, suggesting roughly 34–150 sessions before overlap under guessed rates. A thousand isn't established by those channels. At hypothetical 70% result/choice completion, 1,000 genuine starts would yield 700 choices; measure your own rates before using them as a plan.

These are chosen experiment checks, not nutrition or behavioural-health measures. Save course readings with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour snapshots are not additive acquisition.
