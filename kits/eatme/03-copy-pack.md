## #5 — Maker listing

**Tagline:** One spin, a small set of meal ideas, a decision.

EATME is a meal-choice wheel with FEEL GOOD, TREAT ME and SURPRISE ME modes. The basic anonymous flow returns a suggestion and alternatives. It is not a medical diet plan, food delivery promise or verified nutrition service. I'd like feedback on whether people choose a real next meal after the result.

**Maker comment:** Please try a spin if you're genuinely stuck, and tell me where the suggestions stop matching what you can eat. Repeated spins from one person won't be counted as new users.
