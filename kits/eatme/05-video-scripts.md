# EATME — Video scripts

Use your own phone recording. No private diet details, fake nutrition transformation or staged delivery order.

## A. Real decision flow — 35 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“We've discussed dinner for too long.”**

2–10: Open EATME, choose an actual mode and spin. “One small set, not another menu to scroll.”

10–20: Hold the real result and alternatives long enough to read. “The winner isn't a command. Pick what you can actually eat.”

20–28: Choose an option and explain the actual LET'S EAT action only after testing it. If not verified, stop at the choice and say “This is the meal I'd choose.”

28–35: “One decision, then close the tab. eatmewheel.in.” Use as the homepage/listing demo.

## B. Dinner debate — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Anything is fine. Except that.”**

2–10: Show fictional text choices, then the real wheel result. “We needed a shortlist, not more suggestions.”

10–15: “Pick the winner or an alternative. EATME.” URL. First clip.

## C. Not the winner — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Don't want it? Don't spin forever.”**

2–14: Show actual alternatives and pick one. “There's another option on the same wheel. Choose what fits your ingredients and preferences.”

14–20: “A choice helper, not a diet prescription.” URL. Second clip.

## D. Close the tab — 15 seconds

0–2: **“The best result is leaving.”**

2–10: Real choice screen, then put the phone down. “We picked a meal. The app's job is done.”

10–15: “EATME. One spin, then dinner.” URL. Third clip; if you show a meal, make it the actual chosen one without a fake outcome claim.
