# EATME — Distribution map

**Catch the dinner-decision moment in existing roommate/campus circles.** A midday startup launch is less relevant than a clear example at 19:00 IST. No paid food sponsorships.

All forecasts are unmeasured assumptions for the scheduled volume.

| Route | Execution / rule | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ten consenting roommate/friend pairs | Ask them to use it for a real meal choice, not just tap for you. Count people/sessions carefully; one phone isn't two signups automatically. | 10 pairs × assumed 60–80% try = 6–8 decision sessions; perhaps 4–6 chosen meals. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned hostel/class circles | Admin-approved one-time message at dinner time; no unrelated group blasts. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 20–40% spin = 16–32 sessions; completion unproved. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Dinner debate, result/alternatives, stop-spinning choice at 19:00 IST. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% qualified start = 6–90 sessions. | #3 |
| 4. One informal dinner demo | In a place you already have permission to be, no food purchase/event spend required. Invite 5–10 willing adults, don't film without consent. | 3–8 sessions if the demo actually happens. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing; show actual anonymous working spin, not a staged order. No vote exchanges. | Assume 5–20 visits and 3–12 spin sessions. | #5 |

The base scenario is roughly 34–150 sessions before overlap, not an evidenced thousand. A viral clip is upside, not a budgeted certainty. Deduplicate repeat spinners and your own demos.

## Communities and accounts

No current food-subreddit commercial permission or food-newsletter placement was verified. Don't post tool links into recipe/help communities as if they invited promotion. Reply to real “can't decide dinner” conversations on your own profiles with one useful suggestion, then link only if asked.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is an optional free directory after mobile/result checks, with zero forecast until observed and no upvote requests. [Wheel of Names](https://wheelofnames.com/) is a real comparison, not a place to advertise. Coordinate dinner-circle dates with Kitchen Commune or other cohort food tools; one group shouldn't receive competing dinner pitches in the same week.
