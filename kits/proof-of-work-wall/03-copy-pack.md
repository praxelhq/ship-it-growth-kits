# Proof of Work Wall — Copy pack

**1. Builder invitation**

“Do you have one marketing experiment you can describe publicly, including something that didn't work? I built Proof-of-Work Wall. A brick shows the brief, your role, what you did and the evidence you actually have. Core posting is free. I want feedback on whether the format makes your work clearer, not a promise it will get you hired. Can I send the link? Please leave out client/private information.”

**2. Admin request and accepted bulletin**

“May I offer one optional case-study critique in your adult marketing circle? I built the wall. People can use work they own or a clearly labelled class exercise; no private client figures or contact list. I'll post once only if you approve.”

Accepted: “Bring one experiment, including a flop. Write the brief, your specific action, the actual result and the limitation. If there is no trustworthy number, say what you can evidence rather than inventing one. Proof-of-Work Wall is free for core posting: https://www.proofofworkwall.app/. I'm the builder. Publishing is optional; permission and visibility come first.”

**3. Owned maker post**

“A portfolio shouldn't turn every experiment into a win. I built Proof-of-Work Wall around a small case: what was the brief, what did you actually do, what happened, and what would you change? The current wall is starting from an honest empty state. No borrowed case studies or pretend recruiters. I'd rather have five real, inspectable cases than fifty polished claims nobody can check. Core posting is free. https://www.proofofworkwall.app/”

**4. Listing description**

“Proof-of-Work Wall helps students and early-career marketers publish short case studies and discover work. Core marketer/recruiter features are free; Pro is optional. Bricks are self-posted, not independently verified. I made the project and want feedback on the first publish/visibility flow and the usefulness of peer critique. Please share only work and evidence you have permission to publish. No hiring outcome is promised.”

**5. Hiring-professional email**

Subject: Would this marketing case format help you assess work?

“I built Proof-of-Work Wall and have a small set of permission-safe cases ready to review. Could you look at whether the brief/action/evidence format helps you understand a candidate's contribution? I'm asking for format feedback, not a job posting, endorsement or access to candidates. Please don't share private hiring information.”

Send only after real cases exist. Do not claim a set is ready if the wall remains empty.

**6. Consented follow-up**

“Did you publish a case you were comfortable making visible, and did another person give useful feedback on the work? No need to send private results or promise a testimonial. Where did publishing or visibility become unclear?”

Change the case/question between circle posts. Never name a reviewer or employer without permission.
