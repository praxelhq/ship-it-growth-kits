# Proof of Work Wall — Distribution map

**Creator supply before recruiter demand.** Reach hypotheses match 20 individual invitations, two circle bulletins and three owned posts in `06`. They are not historical performance.

| Route | Access and rules | Expected result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 known adult marketing builders | Ten personal invitations on Days 6/9. Invite only people with genuine work they may share; no forced public portfolio. | 6–12 accounts, 3–7 first bricks; #1. |
| Two existing marketing/project circles | Admin permission and different circles/weeks from CV tools. One accepted critique exercise/bulletin each; no contact list. | Combined 60–160 views, 3–10 accounts; #2. |
| Your [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) profile | Three original maker posts, ownership disclosed. No recruiter tagging, invented placements or engagement exchange. | Combined 90–360 impressions, 1–7 accounts; #3. |
| Two known hiring professionals, after real supply exists | Ask for feedback via a legitimate existing route. No candidate scraping or implied job. | 0–2 review conversations, zero forecast candidate accounts; #5. |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Optional free usable-product entry, current slot/prior listing checked. No vote requests; show real examples with permission. | 0–5 accounts, excluded from base; #4. |

Base: 10–29 candidate accounts before deduplication, perhaps 5–18 original bricks if the flow works. That is a small proof of use, not support for 1,000. No verified newsletter/editorial placement or marketing subreddit permission supplies extra reach. An existing organizer bulletin can carry #2 only if accepted.

Keep this distinct from Kriya's CV scoring and other career-evidence tools: one inspectable marketing case, not another resume upload or promise of employment. No organizer should receive multiple cohort tool pitches that week. Recruiter feedback is not a partnership or endorsement unless explicitly agreed.
