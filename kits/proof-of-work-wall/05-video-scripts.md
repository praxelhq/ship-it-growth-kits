# Proof of Work Wall — Video scripts

Use your own permission-safe work. No private client figures, fabricated recruiters or paid editing. Synthetic material must be labelled throughout.

**A. One inspectable brick, 45 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “The campaign can flop and still make a useful case study.” Show one original permitted experiment. 0–12s explain brief and your exact role. 12–25s show the real submission flow after testing it, hiding account details. 25–35s show evidence and limitations. Say, “These are the numbers I can support; this part is uncertain.” 35–45s show actual published result and visibility. End: “Core posting is free. Self-posted, not independently verified.” If publishing fails, show the blocker as a build update instead.

**B. Your contribution, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “What did you do, specifically?” Show fictional “We grew the brand” then “I wrote and tested two landing-page messages,” labelled synthetic. Say, “A reviewer should understand your part without guessing.” End: “No invented outcome needed.”

**C. Empty honestly, 20 seconds; owned post.** Hook: “An empty wall beats fake proof.” Show actual empty state if still current, then a labelled example structure. Say, “I'm starting with real work people may publish. A sample isn't a member, and a peer mark isn't independent verification.” End on the real site URL.

**D. Before public, 20 seconds; accepted circle.** Hook: “Would your client be comfortable seeing this?” Show a synthetic draft with a made-up private field redacted, then publish preview. Say, “Permission and visibility come before sharing. Explain your role without exposing someone else's information.” Demonstrate only controls actually implemented and tested.
