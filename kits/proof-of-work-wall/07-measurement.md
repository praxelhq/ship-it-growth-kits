# Proof of Work Wall — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real candidate/marketer accounts, with **a first original, permission-safe case-study brick published** beside it. Recruiter accounts, case count and contact views are separate. A peer mark does not establish the truth of all claims or a hiring outcome.

Day-0 account counts, published bricks, returners, leaderboard and analytics status are unknown. The public empty wall and “0 of 25” badge display are observed UI states, not an audited database total. Record actual authorized aggregate counts and date windows. [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) setup/masking was not verified; exclude private drafts, evidence uploads, contacts and account fields from replay and event payloads.

Track source→account success→draft→permission/visibility check→published first brick→useful real feedback→later voluntary return. Exclude founder/internal tests, synthetic examples and duplicate accounts. Publish minimum aggregate outcomes, never private candidate names or recruiter contact details. A hiring professional reviewing the format is not an employer partnership or job offer.

`06` has 20 builder invitations, two accepted circle bulletins and three owned posts. `02` guesses 10–29 candidate accounts before overlap, not 1,000. With a hypothetical 20% qualified-visit→account rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 qualified unique visits. Optional launch results are zero until observed; two recruiter review requests do not create a hidden candidate funnel.

W1: repair account/publish/permission confusion before reach. W2: if accounts stay blank, help people define one real contribution without writing fake success stories for them. W3: if cases exist but no useful feedback arrives, test a small critique format before recruiting more empty profiles. W4: expand only sources producing real cases and voluntary return. Report hiring only if a real, consented outcome is verified separately. Revenue requires actual live paid delivery, not reading the Pro page or clicking upgrade.
