## Your first person

A college commuter with one novel already chosen, twenty minutes of travel and an unfinished reading habit. They want the story, not productivity advice or an exam summary. Start with readers willing to compare the opening chapter to the source and tell you where the voice or meaning was lost.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Turn a book you have permission to use into a shorter story you can read on your commute.

For a bio or a short reply:

> Your book, retold as chapter-sized scrolls. First transformation free.

## Your opening use case

Use one short, rights-cleared classic and a single chapter. Show the original opening beside its Narra version, then ask which character motivation became less clear. That is a much stronger first test than claiming someone has “read” a whole novel after twenty minutes. Do not ask a campus group to upload textbooks, unpublished manuscripts or books they lack permission to transform.

## The real alternative

[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) offers free full texts without registration, mainly older works whose US copyright has expired; check rights where you are. It wins on completeness. Your test is whether a reader prefers a condensed narrative in the time available. A self-written reading note costs no extra software; do not pretend it preserves prose or dialogue the same way as the original. No unverified paid-summary-app price is needed to make this comparison.

## What you need to prove

Publish a labelled sample with the source edition, the chapter selected, the original passage and your edited output. Have five readers identify one preserved and one lost detail. Keep a factual error visible in your learning notes until it is fixed; do not turn a model’s confident retelling into evidence of fidelity.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
