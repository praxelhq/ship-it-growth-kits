# Narra — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 60 seconds · homepage and reading-circle follow-up

**First two seconds:** “What would you cut from this chapter?”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–15s: source edition and a rights-cleared chapter, labelled. | I’m using a chapter I have permission to transform. This is the source. |
| Middle | 15–38s: upload and a clearly labelled time cut to narrative cards. | Narra makes a shorter narrative. I’m checking event order and why the character acts, not just the word count. |
| Payoff | 38–55s: show source and output side by side. | This part survived. Here is a detail the shorter version leaves behind. |
| Close | Hold `https://narratale.site/` legibly. | Compare a sample, then decide whether the format is for you. |

Name the source edition; do not upload copyrighted material without permission or claim generation happened instantly.

## B — the problem hook · 20 seconds · Instagram Reel

**First two seconds:** “A shorter book is still missing something.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–5s: chapter title beside one output card. | Here is the same scene in a different format. |
| Middle | 5–14s: point to one changed or omitted detail. | This is the detail I’d ask a reader to check. |
| Payoff | 14–18s: scroll one legible card. | If the story still pulls you in, try the first transformation free. |
| Close | Hold `https://narratale.site/` legibly. | Narra. A format experiment, not a perfect substitute. |

Use the actual sample, not the landing page’s example reader quote.

## C — the honest comparison · 30 seconds · LinkedIn and campus screen

**First two seconds:** “Twenty minutes on a commute. What would you read?”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–8s: phone with a sample chapter, no moving vehicle filming. | I want a story in the time I actually have. |
| Middle | 8–21s: show readable cards and progress. | Narra shortens the narrative into chapter cards. The original still has more to give. |
| Payoff | 21–27s: show free-first-book and Plus price. | One transformation is free; the paid plan is a monthly choice, not required for this test. |
| Close | Hold `https://narratale.site/` legibly. | Try the sample before you choose a plan. |

Do not promise a measured twenty-minute completion unless the actual book was timed.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Record source title, a readable original paragraph, the upload boundary, three output cards, the progress control and the pricing boundary. Keep source and generated version visually distinct.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
