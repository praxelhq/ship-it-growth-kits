# Narra — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://narratale.site/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Read less. Feel more.”**; the action is **“Transform my first book”**. The first transformation is free. Plus is displayed at ₹299/month with unlimited transformations and additional reading features. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a commuter wondering whether the shorter version is worth their attention. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Put fidelity beside the promise

**Current:** “Read less. Feel more.” is memorable but does not explain what is preserved.

**Write this instead:**

> Your book, retold in shorter chapter stories. Compare a sample with its source before uploading.

**Verify:** Use one source-linked sample with a clear account of what is shortened. Do not claim a universal reading-time reduction.

## Fix 2 — Make upload expectations explicit

**Current:** The page says supported PDF or EPUB and sends “Transform my first book” to /start.

**Write this instead:**

> Choose a PDF or EPUB you have permission to use. Your first transformation is free.

**Verify:** Before upload, show real supported limits and data handling after checking the implementation; do not invent a file-size limit.

## Fix 3 — Remove example quotes from proof

**Current:** The page labels proposed reader reactions “Example · Coming soon”.

**Write this instead:**

> See a sample transformation. We’re still collecting reader feedback.

**Verify:** Keep the current disclosure or remove the quote cards until real permissioned reviews exist. Never relabel examples as testimonials.

## Fix 4 — State the payment boundary

**Current:** Free and ₹299/month Plus appear after a long format explanation.

**Write this instead:**

> First book free. Plus is ₹299/month for more transformations and reading features; review the plan before subscribing.

**Verify:** Test the transition from a free completion to an upgrade and confirm cancellation language matches the current terms.

## Fix 5 — Offer the chapter before the atmosphere

**Current:** Fonts, themes and rain/fireplace ambience get substantial space.

**Write this instead:**

> Read one sample chapter first. Change the mood when you know the format suits you.

**Verify:** On a narrow phone screen, reach a readable sample without hunting through ambience controls.

## Fix 6 — Share the specific reading job

**Current:** “400 pages → 20 minutes” can be mistaken for a guaranteed reading time.

**Write this instead:**

> Narra — a shorter way through a story. Try a chapter-sized sample; first transformation free.

**Verify:** Use a readable chapter card for the link preview. Keep original length, transformed length and measured time separate if you publish numbers.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
