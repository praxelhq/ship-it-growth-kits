# Narra — 30-day calendar

Day 1 is the first working day after the conversion route is usable. If the activity is unavailable, finish the recovery work in `04` before starting the public calendar. Build days and posting days are separate below. Use 09:00 IST for a student/professional post or 19:30 IST for a leisure clip as a first experiment, not a claim of a universal best time.

Every day: spend 15 minutes answering existing questions and 10 minutes recording starts, activations and the source. On posting days, stay available for the first hour. If support exceeds your time budget, postpone the next post.

## Keep the month honest

Use Days 10–13 for the first shared-campus reading invitation. Infolio, Footnotes and C1’s reading products serve different jobs: explainers, curated reading and community. Ask organizers which single activity they want; do not send three “read more” pitches to the same group.

Check the weekly gates in `07` on Days 7, 14, 21 and 28. If a public post produces the wrong audience, do not paste the same one elsewhere. Rewrite around the exact first use in `01`. If a partner has not agreed to a session, replace that day's event with five individual opt-in tests; never report an unheld event as reach.

A promising post changes the next two days: answer the people it brought, record the objections and help them finish the activity. It does not justify a week of unattended posting. End Day 30 with the number you can verify and the distribution gap you still need to solve.
