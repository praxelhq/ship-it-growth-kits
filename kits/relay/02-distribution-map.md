FuelBar's preferred builder window is Day 10; Relay's Day 18; SnipText's Day 24. These are internal coordination preferences, not booked platform slots. One organization receives one primary product invitation per week. Moderator rules override all cohort windows.

## Honest first-month scenario

Before the release gate, expect a **two-to-five-person reviewed-handoff experiment**, not a thousand installs. After a reliable signed build and accurate support docs, owned posts plus a conditional launch might yield **10–40 activated users** under the guessed ranges. A larger pickup is upside; the target gap must remain explicit. Don't add the same builder across DEV, Peerlist and a test call as three different users.
