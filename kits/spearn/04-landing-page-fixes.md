# Spearn — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from the live homepage, app and cards route. “Get Started” reaches `/app`; “Explore cards” reaches `/app/cards`. The app says “Welcome, Demo,” the catalogue says illustrative terms, and redemption is simulated. No real application, payment or redemption was performed.

## 1. Put the demo status before the promise

**Current:** “Every Spend Has a Return,” with a demo dashboard and “Credit card rewards, optimised.” The cards route is explicitly sample data.

Replace the headline with:

> **Understand the assumptions behind a reward estimate.**
>
> Explore fictional spending and illustrative card terms. Compare how fees and point values change the result. This is a sample learning workflow, not live issuer data.

Keep **“Sample / simulated”** visible on every ranking, recorded purchase and redemption result. Don't let a shared screenshot remove that context.

## 2. Rename the first action

**Current:** “Get Started” and “Explore cards.”

Use **“Explore the sample comparison”** with **“No real card connection or redemption.”** A sample can be genuinely useful when its scope is clear. Test the actual first card/fictional purchase loop before telling people it works; route access alone is not that test.

## 3. Show the arithmetic, not just net value

For one sample result, show the fee, categories, point-value assumption and exclusions/data limits. Label **“Illustrative estimate, not a promised return.”** Do not display a current-bank verification date unless someone actually checked its official terms. A brand name plus a polished chart isn't evidence the sample matches a real card today.

## 4. Separate simulated redemption from a marketplace

**Current:** “Redemption marketplace” is described as simulated with the user's balance.

Use **“Redemption simulation”** consistently. Proposed helper: **“No booking, voucher purchase or transfer happens here.”** Verify current behaviour before adding any real transaction action and explain its additional terms. Do not let a demo button look like an executed redemption.

## 5. Explain demo persistence and price

The writer saw no paid software amount and no authenticated personal account flow. Publish actual storage/device persistence and any price/limits before requesting personal information. Use **“Try fictional inputs first”** rather than ask for card numbers or statements. Don't claim balances are synced with a bank or safe to rely on as actual rewards.

## 6. Use issuer sources for any real data later

Before changing from illustrative to current comparisons, maintain exact issuer source, checked date, applicable conditions and a correction owner for every displayed term. Do not scrape a marketing headline and call that complete verification. If a term expires or cannot be checked, label it unavailable instead of silently retaining a stale ranking.

## 7. Phone and preview

Proposed title: **“Spearn — explore sample credit-card reward estimates.”** Description: **“Illustrative terms and simulated redemption. Inspect assumptions, then check the real issuer before a decision.”** Test actual previews, readable comparison tables and amount inputs on phones. No measured mobile/load defect was established. A fresh reviewer should immediately know the dashboard isn't a bank balance and the ranking isn't personalized financial advice.
