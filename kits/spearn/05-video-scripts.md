# Spearn — Video scripts

Fictional spending and sample terms only. Never show a card number, account statement, real bank login or invented reward redemption. Screen recording, phone microphone and readable captions are sufficient.

## A. Change one assumption —60 seconds

**Where:** landing page, Peerlist and literacy session. **Hook:** “This reward number changes when this assumption changes.”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|Sample estimate beside its input|This reward number changes when this assumption changes.|
|2–14 s|Actual catalogue's sample label|This is illustrative data, not live bank terms.|
|14–30 s|Enter fictional spending and inspect one comparison|I'm checking what the fee and point value do, not applying for a card.|
|30–43 s|Change one actual supported input; show result|A useful estimate tells me why it moved.|
|43–52 s|Show simulated redemption label|This doesn't book anything or move points.|
|52–60 s|URL and source-check reminder|Explore the sample at spearn.sbs. Check the issuer before a real decision.|

If the supported input/result doesn't work, don't simulate it; fix or narrow the video to the available sample.

## B. A demo isn't your balance —20 seconds

**Where:** owned feed. **Hook:** “These aren't my real points.” Show the labelled homepage/app demo and the empty sample account. Say, “A demonstration explains the workflow. It shouldn't look like a live bank connection.” End with the sample scope and founder disclosure, not a claim of earned rewards.

## C. The fee beside the total —25 seconds

**Where:** financial-literacy session and social post. **Hook:** “Don't hide the fee below the headline.” Use clearly fictional numbers and show the effect of one fee assumption. Say, “This teaches the relationship; a real card has actual terms, conditions and eligibility you still need to check.” Don't name a current best card from illustrative inputs.

## D. Simulated, not redeemed —25 seconds

**Where:** app help and launch gallery. **Hook:** “Nothing was booked here.” Show an actual simulated redemption screen and its label. Say, “The exercise helps understand a possible point value. It isn't a voucher, flight or transfer.” End with a source-check reminder and no spending inducement.

Capture sample label, input, assumptions, result and simulated-action confirmation. Keep “illustrative” visible in every crop so a short clip can't become a false financial claim.
