The [issuer's official site](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards) is a verification source, not a place to advertise or imply a partnership. Do not lift promotional rates without checking the exact current terms and scope. No current promotional permission in a named credit-card subreddit/group was verified, so none is scheduled.

Twenty warm invitations, eight owned posts, one conditional session and a directory listing might yield **10–30 completed sample comparisons**, removing overlap. This isn't an evidenced route to 1,000 first sessions. Increase reach only after the first users understand the assumptions and an actual organizer accepts distribution. A simulated high-value reward is not a customer outcome or a reason to raise the forecast.

Keep audience lists separate from FamilySubSaver's household-renewal exercise and StockIQ's source-reading education. One organization receives one primary product invitation per week; use actual approved dates, not a shared spreadsheet of private financial contacts.
