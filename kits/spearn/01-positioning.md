# Spearn — Positioning

**Live:** https://spearn.sbs/ · **Category:** Consumer rewards education · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/spearn

**Your current catalogue is a simulation, not live issuer data. Say that before the first recommendation.** The headline is “Every Spend Has a Return.” “Get Started” links to `/app`, which renders “Welcome, Demo”; “Explore cards” links to `/app/cards`. That page explicitly says “Sample catalogue — illustrative terms, not live issuer data.” The redemption route is described as simulated. No paid software amount was visible. No purchase, application, real account connection or redemption was performed by this audit.

## Your first user

An adult who already has a credit card and wants to understand how assumptions about fees, categories and point values change a reward estimate. They are not asking you to recommend more borrowing, guarantee a return or execute a real redemption. Start with a fictional monthly budget and one sample comparison so the user can inspect the arithmetic without entering financial identifiers.

## The pitch

> Explore how reward assumptions change a card comparison. Use sample spending, inspect fees and point values, then check the actual issuer terms before any real decision.

Bio version: **Understand the reward estimate before trusting the ranking.**

The wedge is discovering that a headline reward rate does not tell the whole story. A sample can teach the effect of a fee or point-value assumption, but its answer is only as good as those inputs. Don't reproduce the catalogue's illustrative card terms as current bank facts in your marketing.

## Alternatives

The [issuer's own card information](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards) is a real source for that issuer's current offers and documents. Reading it is not a card approval or a statement that any card is free. A local spreadsheet costs₹0 in additional comparison-software fees and lets a user make the assumptions explicit. [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/) provides free financial education, although it isn't your specific card-simulation workflow.

Your difference is a compact sample purchase/reward/redemption learning loop. It must make assumptions easier to inspect than a spreadsheet, not pretend a polished dashboard contains live bank balances. The homepage's 18,420 points/₹9,210 preview is labelled demo; it is not customer proof.

## The weakness to keep visible

A sample ranking can look like personalized advice. Label demo state, estimated values, data date/source and simulated actions in the result itself. No “best card for you,” live redemption or guaranteed savings claim is earned by the current illustrative catalogue. Don't encourage spending more to earn points.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-signup target** under the no-account session definition: unique first app sessions, with sample comparison completion alongside. Report actual accounts separately if introduced later. Real card applications, credit outcomes and redemption value are not traction this demo has established.
