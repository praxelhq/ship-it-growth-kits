# Cutroom — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** A paid creator's business must meet [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; its own authorized project/data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known owner name and phone. Keep personal identifiers outside this repository. Paying is not required; paid checkout is currently unavailable.

Activation: a creator and its existing editor complete one real version-specific review, with the intended note and decision visible to the appropriate people. Report local-only setups separately. One pair, multiple editors or multiple projects do not multiply a business customer.

The baseline is unknown; no private account records were accessed.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine creator businesses and latest independent use | Workspace records plus private permitted owner register; exclude tests. |
| Own projects created | Actual creator-owned work, not your seeded sample. |
| Version-specific reviews completed | Both-party status and actual relevant note, not a comment button click alone. |
| Approval clarity / wrong-version reports | Private issue log without client files or access tokens. |
| Actual video exports / remaining allowance | Export records; per-version first export versus repeated downloads. |
| Traffic/course metric | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/cutroom), dated and period-labelled. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate. Exclude uploaded video, private briefs, comments, team identities, account/payment screens and project access links from replay. Aggregate events: ownprojectcreated, versionadded, reviewopened, timestampnoted, versionapproved, exportcompleted, laterindependentuse. Keep assisted demo activity separate from independent work.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If the second person cannot see the intended project, pause collaboration outreach and repair permissions/persistence. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare owned jobs with completed shared reviews. A good-looking local workspace does not prove collaboration. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Audit independent recent use, version errors and actual allowance friction. Do not assume free capacity resets monthly. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Check all four tests and count distinct creator businesses, not seats. |
| Day 30 | Report actual qualifying businesses against ten, shared reviews, exports, hours and honest target gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two editor forwards are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. A creator whose only active job ended may have received value but fail the final last-seven-day rule; report that separately rather than prompting an artificial login.

No paid revenue can be assumed while checkout is disconnected. A plan card is not a purchase, an export is not a client's acceptance, and a final cut is not proof the tool saved hours. Record any real observed time comparison with its actual sample size and conditions; obtain permission before quoting a creator.
