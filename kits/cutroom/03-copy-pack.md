# Cutroom — Copy pack

## 1. Creator opener

Hi — when your editor sends the next cut, how do you keep the brief, timestamped changes and final approval tied to the same version?

I build Cutroom. I am testing it with creators who already have an editor and an actual paid project. The first test is one small job, not moving your whole business or finding you a new editor.

Would a two-device sample review be useful? No client footage needed for the first conversation.

## 2. Trial email

**Subject: Which version did you approve?**

A short review gets confusing when the brief is in one chat and “approved” refers to an older file.

Cutroom is built around projects, version reviews, timestamped notes and your final decision. I would like to test one existing creator-editor workflow and see whether the next action is clearer for both people.

The current free allowance is three video exports total, not three each month. Paid plans are displayed but checkout is not connected; please do not expect a paid upgrade today. https://cutroom.space/

Start with an owned sample. Only move actual work after you and your editor have checked access and file handling. If the workflow is not relevant, say no and I will stop.

## 3. LinkedIn post

“Approved” is only useful if everyone knows which version it describes.

For one edit, I want four things together: the brief, the current cut, the notes that still need action and the final decision.

That is the workflow I am testing in Cutroom with people who already work with an editor. A labelled sample is enough for the first demonstration; private client footage does not belong in a public product clip.

The free plan currently includes three total video exports. Paid checkout is not connected yet. Try the real current workflow, with its limits stated: https://cutroom.space/

Which part gets lost most often in your own review process?

## 4. Editor introduction ask

Do you have a willing creator client who loses track of version notes? Could you ask whether a sample Cutroom review would help? Please do not send me their footage, contact details or private brief without permission. This is for an existing working pair, not a promise to supply editing jobs.

## 5. Two-person trial invitation

Would you and your editor try one harmless clip on separate devices? We will check that the right person sees the right version and note before using an actual job. Then you can decide whether it belongs in your workflow. A local-only preview is not the same as a completed shared review.

## 6. Consented follow-up

Could both people identify the current version and the next change without you explaining it in another chat? What failed: access, upload, timestamp, version naming, approval or export? A description is enough; please keep private project links and client files out of public replies.

Never promise paid-plan availability, unrestricted exports, cloud recovery or finished collaboration that you cannot demonstrate. Record any observed saved time as a specific small test, not a general guarantee.
