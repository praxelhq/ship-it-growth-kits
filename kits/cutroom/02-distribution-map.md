# Cutroom — Distribution map

Lead with existing working pairs. A creator looking for an editor is a different problem from a creator unable to identify the approved cut.

| Route | Execution and permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known paid creators with freelance editors | Qualify one active commission, two willing participants and permission to process the footage. Start with an owned sample before any client file. | Ten blocks of four creator businesses =40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two editor introductions | Ask an editor to forward the invitation to a consenting client who has complained about version confusion. No client list or confidential work disclosure. | Days 12 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Explain a precise review/approval failure and show a labelled sample, not invented time savings. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One existing-pair trial | Creator and editor test a real version note on two devices after sample permission checks. | Day 14; #5. Activation support, not extra reach. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-job setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 creator businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. The ten four-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,12,15,17,22,24,27. Posts and forwarded introductions add no guaranteed conversions; deduplicate.

Reserve Days 12–15 for the existing-pair review wedge in [creator coordination](../../shared/a2-creator-operations.md). CreatorForge sells editing delivery; Cutroom organizes an existing editor relationship. Kreator runs deal administration; CreatorDeal scope/rights and booked fulfilment have separate later reservations. Do not send the same creator five overlapping invitations or ask a general campus organizer to distribute every creator tool.

No newsletter feature, creator-group promotion permission or free editor supply has been established. Ask before any group demonstration and use one primary invitation per organizer per week. Own accounts and consented professional contacts are the zero-budget routes. No automated DMs, paid creators, scraped followers or promised discounts. One relevant follow-up after seven days; a decline ends contact.

If the product cannot move a real owned sample between two permitted devices, pause the collaboration pitch and lower the calendar's actual outreach total. A disabled checkout is a reason to use the real free allowance honestly, not invent a way to buy more exports.
