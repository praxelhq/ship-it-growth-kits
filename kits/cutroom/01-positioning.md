# Cutroom — Positioning

**Live:** https://cutroom.space/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/cutroom

Checked 29 October 2026 against [cutroom.space](https://cutroom.space/), including the rendered workspace and Plans & exports view. Actual headline: **“less admin. more action.”** Actual buttons: **“New project”** and **“Your projects”**. A separate **“Log in / sign up”** link points to `/login`. The public workspace starts with zero projects/editors and describes a private browser workspace. These are entry-state observations, not proof that accounts, invitations, cross-device collaboration, uploads or exports work.

## The first business

A solo paid creator with an existing freelance editor and a real weekly short-form commission. They already have the relationship; the problem is a brief in one chat, comments on another link and uncertainty about which cut is approved. Do not start by promising a marketplace of available editors.

**Pitch: Keep one commissioned edit's brief, versions and approval in one reviewed workflow.**

Short version: **One brief. The right version. Clear approval.**

The wedge is one current creator-editor job: agreed brief, a version the creator can inspect, a timestamped note and an explicit final decision. A local sample that only the creator can see is useful for learning but does not establish a working two-person review. Do not ask an editor to abandon its existing delivery route until the second-device test succeeds.

## The actual offer

The rendered Plans & exports page lists Free ₹0 forever with **three video exports total**, original uploaded quality and projects/reviews/collaboration. Free exports do not reset. Creator is advertised at ₹1,799/month for 20 exports; Unlimited ₹12,000/month. **Both paid buttons are disabled: “Checkout not connected.”** The page says no payment is collected until connected. Do not market these as purchasable subscriptions.

The page says each video version uses a slot on its first export, repeat downloads do not use another, unused monthly exports do not roll over, and publishing metadata is free. Those are published rules, not an exercised export quota or paid renewal. Actual cancellation/account persistence also remain untested.

## The alternative

An existing pair can use a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) review log: file/version link, timestamp, requested change, editor response and approval date. A personal [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) page is another $0 organizer, without claiming paid team/AI features are free. Cutroom must earn adoption by reducing version ambiguity and making notes easier to act on. It does not need to claim other review systems do not exist.

Before acquisition, verify two authorized accounts/devices can see the intended project and no other private one. Use an owned short test clip, not a paying client's unpublished footage. If the workspace is local-only in practice, state that limitation and repair sharing before promising collaboration.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** A creator using it for paid work is B2B under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count the creator business once, not each project, editor or seat. All four tests require its own actual work/data onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, a genuine operating business and a privately known owner name/phone. A fictional project in your browser is not a customer.
