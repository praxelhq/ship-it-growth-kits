The illustrated engagement/status counts are not customer evidence.

## The first business

A small audit practice with one live engagement, an existing Excel PBC request list and client authorization to handle the supports. PBC means provided-by-client evidence requests in this workflow. The job is matching a received support to the right request, with an auditor reviewing uncertain matches before acceptance. It is not recurring bookkeeping chase, automatic audit conclusions or client-account reconciliation.

**Pitch: Match the client's supports to the engagement request list, then review what is still outstanding.**

Short version: **Received is not accepted. Keep the request trail clear.**

Start with a synthetic engagement and a handful of fictional requests/supports. Verify import, uncertain match, manual decision and evidence pack. Only then should the authorized auditor decide which minimally necessary real engagement records belong in the system. A received file does not prove sufficiency or correctness of audit evidence.

## Actual offer

[Pricing](https://pbctracker.tech/pricing) shows Free ₹0 forever: one open engagement, 150 requests across engagements, 25 MB evidence storage. Pro ₹499 for 30 days or ₹4,999 for 365 days, GST included: 20 open engagements, 2,000 requests and 200 MB. **These are prepaid periods, not auto-renewing subscriptions.** Buying again adds time; the page says expiry does not delete records but limits additions to Free capacity. These are published terms, not tested entitlements or storage recovery.

A launch concession applies to workspaces already in use when paid plans began, through 31 March 2027. Do not promise every new trial free Pro. Razorpay card/UPI/net-banking/wallet payment and immediate upgrade are advertised but untested.

The page lists read-only Gmail/Outlook domain-filtered intake, saved .eml/.msg, private no-signin client uploads, transparent scoring/review queue, append-only trail, ZIP/Excel export and roll-forward. India/Mumbai hosting is a publisher claim, not an independent residency/security audit. “One authorised user” limited to the auditor's own email needs clarification before a firm-wide sales promise.

## Alternative and first proof

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) register can track request ID, support reference, received/review/accepted state and outstanding action beside the practice's approved evidence store. PBC Tracker must earn adoption through safer matching and traceable review, not pretending a file automatically completes an audit procedure.

Prove separate workspace access and a protected client-upload link using synthetic records. A private bearer link can be forwarded; no-signin does not mean public access is safe. Test wrong-domain email, ambiguous support, duplicate file, revoked access and export completeness before actual evidence. Do not connect a real mailbox merely to verify a marketing claim.

**Target: 10 real practice businesses in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each requires its own actual authorized engagement data onboarded, independent last-seven-day use and a privately known owner name/phone. Clients, engagements, requests and auditor seats are not extra software customers. NudgeBooks separately owns recurring monthly collection; PBC owns one audit evidence-matching engagement.
