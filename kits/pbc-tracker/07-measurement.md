# PBC Tracker — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Count genuine audit practices under [all four policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): own actual authorized engagement data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; real operating business; privately known owner name/phone. Keep personal identifiers outside the public repo. Free-active practices count; sample-only or paying-inactive ones do not.

Activation: an authorized practice imports its actual request list, reviews a proposed support match and correctly updates the intended request state. Report this beside customer totals. A received file is not accepted evidence, an accepted match is not an audit conclusion, and ten engagements in one practice are not ten businesses.

No private workspace was accessed; baseline values and actual customer capacity are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine practices / latest independent use | Workspace records plus private owner register, excluding assisted-only tests. |
| Actual authorized engagements imported | Real owner data, not the illustrated sample. |
| Received/proposed/reviewed/accepted matches | Separate status metadata and actual auditor decision. |
| Incorrect matches / export or access errors | Private issue categories, synthetic reproductions, no client evidence in growth logs. |
| Free/Pro capacity, expiry, payments/refunds | Actual configured counters/provider records, not price-card clicks. |
| Traffic/course metric | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/pbc-tracker), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude working papers, email contents, client/practice identities where private, upload links, filenames that identify clients, account/OTP/payment fields and access tokens from replay. Minimal events: ownengagementimported, supportreceived, matchreviewed, requestaccepted, packexported, laterindependentuse. Never store evidence content in marketing analytics.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If outside auditor access/isolation or actual intake is unclear, pause real evidence onboarding and repair. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare actual own engagements and review completion, not requests created in sample accounts. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent fieldwork use, wrong-period matches and unresolved support. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests; practices, not seats/clients/engagements. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying businesses against ten, activation, independent use, exports, payments/expiry, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two introduction asks are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. Access or confidentiality barriers may make the actual count far lower; do not turn a synthetic test or restricted single-user deployment into invented customer capacity.

Pro ₹499/30 days and ₹4,999/365 days are prepaid periods, not automatic recurring subscriptions. Existing-workspace concession is conditional, not a universal new-user offer. Expiry/no-deletion and storage limits are published terms to verify. No residency certification, audit-quality improvement, hours saved or client outcome can be inferred from a marketing example. Report real permitted observations and their limitations only.
