# PBC Tracker — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://pbctracker.tech/) and [pricing](https://pbctracker.tech/pricing), 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Track every PBC request. Supports file themselves.”** CTA: **“Get started free”** to `/login`. Free ₹0/one engagement/150 requests/25 MB; Pro ₹499/30 days or ₹4,999/365 days,20 engagements/2,000 requests/200 MB, GST included, no auto-renewal. Authenticated intake/matching/export, mailbox scope and payment were not tested.

## 1. Bring human acceptance into the hero

Current headline sounds as though supports fully decide their own filing/status; the body correctly promises uncertain-match review.

Replacement support: **“Match incoming supports to your PBC requests. Review uncertain matches and accept evidence yourself.”**

Keep received, proposed match and accepted visibly distinct. Do not imply matching establishes audit sufficiency.

## 2. Clarify one-authorized-user versus new workspaces

Current security copy says one-time email limited to the auditor's own address; the CTA promises your own private workspace.

Replacement onboarding explanation: **“See who may create a workspace and which single auditor identity controls it. Do not share a login to simulate firm access.”**

Verify isolated accounts and email eligibility with synthetic records before inviting outside practices. Do not market multi-seat permissions that were not established.

## 3. Explain exact read-only mailbox scope

Current claims: client-domain-only reads; nothing moved/deleted/marked read.

Replacement permission summary: **“Review the provider permissions and configured domain filter before connection. Use a saved synthetic email first.”**

Test wrong domain, forwarded thread, attachment handling, revocation and scope using authorized test accounts. Read-only does not mean no sensitive processing; no staff inbox should be connected to prove a sales point.

## 4. Treat the client link as an access decision

Current private per-engagement page needs no client sign-in.

Replacement warning: **“Share this engagement link only with the intended client. Review exactly what it exposes and how to revoke it before sending.”**

Test direct file access, link forwarding, expiration/revocation and whether only intended outstanding requests appear. Do not assume the word private proves safe isolation.

## 5. Make prepaid price/limits unmistakable

Current pricing titles say Pro Monthly/Yearly but actual terms say prepaid 30/365 days with no automatic renewal.

Replacement strip: **“Free: one open engagement, 150 requests, 25 MB. Pro: ₹499/30 days or ₹4,999/365 days, GST included. No automatic renewal.”**

Clarify storage/request counting, expiry and capacity after downgrade. Existing-workspace concession through 31 March 2027 is not a universal new-user offer. Verify upgrade/delivery before advertising instant activation.

## 6. Prove the evidence pack and trail

Current promises append-only history and ZIP/Excel export.

Replacement output note: **“Check the exported request IDs, statuses, history and support files against the engagement before relying on the pack.”**

Test import-column mapping, duplicate support, ambiguous match, correction, acceptance, export completeness and roll-forward using synthetic records. Host-region claims require actual verification before stronger certification language. Keep real working papers out of public footage and session replay.
