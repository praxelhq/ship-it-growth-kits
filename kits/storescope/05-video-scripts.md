# StoreScope — Video scripts

Use fictional sample products and your own voice/screen. No real merchant token, account, unreleased product range or private pricing. Label every planned integration.

## A. Review the catalogue, not a competitor —65 seconds · own LinkedIn

0–3 s: sample product with two variants. **“Which variant actually changed?”**

3–18 s: actual fictional preview. “This sample shows the intended review, not a connected merchant.”

18–32 s: product/SKU/variant association and dates if available. “A change needs the right product and two clear snapshots.” If dates aren't implemented, show the proposed checklist separately.

32–47 s: actual sample CSV preview. “This is a report, not an automatic store update.”

47–57 s: permission-first release plan. “Live access waits for merchant authorisation and safeguard checks.”

57–65 s: proposed-price disclosure and URL. “Explore the fictional preview. storescope.me.”

## B. Permission hook —20 seconds · Short

0–3 s: sample catalogue and ownership label. **“Whose store authorised this?”**

3–13 s: current permission-first plan. “The merchant's own products, with explicit access. No unconnected competitor extraction.”

13–20 s: preview-only label and URL. No fabricated installation success.

## C. Snapshot hook —25 seconds · merchant follow-up

0–3 s: sample price difference. **“A missing row isn't always a deleted product.”**

3–16 s: fictional partial-read versus complete-snapshot example, clearly labelled. “Check coverage, variant identity and snapshot time before calling it a change.”

16–25 s: report review and URL. “Keep uncertainty visible.” No invented live monitoring or automatic repricing.
