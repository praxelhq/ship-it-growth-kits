# StoreScope — Landing-page fixes

Read home 29 October 2026. Actual heading **“Your catalog. A clearer view.”** CTAs **“Explore sample catalog”** / **“Try the sample preview”**→`/demo/`. Current preview $0; proposed Starter $49/month/Agency $149/month unavailable. No installation/account/payment tested.

1. **Keep preview versus live above the fold.** Current disclosure says fictional/no install. Replacement support: **“Explore a fictional catalog report. Live merchant connections are not available yet.”** Preserve on mobile, preview and screenshots; never splice a sample into a claimed real store.
2. **State the first merchant job.** Replacement: **“Review your product names, SKUs and variant prices; compare dated snapshots without changing the store.”** Mark planned controls as planned. Don't imply today's demo can access a Shopify catalogue.
3. **Keep proposed price labels attached.** Replacement plan strip: **“Pricing proposal, not available to purchase. Starter $49/month and Agency $149/month need validation before launch.”** No checkout CTA or false subscription revenue.
4. **Make access proof understandable.** Current plan lists permission-first installation. Replacement merchant note: **“Before connection, review the exact store, requested product-read permission and how to revoke access.”** [Official scopes guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/authentication-authorization/manage-access-scopes) is an implementation reference, not proof of approval. Verify actual granted scopes and supported distribution.
5. **Label snapshots and gaps.** Replacement report heading: **“Store authorised for this report · snapshot time · compared snapshot · included fields · incomplete reads.”** Never interpret a partial/paginated failure as deleted products or a real price change. Use actual supported fields only.
6. **Prove isolation/removal before agency offer.** Replacement agency note: **“Each merchant authorises its own store. Separate access and deletion are required.”** Test two controlled stores, denied scopes, pagination, throttling, revoke/uninstall and deletion. Do not claim security certification or unlimited coverage from a read-only slogan.
7. **Phone/CSV proof.** Use fictional sample to verify variant association, currency/decimal preservation, CSV escaping and empty states. A CSV export is reporting, not a write to the store. Keep tokens/private catalogue data out of replay. No competitor-store scraping or customer/order data needed for this wedge.
