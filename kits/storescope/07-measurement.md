# StoreScope —Measurement

**Target:10 real business customers**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Four tests: genuine merchant business; own authorised catalogue data onboarded; independent use in last seven days; privately known responsible owner name/phone. Count businesses, not product rows/stores/agency tabs automatically. Each agency-managed merchant needs express authorisation and actual qualifying use.

Signup means an actual permitted live merchant workspace, not the fictional preview. Activation: a merchant reviews a complete own-catalogue report/snapshot and verifies its variant/SKU/price coverage. A sample CSV download is not live activation. Until integration exists safely, qualifying live adoption is zero.

Private baselines unknown: genuine merchants, supported installations, granted scopes, complete/partial snapshots, reviewed changes, exports, independent recent use and real payments. Proposed paid plans yield no assumed revenue. Save current rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/storescope); unknown isn't zero and rolling readings aren't cumulative.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before tagging. Exclude store tokens, private products/prices, merchant accounts and access links. Aggregate events: preview_opened, actual_permission_granted, own_snapshot_completed, partial_read_flagged, change_reviewed, csv_completed, access_revoked, independent_return. No customer/order data required for this product-report wedge. Analytics must not undermine revocation/deletion promises.
