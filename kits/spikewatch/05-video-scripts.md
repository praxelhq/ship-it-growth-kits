# Spikewatch — Phone-demo scripts

Use wholly synthetic hourly usage and a labelled sample Slack image, your screen recorder and voice. No keys, business usage, webhook URLs or live API spending. Sending even a test message is a separately authorized action; a public demo can show a preview without sending.

## A. One unusual hour — 65 seconds

0–3 s: synthetic hourly curve. **“Bug, or the traffic you wanted?”**

3–17 s: actual checker sample load, if exercised. “These are fictional hours, not a customer's bill.”

17–32 s: actual rule controls and available history. “Inspect the baseline, threshold, floor and cooldown.” Leave one real supported change uncut; if no run completed, label the public controls as untested.

32–47 s: real sample result or clearly labelled alert preview. “Estimated dollars and an unusual hour ask a person to investigate.” Do not send a webhook during recording by assumption.

47–55 s: actual self-host/hosted boundary. “A checker result is not a scheduled monitor. Hosted access is reserved until live.”

55–65 s: current founding offer and URL. “Review the setup and permissions first. spikewatch.live.”

## B. Floor hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: two tiny synthetic spend bars. **“Three times tiny can still be tiny.”**

3–12 s: actual threshold and dollar-floor controls. “The ratio and absolute floor are separate checks.”

12–20 s: human-response note and URL. “Choose the rule for your own operation.” No universal finance recommendation.

## C. Availability hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: hosted reservation CTA. **“Reserved is not live.”**

3–15 s: actual checker/self-host links beside hosted terms. “These paths have different setup and maintenance. The hosted offer charges only once alerts are live.”

15–25 s: code/security review and URL. “Know the path before connecting business usage.”

Capture exact headline/CTA, external reservation destination, sample label, minimum history, rule/estimate boundaries and no-auto-stop FAQ. Label waiting cuts. No fake successful alert, customer incident, saving, certification or key-scope test.
