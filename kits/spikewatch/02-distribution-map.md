# Spikewatch — Distribution map

Lead with small Anthropic-using SaaS teams and a named operational responder. Do not sell a waitlist as a live hosted service or recruit developers without real business usage.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing SaaS technical-owner relationships | Qualify real team API spend, authority to evaluate usage processing and a person who can review alerts. Start with the sample, no credentials. | Ten blocks of four businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two technical-founder introductions | Ask a known operator to forward one sample/checker invitation; no provider credentials, usage exports or team lists. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a synthetic hourly curve, threshold/floor/cooldown and human response boundary. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One authorized setup review | Owner evaluates browser/approved self-host path, permission scope and controlled test delivery using synthetic data. | Day 12; #5. Existing-trial support. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% authorized own-usage setup × 70% independent recent operational use = 9.8, about 10 teams. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Ten four-team blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts/intros add no guaranteed conversions. This adoption model is not ten hosted-paying accounts; hosted capacity is unknown and may be zero.

Separate a one-off checker result from an actually monitored team. A self-hosted team may qualify if all policy tests hold and its operational usage is real, but report that path explicitly rather than implying managed-service sales. A reserved $15 spot with no own data/use is not a customer.

No developer newsletter, engineering Slack, FinOps group or official provider endorsement is established. Ask the known organizer before posting. No paid listings, automated DMs, scraped GitHub emails, API-key requests or manufactured paid incidents. One primary invitation per organizer/week, one relevant follow-up after seven days, stop after decline.

The [GitHub source](https://github.com/shantanupatil-debug/spikewatch) is a code-review resource, not permission to advertise through unrelated issues. Read the actual self-host/security documents before an owner connects anything. Do not run credentials, deploy scheduled jobs or send Slack tests during a marketing demonstration without separate owner authorization.
