# MedDraft — Entry-page fixes

Read [the actual entry](https://drbirmolehospital.com/meddraft), 29 October 2026. Headline **“Gurukrupa Hospital”**, heading/action **“Sign in”**, secondary **“New here? Request access.”** Administrator approval is mandatory. No price or external-hospital offer was visible. Authenticated functions, access approval and patient-data processing were not tested.

## 1. State who this workspace is for

Current hospital branding and approval requirement imply an institutional system, while a visitor may arrive expecting a generic MedDraft product.

Replacement note: **“This is the Gurukrupa Hospital workspace. Access requires approval by its administrator. Do not enter another institution's records here.”**

Verify actual scope with the owner before publishing. If external tenancy is supported, show a separate truthful route, not a shared request-access funnel into one hospital.

## 2. Explain the request outcome

Current button says Request access, followed by administrator approval wording.

Replacement supporting text: **“Requesting access does not grant access. Use the hospital's approved invitation process and wait for the administrator's decision.”**

Test approved/denied/pending states with authorized synthetic accounts. Do not promise approval within a time that has not been established. Empty sign-in disabled is expected validation, not proof authentication is broken.

## 3. Make metadata match verified scope

Current metadata says prepare, review and approve by voice; the entry cannot demonstrate those capabilities.

Replacement public description: **“Authorized hospital-document workspace. Sign in through the hospital's approved process.”**

Add specific document/voice claims only after the owner verifies supported steps. Do not create a public promotional feature grid from assumptions about a clinical system.

## 4. Provide a fictional evaluation route

Current public entry offers no no-account feature demonstration.

Replacement link, only after built and approved: **“See an approved fictional workflow example.”**

Label every record fictional; remove patient names, images, identifiers, dates and real staff credentials. A static illustration must not imply a real draft, signature or approval completed.

## 5. Explain data handling before actual records

Current sign-in does not establish processing/storage/retention/export/deletion behavior.

Replacement pre-use notice: **“Use only information your institution authorizes for this system. Review its data-handling and access policy before entering actual records.”**

Provide the real institution-approved policy and support route. Test owner/staff access, direct document links, session expiry, revoked access and deletion with fictional records. Do not invent certifications or local-only processing.

## 6. Keep external acquisition separate from the existing system

Current public deployment is one hospital workspace, not evidence of a general subscription service.

If expansion is intended, first establish isolated institution setup, authorized contracting, support and exact current scope. Then publish a separate institutional enquiry route with real terms. Do not fabricate a price or open access merely to satisfy a ten-business course target.

The site is live. An auth gate is not downtime; a metadata tagline is not a verified clinical capability. Keep real patient data out of growth tests and preserve mandatory human review of any actual professional output under the institution's process.
