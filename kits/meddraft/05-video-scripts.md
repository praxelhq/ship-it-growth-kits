# MedDraft — Authorized demo scripts

Record only a hospital-approved fictional workflow. Use your own phone microphone/screen recording, no patient material, real dictation, credentials, internal access links or staff faces without consent. If no authenticated demonstration is authorized, record only the public boundary explainer and label every feature unverified.

## A. Scope before claims — 65 seconds, internal review

0–3 s: current public entry. **“Which workflow is actually ready?”**

3–15 s: administrator-approved sample account/role, with access details masked. “This is a permitted internal test, not open access to the hospital.”

15–32 s: actual supported fictional drafting step. “The example describes no real patient. We compare the output with the supplied facts.” Do not invent a document type or voice capability that does not exist.

32–47 s: actual reviewer correction and next role, only if implemented. “A draft is not approved because it looks complete.” Leave a real edit uncut. If approval is unavailable, state that rather than simulate it.

47–57 s: verified storage/deletion scope or an explicit unresolved checklist. “We need to know who can access and remove the test record.”

57–65 s: current scope statement. “One authorized institution's evaluation. No clinical outcome or multi-hospital claim.” No public call to upload records.

## B. Account-count hook — 20 seconds, internal course update

0–3 s: ten fictional staff icons inside one hospital outline. **“Ten staff are not ten hospitals.”**

3–12 s: one institution count. “A hospital business is the customer. Accounts and documents are separate activity.”

12–20 s: scope checklist. “Keep the target honest. Establish safe external onboarding before expansion.” No invented institutional logos.

## C. Fictional-example hook — 25 seconds, approved public note

0–3 s: sample label. **“This is not a patient record.”**

3–15 s: fictional input beside actual output only when approved. “A demonstration can show where editing is needed without exposing someone's care.”

15–25 s: human review and permission checklist. “Describe the real capability and the unresolved work. Do not turn a sample into clinical proof.”

If the institution does not approve publication, keep all footage internal and skip the public clip. A missing authorized demo is a real constraint, not a reason to fabricate a working product video.
