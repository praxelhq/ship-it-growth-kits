# MedDraft — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, unchanged under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count distinct operating hospital/institution businesses only when all four tests hold: own actual authorized business data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; real operation; privately known authorized owner name/phone. Store identities outside the public repo.

A hospital with ten staff is one business. A department, doctor login, request-access form, document or synthetic sample is not another customer. The public deployment currently supports evidence of only one named hospital workspace; no independent external tenancy was established. State a capacity of at most one under current evidence, not a fabricated ten-customer pipeline.

Activation: the authorized institution completes a genuinely supported document workflow with required human review under its own process. Fictional tests are feasibility evidence, not actual-business-data onboarding or clinical validation. Actual data must only be used when the institution has authorized the system and processing; do not acquire patient information merely to satisfy a course metric.

Private records were not accessed; existing actual institutional adoption is unknown, not zero.

| Baseline | Source and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Authorized supported institutions | Product owner/admin confirmation and real isolated tenant configuration if any. |
| Approved staff/test accounts | Administrative records, separately reported from businesses. |
| Demonstrated workflow and unresolved steps | Approved synthetic test log; exact scope, no patient content. |
| Actual permitted business onboarding / independent use | Institution's authorized records and minimal timestamps, only if legitimate processing already exists. |
| Access/deletion/review failures | Synthetic reproductions and private issue categories. |
| Price/payment/offer status | Actual configured offer if any; no price inferred from hospital branding. |
| Course baseline | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/meddraft), current value/date/period when available. |

No patient text, audio, images, identifiers, clinical records, staff credentials or document links belong in growth analytics. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and exclude all authenticated clinical/document screens; do not add replay to measure care-related workflow. Minimal approved aggregate events may record permissionrequested, accessapproved, synthetictestcompleted, workflowreviewed and laterindependentinstitutionuse only where permitted. A login alone is not approval or activation.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | One administrator invitation scheduled. Record permission and scope; zero qualifying customers may still be the honest state. |
| Day 14 | One institutional test at most. Report demonstrated steps and unresolved data/access controls. Ten staff do not close the gap. |
| Day 21 | Up to five external discovery invitations only if expansion is authorized/supported. Conversations remain discovery until all policy tests hold. |
| Day 28 | Audit qualifying institutions and latest independent use. If only the current hospital qualifies, report one against ten and a gap of nine. |
| Day 30 | Actual institutions, authorized scope, activation, independent use, unresolved expansion/data gates and next owner actions. |

No percentage funnel is justified for external hospitals before a real offer and onboarding exist. Base supported capacity is one institution; even that may remain zero qualifying customers if only fictional/assisted tests occur. A ten-customer target does not override approval, confidentiality or actual product boundaries. Preserve the target and explain the gap.

Do not claim clinical accuracy, safer care, saved clinician hours, regulatory compliance, hospital endorsement or patient outcomes without appropriate actual evidence. Publish only institution-approved aggregate product observations. Internal permissions and a synthetic demo are progress; they are not permission to advertise a general clinical product as ready.
