# Parchi — Landing-page fixes

Read [parchi.site](https://parchi.site/) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Stop retyping WhatsApp orders into bills.”** CTAs **“Get started”** and **“Try the 30-second challenge.”** Current 30-day non-renewing passes: ₹249/100 invoices/120 reads, ₹349/250/300, ₹499/500/600, GST included. Signed-in extraction, PDF, sending and billing were not exercised.

## 1. Keep the review step in the hero

Current copy promises a clean invoice in seconds, while the workflow correctly says check details.

Replacement support: **“Paste a permitted order. Review the item match, size, quantity, rates and delivery. Send only after the draft matches your check.”**

Do not equate quick extraction with a correct or legally reviewed bill. Measure actual generation time before advertising a typical duration.

## 2. Label the challenge at entry and exit

Current challenge already says sample seller, nothing sent and unusable sample bill.

Replacement action: **“Try the sample challenge — no invoice sent.”** Keep **“Demonstration only; cannot be used as an invoice”** on the output and any shared clip. Do not count game play as merchant activation or sample amounts as revenue.

## 3. Make uncertain product mapping visible

Current prices come from the saved product list and messages can be messy/Hinglish.

Replacement review state: **“Confirm the intended product/variant. A missing or ambiguous item needs your choice before finalizing.”**

Test similar names, sizes, absent rates, quantity corrections and delivery charges with synthetic data. Never silently invent an item or borrow the nearest price.

## 4. Separate invoice and AI-read allowances

Current plans have distinct counts and rough orders/day descriptions.

Replacement plan note: **“This pass includes the invoice and AI-read quotas shown. Review how retries, edits and failed reads count before purchase.”**

Publish actual quota behavior after testing. Keep ₹249/349/499 and 30-day no-auto-renewal terms beside payment. Do not call a thirty-day pass lifetime or an unused quota monthly recurring revenue.

## 5. Keep tax wording specific and reviewed

Current claims include GST-ready invoice, tax calculations, QR, invoice register and HSN summary.

Replacement warning: **“Review your business details, classification, tax treatment and invoice content before sending. Consult your own adviser for your circumstances.”**

Verify supported fields/output without inventing tax rules or compliance certification. A HSN summary is not a filing acknowledgment or professional approval.

## 6. Protect customer data and actual sending

Current order examples include full-looking customer details; real sellers' messages can be sensitive.

Replacement import note: **“Use customer information only when authorized. Keep private messages and invoices out of public demonstrations.”**

Test privacy/deletion, repeat customer access, actual PDF, failed export and sending confirmation using owned synthetic records. A click in the challenge sends nothing; a real invoice should not be delivered during a demo without the owner's consent. Keep source data in the existing process until the actual output is reconciled.
