# Parchi — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each seller must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; own actual permitted product/order data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identifiers outside the public repo. Paid-but-inactive sellers do not qualify; free-active ones can if an actual supported allowance exists.

Activation: the owner reviews a real draft against its own order and produces a correct usable bill/PDF through the actual product. Report this beside customer totals. Customer rows, invoices, AI reads and sample challenge plays are separate counts, not additional seller businesses. Generated/sent/paid remain separate states.

No private workspace was accessed; baseline values are unknown until recorded.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real sellers / latest independent use | Product records plus private permitted owner register, excluding tests. |
| Own products/orders onboarded | Seller-controlled actual data, not copied landing examples. |
| Reviewed bills/PDFs and corrections | Status/aggregate error categories, no customer data in growth logs. |
| Quota consumption / failed reads/exports | Actual AI-read and invoice counters, separately measured. |
| Pass payments, expiry and refunds | Actual billing/provider records; no auto-renewal assumed. |
| Traffic/course metric | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/parchi), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude original chats, customer identities/addresses/phones, GST details, invoices, payment and account screens from replay. Minimal events: ownproductsadded, orderdrafted, correctionmade, billreviewed, pdfcompleted, laterindependentuse. Do not send raw order text or invoice contents into custom analytics.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If item/quantity/rate cannot be verified, repair before actual bills or more acquisition. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare qualified sellers, own-data setup and reviewed output. Game activity does not close the gap. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent real billing use and error patterns, not just AI-read volume. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four business tests and separate passes purchased from active sellers. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying sellers against ten, activation, independent use, real payments/expiry/refunds, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three statuses and two introduction requests are upside only. Replace assumptions after eight approaches and again after 20. Do not prompt a test bill solely to make the owner look active.

Starter ₹249 for 100 invoices is ₹2.49/invoice only at full use; unused allowance changes effective cost. Growth/Pro rounded unit prices are not separate purchase prices. A 30-day pass is not guaranteed recurring revenue. A GST-ready label does not prove correct classification, filing or compliance. Quote actual permitted observed outcomes, never invented seller revenue, time savings or testimonials.
