Checked 29 October 2026 against [parchi.site](https://parchi.site/). Actual headline: **“Stop retyping WhatsApp orders into bills.”** CTA: **“Get started”** to `/signin`, with **“Try the 30-second challenge”** to `#challenge`. The challenge explicitly uses sample orders, sends nothing and cannot produce a usable invoice. Account creation, order extraction, actual PDF, sending and checkout were not tested.

## The first business

A small made-to-order clothing seller taking real WhatsApp orders for a stable range of kurtas and accessories. The owner already knows the product, price and buyer relationship, but retypes sizes, quantities and delivery details. Start with a seller who can inspect every line, not a business expecting AI to decide unknown items or tax treatment.

**Pitch: Turn a permitted chat order into a draft bill, then check the items, quantity and amount before sending.**

Short version: **Read the order once. Review the bill carefully.**

The first useful job is a real owned product list plus one authorized order, inspected against the original message. A blue item in size M is not interchangeable with a similarly named product. Missing delivery charge, an unclear quantity or a wrong address must remain unresolved until the owner checks it.

## The public offer

Plans are 30-day passes, **no auto-renewal**, prices include GST: Starter ₹249 for 100 invoices/120 AI reads; Growth ₹349 for 250/300; Pro ₹499 for 500/600. Payment is advertised through Dodo by UPI/card. The page says passes are not refundable once started; review [current refund terms](https://parchi.site/refunds) before purchase. No payment or entitlement was exercised.

“About three orders a day” is a rough plan-fit description, not an extra unlimited allowance. The unit cost applies only if all included invoices are used. A read and an invoice are separate quota units; verify failed/repeated reads before recommending a plan.

The product promises GST-ready invoices, saved-product pricing, PDF, UPI QR, register and HSN summary. These are public feature claims, not a tax review. The seller/its adviser must check actual tax identity, classification and treatment. Do not advertise automatic compliance, correct filing, guaranteed delivery accuracy or an AI interpretation as a final customer order.

## The alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) order/invoice checklist can keep product, size, quantity, rate, delivery and total visible alongside the owner's existing billing process. Parchi must earn adoption by reducing re-entry while preserving review, not by claiming professional-looking PDFs make every bill legally correct.

Prove a synthetic order with a quantity correction, ambiguous item and delivery charge. Verify saved-price mapping, totals, actual PDF and repeat customer access using permitted samples before client data. Never copy the landing sample's personal name/address into outreach or treat it as a real testimonial.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each seller must onboard its own actual permitted product/order data, use independently within seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Invoices, customers inside a register and challenge-game players are not extra seller businesses.
