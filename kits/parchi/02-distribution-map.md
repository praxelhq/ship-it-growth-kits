# Parchi — Distribution map

Lead with clothing sellers who already bill conversational orders. Keep the list separate from Apna Catalog's wholesale jewellery and OneThread's print-shop enquiry/handoff workflow.

| Route | Execution / permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small clothing sellers | Qualify real orders, stable product names/rates and owner responsibility for checking bills. Use genuine shop visits or public business enquiries, never pretend to place a clothing order to pitch software. | Ten blocks of four businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two packaging/bookkeeping introductions | Ask a known professional to forward an invitation, not disclose its customers, tax data or chats. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own WhatsApp Status | Show a synthetic Hinglish order and the owner's correction, not a customer screenshot. | Days 9,18,26; #3. Guess 20–100 views and 0–2 relevant replies/status; excluded from base. |
| One seller-owned bill review | Owner compares one permitted order and draft against its normal process after privacy/quota tests. | Day 12; #5. Support for an existing trial, not extra reach. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-product/order setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. These are assumptions, not observed seller conversion benchmarks. Ten four-business blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Status/introduction results are upside only and deduplicated.

Choose one small clothing-seller segment and one real order week. Do not recruit consumer shoppers, challenge-game players or CAs as seller customers unless their actual business is the buyer. A local merchant organizer receives one primary invitation/week across tools; ask permission before any group message.

No Instagram-seller newsletter, market association, WhatsApp community or bookkeeping endorsement is established. No paid creator campaign, bulk DMs, scraped seller/customer lists or fake product enquiries. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after a decline. Never request an actual customer message in a public thread.

If extraction or invoice output cannot be checked against the original, pause acquisition and repair. A paid pass is not successful onboarding; a challenge demo is not real billing. The first forecast is only credible when the owner can complete a reviewed actual order independently.
