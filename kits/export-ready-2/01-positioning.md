# Export Ready — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.exportready.co.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/export-ready-2

**Your ten are actual packaging/export businesses using a qualified-review preflight—not ten people receiving an “official certificate.”** Start with a small packaging studio reviewing one consenting client's artwork for one destination, under a qualified regulatory reviewer's supervision. Don't start by promising coverage across pharmaceuticals, infant products and every market.

The actual headline is **“Is Your Product Artwork Export Ready?”** **“Upload & Audit Your Artwork”** leads to `/analysis/new`. [Pricing](https://www.exportready.co.in/pricing) offers three free artwork checks and $5/month Pro. These are public offers; generation, private storage and real checkout weren't independently tested.

## A promise the terms can support

> Get a packaging preflight's possible issues and source references ready for a qualified reviewer before the print decision.

Bio: **An AI-assisted packaging pre-check, not clearance or certification.**

The [actual terms](https://www.exportready.co.in/terms) explicitly call the product a pre-check/educational platform, disclaim legal advice and clearance warranties, and require licensed local regulatory review before commercial print. That is narrower than homepage “Everything Needed to Pass Border Compliance” and pricing “Official PDF … Certificates.” Fix the conflict before acquisition.

## The first practical job

Use one synthetic or expressly authorized draft label, choose one destination, inspect the proposed findings and compare them with authoritative sources. The professional records what is correct, wrong and unresolved. A 0–100 score does not approve the artwork. A screenshot without reliable print scale cannot establish a physical font measurement; don't sell visual precision that wasn't validated.

The manual alternative is a reviewer-owned checklist and the official material. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace and can track findings; the [European Commission's food-information page](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/food-information-consumers-legislation_en) is an authoritative starting reference for its own jurisdiction, not an ExportReady endorsement. No universal consultant price was verified; don't repeat the site's $2,000–$5,000 comparison as market fact.

## The trust boundary

The [privacy policy](https://www.exportready.co.in/privacy) describes cloud storage and Google AI processing. Do not call it local-only or guarantee provider zero retention because a prompt says so. Start with authorized non-sensitive artwork. Ten customers require actual businesses, permitted workflows, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact privately—not ten sample reports.
