# Export Ready — Measurement

**Target:10 real packaging/export businesses.** Activation beside it: **businesses independently using an authorized preflight and qualified review workflow**. A report download or synthetic label isn't a customer or a compliance outcome.

## Day 0

Query actual businesses, authorized files, reviewer-completed preflights, false positives, missing issues and independent returns from your own records. Homepage samples prove none of those totals. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding artwork/formulation content. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/export-ready-2) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero. Verify real billing state before counting any $5 subscription receipt.

Events: `authorized_preflight_started`, `report_generated`, `finding_reviewed`, `finding_corrected`, `reviewed_report_exported` and `business_returned`. A high score isn't approval; an AI reference isn't verified applicability; a PDF isn't an official certificate. Keep those distinctions in dashboards and the final report.

## Four business tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a legitimate business, its own permitted actual workflow onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required. Several designers in one studio are one business; several labels from one client don't multiply customers.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 20% response ×50% authorized reviewer-led trial ×50% independent reuse = **2 businesses**. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 2–4 is below ten**, with permissions and qualified review a real gate. These aren't measured regulatory-tool conversion rates. Replace them after 20 approaches, using actual repeat prepress work rather than report generation volume.

Have a qualified person assess the first ten permitted findings for correct applicability, current source and measurable evidence. A dangerous miss or unsupported clearance claim takes priority over traffic. Do not report predicted avoided fines, customs success or regulatory approval without actual independently supportable evidence.
