# Export Ready — Video scripts

Synthetic or expressly authorized artwork only. No commercial approval, legal interpretation or customs-success claim. Show real output and reviewer correction, with clear advisory labels. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · The reviewer, not the score · 60 seconds · packaging professional call

0–2 s hook: **“This score doesn't approve your label.”** Show a synthetic report. 2–15 s: choose one actual supported destination/file: “The first test needs a defined scope and permission to process the artwork.”15–30 s: inspect a proposed issue and source: “A citation still needs checking for applicability and current text.”30–45 s: show a reviewer correction or unresolved field: “If the tool doesn't know the scale or formulation, it must not pretend.”45–55 s: show advisory export label: “This is a pre-check report, not a certificate or clearance warranty.”55–60 s: “Qualified local review before commercial print. Export Ready.”

## B · Official-looking isn't official · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“A PDF template can't certify compliance.”**0–8 s: show a synthetic report with “Advisory pre-check” visible. 8–15 s: “The report can organize possible issues. The qualified reviewer still decides what the artwork needs.”15–20 s: “Keep the product's promise as narrow as its actual evidence.”

## C · What can an image measure? · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“Where did this millimetre figure come from?”**2–12 s: show synthetic unscaled artwork and a proposed size finding. 12–20 s: “Without reliable print scale, a precise number can be a guess. Keep that limit visible.”20–25 s: “Ask for the actual proof, not a confident badge.”

Use captions and readable source context. Don't stage a regulator approval or claim a live private analysis worked if it didn't.
