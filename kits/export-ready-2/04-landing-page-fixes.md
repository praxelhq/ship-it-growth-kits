# Export Ready — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, analysis entry, pricing, terms and privacy read 29 October 2026. No artwork, private report or payment was submitted. Regulatory correctness and security implementation weren't independently validated.

## 1. Remove certification and clearance certainty

**Current:** headline “Is Your Product Artwork Export Ready?”, “Everything Needed to Pass Border Compliance” and “Official PDF … Certificates” conflict with pre-check-only terms.

**Write:** **“Packaging preflight findings for qualified review.”** Replace certificate language with **“Advisory pre-check report—not official certification or clearance.”** Put this beside every upload, score and download. Don't drive traffic until public promises match the actual terms.

## 2. Make the first upload narrow and authorized

**Current CTA:** “Upload & Audit Your Artwork” opens a sector/destination workflow.

**Write:** **“Pre-check artwork I am authorized to process.”** Supporting line: **“Choose the correct destination. Review every proposed finding with qualified local counsel before commercial printing.”** Test a synthetic file through actual output/export. If required steps fail, repair before real-data onboarding; don't assume a sector selector proves analysis works.

## 3. Show uncertainty, not approval-by-score

**Current:** a sample report has a 0–100 score, findings, citations and estimated fix time.

**Write:** **“Possible issue · source · evidence · limits · reviewer decision.”** A score cannot mean the label is approved. Leave missing ingredients, scale and destination specifics unresolved. Don't state physical typography dimensions from an unscaled image as a measured fact. The reviewer must verify actual print evidence.

## 4. Align pricing with advisory scope

**Current:** three free checks and $5/month Pro emphasize unlimited audits and official dossiers/certificates.

**Write:** **“Three free advisory pre-checks. Pro: $5/month for the actual supported pre-check features.”** Confirm real checkout, tax, cancellation and actual limits before a paid promise. Do not repeat an unverified consultant-cost or seconds-versus-weeks comparison as measured savings.

## 5. Explain cloud processing honestly

**Current privacy:** artwork may be processed through Google AI and stored for authenticated users; deletion/retention claims are stated.

**Write before upload:** **“Artwork may go to the stated AI provider and account storage. Upload only with permission; read current handling/deletion terms.”** Have the owner verify configuration. A “zero-retention prompt” does not independently establish provider retention policy. Never call this browser-only processing.

## 6. Make mobile source review possible

**Current:** many sectors,15+ jurisdictions and a dense sample compete for attention.

**Write the first example around one reviewed destination**, with **“Synthetic example. Not legal approval.”** Test source links, long findings, large text,360 px and failed-upload recovery. Preview title: **“Export Ready — packaging pre-checks for qualified review.”** No mobile/load defect was measured; test rather than allege it.
