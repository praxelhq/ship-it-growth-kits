# Skill Prep — 30-day execution calendar

**This calendar is pinned to a real date.** CAT 2026 is **Sunday 29 November**. If day 1 is **30 October**, then **day 30 is 28 November — the day before the exam**, and your audience's attention disappears the morning after.

That is the most important fact in your kit. **There is no week 5 and no recovery week.** Front-load everything.

Two dates to put in your phone now:

- **4 November (day 6) — admit cards release.** Every aspirant in India logs into iimcat.ac.in that day and panic spikes. **It is the single highest-attention day of your month.**
- **29 November (day 31) — exam day.** Post nothing promotional. See the last section.

Open this file every morning. One or two things a day, properly.

## 29 November — exam day — and the morning after

**Day 31 is exam day. Post nothing promotional. Not one thing.** Your entire audience is in an exam hall. A promotional post that day is the only genuinely unforgivable thing in this kit.

**30 November is the day your rented channels close.** 284,000 people stop caring about speed math overnight.

So the single most valuable two hours of your month is **day 28: writing the December plan**, which has exactly three parts:

1. **Your Telegram subscribers and email list.** The only audience you own. In January, CAT results land and roughly 50,000 of these people get interview calls — and then they need guesstimates, speed math and DI again, for GD/WAT/PI season. **Message them in January, not December.**
2. **The coaching centres and college aptitude cells that said yes.** College placement cells run aptitude training *all year* and do not care about CAT dates. **This is your non-seasonal channel** and you now have warm relationships in it. Name every one of them in writing while you still remember who said what.
3. **XAT (early January) and the other exams.** Switch the countdown on 30 November. Same product, same drills, smaller but real audience, and it bridges you to interview season.

---

## The five things that decide this month

1. **The hero question staying ungated and above the fold** (`04` Fix 1). It's the best conversion asset in this cohort. Everything else is traffic.
2. **Posting a reel every single day.** With 284,000 people in the audience, one breakout reel is worth more than twenty good ones — and you only get breakouts from volume.
3. **Admit-card day, 4 November.** Your highest-attention day. Be ready the day before, not that morning.
4. **The Telegram channel from day 1.** It's the only thing you own on 30 November.
5. **Being useful rather than promotional in the last ten days.** Panic season rewards generosity and punishes selling, and this audience talks to each other constantly.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 13 — compounds for CAT 2027, won't help now), **LinkedIn** (day 17), **Peerlist** (day 7 — builder audience, not aspirants).

**Never drop:** the daily reel, the daily Telegram question, day 6 (admit-card day), days 9 and 11 (coaching outreach), day 28 (the December plan).
