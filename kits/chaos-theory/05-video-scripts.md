# Chaos Theory — Video scripts

Record your own gameplay and voice. Keep actual failures; hide private room codes and player names. No paid editor or borrowed game footage.

## A. One clear mission — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “I need five fuel cores and one clean exit.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Start campaign, objective readable|“This is the first Chaos Theory mission.”|
|10–25s|Real movement and fuel collection|“Collect the cores. The goal stays on screen.”|
|25–40s|Clear lane and attempt extraction|“A miss stays in the recording; I want to know whether the game explained it.”|
|40–52s|Actual result|“This is what I completed, not a staged score.”|
|52–60s|URL and friends menu|“Try the mission first. Then test a private room with people who want to play.”|

Post to itch and your channel. If you don't complete the mission, show the attempt honestly; don't splice a win into it.

## B. One near miss — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “That gap was not as wide as I thought.” Show a real obstacle approach, near miss/collision and the recovery/result. Say: “One short mission. Tell me whether that miss felt fair.” End with the domain. No claim of a high score not earned.

## C. Two devices, one room — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “An empty room isn't multiplayer.” Show host creation, guest joining on a second device and both actually playing, with codes hidden. Say: “The test is whether a real friend gets in and stays connected.” Record only after a successful test; otherwise publish a candid bug update instead.

## D. Choose the mission — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Don't explore every menu before having fun.” Show the busy menu, highlight Start campaign, then the first objective. Say: “Campaign first. Learn one loop. The hangar and other modes can wait.” Use this practical clip to improve onboarding rather than hiding the actual interface behind a cinematic trailer.
