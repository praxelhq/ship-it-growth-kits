# Chaos Theory — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, interpreted as distinct new player starts under [the no-account policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) only if the actual playable flow has no required account. Label it **new player starts**; if signup is required, count unique real accounts instead. Choose the real definition before outreach and do not switch to inflate the result.

Report **completed first missions** beside starts. For social play, separately count private rooms with at least two real players who both reach active gameplay, and completed group sessions. A host-created lobby with no guest is not multiplayer activation.

## Day 0

Record existing real players/accounts, first-mission completions, actual joined rooms and seven-day sessions from your own records; those private counts were unavailable in research. Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) receives visits without room codes or private names. Record the current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/chaos-theory) reading, period and rank. Fresh-page level/star counters are not cohort-user counts.

Instrument `mission_started`, `first_mission_completed`, `room_created`, `guest_joined`, `multiplayer_started`, `session_completed` and `connection_failed`. Keep room analysis private and aggregate; never publish live codes as analytics labels. Separate solo and multiplayer players and deduplicate people who use both.

## Honest reach and decisions

Five host invitations, one optional six-person session and six Shorts form this calendar. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 starts need 2,000 qualified visits. At 1% view→visit, about 200,000 views are required. The current plan does not establish that reach. Measure hosts who voluntarily bring a new group before extrapolating a network effect.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|First-mission completion and guest-join errors|If new players can't understand the objective or connect, stop adding traffic.|
|Day 14|Rooms created versus rooms with 2+ real active players|A large gap is a joining/coordination problem, not success.|
|Day 21|Later-day return and hosts organizing unaided|If every room needs your support, simplify joining and test recovery before scaling.|
|Day 30|New starts/accounts, first missions, active group sessions, returners, actual reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000 and which mode produced genuine repeated play.|

Keep one weekly actual row of these counts, broad device errors and hours. Stars earned, ships displayed, daily-menu taps and unverified Plus interactions are not additional users or revenue.
