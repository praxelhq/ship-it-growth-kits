# Judge My Chat — Distribution map

**Lead with a harmless synthetic joke and a clear privacy boundary.** Don't promote a tool for exposing arguments or making real legal accusations.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult testers | Synthetic or mutually agreed harmless chats, no file sent to you. Ask whether the verdict is funny and limits clear. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed verdicts if the flow works. | #1 |
| Three existing friend/campus groups | Admin-approved invitation, one message each; no raw exports/results of others posted in the group. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | Synthetic case, court flavour, verdict preview and sharing caution on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–120 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Toy-court/real-privacy product lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–24 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback after usable synthetic trial and price/privacy tests; current rules checked. No votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No legal-advice community, conflict-support group, paid meme page, influencer or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. A free campus newsletter can carry a synthetic demo only after permission, zero forecast until accepted. Don't post under a real person's dispute to pitch entertainment.

At 50% visitor-to-case-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct qualified visits. The initial tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Don't assume a verdict share will spread if sharing is inappropriate; privacy can rightly keep it private. Use different groups from Scrollback's gift-story tests and Kalesh Judge's public-voting concept.
