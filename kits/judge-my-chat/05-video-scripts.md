# Judge My Chat — Video scripts

Every conversation is synthetic. No real private argument, financial allegation, name or legal claim is needed for a funny demo.

## A. Toy-court demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“The fictional chai promise is on trial.”**

2–12s, show an owned made-up chat and privacy/limit note. “This isn't a real person's evidence.”

12–25s, run the actual trial after it works. “A comic verdict, not a legal finding.”

25–38s, inspect the card for names/quotes. “Review before sharing, even when it's a joke.”

38–50s: free-trial/actual-price boundary and URL. Don't edit a failed response into success.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “Tomorrow's chai. Today's fictional trial.”

2–10s: synthetic promise and actual comic output. “No law degree implied.”

10–15s: URL and **Entertainment only · made-up chat**.

## C. Privacy hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “'Not stored' doesn't answer every privacy question.”

2–12s: show actual processing explanation. “Know where a file goes before using it.”

12–20s: synthetic sample path. “You can test the joke without exposing a real conversation.”

## D. Share-preview clip — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Would you send this without checking it?”

2–12s: inspect an actual synthetic card for identifiers/quotes. “The people in the joke should choose whether it travels.”

12–20s: keep private or share deliberately. No staged public humiliation or fake conflict.
