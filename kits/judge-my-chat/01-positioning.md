# Judge My Chat — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.judgemychat.live/ · **Category:** Chat entertainment · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/judge-my-chat

The actual page title checked 29 October is **“Judge My Chat.”** The CTA is **“Put a Chat on Trial”**, linking to `/court`. The court page says **one free case trial, no signup required upfront**. No paid price was visible in the inspected pages; disclose any later limit/charge before inviting users.

Your first user is an adult friend group laughing about a harmless, mutually agreed chat moment. Use a fictional unpaid-chai promise for the public demo, not a real conflict, private allegation or someone else's unconsented export.

**Pitch:** Turn an agreed chat joke into a clearly fictional courtroom verdict you can inspect before sharing.

**Short version:** A toy court for a chat joke, not a real judgment.

## The wedge

One short synthetic conversation, one playful verdict card. The page describes country choices and “laws,” but entertainment output is not legal analysis or authority. Don't market it as evidence that a real person broke a law, owes money or deserves public blame.

The $0 alternative is a [WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) poll or a friend narrating the joke. The cohort's [Scrollback](https://scrollback.in/) offers a different job—local story preview, then ₹49 full story—not an AI court. Judge My Chat earns attention through the comic format, if it is genuinely funny without exposing people.

## The uncomfortable bit

The court page says a chat is read once and never stored. That isn't the same as device-only processing; the product describes an AI judge. Explain actual server/provider processing, retention and sharing before a file is selected. Don't borrow Scrollback's offline privacy claim.

The public pages and upload destination were read; no chat or payment was submitted. Test synthetic upload, verdict, failure, sharing and any paywall before growth. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful case sessions, with completed verdicts beside it. A file-picker click or repeated test isn't a new user.
