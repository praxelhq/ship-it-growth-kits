# Judge My Chat — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** The no-upfront-signup trial uses **distinct people's first meaningful case sessions** as its signup equivalent. Report **completed verdicts** and **cards deliberately reviewed before sharing** beside it. A file-picker click or second joke isn't another person.

Private baseline values weren't accessed. Record actual totals and free/paid offer before posting.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| First case sessions / last 7 days | Aggregate status events, guest deduplication limits stated |
| Valid files, completed verdicts and failures | Status/error code only, no chat content |
| Card previews/downloads | Aggregate events, no identifiers/quotes |
| Real payments/refunds if any | Verified payment records, not assumed pricing |
| Traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics / [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Course metric/rank/time | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/judge-my-chat) |

Check existing Clarity before adding scripts. Exclude uploads, screenshots, text messages, verdict content, names, tokens and payment details from replay. Track `trial_started`, `file_validated`, `verdict_completed`, `card_previewed`, `card_downloaded` as status only. A share click isn't a new recipient.

| Checkpoint | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Can five willing testers finish a synthetic case and identify entertainment limits? | Fix the first blocked/misleading step. |
| Day 14 | Is the free allowance clear before upload? | Clarify the offer rather than surprising users afterward. |
| Day 21 | Do people enjoy the output without exposing others? | Improve joke/preview safety and clarity, not humiliation. |
| Day 30 | First sessions against 1,000; verdicts/limits beside it | Report target gap and useful channel honestly. |

At 50% visitor-to-case-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 relevant unique visits. The scheduled small tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Replace assumptions after three clips; repeated synthetic QA doesn't count as traction.

Course 24-hour readings overlap and aren't cumulative users. A verdict doesn't prove a legal violation, debt or real-world blame. The meaningful result is a clearly framed entertainment experience with privacy and price understood.
