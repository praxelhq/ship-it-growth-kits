# MyWorkFolio — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“Congratulations, you’re hired.”** CTA **Start your vault free** → `/login?mode=signup`; free ₹0 forever, Pro ₹99/month early access. Public privacy/refund policies read, no account/upload/ZIP/payment tested. Recommendations are ranked before acquisition.

## 1. Separate delivered free features from future paid benefits

The page says “Everything above is free” after an in-development Voice AI section, and the refund policy says Pro unlocks immediately while major benefits say at launch. Replace the summary with:

> **Live free workflow:** manual private vault, three pack types, ZIP downloads and basic missing/stale/pack-gap reminders. **In development:** Voice AI and any features explicitly marked at launch. Don't pay expecting an unfinished feature today.

Owner lists exactly what Pro delivers now, whether early-access payment is available and what buyers receive/can cancel. If no additional live benefit exists, pause paid promotion until terms/delivery are concrete. Keep ₹99/month recurring/cancel-anytime scope clear; don't describe it as one-time.

## 2. Correct the implied automatic filing

“Upload once, and everything sits in the right silo” plus the animation can imply scanning. Suggested replacement:

> Choose a document type and silo when you upload. MyWorkFolio stores the file and uses the details you provide to build packs and reminders; it doesn't scan the file's contents or verify the document.

Show the real manual choice in the first demo. Owner tests PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP/Word and 10 MB limit with synthetic files, plus failures. Don't invent OCR, encryption mode or verification.

## 3. Scope “only you” to the actual access policy

The [privacy policy](https://myworkfolio.in/privacy) discloses operator administrator access for requested support/law, expiring five-minute file links and backups up to 30 days. Suggested plain block:

> Other users can't open your private vault. The operator has technical admin access for support you request or legal requirements. Active files can be deleted; backup copies expire within 30 days. Read the full policy before uploading sensitive paperwork.

Don't claim end-to-end encryption, zero operator access or instant deletion of every copy. Owner verifies access isolation with owned test accounts, file-link expiry, pack removal, masking and actual deletion/export. These are tests, not an invitation to probe strangers' records.

## 4. Make the first try safe and small

Put this before the signup CTA:

> Try three clearly marked synthetic files first. No real Aadhaar, PAN, payslip or signature needed to understand the workflow. For adults 18+; upload only files you own or are authorized to hold.

Let the maker demonstrate the ZIP contents privately with fake filenames/data. Avoid sharing a fake document that resembles a usable official identity; mark every page SAMPLE. Don't ask students to screen-share their real vault during a classroom demo.

## 5. Label the quiz's hours as illustrative

“Paperwork Chaos Score” and hours adding up can imply measured savings. Proposed explanation:

> An illustrative self-check, not a validated assessment or measured estimate of your time. Your actual paperwork and effort can differ.

Let people skip to the free vault without completing seven cards. Remove unsupported “takes seconds every time” promises; show an actual small-pack walkthrough and measured owner test if relevant, not a universal job-switch guarantee.

## 6. Keep account control and billing findable

Place Settings export/delete, privacy/refunds and actual support route near account/billing. The posted refund terms: first payment request within seven days, renewals not generally refunded, cancel stops next renewal while documents remain Free. Don't turn that into legal advice or guarantee settlement timing. Verify cancel/entitlement paths and avoid showing private billing identifiers in replays.

Success criteria: first synthetic pack correctly assembled/downloaded, privacy understood and no false expectation of automatic scanning/future Voice AI—not just quiz completion.
