# MyWorkFolio — Video scripts

All files must be fully synthetic, watermarked SAMPLE, with no real ID numbers, salary, signatures, institutions or student names. Hide account/email/storage URLs, never record a real career vault. A fictional screenshot is a demo, not customer proof.

## A. Main demonstration — 55 seconds, landing/session

**0–7s:** Three fake generic files in separate clean folders. “The next paperwork request is easier if the files are already together.” Overlay synthetic examples.

**7–20s:** Actual upload/manual type/silo selection. “I choose the type. The app doesn't read the document to decide for me.” Show the real file-size/format validation, no fake automatic sorting animation.

**20–32s:** Actual pack builder. “These are the files selected for this example pack.” Inspect a missing item honestly; don't imply every HR requirement is automatically known.

**32–42s:** Actual ZIP download/open in a clean folder. “Check the contents before sending anything.” No HR delivery or expiring share link unless currently working/tested.

**42–49s:** Public privacy excerpt: operator admin support access, backup-retention boundary. “Read the storage/access terms before adding real private files.”

**49–55s:** CTA current free vault/packs, 18+, account required; Pro/future features separate. URL. No resume-AI or measured time-saving claim.

## B. Hook — 23 seconds, Day 10

**0–5s:** “HR needs the pack tomorrow” on a fictional request card. “This is the part I wanted to make calmer.”

**5–15s:** Real synthetic pack selection/ZIP. “Choose the right files, then check what the ZIP contains.”

**15–23s:** “Try fake files first. Don't send me your documents.” URL/free/18+ card. Don't turn a fake HR request into a customer testimonial.

## C. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 16

**0–6s:** Manual type control. “This isn't automatic document AI.”

**6–17s:** Real user-selected silo/type, then pack. “You label the file; the app organizes from those details. It doesn't scan your payslip.”

**17–25s:** “Know the workflow before uploading anything private.” URL and synthetic-file label. Don't show a password-protected real file or personal filename.

## D. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 24

**0–6s:** Free/live versus Pro/in-development columns using actual page labels. “A future feature shouldn't look like a delivered benefit.”

**6–17s:** Current free pack workflow, not Voice AI animation. “This is what the demo shows today. Check the live terms before paying for Pro.”

**17–25s:** Privacy/refund links and URL. “Start with the free synthetic test.” No claim that cancellation/deletion was independently verified if only policy read.

Repurpose clean footage for six calendar posts; use two text/process stills rather than constantly producing new clips. Correctness and safe private-data handling come before polished transitions.
