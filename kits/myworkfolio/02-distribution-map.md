# MyWorkFolio — Distribution map

This route earns trust before asking for sensitive storage. Target adults handling their own job switch, not recruiters seeking someone else's documents. Separate contacts from FirstDoor's startup outreach and the diary/reference cluster. One organizer gets one primary product invitation per week.

## Ranked routes

**Twenty warm adult job-switcher invitations**, five each Days 5,12,19,26. Offer a synthetic pack exercise first using 03 #1. No request for actual IDs, salary, certificates, account access or a screen-share of a real vault. The 18+ policy is a qualification condition. One follow-up maximum, no public contact list.

**Six owned educational posts**, Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Show manual document typing, a synthetic three-file pack, private ZIP retrieval and clear operator-access limits. Use 03 #2/#3 and 05 videos. Post manually; [LinkedIn policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) disallows unauthorized automation/scraping and fake engagement. Don't mass-DM everyone announcing a new job or infer their document needs from public posts.

**One conditional twelve-person adult paperwork session**, Day 15. Ask a known alumni/career organizer Day 8 with 03 #4; use maker-owned synthetic files only. Proposed twelve-person cap isn't secured access/attendance. No minors, no document collection, no required account or purchase just to attend. If declined, individual synthetic tests and zero event reach.

**Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad, current eligibility/slot/existing listing check Day 18 and optional Day 23 submission. A functioning vault and honest live/free versus future/Pro features are prerequisites. No booked slot, vote requests or guaranteed traffic.

**Product Hunt**, https://www.producthunt.com/launch, optional Day 25 after existing-listing/readiness review. Real maker account, meaningful working product/iteration, no paid hunter or upvote asks. Don't market an early-access feature as shipped.

**One introduced adult career editor**, Day 22 with 03 #7. Offer an original synthetic pack-readiness checklist, disclose ownership, no guessed audience size/contact. Zero base traffic until accepted/published. The checklist shouldn't solicit identity documents.

**AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional factual private-vault app entry after verified email/category check. Free reviews may be indefinite, paid priority outside budget; zero traffic until accepted. Don't imply a directory listing is a privacy endorsement.

## Forecast and constraints

Planning assumptions only: 20 warm invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12 visits; × 25–50% account/synthetic first pack = 2–6 activations. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 10–25% account/first pack = about 1–8. Accepted twelve-person session × 60–80% attendance = 7–10; × 20–40% later account/first pack = about 1–4. Remove known overlap and don't call a synthetic trial a real document migration.

Rough base 3–14 activations without the event, 4–18 with one before deduplication. Real private-document adoption is a separate voluntary measure, never a requirement to count workshop participation. No unaccepted editor/launch/directory reach is in the base. This cannot credibly promise 1,000 accounts. Keep the target, report the actual gap and pursue larger **accepted** adult career partnerships only after privacy/retrieval works. Don't compensate for trust friction by collecting more sensitive data.
