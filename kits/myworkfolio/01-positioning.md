# MyWorkFolio — Positioning

**Live:** https://myworkfolio.in/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/myworkfolio · Checked 29 October 2026. One kit covers duplicate roster entries; don't create a second acquisition campaign.

Your first user is an adult job switcher who already owns career documents but retrieves them from scattered places whenever HR asks. Start with a pack assembled from a few non-sensitive synthetic documents in a demonstration, then let users decide privately whether to store their real files. Don't begin by asking a student group for Aadhaar, PAN or payslips.

Actual H1 **“Congratulations, you’re hired.”** It leads into the next-day HR document request. **Start your vault free** → `/login?mode=signup`; **Get Pro · ₹99/month** is advertised separately. This audit read landing/privacy/refunds pages, not authenticated storage, ZIP generation, payment or account deletion.

## The pitch

> Keep your career paperwork together before the next HR deadline. Choose the document types, assemble a pack and retrieve it privately.

Bio: **Your career documents, ready for the next request.**

The free plan is **₹0 forever**, ten silos, job-switch/MBA/BGV packs, ZIP download and basic stale-resume/missing-payslip/pack-gap reminders. Users choose silo and type manually. The FAQ says **nothing scans file contents**; don't advertise AI auto-sorting or automatic document verification from the filing animation.

Pro is **₹99/month billed monthly**, cancel anytime. Voice AI resume pointers are explicitly **in development**, email/WhatsApp reminders and expiring HR links **at launch**. The refund page says Pro unlocks immediately while the page describes major benefits as future. Resolve actual delivered benefits before asking anyone to pay; do not sell a future feature as working.

## Alternatives and the useful difference

A well-organized existing local folder plus a checklist costs ₹0 in extra specialist software. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can hold a manual document inventory without uploading the documents themselves. That may be enough for someone who doesn't want another vault. MyWorkFolio earns trust through correct packs, easy private retrieval and clear controls, not proof that everyone loses three hours per HR email.

The landing's thirteen scattered-file examples and seven-card “Paperwork Chaos Score” are illustrative. Their time estimates are not validated population research or measured user savings. A demo company/name/ID is not a real customer. Never create quantified resume achievements or paperwork-savings testimonials without real evidence and consent.

## Privacy and target

[The privacy policy](https://myworkfolio.in/privacy) says **18+**, Mumbai Supabase storage, encrypted transit/rest, account rules, five-minute file links and operator administrator access when needed for requested help/law. “Only you” means not shared with other users, not zero technical operator access. Deleted active files are removed, but database backups expire within **30 days**. Don't claim end-to-end encryption or instant erasure of every copy. These are operator claims, not a security/legal compliance audit.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**, alongside first correctly typed private document and a usable pack download. Initial public demonstrations must use fully synthetic files, not real salary/identity records. The customer is an individual career owner, not an HR department procuring a records platform.
