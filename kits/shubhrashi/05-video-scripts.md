# shubhrashi — Video scripts

Use your actual product and a redacted specification. Do not film certificates, farms or processing lines that are not yours.

## A.70-second buyer walkthrough — direct follow-up / homepage
0–2: two quote sheets; text **“Same spice. Different order.”**
2–15: say “Before comparing price, identify grade, quantity and packing.” Zoom those fields.
15–30: show a permitted real batch/spec document or a clearly synthetic example. “Documents must apply to the lot being quoted.”
30–50: open Request Bulk Quote and fill only a synthetic demonstration locally; do not submit. “Tell us product, quantity, destination and date.”
50–70: show the written quotation format: taxes, freight, validity, sample and payment terms. “A quote is a starting agreement, not proof of delivery. Ask us what can actually be supplied.”

## B.20-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: hold retail pouch and bulk sack; **“$4.99 is not a per-kg bulk quote.”**
2–12: “Pack size, grade and delivery change the comparison.”
12–20: show the quote checklist. “Compare the complete order. shubhrashi.com.”

## C.25-second hook — buyer education
0–2: close-up MOQ label; **“Thirty kilos or a thousand?”**
2–12: “If a page gives three minimum quantities, the buyer cannot plan.”
12–22: show your corrected single product specification after 04 is fixed.
22–25: “One product. One confirmed minimum.”

Use captions, steady phone shots and hard cuts. Never say a product passed a lab test unless the actual report supports it. Do not publish customer-specific price or purchase data.
