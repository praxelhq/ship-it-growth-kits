# shubhrashi — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days** under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). An enquiry is not a customer; a retail checkout from a classmate does not become a trade account.

Activation: a genuine buyer's product specification/quantity/destination is onboarded and a real sourcing/sample/order workflow is underway on agreed terms. Report fulfilled correct lots separately. Count only real businesses with their actual requirements in the workflow, independent use during the last seven days, and a privately known decision-maker name/phone. Exclude quote-only curiosity and demo data. No contact ledger belongs in this public repo.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and confirm public-page events, masking enquiry text/contact details. Record real trade enquiries, qualified buyers, approved specifications, samples sent/accepted, actual orders, fulfilled lots and repeat sourcing. Values are not available to this kit; do not assume zero. Capture current rank/reading/sync at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/shubhrashi).

Events: bulk_quote_opened, enquiry_received, requirement_confirmed, sample_agreed, lot_fulfilled, independent_repeat_request. Only mark received after actual team receipt, not a button animation.

| Review | Scheduled input and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 |8 approaches. No replies: check owned contact route and buyer fit first. |
| Day 14 |16 approaches, 4 meaningful specification conversations desired. Quotes but no samples: check MOQ, price basis and proof. |
| Day 21 |24 approaches, 3 independently active businesses desired. Delays or disputed specs: pause new acquisition. |
| Day 30 |32 approaches;10-customer target retained. Report achieved businesses, samples, orders and correct fulfilment beside it. |

The 02 planning assumptions yield about 5 active relationships. Overseas procurement may take longer; be candid about the gap. Do not relax the customer definition to make a headline.

Sunday ledger: unique businesses by source, conversation→specification→sample→order→recent use, actual lead time, discrepancy/complaint count and hours. If the site claims a certificate that cannot be produced, remove the claim immediately. A high enquiry count with no fulfilled trustworthy supply is not growth.
