# shubhrashi — Positioning

**Live:** https://shubhrashi.com/ · **Category:** E-commerce / B2B spices · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/shubhrashi

Your live headline is “Premium Indian Spices & Snacks.” **Request Bulk Quote** opens an export view, then **Request a Quote** opens the contact form. The retail cumin card starts at $4.99; that is not a verified wholesale price per kg.

**Fix credibility before outreach.** The public contact number is the sequential 12345-67890 number. The homepage says MOQs start at 30 kg, export overview says 500 kg, and several product cards say 1,000 kg. The homepage claims 15+ years while the export view says 10+. Certification badges, ratings and 300+ B2B clients are website claims we have not independently substantiated. Do not repeat them as facts in outreach.

Your first ten should be reachable domestic spice buyers: a restaurant group, local speciality grocer or spice packer with a defined cumin/coriander requirement. Choose one actual grade and feasible lot size, then earn a repeatable sourcing relationship. Ten international container orders inside 30 days is not an honest zero-budget plan.

**Pitch:** Get a written specification, sample process and landed quote for the spice lot you actually need.
**Short version:** Clear spice specs and one accountable bulk quote.

The wedge is one product with an actual available batch: cumin, exact purity/moisture specification, pack size, quantity and destination. [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/) and [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) are real alternative supplier-discovery routes. Browsing is available without a paid sourcing engagement; wholesale prices remain supplier/lot quotes. Do not invent a market comparison from consumer pack prices.

Your weakness is proof. A buyer needs batch records, the legal supplier identity, traceable certifications where applicable, fulfilment terms and a working contact. Start with what can be documented. Remove claims you cannot support.

**Target: 10 real business customers**, with their actual sourcing requirements onboarded and independent recent use. Enquiries, contact-form submissions and a friend's sample tasting do not count.
