# shubhrashi — Distribution map

**Local buyer conversations first; export directories later.** Your first month should produce trusted sourcing relationships, not a pile of unqualified overseas leads.

| Route | Exact use and rules | Scheduled scale / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) | Manually identify nearby grocers, restaurant groups and food packers; verify their own site and advertised business contact. Be explicit that you are a supplier, not a customer. No bulk contact harvesting. |8 blocks × 4 unique buyers=32 approaches; #1/#2. |
| [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/) | Inspect competing product descriptions to learn specification language. Create only your truthful supplier listing if free eligibility fits; no paid upgrades or fake buyer enquiries. |1 listing attempt; no assumed reach or sales; #3. |
| Existing food-business introductions | Ask two known business contacts for one permissioned introduction each. Bring a written specification; samples only if commercially available without an acquisition subsidy. |0–4 additional conversations, not guaranteed; #4. |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 9,19,26 at 09:00 IST: explain one real specification or quote ambiguity. No unverified export success or certification claims. |Planning guess 100–400 impressions/post, 0–2 relevant visits; #5. |

Assumed funnel: 32 approaches × 50% buyer conversation × 50% qualified sample/specification trial × 60% independent active relationship ≈5 customers. This is an explicit shortfall against 10. Higher actual conversion, accepted introductions or a longer sales cycle may change it. Report what happened; don't count quotes as customers.

No named food-importer newsletter submission or group-promotion permission was verified, so none contributes to the plan. Do not buy trade leads, export promotion packages or pay to attend exhibitions.

Keep outreach domestic until fulfilment and documentation support an overseas lot. Never imply that an online certification logo is regulatory clearance for a destination market.
