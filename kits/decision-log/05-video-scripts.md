# DecisionLog — Video scripts

Use synthetic decisions and local demo data. Never film an actual investor, hiring or relationship log. Show the real current interface; label any compressed passage of time. Don't pretend an audit tested private AI reports.

## A. Product demonstration — 50 seconds, landing page and accepted session

**0–6s, camera on a fictional written question:** “Will working in the library tomorrow help me finish the outline?” On-screen: *Invented example.*

**6–16s, real log form:** “Before I know, I write the expected result, confidence and a check-in date.” Enter the synthetic expectation and show date selection. Never type a real person's name.

**16–25s, saved state:** “Guest entries stay in this browser. An account is optional for sync.” Show save and reopen after reload only if actually verified. If not, record that verification first.

**25–34s, Today/calendar control:** “The due entry appears here. Add it to your calendar for a separate reminder.” Show the real control; don't splice in an imaginary email.

**34–43s, explicitly labelled later demonstration:** “When the result is genuinely known, compare it with what you wrote.” Use an already prepared fictional outcome, mark *Time compressed; sample data*.

**43–50s, original expectation beside result:** “One honest entry first. Reports need enough resolved decisions.” End CTA **Log a decision**, live URL. No claim that a 50-second video demonstrates months of improved judgment.

## B. Hook — 22 seconds, owned post Day 10

**0–5s:** Split frame “I knew it” versus a dated original expectation. Spoken: “Did I know it, or did I rewrite the memory?”

**5–15s:** Zoom to the original prediction, then sample outcome. “DecisionLog keeps the earlier words next to what you later report.”

**15–22s:** “Try one small decision. Keep it private.” Show URL and *Fictional example, not a psychological test*. Use on the maker's own feed, not unsolicited group replies.

## C. Hook — 25 seconds, owned post Day 16

**0–6s:** Show a six-month decision date. “This report isn't honestly ready next week.”

**6–17s:** Show ten-resolved-entry requirement and an unresolved example. “Full reports need ten resolved decisions. Unknown results should stay unknown.”

**17–25s:** Show first-save CTA. “The value today is remembering what you expected.” Keep no exaggerated “improve decisions by X%” overlay. Use to filter people seeking instant AI answers.

## D. Hook — 24 seconds, owned post Day 24

**0–6s:** Calendar graphic beside Today. “A date inside an app isn't automatically a notification.”

**6–16s:** Actual Add to calendar control with synthetic entry. “A due decision appears in Today. Add it to your own calendar if you want an external nudge.”

**16–24s:** Return to log. “Then compare the real outcome, when it exists.” URL and guest-storage note. Don't show a calendar email address or pretend the native reminder was tested if only the control was inspected.

Export captions, readable screen crops and a clean still of the original/result comparison. Do one retake after a real UI change; avoid daily production that crowds out first-use support.
