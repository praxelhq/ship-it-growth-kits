# DecisionLog — Distribution map

Prioritize individuals who already review their choices, not a generic “improve your life with AI” audience. Shared private-record research is in [the cluster note](../../shared/b1-private-records-cluster.md). Use a different organizer/contact list from Afterthoughts' evening diary demo and Mimori's design references. One organizer gets one primary product invitation per week.

## Ranked routes

1. **Twenty warm individual invitations**, five each on Days 5,12,19,26. Qualify people who will actually revisit a small decision within the month. Offer a synthetic demonstration first, then let them keep real entries private. Use 03 #1. One follow-up after a week; stop after a decline or silence after that. The CTA is one entry, not an immediate purchase or a screenshot of their private log.
2. **Six owned posts**, Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Use a fictional prediction made before the result and show the original alongside the outcome. Rotate 03 #2/#3 and 05 videos. Manual [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) posting only; no scraping, automated DMs, engagement exchange or borrowed founder contact lists. Don't target another lane's campus clinic.
3. **One conditional 15-person decision exercise**, Day 15, through an organizer you actually know. Ask on Day 8 with 03 #4; invite only after acceptance. Fifteen is a proposed attendance cap, not an existing audience. Nobody has to read a personal decision aloud. If declined, spend the time on individual usability tests and count no event reach.
4. **Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad, a checked launch destination. Inspect current rules, availability, eligibility and an existing listing on Day 18; submit Day 23 only if the working loop is ready. A past launch page is not a booked current slot. Use 03 #6, no votes requested.
5. **Product Hunt**, https://www.producthunt.com/launch, optional Day 25. A real maker account and working product/meaningful iteration come first. Inspect existing listing; don't repackage an unchanged launch or ask for upvotes. Zero secured reach until published.
6. **One introduced learning-newsletter editor**, Day 22 using 03 #7. Offer a no-login decision exercise with a fictional example, disclose ownership and ask if it fits. No guessed newsletter names, subscriber counts or guaranteed placements.
7. **AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional only after finding the correct category. Verified email and an accurate application entry are required; free reviews may wait indefinitely. Paid priority is outside this plan. Forecast zero traffic until accepted.

## Forecast, with the actual calendar's inputs

These are planning guesses, not channel benchmarks. Twenty warm invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12 visits; 40–60% of those save an entry = about 3–7 activated first-use sessions. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000 impressions; at 1% click-through, 6–30 visits; at 20–40% first-save, about 1–12 activations. An accepted 15-person session × 60–80% attendance = 9–12 attendees; × 40–60% first-save = about 4–7 activations. Overlap is removed, not added twice.

With no accepted session, these routes plausibly produce roughly 4–19 activations before deduplication; with it, roughly 8–26. Launches/editorial are zero in the base because none is secured. Even 100% conversion of the base visits would miss 1,000. Report that gap honestly. A second month needs retained users, an organizer who explicitly wants a repeat and a larger accepted distribution partner; increasing unsolicited message volume isn't a credible bridge.

Don't count quiz completions, sample-report clicks, log entries or returning sessions as 1,000 distinct people. Review each route's first-save and genuine-check-in rate before repeating it.
