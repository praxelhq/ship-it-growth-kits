# DecisionLog — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.decisionlog.in/ · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/decision-log · Consumer decision journal, checked 29 October 2026.

Your first user is an individual who repeatedly misremembers what they expected. Start with a reversible ordinary decision: whether to work in the library tomorrow, with an expected result and a genuine check-in date. Don't start with a five-year investment outcome, diagnosis or an employer's confidential hiring decision.

The actual H1 is “Your track record, made visible.” **Log a decision** opens the in-page log (`#dTitle`); **Take the 60-second test** opens the eight-question confidence exercise. The public promise is no signup and browser-local guest decisions. Optional email login moves entries to private account sync. This audit read the public experience; no account, payment, real decision or AI report was submitted.

## A narrower promise

> Write what you expect before you know what happens. Come back on the date you chose and compare the two.

Short bio: **Keep the prediction. Revisit the outcome.**

Lead with the loop the free plan supports: decision, expectation, confidence, check-in, self-reported outcome. A graph is a later reward, not an instant diagnosis of someone's judgment. Full reasoning reports require ten **resolved** decisions. Someone who makes a real six-month career decision cannot honestly unlock that report by next week.

The eight-question quiz is an accessible demonstration of confidence versus accuracy, not a validated psychological assessment. The illustrated 14-decision graph, nine-resolved-entry sample and founder/investor examples are explicitly samples, not customer evidence. Preserve those labels in screenshots.

## Actual offer and alternatives

Personal is ₹0 for up to five active decisions. The page says **every feature is free during early access**. Pro is a ₹299 one-month or ₹2,999 one-year **non-renewing pass**; Team from ₹999/person/month is **coming soon**. The headline pricing's “/month” must be reconciled with the pass explanation before purchase outreach. This kit doesn't assume a payment was processed or early access has ended.

A paper prediction log or local text file costs ₹0 in extra specialist software. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) provides a manual structured alternative with dates/confidence/outcomes. DecisionLog earns adoption through check-in organization and evidence-preserving comparison, not by claiming that no one can record decisions elsewhere.

## Trust and the target

A due entry appears in Today; an actual external nudge requires **Add to calendar** into the user's calendar. Don't promise email or push reminders. Browser-local entries require backup/device care; signed-in sync has a different storage boundary. Publish both clearly and never send journal text to analytics.

Keep the **1,000 B2C first-use target**. Because guest use needs no account, measure unique first-logging sessions separately from optional accounts. Activation means a real decision saved with a check-in; deeper value means a genuine outcome compared later. Avoid manufacturing ten short-lived entries simply to make the report metric look good. Professional role examples don't turn this into a company procurement campaign.
