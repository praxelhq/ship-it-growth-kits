# Ten Ring — Measurement

**Unchanged board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** The roster assigns B2C, but the live offer is an academy pilot followed by ₹2,500/month. Keep that mismatch explicit. Only count unique end users actually starting a real scoring session if they can be measured reliably; never count pilot-button clicks, athletes entered by a coach or repeated target photos as new signups.

Beside the headline report **unique users who calibrate, check and save a real training card**. The current device-local app has no academy accounts or verified unique-person measurement path; therefore the thousand-user target is unsupported today. State this limitation rather than inventing a consumer funnel.

## Day 0

Read the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/ten-ring) for latest reading date, traffic and rank. Record actual current users if known, local saved sessions, academy requests, accepted pilots, independent weekly use and paid continuation. Private totals are unknown to this kit; use **not measured**, not assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on public pages, excluding target/athlete records and names. Keep academy contacts privately. A device-local session counter cannot establish unique human users across phones or browsers.

## Separate academy diagnostic

An additional **ten-real-academy** diagnostic is useful, **not a replacement board target**. Apply the B2B policy's four tests: real academy, its actual training records onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, and a known responsible contact reachable privately. Payment isn't required. Requests, demos and synthetic squad data don't qualify.

## What this plan might produce

Twenty manual approaches with assumed 30–50% conversations and 30–50% pilot acceptance gives roughly 2–5 pilots. Three demos mostly overlap those prospects; don't add them again. Referrals might add 0–1. These are assumptions, not observed benchmarks. A few academy pilots cannot honestly be renamed 1,000 individual signups.
