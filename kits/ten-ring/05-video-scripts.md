# Ten Ring — Video scripts

Use a synthetic target or a coach-approved anonymized card away from active range operation. No athlete identifiers, unsafe range filming or official-scoring claim.

## A. The checked card — 60 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“Don't trust a decimal score you can't check.”**

2–15: Photograph the whole card in even light. “Ten Ring starts with the paper target you already use.”

15–30: Show the actual two calibration taps and ring overlay, uncut. “Centre, black edge, then check whether the computed rings agree with the print.”

30–42: Show score/group metrics and saving to the real board. “The record helps compare training sessions; your coach checks the result.”

42–52: Show **“Saved on this device only.”** “Current records stay on this device. Keep the original and existing record; shared academy accounts aren't here yet.”

52–60: “Two-week academy pilot; details at tenringtargets.com.” Use in personal coach demos.

## B. Two taps — 20 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“The ring check is the important bit.”**

2–14: Real calibration and overlay on a labelled sample. “These taps set scale. Inspect the rings before accepting the score.”

14–20: **“Training tool, not an official match result.”** URL. First LinkedIn post.

## C. One device — 25 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Where will this record be next week?”**

2–15: Show device-local notice and a test board. “Ten Ring currently saves on the device that scored the card. Clearing browser storage removes that record.”

15–25: “That is a pilot limitation to discuss up front, not after a coach relies on it.” URL. Second LinkedIn post; no fake cloud-sync demonstration.

## D. What the pilot tests — 30 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“One batch before a whole academy.”**

2–12: Coach-approved sample card and existing notebook. “Keep your normal scoring. Compare time, discrepancies and usefulness.”

12–23: Show two-week pilot and paid-continuation terms. “No hardware purchase. Continue at ₹2,500/month only if the actual service fits.”

23–30: “If storage, connection or calibration makes it unsuitable, say so.” URL. Send only after a coach agrees to see a demo.
