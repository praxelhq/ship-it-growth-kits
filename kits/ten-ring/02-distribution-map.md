Your realistic experiment is a few academies, with overlap between approaches and demonstrations. It is not a route to a thousand individually verified athletes this month.

## Real prospect examples, not invented customers

The official list includes Ramagya School Shooting Range (ShootX), Amity Shooting Club, Gyanshree School Shooting Academy and Shooting Guru Shooting Academy in Noida. These are examples to research, **not confirmed current buyers, contacts or willingness to pilot**. Pick one district you can actually serve and verify discipline, training schedule and public business route before approaching.

[NRAI's official site](https://www.thenrai.in/) is a reference for the sport, not an advertising board or an endorsement. No current coach-forum promotional rule or newsletter slot was verified, so no such placement appears in the forecast. A [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) build note can document the software later, but maker votes are not academy validation. Skip consumer launch directories until the pilot workflow and durable storage are clear.

Keep the private contact/pilot log out of this public repo. One initial message, one consented follow-up, then stop. Do not cold-message junior athletes or request their identifying records for a demo.
