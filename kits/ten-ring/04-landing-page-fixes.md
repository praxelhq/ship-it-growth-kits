# Ten Ring — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://tenringtargets.com/), [board](https://tenringtargets.com/app) and [scoring route](https://tenringtargets.com/app/score) were opened 29 October. The board is a working empty local state, not a failed page. No athlete image, pilot form or payment was submitted by this kit.

## 1. Put the record-storage limitation beside the action

Current H1: **“TEN RING.”** Prominent supporting headline: **“KEEP THE WHOLE PICTURE.”** Primary action **“Score a target”** reaches the app. The limitations section says clearing the browser clears the records.

Add immediately beside scoring/saving: **“Training records are saved on this device only. Clearing browser data removes them. Keep your original target and existing record.”** Make that visible before a coach relies on the squad board. Durable export/backup is a conversion priority, not a distant cosmetic feature.

## 2. Say who the product is for

Replacement subhead: **“For 10m air-rifle and air-pistol coaches: photograph a paper target, check the rings, and keep a training record on this device.”**

CTA labels: **“Try scoring a training card”** and **“Ask about a two-week academy pilot.”** Don't mix an academy subscription with a consumer-install headline.

## 3. Make calibration evidence the proof

Current sample shows 104.2 and precise millimetre metrics. Keep it labelled illustrative. Add a real permitted/anonymous card with the ring check visible and reference scoring explained. Don't claim certified accuracy from a demo. If the coach finds a disagreement, show how it is corrected or discarded.

## 4. Explain the pilot's paid continuation

Current offer is two weeks free, then ₹2,500/month per academy. Replacement: **“One real batch. Two weeks at no cost. We review fit with you; continue only by agreement at the listed ₹2,500/month. No international price set yet.”** Clarify current included support and data handling before anyone accepts, and do not imply a checkout/subscription was tested here.

## 5. Keep the missing-feature disclosure near the buyer's decision

Multi-coach accounts, parent reports, notes, full 60-shot match mode, offline capture and PDF export are not built. Summarize **“Single-device training pilot today; not a shared academy system or official match scorer.”** Preserve the full list. Don't turn future features into present-tense sales bullets.

## 6. Improve phone capture instructions before upload

The app already gives framing/light/surface guidance. Keep it next to capture, add a clear wrong-photo example, and preserve the actual card after an error so the coach needn't re-enter everything. Test range lighting and a weak connection before claiming “any phone” works reliably.

## 7. Make the shared preview specific

Title: **“Ten Ring — checked paper-target training records for coaches.”** Description: **“Phone photo, ring check and a device-local squad board. Free two-week academy pilot; ₹2,500/month if continued.”** No athlete names/photos in preview cards. A coach should understand storage, price and use case before booking time.
