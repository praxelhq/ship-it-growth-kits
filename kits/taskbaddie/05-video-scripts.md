# TaskBaddie — Video scripts

Use invented tasks, no grades/private schedules, and actual app behavior. A full-session demo can be compressed only with explicit labels and real elapsed-time evidence. No diagnosis or guaranteed productivity outcome.

## A. Main demonstration — 55 seconds, landing/session

**0–7s:** Three fictional tasks. “Which one will I actually start?” Sample label.

**7–19s:** Actual matrix sorting. “Urgent and important aren't always the same thing.” Don't imply the app knows importance automatically; show the human choice.

**19–29s:** Select one task and real 25-minute session. “Now one focus attempt, not another list redesign.” Show actual controls.

**29–40s:** Clearly marked time-lapse from an actual test. “Time compressed from a real session.” If only landing footage, say eight-second demo and don't present completion as work.

**40–48s:** Actual pause/end/break behavior. “A completed timer isn't proof the work was good. Decide what helped.” No fake confetti performance study.

**48–55s:** Free guest/no-signup CTA, optional sync, Pro recurrence separate, email reminders planned. URL.

## B. Hook — 22 seconds, Day 9

**0–5s:** Endless fictional list. “Organizing can become the distraction.”

**5–15s:** Three tasks into boxes, one chosen. “Sort just enough to start one thing.”

**15–22s:** Real timer start. “Free guest mode. No perfect streak required.” URL, sample-task label. Don't diagnose procrastination.

## C. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 14

**0–6s:** Landing timer with its short-demo label. “Eight seconds isn't a Pomodoro.”

**6–17s:** Actual 25/50/75 selection. “The landing previews the interaction; the app is where the real session happens.”

**17–25s:** Real test timing card if measured. “I'm checking reliability, not counting demo completions as study.” URL. No invented background-tab result.

## D. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 24

**0–6s:** Guest versus account labels. “No signup doesn't mean your task list is backed up everywhere.”

**6–17s:** Actual storage/sync explanation with synthetic tasks. “Guest data stays in this browser. Account sync is optional. Clearing site data can remove local tasks.”

**17–25s:** One task chosen. “Try the workflow before moving your day into it.” URL. No claim a YouTube music embed is offline/private by default.

Use four assets and two text/process posts for the six scheduled posts. Don't film a new motivational montage each day instead of testing the full timer.
