# TaskBaddie — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“Your to-do list, but make it baddie.”** **Start free — no signup** → `/app`; Free $0 forever, Pro $5/month USD recurring tasks. The landing says the timer demo is about eight seconds. Public app extraction didn't verify interactive persistence/timer. No account, payment, PWA or reminders tested here.

## 1. Make the full first task visible

Suggested support copy:

> Sort three tasks by urgent/important. Choose one and start a real 25-minute focus attempt. Try as a guest; no signup required.

CTA **Choose my first task — free**. Keep playful tone without promising grades, cured procrastination or a productivity percentage. Don't force an account/streak/profile before a useful action. Owner tests guest task save/reload, quadrants, start/pause/reset and actual completion.

## 2. Make the short demo unmistakable

The page already labels the eight-second timer; retain it in crops/share cards and add next to start:

> This landing demo lasts about eight seconds. Open the app for actual 25/50/75-minute sessions. A demo completion isn't a completed focus session.

Owner tests real duration, background tab, sleep/wake, screen lock and notification behavior. Don't promise background accuracy or alerts from a visual countdown alone. Label elapsed-time compression in videos, never fake 25 minutes passing in eight seconds.

## 3. Replace the unfair plain-list comparison

A plain to-do can have priorities/no signup and a phone can time a session. Suggested comparison:

> Paper/list + timer may already be enough. TaskBaddie puts four boxes, a sand timer and playful rewards together. Choose the workflow that helps you start.

Don't mark all plain lists incapable of priority boxes or no-signup use. Actual differentiation is integrated feel, not exclusivity of a known matrix method. Measure first task started, not comparative checkmarks.

## 4. Separate guest-local, sync and music data

[Privacy](https://www.taskbaddie.app/privacy) describes guest localStorage, account tasks on Supabase Tokyo, GA/Clarity and YouTube embeds. Suggested first-run note:

> Guest tasks stay in this browser; clearing site data removes them. An optional account stores/syncs tasks. Playing music uses YouTube's embedded player under its own data/cookie terms. Website analytics is separate from guest task storage.

No claim of totally offline/tracker-free service. Owner verifies text masking, account task cleanup on signout, sync/migration and actual export/deletion options before describing them. Don't invent backup/export controls absent from the app.

## 5. Keep future reminders and paid recurrence accurate

Suggested feature label:

> **Email reminders — planned, opt-in; not sending today.** Free includes matrix, planner, timer, music and optional sync. **Pro $5/month USD:** recurring daily/weekdays/weekly tasks. Cancel anytime under actual terms.

Owner tests recurrence timezone/duplicates/missed days, checkout/entitlement/cancellation using controlled accounts. Don't make music paid or imply an email will arrive because a reminder preference exists. Review current refund terms rather than inventing a guarantee.

## 6. Let stopping be normal

Suggested empty/end state:

> One task attempted. Take a break or stop here. Come back when the next session helps.

Avoid guilt about broken streaks. Verify PWA installation on actual devices before promising phone mini-widget behavior; a browser install prompt isn't proof of app-store/native-widget availability. Keep music optional and usable without forcing a third-party embed load before someone asks for it.
