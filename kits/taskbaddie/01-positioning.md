## The pitch

> Sort three tasks. Pick one. Give it a real focus session, without creating an account first.

Bio: **Less rearranging. One task started.**

Free is **$0 forever** for matrix, Today/Week, sand timer, music and guest/synced use. Timers offer 25/50/75 minutes. Pro is **$5/month USD**, recurring daily/weekdays/weekly tasks, cancel anytime; music remains free. Email nudges are **planned, opt-in**, not currently promised delivery. Don't sell Pro as necessary for basic music or a focus session.

The Eisenhower Matrix is a known urgency/importance method; a plain list or paper can also contain quadrants and no signup. Replace the page's categorical “plain to-do can't have priority boxes” framing with an honest workflow comparison. Existing phone timer plus paper costs ₹0 extra specialist software. TaskBaddie wins only if playful sorting/timing makes starting easier for this person.

## Privacy and limits

[The policy](https://www.taskbaddie.app/privacy) says guest tasks stay in browser localStorage and clearing site data removes them; optional account sync stores tasks on Supabase in Tokyo. Website GA/Clarity and YouTube music embeds have separate network/data behavior. Guest-local tasks don't mean the entire site is offline or tracker-free. Don't put private task words in events/replays; verify masking with synthetic tasks.

No documented native app-store launch was verified: “install as an app” means the browser/PWA route as described, not proof of a shipped iOS/Android widget. Test real devices before repeating those claims. Don't infer seven-day retention from a confetti animation or an eight-second demo.

Keep the **1,000 B2C first-use/signup target**. For guest access report unique first-use sessions separately from optional verified accounts, with honest deduplication limits. Activation is three tasks sorted and a real focus session started; completing that session/returning voluntarily is deeper value. Kaira in this lane leads with dated tasks and urgency reasons; TaskBaddie leads with deliberate focus, using different groups and a later exercise window.
