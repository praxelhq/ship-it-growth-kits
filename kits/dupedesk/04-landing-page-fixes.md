# DupeDesk — Landing-page fixes

Home/pricing/scan/privacy/terms/refunds read 29 October 2026; browser report route confirmed email-link access. No finder result, report, photo, account or payment was submitted. Similarity and savings percentages are site outputs, not independently validated efficacy/price outcomes.

## 1. Resolve report availability before traffic

**Current:** home says full personalized reports open soon via early access; pricing says unlimited full dupe reports free, and `/reports/new` redirects to free email-link account entry.

**Write the actual tested state:** **“Finder: no signup. Full report: free email-link account.”** Use only after the report generates successfully. If personalized reporting is still unavailable, label that specific feature and don't offer it as delivered. Remove stale early-access copy or distinguish exactly what remains on the roadmap. A free account form isn't proof of a completed report.

## 2. Keep differences with the real headline

**Current headline:** “Love the formula, not the price tag.” CTAs “Find my dupe” and “Try AI skin scan.”

**Keep the headline; write helper:** **“Compare listed ingredients and unit prices. Similar isn't identical; effects and suitability can differ.”** First action: **“Compare a basic product.”** Keep the optional scan secondary. Don't promote the comparison as a clinical substitute or an excuse to stop prescribed products.

## 3. Explain what a similarity score measures

**Current:**74%/86% and other similar-formulation labels appear beside savings figures.

**Write:** **“Ingredient-list matching score, not a clinical equivalence or safety score.”** Explain actual matching inputs, missing concentrations and version/date limits. Source each ingredient list and show shared/missing/extra items before a large percentage. Do not infer a universal concentration boundary or treatment efficacy from list order; have qualified reviewers check educational claims before amplification.

## 4. Source unit price and affiliate context at the choice

**Current:** large savings percentages, Indian retailer links and a footer affiliate disclosure.

**Write on the result:** **“Price per ml from the named size and checked seller price. Verify current stock, shipping and final amount.”** Keep affiliate disclosure next to relevant links and clarify it doesn't establish a retailer partnership. A selected seller price or list discount isn't guaranteed money saved by a user.

## 5. Bound selfie claims and prove the data path

**Current scan:** AI reads skin type/visible concerns, own-photo 18+ consent, photo never leaves device, lighting/makeup affect results, not diagnosis.

**Write:** **“Optional appearance description from this photo, not a diagnosis or validated product-suitability test.”** Independently check browser/network/storage behaviour on a controlled permitted image before calling no-upload proven. Don't require photos for a label comparison or use sensitive skin appearance as viral content. Marketing can stay entirely photo-free.

## 6. Make the paid report and refund conditions plain

**Current:**₹199 Premium routine report; no subscription. Refund page allows unused report refund within seven days and technical-failure remedy, but no refund after successful creation.

**Write before purchase:** **“₹199 for one routine report. No subscription. Review content, limits and refund conditions before paying.”** Don't advertise a generic no-questions refund or guaranteed “ingredient clashes fixed.” The output remains information to review, not a clinician-approved regimen. Verify actual checkout and failed-report handling with authorized testing.
