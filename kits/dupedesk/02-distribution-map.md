[INKEEDecoder](https://incidecoder.com/about) is a verified ingredient-literacy source/alternative, not an advertising board. Its editorial subscription exists, but no free guest feature or promotion permission was established. Don't harvest its users or copy ingredient explanations wholesale. No current skincare subreddit promotion rule or dermatologist endorsement was verified, so none is scheduled.

Base **11–46 accounts before overlap**, from twenty invitations, six posts and optional small session/launch. Far below 1,000. At day 14 inspect whether users understand differences and price sources. Don't inflate the number with anonymous searches, waitlist emails or repeated face scans, and never make a clinical claim to earn a more dramatic clip.
