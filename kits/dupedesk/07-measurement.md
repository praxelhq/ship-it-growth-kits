# DupeDesk — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 confirmed individual accounts** for full free reports. Activation beside it: **one actual ingredient/unit-price comparison reviewed**, with a later independent comparison separately reported. Anonymous finder sessions, early-access emails, generated lines, selfies and affiliate clicks don't become accounts. No purchase or application of a product is required to count useful information.

## Day 0

Record actual email-link accounts, successful free reports, failed reports, difference/source reviews, independent returns and real Premium report purchases/refunds from owner records. No private baseline was established here. Capture current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/dupedesk) rank, reading and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Home ingredient/product counts, example 86% score and advertised savings aren't user outcomes.

Follow the actual [Privacy](https://www.dupedesk.shop/privacy) processing boundary. It says no advertising/tracking cookies and anonymous retailer-link counts, while reports/profiles may use hosted/AI providers. Don't install [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) or another recorder because a generic checklist mentions it; first verify lawful/policy-consistent measurement with the owner. No skin photo, concern, profile, report text or email in analytics. Proposed minimal internal counts only if supported: `account_created`, `finder_result_ready`, `report_completed`, `report_failed`, `differences_reviewed`, `price_source_opened`, `independent_return`, `premium_report_purchase_confirmed`.

## Forecast tied to `06`

Assumptions, not skincare-app benchmarks:

- 20 invitations × 50% try × 60% account = six accounts.
- Six posts × 200–700 impressions × 2% visits × 20% account ≈ 5–17 accounts.
- One accepted session: 12 invited × 50% attend × 50% account = three, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–100 visits × 20% account = 0–20 accounts.

Rounded base **11–46 before overlap**, below 1,000. If two-thirds review a comparison, roughly 7–31 activate. At an assumed 20% visitor-to-confirmed-account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified visits; the calendar doesn't establish that reach. Recompute after 15 invitations. Don't expand the forecast with a selfie trend or an unsupported medical/identical-product claim.
