# DupeDesk — Video scripts

Use current permitted public product-label data or a clearly labelled synthetic comparison. No real skin photo, health history, diagnosis or routine advice. Show actual source/price checks; phone voice and screen capture suffice.

## A · Shared, missing, extra ·60 seconds · own feed/listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
|0–2 s|Actual comparison label. **“Similar doesn't mean identical.”**|“I want the differences before the percentage.”|
|2–14 s|Supported basic-product input.|“This is a label comparison, not a treatment recommendation.”|
|14–29 s|Actual shared/missing/extra sections.|“Which ingredients appear in both, which don't, and what is still unknown?”|
|29–43 s|Current pack size/price source.|“I check the exact size and current seller before trusting a per-ml saving.”|
|43–53 s|Score limitation and affiliate disclosure.|“The match isn't clinical equivalence, and a link may earn a commission.”|
|53–60 s|Free/account boundary and URL.|“Start with the free finder at dupedesk dot shop. No selfie needed.”|

If the actual result doesn't generate or the label source is stale, fix it before filming a successful report. No invented skin benefit or purchase result.

## B · Read past the percentage ·20 seconds · short clip

Hook: **“What is missing from this match?”** 0–8 s, actual missing/extra section.8–15 s: “A high ingredient score can still leave differences that matter to the shopper.”15–20 s: “Read them before choosing.” No claim a specific missing ingredient determines clinical efficacy.

## C · Unit price has inputs ·25 seconds · comparison help

Hook: **“Which bottle size made that saving?”** 0–8 s, clearly synthetic pack-size/price pair.8–18 s, show arithmetic: “Price divided by volume needs the exact current product and seller.”18–25 s: “Shipping, stock and final price still need checking.” Don't portray invented numbers as today's retailer offer.

## D · No selfie for the first test ·20 seconds · privacy introduction

Hook: **“You can compare labels without a face photo.”** 0–8 s, show finder and separate scan route.8–15 s: “My first test is ingredient information, with no skin assessment.”15–20 s: “If you choose a scan, read its consent/data limits separately.” Don't upload a user image to prove a privacy claim or suggest the scan diagnoses a condition.
