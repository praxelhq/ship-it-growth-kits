# DupeDesk — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.dupedesk.shop/ · **Category:** E-commerce · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/dupedesk

**An ingredient match is not proof two products work the same on someone's skin.** The actual headline is **“Love the formula, not the price tag.”** The page promises more affordable skincare with similar formulation, ingredient comparison and India retailer links. **“Find my dupe”** opens the public finder; **“Try AI skin scan”** is a separate selfie route. The page explicitly rejects “identical” claims, which should survive every ad and result screenshot.

Current [pricing](https://www.dupedesk.shop/pricing) says finder/top-three matches/full dupe reports/scan are free; **Premium is ₹199 per routine report**, a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Browser inspection of `/reports/new` redirected to email-link sign-in with **“Save your skin profile and get full dupe reports, free.”** No search result, email account, report, selfie processing or payment was completed here. The home still says early access to full personalized reports is coming; reconcile that with today's free report offer before a launch.

## Your first person

An adult already using a basic moisturizer, who wants to inspect ingredient differences and current price-per-ml before considering an alternative. Start with information, not treating a condition or replacing prescribed products. Don't lead the first campaign with sunscreen equivalence, active-treatment routines or a selfie diagnosis.

> Compare the listed ingredients and current unit prices, see what's different, and decide which questions to check before buying.

Bio: **Similar ingredients, differences visible. No identical-product claim.**

The first useful action is one checked comparison: original INCI list, shared/missing/extra items, pack size and current retailer source. A similarity percentage is the matching engine's label, not a clinical effectiveness score. A reported 93% saving isn't money saved until a real buying decision/outcome supports it.

## Alternatives

[INKEEDecoder's official about page](https://incidecoder.com/about) explains ingredient-list decoding and links its public tools; it now identifies the service as INKEEDecoder. No current paid amount was established, so don't invent one or claim ingredient explanation exists only behind subscriptions. A user can also read two current manufacturer labels side by side without a new comparison-software fee.

Your potential difference is an India-available, inspectable comparison and unit-price workflow, not a guarantee of matching texture, concentration, safety or effect. Retailer prices/stock and formulations change. Keep the affiliate disclosure with shopping choices; the site says some links earn a commission, not that every retailer is a partner.

## The privacy and product limit

The [scan page](https://www.dupedesk.shop/scan) says 18+, own photo, browser-only/no upload, affected by lighting/makeup and not diagnosis. These are operator claims, not independently tested implementation or validated skin assessment. You don't need selfies to test the comparison wedge. Optional skin profiles and saved reports have separate hosted/possible AI processing under [Privacy](https://www.dupedesk.shop/privacy).

Keep **1,000 confirmed individual accounts** as the target for full free reports, with **one ingredient/unit-price comparison reviewed** alongside. Anonymous finder sessions, early-access emails, selfies and affiliate clicks are separate. The first month should earn trust in differences and limitations, not persuade people to change a routine or reveal skin information for growth.
