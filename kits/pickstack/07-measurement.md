# PickStack — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers**, unchanged under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). All four tests: operating buyer business, own actual buying brief onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, privately known responsible owner name/phone. One firm comparing five CRMs is one customer. Software vendors and teammates are not extra customers.

Activation: owner completes its requirement, reviews at least one source/unknown and creates a practical buying test. Report assisted setup, source checking, shortlisted decisions and actual purchases separately. None proves the chosen software is best or caused revenue.

No private data was accessed. Baselines are unknown: genuine firms, own briefs, completed comparisons, source dates, reviewed/discovery selections, saved-decision success, independent recent use, actual offer and payments. Save current rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/pickstack); unknown isn't zero and rolling readings aren't cumulative.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a tag. Mask confidential budgets, procurement details, free text, accounts and access links. Aggregate events: own_brief_completed, option_reviewed, source_opened, unknown_recorded, buying_test_written, saved_decision_reopened, independent_return. Keep identifying customer checks privately outside the repo. Source opening alone isn't source agreement.

Base guesses: 40 × 40% × 70% × 70% = 7.84 firms; at 20% conversation, 3.92. Three posts/two introductions are upside only. Reforecast after eight and twenty approaches. A finished one-off buying decision may be useful yet fail the final recent-use test; show both facts.

Measure incorrect/stale prices, unsupported integrations and reasons the buyer rejects an option. Do not claim procurement savings without an actual comparable baseline and final terms. No invented customer wins, vendor endorsements or security verification.
