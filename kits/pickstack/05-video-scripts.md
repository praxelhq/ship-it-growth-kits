# PickStack — Video scripts

Use a fictional service team and no confidential budget or procurement documents. Record real controls; public samples stay labelled.

## A. A shortlist you can challenge — 65 seconds

0–3 s: CRM logos behind one requirement. **“What must happen after the lead arrives?”**

3–17 s: actual brief entry with fictional team size, budget and existing tools. “Describe the work before choosing a name.”

17–32 s: reviewed versus discovery labels and a source. “These records don't have the same evidence status.”

32–47 s: one missing integration and a proposed acceptance test. “Unknown stays unknown until the vendor or trial resolves it.”

47–57 s: budget arithmetic and setup owner. “The monthly seat total is only part of the decision.”

57–65 s: URL. “Confirm current vendor terms before purchase. pickstack.tech.”

## B. Budget hook — 20 seconds

0–3 s: $35 beside fifteen seats. **“Per user is not the team total.”**

3–13 s: $525 illustrative monthly target, before add-ons/taxes. “Write the whole budget before ranking options.”

13–20 s: source/date and URL. “Then confirm what the vendor actually charges.”

## C. Gap hook — 25 seconds

0–3 s: unknown integration. **“A blank field is a buying question.”**

3–16 s: actual evidence status and a separate trial checklist. “Don't assume a connection works because two product names appear together.”

16–25 s: test input/expected result and URL. “Make the shortlist earn the decision.” No invented integration success.
