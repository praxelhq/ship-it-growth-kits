# PickStack — Copy pack

## 1. Buyer opener

Hi — are you choosing a CRM for a real team this month? Before comparing names, how do you record what must happen when a new lead arrives?

I build PickStack. It uses the work, seat count, budget, existing tools and setup capacity to produce options with reasons and gaps. Would a sample buying brief be useful? No confidential procurement files needed.

## 2. Interested-owner email

**Subject: A CRM shortlist needs a buying test**

Start with one required workflow, not a long feature wishlist. For example: a new lead reaches the CRM, gets the right owner and appears where the team already works.

PickStack shows reviewed records separately from discovery options. Open the sources and confirm pricing, integrations, security and contract terms with the vendor before buying. Scores depend on the brief and available records; they are not a guarantee of fit.

Try a harmless brief: https://pickstack.tech/#match

Answers currently stay in this browser; sign-in is described as syncing saved decisions. Check the actual offer and recovery before storing anything confidential. If irrelevant, say no and I will stop.

## 3. LinkedIn post

“$35 per user” is not the whole buying decision.

For fifteen users, the starting target is $525/month before taxes and add-ons. Then come the questions: does the required lead route work, what must be connected, and who will set it up?

PickStack's public CRM example is illustrative, not a vendor quote or purchasing advice. The useful output is a shortlist with sources, unknowns and a test the buyer can actually run.

What would make your team reject a good-looking CRM after the first trial? https://pickstack.tech/

## 4. Introduction ask

Do you know a service-business owner making a CRM decision now? Please ask whether a source-and-requirements review would help. No need to send their private budget, contact details or vendor proposals without permission.

## 5. Private review invitation

Would you turn one must-have requirement into a trial check? We can record the input, expected result, current evidence and remaining question. You decide what to buy and confirm final terms with the vendor. A missing source stays missing; we will not fill it with a confident guess.

One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop on no. No invented savings, vendor endorsement or guaranteed ranking.
