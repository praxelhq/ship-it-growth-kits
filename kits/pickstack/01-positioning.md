**Live:** https://pickstack.tech/ · **Category:** Business software selection · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/pickstack

Checked 29 October 2026: [home](https://pickstack.tech/) headline **“Choose software for the way your team actually works.”** The **“How the matching works →”** link leads to methodology; the matching entry `#match` renders **“Let’s get the brief right.”** It says answers stay in this browser and sign-in syncs saved decisions. No brief, account or purchase was submitted. No PickStack software price was established.

Start with a small service-company owner replacing a CRM for a real sales team. The buyer has a seat count, spending ceiling, existing tools and someone responsible for setup. This is a purchasing workflow, not a general software-discovery audience.

**Pitch: Turn a CRM requirement into a source-checkable shortlist and a buying test.** Short version: **The reasons and gaps before the product name.**

The public example has 15 salespeople at $35/user: $525/month before taxes/add-ons. It is an illustrative budget, not a PickStack fee or a verified vendor quote. Reviewed records and discovery records are labelled separately; 37 categories do not mean 37 independently verified markets. A score is conditional on the brief and currently reviewed records.

The free manual alternative is a [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) with required workflow, seats, current vendor URL/date, unknowns and a trial checklist. PickStack must save comparison/review effort without hiding missing integrations or setup costs. Verify final prices, terms, security and integrations directly with the vendor before committing money. Do not promise the chosen CRM will increase revenue.

Prove one harmless requirement through comparison, source inspection and saved-decision recovery. Browser persistence is not cloud recovery; test actual sign-in sync separately. **Target: 10 real business customers**, each with its own buying brief onboarded, independent recent use and privately known owner name/phone under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Vendors, software records and team seats are not ten customer businesses.
