Keep this buyer-side CRM selection list distinct from FiguredIt's account prioritization and LoopLead's outreach preparation. Do not approach vendors as if you are buying merely to sell them software. No trade newsletter, procurement forum or group promotion permission was established. One organizer gets one primary invitation per week across kits. Ask first, stop on no; one relevant follow-up after seven days. No bought lists, automated DMs or paid directory boosts.
