# Terms Apply — Distribution map

**Start with permissioned “one task by tomorrow” exercises and owned examples.** Your result needs a later check-in, so a one-day launch spike is weaker evidence than ten people completing the lifecycle.

All forecasts are assumptions, not observed benchmarks. Zero is possible; deduplicate people across routes.

## Accounts, communities and newsletters

[Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) is a real builder feed. Reply to an actual discussion about finishing work with a concrete definition-of-done example, not a guilt message or link spam. [Loop's site](https://loophabits.org/) is a verified alternative/resource, not an advertising board.

No productivity subreddit/Discord rules or newsletter editor route were verified for this product. Do not invent them. Ask a real circle organizer whether a bulletin accepts the exercise, and use copy #2 only after permission. Budget zero until it is accepted and published.

## Honest total

Three sessions, twenty opt-in invitations, six owned posts and one project listing model **25–104 accounts before overlap**. That does not support a guarantee of 1,000. At an assumed 25% visit-to-account rate, 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified unique visitors. The useful expansion route is a circle voluntarily repeating the exercise and introducing you to another organizer after the first reports arrive.

Terms Apply gets the early one-deadline window, Days4–7. MakeHabit's anchor exercise follows Days10–13, Ughfix's starting step Days17–20, HabitForge's private cohort Days24–27 after access is resolved. Use different organizers and actual dates. One invitation per organization per seven days prevents the cohort from turning a helpful exercise into a barrage.
