# Terms Apply — Landing page fixes

Home, `/new` and `/auth` read 29 October 2026. Saving, cross-device access and emails were not tested. Links exist; do not claim the complete lifecycle works until three strangers try it.

## 1. Put the non-enforcement truth at the action

Current headline: “Put a date on it.” Current CTA: “Make a commitment.” The page explains lower down that it records answers but does not verify actions or enforce consequences.

Keep the direct headline, and sharpen the first action:

> **One task. Your date. An honest check-in.**
>
> Write what done means and the terms you choose. Later, report what happened. We keep the record; we do not enforce the pledge.
>
> **Write my terms**
>
> Free. Email reminders optional. No money collected.

## 2. Show the full lifecycle, not only the promise

The page's walking example is clear. Add its later states visibly: on time, late, missed, unreported. Use a labeled example, not a fabricated user record.

Copy:

> **A missed task can still have an honest record.** Unanswered stays unreported. You decide what happened; the app does not mark silence as success.

Show the original terms next to the report so someone understands why this differs from a note.

## 3. Keep pledges manageable and optional in tone

The creation page already asks for something manageable beyond ordinary obligations. Preserve that guidance beside the pledge field:

> Choose something small and manageable. Do not use essential needs or commitments to other people as a punishment. Leave enough time to report the pledge separately.

Avoid copy that pressures someone to escalate consequences after a miss. Do not turn the acquisition demo into a financial-stakes or public-shaming challenge.

## 4. Test creation, review and the later report

Ask three adults to create a short ordinary commitment, inspect the review screen, confirm it, return after the deadline and report an outcome. Test a late report, unanswered check-in, changed timezone, canceled creation and duplicate login. If essential persistence fails, fix it before inviting groups.

Useful review copy:

> These are the terms you are about to confirm. Check the definition of done, deadline, timezone and pledge check-in before saving.

Only promise immutability or edit history if the actual implementation preserves it.

## 5. Separate optional emails from the commitment

Current offer says email reminders are optional. Make the control specific:

> Email me a reminder and a later check-in. I can turn these off; the commitment record still exists.

Verify actual unsubscribe/settings behaviour before promising it. Test delivery without exposing email links or addresses in public examples. Do not count consent clicks as activation.

## 6. Explain account/cross-device use and privacy

The auth page says commitments and reports can be accessed on other devices. Explain the actual guest/account boundary at save, not after the user types a private task. Do not imply cloud backup for a local-only draft or no-account use if an account is required.

Mask tasks, pledge text and email in analytics. Show a non-sensitive sample in the hero. A “private from other users” statement is not the same as offline storage; use the actual privacy explanation.

## 7. Check mobile and sharing

No visual audit was run here. Test 360px, large text, keyboard navigation, date/time entry and a slow sign-in return. Suggested share title: “Terms Apply — one task, your terms, an honest check-in.” Description: “Free commitment records with optional reminders. No enforced stakes.” Never put a private task into a public share preview by default.
