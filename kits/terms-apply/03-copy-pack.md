# Terms Apply — Copy pack

Use one real ordinary task and the outcome you actually reported. Recipient fill-ins are intentional; public results are never required.

## 1. Organizer invitation

> Hi [name], I built Terms Apply for one small promise people keep postponing. Could I show your adult study/project circle a free 20-minute exercise?
>
> Each person chooses one task for the next day or two, writes what done means and sets a manageable optional pledge. Later they report on time, late or missed. The app records the original terms; it does not enforce consequences or verify the action.
>
> Nobody has to share the task publicly or sign up for emails. Happy to show you the exercise first.

One follow-up a week later, then stop.

## 5. Honest outcome post

> The useful part of a commitment record is the answer afterwards.
>
> Did I finish by the date I chose? If not, was the task vague, the deadline unrealistic, or did I avoid the work? “Unreported” is not the same as “done.”
>
> I am testing that full lifecycle in Terms Apply, not collecting impressive promises. If you try it, keep the task small enough to report honestly.

Only attach your actual outcome. Do not rewrite a missed task into a motivational success story.
