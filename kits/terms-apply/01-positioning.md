# Terms Apply — Positioning

**Live:** https://termsapply.me/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/terms-apply

**You record a promise; you do not enforce it.** That is the honest difference to lead with. Do not sell this as a financial-stakes app or call a self-reported outcome verified.

The [live headline](https://termsapply.me/) is “Put a date on it.” The CTA “Make a commitment” leads to `/new`; `/auth` provides an account entry for commitments across devices. Both pages were read on 29 October 2026. Saving a commitment, authentication and email delivery were not completed in this inspection.

## Who starts here

An adult student or early-career worker putting off one ordinary, bounded task: send the draft, finish a short application, clear a small admin backlog. They do not want a new life-management system. They want to put a clear definition of done and a date beside one thing, then face the record afterwards.

The first task should fit the next day or two, matching your own creation-page guidance. Avoid big health, money or relationship pledges as a demo. A manageable optional consequence is not a reason to deny sleep, food, necessary spending or contact with others.

## Pitch

> Write one clear commitment, choose the date and report what actually happened.

Short version:

> One task. Your terms. An honest check-in.

Your first activation is a confirmed commitment with a definition of done and deadline. Your important second action is a reported outcome after the deadline: on time, late, missed or explicitly unreported. An untouched reminder is not a successful task.

## Price and real alternatives

The public offer is **free to use**, with optional email reminders. It does not collect or enforce a stake. Do not invent a premium plan, payment requirement or legal contract.

## The weakness

A pledge can become a theatre of self-discipline without changing the task. If people keep making commitments but never report, your product is a nicer promise box. The first month should follow one completed lifecycle, including an honest miss, rather than celebrate the number of promises created.

Keep the cohort target **1,000 signups**, paired with confirmed commitments and post-deadline reports. `02` shows that the initial small-circle plan is far short of a guaranteed thousand; do not inflate the headline with form starts.
