# ReconOS — Video scripts

Use only fictional sample records or owned synthetic exports. No bank details, AWBs linked to people, private rates or real partner accusations. Screen and phone voice are enough.

## A. Follow the gap —65 seconds · owner follow-up

0–3 s: fictional unmatched payout. **“Which order explains this number?”**

3–18 s: actual sample order, settlement and bank trail. “Check the same period and the source references.”

18–32 s: confirmed-rate screen and beta mapping. “Your agreement sets the rate, not the most common charge.”

32–47 s: actual Claim/Check detail. “These labels tell us what to inspect; they don't guarantee recovery.”

47–57 s: missing-file/check-not-run state. “A missing report is not a clean audit.” Show actual behavior or label the pending test.

57–65 s: fictional label, first-free/Pro boundary and URL.

## B. Evidence hook —20 seconds · LinkedIn

0–3 s: large sample amount. **“Claimed is not recovered.”**

3–13 s: source trail and owner review. “Check the record, timing and terms before contacting the partner.”

13–20 s: clearly fictional sample and URL. No before/after revenue claim.

## C. Deadline hook —25 seconds · owner onboarding

0–3 s: sample weight charge. **“Will a monthly review be too late?”**

3–16 s: actual dated partner evidence or clearly labelled checklist. “Confirm the current charge-specific deadline in the partner dashboard.”

16–25 s: twice-weekly review habit and URL. “Do not invent a deadline from an incomplete report.” No universal countdown or guaranteed dispute success.
