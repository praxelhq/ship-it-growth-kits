# ReconOS — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.reconos.in/ · **Category:** D2C reconciliation · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/reconos

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Find the money your couriers and payment gateways still owe you.”** Actual offer section **“Get monthly audits”** says first audit free, then Pro **₹999 + GST/month**. The page describes browser-local file reading, partner-specific checks and claim emails. No merchant file, account, audit or payment was submitted.

Your first ten are independent Indian Shopify brands with roughly 300–3,000 monthly orders, COD shipments and one owner checking courier/gateway settlements manually. Start with one month, one courier and one gateway, plus actual agreements. Broader 30,000-order capacity is a site claim, not tested scale.

**Pitch: Trace a month's order-to-bank gaps, then review the evidence before contacting a partner.** Short version: **The order, payout and bank evidence together.**

Indigo Loom is explicitly fictional. Its ₹19,831 Claim and ₹4,295 Check across 34 orders are sample arithmetic, not recovered money or proof of actual partner debt. Even a Claim label needs owner review of complete files, timing, refunds and contractual terms. A sent claim is not accepted liability or recovery.

Partner parsers are in beta, built from published formats and still being tested against real exports. Rates must come from the merchant's negotiated agreement; a commonly charged rate is not proof the rate was agreed. Marketplace settlements, non-Indian gateways and enterprise approval/Tally workflows are outside current scope.

The free alternative is a manually reviewed [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) matching order/AWB, settlement reference, bank credit and confirmed rates. ReconOS must make exceptions traceable, not replace professional accounting/tax judgment. The site claims raw files remain in-browser; account saves rates, totals and claim lists. Don't turn that into “nothing is stored” or an independently audited privacy guarantee.

[Shiprocket's official page](https://www.shiprocket.in/weight-dispute-management/) mentions seven working days, but its FAQ also says seven days. Confirm the actual charge date, applicable dashboard deadline and current contract; no universal deadline guarantee. A monthly review can be too late for a short dispute window.

**Target:10 real businesses**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): own authorized records, independent recent use, real operation and privately known owner name/phone. Orders, partners, claims and recovered rupees are separate metrics.
