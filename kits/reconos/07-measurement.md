# ReconOS —Measurement

**Target:10 real business customers**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Four tests: genuine brand business; own authorized files/rates onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known responsible owner name/phone. One brand with six partners is one customer. Orders, claims and rupees aren't customers.

Activation: owner completes a real permitted-period reconciliation, reviews coverage/mapping/rates and inspects an exception or genuine no-exception result. A sample audit, file drop or Claim total alone isn't activation. Distinguish assisted audits, independently repeated checks, submitted partner queries, accepted claims and actual recovered bank receipts.

No private data accessed: baselines unknown for firms, own audits, beta-parser errors, mapping corrections, evidence reviewed, actual partner outcomes, independent use and billing. Save current rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/reconos). No rolling-reading sums or assumed zeros.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before tagging. Exclude raw reports, bank references, private rates, claim contents, account/payment screens and file names containing identities. Aggregate events: own_audit_started, mapping_confirmed, rates_confirmed, checks_completed, exception_reviewed, claim_exported, independent_return. Do not upload raw files for growth analytics. Public browser-local processing claims aren't a security audit.

Assumed 40 ×50% ×70% ×70%=9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation,4.9. Posts/intros upside only. Replace rates after eight/twenty approaches. One monthly audit early in the month may fail final-seven-day policy despite value; report useful one-off audits separately rather than prompt fake activity.

Weekly quality log: wrong mappings, missing reports, overlapping periods, disputed rates, false exceptions and stale deadlines. Actual recovery needs a documented receipt and causal limits; sample claims cannot establish owed debt, tax correctness, provider misconduct or ROI.
