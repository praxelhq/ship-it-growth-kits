## Why the forecast doesn't clear the target

Two accepted small demos might produce about six installs, warm peer tests a few more, and owned posts/directories perhaps another handful. **A 10–35 activated-install experiment is a plausible scenario**, with substantial overlap and uncertainty. It is not evidence of 1,000 installs. The next multiplier must come from an actually accepted community/demo partnership or observed search demand. If neither appears, report the narrower paid-utility result honestly.
