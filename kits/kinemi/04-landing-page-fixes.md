# Kinemi — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“Drop in your clips. Get back a film.”**, **Make a film**→`/studio`. Actual studio here showed **“This browser can’t develop films yet”** with recent desktop Chrome/Edge/up-to-date Safari suggestion. First 3 free/account, then$1.99/3,$5.99 monthly,$99.99 once; country prices vary. No account/render/payment tested. Ranked recommendations.

## 1. Test compatibility before asking for clips/sign-in

Suggested first screen:

> Check whether this browser can render an MP4 before choosing files. Recent desktop Chrome/Edge is recommended; actual codec support can differ. If unsupported, keep your clips and try a supported browser—nothing has uploaded.

Owner tests real desktop browser/version/OS and supported codecs, actual sign-in/export. The audit's unsupported message is not proof all users fail. Don't market mobile/Android availability from responsive imagery; Android is explicitly in development/not Play Store.

## 2. Define a free film beside the first render

Suggested count explanation:

> First three distinct clip sets free, with all features. Remaking the same set with another look/music/shape/length is free. Adding, removing or swapping a clip makes a new film. Review the count before confirming.

Owner verifies successful/failed/cancelled renders and remakes don't wrongly consume allowance, exact clip-set recognition and account recovery. Don't encourage multiple accounts to replenish free films; terms say one account/person. Show 1080p/up-to 90 seconds without implying unlimited free new films.

## 3. Scope “nothing uploaded” to media

[Privacy](https://kinemifilms.com/privacy) says raw media stays local, but account/email/plan/free count, clip-set fingerprint from filename/size/length, network abuse counts, licence checks and error metadata exist server-side. Suggested summary:

> Videos/audio are processed locally, not uploaded. Account, allowance, clip-set fingerprints and short non-media error reports still use our servers. Keep originals; closing the tab clears the page's media. Download your finished MP4 before leaving.

Don't call the service air-gapped or entirely anonymous. Verify actual no-media-in-errors/replays and masking on owner samples. No private video, frame/face or filename in growth reports.

## 4. Replace infallible editing promises with review

“No awkward cuts”/best-moment/face-crop claims need actual tests. Suggested result instruction:

> Review the selected moments, speech cuts, crop and timing before sharing. Automatic choices can miss what matters to you; keep your originals and use actual available controls to remake.

Don't promise manual correction controls not implemented. Test landscape→vertical, quiet speech, motion, varied lighting and failed encodes on permitted clips. Record actual specs/sample/time; no universal instant performance or perfect face tracking.

## 5. Put music obligations beside export

Terms say included tracks are Kevin MacLeod CC BY 4.0; [the source](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) confirms attribution for free Creative Commons use. Suggested export note:

> Keep the film's music credit and copy the provided credit into your caption. Your own song/footage needs your rights. Platform automated claims can still need resolution; don't remove attribution assuming the app cleared every context.

Don't imply artist endorsement or that credits alone cover any third-party song. Keep stock simulation licence/manufacturer non-endorsement clear; “developed on film” is an aesthetic simulation, not physical processing.

Pricing shows USD/tax included; terms also show India prices₹59/3,₹149 monthly,₹1,999 once. Show actual country amount and plan recurrence in checkout, no invented currency conversion. Monthly auto-renews until cancelled; receipt/account/entitlement must work.

Don't advertise blanket money-back. Owner tests controlled delivery/cancel/refund revocation before paid promotion.
