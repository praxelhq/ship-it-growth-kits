## Milestones and privacy

Activation: verified account plus first real reviewed/downloaded MP4 from permitted clips. Track render started/success/failure separately; deeper value is voluntary free remake or second distinct clip-set film. Paid pack/monthly/lifetime, cancellation/refund and free allowance are separate. A changed look/music doesn't consume a new film per terms; verify actual count rather than inflate production metrics.

Use actual policy-consistent aggregate account/allowance/error data only. Privacy says media stays local but clip fingerprints/account/usage metadata exist; don't call it zero metadata. No raw clip/frame/audio, filenames, face information, original location, private song, licence key or account email in growth events/replays/public reports. Error reports should contain step/settings/browser/count, not media/names. Audit existing Clarity/Cloudflare and don't add duplicate trackers or fingerprinting.

## Weekly decisions

**Day 7:** Can five voluntary supported-browser tests produce/download a real sample MP4? Five is a learning gate. If fallback is browser-specific, explain compatibility; don't call whole site dead. If no supported export works, pause acquisition and fix. A preview isn't a download.

**Day 14:** Split account, import/codec, analysis, render and download failures. If a cancelled render consumes allowance or same-set remake counts again, fix fairness before paid promotion. Review crop/speech selection honestly; no perfect-moment or universal rendering-speed claim.

**Day 21:** Look for voluntary remakes/second films among eligible accounts. A single film remade ten times isn't ten new customers. Check credits remain in film/caption and don't imply artist endorsement or every platform-rights claim is impossible. Keep originals; catalogue/render isn't backup.

**Day 28:** Verify country price, actual entitlement, recurring renewal/cancel and posted refund conditions with owner controls. A pack has different refund terms from monthly/lifetime. Android in development means no Android install totals. Support time belongs beside conversion.

**Day 30:** Report actual accounts, successful first exports, remakes/second sets separately, eligible return, failure categories, paid buyers/refunds, source overlap, hours/spend and 1,000-account gap. 02 assumptions yield only roughly 3–14 activations without an accepted event or 4–18 with it before overlap, gated by actual supported-browser export. Unaccepted launch/creator/directory reach is zero. No claims of independently audited privacy, perfect editing or guaranteed platform music clearance.
