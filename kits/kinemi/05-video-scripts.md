# Kinemi — Video scripts

Use six short owned/consenting clips, no children/private events/locations without permission. Keep music credits. Don't collect user footage for the campaign or imply actual browser tests passed if only a preview exists.

## A. Main demo —60 seconds, landing/session

**0–8 s:** Six permitted sample clips. “A day out shouldn't stay an unedited folder.” State actual desktop browser/version and sample rights.

**8–20 s:** Actual studio import/capability check. “The browser needs the right encoders; these clips stay on this computer.” Don't show an unsupported browser magically rendering.

**20–32 s:** Actual selected moments/look preview. “Review what it chose. Change only what the actual controls support.” No fake manual timeline or perfect selection claim.

**32–44 s:** Real export, time-compressed with measured elapsed-time label if needed. “Here is the MP4 from this test.” Don't substitute stock film as generated output.

**44–52 s:** Play a readable sample with credit intact. “Keep the music attribution; your own media still needs rights.”

**52–60 s:** First 3 free/sign-in/same-set remakes free; actual pricing/desktop limits and URL. No Android install CTA.

## B. Hook —23 seconds, Day 10

**0–5 s:** Original clip set beside finished actual sample. “One short film, not another timeline project.”

**5–16 s:** Real chosen moment/crop and downloaded file. “I still review what it picked.”

**16–23 s:** “First three films free in a supported desktop browser; sign-in needed.” URL. No fake render speed or perfect-best-moment claim.

## C. Hook —25 seconds, Day 16

**0–6 s:** Same clip set, two looks. “A new look isn't another paid film.”

**6–17 s:** Actual remake with same clips and music/look change. “Same set, free remake. Change the set and it's a new film.” Verify actual allowance before footage.

**17–25 s:** “Know the count before rendering.” URL. Don't advocate creating extra accounts for free films.

## D. Hook —25 seconds, Day 24

**0–6 s:** Finished film's music credit. “Don't crop this out just because the edit is done.”

**6–17 s:** Actual credit/caption line and terms. “Included tracks require attribution. Your own song needs rights too.” No promise universal platform clearance.

**17–25 s:** “A small film you can share responsibly.” URL. Preserve source/artist credit; no artist endorsement claim.

Use four assets plus two actual compatibility/process text posts. Export testing and review matter more than cinematic claims about footage never actually rendered.
