# Adya Capital — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026: homepage, FAQ, Academy, Fund Vault and status routes. No private application, invitation redemption or payment was submitted. Raw markup includes several application states; that does not prove a fresh visitor sees them simultaneously.

## 1. Sell an understandable deliverable before exclusivity

Current hero: “Not Everyone Gets In. That Is the Point.” Replace with **“Know what to fix before your next investor conversation.”** Subheading: **“Submit your deck for a structured readiness review. See the reviewer, sample feedback, current queue and application terms before you decide.”** Only promise each item once you publish it. Keep admissions information below the review explanation.

## 2. Put fee and admission terms next to the first action

Current actions include “Request entry” and “Apply anyway”; the open route costs ₹499. Replacement CTA pair: **“See a sample review”** and **“Apply for deck review”**. Under both: **“Open application: ₹499 once. Invited application: free. Review does not guarantee admission, introductions or funding.”** Repeat the terms immediately before payment.

## 3. Identify who is doing the work

Current copy describes an Investment Committee and Partners without this research establishing who they are or their track record. Add a reviewer panel with real role, relevant experience, review responsibility and verifiable professional links, with permission. Until established, replacement: **“A structured review by the Adya team. No investor endorsement is implied.”** Remove claims of access you cannot document.

## 4. Show the output, queue and clock precisely

The FAQ says ten dimensions, a score, a written breakdown and three priorities. Show one redacted example headed **“Example feedback — shared with the founder's permission.”** If synthetic, label **“Illustrative review, not a customer result.”**

Do not turn “within 24 hours of evaluation” into “reviewed in 24 hours.” Replacement queue line: **“Current intake and expected review date are shown before checkout. Feedback follows evaluation; a payment receipt is not a review.”** Add this only after the queue information is operational.

## 5. Clarify deck access and sharing

Current FAQ tells applicants to share a publicly accessible Drive link or PDF up to 10 MB, and describes privacy. Do not encourage founders to make confidential material broadly accessible without explaining who can retrieve it. Replacement: **“Submit a deck you are authorized to share. We explain who can access it, how approved referrals work, retention and deletion before upload.”** Provide a restricted-upload route where possible. Never display a real deck or applicant identity in public analytics.

## 6. Separate the three account outcomes

Current status route asks for email and ticket. Label confirmation **“Application received”**, review **“Feedback delivered”**, and decision **“Admitted / declined”**. Add: **“Save your ticket. If payment succeeded but submission did not, use the support route; do not pay again.”** Test that recovery on a controlled test account before driving traffic; the kit has not verified it.

## 7. Make the mobile preview factual

Title: **“Adya Capital — structured deck review and founder resources.”** Description: **“See the review terms, example feedback and admission process before applying. Payment is for review, not funding.”** On a phone, ensure fee disclosure is visible before the upload/payment action. Preserve readable text, keyboard focus and a visible form error rather than adding more scarcity animation. Preview the link in your own chat before the organizer invitation.
