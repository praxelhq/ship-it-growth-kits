# Adya Capital — Positioning

**Live:** https://adyacapital.me/ · **Category:** Education · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/adya-capital

**Your bottleneck is trust in a paid review, not lack of exclusivity.** The public hero says “Not Everyone Gets In. That Is the Point.” Open applicants pay ₹499 for review; invited applicants pay nothing. Paying does not buy admission. Before promoting, make the reviewer, deliverable, queue and use of a founder's deck clear. Claims about a committee or investor connections are your claims, not independently verified outcomes.

## Your first founder

Start with a founder with a real prototype or pilot, a rough 10–15-slide deck, and a specific question about what to fix before approaching investors. She needs useful feedback more than another searchable investor list. Do not target somebody with no company who only wants access to a prestigious room.

**Pitch:** Submit your startup deck for a structured readiness review, with clear written feedback and a separate admission decision.

**Bio:** Deck feedback with transparent terms. Review is not a funding promise.

The first-use wedge is **the three changes to make before the next investor conversation**. The [FAQ](https://adyacapital.me/faq.html) describes ten review dimensions and a written breakdown on the status page. It says feedback normally appears within 24 hours **of evaluation**, not within 24 hours of submission. Do not change that into a one-day turnaround guarantee.

## Why somebody chooses you

[YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/) is a free founder course. [OpenVC](https://www.openvc.app/pricing) advertises a free core investor-search/CRM tier; Premium is $99/month or $299/year. These are real alternatives to paying for information or discovery. A directory alone does not justify a review fee.

Your differentiator must be a concrete, attributable review of the founder's actual deck. Publish a consented, redacted example and explain who did the work. Do not describe a generic AI score as personal committee attention. Your Fund Vault and Academy can support the review, but they are not proof that a fund will meet the applicant.

## Keep the target honest

The agreed audience policy remains B2C: **1,000 signups in 30 days**, reported alongside completed applications and delivered reviews. An accepted waitlist/contact registration may be a signup only if it is a distinct, intentional registration with a receipt; a visitor or invitation-code view is not one. If the only real registration is the submitted application, count that instead and say so.

The uncomfortable arithmetic: 1,000 reviews at an assumed 20 minutes each require about 333 reviewer hours. That is a workload scenario, not your verified review duration. One student cannot promise it casually. Capacity-limit intake and show the shortfall rather than collecting fees for a queue you cannot service. Ten admissions or ten investments are not substitutes for the signup number.
