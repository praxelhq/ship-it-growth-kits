# Adya Capital — Copy pack

Use only claims you can personally support. Do not insert investor names, success rates, review times or testimonials without permission and evidence.

## 1. Campus organizer invitation

> Could I run a free 30-minute deck-review walkthrough for founders in the venture programme? I'll use a consented, redacted example to show how we review problem clarity, traction and the ask, then explain the application terms. ## 2. LinkedIn: the terms before the pitch

> Paying for a deck review should buy a clear piece of work, not the suggestion that an investor owes you a meeting.
>
> At Adya Capital, an open application carries a ₹499 review fee. Invited applications are free. The review and the admission decision are different things. Paying does not buy admission or funding.
>
> Before asking more founders to apply, I'm making the reviewer, written deliverable and queue visible. A founder should be able to decide whether the feedback itself is worth it.

If those improvements are shipped, replace the last paragraph with a link to the actual terms and sample. Do not say they are live while they are still being built.

## 3. LinkedIn: useful lesson

> Before sending your next deck, put the traction slide on its own and ask three questions.
>
> Does every number have a date? Can a stranger tell what “active” means? Does the evidence support the next milestone you are asking money to reach?
>
> A simple, dated number is more useful than an impressive number nobody can interpret. You can do this check yourself before paying anybody for feedback.

Link a genuinely redacted example only after consent. This post needs no invented founder story.

## 4. Private founder message

> You mentioned preparing your deck. I'm building Adya's structured readiness review and would value a short conversation about what useful feedback looks like. I won't ask you to upload the deck just to talk. Open review is ₹499, invited applications are free, and admission is separate. Would a ten-minute discussion be useful? No problem if it isn't relevant.

One message; after silence, stop. After interest, send https://adyacapital.me/faq.html and let the founder decide.

>

Personalize the first sentence to the organization's actual programme. One follow-up after seven days, then stop; never imply an existing partnership.
