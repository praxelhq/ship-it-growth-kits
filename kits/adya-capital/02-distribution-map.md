# Adya Capital — Distribution map

**Lead with a transparent review demonstration and organizer introductions.** Broad scarcity messaging will attract curiosity without the trust needed to submit a confidential deck. Use your own actual network; no purchased founder lists.

The shared founder cohort reserves **Days 24–27** for your broad organizer session. Earlier work is private interviews, terms and reviews already requested. These are coordination windows, not booked events.

| Route | Action, timing and permission | Planning result / basis | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Your campus venture-program organizer | Ask privately for one 30-minute review walkthrough on Day 24 at 18:00 IST. Show a sample review before asking for applications. No blast without permission. | 30 invitations × assumed 50% attendance × 40% voluntary registration = 6 signups. Not a measured benchmark. | #1, #4 |
| 2. [Headstart city chapters](https://www.headstart.in/) | Find the current organizer through the official site; offer an unpaid, practical deck-feedback session. Do not claim an old event is still upcoming. | Five organizer notes × assumed 20% acceptance = one possible session. Budget zero until accepted. | #5 |
| 3. Your LinkedIn feed | Four posts on Days 8, 12, 19 and 25 at 09:00 IST: review terms, a redacted example, one lesson, and the permitted event. No invented investor quotes. | 4 × assumed 250 impressions × 3% visits × 10% registration = about 3 signups. Zero is possible. | #2, #3 |
| 4. [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) | Use public incubator and ecosystem information to find relevant organizations manually. It is not an advertising wall and does not imply government approval. | Research five relevant organizations; zero acquisition forecast until a real introduction happens. | #5 |
| 5. Existing members who actually received value | Ask for one voluntary founder introduction after delivering feedback, not five code shares before value. Nomination rights must match the actual product. | Ten satisfied recipients × assumed 30% introduction × 50% registration = 1–2 signups. Deduplicate. | #6 |

Follow public teaching from [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/) and use its curriculum as a free alternative you can recommend. Do not contact its founders as though the course supplies an outreach list. Your own opt-in monthly review lesson can become a newsletter; no unverified editorial placement belongs in this forecast.

Skip Show HN: an application/review funnel is not a playable maker project under [its rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html). Product Hunt is optional only after clear review capacity and delivered work exist; its [guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) prohibits soliciting upvotes and requires a personal maker account. Do not use a launch to hide an unserved application queue.

This first plan supports roughly 10–25 signups if private interviews and events convert well; it does not establish a route to 1,000. Record that gap. Expand through evidence of valuable delivered reviews and organizers willing to repeat the session, not a louder claim that most people are rejected.
