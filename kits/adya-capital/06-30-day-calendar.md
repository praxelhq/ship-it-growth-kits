# Adya Capital — 30-day calendar

Day 1 begins after fee disclosure, receipt recovery and the sample deliverable are ready. Every day includes 20 minutes responding to existing applicants and recording the queue. Stop new paid intake if promised service cannot be delivered. Times IST; organizer outreach is staggered to Days 24–27.

| Day | Main action | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Record `07` baseline and actual reviewer capacity; test application states with a test account. | 3h |
| 2 | Publish reviewer responsibility, deliverable and terms (`04` Fixes 1–3). | 3h |
| 3 | Prepare a clearly labelled synthetic sample review; get consent before using real examples. | 3h |
| 4 | Test payment/submission recovery and status lookup without duplicate payment. | 2h |
| 5 | Speak privately to two founders using #4; no confidential upload required. | 1h |
| 6 | Fix the questions those founders could not answer from the page. | 2h |
| 7 | Checkpoint: confirm delivered-feedback capacity before expanding intake. | 1h |
| 8 | Publish terms post #2 to LinkedIn at 09:00. | 2h |
| 9 | Run two more founder interviews; document their actual questions. | 1h |
| 10 | Record videos A/C, keeping illustrative examples labelled. | 2h |
| 11 | Complete requested reviews already in the queue; log actual minutes each. | 3h |
| 12 | Publish a consented/redacted example or labelled synthetic example at 09:00. | 2h |
| 13 | Research five current organizations through Headstart/Startup India. No group blast. | 1h |
| 14 | Checkpoint: calculate queue wait and observed registration→application rate. | 1h |
| 15 | Send two organizer emails #5 for a Days 24–27 slot; check shared calendar. | 1h |
| 16 | Work the existing review queue; improve the feedback explanation. | 3h |
| 17 | Ask two completed-review recipients for optional feedback, not a public testimonial obligation. | 1h |
| 18 | Send three remaining organizer notes #5, one per actual organization. | 1h |
| 19 | Publish practical traction lesson #3 at 09:00. | 2h |
| 20 | Invite a referral only after value, #6; cap at ten actual satisfied recipients total. | 1h |
| 21 | Checkpoint: stop intake if review delays exceed disclosed expectations. | 1h |
| 22 | Follow up once with Day 15 organizers; no repeated chase. | 1h |
| 23 | Confirm session permission, date and capacity; prepare #7. | 1h |
| 24 | If accepted, invite up to 30 founders with an adapted #4 explaining the actual session, send #7 only to opted-in attendees, and run the free walkthrough at 18:00. Otherwise deliver a recorded public example. | 3h |
| 25 | Publish the actual session lesson on LinkedIn at 09:00; follow up once with Day 18 organizers. | 2h |
| 26 | Answer attendees' terms questions individually; no automatic paid application. | 2h |
| 27 | Complete the next promised reviews before inviting more people. | 3h |
| 28 | Audit fees, receipts, review delivery and private-data handling. | 2h |
| 29 | Interview three people who declined to apply; do not argue them into paying. | 1h |
| 30 | Report signups, applications, delivered reviews, admissions and queue separately. | 2h |

A sudden registration spike is a capacity test. Publish the queue, pause paid intake if necessary and deliver work already promised. Never accelerate by sending the same founder invitation into Astitva, PitchPanel or Postifyy's reserved group window.
