# Adya Capital — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the agreed policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Do not quietly relabel this as ten customers. Also do not count a paid review as an investment outcome.

## Define the funnel before counting

Use the product's actual intentional registration event. If account registration exists, count unique non-test accounts. If the only registration is a submitted application, count unique completed applications and state that definition. An anonymous visitor, a code generated by you, a forwarded invite or an unfinished payment is not a signup. Do not build a fake email action merely to inflate the board number.

Report **feedback delivered** beside signups: a founder can see the completed written review on the status page. Also report applications, paid/open versus invited/free, admissions, declines and the current queue separately. You cannot infer admission or funding from a payment receipt.

These private counts were not available during research. Write the real numbers on Day 0; unknown is not zero. Confirm analytics is receiving visits without recording decks, email, ticket values or payment details.

Instrument aggregate `terms_viewed`, `sample_review_opened`, `application_started`, `application_received`, `payment_confirmed`, `review_delivered` and `decision_released`. Keep payment confirmation authoritative to the server/provider, not a browser button click. Use anonymous internal identifiers in private analysis and publish aggregates only.

## The capacity calculation that matters

At an assumed 20 minutes per review, 1,000 applications require 333 hours, before support and quality control. Replace 20 with the median of your first five actual reviews. Available reviewer hours divided by real review time is the maximum you can responsibly promise; a waitlist is not completed service.

The small plan in `02` reaches roughly 10–25 registrations under uncertain assumptions. It does not substantiate 1,000. To reach 1,000 at an assumed 10% visit→registration rate requires 10,000 qualified visits; at 3% impression→visit, about 333,000 impressions. Show the observed gap rather than attributing another organization's entire membership to your reach.

| Checkpoint | Record | If behind or unhealthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Five real review-time observations if available; terms comprehension | If founders misunderstand what ₹499 buys, rewrite terms before more outreach. |
| Day 14 | Signups→applications→feedback delivered, queue age | If applications accumulate faster than reviews, pause paid intake and publish a truthful next date. |
| Day 21 | Delivered-review usefulness, referral requests and unresolved payments | Any unresolved paid-but-missing application takes priority over acquisition. |
| Day 30 | All funnel stages, on-time delivery, actual reach and hours | Explain the signup shortfall and service capacity; do not report admissions as funding. |

Keep one weekly row for actual registrations, completed applications, reviews delivered, admitted, declined, paid/open, invited/free, refunds and oldest queue age. Do not pre-fill successes. An honest smaller month with useful feedback delivered is better evidence than a large unpaid backlog.
