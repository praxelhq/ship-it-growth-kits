# Gift Finder — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from rendered headline/buttons and public copy. “Find my gift” is a JavaScript action; the lack of an href doesn't make it broken. Quiz generation, results, saves and retailer links were not submitted/tested by the kit writer.

## 1. Prove the first result before traffic

**Current:** “Find my gift” and “Let's find a gift” start the quiz; the page promises five questions, three ideas and no signup.

Run three fresh-phone tests through an actual result. Keep entered choices after a failed request and show **“We couldn't make the shortlist. Your answers are still here; try again.”** If generation doesn't work, stop acquisition and fix it. Don't replace the working-quiz promise with a wishlist while continuing to call it a completed product.

## 2. Make the promise less absolute

**Current:** “Find a gift they'll actually like.”

Try:

> **Three gift ideas with a reason each fits.**
>
> Tell us the occasion, their interests and your budget. Get a shortlist to consider, then check the seller, current price and delivery before buying.

Keep the warm existing voice. No algorithm can guarantee someone's reaction. The reason for the suggestion is your most useful proof.

## 3. Show a complete example

**Current:** the landing page explains the three-step process but doesn't establish the actual quality of a result in the extracted text.

Add a clearly labelled fictional recipient card and the actual generated shortlist, including one recommendation you'd reject and why. Caption: **“Example only. Use what you know about the person; you can reject every idea.”** Show the budget assumption and when a listing price was checked if you include one. Don't invent live stock or reviews.

## 4. Test the one-minute claim

**Current:** “Less than a minute” and “Takes less than 60 seconds.”

Time five new people from first click to usable result. If it isn't consistently true, use **“Five quick questions. Three ideas to consider.”** A long generation wait should have an honest progress state rather than a fake instantaneous result. Improve the slow question before adding more steps.

## 5. Keep corporate gifting secondary

**Current:** “Get corporate gift ideas” goes to `/corporate`.

Label it **“Planning team or client gifts? Corporate enquiries are separate.”** Keep the consumer quiz primary. Don't make an ordinary birthday shopper complete a bulk-gifting lead form or imply the consumer result includes procurement/fulfilment.

## 6. Explain saves, links and data

**Current:** a “Saved” button is visible and the homepage says no signup.

Explain actual persistence: **“Saved on this device”** only if that's how it works; cross-device saving requires a different tested statement. Disclose affiliate relationships beside applicable links. Avoid collecting a recipient's full name/address as quiz data and keep interests/memories out of session recordings. Publish only accurate storage/deletion terms.

## 7. Phone and preview checks

Proposed title: **“Gift Finder — three ideas, chosen with a little thought.”** Description: **“Five questions about an occasion, interests and budget. No signup; check real seller details before buying.”** Test the actual preview, keyboard and result cards on a phone over mobile data. No measured clipping or slow load was established. The result's explanation and source link should be readable before decorative imagery takes the screen.
