# Gift Finder — Video scripts

Use fictional recipients and a screen recording with your phone microphone. Don't show a real friend's private interests, address or surprise gift. Use real output; label any shortened generation wait.

## A. Same budget, different person — 60 seconds

**Where:** landing page, own social feed and approved group invitation. **Hook:** “They both like coffee. That isn't enough.”

| Time | Shot | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Two fictional recipient cards | They both like coffee. That isn't enough. |
| 2–14s | Start the actual quiz on a phone | One makes it at home. One enjoys meeting friends at cafés. Same budget, different purpose. |
| 14–30s | Answer actual five questions with generic details | I'm looking for three ideas, not a catalogue. No real names needed. |
| 30–45s | Show actual result and its explanation | This reason is the useful part. I can keep it or reject it. |
| 45–53s | Open only a verified safe listing/example, or show the check list | Before spending, I still check seller, current price and delivery. |
| 53–60s | URL and no-signup statement | Try a fictional example at gifts.ayushiuniyal.me. Five questions, three ideas. |

Don't show an invented retailer price or staged successful purchase.

## B. Useful, funny or shared? — 20 seconds

**Where:** Instagram/LinkedIn native clip. **Hook:** “What job should the gift do?” Show three handwritten cards: useful, funny, shared experience. Say, “The same interest can lead to three different gifts. Decide the purpose before you browse.” End with a fictional shortlist and small founder disclosure/URL.

## C. The idea I rejected — 25 seconds

**Where:** follow-up post and Peerlist gallery. **Hook:** “This suggestion looks good. I wouldn't buy it.” Show actual output for a fictional recipient. Explain a real mismatch: they already own it, it needs equipment or it misses the occasion. Say, “A reason to reject is useful too. The quiz doesn't know everything about your friend.” End on the explanation field and URL.

## D. One minute, honestly — 30 seconds

**Where:** homepage after timing tests. **Hook:** “Let's time the whole thing.” Start a visible timer at first click, show the actual quiz at real speed or clearly disclose sped-up sections, and stop only when results are readable. If longer than the claim, say so and change the landing copy. End with the no-signup boundary and seller-check reminder, not an edited fake speed claim.

Bank first click, one question, result explanation, rejected idea and save/revisit only if tested. Text should be legible on a small phone; no music or effects needed to explain a decision.
