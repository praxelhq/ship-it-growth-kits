# Gift Finder — Distribution map

**Lead with occasion-specific personal sharing and a short real quiz demo.** No paid ads, gifting inventory or retailer sponsorship is needed. The ranges below are assumptions for testing, not channel benchmarks or guaranteed results.

No promotional permission in a named gifting subreddit, Discord or Facebook group was verified, so none is scheduled. Don't invent a gift influencer introduction to make the map look larger. Answer actual questions in circles where you already belong, and disclose that you build the tool.

Two accepted small-group shares, 20 warm tests, eight small-account clips and a directory might produce **20–60 completed first quizzes**, with overlap removed. That doesn't establish 1,000 unique first sessions. The next multiplier must be earned through actual sharing of useful, privacy-safe shortlists or accepted organizer introductions; don't count “send to five friends” as five new users.

Keep corporate gifting separate this month. A company wanting bulk procurement needs a different service and a different target; don't abandon your current consumer goal simply because a corporate link exists.
