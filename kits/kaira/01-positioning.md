# Kaira — Positioning

**Live:** https://kairaprod.online/ · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kaira · Checked 29 October 2026.

Your first user is a student with three deadlines and no clear starting point. They don't need to configure an entire life-management system before doing a task. Help them enter three real tasks, inspect why one ranks first and finish that one. Habit logs and countdowns can wait until the first useful day.

Actual H1 **“Your day, organized.”** The page calls Kaira a calm personal productivity app. **Get started free** goes to `/auth?mode=signup`; the destination says **Create your account** and **Free plan included — no card needed**. Existing-account login is separate. No paid price is visible. This audit read landing, signup and privacy pages; no account was created and no authenticated task, reminder, sync or export was tested.

## The pitch

> Three tasks. A reason to start with one. Plan the next useful step in your own time zone.

Short bio: **A calmer first task, not another setup project.**

Focus Now ranks by **urgency first**, with a plain reason. The natural-language example is “submit report friday 5pm.” The privacy policy says rankings and streaks use predictable rules and data isn't fed into AI models. Don't turn this into an AI prioritization claim, an assurance it knows someone's true life priorities, or an automatic calendar assistant.

Calendar includes tasks/events/countdowns. Reminders nudge **while Kaira is open**. There is no verified background push/email promise. Trackers include habits with rest days, custom-field logs and days-since counters; show them only when a user has a real need, not as compulsory onboarding.

## Alternatives and differentiation

An existing paper list or local notes costs ₹0 in extra specialist software. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can organize a manual task/deadline table. These can be better for someone who doesn't want another account. Kaira must earn the account through date entry, visible urgency reasons and a useful first task, rather than claiming checklists are obsolete.

The actual signup offer is a free plan with no card. Don't invent a free-plan cap, paid subscription amount or “free forever” guarantee not stated. If limits exist inside the account, surface them before promoting a workflow that needs them.

## Trust and target

The [privacy policy](https://kairaprod.online/privacy) describes account email/display name/timezone/plan and task/tracker/calendar records, strict-masked Microsoft Clarity with anonymous account identifier/plan, export and permanent account/data deletion in Settings. These are operator claims, not an independent database/security audit. Test masking, export and deletion on synthetic owner data before broad invitations.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**, alongside first dated task, first useful Focus Now choice and voluntary next-day use. A countdown or streak can be fun without proving real progress. Don't call urgency the same as importance or use missed habits to shame users into returning.
