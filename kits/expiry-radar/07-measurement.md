# Expiry Radar — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real accounts created. Always report **one genuine item saved with a user-checked expiry/reset rule** alongside it. Ten tracked credits in one account are one user; a handoff/auth-page visit is not signup.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/expiry-radar) traffic/rank. Record existing accounts, first items, corrected rules, return days and any actual reminders delivered. This kit doesn't know private totals or notification implementation; use **not measured** rather than assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on safe public/account-entry surfaces with private fields masked. Do not log item names, amounts, account credentials or private financial records. Store only aggregate events needed to understand the conversion path.

## Funnel

`branded_entry → app_auth_opened → account_created → first_item_saved → rule_checked → returned_before_due`.

Track redirects/auth errors separately from failure to understand expiry/reset. If notifications exist, measure scheduled→sent→received independently; don't claim delivery from a saved date. Any savings claim needs an actual legitimate use/avoided charge, not a sum of unused values.

## Projection and checkpoints

The calendar's 15 invitations, two circles, three clips and one listing suggest roughly 9–56 accounts before overlap under guessed rates. At a hypothetical 20% qualified-visit-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visits. Current reach doesn't establish that. Activation loss after signup is another number to report, not hide.
