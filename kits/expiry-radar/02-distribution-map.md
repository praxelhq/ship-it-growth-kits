# Expiry Radar — Distribution map

**Lead with one expiring thing in an existing adult circle, then demonstrate the focused tracker.** Don't buy lists of subscribers or ask people to expose statements, logins or account numbers.

All ranges are planning assumptions for the actual calendar, not measured benchmarks.

| Route | Permission / execution | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fifteen relevant adults | Individually ask about a pass/credit they can track without sharing credentials. Try one genuine non-sensitive item privately. | 15 × assumed 60% try × 60–80% account/item completion = 5–7 activations. | #1 |
| 2. Two existing adult class/alumni circles | One admin-approved post each; no financial details in group replies. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–25% visit × 20–40% account signup = 2–8 accounts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Expiry view, usage versus claimed savings, domain/account handoff at 18:30 IST. Use fictional items. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after signup/first-item checks; no vote exchange or false savings proof. | Assume 5–20 visits and 1–5 accounts. | #4 |

The base plan suggests roughly 9–56 accounts before overlap. It doesn't establish a thousand, and signup→real-item activation must be measured separately.

## Communities, newsletters and accounts

No current personal-finance-community commercial permission or newsletter placement was verified. Don't slip a product ad into advice about someone's debt or private finances. Use the no-link checklist #5 only when relevant and allowed.
