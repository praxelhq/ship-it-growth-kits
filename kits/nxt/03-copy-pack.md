Several posts can share a contribution template, a product name or generic vocabulary while describing different needs. Counting them together can create a convincing-looking number with a weak underlying claim.

I am testing NXT around the source audit: what is the recurring pain, who experiences it, which posts actually support it, and what is still only a hypothesis?
