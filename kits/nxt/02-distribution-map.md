# NXT — Distribution map

**Private evidence-review sessions first.** A broad “AI validates ideas” launch will amplify the wrong claim.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned scale / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical founders you can qualify through their own business sites and existing professional network | Choose a live developer-tool/service business and one current problem question. No scraped community-poster DM list. Eight blocks × four firms =32 approaches. | #1/#2 |
| Two warm technical-founder introductions | Ask permission to review one source-backed dossier together. No shared customer data required. | #3;0–4 accepted conversations possible. |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 9,18,25 at 09:00 IST: explain a real source audit and its limits. | #4; guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post. |
| [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) | Optional only after usable source inspection and honest gate labels. Rules require personally made work people can try; no landing-page-only launch or solicited votes/comments. | #5; no forecast customers or scheduled launch. |

Assumptions: 32 approaches ×50% conversation ×75% own-question trial ×60% independent return ≈7 businesses. The target stays 10; accepted introductions or higher observed conversion must close the real gap. Public impressions do not close it automatically.

NXT's private founder review window is Days 4–7, using technical-business lists distinct from Astitva's deck clinic. Do not pitch the same campus organizer with five research tools. No unverified newsletter placement or group permission enters the forecast.
