# NXT — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is an account; activation is a business's own research question inspected against actual source evidence, with a saved/exported useful conclusion or rejection.

Count genuine businesses, their actual question/data onboarded, independent use within the last seven days, and a privately known decision-maker name/phone. Guest views, student brainstorming and manually nudged opens do not satisfy all four tests.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate tag; mask notes and dossier/user context. Record current accounts, business users, dossiers opened, sources actually inspected, useful/rejected clusters, exports and independent return. Private values were unavailable, not zero. Save course rank, reading and sync from [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/nxt).

Events: question_started, dossier_opened, original_source_opened, cluster_rejected, conclusion_saved, export_completed, independent_return. Record export only after actual file availability.

| Day 7 |8 approaches; one coherent, source-traceable review desired. |
| Day 14 |20 approaches;5 real-question trials desired. No trials: refine buyer/question, not scrape more indiscriminately. |
| Day 21 |24 approaches;4 independent active firms desired. Source opens without useful decisions: audit cluster relevance. |
| Day 30 |32 approaches;10 target retained. Report actual business count plus useful/rejected dossiers and repeat use. |

The 02 scenario yields roughly 7 active firms, leaving a gap to 10. Accepted introductions or improved observed rates must close it.

Sunday quality review: inspect a sample of cluster/source relationships, record boilerplate contamination, missing sources and unsupported demand claims. Counts, cross-community labels and Jaccard scores are not scientific validation of a market. A useful rejected idea can be a success; an impressive number without coherent evidence is not.
