# NXT — Positioning

**Live:** https://nxt-ai.me/ · **Category:** Founder problem research · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/nxt

The actual headline is **“Find problems worth solving.”** Public controls include **Inspect dossier**, **Export** and **Sign In** to sync saved problems. No public price was established; do not advertise an invented free or paid plan.

**Do not call the site empty.** The first rendered fetch showed zero signals, but a later real browser loaded 15. On 29 October 2026, an inspected dossier showed 193 posts, 7 communities and 0.48 coherence, passing 1 of 3 displayed gates. Its title was “Forward / Rewards label / Contribute move,” and the summary repeated a GitLab contribution prompt. That is an evidence-quality problem to inspect, not proof no data exists.

Your first ten are technical founders running real small businesses who are willing to audit a proposed developer-workflow problem before building. The job is an evidence dossier they can challenge, not a list of automatically validated startup ideas.

**Pitch:** Inspect recurring public complaints, trace a proposed problem back to its sources, and decide what to investigate with customers.
**Short version:** Evidence before the engineering sprint.

The free manual alternative is public issue/discussion reading organized in [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/). Sheets is available with a Google Account without buying Workspace; it does not provide automatic clustering. NXT must save careful review time while keeping sources accessible.

The weakness is false coherence: shared boilerplate can produce a cluster without one shared buyer problem. A 50-post threshold or word-overlap score does not by itself establish statistical significance, independent communities or willingness to pay. Fix the interpretation before promoting confidence.

**Target: 10 real business customers**, with their own research question onboarded and independent recent dossier use. Guest page views or student idea browsing do not count.
