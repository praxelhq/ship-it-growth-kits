# Case Forge — Landing page conversion fixes

**Days 1–3 are page and product work. Nothing in `02` or `03` starts before day 4.**

Your page is **295 characters of text**: a headline, a subheadline, one sentence about who it's for, and a login button. Both CTAs go to `/login`. There is no explanation of how matching works, no pricing, no FAQ, no screenshot, no proof — and no answer to the only question a visitor actually has, which is *"will there be anyone to practise with?"*

**What you do have is the best headline in the cluster.** *"Crack the case. Together."* is better than anything the other ten projects wrote. Build the page around it.

---

## Fix 1 — Build a landing page 🔴🔴

One day. In this order and no more than this:

> # Crack the case. Together.
> ### No case partner? Get matched with someone at your level, practise a live case, and get feedback afterwards.
>
> **Next practice night: Thursday 9pm IST · 11 people in · [Take a spot]**
>
> `[Join a practice night]`   `[How matching works]`

Then:

1. **"How it works", in four lines.** Right now a visitor cannot form a mental model of your product at all:
   > **1.** Pick a practice night. **2.** You're matched with someone at your level. **3.** 30-minute live case — one of you interviews, then you swap. **4.** You both get AI feedback on structure, numbers and communication.
2. **Answer the liquidity question on the page.** It's the first thing she thinks and your silence confirms her fear. See Fix 2.
3. **One screenshot** of a session or the feedback report. A visitor needs to see something.
4. **"Free for students."** Three words.
5. **A "my campus isn't live yet" path.** See Fix 4.
6. **`og:image`** — you don't have one, and your entire distribution is a link pasted into a WhatsApp group. Twenty minutes.

**What to leave off:** a features grid, a pricing table you don't need, testimonials you don't have, and the phrase "AI-powered".

---

## Fix 2 — Replace open-ended matching with fixed practice nights 🔴🔴

**This is the most important change in your kit and it's a product decision, not a page one.**

A "get matched" button with an empty pool behind it is the worst possible first experience: she signs up, waits, nothing happens, she leaves and tells her batch it doesn't work. **One of those costs you more than ten signups gain you.**

**Fixed practice nights fix this with no engineering:**

> **Tuesdays & Thursdays, 9:00pm IST.** Show up, get matched, do a case.
> **Next: Thursday 30 Oct — 11 people in.**

Why it works:

- **Twelve people at 9pm is six matches. The same twelve across a week is zero.** Same users, completely different product.
- **"11 people in" is social proof and a liquidity answer at once.** It's the only thing that honestly answers *"will anyone be there?"*
- **A specific time is something you can recruit to.** Every message in `03` asks for Thursday 9pm rather than for a signup — and a commitment to a time is one people keep.
- **It removes the thing that kills real case partnerships**, which is arranging each session. Say that on the page; everyone has lived it.

**Never show a practice night with fewer than 6 people committed.** If a night is short, email everyone and move it rather than running a session where half the people have no partner.

## Fix 3 — Fix or drop the college-email gate 🔴

*"Join with your college email →"* with **no list of accepted domains.**

Everyone whose domain isn't recognised hits a dead end with no explanation. And the gate silently excludes audiences you want: pre-MBA candidates, lateral candidates, anyone whose college email has expired, anyone at a college whose domain you haven't added.

**Options, best first:**

1. **Drop it.** Let anyone sign up. The gate buys you very little — you can ask for campus as a profile field, which is all matching actually needs.
2. **Keep it and list the domains**, with *"not listed? Use any email and tell us your campus."*

**Either way the error state must be human:** not *"invalid email"* but *"We don't recognise that domain yet — sign up with any email and tell us your campus, and we'll add it."*

**Note what the gate is actually for.** If it's trust — knowing people are real students — a profile field and your own moderation do that job without blocking anyone at the door.

## Fix 4 — Give "my campus isn't live yet" a real path 🟠

Most early visitors will be from campuses with no pool. **Right now they hit nothing, which wastes them.** They're your best future users and they'll never come back.

> **Your campus isn't live yet.**
> Get 8 people from your batch to pick one evening, and we'll run your first night that week — I'll host it myself.
> `[Request my campus]` · *or join the open Thursday night and get matched with someone from another campus*

Two things this gets you:

- **A waitlist organised by campus**, which is a map of exactly where to go next.
- **A recruiter on each campus.** Someone who requests her campus and brings eight people has done your distribution for you, and `02` is built on precisely that unit.

**Also offer the cross-campus open night** as a fallback. Matching across campuses is worse — less in common, more awkward — but it is much better than nothing, and it keeps her from bouncing.

---

## Fix 5 — Make "then you swap" visible 🟠

Your product has two people in it, which means **one of them is the interviewer** — holding the facts, deciding whether to push. That's a real feature and it's nowhere on your page.

> **You interview too.** Half of every session you're on the other side of the table, holding the case. **Running a case teaches you what a good answer sounds like faster than doing one.**

It makes the session sound twice as valuable (it is), and it explains why a peer at *your* level is the right match rather than an expert.

---

## Fix 6 — Decide the name question this week 🟠

**`caseforge.in` is a different product in your own cohort, one word from your name, with a full landing page, published pricing and a better search position.** A student who hears "Case Forge" in a group chat and types it into Google may well land on them.

**You are three weeks old with 295 characters of website. You have almost no brand equity to lose, and this is the cheapest moment you will ever have to change a name.**

- **If you keep it:** always post the full URL, and always use the words they can't — *live*, *peers*, *together*, *matched*.
- **If you rename:** pick something that says what you do. A name about pairing, matching or partners works for you instead of needing to be defended.

Either way, **do not leave this unresolved for three months** — the cost compounds with every post you make.

---

## Fix 7 — Two small things 🔵

- **Both CTAs go to `/login`.** A visitor who doesn't understand the product yet has only one action available, and it's a commitment. Add *"How matching works"* as a real second CTA that scrolls, not logs in.
- **Test a full session on a phone, on mobile data** — including the live call and the feedback report. A 9pm case from a hostel room on patchy wifi is the real condition. If the call drops at minute four, your core product doesn't work and you'll hear about it from twenty people at once.

## The test that tells you you're ready

**Give your phone to a batchmate who has never seen it and say nothing.** Three things:

1. Does she understand in ten seconds that this matches her with a *person*?
2. **Does she find the answer to "will anyone be there?" without asking you?**
3. Does she commit to Thursday 9pm?

If the second one fails, Fix 2 isn't done — and that's the one that decides whether anything else in this kit matters.
