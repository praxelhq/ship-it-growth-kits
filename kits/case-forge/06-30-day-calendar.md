# Case Forge — 30-day execution calendar

**Days 1–3 are page and product work.** You have 295 characters of landing page and an open-ended matching promise with no pool behind it. Spending channels before `04` ships means users who sign up, get no match, and tell their batch it doesn't work.

Then the month has one shape, and it is deliberately narrow:

> **Weeks 1–2: one campus, completely. Weeks 3–4: one more campus at a time.**

**Fix your practice nights now and never move them: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:00pm IST.** Everything below assumes them, and you host every one.

**Your public window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 11–15.** You barely need it — your first fortnight is a group chat and forty conversations.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **The page and the fixed practice nights, by day 2.** Open-ended matching against an empty pool is the one mistake that cannot be recovered — each user it burns tells her batch.
2. **Saturating your own campus before touching a second.** Forty users on one campus is a working product; four hundred across forty campuses is nothing. **This is the hardest discipline in the kit and the most important.**
3. **Forty in-person conversations in week 1.** Unglamorous, highest-converting thing available to you, and three days' work.
4. **Asking every club for a launch night**, not just for a forward. Twenty people on one evening is ten matches; twenty across a week is zero.
5. **Recruiting a host per campus from week 4.** You cannot host thirty campuses. A campus with its own host is a channel that keeps running; one without is a channel that stops when you do.

## The target problem, and what to tell the board

**1,000 signups is a stretch for you and it is also the wrong target**, for a reason specific to your product: **a thousand accounts that never get matched is a failed month that looks like a successful one.**

Report these first, and say so in week 1 rather than week 4:

| | Target |
| --- | --- |
| **Matched sessions completed** | **300** |
| **Campuses with twice-weekly density** | **3** |
| **Users who completed ≥2 sessions** | **120** |
| Signups | 1,000 |

Three campuses with real density is a product that works and can be repeated weekly forever. A thousand signups with forty sessions is a landing page.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **r/MBA** (day 15 — and it actively delivers users you can't serve), **Peerlist** (day 13), **Discord nights** (day 19).

**Never drop:** days 1–2 (page + practice nights), day 5 (the forty conversations), every Tuesday and Thursday night, days 9/12 (club DMs with the launch-night ask), day 24 (recruiting hosts).
