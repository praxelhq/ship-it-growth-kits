# Case Forge — Distribution map

**Read this first. It is the opposite of every other distribution map in this cluster.**

Every other project wants reach. **You want density.** A live peer-matching product needs two people online at the same time at the same level, which means:

> **Forty users on one campus is a working product. Four hundred users across forty campuses is nothing.**

So this document is not ranked by reach. **It is ranked by how many people a channel delivers who share a calendar.** Broad channels — Reddit, Quora, SEO — actively hurt you this month, because they deliver scattered individuals who sign up, find no match, leave, and tell people it doesn't work.

**Your window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 11–15** for anything public. That's fine: your first two weeks are one campus, and that doesn't need a public launch at all.

---

## Tier 1 — One campus, completely 🔴

### 1. Your own campus — weeks 1 and 2, nothing else

**Target: 40 active users from one batch, with sessions happening daily by day 14.** That is the entire goal of your first fortnight, and it is more valuable than a thousand scattered signups.

| Where | Reach | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your batch WhatsApp group** | 60–400, 30–60% open | Not "sign up". **"Who wants to do a case at 9pm Thursday?"** A specific time recruits; a link does not. |
| **Section groups** | 60–90 each, 4–8 groups | Post in each separately. Higher response than the big batch group. |
| **Your consulting club** | 30–150 | **The densest group on campus and every member is exactly your user.** Ask the secretary for one announcement. |
| **The juniors' group** | often keener and freer | Second cohort once your batch is running |
| **Common rooms, the mess, the library at 11pm** | — | **Genuinely: ask people in person.** Forty users is forty conversations. You can have forty conversations in three days on your own campus and nothing in this kit converts better. |

**The mechanic that makes this work: fixed practice windows.** Not "sign up and we'll match you" — **"Tuesday and Thursday, 9pm, everyone comes."** Twelve people at 9pm is six matches. The same twelve spread randomly across a week is zero. `04` Fix 2.

### 2. One more campus at a time, via its consulting club

Only after your own campus has daily sessions. **Then repeat the pattern, one campus at a time**, and give each one its own practice window so its pool doesn't dilute.

| Target | Realistic | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Consulting clubs at new IIMs and tier-2 campuses** | 25 DMs → 8–12 replies → **3–6 who announce it to members** | Each is 30–150 people sharing a calendar. **Far more responsive than old IIMs**, who get pitched constantly. |
| **Clubs at engineering colleges with consulting ambitions** | 12 DMs → 4–6 replies → 2–3 | Larger cohorts, less competition for attention |
| **Pre-MBA cohort communities** (GMAT groups, pre-MBA WhatsApp cohorts) | 8 outreach → 2–4 | Already cohort-shaped, which is exactly what you need |

**The pitch to a secretary is unusually easy for you:** running a mock-practice programme is one of a club's standing jobs, and it's done with a WhatsApp spreadsheet. **You automate his worst admin task.** `03` #1.

**Rule: don't open a campus you can't fill.** Three campuses with real session density beat fifteen with none. Ask each secretary for a launch window — one evening when he tells his members to all show up — so the pool starts dense rather than trickling.

### 3. Pairs who already have each other

**The easiest users in your kit and your page doesn't speak to them.** Two friends who've agreed to prepare together arrive pre-matched — you don't have to solve liquidity for them at all.

| Where | Reach | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Looking for a case partner" threads** — Reddit, Discord, campus groups, LinkedIn | 50–500 each | **These threads exist everywhere and they're full of people whose exact problem you solve.** Be the answer in the thread, and bring both people in. |
| **Existing study pairs** | — | Ask every user *"who are you preparing with?"* and invite that person directly |
| **Club buddy-system programmes** | 20–80 per club | Many clubs already pair members up badly, by spreadsheet. Offer to be the tool. |

**Pairs are also your matching seed.** Two people who practise together will happily be matched with others once they've run out of each other.

| Channel | Reach | Notes |

## Tier 3 — Actively avoid this month 🔵

Unusually for this cluster, **some of these are things you should avoid because they'd work:**

- **SEO and Quora.** They deliver one person from one college at a time, with no cohort behind her. **She will never get a match.** Great channels in month 4; damaging in month 1.
- **Broad Reddit volume posting.** Same problem, faster.
- **Product Hunt, Hacker News, X/Twitter.** Wrong audience anyway.
- **Opening campuses you can't fill.** The single most tempting mistake in your kit.
- **Paid anything.** Budget is zero.

## Reach summary — and why it's the wrong table for you

| Channel | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own campus (groups + club + in person) | 200 | 600 |
| Other campuses via clubs (45 DMs) | 400 | 2,500 |
| Pairs from partner-seeking threads | 150 | 1,000 |
| Discord cohort nights | 150 | 900 |
| LinkedIn (posts + comments + secretary DMs) | 250 | 2,000 |
| r/MBA + Peerlist + Unstop | 250 | 2,000 |
| **Total addressable visitors** | **~1,400** | **~9,000** |

**This is the narrowest plan in the cluster, and that's deliberate.**

**The honest read: 1,000 signups is a stretch and it is also the wrong target for you.** 9,000 visitors at a 10% conversion is 900 — reachable only at the high end, and only if `04` ships.

**But a thousand accounts that never get matched is a failed month that looks like a successful one.** Your real target, and what `07` reports first:

| | Target |
| --- | --- |
| **Matched sessions completed** | **300** |
| **Campuses with daily sessions** | **3** |
| **Users who completed ≥2 sessions** | **120** |
| Signups | 1,000 |

**Say this to the board in week 1, not week 4.** Three campuses with real density is a product. A thousand signups and forty sessions is not, and you should not be rewarded for the second or punished for choosing the first.

The ranked levers: **ship the page and the practice windows** (`04`), **saturate your own campus in two weeks**, then **one club at a time with a launch night each.** Everything in Tier 2 is support.
