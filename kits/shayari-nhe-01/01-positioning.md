## Alternatives and real limits

A local diary or notes app can preserve a detail without another social persona or additional specialist subscription. Those are legitimate choices. Your possible distinction is an explicit continuing persona and inspectable memory, not a guarantee of remembering everything or a claim competitors cannot hold context.

The rendered Privacy Policy says conversations, memories and an emotional read are stored; messages go to AI providers to generate replies. Privacy Shield is described as optional masking of certain patterns, not a promise that every sensitive detail disappears. ## The growth constraint

You cannot acquire 1,000 active users by collecting 1,000 waitlist emails while access remains gated. Preserve the **1,000 consumer-account target**, distinguish waitlist from admitted accounts and report **a real user completing a later memory check** as activation. A limited beta needs a capacity and admission plan; state the feasible shortfall. Never encourage exclusive dependence, guilt on leaving or public disclosure of private chats to recruit others.
