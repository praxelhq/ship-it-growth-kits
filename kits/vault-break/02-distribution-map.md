# Vault Break — Distribution map

**Lead with a fair challenge link from a real completed run.** A screenshot saying “please try my game” is weaker than “same questions, can you beat this?” Never publish answers in the invitation.

| Route | Action and rules | Expected result / explicit assumption | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Your first newsletter is a permitted item in the campus organizer's own bulletin, not an assumed placement in a large games newsletter. Show the playable link, free opening and optional $1 terms. Contact one organizer once; no scraped mailing lists.

Initial reach supports tens of players. At an assumed 40% completion rate and 25% of completers sharing to one new starter, each starter produces only 0.1 additional starter. That is not a self-sustaining loop. Measure actual sharing before claiming viral growth or a route to 1,000.

Keep your broad campus game invitation in the c1 Days10–13 window; classmates in the c2 game cluster use Days17–20. These preferences do not replace actual permission and dates. Do not flood one group with every cohort game.
