# CaseFlex — Distribution map

**Read this first:** you have two audiences and they live in completely different places.

- **Volume comes from B-school clubs**, reached top-down with your Team plan — the instrument no other project in this cluster has.
- **Revenue and retention come from early-career professionals**, reached on LinkedIn and in professional communities, where no cohort-mate is competing at all.

**Your launch window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 15–19.** That's late, and it's fine — your two best channels (clubs and professionals) aren't the contested ones. Don't jump the queue in r/MBA.

Reach estimates state their basis. **Verify before planning around them.**

---

## Tier 1 — Clubs, top-down 🔴

### 1. B-school consulting and case clubs, pitched with the Team plan

This is your single best channel and **you can run it in a way nobody else in this cluster can.** Everyone else asks a club for a favour. You arrive with cohort leaderboards, an admin dashboard and custom cases — things a club secretary actually wants for his members.

**The offer this month: the Team plan, free for the season.** Not discounted. Free. You are buying signups and a case study, not $12 a seat.

| Target | Realistic | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Consulting clubs at new IIMs and tier-2 campuses** (IIM Udaipur, Trichy, Ranchi, Raipur, Kashipur; MDI, IMI, IMT, SPJIMR, Great Lakes, TAPMI, XIMB, NMIMS) | 35 DMs → 12–18 replies → **5–9 who distribute** | **Far more responsive than the old IIMs**, who get pitched constantly and already have alumni-run mock programmes |
| **Old IIMs and ISB** | 8 DMs → 2–4 replies → 1–2 | Worth the hours only because the names help your next pitch |
| **Case clubs at engineering colleges with consulting ambitions** (BITS, NITs, DTU, VIT) | 15 DMs → 5–8 replies → 2–4 | Undervalued; large, keen, zero competition for their attention |
| **Pre-MBA and GMAT prep communities with cohort structures** | 10 outreach → 3–5 | Your Rookie level is written for exactly these people |

**Each club that distributes is 30–150 members who are all exactly your user, during the one month they care.** Six of them is most of your month.

**Why the leaderboard matters more than you think:** a cohort leaderboard makes your product spread *inside* a club without you. One secretary turns it on and eighty people see each other's streaks. That's the only viral mechanic available to anything in this cluster, and you're the only one with it. **Ask every club to turn it on, and ask what their cohort's weekly rep count is — that number is your best content.**

### 2. Early-career professionals on LinkedIn (your revenue audience)

No campus, no case partner, a salary, and no other project in this cohort is built for them.

| What | Reach | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your own posts** | 1,000–15,000 impressions when one lands | The "you don't have a campus giving you mocks" angle is **completely unaddressed** by anyone else. `03` #2. |

## Tier 3 — Don't 🔵

- **Product Hunt, Hacker News.** Wrong audience.
- **X/Twitter.** Negligible here.

- **Undergraduate channels and campus WhatsApp at $19.** Until pricing is localised, **student channels will give you traffic and no conversions**, which is worse than no traffic because you'll misread it as a product problem.
- **Paid anything.** Budget is zero.

**The honest read.** 1,000 signups needs roughly 9,000–10,000 visitors, so the low end misses and the high end clears.

But for you, more than for anyone else in this cluster, **the binding constraint is not traffic. It's price.**

At $19 with no INR option, your Indian student traffic converts at 1–3% and your signup number will miss badly no matter how many channels you run. **Localise to ₹399–599 and the same traffic converts at 8–12%.** That single change is worth more than every channel on this page combined, which is why it is `04` Fix 3 and day 1 of `06`.

The second-biggest lever is **ungating "Score my 60 seconds"** (`04` Fix 1). You have the best low-commitment hook in the cohort and you currently charge an account for the answer.

**And one structural advantage worth stating plainly:** five or six clubs turning on cohort leaderboards is 300–900 signups that spread without you posting anything. **No other project in this cluster has a mechanic that works while they sleep.** Spend your month there.
