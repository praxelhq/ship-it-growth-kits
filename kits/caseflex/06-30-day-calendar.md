# CaseFlex — 30-day execution calendar

**Day 1 has one job: rupee pricing.** Nothing else in this kit works until `04` Fix 3 ships. At $19 your Indian student channels produce traffic and no conversions, and you'll spend three weeks misdiagnosing it.

After that, the shape of your month is **clubs, then professionals**:

- **Weeks 1–2: 68 club DMs.** Your Team plan is an instrument no cohort-mate has, and it's where your volume comes from.
- **Weeks 2–4: the professional audience on LinkedIn.** Where your revenue and your retention come from, and where nobody is competing.

**Your public window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 15–19**, which is late and suits you — your two best channels aren't the contested ones.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **Rupee pricing on day 1.** At $19 every Indian channel in `02` is wasted. There is no second-most-important thing here.
2. **68 club DMs in weeks 1–2, and setting up every yes yourself.** Each club is 30–150 people who are exactly your user. **And the cohort leaderboard is the only mechanic in this entire cluster that spreads without the founder posting anything.**
3. **Ungating the 60-second score.** You have the best hook in the cohort and you currently charge an account for the answer.
4. **Owning the professional audience.** Nobody else in this cluster is talking to the person with no campus and a salary. It's your only uncontested audience and it's the one that pays.
5. **Day-7 return above 15%.** You sell reps and a streak. If people don't come back, the progression system is decoration.

## The advantage worth protecting

**You are the only project in this cluster with a product built for selling into clubs** — leaderboards, an admin dashboard, custom cases. Everyone else has to ask a club for a favour; you arrive with something a secretary wants for his own job.

**That is worth more than any feature on your roadmap this month.** It's why days 4–6 build the page and start the DMs before anything public happens, and why day 28 asks your best club for a cohort challenge rather than asking for more users.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 18 — compounds into January), **Peerlist** (day 16 — builder audience), **the 100 professional DMs** (day 19 — high effort, slow).

**Never drop:** day 1 (pricing), days 4–6 (clubs page + first DMs), day 11 and 14 (converting the club yeses), day 15 (r/MBA — one shot).
