## 6. The 60-second challenge post (Instagram / LinkedIn / Twitter, weekly)

You have the best low-commitment hook in your cohort. **Use it as content.**

> **Structure this in 60 seconds.**
>
> A regional airline's load factor is down 6%. Competitors are flat.
>
> Go. Don't read on until you've actually done it.
>
> ---
>
> Mine: supply vs demand. Supply — did they add capacity, change the schedule, change the fleet? Demand — route mix, pricing, seasonality, a competitor's move.
>
> **The trap:** going demand-first because it's more interesting. If they added capacity in that period, the load factor drop is arithmetic and demand has nothing to do with it. You've just spent your best ten minutes in the wrong place.
>
> Scored version with feedback: caseflex.app

**Post one a week.** It teaches something real, it's the product in miniature, and the "don't read on until you've done it" line makes people actually participate.

**"Is this just ChatGPT?"**

**"What's a Founder's Office case?"**

> The kind of problem you'd actually get handed in a founder's office — a metric fell, should we enter this city, is this hire worth it — judged on whether you'd be useful on Monday rather than on framework completeness. It's a real and growing hiring market in India and nobody builds prep for it.
