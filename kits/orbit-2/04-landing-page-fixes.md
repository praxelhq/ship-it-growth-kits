# ORBIT — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://withinorbit.space/), [signup](https://withinorbit.space/login?mode=signup), [privacy](https://withinorbit.space/privacy) and [terms](https://withinorbit.space/terms) were read 29 October. No account, resume, intro or booking was submitted.

## 1. Resolve the operator payment-state mismatch

The page lists ₹15,000/building/month but its observed purchase link goes to a test checkout. Do not advertise it as live paid access. Use **“Request an operator demonstration”** until actual live billing/delivery is verified, or clearly label the sandbox. A return-page success message isn't payment or an active building.

## 2. Make building availability explicit

Current H1: **“Meet the three people in your building worth knowing this week.”** CTA: **“Join your building.”** Add **“Available inside participating buildings. Check your building before setting up a profile.”** Don't promise three reciprocal matches if the real cohort is too small or not yet configured.

## 3. Explain matching versus consent

Replace the unconditional weekly promise with **“See relevant reciprocal suggestions when available. Both people choose whether and when to meet.”** Test decline, reschedule, no-show and cancellation flows before claiming the app handles scheduling end to end.

## 4. Show a complete, labelled example

Use fictional give/want profiles, reason for the match, mutual acceptance and the actual supported calendar/email step. Label **“Example introduction”**. A sample is not a real colleague or a successfully attended meeting.

## 5. Put private data boundaries before resume upload

The policy says resume text pre-fills fields, the file isn't retained, profiles stay within the building and operators see aggregates. Verify these promises against the actual flow before recruitment. Offer **“Type a few lines instead”**. Explain which fields another member sees before they are shared; don't require a resume for a small introduction.

## 6. Make a new/empty building useful

Show **“Not enough compatible members yet. We'll explain the next step; no meeting is booked without your choice.”** Don't manufacture members or match counts to avoid the cold start. An honest opt-in wait is better than a fake live network.

## 7. Keep phone and shared previews discreet

Title: **“ORBIT — reciprocal introductions inside participating buildings.”** Description: **“Free member access; choose a relevant conversation, without a public feed.”** Never expose member floor, profile, resume or meeting details in shared cards/analytics. Test work-email entry, return links and scheduling on a phone.
