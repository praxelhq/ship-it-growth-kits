# ORBIT — Video scripts

Use fictional profiles and a demo building. No tenant list, work email, resume or private calendar details in recordings.

## A. Why both people meet — 45 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“Why should both people want this coffee?”**

2–15: Show two fictional give/want profiles. “One can help with a question; the other has something useful in return.”

15–27: Show the actual supported match explanation and mutual choice. “A suggestion isn't an obligation. Both choose.”

27–37: Show scheduling only if verified, with sample labels. “A sent intro and an attended meeting are different outcomes.”

37–45: “One participating building first. withinorbit.space.” Use in manager demos.

## B. Reciprocity — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Not ‘you should network more.’”**

2–13: Show a concrete fictional give/want pair. “A useful reason for both people to talk, a lift ride away.”

13–20: “ORBIT. Opt-in, inside a participating building.” URL. First owned clip.

## C. The meeting count — 20 seconds

0–2: **“An email isn't a coffee.”**

2–12: Show labelled stages: suggestion, mutual acceptance, booked slot, attended meeting. “Count each honestly.”

12–20: “A small real network beats inflated intros.” URL. Second owned clip; no fake attendance.

## D. Operator scope — 30 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“Your tenants don't become a contact list.”**

2–15: Show the actual privacy/operator explanation. “The program is opt-in; the stated operator view is aggregate engagement.”

15–25: Explain price and actual test/live setup state. “A demonstration comes before a rollout decision.”

25–30: “Let's check fit for one building.” Operator entry URL. Send only to a manager who agreed to the demo.
