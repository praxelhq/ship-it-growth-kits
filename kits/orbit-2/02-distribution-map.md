# ORBIT — Distribution map

**Operator permission and one building's density lead.** A broad builder launch can't supply two people who share a building and want to meet.

Forecasts are untested scenarios for the actual calendar, not benchmark conversion rates.

The experiment might produce a few dozen members in one building, not an evidenced thousand. At 20 accounts per active building, 1,000 requires 50 buildings with actual participation; your three manager conversations don't establish that reach.

## Communities and accounts

The manager's own noticeboard/email route is the primary channel only after permission; no public tenant association, newsletter or influencer has promised distribution. Ask a real manager you can reach, not a list of invented coworking communities. No tenant contact list needs to be copied into this public repo.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) may document the build but is not a substitute for local matching density. Skip broad directories until a complete opt-in intro/booking/attendance loop works. Coordinate with Café Xtroverts, DilliScenes, Crewup, Adda and Shotgun: your job is professional reciprocity in one building, not café discovery or an existing friend-circle plan. Reserve separate organizer dates and no engagement trading.
