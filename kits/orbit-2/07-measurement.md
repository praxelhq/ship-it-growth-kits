# ORBIT — Measurement

**Unchanged board target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real member accounts. Report **a reciprocal intro accepted by both and an actually attended coffee** alongside it. Keep acceptance, booking and attendance as separate stages. A sent email isn't activation, and one member's three suggestions aren't three users.

Operator subscriptions at the listed ₹15,000/building/month are a separate metric. The observed test checkout doesn't establish live revenue or a paid partner.

## Day 0

Read latest date/traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/orbit-2). Record actual agreed buildings, eligible members, accounts, useful profiles, suggestions, mutual acceptances, bookings, attendance, cancellations and returns. This kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured**, not zero. Don't merge with the separate `orbit` project.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on safe public/entry surfaces. Exclude resumes, work-email codes, floor/location context, profiles and meeting contents. Operators' aggregate-view promise should be checked against actual permissions; a policy sentence isn't a security audit.

## Funnel and density

`building_agreed → eligible_member_invited → account_created → useful_profile → reciprocal_intro_accepted → coffee_attended → next_intro_wanted`.

Use a private aggregate attendance check, not a demand for photos or conversation details. A no-show is not a meeting. Manual profile entry and declining a suggestion should remain legitimate paths.

## Honest scale

`02` schedules three manager approaches, 20 member invitations, one possible notice and two clips, supporting tens of accounts before overlap if a building participates. At 20 real accounts per building, 1,000 requires 50 buildings; that distribution isn't established. Preserve the target while reporting the actual density/fit and gap.

These are chosen learning checks, not professional-success guarantees. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings and tenant footfall aren't additive app users.
