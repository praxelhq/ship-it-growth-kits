# Bae Talk — Video scripts

Use fictional adults/messages. Keep **“Sample preview — not live AI”** visible. No tester screenshots or imitation of someone else's private voice.

## A. Tone-choice preview — 40 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Same text. Which reply sounds like you?”** Show a fictional “Had a nice time yesterday.”

2–12: Show the current sample's casual/playful/direct options. “These are example tones, not instructions about how you should feel.”

12–22: Read two choices plainly. “A clever answer isn't useful if you would never say it.”

22–32: Show the sample-state label. “BaeTalk is in development. I'm testing this reply-choice job before calling the AI chat live.”

32–40: “Try the fictional sample. Launch updates are optional. baetalk.social.” Use on owned profiles/homepage, not as a live-product demo.

## B. The direct reply — 15 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“You don't need the perfect line.”**

2–9: Show a fictional direct reply: **“I had a good time too. Would you like to meet this weekend?”** Voice: “Sometimes saying what you mean is the whole improvement.”

9–15: “BaeTalk sample preview. You decide what to send.” URL and prelaunch label. First short clip.

## C. No mind-reading — 20 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Two days isn't a verdict.”**

2–12: Show fictional delayed-message bubbles. “One delay doesn't tell a tool what a stranger thinks. A useful companion should admit what it can't know.”

12–20: “I'm building BaeTalk around choices, not certainty about other people. Sample preview at baetalk.social.” Second clip, no diagnostic labels.

## D. What exists today — 25 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“A preview isn't a live chat.”**

2–12: Record the actual site sample. “This version shows example tone choices.”

12–20: Show launch-interest explanation. “You can opt in for launch news. That isn't a paid plan or an activated chat account.”

20–25: “I'll show the real reply flow when it is ready.” URL. Third clip; transparency replaces fake AI typing.
