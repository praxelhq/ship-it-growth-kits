## #6 — Useful no-link conversation reply

One message rarely proves someone's intentions. If you want to see them again, a clear reply about what you'd actually like is usually easier to understand than a line designed to sound maximally clever. You can be warm and still say no. Don't send a message that doesn't feel like something you'd stand behind later.

Use only in an appropriate conversation; don't turn support threads into product discovery. Disclose your professional interest if asked.
