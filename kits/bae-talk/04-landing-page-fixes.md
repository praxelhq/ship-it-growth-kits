# Bae Talk — Landing-page fixes

Evidence: [live page](https://baetalk.social/) and raw page copy opened 29 October. The sample is explicitly labelled; the observed promotional links lead within the page. Private chat, paid checkout and voice functionality were not tested.

## 1. Make prelaunch state the primary truth

Current H1: **“Dating is confusing. Your AI bestie doesn't have to be.”** Current primary action: **“Talk to BaeTalk.”** Elsewhere the page says real AI replies arrive at launch.

Replacement action: **“Try the sample preview.”** Secondary: **“Get launch updates.”** Under both: **“BaeTalk is in development. This preview uses example replies; live AI chat isn't available here yet.”** Don't drive traffic until the actions and outcome match.

## 2. Rename plan claims as planned, or remove them

Current displayed tiers: Free $0, Plus $7.99/month, Premium $14.99/month, with message quotas. If payment/service isn't enabled, use **“Planned options — not available to buy yet”** and one launch-interest action. Do not ask someone to “Get Premium” when they cannot obtain the promised service. Verify actual live availability before changing this status.

## 3. Make the narrow job visible

Subhead replacement: **“Compare ways to answer a text, adjust the tone, and choose what sounds like you. You decide what to send.”** Avoid suggesting the tool knows what a partner secretly thinks. The first example should require no private upload.

## 4. Explain private material before offering screenshots or memory

The page advertises screenshot inputs and optional memory. Before either is enabled, publish what is sent to a provider, what is saved, how long, and how deletion works. In the preview use **“Use a fictional example. Please don't upload someone else's private conversation.”** Don't invent a local-only or never-stored assurance.

## 5. Make opt-in and success states explicit

Waitlist action: **“Email me when BaeTalk is ready.”** Confirmation: **“You're on the launch-interest list. This doesn't create a chat account or start a paid plan.”** Test the form with your own address, including duplicate/error/unsubscribe cases, before claiming it works. No prechecked marketing extras.

## 6. Remove outcomes the sample cannot prove

Sample confidence and tone changes are examples, not measured dating improvements. Use **“Example reply options”**, not “guaranteed better replies.” Do not manufacture successful dates or testimonial counts. A useful first result is a person recognizing their own voice.

## 7. Give shares and phones the same honest expectation

Title: **“BaeTalk — dating reply companion, in development.”** Description: **“Try a fictional tone-choice preview and opt in for launch updates. Live AI replies arrive at launch.”** Test the actual card, keyboard and form on a phone. Keep privacy explanation near inputs; don't bury it below the colourful chat sample.
