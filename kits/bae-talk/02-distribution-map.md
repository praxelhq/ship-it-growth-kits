## Communities and directories

Dating-help communities involve real vulnerable conversations. No current promotional permission was verified for a specialist subreddit, so the base plan does not include a sales post there. Offer the no-link advice in #6 only when relevant and permitted; do not collect personal screenshots via DMs.

[Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) explicitly exclude signup pages and work people cannot try. Skip it now. [Product Hunt's guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is a future directory route after there is a usable product and truthful plan availability; forecast zero month-one users from it today. No newsletter or relationship account has promised a feature.
