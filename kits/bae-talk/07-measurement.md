# Bae Talk — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Retain the target, but distinguish genuine product accounts from a prelaunch interest list. Activation is **a real user receiving a functioning reply suggestion and choosing/editing one**, once the live product exists. Sample-button taps and email opt-ins do not establish that outcome.

## Day 0

Record the date and latest traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/bae-talk). Record existing unique interest emails, consent/unsubscribe status, website sample use, actual chat-account count and whether live AI is enabled. This kit does not know private totals. Use **not measured** or **not available**, not a fabricated zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on public marketing pages only. Don't record private conversation inputs, screenshots, voice or memory content. Your product's data-handling explanation must match the actual providers/storage before real material is accepted.

## Two separate funnels

**Prelaunch:** `preview_opened → sample_used → launch_interest_opted_in → launch_notice_requested`.

**Product, when ready:** `account_created → real_reply_received → reply_chosen_or_edited → later_session_used`.

Keep these in separate columns. Don't add them together for “total users.” If the account flow does not exist, the account target currently has no demonstrated conversion path. Say that plainly in the board update.

## Forecast and decisions

`02` budgets 15 peer invitations, two permissioned groups, three clips, one LinkedIn builder post and one Peerlist update. Its roughly 6–46 guessed interest emails before overlap are not a forecast of activated product accounts. At an illustrative 20% qualified-visit-to-account rate after launch, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visits; current reach and readiness do not prove that.

| Review | What to inspect | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Can 5 testers correctly identify sample versus live chat? | If 2 misunderstand, correct CTAs before distribution. |
| Day 14 | Naturalness feedback, genuine opt-ins, unsubscribe requests. | Improve the narrow task, not the number of personality labels. |
| Day 21 | Is there a working real-reply path and clear handling of private inputs? | If no, no live-product launch and no activation claim. |
| Day 28 | For any live pilot, real replies delivered/chosen and later use. | If suggestions don't sound like users, fix the product before adding groups. |
| Day 30 | Product accounts, activations and interest emails separately. | Report the gap to 1,000 and the exact readiness work still needed. |

These thresholds are selected experiment rules, not validated industry standards. Track whether people felt pressured or misunderstood the tool as knowing someone else's intentions; that is a product-copy problem to fix, not a signal to write stronger promises.

Keep course snapshots dated and don't sum overlapping 24-hour readings. An entertaining sample can attract traffic while the real product is still unbuilt.
