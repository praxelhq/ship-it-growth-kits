# Offer Decoder — Distribution map

**Lead with a fictional, auditable offer breakdown.** Private permission-first candidate testing beats collecting salary screenshots. Estimates are guesses for the exact workload in `06`.

| Route | Rules and useful action | Assumed result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 known adult applicants | Personal invitation, ten on Day 6 and ten on Day 9. Ask to use a fictional offer first; no public upload request. | 6–12 starts, 3–8 checked breakdowns; #1. |
| Two existing adult career-circle bulletins | Ask each organizer before posting; different circles/weeks from Kriya and other CV tools. No recruiter endorsement or attendee list. | Combined 60–160 relevant views, 3–10 starts; #2. |
| Your [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) profile | Three original explainers, disclose you built it. [Basic is free](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545596); no unsolicited tagging or salary polls. | Combined 90–360 impressions, 1–7 starts; #3. |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Optional free usable-product submission after data/calculation gates; check current slot and previous listing. No vote exchange. | 0–5 starts, not in base; #4. |

Base: 10–29 starts before deduplication, perhaps 5–18 checked breakdowns. No benchmark justifies 1,000 from these channels. The ClearTax calculator is competitor research, not permission to advertise in its comments. No verified newsletter or finance community posting slot is assumed. Skip paid directory placement and unsolicited DMs under someone's financial disclosure.

One adult career organizer may host a small fictional-offer exercise only if accepted; it is included within the two circle invitations, not an extra unexplained traffic source. Use `03` #5. Do not turn the exercise into personal financial advice.
