# Offer Decoder — Positioning

Live: https://offerdecoder.online/ · Other / B2C · [Course profile](https://shipped.money/p/offer-decoder)

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, transparently counted as unique genuine decode sessions for this no-account first use. Report completed, source-checked breakdowns and one understood compensation question beside starts. Optional saved-report accounts are separate, never added to those same people.

The live brand is OfferDecode. Inspected 29 October 2026: **“Your CTC isn't your salary. See what actually lands.”** Public entry offers PDF/photo/pasted text/manual input, five AI reads daily and unlimited Quickscan/manual use. Decode does not require an account; Google sign-in saves reports. We inspected routes and [privacy](https://offerdecoder.online/privacy), not a real offer upload, calculation, saved report or share link. No product price was publicly verified.

First user: an adult early-career applicant comparing an Indian offer's fixed, variable and deferred components, who currently asks a friend to translate the letter. The first test should use a fictional letter. Never solicit actual offer documents in public comments or class groups.

Pitch: “Turn an offer's compensation terms into a breakdown you can check and questions to ask payroll.” Bio: “Read the offer beyond the CTC.” Wedge: one decoded fictional offer, extraction checked against its source, then one specific clarification such as when a variable component is paid. Do not present an estimated figure as the guaranteed monthly deposit.

The homepage's FY 2026–27 tax precision is a product claim, not accuracy established by this kit. Get the current calculations independently checked against official rules and relevant assumptions before advertising accuracy. This kit supplies no tax-rate advice. [ClearTax's public salary calculator](https://cleartax.in/s/salary-calculator) is a real no-purchase-gate alternative for manual inputs; an existing spreadsheet is another. OfferDecode must distinguish itself through source extraction, transparent assumptions and useful questions, not “calculators don't exist.”

**Privacy is the immediate weakness.** “Quickscan offline (no AI)” can suggest device-only use, but the policy says Quickscan/tax math run on the server. AI extraction sends letter text/images via OpenRouter to model providers. Explain that before intake. Manual entry avoids sending a whole letter to an AI model, but does not make computation local. Do not invite real offers until these choices and actual handling are clear.
