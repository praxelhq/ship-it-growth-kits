# Offer Decoder — Landing page fixes

Observed 29 October 2026: **“Your CTC isn't your salary. See what actually lands.”** PDF/photo/paste/manual intake is public; five daily AI reads and unlimited Quickscan/manual use are stated. No real input or computed accuracy was tested.

1. **Fix the offline/server contradiction before real uploads.** Current “Quickscan offline (no AI)” conflicts with the [policy](https://offerdecoder.online/privacy): code runs server-side. Replacement **“Quickscan: no AI model. Processing still uses our server.”** AI option: **“AI read: selected letter text/images are sent through OpenRouter to model providers. Read their data practices before continuing.”** Show the actual model/provider list and choices, not a vague privacy badge.
2. **Replace guaranteed precision with inspectable estimates.** Keep the CTC insight, but replace “See what actually lands” with **“Check the components behind your estimated take-home pay.”** Support **“Verify extracted figures and calculation assumptions. Your payroll treatment and circumstances can change the result.”** Get current financial-year calculations independently checked before advertising tax accuracy; a sample result is not that check.
3. **Make the lower-data route visible.** CTA **“Try a fictional example”**, secondary **“Enter components manually”**. Explain **“Manual entry avoids uploading a whole letter to an AI model; computation still uses our server.”** Do not put a real candidate's offer in the example. Test extraction errors, unsupported files and daily allowance behavior.
4. **Explain saving and sharing before exposure.** Current policy: Google sign-in saves private reports in MongoDB; optional share links are readable by anyone holding the link and expire after 90 days. Replacement **“Save privately to your account. Sharing is optional; anyone with the link can read it until expiry.”** Verify revoke/expiry/access behavior before asserting it works. Do not treat account authentication as a guarantee of share-link confidentiality.
5. **Make retention concrete.** Policy claims file bytes discarded after memory processing and extracted fields/hash cached for 24 hours. Reconcile implementation/provider logging with this before making an end-to-end “not stored” claim. Mask/exclude intake/results from any Clarity recording; the policy's no-offer-text statement needs implementation verification.
6. **Preview and phone.** Proposed title **“OfferDecode — check the compensation behind your CTC”**. Preview: fictional components, no employer or candidate identity. Test mobile paste, PDF errors, visible assumptions, keyboard and return navigation. Show actual access/price limits before an unexpected charge; no public price was established here.
