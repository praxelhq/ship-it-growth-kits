# Offer Decoder — Video scripts

Use fictional compensation and your own recording. No real letter, identifying employer data or financial recommendation. Do not quote a tax rate from memory.

**A. Check one offer, 45 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “Don't divide the headline by twelve yet.” 0–10s show a clearly fictional offer with components. 10–20s show the actual processing choice and disclosure. Say, “I choose the data route before entering anything.” 20–35s record the real resulting extraction after testing it; compare each component with the source. 35–45s highlight assumptions and a genuine clarification question: “When is this variable component paid?” End: “Estimate, not guaranteed payroll. offerdecoder.online.” Never fake a completed decode.

**B. No AI isn't offline, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “No AI doesn't always mean on your device.” Show current policy and corrected Quickscan label. Say, “This route uses server-side code. The AI route adds model-provider processing. Explain the difference before the upload.” End card: “Choose with the actual data information.” No claim of a privacy audit.

**C. The unanswered question, 20 seconds; accepted circle.** Hook: “A breakdown should leave you with a better question.” Show fictional variable/deferred lines, then type “What conditions and timing apply?” Say, “The useful output isn't a confident number alone. It's knowing what needs clarification.” Do not simulate a recruiter reply or recommend accepting/rejecting an offer.

**D. Sharing choice, 20 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “Anyone with the link means anyone with the link.” Show the actual disclosure without creating a private report. Say, “Save and share are different choices. Read visibility and expiry before copying a link.” End: “Use a fictional report for a demo.” No untested promise that deletion revokes every copy.
