# Draftly — Video scripts

These are recovery/readiness scripts until the real product can be inspected. No fictional writing tool, output or price is invented.

## A. Access-and-first-use demonstration — 45 seconds

Record only after recovery. 0–2s: **“This is the canonical Draftly link we're testing.”**

2–12s: open the actual URL in a fresh browser; show its real offer and price/limits. 12–30s: follow the actual CTA with owned/synthetic data and keep the real wait/result visible. 30–40s: refresh/reopen the result and show any boundary. 40–45s: verified canonical URL.

If any step fails, don't edit it into success. Record a factual issue for the owner instead of a promo.

## B. Recovery hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: **“A working screenshot isn't a working first-use path.”**

2–10s: show the canonical page and actual successful action only after verified. 10–15s: explain what was tested and what remains untested. No “everything fixed” claim from one browser.

## C. Domain clarity — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Draftly is the project. Is drafty.co.in the right address?”**

2–12s: show the owner's confirmed link and updated course listing. 12–20s: demonstrate the correct destination. Don't publish this until the spelling is confirmed; don't point to a guessed alternative.

## D. Limit explainer — 20 seconds

After a real offer exists, show its actual free/paid boundary and one genuine limitation in plain language. Use the real interface, not a mock output. No rendered effects or paid tools required; a screen recording and your own voice are enough.
