Approximately 27–165 gross sessions before overlap. At 60% qualified visit→meaningful comparison, 1,000 requires about 1,667 distinct relevant visitors. Browsing the hero or opening a sample score isn't automatically a meaningful comparison. Four clips don't guarantee reach; replace rates after two clips.

No supplement-brand payments, product giveaways, paid fitness influencers or affiliate sales assumptions. Do not arrange a discount that conflicts with the independence promise. A gym newsletter mention requires permission, zero until accepted. Public source data isn't partnership access to another site's audience.

Use distinct organizers from PeakX's logging test and WhatNxt's exercise-selection test; c1 gym preference Days 17–20, c2 gym Days 24–27, actual dates subject to consent. No same-group second pitch within seven days. Don't turn a source-reading exercise into recommending a supplement, identifying a deficiency or collecting lab reports.
