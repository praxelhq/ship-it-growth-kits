# SachLabel — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count distinct meaningful real comparison/evidence-inspection sessions under the no-account policy. Opening the homepage or an illustrative score alone isn't enough: record actual product/evidence exploration consistently. Put **two products/source bases inspected**, **one limitation understood** and voluntary return beside signup. Optional signed-in profiles, early-access emails and test supporter actions are separate and overlapping.

No private analytics/profile was accessed. Fill baseline, unknown isn't zero.

| Baseline | Source |

| Actual product/evidence/source inspection | Events, no personal supplement list |
| Reader understanding of missing test/limited data | Optional feedback, no health details |
| Voluntary return for another evidence check | Privacy-consistent aggregate activity |
| Supporter test versus real paid state | Separate billing readiness/status, no invented revenue |
| Traffic/course metric/rank/time | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/sachlabel) |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding scripts. Verify privacy's Me/profile/early-access/payment-return exclusions; never send personal diet, pregnancy/breastfeeding, lists or account tokens into conversion data. Track `comparison_started`, `product_evidence_opened`, `source_opened`, `method_limit_opened`, `return_compare`. A view of a score doesn't prove the reader understands it.

`02` estimates roughly 27–165 gross sessions before overlap. At 60% qualified visit→meaningful comparison, target needs about 1,667 relevant distinct visitors. Four clips don't guarantee that. Replace rates after two clips; don't count profile variations or every product view as new people.

Support checkout is test-only; ₹99/month written terms aren't current revenue and roadmap reports aren't delivered proof. No metrics establish product safety for an individual, medical benefit, lab accuracy or an independence audit. Overlapping course traffic windows aren't cumulative users; public catalogue counts aren't customers.
