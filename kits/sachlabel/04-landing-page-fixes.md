# SachLabel — Landing-page fixes

Homepage→free category inspected in browser; methodology/support/terms/privacy read, 29 October. No personal profile/list, purchase or lab reproduction. Underlying tests, prices and clinical claims weren't independently audited.

1. **Make score limits travel with the hero.** Actual H1 “Get the sach about your supplements.” CTA “START COMPARING →” opens multivitamins. Proposed support **“Compare published label and lab evidence, see missing information and inspect the original sources. Scores aren't advice on what to take.”** Button **“Inspect a free evidence comparison.”** Don't use “every supplement” if coverage is only the catalogue.

2. **Don't make high score mean tested/safe.** Hero highlights 100/100 and daily-need percentage. Methodology omits/reweights missing lab evidence. Put evidence status, batch/date and method coverage beside every score, including the hero. **“High rubric score does not establish suitability for you or prove independent testing.”** No top-ranked product prescription.

3. **Keep default profile visible.** Category says scores for Adult woman (default). A visitor may mistake it for personal need. Use **“Default comparison profile—not your health assessment.”** Make changing profile optional and explain sensitive input processing before sign-in. No pregnancy/diet/health data needed for general source inspection; don't use those attributes for acquisition targeting.

4. **Preserve evidence distinctions.** Separate batch reports publishing passes/failures, certification lists, brand labels and retailer listings. Absence from a certification list isn't failure. Additive preference flags aren't safety findings. A study about one form doesn't prove a specific product's effectiveness. Actual source links and revision dates matter more than a universal linked-facts counter.

5. **Make price units honest.** Method uses dated pack price/MRP and daily labelled dose, not a personal recommended dose. If dose missing, show per-serving/unknown daily cost, not an invented number. Explain that budget filters hide records lacking daily cost. Don't imply the displayed retailer price remains available or price changes quality score.

6. **Label supporter test/roadmap beside all actions.** Terms describe ₹99/month recurring; Support says no real charge in test mode, protein reports/guidebook coming soon. Use **“Test checkout only—no real payment. Extra reports not yet published.”** Don't call it a donation or promise complete paid content. Verify production billing/cancel/delivery only with authorized tests before changing the offer.

7. **Check sources and privacy on phones.** At 390px, test side-by-side products, long evidence links, limited-data labels and original-source return. Verify Me/early-access/profile screens excluded from replay as policy states, not assumed audited. Preview **“SachLabel — inspect supplement evidence and its limits.”** No private list, dosage recommendation or false certification badge.
