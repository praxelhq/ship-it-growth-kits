# SachLabel — Video scripts

Use public product pages without endorsing a supplement. Don't display real profiles, private lists, lab reports from users or copied full test documents. No dosage/medical advice or invented lab results.

## A. Evidence-comparison demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Does this score include an independent test?”**

2–12s: open actual category and two suitable public entries; label demonstration, not recommendation. “Score and price answer different questions.”

12–25s: inspect real evidence-status/date/missing-data labels. “No test found isn't a failed test. Check the original batch.”

25–38s: open methodology and original source. “This is an owner-defined rubric, not a medical suitability assessment.” Don't repeat any product claim the source doesn't support.

38–50s: URL/free-comparison and test-only-support note. “Use evidence to ask a better question, not choose what to take from a number.”

## B. Missing-test hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Untested and failed aren't synonyms.”**

2–12s: actual evidence labels side by side, no medical conclusion about the products. “Know which statement the source supports.”

12–18s: methodology/URL. No independent audit implied.

## C. Coverage hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“How much of the rubric was actually used?”**

2–12s: real limited-data/method-coverage label. “Missing pieces can change the denominator. Read that before the ranking.”

12–20s: source link and free comparison. Don't invent a score change for drama.

## D. Price hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Per serving isn't always per day.”**

2–12s: show real label-dose/unknown-day-cost distinction without advising a dose. “The unit and price date need to be visible.”

12–20s: actual methodology/URL. “A comparison isn't a prescription.” No product purchase CTA or savings guarantee.
