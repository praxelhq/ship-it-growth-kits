Approximately 13–74 gross accounts before overlap **only after readiness**. At 30% qualified visit→account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 distinct visitors. Actual private-check-in activation is smaller. If only public-screen feedback is possible, genuine account conversion remains unknown, not this forecast. Two posts/three clips don't guarantee reach.

No paid quit communities, health-influencer sponsorships, stakes/prize pool or affiliate tobacco/vape tie-in. No scraping, inference or public label of who smokes. Ask permission before any free newsletter mention; zero until accepted. Don't use shock imagery, humiliation or medical scare tactics to acquire accounts.
