# SNITCH-SMOKE — Video scripts

Only public screens or authorized fictional test accounts. No real smoking-status disclosure, private photos, friends, money transaction, medical claim or invented quit streak.

## A. Consent-flow demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Who chose to see this?”**

2–12s: show actual public entry and the factual account boundary. “Private features need their own test.”

12–27s: show verified fictional invitation/visibility controls if implemented. “A participant should understand what someone else can see.” If unavailable, show a clearly labelled proposed flow; don't imply it is live.

27–38s: verified fictional decline/leave/correction. “Leaving should be clear before joining.” No irreversible deletion or real invitation needed.

38–50s: current readiness/URL. “Peer accountability project, not a proven treatment. No real user data in this demo.”

## B. Visibility hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Findable shouldn't mean exposed.”**

2–12s: actual discoverability statement/control only after verified, or labelled design question. “Which fields become visible, and to whom?”

12–18s: URL/current access. No friend's identity or smoking label.

## C. Leave hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Can I say no without explaining a private habit?”**

2–12s: fictional invitation decline/leave, verified or explicitly proposed. “Participation should be voluntary.”

12–20s: privacy/scope status. No punishment, forfeited money or public humiliation used as a hook.

## D. Honest-evidence hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A streak screenshot doesn't prove treatment works.”**

2–12s: clearly labelled fictional diagram separating account, check-in and health outcome. “Those are different claims.” No fabricated product streak.

12–20s: public entry and current limits. “First verify a usable private routine.” No medical endorsement or quit-rate claim.
