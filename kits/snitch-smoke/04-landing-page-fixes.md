# SNITCH-SMOKE — Public-entry and readiness fixes

Root→account screen inspected in browser 29 October. No account, habit information, friend invitation, photo, stake/payment or private feature was tested. Auth required is not an outage.

1. **Explain the job before collecting identity.** Actual H1 “No sutta. Full izzat.” CTA “Create account,” supporting line “Make an account so your dosts can find you.” Add a non-sensitive explanation of actual implemented features, exact access/cost and a fictional demo before registration. Proposed heading **“Choose who supports your private check-in.”** Publish only if that flow is verified; otherwise say **“Private pilot in preparation.”**

2. **Make discoverability explicit.** “So your dosts can find you” can expose a sensitive association. Explain who can search, what a profile reveals and whether discovery can be off. Use **“No one is notified or added without your choice.”** only after testing. Don't auto-upload contacts, suggest known smokers or let a public account imply someone's habit.

3. **Publish actual privacy/terms before account creation.** No visible public privacy/terms link was established at the inspected boundary. Owner must supply readable purpose, storage/providers, friend/photo visibility, deletion, access/cost and support terms before asking for a real sensitive-data pilot. This isn't evidence no policy exists anywhere; make it accessible where consent happens. Don't invent encrypted/private guarantees.

4. **Separate metadata from implemented features.** Profile describes money stakes/streaks/photo proof; account screen doesn't verify them. Label unavailable/untested mechanics honestly. Do not market stakes, automatic collection, forfeits or photo accusations until actual rules, opt-in, handling and recovery are clear and appropriate. This month's acquisition doesn't require money or photos.

5. **Keep a lapse from becoming punishment.** Verify decline/leave/block/report, mistaken check-in correction and visibility revocation using fictional accounts. Don't publicly shame, notify bystanders or frame one missed entry as a clinical fact. A supportive routine should permit stopping without an invented penalty or humiliation.

6. **Keep health scope clear.** Add **“Peer accountability, not diagnosis, medication advice or proven treatment. Professional support is separate.”** Public support resources can be linked after current/local verification, without implying partnership. Don't copy a displayed phone number into campaign copy without verifying it, or use unverified outcome statistics.

7. **Check mobile/account errors.** At 390px, test readable password guidance, email confirmation, errors, login return and accessible privacy links. Mask all identities, private activity, images and tokens from replay. Preview **“SNITCH — a private accountability project with consent first.”** No public smoking label, private friend list or stakes amount in screenshots.
