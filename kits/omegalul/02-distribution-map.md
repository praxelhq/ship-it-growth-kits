# OMEGALUL — Distribution map

**Recruit people who want to write, not just scroll.** A lesson clinic with original material is stronger proof than an unauthorized celebrity montage.

| Route | Calendar quantity and permission | Assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Maker feedback | Optional [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) Day 22 after current rule/slot check. | Zero baseline; builder feedback isn't performer demand. | #6 |

Roughly 17–113 gross accounts before overlap under these assumptions. At 40% visit→account, 1,000 needs 2,500 distinct relevant visits, with actual drill completion another step. Replace estimates after two clips; don't count every embedded-video play as signup.

No paid comedy newsletters, open-mic stage, creator endorsement or club partnership assumed. An organizer's free newsletter mention is optional after acceptance, zero until then. Club names on your site don't make distribution partners. Use only your own performance material in external clips; refer people to the official embedded player for the original.

Keep this an adult writing exercise distinct from Rythmic Beats' dance rehearsal, Create or Die's creative routine and c2 game sessions. Ask before inviting the same arts organizer twice in seven days. Suggested Day 18 session is not a booked event; confirm actual date and venue/online format before invitations.
