# OMEGALUL — Video scripts

Use your own original lines and screen recordings of authorized app output. Refer to creator clips through their player; do not download/repost a celebrity montage or pretend a practice score proves real laughs.

## A. First-drill demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“What would you change in this line?”**

2–12s: say your own harmless observation, labelled original demonstration. “I'm trying to make the turn clearer.”

12–25s: open the actual first free lesson after authorized login. “Learn the technique, then try a fresh example.” Don't film a fake signed-in screen if unavailable.

25–40s: enter your original practice and show actual feedback with cuts labelled. “This is automated coaching, not a guarantee a room will laugh.”

40–52s: say the real revision and identify one change. “I can accept or reject the suggestion.”

52–60s: URL and **First lesson free with account · paid Pass optional**. If a step fails, fix before recording a success demonstration.

## B. Setup hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: **“The setup was longer than the point.”**

2–10s: your own original long version and shorter revision. “One detail stayed. The detour went.” Use actual versions, not a fabricated user quote.

10–15s: lesson page/URL. “Practise a technique, not somebody else's routine.”

## C. Judge-limit hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“An AI score isn't a laugh track.”**

2–12s: real feedback on your own line and the reason you kept/rejected a suggestion. “The useful part is a specific revision I understand.”

12–20s: first-lesson boundary/URL. No staged audience laughing or endorsement.

## D. Rights/offer hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“The clips are free. The practice is the paid offer.”**

2–12s: show free Bits/Clips and the actual lesson/Pass distinction. “Creators own their videos. The subscription covers original analysis and drills.”

12–20s: price/renewal note. “One lesson free with account. Pass renews until cancelled.” Don't describe Coming Soon Originals as available.
