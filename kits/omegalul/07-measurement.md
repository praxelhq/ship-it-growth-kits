# OMEGALUL — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real learner accounts once; the lesson entry requires an account. Put **first original drills completed** and **useful deliberate revisions** beside signup. Free embedded-video plays, catalogue browsing and coming-soon interest are separate, not activated accounts.

Private metrics weren't accessed. Record actual baseline; unknown is not zero.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |

| Free first lessons and drills completed | Product status, not private line content |
| Feedback that led to a voluntary revision | Opt-in feedback without collecting writing |

| Real Pass sales, renewals, cancellations/refunds | Verified payment/entitlement records |
| Traffic/course metric/rank/time | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/omegalul) |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding scripts. Mask sign-in emails, private practice answers, feedback/history and payment tokens. Track `account_confirmed`, `first_lesson_opened`, `drill_submitted`, `feedback_delivered`, `revision_attempted`, `return_drill`, `pass_verified`. Status events are enough; don't publish lines without permission.

`02` suggests about 17–113 gross accounts before overlap. At 40% visit→account, 1,000 needs 2,500 qualified distinct visits; first-drill completion reduces activated learners further. Four Shorts don't guarantee those visits. Replace assumptions after two clips and distinguish comedy-fan traffic from learners.

The Pass renews automatically until cancelled; $29.99 is an annual bill, not a monthly rate. Apply actual refund rules rather than promising all renewals refundable. Coming-soon Originals aren't delivered content. Don't sum overlapping course traffic windows or use AI scores, staged laughs or celebrity names as evidence of improved real-world social outcomes.
