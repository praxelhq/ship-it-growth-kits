# First Take — Distribution map

**Lead with student recording demonstrations and opt-in campus trials.** The buyer can recognize the problem in fifteen seconds. A general launch page explains less than an actual stumble-and-pause comparison.

Every range above is an explicitly uncertain planning assumption unless marked as an observation. There is no verified median for a new account. Zero is possible. Replace assumptions with the first three posts and first twenty invitations; record the numerator and denominator rather than copying someone else's conversion rate.

## A month that adds up

The scheduled inputs are four campus sessions, six LinkedIn posts (Days 4,8,13,19,25,29), four Shorts (Days 6,11,20,26), one optional Peerlist launch, one gated Show HN and twenty individual invitations. The scenarios produce roughly 55–140 first users before overlap; use 40–110 as a deduplicated working range. This does not honestly support 1,000. To close the gap, you need an organizer with a much larger recording assignment cohort or substantially better measured clip response. Confirm either before forecasting it.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

For an organizer shared with the other content/learning kits, prefer Days 10–13 for the first recording sessions. Ask again for later follow-ups; do not overlap the Postifyy project-to-first-post invitation in Days 17–20 without the organizer agreeing that the two activities serve different needs.

## Directories, letters and accounts

Peerlist and Show HN are verified routes, subject to the conditions above. No creator newsletter placement was verified, so it is not part of this calendar. The real Teleprompter.com account and product are competitors to learn from, not comment sections to advertise under.

Paid listings, sponsorships, prizes and promoted posts are outside your zero budget. An editor accepting a story is upside, not a scheduled conversion event. When a page's posting rules cannot be read, do not infer permission from another creator's post.
