# First Take — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://getfirsttake.app/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“A teleprompter that listens.”**; the action is **“Try it free”**. The public offer is ten scripts and three video downloads free, with no account; the early unlimited licence is ₹199 / $2 once, not a subscription. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a student with a written introduction and an imminent recording deadline. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Show the actual pause

**Current:** The hero says “A teleprompter that listens.” and the illustration explains a voice-follow flow.

**Write this instead:**

> Your script waits when you pause. Watch a real 20-second recording, then try your own.

**Verify:** Replace or pair the illustration with a real normal-speed pause test. Label tested browser/version; do not simulate a successful recording.

## Fix 2 — Explain free before the first permission

**Current:** The free allowance appears below the hero action.

**Write this instead:**

> Try without an account: 10 scripts and 3 video downloads. We’ll ask for the microphone when you turn on voice follow.

**Verify:** A new visitor should understand the allowance before the permission dialog and see why access is requested.

## Fix 3 — Keep the privacy distinction

**Current:** The page says video stays on the computer; the privacy policy explains browser speech-service processing.

**Write this instead:**

> Your video stays on this device. Voice follow uses your browser’s speech recognition, which may send audio to the browser maker. Prefer steady scroll? You can switch.

**Verify:** Place this near the voice-follow control, not only in the policy. Do not say the whole workflow is offline.

## Fix 4 — Name the output

**Current:** Download formats differ by browser: WebM in Chrome/Edge and MP4 in Safari.

**Write this instead:**

> Download your take: WebM in Chrome or Edge, MP4 in Safari. Play the file once before sending it.

**Verify:** Record a sample on supported devices and confirm the downloaded file opens in the destination the student needs.

## Fix 5 — Make the paid boundary concrete

**Current:** The page advertises ₹199 / $2 once and unlimited scripts/downloads.

**Write this instead:**

> Keep the free trial, or buy unlimited scripts and downloads for the early one-time price of ₹199 / $2. No subscription.

**Verify:** Confirm the live checkout, licence restoration and taxes before promising an exact payable amount in a campaign.

## Fix 6 — Give sharing a clear preview

**Current:** The main benefit needs a pause to be understood, while a bare screenshot hides it.

**Write this instead:**

> First Take — a teleprompter that waits when you pause. Try recording in your browser, no account needed.

**Verify:** Use that title/description with a real legible recording preview. Inspect a phone link preview rather than assuming the social card updates instantly.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
