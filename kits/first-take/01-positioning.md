# First Take — Positioning

**Live:** https://getfirsttake.app/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/first-take

## The thing to face first

Your hardest claim is not the price. It is that the script genuinely waits when the speaker pauses. Show that pause in an uncut recording before asking anyone to trust you. Also keep video privacy separate from speech recognition: your own privacy policy says the browser maker may process microphone audio when voice follow is on.

## Your first person

An MBA student who has to submit a one-minute project or application video this week, has already written the script, and keeps restarting because fixed-speed text runs away. Start with this person rather than professional production teams. She can try the browser flow immediately and judge the pause for herself.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Record your one-minute presentation with a script that waits when you stop speaking.

For a bio or a short reply:

> A voice-following browser teleprompter. Ten scripts and three downloads free.

## Your opening use case

The pause test: paste a sixty-word introduction, speak the first sentence, stop for two seconds, then continue. Let the student see whether the highlight waits and whether the saved video is usable. The first benefit is fewer fights with the scrolling text, not a promise that the video will sound confident or win an interview.

## The real alternative

[Teleprompter.com](https://www.teleprompter.com/pricing) has a free Starter with a watermark and a displayed $19.99/seat monthly Pro plan; its feature list also includes voice-activated scrolling. You cannot claim to have invented the category. Your narrower offer is a low-cost browser flow, local video storage, no-account trial and a one-time early licence. Reading from a second window costs no extra money and remains a useful control in your own side-by-side test.

## What you need to prove

Record one honest test in each browser you recommend. Show the script stopping, recovering after a missed word and downloading a playable file. Publish the tested browser/version beside the clip. If voice recognition does not work for someone, explain the steady-scroll fallback rather than declaring every device supported.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
