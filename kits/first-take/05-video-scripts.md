# First Take — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 60 seconds · homepage and LinkedIn

**First two seconds:** “Watch what happens when I stop talking.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–12s: sample script and face, on-screen “Sample introduction”. | I need to record this introduction. First I paste the script. |
| Middle | 12–38s: speak one sentence, pause for two full seconds, then continue. Text “Pause left in”. | The highlight follows my words. When I pause, the script waits. Now I carry on. |
| Payoff | 38–55s: stop, preview, download, play the file. | I can check the take and save it on this device. I’m not sending the video to First Take. |
| Close | Hold `https://getfirsttake.app/` legibly. | Try a sample script free at getfirsttake.app. |

Keep the pause continuous. Put the browser speech-service caveat in the caption; never use confidential application details.

## B — the problem hook · 20 seconds · YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn

**First two seconds:** “The text ran away again.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–5s: your own fixed-speed sample continues during a pause. | I stop. The script doesn’t. |
| Middle | 5–15s: same sample in First Take waits. | This time my voice sets the pace. |
| Payoff | 15–18s: free allowance on screen. | Ten scripts and three downloads free, no account. |

Use a fair comparison with identical text and no claim that all competing tools lack voice follow.

## C — the honest comparison · 30 seconds · campus organizer follow-up

**First two seconds:** “You can test this without sending me your video.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–8s: open a harmless script in the browser. | Use something you don’t mind reading aloud. |
| Middle | 8–20s: show microphone control and steady-scroll option. | Voice follow uses browser speech recognition. You can choose steady scroll instead. |
| Payoff | 20–27s: download sample locally. | Check the saved file yourself. You don’t need to upload it to me. |
| Close | Hold `https://getfirsttake.app/` legibly. | If the browser loses your place, tell me where. |

A useful privacy explanation is stronger than claiming the microphone never leaves the device.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture a script paste, a two-second pause, a missed-word recovery, the voice/steady-scroll switch, the download menu and a local playback. Keep the same sample script throughout.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
