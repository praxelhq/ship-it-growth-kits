# Linkup — Landing page fixes

Based on [thelinkup.in](https://thelinkup.in/) and `/demo`, checked 29 October. Real member signup/matching/contact weren't completed.

## 1. Resolve access contradiction

The CTA says **“Try LINKUP — No invitation needed”** while the FAQ says invite-only community. The demo explicitly contains five fictional profiles. Use separate actions: **“Try the fictional public demo”** and **“See real-pilot access.”** State the actual membership rule and any price before signup. Do not make demo access imply live member access.

## 2. Keep the headline, explain the evidence

Current headline: **“Big goals need the right people.”**

Suggested support: **“Turn a blocker into a focused help brief. See why a willing, discoverable member may fit, then decide what to send.”** Primary CTA: **“Try a sample help brief.”** Label the sample consistently.

## 3. Make illustrative people unmistakable

The homepage labels profiles illustrative; keep that next to every card and in screenshots. Replacement: **“Fictional demo profile — not a person you can contact.”** A first-message button should not imply a real expert is awaiting the request.

## 4. Verify a real minimum circle

Before promotion of membership, confirm discoverable real profiles, stated help preferences, permission controls, clear match evidence, editable drafts and no automatic contact. Test a request with no suitable match. Copy: **“No appropriate opted-in match yet. Narrow the brief or try later.”** Don't fill the gap with a fictional person.

## 5. Preserve the LinkedIn boundary

The FAQ says LinkedIn sign-in/network access isn't active. Keep **“This beta uses only profiles shared within Linkup with discoverability enabled.”** Don't use a LinkedIn logo or “your network” language that implies imported connections.

## 6. Explain profile visibility and private brief handling

Show who can view a profile, what fields become discoverable, how to stop visibility and how to delete a brief/account. Do not promise encryption/storage behaviour not verified. Keep personal blockers out of replay.

## 7. Check phone request writing

At 390px, test a long brief, empty result, back navigation and copying/editing a message without accidental sending. Preview: **“Linkup — a clearer professional ask, with reasons.”** No guaranteed mentor, job, funding or introduction promise.
