# Linkup — Positioning

**Live:** https://thelinkup.in/ · **Category:** Professional help / introductions · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/linkup

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“Big goals need the right people.”** The CTA says **“Try LINKUP — No invitation needed.”** But the FAQ calls membership invite-only. The [public demo](https://thelinkup.in/demo) resolves part of this: it searches **five fictional profiles**, with browser-local drafts, not real member data. State that distinction before inviting anyone.

Your first real member is an early professional with one specific project blocker and a willingness to help someone else in their own area. Start in one existing alumni/project community where consent and expertise can be checked, not a universal professional network.

**Pitch:** Turn a professional blocker into a clear help brief and a first message to someone who has chosen to be discoverable.

**Short version:** A reason to ask, not another list of names.

## Your wedge

One brief, one evidence-backed match, one editable message. The user chooses whether to contact the person. The site explicitly says LinkedIn sign-in/network access are not active and that it only uses discoverable member profiles. Do not market imported connections, automatic introductions or a real expert behind a fictional card.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) provides a free professional profile and builder network. A normal alumni introduction or free WhatsApp message is another existing route. Linkup must earn interest through clarifying the request and explaining a willing person's relevance, not claiming only it can find helpful professionals.

## The uncomfortable bit

A polished demo with five fictional people doesn't prove a live directory can answer a real request. Before growth, recruit a small, explicitly opted-in circle and confirm what help they offer. Never promise access to someone who did not agree.

The public page/demo were read; member registration, real matching and contact weren't tested. No public pricing was visible, so disclose the actual membership offer before signup. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 confirmed real members, with an actionable brief and a consensual real conversation beside it. Demo sessions are a separate metric, not real membership.
