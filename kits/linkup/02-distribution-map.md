# Linkup — Distribution map

**Build one opted-in help circle before broadcasting.** A thousand seekers and no willing helpers is not a useful network.

| Route | Scheduled action / consent | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Twenty willing alumni/project peers | Invite ten people with a specific help need and ten willing to offer a bounded kind of help; roles may overlap. Confirm discoverability separately. | Up to 20 real members, 5–10 useful briefs; conversations must be observed/voluntarily confirmed. | #1 |
| Two existing alumni/project groups | Admin permission, one invitation each, exact demo-versus-member distinction. No contact-list exports. | 2 × 40 readers × 10–25% join = 8–20 members if access is actually open. | #2 |
| Three LinkedIn posts | Help-brief, relevance-evidence and consent lessons on Days 8, 16, 25 at 12:30 IST. | 3 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 20% member signup = 2–27 members. | #3 |
| Three short demos | Fictional profiles clearly labelled on Days 11, 19, 26. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 20% signup = 1–24 members; demo-only traffic separate. | #4 |
| One existing community organizer | Offer a bounded help-brief workshop, not access to “experts” you haven't recruited. | 10–20 attendees if accepted; 3–8 members assumed. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker-feedback listing once membership/access is clear; no vote requests, current rules checked. | 0–20 members, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No paid networking communities, purchased LinkedIn data, newsletter sponsorships or invented mentors. A club newsletter can share one free invitation only after acceptance, with zero forecast until then. Reply to people asking a relevant public question with actual help; don't drop a generic matching pitch under unrelated posts.

At 20% qualified visitor-to-member, 1,000 needs 5,000 distinct relevant visits. The initial circle is tens, not thousands. Measure whether it solves real requests before widening. Use a different alumni segment from resume/portfolio clinics: this is a help-brief exchange, not a placement promise or a founder funding desk.
