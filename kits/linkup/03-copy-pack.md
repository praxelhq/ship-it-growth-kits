Try the clearly fictional public demo: https://thelinkup.in/demo

## #3 — LinkedIn post

The useful part of a professional introduction is the reason.

Why this person? What can they reasonably help with? What is the smallest request they can say yes or no to?

That's the Linkup test: turn a blocker into a clear brief and show the evidence behind a suggested willing member. The public demo uses five fictional profiles; it is not proof of a live expert network.

I'm building a small consent-based circle before claiming scale. No LinkedIn graph import or automatic outreach. https://thelinkup.in/
