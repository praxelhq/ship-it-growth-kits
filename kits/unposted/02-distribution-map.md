## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. Google-account creation is the signup measure; a landing visit or free allowance view is not. Keep finished designs and saved/exported designs beside that headline, and never count the same design saved twice as two users. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Prefer Days 17–20 for a shared adult creative invitation. Coordinate with Comic World and Woof Chapter: this session is one photo/story design, not personal comic drawing or pet-story prototyping.

## Directories, letters and accounts

Peerlist is the verified optional builder directory. Adobe Express is a real alternative, not a promotional partner. No Instagram theme page, Pinterest board owner or creator newsletter has agreed to distribute the product. No newsletter placement, editorial acceptance or external community promotion permission has been established; do not count one in the forecast.

Paid listings, sponsorships, prizes and promoted posts are outside your zero budget. An editor accepting a story is upside, not a scheduled conversion event. When a page's posting rules cannot be read, do not infer permission from another creator's post.
