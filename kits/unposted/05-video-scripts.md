# Unposted — Video scripts

Record with your own photo and a permitted reference. Only show an edit/save you have actually completed. The public first-use route requires Google sign-in; hide account details. Do not stage an Instagram auto-post or a coming-soon Reel feature.

## A. One photo, one story — 50 seconds

**Use:** homepage, creative-session invitation, Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Your own unused trip photo. Text: “Still no story?” | “Still unposted?” |
| 3–15s | Permitted reference and photo in the real editor. | “In Unposted, I choose a reference I can use, then add my own photo.” |
| 15–32s | Actual edit and Ta-da control. | “Before making a new design, I check the allowance. Three stories are free each day.” |
| 32–45s | Real saved result. | “Now I review and save it. Saving this same design again is free.” |
| 45–50s | Editor route. | “Try one still-image story at unposted.studio.” |

## B. Save one — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short or Reel.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Owned photo, then the reference. | “One photo. One good design.” |
| 3–13s | Real edit at normal speed. | “Add your photo, review the result and decide whether you want to save it.” |
| 13–17s | Saved result and free-story label. | “Start with a free story.” |
| 17–20s | URL. | “Google sign-in at unposted.studio.” |

## C. Where the allowance goes — 25 seconds

**Use:** maker post and Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Ta-da and actual allowance. | “Ta-da makes a new design.” |
| 4–15s | Actual allowance change, only if verified. | “That uses the relevant free allowance or credits. Stories, posts and carousels have different rules.” |
| 15–22s | Resave the same design, only after testing. | “Saving this same design again is free.” |
| 22–25s | URL. | “Know the boundary before you create.” |

If an allowance change or resave has not been tested, record the visible terms/control explanation instead and label it as an explanation. Do not pretend an untested action succeeded. Keep future Reels, LinkedIn posts and Brand Kit out of the demonstrated feature set.
