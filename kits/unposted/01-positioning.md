# Unposted — Positioning

**Live:** https://unposted.studio/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/unposted

## The thing to face first

Your live first-use route asks for Google sign-in, and credits are spent when a new design is made with Ta-da. Make those boundaries visible before someone adds a photo. Reels, LinkedIn posts and Brand Kit are coming soon, not current outputs. Inspiration also needs permission: your terms say only post designs you have the right to share.

## What is actually on your page

Checked 29 October 2026: **“your photos are UNPOSTED in your camera roll.”**. The next explanation is a photo-to-story/post/carousel editor using a chosen inspiration or trend. The action is **“Start free”**. Current terms give three free stories daily, two initial single posts and two initial carousels. Extra stories are 10 for ₹49; a post ₹19 or 10 for ₹99; a carousel ₹29 or 10 for ₹149. Prices include applicable taxes. These are observations from [your public product page](https://unposted.studio/), not a completed purchase or a promise that every authenticated flow works.

The CTA goes to /#/app, which shows Google sign-in and says Pinterest can be connected later. Terms say credits are used on Ta-da for a new design and saving the same design again is free. Generation, export, Pinterest authorization and Instagram publishing were not tested. “Straight to Instagram” should not be turned into an independently verified auto-posting claim.

## Your first person

An adult college student with one photo from a recent trip or event who wants a presentable story without learning a full editor. They have their own photo and a reference they are allowed to use, not a plan to copy a creator’s protected design wholesale.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Turn one photo you own into a story design, with the free allowance and credit spend clear before you create.

For a bio or a short reply:

> Your photo into a story design. Three free stories a day.

## Your opening use case

Make one story with your own photo and an owned or licensed reference. Show the change, inspect the output and save it. The first useful result is a design someone actually wants to keep, not a Pinterest connection or a paid credit pack.

## The real alternative

[Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing) is a real template/image-editor alternative; its current price table was not reliably extracted here, so no paid amount is invented. Your phone’s existing photo/story-editing tools require no separate Unposted purchase for a manual edit. Unposted must earn use through its particular reference-to-photo workflow, not an unsupported claim that other editors are costly or cannot do templates.

## What you need to prove

Record a real edit using your own photo and a reference you have rights to use. Show the free allowance, Ta-da spend and saved result. Do not imply a coming-soon video feature works or show a staged Instagram publication as a completed integration test.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
