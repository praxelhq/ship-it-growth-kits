# Unposted — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://unposted.studio/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“your photos are UNPOSTED in your camera roll.”**; the action is **“Start free”**. Current terms give three free stories daily, two initial single posts and two initial carousels. Extra stories are 10 for ₹49; a post ₹19 or 10 for ₹99; a carousel ₹29 or 10 for ₹149. Prices include applicable taxes. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an adult choosing whether to sign in and spend a free design allowance. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — State sign-in before the click

**Current:** The hero says “Start free”; /#/app asks for a Google account.

**Write this instead:**

> Start with Google. Your new account includes the free allowances shown here.

**Verify:** Test the real consent scope and explain it accurately; do not promise no account.

## Fix 2 — Show spend at the decision

**Current:** Terms say Ta-da uses credits on a new design.

**Write this instead:**

> Creating this new design uses one story allowance. Saving this same design again is free.

**Verify:** Use the actual remaining balance/type before generation, not a surprise after upload.

## Fix 3 — Keep live and future outputs separate

**Current:** Reels, LinkedIn posts and Brand Kit are labelled coming soon.

**Write this instead:**

> Available now: the still-image outputs listed in your editor. Reels and the other marked features are not live yet.

**Verify:** Do not use a future-feature animation as product proof.

## Fix 4 — Clarify reference permission

**Current:** The page suggests screenshots and Pinterest inspiration; terms require rights to share the design.

**Write this instead:**

> Use your own or permissioned design reference. Review the result before posting; inspiration is not a licence to copy another creator’s work.

**Verify:** Make this visible when a reference is chosen, not only in terms.

## Fix 5 — Explain the daily reset

**Current:** Terms say three free stories reset at midnight India time.

**Write this instead:**

> Three free stories each day, resetting at midnight IST. Other output types have their own allowance and prices.

**Verify:** Do not imply three free carousels daily or apply one output’s allowance to another.

## Fix 6 — Show an actual saved result

**Current:** “Straight to Instagram” can be read as automatic publishing.

**Write this instead:**

> Create, review and save your design. Use the actual sharing route available on your device.

**Verify:** Test export/share separately and describe only the integration that really works.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
