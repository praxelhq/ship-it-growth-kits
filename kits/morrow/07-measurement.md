# morrow — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, transparently interpreted as **distinct new meaningful session starts** for the no-account prototype under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count a real listening start or the beginning of the visualization task, not a library page view. Report optional accounts separately and do not add both paths together as different people.

Activation is a **completed real experience**: a listening session actually played meaningfully or a completed local visualization result. Define a consistent playback/completion threshold after testing the real player. Navigation to a detail screen, synthetic pre-filled content, a loading state or an AI consent click is not activation.

## Day 0

Record actual starts/completions and later-day return counts from your own system; these private values were unavailable during research. Keep prototype-path failures visible. Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on appropriate public surfaces, excluding private-answer/result content from replay. No goals, feelings, personal text, audio or AI prompts in analytics labels.

Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/morrow) reading, date window and rank. Minimum aggregate events are `session_started`, `local_flow_completed`, `listening_completed`, `flow_failed` and `later_day_returned`. Use privacy-preserving counts and state anonymous deduplication limits instead of inventing exact identities.

## Honest funnel and reviews

Ten private invitations, four owned-feed posts, six Shorts and one optional ten-person demo support tens of sessions under uncertain assumptions. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 relevant visits; at 1% view→visit, 200,000 views. This is a gap to test, not a promise that the calendar reaches it.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Actual completed local/listening paths and prototype comprehension|If the finish is broken or the AI choice unclear, repair before more invitations.|
|Day 14|First completion and later voluntary returns|If people like the atmosphere but can't name a next action, simplify the ending.|
|Day 21|Wizard abandonment, narrator failures and optional-path differences|Don't hide an incomplete AI/audio route behind aggregate homepage visits.|
|Day 30|New starters, completed experiences, returners, actual reach and unresolved limits|State the gap to 1,000 without claiming health, career or financial outcomes.|

Keep weekly aggregate rows with source and hours. A synthetic goal or generated demo is not a customer's life change; a willing tester's feedback is not a testimonial unless separately authorized.
