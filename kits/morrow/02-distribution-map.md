**Show one modest, complete reflective interaction.** Prototype honesty and a clear next action matter more than dreamy lifestyle imagery.

Medito's [official product explanation](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app) is a useful comparison for clear listening access. Don't advertise on its users' personal stories. An organizer's own opt-in bulletin can share #4 if accepted; no external newsletter placement is assumed. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional once a complete local experience works, using a personal account and no direct upvote requests.

This plan supports tens of sessions, not a proven 1,000. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. At 1% video-view→visit, that would need 200,000 views; six clips do not establish that. Measure whether someone returns by choice and takes an actual small step, without demanding they share private content.
