# morrow — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 with rendered fetch and live browser navigation: home→`#create` first step, `#library` and a session detail. No answers were entered; no narration, AI or completed result was tested.

1. **Keep the real headline but give it a small first action.** Current “Meet the person you’re becoming” is evocative. Add **“Take a short pause. Put one direction into words. Choose a small next step.”** Offer **“Try a short listening session”** as a low-effort first action, then **“Create my visualisation.”** Test whether the library actually plays before making listening the main promise.

2. **Explain the eight-step effort before the CTA.** Current “Create my visualisation” opens step 1 of 8. Add **“Eight short steps. Use a local template without AI, or choose optional AI after reading what it sends.”** Don't invent a completion time; measure it with three fresh testers first.

3. **Bring local/AI consent forward.** The first step already says answers stay on-device and use a local template without consent. Repeat near the CTA and explain what changes when AI is enabled, including provider/content handling. Don't call the whole product offline or private-by-encryption from one local-mode statement.

4. **Make narrator limits visible before listening.** Session detail says AI narrator when configured or device voice. Use **“Prototype narration: your device voice, or configured AI narration. Read the text preview first.”** Do not market session-length labels as verified audio durations or a human-recorded library. Provide readable text if audio isn't available.

5. **Keep prototype status near the action.** Current footer says “Prototype experience.” Move a short label nearer the first CTA: **“Prototype — we're testing the complete flow and making limits clear.”** Show which path works today and an honest recovery if generation fails. Never turn a loading animation into proof a result was created.

6. **End with agency, not a guaranteed future.** The page has career, confidence and abundance categories. Result copy should say **“What is one small step you choose next?”** rather than promise a job, income, treatment or transformation. No leaderboard or compulsory positive streak is needed for this job.

7. **Preview and phone checks.** Title **“Morrow — a quiet pause and a small next step”**; description **“A reflection prototype with short sessions and an optional personal-visualisation flow. AI choice and current limits are explained.”** Test keyboard, back/save-for-later, local recovery, text size and muted/available audio on a phone. The observed navigation worked, but end-to-end generation and mobile behavior remain untested here.
