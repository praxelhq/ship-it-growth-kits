# morrow — Video scripts

Use your own voice and neutral synthetic answers. No real private goal, health record, borrowed lifestyle footage or promise of a changed life.

## A. One modest experience — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “A future-self exercise shouldn't promise your future.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Actual homepage and prototype label|“Morrow is a small reflection prototype.”|
|10–25s|Library or first-step choice, synthetic example labelled|“Start with a short session, or put one direction into your own words.”|
|25–40s|Actual local/AI choice|“AI is optional. Read what changes before choosing it.”|
|40–52s|Actual completed local result, only after testing|“The useful finish is a small next action, not a guaranteed outcome.”|
|52–60s|URL and clear limitation|“Try the path that works today. Tell me where it gets vague.”|

If the result doesn't complete, show the blocker and call it a build update. Don't fake a generated result or imply device narration is a studio recording.

## B. Eight steps, honestly — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Tell me the effort before I click.” Show the real CTA, step 1-of-8 indicator and save-for-later option. Say: “This is an eight-step flow. It should say that before you begin.” Use a design lesson, not a made-up completion-time claim.

## C. Local first — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “AI should be a choice, not a surprise.” Show the actual local-template disclosure, then the opt-in explanation without enabling it. Say: “Read what is sent and what stays here. You don't need to reveal a real personal goal to test this.” No encryption or offline claim beyond the verified scope.

## D. Text still works — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “If the narrator isn't ready, don't hide it.” Show session text and the actual narrator disclosure. Say: “This prototype can use a device voice or configured AI. The text and limitations should remain visible.” Do not play loud audio during filming setup or use an unverified voice identity.
