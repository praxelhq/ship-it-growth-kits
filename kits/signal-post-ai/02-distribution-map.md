# Signal-Post-AI — Distribution map

**Lead with useful before/after post plans on your own LinkedIn and a small, approved working session.** A wishlist launch is weaker than letting someone inspect `/try` immediately. Do not add unverified groups to this plan.

The ranges are explicitly uncertain planning assumptions, not industry benchmarks. Zero reach is possible. Count actual tagged visits after the first three posts and revise the table.

[LinkedIn's policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) prohibits unauthorized automation and artificial engagement. A manual working session is not permission to scrape attendee profiles or post for them. People can leave the session with a draft and decide never to publish it.

## Who to reply to

Use actual posts from your own internship supervisor's public professional account, your club's page and colleagues who discuss the field you understand. Choose the people yourself from existing relationships; this kit does not invent named accounts or promise access. Add a useful distinction about audience or evidence. No promotional link under another person's achievement post.

## Shared-campus timing

Your primary invitation window is **Days 10–12**. Lynkr uses Days 4–6, Postifyy uses Days 17–20, and TwoCents uses Days 24–26. Actual dates go into the organizer's calendar. Do not send a second product invitation to the same organization in the same week.

## The honest ceiling

Two accepted sessions at about seven activations, five warm-peer activations and a small directory/post response might yield **20–50 completed first plans**. A stronger creator or club partnership could change that, but it is not already secured. The 1,000 target therefore requires substantially more qualified distribution. Ask successful organizers for one introduction and earn a repeatable session before forecasting a campus network you do not have.
