# Signal-Post-AI — Copy pack

Use real observations and primary sources you have read. These drafts make no claim that you ran a study or already produced customer results. Keep the live demo and wishlist clearly separate.

## 1. LinkedIn: the audience changes the post

> A topic is not a post yet.
>
> Take “our team changed its onboarding.” A new joiner needs to know what got easier. A team lead needs to know which decision removed confusion. A founder might care about what we stopped doing.
>
> Same event. Three different points. Writing five captions before choosing the reader just produces five versions of the wrong post.
>
> I'm building SignalPost AI to work through the audience, point, tone, length and visual before you publish. You can inspect the public try page here: https://signalpostai.com/try.
>
> Which decision slows you down most: what to say, how to say it, or whether it needs an image?

Use a synthetic onboarding example unless it is genuinely your own experience.

## 2. Value-first comment

> I would separate the announcement from the implication for one reader. What would a person in that role do differently after reading it? That usually gives you a more useful opening than restating the headline.

Adapt it to a relevant post; do not paste the same comment repeatedly. No link unless asked, and do not claim expertise you don't have.

## 3. Organizer invitation

> Could I run a free 30-minute writing session for a small group? Everyone brings one non-confidential work observation. We turn it into a post plan for a specific reader, choose a format, and check which claims need evidence. I build SignalPost AI, so I'll disclose that and show the tool, but nobody has to sign up or publish. May I send you the outline?

## 4. Approved group broadcast

> Have a topic for LinkedIn but no clear point yet? I'm hosting a small working session on turning one observation into an audience, angle and format. Bring something from your own work that you can discuss publicly. We'll check the claims before drafting; no pressure to publish. The free try page is https://signalpostai.com/try. Reply if you're interested and I'll confirm the actual session details directly.

Send only after approval. The wishlist on the homepage is for product updates; say so if you mention it.

## 5. Warm test request and one follow-up

> You said deciding what to post takes longer than writing it. Would you try SignalPost's public post-plan form on one topic you already know? I'd like to see whether the angle and visual suggestion fit your intended reader. Honest criticism is more useful than a nice comment. Here's the try page if you'd like it: https://signalpostai.com/try.

After seven days, once:

> One follow-up on the post-plan test. If it's not useful right now, no problem; I won't keep nudging. If you tried it, the most helpful thing to know is which suggestion you rejected and why.

## 6. Peerlist launch copy

**Tagline:** Decide the angle, tone and format before writing your next post.

> SignalPost AI starts with a topic, an audience and your objective. It suggests a hook, key points, draft and visual direction. The public try flow and the update wishlist are separate. I'd like feedback on whether the plan makes a useful choice or just gives you more generic options. Please verify facts before publishing, especially when your topic is a current event.

## 7. Product Hunt maker comment

> I built SignalPost because a blank caption box hides several decisions: who am I writing to, what's my point and what format helps? This is an early product. The useful test is whether you can explain why you'd keep or reject the suggested angle. It does not guarantee engagement or verify a news claim for you. Try the actual generator rather than treating a wishlist signup as product access.

Use this only after a successful fresh-user generation test and an appropriate launch slot.

## 8. Editor pitch

**Subject:** A free worksheet for choosing a post angle

> I wrote a short worksheet your members could use before drafting a professional post: choose the reader, write the one takeaway, list the evidence, then pick the format. Would it fit a future edition? I can send the full worksheet with a clearly labelled worked example and a brief disclosure that I build SignalPost AI. No paid feature or subscriber list needed.

For repeat posts change the actual problem: a project lesson, an industry announcement, a mistake or a useful resource. Do not merely swap the headline and repeat the sales pitch. Share output failures honestly and with permission; never paste someone else's private work into a public example.
