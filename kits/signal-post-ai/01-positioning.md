# Signal-Post-AI — Positioning

**Live:** https://signalpostai.com/ · **Category:** AI writing · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/signal-post-ai

**You have a public try page and a wishlist on the same landing page. Make the working product the first destination.** On 29 October the headline is “Stop staring at a blank box. Post what actually gets noticed.” The “Try it free” link goes to `/try`; “Add me to the wishlist” submits a separate form. The try page describes generating a hook, key points, a full draft and visual idea. No generation was submitted for this audit, so successful output and wishlist delivery still need fresh-user tests.

## Start with one person and one decision

Your first user is an early-career professional who has read an industry announcement and has a real opinion, but cannot decide whether to write a short observation, an explanatory post or a visual. They don't need another pile of captions. They need to make four choices: audience, point, tone and format.

Start with students in internships or their first job who can explain one work lesson without confidential details. Avoid selling generic “viral posts,” political hot takes or instant expertise in fields the user does not understand. The site's budget-announcement example is an angle demonstration, not evidence the product verifies news or financial claims.

## Your pitch

> Bring one topic and a point you actually believe. SignalPost turns it into a post plan: hook, draft, tone, length and a visual idea.

Bio version: **From “I should post about this” to one clear post plan.**

The wedge is deciding what to say before polishing a paragraph. Show the same topic aimed at two different audiences, and explain why the plan changes. A founder addressing customers and a student addressing recruiters should not get the same opinion in different adjectives.

## What your alternatives already do

[ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) offers a free general-purpose writing route. [Grammarly Free](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) is $0/month for writing assistance, including grammar and tone features. A notes app and a friend are also credible zero-cost alternatives. Do not sell “AI can write a caption”; those options already cover that.

Your difference must be a visible decision trail: “This is the audience; this is the point; this visual helps because…” If the output could equally fit another person's career and industry, the personal-voice promise has not been earned.

The checked page says joining is free and early wishlist members get founding pricing. **No paid amount is published in the checked page.** Don't invent a launch discount, trial allowance, paid plan or release date. Keep the 1,000-consumer target and report successful first post plans beside first-use sessions. Wishlist emails are a separate measure, not evidence of product use.

## Your honest weakness

“Gets noticed” implies an outcome you do not control. The product can help someone form a clearer point; it cannot promise impressions, recruiter interest or news accuracy. Lead with the plan a user can inspect and reject. For a current event, the user supplies and checks the primary source before generating. Never manufacture an opinion from a headline the user has not read.

Lynkr serves factual one-person outreach; Postifyy starts from a voice note; TwoCents helps comments. Your month is the **topic-to-angle-and-format** job. Keep that distinction in every demo and campus invitation.
