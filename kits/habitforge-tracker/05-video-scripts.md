# HabitForge Tracker — Video scripts

Use your own ordinary habits, actual save state and real dates. Screen recording, voiceover and hard cuts are enough. No invented telemetry, streaks or customer charts.

## A. A real ledger starts empty—60 seconds

**Where:** own profiles/private cohort. **Hook:** “A new account should not have a success story.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Actual empty ledger. | “A new account should not have a success story. Mine starts empty.” |
| 8–22 s | Actual access path, chosen ordinary habits. | “I enter through the stated access route and choose what I want to record.” |
| 22–35 s | Log a real day and note. | “This is what I actually did. The denominator and missing entries stay clear.” |
| 35–48 s | Leave/return; show actual storage status. | “Now I check what really saved. A sync label is not enough.” |
| 48–60 s | Free/paid distinction and URL. | “Daily tracker first, optional insights later. HabitForge.” |

Do not record a broad-access claim until an unfamiliar account passes. If sync is local-only, say so.

## B. Missing is not done—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “No entry does not mean complete.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Actual unlogged day. | “No entry does not mean complete.” |
| 6–14 s | Compare actual partial/completed states. | “The useful chart keeps those different. I want an honest record, not a prettier streak.” |
| 14–20 s | URL, limited-job line. | “That is the first HabitForge test.” |

## C. Where did it save?—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Saved where?”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Actual storage label. | “Saved where? Browser and account are different promises.” |
| 7–18 s | Real refresh/return test, private data hidden. | “I test the current release instead of trusting a success toast. These are the actual limits.” |
| 18–25 s | Clear mode label and URL. | “Read the boundary before relying on the ledger.” |

## D. Paid insights later—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Don't buy a chart before you have entries.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Free tracker/empty account. | “Don't buy a chart before you have entries.” |
| 6–14 s | Actual pricing/free-versus-paid distinction. | “The tracker is the first job.₹149 one-time insights are optional under the stated terms.” |
| 14–20 s | Real week record if it exists. | “Judge the record before the metaphor.” |

Six owned posts are scheduled. Reuse setup footage, but each outcome needs a real later date. Never present a sample 75% dashboard as customer retention.
