# HabitForge Tracker — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.habitforgetracker.in/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/habitforge-tracker

**Resolve who can enter before asking strangers to join.** Your home page says access is limited to authorized Google accounts; pricing says the tracker is free for everyone forever. Those are different promises. The site is live, not proven down. Do not run a broad launch until an unfamiliar account can use the promised free tracker or the private-pilot boundary is explicit.

The [actual headline](https://www.habitforgetracker.in/) is “Forge your fate.” It asks the visitor to sign in with Google and offers “See the forge plans.” The [pricing page](https://www.habitforgetracker.in/pricing.html) offers Kindling at ₹0 and Crucible insights at ₹149 once, lifetime. Public home, plans, tracker and locked dashboard were read on 29 October 2026. Authentication, storage persistence and purchase were not tested.

## Your first person

An adult student builder in a small project/study cohort, who likes a 90-day campaign metaphor and wants an honest daily ledger. They are willing to choose a few real habits and record misses as well as completions. They do not need military-sounding labels to promise a new identity.

Start with a private group only if that is the actual release state. Make authorization clear and onboard people individually. Do not sell paid insights to a person who cannot reach their own free tracker.

## Pitch

> Keep an honest 90-day ledger for the habits you chose, with daily notes and optional paid insights.

Short version:

> Your habits, an honest daily ledger.

The wedge is the first seven days of real entries: choose habits, log one actual day, return independently, record a miss without hiding it. The 90-day frame is a plan, not a 90-day result you can claim after launch week.

## Actual offer and alternatives

Free Kindling advertises unlimited habits across four pillars, daily notes, a 90-day campaign, tour/reset controls. Crucible advertises dashboard patterns, weekly protocol and heatmap insights for ₹149 once. The price page mentions a seven-day refund policy. Verify final tax/payment/entitlement before quoting an all-in purchase or guaranteed access.

## The second trust problem

The public tracker text shows both browser-local storage and sync notices mentioning external systems. The dashboard also contains a locked state and empty-ledger explanation. Public text alone cannot establish what actually persists or syncs. Test the exact current release and label sample/placeholder telemetry before promoting cross-device reliability.

Keep **1,000 signups** as the cohort target, with first-day loggers and independent later-day loggers beside it. A restricted pilot cannot honestly promise that count. `02` and `07` make access/capacity an explicit gate rather than inventing demand.
