No productivity subreddit/Discord rules or newsletter editor route were verified. None is scheduled. If your actual cohort organizer has an opted-in bulletin, ask whether copy#2 fits and count no reach until it runs. Do not invent a campus club or paid promotional slot.

## Composition and target gap

Two sessions, twenty personal invitations, six posts and one listing model **28–104 accounts before overlap**. This assumes the product is usable by the intended people. If authorization remains restricted to a smaller pilot, its real capacity is the maximum possible account count until the gate changes; record that capacity rather than assuming 1,000.

At assumed 25% visit-to-account, 1,000 accounts need 4,000 qualified unique visitors. Expansion requires a useful real-week ledger and voluntary organizer introductions. Do not count access requests, purchases or sample dashboard numbers as users.

Your cohort organizer window is Days 24–27, after Terms Apply, MakeHabit and Ughfix's distinct exercises. Use separate contacts and actual dates; leave seven days between primary invitations to the same organization. Do not overwhelm a single study group with four productivity tools.
