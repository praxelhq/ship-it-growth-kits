# The First Story — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Free discovery uses the policy's session equivalent: **one person's first meaningful name-discovery session**, such as opening an actual entry. Report **source-context opens** and **first saved shortlist** beside it. Where an account is required for saving, record confirmed accounts separately and don't add overlapping counts.

Private totals weren't accessed. Record actual Day-0 values, not zero.

Check existing Clarity before adding it. Mask account/contact fields and private shortlist notes. Events: `entry_opened`, `source_opened`, `name_saved`, `shortlist_reopened`, `compare_used`, `pass_preview_opened`. A Pass preview is interest, not a purchase.

| Checkpoint | What to learn | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Can five willing readers inspect a source and save a name? | Fix the first blocked step or unclear free gate. |

| Day 30 | Actual first sessions against 1,000; sources/shortlists beside it | Report honest shortfall and repeatable channel. |

At 50% visitor-to-entry, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. The small scheduled tests may yield tens or low hundreds; replace rates after three clips. Repeated opens by one family do not become new people automatically.

The ₹499/30-day Pass is preview-only and payment is not available. Do not report preview clicks or test transactions as revenue. Course 24-hour readings overlap and are not cumulative users. No metric establishes that choosing a name determines personality, destiny or success.
