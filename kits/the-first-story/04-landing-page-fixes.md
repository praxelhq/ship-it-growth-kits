# The First Story — Landing page fixes

Based on the [homepage](https://thefirststory.live/), `/discover` and `/premium`, checked 29 October. Save/account/payment actions were not completed.

## 1. Make Pass availability consistent

The homepage says unlock deeper material for **₹499/30 days**. The Pass page says **“Preview only. Payment will be available in a future release.”** Put the preview limitation on every Pass invitation: **“Preview the planned Pass — not available to purchase yet.”** Free discovery should stay clearly open. Do not count clicks or interest as purchases.

## 2. Keep the story promise, sharpen the first action

Current headline: **“What if choosing a name meant discovering a story?”** CTA: **“Explore Names.”**

Suggested support: **“Explore the language, script and source behind an Indian name, then keep the ones that matter to you.”** Primary CTA: **“Explore a name and its source.”** Secondary: **“See how review labels work.”** Don't make visitors infer what the archive contains.

## 3. Explain review labels before they imply equal certainty

The archive shows both “Source reviewed” and “Editorial review.” Add a brief key with the actual process and remaining uncertainty. Suggested wording: **“A source can support a spelling or meaning without proving every cultural association. Open the reference to see what it establishes.”** Avoid a blanket “all stories verified” claim.

## 4. Preserve image provenance

Current entries label images artistic representations, not historical depictions. Keep that beside the image, including on share cards. Don't let a copied social preview lose the caveat and imply a real portrait or artifact.

## 5. Test the shortlisting job

Open a name, inspect source, save it, compare with another permitted entry, reopen the shortlist. Check what requires an account and which tools are genuinely free. If save/compare is unavailable, say so before the button rather than letting the visitor discover a surprise gate.

## 6. Read native scripts on a phone

At 390px, test long names, pronunciation aids, scripts, filters, source links and back navigation. Don't reduce the native-script name to a decorative image with no text alternative. Preview: **“The First Story — discover the source behind a name.”** No destiny, health or guaranteed-personality promise.
