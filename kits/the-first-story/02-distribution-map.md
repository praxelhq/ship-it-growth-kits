# The First Story — Distribution map

**Lead with source-backed name stories, then an actual family shortlist.** A shareable card should invite reading, not claim a lucky name guarantees an outcome.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing family shortlist-makers | Parents-to-be or people who asked for name context; no unsolicited pregnancy targeting or personal-detail collection. | 10 trials, 5–10 source/shortlist activations if useful. | #1 |
| Three existing family/language groups | Ask admins for one useful story invitation each. No religious authority or naming-pressure claim. | 3 × 30 relevant readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | One name's source/context, native-script reading and honest review labels on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–120 sessions. | #3 |
| Four [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/) pins | Owned art, source-labelled name stories and product link on Days 9, 15, 22, 27. No copied historical image claim. | 0–40 visits / 0–20 sessions this month; slow uncertain search route. | #4 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | A thoughtful product/source-quality lesson on Days 11/24. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–24 sessions. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker feedback after discover/save tests; check current slot/rules, no vote requests. | 0–20 sessions, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No unverified parenting subreddit, Discord or newsletter is assumed. Ask an existing language/reading-club organizer for one free mention only after source quality is demonstrable; zero forecast until accepted. Do not harvest expectant-parent contact lists or message strangers about pregnancy.

At 50% visit-to-meaningful-discovery, 1,000 needs 2,000 relevant unique visits. The initial experiments may yield tens or low hundreds. Replace rates after three clips; don't multiply every family forward as a guaranteed new account. Coordinate Days 17–20 for your family shortlisting tests, using different contacts from the cohort's story-gift products.
