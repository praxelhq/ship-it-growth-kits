# The Docket Dispatch — Video scripts

Use original screen capture and source-reading explanation. No film clips, private applications, invented open calls, awards or credentials. If subscription remains unconnected, say prototype; don't film a pretend successful checkout.

## A. Source-check demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“Can this project actually apply?”**

2–12s: show an illustrative Docket card with its sample label. “This is format, not a live funding recommendation.”

12–30s: open one actual official source chosen and checked immediately before recording. “Find the project stage, applicant geography and deadline/time zone.” If there is no current call, explain that rather than inventing one.

30–45s: compare stated facts with the draft listing. “Unknown stays unknown. The checked date matters.”

45–55s: show publication readiness status, not an email submission. “The newsletter needs a working signup and delivery before subscribers are invited.”

55–60s: URL and **Verify the funder before applying · no funding guarantee**.

## B. Deadline hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“The date on a sample isn't a deadline to apply.”**

2–12s: sample label beside a realistic-looking card. “Open the actual funder's page. Check the current round and time zone.” Don't read the illustrative date aloud as current.

12–18s: official-source/checked-date fields and URL.

## C. Eligibility hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“US audience doesn't mean every US project qualifies.”**

2–12s: a fictional project-stage checklist, clearly labelled exercise. “Lead applicant, stage, length and other restrictions depend on the funder.”

12–20s: actual source-reading process. “A digest narrows research. It doesn't decide eligibility.”

## D. Offer-honesty hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A form can look live without reaching the publication.”**

2–12s: show the current privacy's local-only statement without entering personal data. “This status has to match the subscribe button.”

12–20s: correct current availability. If the service is later connected, demonstrate authorized confirmation/delivery and update the script; don't leave the old caveat or invent past issues.
