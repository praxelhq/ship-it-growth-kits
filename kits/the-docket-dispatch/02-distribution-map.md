# The Docket Dispatch — Distribution map

**Prepare one trustworthy issue, then ask relevant filmmakers to inspect it.** Public subscriber acquisition waits until actual list confirmation, delivery and offer availability are verified.

Approximately 15–110 gross signups before overlap if all readiness gates pass. With 30% visitor→confirmed signup, 1,000 needs about 3,334 distinct qualified visits. If the only offer is paid, measure its actual conversion separately; don't assume free-interest rates apply. Twenty-source claims don't establish twenty partners or a reach multiplier.

No paid filmmaking newsletters, professional membership fees, scraped filmmaker emails or open subreddit permission. Existing groups require admin approval and relevant actual US eligibility; don't cross-post the same pitch everywhere. A free newsletter mention has zero forecast until accepted.

Coordinate a different film-production organizer from creative-habit, comedy and game groups. Monthly cadence must not imply continuous deadline coverage. A call appearing after the issue may close before the next; make that limitation visible rather than promise no deadline will ever be missed.
