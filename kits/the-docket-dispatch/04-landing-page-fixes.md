Actual listings need direct official links, not a promise that the useful evidence arrives only after payment.

6. **Make uncertainty an editorial field.** Add official deadline/time zone, project stage, residency/lead applicant criteria, source-check date and **“Not stated by the funder”** where needed. Monthly publication can't promise every change arrives in time. Reader must check the primary source before applying; no funding or eligibility guarantee.
