## 3. Own LinkedIn post

A scheduled message should still have an owner.

Who is it for? Why should they receive it? Which timezone controls the send? Can it be cancelled? What happens after a bounce or a disconnected inbox?

Those are the checks I am testing in Outreach Scheduler. The public demo uses sample data. It does not prove deliverability, and a numerical health score is not permission to keep sending.

If you manage relevant business correspondence, inspect the queue before connecting a mailbox: https://outreachscheduler.online/
