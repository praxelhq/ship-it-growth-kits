# Outreach Scheduler — Positioning

**Live:** https://outreachscheduler.online/ · **Category:** Business email queue · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/outreach-scheduler

Checked 29 October 2026: [public site](https://outreachscheduler.online/) says **“Outreach Scheduler warms up your sending gradually: 10 emails on day 1, a bit more each day, spaced with random gaps, business hours only.”** Actual CTA **“Try the demo”** is accompanied by **“No inbox needed. Demo mode comes with sample data.”** Public destinations include `/#/login` and `/#/signup`; those render Log in and Create your account respectively. No account, mailbox connection or email was sent. No public price was established.

Start with a small B2B service firm whose owner already has legitimate, relevant business correspondence and wants to inspect a bounded sending queue. Do not recruit someone who wants purchased lists, artificial replies or a way around a provider's restrictions. More email is not the value proposition.

**Pitch: Inspect a business-hours sending queue and its stop conditions before connecting a real inbox.**

Short version: **Know what is queued, when it sends and when it stops.**

The page describes daily growth, random gaps, a score based on bounces/replies, and stops above 5% bounces or below 60 health. These are product rules, not verified deliverability thresholds or proof the next message is appropriate. A high score does not establish consent, inbox placement or provider compliance. Do not automatically increase volume just because the software permits it.

The zero-extra-tool alternative is an owner's existing email client plus a free [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) recording intended recipient, legitimate purpose, due time and response. Outreach Scheduler must earn adoption through understandable queue review, cancellation and safe recovery. It should not sell warming as a guarantee of reputation or a way to manufacture engagement.

Before any live pilot, verify actual mailbox permissions, token storage/revocation, timezone, duplicate prevention, cancellation and stop enforcement with an owned test mailbox and consenting internal recipients. Do not hand over passwords in a message. Demo records prove only that the sample can be inspected. Keep price and real connected-inbox availability unknown until checked.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each needs its own permitted workflow onboarded, independent recent use and privately known owner name/phone. Ten mailboxes, scheduled messages or demo accounts do not become ten firms. If connected sending is not usable safely, report the release gap and zero qualifying live adoption rather than count sample browsing.
