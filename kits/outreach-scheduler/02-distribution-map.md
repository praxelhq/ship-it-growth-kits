# Outreach Scheduler — Distribution map

Lead with queue control for a small service owner. Keep the list separate from LoopLead's research/draft users and FiguredIt's lead-ranking consultancies. A provider reputation claim is not an outreach offer.

| Route | Execution and boundary | Input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing owner-operated B2B service firms | Ask about legitimate correspondence already planned; one personal approach through their actual business channel. No bought recipient list or fake enquiry. | Ten blocks of four firms = 40; #1/#2. |
| Two known operations introductions | Ask a colleague to forward an invitation, without sending mailbox credentials or client contacts. | Days 5 and 16; #4. Upside only. |
| Own LinkedIn | Show a synthetic queue, a cancellation and an honest stop condition, not inbox-placement claims. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Assumed 100–400 impressions and 0–2 relevant visits each, excluded from base. |
| Owner-led sample review | Inspect timezone, recipient purpose and stop/recovery behavior using an owned mailbox only after permissions are clear. | Day 12, conditional; #5. Supports an existing trial. |

Base assumptions: 40 approaches × 40% substantive conversations × 60% permitted own-workflow setup × 70% independent recent use = 6.72, about seven businesses. At 20% conversation, 3.36. The gap to ten is explicit; better actual adoption or accepted introductions must close it. No connected-inbox capability means zero live customers regardless of interest. Blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,12,16,18,23,25,29.

No email-marketing newsletter, operator community or provider partnership was verified. Public profiles can identify a relevant owner but do not grant permission to post commercially in a group. One organizer gets one primary invitation per week across kits. Do not ask communities to send artificial replies or warm each other's accounts. No automated DMs, purchased leads or paid distribution. One relevant follow-up after seven days, then stop; any decline ends contact.

Publish only after the queue, cancellation and provider-permission tests pass. Never treat the product's 10-message starting rule as legal advice, a platform quota or a recommended sending target for every business.
