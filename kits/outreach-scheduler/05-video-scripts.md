# Outreach Scheduler — Video scripts

Use synthetic recipients and an owned test mailbox only. Never film real addresses, message bodies or access tokens. A demo is not an inbox-placement experiment.

## A. Before the send — 65 seconds

0–3 s: synthetic queued message. **“Can you stop this before it sends?”**

3–18 s: enter the actual sample; show its sample label. “First inspect the queue without connecting an inbox.”

18–32 s: show recipient purpose, time and timezone where supported; otherwise annotate your own review sheet separately. “A scheduled message still needs a reason and an owner.”

32–47 s: show actual cancellation or pause behavior after an owned test. If unverified, show the public rule and say the control still needs testing. Never splice in fake success.

47–57 s: inspect the health score and limits. “This signal is not a guarantee of delivery.”

57–65 s: URL. “Review permissions and current terms before a live inbox. outreachscheduler.online.”

## B. Timezone hook — 20 seconds

0–3 s: two clocks beside one queued email. **“Whose business hours?”**

3–13 s: inspect the actual configured timezone and next-send time, or label the missing test. “Check the recipient context and the real schedule.”

13–20 s: sample label and URL. “Read the queue before trusting the timer.”

## C. Stop hook — 25 seconds

0–3 s: paused state. **“Paused is a question to investigate.”**

3–16 s: actual or labelled sample cause/denominator. “Check the failed address, the context and what remains queued.”

16–25 s: safe review step. “A better score does not make an irrelevant message relevant.” Hold URL.

Phone voice and captions are enough. No fabricated replies, provider endorsement, guaranteed reputation improvement or advice to evade restrictions.
