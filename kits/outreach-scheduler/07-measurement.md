# Outreach Scheduler — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, unchanged under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each needs a genuine business, its own permitted workflow onboarded, independent use in the last seven days and privately known owner name/phone. One firm with five inboxes is one customer. Sample browsing, internal recipients and email counts are separate.

Activation: an owner configures a legitimate own-business queue, understands its timing and can cancel/inspect stop behavior after a controlled test. Report assisted setup separately from independent work. Connected sending is a release gate, not an observed achievement in this research.

No private records were accessed. Baselines are unknown until recorded: genuine firms, connected inboxes, own queues, actual cancellations/pauses, failures, independent return use, current plan terms and real payments. Save rank, reading, period and sync from [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/outreach-scheduler); unknown is not zero and rolling readings must not be added together.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a tag. Exclude recipients, message text, credentials, tokens and account screens. Use aggregate events such as sample_opened, permission_reviewed, owned_connection_tested, own_queue_created, cancellation_confirmed, sending_paused and independent_return. A click is not a successful cancellation. Keep private identity checks outside the public repo.

Base guesses: 40 × 40% × 60% × 70% = 6.72 businesses; at 20% conversation, 3.36. Three posts and two introductions are upside only. Replace assumptions after eight and twenty approaches. If the product supports only a sample, qualifying live adoption is zero, regardless of the scenario.

A reply rate, bounce rate or proprietary score cannot establish inbox placement, lawful contact, revenue or provider endorsement. Record real denominators and time windows, not a generic “health improved” case study. No invented sending results or recipient testimonials.
