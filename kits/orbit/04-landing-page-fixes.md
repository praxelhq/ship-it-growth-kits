# Orbit — Landing page fixes

Based on the homepage and actual CTA-to-setup browser view on 29 October. No personal data/profile was submitted.

## 1. Bring prototype limits before profile collection

The setup page explains local-browser storage/no server accounts, but that matters before someone enters email/handle. Put **“Local prototype: profile stays in this browser, no cross-device account or live Instagram connection”** beside **“Set Up Your Creator Profile.”** Explain why email is requested at all if no contact/account service uses it.

## 2. Keep the headline but narrow the promise

Current headline: **“Your people. Your next orbit.”** CTA: **“Set Up Your Creator Profile.”**

Suggested support: **“Explore a prototype for small, relevant draft-feedback circles. Sample activity is illustrative; no live campaigns or connected crew is implied.”** Button: **“Try the local creator-profile demo.”**

## 3. Label every sample consistently

The homepage already says sample activity. Carry **“Fictional demo profile”** and **“Sample brief—not an available campaign”** into the dashboard and screenshots. Don't let later screens erase the caveat and imply a real paid opportunity.

## 4. Remove the dead integration expectation

Setup shows **“Connect Instagram (optional)”** but says it isn't active. Disable or relabel it **“Instagram connection not available in this prototype.”** Manual handle entry is not verification or access to account analytics.

## 5. Test local save and reset

With fictional data, save a profile, reopen on the same browser, try another browser and clear the demo. Explain the actual persistence limit: **“This profile does not sync and may be removed when browser data is cleared.”** Do not claim registration success in a server-backed network.

## 6. Describe healthy feedback, not engagement obligations

Replace vague “support posts” wording with **“Offer specific, voluntary critique on drafts. No required likes, follows or copied public comments.”** A feedback tool should improve the creator's work, not sell access to a pod.

## 7. Check phone setup and preview

At 390px, test long handles, follower-count input, photo optionality and clear sample labels. Preview: **“Orbit Creator Collective — a local draft-feedback prototype.”** Use the full brand/URL to distinguish the cohort's other Orbit. No user name, private email or fake brand-deal amount in marketing images.
