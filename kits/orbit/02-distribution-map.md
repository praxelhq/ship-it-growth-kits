# Orbit — Distribution map

**Lead with a disclosed prototype and a small draft-feedback test.** The invitation must not imply real campaigns or Instagram integration.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fifteen willing niche creators | One existing trust circle, drafts reviewed voluntarily. Explain local demo versus manual pilot. | 15 prototype trials; 5–10 useful draft reviews if people choose to help. | #1 |
| Two existing creator/project groups | Admin-approved prototype invitation, no follower/like quotas or member-list exports. | 2 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 12–24 sessions. | #2 |
| Three LinkedIn posts | Profile clarity, useful critique and demo limits on Days 8, 16, 25 at 12:30 IST. | 3 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 5–68 sessions. | #3 |
| Three own-account short clips | Local profile/demo labels and actual draft revision on Days 11, 19, 26. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 2–60 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Disclosed prototype maker feedback after local save test; current slot/rules checked, no votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No paid creator, brand-deal newsletter, community access or active campaign is assumed. A creator-club newsletter can share the prototype only after permission, with zero baseline forecast. The accounts worth replying to are creators publicly asking a draft question you can genuinely answer; no generic app pitches under unrelated work.

At 50% visitor-to-meaningful-demo, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. These small tests may yield tens or low hundreds. A local prototype can earn feedback before a full network exists, but don't relabel that as connected membership. Use different contacts from Postifyy's student-posting clinic and the B2B creator-business tools; Orbit's wedge is voluntary pre-publication critique.
