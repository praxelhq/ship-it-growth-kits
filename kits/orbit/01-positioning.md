# Orbit — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.orbitcrew.me/ · **Category:** Creator-community prototype · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/orbit

The browser-visible headline checked 29 October is **“Your people. Your next orbit.”** The CTA is **“Set Up Your Creator Profile.”** Clicking it opens a profile form that explicitly says the profile is saved **in this browser on this device**, with no cross-device accounts/server authentication. Instagram connection is inactive; a handle can be entered manually. No profile was submitted in this research.

**This is a demo, not evidence of a live creator network.** Crew activity and brand briefs are samples. Do not market them as real members, campaigns or available paid work. Always use the full URL: another cohort product also uses Orbit for a different audience.

Your first tester is an adult creator who posts in one narrow niche and wants thoughtful feedback on a draft. Start with people already willing to review each other's work, not a promise of algorithmic growth.

**Pitch:** Try a creator-profile and small-crew feedback workflow, then help test whether it deserves a real community.

**Short version:** Better feedback for a draft, not another engagement quota.

## The wedge

One draft hook, one specific critique and one visible revision. That can be tested with a small voluntary pilot while the app is a local prototype. Do not pretend a manually organized pilot is server-backed app activity; report the two separately.

A private [WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) group already offers free messaging for a real feedback circle. Orbit must earn interest through relevant grouping and an understandable review workflow, not by claiming friends can't critique drafts. No paid plan was visible in the inspected entry/setup screens; don't invent a subscription or free-forever promise.

## The uncomfortable bit

Collecting a name/email/handle in a local form doesn't create a connected membership system. Local data can be lost with browser clearing and won't appear on another device. Explain that before collection and never count sample profiles as traction.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful prototype sessions under the no-account policy, with completed local profiles and voluntary real feedback pilots beside it. Those are not 1,000 connected creators or brand deals. Avoid mandatory likes, copied comments, vote exchanges or engagement pods; the useful result is better work, not coordinated manipulation.
