## Weekly review

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Ten test questions and five willing readers inspect sources | Misaligned citations: pause promotion and fix retrieval/context. |
| Day 14 | At least half of observed first-answer readers open a source | Low source use: make the citation/context action more visible, not more authoritative. |
| Day 21 | Repeat readers ask new genuine reading questions | No return: check usefulness and coverage, not just landing copy. |
| Day 30 | Actual accounts against 1,000; source opens/repeats/errors beside it | Keep the channel producing evidence-minded readers. |

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs roughly 3,334 distinct relevant visits. `02`'s small tests don't guarantee that volume. Replace forecast rates after three posts and document the remaining gap. Never count five daily questions as five users.
