# Mimir — Distribution map

**Lead with a reading question whose source can be inspected.** Reach readers and students, not people seeking a famous person's invented prediction.

| Route | Scheduled action and boundary | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing readers | Ask them to find an answer and inspect the source, using public texts only. | 10 trials, 5–10 source opens if the flow works. | #1 |
| Three existing MBA/book-club groups | Admin-approved one-question reading challenge. No author endorsement implied. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 accounts. | #2 |
| Three LinkedIn posts | A source check, unsupported-question example and reading workflow on Days 8, 16, 25 at 12:30 IST. | 3 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% account = 3–41 accounts. | #3 |
| Three short screen demos | Real question → citation → source context, Days 11, 19, 26. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% account = 1–36 accounts. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Builder-feedback listing after usable query/source tests and current Monday-slot check. No votes requested. | 0–30 accounts, uncertain upside. | #5 |
| [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) | Only a personally built, usable retrieval demo, not a signup page. No friend vote/comment requests. | Zero baseline; technical evidence feedback, not a promised audience. | #6 |

Your own reading-club newsletter can carry the challenge only after the organizer agrees. No public newsletter placement is verified, so forecast zero. The named primary-source authors' accounts are not advertising boards; discuss their actual text and do not paste a product pitch under unrelated posts.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 requires about 3,334 distinct qualified visits. The scheduled tests plausibly yield dozens or low hundreds. A useful source-comparison post may travel further, but don't book that as a guaranteed result. Avoid the founder-cluster's fundraising invitations: your wedge is reading provenance, not pitch evaluation.
