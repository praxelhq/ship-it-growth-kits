# Mimir — Landing page fixes

Based on [askmimir.in](https://askmimir.in/), checked 29 October. This was a public-page review, not a corpus audit or signed-in question test.

## 1. Test citation alignment before promoting certainty

The page uses “No Hallucinations” and “Verbatim citations guaranteed.” Replace absolute certainty with a checkable promise: **“Answers linked to indexed source passages. Open the original and review the context; coverage can be incomplete.”** First test ten questions, including unsupported topics, and verify each answer against its actual passage. A link existing is not enough.

## 2. Keep the strong example, clarify the action

Current headline: **“Ask Buffett about inflation. Get the 1981 letter.”** CTA: **“Start asking.”**

Suggested support: **“Ask a reading question across five indexed writers. Follow the source passage and see when the corpus cannot answer.”** Primary CTA: **“Try a question and inspect its source.”** Don't imply the author is personally answering.

## 3. Make the sample inspectable

The landing page includes a citation demonstration. Every card should open the exact passage/context, identify author and publication, and distinguish a paraphrase from a quotation. Add **“Illustrative question; verify the original passage.”** Show a refusal example as prominently as the successful retrieval.

## 4. Keep planned Pro out of live checkout expectations

Current Free: five questions/day. Current Pro: **“Coming soon”**, $9/month. Use **“Planned Pro — not available to purchase yet.”** Do not attach a buy button or count interest as paid revenue. Publish limits and timing only when actually decided.

## 5. Explain corpus boundaries

The page lists authors and counts. Show coverage dates, source types and missing material without implying the entire life's work is indexed. Suggested note: **“Search covers the listed collection, not everything this author has ever said.”** Counts require an update process, not static authority decoration.

## 6. Test the reading handoff on a phone

Open onboarding, ask a supported and unsupported question, follow citation, return without losing the answer, and test a broken source. At 390px, source cards need readable titles and context. Error: **“This source couldn't be opened. The answer is not independently verified by this page.”** Don't silently swap in a different source.

Preview title: **“Mimir — find the original writing behind an answer.”** No portrait treatment that implies endorsement. Keep medical, financial or other professional decisions outside a generic author's quoted persona.
