# Mimir — Video scripts

Use public writings and short excerpts only where needed. Show source context; do not fabricate quotes, voices or endorsements.

## A. Reading demo — 60 seconds, LinkedIn and site

0–2s, answer beside closed citation: **“A link isn't enough. Open it.”**

2–15s, enter one narrow reading question. “I'm asking what the indexed writing says, not what a simulated author would advise me.”

15–30s, show actual answer and open cited passage. “Here is the claim, and here is the original context.”

30–45s, compare the two without long verbatim quotation. “Does this passage support that summary?”

45–55s, ask an unsupported question and show actual limitation. If the app invents an answer, stop the video and fix it.

55–60s, URL: “Five free questions daily. Read the source.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Short

0–2s: “Did they actually write that?”

2–10s: question → real citation → original page. “Find the passage before repeating the quote.”

10–15s: askmimir.in and **Source context matters**. No invented answer shown for drama.

## C. Refusal hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Sometimes the useful answer is: not in this collection.”

2–12s: actual unsupported query and response. “An indexed author didn't necessarily discuss your exact startup or decision.”

12–20s: show source boundary. “Missing evidence should stay missing.” URL last.

## D. Reading habit — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Read one paragraph beyond the citation.”

2–14s: open source and surrounding context, using brief highlight rather than a copied essay. “The date and argument can change what the sentence means.”

14–20s: return to answer and note a qualification. “A reading assistant should send you back to the writing.”
