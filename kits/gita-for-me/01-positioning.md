# Gita For Me — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.gitaforme.me/ · **Category:** Spiritual reflection · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/gita-for-me

The current headline checked 29 October is **“When life feels heavy, find clarity.”** The CTA is **“Begin your reflection.”** The live experience is a **curated browser-based verse match**, not live personalized AI guidance. One personal thought per browser is free; example verses remain free. The **₹99/month membership is proposed and not available to purchase**.

Your first user is a student familiar with the Gita who has spent the evening comparing their progress with everyone else's. They want a short reading and one small reflection, not a prediction or a treatment claim.

**Pitch:** Take one everyday concern to a curated Gita reflection, read its context and choose one small step.

**Short version:** A verse to reflect on, not an answer to obey.

## The wedge

One specific moment of comparison, one verse, one contextual explanation. The live example links to [Chapter 3, Verse 35](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/3/verse/35), distinguishes paraphrase from app interpretation, and offers a practical reflection. Preserve that distinction. Do not present the app's comforting sentence as a literal scripture quotation.

The $0 alternative is reading the verse and commentary directly on the linked source or reflecting with a notebook. You earn attention through a gentle entry and accessible context, not by claiming exclusive access to wisdom. Different translations and interpretations exist; don't market one short app card as the final meaning of a tradition.

## The uncomfortable bit

The page already discloses curated preview, local processing and disabled payments. Don't erase those limits in social copy. A user asking for “personal AI guidance” would be buying a promise the product says is not live.

The launch-updates CTA points to an earlier ChatGPT-hosted site. Make the destination and data handling clear before sending people there. The public page was fetched, but no personal concern or email was submitted in this research.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful reflection sessions, with a completed reading/context-open beside it. This is spiritual reflection, not medical care, therapy, crisis support or a guarantee of feeling better. The user's private concern is not marketing evidence.
