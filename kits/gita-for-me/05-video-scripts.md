# Gita For Me — Video scripts

Use a fictional everyday concern and your own voice. Don't stage distress, promise relief or present an app-written sentence as a scripture quotation.

## A. Demo — 50 seconds, site and LinkedIn

0–2s: **“The verse and the app's interpretation are different things.”**

2–12s, enter a fictional comparison concern. “This is the current curated preview, not live personalized AI.”

12–25s, show the result labels. “Here is the source verse. Here is a paraphrase. Here is the app's interpretation.”

25–38s, open source context. “Read around the line before deciding what it means for you.”

38–50s, one voluntary practical prompt. “One free personal preview; examples remain free. The proposed membership isn't open. gitaforme.me.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “Not every comforting quote is from scripture.”

2–10s: show the explicit app-reflection label. “This sentence is ours. The verse has its own source.”

10–15s: source link and URL. Text: **Curated reflection preview**.

## C. Small-step hook — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “You don't need to decide your whole life tonight.”

2–12s: show one fictional concern and a small self-chosen action. “Read, reflect, choose a reversible next step.”

12–20s: free-preview CTA. “Keep the private thought to yourself. Feedback can be about the explanation.” No promise the person will feel better.

## D. Source-context clip — 25 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “One line isn't the whole chapter.”

2–15s: open the linked verse page and point out translation/commentary, without copying a long passage. “Context matters, and interpretations can differ.”

15–25s: return to the app's clearly labelled prompt. “The app is a starting point for reflection, not an authority over your choice.” End URL.
