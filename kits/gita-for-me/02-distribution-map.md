# Gita For Me — Distribution map

**Start with willing readers and a truthful free-preview demonstration.** Avoid mass messaging spiritual groups or implying religious authority.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing readers | People who already want a Gita reading/reflection tool. Ask about clarity, never demand their concern. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed reflections if the preview fits. | #1 |
| Two existing reading/campus groups | Ask admins for one clearly disclosed free-preview invitation. No forced prayer/participation. | 2 × 30 relevant readers × 20–40% try = 12–24 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | A curated reflection, source-context check, one small action and free/coming-soon explanation on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–80 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Thoughtful student-building story on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST; no authority or health promise. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–24 sessions. | #4 |
| One invited reading circle | Ask an existing organizer for a voluntary 20-minute contextual reading using public examples. | 8–15 attendees if accepted; 4–10 trials assumed, zero otherwise. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker-feedback post, current slot/rules checked. Explain it is a curated local preview. | 0–20 sessions, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No unverified religious subreddit, Discord or newsletter placement is assumed. The linked verse source is a reading resource, not a place to advertise. If your reading circle has a newsletter, ask for one free inclusion; forecast zero until accepted. No paid spiritual influencers or invented endorsements.

At 50% visitor-to-first-reflection, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. These small tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Show the gap honestly and use observed completion before widening reach. Use Days 10–13 for one reading-circle invitation, distinct from other cohort reflection apps; don't send several products to the same group that week.
