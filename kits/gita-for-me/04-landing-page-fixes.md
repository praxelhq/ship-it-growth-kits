# Gita For Me — Landing page fixes

Based on [gitaforme.me](https://www.gitaforme.me/), checked 29 October. No personal thought, waitlist email or payment was submitted.

## 1. Keep the preview limitation beside the first CTA

Current headline: **“When life feels heavy, find clarity.”** CTA: **“Begin your reflection.”** The page later explains curated preview, not live AI.

Suggested headline: **“A Gita reading for a moment you want to reflect on.”** Support: **“Try one browser-based match from a curated collection. Read the source context and choose a small step for yourself.”** Button: **“Try one free reflection.”** Don't let a first-time visitor infer an unlimited personalized guidance service.

## 2. Test local processing and the one-preview boundary

Use a fictional concern, check the actual result and failure state, and confirm personal input isn't sent where the page says it stays local. Test what happens after the first preview and in a fresh browser. If the flow fails or the privacy claim is wrong, stop promotion and repair it.

## 3. Preserve the three labels

The live example separates **“Paraphrase,” “App interpretation”** and **“App reflection.”** Keep these visible on every result, not just the sample. Link the exact source verse and its context. Add **“Different translations and readings may frame this differently; explore the context.”** Never place an original app sentence in quotation marks as scripture.

## 4. Make planned pricing unmistakable

Current: **“₹99/month · proposed”**, payments disabled. Suggested card: **“Planned membership — not open for purchase. The offer may change before launch.”** Keep free examples usable without an email or payment. No “subscribe now” language before production verification.

## 5. Explain the external waitlist destination

The launch-updates link points to a previous ChatGPT-hosted site. Either move the form to the current domain or tell people: **“Launch updates open on our earlier site. Check its form and privacy information before submitting.”** Test confirmation and do not count a click as an email signup.

## 6. Make the reflection easy to read on a phone

Check Hindi/Sanskrit line wrapping, source-link reachability, long input, keyboard overlap and a failed result. The default result should be readable without animation. Preview: **“Gita For Me — a curated reflection, with verse context.”** Do not use a distressed person's private words in screenshots or imply clinical benefit.
