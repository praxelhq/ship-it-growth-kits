# Infolio — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://infolio.co.in/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“The City That Planned Its Drains Before Its Palaces”**; the action is **“Read”**. The observed articles and question can be opened publicly without an account. No paid plan or price was shown in the checked flow. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a curious student deciding which piece to read first. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Give the library a first path

**Current:** The first visible title is “The City That Planned Its Drains Before Its Palaces”, while the product tagline is in metadata.

**Write this instead:**

> Start with one short idea. Pick this two-minute explainer, then try its question.

**Verify:** Keep one clear first piece above an overwhelming subject list.

## Fix 2 — Make the action specific

**Current:** Cards use the short action “Read”.

**Write this instead:**

> Read this piece · about two minutes · no background needed.

**Verify:** Verify that the exact card opens its own article route on a phone.

## Fix 3 — Separate illustration and evidence

**Current:** Pieces use story characters and broad history/law/finance explanations.

**Write this instead:**

> Illustrative scene. Factual sources are linked below; this story is not a reported case.

**Verify:** Apply only where it is actually an invented teaching scene and add real sources for factual claims.

## Fix 4 — Expose the end of the read

**Current:** The actual article has a final question and quote-card prompt.

**Write this instead:**

> Finished the piece? Try one question, then choose whether to explore more.

**Verify:** Do not make sharing a requirement for completing the reading.

## Fix 5 — Explain map/passport state

**Current:** The library links to map and passport routes without establishing their storage behaviour in this audit.

**Write this instead:**

> See the ideas you explored. Check what is saved on this device or account before relying on it.

**Verify:** Test actual persistence, then replace this with the exact supported behaviour.

## Fix 6 — Make the preview a reading invitation

**Current:** Metadata says “Hard ideas, told simply, by a fellow learner.”

**Write this instead:**

> Infolio — one short explainer, one idea to say back. Start with a public sample.

**Verify:** Use the piece title and a legible reading card, not an invented knowledge score.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
