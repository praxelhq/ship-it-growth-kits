## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. If there is no account gate for this reading flow, eligible first reading sessions can be the consumer headline, with the anonymous-identity limitation stated. A library-card view is not a reading start. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Prefer Days 10–13 for a shared-campus reading invitation, coordinated with Narra/Footnotes and C 1’s earlier reading window. Infolio’s job is an explainer, not a book summary or social reading club; let the organizer choose one clear activity.

## Directories, letters and accounts

Peerlist is the verified optional builder directory. Project Gutenberg and Finshots are reading alternatives, not agreed newsletter placements or comment sections to advertise under. No newsletter placement, editorial acceptance or external community promotion permission has been established; do not count one in the forecast.

Paid listings, sponsorships, prizes and promoted posts are outside your zero budget. An editor accepting a story is upside, not a scheduled conversion event. When a page's posting rules cannot be read, do not infer permission from another creator's post.
