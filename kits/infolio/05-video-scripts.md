# Infolio — Video scripts

Record your own screen and voice. Use a short excerpt, not the whole article. Show the public piece at a readable pace; no invented test score or quote-card export.

## A. One idea — 45 seconds

**Use:** homepage, reading-circle invitation, Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Open the history piece. Text: “One idea for two minutes.” | “Try one idea before another scroll.” |
| 3–14s | Title and reading-time label. | “This is a short history piece in Infolio. You can open it without an account.” |
| 14–28s | Hold a short, legible section. | “I wanted the little gaps in the day to contain something worth thinking about.” |
| 28–40s | Show the actual final question. | “At the end, try the question. Can you explain the idea without going back?” |
| 40–45s | Direct piece route or tested readable URL label. | “Start with one piece at infolio.co.in.” |

## B. Read, then say it back — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short or Reel.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | A library card, then the article. | “A title isn’t a read.” |
| 3–12s | Readable passage at normal speed. | “Pick one Infolio piece and stay with its idea.” |
| 12–17s | Final question. | “Now try saying it back.” |
| 17–20s | URL. | “The public sample needs no account.” |

## C. Story versus evidence — 25 seconds

**Use:** LinkedIn maker post.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Text card: “A story is not a source.” | “A good story still needs a clear boundary.” |
| 4–15s | Your own labelled example of an illustrative scene. | “If I imagine a scene to explain an idea, I should say it is an illustration.” |
| 15–22s | Show real source links only after adding/checking them. | “Factual claims need sources the reader can inspect.” |
| 22–25s | URL. | “That is the Infolio standard.” |

Do not fake a source label that is not present in the product. Add and verify it first, or use the text-card explanation without pretending it is a completed feature.
