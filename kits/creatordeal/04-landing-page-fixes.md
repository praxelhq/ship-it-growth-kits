# CreatorDeal — Landing-page fixes

Public page, pricing and action destinations read 29 October 2026. No private agreement was uploaded and authenticated extraction wasn't independently tested.

## 1. Make the review boundary part of the promise

**Current headline:** “‘One small change’ can be a bigger ask.” Supporting text says see what a quick favour adds before saying yes.

**Write:** **“Compare a new brand request with your saved deal. Check the difference before you reply.”** Supporting line: **“AI helps find the details; you review the agreement and decide.”** Keep the useful specific moment, remove any implication the tool decides legal rights or the extra price.

## 2. Keep the no-upload example first

**Current CTA:** “Try a brand request” opens the fictional example; “Check my own deal” leads toward signup.

**Write:** **“Try a fictional request—no upload.”** Secondary: **“Review my own agreement.”** Keep the example's made-up/sample disclosure visible at the comparison and suggested reply. Test the real upload → extraction → review → save flow with authorized non-sensitive data before directing traffic there. If it fails, keep promotion on the example until repaired.

## 3. Separate today's free access from future plans

**Current:** Free/$24 Pro cards are described as starting when paid plans launch; early access currently has 20 checks/day.

**Write above the cards:** **“Available now: free early access, up to 20 AI checks/day while available. No automatic paid upgrade.”** Put future plans under **“Planned pricing—not available to buy.”** Match the actual billing screen before adding Buy or subscription language.

## 4. Make every extracted field correctable

**Current:** “AI does the reading. You make the decision” promises review before details are added.

**Write on review:** **“Compare this field with the original agreement. Correct missing or uncertain details before saving.”** Highlight uncertainty beside dates, territories and usage instead of presenting a polished but unsupported answer. A missing clause should remain unknown, not become a default unlimited right.

## 5. Explain confidentiality before an upload

**Current:** private agreement uploads are a listed feature.

**Write:** **“Upload only agreements you're authorized to process. Read the current storage, provider and deletion terms first.”** State the actual data handling in plain language after verifying it; don't invent local-only processing or permanent deletion guarantees. Use redacted examples in public demos.

## 6. Verify a readable scope difference on phones

**Current:** original deal and new request compare several fields.

**Write the key result plainly:** **“Original scope”**, **“New request”**, **“Difference to review.”** Keep the disclaimer adjacent. Test 360px, large text, focus order and a shared preview titled **“CreatorDeal — brand requests, compared with your saved scope.”** No mobile overlap or performance defect was measured; these are checks, not alleged bugs.
