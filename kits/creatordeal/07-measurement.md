# CreatorDeal — Measurement

**Target: 10 real creator businesses.** Activation beside it: **creators who review and save their own authorized deal scope, then independently compare an actual brand request**. A fictional-demo check isn't an onboarded business.

## Day 0

Record actual businesses, authorized saved deals, reviewed extra-use comparisons and independently returning creators from your own records. These values weren't public. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, masking agreement/reply content. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/creatordeal) rank, saved reading and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Today's early-access usage is free; future $24 plans and test receipts aren't revenue.

Events: `deal_review_started`, `extracted_field_corrected`, `reviewed_deal_saved`, `request_compared`, `reply_reviewed` and `creator_returned`. Don't log raw contract text or brand correspondence. A reply generated isn't proof it was sent or legally correct; keep those outcomes separate.

## All four business tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires an actual creator business, its own permitted real-deal data onboarded, independent product use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable number held privately. Paying isn't required. One creator with ten deals is one business. A person who only likes the demo doesn't qualify.

## Forecast and weekly decisions

40 first approaches × assumed 25% response × 60% trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 customers. Ten warm introduction requests might add 0–2 without duplicating them. **Base 3–5 leaves a gap to ten.** Replace these assumptions with observed counts after 20 approaches. If creators have no second real scope job, don't manufacture activity with reminders.

| Review | Working milestone | If behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Three reviewed deal trials, one independent comparison | Observe source correction and confidentiality hesitation before more outreach. |
| Day 14 | Three qualifying businesses and some actual return use | Narrow to creators with ongoing brand work; repair wrong field confidence first. |
| Day 21 | Five qualifying businesses | Ask active creators for permissioned introductions; avoid broad public legal claims. |
| Day 28 | Eight qualifying businesses | Audit final-week independent use, real business status and duplicate accounts. |
| Day 30 | Ten or actual shortfall | Report businesses, reviewed comparisons, corrections, return use and hours separately. |

Manually review the first ten permitted sample extractions for missed dates, territory and usage scope. A high critical-field error rate is a reason to stop promoting real uploads, even if a social post brought registrations. The relevant proof is a creator making an informed reviewed decision, not a claimed automatic legal answer.
