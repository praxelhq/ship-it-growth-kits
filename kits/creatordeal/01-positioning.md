**Your ten are working creator businesses with real brand deals.** Don't count followers, people trying the public example or classmates uploading pretend contracts. Start with solo UGC creators who already receive paid brand requests and have to answer scope changes themselves.

## The job this month

> Compare a new brand request with the usage scope you saved, then review the difference before writing your own reply.

Bio: **Keep brand usage scope, end dates and payment details together.**

Choose one narrow moment: a creator sold an organic post and the brand later asks to run it as an ad or keep using it longer. Your product helps make that difference visible. It does not determine enforceable rights, provide a lawyer's opinion or set a mandatory extra fee.

The live demo's $800 Reel is explicitly fictional. Keep that label, and never use the sample rate as market pricing. Actual permissions depend on the creator's actual agreement; no sample proves what every contract means.

## Why choose you over existing work?

The real alternative is email/PDF plus a manual end-date and payment checklist. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace and can track those dates, but doesn't read the agreement and compare a new request. Don't claim your AI replaces a qualified adviser or that every flagged difference entitles the creator to more money.

Your first win is a creator correcting the extracted scope against their real contract, saving it and using it again for an actual brand request. The weak spot is trust: if the extraction is wrong or confidential content is exposed, a convenient reply draft is not worth it. Start with a redacted non-sensitive example and get permission before any real upload.

The business target requires real deal data onboarded, independent use during the final seven days, and a callable responsible contact held privately. One creator with five agreements is one business, not five customers.
