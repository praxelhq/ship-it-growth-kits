# CreatorDeal — Video scripts

Use the disclosed fictional example, not a real confidential contract. No brand accusation, legal conclusion or sample-rate recommendation. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · The same Reel, a different ask · 60 seconds · creator call / LinkedIn

0–2s hook: **“Same Reel. Did the brand ask for the same use?”** Show original/new-request columns, label “Fictional example.” 2–15s: highlight organic use, duration and territory: “These are the saved sample terms, not a standard contract.” 15–30s: choose an extra-ad-use request: “Now the request changes. The tool compares it with the saved scope.” 30–45s: correct an extracted field against the sample agreement: “Check the AI reading before relying on the difference.” 45–55s: show editable reply: “You decide what to ask and what to agree. No automatic extra-fee calculation is a legal entitlement.” 55–60s: “Try the example first. CreatorDeal dot in.”

## B · The sample price isn't your rate · 20 seconds · X / own feed

Hook: **“This $800 isn't a rate card.”** 0–8s: enlarge the fictional-example label. 8–15s: “The example helps explain scope. Your fee and rights depend on your real agreement and negotiation.” 15–20s: “Use a sample to understand the workflow, not to price your business by copying it.”

## C · A field the AI missed · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2s: **“Don't save a date you haven't checked.”** 2–10s: show a synthetic extraction with uncertain end date. 10–20s: compare the source clause and correct it: “A clean dashboard can still contain a wrong reading. The review step is the product.” 20–25s: “Keep unknown information visible until you confirm it.”

Use readable field zooms and captions. If the current product can't show uncertainty or source context as described, label that as an improvement you're testing; don't fake a feature in the video.
