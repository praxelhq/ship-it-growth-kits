# Kuchu Puchu — Positioning

**Live:** https://kuchupuchu.me/ · **Category:** Mac companion · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kuchu-puchu

The live page checked 29 October says **“Little companions. Big feelings.”** and offers **“Get Kuchu Puchu — ₹100.”** This is a one-time, tax-inclusive early-beta price. The purchase CTA goes to Dodo; the download is delivered by email/customer portal. This research did not purchase, install or exchange a greeting.

**Your first users are two people who both use Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14+ and miss each other during desk time.** Long-distance partners, siblings or close friends fit. People on Intel, Windows or phones do not fit the current beta. Put that restriction before the purchase, not after the emotional pitch.

## Your pitch

**Send a small hello from your Mac to a person you love, as a companion appearing on their desktop.**

Short version: **A little “thinking of you,” across two Macs.**

The wedge is one accepted friendship and a greeting played on the other screen. Both apps must run and have internet; offline messages wait. This is not a phone notification service or a replacement for a call. The site now includes avatar customization, scenes, chats and optional AI features, but none should obscure that first two-person moment.

## The alternatives

[WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) offers free messages/calls, and [Widgetable's App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/widgetable-besties-pets/id1641107226) is free with in-app purchases, focused on iPhone widgets and shared companions. You are not cheaper than free. Your difference is the native Mac desktop gesture and the person's chosen character. Do not claim other products cannot connect friends or show moods.

## The uncomfortable bits

The site says the beta is locally signed and **not Apple-notarized**. Installation trust is a real acquisition constraint. Do not hide the warning or tell people to disable device protections. Improve release signing/notarization through an existing authorized account if available; otherwise stay honest about an invited early beta.

The [privacy notice](https://kuchupuchu.me/privacy.html) says chats are stored by the service and are not end-to-end encrypted. Friend/circle email visibility and app-running requirements matter. Never advertise “fully offline” or “nothing leaves your Mac.” Optional AI photo generation sends prepared input to a provider after Generate.

Your B2C target remains 1,000 installs/first launches, with accepted-pair greeting activation beside it. Two purchases do not establish a working pair. A narrow paid two-Mac beta may land far below 1,000; prove the pair before widening distribution.
