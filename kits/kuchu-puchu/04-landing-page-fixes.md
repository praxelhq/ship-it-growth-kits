# Kuchu Puchu — Landing page fixes

Based on [kuchupuchu.me](https://kuchupuchu.me/) and its [privacy notice](https://kuchupuchu.me/privacy.html), checked 29 October. No purchase, install or greeting was completed during research.

## 1. Test the whole two-person path

Current CTA: **“Get Kuchu Puchu — ₹100.”** It has a Dodo checkout destination; delivery, install, sign-in, friend acceptance and greeting arrival remain a separate test. Check them with two compatible Macs and an authorized test purchase. If a paid user cannot receive the file or connect, pause acquisition and repair delivery.

## 2. Bring compatibility and beta trust above payment

Current supporting line: **“₹100 exclusive launch price · Apple Silicon · macOS 14+.”** Keep it. Add **“Early beta. Not Apple-notarized yet. Read the installation notice before buying.”** Do not bury a trust-critical restriction beneath a long feature list or instruct people to turn off protections.

## 3. Make the paired result the hero

Current headline: **“Little companions. Big feelings.”**

Replacement: **“Send a little hello from your Mac to theirs.”** Support: **“Choose your companion, connect after an accepted request, and send a greeting across two Macs. Both apps need to be running.”** Keep the emotional line as a secondary caption.

## 4. Show a real recipient-controlled demonstration

Use one continuous two-screen clip: accepted friend, send, recipient playback, Work mode. Label any synthetic text. Caption: **“Real beta demonstration. Recipient controls stay in charge.”** Do not imply delivery when the app is fully quit or offline.

## 5. Separate greetings, chats and optional AI

The live page includes AI advice and photo generation. Put a simple core path first; optional AI gets its own clearly labelled section. Suggested privacy summary: **“Chats are stored by the connection service and are not end-to-end encrypted. AI generation sends the prepared photo/prompt only when you choose Generate.”** Link the full notice; don't claim fully local operation.

## 6. Make delivery and updates unambiguous

Current price: ₹100 once, tax included, Dodo email/customer portal delivery, existing-user updates free. Put that next to the buy button: **“One payment · tax included · download by email/customer portal · free updates for existing users.”** Do not promise hosting/service availability forever; the notice says quotas and interruptions are possible.

## 7. Phone browsing still matters

A person may watch the demo on a phone but install later on a Mac. Provide a copyable product link and **“Open this page on your compatible Mac.”** Never offer an iOS download. Test long emails, codes, receipt instructions and support links. Preview: **“Kuchu Puchu — little greetings across two Macs”**, with compatible-platform text in the image.
