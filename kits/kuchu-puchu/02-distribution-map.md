**Start with compatible pairs, then show the real two-Mac delivery.** A single-device cute clip can attract people who cannot use the beta.

The scheduled base is likely dozens of installs, not 1,000. At 10% qualified visitor-to-paid-first-launch, 1,000 requires 10,000 qualified visits—and enough two-Mac pairs. State the gap. Do not inflate installs using downloads, update checks, or creator codes you have not actually issued.

This is distinct from the cohort's sports-notch utilities and solo desktop pets. Your first-week invitation is for long-distance pairs; use separate groups from their developer launches. No paid creators, affiliate commissions, directory boosts or purchased accounts. A newsletter pitch can wait until a stable release and a real paired demonstration exist.
