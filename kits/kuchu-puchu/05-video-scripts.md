# Kuchu Puchu — Video scripts

Record two compatible Macs, with willing testers and no private emails, friend codes or chats visible. Use your own voices and captions. Do not fake delivery or hide an installation warning.

## A. Paired demo — 60 seconds, LinkedIn and site

0–2s, both desktops in view: **“This hello is going to the other Mac.”**

2–14s, choose a character. “Make a companion feel like you.”

14–25s, show a masked request and deliberate acceptance. “The other person accepts before you connect.”

25–40s, send an owned greeting and show actual arrival without a cut. “Both apps are running. Now the hello appears there.”

40–50s, recipient opens Work mode. “And they can make the desktop quiet.”

50–60s, show price and requirements. “₹100 early beta. Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Read the install/privacy notes first.”

## B. Emotional hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “Not another ‘wyd?’”

2–10s: real send/arrival of a tiny hello. “Sometimes a small hello is enough.”

10–15s: both screens and URL. Text: **Both Macs running · compatible Mac beta · ₹100 once**. No fabricated partner reaction.

## C. Recipient-control hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Cute shouldn't mean impossible to mute.”

2–12s: show Work mode and a deliberately quiet desktop. “The person receiving it chooses the interruption.”

12–20s: turn back only when wanted. “A little company, with controls.” End platform and beta disclosure.

## D. Before-you-buy clip — 25 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Will this run on both your Macs?”

2–15s: show Apple Silicon/macOS 14+, both apps running, internet, friend acceptance as a checklist. Say each plainly.

15–25s: show ₹100 tax-inclusive price and current notarization notice. “If an early beta isn't for you, wait for a release you're comfortable installing.” Trust matters more than an accidental purchase.
