# PostFlow — Positioning

**Live:** https://postflow.in/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/post-flow

**Start with one true professional observation, not an imitation of a famous creator.** Actual headline checked 29 October: “One line in. A LinkedIn post you'd actually publish, out.” “Start drafting” leads to `/signup`, which was inspected in a browser without creating an account. The site promises three draft formats, then manual review/edit/copy/publish. It never connects to LinkedIn.

Your first user is an adult early professional who has a genuine lesson from their work but freezes at the blank draft. They should already want to post; don't sell a content habit or a reach guarantee to someone who doesn't need one.

**Pitch:** Turn your own rough observation into draft options, then edit the one that still sounds true before posting it yourself.

**Bio:** Draft options from your experience. Your judgment before publishing.

The offer is **two free posts, then US$149/month for 21 posts per billing month**. This is dollars, not ₹149. [Terms](https://postflow.in/terms) say Generate and Regenerate each consume one post, and unused posts expire. At full use that is roughly $7.10 per generation; repeated regenerations reduce the number of final posts. Don't quietly lower the price or describe three draft options as three free credits.

The zero-cost alternative is writing in a notes editor and publishing manually on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/). Your wedge is useful variation grounded in the person's actual experience—not automation, scheduling, private-network access or a promise of better engagement. The $149 offer needs willingness-to-pay evidence, not a student-discount invention. Keep initial acquisition on the two free generations.

The [privacy notice](https://postflow.in/privacy) says samples/profile/topic go to Google Gemini and that free-plan content may be used to improve products. Don't invite confidential employer/client material. Creator samples must be permitted; structure inspiration isn't permission to copy ideas or sentences. The homepage's named sample is illustrative, not verified customer proof.

No generation, account, checkout or output quality was tested. Keep B2C **1,000 signups** as real accounts, with a fact-checked, materially edited usable draft beside it. Manual published posts are an optional later result, not something a Copy event proves.
