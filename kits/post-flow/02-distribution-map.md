# PostFlow — Distribution map

**Recruit people with an observation, not people looking for fake authority.** Your trial needs one useful edited draft before a paid pitch.

| Route | Calendar action / boundary | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult professionals | Five Day 5, five Day 10; use public/fictional non-confidential experience. | 10 visits × 40–70% signup = 4–7 accounts. | #1 |
| Two existing writing/alumni groups | Admin request Day 9, one approved invitation Days 12/17; different contacts from Postifyy/Orbit. | 2 × 40 readers × 20–40% visits × 40% signup = 6–13 accounts. | #2 |
| Four LinkedIn posts | Days 8/13/20/26 at 12:30 IST; publish an actual own lesson with disclosed drafting assistance where relevant. | 4 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 40% signup = 5–72 accounts. | #3 |
| Three short process demos | Days 11/19/25; real own-input/draft/edit, no fabricated reach screenshots. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 40% signup = 2–48 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited drafting clinic | Ask one existing organizer Day 16, optional eight adults Day 18. No private work required. | 8 × 40–60% signup = 3–5 if accepted; zero otherwise. | #5 |
| Maker feedback | Optional [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) Day 22 after current rule/slot check; disclose actual price. | Zero baseline; builder feedback is not paid demand. | #6 |

That is roughly 20–145 gross accounts before overlap, not a 1,000 guarantee. At 40% visit→signup, 1,000 requires 2,500 distinct qualified visitors. If fewer than half the first testers can produce a true usable draft inside two generations, improve that flow before broad acquisition. A polished post full of invented achievements is a failure.

No paid creators, newsletter sponsorship or automated LinkedIn outreach. Ask before any free newsletter mention, zero forecast until accepted. Reply to real writing questions with useful editing advice, not repeated product links. Never request passwords, export contacts or promise native LinkedIn scheduling.

Postifyy covers a student posting clinic and Orbit a creator feedback prototype. Use a separate early-professional segment and organizer; proposed Days 17–20 are coordination preferences, not bookings. Don't send the same group another product invitation inside seven days.
