# PostFlow — Video scripts

Use your own public observation or a clearly fictional example. Never show employer/client information, copied creator material, account credentials or a fabricated engagement result.

## A. Draft-and-edit demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“The edit is the part I want to show.”**

2–12s: show your real rough note and the processing boundary. “No private work goes into this example.”

12–27s: generate the actual options after authorized account setup. “These are draft choices, not a finished statement of fact.” Label waiting cuts.

27–45s: highlight an actual sentence you shorten, qualify or remove. “This didn't sound like me / this wasn't supported, so I changed it.” Don't invent an error if none occurred.

45–53s: copy the edited draft without publishing. “You decide what to post. The app doesn't connect to LinkedIn.”

53–60s: URL, **2 free generation credits · optional US$149/month for 21**. If generation fails, fix it before recording a successful walkthrough.

## B. Fact hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Did I actually say that happened?”**

2–12s: compare your own prompt and actual draft. “Check every claim before a polished sentence becomes your public claim.” Use a real comparison, not a fabricated output.

12–18s: edited version/URL. “Draft help, human responsibility.”

## C. Voice hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Three options. Which sentence would I actually use?”**

2–12s: show actual formats and choose one sentence, explain why in ordinary words. “This is closer; this still needs an edit.”

12–20s: manual editor/URL, trial credit boundary. No “perfectly cloned voice” or celebrity imitation.

## D. Offer hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A regenerate isn't a free undo.”**

2–12s: show actual remaining-credit display after verified. “Generate and Regenerate consume credits. Know that before another click.”

12–20s: current offer/URL. “Two free generations. Paid plan is US$149/month for 21, unused credits expire.” Don't stage a decrement if it wasn't observed.
