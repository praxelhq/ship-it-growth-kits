# PostFlow — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real accounts once, excluding tests. Place **first fact-checked, materially edited usable drafts** beside signup. Record generated options, copied text and voluntarily confirmed manual publication separately; Copy does not prove a published post or business outcome.

No private dashboard was accessed. Fill actual baseline; unknown is not zero.

| --- | --- |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding scripts; exclude samples, creator text, drafts, identities and payment/session tokens from replay. Track `account_confirmed`, `generation_started`, `generation_completed`, `generation_failed`, `draft_copied`, `usable_draft_confirmed`, `subscription_verified`. A regenerate is credit usage, not another person.

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Five willing adults can generate/edit with understood credit cost | Fix blocked steps and misleading offer copy. |

| Day 30 | Accounts versus 1,000; usable drafts/real paid adoption beside it | Report gap and best channel, not inflated publishing outcomes. |
