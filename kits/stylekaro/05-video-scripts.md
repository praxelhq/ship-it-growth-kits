# StyleKaro — Video scripts

Use generic garment descriptions/owned clothing and fictional adult preferences. No body/face photos, measurements, actual event details or private results. Don't overlay retailer prices/stock on editorial imagery or show a proposed brief as shipped output.

## A. Main demonstration — 55 seconds, after guest flow verified

**0–7s:** A maker-owned generic shirt/kurta. “Start with something you own.” No body modelling required; label sample garment.

**7–19s:** Actual manual occasion/budget/exclusion form. “One occasion, a spending cap and a hard no.” Show no exact event date/location.

**19–31s:** Add actual supported owned-item description; optional local photo not necessary. “Describe the piece yourself. No body or AI photo scan.”

**31–42s:** Actual returned brief. “Check whether it respects the exclusion and uses the piece sensibly.” If the flow remains session-checking, stop and make an honest readiness update instead of faking output.

**42–49s:** Actual independent official collection link. “This is a place to explore, not a product quote or stock guarantee.” No unverified exact item.

**49–55s:** Free guest CTA, current catalogue limits and URL. ₹199 planning pack separate and only when available, no clothing purchase implied.

## B. Hook — 23 seconds, Day 9

**0–5s:** Owned garment beside an invitation with no real details. “A new occasion doesn't always need a whole new outfit.”

**5–16s:** Actual description→brief reuse suggestion, if verified. “Start here before opening twenty shopping tabs.”

**16–23s:** “Free guest styling, no body scan.” URL/editorial-not-inventory label. No claim of measured money saved.

## C. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 14

**0–6s:** Spending cap versus product price cards. “A budget slider isn't a price tag.”

**6–17s:** Actual brief and official collection browse. “This suggests a spending plan. You check real price, size and stock with the retailer.”

**17–25s:** “No fake catalogue outfit to fill the gap.” URL/current independent-link note. Don't copy retailer photos or invent discounts for the shot.

## D. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 24

**0–6s:** Generic “no heels” exclusion. “A personal style tool should keep your hard no.”

**6–17s:** Actual brief/refinement respecting it. “Preferences, not a prescription for your body.” Only show actual tested behavior; a failure is a fix, not something to cut away.

**17–25s:** “Your piece, your budget, your choice.” URL/free guest/current catalogue boundary. No universal fit, flattering-body judgment or retailer endorsement.

Use four assets plus two honest process/catalogue-state posts for the six-post calendar. A real useful brief is more persuasive than a glamorous editorial picture pretending to be available inventory.
