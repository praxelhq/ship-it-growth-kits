## Target and milestones

Keep the **1,000 B2C first-use/signup target**. Report unique guest first-brief sessions separately from optional verified accounts, with legitimate attribution/deduplication limits. Session-storage resets can affect guest counting; don't fingerprint people to invent precision. Product account, share card, retailer click, pack purchase and clothing purchase are distinct.

Activation: a returned usable brief honoring a manual exclusion and an owned garment description. Deeper value: voluntary second occasion/reuse or user-reviewed share. Neither requires buying clothes or disclosing body/size/event details. Paid ₹199 pack is a planning purchase, not five outfits; count only provider-confirmed legitimate entitlement, never a redirect or checkout click. If payments aren't available, paid conversion forecast is zero.

Policy-consistent coarse events only with the relevant opt-in: guest brief success/error, owned-item count category, excluded-preference constraint honored where legitimately measured without text, account save, reviewed share, pack preview/purchase access, return. Public Clarity and signed-in analytics have separate choices. No raw/manual preference words, body descriptions, photo, sizes, location/event timing, private brief/card, account identity or payment references in marketing events/replays. Clothing photos stay page-memory, but manual labels/preferences go to the styling server; don't claim everything is local. Paid AI calls disabled means no invented model-quality metric.

Private aggregate ledger: date, source visits, delivered guest briefs, optional accounts, volunteered usefulness/reuse/constraint feedback, session errors, retailer clicks separately, live pack entitlements/refunds if legitimate, opt-outs, hours/spend. ## Weekly decisions

**Day 7:** Can five voluntary adults get a real guest brief and understand caps versus product quotes? Five is a learning gate. If session checking never completes, fix delivery. If exclusions are ignored, fix correctness before adding reach; don't blame personal taste for a hard-no violation.

**Day 14:** Split session, form, manual item, brief and catalogue-coverage errors. If people expect scanned-body styling or exact-stock outfits, correct editorial labels. No available catalogue must result in honest limited guidance, not fabricated retailer products.

**Day 21:** Look for voluntary reuse/second brief or reviewed sharing. A user choosing not to buy clothes can be success. Don't pressure them to share a private card or imply that outbound clicks equal satisfied styling. No measured savings/fit claim from a fictional brief.

**Day 28:** Verify actual pack payment/entitlement/download, account deletion and minimal retained payment references. If paid availability remains closed, count interest separately. Current collection links aren't affiliate revenue or partnership proof; future disclosures must appear when actually approved.

**Day 30:** Report actual invitations, guest first-brief sessions, accounts separately, useful activation/eligible return, pack purchases only if live, source overlap, errors, hours/spend and 1,000-user target gap. 02 guesses only 4–19 activations without an event or 7–25 with it before overlap **after guest readiness**; zero successful briefs until delivery works. Unaccepted creator/launch/directory traffic is zero. No fabricated product availability, body assessment, retailer endorsement or guaranteed shopping outcome.
