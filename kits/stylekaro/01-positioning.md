## The pitch

> Start with what you own, the occasion and what you want to spend. Get a styling plan, then explore official retailer collections on your own terms.

Bio: **A useful outfit plan, with your hard no's intact.**

Free guest styling is **₹0, no account/subscription/card** according to the page. Manual preferences can include colors, exclusions and owned clothing. No face/body scan, biometric assessment or inferred measurements. Optional **clothing** photos remain local previews; the user labels them, no AI image analysis/upload. The color studio is an original visual study, not virtual try-on or a garment simulation.

Catalogue coverage is still being built. Editorial outfit photos are **not inventory**; official collection links are browse destinations, not product-level quotes, fit/stock guarantees or partnerships. The [retailer directory](https://stylekaro.app/retailers) currently lists fourteen destinations and says no commissions are earned. Affiliate links are planned only after approval and must be labelled. Don't invent a retailer deal, exact outfit under ₹5,000 or live product recommendation from a slider.

## Actual paid planning offer

[Pricing](https://stylekaro.app/pricing) describes a **₹199 one-time wedding-season edit**, five plans plus shared shopping/reuse list, first plan preview free, tax included, clothing purchases separate. Secure checkout appears **when payments are available**. Don't imply payment is open or an order was fulfilled from the price alone; owner verifies preview/payment/entitlement/download. It is planning, not five purchased outfits or a subscription. [Privacy](https://stylekaro.app/privacy) says verified provider records—not a redirect—control pack access.

## Alternatives, privacy and target

An existing wardrobe list and a trusted friend's suggestions cost ₹0 extra specialist software. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can hold an occasion/owned-piece/spending plan. StyleKaro earns adoption through a coherent brief that honors exclusions and reuse, not by judging bodies or claiming every shopper needs AI.

The policy is **18+**; guest preferences are in browser session storage **and sent to the styling server when requesting an edit**. Local photos don't mean the whole workflow stays local. Signed-in saved data/packs use account storage; minimal purchase records can remain after account deletion. AI paid calls are disabled pending authorization; don't advertise integrated image AI. Optional public-page Clarity and signed-in usage analytics have separate opt-ins.

Keep the **1,000 B2C first-use/signup target**, reporting unique guest brief sessions separately from optional accounts, with honest deduplication limits. Activation is one usable brief honoring a hard no and owned piece; later voluntary reuse/share or a paid planning pack is separate. No clothing purchase required to be successful. Use a different adult guest circle from Biaah's family-budget rehearsal, not another pitch to the same wedding organizer that week.
