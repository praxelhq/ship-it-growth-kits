# sole match — Video scripts

Record after restoration and a real result/link test. Use your own ordinary preferences; no foot/body photos, medical history or private checkout. No retailer logo as endorsement or invented delivery/price. Phone capture and voice are enough.

## A · Why these options? · 60 seconds · own feed / listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Your ordinary occasion/budget. **“Why this pair?”** | “I want the reason, not another endless list.” |
| 2–14s | Actual recovered preference input. | “This is the supported input for this release.” |
| 14–30s | Real output, loading cuts labelled. | “These are candidates. I inspect why each one appears.” |
| 30–43s | Reject a genuinely unsuitable option. | “This one doesn't meet what I want. The suggestion isn't the final choice.” |
| 43–53s | Open actual seller page without purchasing. | “I still check current size, price, returns and delivery.” |
| 53–60s | URL and limits. | “Try the current sole match flow. It isn't a fit or comfort guarantee.” |

If the recovered flow supports a different task, rewrite the shots before filming. Never stage output to make a broken recommender look live.

## B · Three different questions · 20 seconds · short post

Hook: **“Style, budget and fit aren't one score.”** 0–8s, three paper cards. 8–15s: “A short list can help me compare style and spending. Fit still needs its own check.” 15–20s: “Keep the reason visible.” No health claim or manufactured shoe test.

## C · The option I rejected · 25 seconds · follow-up reel

Hook: **“I wouldn't buy this suggestion.”** 0–10s, show actual output and your stated preference. 10–20s: “This misses what I asked for, and that is useful feedback.” 20–25s: “A good shortlist needs room for a no.” Use a real observed mismatch or clearly labelled hypothetical explanation; don't invent a customer failure.

## D · Check where the link goes · 20 seconds · trust help

Hook: **“A card isn't a checkout.”** 0–8s, open a genuine result's retailer link. 8–15s: “The retailer confirms price, size, delivery and returns. The recommendation didn't reserve it.” 15–20s: “Check before spending.” Keep personal browsing/account details hidden and never complete a purchase for the clip.
