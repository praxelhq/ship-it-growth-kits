# JustWriteIt — Video scripts

Use your own non-sensitive work and the actual manual mode. No private client draft, credential, access token or authenticator secret. Assistant/cloud/full autopilot remain off. If the manual-only path isn't supported, repair it before filming a public success.

## A · Three facts, one reviewed draft · 60 seconds · LinkedIn / launch

| Time | Shot / on-screen text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Your rough note. **“What actually happened?”** | “This post starts with the facts I can support.” |
| 2–13s | Three real bullets: tried, observed, unknown. | “I supply what I did, what I saw and what still needs testing.” |
| 13–28s | Actual question/draft path; show credit notice. | “Starting uses a post credit. I answer what the draft still needs.” |
| 28–43s | Inspect/edit output, with no invented edit story. | “I check every claim and rewrite the part that doesn't sound like me.” |
| 43–53s | Manual approval state, assistant/autopilot off. | “I choose whether to publish. This test doesn't automate comments or connections.” |
| 53–60s | Current free allowance and URL. | “Two post starts free. JustWriteIt dot online.” |

Show the actual result. If the output invents a number, correct it visibly; don't call that impossible because the homepage does.

## B · Smaller, truer sentence · 20 seconds · own-feed short

Hook: **“Can I prove this sentence?”** 0–7s, show your own unsupported draft phrase or a clearly labelled fictional one. 7–15s, replace it with a concrete observed fact: “A clearer post doesn't need a bigger claim.” 15–20s, show the reviewed draft: “Keep the part you can stand behind.” Never make up an impressive result to illustrate removing an impressive result.

## C · Starting is the credit event · 25 seconds · pricing help

Hook: **“The credit is used before I publish.”** 0–8s, show actual current balance and start notice. 8–17s: “Two post starts are free. A new draft uses one when it begins.” 17–25s, show pack terms: “The $6 pack has six of each type, not unlimited posts. Check value before buying.” Don't spend a user's credits or purchase a pack just to film.

## D · Your final decision · 20 seconds · product onboarding

Hook: **“A timer isn't my approval.”** 0–8s, show manual mode versus actual autopilot labels, without enabling automation. 8–15s: “For my factual draft test, the assistant and autopilot stay off.” 15–20s: “I read the post and choose what happens next.” Keep actual available controls visible; no staged mode toggle if it isn't implemented.
