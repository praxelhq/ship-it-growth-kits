# JustWriteIt — Positioning

**Live:** https://justwriteit.online/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/justwriteit

**Fix the public trust contract before inviting LinkedIn connections.** Both [privacy](https://justwriteit.online/privacy) and [terms](https://justwriteit.online/terms) explicitly call themselves draft text requiring review before launch. The privacy page describes optional storage of cloud-login credentials, then later says passwords are never stored. Those claims need reconciliation with the actual modes. This kit does not ask anyone to provide a password, authenticator secret or cloud-browser access.

The actual headline is **“You do what you do. We post and engage on your behalf.”** The main action says **“I'm ready to grow my network”** and leads toward `/welcome`; **“Start with 2 free posts”** and signup/login routes are also public. The page promises a draft in your voice from a rough idea, with questions rather than invented stories/numbers. These are operator claims, not an independently tested guarantee of factual AI output. Account creation, publishing, extension installation and billing were not completed here.

Current offer: **two posts, two comments and two connection requests free, no card; $6 once buys six of each, credits don't expire.** A post uses a credit when started, not when published. Comments/requests use one when actually sent. Practice/skips/failed sends are described as free. Don't call the pack six dollars for unlimited posting, or count unused comment credits as proof the manual-post offer is good value.

## Your first person and sentence

An adult solo builder who has a real project update to share, dislikes turning it into LinkedIn prose, and wants to review every word before publishing. Focus on their own experience and evidence, not junior analysts' news commentary or students' first career story.

> Turn one rough project note into a factual LinkedIn draft you edit and approve yourself.

Bio: **Your real work, a clearer post, your final decision.**

The first job is a short update with three supplied facts: what you tried, what happened and what you still don't know. The user checks names/numbers, removes anything invented and chooses whether to publish. Leave assistant, cloud automation and full autopilot off. Verify that this manual mode actually works before promising it.

## Alternatives and actual risks

[Buffer's pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing) offers a Free plan for three channels with ten queued posts per channel and AI assistance. Essentials is advertised from $5/channel/month billed annually, not a cancellable five-dollar monthly charge. A LinkedIn draft plus an existing notes app can cost no extra specialist-software fee. Your differentiation must be a useful question-led factual draft and transparent per-start credit use.

The product itself says LinkedIn's terms forbid automated comments and requests and accounts can be restricted. Running in Chrome, practising first or sending only a few a day does not create permission. Do not market those limits as a safe workaround. The homepage also says every post waits for approval, then describes full autopilot publishing after a 6/12/24-hour stop window. Those are different modes, and silence after a timer is not explicit approval of every post.

Keep **1,000 individual accounts** as the target, with **one fact-checked draft approved by the user** alongside. Actual publishing and later independent drafting are separate outcomes. The legal/manual-mode gate may consume the first week; report that constraint instead of recruiting into an unclear account-access promise.
