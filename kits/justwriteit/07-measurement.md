# JustWriteIt — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Report **one factual draft checked and explicitly approved by its user** beside signups. Publication is a separate confirmed action; an approval click isn't proof LinkedIn received a post. Later independent drafting shows more than a one-time free-credit experiment. Exclude tests, duplicate accounts and generated drafts with no real user review.

## Day 0

Record actual accounts, post starts, completed drafts, approval, confirmed publication, errors and independently returning users from owner records. No private baseline was established here. Capture current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/justwriteit) rank, saved reading and timestamp; unknown is not zero. A credit balance or public pack price does not establish purchases.

The current draft policy says optional PostHog runs after consent and Clarity records only the public page after consent, never private account page recordings. Verify the implemented boundaries and final reviewed policy before changing measurement; don't add a second tracker or record drafts. Suggested content-free counts: `account_created`, `post_started`, `draft_ready`, `draft_reviewed`, `explicit_approval`, `publish_confirmed`, `return_draft`, `credit_purchase_confirmed`. These are proposed events unless already instrumented. Don't send text, profiles, tokens, passwords or recipient names in analytics.

Separate post-credit spent, failed draft, unused credit and refunded purchase. A $6 pack includes different credit types; six comments aren't six additional posts. Assistant activity and full-autopilot output are outside this manual launch experiment and must not inflate approved-draft activation.

## Forecast matching `06`

All rates below are assumptions:

- 40 invitations × 40% try × 75% create account = **12 accounts**, about six activations if half approve a checked draft.
- Six posts × 200–800 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup = **6–24 accounts**.
- One optional launch: 0–150 visits × 20% signup = **0–30 accounts**.
- Five optional introduction requests have no guaranteed new-user contribution; report actual accepted results separately.

Base **18–66 accounts before overlap**, likely lower after deduplication, far below 1,000. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, the target needs 4,000 qualified visits. Six small-account posts don't establish that reach. Recompute after 20 invitations and seek a real accepted distribution route only if the manual draft loop earns returns.
