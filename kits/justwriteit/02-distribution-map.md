# JustWriteIt — Distribution map

**Lead with your own factual draft demonstration and opt-in individual tests.** No automated commenting, connection requests, cloud assistant or additional campus clinic belongs in this launch. Coordinate distinct contacts with Postifyy, The Steady Post, Creative Studio, Lynkr, SignalPost, TwoCents and Postwire; don't send the same person a sequence of cohort writing-tool pitches.

| Route | Action and boundary | Explicit planning assumption / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known solo builders with real updates | Five opt-in invitations on days 5,8,11,14,17,20,23,26 = **40 total**. Ask to inspect their drafting process, never ask for private LinkedIn credentials. | 40 × 40% try × 75% create account = 12 accounts; half activate = six. #1 |
| 2 · Own LinkedIn feed | Six distinct posts on days 5,10,15,20,25,29, each showing your own actual work and edit. Follow [LinkedIn professional policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies). No pods, automated comments or vote requests. | Six × 200–800 impressions × 2% visits × 25% account = 6–24 accounts. Guesses, not measured rates. #2, #3 |
| 3 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Official site advertises Monday Launchpad. Check the actual available Monday and eligibility after the reviewed legal/manual flow is ready. One maker launch, working demo, no vote swaps. | 0–150 visits × 20% signup = 0–30 accounts. #4 |
| 4 · [Product Hunt launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) | Optional later after real first-draft feedback. Check existing listing and eligibility; no automatic relaunch or asking friends for upvotes. | Zero base month-one traffic. #4 adapted |
| 5 · Active user's permissioned introduction | Ask an independently returning drafter whether one peer wants the link. They request permission before sharing contacts. At most five requests on day 24. | Five requests may produce zero to three accepted introductions; not guaranteed signups and excluded from base forecast. #5 |

[Buffer's official resources/pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing) help you understand the free alternative and current product claims; a competitor's help discussion isn't your advertising board. Reply from your own account only when you can answer a specific drafting question. No current Reddit promotion rule, newsletter feature or private founder-group access was verified. Don't invent one or turn a permission request into a group blast.

Base forecast: **18–66 accounts before overlap**, from exactly 40 invitations, six posts and one optional launch. Actual activation might be half. This does not support 1,000 accounts; at day 14 inspect factual draft usefulness and access friction before seeking a real distribution partner. Do not use the automated assistant to acquire users for its own launch.
