# Vantage Daily — Video scripts

Demonstrate reading structure and evidence. No targeted political persuasion, claimed attitude change, private beliefs or current-event claims inferred from the illustrative sample.

## A. Label-and-source demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Which part is the fact, and which part is the take?”**

2–12s: open actual public sample; keep illustrative label visible. “This demonstrates the format, not a current proposal.”

12–25s: point to the four parts. “Reported facts, implications, competing arguments and the publication's opinion have different jobs.”

25–38s: use an authorized live brief only after verified access, and inspect its actual source. Otherwise show a clearly labelled proposed source-check workflow. “A link needs to support the exact claim.”

38–50s: URL and actual access state. “Read the evidence and form your own view.” No implication a polished sample validates all live briefs.

## B. Take hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“You should be allowed to disagree with the take.”**

2–12s: show opinion label versus factual section without advocating its political conclusion. “The label should tell you what kind of claim you're reading.”

12–18s: sample/URL. “Illustrative example, not current reporting.”

## C. Source hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Two links aren't automatically two independent sources.”**

2–12s: a clearly labelled fictional sourcing diagram: two reports citing one original. “Check provenance before calling it cross-checked.” No fabricated live product result.

12–20s: actual methodology/source entry. “This is the quality question I'm testing.”

## D. Access hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“The public sample and the reader account are different.”**

2–12s: show sample and real email-link sign-in screen without entering email. “Check current access. The terms describe three free daily briefs for signed-in readers.”

12–20s: optional ₹99/month recurring offer if shown, current status precise. “No need to buy just to judge the public sample.” Don't stage billing or a successful private read.
