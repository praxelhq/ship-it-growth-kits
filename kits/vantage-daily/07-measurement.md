# Vantage Daily — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real usable reader accounts once. Put **first sourced brief completed**, **source opened** and **fact/opinion distinction understood** beside it. Waitlist emails and illustrative sample views are separate, not account activation. A subscription is a later verified billing event.

Private analytics weren't accessed; live-account access remains untested. Record actual baseline, unknown isn't zero.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Usable reader accounts / last seven days | Authentication/access records, excluding tests |
| Waitlist and invitation pending | Separate status, not summed into accounts |
| First full brief completion/source opens | Product status with no belief payload |
| Voluntary return and label comprehension | Aggregate records/opt-in feedback, no party preference |
| Source errors, corrections and paid fulfilment | Editorial QA and verified billing records |
| Traffic/course metric/rank/time | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/vantage-daily) |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding scripts. Exclude reading preferences, political inputs, history, identities and payment/sign-in tokens from replay. Track `reader_confirmed`, `first_brief_completed`, `source_opened`, `correction_reported`, `return_read`, `plus_verified` as status. Do not infer political views from reading or send them into campaign targeting.

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Five adults can access intended flow and understand sample/live distinction | Fix access or copy before wider invitations. |
| Day 14 | Readers identify one supported fact and the labelled take | Improve source/label quality, not ideological persuasion. |
| Day 21 | Voluntary return with visible corrections and privacy controls | Repair trust failures before increasing reach. |
| Day 30 | Accounts versus 1,000; sourced-read activation beside it | Report actual gap and unresolved invitation/access constraints. |

`02` estimates roughly 20–145 gross accounts before overlap if open. At 40% visitor→usable account, 1,000 needs 2,500 distinct qualified visitors; invite friction may increase that. Four posts/three clips aren't a reach guarantee. Recalculate after two posts; don't count waitlist and account for one person twice.
