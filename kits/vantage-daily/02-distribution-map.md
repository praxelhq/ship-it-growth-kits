# Vantage Daily — Distribution map

**Lead with source and opinion labels.** Distribution invites general reading comprehension, not agreement with an argument or party.

| Route | Calendar quantity / permission | Assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult readers | Five Day 5, five Day 10; fictional/public sample until real access verified. | 10 visits × 40–70% usable account = 4–7 if open; zero otherwise. | #1 |
| Two existing civic-reading/learning groups | Admin ask Day 9, one approved note Days 12/17, not party campaign groups. | 2 × 40 readers × 20–40% visits × 40% signup = 6–13 accounts if access open. | #2 |
| Four LinkedIn posts | Days 8/13/20/26 at 12:30 IST, evidence/label methodology rather than advocacy. | 4 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 40% signup = 5–72 accounts. | #3 |
| Three original short demos | Days 11/19/25, sample labelled; no targeted political message. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 40% signup = 2–48 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited reading-literacy session | Ask one existing organizer Day 16, optional eight adults Day 18. No required beliefs, votes or private preferences. | 8 × 40–60% signup = 3–5 if accepted/access open; zero otherwise. | #5 |
| Maker feedback | Optional [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) Day 22 after current rule/slot check. | Zero baseline; feedback is not an active reader network. | #6 |

These assumptions imply about 20–145 gross accounts before overlap if access is open, not a 1,000 guarantee. At 40% visit→usable account, target needs 2,500 qualified distinct visitors; waitlist/invite friction can lower conversion. Keep waitlist and reader accounts separate rather than changing the definition to whichever is larger.

No paid political ads, voter targeting, scraped emails, publisher partnerships or newsletter placements assumed. A learning organizer's free newsletter mention requires acceptance, zero baseline. Source links aren't permission to republish full articles. Don't insert advocacy under distress/communal conflict posts to gain attention.

Use different organizers from Trendizz's source-literacy exercise and other cohort news briefs. Actual dates must be agreed; a proposed Day 18 exercise isn't booked. No same-group second pitch within seven days. Evaluate whether readers can locate evidence and distinguish the take, not whether their political position moves.
