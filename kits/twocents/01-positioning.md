# TwoCents — Positioning

**Live:** https://2cents.live/ · **Category:** AI / Chrome extension · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/twocents

**Make the account-risk disclosure visible before installation.** The headline is “Grow your LinkedIn network, one comment at a time.” The CTA, “Add to Chrome, it's free,” leads to a real [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/twocents/opfdpppjfinppbhibbiiomkifbbibhok). The site offers seven free comments; Starter is $7/month and Pro $15/month. The store also publishes India prices of ₹299/₹499 including GST. Reconcile the user's actual currency at checkout before quoting a local price in outreach.

The product says users press Post and comments go through an official API. That is a product claim, not independent proof the whole extension is approved. Its own FAQ acknowledges risk because [LinkedIn restricts extensions that read or modify its site](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387). Never advertise “ban-proof” or treat a daily cap as permission. The kit writer inspected the listing, not an installed or authenticated workflow.

## Your first user

A job switcher who already reads ten people in their field, has something specific to contribute, and loses track of conversations. They are not buying a thousand generic comments. They want to remember who they spoke with and write a useful sentence without sounding like an engagement bot.

Start with adults who understand the extension's permissions and can choose whether the account risk suits them. Keep placement-clinic outreach separate from Postifyy's post-writing session. Do not make students install it as a condition of attending a free workshop.

## The pitch and wedge

> Write a comment you mean, then remember the conversation. TwoCents suggests a short reply and keeps your relationship notes; you review what gets posted.

Short version: **Thoughtful comments, plus a memory for the people behind them.**

Your wedge is returning to the same relevant people, not commenting on every trending post. The smart CRM's last-interaction context is more defensible than “AI comments in your voice,” which many free tools promise. Demonstrate a useful follow-up to a previous discussion without exposing anyone's private notes.

## Alternatives and the honest reason to switch

[ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) offers general drafting without buying a commenting subscription. [Grammarly Free](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) is $0/month for basic writing assistance. A spreadsheet and manually written comments cost nothing and avoid installing a LinkedIn overlay. TwoCents has to earn its extra friction by saving the context of actual relationships and producing drafts worth editing, not by inflating comment volume.

The store showed **29 users and 16 ratings** when checked on 29 October, updated 26 October. That is a dated listing observation, not a measure of active customers, outcome quality or independent compliance. Don't turn a small review count into “trusted by thousands.”

The target is **1,000 consumer signups**, counted here as new, deduplicated installs; activation is first reviewed, user-posted comment. Report both. The current small-account plan does not establish a thousand installs. If account risk blocks adoption, a standalone manual drafting option is worth testing, but don't claim one exists before it does.
