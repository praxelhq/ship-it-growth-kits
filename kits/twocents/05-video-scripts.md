# TwoCents — Video scripts

Record your own public example discussion with permission. Blur account identifiers and never show private CRM notes. Use your own voice, readable captions and simple screen cuts. No paid production or rendered video needed.

## A. A suggestion you actually edit —65 seconds

**Use:** website/store gallery and Peerlist. **First 2 seconds:** “Read the post before writing the comment.”

| Time | Shot | Spoken line / text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Your own public example post | Read the post before writing the comment. |
| 2–12 s | Highlight one concrete detail | I have something to add about this detail, not just “great insights.” |
| 12–25 s | Open the actual extension suggestion | TwoCents gives me a starting point. It isn't my judgment. |
| 25–40 s | Edit a generic sentence into your real observation | I change anything I wouldn't say and check the context again. |
| 40–48 s | Show final review; post only to your permitted example if chosen | I decide whether to post. Skipping is fine. |
| 48–57 s | Show a synthetic relationship card | This helps me remember a conversation, not pressure someone for a reply. |
| 57–65 s | URL, seven-free limit and account-risk disclosure | Seven comments free. This is a Chrome extension with LinkedIn terms risk; read the permissions first. |

Never simulate an installed workflow if it doesn't actually work. You can record a manual demonstration while testing, but label it as such.

## B. Delete “great insights” —20 seconds

**Where:** personal LinkedIn. **Hook:** “This comment could fit any post.” 0–5 s: show a generic compliment on your synthetic example. 5–13 s: replace it with one specific detail plus your real perspective. 13–20 s: say, “If you can't name what you're responding to, don't post yet.” End with a founder disclosure and URL. The lesson stands without installing anything.

## C. More comments isn't the goal — 30 seconds

**Where:** adult alumni session and LinkedIn. **Hook:** “You don't need to reply to everyone.” Show a list of three chosen professional topics, then one relevant public discussion. Say, “Choose people whose work you actually read. Add something useful. Return when you have a genuine reason.” Show a synthetic relationship reminder; dismiss one as not relevant. End, “A reminder isn't an obligation to contact someone.”

## D. Before you install —35 seconds

**Where:** landing page next to installation and store screenshots. **Hook:** “An official API doesn't answer every permissions question.” Show the actual public permissions/disclosure text and the platform policy link. Explain in plain words that the tool reads relevant public-post context, the user reviews comments, and remaining extension risk must be considered. Don't imply you have independently audited the API. End on “Choose for your own account; no installation required for the workshop.”

Bank source-post, draft, edit, review and synthetic relationship-card clips. Use actual output imperfections as teaching material, not fake viral notifications or invented DM replies.
