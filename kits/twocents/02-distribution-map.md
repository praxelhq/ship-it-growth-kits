Specific accounts to work with are the actual people you already follow for your field: a former colleague, a public hiring manager's relevant post, an alumni organizer. This kit deliberately doesn't invent influencers, guaranteed reply routes or newsletter features. An unsolicited plug under a job loss or a personal achievement is inappropriate even if technically postable.

[LinkedIn's own policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) is broader than “don't auto-post.” Do not tell users to ignore an account warning or evade a restriction. Human approval is necessary for authenticity, but not proof that an overlay complies with platform terms.

Lynkr's shared-campus invitation window is Days 4–6, SignalPost Days 10–12, Postifyy Days 17–20, yours Days 24–26. Coordinate actual dates and use a distinct adult job-switcher audience. One organization receives one primary product invitation per week.

Eight owned posts,20 warm invitations, one conditional session and a directory listing might yield **5–25 activated installs**, with deduplication. That is an early adoption experiment, not an evidenced route to 1,000. Improving listing conversion and earning voluntary referrals from people who genuinely continue the conversations is the next hypothesis; don't manufacture reach to close the gap.
