# TELOS — Copy pack

## 1. Provider opener
Hi — do you run the plumbing business, and how do you remember the next step on each active job?

I am building TELOS. It keeps the lead, agreed timeline, updates and files with the project. I am not promising new leads. I want to test whether one of your existing jobs is easier to manage this way than through scattered calls and messages.

Could we look at your current process for five minutes at a quiet time?

## 2. Follow-up after interest
We can test one real job you are authorized to manage: the customer's request, the next agreed action and who needs the update. Before you create an account, I will confirm actual provider pricing/commission and what the business-review process means.

The business entry is https://telos.codes/business/login
Please do not put a customer's private details into a public demonstration. If this is not useful, tell me and I will stop. One follow-up after seven days, then close.

## 3. Introduction request
Do you know an independent plumbing-business owner with several active jobs? Please ask whether they want to test a next-step/project tracker. I do not need their customer list, and I am not asking them to buy leads.

## 4. Customer-update message, sent by the provider
We are using this project's private workspace to keep the agreed next step and updates together. Please check the scope and timeline with us before any work changes. Use only the access link your provider confirms for your project; do not forward it.

Send only after project sharing/permissions have been tested. Never use a public sample project as if it were the client's live workspace.
