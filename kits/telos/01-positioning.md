# TELOS — Positioning

**Live:** https://telos.codes/ · **Category:** Service-business marketplace/workspace · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/telos

Your headline is **“Trusted professionals for every project.”** **Become a professional** opens /business/login, where an owner can choose Create account, email or Google access. Customer categories route to /customer/login. No price or commission was established on the inspected public screens; neither account creation nor a live project was submitted.

Your page covers plumbers, electricians, website builders and more. That is too broad for your first ten. Start with **independent plumbing businesses in one reachable neighbourhood**, each with current real jobs and an owner who tracks leads/next steps by phone.

**Pitch:** Keep your plumbing leads, agreed next steps and active client projects in one workspace.
**Short version:** One place for the next step on every plumbing job.

The marketplace can become useful later. First prove a business can run an existing job through the portal; do not promise new leads or verified customers merely because category buttons exist.

The named alternatives are [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) for business discovery and a manual [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) for tracking existing jobs. Public browsing/manual Sheets do not require a paid new platform; premium directory services are separate and not in this budget. TELOS must improve progress visibility and client communication, not pretend providers have no existing lead source.

**The weakness is trust without visible process.** The site says every business is reviewed, but this read-only inspection did not establish how review works. Define what “verified” means before inviting customers.

**Target: 10 supply-side service businesses**, each with its own actual business/project onboarded and independent recent use. Thirty consumer enquiries or ten tradespeople from one firm do not count as ten customers.
