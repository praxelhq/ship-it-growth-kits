# TELOS — Measurement

**Target: 10 real supply-side service businesses in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is a business account; activation is the owner's real business/project using an agreed next-step/update workflow.

All four tests apply: genuine business, actual business/job data onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, privately known owner name/phone. Payment is not mandatory. Exclude empty profiles, fabricated requests, customer-only accounts and multiple staff at one firm.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag; mask client/job/contact data. Record business accounts, reviewed providers, actual projects, completed client updates, active last seven days and genuine inbound requests. Private values were unavailable; record actuals rather than zero. Save course rank, reading and sync at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/telos).

Events: business_account_created, provider_review_completed, real_project_started, next_step_updated, client_update_viewed, independent_return. Keep public acquisition metrics separate from private project activity.
