# TELOS — Video scripts

Use synthetic job/contact data unless everyone has agreed to a real example. Never film inside a customer's home for a marketing clip without permission.

## A.65-second provider demo
0–2: three sticky notes; **“Which job needs a call today?”**
2–15: show business entry and one sample job. “A lead needs a next step, not just a name.”
15–35: set an agreed action/timeline and attach a harmless sample reference.
35–50: show the customer preview, clearly labelled, and the provider's updated status.
50–65: “Test the workflow on one permitted real job. We confirm provider terms and approval before onboarding. telos.codes.”

## B.20-second hook — local-business follow-up
0–2: message list, **“The update is somewhere in here.”**
2–12: show a sample project consolidating one agreed next step.
12–20: “Keep the conversation with the job, not in memory.”

## C.25-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: marketplace category grid, **“A listing isn't a customer.”**
2–15: “A real provider still needs a real job and a client who gets the right update.”
15–22: show the tested project loop.
22–25: full URL.

Use phone screen recordings, captions and hard cuts. Do not promise guaranteed leads, jobs or verified service quality from a staged demo.
