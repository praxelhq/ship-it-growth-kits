# Daytrac — Video scripts

Use fictional sample records only. No private health screenshot, medical claim, real wearable account, OAuth token or unsupported native-app footage. Show release limitations on screen.

## A. Source-and-gap demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Where did this number come from?”**

2–12s: open Explore a demo, show fictional banner/sample source. “These are fictional records, not anyone's health.”

12–25s: inspect a metric/source and recorded-day denominator. “A missing day stays missing; a source label matters.” Don't diagnose from a number.

25–38s: Devices explanation of preference/fallback. “Overlapping daily totals shouldn't be added together.” Show actual text, not an invented ingestion result.

38–50s: privacy/release status and URL. “General wellness. iPhone release and subscription checkout aren't available yet.”

## B. Missing-day hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“No record isn't a zero.”**

2–12s: actual demo denominator/gap, or clearly labelled synthetic test after verified. “The chart should tell you how many days it knows about.”

12–18s: URL/sample label. No sleep/heart result interpreted as medical information.

## C. Connector hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A logo isn't a completed sync.”**

2–12s: show actual awaiting-setup/manual-available labels. “Check what works today and what needs an account, permission or unreleased app.”

12–20s: native-release disclosure/URL. Don't simulate successful OAuth or show a paid app listing that doesn't exist.

## D. Storage hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“This demo stays in this session.”**

2–12s: show sample footer and actual storage dialog. “Browser mode and account mode aren't the same storage promise.”

12–20s: source/choice controls, no clicking Delete. “Understand where records go before importing your own.” No claim of independent security audit.
