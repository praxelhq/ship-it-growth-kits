Approximately 27–165 gross demo/intro sessions before overlap, not signups. Conversion from these introductions to genuine own-space tracking starters is unknown until readiness is verified. If 60% of qualified visitors later start genuine own-space tracking, 1,000 would require about 1,667 distinct visitors; that is an unvalidated conditional rate, not the demo conversion. Real own-record activation will be smaller and must be separately measured; don't count every fictional metric as a conversion. Four clips don't guarantee reach.

No paid wearable creators, gym fees, medical communities or purchased devices. Don't post in patient/support groups as a health intervention. An organizer's free newsletter mention requires permission, zero until accepted. Source/connector names aren't co-marketing partnerships; no access to their users is assumed.

c1 wellbeing demo preference Days 24–27, c2 wellness Days 15–18. Actual dates and different organizers need confirmation. No same-group second pitch within seven days. Don't schedule a blood/heart/sleep-data sharing session; a screen walkthrough with fictional data is sufficient for format feedback.
