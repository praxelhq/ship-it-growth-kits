# Daytrac — Positioning

**Live:** https://daytrac.org/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/daytrac

**Begin with honest records and gaps, not an all-devices-connected promise.** Actual headline checked in browser 29 October: “Different devices. One continuous you.” Actions “Start my journey” and “Explore a demo.” Demo opens fictional sample metrics, explicitly labelled sample sources and “Changes stay in this demo session.” No health information, account, connector, import or permission was submitted.

Your first user is an adult who already records a small number of daily totals and wants to understand which source a number came from. They don't need another wearable purchase or a clinical readiness score. Start with manual records and one understandable source-selection rule before advertising every connector.

**Pitch:** Keep a few daily wellness totals together, see their sources and missing days, and reflect without treating estimates as diagnosis.

**Bio:** Daily records, with their sources and gaps intact.

Browser Devices says manual totals/Daytrac JSON import “Available now,” Apple Health “Awaiting iPhone setup,” smart rings via Apple Health, and account connector buttons for Oura/Strava/Polar/Withings. Those buttons weren't tested. Privacy & data explicitly says **subscription checkout and iPhone release not yet available**; AI reflections need a configured cloud service. Do not tell readers to download a released iPhone app or promise functioning OAuth because a logo is present. No current paid price was established.

The same dialog distinguishes browser-local data from account data in a connected database. That is a product disclosure, not a security audit. It says general wellness, not medical care. Source preferences choose/fallback per metric rather than summing overlaps; missing is not zero. Those are useful principles to verify on real authorized records.

The no-additional-app-price alternative for an existing compatible iPhone owner is built-in [Apple Health](https://www.apple.com/ios/health/); hardware/feature availability varies, and no new hardware is included in this plan. A simple spreadsheet is another $0 record log. Daytrac's difference must be readable provenance and optional cross-source organization, not a claim existing tools cannot show health data.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups** as distinct genuine own-space tracking-session starters under the no-account policy (or confirmed accounts if the verified flow requires one), excluding fictional demo-only visits. Put an actual record saved/source understood and voluntary return beside it. Label demos separately: a fictional sample step count is neither a user's achievement nor a connected device. No medical improvement, wearable accuracy or released native app proof is established here.
