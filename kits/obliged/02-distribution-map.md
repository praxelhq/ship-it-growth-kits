# Obliged — Distribution map

**Restaurant-owner design research first; live acquisition only after the release/legal-review gate.** No mass email, paid directory or claim a regulatory authority endorses the product.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Local owner introductions | Ask ten known contacts to request an introduction to an actual owner in your selected supported city. No licence scans or private contacts sent without permission. Assumed 3–5 conversations; 0–1 live pilot if the release gate passes. | #1 |
| 2 · Restaurant-owned public business routes | Manually qualify one real owner and a current document/admin problem. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. Hypothetical 20% response × 40% permitted live pilot × 50% independent use ≈ 1–2 restaurants after release; research interviews aren't customers. | #2 |
| 3 · Qualified local professional review | Two private requests to review actual proposed city/coverage boundaries using synthetic records. No professional-pool promise, assumed free appointment or legal endorsement. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, explain document recorded versus authority approved using a synthetic record. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 useful visits, possibly zero. No claimed fine avoided or deadline guarantee. | #4 |
| 5 · Owner-approved small walkthrough | Only after the owner agrees; no access to staff or business groups assumed. A synthetic demo is design feedback until real services are ready. | #3 |

Specific source to inspect is the product's own release-status/coverage screen, then the responsible city's actual authority material with a qualified professional. This kit doesn't establish a legal checklist or interpret those rules. [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/) is a checked business-presence source, not a permit register or permission to scrape owners.

No restaurant newsletter placement, association-group access or professional referral partnership was verified. If an organizer asks for a free opt-in blurb, use #3 with the sample/review boundary. Base **1–3 live restaurants** is below ten and can be zero if storage/sign-in/coverage aren't ready. At day 14 choose a real scoped release or keep research separate; don't market the sample as a completed product.
