# Obliged — Measurement

**Target:10 real restaurant businesses.** Activation beside it: **owners independently maintaining a permitted actual document/action record under reviewed local scope**. A synthetic sample, design interview or calendar download isn't a live business customer.

## Day 0

Record release state, actual authorized restaurants, reviewed-city coverage, live reminders/storage and independent returns from your own records. The sample's two fictional outlets establish no customer baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding private documents. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/obliged) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero; no actual price/billing was established.

Events only after real functions exist: `live_scope_authorized`, `real_document_added`, `trigger_reviewed`, `action_recorded`, `notification_delivered` and `restaurant_returned`. Calendar download isn't notification delivery. Recorded submission isn't authority approval. A document match isn't proof of compliance. Keep all sample events separate.

## Four tests and release gate

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a legitimate restaurant business, its own permitted actual workflow onboarded, independent use in the final seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required. Two outlets of one business aren't automatically two customers. If sign-in/vault/qualified scope isn't ready, the current release doesn't support ten live customers.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 20% reply ×40% permitted live pilot ×50% independent use ≈1–2 restaurants. Ten warm requests may add 0–1. **Base 1–3 is below ten and can be zero if live gates fail.** These aren't measured restaurant-compliance acquisition rates. Recompute after 20 approaches from actual permitted workflow evidence, not polite interest.
