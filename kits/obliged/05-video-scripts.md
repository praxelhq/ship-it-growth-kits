# Obliged — Video scripts

Use only the disclosed synthetic sample. No real licence, restaurant address, legal conclusion or authority approval. Screen recording and phone voice are enough.

## A · What actually needs action? · 60 seconds · owner/reviewer call

0–2 s hook: **“Is this a real deadline—or is the trigger missing?”** Show a synthetic undated item. 2–15 s: open source/document context: “The record should say what was actually reviewed.”15–30 s: inspect the missing trigger and scope: “If information is missing, don't guess a date.”30–45 s: show a recorded submission: “Submitted by the team is not approved by an authority.”45–55 s: show sample/release limits: “This is a synthetic walkthrough, not a live compliance service.”55–60 s: “Qualified local review before real use. Obliged dot site.”

## B · A file isn't compliance · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“Uploading the document doesn't settle the question.”**0–8 s: show a synthetic matched document. 8–15 s: “Applicability, expiry trigger and authority status still need the right review.”15–20 s: “Keep the unresolved question visible.” No fine-avoidance claim.

## C · A calendar entry isn't a delivered reminder · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“Will this actually notify the owner?”**2–12 s: show the current calendar-download/email-off boundary. 12–20 s: “A scheduled record and a delivered message are different. Test the actual channel before relying on it.”20–25 s: “The sample should say what works today.”

Don't stage Google sign-in, vault upload or an expert acceptance that isn't live. A truthful limitation is more useful than a fake complete-product montage.
