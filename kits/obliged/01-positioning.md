# Obliged — Positioning

**Live:** https://obliged.site/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/obliged

**The current site is a synthetic sample, not a live restaurant compliance service.** Keep the assigned ten-business target, but don't count demo visitors or ask owners to rely on draft legal coverage. Real-data onboarding is a release gate.

The public client headlines **“Compliance documents, renewals and fine print in one place.”** It offers **“Sign in with Google”** and **“Explore the sample workspace.”** The sample's settings explicitly say Google setup pending, private vault storage not connected, regulatory coverage draft, daily email off and professional pool not active. The visible workspace says all records are synthetic and nothing is filed without the user's action. No live subscription price was established.

## A credible first buyer

Choose an independent restaurant owner in **one supported city**, with an authorized local professional reviewing the scope. The sample names New York City and Mumbai; choose one, not both, for the first pilot. Don't supply legal interpretations, statutory deadlines or compliance assurances from the draft library.

> Keep reviewed restaurant documents and their actual next actions together, with a qualified professional checking what is covered and what remains outside scope.

Bio: **A reviewed restaurant-document record, not a compliance guarantee.**

The first conversation uses synthetic documents and asks whether the owner understands the action record. Real use waits for functioning sign-in, private storage/access, actual reminders and qualified local coverage review. This is a pilot plan, not a claim those missing services are already available.

## The real alternative

The owner may already use a folder/calendar and a qualified professional. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace and can track manual tasks, but doesn't establish legal coverage or secure document custody. No restaurant-compliance competitor price was verified; don't invent one or imply an expert review is free.

## The weakness

“Submitted” in the sample means the user recorded a filing, not an authority approved it. A document match isn't proof of compliance; missing trigger dates shouldn't become invented deadlines. The professional pool isn't a current referral offer. Ten customers require real restaurants, permitted actual workflows, independent final-week use and a callable responsible contact privately. If the release remains a sample, the customer result can honestly be zero.
