# Obliged — 30-day calendar

Start with release/coverage gates. Owner design interviews can proceed with synthetic data, but real customer acquisition waits for permitted live capability. Daily:20 minutes replies and ten minutes private permission/readiness log. Four first approaches on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 = **40 total**; ten warm requests on day 4. One follow-up after seven days, stop on no.
