# Bond — Distribution map

**Start with one city and an existing trust network.** You need a real host and enough willing people at one time, not a nationwide launch.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Twenty willing adult prospects | One supported city and one interest. First ten are a listening/entry-flow test; next ten see a real confirmed pilot offer. No contact list scraping. | Up to 20 accounts, 4–10 genuine pilot participants if the offer fits. | #1 |
| Two existing alumni/colleague groups | Admin-approved local invitation with actual city/date/price on the product page. No unsolicited group additions. | 2 × 40 relevant readers × 10–25% signup = 8–20 accounts. | #2 |
| Three own-account clips | Public entry/real pilot details, not stock-member proof, Days 8, 16, 25. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 20% signup = 1–24 accounts. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | New-city group-building lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 20% signup = 1–12 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited alumni organizer | Ask for one free distribution of a genuine local plan; no implied university endorsement. | 10–20 relevant readers/attendees if accepted, zero otherwise. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional builder feedback after city/offer/entry is clear; current rules checked. | Zero acquisition baseline; feedback from builders is not local circle supply. | #6 |

No paid hosts, venue subsidy, influencer or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. If the activity has a participant cost, disclose it clearly and do not fund it from an imaginary marketing budget. An existing organizer's free newsletter mention is optional, zero until accepted.

At 20% visitor-to-member, 1,000 needs 5,000 distinct qualified visits. These first tests may yield only dozens. The larger constraint is real local capacity. Coordinate a different city/neighbourhood and contact segment from the cohort's café, building-network and meetup products; do not send several invitations to one group in a week.
