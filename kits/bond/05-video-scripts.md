# Bond — Video scripts

Show real available pilot details or label the screen a proposal. No footage implying strangers are members without permission.

## A. First-offer demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Before joining a circle, what is the actual plan?”**

2–15s, show city, interest, host/date/cost once real. “A small local offer should answer these before signup.”

15–30s, show the actual entry flow without creating another person's account. “Then decide whether this is for you.”

30–42s, show leave/cancel/report information after verification. “Joining doesn't remove your choice.”

42–50s: URL and actual pilot status. If it isn't open, say so; don't fake a live event.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “Same city isn't the whole plan.”

2–10s: show an actual interest/time/cost card. “A reason, a time and enough willing people.”

10–15s: product URL. Text: **Check current pilot details**.

## C. Empty-network lesson — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “A thousand scattered signups can still feel empty.”

2–12s: show a fictional city map clearly labelled. “Start with one circle that can actually meet.”

12–20s: real supported-city details if available. “That's the first Bond test.” No invented density or attendance.

## D. Real learning clip — 20 seconds

0–2s: “What made people hesitate before joining?”

2–12s: explain one genuine, anonymized interface/offer question from willing testers. No personal story or safety accusation.

12–20s: show the actual fix. If you have no feedback yet, use the offer-explanation demo instead of inventing a quote.
