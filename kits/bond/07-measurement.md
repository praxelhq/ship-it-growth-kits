# Bond — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real member accounts once. Report **real circles joined**, **voluntarily confirmed first-plan participation** and **second-plan interest/return** beside it. A lifestyle-image view is not membership.

No private records were accessed; no public price/city inventory was visible at entry. Record actual baseline and offer first.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Real members by actually supported city | Authentication/member records, no tests |
| Live circles with real host/time/cost/capacity | Operating records, not sample cards |
| Join/leave and actual plan participation | Aggregate status plus voluntary confirmation |
| Waitlist versus open membership | Separate records; don't combine |
| Traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics / [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Course metric/rank/time | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/bond) |

Check existing Clarity before adding scripts. Mask personal contact, location and private-circle content. Track `offer_viewed`, `member_confirmed`, `real_circle_joined`, `participation_confirmed`, `circle_left`, `second_plan_joined` as status only. Do not infer attendance from a page open or precise location tracking.

| Checkpoint | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Can ten willing people understand one genuine local offer? | Fix price/access/detail gaps before broader promotion. |
| Day 14 | Is a real circle operating rather than just collecting accounts? | Concentrate host/time/capacity; don't add cities. |
| Day 21 | Do people voluntarily want another plan? | Ask about fit and clarity, not fake popularity. |
| Day 30 | Actual members against 1,000; local participation beside it | Report honest target gap and operating capacity. |

At 20% visitor-to-member, 1,000 needs 5,000 relevant unique visits. The small scheduled tests may yield only dozens, and a larger audience doesn't create local capacity automatically. Replace assumptions by Day 14.

Count payments only against the actual verified offer, not the word “premium.” Course 24-hour traffic readings overlap and aren't cumulative members. Don't claim verified safety, friendships formed or successful events from signups alone.
