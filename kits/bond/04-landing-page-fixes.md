# Bond — Landing page fixes

Based on the live public entry viewed in a browser on 29 October. No account, terms acceptance, circle or payment was submitted.

## 1. Publish the actual first offer before GET STARTED

The entry shows **“GET STARTED”**, but no public city, price, free allowance or real circle. Add those facts before registration. If the pilot is not open, say **“Pilot not open yet—see what we're testing.”** Do not turn an unavailable service into a signup promise.

## 2. Make one circle concrete

Current headline: **“Find your people. Same city, same vibe.”** Support calls Bond a premium concierge.

Suggested heading: **“A small local circle, with a real first plan.”** Support: **“See the supported city, activity, host, time and cost before deciding whether to join.”** Button: **“See the current pilot details.”** Use it only after those details exist.

## 3. Explain the terms before continuing

The page says continuing agrees to Terms and Privacy. Make those actual documents accessible before the entry action, with clear membership/cancellation details. Don't make someone accept an undefined offer just to find the price.

## 4. Label editorial imagery and examples

Lifestyle imagery is not evidence of Bond attendees. Use **“Illustrative image”** where appropriate. A circle card should distinguish an example from live availability and identify a real host only with permission. Never imply verified safety/background checks unless those processes exist and their scope is clear.

## 5. Test the first member journey

Fresh visitor → offer → terms → account → actual circle → first-plan information → leave/cancel/report route. Test no available circles and a full/cancelled plan. Suggested empty state: **“No suitable circle is open here yet. Don't make plans based on this example.”** Fix failures before acquisition.

## 6. Check local/phone clarity

At 390px, test long city names, keyboard, back navigation and the first-plan details. Do not request precise location before explaining why it is needed; a supported city choice may be enough. Preview: **“Bond — a small local circle with clear first-plan details.”** No fake member count, friendship guarantee or unsupported verification badge.
