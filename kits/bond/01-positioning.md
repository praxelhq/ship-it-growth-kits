**Live:** https://bond-968340508411.asia-southeast1.run.app/ · **Category:** Local social circles · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/bond

The actual browser-visible headline checked 29 October is **“Find your people. Same city, same vibe.”** The CTA is **“GET STARTED”**, with a separate **“LOG IN.”** The page calls Bond a premium social concierge for local circles, with nightlife/food/wellness themes. No price, first city, live circle inventory or free allowance was visible before that entry action.

**Your first job is to make the offer inspectable.** Don't advertise a free city community or guaranteed introductions when the public page doesn't establish either. The entry screen was opened in a browser; signup, terms acceptance and circle participation were not completed.

Your first member is an adult who recently moved to the one city you can actually serve and wants a small, specific group activity. They do not need every interest and every city on Day 1. Choose one supported city, one theme and one real gathering window before outreach.

**Pitch:** Join a small local circle around something you genuinely want to do, with clear details before you commit.

**Short version:** One city. One interest. A real first plan.

## The wedge

A food/coffee or another genuinely supported low-complexity circle with a real host, date, cost and attendance expectation. This is an operating choice for your pilot, not a claim that the app already has bookings, verification or hosting features. Don't promise access to an “exclusive” group that doesn't exist.

The $0 alternative is a [WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) group with friends or colleagues arranging a plan. Bond earns attention by helping a newcomer find a suitable willing circle, if it can actually deliver that first event. It does not win by adding another empty feed or a glamorous stock image.

## The uncomfortable bit

The public page's lifestyle image and “premium concierge” language aren't evidence of active members, safe hosts or successful gatherings. Show the actual operating model and limitations. No fictional attendees, unverified safety guarantees or invented testimonials.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 confirmed members, with a genuine circle joined and a voluntarily confirmed first gathering beside it. A thousand signups spread over ten empty cities would be a weaker result than one functioning local circle.
