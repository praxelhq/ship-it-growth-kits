# Depthlab — Positioning

**Live:** https://depthlab.me/ · **Category:** Education · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/depthlab

**Your paid membership is not open, and a read brief is not proof of understanding.** The homepage lists ₹499/month but explicitly says payments are not open and directs people to a waitlist. Keep that distinction in every invitation. Sell the actual learning loop first, not a subscription you cannot deliver.

Current headline: “Know it well enough to explain it.” “Explore the library” opens 36 research briefs across 12 subjects. A checked philosophy brief has an explanation, worked example, limits, sources and a practice prompt. Its discussion route offers speaking or typing; the review page asks for sign-in to see the queue. These pages were read. No answer, microphone session, magic-link email, review submission or payment was sent.

Your first learner is an adult university student who can recognize a concept in a paragraph but struggles to explain it without the text. The wedge is **read one brief, close the explanation, teach it back, apply it to a new case, then return later**. Start with one 12-minute brief rather than asking the learner to explore all 12 subjects.

**Pitch:** Read one idea, explain it in your own words, and test whether you can use it somewhere new.

**Bio:** Don't stop at recognizing the idea. Explain and apply it.

[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/about) offers a free core learning platform, and [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) provides free materials with no registration required. Your difference cannot be “learning content exists online.” It is the short path from one sourced question to a teach-back, application and later retrieval, if the actual feedback/review flow works.

Your weakness is the credibility of feedback. A confident tutor response isn't a validated assessment. The checked brief itself says it has not had specialist peer review. Retain source-access notes and intellectual uncertainty; don't promise mastery, grades or improved reasoning from a single automated score.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**, using the real account event once verified or the permitted no-account meaningful-session definition if that is the live first-use path. Report completed teach-back/application attempts and later reviews beside it. Waitlist emails and page opens stay separate.
