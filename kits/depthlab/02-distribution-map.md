# Depthlab — Distribution map

**Run a small explain-it-without-looking session, not a generic course launch.** The first learner should encounter a concept she can actually attempt, then see the limits of the feedback.

|Route|Specific action and boundary|Planning outcome, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Existing campus learning/discussion organizer|One permitted six-person teach-back session in Days 4–7, using a public 12-minute brief. Different organizer from the book-community launches.|6 invites × 80% attend × 60% attempt = about 3 teach-backs.|#1,#4|
|Ten classmates interested in a real topic|Five permission-first notes Days 9/16. Ask for a typed attempt, no compulsory microphone or public grade.|10 × 50% start × 50% attempt = 2–3 activations.|#5|
|Your LinkedIn feed|Four posts Days 8/15/22/29 at 09:00 IST, one original learning question with sources and no claimed efficacy.|4 × assumed 200 impressions × 3% visits × 30% start = about 7 starts; zero possible.|#2|
|Your own YouTube channel|Six original explain/apply clips and one demo under [YouTube policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/).|6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits.|#3,videos|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Usable brief/discussion route; state payment-not-open status. Check current slot, no votes requested.|0–10 relevant testers, optional upside.|#6|

[MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/) and [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/about) are verified educational alternatives and sources of expectations, not places to advertise to their students. Your own organizer bulletin can carry #1 after permission. No external newsletter coverage or institutional endorsement is assumed.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after a real teach-back/feedback loop works: personal maker account, inspect existing listings and never solicit upvotes. General Reddit communities aren't in the forecast because no relevant current promotion permission was verified.

The initial plan supports tens of learning starts, not a demonstrated 1,000. At an assumed 50% visit→meaningful-start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. Don't use 12 subjects or 36 briefs as a substitute for that audience. Scale the session people voluntarily repeat, rather than collecting only a waitlist for planned billing.
