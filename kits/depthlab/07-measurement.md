# Depthlab — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Use the actual account-created event once verified. If meaningful practice is available without an account, the permitted alternate headline is distinct new learning-session starts, labelled clearly and not added to accounts. Waitlist emails and passive brief views are separate.

Activation is a **completed teach-back plus application attempt with a returned result**, not an inferred understanding score. Also track later voluntary review attempts. These show product use, not proven mastery, academic outcomes or a validated assessment.

## Day 0

Record existing accounts, meaningful practice starts, completed attempts and later-review counts from your own system; private values were unavailable during research. Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/depthlab) reading, period and rank. Check [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is correctly connected on public surfaces without answer text, voice, email or private conversation content in replay/events.

Minimum aggregate events: `brief_opened`, `practice_started`, `teachback_submitted`, `application_submitted`, `feedback_returned`, `account_created` and `later_review_attempted`. Keep microphone capture/retention choices separate from acquisition counts. A planned ₹499 membership or a test checkout is not revenue; payments are currently described as not open.

## Honest funnel and decisions

The calendar has six session invitations, ten classmate notes, four owned-feed posts and six Shorts. At an assumed 50% visit→meaningful-start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. It doesn't establish that reach. At an assumed 50% start→completed-attempt rate, 1,000 starts produce 500 activations; replace both inputs with observed results.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Starts, submitted answers, returned results and source errors|If reading doesn't become an attempt, fix first-question clarity before inviting more.|
|Day 14|Later retrieval attempts and feedback complaints|Wrong/overconfident feedback takes priority over distribution.|
|Day 21|Voluntary review, account-return success and typed/voice friction|Don't call page rereads spaced-practice completion.|
|Day 30|Accounts or defined starts, completed attempts, later reviews, actual reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000 without claiming a measured learning gain.|

Keep weekly actual aggregate rows with best source and hours. 36 briefs are editorial supply; a genuine learner attempting a new case is the activation to report beside signups.
