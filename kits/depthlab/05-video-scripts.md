# Depthlab — Video scripts

Record your own reading/answer and cite the public brief. No learner answer, voice or score from another person without consent. No paid rendering.

## A. Close the brief — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “Can I explain it after closing the text?”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–12s|Actual brief title, source and duration|“I choose one question, not all twelve subjects.”|
|12–25s|Your own short notes, then hide the explanation|“Reading is only the first step.”|
|25–40s|Type an original teach-back in the real discussion screen|“Now I explain it without copying.”|
|40–52s|Actual feedback/new application only after tested|“A new case checks where my understanding stops. Feedback isn't a certified grade.”|
|52–60s|URL and payment-not-open note|“Try the public learning flow; the planned paid membership isn't open yet.”|

If feedback doesn't complete, show the blocker; don't invent a response.

## B. Same answer, different reason — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A lucky answer can still be right.” Show your own simple illustration of a correct guess versus a checked method. Say: “The question is how you got there. Try explaining the difference without the text.” Link the relevant brief/source, not a claim you proved a theory.

## C. Type or speak — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “You don't have to record your voice to practise.” Show the actual typed-answer field and visible microphone-off state. Say: “Use the mode you choose. Read the capture/save controls before speaking.” No microphone permission or user recording is needed for this clip.

## D. Return later — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Recognizing it today isn't recalling it next week.” Show a real review prompt, if implemented, then attempt it yourself before reopening the source. Say: “I want to retrieve the idea, not just recognize the paragraph.” No invented retention percentage or grade improvement.
