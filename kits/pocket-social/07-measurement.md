# Pocket Social — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **one permitted non-sensitive card reviewed and retrieved on a later day**. A fictional test card is quality evidence, excluded from real traction. Record account creation, first save, review, later lookup and independent return separately. Ten people inside one account are not ten users.

## Day 0

Record actual accounts, saved reviewed cards, successful later lookup, independent returns and real purchases/refunds from owner records. No private baseline was established in this research. Capture fresh [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/pocket-social) rank, saved metric and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Published free limits and lifetime-plan terms don't establish paid sales.

The [policy](https://pocketellie.social/privacy) says Clarity runs only on public landing/pricing/legal/login pages, with login fields masked, and never on signed-in cards/PIN screens. Preserve that boundary; don't add a second script or private-page recording to measure activation. Internal aggregate events must avoid names, facts, dictation transcripts, photos, quizzes about real people and PIN. Proposed counts only if genuinely supported: `account_created`, `card_saved`, `fact_reviewed`, `card_retrieved_later`, `independent_return`, `export_requested`, `purchase_confirmed`.

Don't interpret a quiz score as better friendship or human memory improvement. Don't inspect real private cards to grade activation; use content-free events or a voluntary workflow response. Keep backend correction/deletion tests synthetic and authorized.

## Forecast from `06`

Assumptions, not social-memory benchmarks:

- 30 invitations × 50% try × 70% create account ≈ **10–11 accounts**.
- Six posts × 200–700 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup = **6–21 accounts**.
- One accepted session: 15 invited × 60% attend × 50% signup ≈ **four**, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–150 visits × 20% signup = **0–30 accounts**.

Rounded base **16–66 accounts before overlap**, lower after deduplication. Half activating would be roughly 8–33 later-card users, not all 66. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, the target requires 4,000 qualified visits. The calendar doesn't supply that scale. Keep the gap to 1,000 and improve observed conversion/return before forecasting a new partnership.
