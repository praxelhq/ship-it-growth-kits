# Pocket Social — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, rendered `/auth`, terms/privacy/refunds read 29 October 2026. Account entry is real; no account, dictation, PIN, AI sorting, sync, export/deletion or payment was tested. Policies describe behaviour; they are not independent proof it works.

## 1. Replace perfect recall with a reviewable task

**Current headline:** “Ellie never forgets, so you don't have to.”

**Write:** **“Keep the small details you want to remember.”** Subtext: **“Save a permitted ordinary note, check the card and find it before you meet again.”** Make correction part of the promise. Never imply a service can't lose data or misfile a fact; the terms explicitly say AI facts/starters can be wrong.

## 2. Make private account access different from provider processing

**Current:** “100% private. Like, actually” and locked-behind-PIN language. Privacy says synced hosted notes, browser-vendor speech processing and AI text sorting.

**Write:** **“Your cards aren't public social profiles. Notes sync online; providers process text to sort it. Read the data flow before saving.”** Explain PIN scope accurately, without end-to-end or local-only claims. Verify account isolation and controls on synthetic records. Don't expand homepage analytics into private cards merely to count activation.

## 3. Use safer demonstration facts

**Current:** sample cards include family details and a bereavement under “Tread lightly.”

**Write on demos:** **“Fictional people and facts.”** Lead with **“Enjoys sketching”** or another harmless invented interest instead of private grief or family circumstances. Keep the rule **“Only store information you came by fairly and have a right to keep.”** Terms bar unauthorized sensitive records and discriminatory use; don't make those examples the growth hook.

## 4. Show free limits before account creation

**Current CTA:** “Start remembering — it's free” → `/auth`, where Create account and sync notice appear. Terms specify ten people, 15 dictations/month and one quiz/day.

**Write CTA helper:** **“Free: up to 10 people, 15 dictations/month, 1 quiz/day. Adults 18+.”** Show actual paid amount/entitlement before upgrade; current public paid amount wasn't established here. Pro/Student are one-time lifetime-for-service purchases, not recurring. Student needs a genuine college email; no shared or invented credentials.

## 5. Demonstrate typed note, review and later lookup

**Current:** Talk → We sort → You remember suggests fast automatic filing.

**Write workflow:** **“Type or dictate → review the sorted fact → save → find it later.”** Keep voice optional and explain browser speech processing before mic permission. Test a wrong name/date correction and duplicate-person case. Never infer a personal trait from a generated starter or send anything to the person automatically.

## 6. Make export/delete consequences clear

**Current policy:** Settings → Your data offers free export/deletion; photo links expire after seven days and account deletion removes originals. Deleting isn't a refund.

**Write confirmation:** **“Download what you want to keep before deleting. Deletion is permanent and doesn't request a refund.”** Match actual controls and test on authorized synthetic data. Don't promise every deletion instantly removes provider backups or expose a real person's card to demonstrate privacy.

## 7. Check phone and share preview

**Current:** homepage brand Pocket Ellie differs from roster slug Pocket Social; a new user needs the same name throughout the path.

Preview title: **“Pocket Ellie — small details, reviewed and easy to find.”** Description: **“Personal notes for adults, free limits and clear provider/storage terms. Start with a fictional card.”** Test actual 360px signup, keyboard, PIN recovery, declined analytics and later lookup. No measured speed/mobile defect is alleged.
