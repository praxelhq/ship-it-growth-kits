# Pocket Social — Distribution map

**Adult opt-in social-memory tests first.** This is personal recall, distinct from Shayari's conversation persona, Afterthoughts' diary and business CRM tools. No broad networking-club broadcast until sample and privacy controls are clear.

| Route | Action / permission boundary | Planning assumption and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known adults in ordinary hobby/project circles | Five opt-in invitations on days 4,8,12,16,20,24 = **30**. Start with a fictional or permitted harmless fact. No third-party data lists, private grief stories or forced dictation. | 30 × 50% try × 70% account = about 10–11 accounts; half later retrieve a reviewed card ≈ five activations. #1 |
| 2 · Own LinkedIn/Instagram/X feed | Six posts on days 5,10,15,20,25,29 using fictional cards. Follow [LinkedIn policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies), no automated DMs/connection requests or tagging people whose notes appear. | Six × 200–700 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup = 6–21 accounts. #2, #3 |
| 3 · One known adult hobby-club organizer | Request an optional 15-person “remember a harmless detail” demo on day 6; session only if accepted, day 11. No member list, personal information exchange or public memory quiz about actual people. | 15 × 60% attend × 50% signup ≈ four accounts if accepted, otherwise zero. #4 |
| 4 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Check real Monday Launchpad slot/eligibility after signup/card/retrieval controls work. One maker launch, synthetic demo, no vote asks. | 0–150 visits × 20% signup = 0–30 accounts. #5 |
| 5 · [AlternativeTo FAQ](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) | Optional factual free app submission. Email verification/review required; large backlog/no guaranteed timing. No paid priority and no assumed ranking against Monica. | Zero guaranteed month-one traffic. #5 adapted |

[Monica's own editorial/product material](https://www.monicahq.com/) is useful for understanding personal-memory language; its support/community is not your lead list. No relationship subreddit promotion rule or newsletter placement was verified. Don't pitch under a post about loneliness, bereavement or someone forgetting a sensitive event. A useful reply can discuss a private ordinary note without a product link.

Base **16–66 accounts before overlap**, from 30 invitations, six posts, one optional session and one optional launch. That is well below 1,000. Improve the card-review/return loop before adding another social circle. A request to share with a friend doesn't count until a different adult actually joins; sharing the tool should never require sharing the people recorded inside it.
