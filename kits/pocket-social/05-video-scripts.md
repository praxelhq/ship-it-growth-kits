# Pocket Social — Video scripts

Use invented people and harmless interests; no real names, family circumstances, health details, contact info, PIN or private card links. Label every demo. Screen capture and phone voice are enough.

## A · One detail, found later · 60 seconds · own feed / listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Fictional hobby note. **“What was the hobby?”** | “I want to find one harmless detail, not build a dossier.” |
| 2–14s | Actual typed-note path. | “This is an invented person and fact. Typing is enough for the test.” |
| 14–28s | Review actual sorted output. | “I check the name and category before saving. The AI can get it wrong.” |
| 28–42s | Actual card with any real correction. | “This is what I chose to keep.” |
| 42–53s | A later recorded lookup, cut/time labelled. | “Now I find the card again. That's the useful moment.” |
| 53–60s | Free limits and data note. | “Pocket Ellie is 18+. Read the storage/provider terms and start with a fictional card.” |

If save/retrieval fails, show the issue and fix it before posting a success claim. Don't imply a PIN keeps the text from every service provider.

## B · A note I'd be comfortable explaining · 20 seconds · short clip

Hook: **“Would I be okay with them knowing I saved this?”** 0–8s, show a fictional hobby card. 8–15s: “Harmless, fairly shared and useful to remember. That is the boundary for my test.” 15–20s: “No sensitive dossier, no public profile.” Don't use this as a claim that every possible note is legally permitted.

## C · Check the date · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook: **“A sorted date can still be wrong.”** 0–8s, show a real observed error in a fictional card, or a labelled hypothetical. 8–18s, use the actual edit control if supported: “I correct it before relying on it.” 18–25s: “A helpful reminder starts with a checked fact.” No claim of perfect date extraction.

## D · Private doesn't mean local-only · 20 seconds · trust help

Hook: **“The PIN isn't the whole data story.”** 0–8s, simple note → hosted storage/AI diagram. 8–15s: “Cards aren't public, but saved text syncs and providers process it. Voice recognition may involve your browser vendor too.” 15–20s: “Read the real policy before entering anything.” Keep all identifiers and private notes out of the screen.
