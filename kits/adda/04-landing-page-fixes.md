# Adda — Landing page fixes

Based on the public page and [policies](https://www.adda.works/policies.html), checked 29 October 2026. These are content and flow recommendations; no mobile screenshot audit or successful plan submission is claimed.

## 1. Prove the planner-to-vote journey before sending traffic

Current CTA: **“Find our fair adda”**, a `#planner` anchor. A destination exists; that is not proof a plan is generated or a friend can vote.

Test in a fresh phone browser: enter broad starting areas, generate, shortlist, create a vote link, open on a second phone, vote, return to the organizer. If any step fails, fix it before posting. Use a clear failure message: **“We couldn't calculate this plan. Your starting areas are still here. Try again, or change an area.”** No fake loading success.

## 2. Make the group input explicit

Current headline: **“Less time in traffic. More time together.”** Good emotion; the mechanism is in the paragraph.

Suggested headline: **“A fair meeting zone for friends across Bengaluru.”**

Support: **“Add everyone's starting area. Compare traffic-aware journeys, shortlist real venues and let the group vote.”**

Primary button: **“Compare our starting areas”**. Keep the current brand line below the demonstration rather than losing it.

## 3. Show one understandable result

Current process says traffic finds a fair zone, but an organizer needs to know what “fair” means. Place one labelled demonstration beside the inputs: three broad areas, estimated journey times, generated-at time and the chosen tradeoff. Caption: **“Example plan. Traffic and venue details change; check again before you travel.”** Do not present a sample as a real user result.

## 4. Keep Spotlight out of the planning decision

Current section: **“Business partnerships are coming soon.”** Preserve that honesty. Replace any implied active-business invitation with **“Spotlight is planned. Consumer planning stays free. Partnership enquiries are not open yet.”** Sponsored venues must remain labelled when launched. Do not spend this month selling a feature the page says is unavailable.

## 5. Explain the private link before sharing

The policy says shared-plan links let invited people view and vote. Put this beside the share action: **“Anyone with this private link can view the plan and vote. Send it only to your group.”** Do not include precise home coordinates in previews, analytics or public cards.

## 6. Make the phone test concrete

At 390px, check keyboard overlap, long area names, denied location permission and a slow-network retry. Manual neighbourhood selection must remain usable. Copy: **“Prefer not to share location? Choose a neighbourhood instead.”** These are checks, not observed defects.

## 7. Give every shared product link context

Suggested preview title: **“Adda — find a fair Bengaluru meeting zone”**. Description: **“Compare starting areas and let your friends vote on a real venue.”** Use a synthetic result image, not a private plan. Optional analytics should follow the site's consent choice; essential-only must still allow planning.
