# Adda — Video scripts

Record your screen with broad example areas. No real home coordinates, private links, friend names or invented journey savings. Add readable captions; phone microphone and hard cuts are enough.

## A. Demo — 50 seconds, LinkedIn and homepage

| Time | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Three neighbourhood names | “Your central café isn't central for everyone.” |
| 2–10 | Enter three example starting areas | “Our friends are starting in different parts of Bengaluru.” |
| 10–22 | Generate the actual zone; retain real waiting time | “Adda compares a traffic-aware meeting zone before we choose a place.” |
| 22–32 | Read the displayed tradeoff, no invented minutes | “This is the comparison it gives us right now. Traffic can change.” |
| 32–42 | Shortlist a venue and open private vote on second phone | “Now the group can vote instead of everyone sending another pin.” |
| 42–50 | Return to product URL, hide private token | “Free to plan. Check the venue's hours and seats before leaving. adda.works.” |

Only record the successful product steps after the Day-1 test. If the flow fails, fix it rather than editing around it.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel/Short

0–2s: a fictional chat bubble, **“Let's meet near me.”** Say: “The most suspicious sentence in a Bengaluru group chat.”

2–8s: show broad starting areas, then the actual comparison. Say: “Try everyone's starting point before anyone picks a café.”

8–15s: venue vote, then adda.works. Say: “A traffic-aware zone, then a group vote. That's Adda.” On-screen: **Example locations · not a booking**.

## C. Hook — 20 seconds, Story to an existing group

0–2s: organizer looks at several map pins. “I didn't volunteer to be everyone's route planner.”

2–12s: one continuous planner input-to-result clip. “Put the starting areas here. Compare the zone. Choose a shortlist.”

12–20s: the same person opens the vote on a second phone. “Send the private link to your friends. Let them decide together.” End: **Try one real plan this weekend**.

## D. Useful clip — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “A midpoint on a map isn't a promise about traffic.”

2–15s: compare the concept of a geometric midpoint with Adda's actual displayed travel estimates. Explain: “Start with journeys, then choose a place. And check again before you leave.” Do not draw a numerical before/after unless you have real measurements.

15–25s: show the venue checklist: opening hours, seats, final price. “A planning tool doesn't reserve your table.” Product URL in the last frame. This clip earns trust by being clear about the limit.
