## The gap to 1,000

These first tests plausibly produce tens or low hundreds, not a defensible 1,000. To reach 1,000 first planner sessions at 50% visit-to-try conversion, you need about 2,000 distinct relevant visits. Find out whether each activated organizer brings three real participants and whether one participant becomes a new organizer. That is your scale test; do not multiply every invitation as though it always succeeds.

Use Days 4–7 for your first group-planning test, distinct from café-operator products in the cohort. No paid cafés, ads, directory upgrades or newsletter sponsorships. A newsletter pitch is not worth your time before you have a real group story and its permission to share it.
