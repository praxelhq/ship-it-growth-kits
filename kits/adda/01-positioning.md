# Adda — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.adda.works/ · **Category:** Other / group planning · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/adda

Your first user is the friend who always organizes dinner for four people living in different parts of Bengaluru. They already have friends and a WhatsApp group. They need a fair place, not another social network.

The live page checked on 29 October says **“Less time in traffic. More time together.”** Its CTA is **“Find our fair adda”**, leading to the planner on the same page. Consumer planning is free; the separate Spotlight business offer says partnerships are coming soon. Do not advertise sponsored placement as available.

## The pitch

**Put your Bengaluru starting areas into Adda, compare a traffic-aware meeting zone, and let your friends vote on a real venue.**

DM version: **A fair meeting spot for friends starting from different parts of Bengaluru.**

Lead with the unfair journey: one friend always travels across town while another walks downstairs. Your first demonstration uses three neighbourhoods and shows the travel-time tradeoff. Never promise everyone the same journey, a guaranteed fastest route, available seats, or a saving you have not measured.

## The narrow wedge

One existing group, one dinner this weekend. The organizer generates a zone; at least two friends vote; they check the venue and choose a plan. Afterward ask whether they actually met. A planning tool is useful even without accounts, so your policy signup equivalent is a new person's first valid planner session; report group-vote activation beside it.

## What you compete with

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/about/) already offers places, traffic-aware navigation and shared lists with reactions. The consumer app is a no-charge alternative; you are not replacing its maps. You package the **group starting-point comparison before the venue choice**. A free WhatsApp poll is the other alternative: convenient, but it starts after someone has already nominated places. Do not claim either competitor lacks features you have not checked.

## The uncomfortable bit

Your [policy page](https://www.adda.works/policies.html) says shared-plan links are private and planning is not a reservation guarantee. Do not put a real group's location inputs or private vote link in a public Reel. Use broad neighbourhoods and a demonstration plan.

The public page and planner anchor were fetched; this research did not submit a real location, generate a plan or test voting. Day 1 must prove that journey on two phones. If it fails, stop acquisition until it works. The 1,000 target remains, but a handful of local groups will not produce it without a repeatable invitation loop.
