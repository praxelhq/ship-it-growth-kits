# SATHI — Positioning

**Live:** https://sathi.world/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/sathi

## The thing to face first

This is an adult India beta, not therapy, a person or an emergency service. Its safety page says independent clinical review has not happened yet. Its privacy disclosure also says a beta AI provider may use messages for product improvement and human review. Do not turn “private AI” into a promise that nobody outside the app can ever process the text.

## What is actually on your page

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Talk through hard days with a private AI”**. The next explanation is an English, Hindi or Hinglish wellbeing chat with human-support routes available. The action is **“Open on iPhone”**. The current beta is free and nobody is charged. Planned Plus is ₹199/month, with a stated ₹99 first-month offer; the pricing page says Plus opens soon. These are observations from [your public product page](https://sathi.world/), not a completed purchase or a promise that every authenticated flow works.

The iPhone action goes to /app, which returns an application shell. Android links to a direct APK rather than a Play Store listing. The public pages explain age/consent, deletion and provider handling. Actual chat replies, deletion and APK installation were not tested in this research. Treat those as release checks, not claims proved by reading a landing page.

## Your first person

An adult in India who wants to put an ordinary difficult workday into words, understands they are talking to software, and prefers Hindi or Hinglish. Recruit through existing adult relationships and neutral product demos; do not approach people in distress posts or crisis-support groups.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> An AI place to put an ordinary hard day into words, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

For a bio or a short reply:

> SATHI: an adult India beta for everyday wellbeing chat. AI, not therapy.

## Your opening use case

A fictional, low-stakes workday example: “today was tiring and I do not know how to switch off”. Show the language, the AI label, the limits and the way to delete. The product must earn a voluntary return; it should not create dependence or turn a long conversation into the success metric.

## The real alternative

[Wysa](https://www.wysa.com/) is a real wellbeing-support alternative whose public site describes human oversight and evidence-based pathways; no directly comparable consumer price was verified here, so do not invent one. [Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) is $0/year for private journaling. These serve different jobs: a journal can be enough, and a person or professional is a different choice. Your narrow test is whether this beta helps someone express an ordinary day in their language, not whether it replaces care.

## What you need to prove

Demonstrate the current AI and privacy disclosures before a chat starts. Test language switching, failed replies and delete using fictional text. Publish the review status honestly. Do not solicit emotional testimonials or say a safety test proves clinical effectiveness.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
