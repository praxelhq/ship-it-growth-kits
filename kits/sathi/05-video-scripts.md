# SATHI — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 50seconds · neutral product walkthrough

**First two seconds:** “This is an AI demo, not someone’s real chat.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–12s:AI/adult India/beta labels. | Before trying, read what this beta is and how messages are processed. |
| Middle | 12–32s:fictional ordinary workday sentence and response. | This is sample text in Hinglish. The reply is from software, not a person. |
| Payoff | 32–45s:show exit, privacy and delete/support controls. | The controls should be easy to find without continuing the conversation. |
| Close | Hold `https://sathi.world/` legibly. | Inspect the limits before deciding to use SATHI. |

Do not stage a crisis or show someone’s personal story. Do not claim a clinical outcome.

## B — the problem hook · 20seconds · Shorts

**First two seconds:** ““Private” still needs an explanation.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–6s:privacy page with provider section. | Encrypted storage is one part of privacy. |
| Middle | 6–14s:highlight beta provider disclosure. | The provider still processes text to create a reply. |
| Payoff | 14–18s:back to consent action. | Read that before sharing anything personal. |
| Close | Hold `https://sathi.world/` legibly. | The disclosure is part of the product. |

Use the live current wording; avoid a blanket secrecy promise.

## C — the honest comparison · 25seconds · Peerlist

**First two seconds:** “A useful beta test needs no private transcript.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–7s:fictional sample and AI label. | Tell me whether the wording is clear. |
| Middle | 7–18s:show a failed-reply state if actually reproducible. | If a reply fails, the app should say so plainly. |
| Payoff | 18–22s:privacy and controls. | Your personal conversation is not test evidence to send me. |
| Close | Hold `https://sathi.world/` legibly. | Feedback on controls is enough. |

If failure is not reproduced, show the privacy/control walkthrough instead of faking it.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture AI/beta labels, provider privacy disclosure, browser entry, a fictional sample, and exit/delete/support controls. Record no real emotional disclosure or medical information.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
