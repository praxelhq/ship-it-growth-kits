# SATHI — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://sathi.world/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Talk through hard days with a private AI”**; the action is **“Open on iPhone”**. The current beta is free and nobody is charged. Planned Plus is ₹199/month, with a stated ₹99 first-month offer; the pricing page says Plus opens soon. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an informed adult deciding whether to try a beta AI conversation. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Put the beta limits before the action

**Current:** The hero says “Talk through hard days with a private AI”.

**Write this instead:**

> AI wellbeing chat for adults in India. Not therapy or a person. Read the beta privacy limits before you begin.

**Verify:** Keep the provider disclosure close enough to consent to be seen, not a buried surprise.

## Fix 2 — Separate storage from provider processing

**Current:** The page describes encrypted storage and also beta providers that may process messages outside India.

**Write this instead:**

> Stored data is encrypted. AI providers still process your message to reply; read their current beta data practices before sharing.

**Verify:** Do not replace the detailed disclosure with an absolute promise that no human can ever see text.

## Fix 3 — Make browser entry as clear as APK

**Current:** Android uses a direct APK and iPhone uses /app.

**Write this instead:**

> Try in your browser, or review the Android beta download instructions. The APK is a direct beta install, not a Play Store release.

**Verify:** Test both routes on supported devices. Do not say an app-store listing exists.

## Fix 4 — Keep payment future tense

**Current:** The pricing page says Plus opens soon and the beta is free.

**Write this instead:**

> Free during this beta. Plus is not charging yet; you will see terms before choosing any future subscription.

**Verify:** No current charge or conversion should be attributed to a disabled paid plan.

## Fix 5 — State review status where trust is built

**Current:** The safety page says independent clinical review has not happened.

**Write this instead:**

> Independent clinical review is pending. Published product safety tests are not a clinical endorsement.

**Verify:** Keep the date and scope of review truthful after any later change.

## Fix 6 — Show controls without personal text

**Current:** The page describes Delete everything and support access.

**Write this instead:**

> Use sample text to explore the controls. You can leave, delete or choose human support without buying a plan.

**Verify:** Reproduce the controls in a real release check. Do not expose chat content in public proof.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
