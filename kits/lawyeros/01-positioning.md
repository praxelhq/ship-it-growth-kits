# Lawyer OS — Positioning

**Live:** https://lawyeros.online/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/lawyeros

Checked 29 October 2026 against [lawyeros.online](https://lawyeros.online/). Actual headline: **“Your cases, hearings and documents in one place.”** CTAs: **“Start free”** to `/sign-up` and **“See demo”** to `/demo`. The demo explicitly uses sample data and saves nothing. Account creation, case storage, files, permissions and billing were not tested.

## Your first business

An independent advocate with a small active civil-practice docket and a real habit of checking the next hearing against its existing diary. The first job is a clear supplementary matter overview, not replacing court records, professional judgment or the lawyer's established deadline controls.

**Pitch: Review the next hearing and papers for your own matters in one case overview.**

Short version: **Know which matter needs attention next.**

Start with two authorized, minimally identifying actual matter records and no confidential attachments until access/storage controls are reviewed. The advocate verifies every hearing/deadline against its authoritative source and retains the existing diary during evaluation. Do not recommend relying on the app alone for a filing deadline or promise a court integration.

## Actual offer and unavailable features

The public price section says USD per month: Free $0 up to five cases, Individual $4.99/month unlimited cases for one lawyer, Firm $20/month up to ten lawyers total. Document notes/files and deadlines are listed; attachments up to 20 MB are advertised. No INR conversion, tax-inclusive total, trial period or payment outcome was established.

**WhatsApp/SMS hearing reminders, CNR case import and expiring client-update links are coming soon.** Do not advertise them as current reasons to adopt. The page's “so nothing sits in a notebook” line is marketing copy, not proof of missed-deadline prevention.

Firm sharing promises chosen cases with up to ten lawyers and private cases hidden even from a firm owner. That is an important permission claim to test, not a proven breach or a security guarantee. A sample demo cannot establish real confidentiality or persistence.

## The alternative

An advocate's existing diary plus a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) minimal matter-status list can provide an overview. Do not suggest moving sensitive client records into any free tool without evaluating its actual permissions and the firm's requirements. Lawyer OS must earn adoption through clearer retrieval and case organization, not legal advice or guaranteed hearing reminders it has not shipped.

Prove a synthetic case from creation through edit, return, file access and deletion, then test separate owner/member views with permitted sample accounts. Confirm export/recovery before real files. If those controls cannot be established, keep recruitment at sample evaluation and report the business-target gap honestly.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count practices, not their cases, clients or firm seats. Each requires actual permitted business data onboarded, independent recent use within seven days and a privately known owner name/phone. A lawyer clicking the no-save demo is not a customer.
