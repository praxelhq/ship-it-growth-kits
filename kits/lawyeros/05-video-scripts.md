# Lawyer OS — Phone-demo scripts

Use fictional case references, dates and documents, your own voice and screen recording. No client names, case strategy, real files or private links. The demonstration is administrative product use, not legal advice or an instruction about a real deadline.

## A. Tomorrow's matter review — 65 seconds

0–3 s: synthetic diary beside a fictional overview. **“Which matter needs review next?”**

3–17 s: actual sample case with status, next hearing and source-review note. “The overview brings the fields together. The advocate still verifies the record.”

17–32 s: actual document-note/sample attachment route. “Keep the papers with the intended case, after checking permissions.” Leave one supported interaction continuous.

32–47 s: actual private-versus-firm permission views only if verified using test accounts. “Test who can see a case before using client records.” Otherwise show the public permission claim and label the missing test.

47–55 s: visible coming-soon list. “Messaging reminders and CNR import are planned, not a promise for this trial.”

55–65 s: free limit and URL. “Five cases on Free. Start with the no-save demo at lawyeros.online.”

## B. Date hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: synthetic hearing date with a source check. **“A date needs a source.”**

3–12 s: owner checks its fictional diary against the overview. “Review what was entered before relying on the display.”

12–20 s: public demo and URL. “A clearer review, not an automatic court update.” No missed-deadline guarantee.

## C. Access hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: fictional private/firm case labels. **“Shared with whom?”**

3–15 s: verified synthetic permission test or public description labelled untested. “Check the actual owner and member views before entering confidential material.”

15–25 s: privacy/recovery review and demo link. “A sample is enough for the first conversation.”

Capture exact demo/no-save label, current case/date/document fields, plan limits and coming-soon features. Do not invent an export, invite or permission success. Label shortened waits and keep private contact/attachment data out of footage.
