# Lawyer OS — Distribution map

Lead with a small practice's own case overview. Avoid selling unshipped court imports or reminders, and never request a client matter through a public comment.

| Route | Action and permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing independent advocate relationships | Qualify an operating practice, a docket-review task and authority to evaluate processing. Use genuine professional introductions or the practice's public business enquiry route honestly. | Ten blocks of four practices = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two professional introductions | Ask a known practitioner to forward one sample-demo invitation; no client list, case facts or private contact disclosure. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a synthetic matter overview, date-source check and permission boundary. No legal advice or client-success story. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One practice-owned workflow review | Owner checks the overview against its existing diary privately; actual records only after permission/storage review. | Day 12; #5. Support for an existing trial, not another customer. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% authorized own-data setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 practices. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. This model is optimistic because confidentiality/permissions may prevent actual onboarding. Do not bypass those barriers to achieve the number.

Keep one local independent-practice segment and separate contacts from CA/audit tools and law/career-prep students. A law student is not a practice customer. No bar association newsletter, professional WhatsApp group or court endorsement is established. Ask a known organizer before a group message, allow one primary invitation per organizer/week and count no event reach until accepted.

No paid association listings, bulk scraped advocate numbers, automated DMs or pretending to be a prospective legal client to pitch the tool. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after a decline. Do not give advice about a client's case in the sales conversation.

The first source for a hearing/deadline remains the lawyer's own authoritative record. The current demo saves nothing; a trial that never moves beyond synthetic data may produce valuable feedback but cannot satisfy the own-business-data policy. Report that distinction openly.
