## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. Comparisons work without login according to methodology. Count eligible first comparison sessions separately from optional accounts. No acquisition forecast is valid until the first-result loading gate works on a real test device. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.
