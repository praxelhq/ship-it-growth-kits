**Week 1 is a build week.** You cannot run the distribution plan against a sign-in wall. This is the only wave-1 kit where I'd tell you to post almost nothing in the first seven days.

## The three things that decide this month

1. **Did the front page ship by day 4?** Everything else is downstream. A week of building beats a month of posting into a wall.
2. **Did you do 10+ club subreddit posts?** This is the most leverage available to you and nobody else can execute it at your speed.
3. **Did you answer every "run this tactic" request?** Each one is a personally-onboarded user, a piece of content, and a public demonstration that the thing works. This is your community, forming.

---

## Recurring weekly rhythm to keep after day 30

- **Monday:** announce the challenge
- **Wednesday:** a club or tactical finding post
- **Saturday:** match-day topical post
- **Sunday:** challenge results

Four posts a week, each generated by using your own product. That's a sustainable engine, not a campaign.
