# Mezzala — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.mezzala.dev/ · **Category:** Games · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/mezzala

> **Read `04-landing-page-fixes.md` first.** When I fetched mezzala.dev on 28 Oct 2026, the page was a sign-in wall: a formation picker, *"Test your 4-3-3 in eight minutes"*, `Continue with Google` and `Sign in as Guest`. There is no marketing page, no explanation of what the game is, and no way to see it working before committing. **Driving traffic to that page would waste almost all of it.** Week 1 of this kit is building a front page; the distribution plan starts in week 2.

---

## Who your first 1,000 users actually are

**People who argue about formations on the internet.**

That is a real, enormous, obsessive and extremely well-organised population:

1. **Football Manager players.** Millions of them, deeply invested in tactics, and currently underserved by anything that is quick. FM takes 40 hours to see if a tactic works. You take eight minutes. **This is your single best audience and your positioning should be built for them.**
2. **Tactics-nerd football fans** — the people who read The Athletic's tactical columns, watch Tifo/The Coaches' Voice, and post diagrams on r/soccer. They have opinions they cannot test.
3. **FPL and football-Twitter people** who want a quick, competitive, shareable thing.
4. **Students and casual players** looking for a browser game that isn't nonsense.

What unites them: **they have a theory about football and no way to test it.** Every pub argument about whether a back three beats a 4-3-3 is a user.

## The one-line pitch

> **Build a budget-capped squad, pick your shape, and find out in eight minutes whether your tactics actually work.**

Shorter, for Reddit titles and bios:

> **A football tactics simulator you can finish in eight minutes. Free, in the browser.**

## The wedge use case

**Settling the argument.**

Not "football simulation" — FM owns that and you will not beat it. Your wedge is **speed**: a complete tactical test, start to finish, in one tea break. That is a thing Football Manager structurally cannot offer and it is why someone would use you *alongside* FM rather than instead of it.

Lead with the eight minutes. Your sign-in page already says *"Test your 4-3-3 in eight minutes"* — that is the best line you have and it should be the headline of the site you don't have yet.

## Why someone picks you over the alternatives

1. **Eight minutes.** The entire proposition.
2. **No install, browser, free, guest mode.** FM is £50 and 10GB. You are a link in a Reddit comment.
3. **Budget cap.** It's a constraint, which makes it a *puzzle* rather than a sandbox — and puzzles are shareable and comparable. This is more important than it sounds: it means two people can run the same budget and compare results, which is the seed of every competitive community.
4. **Shape vs shape.** 4-3-3 against 4-4-2 is the oldest argument in football. You can answer it.

## The strategic choice you need to make now

Your product can be positioned two ways and they lead to different kits:

- **(A) A game.** Fun, fast, shareable, leaderboards. Reach: large. Depth: shallow. Distribution: r/WebGames, browser-game sites, TikTok.
- **(B) A tactics laboratory.** Serious, for people who argue about pressing triggers. Reach: smaller. Depth: enormous, and the users never leave. Distribution: r/footballtactics, FM communities, football Twitter.

**Take B, with A's packaging.** The tactics audience is smaller but it is rabid, concentrated, and starved of tools — and it will produce the content (screenshots of results, "I tested X and here's what happened") that pulls in the casual audience for free. Going casual-first gets you a spike and no community.

Practically: your copy is serious about football, your onboarding is eight minutes, and your leaderboard is competitive.

## What NOT to say

- **Don't position against Football Manager.** You'll lose and you'll annoy the exact people you need. Position as the thing you do *between* FM sessions: *"test the idea in eight minutes, then build the save around it."*
- **Don't say "AI-powered".** Say "the AI manager picks a counter-shape" — describe the behaviour.
- **Don't launch with "sign in with Google" as the first thing a human sees.** See `04`. This is the most urgent problem in the whole kit.

## The name

"Mezzala" is a great name for audience (B) and meaningless to audience (A). That's another reason to commit to B — your name is already doing the targeting. Anyone who knows what a mezzala is, is your user. **Consider making that the joke on your homepage:** *"If you know what a mezzala is, this is for you."* It's a perfect qualifier and a shareable line.
