# Mezzala — Video scripts + shot lists

**Your product is inherently watchable** — a match playing out on a pitch diagram is good video with no production effort. That's rare. Use it.

Everything here is screen-recorded. You barely need to appear on camera.

---

## Video A — The front-page demo (0:40, horizontal + vertical cut) 🔴 build this first

**This is not optional — it is the thing that makes `04` Fix 0 work.** Right now a visitor has no idea what a Mezzala match looks like.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | Squad builder, budget counter ticking down as you add players | "Pick a squad under the cap." |
| 0:06–0:12 | Formation picker, you select 4-3-3 | "Pick a shape." |
| 0:12–0:18 | The AI picks 4-4-2. Both blocks appear on the pitch. | "The AI picks its own." |
| 0:18–0:32 | **The match**, sped up, blocks shifting, a chance, a goal | "Then you watch eight minutes and find out." |
| 0:32–0:38 | The result screen — score, possession, where it was decided | "And it tells you where it was won." |
| 0:38–0:40 | `mezzala.dev · free · no account` | "Free. In the browser." |

### Shot list
- Clean recording, no browser chrome, no bookmarks bar.
- **Use a match with an actual goal in it.** Record five matches and pick the one with the best moment. A 0-0 is a bad advert.
- Speed the match up 4–6x but **keep two or three moments at normal speed** — the goal, and one tactical shift. The rhythm change is what makes it watchable.
- No music needed; if you use any, keep it under the voice.
- Export a 15-second silent GIF of just the match playing. That GIF goes in every Reddit post and every Discord message.

## Video B — "I ran this 50 times" (1:30, horizontal) — the content format

**This is your repeatable series.** Make one a week; each one is also a Reddit post, an X thread and three Shorts.

**0:00–0:10 — The question**
> "Does a 4-3-3 actually beat a 4-4-2 low block, or do we just think it should?"

*Shot: the two shapes side by side, static. Let the question sit.*

**0:10–0:20 — The setup**
> "Same budget both sides. Same players' quality. Only the shape is different. Fifty matches, no manual changes."

*Shot: the budget screen, identical on both sides. Showing the controls is what makes the result credible.*

**0:20–0:50 — The match**
> *(commentate over a sped-up match, 2–3 observations)*
> "Here's the thing that kept happening. They sit, we have the ball, that's fine — and then we lose it here and there's nobody home."

*Shot: the match. **Slow down and point at the specific moment** where the pattern shows. This is the bit that makes a tactics audience trust you.*

**0:50–1:10 — The results**
> "Across fifty: won X, drew Y, lost Z. And almost every loss came from the same transition."

*Shot: a results table or chart. Make it readable on a phone — big numbers, few of them.*

**1:10–1:30 — The fix and the invitation**
> "One change — [the adjustment] — and it goes to X-Y-Z. Same shape, same budget.
>
> Run your own at mezzala.dev. Comment a shape and I'll test it."

### Shot list
- **Never fake the numbers.** This audience will smell it and your credibility is the entire product.
- Charts: big, simple, dark background, readable at thumbnail size.
- Record the matches in advance, pick the clearest illustrative one.
- Your own voice, football-literate, no hype. Talk like a podcast, not a trailer.

## Video C — The 40-second reply video *(your volume engine)*

When someone comments "run a 3-5-2 against a high press":

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Screenshot of their comment | "[Username] asked for a 3-5-2 against a high press. Let's see." |
| 0:04–0:10 | Setting it up | "Same budget. Here's the shape." |
| 0:10–0:32 | The match, sped up, one commentary beat | *(one observation)* |
| 0:32–0:40 | The result | "2-1. The wing-backs were the whole story. Next shape in the comments." |

**Make 2–3 of these a day once requests start coming in.** They cost fifteen minutes, the algorithm loves reply-videos, and every single one is a product demo with a person's name on it.
