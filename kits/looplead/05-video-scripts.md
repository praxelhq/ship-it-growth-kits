# LoopLead — Video scripts

Use a clearly synthetic company or permitted public business facts. No private prospect record, personal-life trigger or fabricated contact. Phone voice and screen recording are enough.

## A · Fact, inference, draft · 60 seconds · founder walkthrough

0–2 s hook: **“Did the company say that, or did we guess?”** Show a labelled sample finding. 2–15 s: open the current public source: “This line is the fact we can inspect.”15–30 s: show inference/hypothesis labels: “These are possible interpretations, not proven pain.”30–45 s: delete an unsupported draft sentence and confirm the contact: “The user owns the final judgment.”45–55 s: copy the actual draft without sending: “LoopLead doesn't send it for you.”55–60 s: “Ten one-time packages free. LoopLead dot tech.”

## B · Specific isn't personal · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“A business fact can be relevant without being creepy.”**0–8 s: show a synthetic product launch/careers fact. 8–15 s: “Use a relevant company detail, not a person's private life or an invented story.”15–20 s: “If you can't point to the source, remove the claim.”

## C · The contact still needs checking · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“Is this still the right person?”**2–12 s: show a synthetic suggested role labelled uncertain. 12–20 s: “Check the current company page before copying the note. A plausible name isn't a verified contact.”20–25 s: “One checked message beats a list of confident guesses.”

Film the real source label and editable text. If research fails, show a recoverable failure; don't replace it with a fabricated success or a meeting-booked animation.
