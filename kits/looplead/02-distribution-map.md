# LoopLead — Distribution map

**Founder-led B2B sales conversations first.** Use the product's own standard: one relevant public business fact, no invented pain or creepy personalization. No bulk outbound tools are needed.

| Route | Action, boundaries and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm SaaS founder introductions | Ask ten known builders to request an introduction to an actual company doing founder-led sales. No personal contact list transfers. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 independent business trials. | #1 |
| 2 · Public SaaS company/owner business routes | Manually qualify a real product, specific buyer and current outbound task. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. One follow-up after seven days, stop on no. Hypothetical 25% response × 60% trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 companies. No fake customer enquiry or automated DMs. | #2 |
| 3 · [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) organizer route | Real network, no current free event/group permission assumed. Ask privately for an evidence-first small sales-workflow session using synthetic data, not a promise of booked meetings. Avoid existing founder-clinic invitations. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn / X | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show fact/inference/hypothesis using a synthetic package. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 relevant visits, possibly zero. No mass mentions or manufactured engagement. | #4 |
| 5 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Check current Monday Launchpad eligibility. Use a real working package demo, with source labels and no auto-send claim. Optional 0–100 visits, 0–2 business trials. | #5 |

Specific sources worth following are a prospect's own company changelog/careers/product pages, because those are the public facts the package should cite. Don't treat a personal life event as a sales trigger. [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) can identify organizations, not supply a bulk marketing list or guarantee contact permission.

No newsletter placement, sales Discord access or Reddit promotion rule was verified. If an organizer requests a free opt-in educational blurb, use #3 after approval. Founder readiness clinics have a coordinated schedule; use a distinct private buyer list rather than another blanket invitation.

Base **3–5 active businesses** is below ten. At day 14 inspect independent package reuse and source-correction burden. Better evidence and customer fit can improve the result; a bigger unqualified list or a fabricated reply-rate claim cannot.
