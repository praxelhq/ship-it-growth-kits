# LoopLead — Measurement

**Target:10 real B2B businesses.** Activation beside it: **businesses independently reviewing a source-grounded package for a real permitted target and using their own final draft**. Ten lead packages from one company are one customer.

## Day 0

Query actual business accounts, successful packages, reviewed/corrected claims, copy/export success and independent returns. Sample companies establish no baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, masking private prospect/draft content. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/looplead) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero.

Events: `business_onboarded`, `research_completed`, `source_opened`, `unsupported_claim_corrected`, `draft_reviewed`, `copy_export_succeeded` and `business_returned`. Copying isn't proof of sending or a reply. If users volunteer outcomes, label them self-reported and keep prospect identities private. Real billing receipts are separate from client sales or meetings.

## Four tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a real business, its own permitted actual workflow onboarded, independent use in the final seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Payment isn't required. Several reps/seats in one company are one customer. Founder-produced sample packages aren't adoption.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 25% response ×60% trial ×50% independent reuse ≈3 businesses. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–5 is below ten.** These aren't measured lead-tool acquisition rates. Recompute after 20 approaches from useful independent work, not “sounds interesting” replies.

Review ten permitted packages for stale facts, wrong contacts and hypotheses stated as certainty. Don't claim reply uplift, time savings or booked meetings without a defined actual sample. A checked relevant draft matters more than a high generated-lead count.
