# LoopLead — Positioning

**Live:** https://looplead.tech/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/looplead

**Your ten are real B2B businesses, not ten researched leads.** Start with a founder-led SaaS business doing its own small, carefully selected outbound list. They already know who they sell to and need one defensible first message, not a purchased contact database or automatic sending machine.

The actual headline is **“One lead in. One personalized outreach package out.”** **“Start free — 10 leads”** links to signup. The free allowance is ten one-time packages, not ten every month. Public monthly plans are Starter $39/100 packages, Pro $99/400, and Team $79/user with a three-seat minimum. The page says the user reviews and sends; no email/LinkedIn automation, follow-up sequences, open tracking or LinkedIn scraping.

## Your narrow offer

> Research one target company's public business context, check the evidence and edit a first message before sending it yourself.

Bio: **A source-checkable first outreach package, not auto-send.**

The first useful result is a founder rejecting an unsupported claim and using a relevant fact they can verify. Don't promise a reply, booked meeting or conversion uplift. The sample company and contact on the page are labelled sample data, not customer success.

## Why choose it over manual work?

[ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) exists, and a founder can already combine their own source notes with a general assistant. Don't claim other assistants cannot research or retain context. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can hold a manual prospect log without a specialist subscription. LoopLead's potential advantage is the integrated company/contact/angle/draft package with clear evidence labels, demonstrated on an actual permitted target.

## The weakness to keep visible

“Verified,” “Inference” and “Hypothesis” mean different things. A plausible pain point isn't a fact about a prospect. A suggested contact needs checking on the company's current site; never infer a personal email address or reference private/personal posts to make the message feel intimate. The advertised two-minute package time is a site claim, not a measured customer saving.

Ten businesses require their own real allowed prospect workflow, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact privately. A business researching ten leads is still one customer. Public routes and plan descriptions don't establish that authenticated research/export/checkout were tested.
