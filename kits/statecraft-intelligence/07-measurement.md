# Statecraft Intelligence — Measurement

**Target: 10 real active organizations.** Activation beside it: **authorized neutral research teams independently completing a reproducible public-data check**. “Instant Demo Organizations,” anonymous logins and displayed coverage counts aren't customers.

## Day 0

Query actual authorized organizations, pilot permissions, independently completed checks, source completeness and returning organizational users. The gateway alone establishes none of those totals. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding restricted research data and credentials. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/statecraft-intelligence) rank, latest reading and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero, and overlapping rolling readings aren't cumulative traffic.

Events: `pilot_scope_approved`, `organization_onboarded`, `source_opened`, `comparison_reviewed`, `reproducible_export_completed` and `organization_returned`. Instrument only supported features; don't invent a completed export event before the function exists. Keep individual voter data, sensitive attributes and passwords out of public analytics/reports.

## Four tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): a real business/institutional organization, its own permitted actual research workflow onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible authorized contact known and callable privately. Payment isn't required. Ten researchers inside one institution are one organization, not ten customers. If current access/features can't support the scoped job, the target is gated on that capability.

## Forecast and weekly review

40 first approaches × hypothetical 20% response × 50% permitted pilot × 50% independent use = **2 organizations**. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 2–4 is below ten**, with procurement and source-review delays a real constraint. These are planning assumptions, not measured civic-data acquisition rates. Recompute after 20 approaches rather than inventing institutional demand.

A chart that looks complete is not evidence of official accuracy, and a model prediction is not a verified electoral outcome.
