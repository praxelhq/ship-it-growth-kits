# Statecraft Intelligence — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.statecraftintelligence.me/ · **Category:** Other · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/statecraft-intelligence

**Your ten should be legitimate organizations doing neutral electoral-data research.** This kit does not sell voter profiling, caste-based persuasion or campaign targeting. Choose civic research, academic or newsroom data teams that need source traceability and reproducible public-data checks.

The actual visible title is **“Statecraft Intelligence”**, followed by **“Electoral Data & Demographic Intelligence System.”** The entry asks for an official Organizational ID/password and offers **“Instant Demo Organizations.”** The gateway also displays “ECI Certified Access,” “288 ACs Verified” and encryption/audit claims. This research saw those claims but did **not** verify certification, coverage accuracy, security implementation or private data. No public price or independent organization signup was established.

## A narrower credible pitch

> Review public electoral datasets with clear source dates, boundary definitions and reproducible checks before publishing a neutral analysis.

Bio: **Source-traceable electoral-data research for authorized teams.**

This is a proposed acquisition wedge, not a claim that the current private workspace already supports every check. The first job is a controlled comparison of a small public dataset against its authoritative source, with no individual voter records or inferred sensitive traits. If source lineage/export isn't available, fix that before offering a real research pilot.

## The actual alternative

Researchers can start from [ECI's official statistical-report route](https://www.eci.gov.in/statistical-reports) and their existing spreadsheet tools. Official search results identify the reports; full-page retrieval was blocked during research, so no current figures are quoted here. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) allows creation with a Google account; paid AI features aren't needed for manual reconciliation. No competing political-data service price was verified, and none is invented.

Your tool must earn its place by making public-source verification easier, not by looking more “secure” than a spreadsheet. Aggregated data, a model inference and an audited fact must stay visibly different. A swing model isn't a factual result, and this kit doesn't promote predictions as electoral certainty.

## The weak point to fix first

An unsupported “ECI Certified” badge can destroy trust immediately. Link to a real, applicable authorization if one exists; otherwise remove the wording. Restricted access is not evidence of authority. Ten anonymous demo organizations cannot become ten customers. Count only real organizations with their own authorized research workflow and independent recent use.
