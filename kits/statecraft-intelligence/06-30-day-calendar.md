# Statecraft Intelligence — 30-day calendar

Begin after unsupported affiliation claims are corrected and a scoped authorized research walkthrough exists. Daily: 20 minutes replying and ten minutes source/permission logging. Four first professional approaches on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 = **40 total**; ten warm requests on day 4. One follow-up after seven days; stop after no.
