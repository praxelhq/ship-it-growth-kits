# Statecraft Intelligence — Video scripts

Use only synthetic or genuinely public aggregate examples. No individual voter records, inferred caste profiles, political persuasion advice or restricted organizational credentials. Don't show a fictional demo as a real customer's workspace.

## A · Trace the displayed value · 60 seconds · research lead walkthrough

0–2s hook: **“Where did this number come from?”** Show a synthetic value with “Demonstration” label. 2–15s: open the source reference/date: “A researcher needs the original record, not just a dashboard total.” 15–30s: show boundary and aggregation notes: “The same label can refer to a different area or date.” 30–45s: reproduce a small comparison/export in the actual supported flow: “This is the check I want another researcher to repeat.” 45–55s: show uncertainty: “Unverified information stays unverified. A model output isn't an official result.” 55–60s: “Statecraft Intelligence: a scoped research pilot, not a prediction guarantee.”

If the source/export feature doesn't exist, say it is a required improvement and don't stage a successful flow.

## B · A badge is not a source · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“A secure-looking login doesn't verify the data.”** 0–8s: use a neutral synthetic login mock, no official emblem. 8–15s: “Official affiliation, security controls and dataset correctness need separate evidence.” 15–20s: “Ask for the source, scope and actual check.” No implication of ECI endorsement.

## C · Missing isn't zero · 25 seconds · researcher onboarding

Hook 0–2s: **“This blank isn't a zero.”** 2–12s: compare synthetic missing and zero cells. 12–20s: “A missing source or unmatched area should remain visible. Filling it with a confident number changes the analysis.” 20–25s: “Reproducible gaps beat a complete-looking guess.”

Use captions and one large field at a time. No election-winning claim or demographic-targeting suggestion belongs in the demo or social caption.
