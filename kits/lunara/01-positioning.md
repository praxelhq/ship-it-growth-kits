# Lunara — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.lunara.works/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/lunara

**Observation is your credible offer; diagnosis is not.** The homepage promises to explain what fractures sleep and labels a paid feature “Root-Cause Analysis,” while its medical notice says Lunara does not diagnose or treat. Resolve that contradiction before recruiting people to enter sensitive sleep/symptom records. The 30-night demo history is pre-loaded sample data, not evidence of real users or clinical validation.

Current headline: “The nights changed. Let's find out why.” The CTA “Start your 14-day trial” opens `/auth`, which also offers an instant demo. The actual homepage lists Free Pilot at $0, Plus at $9/month or $79/year, and a 14-day full-access trial with no card. The free plan includes recent 7-night history; paid pattern reports and printable summaries are separate. These were read, not purchased or tested with personal data.

Your first user is an adult experiencing changing sleep around perimenopause who already wants to take clearer observations to a clinician. She needs a simple morning record, not an app telling her what caused a particular night.

**Pitch:** Keep a short morning sleep-and-symptom record, then bring a clear observation summary to your clinician.

**Bio:** Your nights, recorded clearly. Patterns to discuss, not a diagnosis.

The wedge is **seven consistent morning observations** and a readable summary, not “sleep better tonight.” [NHS information](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/menopause-and-perimenopause/symptoms/) recognizes sleep problems can accompany menopause and differ between people; that is context, not validation of Lunara's algorithm.

[Balance](https://www.balance-menopause.com/balance-app/) is a real symptom-tracking/information alternative; its applicable paid-price terms were not established here. A private paper sleep log costs nothing and requires no account. [Medito](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app) is free for meditation/breathing content, so generic calming tools alone are not your differentiation. Win with easy, truthful recording and a useful summary, if the actual product delivers them.

The weakness is trust in statistical interpretation and data access. “100% private & encrypted” and the auth page's security labels are product claims, not an independent privacy audit. Explain actual storage/access/deletion and show uncertainty/sample size in reports before widening intake.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**, with completed first observations and voluntary multi-day recording beside it. Demo opens, synthetic nights and speculative health improvement are not activation or outcomes.
