# Lunara — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026: raw homepage, rendered homepage/pricing and auth. No account, personal health data, statistical report or checkout was submitted.

1. **Align the real headline with an observation-only job.** Current “The nights changed. Let's find out why.” implies an answer the app may not establish. Replacement **“The nights changed. Keep a clearer record.”** Supporting line **“A short morning sleep-and-symptom log, with summaries to discuss with your clinician.”** Primary CTA **“Try the synthetic demo”**; secondary **“Start the 14-day trial”** → `/auth`. Ship the label before claiming the demo button has changed.

2. **Remove unsupported causal/clinical certainty.** Current paid copy says “Root-Cause Analysis” and “Pinpoints sleep loss vs hot flashes & stress.” Replace with **“Explore patterns in your recorded nights”** and **“Shows associations in your entries, with sample size and limitations. It does not establish a cause or diagnosis.”** Reports must display uncertainty and missing data; don't write a scientific-sounding conclusion from a handful of nights.

3. **Label the demo everywhere it appears.** Auth offers an instant demo with 30 pre-loaded nights. Use **“Synthetic demonstration data — not your records, not customer outcomes.”** Repeat on charts and export previews. Demo history must never be counted as real observation activity or used as a testimonial.

4. **Explain actual data controls before health entry.** Current “100% private & encrypted” and auth security labels are broad. Publish actual storage, operator/provider access, optional processing, retention, export and deletion behavior. Replacement **“Read how your observations are stored and who can access them before creating a record.”** Technical labels alone do not verify privacy; no audit certification is established here.

5. **Clarify trial versus free and paid.** The raw homepage lists Free Pilot $0, Plus $9/month or $79/year, 14-day full access and 7-night free history. Show those terms before registration. Explain what remains usable after trial and whether/when a paid renewal starts. “Lock in lifetime founding member pricing” must not look like a one-time lifetime purchase.

6. **Show a useful, modest report.** Use an explicitly synthetic observation summary with dates, entry count, missing days and questions the user can discuss. Replace “Clinical report” with **“Printable observation summary.”** Do not attach an unverified clinician name or imply that a report replaces a consultation.

7. **Make 3am and morning use readable without overclaiming relief.** Test low-light contrast, large text, keyboard behavior, save confirmation and exit on a phone. Preview title **“Lunara — record sleep observations clearly”** and description **“Short morning notes and readable summaries. Pattern observations are not diagnosis or treatment.”** No sleep benefit or measured load performance is claimed from this page review.
