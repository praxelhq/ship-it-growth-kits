# Lunara — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real accounts, excluding demo/test accounts. Report **completed first real observations** and voluntary later-day recording beside signups. Neither is evidence of improved sleep or a diagnosed cause.

The pre-loaded 30-night demo is synthetic. Demo opens, demo entries and exported demo reports never count as real user history, activation or clinical outcomes. Keep Free Pilot, trial, paid Plus and expired trial states distinct.

## Day 0

Record actual existing accounts and minimum aggregate first-entry/return counts from your own system; private numbers were not available during research. Note unresolved causal/privacy/access wording as readiness gaps. Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/lunara) reading, period and rank. Don't use pre-loaded chart values as growth data.

Use [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on appropriate public acquisition surfaces, excluding health-entry/report/account content from replay. No symptom values, private notes, medical history or identifiers in analytics labels. Minimum aggregate events: `signup_completed`, `first_observation_completed`, `later_day_returned`, `summary_opened` and authoritative paid-plan confirmation. Privacy choices take precedence over a more granular funnel.

## Honest funnel and gates

Ten private invitations, four owned-feed posts and one optional ten-person demo support a small informed cohort. At an assumed 20% visit→signup rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 qualified visits. There is no verified distribution composition supporting that scale yet. Expand with consent and actual useful recording behavior, not medical promises or patient-contact lists.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Trust/claims gate, first real observations, questions about demo/history|Confusion about causation, privacy or synthetic data pauses promotion.|
|Day 14|First observations, later voluntary entries, free/trial comprehension|If people can't understand the summary or access limits, fix them before more invites.|
|Day 21|Multi-day recording, support issues, actual summary use|Do not interpret a small correlated sample as a clinical result.|
|Day 30|Accounts, real first observations, returners, paid plans, actual reach and unresolved gaps|State the shortfall to 1,000 and keep usage separate from health outcomes.|

Keep weekly aggregate actual rows with source and hours. A clinician reviewing wording does not mean endorsement or validation; report what was actually reviewed and obtain permission before naming anyone.
