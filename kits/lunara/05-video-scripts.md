# Lunara — Video scripts

Use only clearly labelled synthetic data. Do not demonstrate treatment, prescribe a breathing routine or show a real person's health history. Record your own voice and screen.

## A. Record, don't diagnose — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “A pattern is a question, not a diagnosis.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Synthetic-demo label and actual access terms|“I'm using Lunara's demo, not a person's health record.”|
|10–25s|Actual morning entry flow, synthetic values|“The first job is a short, understandable observation.”|
|25–40s|Summary with entry count and missing data visible|“A summary should show what was recorded and what is missing.”|
|40–52s|Limitations and printable observation preview|“Associations can guide a conversation. They don't establish the cause.”|
|52–60s|Plain URL and voluntary demo invitation|“Try the navigation with synthetic data before choosing an account.”|

Publish only after the wording/data-control gate. No before/after sleep-success claim.

## B. Thirty demo nights — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “These nights belong to nobody.” Show the synthetic label, pre-loaded chart and a blank real-account state only if tested. Say: “Demo history helps explain the interface. It isn't traction, clinical evidence or your sleep.” Use as an honest maker clip.

## C. The missing days — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A chart should show what it doesn't know.” Show an explicitly synthetic summary with a gap and entry count. Say: “A missing night isn't zero symptoms. The report needs to show the gap instead of hiding uncertainty.” Don't fabricate a feature; if missing-data display isn't built, describe it as a change in progress.

## D. Trial clarity — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Fourteen days isn't a lifetime purchase.” Show current Free Pilot/Plus terms and the founding-pricing wording. Say: “See what's free, what changes after trial and what a subscription actually costs before choosing it.” Use the real current prices and region; don't imply the kit tested billing.
