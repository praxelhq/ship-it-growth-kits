# Lunara — Distribution map

**Start with informed, private adult usability tests and a qualified review of wording.** Do not promote inside patient/support discussions without permission, and do not target people based on disclosures of symptoms.

|Route|Action and boundary|Planning result, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|

The [Balance organization's own page](https://www.balance-menopause.com/balance-app/) links its official accounts and educational material. Use that as a source of user questions, not an audience to poach with unsolicited DMs. [NHS symptom information](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/menopause-and-perimenopause/symptoms/) is educational context, not a posting channel or endorsement.

Your organizer's opt-in bulletin may share #4 if approved. No specialist newsletter placement, clinic distribution agreement or patient list is assumed. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after the data/claims gate, using a personal maker account and no upvote requests; don't treat a builder audience as a thousand qualified users.

Initial reach supports a small informed tester cohort. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→signup rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 visits. This calendar does not demonstrate that reach. Expand only after people understand the limitations and choose to return; privacy-preserving referrals must never expose a symptom history.
