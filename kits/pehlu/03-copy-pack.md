## 5. Peerlist description

**Tagline:** A daily question, with room for your own perspective.

Pehlu combines Telegram prompts, saved reflections and a weekly perspective you can correct or reject. It is for self-reflection, not diagnosis or therapy. Please read how Telegram and the AI providers process information before sharing anything sensitive. Feedback on the first-answer and notification controls is welcome: https://pehlu.app/
