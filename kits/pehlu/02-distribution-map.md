# Pehlu — Distribution map

**Lead with private, consented demonstrations and your own explanatory posts.** Nobody needs to share a personal answer in order to understand the product.

| Route | Exact entry / permission | Planning outcome and basis | Copy |

| Opted-in follow-up | Only testers who asked to hear again. No request for their answer text. | Ten follow-ups teach retention; they are not ten new accounts. | #6 |

These are workload scenarios, not measured platform medians. Zero is possible. The calendar contains exactly twenty personal invitations, four LinkedIn posts, six clips, two accepted sessions and one optional listing. The range is roughly 33–90 accounts before overlap; use **25–70 distinct accounts** as a first working range. It does not honestly support a promise of 1,000. Replace it after the first cohort with delivered visits, account creation and completed-answer rates.

For shared campus organizers, prefer **Days 15–18** for this neutral adult wellbeing-product walkthrough. Coordinate with SATHI, Luna and Lumi: one organizer gets one broad invitation in that window. A private demo is not therapy or an institutional endorsement.

Day One is a real alternative to understand, not a place to advertise. No wellbeing newsletter, clinician, support group or Telegram community has agreed to distribute Pehlu. Do not invent their permission or count a newsletter pickup. Telegram here is the delivery mechanism the person opts into, not a source of strangers to message. Stop after a decline; do not add anyone to a group or bot.
