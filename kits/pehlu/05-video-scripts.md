# Pehlu — Video scripts

Use a fictional sample account and answer. Never record a real reflection, chat identifier or weekly insight from a user. Demonstrate delivery/correction only after those actions work in a real test.

## A. One daily question — 45 seconds

**Use:** homepage and adult product-session invitation.

| Time | Picture / text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Sample prompt. Text: “One question.” | “One question, at your pace.” |
| 3–15s | Actual preferences and sample delivery. | “Pehlu sends a reflection prompt on Telegram at a time you choose.” |
| 15–29s | Fictional answer and saved reflection. | “This is a fictional answer. You can keep a little reflection without sharing it with a group.” |
| 29–40s | Correction and pause controls. | “A weekly perspective is something you can challenge. Messages can be paused.” |
| 40–45s | Public URL. | “Read the product and privacy details at pehlu.app.” |

## B. Not a verdict — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short or Reel.

| Time | Picture / text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Labelled sample insight. | “An insight is not a verdict.” |
| 3–13s | Actual correction control. | “If Pehlu’s reading misses something, add context or disagree.” |
| 13–17s | Reflection link. | “Your perspective still matters.” |
| 17–20s | URL. | “Start with one question.” |

## C. Pause means pause — 25 seconds

**Use:** maker post and Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Notification preferences. | “You choose the rhythm.” |
| 4–16s | Pause/stop route, using a test account. | “A daily prompt should not become a demand. Find the pause control before you connect.” |
| 16–22s | Privacy link. | “Telegram and AI providers have their own processing roles.” |
| 22–25s | URL. | “Read those details first.” |

If a control cannot be reproduced, fix it or show only the public explanation, clearly labelled. No staged successful notification, health outcome or emotional testimonial.
