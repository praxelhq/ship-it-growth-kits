# Pehlu — Positioning

**Live:** https://pehlu.app/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/pehlu

## The first thing to get right

Your product is a reflection practice, not a system that knows the hidden truth about someone. The privacy page says relevant answers are processed by OpenRouter and a model provider, and Telegram handles its own messages. Do not translate “private reflection” into “everything stays on your device”.

## The actual offer

Checked 29 October 2026: the headline is **“There’s more to your story.”** The page offers one question daily, a weekly perspective and room to disagree with an insight. **“Find your Pehlu”** leads towards account creation; the public links include `/auth?mode=signup`. The public page did not establish a paid price. Do not invent a free-forever allowance or a monthly fee.

The page describes Telegram delivery at a chosen time, an account-held journal and notification pausing. The [privacy summary](https://pehlu.app/privacy) makes the processing/storage limits explicit. Account creation, bot connection, scheduled delivery and deletion were not completed in this research; reading their descriptions is not an end-to-end test.

## The person to start with

An adult who already likes answering a thoughtful journal prompt but does not keep opening a separate app. They use Telegram and want a small recurring invitation, not a diagnosis or a personality score. Start with people who volunteer for a neutral product demonstration, not people posting distress or private relationship problems online.

**Pitch:** One thoughtful question on Telegram, with space to answer and a weekly perspective you can challenge.

**Short version:** A daily reflection question, at your pace.

The first useful moment is answering one question and finding that answer again. The weekly insight is a later test: does it cite the person's actual reflections, and can they reject a reading that does not fit? Do not sell certainty about their personality.

## Why choose this over a journal?

[Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) is $0/year with unlimited text entries and prompts; a paper journal also needs no subscription. Pehlu must earn use through the chosen-time Telegram rhythm and evidence-linked, correctable weekly perspective. It cannot win by pretending ordinary journaling is expensive or an AI interpretation is authoritative.

Your board target remains **1,000 consumer signups in thirty days**. Count accounts and completed first reflections separately. The small-network scenario in `02` is below that target; report the gap rather than turning every notification into a user.
