# Crimson Farm OS — Landing-page fixes

Read [entry](https://crimsonearthos.in/) and [pricing](https://crimsonearthos.in/pricing), including a real-browser inspection, on 29 October 2026. Actual heading: **“Who is signing in?”** Actual CTAs: **“I'm the Owner”** / **“I'm Staff”**. The offer states a 14-day trial, then monthly/yearly per-farm billing; no numeric price rendered. Authenticated records, voice entry, role boundaries and payments were not tested.

## 1. Explain the job before the role choice

Current role gate assumes the visitor already knows the product.

Replacement introduction: **“Keep the farm's daily records together. Owners review; staff record the work they are allowed to enter.”** Keep the two actual role buttons and Hindi option.

Show an owned synthetic day log without requiring a private farm upload. Do not remove the useful role distinction merely to make a generic landing page.

## 2. Publish the actual amount

Current pricing says simple per-farm pricing but renders no rupee subscription value.

Replacement offer structure: **“14-day trial — no payment to start. After trial: your current monthly or yearly farm price, shown before payment.”** Fill the actual amounts from configured billing before launch; do not guess them. Show taxes, renewal timing and cancellation route.

## 3. Reconcile staff expense entry and money visibility

Current staff description includes expenses, while pricing says staff never see money.

Replacement permission summary: **“Staff can enter only the records assigned to their role. See exactly which amounts and reports are visible before inviting them.”**

Test real owner/staff accounts with synthetic records and publish the actual permission matrix. Do not declare an access flaw without reproducing it, or guarantee confidentiality from a slogan.

## 4. Make voice review unavoidable

Current pricing promises Hindi voice records for expenses, labour, spraying and harvest.

Replacement confirmation: **“Review the date, crop, quantity, unit and amount before saving. Correct any word or number that was heard incorrectly.”**

Verify the supported confirmation/edit flow before promising it. If voice records save immediately, prioritize a clear correction/review mechanism and explain the actual behavior. No agronomic dosage recommendation belongs in generated marketing examples.

## 5. State connection and sync limits

Current “works on any phone; updates live on every device” is a broad promise.

Replacement: **“Check your connection and confirm the record appears in the owner's view before relying on it.”** Add exact device/browser support and offline behavior only after testing.

Test weak network, retries, duplicate entries and another phone. A success toast alone is not proof the record reached the owner.

## 6. Give reports a reconciliation path

Current crop-wise cost reports sound useful but need traceable source records.

Replacement report note: **“Totals come from the records entered here. Review missing days, duplicate items and units before using the report.”**

Use synthetic records to verify edit/delete effects, crop assignment and totals. Do not call an unaudited report financial advice or a profit guarantee. Check privacy, retention and export before actual worker/financial data. A root login redirect is not a broken website.
