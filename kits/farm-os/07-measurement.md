# Crimson Farm OS — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** All [four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) apply: real operating farm business; its own actual records onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; owner name and phone privately known with permission. Keep identifying details outside the public repo. Count separate businesses only when genuinely separate, not each crop/field/device/worker.

Activation: a supervisor/owner records one actual workday and the owner reviews date, crop, units and amounts against the original record. Report reviewed days beside the business total. A voice attempt, a trial signup or your synthetic demonstration is not activation. Payment is separate from recent real use.

Private records were not accessed; baseline values are unknown until recorded.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine farm businesses / latest independent use | Product records plus private owner register; exclude internal accounts. |
| Owned workdays recorded and reviewed | Actual entry/review status, not microphone clicks. |
| Corrections and duplicates | Error categories, unit/date/crop mistakes; no worker identity in growth analysis. |
| Staff visibility and sync failures | Synthetic role tests and permitted issue reports. |
| Trial expiry, actual paid amount, refunds | Configured billing/transaction records, not an invented public rate. |
| Traffic/course baseline | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/farm-os), with date/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude worker names/attendance, wages, private finance, voice transcripts, precise farm location and account/payment fields from replay. Aggregate events can record ownfarmcreated, workdayrecorded, entrycorrected, ownerreviewed, syncconfirmed, laterindependentuse. Store only what matches the actual privacy notice and permissions; do not collect raw farm registers for a growth dashboard.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Six approaches scheduled. If role permissions or units cannot be verified, pause actual financial data and fix before wider adoption. |
| Day 14 | 15 approaches. Compare substantive conversations, own-record setup and owner-reviewed days; no field/worker multiplication. |
| Day 21 | 21 approaches. Inspect independent recent use and unresolved duplicate/missing records. Ask about the workflow, not crop yield. |
| Day 28 | 30 approaches. Audit all four tests against ten businesses. |
| Day 30 | Report actual qualifying farms, reviewed days, corrections, trial/paid status, hours/travel and shortfall. |

Base assumptions: 30 × 60% × 70% × 70% = 8.82 businesses, about nine, leaving a gap of one to ten. At 30% conversation, 4.41. Three statuses and two introduction requests are upside only. Replace rates after six approaches and again after 15. If geography makes 30 approaches infeasible, show the lower input; do not pretend a long travel day produced three owner conversations.

An owner-led assisted trial is useful but fails independence if no one returns without your prompt. A completed daily log does not prove higher yield, lower chemical use or profit. Quote only permitted specific observations, with actual dates and sample size; never publish private farm/worker data or invented case studies.
