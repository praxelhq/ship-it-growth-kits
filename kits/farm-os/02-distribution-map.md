# Crimson Farm OS — Distribution map

Start with accessible commercial polyhouses and the supervisor who makes the daily record. Do not advertise to every person interested in farming.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing commercial-grower relationships | Qualify active production, a real owner and daily record responsibility. Arrange visits; never enter a farm uninvited or interrupt spraying/harvest work. | Ten blocks of three farms = 30 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two supplier/agronomy introductions | Ask a known professional to forward the workflow invitation, not disclose its customers or recommend agronomic advice. | Days 8 and 15; #4. No guaranteed customers. |
| Own WhatsApp Status | Show an owned synthetic day log and the human correction step. | Days 9, 18 and 26; #3. Guess 20–100 views and 0–2 relevant replies per status, excluded from base. |
| One owner/supervisor trial | Test a single day's own permitted records after a role/data check. | Day 12; #5. Onboarding for one farm, not extra reach. |

Base assumptions: 30 approaches × 60% substantive conversations × 70% own-record setup × 70% independent recent use = 8.82, about 9 farms. **This leaves a real gap of about one to the target of 10.** At 30% response, about 4.4. These are guesses, not observed farm-software benchmarks. A higher actual conversion or additional accepted introductions must close the gap; do not count staff seats or increase the forecast silently.

The ten three-farm blocks are Days 4, 5, 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, 22, 24 and 27. A block can be scheduled owner calls rather than three distant visits; travel is not free. Reserve one local commercial-growing area and combine only agreed nearby visits. If travel or harvest timing cuts a block, lower the real approach count and reforecast.

[MANAGE](https://www.manage.gov.in/) is an official agricultural-management information entry point, not an endorsed distribution partner or a verified product-promotion forum. No grower newsletter, FPO mailing list or WhatsApp group permission is established. Ask a known organizer before any invitation, and coordinate one primary pitch per organizer/week across agricultural kits.

No paid agri fairs, scraped farmer numbers, bought lists or product trials disguised as crop advice. Do not collect worker personal records in outreach. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after a decline. The first proof is a reliable reviewed daily log, not a promise to improve yield or farm profit.
