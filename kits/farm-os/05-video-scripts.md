# Crimson Farm OS — Phone-demo scripts

Use fictional farm/worker records, your own voice and a phone screen recording. No real worker names, wages, private land location, crop treatment or payment information. Record permission tests with authorized sample owner/staff roles.

## A. One reviewed workday — 65 seconds

0–3 s: sample notebook beside the app. **“Does today's record match what happened?”**

3–15 s: choose actual staff entry and a synthetic crop/date. “Start with one day and the right role.”

15–30 s: speak a harmless Hindi harvest entry in the verified voice flow. “Check the quantity and unit, not just whether the microphone moved.” Leave one actual review/correction uncut; do not fake a save.

30–45 s: show the owner on a second device inspecting the actual saved record. “The owner compares it with the original note.” If sync fails, repair or show the failure, not a staged success.

45–55 s: actual owner/staff permission difference after verification. “Know what each role can see before using private records.”

55–65 s: trial and pricing page. “Fourteen-day trial, no payment to start. Confirm the ongoing farm price. crimsonearthos.in.”

## B. Unit hook — 20 seconds, Status

0–3 s: fictional quantity beside two different units. **“The number alone is not the record.”**

3–12 s: real review of crop/date/unit. “Check every field before using the daily total.”

12–20 s: owner confirmation and URL. “A reviewed day first. Crimson Farm OS.” No transcription-accuracy statistic.

## C. Role hook — 25 seconds, Short

0–3 s: owner/staff entry choices. **“Two roles need two real tests.”**

3–15 s: show verified permitted views with synthetic data. “Check which entries, amounts and reports the staff account can actually see.”

15–25 s: owner review and URL. “Keep confidential data out until the permission boundary is clear.” If not verified, record only the public entry and say the role test is still pending.

Capture exact role choice, actual voice/edit controls, original versus saved entry, second-device receipt, published trial and missing numeric price. Label waiting-time cuts and fictional records. Do not use a real farmer's livelihood claim as an invented emotional hook.
