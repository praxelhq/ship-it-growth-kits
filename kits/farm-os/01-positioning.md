## The first business

An owner-managed commercial polyhouse with one supervisor and a daily harvest/expense notebook. Start with one active crop and one worker willing to record the day's work. A hobby gardener, an agronomy student and a farm-input seller are different customers.

**Pitch: Get today's farm records into a reviewed owner log before the notebook and voice messages drift apart.**

Short version: **One crop, one daily log, checked by the owner.**

Use a single day's attendance, harvest quantity and permitted expense record. The owner must check units, date and field/crop assignment. Hindi voice entry is a published feature claim, not verified transcription accuracy; reject a wrong number rather than silently treating it as a crop-cost fact.

## What the offer actually establishes

The [pricing page](https://crimsonearthos.in/pricing) says **14-day free trial, no payment needed to start**, then monthly or yearly per-farm billing in INR by UPI Autopay or card, processed by Razorpay. It lists unlimited team members, crop-wise costs/reports and updates across devices. **No numeric subscription price rendered**, including in a real browser. Do not invent a rupee rate, a free-forever plan or a paid discount.

It also says **“staff never see money”**, while the staff entry says staff can record expenses. Those may be compatible through carefully limited entry/view permissions, but they require a role test. We did not establish a privacy vulnerability or prove those permissions safe. Have authorized owner/staff accounts inspect what is actually visible before using payroll or confidential accounts.

## The manual alternative

An existing paper register plus a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) daily summary can record attendance, quantities and expenses. Crimson Farm OS must earn adoption by making the supervisor's record easier to enter and the owner's reconciliation easier to inspect. It should not claim agronomic recommendations, better yields or pesticide safety from administrative data entry.

Verify a harmless Hindi sentence with an actual local unit: the correct date, crop and quantity must survive confirmation and owner review. Test a weak connection, a duplicate entry and another device. Do not invent offline support because the page says any phone.

**Target: 10 real farm businesses in 30 days.** Under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), each must be a genuine operating farm business, have its own actual records onboarded, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Staff seats, fields and multiple crops do not automatically become separate business customers.
