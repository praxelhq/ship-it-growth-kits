# FiguredIt — Distribution map

Lead with operators who already have a list and can challenge its ranking. The problem is prioritization, not buying another contact database.

| Route | Action and permission | Quantity / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small B2B consultancy relationships | Qualify one concrete offer, a permitted company list and a founder who owns outreach decisions. Use company enquiry routes or consented professional introductions. | Ten blocks of five businesses = 50 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two founder/sales-operator introductions | Ask a known operator to forward one invitation, never expose its CRM or customer list. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a labelled synthetic score, missing evidence and a disqualifier that changes ranking. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, not a base conversion claim. |
| One operator-owned ranking review | Review a willing business's top accounts privately, using sources it is authorized to process. | Day 12; #5. Existing-trial activation, not another customer. |

Base guesses: 50 approaches × 40% substantive conversations × 70% owned-list setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, approximately 10 customer businesses. At 20% conversation, 4.9. The ten five-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts and introductions add zero guaranteed conversions. Deduplicate the consultancy, not merely account emails.

Keep this service-consultancy lead-ranking wedge separate from NXT's technical complaint research, PitchPanel's consumer-market clinic and Before's product concept tests. One founder organizer receives one primary invitation per week. Ask before a group post; no workshop or newsletter acceptance is established. A public demo is a useful maker-feedback destination, not a reason to launch into every startup directory.

No purchased leads, scraped personal contacts, automated DMs, fake customer enquiries or paid directory boosts. The tool's contacts-export feature is not permission to use personal data indiscriminately. Ask for one relevant introduction, allow an opt-out and stop after a decline. One relevant follow-up after seven days is enough.

If output evidence cannot be checked, pause the “explainable” pitch and repair the breakdown. Do not sell a numerical score as confidence that the target will reply or buy. Record real outreach outcomes only through channels and data use the operator is authorized to run.
