# FiguredIt — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://figuredit.in/) and [pricing](https://figuredit.in/pricing), 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Figure out who your best customers are.”** CTA: **“START FREE ANALYSIS”**, plus **“VIEW PRICING.”** Free $0 one dataset/25 companies; Starter $24/month; Pro $59/month in the observed USD display. Account, upload, analysis, export and payment were not exercised.

## 1. Bring the hypothesis boundary into the first screen

Current hero says best customers; the lower section correctly says an ICP needs real outcomes.

Replacement: **“Prioritize accounts with a fit hypothesis you can inspect.”** Support: **“Use your offer and lead evidence to choose what to validate next. A score does not prove who will buy.”**

Keep a public demo before account creation and preserve the useful rule-based score explanation.

## 2. Keep fictional company labels attached

Current sample is explicitly fictional but contains precise headcount, revenue, stack and budget claims.

Replacement label on every sample component: **“Fictional account · illustrative inputs and recommendation.”**

Do not let a screenshot strip away the disclosure. Remove a sample implication that headcount/revenue automatically ensures a named role or discretionary budget; those are hypotheses to verify.

## 3. Distinguish evidence, inference and missing data

Current breakdown includes “detected” stack and high-conviction language.

Replacement evidence block: **“Observed source and checked date. Inference from those inputs. Unknown fields and reasons to disqualify.”**

Test missing/recently changed evidence and contradictory sources. A decimal score needs its actual rule explained; do not display fake confidence from a guessed missing field.

## 4. Explain Free as one total dataset

Current comparison says one total dataset, while some headings use datasets/month.

Replacement strip: **“Free: one dataset total, 25 company rows and 25 analyzed company domains. Companies-only CSV; report watermarked.”**

Confirm duplicate-domain handling, 30-day reruns and whether editing counts as a new dataset. Starter's PDF remains watermarked according to the comparison; do not imply every paid report removes it.

## 5. Reconcile the currency language

Current amounts render USD while “India-friendly pricing” promises rupees.

Replacement: **“Current displayed currency and billing amount: shown before checkout, with applicable taxes.”** Provide an actual working currency choice if supported; otherwise remove the conflicting rupee promise. Do not invent an exchange rate or annual plan from refund copy.

## 6. Make upload permission and export clear

Current paid plans offer contact exports and the page says uploaded data is processed for AI-assisted analysis.

Replacement upload note: **“Use data you are authorized to process. Company-only input is enough to try the fit review. Read the privacy notice before including personal contacts.”**

Test a permitted synthetic upload, wrong columns, duplicate domains, row limits, export contents and deletion. Check the report keeps sources/inferences distinct. Never send a real personal-contact list just to verify a feature; public sample output is not proof the authenticated pipeline works.
