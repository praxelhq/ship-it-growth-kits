# FiguredIt — Phone-demo scripts

Use fictional company-only rows and a specific fictional service. Record your screen and voice. No personal contacts, real CRM screenshots or confidential deal notes. Label all sample metrics.

## A. Challenge the ranking — 65 seconds

0–3 s: two fictional account rows. **“Why is this one first?”**

3–15 s: show a permitted sample list and one clear offer. “The offer matters. I am not ranking every company for every business.”

15–30 s: real analysis or public sample, labelled accurately. “This score is a working fit hypothesis.” Leave one breakdown expansion continuous.

30–45 s: inspect a source/inference distinction and a disqualifier. “A missing budget or wrong need can change the next conversation.” Do not fake live sources if the sample is illustrative.

45–55 s: show how the operator records the changed order, in the actual product or a clearly separate review sheet. “Your judgment still controls the list.”

55–65 s: allowance and URL. “Free is one dataset, 25 companies. Start with the demo at figuredit.in.”

## B. Precision hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: fictional 91.5 score beside a question mark. **“This is not a 91.5% chance of buying.”**

3–12 s: show the actual score components and sample label. “A rule-based fit score summarizes inputs. It cannot know the deal outcome.”

12–20 s: evidence question and URL. “Inspect the reason before writing the opener.” No invented conversion result.

## C. Disqualifier hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: apparent top account marked for review. **“The most useful result might be a no.”**

3–15 s: one harmless hypothetical mismatch, clearly labelled. “If the service cannot solve this need, a big company is not a good prospect.”

15–25 s: changed priority and public demo URL. “Test the fit hypothesis. Keep what the evidence supports.”

Capture actual CTA/auth boundary, public demo label, score breakdown, missing evidence, Free limits and observed USD pricing. If upload or analysis has not been verified, demonstrate the public sample honestly; do not splice it into a claimed customer result. Label shortened waiting time.
