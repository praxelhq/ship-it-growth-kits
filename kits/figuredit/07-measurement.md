# FiguredIt — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each consultancy/operator business must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; its own permitted lead data and offer onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep contacts outside the public repo. The prospect companies inside one dataset are not customers of FiguredIt.

Activation: the operator completes an own-list fit analysis, inspects the reasons for at least one priority and records a validation question/disqualifier. Report this beside customer totals. A sample-score toggle, 25 uploaded domains or a download attempt is not 25 customers. Paid conversion and actual outreach outcomes are separate measures.

No private account data was accessed; baseline values are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real customer businesses / recent independent use | Workspace records plus private permitted owner register; exclude tests. |
| Own-list setups and analyses completed | Actual dataset status, not homepage samples. |
| Evidence reviews / changed priorities | Status events or consented aggregate feedback; no copied CRM content. |
| Missing/incorrect evidence | Private quality log using synthetic reproductions where possible. |
| Real exports, paid plans/refunds | Completed output and billing records, not plan-card clicks. |
| Traffic/course metric | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/figuredit), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude personal contact lists, private company strategies, CRM rows, analysis text where confidential, account/payment screens and access links from replay. Aggregate events can record ownlistadded, analysiscompleted, breakdownopened, priorityreviewed, exportcompleted, laterindependentuse. No raw personal lead data is required for acquisition analytics.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Ten approaches scheduled. If operators cannot distinguish sample scores from real evidence, fix 04 and the demo before more promotion. |
| Day 14 | 25 approaches. Compare conversations, own-data setups and completed review. If operators cannot inspect evidence, repair the explanatory workflow. |
| Day 21 | 35 approaches. Check independent recent reviews and actual outcome feedback. A list's top score is not a customer win. |
| Day 28 | 50 approaches. Audit all four business tests, with one customer per operating consultancy. |
| Day 30 | Report qualifying businesses against ten, activation, return use, exports, revenue/refunds, hours and gap. |

Base assumptions: 50 × 40% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 20% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two introduction asks are upside only. Replace rates after ten approaches and again after 25. If the tool is used once for a single list and then abandoned, report useful one-off analyses separately from final-week independent customers. Do not manufacture return use with a reminder right before measurement.

Real outreach/win/retention outcomes can challenge an ICP over time; a small month-one sample does not validate the scoring model. Never report a sample revenue/stack as real company evidence, an inferred role as a verified buyer, or a high score as purchase intent. Publish aggregate lessons only with the operator's permission and actual sample limitations.
