# FiguredIt — Positioning

Checked 29 October 2026 against [figuredit.in](https://figuredit.in/) and [pricing](https://figuredit.in/pricing). Actual headline: **“Figure out who your best customers are.”** CTA: **“START FREE ANALYSIS”**, leading to authentication, with **“VIEW PRICING”** and a public `/demo` alternative. Upload, analysis, account creation, exports and checkout were not tested.

Your strongest caveat is already on the page: **a lead list cannot prove who will buy**. Treat the ICP as a working hypothesis until outreach, deal outcomes and retention support it. A 91.5 sample score is not a 91.5% purchase probability.

## The first business

A small B2B implementation consultancy with an existing permitted list of 20–25 company domains and one clearly described service. It has a founder doing outreach and actual past outcomes it can compare. Start with one service and one buyer problem, not a generic list of every funded company.

**Pitch: Review why each account fits your offer before choosing the next conversation.**

Short version: **An account-fit hypothesis you can inspect.**

The opening job is to rank an owned lead list, inspect the evidence behind the top few companies and record one disqualifier that changes the outreach order. The operator must validate need, timing, authority and budget through permitted real conversations. Inferred buying roles are not confirmed contacts or consent to send email.

## Actual offer

[Pricing](https://figuredit.in/pricing) renders Free $0: one dataset total, up to 25 company rows, 25 AI-analyzed companies, companies-only CSV and a watermarked report. Starter $24/month: three datasets/month, 100 companies each, filtered CSV/XLSX, contacts export, segments/5C strategy and PDF. Pro $59/month: unlimited datasets, 500 companies each, unwatermarked PDF and priority queue. The comparison says Starter PDF remains watermarked. Duplicated contacts count as one company by domain; reruns within 30 days are described as free.

The page says “India-friendly pricing” and mentions rupees, but the observed amounts were USD. Do not invent an INR equivalent or tax-inclusive rate. Verify actual currency, GST, billing and refund terms before purchase. A Free allowance is one dataset total, not a fresh monthly entitlement.

The public example explicitly uses fictional accounts, scores and recommendations, including headcount/revenue/technology assumptions. Do not name those as real prospects or reuse their metrics as verified company facts. The scoring is rule-based and AI-assisted, not a proven causal model of customer fit.

## The real alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) list can hold domain, observed need, source/date, disqualifier and actual outcome. A founder can score it manually. FiguredIt must earn its place through a clearer evidence breakdown and review process, not by pretending a list of guessed attributes becomes a validated ICP.

Prove one permitted upload, exact row/domain limits, transparent missing evidence and an export that keeps source and inference distinct. Start with company-only data; personal contacts are unnecessary for the first ranking test.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count consulting businesses using FiguredIt, not the companies inside their lead lists. Each must onboard its own permitted data, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Demo viewers and twenty-five scored domains are not twenty-five customers.
