# Chaptr — Video scripts, recovery-first

Use your own recording and original reading commentary. **Do not film an owner-only screen as if strangers can use it.** No private team access, real account details, copied reviews or book spoilers.

## A. Fresh reader, real route — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “If a new reader can't open it, the launch hasn't started.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Current public state, no private dashboard details|“I test Chaptr in a fresh browser, not my owner session.”|
|10–25s|Recovered public page only after fixed|“This is the intended public explanation and actual first action.”|
|25–42s|One uncut real supported action|“I'm showing what a new reader can really finish, not an imagined feature.”|
|42–52s|Actual success or honest blocker|“This is the result. Anything still limited stays visible.”|
|52–60s|URL and small reading-circle invitation|“Once it works, bring one book and test the real experience.”|

If access remains blocked, make this a private recovery recording, not a polished launch video. Don't invent the missing shots.

## B. A useful question — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A plot summary isn't a discussion.” Show your own spoiler-free question about a book you've read, then the real supported conversation surface only after verified. Say: “I want to know what another reader noticed, not spoil the ending.” No copied book passage or fake reply.

## C. Logged-in isn't public — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “It works for me is not the public test.” Show an owner-authorized test browser with no private data, then a fresh public browser result. Say: “The second one is what a new reader gets. That's the route to fix first.” Do not reveal deployment credentials or bypass access controls.

## D. One real exchange — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “Two real readers beat twenty empty profiles.” Show your original note and a consented reply, or an example labelled synthetic. Say: “The useful result is one exchange somebody chooses to return to. A staged demo is not community traction.” Record only after confirming the actual feature and visibility controls.
