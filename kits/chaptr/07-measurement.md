## Weekly gates and honest arithmetic

|Review|Evidence|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Fresh public access, actual headline/cost/CTA, completed first user action|If absent, acquisition remains blocked; recovery is the progress to report.|
|Day 14|Real signup definition, activation event and first testers' return|Don't count an organizer conversation or a restored homepage as activation.|
|Day 21|Actual reader exchange and best qualified acquisition route|If no repeated useful action exists, revise the job instead of buying reach.|
|Day 30|Recovery status, actual signups, activations, returners, reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000 and all still-unverified features/access limits.|

One 12-reader invitation, five organizer notes and four feed posts are a small initial experiment. At an assumed 20% visit→registration rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified visits; that rate is a scenario input, not a measured benchmark. If access delays the month, date the acquisition start accurately rather than pretending 30 days of promotion occurred.

Keep weekly actual rows with access status, visits, real signups, defined first actions, later returns, source and hours. No seeded discussion, synthetic review or hidden owner-only action is public community traction.
