Even after recovery, this small plan supports tens of accounts, not a substantiated 1,000. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→registration rate, 1,000 requires 5,000 visits. The first task is to measure actual access, first-use and real reader exchange. A large community membership count cannot fill that forecast gap.
