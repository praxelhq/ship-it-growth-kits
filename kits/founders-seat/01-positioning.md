# Founder's Seat — Positioning

**Live:** https://fintech.pelicanx.in/founders-seat/play/ · **Category:** Games · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/founders-seat

**A simulated consequence should start a debrief, not certify someone a CEO.** Actual game-entry H1 checked in browser 29 October: “Take the seat.” CTA “Create account and start trial.” It describes a post-seed logistics SaaS startup in Bengaluru, 36 months, seven free days then **₹269 once**. No account, business decision or payment was submitted. The initial extracted “Loading” did not mean the site was down; the rendered account screen is available.

Your first player is an adult MBA student who understands basic cash/runway but wants to experience delayed tradeoffs between hiring, product and sales. Start with a short first-month exercise and an explanation of the decision, rather than requiring a full campaign in one classroom break.

**Pitch:** Run a fictional startup month by month, make constrained choices and inspect the consequences and debrief.

**Bio:** One fictional company. Thirty-six months of choices.

The [landing page](https://fintech.pelicanx.in/founders-seat/) calls the alpha live, labels scenario metrics illustrative and offers individuals a seven-day trial followed by one-time unlock. [Terms](https://fintech.pelicanx.in/founders-seat/terms/) say ₹269 inclusive of applicable taxes, never renews. Production entitlement/Instamojo payment weren't tested. Refund policy is conditional for description mismatch and requests within its stated window; don't invent a seven-day no-questions guarantee.

Faculty offers are separate: first-three-programme pilot and indicative cohort pricing are institutional sales, not the assigned consumer goal. Instructor dashboards, common seeds and individual judgment grading are claims to verify, not tested teaching outcomes. Public landing says nine panels; game entry says ten. Don't advertise exact panel count as a differentiator until reconciled.

The $0 alternative is a spreadsheet plus a fictional case discussion with peers. It can make assumptions explicit without pretending to reproduce reality. Your difference is the model's delayed consequences and replay/debrief, if those behave consistently. In-game EV, ownership and returns are fictional; don't imply real investment guidance, admission, hiring evidence or founder competence from a score.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups** as real individual accounts, with first completed month and reasoned debrief beside it. Pilot enquiries, faculty seats and browser-local saves don't become extra students. Privacy says faculty may see progress for programme access: explain visibility before anyone joins.
