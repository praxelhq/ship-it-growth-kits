# Founder's Seat — Video scripts

Use an authorized test account and fictional game data. No real cap table, employees, company financials, classroom names or implied assessment certificate.

## A. First-month demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“What am I choosing not to do this month?”**

2–12s: show actual trial entry/first dashboard after authorized setup. “A fictional post-seed company, not your real business.”

12–27s: inspect one genuine decision and available information. “These are the constraints and the assumption behind my choice.”

27–42s: make the actual choice and advance as appropriate, cuts labelled. “The model may delay the consequence.” Don't invent a month-four effect without playing it.

42–54s: show the real decision log/debrief after verified. “Does it explain what I could know, not just the outcome?”

54–60s: URL, **7-day trial · ₹269 once afterward · modelled outcomes**. If the log isn't available, fix it or state the limit rather than mock a successful result.

## B. Focus hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Enough ideas. Not enough attention.”**

2–12s: actual Focus constraint and two available choices. “Doing one thing means delaying another.” Don't fabricate a resource display.

12–18s: product/URL. “Explain the tradeoff before clicking.”

## C. Outcome hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Good result. Good decision?”**

2–12s: real own-test result and available information at decision time. “Those aren't automatically the same.” No fake success chart or real-world return.

12–20s: actual debrief and model-limit label. “A useful simulation invites the question.”

## D. Offer hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“An individual trial isn't an institutional contract.”**

2–12s: show ₹269 individual one-time offer versus separately labelled programme offers. “Try as an individual; cohort products have their own terms and readiness.”

12–20s: actual trial/URL. No assertion faculty dashboards were tested or an unnamed university endorsed it.
