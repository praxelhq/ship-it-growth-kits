# Founder's Seat — Distribution map

**Start with individual players and a bounded decision debrief.** No tuition, prizes or institution-wide adoption required to test the first month.

| Route | Calendar volume / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult MBA/early-career peers | Five Day 5, five Day 10; use trial, not payment pressure. | 10 visits × 40–70% signup = 4–7 accounts. | #1 |
| Two existing strategy/game groups | Admin requests Day 9; one approved invitation Days 12/13. No exported roster. | 2 × 40 readers × 20–40% visits × 40% signup = 6–13 accounts. | #2 |
| Four LinkedIn posts | Days 8/15/20/26 at 12:30 IST, actual model/debrief lessons. | 4 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 40% signup = 5–72 accounts. | #3 |
| Three original process clips | Days 11/19/25, actual authorized game footage with synthetic company data. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 40% signup = 2–48 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited peer debrief | Ask one known organizer Day 10; optional eight adults Day 13, no claimed faculty endorsement. | 8 × 40–60% signup = 3–5 if accepted; zero otherwise. | #5 |
| Maker feedback | Optional [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) Day 22 after current rule/slot check. | Zero baseline; builder interest isn't an activated student. | #6 |

Roughly 20–145 gross accounts before overlap under these assumptions. At 40% visit→account, 1,000 needs 2,500 distinct qualified visitors. First-month completion/debrief will be fewer. Four posts and three clips don't guarantee the reach; replace rates after two posts.

No paid MBA newsletters, faculty licence purchases, prize pools or fictional free institutional seats. A faculty member may permit a free optional exercise, but a class roster is not a marketing list. Newsletter mention requires actual approval, zero baseline until accepted. Don't promise exam/career benefits or use student names/scores as public social proof.

Coordinate different organizers from F1/social-game sessions and founder-readiness products. Preferred c1 game debrief Days 10–13; c2 games Days 17–20 and Adya founder review Days 24–27. These aren't booked slots. No second product pitch to one group inside seven days; do not frame a simulation exercise as fundraising access.
