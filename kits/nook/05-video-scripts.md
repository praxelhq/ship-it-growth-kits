# Nook — Video scripts

Don't film a fictional app. The first recording is a recovery proof; a true product demo follows only after the canonical destination and task exist. No private preview URLs, tokens, account identifiers or deployment settings on screen.

## A · The corrected first-use path · 60 seconds · private reviewer introduction

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Controlled title card. **“Does the link reach the product?”** | “The first test is reaching the actual product.” |
| 2–12s | Old shortener outcome, no advertising clicks. | “The submitted short link reached an intermediary, not an explanation of Nook.” |
| 12–25s | Open the recovered canonical page in a fresh browser. | “This is the verified public entry.” |
| 25–45s | Perform one actual supported non-sensitive task, naming what it does. | Use the real task and controls, not a prewritten feature invented for this kit. |
| 45–55s | Show the actual result and access/data limits. | “This is what a new visitor can actually complete today.” |
| 55–60s | Hold verified public route. | “If the first action differs from the explanation, tell me where.” |

If the destination is still unresolved, stop after the honest recovery explanation. Do not present the partial clip as a working-product demo or publish it to attract signups.

## B · A page response isn't a product result · 20 seconds · recovery lesson

Hook: **“A 200 response isn't a successful first use.”** 0–8s, show the third-party redirection heading. 8–15s: “This page loaded, but it didn't establish what Nook does.” 15–20s: “Check the destination and the actual task.” Use for a factual technical lesson, not as invented product functionality or proof the entire domain is dead.

## C · Explain it back · 25 seconds · private usability review

Hook: **“What did you expect this button to do?”** 0–8s, show the actual recovered first action without clicking. 8–18s, show its real result: “The page's explanation and outcome should agree.” 18–25s: “If they don't, fix that before adding traffic.” Narrate the true supported job once verified. Don't use a real reviewer's face, voice or private inputs without permission.

## D · The honest missing fact · 20 seconds · maker update

Hook: **“Don't borrow the shortener's description.”** 0–8s, compare intermediary heading with a plain Nook status card. 8–15s: “Metadata from the middle of a link isn't evidence of the destination's features.” 15–20s: “Recover the real page, then demonstrate the real task.” No fabricated customer story or launch-success claim.
