## Honest arithmetic

The recovery calendar schedules three known-reviewer invitations. At an assumed two-thirds acceptance, that is **two diagnostic reviews**. Test accounts/sessions are excluded from real traction. One corrected-entry status post has **zero forecast qualified acquisition** because the audience and reachable demand aren't known. Optional directory routes likewise contribute zero until the real job and eligibility are verified.

No responsible conversion forecast to 1,000 can be derived from these inputs. After recovery, calculate required visits as target divided by observed visitor-to-defined-user conversion, and apply the actual channel volumes scheduled. Do not borrow a generic percentage and claim it is a Nook benchmark. Keep the remaining target gap visible.
