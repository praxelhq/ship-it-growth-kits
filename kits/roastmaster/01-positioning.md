# RoastMaster — Positioning

Live: https://roastmaster.online/ · Other / B2C · [Course profile](https://shipped.money/p/roastmaster)

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, labelled unique genuine one-pager roast starts if no account is required. Beside starts report completed reports and one useful, truthful revision. Waitlist emails, sample opens and test-checkout clicks are separate.

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Everything you made is mid. Let's prove it.”** “Roast me” leads to a real [one-pager form](https://roastmaster.online/roast/one-pager): **“Roast my one pager.”**, 200–8,000 characters, and **“We roast the document, not you.”** This is not merely a waitlist. No private text was submitted and no generated response was verified.

The homepage displays free roasts and **Fix ₹99** for One Pager. LinkedIn, dating and store cards are coming soon; don't promote them as usable. The [sample report](https://roastmaster.online/r/sample) shows ₹99 before/after rewriting but explicitly says **“Payments are in test mode during launch.”** Treat the fix as a displayed test offer, not a verified live purchase or entitlement. The 4/10 sample is illustrative, not a real customer's result.

First user: an adult student with one factual project/resume page who wants blunt feedback on vague writing and can tolerate a playful tone. Not everyone wants ridicule; make a plain-feedback option or clear tone preview available before intake. The wedge is one vague bullet turned into a clearer, still truthful statement. Never invent metrics to satisfy the roast.

Pitch: “Get a sharp critique of your one pager, then make one truthful improvement.” Bio: “Roast the document. Keep the facts.” [Resume Worded's public free checker](https://resumeworded.com/score) is a real alternative; paid pricing was not established. A trusted peer reviewing a document costs no tool fee. RoastMaster must win on specificity and actionable, respectful feedback, not a promise of employment or a crueler personality.

The weakness is comedy without useful correction, plus unclear live payment status. Resolve data handling before private resumes, test the actual free report, and stop implying the paid fix is ready until provider mode and delivery are verified. A suggested number is never permission to fabricate work.
