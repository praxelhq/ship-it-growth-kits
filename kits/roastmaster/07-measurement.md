# RoastMaster — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, interpreted as unique genuine one-pager roast starts only if the tested flow is no-account. Choose the actual account/session definition before outreach. Report **completed reports and one useful truthful revision** beside it. Static sample views, waitlist signups and payments in test mode do not qualify as product activation or revenue.

Day-0 actual users, completed reports, accounts, paid orders, leaderboard position and analytics status are unknown. Record dated aggregate numbers from authorized systems. [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is free, but setup and masking were not audited; exclude pasted text, resumes, report content, emails and payments from replay/event payloads. Do not copy a user's document into a growth sheet.

Track source→task started→submission accepted→report returned→useful correction understood. Ask consenting users about the step, not their employment history. Keep fictional navigation testers separate from genuine document-use activation. Deduplicate retries and return visits with privacy-preserving counting; disclose anonymous limitations. A second report is not a second person.

`06` budgets 20 invitations, two circle bulletins and three owned posts. `02` guesses 10–27 starts before overlap, not 1,000. At an assumed 25% qualified-visit→start rate, reaching 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified unique visits. That is a requirement to test, not expected traffic. Optional listing upside remains zero until observed.

W1: if reports fail, pause invitations; if comedy overwhelms useful correction, change the tone/rubric. W2: if reports finish but no truthful revision is possible, improve specificity rather than insisting on numbers. W3: check repeat use, privacy questions and false-claim suggestions. W4: expand only a source producing useful honest revisions, otherwise report the gap. Paid customers require an actual live payment and delivered entitlement; the sample's test-mode purchase cannot establish either. No hiring, dating or business outcome is inferred from a roast score.
