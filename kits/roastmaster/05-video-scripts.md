# RoastMaster — Video scripts

Use fictional resume text and your own voice. No public humiliation, real candidate identity or paid editor.

**A. One useful revision, 45 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “If the roast only says ‘add numbers,’ it hasn't finished the job.” Show a fictional vague bullet, labelled. Show the real form and length requirement. After testing, record the actual returned critique. Say, “What can I change without inventing a fact?” Show your own grounded revision and label it your revision, not paid output if that did not generate. End: “Free one-pager critique today; paid fix in test mode. roastmaster.online.” Never splice the static sample into a pretend live response.

**B. Roast the document, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “The document can be weak without the person being weak.” Show that actual form sentence, then a fictional vague claim and a more concrete action. Say, “Critique needs to point somewhere useful. A personal insult doesn't.” End: “Tell me whether the tone helps you revise.”

**C. No made-up metric, 20 seconds; accepted circle.** Hook: “Don't invent 37% to improve a bullet.” On-screen show fictional “Helped with research” then “Compared five public pricing pages and summarized differences” labelled synthetic. Say, “Clear scope is better than a fabricated outcome.” Do not imply those five pages were a real applicant's work.

**D. Sample versus result, 20 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “This 4/10 is a sample, not your score.” Show the labelled sample and test-payment notice. Say, “A demo can explain the tone. It isn't proof a report generated for you or that a paid fix is live.” End on the actual free form URL.
