# FirmQuote — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each freelance web-builder business must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; own actual proposal data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identifying details outside the public repo. First-quote anonymous ownership still needs a privately permitted way to verify the business; a browser count alone is insufficient.

Activation: the builder produces a complete own proposal and a real intended client can review the exact version. Report separately actual approvals and priced changes. A client view, a quote version or a booking-page addition does not become another software customer. Payment is not handled by the product according to the current page.

No private records were accessed; baseline values are unknown until recorded.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine builders / latest independent use | Product/ownership records plus private permitted business register, excluding your tests. |
| Own quotes created | Actual proposal records, not a seeded fictional example. |
| Client reviews and exact-version approvals | Actual status/version/timestamps; opening a link is not acceptance. |
| Pending/accepted changes and total accuracy | Real records with arithmetic checked; no private client terms in growth logs. |
| Lost links/access problems | Private issue categories; never log full editor tokens in analytics. |
| Current allowance/pricing and traffic | Configured offer, existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/firmquote), dated and period-labelled. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before installing another tag. Exclude proposal text, private prices, client names, acceptance names, editor URLs and account fields from replay. Aggregate events: ownquotecreated, clientviewopened, versionaccepted, changeproposed, changeaccepted, laterindependentuse. A legal/payment outcome cannot be inferred from those events.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Ten approaches scheduled. If private edit access or exact-version acceptance is unclear, fix before public distribution. |
| Day 14 | 25 approaches. Compare conversations, real owned proposals and independent use. A template admirer is not a business customer. |
| Day 21 | 35 approaches. Check latest independent proposal/change activity and real version clarity. Do not prompt everyone to log in before measuring. |
| Day 28 | 50 approaches. Audit all four policy tests, counting builder businesses rather than their clients. |
| Day 30 | Report actual qualifying builders against ten, activation, approvals, changes, hours and target gap. |

Base guesses: 50 × 40% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 20% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two introduction asks are upside only. Replace rates after ten approaches and again after 25. A builder with only one quote may receive value but fail the final last-seven-day rule; show that useful one-off use separately rather than inventing retention.

An approval is not verified identity, a legal signature, collected revenue or a guarantee extras will be paid. Record downstream project results only when actually known and permitted. Never publish real client disagreements, fictional testimonials or invented saved/recovered revenue.
