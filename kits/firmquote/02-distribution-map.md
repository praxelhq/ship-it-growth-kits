# FirmQuote — Distribution map

Lead with a fixed-price proposal due now. People who only admire a template are unlikely to return independently in the final week.

| Route | Execution / permission | Input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing freelance web builders | Qualify real paid web work, an imminent fixed-price quote and responsibility for scope. Use an owned professional account or the builder's public business enquiry route honestly. | Ten blocks of five builders = 50 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two design/developer introductions | Ask a known professional to forward an invitation to a consenting builder; no client list or private proposal disclosure. | Days 5 and 8; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a fictional scope, one exclusion and a priced change; no fake earnings. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One quote-clarity review | A willing builder checks its own deliverables/exclusions privately, with client permission where relevant. | Day 12; #5. Existing-trial support. |

Base guesses: 50 approaches × 40% substantive conversations × 70% own-quote setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 builder businesses. At 20% conversation, 4.9. Blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Three posts and two introduction asks add no guaranteed conversions. A builder with five clients is one business.

Use Days 5–8 for fixed-price web-builder invitations. CreatorDeal later owns creator usage-rights/scope, booked solo paid UGC fulfilment, QuotePilot distributor RFQs and AsliRate buyer comparisons. FirmQuote should not pitch the same creator deal as those products; it needs website deliverables and change orders. Consult [creator coordination](../../shared/a2-creator-operations.md) before using a shared freelance organizer.

No professional newsletter, Discord server or developer-group promotion permission is established. Ask a known organizer before posting and keep one primary invitation per organizer/week. No automated DMs, scraped freelancer contacts, purchased directory boosts or pretending to hire a builder just to sell software. One relevant follow-up after seven days; a decline ends contact.

Do not call the acceptance record an e-signature service or promise the client will approve/pay faster. A client refusing unclear scope is useful feedback, not a conversion failure to hide. If version/approval behavior fails, stop public distribution until repaired.
