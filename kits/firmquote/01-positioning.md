# FirmQuote — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.firmquote.tech/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/firmquote

Checked 29 October 2026 against [firmquote.tech](https://www.firmquote.tech/). Actual headline: **“Your client approves the exact scope. Extras get priced.”** CTA: **“Write a quote”**, pointing to `#quote-builder`; **“See how it works”** is separate. The page says the first quote needs no account and gives a private editor link; more quotes use email sign-in, while clients never need an account. No quote, client acceptance, email or payment was submitted during research.

## The first business

A freelance web builder with an actual fixed-price small-business website proposal due this week. The builder already knows the client; the missing agreement is what the price includes, what it excludes and what happens to an extra booking page after approval. An hourly consultant and a person selling generic proposal templates are different starting customers.

**Pitch: Agree the exact website scope first, then price additions on the same client link.**

Short version: **A dated yes to this version, not a vague yes to everything.**

The first useful workflow is one builder's own proposal, an explicit revision allowance, client review of the exact version and a separately priced change. The landing example's website $2,500 and booking-page $400 are illustrative quote values, not FirmQuote subscription prices or customer revenue.

## What is and is not promised

The page describes accepted quotes locking, edits becoming new versions and accepted change orders adding to the running total. It explicitly says an approval is **not a legal signature**, does not verify identity and does not take payment. Preserve that boundary. A typed name/date can support communication without proving legal enforceability or that an invoice was paid.

Supported currencies are INR, USD, EUR and GBP with no automatic conversion. No paid software-plan amount was displayed in the checked entry. Do not invent a monthly fee, a payment-collection feature or an unlimited-free-forever promise. Verify the actual first-quote allowance and ongoing terms before a larger rollout.

## The alternative

A builder can write a scope in a document and keep version/approval/change rows in free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/). FirmQuote must earn adoption through a clearer version history and client acceptance path, not by claiming email or documents cannot record an agreement. It is not a substitute for qualified advice about a particular contract.

Prove one harmless internal quote end to end: private editor access, public client view, edit/new-version behavior, acceptance lock and change total. Test that a client link cannot edit the quote and an old link does not silently accept the wrong version. Never expose editor links in a public recording.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Freelance builders buying a work tool are B2B. Each must onboard its own actual proposal data, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Client viewers, quotes and change orders are separate counts, not additional software customers.
