# Usurp / Crest Studio — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://usurp.games/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Your Family Coat of Arms, Designed in Minutes”**; the action is **“Design your coat of arms”**. Designing is free without an account. The personal download pack is $9 once; the commercial Crest Logo Kit is $29. These are separate offers. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an adult choosing a small family keepsake, not seeking a genealogy certificate. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Make the pivot unmistakable

**Current:** The URL says Usurp while the live hero is a family coat-of-arms designer.

**Write this instead:**

> Crest Studio by Usurp — design an original family coat of arms.

**Verify:** Use the current product name in title, link preview and cohort profile; remove obsolete ladder claims.

## Fix 2 — Repeat originality at the decision

**Current:** The FAQ explains that arms are not automatically shared by everyone with a surname.

**Write this instead:**

> A new design for your family today. Not a claim to inherited or registered historical arms.

**Verify:** Place it near the surname field and before checkout, not only in the FAQ.

## Fix 3 — Separate free design and paid files

**Current:** “Free to design” and “Everything you get for $9” can be conflated.

**Write this instead:**

> Design free. Download the personal print-and-share pack for $9 once.

**Verify:** Show the exact boundary before the final download click. Confirm taxes and actual deliverables at checkout.

## Fix 4 — Keep the commercial kit secondary

**Current:** A $29 Crest Logo Kit sits beside the personal family gift.

**Write this instead:**

> Making a family keepsake? Choose the $9 personal pack. Need a commercial brand kit? See that separate offer.

**Verify:** Do not imply the two packs contain identical rights or files.

## Fix 5 — Label the example family

**Current:** The page shows six Carter designs.

**Write this instead:**

> Example family, example designs. Type your own name to start a new design.

**Verify:** A visitor should not confuse samples with previous customer purchases.

## Fix 6 — Show the file after the editor

**Current:** The page promises PNG/SVG and a printable certificate.

**Write this instead:**

> See a sample file pack: artwork, certificate, wallpaper and profile image.

**Verify:** Use a labelled sample download and test print dimensions; do not simulate a paid customer export.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
