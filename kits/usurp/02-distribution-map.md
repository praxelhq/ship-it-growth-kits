**Lead with family-gift demonstrations and permissioned personal sharing.** People can understand the gift by seeing a motto change. The domain’s medieval games and commercial kit dilute this first audience if all are pitched together.

## A month that adds up

Six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), four maker posts, twenty personal invitations, two accepted sessions, one optional Peerlist and one gated Show HN support roughly 53–164 design starts before overlap. Use 40–130 distinct people as a working range. The target remains 1,000; this modest network alone does not reach it. A larger real gifting partner or much stronger observed clip response is required. Purchases are separate from free design starts.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Use the shared game/creative organizer window Days 17–20 for a broad campus event only if the audience is adult and interested in original design. Do not pitch the family gift to someone expecting the old spend ladder; tell them what changed.

## Directories, letters and accounts

Peerlist and a usable Show HN are verified optional discovery routes. No genealogy forum, gift newsletter or heritage society has agreed to promote the tool. Do not advertise in ancestry discussions as though this product verifies lineage.

Paid listings, sponsorships, prizes and promoted posts are outside your zero budget. An editor accepting a story is upside, not a scheduled conversion event. When a page's posting rules cannot be read, do not infer permission from another creator's post.
