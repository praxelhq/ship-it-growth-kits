# Crewup — Video scripts

Use a real plan with consenting adults or a clearly labelled non-joinable demo. No private addresses, attendee contacts or unconsented faces. Phone footage/screen recording, your own voice and captions are enough.

## A. “Sometime” becomes a plan —60 seconds

**Where:** homepage and private organizer invitation. **Hook:** “This isn't a plan yet.”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|Synthetic “we should do something” chat, no real names|This isn't a plan yet.|
|2–14 s|Actual plan creation, if tested|We need a host, a time, a place or link and a headcount.|
|14–30 s|Show the real/demo-labelled details and cost|People should know what they're joining before creating an account.|
|30–43 s|Actual successful RSVP and capacity/update state|A clicked button isn't enough. I want a confirmed place and clear updates.|
|43–52 s|Consented activity setup, not invented attendance|Start with a circle that already knows one another.|
|52–60 s|URL and actual trial terms|Crewup has a ten-day trial; check current post-trial terms before joining.|

If a function isn't tested, label the shot proposed and don't present it as a completed outcome.

## B. Five details before a group invite —20 seconds

**Where:** own social feed. **Hook:** “Pick these before sending the link.” Show cards: host, time, place/link, cost, headcount. Say, “One specific plan beats twenty suggestions.” End with founder disclosure and product URL, no private invitation details.

## C. The update people need —25 seconds

**Where:** plan-help clip. **Hook:** “The location changed. Did everyone see it?” Use authorized test accounts and a real tested update. Show confirmation or an observed limitation honestly. Say, “A plan tool has to keep the changed details clear, not just collect the first RSVP.” No claim of notifications unless they actually arrive.

## D. Empty feed, honest start —25 seconds

**Where:** Peerlist gallery. **Hook:** “Don't fill an empty feed with fake events.” Show a labelled empty/demo state and creating one actual small plan. Say, “I'm testing one group doing one thing before promising a whole city.” End with the current scope and cost explanation.

Capture creation, detail view, confirmed RSVP, update and cancellation only as implemented. Never stage a large meetup to make early traction look bigger.
