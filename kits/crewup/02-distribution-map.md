No access to a named café, venue or private group is promised. The first plans use resources/relationships you already have with permission; no room hire, ads, tickets or paid creators. If a group organizer says no, don't invite the members individually to bypass that decision.

Four plans, 40 relevant invitation attempts, two co-host asks and small owned/directory discovery plausibly form a **15–40 activated-account experiment**, with repeated people removed. This does not establish 1,000 accounts. Your next step is a second genuine circle adopting the plan habit, not a wider geography filled with empty cards.

Keep this distinct from the cohort's café-presence, building-introduction and fair-meeting-zone products. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week. Repeat Crewup plans can stay in the same consented circle; don't add every cohort product to it.
