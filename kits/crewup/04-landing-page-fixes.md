# Crewup — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from rendered copy and controls. “Explore activities” opens the discovery section; “Post a plan” and “Get started” are JavaScript actions. No authentication, posting, RSVP or attendance was tested. Lack of cards in one extraction does not establish that no activities exist.

## 1. Explain the price after the trial

**Current:** “Sign up to start your 10-day full-feature trial. Fees are waived during the trial.” No post-trial amount was visible in the checked page.

Before signup, publish the actual platform fee, when the trial starts/ends, which actions change afterwards and whether anything renews automatically. Until verified use **“Review current trial and post-trial terms before joining.”** Don't say free forever or encourage repeated accounts to extend a trial. No broader invitations until a new participant can understand the actual cost.

## 2. Narrow the headline to one plan

**Current:** “Your people. Your plans. Your kind of fun.”

Try:

> **Turn your group's next idea into one actual plan.**
>
> Choose a host, time, place or online link and headcount. Start with people you know, then keep everyone updated when details change.

Keep the friendly visual direction. A useful plan card should lead the first screen, not an unproven claim that every visitor will find new friends nearby.

## 3. Give discovery an honest empty state

If no relevant results exist for a filter, show **“No matching plan here yet. Try another filter or organize a small plan with your existing circle.”** Do not generate fake hosts, locations, participant counts or urgency. A synthetic demo card must be visibly labelled and not joinable as though real.

## 4. Make a plan inspectable before commitment

Show actual host identity at the appropriate privacy level, date/time/time zone, format/place, cost, capacity, expectations and how updates/cancellations work. Verify the current implementation rather than promise controls from a mockup. “Women-only” is a participation filter, not evidence of screening or guaranteed safety.

## 5. Separate public browsing from private details

Explain what is public, what joining reveals and what the host can access before an RSVP. Start tests with known adults and a non-sensitive meeting place/online room. No full private addresses, phone numbers or attendee lists in open marketing screenshots. Publish implemented block/report/contact behaviour, not a fictional moderation team.

## 6. Prove the full loop

Run an authorized test: create a real free plan, join from another willing account, change a detail and confirm the participant sees it. Test cancellation and capacity. Suggested failure copy: **“Your place wasn't confirmed. Check the plan before travelling.”** Do not equate a pressed Join button with an accepted RSVP unless it actually succeeds.

## 7. Phone and preview

Proposed title: **“Crewup — one plan your group can act on.”** Description: **“Host, details and headcount for a real activity. Check current trial/cost and participant privacy before joining.”** Test actual link previews, date/time and join controls on phones. No measured clipping/load defect was established. Keep public demo content separate from a private event link.
