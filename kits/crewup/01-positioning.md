# Crewup — Positioning

**Live:** https://crewup4you.me/ · **Category:** Consumer social planning · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/crewup

**Start with one real plan and people who already know one another.** A citywide discovery feed with no relevant activity nearby is not a reason to create an account. Don't invent hosts or attendance to make the directory look busy.

The real headline is “Your people. Your plans. Your kind of fun.” “Explore activities” goes to the discovery section; “Post a plan” and “Get started” are JavaScript controls. The page offers a ten-day full-feature trial with fees waived during that period. It doesn't show the post-trial amount in the checked public text. Explain the actual fees and trial end before asking someone to commit to a plan. Signup, hosting, RSVP and attendance were not tested by the writer.

## Your first group

An existing adult friend or student circle that keeps suggesting a board-game or study evening but never settles the details. They already have a free place or an online room and their own activity materials. They need a concrete plan, headcount and a host, not strangers in every city.

Pick one repeatable activity, one manageable group and four weekly plans. A second host taking responsibility is more useful than a thousand people browsing unrelated cards. The free event itself should not conceal a platform charge after the trial.

## The pitch

> Turn your group's “we should do this” into one plan: a host, time, place or online link, headcount and a clear way to join.

Bio version: **One actual plan, with people ready to show up.**

The wedge is getting from a group-chat idea to a real, attended first activity. Keep public marketing distinct from private event details. Don't claim “verified members,” guaranteed safety or a women-only filter as proof of screening; the checked page establishes a filter, not a vetting process.

## Alternatives

A normal group chat and calendar invitation cost₹0 in extra planning software and already hold the relationships. [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/) publicly states membership is free, while individual events/organizer arrangements can differ; don't invent organizer pricing. Crewup must earn the extra account with clearer plan/headcount handling, not pretend group chats cannot organize anything.

## The honest weakness

You have a density problem before a marketing problem. One good plan with eight interested known people beats an empty citywide feed. Test whether a new participant can see the actual cost, host, expected conduct and cancellation/update route. Do not require private contact details in public posts or turn personal attendance into a public leaderboard.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-account target**, with actual RSVP and attendance/host activity alongside. The first small-group month is unlikely to reach it. Report unique people, repeated attendance and the target gap separately; a host creating four plans is not four customers.
