# Crewup — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 new consumer accounts in 30 days.** Report **confirmed RSVPs and actual attended/hosted activities** alongside. An account is activation only when a person confirms a real plan; attendance and a host running a plan are stronger separate outcomes. Four plans by one host are not four distinct users.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another script. Mask identities, contacts, private locations and participant lists. Minimal events may include `account_created`, `plan_published`, `rsvp_confirmed`, `plan_updated`, `rsvp_cancelled`, `plan_cancelled` and voluntary aggregated `attended` only where actually measurable. A discovery view isn't an event and a Join click isn't a confirmed place.

## Arithmetic

Four plans×10 relevant invitations×50% RSVP×80% attendance produces 16 attendances under the assumed rates. Unique attendees may be 12–16 or fewer because people repeat. The broader first experiment might yield 15–40 activated accounts with owned/directory discovery. It is not an evidenced path to 1,000 accounts. At an assumed 20% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 would need 5,000 qualified visits; no such traffic is already secured.

Measure repeat attendance and a second real host as evidence the plan habit works. Don't force a bigger geography or fake supply to inflate the first number. Invitations sent, event-card views and unconfirmed interest remain separate.

## Weekly decisions

| Day | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | Can known adults understand cost, details and RSVP status? If not, fix before a second plan. Record actual attendance or cancellation. |
| 14 | Does the ten-day trial ending confuse or block people? Explain real terms; no new-account workaround or implied free continuation. |
| 21 | Did someone besides you host, or a participant return voluntarily? If not, ask what the existing group chat does better. |
| 30 | Report accounts, confirmed RSVPs, unique/repeat attendees, distinct hosts, actual completed/cancelled plans, hours and target gap. |

These are experiment decisions, not social-network benchmarks. An honestly cancelled plan is better than an event left publicly joinable when it won't happen. Keep participant consent and private details intact when sharing a founder lesson.
