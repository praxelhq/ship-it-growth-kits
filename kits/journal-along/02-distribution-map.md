# Journey Along — Distribution map

**Lead with adults who want a small daily planning/review loop.** Keep personal entries private; a public accountability confession is not the acquisition task.

| Route | Access/rule | Planning assumption | 03 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Known adult students/solo workers | Twenty personal invitations, no health/procrastination diagnosis or scraped contact list. | 20 × 60% visit × 50% meaningful start = 6. | #1 |
| Own LinkedIn | https://www.linkedin.com/ — four process/build posts, no invented follow-through percentages. | 4 × 300–900 views × 1% visit × 50% start = 6–18. | #2 |
| Own short demos | https://www.youtube.com/ / https://www.instagram.com/ — four staged harmless promise examples, clearly labelled. | 4 × 500–2,000 views × 1% visit × 50% start = 10–40. | #3 |
| Approved adult planning-tool walkthrough | Two actual organizers agree to an optional private exercise; no entry sharing or therapeutic session. | 2 × 15 adults × 50% start = 15. | #4 |
| Peerlist | https://peerlist.io/ — optional available slot after real save/check-in works, no vote requests. | One listing: 0–5 starts, uncertain. | #5 |

Scheduled inputs yield **37–84 meaningful starts before overlap**, provisionally **28–65 distinct people**. Rates/reach are assumptions, not platform benchmarks. Sample-entry explorers and repeated starts are not new adopters. Paid-plan conversion is unknown and is not included in the activation forecast. This does not establish 1,000 starts.

Prefer Days 15/18 only with different actual organizers from Pehlu, Bloomie, Journaleaf and other journaling/wellbeing products. Confirm real dates; at most one broad product invitation per shared group per week. No study community, newsletter, therapist or campus body has agreed to promote Journey Along. Do not enter someone's personal struggle thread with a product pitch or imply that a checklist substitutes for care.
