# Journey Along — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** The actual app permits no-account use, so count a new person's meaningful first real promise session under the existing policy: they create their first day's promises. Exclude homepage views, sample exploration, your tests and repeat days. Deduplicate later sign-in where consented measurement permits and disclose local/browser limits.

**Activation:** first real three-promise set followed by an evening check-in. Report later days and weekly reflection review separately. A self-trust score, AI reply or sample percentage is not validated behaviour change or psychological assessment. Do not require a weekly reflection before calling a first day useful; some plan entitlements differ.
