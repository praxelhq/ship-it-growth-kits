# Journey Along — Video scripts

Use harmless invented task text, visibly labelled as your demonstration. Do not present sample entries as real results or claim a full week was completed. Verify the actual real-day loop before using a save/check-in demo.

## A. A smaller promise — 35 seconds, homepage/planning invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | “Write the first paragraph.” | “Make the next step smaller.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual three-promise interface with demo label. | “Journey Along starts with three promises for today, small enough to describe clearly.” |
| 14–25 s | Actual evening check-in interface. | “Tonight, check what happened. Kept, partly done or missed can all be honest answers.” |
| 25–31 s | Sample versus real entry label. | “Try the sample first if you only want a look.” |
| 31–35 s | URL. | “Start a day at journeyalong.online.” |

## B. No public diary — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Harmless example promise. | “Your journal needs no audience.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual private-entry interface, no personal text. | “Use the daily loop for yourself. Feedback on the product does not require sharing what you wrote.” |
| 14–20 s | URL/sample option. | “Inspect Journey Along's sample first.” |

## C. Sample means sample — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Visible sample label. | “These are example entries.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual sample Mind Mirror with label kept. | “The sample shows the format. Its numbers are not a real user's progress or a promise of yours.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Three small promises. An honest check-in.” |

No mental-health, self-worth or productivity outcome claim follows from a score. Hold a real save/review clip until the actual flow succeeds.
