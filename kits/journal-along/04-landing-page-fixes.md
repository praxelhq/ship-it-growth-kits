# Journey Along — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Make the first task more concrete than the score

Current headline: **“Stop counting streaks. Start trusting yourself.”** CTA: **“Make today’s three promises”**, linking `/app`. Actual app entry offers a no-account path and sample entries. Keep those choices, add **“Choose three small things for today. Check in tonight without needing a perfect record.”** Do not claim the product measures self-trust as a validated personal trait.

Privately test real promise creation, evening check-in, reload and data recovery before wide sharing. Sample mode opening is not a successful real-day test.

## 2. Explain the actual billing interval

Homepage says Plus ₹199/Deep ₹499 “a month”; actual plan dialog says **“One payment covers 30 days. It doesn’t renew on its own.”** Repeat that near every price and checkout action. Do not call it a subscription until the actual model changes. Test payment/entitlement separately before claiming an upgrade works.

## 3. Keep sample labels persistent

Homepage sample Mind Mirror includes score 72 and comparison percentages. Sample exploration opened a full sample dashboard whose numbers can look personal after the entry screen. Put **“Sample entries — illustrative results”** on every sample tab/card and exported image. Exclude those entries from real counts and never use them as a testimonial.

## 4. Clarify storage and AI before writing

Homepage says entries stay in the browser, while app entry offers later sign-in for cross-device saving and paid AI replies. Explain which mode stores locally, what sync sends, which provider sees AI text and how deletion/export work. Publish the actual answers before personal-data acquisition; do not replace missing details with “nothing ever leaves your device”. Use harmless sample text for demonstrations.

## 5. Reduce judgement and future-feature confusion

Keep incomplete promises as an honest check-in option. Frame a Mind Mirror as **“A reflection to consider; correct or ignore what does not fit”**, not a verdict. Challenges and physical journals are forthcoming; no paid campaign should imply they are included today. Check phone input/keyboard, date boundaries, return-to-day and local-data clearing. Check existing analytics; exclude journal, score, identity and payment content from replay.
