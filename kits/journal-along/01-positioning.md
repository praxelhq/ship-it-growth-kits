# Journey Along — Positioning

**Live:** https://journeyalong.online/ · **Category:** Personal accountability journal · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/journal-along

## The live name is Journey Along

The roster slug remains journal-along. Actual 29 October 2026 homepage headline: **“Stop counting streaks. Start trusting yourself.”** CTA **“Make today’s three promises”** goes to `/app`. Actual Chrome entry says the tool works without an account and offers **“Make my first promises”** or **“Explore with sample entries”**, with optional sign-in later for saving across devices. The labelled sample-entry route opened a sample Mind Mirror; its numbers are not customer evidence or our outcomes.

Starter is free. Plus is advertised **₹199** and Deep **₹499**. Important billing detail from the actual in-app plan dialog: **one payment covers thirty days and does not renew on its own**. Do not describe these as tested auto-renewing subscriptions just because the homepage says “a month”. The checkout button was inspected, not clicked; no account, payment, personal journal entry or real generated partner reply was tested. Challenges and a physical journal are future items.

Start with an adult student or solo worker who makes an overly broad daily task list and wants three small explicit promises plus an evening review. The wedge is morning→evening→weekly reflection, not daily mood tracking, Telegram prompts or a diagnosis of procrastination. Pehlu's prompt/weekly-perspective flow is a separate invitation.

**Pitch:** Make three small promises, check in tonight and review what made them easier or harder.

**Bio:** Three small promises. An honest evening check-in.

[Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) offers Basic at $0/year; [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for personal use. A paper checklist is another no-subscription alternative. Journey Along must earn use through its specific promise/check-in loop and useful reflection, not a claim that other journals harm people or that its score clinically measures self-trust.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**. A meaningful first no-account promise session can count under the existing policy; deduplicate later accounts and exclude sample-mode visits. Activation is the first real three-promise set followed by an evening check-in. The “72”, “81%” and similar sample figures prove only the format, not effectiveness. Clarify local versus signed-in/AI data processing before people add private entries; do not promise all text never leaves the browser.
