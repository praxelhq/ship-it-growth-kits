# Closet Stylist — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation alongside it: **five real user-owned garments/accessories saved with reviewed details and one outfit viewed/selected**. Record a later wear-history return separately; a suggestion shown isn't proof it was worn. Don't require ten photos to count the first useful wardrobe, and don't count the duplicate roster entry twice.

## Day 0

Record actual accounts, successful saves, activated small wardrobes, outfits selected, wear-history returns and paid subscriptions from owner-controlled records. Public Free/Plus/Max cards are offers, not customers. Capture fresh [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/closet-stylist) rank, saved metric and timestamp. No authenticated baseline was established here; unknown is not zero.

The rendered draft privacy notice says optional Clarity is limited to the public homepage after consent and isn't loaded on private wardrobe/sign-in/billing routes; no custom identifiers/events are currently sent. Confirm the actual setup rather than adding another script or expanding tracking into private photos. Use aggregate application events only after implementing them appropriately. Never send clothing images, private links, account emails or raw outfit histories to public analytics/reports.

Suggested counts: `account_created`, `garment_save_succeeded`, `item_details_reviewed`, `first_outfit_selected`, `wear_record_saved`, `wardrobe_returned`. These are proposed measurements, not claims already instrumented. A Save click that failed is not a saved piece. Keep upgrade initiated, paid charge confirmed, cancellation and refund distinct.

## Forecast from the scheduled work

Assumptions, not measured wardrobe-app rates:

- 50 personal invitations × 40% try × 70% account creation = **14 accounts**.
- Two accepted groups × 20 viewers × 25% signup = **10 accounts**, zero if not accepted.
- Six own-feed posts × 200–800 impressions × 2% visit × 25% signup = **6–24 accounts**.
- One optional launch × 0–150 visits × 20% signup = **0–30 accounts**.

Total **30–78 before overlap**, and lower after deduplication. If half activate, that is only 15–39 activated wardrobes. The 1,000 target stays visible. At an assumed 25% qualified-visit-to-account rate, reaching it needs 4,000 qualified visits; this calendar does not supply that reach. Recompute with actual rates after 25 invitations rather than calling general fashion impressions users.
