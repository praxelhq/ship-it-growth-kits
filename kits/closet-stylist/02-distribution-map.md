# Closet Stylist — Distribution map

**Small, permissioned outfit experiments first; own-feed demonstrations second.** Launch only after the privacy/request route and fresh signup/save flow work. Keep this about existing clothes. Don't reuse Label Kanika Bhutani's wedding-shopping audience or Vibe Check's broad mood-decision pitch.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known student/young-worker circles | Five individual opt-in invitations on ten named days in `06` = 50. Ask about tomorrow's clothes; no body photos, borrowed account credentials or group additions. Hypothetical 40% try × 70% finish signup = 14 accounts. | #1 |
| 2 · Approved campus clothing-repeat session | Ask one sustainability/fashion organizer whether a no-shopping, five-piece session fits. No partnership or free slot assumed. At most two approved invitations to distinct consenting groups, days 6 and 20. Assumption: 20 viewers/group × 25% signup = 10 accounts total if both accepted. | #2 |
| 3 · Own Instagram/LinkedIn/X | Six posts on days 4,9,13,18,24,29, using your own garments. No reposting another person's wardrobe or manufacturing before/after savings. Assumption: 200–800 impressions/post × 2% visits × 25% signup ≈ 6–24 accounts. | #3, #4 |
| 4 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Official site advertises Monday Launchpad. Check the actual available Monday and eligibility; one working-product launch, no vote asks or duplicate accounts. Hypothetical 0–150 visits × 20% signup = 0–30 accounts. | #5 |
| 5 · [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) | Free submission needs an account/email verification and application review. Its FAQ says review backlog has no guaranteed timing. Optional correct listing only; no paid priority and zero guaranteed first-month users. | #5 |

[Whering's own editorial material](https://whering.co.uk/thoughts) is a checked source for wardrobe-audit vocabulary and examples, not a place to insert competing ads or copy testimonials. Its [official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/whering__/) is linked by the site; reply only when you have a relevant clothing-repeat observation. No access to its followers, newsletter or customer community is assumed.

No Reddit promotion permission or fashion-group rule was established. Don't invent a public-posting entitlement. A campus organizer can approve a small optional session; that does not authorize copying members' contacts or photos. Someone who dislikes photographing clothes should be allowed to leave without being pushed to finish.

Planning total: **30–78 accounts before overlap**, using the exact 50 invitations, two optional groups, six posts and one optional launch. Most won't activate. This is below 1,000. At day 14, decide whether five-item setup is short enough and whether an actual outfit is worth returning for before asking for more distribution.
