# Closet Stylist — Video scripts

Use your own garment photos, no faces, addresses or private wardrobe links. Label the account a demonstration. Screen recording and phone voice are enough. Show the real supported flow; don't fabricate an outfit result if the app fails.

## A · Five pieces, tomorrow's outfit · 60 seconds · own feed / launch

| Time | Shot and on-screen text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Five garments laid flat. **“Tomorrow is already here.”** | “My next outfit might already be in these five pieces.” |
| 2–14s | Select your own garment-only photo. | “I'm starting small, not photographing the whole cupboard.” |
| 14–27s | Inspect and edit the real item details. **“Check before Save”** | “These are suggestions. I correct anything that doesn't match.” |
| 27–39s | Save and show small wardrobe. | “Save adds the photo to my private online wardrobe.” |
| 39–52s | Open actual combination; show chosen adjustment. | “This is what it suggests. I decide whether I'd wear it.” |
| 52–60s | Free quota/URL, no private links. | “Ten photos free, no expiry. Closet Stylist dot in.” |

If outfit generation or saving doesn't work, fix that before recording a public success. Don't imply the browser preview tested private controls.

## B · The forgotten layer · 20 seconds · short reel

Hook, 0–2s: **“I forgot I owned this.”** Show one of your own layers. 2–8s, put it beside the small digital wardrobe: “It was at the back of the shelf.” 8–15s, show an actual combination with it: “I'm trying it with things already here.” 15–20s: “Would I wear it tomorrow? That's the test.” On-screen: **“Use what's already yours.”** No savings or environmental impact claim.

## C · Correct the suggestion · 25 seconds · onboarding reply

Hook: **“The app doesn't get the final say.”** 0–8s, show an actual editable garment field. 8–17s, make a correction appropriate to your item: “I know this piece. I review the category and colour before saving.” 17–25s, show the saved result: “An editable wardrobe is more useful than pretending every guess is right.” If no mistaken suggestion occurs, demonstrate an ordinary user preference change and say so.

## D · Wear it, then remember it · 20 seconds · retention post

Hook: **“A saved outfit isn't a worn outfit.”** 0–8s, show your chosen combination without another person's image. 8–15s, use the actual wear-history action if supported: “I record what I wore so next week's choice has some memory.” 15–20s: “Start with one day, not a perfect calendar.” Don't stage a wear-history control that hasn't passed your test.
