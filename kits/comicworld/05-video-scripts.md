# Comic World — Video scripts

Use your own harmless anecdote, with no unconsenting faces or confidential details. These scripts describe the actual planning job. Record a drawing/export only after you have really completed it; do not simulate a successful purchase.

## A. Storyboard first — 50 seconds

**Use:** homepage and adult creative-circle invitation.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Your own story in /create. Text: “Fix the story first.” | “Edit the story before the pictures.” |
| 3–16s | Type a short, harmless anecdote. | “In Comic World, I start with a story I am comfortable sharing.” |
| 16–32s | Make a real storyboard text/order edit. | “I can review the scenes and words before deciding to pay for drawing.” |
| 32–45s | Hold price/visibility information, not a fake completed order. | “Planning is free. Six-page comics are public. The twelve-page option can be kept off. Check the price before continuing.” |
| 45–50s | /create route. | “Start with your story at comicworld.me.” |

## B. The cheap edit — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short or Reel.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | One storyboard line highlighted. | “Change the line now.” |
| 3–12s | Make the actual edit before drawing. | “Not after every picture is finished. Comic World starts with the storyboard.” |
| 12–17s | Show planning versus drawing boundary. | “Plan free. Decide about drawing later.” |
| 17–20s | URL. | “Try the story-planning page.” |

## C. Public is a choice to understand — 25 seconds

**Use:** LinkedIn and Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Text: “Personal ≠ automatically private”. | “A personal comic is not automatically private.” |
| 4–15s | Actual visibility rule in the current terms/offer. | “Six-page comics are public on Explore. The twelve-page option can be kept off.” |
| 15–22s | Return to the story entry screen. | “Read that choice before adding a story or photo.” |
| 22–25s | URL. | “Then decide whether to begin.” |

These are short spoken lines, not a terms recital. Keep the detailed permission and purchase information in the caption and live page. If the live offer changes, update the script before recording.
