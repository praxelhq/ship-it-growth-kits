# Comic World — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://comicworld.me/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Your story. As a comic.”**; the action is **“Start my comic”**. Current terms say story planning/style/characters/storyboard editing are free. Drawing is paid once for six or twelve pages; the exact amount is shown before payment and was not established by this audit. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an adult deciding whether a personal comic will be public and what costs money. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Disclose visibility before input

**Current:** The hero invites personal/family stories, but terms say six-page comics are always public on Explore.

**Write this instead:**

> Before you start: six-page comics are public. Choose the current private-capable option only after reviewing its terms.

**Verify:** Show this before story/photo input and again before payment; do not bury it in terms.

## Fix 2 — Separate free planning from paid drawing

**Current:** The landing CTA says “Start my comic”; terms distinguish free planning from paid six/twelve-page drawing.

**Write this instead:**

> Plan your story free. Review the storyboard, then see the drawing price before you pay.

**Verify:** Make the actual amount, page count and included redraws visible before checkout.

## Fix 3 — Reconcile gallery and current format

**Current:** Gallery examples show several page counts while current terms describe six/twelve-page purchases.

**Write this instead:**

> Examples may use earlier formats. Your current purchase shows its exact page count and visibility.

**Verify:** Remove stale plan implications; confirm which formats can actually be bought today.

## Fix 4 — Keep approval as the first proof

**Current:** The page says story edits happen before pictures are drawn.

**Write this instead:**

> Review the storyboard first: reorder scenes, rewrite lines, then choose drawing.

**Verify:** Demonstrate a real edit, not a simulated output or customer quote.

## Fix 5 — Set photo permissions clearly

**Current:** Terms require consent for pictured people and restrict public figures/copying.

**Write this instead:**

> Use your own story and photos you have permission to use. Keep confidential details out of public comics.

**Verify:** Test the consent and rejection messages with harmless sample material; do not invent moderation guarantees.

## Fix 6 — Explain early-version limits

**Current:** Terms call this an early version and say drawing limits may change.

**Write this instead:**

> Early version: review current drawing limits, visibility and delivery expectations before buying.

**Verify:** Test a real internal purchase/delivery and describe failures plainly rather than promising flawless output.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
