## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. Free storyboard starts are interest/usage, not paid comics or necessarily accounts. Keep Google-account signups and completed approved storyboards separate; an account is currently required to draw. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Use the shared creative/game organizer window Days 17–20 only with adult participants and permission. Coordinate with Woof Chapter’s pet stories and Unposted’s photo output so each session has one clear job, not a mass AI-content pitch.
