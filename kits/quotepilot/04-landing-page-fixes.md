# QuotePilot — Prototype-page fixes

Read [quotepilot.dhukaan.in](https://quotepilot.dhukaan.in/) in a real browser on 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“QuotePilot”**, supporting **“AI-assisted quote drafting for industrial-supplies distributors”** / **“Demo prototype.”** Actions **“Load sample enquiry”** and **“Extract with AI.”** No software price or production account was established. Extraction and actual approval were not exercised.

## 1. Remove the contradictory real-enquiry instruction

Current section says paste the RFQ exactly as received, followed by a notice forbidding real/confidential requests because third-party AI processes them.

Replacement instruction: **“Use a synthetic sample enquiry only. Do not paste a real customer RFQ or confidential catalog.”** Keep it before the input, not a footer. Make Load sample enquiry the obvious first action.

## 2. Name the provider at the decision

Current notice names Pollinations.ai/open models.

Replacement action help: **“Extract sends this sample text to the named third-party AI service. Review its current processing terms before proceeding.”**

Do not advertise local/offline/private-RFQ processing or imply a provider certification. Real-data support needs a separately approved production data contract, not removal of the warning.

## 3. Keep synthetic prices and stock labelled

Current sample catalog lists industrial items with specifications, units, prices and stock.

Replacement table caption: **“Synthetic demonstration catalog. Not available inventory, a supplier offer or verified market pricing.”** Keep the label on every output/export/clip.

Do not present a sample stock quantity as current availability or a quoted rupee figure as software pricing.

## 4. Preserve ambiguity before any quote total

Current extraction says it flags ambiguous items, while human approval is required in the footer.

Replacement review note: **“Confirm exact item, specification, unit and quantity. Leave uncertain matches unresolved and ask the customer before quoting.”**

Test close specifications, absent quantity, box versus piece and unknown items with synthetic cases. No automatic substitutions or engineering suitability guarantee.

## 5. Put human approval beside the final action

Current footer says approval required before a quote reaches a customer.

Replacement final-step note: **“Draft only. A qualified business reviewer must check commercial and item details before any customer quote.”**

Verify which actual edit/approval/export/send controls exist; never imply customer delivery if only extraction is built. Use a synthetic recipient only for an authorized internal send test.

## 6. Establish a real deployment before acquisition claims

Current page explicitly says a real deployment would use the distributor's catalog/stock/prices; this prototype does not establish it.

Before marketing actual-data adoption, define approved processing, tenant access, own-catalog handling, deletion, support, price and exact current scope. Test with synthetic records first. Until then, call operators design partners and report zero qualifying customers if no legitimate own-data use exists. The course target is not a reason to bypass the public warning.
