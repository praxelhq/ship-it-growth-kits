# QuotePilot — Distribution map

**Synthetic design-partner evaluation first; actual-data acquisition is gated.** This is not a forecast of ten production customers from a public demo that forbids real RFQs.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing local industrial distributors | Qualify real RFQ work and an owner/rep who can discuss it without files or buyer details. Be honest that the demo uses synthetic catalog/data and a third-party model. | Ten blocks of three businesses = 30 discovery approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two supplier introductions | Ask a known professional to forward one synthetic-demo invitation, not share customers, commercial terms or enquiries. | Days 8 and 15; #4. No production customers assumed. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a synthetic unit/spec ambiguity and the mandatory human review, not an actual RFQ. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, no base customer forecast. |
| One rep-led synthetic review | A willing owner/rep inspects the public sample and identifies fields that must remain unresolved. | Day 12; #5. Prototype feedback only. |

Planning discovery scenario: 30 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 60% willing synthetic evaluations = 9 evaluations. At 25% response, 4.5. **Neither result equals customers.** No actual-data conversion rate is defensible until an approved production path exists. Even nine enthusiastic design partners can leave the business-policy count at zero.

Ten three-business blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts/intros are upside only. Record actual willing operators and their permitted general feedback; do not upload their catalog or RFQs as an “onboarding” workaround.

Keep industrial-supplies distributors separate from FirmQuote's web-builder scope, AsliRate's buyer comparison and Parchi's clothing billing. One primary invitation per market organizer/week. No trade newsletter, association endorsement or industrial WhatsApp promotion permission is established. Ask the known organizer before a group demonstration.

No paid marketplaces, bulk scraped distributor contacts, automated DMs, fake industrial buying enquiry or engineering advice. One relevant follow-up after seven days; a decline ends contact. The first outreach is a clearly disclosed prototype review with synthetic data, not a quote service for the next real order.
