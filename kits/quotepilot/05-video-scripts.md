# QuotePilot — Phone-demo scripts

Use only built-in synthetic enquiry/catalog or wholly invented non-confidential text. No real RFQ, buyer name, negotiated price or stock file. Record your screen/voice. Do not submit actual data to demonstrate extraction.

## A. The field a rep must check — 65 seconds

0–3 s: public sample-only notice. **“This is not a customer RFQ.”**

3–17 s: actual Load sample enquiry and synthetic catalog label. “The prototype sends sample text to a third-party AI service.”

17–32 s: actual permitted sample extraction only if you choose an authorized internal test; otherwise show the public input/notice and label extraction untested. “A draft needs review.” Leave an actual result legible; no fabricated output.

32–47 s: inspect exact spec/unit/quantity and ambiguity in the actual supported review. “A similar name is not proof of the correct product.” If editing is unavailable, use a separate labelled review checklist.

47–55 s: human-approval boundary. “No quote reaches a real customer from this demonstration.”

55–65 s: prototype and URL. “Synthetic evaluation first. Production permissions still need to be established. quotepilot.dhukaan.in.”

## B. Unit hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: synthetic piece/box labels. **“One box is not one piece.”**

3–12 s: exact unit and quantity review. “Preserve the buyer's intended unit before a rate becomes a total.”

12–20 s: sample-only notice and URL. “Check the draft; do not invent the missing detail.” No real price/suitability claim.

## C. Deployment hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: synthetic catalog disclosure. **“A demo catalog is not your stock.”**

3–15 s: public prototype versus a labelled requirements checklist. “Own catalog, approved processing and reviewer controls are production requirements, not proof supplied by this table.”

15–25 s: no-real-RFQ notice and URL. “Use the sample to expose gaps, not confidential orders.”

Capture actual headline/buttons, provider warning, synthetic table, real ambiguity behavior if exercised and human-approval notice. Label waiting cuts. Do not invent a successful extraction, customer quote, technical substitute or signed approval.
