## The material constraint

The public footer requires human approval before any quote reaches a customer and says extraction can be wrong. Keep that boundary. A model recognizing an item name does not establish the right bearing seal, dimensions, fastener grade, voltage or pack unit. No engineering suitability or safety judgment belongs in the marketing promise.

No software price, account, private workspace, own-catalog import or production data-handling path was established. Sample catalog rupee values are fictional item prices, not software fees or offers. Do not call this a purchasable quoting subscription or invent a free trial.

## Target gap

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each requires actual authorized own business data onboarded, independent use in the last seven days and a privately known owner name/phone. The current public notice forbids real RFQs and the catalog is synthetic. Therefore synthetic evaluations do **not** qualify, and current production customer capacity is not established. The honest count may remain zero until a safe authorized actual-data deployment exists.

Never ask distributors to violate the notice just to satisfy the target. Keep the ten unchanged, separate design partners from customers and report the product/data gate. A distributor liking the sample is evidence of interest, not onboarding.

## The alternative

The rep's existing approved quote process and a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) review checklist can keep item/specification/unit/quantity/price/source and open questions visible. QuotePilot must earn adoption through a correct reviewed draft and a proper production data contract. It must not sell an unchecked model result as a final quote or a suitable substitute product.

Prove a synthetic malformed/ambiguous enquiry, exact unit preservation, review/correction and human approval if actually implemented. Before real deployment, the owner must establish approved processing, catalog permissions, isolation, deletion and commercial terms. No customer records are needed to evaluate the public prototype.
