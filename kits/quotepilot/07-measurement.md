# QuotePilot — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each distributor must satisfy [all four policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): real operation; own actual authorized business data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identifiers outside the public repo.

**The public prototype forbids real/confidential RFQs and uses a synthetic catalog.** Synthetic tests, accounts that do not exist, positive replies and demonstration quotes cannot satisfy own-data onboarding. Keep the target unchanged but report qualifying customers as zero unless a separate approved production deployment actually exists and all tests are met. Do not remove the warning or request confidential data to improve the number.

Prototype activation is a willing rep inspecting a synthetic extraction, identifying exact item/unit/quantity and reviewing ambiguity. Production activation, if legitimately available later, is a correct reviewed own-data draft under an approved deployment. Report these separately; no technical suitability, inventory truth or final customer acceptance is inferred.

Private business records were not accessed; actual production capacity and customer totals are unknown.

Discovery guess: 30 × 50% × 60% = 9 synthetic evaluations; at 25% response, 4.5. These are assumptions, not customers. Three posts/two intros are upside only. A production customer funnel cannot be forecast responsibly from the present sample-only release. If zero qualifies, the shortfall is ten; explain the missing authorized product path.

A synthetic catalog price is not revenue or a supplier quote. A model extraction is not engineering approval, verified stock or permission to send a customer offer. Publish permitted general workflow observations, no real RFQs, invented accuracy rates, claimed time savings or testimonials.
