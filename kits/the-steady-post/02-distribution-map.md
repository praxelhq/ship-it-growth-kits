# The Steady Post — Distribution map

**Lead with small professional circles and sourced teaching posts.** You need a person who wants a weekly point of view, not a creator chasing daily impressions. Every forecast below is a planning assumption and can produce zero.

## Specific accounts, newsletters and directories

Use [Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) to find people showing real project work. A useful reply names the decision in their post and asks a real question; it does not paste your link. This is relationship work, not a guaranteed acquisition line.

## Honest month-one composition

Three sessions contribute 18–30 accounts, six owned posts 2–43, twenty personal invitations 3–5 and one Peerlist listing 3–50: **26–128 before overlap**. The same alumni may appear in several lines. This is the initial learning plan, not a mathematically sufficient route to 1,000.

At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 requires 4,000 unique qualified visitors. You need wider voluntary distribution after the first sessions, or a public post that substantially outperforms the initial assumptions. Ask each satisfied organizer for one introduction after a useful session; never count the introduction as permission or a signup. If the evidence does not support expansion by Day 21, state the shortfall rather than inflating a trend-view counter.
