# The Steady Post — Positioning

**Live:** https://thesteadypost.app/ · **Category:** AI · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/the-steady-post

**Your first sale is a second useful week, not a streak badge.** A writing product can make the first draft look good while never becoming a habit. Recruit people who already read about their field and help them explain one story twice, a week apart.

## Start with this person

An individual analyst, marketer or product associate with one to three years of experience, who reads industry news but rarely writes publicly. They do not need another feed. They need a credible angle they can connect to work they have actually done.

Keep the first circle narrow: early-career analysts from your own alumni network, using one field at a time. They should have enough experience to say “this is what I observed” without pretending to be an expert. Students can use the product, but Postifyy's first-project clinic already serves that audience; do not pitch the same campus organizers this month.

The [live hero](https://thesteadypost.app/) says “The calm way to post on LinkedIn every week.” The CTA is “Start free with LinkedIn,” implemented as an interactive button. The page also has a `#start` anchor. We read the public page and its links; OAuth, generation and publishing were not completed in this review. Do not describe that button as broken because it has no ordinary href.

## One line and a shorter introduction

> Turn one sourced story from your field into a weekly LinkedIn post grounded in your own experience.

> A sourced idea, your experience, one weekly post.

The source matters. Your page says trends arrive with an explanation and a link the user can check. Make the source-to-angle step the demonstration, not just another polished paragraph.

## The wedge

On Sunday evening the user chooses a story in their field, opens its source, adds one observation from work, edits the draft and confirms whether to publish or schedule. Next Sunday they return with a different observation. Your activation is the first approved/exported or published post; your retention test is that second week's independently initiated post.

Your page also offers carousels, infographics, scheduling and monthly profile audits. Keep those behind the primary job until someone has completed it. A carousel that takes an hour to perfect does not solve a weekly habit problem.

## Costs and alternatives, checked 29 October 2026

Your trial is **14 days or five AI drafts, whichever comes first**, with no card. Pro is ₹499/month or ₹4,999/year. Do not call the trial five drafts per month, or advertise a 14-day unlimited drafting period.

| Alternative | Actual offer | Your reason to exist |

| [Buffer Free](https://buffer.com/pricing) | $0; three channels, ten queued posts/channel, AI assistance | Scheduling alone is free elsewhere. Demonstrate field-specific sources and the user's own observation. |
| [Postifyy](https://postifyy.in/pricing) | Three free posts total; Growth displayed $11/month, Pro $15/month | Its starting material is a voice explanation. Your starting material is a sourced field trend plus relevant experience. |

Do not call the other cohort products inferior or imply they endorse you. These are different workflows, and people may prefer the free one.

## The honest weakness

Your promise depends on trend quality and personal relevance. A generic story with the user's job title pasted into it is not personalization. A source link that does not support the draft is worse than no source. Ask the first ten users to reject irrelevant ideas and show you why. Do not optimize for drafts generated when the useful metric is drafts they were willing to publish.

Your target remains **1,000 individual signups in 30 days**, with activated and returning writers beside it. The initial alumni/community plan cannot honestly guarantee that count; `02` shows the gap and `07` tells you when to change course.
