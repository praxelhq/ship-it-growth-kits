# FeeSathi — 30-day execution calendar

**Two engines run all month:**

1. **Daily outreach quota (weekdays):** 30 personalised WhatsApp messages to tutors from UrbanPro / JustDial / Google Maps. ~75 minutes. This is your highest-converting activity and it is non-negotiable.
2. **Daily group presence:** post or reply in 3 Facebook/WhatsApp teacher groups. ~30 minutes.

Everything else is scheduled around those two.

## The three things that decide this month

1. **Did you fix the Android signup flow on day 1?** If it's broken, everything else is wasted.
2. **Did you hit 30 WhatsApp messages a weekday?** 600+ messages is the floor. This channel converts at 2–4x anything else you have.
3. **Did a teacher-creator say yes?** It's the only path to a four-figure month. Pitch 25, expect 2–3 yeses, and make it trivially easy for them (send them a ready script and a demo page in their name).

---

## If a creator mention lands

Clear the calendar. Watch the signups in real time, be in the comments of their video answering every question personally, and make sure your server and your onboarding hold. A single good mention can deliver more users in 48 hours than the rest of the month combined — and the comments section is where you'll convert the undecided half.
