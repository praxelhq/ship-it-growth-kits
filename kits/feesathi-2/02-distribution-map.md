# FeeSathi — Distribution map

**The uncomfortable truth first:** every channel that normally drives 1,000 signups for a SaaS — Reddit, Product Hunt, Hacker News, X, dev newsletters — is worth approximately **zero** to you, because Indian tuition teachers are not on any of them. Posting there will get you builder applause and no users.

Your three real channels are: **free-tool SEO**, **Facebook/WhatsApp teacher groups**, and **physically finding teachers**. In that order of scale, and reverse order of conversion rate.

---

## Tier 1 — The free receipt generator is your growth engine 🔴

You already have `/receipt` — a free receipt generator, no signup. **You are sitting on the best acquisition asset in wave 1 and treating it as a footer link.**

Here is why it works: a teacher who has never heard of you googles *"tuition fee receipt format"* because a parent asked for one. They land, they get a receipt in 30 seconds, they are now a person with a solved problem who trusts you. *That* is when you offer the fee book.

### The queries to own

Each of these is a real thing Indian teachers search. Build a page for each one.

| Query shape | Monthly intent in India | Your page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tuition fee receipt format` / `fee receipt format pdf` / `fee receipt format in word` | High, steady | The generator, pre-filled for that format |
| `tuition fee receipt generator online free` | Medium, very high intent | The generator |

**Rules for these pages:** the free thing works immediately, with no signup, above the fold. The FeeSathi offer comes *after* they have their receipt, as one line: *"Tired of making these one at a time? Keep a fee book instead — free."*

**Realistic:** nothing in month 1. 300–1,500 organic visits/month by month 4, at a very high conversion rate because the intent is perfect. **This is the only channel here that compounds, so it starts on day 1 even though it pays late.**

Also: publish each template as a **downloadable PDF/Word/Excel file** and submit those to free template directories. They get shared in WhatsApp groups with your URL on them, forever.

## Tier 2 — Facebook and WhatsApp teacher groups 🔴

This is where Indian teachers actually are, in enormous numbers.

| Where | Scale | How to find | Realistic per post |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Facebook groups for Indian tutors** — "Home Tutors India", "Tuition Teachers", "Private Tutors Delhi/Mumbai/Hyderabad/Pune/Lucknow", city-level tutor groups | 5k–100k members each, dozens of them | Search Facebook for `tuition teacher`, `home tutor`, + each city name | 300–3,000 views |
| **Facebook groups for teachers generally** — "Teachers of India", CBSE/ICSE teacher groups, subject-teacher groups | 10k–200k | Search `CBSE teachers`, `teachers group India` | 500–5,000 views |
| **Music / dance / art teacher groups** | 2k–30k | Search by discipline + India | 200–1,500 |
| **WhatsApp teacher groups** | 50–250 members each, extremely high trust | Ask every teacher you meet: *"are you in any teachers' groups?"* Getting into 10 of these is worth more than any post. | 50–250 reads, **5–20% conversion** |
| **Telegram tutor channels** | 1k–50k | Search `tuition India`, `teachers` | 200–2,000 |

**The rule for all of them:** post the **free receipt generator or the free reminder-message templates**, not the product. A group admin will delete a product post and keep a post that gives teachers a free thing they need. Mention FeeSathi in one line at the end, or not at all and let people ask.

**Target: join 25 Facebook groups and 10 WhatsApp groups in week 1.** Post in 3–4 per day thereafter, rotating, never the same text.

## Tier 3 — Tutor directories: a list of your buyer, with phone numbers 🟠

Indian tutors list themselves publicly, with contact details, on:

| Directory | Scale | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UrbanPro** | 100,000s of tutors, searchable by city + subject | The single best prospect list available to you |
| **Superprof India** | 100,000s | Same |
| **JustDial** (search "tuition classes" + city) | Huge, with phone numbers | Phone/WhatsApp reachable |
| **Sulekha** | Large | Same |
| **Google Maps** — search "tuition classes near [area]" in any Indian city | Endless, with phone numbers | Also gives you coaching centres |
| **Facebook Marketplace / local classifieds** — tutors advertising | Medium | Very warm |

**Build a list of 1,000 tutors with names, subject, city and WhatsApp number in week 1.** Two days of work. Then WhatsApp them — 30/day, personalised, Hinglish.

This is cold outreach, so do it properly: never a link first, never a mass broadcast (WhatsApp will ban the number), always from a normal personal number with a real profile photo. Copy pack #6.

**Realistic:** 750 messages over the month → 25–40% reply (teachers reply; they're not jaded by SaaS spam) → **190–300 conversations → 60–120 signups.** This is your highest-conversion channel by far.

## Tier 4 — YouTube and short video 🟠

Teachers in India watch YouTube constantly, and a lot of them *are* YouTubers.

- **Your own channel:** three videos, Hindi/Hinglish — *"Tuition fees ka hisaab kaise rakhein (free tareeka)"*, *"Parents ko fees reminder kaise bhejein without feeling awkward"*, *"Free fee receipt kaise banayein 30 second mein"*. These rank in YouTube search, which is a search engine your audience actually uses. Realistic: 200–3,000 views each in month 1, growing.
- **Comment on teacher-facing videos.** There are large Hindi channels about running tuition classes and teaching as a career. Leave genuinely useful comments on the fee/admin videos. 500–5,000 impressions per well-placed comment, free.
- **Collaborate, don't pay.** Small teacher-creators (5k–50k subs) will happily demo a free tool that helps their audience. DM 20 of them. 2–3 will say yes. One mention is worth **200–2,000 signups** if the audience is right — this is your single highest-variance opportunity.
- **Instagram/YouTube Shorts:** the "awkward fee ask" is relatable content. A 20-second Hinglish reel about *the 5th of the month* will travel further than anything else you make.

## Tier 5 — Offline, local 🟡

Low volume, extremely high conversion, and it generates the testimonials everything else needs.

- **Walk into 20 coaching centres** in your own city. Ask for 10 minutes. Set it up for them on the spot. Realistic: 20 visits → 8 conversations → **4–6 users, and 2 of them will tell other teachers.**
- **Apartment society notice boards and WhatsApp groups** — most urban Indian societies have both, and most have resident tutors.
- **Stationery and photocopy shops near schools** — where teachers print receipts and question papers. A small printed card with a QR code costs ₹200 for 100.
- **Parent-teacher meeting days** at local schools — teachers congregate.

---

## Tier 6 — Quora, and Indian Q&A 🟡

Indian teachers and parents use Quora heavily, and Quora answers rank in Google.

Questions to find and answer properly (search these):
- *"How do I politely ask parents for pending tuition fees?"*
- *"How to manage tuition class records?"*
- *"Best app for tuition fee management in India?"*
- *"How much should I charge for home tuition?"* — adjacent, draws the right reader

One good answer per day, 20 minutes each. Realistic: **200–2,000 views each, permanent.**

## Channels to skip entirely

**Reddit, Product Hunt, Hacker News, X/Twitter, dev communities, LinkedIn-as-primary.** Your buyer is not there. Do Peerlist once for builder credibility (10 minutes) and spend every other hour on Tiers 1–5.

*(LinkedIn is worth one post a week only to reach school administrators and coaching-chain owners — a different, larger buyer worth testing later. Not a month-1 channel.)*
