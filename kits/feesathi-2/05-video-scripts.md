# FeeSathi — Video scripts + shot lists

**Record everything in Hinglish.** Your audience watches Hindi/Hinglish YouTube and Reels. An English-only video halves your reach with this buyer. Shoot vertical on your phone. Do not make it look like a startup ad — it should look like a teacher explaining something to another teacher.

---

## Video A — The hook (0:25, vertical)

**Use:** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook groups, WhatsApp forwards.
**Goal:** a teacher sees themselves and sends it to another teacher.

### Script

| Time | On screen | Voice (Hinglish) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Calendar, the 5th circled. | **"Paanch tareekh."** |
| 0:04–0:09 | You, to camera, in a real tuition room | **"Char parents ki fees pending hai. Aur aap message nahi bhej rahe."** |
| 0:09–0:13 | Close on your face | **"Kyunki awkward lagta hai. Padhana easy hai — paisa maangna sabse mushkil."** |
| 0:13–0:19 | Phone: the FeeSathi page, pending list visible. You tap a student. The ready message appears. | **"Isliye message pehle se ready rehta hai. Naam, amount, month — sab likha hua."** |
| 0:19–0:23 | You tap, it opens WhatsApp, you press send | **"Aap bas padh ke bhej do. Bas."** |
| 0:23–0:25 | `feesathi.tech` on screen | **"Free hai. feesathi.tech"** |

### Shot list
1. **Shoot in a real tuition space** — a room with a whiteboard, plastic chairs, a few notebooks. Authenticity is the entire asset here; a clean desk and a MacBook will lose this audience.
2. Phone in hand, filmed by someone else, natural light. No tripod shots of a laptop.
3. Captions burned in, in Hinglish (Roman script, not Devanagari — Roman reads faster for this audience on Reels).
4. No music, or a very simple bed.
5. **Make a second version where the circled date is the 10th and the number is six parents.** Variations stop the algorithm treating reposts as duplicates.

## Video B — The full demo (2:30, horizontal for YouTube, cut down for everything else)

**Use:** YouTube (where it will rank in search), landing page, the "I'll show you" moment in a WhatsApp conversation.
**Goal:** a teacher believes they can do this themselves in three minutes.

### Script

**0:00–0:20 — The problem, in their words**
> "Namaste. Main [naam]. Agar aap tuition padhate ho toh ye aapko pata hoga — register mein sab likha hai, lekin jab koi pooche 'July ki fees aayi thi kya', toh yaad nahi aata. Aur part payment ka toh bilkul hisaab nahi rehta."

*Shot: a real fee register, pages turning. Honestly show the mess. Do not mock it — your viewer owns one.*

**0:20–0:50 — Making the page (do it live, unedited)**
> "Main abhi aapke saamne naya page bana raha hoon. Dekhiye kitna time lagta hai."

*Shot: screen record the phone. Enter email, page created. **Show the real clock.** If it takes 40 seconds, show 40 seconds. Unedited setup is the most persuasive footage you can shoot.*

**0:50–1:20 — Adding students**
> "Ab student add karte hain. Naam, class, subject, monthly fees. Asha Sharma, Class 10, Maths, do hazaar."

*Shot: type it in for real. Add three students, not one — show it filling up.*

**1:20–1:50 — The pending view**
> "Ab dekhiye. Yahan dikh raha hai is mahine kitna due hai — do hazaar. Kaun paid hai, kaun pending. Agar kisi ne aadha diya hai toh wo bhi dikhega, aur purana balance bhi."

*Shot: the fee book view. Mark one paid. Mark one part-paid. Show the total change.*

**1:50–2:15 — The reminder (the money shot)**
> "Aur ye hai sabse kaam ki cheez. Fees due hai toh message pehle se taiyaar hai — 'Namaste, Asha ki is mahine ki fees do hazaar due hai, jab convenient ho bhej dijiyega, dhanyawaad.' Polite hai, pressure nahi hai. Aap padh lo, change karna ho toh kar lo, aur WhatsApp pe bhej do. Message hum nahi bhejte — aap bhejte ho."

*Shot: the message appearing, then opening in real WhatsApp, then sending to your own number. Show it arriving.*

**2:15–2:30 — The money promise + close**
> "Ek important baat: paisa hum handle nahi karte. Parents aapko hi direct dete hain, cash ya UPI. Hum sirf hisaab rakhte hain aur message banate hain. Bilkul free hai. feesathi.tech"

### Shot list
- Real register, real phone, real tuition room.
- Screen-record on the phone, not a desktop browser. Your viewer will do this on a phone.
- Unedited setup section with a visible timer.
- Your own voice, your own accent. Do not use TTS and do not read a script stiffly — talk.
- Export a **40-second cut** (problem → reminder → close) for Reels and WhatsApp.

## Video C — "Fees reminder kaise bhejein" (0:50, vertical)

**Use:** the shareable one. Teaches something useful; the product barely appears. This is the one that gets forwarded in teacher WhatsApp groups, which is your best channel.
**Goal:** be forwarded.

### Script

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | Text: *"Fees reminder bhejna hai. Kya likhein?"* | "Teachers ye sabse zyada poochte hain. Message kaise likhein ki rude na lage." |
| 0:06–0:16 | ❌ A bad message: *"Sir fees abhi tak nahi aayi hai, please jaldi bhejiye, mujhe bhi kharche hain."* | "Ye mat likhiye. Isme aapki majboori dikh rahi hai, aur parent ko guilt feel hota hai. Guilt se log reply nahi karte — avoid karte hain." |
| 0:16–0:34 | ✅ The good one, typed live: *"Namaste Sir/Ma'am, Asha ki October month ki fees ₹2,000 due hai. Jab convenient ho bhej dijiyega. Dhanyawaad 🙏"* | "Ye likhiye. Teen cheezein: bachche ka naam, kaunsa month, kitna amount. Aur 'jab convenient ho' — ye line sabse zaroori hai. Isse pressure nahi lagta, aur zyadातar log do din mein bhej dete hain." |
| 0:34–0:44 | Text: **"Har mahine same date pe."** | "Aur ek aur baat — har parent ko same date pe bhejiye. Jab sabko jaata hai toh kisi ko personal nahi lagta. Awkwardness isiliye hoti hai kyunki hum sirf tab bhejte hain jab bahut late ho jaaye." |
| 0:44–0:50 | Small end card | "Aise 8 messages maine free rakhe hain, link bio mein. — feesathi.tech" |

### Shot list
1. Type both messages live in WhatsApp. Live typing reads as real.
2. Big Roman-Hinglish captions.
3. Upload natively to each platform (don't share a YouTube link into Facebook — native video gets many times the reach).
4. Post this in every Facebook teacher group **with no link at all**. Let people ask in the comments.

## Video D — The 30-second receipt demo

**Use:** attach to every receipt-generator post. It converts better than a screenshot.

> *(0:00)* "Parent ne receipt maangi hai?"
> *(0:04)* "Ye page kholo. Free hai, signup nahi." *(show the page)*
> *(0:10)* "Naam. Amount. Month. Date."  *(type it)*
> *(0:20)* "Download." *(the PDF appears)*
> *(0:25)* "WhatsApp pe bhej do. Tees second." *(send it)*
> *(0:30)* "[receipt URL] — free."

Shoot once, use for the whole month.
