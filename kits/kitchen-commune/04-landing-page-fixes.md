# kitchen commune — Landing page fixes

Based on [kitchencommune.in](https://kitchencommune.in/), checked 29 October. Registration, live matching, video and payment weren't completed during research.

## 1. Test a real two-person session

Current headline: **“Don't just find a recipe. Find someone to cook it with.”** CTA: **“Match for tonight.”** Verify fresh email sign-in → profile/window → request → acceptance → private room → completion → report/block. If the path fails, stop promotion. A sample profile isn't proof an available person exists.

## 2. Set an honest availability expectation

Suggested hero support: **“Join a small beta dinner window. We'll show people who choose a compatible time; a request is not a guaranteed match.”** CTA: **“See the next cooking window.”** Show a real scheduled cohort only when confirmed. An empty state should say **“Nobody suitable is available in this window yet. Choose another time or invite a willing friend.”**

## 3. Separate beta-free from future pricing

Current plan text says free beta, later from **₹99/$3 per session**. Put **“Free beta today. Later paid session pricing will be shown before any purchase.”** next to the CTA. Don't let India/international payment-provider wording imply a current charge when no payment is needed.

## 4. Mark all sample people and menus

The landing page includes illustrative kitchen/match examples. Label **“Example profiles and menu, not live availability.”** Actual compatible users need their own availability and consent. Don't count samples as supply.

## 5. Keep participant controls visible

The site claims email verification, private rooms, report/block and no phone numbers shared. Show those controls before joining. Copy: **“You choose whether to accept. You can leave, report or block.”** Verify each claim; don't promise anonymity if profile or email information is actually visible.

## 6. Make pantry suggestions bounded

Use **“Suggested dishes—check ingredients, preparation and your own dietary restrictions before cooking.”** Don't imply a model's dietary check guarantees safety. Let users edit or reject a dish and flag a mismatch without forcing a session.

## 7. Test mobile kitchen use

At 390px, check sign-in email return, pantry entry, video permissions, recipe readability and rejoining after interruption. Preview: **“Kitchen Commune — cook online with someone free at the same time.”** No relationship or guaranteed-match claim; no private room link in the image.
