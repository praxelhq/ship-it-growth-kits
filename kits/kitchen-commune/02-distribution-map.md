# kitchen commune — Distribution map

**Recruit same-time cohorts, not scattered signups.** The first channel is an invitation to a specific dinner window with enough willing people to form pairs.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Twenty willing home cooks | Invite ten for the first 19:30–20:30 IST test window and ten for a second. Confirm each person's interest and time privately. | Up to 20 accounts; perhaps 4–8 attended pairs across the tests if availability/flow works. | #1 |
| Three existing alumni/home-cook groups | Admin-approved invitation for a stated window, not a generic “join our community.” | 3 × 30 readers × 10–25% registration = 9–23 accounts; only available people counted as cohort supply. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | Two consenting kitchens, real match/menu/room process on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 2–72 accounts. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | A truthful cohort/matching lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 1–18 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited campus/home-cooking club | Existing organizer, voluntary cohort, no attendee-list export or recording. | 8–16 willing participants if accepted; zero otherwise. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional builder-feedback listing once a real attended pair is demonstrable; check current slot/rules. | 0–20 accounts, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No paid chefs, ingredient gifts, newsletter sponsorships or unverified cooking-group names. Ask permission before a club newsletter mention and forecast zero until accepted. Keep these Days 10–13 online-cooking invitations distinct from local café/meetup products: no organizer collision and no implied physical venue.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 distinct relevant visits, but matching also needs time-window density. The base may yield dozens of accounts. Do not open ten new time zones before one cohort can reliably form a pair. Record no-show and comfort feedback, not just profile totals.
