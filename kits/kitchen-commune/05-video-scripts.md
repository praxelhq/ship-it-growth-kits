# kitchen commune — Video scripts

Film your own kitchens with willing adults. No stranger recording, private room token or personal dietary profile visible. Use existing ingredients.

## A. Real pair demo — 60 seconds

0–2s, two kitchen screens: **“A match only matters if both cooks show up.”**

2–15s, show matching windows and a consensual request/acceptance. “We both chose this dinner time.”

15–28s, enter a few owned pantry items. “These are suggestions from what we have, not a rule about what we must eat.”

28–45s, real private-room arrival and simple cooking. “Two kitchens, one call.” Label time compression and hide tokens.

45–55s, both people choose to end. “Leave whenever you want; controls stay available.”

55–60s: URL and **Free beta · no guaranteed match**.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “The recipe wasn't the lonely part.”

2–10s: two consenting participants in their own kitchens, actual call. “Some company while dinner happens.”

10–15s: URL and next real cohort window only if confirmed.

## C. Pantry hook — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “We don't have the same fridge.”

2–12s: two small pantry lists and an actual suggestion. “Pick something you can both make and actually want to eat.”

12–20s: edit/reject a dish. “Check your own ingredients and restrictions.” No guarantee from the model.

## D. Consent/control clip — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “An invitation isn't an obligation.”

2–12s: show actual accept/decline, leave and report/block controls. “A good small community makes these easy to find.”

12–20s: plain product URL. If a control doesn't work, fix it instead of recording a pretend interface.
