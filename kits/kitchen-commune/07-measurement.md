# kitchen commune — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real accounts once. Report **mutually accepted sessions**, **two-person attended cook sessions** and **repeat pairs** beside it. A profile, request or generated recipe isn't a completed social experience.

No private baseline records were accessed. Record actual starting values.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Verified accounts / last 7 days | Authentication records, no examples/tests |
| Willing people by actual dinner window | Private availability records, not public contact lists |
| Requests, accepts, both-person joins and no-shows | Aggregate session status |
| Voluntary repeat sessions and reports | Aggregate records, no conversation recording |
| Traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics / [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Course metric/rank/time | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/kitchen-commune) |

Check existing Clarity before adding scripts. Exclude private rooms, pantry/diet details, messages and identifiers from replay. Events: `account_verified`, `window_set`, `request_sent`, `request_accepted`, `both_joined`, `session_completed`, `repeat_cook`. If completion is self-reported, label that rather than implying you observed a meal.

| Checkpoint | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Can the first ten people form and attend willing pairs? | Fix timing, acceptance or room flow before broad traffic. |
| Day 14 | Does a second cohort work without your matchmaking intervention? | If not, narrow the window and explain availability. |
| Day 21 | Do pairs/people choose a second cook? | Ask about comfort and usefulness, not just recipe quality. |
| Day 30 | Accounts against 1,000; attendance/repeats beside it | State the target gap and a realistic next cohort. |

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 unique qualified visits, but scattered registrations won't solve availability. Measure available people per slot. The initial scheduled tests may produce dozens, not thousands.

Beta is currently free; planned ₹99/$3 session prices are not current revenue. Don't count sample profiles, one person refreshing a room or unanswered invitations as customers. Course 24-hour readings overlap and are not cumulative participants. A successful month is a small matching system that people voluntarily use again.
