# dumdum — Distribution map

This is a Windows desktop companion, not another Mac productivity-tool launch. The desktop cluster's Mac channels are inapplicable. Keep organizers distinct from KnockKnock/SnipText/Swish. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week; a Windows-specific demo should use a real willing group, not a presumed campus-wide list.

## Ranked routes

**1. Twenty qualified warm Windows invitations.** Five each on Days 5,12,19,26. Confirm 64-bit Windows 10/11 and willingness to download roughly 300 MB before offering 03 #1. Use a normal publisher-verifiable release and a synthetic note, no security-warning bypass. One follow-up maximum. A refusal to install is useful research, not a reason to ask repeatedly.

**2. Six owned short demonstrations.** Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Use the mouth-menu, pet reactions and a real reminder, each tied to a practical task. Post manually to your own Instagram/LinkedIn or existing personal account; account analytics are unknown, so no audience-size claim. [LinkedIn's policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) prohibits unauthorized automation and inauthentic behavior. Don't bulk-message other creators' followers.

**3. One conditional 12-person Windows desk session.** Ask a known organizer on Day 8 with 03 #4. The Day 15 demo uses the maker's machine and fictional files; participants install only after clear compatibility/release information. Twelve is a proposed cap, not secured attendance. If no approval, conduct individual tests and forecast zero event contribution.

**4. Peerlist.** https://peerlist.io/launchpad is checked, not a reserved slot. Day 18 inspect eligibility/current timing/existing listing; Day 23 submit only a working downloadable product with honest platform, account and price terms. Never ask for votes.

**5. Product Hunt.** https://www.producthunt.com/launch documents maker-led launch preparation. Day 25 inspect existing product listing, then use an appropriate new launch or substantive iteration only. A landing page with an unresolved installer isn't ready. No vote requests, paid hunter or invented launch traffic.

**6. One Windows-focused creator introduction.** Day 22 ask a creator you actually know or have been introduced to, with 03 #7. Offer a verifiable review build and a truthful disclosure; no promised endorsement, payment budget or guessed email. Zero forecast until they accept and publish.

**7. AlternativeTo optional listing.** https://alternativeto.net/faq/ requires verified email and factual app details. Free review can be indefinite. Paid priority is outside budget; base month-one visits are zero until accepted. Don't make a desktop-pet claim into a generic converter listing if its primary audience is companionship.

## Feasible arithmetic

Planning assumptions only: 20 qualified invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12 visitors; × 25–50% successful first launch/account = 2–6 people. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000 impressions; × 1% visits = 6–30 visitors; × 10–25% install/account completion = about 1–8. Accepted 12-person session × 60–80% attendance = 7–10 attendees; × 25–50% later successful installation = about 2–5. Remove overlap.

Base: roughly 3–14 first launches/accounts without the session, 5–19 with it before deduplication. None of the three unaccepted external routes is credited in that base. Download size, trust friction and Windows eligibility may reduce results further. This is nowhere near a promised 1,000; retain the target and report the actual shortfall. A broader approved Windows distribution partnership is an experiment after install/reminder reliability, not an imaginary lever in this month's numbers.
