Your first user is a Windows student who wants a small desktop companion and a note/reminder one click away. Start with the creature's personality and a useful reminder, not a sprawling document-conversion/AI/system-monitor bundle. The fun gets attention; a reminder the user actually receives earns a return visit.

Actual H1: **“A small creature that lives on your desktop.”** The page's **Download for Windows** links to `#download`; **Buy Pro** links to `#buy`. ## The promise

> Give a small creature a corner of your Windows desktop. Keep a note, set a reminder and get back to your day.

Bio: **A desktop pet with something useful in its mouth-menu.**

Windows 10/11 **64-bit** only; the installer is approximately **300 MB** because LibreOffice is bundled. Mac is planned, not available. Do not send download invitations to Mac classmates or advertise a lightweight five-megabyte pet.

Free is **$0 forever with a free account**: four color personas, notes/to-dos, Windows reminders, optional startup, file-copy feed/pat and reactions. Calendar sync sends reminder events to Google Calendar or Outlook; it does **not** read existing calendar contents. The optional connection must stay optional.

Pro's actual price is **“Soon · one-time”**, not a numeric offer. The page describes offline LibreOffice document conversions, PDF utilities, read-only System Vitals and AI Mode behind Pro locks. Don't invent a launch price, imply Pro is already purchasable or extend offline conversion to synced notes and AI chats. AI processing/storage needs its own explanation.

## Why this instead of another app?

A paper list or existing Windows note/reminder workflow costs ₹0 in extra specialist software. A separate pet and list may already satisfy someone. dumdum's useful difference is playful immediate access to one simple job, not proof that it outperforms every reminder product. A cute animation isn't proof of reliable notifications.

The file trick takes a **copy**, keeps the original untouched and returns a separately named Desktop copy, per the page. Demonstrate with a synthetic file and verify a name collision; never feed a student's private documents just to make a video. Conversion fidelity is format-specific and untested here.

## The honest weakness and target

A large unfamiliar download and mandatory free account are bigger asks than clicking a web toy. The current SmartScreen advice tells users to bypass a warning; replace that with transparent publisher/version/signing and safe support guidance before expanding reach. Don't assert the binary is unsigned merely because a generic FAQ mentions warnings.

Keep the **1,000 B2C install/account target**, reporting successful first launches and verified accounts separately, deduplicated where legitimately possible. Activation is a first saved note plus a reminder received or a voluntary next-day return; download clicks and website pet interactions aren't installs. Delay paid growth until the numeric price, release delivery and entitlement behavior are real.
