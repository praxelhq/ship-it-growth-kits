# Shipit Studio — Positioning

Live: https://quad.shipitstudio.in/ · Other / B2C · [Course profile](https://shipped.money/p/shipit-studio)

**The actual product is Quad, currently open only to Masters' Union students. Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, retained as genuine eligible student accounts, with confirmed workspace access and one useful real class/group/assignment action beside it. The eligible cohort size is unknown. You cannot substantiate the target by advertising to people the form excludes.

Browser inspected 29 October 2026: H1 **“Quad”**; support **“One calm place for classes, group work and assignments. No more twenty WhatsApp groups.”** Signup asks name, **Masters' Union email**, password and **“Create my account”**. The notice says confirming the college email places the student in their college workspace. No form was submitted, confirmation email tested or private workspace read. Public evidence does not prove messaging, assignments, notifications or permissions work end-to-end. No price was visible.

First user: an eligible adult MU student already in one real class project group, who loses an agreed assignment detail across multiple chats. Test one willing existing group, not a campus-wide migration. This is a workspace adoption job, not a website-building or public launch product merely because the roster calls it Shipit Studio.

Pitch: “Keep one class group's agreed work in its college workspace.” Bio: “Quad: one calmer place for the class project.” These claims need a real verified workspace action before promotion. Choose the actual shipped action, not an invented calendar, LMS integration or automatic WhatsApp import.

The real alternative is that group's existing chat and documents. [Notion Free is $0](https://www.notion.com/pricing) for personal projects, but shared-workspace limits and permissions differ; do not promise a free institutional plan. Quad wins only if classmates can find the right agreed item with less confusion and appropriate access. No public price means no permanent-free claim.

The weakness is an account without a cooperating group, followed by access/identity trust. Establish current eligibility, email confirmation, group permissions and the supported first task. If the verified eligible audience is below 1,000, show that ceiling and gap explicitly. Opening other institutions is a future product decision, not a feature to advertise as live.
