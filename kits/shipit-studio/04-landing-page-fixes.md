1. **Move eligibility beside the headline.** Replacement support **“A class-project workspace for Masters' Union students. Confirm your college email before entering.”** CTA **“Create my MU student account”**. Keep the restriction visible before a visitor invests effort; do not imply any college email works.
2. **Show one real first task before signup.** Current page supplies an illustration, not a usable outcome. Publish a clearly labelled synthetic workspace example and explanation of the actual implemented action. Suggested scope line **“See the supported class-project workflow before joining.”** Do not invent task boards, LMS integration, message imports or reminders. Test the real first useful action first.
3. **Explain confirmation and recovery.** Current “Three fields and you are in” sits beside an email-confirmation condition. Replacement **“Create your account, confirm your college email, then enter the workspace available to your account.”** Provide accurate resend/expired-link and wrong-domain guidance after testing. Do not claim delivery speed or eligibility auto-detection without evidence.
4. **Prove the access boundary.** Before real class data, test two authorized accounts and workspace separation. Written guidance **“Share only material this group's members are permitted to see.”** State actual operator access, storage, deletion and member controls. A college domain alone is not proof every student should see every group.
5. **State access and price honestly.** No public price was observed. Show actual free/trial/paid terms before registration if applicable; do not invent a campus licence or a free-forever promise. Any official affiliation claim must be approved and factual.
6. **Preview and phone.** Title **“Quad — MU class-project workspace”**; description **“Currently for Masters' Union students. See the supported workflow and confirm your college email before joining.”** Test keyboard, password visibility, disabled-button explanation and errors on mobile. Use synthetic messages and names in previews, never real student assignments or contact details.
