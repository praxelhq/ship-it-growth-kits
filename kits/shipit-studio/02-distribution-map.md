# Shipit Studio — Distribution map

**Eligible existing groups first; no broad consumer launch.** The current form excludes non-MU students. All reach estimates are unmeasured assumptions for ten group-organizer invitations, two approved campus bulletins and three owned explanations.

| Route | Permission and purpose | Assumed result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ten known eligible adult project-group organizers | Five personal notes Days 6/10. Ask about one existing task; organizers invite only willing eligible peers. No bulk email or surprise group migration. | 2–4 accepted groups; at 3–5 willing eligible people each, 6–20 account attempts; #1. |
| Two authorized existing MU class/project bulletins | Obtain actual organizer permission, different groups/weeks; no implied institution-wide approval. No copying roster/contact data. | Combined 30–100 eligible views, 2–8 attempts; #2. |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) profile | Three factual builder updates, primarily feedback not general signup recruitment. [Basic is free](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545596). | 60–240 impressions, 0–2 eligible attempts; #3. |
| One legitimate course/workspace coordinator | Ask about eligibility and a permitted pilot, not administrator access or student lists. | One process conversation if accepted, zero forecast accounts; #4. |

Base: 8–30 account attempts before overlap, perhaps 4–20 confirmed eligible accounts if the gate works. This does not substantiate 1,000. Verify the eligible denominator privately before scaling; do not publish student names or course contacts. A three-person group is a planning assumption, not actual adoption.

No public directory/newsletter placement is assigned: ineligible visitors cannot become current users. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) may later support a truthful maker update under its personal-account/no-upvote-request rules, but supplies zero forecast eligible accounts and is not scheduled as a launch. Coordinate campus pilots with other cohort products; a class circle gets one optional pitch that week.
