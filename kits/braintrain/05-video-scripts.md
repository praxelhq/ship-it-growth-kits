# BrainTrain — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 45 seconds · homepage and Peerlist

**First two seconds:** “Remember these tiles.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–10s: actual grid, pattern visible. | Watch the pattern first. |
| Middle | 10–30s: tap remembered tiles and show changed difficulty. | Two clean rounds add a tile; one miss takes one away. |
| Payoff | 30–40s: sample completion and next choice. | That is one game session, not a measure of intelligence. |
| Close | Hold `https://www.braintrain101.in/` legibly. | Try the no-account sample. |

Keep timing honest and the pattern readable; do not fabricate a perfect score.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture a pattern, one success, one miss, the difficulty adjustment, sample completion and free/annual-plan distinction. Use your own game footage.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
