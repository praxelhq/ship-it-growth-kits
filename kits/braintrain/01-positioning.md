# BrainTrain — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.braintrain101.in/ · **Category:** Games · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/braintrain

## The thing to face first

A better game score is not proof of better memory in daily life. Your page says “built on real cognitive science”; your month-one proof should be whether people understand and return to these particular games, not a borrowed claim about intelligence or a study of another product.

## What is actually on your page

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Your daily workout, for your brain.”**. The next explanation is short adaptive games for memory, focus, speed and problem-solving, with a visible Flash Grid trial. The action is **“Start training free”**. The pricing page lists Free at $0 with eight core games and unlimited plays; Premium is $18/year for more games. Taxes may be added at checkout. These are observations from [your public product page](https://www.braintrain101.in/), not a completed purchase or a promise that every authenticated flow works.

The homepage has a no-signup five-round Flash Grid sample and a /play/flash-grid route for a longer session. It describes adaptation: two clean rounds add a tile and one miss removes one. Paid checkout, stored progress and a completed session were not exercised here; the sample streak and workout on the landing page are not user evidence.

## Your first person

A student who enjoys a one-minute memory puzzle between study blocks and wants a private score to revisit. This is a recreational game habit, not a treatment for attention difficulties or a substitute for studying.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Try a one-minute memory grid that adjusts when you get it right or miss.

For a bio or a short reply:

> Adaptive mini-games, private progress, a free Flash Grid sample.

## Your opening use case

Lead with Flash Grid only. Show five lit squares, hide them and let the player tap their memory. Let them experience the changed difficulty before asking them to inspect eight other games. The first repeat should happen because the game is enjoyable.

## The real alternative

[Human Benchmark](https://humanbenchmark.com/) has a real visual-memory and sequence-test interface; no current paid plan was verified, so do not invent one. [Lumosity](https://www.lumosity.com/en/) offers a free training entry and publishes its own study and limitations. Those findings are about Lumosity, not evidence that BrainTrain improves everyday cognition. Your distinction is this adaptive interaction and private progress, not scientific superiority.

## What you need to prove

Capture an actual round adapting after two successes and a miss. Ask five players to explain why the grid changed. Keep the first-session and later-session scores comparable by recording game/difficulty, and do not turn a small practice effect into a health claim.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
