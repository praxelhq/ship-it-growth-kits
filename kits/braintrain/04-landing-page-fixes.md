# BrainTrain — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://www.braintrain101.in/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Your daily workout, for your brain.”**; the action is **“Start training free”**. The pricing page lists Free at $0 with eight core games and unlimited plays; Premium is $18/year for more games. Taxes may be added at checkout. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a casual puzzle player wondering what to tap first. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Show the one-minute game first

**Current:** The hero says “Your daily workout, for your brain.”

**Write this instead:**

> Try five rounds of Flash Grid. Remember the tiles, then tap them back.

**Verify:** Keep the playable sample visible before a broad feature or science pitch.

## Fix 2 — Define the score honestly

**Current:** The page uses cognitive-training language and sample progress.

**Write this instead:**

> This score describes your play in this game. Compare like-for-like sessions; it is not an intelligence or health assessment.

**Verify:** Do not imply a sample workout/streak is a real user outcome.

## Fix 3 — Explain adaptation at the miss

**Current:** The sample changes tile count after successes or a miss.

**Write this instead:**

> Two clean rounds add a tile. One miss removes one. Keep playing at a level you can follow.

**Verify:** Watch five players explain the change without coaching.

## Fix 4 — Separate free sample and free account

**Current:** There is a no-signup sample and a full-session route.

**Write this instead:**

> Try the short sample without an account. Sign in only when you want the account features shown next.

**Verify:** Test whether the start action matches the promise in a clean browser.

## Fix 5 — Keep annual payment visible

**Current:** Premium is $18/year, with a $1.50 monthly equivalent.

**Write this instead:**

> Premium is charged $18 for a year; applicable taxes appear at checkout. The free core games stay available.

**Verify:** Do not present the monthly equivalent as a monthly subscription option.

## Fix 6 — Make privacy visible near progress

**Current:** The pricing page says private progress, visible only to you.

**Write this instead:**

> Your progress is private. Share a score only if you choose.

**Verify:** Verify account isolation and avoid a public leaderboard unless the product actually offers one with consent.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
