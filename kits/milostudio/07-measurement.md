# Milostudio — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers.** Activation beside that number: **businesses using their own brand kit to produce and review a useful real announcement**. The fictional café demo, personal experiments and an agency making three kits are not three customers.

## Day 0 record

| Metric | Source | Baseline treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business accounts and real kits | Your product records | Query the actual value; it was not visible publicly. Exclude demo kits. |
| Independent reviewed outputs | Product events and owner interview | Distinct businesses, not all regenerate clicks. |
| Returning businesses | Successful activity on a later day | Separate founder-assisted output. |
| Sessions and sources | Existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) project, or configure one if absent | Check collection before assuming it works. Mask private kit text. |
| Course rank / saved reading | [Project profile](https://shipped.money/p/milostudio) | Record metric and timestamp at start; unknown here is not zero. |
| Paid plans / refunds | Billing records | Real transactions only, actual reporting period. |

Don't publicly store business contact details or client mood boards. A public source statement is not a substitute for permission to upload private material.

## What qualifies as a customer

Apply all four [policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): real trading business; its actual brand information onboarded; independent use in the last seven days without you asking; responsible person's name and callable number held privately. Payment is optional. An owner praising a sample is a conversation, not a customer.

Instrument `brand_kit_saved`, `draft_generated`, `draft_reviewed`, `copy_or_export_succeeded` and `returning_business_active` without capturing private content. A copy action shows intent, not proof an Instagram post went live; ask separately and don't claim an integration you have not verified.

## A forecast the calendar can support

40 individual approaches × assumed 25% reply × 50% trial × 50% independent active use = **2–3 businesses**. One accepted organizer session might add 0–2. Those are planning assumptions, not measured café conversion rates. The base range of 2–5 is below ten. Close the gap through better observed fit and requested introductions, not by counting meme-wall visitors.

After the first 20 approaches, recompute using actual counts. If only one replies, stop adding the same segment. If ten create kits and none returns, fix the recurring content job before asking for forty more trials.

## Weekly decisions

| Review | Working milestone | If behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Three real kits, one independently useful draft | Observe the first job; narrow away from brands that don't currently post. |
| Day 14 | Three independent businesses, one repeat use | Replace low-yield public-posting time with warm owner introductions; correct tone/accuracy friction. |
| Day 21 | Five qualifying businesses | Determine whether no reply, bad setup or no second content need is the actual problem. |
| Day 28 | Eight qualifying businesses | Audit the four tests; don't send artificial “please log in” reminders to inflate activity. |
| Day 30 | Ten or the actual shortfall | Report active companies beside drafts, registrations, paid plans and hours. |

Watch five sessions that save a kit but never use a draft. Count the factual corrections needed on each of the first ten real announcements. A lower correction burden plus independent return use is stronger proof than a funny demo or a large regeneration count.
