# Milostudio — Distribution map

**Café-owner conversations first.** Keep the agency tier out of your first pitch; a large agency will need approval and client-data features this research has not verified. No paid channels.

All outcomes below are assumptions for planning, not observed benchmarks or platform-approved sending quotas. The 30-day calendar allocates **40 individually researched first approaches** across ten outreach days. If a café declines, stop. One follow-up seven days later is enough.

| Priority / exact route | How you use it, boundaries and expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Cafés you can visit in your own neighbourhood | Walk a compact route outside the breakfast/lunch rush; ask the owner when two minutes is convenient. Show their public menu post, never an invented private problem. Ten visits might produce 2–4 conversations and 0–2 trials. This is a guess; no permission to film or post is assumed. | #1 |
| 2 · Public café websites and their linked Instagram accounts | Manually qualify current posting activity and owner access. Four personal first messages on each of ten scheduled days; no mass DMs, purchased list or repeated link dump. At 40 approaches × assumed 25% reply × 50% trial × 50% independent use, plan about2–3 customers. | #2 |
| 3 · [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) organizer route | For a founder-owned café or small local-brand session, ask the current chapter organizer privately. The official site confirms the network, not a current free event or group permission. Two organizer requests, 0–1 accepted sessions,0–2 additional customers as an uncertain scenario. Never count an unconfirmed session. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn feed | Tuesday09:00 IST, one honest caption comparison; use your own sample or written permission. Model100–500 impressions and0–5 visits per post from a small account; zero is possible. Ask for feedback, not engagement swaps. | #4 |
| 5 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Its public site advertises Monday Launchpad. Check current eligibility and offer a usable product, not a fictional meme gallery alone. Optional listing after two real independent workflows. Model0–100 visits,0–2 trials; most visitors will be builders rather than café owners. | #5 |

Specific relevant account/source: [Gamma's official site](https://gamma.app/) links its social accounts; observe questions about keeping brand outputs consistent, and answer only where you have a useful specific contribution. Do not advertise under unrelated posts. For your actual café buyer, their own latest menu post is the more useful place to understand tone; don't publicly critique it without permission.

No verified café Facebook-group admission or newsletter placement is included. If an organizer offers a free opt-in newsletter slot, use #3 shortened to an invitation; expected contribution stays zero until accepted. You do not need a Reddit launch to find ten local businesses.

**Reality check:** the base scenario is2–5 qualifying businesses, below ten. At day14, replace low-yield posting time with warm introductions from owners who actually used their kit. Do not make up viral reach to close the gap. Coordinate with the cohort's creator-tools writers before approaching the same agency or founder group; your first segment is café owners, not generic LinkedIn writers.
