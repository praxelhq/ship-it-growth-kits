# Milostudio — 30-day calendar

Start after a fresh account can create a kit and usable draft. Reply to existing conversations and log outcomes for 20 minutes daily. Four new first approaches on each of days4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 make **40 total**. Follow platform limits, stop after refusal, and send only one follow-up after seven days. Owner demos replace new outreach if service is busy.
