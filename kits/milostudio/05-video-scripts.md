# Milostudio — Video scripts

Use your own café example or a business's written permission. Screen recording plus phone voice is enough. No stock customer quotes, fake reach claims or invented publishing integrations.

## A · Brand rule to useful caption ·60seconds · LinkedIn / owner demo

**0–2s hook:** “This café would never say ‘hustle’.” Show the brand's “never says” rule, not a logo intro.

**2–15s:** Open the sample kit, text “Fictional café.” Say: “The tone is warm and dry-witty. These are the words it avoids. I'm using an example, not a customer.”

**15–32s:** Enter a concrete announcement in the real app. Say: “Now I give it one real kind of task: a new breakfast item. I still supply the correct price and availability.” Show generation; label any shortened wait.

**32–48s:** Compare first draft and your edit. Say: “This line is generic, so I change it. Keeping the owner in control is the point.” Text: “Draft → reviewed.”

**48–60s:** Show the free-plan limits and URL. Say: “One brand kit and20 generations a month are free. Test your next post before paying for more. MiloStudio dot co dot in.” Do not promise automatic publishing.

## B · The wrong café voice · 20seconds · Reel / X

Hook0–2s: “Same product. Wrong voice.” Show two clearly labelled sample captions.2–8s: highlight a corporate phrase: “This is grammatical, but this café wouldn't say it.”8–15s: show the tone rule and corrected caption: “Save the rule, then judge the next draft.”15–20s: hold URL: “Try one brand kit free. You make the final call.”

Shot list: a readable caption, one highlighted phrase, the matching kit rule, the revised output. Don't expose a real business's rejected draft.

## C · A meme is not a business result ·25seconds · LinkedIn

Hook0–2s: “The meme made you laugh. Did you use the post?”2–10s: show the homepage sample with “Demo” label: “A funny sample proves very little about next week's work.”10–20s: show one announcement moving through kit→draft→manual approval: “Test it on something you actually need to say, and count the corrections.”20–25s: “That's the feedback I want. Not a polite like.”

No exaggerated before/after time saving. If you measured preparation time with a real owner, display the sample size and timing method; otherwise leave the clock out.
