# Milostudio — Landing-page fixes

Public page and login destination checked 29 October 2026. Login rendered an email/password form with an account-creation route; an authenticated generation or checkout was not performed. The site is not classified as broken.

## 1. Replace the clever action with a clear first job

**Current:** “your brand, but make it post.” and “feed milo your vibe.”

**Write:** **“Make your next café post sound like your café.”** Button: **“Create my first brand kit free.”** Supporting text: **“One brand kit. 20 generations/month. You review every word.”** Keep the existing account route. In a new browser, test account creation→kit→caption before outreach. If that path fails, repair it before sending people; a working login form alone is not enough.

## 2. Turn the fictional café into a transparent workflow

**Current:** “pick a brand. watch milo become them.” introduces selectable examples, including Oat & About and its tone rules.

**Write:** **“Sample café, sample announcement. See the brand rules, first draft and owner's edit.”** Keep sample labels attached to the demo. Show a “never says” rule preventing a specific unwanted phrase; a generic regenerate button does not prove that the rules survive revisions.

## 3. Put factual checks beside approval

**Current:** “content drops” promises posts, captions, memes and reel scripts in a weekly calendar.

**Write:** **“Drafts for your calendar. Check prices, opening hours and event details before publishing.”** Don't imply verified scheduling or automatic publishing unless a real user can do it today. The calendar should lead to a clear review state, not make an AI draft look approved by the business.

## 4. Make the plan decision about workload

**Current:** “side-hustle era,” “main character era” and “agency era” obscure what changes between tiers.

**Write:** **“Free: 1 brand kit · 20 generations · 5 AI images/month.”** **“₹299/month: 3 brand kits · 300 generations · 100 AI images/month.”** **“₹999/month: 10 brand kits · 1,500 generations · 500 AI images/month.”** Keep playful labels secondary. Verify renewal, taxes and cancellation in checkout before adding assurances not on the page.

## 5. Let the owner see a readable caption on a phone

**Current:** the meme wall and multiple output types compete for attention.

**Write above the example:** **“Start with one announcement. Read the caption, then choose a reel idea if you need one.”** Preserve the current character of the brand; give the caption and approval the visual focus. Test 360 px width and enlarged text. No mobile clipping or measured speed failure was established by this research.

## 6. Make shared previews match the business offer

**Current:** the page introduces a content team and burnout, then explains brand-conditioned output.

**Suggested preview title:** **“MiloStudio — your brand voice, ready for the next post.”** Description: **“Save tone rules, draft captions and reel ideas, and review them yourself. One brand kit free.”** Test the actual deployed preview and image load on mobile data; don't claim an unmeasured conversion uplift.
