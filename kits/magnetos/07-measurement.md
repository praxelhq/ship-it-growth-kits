# Magnetos — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed personal accounts once. Report **first real destination goals created and reopened** beside it. Multiple magnets, sample balances and progress updates do not create new people.

Private baseline values weren't accessed. Record actual starting totals and the actual offer before promotion.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Personal accounts / last 7 days | Authentication records, no tests/merchant accounts |
| First destination goals and reopened goals | Product status events |
| Progress-input/save failures | Error status, no amounts or personal financial details |
| Voluntary weekly return | Aggregate activity |
| Traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics / [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Course metric/rank/time | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/magnetos) |

Check existing Clarity before adding scripts. Mask recorded amounts, destinations, identities, credentials and private notes. Events: `personal_account_confirmed`, `destination_added`, `goal_saved`, `progress_updated`, `goal_reopened`, using status only. No bank records or goal amount is needed to measure the funnel.

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Five willing testers understand the number and save a goal | Fix scope/offer confusion before broader traffic. |
| Day 14 | People can reopen the goal and explain its progress source | Repair storage or labels; don't imply money custody. |
| Day 21 | Voluntary return/update without your reminder | Ask whether the visual actually supports their chosen goal. |
| Day 30 | Accounts against 1,000; useful reopened goals beside it | Report actual gap and the repeatable route. |

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 qualified unique visits. The small scheduled tests may yield dozens or low hundreds. Replace rates after three clips; don't count each magnet as a signup.

No public consumer price was visible; revenue needs a real verified offer, not sample rupee values. A “Funded” illustration isn't verified savings or a booked trip. Course 24-hour readings overlap and aren't cumulative users. The product signal is a person choosing to revisit their own goal.
