# Magnetos — Landing page fixes

Based on public homepage and actual `/signup` browser view checked 29 October. No account, goal or transaction was submitted.

## 1. Explain what the savings number means

The sample fridge shows saved/funded rupee values. Before signup, state the actual scope: user-entered goal tracking, connected records or another verified source. Do not let a visual balance imply a bank account, money custody, investment return or automatic saving without a real supported service.

Suggested sample caption: **“Illustrative destination goals. These are not real customer balances or money held by Magnetos.”**

## 2. Connect the headline to the first task

Current headline: **“Your travel dreams, one magnet at a time.”** CTA: **“Continue with Google”**, with email and **“Create My Travel Fridge.”**

Suggested support: **“Choose a destination, set your own goal and understand how its progress is recorded.”** Add a readable fictional preview before login. Don't force a personal account merely to understand the product.

## 3. Publish the actual price/free allowance

No consumer price was visible at public entry/signup. If free, say what is free and any limits; if paid, disclose the offer before registration. Do not invent “free forever” to improve conversion. Keep Merchant/Advertiser as a separate route so a personal user doesn't accidentally enter a business offer.

## 4. Test the real personal goal path

Fresh account → destination → self-chosen goal → progress update → reopen → edit/delete. Test Google/email recovery and which data syncs. If a state is only decorative or sample, label it; don't present it as stored user progress.

## 5. Keep the goal private by default

Explain actual visibility, sharing and deletion. A person may not want their destination or recorded progress public. Don't put account identifiers or amounts in marketing/replay. Any share card needs a deliberate review of its contents.

## 6. Check phone fridge navigation

At 390px, test opening/closing the door, tapping magnets, long destination names, forms and keyboard overlap. Provide readable text alongside visual states. Preview: **“Magnetos — a visual place for your travel goals.”** No sample balance presented as customer traction.
