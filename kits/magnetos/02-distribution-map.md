# Magnetos — Distribution map

**Lead with one destination goal, not a promise to fund someone's holiday.** Use people who already want a visual reminder and can judge whether they return.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing goal-setters | Ask about one destination and the actual goal/progress flow; no bank balances or financial documents collected. | 10 trials, 5–10 destination goals if useful. | #1 |
| Three existing friend/alumni travel groups | Admin-approved invitation, no savings/booking guarantee. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% visits × 50% signup = 9–18 accounts. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | Fridge metaphor, one fictional goal, progress/return and data explanation on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 2–72 accounts. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Visual goal/product-trust lessons on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 1–18 accounts. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback after account/goal/return tests, current rules/slot checked. No votes requested. | 0–30 accounts, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No paid travel page, giveaway, financial incentive, advertiser deal or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. A free alumni newsletter mention is optional after permission, zero until accepted. Don't pitch under unrelated money-advice posts or collect someone's financial circumstances to target them.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs around 3,334 distinct relevant visits. The base may yield dozens or low hundreds. Replace assumptions after three clips, and don't claim the channel plan already supports a thousand.

Keep contacts separate from Roamio's practical trip folders and NxtMood's sample experience prototype. Magnetos' first test is the visual goal and voluntary return, not organizing an outing or booking a destination.
