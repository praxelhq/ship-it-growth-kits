# Magnetos — Positioning

**Live:** https://magnetos.in/ · **Category:** Travel goals / visual organization · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/magnetos

The actual browser headline checked 29 October is **“Your travel dreams, one magnet at a time.”** The main action is **“Continue with Google”**, with email entry and `/signup`. Signup shows a Personal Account option and **“Create My Travel Fridge.”** No account was created and no price/free allowance was visible in the inspected screens.

Your first user already has one destination in mind and wants a visual reminder of a goal they set themselves. They need a small personal collection, not a bank account, investment recommendation or travel booking promise.

**Pitch:** Keep a destination you care about on a virtual fridge and see the goal progress you choose to record.

**Short version:** A magnet for the trip you want to work toward.

## The wedge

One destination, one self-chosen budget, one progress update and a return next week. The public page's sample magnets show rupee amounts and “Funded” states; those are not evidence of real user savings or money held by Magnetos. Explain actual tracker/payment behaviour before asking people to trust the numbers.

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/about/) offers saved-place lists for public consumer use without a browsing fee. An existing notes/spreadsheet file can track a travel goal without an extra purchase. Magnetos earns interest through its fridge/magnet metaphor and a motivating return, not by claiming other lists can't remember a destination.

## The uncomfortable bit

The same entry offers Merchant/Advertiser accounts, but the consumer pilot does not need advertising sales. Don't use sample funded balances or an advertiser button as proof of an operating marketplace.

The public page and signup destination were inspected. Destination creation, progress storage, money movement, account recovery and deletion weren't tested. Verify the actual scope before marketing a financial or booking capability.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 confirmed personal accounts, with a real destination/goal recorded and reopened beside it. A rupee figure on a demonstration fridge is not revenue, customer savings or proof a trip happened.
