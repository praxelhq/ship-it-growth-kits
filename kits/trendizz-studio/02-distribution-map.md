# Trendizz Studio — Distribution map

**Show how you handle an unknown, then invite a real read.** Don't manufacture breaking-news authority to acquire readers.

| Route | Calendar quantity and permission | Assumptions | Copy |

Approximately 28–213 gross first sessions before overlap; not a promise of 1,000. At 60% visit→meaningful read, the target needs roughly 1,667 distinct relevant visitors. At 1% clip-view→visit, that's about 166,700 views before overlap. Six clips don't guarantee this.

No paid news pages, scraped readers, sponsored newsletters or unapproved subreddit promotion. Ask permission before a free student-media newsletter mention; zero until accepted. Source links identify evidence, not editorial partnerships. Do not sell a fictional Audience Pulse report to fill consumer targets.

Separate organizers from Vantage Daily's civic explainer, Daily BRF's news briefing and c2 reading sessions. Agree actual dates and avoid the same group's second pitch inside seven days. A source-reading session is learning about evidence, not demographic political targeting. Respond to questions under your own posts with the source and uncertainty, not an assertion your AI is always right.
