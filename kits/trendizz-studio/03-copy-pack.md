A short explainer gets less useful when it compresses them into one confident timeline. The same goes for treating a search-interest number as people or a headline as a full report.

That's what I'm checking in Trendizz: four labelled cards, source links and explicit gaps. If a card can't support its claim, it should say so—or wait for better evidence.
