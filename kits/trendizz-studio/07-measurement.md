# Trendizz Studio — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** With no-account public reading, count distinct people's **first meaningful explainer sessions** under the policy equivalent. Put **four-card completion**, **source opens** and **voluntary next-day/seven-day return** beside it. Waitlist emails and Pro accounts overlap and stay separate; agency leads don't replace the consumer target.

No private baseline was accessed. Public inventory counters aren't traffic or accuracy metrics. Record actual starting values, not zero.

A rating reflects that reader's response, not independent truth verification. Don't encourage repeat ratings to game counts.
