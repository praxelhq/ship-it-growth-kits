# Trendizz Studio — Landing-page fixes

Homepage and public feed inspected in browser; pricing/privacy/terms read, 29 October. No rating, email, account or payment submitted. Counts and selected stories changed across views; they aren't stable proof of audience or accuracy.

1. **Resolve open-feed versus waitlist confusion.** Actual H1 “The internet moves fast. Catch up in 90 seconds.” CTA “Read today's feed” reaches `/feed`; waitlist says we'll let you in when the feed opens. Replace with **“Read the public feed now. Join only if you want an update about future features.”** Keep the actual email purpose consistent with privacy; don't use an outdated access promise to collect unnecessary addresses.

2. **Make a thin source a visible editorial status.** Current cards sometimes say full text unavailable/headline only. Add **“Headline-only context; key details unverified”** before the cards. If there isn't enough evidence for how/why, say **“Not established from available sources”** rather than inferring motive or spread. Better to hold a high-risk story than turn one headline into four unsupported claims.

3. **Distinguish the three dates.** Show event date if known, publication date and trend-detected timestamp separately, with time zone. The source may establish none of them. Don't describe detection time as when the event happened. Current “updated every 4 hours” is an operational claim to test, not proof every item is current.

4. **Keep metrics honest.** Homepage's story/country/source counters are content inventory, not readers or verified truth. Label counting method and avoid fluctuating values as proof of traction. Search-interest scores, social post counts and shares need distinct units; if unverified, don't turn them into a trend-growth percentage. Audience Pulse sample is already “Not real data”; preserve it everywhere.

5. **Put test-only pricing on each upgrade.** India pricing ₹149/month Pro, ₹9,999/month Brands; page says test stub/no Stripe key. Terms say no paid plan activated. Use **“Preview/test only—no charge and no paid plan activation.”** Don't call a test completion revenue or sell sample brand signals. Non-India prices need actual verification before quoting.

6. **Make sources and corrections usable.** A link alone doesn't validate summary wording. Keep source publisher, original date, scope and correction/report path beside each card. Test source destinations and an unavailable/paywalled source without bypassing it. Don't reproduce full copyrighted articles in clips or claim affiliation with publishers.

7. **Check mobile reading and consent.** At 390px, test region/category changes, long cards, source/back navigation and error states. The analytics notice offers a choice; confirm masking and actual cookie behaviour rather than treating policy as an audit. Preview **“Trendizz — four-card context with sources and visible limits.”** No unverified news allegation or private email in the preview.
