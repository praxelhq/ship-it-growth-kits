# Trendizz Studio — Video scripts

Choose a non-sensitive topic and verify source support before recording. Demonstrate the product's evidence boundary without repeating unverified news, graphic incidents, accusations or copyrighted article text.

## A. Four-card source demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Did four cards tell me more than the headline?”**

2–12s: open the actual public feed and one suitable explainer. “No account needed to read.”

12–25s: show the four labels and a real explicit unknown. “This part is known from the source. This part isn't established.” Keep the actual source's scope, no invented facts.

25–38s: open original source without bypassing a paywall. “The summary is a starting point, not evidence the reporting is verified.”

38–50s: public feed URL and **AI-assisted · check the original · paid plans test-only**.

## B. Timeline hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Trending today doesn't mean it happened today.”**

2–12s: show event/publication/detection date fields on an actual suitable item, or clearly labelled editorial diagram if unavailable. “Different clocks, different claims.”

12–18s: source/URL. Don't pretend a proposed field already exists.

## C. Thin-source hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“When the source is only a headline, say that first.”**

2–12s: point to the real limited-source note, without amplifying its factual allegation. “Four labels shouldn't manufacture four new facts.”

12–20s: a source-check or hold-for-review workflow, label proposal if not implemented. URL last.

## D. Price/metrics hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A sample growth number isn't market evidence.”**

2–12s: show the Audience Pulse illustrative label and test-only price state. “Examples explain the format. They aren't actual brand signals or live paid adoption.”

12–20s: public feed. “The reader test is useful context and an opened source.” No invented agency result.

For six scheduled clips, reuse the best structure with a different verified interface/source lesson. Keep citations and uncertainty visible after cropping.
