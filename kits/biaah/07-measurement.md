## Target and milestones

Keep the **1,000 B2C signups target**. Verify what constitutes an actual account or invited collaborator. Separate registered people, households/workspaces, collaborators, guest records, paid orders and legitimate activated access. A sample wedding with 247 guests isn't 247 users. A paid redirect isn't server-verified entitlement; payment tracking isn't vendor payment execution.

Activation after readiness: one authorized household user completes a real sample budget/responsibility action and another authorized view can understand it if collaboration is claimed. Deeper value: voluntary later update/retrieval. Do not require real guest/private budget disclosure to observe a useful fictional rehearsal.

Use coarse legitimate events only after actual policy/consent: signup/join, workspace access, first sample cost/task, shared update success, payment entitlement, export/delete, return. No family names, guest contacts, event dates/locations, diets/health notes, room/travel data, receipts, payment references or private links in marketing analytics/replays. Audit masking/access on owned samples before adding duplicate trackers. Source tags `warm-family-plan`, `owned-budget`, `organizer-plan`, `peerlist`; deduplicate known overlap without fingerprinting.

## Weekly decisions

**Day 7:** Can five voluntary safe sample tests access the actual workspace and complete the first action? Five is a learning gate. If checkout exists but access doesn't, repair delivery; don't optimize ₹51 purchase clicks. If signup doesn't exist, state the product constraint without reclassifying the target.

**Day 14:** Split access, cost status, owner assignment and shared-view failures. If people think the ledger sends vendor money, fix copy. If they mistake sample vendor ratings for verification, remove/label them. No actual market-price or financial-planning assurance from an AI sample.

**Day 21:** Review voluntary repeated useful action, not rows/guest counts. A wedding not yet near execution isn't churn; distinguish not-yet-needed from a broken workspace. If roles/access fail in owned tests, stop real-data invitations and fix before another household enters.

**Day 28:** Audit price/strike-through basis, defined workspace duration, export/delete/refund/support and actual paid fulfillment. Don't promise forever storage from a hosting brand or no family friction from a dashboard. Count refunds/support alongside orders, not just gross checkout volume.

**Day 30:** Report actual invitations/posts, readiness, legitimate signups/households/paid buyers separately, first/shared/repeat actions, source overlap, failures, hours/spend and 1,000-signup shortfall. With all readiness gates/waves, 02 guesses only 3–13 activations without an event or 4–16 with it before overlap; unrecovered access means zero signups forecast. Unaccepted editorial/launch/directory reach is zero. No private family/student details or fabricated customer wedding outcomes in the handoff.
