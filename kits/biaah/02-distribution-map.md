# Biaah — Distribution map

Readiness first: verify actual workspace access and ₹51 purchase delivery before asking families to pay. No private guest-data collection. Biaah uses earlier planning/budget households; Shaadi Board handles day-of handovers with a different actual circle. One organization gets one primary invitation per week.

## Ranked routes

**Twenty warm adult family-planner invitations**, five each Days 6,13,20,27. Offer a fictional one-function budget demonstration with 03 #1, not a payment-first blind trial. Qualify people actively coordinating a family event and let them decline without disclosing details. One follow-up maximum. If access/entitlement gates fail, replace affected waves with owner-led tests and count zero product invitations that day.

**Six owned process posts**, Days 4,9,14,19,24,29. Show a labelled fictional expense/owner plan and actual verified product behavior, or an honest readiness update if not ready. Use 03 #2/#3 and 05. Manual [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387)/maker-owned account, no scraping wedding announcements, bulk DMs or fake engagement. No illustrated couple as testimonial.

**One conditional ten-person family-planning rehearsal**, Day 22. Ask one known adult organizer Day 11 with 03 #4. Maker-owned fictional household, no real guests/payments/purchase/account required to attend. Ten is proposed cap, not secured attendance. If declined, individual tests and zero event reach.

**One introduced wedding-planning editor**, Day 18 using 03 #7. Offer an original generic one-function budget/owner exercise, ownership disclosed, no guessed contact/audience or guaranteed coverage. Zero base traffic until accepted/published.

**Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad, inspect current eligibility/slot/existing listing Day 16; optional Day 23 if actual access/price/privacy are ready. No votes requested or assumed slot. **Product Hunt**, https://www.producthunt.com/launch, optional Day 25 after working-product/existing-listing review, no paid hunter/upvotes/unchanged duplicate launch.

**AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional verified-email accurate app entry. Free review may wait indefinitely; paid priority outside budget. Zero month-one traffic until accepted. Directory inclusion doesn't verify vendor records or privacy.

## Conditional arithmetic

Planning assumptions: 20 qualified invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12; × 20–40% legitimate access/first shared sample action = about 2–5 activations. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 10–25% first access/action = about 1–8. Accepted ten-person rehearsal × 60–80% attendance = 6–8; × 20–40% later product access/action = about 1–3. Remove overlap.

After readiness, rough base **3–13 activations** without the session, **4–16** with it before deduplication. If no real workspace/signup access is verified, **zero signups forecast**, with interviews/concept demonstrations separate. Paid conversion isn't automatically equal to these activations and no test payment is a customer. Unaccepted editorial/launch/directory traffic is zero. Keep the 1,000 target and actual gap; a larger accepted partner can be tested only after safe delivery, not by multiplying a sample guest list into imagined signups.
