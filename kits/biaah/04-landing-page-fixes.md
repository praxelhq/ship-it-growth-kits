# Biaah — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“Your entire wedding, finally in one place.”** **Start Planning Your Wedding/Launch App/Build Your Workspace** are JavaScript buttons with no navigable destination in extracted links. **Get Biaah Pro · ₹51** is a Dodo checkout link returning to a payment-status URL. Broad capability claims and sample workspace are visible; real app/payment/access untested. Ranked recommendations.

## 1. Show access and delivered value before payment

Owner tests actual button behavior, signup/login/workspace creation, successful payment→server-verified entitlement, duplicate/retry/failed checkout and return access with controlled accounts. A `paymentStatus=succeeded` URL mustn't be treated as purchase proof or a delivered workspace by marketing. No actual security implementation review was performed here.

Suggested first CTA after a real sample exists:

> **See a fictional one-function workspace.** Inspect a sample budget and responsibilities before buying. No real family details needed.

Purchase help:

> ₹51 one-time launch price. See exactly how to access your workspace after payment and the actual duration/export/refund/support terms before checkout.

Don't take traffic into an undefined delivery path or invent a free trial.

## 2. Narrow the first useful promise

Suggested support under H1:

> Start with one function's planned costs, commitments and owners. Bring more of the wedding in only after the shared plan works for your family.

Show one actually implemented cost/owner/update loop. Guest tracking, eight functions, roles, vendors, WhatsApp RSVPs and AI need separate verification; don't animate them as proven from a static sample. “Zero chaos” becomes **a clearer shared plan**, without guaranteeing family harmony.

## 3. Label all samples and verification claims

The illustrated couple, 247 guests, ₹18.5L budget, vendor star rating and timeline aren't customer evidence. Put **Fictional sample workspace; not customer data or endorsement** on each crop. “Verified vendor” requires a documented check, source/date and scope before recommendations.

Suggested vendor-state copy if records aren't ready:

> Vendor examples are illustrative. Confirm current identity, availability, quote, scope and terms directly before paying. A sample rating isn't verification.

Don't repeat Delhi/Mumbai AI price benchmarks as real quotes; cite current sources/assumptions or remove them. No wedding-money decisions based on fabricated market prices.

## 4. Replace permanent-storage absolutes with actual terms

“Secure on Supabase — everything saved forever” isn't enough. Owner publishes retention, account ownership, export/delete, service discontinuation, support and access controls. Suggested bounded copy only if implemented:

> Your workspace remains available under the stated access/retention terms. Keep an export/backup of important planning records. Read deletion and service-availability limits before adding private data.

Do not publish a made-up retention period or imply a hosting vendor guarantees forever service. “Lifetime of wedding workspace” must define what ends/continues, and ₹1,499 strike-through needs a truthful pricing basis.

## 5. Separate ledger tracking from money movement

Suggested budget caption:

> Record planned, committed and paid amounts. These entries don't prove a bank transfer or send money to a vendor unless the actual payment feature explicitly does so. Check receipts privately before marking paid.

Owner verifies any genuine money-transfer/integration separately; don't advertise UPI/vendor advances because the software checkout accepts UPI. Keep real receipts/payment identifiers out of replays and examples.

## 6. Verify household privacy before invitations

Test owned sample roles, unauthorized links, invite revocation, export/delete and analytics masking. Publish actual policy/support paths. No guest names, diet/health notes, rooms, travel, exact event timing or family budgets in analytics/public proof. Don't infer privacy from a Supabase logo or call the whole app independently secure.

Success is safe legitimate access plus one useful shared action, not number of guest rows or clicks on a ₹51 launch offer. Keep Biaah earlier planning/budget distinct from Shaadi Board's day-of handover.
