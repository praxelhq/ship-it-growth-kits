Your first user is an adult family member sharing one wedding function's budget and responsibilities with another relative. Start with one fictional function, a few planned expenses and who owns them. Don't ask a family to migrate every guest/travel/payment detail into an unverified broad “operating system” on the first visit.

Actual H1 **“Your entire wedding, finally in one place.”** **Start Planning Your Wedding**, **Launch App** and **Build Your Workspace** are JavaScript buttons without a navigable destination in the rendered link inventory. A payment-success URL isn't proof of a legitimate paid entitlement, created workspace or durable storage. No button interaction, account, workspace, family collaboration, payment or AI was tested here.

## The narrow promise to verify

> Keep one function's planned costs and responsibilities together, so the family can see what is committed and who handles the next step.

Bio: **A shared plan for one wedding, starting with one function.**

The page describes guests, eight functions, roles, budget/payment tracking, vendor discovery and AI guidance. Treat each as an operator claim pending a working demonstration. Start with a verified budget/responsibility loop before selling real-time WhatsApp RSVP, verified vendors or location-price benchmarks. Tracking a payment isn't necessarily making a payment to a vendor.

Actual price **₹51 launch**, crossed-out ₹1,499, **one-time for the lifetime of the wedding workspace**. No free trial was verified. “Everything saved forever” and “zero chaos” are unsupportable absolute expectations without clear retention/service terms. Owner must explain actual access, duration, export, deletion, refund and purchase delivery before traffic. The shared sample wedding, budget, 247 guests, ratings and dates are **illustrations**, not customer traction or vendor verification evidence.

## Alternatives and the real difference

An existing family chat plus a spreadsheet costs ₹0 extra specialist software; [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) is a real shared-table option for expenses/owners. It can work well. Biaah earns adoption if a shared function plan is clearer and safely accessible, not by claiming every wedding chat/Excel sheet creates crises or that software eliminates family disagreements.

Shaadi Board in this lane is the day-of Ops/handover wedge. Biaah is earlier family planning/budget coordination. Use different actual households and organizers, one primary offer per organization per week. Don't double-recruit the same family into rival wedding workspaces.

## Trust and target

Use a completely fictional household with generic roles. No actual names, contacts, guest diet/travel/room details, event dates, ID, receipts or payment screenshots in demos/analytics. A Supabase badge doesn't independently prove secure access or forever retention. Private access, export/delete, masking and entitlement are gates before real family data.

Keep the **1,000 B2C signups target**, alongside legitimate workspace access and a first shared budget/task action. Paid orders, wedding workspaces, collaborators, guests and signups are separate. The visible checkout doesn't establish self-service signup today; confirm the actual model and report the gap rather than invent accounts or convert the target to B2B customers.
