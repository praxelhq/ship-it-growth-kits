# KaleshJudge — Positioning

**Live:** https://kaleshjudge.app/ · **Category:** Public entertainment voting · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kaleshjudge

**Your site works beyond the loading screen.** A rendered fetch initially showed only a spinner, but the actual browser loaded the live courtroom on 29 October. The headline is **“Your story. Publicly anonymous.”** The CTAs include **“Judge a kalesh,” “Join the jury”** and **“File your kalesh.”** The page says read freely and sign in with Google to vote. No paid plan was visible.

Your first user is an adult friend laughing at a harmless fictional dilemma—such as who gets the last snack—not someone seeking judgment on serious harm or a real accusation.

**Pitch:** Read a small, harmless disagreement and offer a playful verdict, with clear public-content limits.

**Short version:** Judge the silly case, not the person.

## The wedge

One safe, original case, a real reader and an independently chosen verdict. This is a human-voting product, distinct from Judge My Chat's AI-output format. A free [WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) poll already lets a private friend group choose a side. KaleshJudge earns interest through a broader playful courtroom, if the public content stays appropriate.

## The uncomfortable anonymity limit

The browser's privacy dialog says Google identity stays off the public feed, but authorized admins can link accounts to activity. Nicknames can appear publicly, and **content is not automatically anonymized**. Public stories/screenshots can identify people even when the author label says Anonymous.

The inspected feed included identifying details and serious-harm language despite the etiquette. Review moderation and content before driving more traffic. Don't copy those posts into marketing, promise absolute anonymity or use real conflict for shock reach.

The browser showed 16 cases and 70 verdicts at the research snapshot—not 70 users. No sign-in, vote, notification permission or post was submitted. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 confirmed real participants/accounts, with first genuine verdicts beside it. A Google account is not guaranteed to equal a distinct person; document deduplication limits.
