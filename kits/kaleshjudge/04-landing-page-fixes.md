# KaleshJudge — Landing page fixes

Based on the fully loaded browser courtroom and privacy dialog checked 29 October. No account, vote, post or notification/analytics permission was submitted.

## 1. Enforce the public-content promise before growth

The etiquette says keep it petty/kind, leave names out and serious harm isn't entertainment. The inspected feed nevertheless included identifying details and serious-harm language. Review moderation, reporting and publication controls before more acquisition. Use fictional safe examples in marketing, not those posts. A rule in the footer doesn't make the feed safe by itself.

## 2. Make anonymity limits inseparable from the headline

Current headline: **“Your story. Publicly anonymous.”** CTA: **“File your kalesh.”**

Suggested support: **“Your Google identity stays off the feed. Your story is public and isn't automatically anonymized; admins can link account activity.”** Button: **“Review posting rules.”** Then allow the actual posting flow. Keep the fuller privacy explanation directly accessible.

## 3. Put content review before publication

Use a preview that highlights names, screenshots and identifying context; users must understand they are publishing. Suggested prompt: **“Remove real identifiers and private details. Is this harmless entertainment, not serious harm or an allegation?”** This is a review aid, not a claim automatic redaction already exists.

## 4. Test the real sign-in/return/verdict path

Fresh browser → original safe case → Google sign-in → return to that case → one independently chosen vote → revisit. Test repeat attempts and clear state. The policy itself notes different Google accounts may belong to one person; don't claim sign-in guarantees unique humans.

## 5. Keep free reading and optional notifications separate

The page says read freely, sign in to vote. Notifications are optional/device-specific and shouldn't block the first case. Show **“Not now”** and explain stopping alerts. Do not enable them or analytics just because a user signs in.

## 6. Preserve existing opt-in analytics boundaries

The privacy dialog says Clarity is off until an 18+ opt-in, with app text/images masked and no Google identity sent. Don't add duplicate scripts or capture story content to improve a funnel. Verify the actual implementation matches those claims.

## 7. Improve slow-load and phone clarity

The first rendered fetch saw a loading state, but the browser later loaded real content. Add a useful timeout/retry without declaring the service dead. At 390px, test case return, long text, report/privacy access and vote labels. Preview: **“Kalesh Judge — harmless cases, clear public-content limits.”** No real user's accusation or identifying text.
