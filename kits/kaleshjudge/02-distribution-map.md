# KaleshJudge — Distribution map

**Moderation first, then harmless original cases.** Don't acquire users by exposing a private person or escalating a real dispute.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult testers | Original fictional cases, sign-in/return/vote clarity; no requested stance or duplicate accounts. | 10 trials, 5–10 independent first verdicts if the path works. | #1 |

| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback after moderation and real participation tests, current rules/slot checked. No votes requested on the listing. | 0–30 accounts, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No conflict-support/legal community, paid meme page, influencer or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. A campus newsletter may carry a safe fictional demo only after permission, zero forecast until accepted. Don't recruit in a real person's distress thread or ask people to mass-vote a desired side.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 relevant unique visits. The scheduled tests may yield only dozens or low hundreds. Replace rates after three clips. Keep these public-court groups separate from Scrollback's private gift stories and Judge My Chat's synthetic AI trial demos.
