## #6 — Before-posting note

Could someone identify a real person from the details, screenshot or timing? If yes, don't post it as an anonymous joke. Keep serious harm out; use the actual report/support route rather than turning it into entertainment.

Never reuse current users' public disputes as promotional copy without permission. Change the original fictional scenario between clips; don't fabricate community consensus or manipulate verdict counts.
