# KaleshJudge — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real participant accounts once, with known duplicate-person limits stated. Report **first genuine verdicts**, **voluntary return** and **moderation response** beside it. A person can have more than one Google account; the app's own privacy explanation acknowledges that.

The browser snapshot on 29 October showed **16 cases and 70 verdicts**. These are dated public counts, not user totals or independently audited activity. Private baseline records weren't accessed.

The privacy dialog already describes optional 18+ Clarity, off until consent, all app text/images masked and no Google identity sent. Don't add duplicate analytics or unmask stories/nicknames. Track `case_opened`, `account_confirmed`, `first_verdict_recorded`, `report_submitted`, `report_resolved`, `repeat_participation` as permitted aggregate status only.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 distinct relevant visits. Small scheduled tests may produce dozens or low hundreds. Don't count 70 verdicts as 70 users or repeat cases as new participants.

No paid plan was visible. Course 24-hour traffic readings overlap and aren't cumulative accounts. Public entertainment verdicts don't establish legal or relationship truth; safe, voluntary participation with clear limits is the useful outcome.
