# KaleshJudge — Video scripts

Use original fictional cases and owned test accounts. No real users' stories, nicknames, identity or private screenshot in a public demo.

## A. Safe courtroom demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Judge the silly case, not a real person.”**

2–12s, open an original harmless fictional scenario. “Reading is open; participation needs sign-in.”

12–25s, show the actual case-return/vote flow using authorized tests. “A user chooses independently.” Don't ask viewers to coordinate a side.

25–38s, open privacy explanation. “Public Anonymous doesn't mean hidden from admins or automatic text anonymization.”

38–50s: rules and URL. “Keep serious harm out. kaleshjudge.app.”

## B. Petty hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “The last snack is a fictional public matter.”

2–10s: original sample case and options. “No real names. No real accusation.”

10–15s: URL and **Entertainment only**. Don't stage actual consensus.

## C. Privacy hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Anonymous is a label, not a redaction tool.”

2–12s: show the actual privacy dialog. “Your words can still identify someone.”

12–20s: fictional preview with identifiers removed. “Review before publishing.”

## D. Moderation lesson — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Serious harm isn't a launch hook.”

2–12s: show rules/report controls without reproducing a harmful real post. “A petty-case app needs the boundary to work.”

12–20s: actual safe fictional case. “The joke can stay small.” No harassment, threats or private relationship allegation for reach.
