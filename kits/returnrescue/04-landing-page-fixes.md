# ReturnRescue — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://returnrescue.dpdns.org/) and [pricing](https://returnrescue.dpdns.org/pricing) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Every return deserves a second chance.”** CTAs: **“Start 14-day free trial”** and **“See the demo.”** Trial no card/100 inspections; Starter ₹499/month or ₹5,388/year; Pro ₹999/month or ₹10,788/year. Authenticated inspection, costs, partner matching, exports and payment were not tested.

## 1. Put physical inspection beside the grade

Current copy says AI grades a photo and picks the most valuable option; the product section also mentions what to check by hand.

Replacement support: **“Use photo-based suggestions alongside your physical inspection. Confirm condition and hidden issues before deciding the item's next route.”**

No photo can certify every product defect. Do not use a grade as a safety/quality warranty or promise a restock is appropriate without the owner's review.

## 2. Keep sample labels with every product

Current cards explicitly call the engine output fictional samples.

Replacement label: **“Fictional return · illustrative grade and economics · not customer recovery.”** Keep it on crops, preview images and demo exports. Do not reuse the sample brand/product as a testimonial or real inventory.

## 3. Expose inputs before “best option”

Current promises trace every rupee to a rate or own price.

Replacement result note: **“Recommended under these assumptions. Review expected resale value, current fees, repair, handling and delivery before acting.”**

Test changed/missing costs and distinguish estimates from actual invoices/contracts. A published platform rate may vary by category/contract; do not certify every tax/fee figure from a marketing claim.

## 4. Distinguish trial, paid AI and post-trial manual use

Current trial has 100 inspections total; Starter 100/month; Pro 300/month fair use. After trial, limited manual calculator up to 15 returns remains, AI/export requires paid plan.

Replacement allowance strip: **“14 days, 100 trial inspections. Choose a plan for further AI/export use. Without a plan, records remain viewable with the limited manual tools described.”**

Show exact expiry and counters before upload. Unlimited stored returns are not unlimited AI inspections. Test failed inspections and quota behavior.

## 5. Make renewal and final charge clear

Current yearly plans renew until cancelled; taxes/final price are shown at checkout through Dodo.

Replacement: **“Review billing period, final INR total and renewal before paying. Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.”**

Verify cancellation, trial end, access and export recovery under an authorized test. Do not promise a refund beyond current terms or imply prepaid non-renewal simply because annual payment happens once.

## 6. Treat partners as unconfirmed options

Current page says nearby repairers/cleaners/liquidators/recyclers are matched.

Replacement directory note: **“Confirm service area, acceptance, cost and handling directly before sending an item. A listing is not a booking or guaranteed buyer.”**

Test actual contact destinations without placing service requests. Keep customer identity/order labels out of photos and replay. A live site after an old down label should be described as live; specific failures belong to exact routes and tests, not an invented outage.
