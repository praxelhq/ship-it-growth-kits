# ReturnRescue — Phone-demo scripts

Use owned sample apparel and a fictional return record, your phone camera/screen recorder and voice. No customer identity, order label, private costs or staged recovery testimonial. The physical inspection and owner review remain visible.

## A. Inspect, compare, decide — 65 seconds

0–3 s: owned sample item. **“What would you do with this return?”**

3–17 s: physical check and permitted sample photo entry. “The photograph is only part of the inspection.”

17–32 s: actual photo-grade result if exercised; otherwise labelled public demo. “This is a suggestion with reasons, not certification of condition.” Leave the result/limitation readable.

32–47 s: change one actual cost input and compare routes. “Expected resale and repair costs change the economics.” Do not fake an editable control or recovered sale.

47–55 s: owner final choice and manual checks. “The business decides after reviewing the assumptions.”

55–65 s: actual trial/paid limits and URL. “Fourteen-day trial. Check the inspection allowance and next plan. returnrescue.dpdns.org.”

## B. Cost hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: synthetic repair-cost field. **“Change one cost. Check the recommendation.”**

3–12 s: actual permitted calculator or labelled sample comparison. “A recommendation is only as current as its inputs.”

12–20 s: owner review and URL. “Expected recovery is not cash received.” No invented margin lift.

## C. Photo hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: owned item's outside/inside inspection. **“One photo cannot show everything.”**

3–15 s: physical check beside the actual sample grade. “Keep hidden issues and manual inspection in the decision.”

15–25 s: human choice and trial limit. “Use the suggestion. Review the item. Decide responsibly.”

Capture actual headline/CTA, fictional sample label, visible/hidden-defect boundary, actual costs/rate basis, plan quotas and owner decision. Label waiting cuts. Do not stage a repair booking, successful resale or customer recovery that did not happen.
