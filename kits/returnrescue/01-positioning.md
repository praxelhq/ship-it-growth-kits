## The first business

A small D2C fashion brand receiving a few apparel returns weekly, with an owner/operations lead who physically inspects them and knows handling/repair costs. Start with ordinary non-safety-critical apparel and one existing return queue. A photo cannot reveal every hidden defect, smell, functional problem or suitability for resale.

**Pitch: Compare the recovery options for a physically checked return, with the assumptions visible.**

Short version: **Inspect the item. Review the economics. Choose the next action.**

The first useful job is one real authorized return, a photo without personal/customer details, confirmed condition and owner-reviewed restock/refurbish/liquidate/recycle assumptions. An AI grade is a suggestion; the physical inspection and decision remain the business's responsibility. A model-selected “best option” is not recovered cash.

## Current commercial terms

The 14-day trial needs no card, includes Pro features and up to 100 AI inspections total. Starter ₹499/month or ₹5,388/year includes 100 inspections/month; Pro ₹999/month or ₹10,788/year includes 300/month fair use and AI-written briefings. Yearly plans renew until cancelled. Prices are INR with applicable taxes as shown at checkout, through Dodo. Actual payment, tax total and entitlement were not exercised.

After an unconverted trial, records stay viewable, manual grading/calculator remain up to 15 returns, while AI and exports need a paid plan; summaries need Pro. No charge occurs unless the user chooses a plan according to the page. Do not call unlimited returns unlimited free AI or promise exports after trial without a plan.

The site promises transparent rates/fees/GST and nearby repair/liquidation/recycling partners. Those are product claims, not independent verification of every platform fee or partner availability. Use the owner's actual current contracts/rates and confirm any service before committing an item. Do not invent resale prices, customer recovery or a guaranteed buyer.

## The alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) return log can hold physical condition, expected realizable value, fees, repair/handling/delivery cost and final actual recovery. ReturnRescue must earn adoption through a clearer reviewed comparison and traceability, not certainty from a photograph.

Prove a synthetic case, changed fee and actual output before own-data use. Preserve original inspection evidence and review private-data handling. The landing's fictional product prices/grades are demonstration values, not verified inventory or real customer savings.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each brand must onboard its own actual authorized return data, use independently within seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Return items, partner profiles and demo views do not become customer businesses.
