# ReturnRescue — Distribution map

Lead with an owner reviewing a small real apparel-return queue. Do not sell a cure for all returns or a promise that AI can certify resale condition.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small D2C apparel brands | Qualify current returns, owner control of operations/costs and permission to process item photos. No buyer contacts or order exports needed for the first demo. | Ten blocks of four businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two fulfilment/repair introductions | Ask a known professional to forward one invitation, not disclose brands, customer orders or private contracts. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a fictional inspected return and an editable cost assumption; no recovery guarantee or customer claim. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One owner-led recovery comparison | Authorized operator checks physical condition and actual cost assumptions, retaining final decision. | Day 12; #5. Existing-trial support. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-return setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 brands. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Ten four-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts/introductions add zero guaranteed conversions. Trial ending after 14 days is a real conversion/capacity issue: qualify manual-use eligibility or a chosen paid plan honestly; do not assume unlimited free AI during week four.

Use an apparel-operations list distinct from Before's notebook concept tests, PitchPanel's snack-market founders and Parchi's chat-billing clothing sellers. If an owner overlaps, choose the immediate job and avoid multiple pitches that week. One primary invitation per organizer/week.

No D2C newsletter, ecommerce community, repair association or logistics partnership has agreed to promote the product. Ask before group posting. No purchased lists, automated DMs, fake return-service order or customer-data scraping. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after decline. Do not arrange transport or sell returned goods as a marketing demonstration.

If grading is misleading or economics cannot be reconciled to actual assumptions, pause acquisition and repair. A shop can reject an AI recommendation and still find the reviewed comparison useful; never treat human disagreement as a metric failure to hide.
