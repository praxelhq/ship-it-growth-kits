# Astitva — Measurement

## Your number

**10 real business customers in 30 days.** Report **businesses independently creating or revising a real deck** beside the headline. Account registrations, free-credit claims and demo attendees are separate funnel stages, not customers. The [target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires all four tests:

1. A real company, not a classmate pretending to be a company.
2. Its own actual business brief, theme and deck are in the workspace.
3. It used the product in the last seven days without you asking it to.
4. You know the responsible person's name and have a callable phone number, held privately.

Payment is not required. Keep paying customers and revenue as additional columns. One founder with three accounts is one business. Your own example company and fictional portfolio brands are excluded.

## Day 0: establish what is known

Your [public course profile](https://shipped.money/p/astitva) showed a saved **15 sessions/24h** Clarity reading dated 28 October 2026 at research time. It is a saved rolling reading, not a signup count, lifetime total or guaranteed current value. Record the latest timestamp when starting. Do not add overlapping daily saved readings together.

The profile also reported **$3.59 net revenue** for its displayed period. This is not profit and does not reveal how many independently active businesses you have. Do not reset either measure to zero for the kit.

Keep raw brief text, investor documents and personal contacts out of public reports and session-recording exposure. Use aggregate events rather than logging confidential deck content.

## The funnel to instrument

Use `signup_completed`, `brief_saved`, `theme_selected`, `free_slides_completed`, `deck_edited`, `export_succeeded`, `credit_purchase_succeeded` and `returning_company_active`. A click on Export is not an export success. Count successful actions by distinct company, deduplicate retries and exclude your tests.

**Activation:** the founder enters their own brief, reviews the generated story and obtains output they can use. **Retention:** that company returns on a later day to revise or export a real presentation. For the policy headline, check the final-seven-day unprompted-use condition separately; a day-5 activation does not automatically count on day 30.

## Forecast, not a promise

The calendar budgets **30 new individual approaches and 10 warm introduction requests**. A rough combined scenario is 40 approaches × 25% substantive replies × 60% trials × 40% independently active = **2–3 customers**, before accepted clinic contributions. The two audiences may convert differently, so record them separately. An accepted clinic might add 0–5 trials and 0–2 independently active businesses, not five automatic customers. **The scheduled base case is about 2–5 customers, materially below ten.** Ten remains the target; meeting it requires better observed conversion, more warm introductions, or replacing low-yield posting time with additional qualified outreach after the day-14 review. Never report the target as already supported by the forecast.

After 20 approaches, replace those assumed rates with observed counts. If two of the first 20 reply, sending the next 80 identically will not repair the offer. If founders trial but never return, more traffic can inflate registrations while the customer count stays flat.

## Weekly checkpoints

| Review | Working milestone, not a forecast | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | 10 substantive conversations, 3 real trials, 1 independent activation | If no useful conversations, narrow to founders with a current presentation job. If trials fail, fix product before outreach. |
| Day 14 | 6 activations and 3 currently qualifying businesses | If no one returns, interview three founders about the next deck revision. Stop pitching a daily-use tool. |
| Day 21 | 5 qualifying businesses, one source producing repeat users | Repeat the measured source. Replace broad launches with introductions if public traffic is unqualified. |
| Day 28 | 8 qualifying businesses, final-seven-day activity checked | Don't prompt people merely to make the activity column green. Identify genuinely recurring use. |
| Day 30 | 10 qualifying businesses, or the honest shortfall | Publish the real funnel and reasons. Keep assisted work and tests outside the headline. |

Each Sunday, record approaches, replies, trials, independent activations, qualifying businesses, repeat exports, paid companies, net receipts/refunds and hours. Watch five Clarity sessions that reached signup but did not complete, with sensitive inputs masked. Look for confusion about two free slides, not just homepage scroll depth.

## Decisions that matter

- Many signup starts but few completed briefs: simplify the first brief and clarify what happens next.
- Many first outputs but few usable exports: inspect actual files and record failures, rather than counting button clicks.
- Many activations but little return use: the one-off deck job may be real, but report it as one-off use and test the next revision workflow.
- Purchases followed by confusion/refunds: make credit cost and free scope clear before another promotion.
- Good business use but little public traffic: keep the channel. Ten relevant companies beats a launch leaderboard spike.
