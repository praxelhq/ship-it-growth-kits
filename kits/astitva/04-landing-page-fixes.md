# Astitva — Landing-page fixes

Checked the public page and CTA destinations on 29 October 2026. The page is live. The signup link points to `/app/#/signup`; its existence does **not** establish that account creation, generation, export and payment all work. These are product-specific recommendations, not claims that an authenticated flow was tested.

## Fix 1 — Prove the brief-to-export path before outreach

**Current:** “Start free · 50 credits” goes to the signup route. The prominent animated workspace is a sample flow. A new visitor has to trust that the real output will match it.

**Write:** **“Create your brand and first 2 slides free.”** Supporting line: **“50 credits. No card needed. Check the result before buying more.”**

Keep the existing signup destination. Test a fresh account on a phone and desktop, enter a non-confidential brief, generate the free output, edit a word and open the permitted export. Watch one unfamiliar founder do it. If any required step fails, stop traffic until repaired. Don't describe an app shell as a dead app.

## Fix 2 — Make the free boundary impossible to miss

**Current:** the hero offers 50 free credits; the full allocation explanation appears farther down. A founder may reasonably hear “free deck.”

**Write below the hero button:** **“Free: strategy, theme, one finished logo and 2 designed slides. Add credits for the rest of your deck.”**

On the Starter card, write **“₹299 + 18% GST · 100 credits · one-time purchase.”** Display the credit balance and cost before each paid action. Use the actual successful-step charging behaviour; do not introduce a trial subscription or auto-renewal claim.

## Fix 3 — Bring one real editable output above the long animation

**Current:** a sample brand/deck image and animated demonstration show the intended experience. Sample portfolio brands are correctly labelled fictional.

**Write:** **“See a real export. Open it, change a sentence, and check the theme.”**

Record the actual product using your own example company. Put a short screen recording and a clearly labelled sample export beside the primary action. Keep the fictional-sample label attached to the asset, including on mobile. Don't replace a sample disclosure with invented customer proof.

## Fix 4 — Unify the decision across desktop and phone

**Current:** desktop says **“Your logo, brand and pitch deck, made from one brief.”** Mobile says **“Your brand and pitch deck, from one brief.”** Both ask the visitor to start free, but free scope is easier to miss while scrolling.

**Recommended headline for both:** **“Your startup's brand and editable pitch deck, from one brief.”** Subhead: **“Choose your theme once. Review the story. Edit the slides and export PowerPoint.”**

Preserve the current editorial identity. Make the real output the focal point, the free scope the supporting line, and decorative motion secondary. Test 360px width, enlarged text, a keyboard and reduced-motion preference. This is a verification task; no mobile overlap or performance measurement was observed in this research.

## Fix 5 — Replace the agency price comparison with a workflow comparison

**Current:** “Design agency ₹50,000+” and a weeks-versus-afternoon comparison. Those are your marketing claims, not a universal independently verified price or duration.

**Write:** **“Already have a working template? Keep it. Astitva is for the founder repeatedly matching a logo, theme and deck by hand.”** Then compare manual matching, connected changes and editable export. [Gamma Free](https://gamma.app/pricing) also offers PPTX export; do not claim that export alone is unique.

## Fix 6 — Give a returning founder a reason to return

**Current:** the page explains initial creation much more than the next presentation revision.

**Write:** **“New traction number? Update the story and check the next export in the same brand.”** Show that operation only if the product actually supports the stated behaviour. Put the next real job after the first successful output, rather than sending founders a generic daily reminder. This directly affects whether a business qualifies as active in `07`.

## Fix 7 — Make shared previews and long-page reading useful

**Current:** social metadata describes the brand-and-deck workflow and uses a hero image. Keep that specificity; don't add fundraising promises.

**Suggested title:** **“Astitva — one brief to matching brand and editable deck.”** Description: **“Start with brand work and two designed slides free. Buy one-time credit packs only when you need more.”**

Check the actual link preview after deployment and ensure the hero asset loads on mobile data. Don't assert a load-time improvement without measuring before and after. In Clarity, inspect clicks on sample outputs and the first signup step; those tell you whether a founder wants evidence or is ready to create.
