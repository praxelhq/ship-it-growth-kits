# Astitva — Distribution map

**Direct introductions and small deck clinics first; public launches second.** A hundred makers browsing a launch page are less useful than five founders with a presentation deadline. Budget roughly two hours a day for prospect conversations, one for onboarding, and one for product corrections.

All reach ranges below are **planning assumptions**, not historical benchmarks. A new account can get zero response. Replace them after your first 20 approaches and first three posts. These channels share an audience: do not add their results as if every person were new.

## 1. Warm founder introductions — primary

Start with your own entrepreneurship cell, venture programme and alumni introductions. Your live page names MBA founders as an audience; that does not give you permission to broadcast into any particular group. Ask the organizer for one 20-minute clinic. **Copy #1** is the invitation; **#4** is the approved group announcement.

Workload: approach 10 people you genuinely know and ask for one relevant introduction each. Planning range: 3–6 conversations, 1–3 trials. You are proposing a useful session, not asking the organizer to endorse an untested output. Record the actual permission and date privately.

The cohort has reserved **days 4–7** for your shared founder-group invitation. PitchPanel follows on days 10–13; Postifyy has a student session on days 17–20; Adya follows on days 24–27. If your start dates differ, coordinate actual dates before messaging the same organizer. No reciprocal upvotes or manufactured comments.

## 2. Manually selected founders — primary

Use public founder posts and the official [Startup India portal](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) to find businesses and organizations, then qualify on the company's own site. The portal is a research source, not an advertising board. Look for a shipped product, a named founder and a current pitch-related need. Write down the reason the company fits before contacting it.

Send at most five new personalized approaches per working day. This is your workload cap, **not a claim that any platform allows five unsolicited messages**. Follow the platform's actual account limits. Use an introduction or a business contact route intended for relevant enquiries. One follow-up after seven days; stop after a refusal. **Copy #2** is the first message and **#3** the email.

The calendar explicitly budgets **30 new individual approaches plus 10 warm introduction requests**, not 100 cold messages. A rough combined scenario is 40 approaches × 25% substantive reply × 60% trial × 40% independently active = **2–3 customers**. Those rates are assumptions, and the warm and cold groups may behave differently. An accepted clinic might add 0–2 independently active businesses. **This base plan leaves a material gap to ten.** At day 14, use observed rates to decide whether to replace weak public-posting slots with more qualified introductions; do not promise ten from the scheduled inputs.

## 3. Headstart chapter or incubator — partner route

[Headstart's official site](https://www.headstart.in/) confirms its startup network and chapter structure. Several visible event listings are old. Find the current local organizer through the official site; **do not announce an event, free ticket or group access that you have not obtained**.

Offer to help founders check a deck's consistency, then show the brief-to-export workflow. **Copy #1** applies. Approach three organizers; model 0–1 accepted sessions and 0–5 trials. Count an accepted session only once an organizer confirms it. If a fee is requested, decline: your budget is zero.
