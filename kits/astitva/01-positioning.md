## Your one sentence

> Turn one startup brief into a matching brand and editable investor deck, then keep the next revision in the same style.

For your bio: **One brief. Matching brand and editable pitch deck.**

Sell the correction the founder can see: change the palette once, inspect the corresponding slides, then open the actual PowerPoint. Do not sell a higher chance of funding. A nicer deck does not repair a weak business or verify its market numbers.

## Your narrow offer this month

Offer a 20-minute **one-brief-to-two-slides clinic**. The founder supplies their real company description, picks their own theme, checks the generated story and exports the permitted output. You observe, do not become their unpaid designer. If they need a full deck, explain the credit purchase before they start it.

For the first two weeks, stay out of general logo-maker searches and agency procurement. Both create long comparison conversations around the wrong job. The sharpest evidence is one founder returning to correct a real deck before a meeting.

Your public [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/astitva) already reports activity; do not claim you have zero traction. A saved Clarity reading and some revenue do not reveal the count of qualifying businesses. Record that separately in `07`.
