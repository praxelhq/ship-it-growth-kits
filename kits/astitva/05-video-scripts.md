# Astitva — Video scripts

Record the actual product with your own example company. Use your phone mic and a screen recording; hard cuts and captions are enough. Hide personal tabs and real founder decks. Label samples and time jumps. Do not show the landing animation as though it were a recorded customer session.

## A — The editable-output demo · 65 seconds · LinkedIn, clinic, site

**First two seconds:** “Can you actually edit the deck?”

| Time | Shot / text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Open the exported file; text: “Real export” | “Can you actually edit the deck?” |
| 2–10 | Back to your own brief; text: “Example company” | “This is a sample startup, not a customer. I describe who it serves and what it does.” |
| 10–23 | Strategy and theme selection; show the exact controls | “The same brief carries into the strategy, logo and theme. I check the words before moving on.” |
| 23–36 | Generate the two free slides; label any cut | “The free account includes two designed slides. Here's the actual output, not the landing animation.” |
| 36–52 | Change a word, export, open the permitted format | “Now I change the sentence and inspect the exported slide. This is the part a founder has to keep working with.” |
| 52–65 | Show credit boundary and URL | “Brand work and two slides are free. More credits start at ₹299 plus GST. Check every business claim yourself. Astitva dot space.” |

If an export requires paid credits, state that on screen at the moment it happens; don't imply the free account includes a capability you could not test. Record the real wait, or label “generation time shortened.”

## B — The mismatched deck · 20 seconds · X, LinkedIn

**Hook:** “These slides look like three different companies.”

0–2s: three slides from your own deliberately inconsistent example; text “One company?” Say the hook. 2–8s: point at three fonts and three logo treatments: “This is the repair work I want to remove.” 8–15s: show the actual shared theme applied through Astitva: “One brief, one theme, then inspect the slides.” 15–20s: hold the two free-slide boundary and URL: “Try the brand and first two slides free. Astitva dot space.”

Do not name a real founder or use their deck to demonstrate a bad example.

## C — Free means two slides · 25 seconds · replies, clinic follow-up

**Hook:** “No, the whole deck isn't free.”

0–2s: the free-plan card; text “2 designed slides free.” 2–10s: show strategy, theme and finished logo: “Your 50 credits cover this brand work and the first two designed slides.” 10–18s: show the Starter card: “For more, the Starter pack is ₹299 plus GST. It's a one-time credit purchase.” 18–25s: show the output preview: “Check the result first. Buy only if the workflow helps your real presentation.”

This is the objection-answer video, not a scarcity offer. Keep current credit prices visible only while they match checkout.

## D — Better-looking is not investor-ready · 30 seconds · LinkedIn

**Hook:** “A beautiful wrong number is still wrong.”

0–2s: a fictional market-size slide with a visible “example” label. 2–12s: cross out an unsupported number: “Before you send an AI-assisted deck, check the market figure, source, dates and your actual traction.” 12–22s: replace it with a source note and the founder's verified input: “Astitva helps connect the story and design. You remain responsible for the facts.” 22–30s: show the editable slide: “Start by checking one slide, not by sending twelve unreviewed ones.”

Use invented example figures only when clearly labelled fictional. This clip can work without a sales link; reply to relevant questions with the product only when it helps.

## One recording session

Capture the blank brief, theme choice, free two-slide output, a real edit, actual credit balance and real export. Bank ten seconds of each. Test captions on a phone at normal brightness. A viewer must be able to read the changed sentence; zoom into that area instead of shrinking the whole desktop into a vertical frame.
