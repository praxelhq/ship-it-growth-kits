No current self-promotion permission in a named pet subreddit/private group was verified. Don't solicit users under a post about an animal's emergency. A useful record-organization reply can stand alone without a product link; medical questions go to the person's vet.

Twenty warm invitations, eight posts, one conditional session and uncertain directory discovery might form a **15–40 activated-organizer experiment**, with overlap removed. That's not an evidenced route to 1,000 accounts. A clinic/organizer may later distribute after actually inspecting it; no such access is already secured.

Keep owner records private and provider roles clear. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week. A map listing isn't a partner, and an emailed appointment request isn't attendance or a customer acquisition.
