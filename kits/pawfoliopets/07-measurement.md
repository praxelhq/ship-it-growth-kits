# Pawfoliopets — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 new consumer accounts in 30 days.** Report **activated owner organizers** alongside: a pet profile, a recorded care date and a record successfully retrieved. Real data entry is the owner's choice; synthetic tests must remain labelled tests and excluded from traction. A directory view or unaccepted appointment request is not the same activation.

The current [privacy policy](https://www.pawfoliopets.me/legal/privacy) limits optional analytics to public pages and excludes account/login/health/sharing/payment pages. Preserve that actual consent boundary; do not add [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) to private records because a generic checklist suggests it. Minimal internal events, if policy-consistent and genuinely measurable, can count `account_created`, `profile_created`, `record_saved`, `record_retrieved`, `care_date_recorded`, `return_record_use` without file content or health details.

## Arithmetic

At an assumed 20% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visits. If 60% complete the organizer job, that is 600 activations. Those are hypotheses, not benchmarks. Twenty warm invitations, eight posts, one conditional session and uncertain directory discovery form a **15–40 activated-organizer experiment**, not evidence of thousands of visitors. Remove people who appear across multiple channels from the total.

## Weekly decisions

|Day|Decision|
|---|---|
|7|Three fresh users must understand organizer versus medical advice and retrieve a synthetic record. Privacy failure or misleading care claim: fix before more invitations.|
|14|Fewer than five of ten willing testers complete the narrow profile/date/retrieval job: observe the exact step. Don't add more map listings to distract from onboarding.|
|21|Who returned without your reminder? Are household access/revocation controls understood? No repeat value: narrow to the actual retrieval moment.|
|30|Report accounts, activated organizers, repeat use, household members, actual accepted bookings, hours and target gap separately. No clinical benefit claimed from a reminder.|

These are experimental decisions, not veterinary standards. A record organizer can be useful without collecting more health detail or promising safety. Keep real owner/pet information private and publish only explicitly permitted, non-sensitive process stories.
