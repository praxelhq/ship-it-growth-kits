# Pawfoliopets — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from the public home/signup/login and current terms/privacy. “Create your account” goes to/signup; “I already have one” goes to/login. No account, health record, reminder or appointment was created. The current public feature claims are not an independently tested workflow.

## 1. Make the first job inspectable

**Current:** visible branding “Pawfolio” and “Every bit of your pet's care in one warm place,” then account creation. The rendered public extraction contains no semantic H1.

Add a real H1:

> **Your pet's record and next recorded care date, easy to find.**
>
> Start with a sample profile. Keep household records organized; confirm care questions with your vet.

Use **“Inspect a sample profile”** only after implementing it, with **“Create my account”** secondary. Keep the warm direction; don't imply an all-purpose clinical service before showing the narrow organizer.

## 2. Explain actual access and pricing

**Current:** signup exists, but no public software amount or full free allowance was readable.

Publish the actual price/limits before account creation. Don't add “free forever” without evidence. Helper: **“Read access, storage and sharing details before adding private records.”** The policy says original-record download is free; that doesn't establish every other feature is free.

## 3. Put the medical boundary beside reminders

The [terms](https://www.pawfoliopets.me/legal/terms) say reminders use recorded dates and missed dates prompt contacting a vet. Repeat **“Dates come from you or your provider. This app does not diagnose, prescribe or decide whether a delay is safe.”** Do not calculate a new medical schedule as a growth feature without appropriate real validation.

## 4. Distinguish directory/request/booking

The actual terms say listings use OpenStreetMap community data and an appointment request isn't a booking. Use **“Directory listing — confirm details with the provider”** and **“Request sent; not yet booked”** until acceptance. A listed opening time doesn't establish availability. Don't use clinic names/logos as customer proof.

## 5. Demonstrate actual household privacy controls

The privacy policy claims private health storage, short-lived household links, revocation, original download and deletion. Test with authorized accounts and synthetic records: another household must not see the file, a revoked link must stop working and deletion must remove access. Publish the implemented result accurately; don't call the policy itself a security audit.

## 6. Keep keepsakes separate from health

The policy says public keepsakes exclude health information and photo location metadata. Verify an actual synthetic export/share rather than assume. Proposed confirmation: **“Review what will be public before sharing.”** No automatic public health timeline or private location in marketing screenshots.

## 7. Phone, consent and preview

The policy says optional analytics run on public pages only and never on login/account/health/sharing/payment pages. Preserve that boundary; don't install a second script for the kit. Proposed preview: **“Pawfolio — pet-care records and household handover.”** Description: **“An organizer for recorded dates and files, not veterinary advice. Inspect access and privacy before adding records.”** Test real previews and phone record retrieval; no measured clipping/load defect was established.
