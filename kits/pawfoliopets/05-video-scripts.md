# Pawfoliopets — Video scripts

Fictional pet and records only. Don't show diagnoses, prescription instructions, owner identities, provider contacts or a private location. Record actual tested controls, narrate with a phone microphone and keep captions readable.

## A. Find the record, not another chat —60 seconds

**Where:** landing page and organizer introduction. **Hook:** “Where did we put that record?”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|A fictional record filename in a synthetic chat|Where did we put that record?|
|2–14 s|Actual tested sample profile|Pawfolio is an organizer, not a veterinary service.|
|14–30 s|Save/find a fictional file and recorded date|The date comes from the owner or provider; the app doesn't decide care.|
|30–43 s|Actual household access explanation|I want to know who can open it before sharing.|
|43–52 s|Retrieve original or revoke synthetic share, only if tested|This is the control I need to trust.|
|52–60 s|URL and actual access terms|Inspect a safe sample and privacy details at pawfoliopets.me.|

If controls aren't implemented/tested, show the issue honestly instead of a simulated live success.

## B. A reminder isn't a medical decision —20 seconds

**Where:** owned feed. **Hook:** “The app didn't prescribe this date.” Show a fictional “date recorded by owner/provider” label. Say, “A reminder helps us remember what was recorded. Questions about safety or care still go to the vet.” No species-specific intervals or treatment advice.

## C. Request isn't booked —25 seconds

**Where:** appointment explanation. **Hook:** “Sent doesn't mean accepted.” Show an actual or clearly labelled proposed request-pending state. Say, “A directory listing and opening time aren't an appointment. Wait for the provider's acceptance and confirm details.” No fake clinic endorsement or staged booking confirmation.

## D. Before a public keepsake —25 seconds

**Where:** sharing help. **Hook:** “Check what leaves the private profile.” Use a synthetic export to show actual public fields and exclusions. Say, “A photo keepsake should not carry private health details or location metadata.” Only demonstrate what you verified; do not expose a real pet/owner record to prove privacy.

Bank sample profile, record retrieval, recorded-date label, permission confirmation and public-share review. A pet's cute image should not hide a missing privacy or booking distinction.
