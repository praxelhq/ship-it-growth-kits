# Pawfoliopets — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.pawfoliopets.me/ · **Category:** Consumer pet-care organizer · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/pawfoliopets

Your first user is an adult pet owner who has one care date already supplied by a vet, a record they keep losing and another household member who sometimes helps. Start with the care record and owner-entered reminder, not an all-purpose pet marketplace.

The public page shows “Pawfolio” and “Every bit of your pet's care in one warm place,” with vets/groomers, records, reminders and photo keepsakes. “Create your account” links to `/signup`; “I already have one” links to `/login`. Signup is a real email/password form. No paid software amount is published in the checked public page; no free-forever plan is assumed. Account creation, records, reminders and provider requests weren't submitted by this audit.

## The pitch

> Keep your pet's records and the care dates your household records in one place. Know what needs checking, and contact your vet when a date needs clarification.

Bio version: **The record and next care date, ready when your household needs them.**

The wedge is retrieving one record and seeing one recorded due date without searching a family chat. It is not diagnosis, a vaccination schedule recommendation or a claim that a delay is safe. [Your actual terms](https://www.pawfoliopets.me/legal/terms) make that boundary clear; preserve it in marketing.

## Alternatives

A folder plus the calendar already on a phone costs₹0 in extra pet-organizer software. [11 pets](https://www.11pets.com/en/price) publishes Free and Premium plans; the checked page doesn't show a premium amount, so don't invent one. It already covers pet profiles, records/reminders and sharing. Pawfolio must earn the extra account with a clear narrow loop and understandable household access, not claim it invented digital pet records.

The public privacy policy describes private health storage, household signed links and owner controls. Those are the operator's claims, not an independent security audit. Demonstrate actual tested access/export/revocation on synthetic records before saying the privacy workflow is proven.

## The honest weakness

An OpenStreetMap listing is not an endorsed or available vet. The terms explicitly say an appointment request isn't a booking until a participating provider accepts it. Don't let the friendly map interface imply clinical quality, immediate appointments or a medical certificate.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-account target**, with a pet profile plus a recorded care date and retrieved record alongside. Pet photos alone, directory browsing and unaccepted appointment requests don't establish that the organizer is useful. Use synthetic data in initial tests and count those as tests, not real traction.
