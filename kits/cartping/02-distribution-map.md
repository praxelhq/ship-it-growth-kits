# CartPing — Distribution map

**Read this first:** you are a B2B tool for a buyer who is findable, nameable and reachable one at a time. That makes this the *least* community-driven kit in wave 1 and the most outbound-driven. Reddit will not get you ten paying stores. **Direct outreach to named Shopify stores will.** Weight your month accordingly.

Reach numbers are realistic expected impressions or replies, not audience sizes.

---

## Tier 1 — the one that actually matters: targeted outreach

### Build the list first (day 1–2)

Indian Shopify stores are enumerable. Free ways to build a list of 2,000+ in two days:

| Source | How | Yield |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BuiltWith free trends / Shopify India list** | Free tier gives samples; the paid export is out of budget but the free lists are a start | 200–500 |
| **myip.ms reverse-IP on Shopify's IP ranges** | Free, crude, gives raw domains | 1,000+ |
| **Instagram hashtag mining** | `#shopsmallindia` `#indianbrand` `#smallbusinessindia` `#d2cindia` `#handmadeinindia` — the bio link is usually the Shopify store | 500–2,000, highest quality |
| **Shopify-powered store directories** (storeleads free tier, Shopify "Shop" app browse) | Free tier | 200–500 |
| **Instagram "similar accounts" from any one small D2C brand** | Instagram's own suggestions are a Shopify-store-finding machine | unlimited |

**Qualify each one:** does the site run Shopify (view source → `cdn.shopify.com`), does it have a WhatsApp chat button or a phone number in the footer, does it have fewer than ~20k Instagram followers? If yes to all three, it is your buyer.

### Instagram DM — your single highest-yield channel

Small Indian D2C brands read their Instagram DMs. Their founder usually *is* the account.

- **Volume:** 20–30 per day, by hand, personalised. Not more — Instagram throttles and blocks bulk DMs fast.
- **Realistic:** 25 DMs/day × 25 days = ~600 DMs → 15–25% reply rate → **90–150 conversations → 30–60 signups.**
- Rules: follow the account first, like 2 posts, wait an hour, then DM. Never send a link in the first message. Never copy-paste the same first line.
- Copy pack #7.

### Cold email to the store's support address

Every Shopify store has `hello@` / `support@` / `orders@` in the footer.

- **Volume:** 40–60/day from your own domain (warm up first, use a signature, plain text).
- **Realistic:** 1,200 emails over the month → 4–8% reply → **50–100 conversations → 20–40 signups.**
- Rules: one email, one follow-up seven days later, then stop. Include an unsubscribe line. Do not use a bulk sender for the first 500.

Note how little of this is Reddit. That is deliberate and correct for this buyer.

## Tier 3 — partnerships (highest leverage, slowest)

One agency with 30 Shopify clients is worth 600 cold DMs.

| Partner type | How to find | The offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify development agencies in India** | Shopify Partners directory, LinkedIn "Shopify agency India", Instagram | Free forever for their own use + revenue share or just goodwill. Ask them to mention it to 5 clients. |
| **D2C enablement consultants** on LinkedIn | They post constantly about D2C metrics | Give them the data from your users (anonymised) for their content; they mention you |
| **Courier / packaging / photography vendors to small D2C** | Instagram | They already have the relationship; a referral costs them nothing |
| **Shopify theme developers** | Same | — |

Realistic: **5–15 partner conversations, 2–4 that produce anything, 20–80 signups.** Worth the week-3 slot.

## Tier 4 — the compounding asset: get into the Shopify App Store

Not a 30-day channel. Start it on day 3 anyway.

- Shopify App Store search is where every Shopify merchant looks for every Shopify tool.
- "Abandoned cart" is one of the most-searched terms in it.
- Approval takes weeks and requires a real app listing, a privacy policy, and an embedded install flow — which you deliberately avoided. **Build the installable version as a second path, keep the no-install path as the headline.**
- Also list on: **Shopify Community Apps**, **G2**, **Capterra**, **SaaSHub**, **AlternativeTo** (as an alternative to Recart, Wati, Interakt, BiteSpeed). Those take an afternoon and send traffic for years.

## Tier 5 — content that ranks

Your buyer googles problems, not products. Write 4 posts in month 1:

1. *"How much are abandoned carts actually costing your Shopify store? (with the math for Indian stores)"*
2. *"WhatsApp vs email for cart recovery in India: what actually gets opened"*
3. *"What to say in a cart-recovery WhatsApp message (9 messages that worked)"* — give the templates away free; this is your best lead magnet
4. *"Recart vs Wati vs Interakt vs doing it yourself: what a 50-order-a-month store should actually use"* — comparison pages convert best and rank fastest

Publish on your own blog, cross-post to LinkedIn and Medium. Realistic month-1 traffic: small. Month 4: meaningful.

---

## Channels to skip

- **Product Hunt / Hacker News.** Wrong audience entirely. A Shopify store owner in Jaipur is not on HN. Do Peerlist (Indian builder audience, free, 1,000–5,000 views) and skip the rest — or do PH only for the builder-credibility, not for users.
- **Twitter/X.** Indian D2C founders are on LinkedIn and Instagram, not X.
- **TikTok.** No.

**Your board target is 10 active stores, not a four-figure signup count** ([`TARGET-POLICY.md`](../../TARGET-POLICY.md)) — which is the right shape of number for a tool with a 2,000-store addressable list you build by hand.
