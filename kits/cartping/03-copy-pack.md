## 6. Reddit — r/shopify *(comment on an existing thread only, never a post)*

Search "abandoned cart" sorted by New, daily. When someone asks what to use:

> Depends a lot on your volume. Honest breakdown:
>
> - **Under ~50 orders/month:** don't buy anything. Shopify's built-in abandoned checkout email is free, and then just message people yourself. At your volume, manual beats automated — people reply to a human.
> - **50–300/month:** this is the painful middle. The tools (Recart, Wati, Interakt, BiteSpeed) are built for bigger stores and priced like it. Manual still works better but you need something to tell you *when*, because the window is short.
> - **300+/month:** now the automation is worth the money.
>
> Two things that matter more than the tool, whatever you pick:
> 1. **Speed.** A follow-up at 30 minutes converts several times better than one at 24 hours. Same message.
> 2. **Channel.** In India, a WhatsApp message from the owner's own number gets read. An email doesn't and a branded WhatsApp API blast gets blocked.
>
> (I build something in the 50–300 bracket so take that with the appropriate salt, but the above is true regardless of what you use.)

## 11. The message your *users* should send to their customers

Include this in your onboarding — it makes the product work better, which makes it retain. It is also your best content asset (give it away as a free download; see `02` Tier 5).

> **Good (for a ₹1,000–3,000 cart, sent 15–30 min after):**
> *"Hi Priya! Saw you were looking at the linen shirt on [brand] — did something go wrong at checkout? Happy to help if the payment didn't go through. — Ananya"*
>
> **Good (for a high-value cart):**
> *"Hi Rahul, this is Ananya from [brand]. I noticed your order didn't complete — just checking the size was right? The [product] runs slightly large, happy to swap it before you order."*
>
> **Bad (do not send):**
> *"Hi! You left items in your cart 🛒 Complete your purchase now and get 10% OFF! Use code BACK10. Hurry, offer expires in 2 hours!"*
>
> Rules: use their name, name the product, offer help not a discount, sign off with a human name, and never send more than two messages.
