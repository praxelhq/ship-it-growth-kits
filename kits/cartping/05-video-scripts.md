# CartPing — Video scripts + shot lists

Scripts only; you record them. Your audience watches on a phone, muted, from a DM or a LinkedIn feed. **Vertical, captioned, under 60 seconds** is the default for this product — not a polished SaaS demo reel.

---

## Video A — The hook (0:20, vertical)

**Use:** Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, the second message of an Instagram DM, Facebook groups.
**Goal:** a store owner feels the loss.

### Script

| Time | On screen | Voice / caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Shopify admin, real store. You click Analytics → Reports → **Abandoned checkouts** | **"Open your Shopify. Analytics. Reports. Abandoned checkouts."** |
| 0:04–0:09 | The number appears. Hold on it. Don't talk over it. | *(silence — let them read it)* |
| 0:09–0:14 | Zoom on the figure | **"That's money that got all the way to your checkout and left."** |
| 0:14–0:18 | Cut to a phone: a WhatsApp alert arrives — name, number, ₹2,499, "abandoned 5 minutes ago" | **"This is what it looks like when you find out in five minutes instead of five days."** |
| 0:18–0:20 | Hold on `cartping.online` | **"Free. Five-minute setup."** |

### Shot list
1. Record the Shopify admin on a **real store** — yours or a friend's who consents. Blur the store name if needed, never the number. The realness is the whole video.
2. Record the WhatsApp alert on a **real phone**, screen-recorded, with the real notification sound.
3. Shoot vertical natively for this one — it's all phone-shaped content.
4. Captions burned in, large. This will be watched muted in a feed.
5. No music, or a very quiet bed. The silence on the number is the strongest two seconds.

## Video B — The demo (1:15, vertical or square)

**Use:** landing page, Peerlist, Product Hunt, the DM follow-up, your LinkedIn.
**Goal:** a store owner understands the entire loop and believes setup is genuinely five minutes.

### Script

**0:00–0:10 — The moment**
> "A customer adds two things, starts checkout, types their phone number — and leaves."

*Shot: screen recording of your own storefront, real checkout flow, abandon at the payment step. Do it for real.*

**0:10–0:25 — The alert**
> "Five minutes later, this arrives on my WhatsApp. Her name. Her number. What was in the cart. What it was worth."

*Shot: phone, real notification, you open it. Hold on the message long enough to read every line — about 6 seconds. This is the product.*

**0:25–0:45 — The recovery**
> "I tap the recovery page. I can call her, or send her a message. From my own number — she sees me, not a brand-name bot. I'm not going to offer her a discount, I'm just going to ask if something went wrong."

*Shot: tap through to the recovery page, type a real message, send it. Show the sent message in a real WhatsApp thread.*

**0:45–1:00 — The result**
> "And then I see whether it worked. Here's last week: ₹46,000 abandoned, ₹18,000 recovered."

*Shot: the dashboard. **Use real numbers if you have them.** If not, say "here's what the dashboard shows" and label it an example on screen — see `04` Fix 2.*

**1:00–1:15 — The objection, handled**
> "No app installed on your store. Nothing touching your theme. I never message your customers — you do. And setup is about five minutes."

*Shot: the setup flow, sped up 3x, with a timer counting in the corner. **The timer is the point.** If it genuinely takes five minutes, prove it on camera, unedited if you can bear it.*

### Shot list
- Use a real Shopify store end to end. A fake storefront is visible to anyone who runs one.
- Real phone, real notification, real WhatsApp. No mockup animations.
- Record the setup once, unedited, with a visible clock. That raw clip is worth more than the polished cut.
- Your own voice, Indian English, no script-reading cadence. Speak like you're explaining it to a friend who runs a shop.
- Hindi/Hinglish version: record it twice. For Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities the Hinglish cut will outperform the English one.

## Video C — "What to actually say" (0:45, vertical)

**Use:** the share-worthy one. Reels, LinkedIn, Facebook groups. Gives value with the product almost absent.
**Goal:** get shared by store owners who don't even sign up, because it's useful.

### Script

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Text on screen: *"Someone abandoned a cart. What do you send?"* | "Most stores get this wrong in the same way." |
| 0:05–0:15 | Show the bad message: *"Hi! You left items in your cart 🛒 Complete now for 10% OFF! Hurry!"* with a red X | "This. Don't send this. You've trained them to abandon carts to get a discount, and you've told them you're a bot." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Show the good one, typed live: *"Hi Priya! Saw you were looking at the linen shirt — did something go wrong at checkout? Happy to help if the payment didn't go through. — Ananya"* | "Send this instead. Their name. The actual product. Offer help, not money. Sign it with your name." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Text: **"Within 30 minutes."** | "And send it within thirty minutes. Same message at 30 minutes versus 2 days is a completely different conversion rate — they still have the phone in their hand." |
| 0:40–0:45 | Small end card | "I build a tool that tells you when. But the message matters more than the tool. Steal it." |

### Shot list
1. Type the messages live in a real WhatsApp window. Typing reads as real; a graphic reads as an ad.
2. Big text, high contrast, burned-in captions.
3. Post natively to each platform (upload the file; don't link it). Native video gets 5–10x the reach of a link.
4. In Facebook groups, post this with no link at all. Let people ask.

## Reusable clips to bank in one session

- Shopify Analytics → Abandoned checkouts, number revealed (10s)
- Real abandon on a real storefront (15s)
- WhatsApp alert arriving on a phone, notification sound (8s)
- The recovery page, tap to message (10s)
- Setup flow with a visible timer (5 min raw, cut to 20s)
- Dashboard with real numbers (8s)

Every post this month can be built from these six.
