# CartPing — 30-day execution calendar

This calendar is **outbound-heavy by design.** Your buyer is nameable, so the work is finding and messaging them, not broadcasting. The daily DM/email quota is the backbone — everything else is secondary.

**Every single weekday, no exceptions:**
- 25 Instagram DMs (personalised, 1 hour)
- 50 cold emails (30 min once the list exists)
- Reply to every reply within 2 hours

That's ~90 minutes/day and it is 60% of your results.

## The two things that will decide your month

1. **Did you hit the DM/email quota every day?** 600 DMs and 1,200 emails is the floor. Miss a week and the month fails; no post makes up for it.
2. **Did the Shopify App Store submission go in during week 1?** It won't help this month. It is the difference between month 3 being hard and month 3 being easy.

---

## If something hits

If a Facebook group post or LinkedIn post takes off, **stop the calendar and work the comments for 48 hours.** Every comment on a D2C founder post is a lead with a name attached. Reply to all of them, then DM everyone who engaged.
