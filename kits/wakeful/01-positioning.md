# WakeFul — Positioning

**Live:** https://wakeful.online/ · **Category:** Short browser-game break · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/wakeful

## Keep the ending in the promise

Actual Chrome rendering on 29 October 2026 showed **“Can’t get your brain to switch on?”**, followed by **“Give us five minutes. Then get back to work.”** Clicking **“Start WakeFul”** opened **“Sign up to play”** with an email/code form. No email was submitted, account created, full run completed or purchase tested. The ten-second Pop Field preview is a separate action, not proof that the five-round account flow works.

The offer is three free runs, then prepaid packs without subscription or expiry. The FAQ lists India packs **5 runs ₹99, 12 ₹199, 30 ₹449**; US packs **10 $4.99, 30 $11.99**. This browser's hero showed dollars, so verify region/currency before sharing Indian prices. Five rounds each take a minute and start on a click; pauses mean the whole visit may exceed five minutes.

Start with an adult desk worker who wants a bounded playful transition before an ordinary task. Pop Field, Memory Sequence, Visual Scan, Rule Switch and Final Sprint are the five described rounds. Your difference is the end: no endless levels, ads or leaderboard chase. Do not market it as treatment for fatigue/ADHD, an intelligence test or proven productivity improvement. The page explicitly says it does not claim the game makes people sharper; it is testing whether the warm-up helps.

**Pitch:** Five one-minute mini-game rounds, then return to the task you chose.

**Bio:** A short game warm-up that ends. Three runs free.

[Human Benchmark](https://humanbenchmark.com/) is a real short-test alternative; [Lumosity](https://www.lumosity.com/en/) has a free training entry. No current paid competitor rate was established, so do not invent one or borrow their research as evidence for WakeFul. A no-tool break is also an option. WakeFul must earn use through a pleasant finite loop, not claims that rest or another game is inferior.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, counting new verified-email accounts, with first finished run and voluntary return-to-task feedback beside it. Privacy text says accounts hold email/run allowance, anonymous run scores/ratings can be sent with an opt-out, and Clarity stays off until consent. The actual consent panel was visible. Verify each behaviour before claiming it is a completed privacy audit.
