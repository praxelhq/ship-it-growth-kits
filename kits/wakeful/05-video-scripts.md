# WakeFul — Video scripts

Record your own actual game after testing the full run. Use no personal email, private ratings or invented before/after outcome. A short preview is usable only when labelled as the preview.

## A. Built to end — 35 seconds, homepage/session invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | End screen or clear five-round list. | “A game break with an ending.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual mini-game snippets; label cuts. | “WakeFul has five one-minute rounds. Each starts when you choose, and sound stays off unless you want it.” |
| 14–25 s | Actual finish after a tested run. | “Finish the run, then return to the task you meant to do. There are no endless levels.” |
| 25–31 s | Honest-limit text. | “It is a game, not a brain test.” |
| 31–35 s | URL/free allowance. | “Three free runs after signup.” |

## B. Mint, not coral — 15 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Actual Pop Field preview. | “Mint bubbles. Leave coral.” |
| 2–10 s | Your real preview taps. | “This is the ten-second preview, not the full run.” |
| 10–15 s | URL. | “Try WakeFul and see how it feels.” |

## C. Close the tab — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual completed run. | “Now close the game.” |
| 3–14 s | Put phone down; show ordinary harmless task. | “The ending matters. A warm-up should leave room for the thing you wanted to start.” |
| 14–20 s | URL and no-efficacy label. | “WakeFul is testing that idea, not promising a focus boost.” |

Hold A/C if no full run has completed. Do not use an altered alertness score or claim a filmed task proves improved productivity.
