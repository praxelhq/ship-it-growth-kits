# WakeFul — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one new verified-email account, excluding tests and duplicate logins. Clicking Start or asking for an email code is not a completed signup. **Activation:** one completed five-round run. Report first-run completions, voluntary return play and verified paid packs beside signups. A preview play is a different event.

## Baseline and data

Record actual accounts, code-delivery/login failures, first-run starts/completions, exits/resumes, returns, pack purchases and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/wakeful. Public research did not establish private totals; unknown is not zero. The page describes anonymous run scores/ratings with opt-out and consent-first Clarity. Inspect those existing controls rather than adding a tracker. Keep email, private ratings and account/checkout content out of replay and public reports.

| Checkpoint | Scheduled denominator | Diagnostic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | 5 invitations, 1 clip, 1 post. | Can people receive a code, play all rounds and stop/exit clearly? |
| Day 14 | 10 invitations, 2 clips, 2 posts. | Replace 02 guesses with observed account/full-run conversion. |
| Day 21 | 15 invitations, 3 clips, 3 posts; real accepted-event attendance. | Check returns and limit/consent confusion. |
| Day 28 | 20 invitations, 4 clips, 3 posts. | Deduplicate accounts, distinguish preview/full runs and paid packs. |
| Day 30 | 4 maker posts total, actual listing/hours/outcomes. | Show target gap and complete runs beside signups. |

The plan assumes 28–65 distinct accounts, below 1,000. Self-rated alertness before/after is subjective optional feedback, not causal evidence that WakeFul improves focus, intelligence or productivity. Report no medical or cognitive outcome claim from these growth observations.
