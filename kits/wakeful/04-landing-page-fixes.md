# WakeFul — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Keep the concrete run beside the broad question

Actual headline **“Can’t get your brain to switch on?”**, supporting line **“Give us five minutes. Then get back to work.”**, CTA **“Start WakeFul”**. It opened the actual email-code signup screen; no code was requested. Add **“Five one-minute mini-game rounds. Three free runs after email signup.”** beside that action. Keep the separate **“Play 10 seconds”** preview clearly marked as a preview.

Test signup→one complete run→resume/exit before broad promotion. A homepage preview is not a full-run test. Do not promise exactly five elapsed minutes when rounds wait for individual clicks.

## 2. Clarify region and pack prices

This browser's hero showed packs from $4.99; FAQ describes India ₹99/₹199/₹449 packs and US $4.99/$11.99 packs. Display the actual chosen currency and run count before checkout: **“5 runs — ₹99. No subscription; runs do not expire.”** only on the verified Indian offer. Do not silently convert dollars or imply one run price applies everywhere.

## 3. Keep the evidence boundary near scores

Existing honesty text says **“Your score describes one run. It isn’t a measure of intelligence”** and does not claim sharper thinking. Preserve it beside the results: **“A game score, not a cognitive assessment.”** Ask optional feedback about the experience without presenting before/after ratings as causal proof of effectiveness.

## 4. Make data choices operational

The consent panel was visible and could be declined. Verify that declining actually prevents recording. Show results-sharing opt-out before/after play and link real view/download/delete controls. Do not make recordings a condition of the free trial or share an individual's alertness rating in marketing.

## 5. Check phone and stopping point

Test touch targets, colour/shape instructions, mute, pause/resume and no-extra-run consumption after interruption according to the current offer. End with **“Your run is complete. Return to your task.”** rather than a manipulative endless replay prompt. Existing Clarity is opt-in by the page's description; inspect before adding a second tag. No fabricated study or testimonial is needed.
