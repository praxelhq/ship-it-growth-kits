# StockIQ — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from the live homepage and one public sample. “Try StockIQ free” scrolls to analysis; “Sign in to analyse” opens email-link login. New generation, authentication, payment and course purchase weren't performed. Findings below concern the displayed report claims, not the investment merits of a company.

## 1. Make coverage control the verdict

**Current:** the inspected public sample says “FINANCIALLY SOUND” with two passes and six checks not assessed; “FORENSIC CLEAN” appears with twelve data gaps.

Replace broad clean/sound labels in such cases with **“Incomplete assessment — key evidence missing.”** Show **“Assessed:2/8. Unassessed:6/8”** beside the summary and name the missing categories. A missing check cannot silently become a pass. Fix the actual conclusion logic and sample, not only add a disclaimer under the same confident verdict. Verify across all public samples before promotion.

## 2. Lead with source review

**Current:** “Check a stock with a checklist, not tips.”

Try:

> **Read the evidence behind the company checklist.**
>
> See dated sources, assessment coverage, assumptions and open questions. Use the report to guide your own reading, not as a buy/sell instruction.

Keep the process-focused direction. Don't replace the headline with a promise of finding winners or avoiding every bad stock.

## 3. Make sample review the first action

**Current:** “Start learning” goes to the course, “Try StockIQ free” to analysis, and public samples are lower down.

Use **“Inspect a sample and its sources”** first, with **“Two reports with a free account;₹99 per additional new report.”** Keep course purchase separate and show its actual platform price only after checking it. A new visitor should understand what is free before entering an email.

## 4. Separate facts, guidance and assumptions

For each material number show source, period/date and whether it is historical, management guidance or a model assumption. Use **“Scenario, not forecast”** for sensitivity calculations. Don't let a calculated ratio's precision imply that the growth input is certain. A source link alone isn't proof the extraction or arithmetic is correct; provide a correction path and test it.

## 5. Make report status honest during failure

Test a missing source, extraction failure and unsupported company on authorized test inputs. Proposed copy: **“This section couldn't be assessed from available evidence. No conclusion is drawn.”** Keep the original source accessible. Verify failed-generation allowance/payment behaviour before claiming refunds or free retries. This audit didn't execute new generation or diagnose rendering blanks as actual missing data.

## 6. Remove implied third-party endorsement

If a framework references an investor, research firm or educator, distinguish your interpretation from that organization's own service or approval. Use your own scope explanation and attribution accurately; don't add certification logos. Keep claims about a named company limited to substantiated source statements and explicit uncertainty.

## 7. Mobile and preview

Proposed title: **“StockIQ — a company checklist with sources and gaps visible.”** Description: **“Inspect a public sample. Two free-account reports,₹99 per additional new report. Education, not investment advice.”** Test actual previews, long source tables and coverage labels on phones. No measured mobile/load defect was established; don't hide unknowns in a tiny accordion to improve appearance.
