# StockIQ — Video scripts

Use public samples and primary sources with the report date visible. No personal portfolio, broker balance, price target, simulated profit or buying instruction. Record actual corrected behaviour, with phone narration and readable captions.

## A. The check that wasn't assessed —65 seconds

**Where:** homepage, Peerlist and source-literacy session. **Hook:** “Missing evidence isn't a pass.”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|A clearly labelled unassessed sample check|Missing evidence isn't a pass.|
|2–14 s|Actual corrected report coverage|Before reading the color, I want to know how much was assessed.|
|14–30 s|Open one real source and its date|This is the source. This is the period. Now I can check whether the summary follows.|
|30–43 s|Identify an assumption versus a fact|A scenario input isn't a promise about the future.|
|43–55 s|Show a remaining gap and correction route|If the evidence isn't there, the report should say so.|
|55–65 s|Sample URL and pricing|Inspect samples at finlite.app. Two free-account reports, then₹99 per new report. This is education, not a stock call.|

If coverage logic isn't corrected, show that as an issue being fixed rather than a polished successful product claim.

## B. Fact, guidance, assumption —25 seconds

**Where:** LinkedIn. **Hook:** “These three lines don't mean the same thing.” Show generic labelled examples, not a current investment forecast. Say, “What happened, what management expects and what a model assumes need different labels.” End with a real source-date view and founder disclosure.

## C. A precise ratio can have an uncertain input —25 seconds

**Where:** teaching session and owned feed. **Hook:** “Two decimals don't make the assumption true.” Use a fictional sensitivity calculation and change one input. Say, “The arithmetic may be correct while the premise remains uncertain.” No real-company price or buy/sell conclusion is needed.

## D. The free sample test — 30 seconds

**Where:** launch gallery. **Hook:** “Don't create an account before inspecting this.” Open the actual public sample without login, show coverage, source, date and a gap. Say, “Can you explain what it assessed and what it didn't? That's the useful test.” End with the actual free/paid report allowance, keeping the separate course out of the demo.

Bank corrected coverage summary, source opening, date, assumption label and gap. Avoid animations of a rising stock chart that suggest product-generated returns.
