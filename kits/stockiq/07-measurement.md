# StockIQ — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 new consumer accounts in 30 days.** Report **completed source-review activations** alongside: a person opens/reviews a report, inspects at least one cited source and identifies an assumption or unresolved check. New report generation is a separate event. Public sample readers, course buyers and account holders can overlap; don't add them as separate users.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another script. Mask account inputs and don't collect holdings, broker details or private research notes. Minimal events can include `sample_opened`, `account_created`, `report_requested`, `report_succeeded`, `report_failed`, `source_opened`, `coverage_opened`, `return_source_review`. Don't claim a source-open event measures comprehension; use a small voluntary question for that.

## Arithmetic and quality

At an assumed 20% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visits. If half complete a source review, that is 500 activations. Those are hypotheses, not benchmarks. Twenty warm invitations, eight posts, one conditional session and directory discovery form a **10–30 first-review experiment**, not an evidenced route to thousands of visitors.

Track report coverage separately: which checks have adequate evidence, which are unassessed and which summaries need correction. A zero-flag output with missing data isn't validated clean. Don't advertise a model-generated accuracy or return percentage without a genuine evaluation.

## Weekly decisions

| Day | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | Three fresh readers must distinguish fact, assumption and missing check. Any broad clean/sound verdict unsupported by coverage: correct before more report promotion. |
| 14 | Fewer than five of ten willing learners complete source review: inspect the confusing step. Don't replace education with a more exciting stock claim. |
| 21 | Who voluntarily returns for another source or checklist lesson? No repeat value: narrow the learning job before adding report length/features. |
| 30 | Report accounts, learning activations, report generations, repeat use, report/course purchases/refunds, hours and target gap separately. No claimed trading outcome. |

These are experimental decision thresholds, not investment standards. Publish only substantiated process outcomes and keep individual financial decisions private. A user recognizing insufficient evidence and declining to act is a valuable learning result.
