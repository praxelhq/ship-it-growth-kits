# Clockwise — Distribution map

**Trust-led campus referrals first.** A candidate is buying your attention. A broad product launch cannot create more coaching hours.

Forecasts are planning assumptions. The calendar schedules two permitted group messages, two LinkedIn teaching posts, two short clips, ten private introductions and one optional workshop. No purchased lists or paid distribution.

| Route | What you do and permission rule | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing placement/alumni WhatsApp groups | Ask two admins for one free bullet clinic invitation; no collecting group member numbers. Name the actual coach and current available slots on your own site, not in this public kit. | 2 × 40 relevant readers × 10–25% registration = 8–20 leads. | #1 |
| Your LinkedIn | Two posts at 12:30 IST showing how a question turns a vague task into a truthful bullet. No fabricated revenue metrics. | 2 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 20% registration = 1–12 leads. | #2 |
| Warm referral conversations | Ten people who know your work and have asked about job search. Ask for an introduction, not a contact export. | 10 × 20–50% willing introduction = 2–5 conversations, not guaranteed bookings. | #3 |
| One campus career club | Email the publicly listed organizer or use your existing campus contact; offer one 30-minute group clinic, with no paid upsell requirement. | 10–25 attendees if accepted; 2–8 diagnostic registrations assumed. Zero if no invitation. | #4 |
| Two own-account Reels | Short fictional resume-edit demonstration; caption discloses coaching is paid. | 2 × 300–1,500 views × 0.5–2% visits × 20% registration = 0–12 leads. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Use its free proof-of-work profile as your own coaching portfolio. Do not pretend the service is software for Launchpad. | Zero forecast; credibility page rather than acquisition bet. | #6 |

Your low-case total is dozens, not 1,000. At 20% page-to-registration, the target needs 5,000 qualified visits. Seek larger club distribution only after proving the free registration can be served honestly. Do not offer 1,000 calls to hit the number.

No Reddit or newsletter is assumed: no suitable posting permission or free editorial placement was verified. The relevant specific accounts are the placement committee and career-club accounts you already know; check their official university directory before contact. Your outreach list stays private. No unsolicited tagging of employers from the logo section.
