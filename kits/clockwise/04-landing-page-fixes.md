# Clockwise — Landing page fixes

Live [Anvaya page](https://anvayacareers.in/), checked 29 October 2026. Payment routes exist; successful payment and Calendly booking were not tested.

## 1. Explain the brand mismatch

The course project is Clockwise; the page presents Anvaya. Add a temporary line: **“Looking for Clockwise from Ship It? You're in the right place. Our live service is Anvaya.”** Correct the directory name when authorized. Do not make the student explain the discrepancy in every DM.

## 2. Make the offer behind the CTA specific

Current headline: **“Your resume should not be the reason you get rejected.”** Current CTA: **“Book a Session.”**

Suggested headline: **“Build a resume you can defend, live with a coach.”**

Support: **“Three 45-minute sessions. Your own experience, extracted on a shared sheet and written together. Complete Resume Build: ₹2,250.”**

Primary CTA: **“See the ₹2,250 resume plan”**. Secondary: **“Not sure? Request a free 15-minute diagnostic.”** Don't imply the paid session is free.

## 3. Test the complete booking path

The page routes to `anvaya-payment.html`, says payment leads to Calendly, and lists non-refund/reschedule terms. In your own test environment, check plan selection, amount, receipt, booking availability and failed-payment recovery. A real user must not pay and discover no available times. Add: **“See available start dates before paying. After payment, book Session 1; we email the same booking link.”** Publish this only once true.

## 4. Replace unsupported authority shorthand

The page displays “USABiz Insider approved” and employer logos. Provide the exact publication/reference and distinguish an interview call from an offer. If you cannot substantiate a claim, remove it. Replacement: **“Candidate outcomes vary. These are interview opportunities reported by participants, not employer partnerships or guaranteed results.”** Only use that wording if the records support it.

## 5. Label the shared-sheet sample

Current process is unusually concrete: **“Built live, on a shared Excel sheet.”** Keep it. Put one readable anonymized or fictional before/interview-question/after example above the plan cards. Caption: **“Illustrative example. Every paid resume uses the candidate's own facts.”** Do not paste private CV data into screenshots.

## 6. Publish capacity, not artificial scarcity

Current closing says **“Limited slots.”** Replace with the real next start date and number of weekly sessions you can deliver. A diagnostic signup should confirm a response window, not imply an immediate appointment. Copy: **“We'll confirm whether a session fits your goal and current availability before asking you to book.”**

## 7. Check the phone handoff and preview

At 390 px, read both plan prices, refund/reschedule terms and the free diagnostic without hover. Test one screen reader/keyboard route through the form. Suggested preview: **“Anvaya — live resume coaching from your own experience”**. No job guarantee in title, description or screenshot. These are required checks, not observed mobile defects.
