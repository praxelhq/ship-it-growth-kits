# Clockwise — Video scripts

Use a fictional internship. No student CV, employer confidential data or testimonial without consent. Record the shared-sheet method with readable text and your phone voice; no paid edit tools needed.

## A. Demo — 60 seconds, LinkedIn and site

0–2 s, screen shows “Helped with marketing”: **“Don't replace this with a made-up metric.”**

2–15 s, shared sheet with columns task/tool/audience/evidence: “First we ask what you personally did, with what, and who used it.”

15–35 s, fill an explicitly fictional example: five competitor pages compared, pricing table used by a project team. “If the evidence is scope, use scope. Don't claim revenue you didn't measure.”

35–48 s, write: “Compared five competitors' pricing and summarized differences for the project team's launch discussion.” “This is a line the candidate can explain.”

48–60 s, show the actual plan page: “That's the live Anvaya process. Three 45-minute resume sessions, ₹2,250. Check whether it fits before booking.” End URL and **Fictional example · no job guarantee**.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2 s, two bullets: vague task versus fabricated revenue. “Both can let you down.”

2–10 s, highlight a third bullet with truthful scope. “Ask one more question. Use the work you can prove.”

10–15 s, show sheet and URL. “Build the line together. Anvaya.” Text: **Live coaching · paid plans on site**.

## C. Hook — 20 seconds, campus Story

0–2 s, cursor over “responsible for”: “Can you defend this line in an interview?”

2–12 s, three prompts: What did you do? Who used it? What evidence exists? Read each aloud.

12–20 s, one sentence rewritten without inventing a number. “Bring one anonymized bullet to the free clinic. Keep your full CV private.” Only announce a clinic after an organizer accepts.

## D. Explainer — 25 seconds, diagnostic page

0–2 s: “A free diagnostic isn't a free resume rewrite.”

2–15 s, show service comparison: “We use 15 minutes to understand your situation and decide which next step fits. The paid rebuild has three live sessions.”

15–25 s, price and booking terms. “Read the scope, available dates and reschedule policy before paying.” This clip prevents mismatched expectations and protects your delivery hours.
