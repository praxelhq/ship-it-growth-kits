## #2 — LinkedIn teaching post

Before rewriting a resume bullet, ask a better question.

“Supported customer research” could mean taking notes in one call or analyzing a month of interviews. The sentence doesn't tell us.

I ask: What did you personally do? Who used the work? What changed? Which part can you prove?

Sometimes there is no revenue number. That's fine. A truthful scope is stronger than a made-up percentage.

That's the live process behind Anvaya: a shared sheet, specific follow-ups, and a resume built together. Plans and the free diagnostic are at https://anvayacareers.in/
