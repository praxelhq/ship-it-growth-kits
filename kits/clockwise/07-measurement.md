# Clockwise — Measurement

**Policy target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Define a signup as one real person's confirmed opt-in diagnostic/service registration. Report calls attended and completed paid resumes alongside it. A form lead is not a coached candidate, and a purchase is not a placement.

Private totals were not available for this kit. The page's public outcome claims are not a substitute for your Day-0 records.

| Day-0 baseline | Source / method |
| --- | --- |
| Existing confirmed registrations | Form/booking records, deduplicated |
| Calls attended / last 7 days | Your calendar, excluding tests |
| Paid clients and completed resume plans | Payment plus delivery records; refunds separate |
| Next available session and committed hours | Calendar; include preparation and revisions |
| Seven-day traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics or [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Current course metric/rank and timestamp | [Clockwise profile](https://shipped.money/p/clockwise); unknown until you read it |

Check Clarity is already present before adding it; mask all CV, contact, payment and questionnaire fields. Track aggregate `registration_confirmed`, `diagnostic_booked`, `diagnostic_attended`, `plan_purchased`, `first_session_completed` and `resume_delivered`. Keep personal candidate notes private.

## Capacity beats a misleading count

At 15 minutes each, 1,000 diagnostics require 250 hours, with no preparation. At three 45-minute sessions, ten resume clients require 22.5 live hours plus preparation. Publish a bounded number of real slots. Your free material may reach 1,000 registrants; it cannot imply 1,000 live calls this month.

The scheduled channel tests in `02` forecast roughly tens of leads, not 1,000. At 20% visit-to-registration, 1,000 requires 5,000 qualified visits. By Day 14, use the actual rate and remaining service capacity before requesting a larger club distribution.

| Checkpoint | What you report | What you change if behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Group invitations, registrations, calls attended, capacity committed | Few registrations: clarify free versus paid. Missed calls: check confirmation/reminders, not more promotion. |
| Day 14 | Suitable candidates, purchased plans, hours per candidate | Low suitability: narrow the internship/target-role message. Full calendar: stop new calls. |
| Day 21 | Delivered resumes, candidate ability to explain bullets, revisions | Weak delivery: pause acquisition and improve extraction. Never add unsupported outcomes. |
| Day 30 | Unique signups against 1,000; paid/completed plans; waiting time; refunds | Report honest shortfall and the repeatable channel. A small, well-served cohort is evidence; fake leads are not. |

Every Sunday, review five consented landing sessions and three booking exits. Ask whether they knew the price and number of sessions before clicking. Course 24-hour readings overlap and are not cumulative signups. Employer logos are not a conversion metric, and interview opportunities are not guaranteed jobs.
