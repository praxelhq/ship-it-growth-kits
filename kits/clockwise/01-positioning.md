# Clockwise — Positioning

**Live:** https://anvayacareers.in/ · **Category:** Career coaching · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/clockwise

**Your roster name and live brand disagree.** The course lists Clockwise; the live site is Anvaya. Use **“Anvaya — listed as Clockwise on Ship It”** until the listing is corrected. Do not send a candidate to a different-sounding brand without explanation.

The actual headline is **“Your resume should not be the reason you get rejected.”** The CTA is **“Book a Session”**; paid routes lead to `anvaya-payment.html`. The page offers a ₹2,250 resume rebuild across three 45-minute live sessions, ₹3,999 full profile strategy, other individual services, and a free 15-minute diagnostic. These are the public offers checked 29 October; checkout and booking completion were not tested.

## Your first person

An Indian final-year student with one or two internships who cannot explain what they did without saying “helped with.” They are applying to a specific role next week. They need to extract truthful evidence, not buy another decorative template.

**Pitch:** Build an interview-defensible resume with a coach, live, from the work you actually did.

**Short version:** Your own work, turned into clear resume bullets together.

## The wedge

One internship, one target role, one bullet the candidate can defend. Show the live shared-sheet questioning process using a fictional candidate. You are a coaching service, not an AI app. Do not turn your own positioning into an automation promise.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) offers a free professional profile and project showcase, and a student can write a resume using their existing document editor for no extra purchase. Those alternatives do not involve paying for your live extraction process. You win when a candidate wants a human to ask the follow-up questions; you lose when they only need a template. Do not manufacture competitor coaching prices.

## The capacity limit

The site includes outcome logos and testimonial claims; they are the site's claims, not independently verified placement evidence. Never promise a shortlist, employer endorsement or offer. Consent and the underlying records come before reusing any case study.

The policy target remains **1,000 consumer signups**, but you cannot deliver 1,000 free 15-minute calls in a student month: that is 250 call-hours before paid work. Count opted-in diagnostic registrations separately from calls delivered and paying clients. Publish a real wait time and cap bookings to hours you can fulfil. Quality of ten finished resumes is more useful than a fictional thousand-client report.
