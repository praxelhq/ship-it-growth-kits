# Cultcool — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is an account; activation is a real agency/brand using its own category/region question, inspecting sources and accepting a useful brief output.

Count only real businesses, their own context onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, and privately known decision-maker name/phone. Free users can count. Sample readers, unrelated curious consumers and two strategists at one firm do not count separately.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Mask client briefs and report text. Record business accounts, first completed searches, source inspections, accepted/rejected actions, independent return searches and paid packs. Private values were not available; record actuals, not zero. Save course rank, reading and sync from [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/cultcool).

Events: signup_completed, contextual_search_completed, source_opened, report_saved, action_accepted, independent_return_search. Source opening alone does not prove trust or quality.
