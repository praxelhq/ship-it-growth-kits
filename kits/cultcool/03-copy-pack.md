## 3. LinkedIn
Knowing a cultural term is not the same as knowing what a brand should do with it.

Before using one in a brief, I want four answers:
What does it describe?
Who is actually using it?
What do the dated sources support?
What would make it a bad fit for this brand?

That is the review I am building Cultcool around. It gives a starting report; the strategist still checks the evidence and decides.

## 5. Useful answer
A term's rising search interest tells you attention changed, not that consumers want your product. Compare the same region and time window, read the original sources, and write the assumption you still need to test with buyers.

Change the actual term and evidence on repeat posts. Never invent a campaign result or claim a named publication endorsed Cultcool.
