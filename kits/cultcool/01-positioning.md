# Cultcool — Positioning

**Live:** https://cultcoool.tech/ · **Category:** Cultural research for brands · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/cultcool

The actual headline is **“Know what culture is saying — before it's everywhere.”** **Get your free reports** opens account creation; the first 5 searches are free, then $7.99 buys 10 searches without a subscription. The domain has three consecutive o's in cultcoool: always share the full link.

Your first ten are small beauty/fashion agency strategists preparing a current client brief for India. They already collect references but need to translate a cultural term into a cautious, sourced creative angle. This is B2B under the actual offer, resolving the roster's low-confidence audience flag.

**Pitch:** Turn a cultural question into a sourced report you can inspect before using it in a brand brief.
**Short version:** Cultural context for the next client brief.

The wedge is one question such as whether a particular aesthetic fits the client's audience now. Do not sell “predict the next trend.” Your page's momentum score and 5–10-minute timing are product claims; a sample 77 score is not independent evidence that a trend is emerging.

The named alternative is free [Google Trends Explore](https://newsinitiative.withgoogle.com/resources/trainings/basics-of-google-trends/) plus manual reading. Google's own training calls Explore free; it measures search interest, not the same cultural interpretation. Your value must be useful synthesis with checkable sources, not pretending raw data is unavailable.

The weakness: broad source domains in a sample are weaker than dated article links and actual excerpts. “100% insights linked” does not guarantee the insight follows from its sources. Review that connection with a strategist before promotion.

**Target: 10 real business customers**, each with its own brief/category onboarded and independent recent use. Trend-curious students and sample readers do not substitute for ten agencies/brands.
