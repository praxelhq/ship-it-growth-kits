# Cultcool — Landing page fixes

Public homepage, sample and account route checked 29 October 2026. Searches, payments and downloaded PDFs were not independently tested.

1. **Make evidence visible before the score.** Current headline **“Know what culture is saying — before it's everywhere.”** leads into scored sample terms. Add **“Inspect the sources and dates behind each interpretation before using it in a brief.”** Show dated article links, not only vogue.com/reddit.com domain labels.
2. **Qualify momentum.** Current 0–100 scores and emerging/peaking/fading labels can imply measurement certainty. Publish the actual time window, region, sources and score method, or label it an analytical estimate. Replacement: **“Momentum is an interpretation of the sources found for this search, not a prediction of sales.”**
3. **Keep the offer simple.** Existing CTA **Get your free reports** is supported by a signup page stating 5 searches free, then $7.99/10. Add that exact allowance beside the hero CTA and explain whether a failed search uses a credit. Do not imply five free reports every month.
4. **Show one complete brief.** Current sample has a term, consumer insight and brand actions. Add the specific brand/category question, what evidence supports the action, and what remains uncertain. Label sample data and avoid fictional client results.
5. **Explain regional relevance.** The product offers region/focus filters. Put **“Choose the market you are briefing. A global term may not fit an Indian audience.”** near search setup. Do not imply every report has representative consumer coverage.
6. **Phone and slow-search states.** Test signup→search→waiting→result→source link on a fresh phone. Show real progress, retry and credit status; no mobile failure was established by this read-only inspection.
7. **Spelling and sharing.** The brand is Cultcool but domain cultcoool.tech. Use the full canonical link consistently in bio, previews and downloadable reports so someone does not guess the wrong spelling.

Gate: a new user can complete a search and inspect its actual evidence before you promise a client-ready brief.
