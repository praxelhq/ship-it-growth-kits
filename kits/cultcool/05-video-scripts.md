# Cultcool — Video scripts

Use a permitted real search and cite the source dates. Do not present the homepage's sample score as a measured trend finding.

## A.65-second demo — LinkedIn / strategist follow-up
0–2: slide “Trending ≠ right for your brand.”
2–15: show one specific brand/category/region question. “A brief needs context, not a list of buzzwords.”
15–32: run the real search; leave loading/time honest.
32–48: open one source and compare it with the report's interpretation. “This source shows X; the brand action is a hypothesis to review.” Use only the actual supported finding.
48–58: mark one action you would reject for this brand.
58–65: “First 5 searches free, then $7.99 for 10. cultcoool.tech.”

## B.20-second hook
0–2: word and big score, **“What does 77 mean?”**
2–12: “Without a date, region and source, a score is not a brief.”
12–20: show those context fields in a real report, or explicitly say they are what you are adding.

## C.25-second hook
0–2: two brand briefs, **“Same trend. Different decision.”**
2–15: show how the same sourced term can fit one positioning and conflict with another.
15–22: “Interpretation needs a brand and a reason.”
22–25: full URL.

Use large captions, no paid effects, and keep source-checking visible. Make no unsupported health, beauty efficacy or sales claim.
