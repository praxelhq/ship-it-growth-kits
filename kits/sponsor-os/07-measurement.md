# Sponsor OS — Measurement

**Target: 10 real active supply-side businesses.** Activation beside it: **authorized organizations independently managing a real sponsorship offer and its actual follow-up**. Sponsor clicks, fictional packages and individual practice accounts are separate metrics.

## Day 0

Count real organizations, authorized owners, non-fictional packages, actual offers, real completed deals, disputes and active supplier days from your own records. The public sample board proves none of those totals. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent; mask private deal/payment inputs. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/sponsor-os) rank/latest reading/timestamp. Unknown is not zero.

Events: `supplier_authorized`, `real_listing_published`, `offer_received`, `offer_accepted`, `deliverable_evidence_added` and `supplier_returned`. Payment events need their genuine provider-confirmed state; do not treat a browser success message, fictional demo or test-mode receipt as money received. Report platform fees, gross funds handled and supplier payouts separately; they are not all revenue or profit.

## Four tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): a legitimate sports/event business organization, its own actual package data onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible authorized contact whose name and callable number you hold privately. Paying isn't required. One entity listing a club and tournament is one business. An unpaid student group doesn't automatically become a business because the target needs ten.

## Forecast

40 first approaches × hypothetical 30% conversation × 60% real listing × 50% final-week independent use ≈ 3–4 suppliers. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–6 leaves a gap to ten.** These are assumptions, not sports-sponsorship conversion data. Recompute after 20 approaches using actual authority, inventory and return use; never fill the gap with fictional teams or sponsor-side registrations.
