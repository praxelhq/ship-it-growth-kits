# Sponsor OS — 30-day calendar

Start only after fictional demos are separated from real payments and an authorized organizer can manage a truthful listing. Daily: 20 minutes replies, ten minutes private organization/evidence log. Four first approaches on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 = **40 total**; ten warm introduction requests on day 4. One follow-up after seven days; stop after no.
