# Sponsor OS — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, how-it-works, terms and contact routes read 29 October 2026. No account, real payment or payout was tested. Payment protection statements remain operator claims.

## 1. Remove real-money actions from fictional demo instructions

**Current:** the how-it-works page says payments are live and invites visitors to pay into a deal involving fictional demo teams.

**Write:** **“Explore a no-money demonstration. Never pay a fictional sample team.”** Separate the real transaction flow from a clearly isolated controlled demonstration. Before any acquisition, have the owner verify that demo clicks cannot create a real-money misunderstanding. Don't stage a successful payout in a video.

## 2. Narrow the headline promise

**Current headline:** “Get your game funded.”

**Write:** **“Build a clear sponsorship package for your next real event.”** Supporting line: **“List verified facts, agree deliverables and track the work. Funding isn't guaranteed.”** Keep the current `/listing/edit/` action only if an authorized organizer can actually complete the listing; test ownership and editing with controlled data.

## 3. Keep the fictional-board disclosure attached

**Current:** “Sample listings—all names are fictional” appears on the board.

**Write on every example card and share:** **“Fictional example. Not an available sponsorship.”** Do not put sample goals or packages into a traction total. Replace examples with real organizations only after authorization and evidence review.

## 4. Explain payment custody without an absolute safety claim

**Current:** “Escrow-protected payments” and 5% fee introduce the flow; terms describe Sponsor OS holding and releasing funds.

**Write beside any real payment action:** **“Read who holds funds, what triggers release, the platform fee and how refunds/disputes work before paying.”** State the actual process and timing after verifying it with the operator and provider. A signature check is not a customer guarantee. Don't invent a regulatory approval, licensed escrow arrangement or instant-payout promise.

## 5. Show source-backed reach and rights

**Current:** a listing includes reach numbers and a rule-based fit score.

**Write:** **“Reach source and date. Assets this organizer is authorized to offer. What remains unverified.”** A fit score should expose missing information rather than make a blank look like verified quality. No guarantee of impressions or sales from a logo placement.

## 6. Make mobile package review possible

**Current:** multiple package/deal steps can require a long read.

**Write a compact summary:** **“Event · date · asset · evidence · price · delivery · payment terms.”** Test at 360px, large text, keyboard use and shared preview. No specific clipping or load failure was measured. The preview title should say **“Sponsor OS — real sport, clear sponsorship packages”**, with fictional labels retained on sample previews.
