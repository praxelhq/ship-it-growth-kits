# Sponsor OS — Video scripts

No real money is needed for these videos. Use synthetic organizations, label them fictional, and never show athlete identities, sponsor budgets or private negotiations. Phone voice and screen recording are enough.

## A · A package a business can inspect · 60 seconds · organizer call / LinkedIn

0–2s hook: **“What is the sponsor actually buying?”** Show one sample package, label “Fictional example.” 2–15s: highlight event, date and organizer rights: “Start with a real event and assets you control.” 15–30s: show deliverables and evidence fields: “A banner, a post and a claimed audience need clear scope and sources.” 30–43s: show offer/acceptance structure without payment: “Both sides should understand the same record.” 43–55s: show fee and terms: “Listing is free; the published fee is 5% on release. Check the actual payment and dispute process before any real deal.” 55–60s: “No promised sponsor. No payment to a demo team.”

## B · A fictional team cannot be traction · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“These aren't our customers.”** 0–8s: enlarge the sample-listing disclosure. 8–15s: “The board demonstrates a package. It doesn't prove those teams exist or that sponsors funded them.” 15–20s: “I'm recruiting real authorized organizers, one truthful offer at a time.” Hold URL, no funding claim.

## C · Proof before reach · 25 seconds · supplier onboarding

Hook 0–2s: **“Where did this audience number come from?”** 2–10s: show a synthetic unsupported field: “Don't put a round number here because it looks impressive.” 10–20s: add a source/date and a clear uncertainty note: “Measured attendance, account analytics and an estimate are different things.” 20–25s: “An honest gap gives the sponsor something real to discuss.”

Film the terms large enough to read on a phone. A no-money walkthrough should never cut to a fabricated paid/deal-complete badge.
