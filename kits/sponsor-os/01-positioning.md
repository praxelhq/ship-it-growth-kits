# Sponsor OS — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.sponsoros.shop/ · **Category:** Marketplace · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/sponsor-os

**Your target is ten real supply-side businesses, not ten fictional teams or sponsor enquiries.** Begin with adult-run local tournament or sports-club organizations that control genuine sponsorship inventory. An athlete's practice account or a friend's invented event doesn't pass the business-customer test.

The actual headline is **“Get your game funded.”** The supplier action **“Create my listing”** leads to `/listing/edit/`. The public [terms](https://www.sponsoros.shop/terms/) say listing is free and Sponsor OS takes **5% of the deal amount when funds are released**. The site calls the payment flow escrow-protected and says real Razorpay payments are live. Those are the operator's claims, not an independently verified financial safeguard.

## The first offer

Choose a local organizer with a confirmed event date, an authorized decision-maker and two sponsorship assets it really owns: a banner position and a fixed number of permissioned social posts, for example. Don't start with national brand procurement or unverified audience promises.

> Put a real event's sponsorship package, price and deliverables in one shareable record, then track what both sides actually agreed.

Bio: **A clear sponsorship offer and delivery record for local sport.**

Your first useful result is an organizer who can send a factually accurate package to an already relevant local business. You are not guaranteeing discovery, funding or a sponsor's approval.

## Alternatives

Most prospects can continue with email/WhatsApp and a manual delivery checklist. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) costs $0 for up to ten collaborators per workspace and can track tasks, but doesn't establish sponsorship rights or provide this deal/payment flow. A spreadsheet is an operational alternative, not proof that all financial custody risk disappears. Compare clarity and evidence, not invented competitor commissions.

## The serious weakness to lead with

The [how-it-works page](https://www.sponsoros.shop/how-it-works/) instructs visitors to run a fictional demo deal through a real payment flow. **Don't send a prospect to pay a fictional team.** Provide a no-money walkthrough or clearly isolated test environment first, and make payment, refund, payout timing and dispute handling explicit before a real deal. A successful payment signature check alone doesn't prove financial protection or guarantee release.

Keep the fictional-board disclosure attached to every screenshot. Real reach must have a source and date; fit scores are the platform's rules, not a forecast of sponsor return. Ten customers means ten legitimate organizations onboarded with their own offer and independently using the workflow in the final seven days.
