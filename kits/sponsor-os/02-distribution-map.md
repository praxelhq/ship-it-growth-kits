# Sponsor OS — Distribution map

**Real organizer inventory first; sponsors second.** Recruit one local sports scene you can verify. Broad outreach before credible packages only creates two empty sides.

| Route | Action, boundaries and assumed yield | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Local tournament/club operators | Manually verify a real upcoming event, the adult organizer's authority and owned sponsorship assets. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. At hypothetical 30% conversation × 60% true listing × 50% independent final-week use, expect about 3–4 organizations. No harvested athlete contacts or access to minors. | #1 |
| 2 · Warm sports/venue introductions | Ask ten known sports contacts to request an introduction to an actual organizer. Do not claim a venue's brand as a partner. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 active suppliers. Ask before passing contact details. | #2 |
| 3 · Organizer-owned sponsor relationships | After the organizer approves a real package, give them #3 to send to existing relevant business contacts. One package sent to ten appropriate contacts might produce 1–3 conversations; this isn't ten customers or guaranteed funding. No purchased sponsor list. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, explain one package and evidence boundary using a synthetic example or permission. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 qualified visits for a small account, possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) local organizer route | Real startup network, not a confirmed sponsor pool or booked event. Ask a current organizer privately whether an evidence-first local-sport discussion fits. No paid access or promises of brand introductions. | #2 |

Use the product's [sponsor briefs](https://www.sponsoros.shop/sponsors/) and [discovery](https://www.sponsoros.shop/discover/) only after verifying which entries are real, current and owner-authorized; fictional samples aren't prospects. Specific relevant sources are the actual event's official announcement and responsible club's own business account. No named sponsor affiliation is assumed.

No verified sports-group advertising rule or free newsletter placement was found. If an organizer requests a free opt-in email blurb, #3 explains the package; don't send mail to their audience yourself. Student sports teams require a responsible adult organization and permission; don't equate a college group with a legal selling entity.

Base range **3–6 active supplier organizations**, below ten. At day 14 inspect whether owners can publish truthful packages and manage a real follow-up, rather than adding speculative teams or promising sponsor ROI. Don't double-count the same organization as club, team and tournament.
