# WashQ — Positioning

**Live:** https://washq.dev/ · **Category:** PG laundry operations · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/washq

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Fewer laundry fights. Fewer calls to the caretaker.”** CTA **“Join the waitlist”** points to `#early-access`; `/demo` is a fake-data sandbox without signup and `/login` is separate. The live page says **“Sign-up opens soon”** and owners will be contacted when onboarding is available. No property, resident, queue or payment was created.

**Waitlist interest is not a live property customer.** Your first business hypothesis is an independently owned local PG with one or two shared washing machines and a caretaker handling queue questions. Start one building with an owner willing to explain a fair process and a backup when the page isn't open. Don't recruit residents as a substitute for business owners.

**Pitch: Give the PG owner and residents one understandable laundry queue, with a caretaker who can intervene.** Short version: **Know who's next without another trip downstairs.**

The page describes QR/browser access, a first-come queue, one position at a time and a ten-minute turn timer. The page updates while open. **Push/SMS alerts, rent tracking and photo maintenance requests are not available.** No automatic notification promise, rent collection or maintenance-document workflow. A timer cannot prove someone physically used a machine or that it is safe/free.

Advertised offer:30-day free trial, no card, then plans based on tenant count with amount shown before payment. Numeric post-trial price unknown. Trial begins only when real property onboarding is available; don't promise immediate 30-day live access from the waitlist. Existing work with “a real PG” is a site claim, not verified adoption or permission to invent a testimonial.

The free manual alternative is a clearly posted queue sheet/whiteboard and caretaker check, or an owner-controlled [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) without publicly exposing resident identities. WashQ must reduce observed confusion, not promise fewer fights/calls without a real baseline. Keep a practical fallback if connectivity or resident access fails.

The site says name, email and room are used; caretaker can manage machines/residents/queues but not account/settings, with two-step verification. These are published claims, not independently audited permissions. **Target:10 real PG businesses**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Own permitted property workflow, independent recent use and privately known owner name/phone required. Ten residents/machines/buildings under one business do not automatically become ten customers. Closed onboarding means zero new qualifying live adoption until release.
