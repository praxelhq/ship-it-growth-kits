# WashQ — Video scripts

Use fake residents/rooms and an owned QR linked to a clearly labelled sample. No real tenant names, phone numbers, bedroom views or private invite links. Phone screen/voice are sufficient.

## A. See the next turn —65 seconds · owner follow-up

0–3 s: sample occupied machine card. **“Who is actually next?”**

3–18 s: scan your sample QR/open the real demo. “This is a fake-data sandbox, not a live PG.”

18–32 s: inspect position and actual turn timer. “One place in line and a visible turn, if the tested flow supports it.” Leave one real action uncut.

32–47 s: show missed-turn/caretaker behavior only if verified in the demo. “The owner needs a clear fallback when the line changes.” Never stage automatic success.

47–57 s: current notification limit. “Keep the page open. No push or SMS alert yet.”

57–65 s: signup-soon/price boundary and URL. “Try the sample; confirm live onboarding before inviting residents. washq.dev.”

## B. Notification hook —20 seconds · WhatsApp Status

0–3 s: open queue page beside a closed phone. **“Will it message you when your turn comes?”**

3–13 s: current FAQ/open-page update. “Not yet. The page updates while open; push and SMS are planned.”

13–20 s: sample label/URL. “Set the right expectation before the first queue.” No fake delivered notification.

## C. Caretaker hook —25 seconds · owner review

0–3 s: fictional missed turn. **“What happens when the next person doesn't arrive?”**

3–16 s: actual demo timer/queue state or a separate labelled policy checklist. “Check the real rule and who can intervene.”

16–25 s: owner/caretaker boundary and URL. “A queue works only when people understand the fallback.” No safety guarantee or invented complaint reduction.
