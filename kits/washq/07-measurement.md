# WashQ —Measurement

**Target:10 real PG business customers**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Four tests: genuine business; its own permitted property/laundry workflow onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known responsible owner name/phone. Count a business once, not ten residents/machines/rooms or automatically ten buildings of one owner. Keep identifying checks private.

Signup is actual live property access, not waitlist submission. Activation: owner/caretaker operates its own correctly configured machine queue with permitted resident access, understood timer/fallback and a genuine queue event. A sample QR scan isn't activation. Report assisted setup, resident use and independent owner/caretaker business use separately. Page states cannot independently prove physical machine use.

Private baselines unknown: existing genuine PG businesses, real onboarding status, own machines, permitted queue use, missed turns, errors, owner/caretaker recent activity, actual trial terms and payments. The site mentions building with a real PG, but its identity/adoption wasn't independently verified. Don't invent zero existing traction. While new onboarding remains closed, zero new live acquisition is the conditional forecast. Save rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/washq); unknown isn't zero and rolling readings aren't cumulative.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before tagging. Exclude resident identities/emails/rooms, queue contents where identifying, invite tokens, private property links and account screens. Aggregate events: waitlist_submitted, actual_property_onboarded, own_machine_ready, permitted_queue_joined, turn_started, missed_turn_recorded, caretaker_intervention, independent_business_return. Follow actual notice/retention; don't copy resident lists into growth logs.
