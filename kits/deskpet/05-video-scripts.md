# DeskPet — Video scripts + shot lists

**For DeskPet, video isn't marketing — it's the product demo, the ad, and the shareable object all at once.** You need **volume of short loops**, plus one good demo. Everything is screen-recorded on your Mac; you barely need to appear.

---

## The workhorse: 10-second loops (make 60 of them)

These are not videos with a beginning and an end. They are **loops** — the last frame should match the first so the viewer watches it three times before realising.

### Production setup (do this once)

- Clean desktop, nice wallpaper, no personal files, no notifications. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Record at 1920×1080 with QuickTime screen recording, then crop to 9:16 around the cat.
- **Crop tight.** The cat should fill a meaningful part of a phone screen. A full desktop shrunk to vertical is unwatchable.
- Text overlay in the first 0.3 seconds, large, high contrast.
- No music or one quiet loop. Never a trending audio that fights the content.
- Export once, post to TikTok + Reels + Shorts.

## Video B — The demo (1:00, horizontal + a 30s vertical cut)

**Use:** landing page hero (Fix 1), Product Hunt, Show HN, Mac media pitches.

**0:00–0:10 — The cat exists**
> *(no narration for the first 6 seconds — just the cat on a real desktop, walking, sitting, blinking)*
>
> "This is DeskPet. It lives on your Mac."

*Shot: real desktop, real work windows behind, the cat in front. Let it breathe. Do not rush this.*

**0:10–0:25 — It does things**
> "You can just talk to it. Log two-fifty mils. Focus for twenty-five minutes. Remind me to stretch at three."

*Shot: say each one out loud, on camera audio. Show the response each time. Three commands, three results, no cuts between them if you can manage it — the unbroken take proves it works.*

**0:25–0:40 — The privacy bit**
> "All of that runs on your Mac. The speech recognition is on-device — watch." *(turn on airplane mode)* "Still works. There's no account, no cloud, and the optional AI stays off until you connect it yourself."

*Shot: the airplane-mode toggle, visibly, then a command succeeding. **This is the most persuasive 8 seconds in the video.** Do not cut it.*

**0:40–0:52 — The wrap-up**
> "At the end of the day it shows you what you actually did. Water, focus sessions, tasks. Seven-day charts, nothing dramatic."

*Shot: the briefing screen, the charts, the cat settling down.*

**0:52–1:00 — Price and close**
> "Fourteen days free, then two dollars. Once. Not two dollars a month. deskpet.app."

*Shot: the cat. End on the cat, not a logo.*

### Shot list
- Real desktop, real apps, real work visible — a sterile empty desktop makes the cat look like a mockup.
- Record system audio so the cat's spoken replies are audible.
- Your own voice, warm, unhurried. This product's whole register is gentle; don't sell hard.
- **Cut a 30-second vertical version** (cat → one command → airplane mode → $2) for social and the top of Reddit posts.

## Video C — "Every Mac app is a subscription now" (0:40, vertical) — the opinion one

This is the video that gets shared by people who will never install your app, which is exactly why it works.

| Time | On screen | Voice / text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | A rapid montage of $x.99/month paywalls from generic small utilities *(use your own screenshots of apps you actually pay for, don't name-and-shame)* | "Every small Mac app is a subscription now." |
| 0:06–0:16 | A calculator: 11 apps × $4.99 | "I counted. Eleven small utilities. Fifty-five dollars a month for apps that each do one thing." |
| 0:16–0:26 | Your app, the price screen | "So when I made mine, I made it two dollars. Once." |
| 0:26–0:36 | The cat, offline, working | "I can do that because there's no server. The voice runs on your Mac. My cost per user is zero, so your cost is two dollars." |
| 0:36–0:40 | End card | "deskpet.app. It's a cat." |

**Post this one to r/MacApps and Hacker News as well as social.** It's the written post from `03` section 8 in video form, and principle content travels much further than feature content.

## Video D — The desk-setup shot (a photo, not a video, but it belongs here)

For r/macsetups and r/battlestations. This is a *photo* production:

1. Clean the desk completely. Remove cables you can remove.
2. Natural light, late afternoon, from the side. No flash, no overhead.
3. Shoot from slightly above, 45°.
4. **The cat must be clearly visible on the screen** and large enough to notice but not the obvious subject.
5. Post it as a setup photo. Mention nothing. Wait for the question.

See `03` section 2 for the reply.
