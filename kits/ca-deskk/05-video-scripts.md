# CA Deskk — Video scripts

Synthetic clients/documents only. No real PAN, bank statement, WhatsApp number or statutory-deadline recommendation. Screen recording with phone voice is enough.

## A · Missing to confirmed · 60 seconds · practice-owner walkthrough

0–2s hook: **“Which client still owes this document?”** Show a synthetic checklist. 2–15s: load the controlled list in the actual supported mode: “First be clear where the record is stored.” 15–28s: inspect the reminder: “The professional checks the missing items and sends it from their own WhatsApp.” 28–43s: add a synthetic returned file and inspect client/type/year: “The proposed match is not the final decision.” 43–55s: confirm only after checking the original: “The firm decides what belongs in the record.” 55–60s: “Current trial and test-payment status: CA Deskk dot in.”

Do not send a message or upload a real client file simply to make the clip more convincing.

## B · Right name, wrong year · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“The client's name matches. The year doesn't.”** 0–8s: show two synthetic documents with different years. 8–15s: “A quick filing action can preserve a mistake. Keep the original and proposed year visible.” 15–20s: “Review before confirming.” No claim that every wrong year is detected perfectly.

## C · Remind only for what's still missing · 25 seconds · owner walkthrough

Hook 0–2s: **“This client already sent that document.”** Show a synthetic checklist with one confirmed and one missing item. 2–10s: inspect the actual reminder draft: “The message should ask for what's still owed, not repeat the whole list.” 10–20s: review the name and remaining item, then open the controlled WhatsApp draft without sending: “Your firm checks and presses Send.” 20–25s: “Keep the next request specific.” Current subscription checkout is test mode; no real purchase is demonstrated.

Keep captions large and use actual supported states. If checklist status and reminder wording disagree, fix it before recruiting another firm. No real client data or filing-deadline advice belongs in the clip.
