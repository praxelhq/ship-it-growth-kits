# CA Deskk — Positioning

**Live:** https://cadeskk.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/ca-deskk

**Your ten are real CA firms, not ten clients or ten staff members.** Start with an owner-led practice where one person tracks missing filing documents across Excel, email and WhatsApp. The first job is one small consented client checklist, not replacing the firm's accounting or filing software.

The actual headline is **“Your smart assistant for ITR, GST, tax audit and TDS season.”** The public landing client labels its actions **“Try it with a sample Excel”** and **“Use your own Excel.”** It promises document checklists, manually triggered WhatsApp reminders and reviewed filing of returned documents. The current offer is **₹5,999/year per firm**, any number of staff, free for a week from first upload. It explicitly says **payments are in test mode**. No live paid revenue should be inferred.

## Your sentence

> Know which documents each client still owes, review a ready reminder and confirm where the returned file belongs.

Bio: **The missing-document memory for a small CA firm's season.**

The first firm uploads a synthetic or authorized small client list, checks the generated requirements, reviews a reminder and manually confirms a returned document's client/type/year. The professional owns filing requirements and deadlines. Don't promise automatic statutory correctness or repeat the sample “31 Dec” as a current filing deadline.

## Why switch?

A shared [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can be created with a Google account; the firm's existing Excel licence and WhatsApp routine may require no new specialist subscription. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace and can hold a request checklist. Your difference is connecting the actual missing-document list, reviewed reminder and document identity/year check—if the real workflow proves it. This isn't NudgeBooks's generic recurring chase or PBC Tracker's one-engagement support matching.

## The contradiction to address before growth

The top says **“No sign-up. Your Excel stays in your browser.”** Later sections describe private firm sign-ins and staff lists. Those can describe separate modes, but the page doesn't establish that distinction clearly. Resolve where real client data goes and who can access it before asking a practice to trust you. The page's masked PAN and privacy claims aren't an independent security audit.

A demo of a document “filing itself” isn't professional approval. Keep the confirmation step explicit. Count only actual firms with their own permitted workflow, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact held privately. Your founder doing the reminders for them is an assisted service, not independent adoption.
