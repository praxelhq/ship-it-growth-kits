# CA Deskk — Landing-page fixes

Rendered public page read 29 October 2026; a plain fetch initially showed an app shell. The site is live. No real client list, reminder send, private firm access or payment was tested.

## 1. Explain the data modes before upload

**Current:** “No sign-up. Your Excel stays in your browser” sits above descriptions of private firm sign-ins and staff lists. The public client labels actions **“Try it with a sample Excel”** and **“Use your own Excel.”**

**Write:** **“Try the labelled browser demonstration with synthetic data. Before real firm use, see how sign-in, storage and staff access work.”** Replace this with the exact verified distinction between the actual modes, not an invented architecture. Test it with controlled records. Don't recruit client-data uploads until the explanation and real controls agree.

## 2. Make the professional's job explicit

**Current headline:** “Your smart assistant for ITR, GST, tax audit and TDS season.”

**Write:** **“Know what's missing. Review the reminder. Confirm the returned file.”** Supporting line: **“Your firm decides the actual requirements and deadlines.”** Keep the season use case; don't imply the software supplies a guaranteed correct filing decision.

## 3. Keep test payment on every paid action

**Current:** ₹5,999/year per firm; free for a week from first upload; payments in test mode.

**Write beside an upgrade:** **“Test mode—no real charge.”** Before live billing, align the annual amount, trial start, tax, renewal and cancellation terms with actual checkout. Don't count a simulated receipt or call it a live subscription while test mode remains.

## 4. Preserve confirmation before filing

**Current:** “Documents file themselves” is followed by “You confirm; it files.”

**Write:** **“Review the client, document type and year before filing.”** Keep source context beside the proposed match and leave uncertain records unfiled until reviewed. A visually confident match shouldn't silently replace professional judgment. Wrong-year sample text isn't a current legal deadline.

## 5. Make WhatsApp manual, not automatic

**Current:** reminders are described as one tap with a message already written.

**Write:** **“Review this client's missing items, then send from your own WhatsApp.”** Show exactly whether the app opens a draft or sends anything. Don't promise approved bulk automation or claim messages were tested from a public page. Let the professional check every name and amount before sending.

## 6. Check phone review and shared preview

**Current:** a broad season screen and multiple document states introduce the product.

**Write the first task around:** **“Who still owes which document?”** Make checklist status, year and confirmation readable at 360px and enlarged text. Verify wrong-year, unmatched and recovery states. Suggested preview: **“CA Deskk — a reviewed client-document chase.”** No specific mobile/load defect was measured; these are verification tasks.
