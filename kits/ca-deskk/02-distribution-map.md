# CA Deskk — Distribution map

**Small-practice owner outreach first.** The right first conversation is “how do you know what's missing?” rather than “would you like AI?” Your shared CA invitation window is **days 4–7**.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm practice introductions | Ask ten known contacts to request an introduction to a real CA practice owner. No PANs, client lists or personal member contacts passed to you. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 independent firms. | #1 |
| 2 · [ICAI's official directory route](https://www.icai.org/) and practice websites | The official site exposes a directory, not advertising permission or endorsement. Manually verify the firm's own business page and use a relevant enquiry route. Four new approaches on ten named days = 40. Hypothetical 25% response × 60% controlled trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 firms. | #2 |
| 3 · Organizer-approved checklist walkthrough | Days 4–7, with a synthetic client list and reminder. Don't request a branch mailing list or imply an ICAI-approved product. A confirmed session may add 0–2 trials; no event is booked. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show the document/year confirmation step rather than a promise to automate tax filing. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 relevant visits for a small account, possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · Existing active practice referrals | Ask only a firm that actually used its checklist independently to request a permissioned introduction to another practice. No sharing client details as a “case study.” | #5 |

Audit extractor owns text-PDF transcription days 10–13, NudgeBooks recurring chase days 16–19 and PBC engagement support days 22–25. Don't send the same organizer another product invitation inside seven days. Specific source worth following is [ICAI](https://www.icai.org/) for its own current professional announcements; don't repurpose a sample reminder as official deadline advice.

No verified accountant Facebook-group rule, paid directory placement or free newsletter slot is assumed. If a practice organizer requests an opt-in email blurb, #3 can be shared after approval. Base **3–5 active firms** is below ten. At day 14 inspect real checklist use and trust in the data modes before increasing contact volume.
