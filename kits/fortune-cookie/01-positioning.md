# fortune-cookie — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.fortunecookie.fit/ · **Category:** Daily entertainment / reflection · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/fortune-cookie

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“A little something you needed to hear.”** The interaction CTA is **“Tap the cookie to crack it.”** The page offers one personalized daily cookie, check-in, calendar/journal and a classic share card free. **Plus costs ₹199/month**, renews monthly and can be cancelled; the live page names Dodo checkout. This research did not complete a check-in, cookie or payment.

Your first person is an adult student who enjoys sending a light, slightly specific card to a friend at breakfast. They want a playful daily ritual, not a prediction to base decisions on.

**Pitch:** Answer a whimsical check-in, crack a daily cookie and keep or share the line that makes you smile.

**Short version:** A tiny daily surprise, not a forecast.

## Your wedge

The first cookie must be worth seeing before a membership pitch. Show the actual questions and card with fictional answers. The page's check-in displays 11 steps; don't advertise a five-second result without measuring the flow. Let a free user reach the promised daily cookie without guessing which controls are paid.

[Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) offers a $0/year journal with prompts. A message from a friend or an already-owned notebook also costs no extra purchase. Fortune Cookie earns interest through the crack-open moment, playful wording and tasteful card, not by claiming journaling or encouragement requires payment.

## The uncomfortable bit

“Manifestation” is a feature label, not evidence that checking in changes external events. Don't promise destiny, health, romance, money or a guaranteed good day. A fortune can be entertainment and a reflection prompt while remaining honest.

The public page exposes a testing control for switching to Free. Remove developer-only controls from production if they confuse the real entitlement. Test the free/Plus boundary and cancellation before marketing unlimited cookies.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first cookie sessions, with completed cookies and voluntary next-day return beside it. Unlimited Plus outputs are not unlimited users; a screenshot isn't a signup.
