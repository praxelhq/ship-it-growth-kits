# fortune-cookie — Landing page fixes

Based on [fortunecookie.fit](https://www.fortunecookie.fit/), checked 29 October. No check-in, cookie or subscription was submitted during research.

## 1. Prove the free first cookie

Current headline: **“A little something you needed to hear.”** CTA: **“Tap the cookie to crack it.”** Test a fresh browser through all check-in steps, first reveal, journal/calendar and next-day state. If the promised free cookie is blocked, stop promotion and fix it. Loading text such as “Baking your cookies” must have a real error/retry state.

## 2. Tell a newcomer what they do before the metaphor

Suggested heading: **“A playful daily check-in. A cookie to open.”** Support: **“Answer today's questions, reveal a personal card and keep the ones you want to revisit.”** Button: **“Try today's free cookie.”** Preserve the warm cookie styling, but don't make the user infer the interaction.

## 3. Show check-in effort honestly

The public screen exposes an 11-step check-in. Show the actual progress and let people know what is required. If a shorter first-cookie flow is possible, test it as one change. Don't promise instant personalization while hiding a lengthy form.

## 4. Keep entertainment separate from certainty

The manifestation section should say **“A space for intentions and reflection, not a prediction or guarantee.”** Do not imply the app's line can establish future events or replace a serious decision. Let users skip a prompt without a guilt message.

## 5. Put recurring price beside Plus

Current Plus is **₹199/month**, renewing monthly. Keep the monthly renewal/cancellation note next to the upgrade button, not only below the feature list. Explain the exact free daily allowance and avoid a fake “last chance” offer. Test upgrade, verified access, cancellation and continued access through the paid period.

## 6. Remove confusing test controls

The fetched page includes **“Switch back to Free (for testing).”** Restrict developer controls to the intended environment/account. A public tester switch can confuse payment state and trust; verify whether it is actually visible to ordinary visitors before changing anything.

## 7. Check privacy, cards and phone layout

Mask check-in/journal fields from replay; explain storage and deletion with the actual policy. On 390px, check the cookie tap target, long fortune text, progress and cancellation link. Preview: **“Fortune Cookie — one playful daily check-in.”** Use fictional answers and no private journal in the image.
