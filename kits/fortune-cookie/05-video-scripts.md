# fortune-cookie — Video scripts

Use fictional answers and your own card. No staged destiny prediction, private journal or fake “this came true” testimonial.

## A. First-cookie demo — 50 seconds

0–2s, closed cookie: **“Is the reveal worth the questions?”**

2–15s, show the actual check-in and progress, with any cut labelled. “This is what you answer before the cookie opens.”

15–28s, actual crack/reveal. “A playful line based on the check-in—not a forecast.”

28–40s, show calendar/journal and card choice. “Keep it, revisit it or share it if you want.”

40–50s, show Free/Plus boundary. “One daily cookie free. Plus ₹199/month, renewed monthly. fortunecookie.fit.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “No cosmic homework.”

2–10s: crack the cookie and read an owned/fictional card. “Take the line if it fits. Leave it if it doesn't.”

10–15s: URL and **One daily cookie free · playful reflection**.

## C. Return hook — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Would I open it again tomorrow?”

2–12s: show real second-day state and calendar. “The daily loop has to earn a second visit.”

12–20s: new actual reveal, no invented streak. “That's the test I'm running.” URL last.

## D. Clear-price clip — 20 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “The free cookie isn't a subscription trap.”

2–12s: show the real daily Free card and separate Plus card. “One a day free. More themes and unlimited cookies are optional.”

12–20s: highlight ₹199/month and cancellation. “Read the recurring offer before choosing it.” Record only after the actual free path and billing controls are verified.
