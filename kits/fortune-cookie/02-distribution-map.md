# fortune-cookie — Distribution map

**Lead with a real cookie reveal and a voluntary friend share.** The daily return is more important than a one-time “cute site” reaction.

| Route | Scheduled action and boundary | Forecast assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult testers | Ask about check-in length, first-card value and next-day return. No private journal screenshots required. | 10 trials, 5–10 first cookies; return must be observed, not assumed. | #1 |
| Three existing friend/campus groups | Admin permission, one invitation each; no astrology/fortune spam or pressure to forward. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels/Shorts | Real check-in, cookie reveal, free/Plus explanation and second-day experience on Days 8, 13, 20, 26 at 09:00 IST. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–120 sessions. | #3 |
| Two own-account story posts | Publish an owned card with a link on Days 11/24; do not imply a private user's words are yours. | 2 × 100–500 views × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 1–15 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker-feedback listing after the free-cookie path works; check current Monday slot and disclose paid tier. No votes requested. | 0–20 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No paid influencers, paid newsletter feature or invented “daily fortune community” is included. A campus newsletter can carry a free test invitation only after an organizer accepts; zero baseline forecast. Reply to genuine questions about the project on your own posts, not unrelated vulnerable-person posts.

At 50% visit-to-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. The base tests may yield tens or low hundreds. A real share card can create more visits, but don't count every share click as a new person. Keep this light entertainment route separate from Gita/Kindred's reading and private-journal groups.
