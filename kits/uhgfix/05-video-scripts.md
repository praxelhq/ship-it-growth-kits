# Uhgfix — Video scripts

Use your own ordinary task, readable screen recording and voice. Hide private text. Hard cuts and captions are enough; let people choose sound. Never fake a completed task or a health outcome.

## A. From avoidance to start —45 seconds

**Where:** owned clips and adult-circle opening. **Hook:** “Assignment nahi, pehli line.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Your real blank draft. | “Assignment nahi, pehli line. Abhi bas itna.” |
| 7–17 s | Enter the ordinary task and deliberately choose a tone. | “Main kaam likhta hoon, aur tone khud choose karta hoon.” |
| 17–28 s | Actual generated starting step, if it completes. | “Yeh step usable hai ya sirf funny? Main pehla action check kar raha hoon.” |
| 28–38 s | Actually start the small action. | “Yeh start hua. Poora kaam done bolne ka drama nahi.” |
| 38–45 s | URL, mute/reduced-motion controls. | “Ughfix. Apna tone, ek chhota start.” |

If generation fails or the step is unusable, repair it instead of staging a result.

## B. Tone is a choice —20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Nag chahiye? Pehle poochho.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Tone selector, quiet control. | “Nag chahiye? Pehle poochho.” |
| 5–13 s | Choose a wanted persona, then mute/change it. | “Funny tabhi hai jab tumhe woh tone pasand ho. Change karo, ya chup kar do.” |
| 13–20 s | Actual first step and URL. | “Joke se zyada important hai usable start.” |

## C. Funny is not finished —25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Hasa diya. Kaam shuru hua?”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | A real funny response, private details hidden. | “Hasa diya. Kaam shuru hua?” |
| 6–17 s | Identify the actual small action, then do it. | “Main iss step ko test kar raha hoon. Sirf screenshot forward karna productivity nahi.” |
| 17–25 s | URL and limited-job line. | “Ughfix is a start, not a promise to finish everything.” |

## D. Pro truth —20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Payment abhi coming soon hai.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Actual coming-soon line. | “Payment abhi coming soon hai.” |
| 6–14 s | Plan/device limitations readable. | “Plans advertised hain, but pehle trial aur browser limits samjho. Main unavailable checkout sell nahi kar raha.” |
| 14–20 s | Back to actual task flow. | “Pehla test: step useful hai?” |

Ten clips are scheduled. Reuse the selector/mute footage, but each new clip needs a real task or observation. Do not mock a participant or reveal their avoided work without permission.
