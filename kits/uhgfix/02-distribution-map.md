## Composition and gap

Ten clips, two accepted sessions, twenty invitations and one project listing model **40–270 first usable task sessions before overlap**. These are not verified accounts and may overcount people using different devices. Do not relabel repeated sessions as new users to reach 1,000.

At an assumed 50% visitor-to-first-task rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified new visits. Wider voluntary sharing must come from a useful, controllable first step, not a forced “send this to your lazy friend” joke. Day 21 is the honest reforecast.

Use the Days 17–20 starting-step window, distinct from Terms Apply's early deadline sessions and MakeHabit's anchor sessions. Separate actual contact lists and leave seven days between invitations to the same organizer. Limits are self-imposed workload boundaries, not platform-approved quotas.
