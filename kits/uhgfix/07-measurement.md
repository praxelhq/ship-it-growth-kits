# Uhgfix — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** For the current no-account entry, use **first-time usable task sessions** as the explicit policy-equivalent headline, with **reported actual starts** beside it. This is session-based adoption, not verified accounts or an exact cross-device person count. [Target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md).

Count the first completed usable task flow conservatively once per browser installation during the campaign. Exclude your tests, bots, reloads, character switches and repeated tasks from the new-user headline. Do not fingerprint people across devices; state that cross-device duplicates and cleared storage limit uniqueness. If an account system is added, report accounts separately rather than silently changing the denominator.

Activation is a usable first step the person reads and reports starting. That is self-report, not proof the entire task is done. Later-day independent returns are separate. A funny screenshot, Pro-page view or muted audio event is not activation.

## Forecast and checkpoints

The actual plan schedules ten clips, two accepted sessions, twenty invitations and one listing. `02` models **40–270 first usable sessions before overlap**. That is not a sufficient forecast for 1,000. At an assumed 50% visit-to-first-task rate, the target needs 2,000 qualified new visits; replace the assumption with actual data.

Milestones are decisions, not predictions. Weekly rows include qualified visits, first usable sessions, usable-step rate, self-reported starts, smaller-step requests, returns, errors and hours. Show numerators/denominators.

Visits without a task suggest the promise/tone feels wrong. Tasks without responses suggest reliability. Responses without starts suggest entertainment without help. Repeated annoyance or unwanted tone means improve control, not escalate the nagging. Do not claim a productivity or health effect from a completion emoji or a joke's share count.
