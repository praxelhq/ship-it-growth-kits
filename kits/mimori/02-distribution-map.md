Use creative-reference practice, not generic “AI inspiration.” Shared private-record research: [cluster note](../../shared/b1-private-records-cluster.md). Use separate actual contacts from diary/decision products. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week; ask a design organizer rather than reusing another lane's campus clinic.

## Ranked routes

**Twenty warm design-learner invitations**, five each Days 5,12,19,26. Qualify people who already collect textures/materials/places for a project. First CTA is the no-account sample, then optional private saving only after auth/storage release checks. Use 03 #1; one follow-up maximum. Don't ask for a private portfolio, student name or copyrighted image collection.

**Six owned process posts**, Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Show one permitted photograph, the maker's original note, literal retrieval and a new interpretation. Use 03 #2/#3 and 05 videos. Manual [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) posting or the maker's existing visual account; no scraped creator lists or engagement exchange. Credit sample images; people pictured aren't endorsements.

**One conditional twelve-person reference exercise**, Day 15, through one known design organizer asked Day 8 with 03 #4. Twenty minutes using bundled credited samples; participants can keep everything temporary/private. Twelve is a proposed cap, not secured attendance. Send 03 #5 only after permission. No accepted organizer means individual tests and zero event reach.

**Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad, current eligibility/timing/existing listing checked Day 18; optional Day 23 submission after real account checks. A live launch destination isn't a reserved slot. No votes requested. Use 03 #6.

**Product Hunt**, https://www.producthunt.com/launch, optional Day 25 after existing-listing and real-product readiness checks. Describe the academic beta honestly; no duplicate unchanged launch, paid hunter or upvote solicitation. A public sample doesn't prove durable personal storage.

**One introduced design-process editor**, Day 22 using 03 #7. Offer an original source-linked interpretation exercise, disclose ownership and image rights. No guessed publication names, subscribers or emails. Zero traffic until accepted and published.

**AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional factual application entry with correct beta/platform/license status after verified email. Free reviews can wait indefinitely; paid priority outside budget. Zero base traffic until accepted.

## Forecast matched to calendar

Assumptions, not benchmarks: 20 warm invitations × 50–70% sample visits = 10–14; × 20–40% later verified account/first private save = about 2–6 activations. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 10–25% account/first save = about 1–8. An accepted twelve-person session × 60–80% attendance = 7–10; × 20–40% later account/first save = about 1–4. Remove sample/account duplication and contact overlap.

Rough base 3–14 private-save activations without the event, 4–18 with it before deduplication. If account/storage release checks fail, forecast zero durable-account activation and report sample learning separately. Launches/editorial/directories are zero in the base because none is secured. Keep the 1,000-account target and the honest gap; a larger **accepted** creative-school partner is a later experiment after private saving works, not imagined month-one reach.
