# Mimori — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.mimori.dev/ (apex redirects here) · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/mimori · Checked 29 October 2026.

Your first user is a design student who notices a texture on a walk, saves it and later forgets why. Start with one observation, the user's own words and finding that reference for a project. Don't pitch an AI moodboard engine: the live Essentials beta doesn't understand images through AI.

Actual H1 **“A quiet desk for your photographs, textures, places and moments”**. **Start collecting** → `/signup?next=%2Fcapture`; **Try the sample workspace** → `/sample`. No paid amount is visible. The [public sample](https://www.mimori.dev/sample) has six credited photographs and an example project. Guest changes stay in the tab's memory and disappear on reload/leaving/reset; they don't transfer to an account. No account registration, confirmation, recovery, cross-device saving or private-storage isolation was tested here.

## The pitch

> Keep what you noticed. Write why it matters. Find it later by your own words and bring it into a project.

Bio: **A private reference library with your thinking attached.**

Search matches literal words in titles, notes and manual tags. A reference titled “woven seat” won't necessarily appear for “coastal calm” unless those words were written. The user writes the interpretation; the app doesn't generate a creative direction. This is a useful constraint, not something to disguise with a semantic-search animation.

The [evaluation guide](https://www.mimori.dev/evaluation) makes the boundary unusually clear: private saving/cross-device retrieval need an account, the sample doesn't prove iPhone compatibility, exports contain metadata rather than original photos/private storage links, and printing/PDF depends on the browser. Quote these limits before asking students to move an important reference collection.

## Alternatives and value

A camera roll plus local notes or a folder-and-spreadsheet reference list costs ₹0 extra specialist software; [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) is one manual structured option. Those can be enough. Mimori earns adoption if capture→literal retrieval→source-linked interpretation feels better than scattered screenshots, not because no other tool stores images.

No visible price means don't invent “free forever,” storage allowance or a subscription. Ask the owner to publish current beta/account limits. The screenshot photos are credited samples and pictured people don't endorse the product; keep those labels in videos and launch posts.

## Avoid borrowing experimental credibility

The guide describes separate owner-local CLIP/SigLIP research, not an integrated site feature. Its small 24-photo/agent-judged comparison isn't independent validation, a mobile benchmark or proof of automated tagging. No model runs on the website according to the guide. Don't advertise research metrics as beta performance or silently merge the prototype into the live proposition.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**, alongside first private reference saved, found through its own annotation and connected to a project interpretation. A temporary sample visit isn't an account; six bundled sample photos aren't six users. Start with one texture/reference exercise, distinct from Afterthoughts' diary and DecisionLog's outcome journal.
