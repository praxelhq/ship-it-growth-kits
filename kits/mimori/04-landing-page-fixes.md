# Mimori — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“A quiet desk for your photographs, textures, places and moments”**; **Start collecting** → `/signup?next=%2Fcapture`, **Try the sample workspace** → `/sample`. No paid amount visible. Public sample/evaluation were read; no account, private upload, confirmation/recovery or real-device compatibility was tested. Recommendations preserve the existing quiet reference-library direction.

## 1. Put the sample's temporary boundary beside the CTA

The sample already explains it, but users can miss that difference when leaving the homepage. Suggested CTA help:

> **Try a temporary sample — no account.** Six credited photographs and an example project. Changes stay in this tab and disappear on reload/leaving/reset. They won't move into a personal account.

Primary-account help:

> **Start a private library.** Account needed for saving and cross-device retrieval. See current beta limits and storage/privacy terms before uploading anything important.

Don't invent storage limits or promise free forever. Owner verifies signup, confirmation, recovery and private storage with owned test accounts before conversion outreach. A working guest sample doesn't establish any of those.

## 2. Make literal search a benefit with a clear limit

Keep the actual phrase “Search is literal keyword matching” near a concrete example:

> Write “weave” in the title, note or manual tags. Search “weave” later. The app matches your words; it doesn't infer a mood or understand a photograph through AI.

Show a genuine matching result and a respectful no-result state. Don't animate a mood-to-unannotated-image result that only the separate prototype could produce. Measure whether people can predict what will be found.

## 3. Keep interpretation clearly human-authored

Suggested board caption:

> Bring the original observation beside your project and write your own interpretation. Mimori keeps the connection; you make the creative decision.

The current screen illustrations are labelled samples. Retain their credits and non-endorsement note in mobile crops, video frames and social cards; don't turn a pictured person into a testimonial. Use original/permitted references for new examples.

## 4. Separate experimental research from the live product

The evaluation guide describes owner-local CLIP/SigLIP experiments and small agent-authored judgments, not integrated capabilities. Suggested small link label:

> Research notes — not a feature in this beta. No model runs on the website. Owner-reported exploratory results aren't independent human validation; mobile testing and rights/licence review remain open.

Don't put research relevance percentages or latency beside the signup button as product-performance proof. No need to repeat model benchmarks in acquisition copy; the current useful workflow is manual annotation and retrieval.

## 5. Explain export and privacy honestly

Suggested sample export copy:

> Export sample metadata or print through your browser. This does not export original photographs or private storage links. Temporary changes aren't a backup.

For account storage, publish actual retention, deletion, download/export, analytics and access boundaries. Don't extend “guest files never leave the browser” to signed-in private uploads. Verify replay masking with synthetic notes, no private image URLs in analytics and owned-account separation; don't call it independently audited.

## 6. Replace a feature tour with one complete exercise

Near the final CTA:

> Choose one reference. Write one observation. Find it using a word you wrote. Attach it to a project and explain how it changes your idea.

Test the complete sample sequence and actual account save/reload separately. Physical iPhone performance is explicitly untested in the evaluation guide; don't call the entire product mobile-proven from a responsive screenshot. First-use success is finding and interpreting a reference, not merely uploading many images.
