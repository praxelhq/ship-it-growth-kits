# FOUNDERSEYE — Distribution map

**Owner-led distributor pilots first.** A general SaaS launch is lower value than seeing one warehouse team use the same record. No paid consultants, subscriptions or ads are required for acquisition.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Local distributor/wholesaler visits | Pick one compact trade cluster and visit owners outside dispatch rush. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. Hypothetical 30% owner conversation × 50% controlled pilot × 50% independent use ≈ 3 businesses. Don't interrupt loading or photograph records without permission. | #1 |
| 2 · Public business websites | Verify actual stock/dispatch operations and a relevant business enquiry route. Use the same prospect log as visits, not a duplicate count. One follow-up after seven days; stop on no. | #2 |
| 3 · Warm accountant/operations introductions | Ask ten contacts to request an owner introduction. No private day books, client lists or phone numbers passed without permission. Assumed 3–5 conversations and 0–2 active pilots. | #3 |
| 4 · Owner-approved staff walkthrough | One warehouse, synthetic records first, actual permissions checked. The owner chooses staff and scope; no unsolicited staff onboarding. | #1 |
| 5 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show one order trail and where people still confirm facts. Assumed 100–500 impressions, 0–5 useful visits, possibly zero. No distributor logo or stock/revenue screenshot without permission. | #4 |

The [live demo](https://www.founderseye.in/demo/) is a verified learning route, explicitly local/sample and unsaved. It isn't a real customer account. Specific sources worth listening to are the actual prospect's product/warehouse pages and their intentionally public operational information—not generic ERP promises. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) is a checked manual alternative; no claim of equivalent stock reservation is implied.

No trade-association group access, newsletter placement or directory promotion permission was verified. If a local organizer asks for a free opt-in walkthrough blurb, use #2 only after approval. Don't target warehouse providers for the coming-soon provider app.

Base **3–5 independently active businesses** is below ten. Recompute after 20 approaches and inspect setup/support hours. A product that needs you to enter every movement can't scale through more cold outreach; narrow the first job instead.
