# FOUNDERSEYE — Video scripts

Use the disclosed synthetic distributor demo or controlled records. No real bank balance, stock, customer address or staff credentials. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · One order trail · 60 seconds · owner walkthrough

0–2 s hook: **“Can this order leave today?”** Show a synthetic order. 2–15 s: inspect controlled stock: “The first question is what is actually available.”15–30 s: place the order and show the real supported next step: “Sales and dispatch need the same trail.”30–45 s: inspect the authorized role view: “The owner should know who can act and who can only see.”45–55 s: show actual dispatch/result, labelled sample: “A demo isn't a live delivery or bank entry.”55–60 s: “Start small, verify the handoff. Founders Eye.”

## B · Different roles, one job · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“A store keeper doesn't need the owner's profit screen.”**0–8 s: show a synthetic role split. 8–15 s: “Check the actual permissions before adding live records.”15–20 s: “Give each person the next action they need—not every private number.” Don't imply an independent security audit.

## C · A sample invoice isn't your books · 25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“Don't migrate a live day book to test a button.”**2–12 s: show a controlled import/sample result. 12–20 s: “Check formats, duplicates, totals and recovery with synthetic data first.”20–25 s: “Keep the accountant responsible for the real record.” No tax-correctness guarantee.

If a promised import/offline/role function fails, repair it rather than staging success. Keep samples clearly labelled in every crop.
