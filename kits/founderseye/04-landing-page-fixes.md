# FOUNDERSEYE — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, signup destination and sample-demo disclosure read 29 October 2026. No real company, import, tax calculation or role isolation was independently tested.

## 1. Give the broad promise a small starting job

**Current headline:** “See your whole business, from stock to cash, on one screen.”

**Write beneath it:** **“Start with one warehouse and one order. Check what sales, dispatch and the owner each need next.”** Keep the broader vision secondary. A practical first step is more credible than implying instant migration of a live business.

## 2. Separate the sample from an actual company

**Current demo:** browser-only sample data, nothing saved or sent to a real account. Signup creates a business account separately.

**Write:** **“Explore fictional data—reload resets it.”** Primary live action: **“Create my business account.”** Test fresh signup → controlled items → one order → actual supported dispatch/result before real operations. Don't count a demo reservation or invoice as customer activity.

## 3. Show role limits before sensitive work

**Current:** none/view/edit permissions and warehouse restrictions are described as database-enforced.

**Write:** **“Review each person's actual access before adding live records.”** Verify with controlled owner/staff accounts that restricted data isn't exposed and recovery works. Don't call it independently audited from a sample role switch. A demo sign-out/change-user isn't a security test.

## 4. Make module prices and accounting boundaries explicit

**Current:** “From ₹499/month” can hide the specific module; all four cost ₹1,999 before GST.

**Write:** **“Choose the modules you need: Logistics ₹499; Inventory, Sales or Finance ₹799 each; the listed bundles have their own total. Prices before GST.”** Keep the actual checkout full charge visible. Tell users to review accounting/tax outputs with their professional; don't imply the tool replaces statutory judgment.

## 5. Keep untested integrations and offline claims testable

**Current:** Tally/Busy imports, deduplication and offline quick entry are advertised.

**Write beside onboarding:** **“Test a controlled import and recovery before using your live books.”** Show actual supported formats and error/duplicate states. Don't promise flawless syncing from a public paragraph. The provider app should stay labelled coming soon, not become a live signup promise.

## 6. Verify the phone's next action

**Current:** many departments/features compete for attention.

**Write the first mobile demonstration around:** **“What can ship next?”** Test 360 px, large text, keyboard, dropped-network recovery and a preview titled **“Founders Eye — one order trail for your team.”** No clipping/load failure was measured. Keep bank/stock/people data out of public screenshots.
