# FOUNDERSEYE — Measurement

**Target:10 real goods businesses.** Activation beside it: **businesses whose own authorized staff independently maintain a real stock/order/dispatch job**. Warehouses, departments and staff logins don't automatically multiply customer businesses.

## Day 0

Query actual businesses, authorized real items/orders, successful handoffs, critical stock/access errors and independent return use. The fictional browser-only demo establishes no customer baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding business/financial records. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/founderseye) rank, saved metric and timestamp; unknown isn't zero.

Events only for real supported actions: `business_onboarded`, `real_item_added`, `order_recorded`, `dispatch_confirmed`, `owner_reviewed` and `business_returned`. A sample invoice isn't an actual sale; a cash forecast isn't bank-confirmed money. Report real software subscription receipts separately from customer-business sales or payments.

## Four tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a real business, its own permitted actual workflow onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required during the free period. Several warehouses/staff at one company are one customer unless genuinely independent businesses are established. Founder-entered records are assisted service work.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × hypothetical 30% owner conversation ×50% controlled pilot ×50% independent use ≈3 businesses. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–5 is below ten.** These aren't measured SME-software rates. At 20 approaches inspect actual setup burden, reliable staff entry and repeat use rather than “interested” owners.
