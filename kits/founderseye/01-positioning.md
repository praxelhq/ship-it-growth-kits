# FOUNDERSEYE — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.founderseye.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/founderseye

**Your ten are real trading businesses, not warehouses or staff logins.** Start with a small distributor whose owner personally phones sales and dispatch to find out what can ship today. Choose one warehouse and a small set of real items, not a company-wide ERP replacement on day one.

The actual headline is **“See your whole business, from stock to cash, on one screen.”** The page offers **“Create a business account”** at `/app/#signup` and a separate [browser-only demo](https://www.founderseye.in/demo/) whose work isn't saved. The public offer is 30 days free, no card; monthly prices before GST are Logistics ₹499, Inventory/Sales/Finance ₹799 each, Inventory+ Logistics+ Sales ₹1,499 and all four ₹1,999. Confirm actual checkout before purchase.

## The first useful promise

> Follow one real order from available stock to dispatch, then see the next action without calling three people.

Bio: **One order trail for a small distributor's owner.**

The site claims reservations, dispatch, invoicing, accounts and role-limited views. Test those functions on controlled data before real operations; this research read the public offer and demo disclosure, not an authenticated company. A sample cash forecast isn't a banking fact, and generated GST figures still need the professional's review.

## Why change the existing process?

The owner may already use Excel/WhatsApp and Tally or Busy. Keep their real accounting process in place while testing a small operational trail; don't claim Tally imports or deduplication are flawless from a marketing paragraph. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 up to ten collaborators and can track manual order tasks, but doesn't establish the connected inventory/finance workflow. No live ERP competitor price was verified; don't repeat “fraction of a CRM” as a measured market comparison.

## The weakness to face first

Breadth makes onboarding expensive. An owner can admire the demo while nobody in the warehouse updates stock. The first win is an authorized staff member recording actual work independently, with the owner checking the result. Use the smallest approved operational subset and don't duplicate live accounting entries blindly.

“Database-enforced access” and Mumbai storage are operator claims, not an independent security audit. Verify role/warehouse boundaries and recovery with controlled records before client use. The warehouse-provider app is coming soon; don't recruit 3 PL providers as live customers for that future product.
