# SoundBuddy — 30-day calendar

Start native promotion after a clean-Mac installation and before-call test pass. Daily allowance: 20 minutes replies and ten minutes private support/compatibility logging, included within the times below. Never test by interrupting another person's meeting. Fifty invitations = five each on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29. Six own-feed posts = days 4,9,13,18,24,29.
