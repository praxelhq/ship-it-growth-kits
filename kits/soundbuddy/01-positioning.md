# SoundBuddy — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.soundbuddy.tech/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/soundbuddy

**Start with Apple silicon Mac owners who deliberately want their Mac microphone selected before joining a call.** They use a Bluetooth headset for listening, have checked their call app's own input setting, and are comfortable evaluating an early development build. Don't invite Intel owners or promise a fix midway through a live call.

The current headline is **“SoundBuddy”**, followed by **“Your Mac. With feeling.”** The practical supporting promise is **“Keep your preferred microphone selected.”** The buying action says **“Get SoundBuddy”**; existing buyers have **“Download latest”** at `/download/`. The public browser experience explicitly says **“Interactive simulation · sample devices · no access to your Mac.”** That is a useful preview, not a native hardware test.

Current launch pricing is **$5 USD or ₹449 in India before tax**, one payment, one Mac at a time, with online licence checks. The planned regular price is $9/₹799; don't call that a current charge or invent a launch deadline. Billing country determines the final total. Native purchase, licence delivery, clean-Mac installation and call behaviour were not independently completed in this research.

## Your useful sentence

> Choose your preferred Mac microphone before a call, keep it selected while SoundBuddy is open, and check your call app's input too.

Short bio: **Your preferred microphone, plus a little keyboard personality. Apple silicon · macOS 14+.**

Lead with one problem. Keyboard sounds are a second, enjoyable reason to try the preview; they shouldn't hide the compatibility and permission requirements. Global typing sounds need explicit Input Monitoring permission. The browser's test pad only responds inside the demo.

## What the user could do instead

[Apple's built-in Sound input settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-the-sound-input-settings-mchlp2567/mac) let a user select an input with no additional app purchase. If that solves their problem, say so. [SoundSource](https://rogueamoeba.com/soundsource/) is a broader Mac audio-control alternative; its [current store](https://rogueamoeba.com/soundsource/buy.php) lists $49 USD plus applicable tax and a free evaluation. Its single-user licence covers the current version on personally owned Macs. It is not a like-for-like keyboard-sound pack or a reason to claim SoundBuddy handles every audio workflow more cheaply.

Your potential advantage is a small preferred/fallback-input workflow with optional typing sounds in one menu-bar companion. Prove that on an actual supported Mac before promising it to strangers. Battery values appear only when macOS or a driver exposes them; there is no remaining-runtime estimate. Current shortcuts are local, not global. Proximity automation, ear detection and Magic Handoff are not implemented.

## The uncomfortable constraint

The page itself says clean-Mac installation and active-call switching remain unverified. A signed/notarized claim doesn't prove installation on someone else's Mac succeeded. Make your first release task a documented clean installation and a before-call input test, with OS/build/device recorded. Never tell someone to disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine to make a demo work.

The course target remains **1,000 individual acquisitions**, defined here as distinct successful native installations, with preferred-input or keyboard-sound activation alongside. Preview visitors and purchases are separate. A paid, narrow-platform early build will need much more reach than a few campus posts; the calendar exposes that gap rather than calling browser taps installs.
