# SoundBuddy — Video scripts

Record the actual native build on a supported Mac. Caption OS/build and label a simulation whenever it appears. Don't expose purchase email, licence key or private call audio. A phone voice recording and screen capture are enough.

## A · Before you join · 60 seconds · LinkedIn / product demo

| Time | Shot and screen text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Mac input field. **“Which mic will this call use?”** | “Headphones on. Which microphone did I choose?” |
| 2–12s | Show Apple silicon/OS/build, then actual default input. | “This is the supported Mac and build I'm testing, before joining a call.” |
| 12–28s | Choose an available preferred input and actual fallback in SoundBuddy. **“Preferred + fallback”** | “I choose my preferred input here. Input Lock works while SoundBuddy is open.” |
| 28–42s | Open the call app's settings without joining a real call. | “The call app can have its own setting. I check that separately.” |
| 42–53s | Show observed result and limit. **“Before-call test”** | “This is the result on this setup. It isn't a guarantee of switching safely during a live call.” |
| 53–60s | Requirements and URL. | “Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. Read the early-build notes at SoundBuddy dot tech.” |

If the input didn't change or the headset input is unavailable, keep that result visible and repair/test; don't cut to a staged success.

## B · The preview can't hear your Mac · 20 seconds · X / own feed

Hook, 0–2s: **“These aren't your connected devices.”** Show the browser's sample-device label. 2–8s, toggle keyboard sounds explicitly: “This is a browser simulation. It has no access to my Mac.” 8–15s, type in its test pad: “The sounds respond here after I turn them on.” 15–20s, show requirements: “Across-app sounds need the native app and explicit permission.” On-screen text: **“Preview ≠ native installation.”**

## C · A little typing personality · 25 seconds · short social clip

Hook, 0–2s: **“One paragraph. Three typing sounds.”** Show a non-sensitive synthetic sentence. 2–14s, choose Blue Click, Brown Tactile and Red Linear in the actual available library, typing the same short phrase at low volume. Say: “Choose the feel you like, or keep it off.” 14–21s, show the actual pause/quiet control: “Sound is optional. Check the current build's quiet behaviour before using a microphone.” 21–25s, show the browser-preview link: “Try the sample sounds first.” Do not claim these are recordings of physical switches; the site identifies synthesized demo sounds.

## D · Battery without a guess · 20 seconds · support reply

Hook: **“A missing battery number isn't a made-up zero.”** 0–8s, show a controlled device with no exposed reading if available. 8–15s: “SoundBuddy can show only what macOS or the driver exposes. It doesn't estimate hours remaining.” 15–20s: “Check your exact device before expecting a battery display.” If you lack that hardware, use a labelled explanatory slide rather than fabricate a device test.
