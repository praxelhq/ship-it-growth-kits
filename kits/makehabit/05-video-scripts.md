# MakeHabit — Video scripts

Use your own ordinary routine and actual saved record. Hide private habits and account details. Screen recording, phone voiceover, hard cuts and readable captions are enough.

## A. Tea to first action —60 seconds

**Where:** owned accounts, small-circle opening. **Hook:** “The tea already happens.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Your real familiar routine. | “The tea already happens. I want one small action to follow it.” |
| 8–20 s | Real guest/account choice and one stack setup. | “I choose the mode and create one pair. This is my own record, not the public checkmark demo.” |
| 20–35 s | Perform the ordinary action, then log it. | “I do the action first. The checkmark records it; it does not do it for me.” |
| 35–48 s | Show real later return if available, otherwise label an intended next-day test. | “The useful question is whether I remember on another day.” |
| 48–60 s | URL, mode boundary and accurate plan label. | “Try one pair at makehabit.me. Read the mode and plan limits before relying on the record.” |

## B. One pair —20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Don't start with ten new habits.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Ten-item example crossed out. | “Don't start with ten new habits.” |
| 5–13 s | One real cue/action pair. | “Choose one cue you already notice and one small action you can recognize as done.” |
| 13–20 s | Actual log and URL. | “Test the pair, not your ability to maintain a perfect screen.” |

## C. Demo versus record —25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “This click is only a demo.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Public checkmark, clearly labeled example. | “This click is only a demo. It is not proof I did anything.” |
| 7–17 s | Actual personal pair and completed ordinary action. | “My real record starts when I create a pair and do the action.” |
| 17–25 s | Mode label and URL. | “Guest and account records have different limits. Check which one you are using.” |

## D. Missed cue —25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “What happens when the cue doesn't happen?”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Real later-day record, including an honest miss. | “What happens when the cue doesn't happen?” |
| 7–18 s | Review the pair; change only through the real control. | “Maybe the cue does not fit my day. I want to learn that, not hide it behind a streak.” |
| 18–25 s | URL; one small next test. | “One pair, an honest record, another try.” |

Do not fabricate a later date or streak. Bank actual cue, setup, action, log and later return. Eight original clips are scheduled; change the true observation rather than promising a new personality.
