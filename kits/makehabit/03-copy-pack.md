# MakeHabit — Copy pack

Use an actual routine and an ordinary small action. No invented streaks or behaviour-change claims. Recipient fill-ins are intentional.

## 1. Organizer invitation

> Hi [name], I built MakeHabit around one simple pairing: after something I already do, I do one small next action.
>
> Could I show your adult study/work circle a free 20-minute exercise? Everyone chooses one familiar cue and one manageable action, then tests the record over a week. No public streak, purchase or health goal is required.
>
> The app has guest entry and a Google sign-in option; an advertised ₹199 lifetime plan is separate. I can show you the exercise first, including what guest storage does and does not keep.

## 2. Permissioned group/bulletin message

> After your morning tea, what is one small thing you want to remember?
>
> Try a one-anchor exercise: choose a routine you already have, attach one ordinary small action, and log what actually happens. Not a new life plan or a public challenge.
>
> I built MakeHabit. You can try without signing in or choose Google sign-in; read the actual storage/plan limits before relying on it. https://makehabit.me/

Only the organizer adds an agreed session time. Do not forward into unrelated groups without permission.

## 3. Personal seven-day test

> I am testing a small cue/action tracker. Would you try one ordinary pair for seven days and tell me what happens on the day the cue does not occur? I do not need a perfect streak or a testimonial.

If they agree:

> https://makehabit.me/ . Start with one pair, not ten. The guest option is separate from a synced account; check which one you are using before expecting your record on another device.

## 4. First video caption

> My tea already happens. Reading does not always follow.
>
> MakeHabit lets me put the two in one simple record: after this cue, do this small action. The video shows my actual setup, not proof of a new habit or a perfect streak.
>
> I built it. Try the guest flow if you want to understand the interaction first: https://makehabit.me/

Change the routine to your real one. Do not claim a seven-day result on the first day.

## 5. Later-day caption

> The useful test is not how satisfying the checkmark feels. It is whether I remember the action on another day.
>
> Today I am recording what actually happened, including a missed cue. I want the tracker to make the routine clearer, not give me another reason to feel behind.

Show an actual later date; do not create fake streak evidence.

## 7. Useful reply

> Before adding five new routines, choose the cue you already notice. Then make the next action small enough to recognize as done. If the cue does not happen every day, the tracker should record that honestly rather than invent consistency.

Disclose ownership if discussing the tool; no unsolicited link under unrelated posts.

## 8. Opted-in follow-up

> Did the pair help you remember the action, or did you only remember to open the tracker? Either answer is useful. You do not need to share the private action, and I will not quote your reply without permission.
