## Composition and coordination

Three sessions, twenty personal invitations, eight clips and one listing model **24–116 accounts before overlap**. That is not a sufficient forecast for 1,000. At an assumed 25% visit-to-account rate the target needs 4,000 qualified unique visitors. Measure after the first fifty visits; do not count every demo click or anonymous guest return as a new signup.

Ask satisfied users if they want to show their own simple cue/action pair to a friend; no forced sharing or public streak pressure. Real organizer introductions are the expansion route after the first seven-day evidence, not a purchased contact list.

Your shared-circle window is Days 10–13, after Terms Apply's deadline exercise and before Ughfix's first-step session. Use different organizers and actual calendar dates. One primary invitation per organization per seven days keeps the cohort useful rather than exhausting the same people.
