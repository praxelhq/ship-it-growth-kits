# MakeHabit — Positioning

**Live:** https://makehabit.me/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/makehabit

**Your job is one remembered action after an existing routine, not a promise to rewire someone's brain.** The app's interaction can be demonstrated. Its broad habit/science claims were not independently validated in this review.

The [current headline](https://makehabit.me/) is “Turn everyday routines into lasting habits.” Rendered controls say “Continue with Google,” “Try without signing in” and “Plans (₹199 Lifetime).” The page demonstrates morning tea followed by reading and a tactile checkmark. Public page/controls were read on 29 October 2026; Google authentication, guest persistence and paid entitlement were not tested.

## Start with this person

An adult student or early-career worker who already makes morning tea, ends a work session or puts their phone on charge, but forgets one small action they would like to repeat. Choose a non-sensitive ordinary action such as opening a book or writing tomorrow's first task. Do not begin with a complete fitness, sleep or mental-health program.

The first promise is simple: “After the thing I already do, I will do this small next thing.” [James Clear's habit-stacking article](https://jamesclear.com/habit-stacking) is a real explanation of that structure. It is not evidence that this app has a specific success rate or that the author endorses it.

## Pitch

> Attach one small action to a routine you already have, then keep an honest record of doing it.

Short version:

> One familiar cue. One small next action.

Your wedge is the first seven days with one anchor/action pair. Activation is creating that pair and logging the first actual action. Retention is an independently logged action on another day, not clicking the public demo checkmark repeatedly.

## Alternatives and actual offer

| Alternative | Verified cost / job | Your reason to exist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Loop Habit Tracker](https://loophabits.org/) | Free/open-source Android habit tracking, reminders and charts | Demonstrate the anchor/action relationship and low-friction first log; a streak grid alone is not new. |
| [Terms Apply](https://termsapply.me/) | Free one-task commitment record with optional email reminders | It ends at a deadline/report. Your job repeats after the same cue. |
| A paper card beside the existing routine | No new app purchase required if the person already has paper | Your app must make logging/returning easier, not add planning work. |

The page advertises a ₹199 lifetime plan. Final payable tax, exact limits and cross-device entitlement were not verified. Do not call the whole product free forever or assume “lifetime” includes every future feature. Guest entry exists without sign-in; explain its actual persistence before asking someone to rely on it.

## The weakness

The public demo can be satisfying without changing a real routine. A seven-day streak shown in the illustration is not seven days of user evidence. Follow your first ten willing testers through a later day and ask what happened when the cue did not occur. A missed day should not erase the useful record or become a guilt campaign.

Keep **1,000 signups** as the cohort target. Count real new accounts consistently; report guest trials and first-stack users separately, not as interchangeable accounts. `02` shows the initial reach gap honestly.
