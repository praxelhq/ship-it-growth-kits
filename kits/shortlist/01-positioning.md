# Shortlist — Positioning

**Live:** https://shortlistproperty.app/ · **Category:** Brokerage buyer shortlists · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/shortlist

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Shortlists your buyers actually open.”** **“Request access →”** leads to `#request-access`; the page says small-group onboarding in Gurugram. Accounts, buyer briefs, private links and checkout were not exercised. Access request is not immediate activation.

Start with independent Gurugram brokerage businesses serving actual residential buyer briefs. The responsible owner can review developer/official sources, has permission to process the buyer's requirements and controls what is sent. Start with one real brief and at most four supported projects; don't sell national coverage or investment returns.

**Pitch: Turn a reviewed buyer brief into a source-backed project comparison the advisor can explain.** Short version: **Four relevant options, with reasons and visible gaps.**

[Pricing](https://shortlistproperty.app/pricing):₹499/month per advisor. First ten pay ₹99 for their first three monthly payments; next twenty ₹299 for three; fourth payment onward ₹499. Current static availability says 10 of 10 left but actual capacity is unverified. Approval precedes plan selection inside the app. Monthly billing through Dodo; cancel with paid-month access. Taxes not established, so don't invent an all-in amount or free trial.

Homepage counts 27 projects,23 HARERA-checked and five developers are dated site claims, not independently audited coverage. Current example prices/possession dates are not buying advice. HARERA matching is a source check, not assurance of project quality, title, future delivery, suitability or appreciation. Preserve “Not published” instead of guessing an absent price/date. Buyer and professional must confirm current facts before a property decision.

The free alternative is an advisor-reviewed [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) comparison with developer sources, dates, unknowns and buyer priorities. Shortlist must earn adoption through phone clarity and explainable matches, not promises every buyer opens or purchases. Private/unindexed/revocable links are public claims requiring tests; unindexed doesn't mean confidential or unforwardable.

**Target:10 real brokerage businesses**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Own authorised brief, independent recent use, real operation and privately known responsible name/phone. Ten advisors at one brokerage, property records and buyer links are not ten businesses.
