# Shortlist —Measurement

**Target:10 real brokerage businesses**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Four tests: genuine operation; own authorised buyer brief onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known responsible owner name/phone. Ten paid advisor seats at one firm are one business. Buyers, projects and share links are separate.

Signup is an approved advisor/workspace account, not an access request. Activation: a real brokerage reviews its own brief, checks supported match reasons/sources and prepares a correct buyer comparison. Track share delivery/opening separately; neither proves suitability, purchase or revenue. Assisted use isn't independent use.

Private baselines unknown: real firms, requests/approvals, own briefs, source reviews, supported matches, unknown fields, withdrawn links, independent recent use and real payments/refunds. Save rank/reading/period/sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/shortlist); unknown isn't zero and rolling readings aren't cumulative.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before tagging. Exclude buyer identities, budgets, phone numbers, private briefs/links, advisor accounts and payments. Aggregate events: access_requested, workspace_approved, own_brief_ready, source_reviewed, shortlist_created, share_opened, link_withdrawn, independent_advisor_return. Do not record private share URLs in analytics. A source badge isn't evidence of current legal/title safety.

| Review | Diagnostic |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches. Request→approval→own brief; diagnose onboarding capacity before more invites. |
| Day 14 | Twenty approaches. Source freshness and accurate matches, not maximum four cards. |
| Day 21 | Twenty-eight approaches. Independent recent business use, separate seats and buyer sessions. |
| Day 28 | Thirty-six approaches; audit all four tests and firm identity. |
| Day 30 | Forty planned. Actual brokerage businesses against ten, activation, source errors, actual revenue/hours and gap. |

Assumed 40 ×50% ×60% ×70%=8.4 firms; at 25% conversation,4.2. Three posts/two intros upside only. Reforecast after eight/twenty approaches and actual approval capacity. Keep useful one-off briefs separate if the firm fails final-week independent use.

Weekly quality log: stale price/date, unsupported match, wrong source status, access/withdrawal failure. No promised appreciation, rental yield, regulatory endorsement, guaranteed opens or invented buyer success. Any real outcome needs permission, current facts and attribution limits.
