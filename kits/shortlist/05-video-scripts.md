# Shortlist — Video scripts

Use a fictional buyer with no real name/contact. Public project information needs current source/date; no purchase recommendation, returns forecast or private brochure. Record screen and phone voice.

## A. Why these options? —65 seconds · owner follow-up

0–3 s: fictional brief beside brochures. **“Why did these projects make the shortlist?”**

3–18 s: actual sample or verified internal brief entry. “Budget, BHK, sector, timing and must-haves come first.”

18–32 s: supported match reason and source/date. “The reason must follow from a checked fact.”

32–47 s: Not published field. “A missing price stays missing. Confirm current information before advising the buyer.”

47–57 s: actual phone comparison and withdrawal only if tested. “A clearer page still needs careful sharing.”

57–65 s: manual-access/current-plan boundary and URL. Never splice a sample into a claimed working customer account.

## B. Gap hook —20 seconds · LinkedIn

0–3 s: Not published price. **“A blank price is better than a made-up one.”**

3–13 s: source/date and confirmation question. “Find the missing fact before turning it into a recommendation.”

13–20 s: fictional brief label and URL. No sales/return guarantee.

## C. Privacy hook —25 seconds · advisor onboarding

0–3 s: sample private link. **“Unindexed isn't unforwardable.”**

3–16 s: actual safe sharing/withdrawal test or clearly labelled checklist. “Check who receives the page and what happens after withdrawal.”

16–25 s: fresh-device result and URL. “Protect the buyer's brief through the whole handoff.” No fabricated security certification.
