## Forecast and workload

Planning assumptions, not bridal-market benchmarks:

- 20 invitations × 50% interest × 60% planning start = **six sessions**.
- Six posts × 300–1,000 impressions × 2% visits × 30% planning start ≈ **11–36 sessions**.
- One accepted small session: 12 invited × 50% attend × 50% start = **three**, otherwise zero.

Total **17–45 first planning sessions before overlap**, below 1,000. Actual consultation confirmation is another step; if half request and 60% of requests are accepted, 30% of planning sessions become accepted appointments, roughly five to fourteen before overlap/capacity. Delivering those 30-minute sessions takes about 2.5–7 hours, plus preparation and follow-up. Use actual rates and staff capacity, not these guesses, before accepting slots.

At an assumed 30% visitor-to-planning-start rate, 1,000 starts needs about 3,334 qualified visits. If all became consultations, delivery alone would be 500 hours. Therefore scale the genuinely working self-serve preview and confirm only serviceable appointments; don't call a waiting request a delivered consultation.
