Start with one function and a small brief, not all brides, last-minute guests or a promise every body should fit a label.

## The honest limit

The site advertises straight-from-atelier value and 100% handcrafted work, but the current collection isn't publicly inspectable. Publish real permitted piece images, materials, price/quote process, alteration scope and achievable dates before a broad shopping campaign. A requested appointment isn't an accepted order; a sample look isn't inventory.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, using the no-account equivalent **distinct first supported styling/planning sessions**, with **a useful shortlist reviewed or a consultation actually confirmed** alongside. This isn't 1,000 garment orders. One thousand 30-minute consultations alone require 500 staff-hours before fitting or fulfilment, so don't route every visitor into a human promise you can't serve.
