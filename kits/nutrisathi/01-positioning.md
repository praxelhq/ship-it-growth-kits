# NutriSathi — Positioning

**Live:** https://nutrisathi.app/ · **Category:** Consumer food information · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/nutrisathi

## Do not sell a photograph as a food test

Actual Chrome on 29 October 2026 showed **“The truth about your food.”** and **“Continue with Google”**, with **“Watch the sample”** separately linked. `/app` redirected to the sign-in landing state. No Google sign-in, AI connection, food/health upload, real check, payment or expert booking was performed. The prominent tomato/cart results explicitly say **prepared example, no AI call**. Their 78/100, health values, local prices and travel assumptions are not measured results for a shopper's item.

Pricing says Free **₹0**, planned Plus **₹99/month** and Pro **₹199/month coming soon**. During launch, Plus uses a fourteen-day trial or an advertised five-friend/75-day reward; paid plans are not open yet. Do not call a referral reward earned, billing live or AI allowance unlimited. The experts page says the first experts are being onboarded: no available qualified consultation was verified. ABHA linking and insurer rewards are future items.

Start with an adult grocery shopper who wants to inspect a food-information result and its evidence/uncertainty before deciding whether the tool is useful. Start without a blood report, exact address, selfie or personal diet history. The first task is a generic food/package check, compared with the original information, not medical/allergy advice or a “healthier than your friend” score contest. SachLabel's label-reading invitation needs a different organizer.

**Pitch:** Explore food information with visible sources, estimates and limits; check the original before relying on it.

**Bio:** Food information, clearly labelled uncertainty. Inspect the sample first.

[Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/) is a free/open collaborative food database and a stated data source, not an endorsement. Reading the original package is another no-tool-cost alternative. NutriSathi's difference must be a useful explanation across input/context, not a claim that it can establish freshness, contamination, authenticity, pesticide residues or allergy safety from a photo. Its own privacy page explicitly excludes those conclusions.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, with first real food-check result inspected against source information beside it. Sample exploration is not real activation. Privacy says photos/descriptions and relevant entered context pass through NutriSathi's server to a provider; browser-held keys do not mean no server processing. Health-profile and saved-thumbnail descriptions differ across sections: clarify the actual data modes before collecting private reports.
