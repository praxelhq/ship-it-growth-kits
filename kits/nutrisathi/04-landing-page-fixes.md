# NutriSathi — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Make the certainty match the evidence

Actual headline **“The truth about your food.”** and supporting reference to **“this tomato”** from **“that dark store”** can imply item/store-specific knowledge. The page's own limits say local points are typical estimates and a photo cannot detect residues. Suggested replacement: **“Understand food information — and what a photo cannot tell you.”** Supporting copy: **“Explore label/public-data explanations. Local context is estimated, not a test of your item or a claim about a specific store.”**

## 2. Separate sample from real sign-in

Actual **“Continue with Google”** is separate from **“Watch the sample”**; `/app` redirected to sign-in state. Keep **“Explore a prepared example”** as the low-friction first action. Verify real sign-in→non-health input→provider→result privately before a broad live-check claim. Keep sample labels on every exported crop; do not say a prepared result analysed someone's photo.

## 3. Remove the health-score competition

Current sample sharing invites “Beat my 78” and mentions a blood-report match. Replace default sharing with **“I explored a prepared NutriSathi example. It shows food information and estimates, not a test of my groceries.”** Link to the public sample without health values, location or referral pressure. A source-checking task is more useful than a personalised-health scoreboard.

## 4. Reconcile plans, experts and data modes

Free ₹0, planned Plus ₹99/month/trial/referral and forthcoming Pro ₹199 should be visibly distinct from open billing. Experts are being onboarded; do not promise a booked professional until actual availability/credentials are verified. Privacy labels health profiles differently from pricing and gives differing saved-thumbnail descriptions across modes. Publish a consistent actual mode table: local history, cloud saves, provider processing and delete controls. No invented device-only guarantee.

## 5. Keep first use non-sensitive

Offer a generic food/package input without requiring a lab report, exact location, selfie or API credential to understand the product. Explain chosen-provider/shared-allowance limits and what is sent before processing. Existing Clarity/PostHog consent/private-screen exclusions are described in privacy: verify them rather than adding a tag. Test mobile loading, original-label comparison and unknown/error states. Do not fill missing information with a confident safety verdict.
