# NutriSathi — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one new non-test account through the actual sign-in flow. Sample viewers, glossary views, referral clicks and provider-key entries are not accounts. **Activation:** a first real non-health food check completes and the person inspects its original/source information and uncertainty. A prepared example is not activation; a high score is not proof the food is safe or suitable for a condition.

## Baseline and data

Record actual accounts, real checks, prepared-sample events, source inspection, returns, provider-limit errors, paid entitlements if available and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/nutrisathi. Public research did not establish private totals. Existing privacy describes consent-controlled Clarity/PostHog with sensitive-page exclusions; verify those before adding anything. Do not store food photos, health values, goals, locations, API keys or private profile content in growth analytics/replay.

| Day 30 | 4 posts total, actual listing/hours/outcomes. | Report target gap and real activation, not health-score volume. |

The forecast assumes 28–65 distinct accounts, below 1,000. No medical effect, allergy safety, freshness, contamination/residue detection, current store price or professional endorsement was established. A referral reward is not earned until the actual rules and delivery are verified; do not pressure five friends or collect reports to manufacture activation.
