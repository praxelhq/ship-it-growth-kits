# NutriSathi — Video scripts

Use the prepared public sample with its label visible. No personal health report, API key, exact address, real medical conclusion or untested live AI output. Use a generic package without people/identifying details for a privately verified live demo later.

## A. What this result knows — 35 seconds, homepage/product invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | “Prepared example” label. | “This is an example, not a food test.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual sample information and source/estimate labels. | “NutriSathi explores food information. I want the difference between source data and estimates to stay visible.” |
| 14–25 s | Actual local-estimate caveat. | “It does not know the stock in a particular store, and a photo cannot test residues.” |
| 25–31 s | Limit statement, no blood values. | “This is not medical or allergy advice.” |
| 31–35 s | Sample URL. | “Inspect the sample before signing in.” |

## B. Check the original — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Generic package information. | “Keep the original in view.” |
| 3–14 s | Product explanation beside its actual source. | “AI can misread or infer. Check the label or source before relying on a result.” |
| 14–20 s | URL/sample label. | “Explore NutriSathi's prepared example.” |

## C. No report required — 20 seconds, organizer invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Public sample entry. | “Feedback needs no health report.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual sample, private fields absent. | “Tell me whether the information, estimates and unknowns are clear. You do not need to share personal health details.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Start with the sample at NutriSathi.” |

Do not crop the sample/estimate labels, turn a score into proof or claim a photo verifies food safety. No frightening residue imagery or promised health transformation.
