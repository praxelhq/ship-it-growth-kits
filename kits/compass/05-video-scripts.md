# Compass — Video scripts

Use the labelled public sample, not someone's application. No paid session or successful outcome was tested. Phone screen recording and your own voice are enough.

## A. Try the work — 45 seconds, homepage/career-club invitation

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Product/consulting/data choices. | “Try the work before the title.” |
| 3–15 s | Actual sample decision; mark “Sample”. | “Compass starts with a small decision from a career path, like choosing what a product team should build.” |
| 15–28 s | Select a real sample choice and reflection. | “Then notice what interested you. This is reflection, not a personality score.” |
| 28–39 s | Show packages and actual inclusions. | “Paid packages add prepared experiences and personal support. The free sample helps you understand the idea first.” |
| 39–45 s | URL; 18+. | “You make the career decision. Try the preview.” |

## B. No career score — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | “No career score.” | “You do not need another label.” |
| 3–14 s | Public reflection sample. | “Try a decision. Notice whether the work held your interest. Use that to choose a next experiment.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “That is the idea behind Compass.” |

## C. What you pay for — 25 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Package comparison. | “What is the paid part?” |
| 3–16 s | Actual prepared-work/feedback/call rows. | “Prepared experiences and personal feedback, with calls in the packages that include them. The sample is free.” |
| 16–22 s | No-guarantee section. | “It is not a placement promise or industry mentoring.” |
| 22–25 s | URL. | “Check the package before applying.” |

Keep prices on screen only while current. Do not film a fake debrief or claim someone found their ideal career.
