# Compass — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Make the service and pilot unmistakable

Current headline: **“A better way to navigate career decisions.”** Current CTA: **“Start Exploring”**. Packages ₹1,999/₹3,999/₹5,999 sit alongside a separate ₹999 pilot. Keep the headline, add **“Try prepared decisions from the paths you’re considering. Reflect with personal support; you make the call.”**

Label the pilot **“Temporary founding pilot — ₹999 one-time”**, and keep its exact inclusions separate from Career Clarity. Remove it when unavailable rather than leaving a cheaper stale offer beside packages.

## 2. Separate preview, application and payment

Use **“Try the free sample”** for `#preview`, **“Apply for your chosen package”** for the actual application and **“Pay for your selected package”** only after the person's choice is clear. The `/pay` page exists and lists all offers; clean-browser test the real form→payment path without charging during research. Do not call it tested merely because its page loads.

Supporting copy: **“Applying is free. Payment is your choice. We confirm path coverage within two working days; if we cannot cover it, the refund policy applies.”** Match the actual delivery/refund wording rather than promising unconditional refunds after completed work.

## 3. Show the real output

Keep every product/consulting/data interaction labelled **“Sample”**. Add a clearly marked sample reflection log/decision brief with invented benign content, not a supposed former student. Explain which package includes written feedback, calls and follow-up. The people running calls are the Compass team, not assumed practising industry mentors.

## 4. Make capacity visible

State a real delivery window before accepting money. If full, offer an honest update list instead of a payment link for an unavailable start. Do not manufacture “three seats left”. Personal preparation and multiple calls are operational commitments, not infinitely scalable app features.

## 5. Test trust and mobile

Keep age 18+, no placement guarantee, privacy, cancellation and refund links beside application/payment. Check long package comparisons on narrow screens, form errors, confirmation message and payment return. The site says Clarity excludes typed inputs and payments: verify that behaviour instead of repeating it as a completed audit. Keep private career reflections out of replay and public screenshots.
