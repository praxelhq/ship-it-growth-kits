# Compass — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Proposed signup definition: a new adult's completed, consented Compass application, if accepted by the course. Record any actual account mechanism separately. Updates-list joins, sample attempts, paid clients and completed packages remain separate counts. Do not count a parent and adult student twice for one service.

**Activation:** first prepared decision completed with a reflection. Service delivery: contracted feedback, brief and any calls delivered according to the purchased package. Neither an application nor a payment proves that work happened. No guarantee of career clarity, employment or a chosen role follows from these numbers.

## Day 0 baseline

Record actual applications, updates-list members, accepted paths, paid packages, current clients, overdue work, marketing sources and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/compass. Unknown private values are not zero. Check existing Clarity exclusion of typed fields/payment screens before adding any tool. Keep reflection text, payment IDs and private career choices out of replay and reports.

The 20–55 application planning range is below the course target. Paid conversion and capacity are unproven. If you exceed delivery capacity, pause sales rather than sell future service as already delivered. Record cancellations/refunds honestly; ask clients for feedback without making it a condition of promised work.
