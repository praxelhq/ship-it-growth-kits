# Ever Minutes — Measurement

**Assigned B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count confirmed real shopper accounts once. Report **scheduled rewards revealed**, **voluntary redemption confirmations** and **repeat shoppers** beside it. Brand applications/claims are supply metrics, not shopper accounts; the assigned target is retained rather than quietly changed.

Private totals weren't accessed. The Wall's initial zero/loading combination is not a verified database baseline. Record the settled actual state.

| Baseline | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed shopper accounts / last 7 days | Authentication records, excluding tests |
| Approved brands and actual approved rewards | Operating records, not applications/sample stalls |
| Unique shoppers with a scheduled reveal | Product events using server state |
| Voluntary redemption and offer complaints | Opt-in feedback/brand records, no invented sales attribution |
| Traffic/referrers | Existing consented analytics / [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Course metric/rank/time | [Profile](https://shipped.money/p/ever-minutes) |

Check existing Clarity before adding scripts. Mask emails, sign-in codes, claim details, reward tokens and payment fields. Track `shopper_confirmed`, `approved_stall_viewed`, `reminder_set`, `eligible_reveal_completed`, `brand_site_opened`, `redemption_confirmed`, `repeat_reveal`. Opening the brand site isn't a sale.

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | At least one approved offer can actually be revealed/redeemed | If not, pause consumer launch and repair supply. |
| Day 14 | Willing shoppers understand timing and code limits | Fix sign-in/window/terms before more clips. |
| Day 21 | Shoppers return for useful offers, not empty countdowns | Improve offer quality and brand fit, not fake scarcity. |
| Day 30 | Accounts against 1,000; reveals/redemptions/supply beside it | Report actual target gap and trial-expiry constraints. |

At 30% visitor-to-shopper-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 relevant unique visits. The small scheduled plan may yield dozens or low hundreds; three brands' overlapping audiences must be deduplicated. Don't add all daily reveals as new users.

Premium booking isn't open and the paid rewards plan is unavailable. Free claims never become paid subscriptions automatically. Plan interest/test transactions aren't revenue, and operator-review terms aren't final commitments. Course 24-hour traffic readings overlap and are not cumulative shoppers.
