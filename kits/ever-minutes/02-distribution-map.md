# Ever Minutes — Distribution map

**Supply first, then the brand's own willing shoppers.** Don't buy a premium minute or run ads to compensate for unverified offers.

| Route | Scheduled action / boundary | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten small-brand conversations | Personally invite ten brands whose owners you know or can approach through a public business contact. Aim to verify three willing founding stalls, no purchase required. | 10 conversations → 2–4 willing brands assumed; actual approval/reward readiness required. Not shopper signups. | #1 |
| Three approved brands' own channels | Each brand chooses one truthful announcement to its own audience after an actual offer is tested. No email-list sharing. | 3 × 100 relevant readers × 10–25% visit × 50% signup = 15–38 shopper accounts. | #2 |
| Twenty willing shopper testers | Invite them to a real scheduled minute; state IST and actual offer terms. | Up to 20 accounts; attendance/reveal not guaranteed. | #3 |
| Four own-account clips | Actual reveal/terms/return process on Days 8, 13, 20, 26, only when supply gate passes. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 2–72 accounts. | #4 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Honest supply/offer-quality lessons on Days 11/24. | 2 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 2–18 accounts. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker feedback after a usable real reveal; current rules/slot checked, no votes requested. | 0–20 accounts, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No paid D2C newsletter, influencer, discount subsidy or premium reservation is in budget. A brand's existing newsletter inclusion is its voluntary choice, not access you can assume. No scraping buyers, importing customer lists or misrepresenting approval.

At 30% visitor-to-shopper-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 relevant unique visits. The initial plan may yield dozens or low hundreds, and stock/offer quality must support any larger reach. If the first brand cannot deliver a redeemable offer, fix that before another Reel. Keep outreach to small consumer brands distinct from the founder-pitch cluster's fundraising clinics.
