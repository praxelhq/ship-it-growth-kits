# Ever Minutes — Positioning

**Live:** https://everminutes.live/ · **Category:** Timed brand-deal discovery · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/ever-minutes

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“A bazaar where every minute has a deal.”** The CTAs are **“Browse the stalls”** and **“Set up your stall.”** But a full consumer acquisition month needs a real approved offer to reveal and redeem first.

The [pricing page](https://everminutes.live/pricing) says the first 30 approved brands can claim an eligible free founding minute and 30 days of rewards, with no automatic charge. Premium exact hours are proposed at ₹5,000/month or ₹10,000 for noon/midnight, but paid booking is not open until verification/testing. The paid daily-rewards plan is unavailable. Don't advertise premium purchasing as live.

Your first consumer is an adult who already likes one small brand and wants to see its genuinely useful daily offer. Don't begin by asking strangers to revisit an empty wall for unspecified rewards.

**Pitch:** Find a brand's scheduled IST minute, return for its approved offer and check the actual redemption terms.

**Short version:** One brand. One daily minute. A real offer to inspect.

## The wedge

One approved brand with an authentic, redeemable offer can introduce its existing willing audience to a single reveal. Recruit three such brands manually before a broad consumer launch. This is supply preparation, not a change to your assigned B2C target.

[GrabOn](https://www.grabon.in/) publicly lists coupons/deals without a browsing fee. It wins when someone wants an offer now. Ever Minutes must earn the scheduled return through a worthwhile brand/offer, not by claiming a countdown makes every coupon valuable. A reveal does not reserve stock or guarantee redemption.

## The uncomfortable bit

The Wall fetch displayed “0 approved brands” alongside “Fetching,” so live inventory was not established in this research. Do not infer a universally empty database, but don't invent active brands either. The [terms page](https://everminutes.live/terms) is explicitly for owner review with unfinished commitments; resolve that before public paid booking.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 confirmed shopper accounts, with genuine scheduled reveals and voluntary redemption feedback beside it. No brand reservation click, fake countdown or test payment belongs in that count.
