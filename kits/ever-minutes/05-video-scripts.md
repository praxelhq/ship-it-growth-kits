# Ever Minutes — Video scripts

Only use real approved brand content with permission. No invented reward, fake countdown, test purchase or guaranteed saving.

## A. Real reveal demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“A countdown only matters if the offer is real.”**

2–15s, open actual approved stall and terms. “Here is the brand, its scheduled IST minute and eligibility.”

15–30s, show actual sign-in/reveal state using an authorized test account. “This is the real window, not an edited timer.”

30–45s, read code limits and open brand redemption page without purchasing. “The brand controls stock and final terms.”

45–60s: “A reveal isn't a reservation. Check before buying. everminutes.live.” If no live offer exists yet, record a clearly labelled prototype walkthrough instead and don't claim a shopper launch.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “Would you return for this offer?”

2–10s: show the actual offer/terms, not just the ticking clock. “That's the first test.”

10–15s: actual next IST window and URL, only when confirmed.

## C. Honest pricing — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Free founding doesn't mean every plan is free.”

2–15s: show founding trial, premium-not-open and rewards-unavailable labels. “These are separate offers with different limits.”

15–25s: current pricing URL. “Read the live offer; don't buy a promise the product isn't selling yet.”

## D. Missed-minute clip — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Missed it? The timer shouldn't pretend otherwise.”

2–12s: actual closed state and next scheduled window. “Clear timing is better than fake urgency.”

12–20s: terms and URL. No pressure to purchase something the viewer doesn't need.
