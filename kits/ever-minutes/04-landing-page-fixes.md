# Ever Minutes — Landing page fixes

Based on homepage, Wall, pricing and terms pages checked 29 October. No claim, account, reveal or payment was submitted.

## 1. Establish real inventory and final offer terms before traffic

The Wall showed “0 approved brands” while still fetching. Verify the actual settled state and at least one approved, redeemable reward through sign-in, scheduled reveal and brand-site redemption. If inventory is not ready, show **“Founding stalls are being prepared. Shopper reveals aren't open yet.”** Don't call a loading failure proof the product is permanently empty.

## 2. Align paid availability across the site

Pricing says premium booking isn't open, paid daily rewards are unavailable, and terms are owner-review work. Use **“Premium booking not open yet”** on every premium action. Finish the actual operator/support/refund commitments before public paid booking; don't advertise an unfinished subscription.

## 3. Make the headline conditional on the real offer

Current headline: **“A bazaar where every minute has a deal.”** CTA: **“Browse the stalls.”**

If only a few stalls are approved, use **“Discover the brands opening their daily minute.”** Support: **“See real approved offers, their IST timing and redemption terms before you set a reminder.”** Don't imply all 1,440 minutes contain an active deal.

## 4. Explain founding versus premium versus rewards

Founding: eligible first 30 approved brands, free non-exact-hour minute and included 30-day rewards trial. Premium: separate monthly reservation, not currently sold. Paid daily rewards: unavailable; after the trial, reveals pause under the stated offer. Present these as three different things, not a single “free forever” promise.

## 5. Put shopper limits next to reveal

The terms say sign-in is needed and codes are shared; brand limits/stock/expiry apply. Copy: **“Sign in to reveal/save. A revealed code doesn't reserve stock. Check the brand's expiry, eligibility and final price before buying on its site.”** No invented savings guarantee.

## 6. Make IST and missed-window behaviour clear

Show the scheduled minute, date/time zone and server-based state. Copy: **“This reveal is closed now. Check the next scheduled IST minute.”** Test clock drift, refresh, slow network and sign-in return. Do not fake a reset countdown or reopen an expired reward for marketing.

## 7. Check mobile, loading and preview

At 390px, test Wall navigation, accessible time labels, sign-in and the actual offer page. Loading needs a clear retry/error, not endless ellipses. Preview: **“Ever Minutes — approved brand offers at scheduled IST minutes.”** No private tokens, claimed slot or unsupported discount in the image.
