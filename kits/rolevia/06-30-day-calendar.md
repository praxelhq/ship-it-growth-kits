# Role.via — 30-day execution calendar

**One or two things a day.** Open this every morning.

Your month has a shape no other kit in this cluster has: **you are not filling a funnel, you are filling rooms.** Every week has fixed GD sessions in it that you personally moderate, and those sessions are both the product and the marketing. Outreach that doesn't put nine people in a room on a specific evening is wasted effort.

You are also inside the Indian placement window, and GD elimination rounds are happening right now. **Verify your own campus's calendar this week** and pull everything forward if it's earlier than you think.

**Fix your weekly slots now and never move them: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8:00pm IST.** Everything below assumes them.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **Forty TPO emails in week 2, and twenty more in week 4.** Each yes is 60–300 students sharing a calendar. Nothing else in this kit comes close.
2. **Fixed Tuesday/Thursday slots that you moderate yourself, every week, without exception.** Nine strangers in an unhosted room produces one bad session and nine people who never return.
3. **Never showing an empty schedule** (`04` Fix 2). One look at a dead calendar loses the user permanently.
4. **Closing the gap between a TPO's yes and students in a room.** Do his logistics for him. This is where most of your yeses will otherwise die.
5. **Show-up rate above 60%.** Below that, your sessions degrade for everyone who did come, and your best channel starts producing bad experiences.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 17), **YouTube comments** (day 18), **Unstop** (day 20). 

**Never drop:** day 5 and every Tue/Thu session (the product only exists when a room runs), days 9 and 11 (the TPO emails), day 15 (converting the yeses). If you only have ten hours this month, spend them on those.
