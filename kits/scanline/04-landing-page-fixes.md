# Scanline — Landing page fixes

Browser checked 29 October 2026: **“Your car's data, in plain English.”** “Try a sample Auto-Scan” rendered a labelled synthetic four-fault report with fixed-rule explanation, AI off. No private import, optional key, export or vehicle connection was tested.

1. **Lead with the safe, working sample.** Current Upload-first entry can ask for private data before trust. Keep the headline, add **“See a synthetic scan first. Then inspect an export you are allowed to use.”** Primary **“Try a synthetic Auto-Scan”**, secondary **“Import my existing export”**. Repeat **“Read-only explanations; not repair authority.”**
2. **Distinguish built-in rules from optional AI.** Actual state reads “AI off · add your key”; sample says built-in fixed rules. Add **“Built-in explanations need no AI key. Optional deeper questions use Claude after you enable it.”** Before key entry disclose actual key storage, provider transmission and potential provider charges; verify implementation. Never place keys or scan contents in replay/analytics.
3. **Keep synthetic and real records unmistakable.** Existing sample labels are good. Keep **“Synthetic demo scan — not your vehicle”** on result/export previews and garage entries. Eight demo cars are examples, not customers or supported-model certification. A synthetic sample saved into Recent must not look like a new real user import.
4. **Narrow confidence and severity.** Current sample correctly distinguishes confidence about scan timing from uncertainty about causes. Add **“This grouping uses the recorded evidence. It does not confirm a repair or establish roadworthiness.”** Avoid treating “Probably old news” as proof a fault can be ignored. Safety-related findings need inspection; never promise safe driving from a report.
5. **Use existing logs, not dangerous collection prompts.** Some samples describe full-throttle pulls. Explanatory copy **“Use supplied synthetic samples or existing logs collected lawfully and safely. Do not operate this interface while driving.”** Do not send novices onto public roads to recreate a demo. A before/after graph alone cannot prove a repair when conditions differ.
6. **Data, mobile and preview.** The consent notice claims scans/cars/notes are never recorded; verify masking/exclusion and storage/export/deletion behavior before repeating it as fact. Title **“Scanline — understand an existing diagnostic export”**. Preview a synthetic graph without VIN. Test malformed CSV, units/time alignment, decimal separators, keyboard and chart labels on phone. No parsing-accuracy benchmark was measured.
