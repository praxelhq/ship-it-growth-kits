# Scanline — Video scripts

Use synthetic files, your own recording and no active driving. Do not show a real VIN, API key, plate or private owner record.

**A. Source to explanation, 45 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “A readable fault summary should still show its evidence.” Open the real “Try a sample Auto-Scan” action. Show the synthetic badge, four-fault grouping and one explanation. Say, “This is a synthetic scan. The built-in explanation uses fixed rules; AI is off.” Show the actual timing/confidence/source link. End: “Explanation, not a repair diagnosis. scanline.live.” Do not repeat product-generated mechanical assertions as independently verified facts.

**B. Sample is not your car, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “These four faults belong to a demo.” Show synthetic badge and masked identifier. Say, “A sample helps explain the screen. It isn't an owner record, a customer result or an accuracy test.” End: “Try the sample before private data.”

**C. Read-only, 20 seconds; accepted circle.** Hook: “This screen doesn't tune your car.” Show the public read-only notice, then sample graph. Say, “Scanline reads exports. It doesn't write values or replace inspection.” No cable connection, coding action or staged repair claim.

**D. Confidence in what?, 25 seconds; owned post.** Hook: “High confidence needs a noun.” Show the sample's qualified timing statement. Say, “Confidence about when a fault was recorded isn't certainty about its cause. Keep that limit beside the explanation.” End on the actual caveat. No safety outcome or money saved.
