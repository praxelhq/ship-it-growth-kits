# Rythmic Beats — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique non-test accounts, not exports. Report **users with a completed playable export** beside signups, then later-day returners who export a real final/rehearsal version. One editor exporting six times is one activated person, not six users.

## Day 0

Record current real accounts, seven-day visits, successful exports by duration, failure/cancellation counts and confirmed credit purchases. These private values were not available during research; unknown is not zero. Confirm [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is receiving public-page visits without audio filenames, private account fields or project content exposed. Record the actual [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/rythmic-beats) reading, date window and rank.

Instrument `signup_completed`, `studio_opened`, `track_imported`, `mix_previewed`, `export_started`, `export_completed`, `export_failed` and authoritative `credits_purchased`. Store only broad file format/duration categories, not songs or private files. A confirmation click is not a finished file; a credit purchase is not a successful export.

## Honest funnel

The plan has ten clinic invitations, ten private notes, six Shorts and one maker post. Its assumptions support tens of accounts, not a proven 1,000. At an assumed 25% visit→signup rate, 1,000 requires 4,000 relevant visits. At an assumed 50% account→successful-export rate, 1,000 accounts produce 500 activated editors. Neither rate is a benchmark; calculate yours after the first ten testers.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Signups, imports, successful exports and play-on-second-device feedback|If fewer than half export, watch the trim→export path before more invitations.|
|Day 14|Failure reason, format/device and free-allowance understanding|If price surprises people, fix the explanation before paid-credit promotion.|
|Day 21|Real rehearsal use and later-day editing without help|If exports are only throwaway tests, target actual deadlines and ask about final-version friction.|
|Day 30|New accounts, activated editors, returners, confirmed purchases/refunds and actual reach|State the gap to 1,000 and the channel producing usable performance files.|

Keep weekly actual rows for those counts, best referrer and hours. Under-minute free exports do not consume the six eligible free exports according to the current pricing page; audit the implementation against that promise. Never inflate revenue using a sample credit balance or count a failed export as activation.
