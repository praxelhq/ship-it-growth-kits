# Rythmic Beats — Video scripts

Use original loops or audio you have permission to demonstrate publicly. No paid editor is needed. Record the real export rather than a mock download animation.

## A. Tomorrow's rehearsal — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “Rehearsal is tomorrow. This cut is still awkward.”

| Time | Screen/text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
|0–10s|Play an abrupt join between your original loops|“I need one clean track, not another app tutorial.”|
|10–24s|Import 2 tracks and trim sections|“Keep the parts the choreography actually uses.”|
|24–40s|Add a crossfade and compare before/after|“Now compare the transition instead of guessing.”|
|40–52s|Show duration, actual allowance/price and export|“Check the duration and price before downloading.”|
|52–60s|Play the exported file in a separate player|“Sound-check the output. That's the finished job.”|

Post on your own channel and the approved rehearsal invitation. Do not say “free export” if the test account has already used its allowance for that duration.

## B. The bad cut — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “This is where the dance loses the room.” Shots: abrupt original transition, timeline handle, smoother transition, each about6 seconds. Say: “Trim the right section, blend the join, listen again. You don't need another effect.” End with the domain. Use as a Short with the audio level consistent between comparisons.

## C. One second changes the credit count — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “1:59 and 2:00 aren't the same export price.” Show the pricing explanation, two labelled demo durations and the actual confirmation. Say: “Completed minutes count after the free allowance. See the price before you export. Under a minute is free.” Don't invent a charge or pressure users to shorten artistic work merely to game pricing.

## D. The sound-check test — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “It worked in the editor. Does the file play?” Show export, open in a separate player, then listen through the join. Say: “Always check the downloaded file before you hand it to the sound desk.” This practical clip is the shareable lesson; it needs no commercial music or participant faces.
