# Rythmic Beats — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.rythmicbeats.me/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/rythmic-beats

Your first user is the student who has to hand a final dance track to the sound desk tomorrow: two or three songs, a strict duration, one awkward transition. **Sell the finished rehearsal mix, not an entire music-production studio.**

Current headline: “Make every beat a moment.” “Create your mix” leads to login with a return path to the studio; signup is offered there. The live page supports up to 5 MP3/WAV files, 50 MB each, and an export up to 5 minutes. It describes browser-local mixing/exporting. No account, audio import or export was submitted during this research.

**Pitch:** Trim your dance tracks, smooth the transitions and export one stage-ready MP3 or WAV in your browser.

**Bio:** One clean mix for tomorrow's rehearsal. No desktop studio to learn first.

The wedge is a 45–60-second rehearsal cut from authorized audio, then a longer final version. Do not imply you supply commercial songs or that exporting grants performance rights. Students bring audio they are allowed to use; public marketing demonstrations use original/authorized material.

The [pricing page](https://www.rythmicbeats.me/pricing) says the first 6 eligible completed exports are free; sub-minute clips are always free and do not consume those 6. Later, each completed minute uses 1 credit; 5 credits cost ₹99.90. A 1:59 mix counts 1 minute, 2:00 counts 2. Failed/cancelled exports do not count. State that unusually specific rounding rule accurately rather than saying every export costs₹20.

[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) is free open-source audio editing; [BandLab Studio](https://www.bandlab.com/) advertises a free music-creation studio. You cannot win on “free audio editing” alone. Your advantage is a focused upload→trim→blend→export route designed around a dance performance, if it actually saves the student effort. Show the exported file playing elsewhere; a beautiful timeline alone is not proof.

Your weakness is deadline risk: if a mix cannot be recovered, exported or played on the event laptop, the whole product failed. Prioritize a reliable saved project and sound-check workflow over more effects.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups** and report successful, playable exports beside accounts. Teacher business-operations products in the cohort have a different buyer; your early outreach goes to student performers, not studio owners handling fees.
