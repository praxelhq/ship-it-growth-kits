## Your first ten
Find a small content agency with two writers and an actual client tone document. Its editor reviews newsletters, landing copy and social captions every week. One writer repeatedly uses phrases the client dislikes. That is a narrow, recurring, observable job.

**Pitch:** Check each client draft against its own brand guide, see the specific slips, and review a rewrite before publishing.
**DM version:** A second pair of eyes for your client brand guide.

Start with one client brand and three real, nonconfidential drafts. Compare what the editor marks with what BrandEcho marks. Sell fewer missed rules, not an objectively correct voice score.

## Alternatives and weakness
[Grammarly Free](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) is $0 and checks general writing/tone. A [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) individual workspace is $0 for keeping a style checklist; team limits apply. Your difference must be traceability from a flagged sentence to the customer's actual rule. “AI rewrite” alone is not enough.

A consistency score is a model judgment. Your page says scoring makes on-brand no longer opinion; soften that. Test false alarms with the editor and let them reject suggestions. Do not promise elimination of review work or quote time saved until measured.
