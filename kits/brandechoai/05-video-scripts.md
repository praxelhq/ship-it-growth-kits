# BrandEchoAI — Video scripts

Record with a phone mic and screen recorder. Use your own synthetic client guide; label sample text throughout. No stock customer claims, paid editing or confidential drafts.

## A. Demo,65 seconds — LinkedIn and homepage
| Time | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Split rule and offending phrase. “Correct grammar, wrong brand.” | “This sentence is fine English. It breaks this brand's rule.” |
| 2–15 | Show one-page guide: plain language, no superlatives. | “I gave BrandEchoAI this sample guide, then pasted a deliberately unsuitable email.” |
| 15–35 | Run a real check; keep loading visible. Zoom a flag and its rule. | “It marks the issue and suggests an alternative. I can inspect the reason.” |
| 35–50 | Reject one suggestion; edit another. | “The editor decides. A bigger score does not make a claim true.” |
| 50–65 | Show finished paragraph and plan limit. | “One brand and 10 checks/month are free. Test it with a permitted guide at brandechoai.studio.” |

## B. Hook,20 seconds — LinkedIn / Shorts
0–2: film two printed client guides; text “Same phrase. Opposite rules.”
2–8: say “Client A wants playful. Client B bans jokes. One generic rewrite cannot satisfy both.”
8–16: screen recording of the same phrase evaluated against each permitted sample profile, only if that switch is supported.
16–20: “Give the checker your rules. Then check its judgment.” End on full URL. If profile switching is not available, show separate labelled recordings.

## C. Hook,25 seconds — editor outreach
0–2: text “Would your editor agree?”
2–10: show three flags from one draft, say “Two are useful. One is wrong.”
10–20: reject the wrong one visibly. Say “That is why this is a review tool, not an approval stamp.”
20–25: “I want editors to test it on one real guide.” Show free limit.

Record at 150% zoom, hard cuts, large captions. Keep one continuous take for the check and rejection. Post B Tuesday 09:00 IST, A Thursday 09:00 IST, C only after an actual mistaken flag is documented; otherwise use a clearly hypothetical editing exercise.
