# BrandEchoAI — Measurement

**Target:10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). A signup means an account created; activation means a customer loads their permitted brand guide and reviews a check on their own draft. Count businesses, not writers.

## Day 0
[Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is free; first check whether it is already installed. Confirm one consent-appropriate public session appears. Mask guideline text and drafts; keep product activity counts in your database, not replayed confidential copy.

Record current accounts, unique businesses, guide uploads, first checks, repeat checks, paid subscriptions, public-page sessions and source. These private values were not available to this kit; record them rather than assuming zero. Also record your current rank and timestamp at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/brandechoai); missing readings are unknown, not zero.

Events: signup_completed, brand_profile_ready, draft_check_completed, suggestion_accepted, suggestion_rejected, second_day_check. Store aggregate counts, not customer draft contents.

## Four tests, every counted customer
A real agency/business, not a friend doing a favour; its own guide/draft is onboarded; it used BrandEchoAI in the last seven days without you asking; you privately know the owner/editor's name and phone. Payment is not required. Keep that contact ledger outside this public repo.

## Checkpoints and decisions
| Day | Working checkpoint | If behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 |12 qualified approaches, 3 editor conversations, 2 actual trials. | Fewer than 3 conversations: revise buyer fit/opener before increasing volume. |
| 14 |5 activated businesses,2 independently returning. | Signups but no guide: watch onboarding and clarify confidentiality. |
| 21 |7 activated,5 qualifying active businesses. | Repeated use absent: ask what their next real editorial deadline is; don't send engagement nudges to inflate retention. |
| 30 |10 qualifying businesses; activation/repeat/paid beside headline. | Report honest shortfall and where the funnel broke. |

These are goals, not statistical norms. Publish the denominator: checks reviewed by an editor, helpful flags, rejected flags and time for the same review task. Ten editor-reviewed drafts are a small study, not proof of accuracy across every brand.

Each Sunday record unique approaches/replies/trials/active businesses by source, actual hours, and the common false flag. Drop a channel after 20 suitable approaches with no substantive response. Pause growth if users cannot remove confidential inputs or if suggestions create unsupported claims. Fix the review experience before buying more attention.
