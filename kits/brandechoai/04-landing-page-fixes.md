Add: **“Your rule: avoid unsupported superlatives. Flagged: ‘the best solution’. Suggested: ‘review drafts against your own guide’.”** Mark the example illustrative. A score alone is not proof.
2. **Make the first action precise.** Current “Start free.” Replace with **“Check a draft ·10 free checks/month”** and helper **“One brand profile. Review every suggestion before using it.”** After signup, land on guideline upload and sample draft, not a blank dashboard.
3. **Correct score certainty.** Current: a 0–100 score so on-brand stops being opinion. Replace with **“A consistent review aid, with reasons you can challenge. Your editor has the final say.”** Show rejected flags as well as accepted ones.
4. **Show data handling before upload.** Current instructions ask for PDF guidelines and copy. Add a factual summary matching your actual policy: what is sent to an AI provider, who can access it, retention and deletion controls. Until confirmed, use **“Try with a public or synthetic guide first; do not upload confidential client material.”**
5. **Explain plan boundaries.** Current free one profile versus FAQ saying separate profiles per brand can confuse agency users. Add **“Free/Starter: one brand profile. Ask us which plan supports your client roster before uploading several brands.”** Replace this after the actual entitlement is confirmed; do not imply unlimited brands from unlimited checks.
6. **Mobile and proof check.** Keep the document-style identity. On a 360 px phone, make one rule/flag/rewrite readable without horizontal scroll and keep the CTA visible after explanation. Test upload progress, unsupported file and retry states with a fresh browser. Do not claim a mobile defect without reproducing it.
