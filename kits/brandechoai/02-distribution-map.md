# BrandEchoAI — Distribution map

**Outbound first.** Your buyer already owns guidelines and publishes weekly. A general AI-tool audience mostly wants another free rewrite.

Numbers below are planning assumptions, not observed benchmarks. The calendar schedules fourteen blocks of six tailored approaches: 84 unique agencies, or 78 if the optional Peerlist launch replaces Day 19 outreach. At assumed 25% reply, 60% trial and 56% retained activation, 84 approaches produce about 21 conversations, 13 trials and 7 active businesses. Ten remains the target; this scenario has a real gap of roughly three. Accepted warm introductions or better observed conversion must close it. These rates are demanding guesses, not benchmarks. If the first 20 approaches produce fewer than three useful conversations, change the list or opener.
