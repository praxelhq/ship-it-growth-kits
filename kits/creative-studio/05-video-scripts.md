# Creative Studio — Video scripts

Use your own public project notes. Hide account details and private drafts. Record a browser at readable zoom with your phone voiceover; hard cuts and captions are enough. No paid editor, stock footage or fabricated success screen.

## A. Three notes into one week — 60 seconds

**Where:** Peerlist, LinkedIn, builder-session opening. **First two seconds:** “You already have the stories.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Three actual notes: a decision, a mistake, a result. | “You already have the stories. These are from the project I actually built.” |
| 8–20s | Bank the notes in the real app. Text: “Public facts only.” | “I add the context I am happy to share. I leave out client secrets and private team details.” |
| 20–34s | Choose a theme and show the weekly plan. | “Now I organize a week. The plan should give each story a different job, not repeat the same lesson seven times.” |
| 34–48s | Draft one post and correct a specific claim. | “I still edit every factual claim and choose the hook. An audit score does not make a claim true.” |
| 48–60s | Real copy/export or explicit one-post approval, no scheduled timer. | “I decide what goes out. Free while in beta at studio.neuronlab.in.” |

If account or generation steps fail, fix them before recording; do not fill the gap with a mockup presented as working software.

## B. Seven ideas, not seven claims — 25 seconds, vertical

**Where:** Peerlist/LinkedIn teaching clip. **Hook:** “Your week does not need seven life lessons.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Seven identical “I learned” openings, labeled teaching example. | “Your week does not need seven life lessons.” |
| 5–15s | Replace with decision, mistake, demo, question. | “A decision, a mistake, a demo and a useful question are different kinds of posts. Start with the work.” |
| 15–22s | One actual plan and the selected draft. | “Creative Studio helps me organize those stories. I choose the one worth writing today.” |
| 22–25s | URL; “Free beta / manual control.” | “One true draft first.” |

## C. Nothing on a timer — 30 seconds, vertical

**Where:** answer the scheduling objection in a session. **Hook:** “Planning a post is not publishing it.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7s | Draft in calendar, clearly unpublished. | “Planning a post is not publishing it.” |
| 7–18s | Copy control and, only if verified, explicit Approve & post control. | “I can copy it myself, or explicitly approve one exact text. There is no scheduled autoposting.” |
| 18–25s | Show final text carefully, then cancel the publication in a test. | “I read the words first. Nothing about a weekly plan gives the tool permission to invent my story.” |
| 25–30s | URL and manual-control line. | “The plan is support. The decision is mine.” |

Do not imply the cancel test proves every publication path safe. It shows the actual observed path, which you should test before the workshop.

## D. The story worth keeping — 20 seconds, vertical

**Where:** LinkedIn follow-up after an actual user interview. **Hook:** “Keep the awkward true sentence.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Your original note, with a real specific detail. | “Keep the awkward true sentence.” |
| 5–13s | Compare generic draft and your corrected version. | “This polished line could belong to anyone. This detail belongs to the work. I put it back.” |
| 13–20s | Original story bank and final edit. | “That is what I want the story bank to protect. Creative Studio, free during beta.” |

Bank four reusable clips: original notes, weekly plan, factual edit and copy/approval. New public posts need a new actual story. Never use a participant's voice, resume or private draft without permission.
