# Creative Studio — Positioning

**Live:** https://studio.neuronlab.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/creative-studio

**Sell the planning session, not autopilot.** Your public page explicitly rules out scheduling and automatic posting. Your privacy policy allows an optional, explicit “Approve & post” action for one exact text through LinkedIn's official API. Those are different from unattended publication. Keep that distinction precise.

## Your first users

Start with an individual student builder or recent graduate who has spent a month making something, has a messy diary of decisions and screenshots, and wants to explain the work publicly. They already have stories; they need to organize them into a week without inventing a new personality every morning.

Choose builders who want to publish manually and keep control. Someone shopping for an agency scheduler is not your first user. Someone with no real story is not helped by seven invented ones.

Your [current hero](https://studio.neuronlab.in/) reads “LinkedIn posts that actually sound like you.” The offer is “Free while in beta,” with “Get started” and “Get started free” links leading to `/login`. The login page describes a planning/drafting workspace. It was fetched, but account creation, AI generation and posting were not completed in this review.

## One line

> Turn your project diary into a week of LinkedIn drafts, using your own stories and approving every word.

Short version:

> Your stories, one weekly plan, manual control.

## The wedge: Sunday project-diary session

The user banks three true stories: a decision, a mistake and a result they can defend. They choose one theme, plan the week and draft one post. They inspect the hook, remove exaggeration and copy or explicitly approve the final text. Only after that first useful post do you introduce carousels or comment drafts.

Your “Hook lab” and “humanize” steps should help edit a real story; they are not evidence a post will perform or sound human to every reader. Show the input story beside the approved text and the edit the person made.

| [The Steady Post](https://thesteadypost.app/) | 14 days or five drafts; then ₹499/month | Its starting point is field trends. Yours is the builder's own project history. |

These are overlapping cohort products, not imaginary competitors. Keep your builder group separate from Postifyy's placement clinic and The Steady Post's alumni-professional circle.

## The uncomfortable issue to fix first

Your [privacy policy](https://studio.neuronlab.in/privacy) says AI actions send relevant text to Gemini and does not confirm which paid/free provider terms apply to the app's account. That matters for resumes, employer stories and private client work. Start demonstrations with your own public project. Make the provider terms and consent clear before inviting people to paste sensitive information.

Your public page also shows 12 stories, three weekly plans and nine drafts in a workspace preview. Treat these as a sample interface, not independently verified customer traction. Your first proof is a permissioned user producing and later using a true post.

Keep **1,000 individual signups** as the cohort target. Report activated planners and returning writers beside it. `02` shows why a few small builder sessions alone will not reach that target.
