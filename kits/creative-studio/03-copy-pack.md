# Creative Studio — Copy pack

Use a project you can discuss publicly. Do not paste employer/customer secrets into the exercise. `[name]` and `[group]` are intentional recipient fill-ins.

## 1. Builder-circle organizer invitation

**Subject:** A project-diary-to-post session for your builders

> Hi [name], I built Creative Studio to turn the stories from a project into a weekly writing plan.
>
> Could I show [group] a free 30-minute exercise? Each person brings three public project notes: a decision, a mistake and a result they can defend. They leave with a week of ideas and one edited post. Publishing is optional, and the app is free while in beta.
>
> I will explain what goes to the AI provider before anyone uses it. People can do the same exercise in a notes app if they prefer. No confidential material is needed.
>
> Happy to show you the session first. If it does not fit, no problem.

Follow up once after seven days. A decline ends the invitation; it does not trigger another channel.

## 2. Permissioned group or bulletin message

> You built something. There are already stories in the work: a decision, a mistake and a result.
>
> Bring three public notes to a free project-writing session. We will turn one theme into a week of ideas, then edit one honest draft. You choose whether to copy or explicitly approve a post. Nothing is scheduled to go out by itself.
>
> I made Creative Studio, the free-beta workspace we will demonstrate: https://studio.neuronlab.in/
>
> No client secrets, private team details or sensitive resumes. The organizer will share the agreed session time. You can participate in a notes app too.

Only send after the organizer has accepted. Do not invent a booking or call attendance a signup.

## 3. Peerlist proof post

> I am trying a smaller promise than “AI content on autopilot.”
>
> Three stories from a real project. One theme for the week. One draft I can explain without the tool.
>
> The attached example shows the notes, the plan and the sentence I changed before approving. Creative Studio is free while in beta. It has a story bank, weekly plans, hook options and manual review; it does not schedule automatic posts.
>
> I would like feedback on the planning step. Does it preserve the differences between your stories, or turn everything into the same generic lesson?
>
> https://studio.neuronlab.in/

Use only after recording that real example. The edit does not need to be dramatic.

## 4. Useful reply in a builder conversation

> A project diary already contains writing prompts. Pick a decision you made, the alternative you rejected, and the thing you learned from the result. That is more specific than a generic “five lessons from building” post.
>
> I work on a planning tool, so I am biased toward organizing stories. I would still start with the original notes, not a prompt asking for a personality.

Do not add the product link unless asked or the thread explicitly welcomes project links. Change the example to the actual discussion.

## 5. LinkedIn demonstration

> I do not want seven posts that all say I learned to be resilient.
>
> I want one decision, one mistake, one result and a few useful questions from the project I actually worked on.
>
> Creative Studio starts with those stories. I bank them, choose a weekly theme, draft, then review the hook and every factual claim. I can copy the words or explicitly approve one post. There is no scheduled autoposting.
>
> The recording shows the original notes beside the edited post. It is a planning demonstration, not a claim about reach or job offers.
>
> Free while in beta: https://studio.neuronlab.in/ . What part of your project history would you struggle to turn into a post?

## 6. Project listing / maker comment

**Tagline:** Your project stories into a weekly LinkedIn writing plan.

> Creative Studio is a personal planning and drafting workspace. Bank your voice and true stories, plan a week, compare hook options, make carousel drafts and review the exact words before copying or approving a post. It is free while in beta and does not schedule posts, scrape a feed or publish unattended.
>
> I am looking for feedback on whether the plan keeps your stories distinct and whether the final draft still sounds like you. Please use non-sensitive material and read the data-use explanation before invoking AI features.

## 7. AlternativeTo description

> Personal LinkedIn content-planning web app with a story bank, weekly plans, editable AI-assisted drafts, hook options, carousel creation and user-entered/imported post metrics. Manual control: copy the result or explicitly approve exact text for one publication; no scheduled or unattended posting. Free during beta. AI actions send selected context to a third-party provider as described in the privacy policy.

Use accurate category/license fields. Free beta is not open source and is not free forever.

## 8. Follow-up to a consenting session participant

> Did any idea from your plan turn into a post you were happy with? If not, what was missing: a true story, a useful angle, or words that sounded like you?
>
> You do not need to publish every planned day. I would rather understand one rejected draft than count seven unused ones.

Ask for permission before showing any answer publicly. Do not turn a private complaint into an attributed testimonial.
