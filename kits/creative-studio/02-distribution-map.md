## Specific accounts and useful conversations

Follow the real project conversations on [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/scroll) and read [Buffer's LinkedIn resources](https://buffer.com/linkedin) for concrete objections about planning and publication. A useful reply explains one decision or factual edit; a link dump is not useful. Neither source is an endorsement or an assumed distribution partner.

For newsletters, ask the actual organizer of your builder circle whether a bulletin exists and accepts a workshop resource. No editor/contact/placement has been verified for your product, so newsletter traffic stays zero until permission and publication. Copy #2 is ready for an accepted bulletin; do not invent a campus publication.

## The honest gap

Three sessions give 15–30 accounts; four Peerlist posts roughly 1–18; four LinkedIn posts 2–29; one project listing 3–38: **21–115 before overlap**. These are learning-stage scenarios, not a sufficient forecast for 1,000 accounts.

At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate you need 4,000 qualified unique visitors for 1,000. To expand, first earn permissioned examples and voluntary introductions from satisfied organizers. A single weak post does not justify a mass-message campaign. Day 21 is the reforecast: if no larger route has materialized, report the shortfall and choose the source that creates actual drafted/published work.

## Keep the cohort from exhausting the same people

Postifyy has a student first-post clinic on Days 17–20; The Steady Post uses a separate professional alumni audience Days 21–23. Your Days 27–29 sessions are for builders with project diaries and use a different organizer list. Coordinate actual dates privately and leave seven days between invitations to the same organization. These are proposed cohort boundaries, not platform rules.

Limit organizer outreach to five thoughtful approaches on a working day and one follow-up a week later. This is a manageable workload, not a LinkedIn-approved sending limit. Do not publish personal contact lists in this public repository.
