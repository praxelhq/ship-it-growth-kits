# Vernacular — Positioning

**Live:** https://learnvernacular.online/ · **Category:** Language practice · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/karmanyemadan-glitchs-org

## Lead with one beginner lesson, not fifteen fluency promises

The 29 October 2026 hero says **“Learn any of 15 languages the way games teach”**, followed by short lessons that **“teach before they test”**, twenty games and AI conversations. **“Start learning free”** links to `/login?mode=signup`; **“Try the demo”** uses `/login?mode=demo` and actually redirected to `/learn/onboarding`. Its first step asks for explanation and learning languages. No account, completed lesson, multiplayer game or purchase was performed in this research.

The course, games and online multiplayer are advertised free. Optional **₹499 once for a 30-day Pass** raises AI limits, without automatic renewal; current limits belong on the Pass page. Do not call it unlimited or the price of the course. Terms say a full refund can be requested within seven days by replying to the receipt; link current terms rather than promising an independently tested refund.

Start with two adult friends beginning one language together, learning a small word set and trying a game built from those words. The immediate question is whether the lesson taught enough to play. The site says beginner level about A1, content not yet native-reviewed, AI corrections fallible and pronunciation not scored. Keep those honest limits.

**Pitch:** Learn a small set of words, practise with hints, then use those words in a game.

**Bio:** Learn first. Play with your new words. Free beginner course and games.

[Duolingo's official explanation](https://blog.duolingo.com/is-duolingo-free/) confirms free courses supported by ads/subscriptions; current paid rates were not verified. A paper vocabulary deck costs no software subscription. Vernacular's reason to try is the teach→practice→game loop and optional friend play, not being the only free language app or a proven route to fluency.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, counting accounts or meaningful first no-account learning sessions under the existing policy, never adding the same person twice. Activation: first taught-word checkpoint completed and one unlocked game attempted. The separate German student-job track describes roughly A2+ typed conversations, no pronunciation practice and no job/visa advice: do not merge that offer into a beginner speaking promise.
