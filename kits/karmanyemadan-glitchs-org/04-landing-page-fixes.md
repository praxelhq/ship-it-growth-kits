# Vernacular — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Make the first small task obvious

Current hero: **“Learn any of 15 languages the way games teach”**, with **“Start learning free”** and **“Try the demo”**. The demo route redirected to language onboarding. Keep it as the lowest-friction entry and test a complete path on a clean phone before launch.

Supporting copy: **“Try one beginner lesson. Practise its words, then use them in a game.”** Button: **“Try one free lesson”**, pointing to the actual demo. Do not imply a one-tap completed course or require an account before an advertised accountless demo.

## 2. Keep limits beside the promise

The existing honest-limits section is valuable. Put a short line near the CTA: **“Early course content; not yet native-reviewed. AI feedback may be wrong.”** Keep **“Pronunciation is not scored”** beside speech features, not hidden under a fluency headline. Check that “Report a problem” actually reaches a useful report path.

## 3. Separate free learning from AI access

Replacement price explanation: **“Course, games and multiplayer: free. AI features: limited free use. Optional 30-day Pass: ₹499 once, no automatic renewal.”** Show actual remaining limits and expiry before checkout. Terms specify the refund request window; use the real current policy. No transaction was performed here.

## 4. Distinguish German work practice

The German student-job page advertises typed scenarios, roughly A2+ for much of the track, no speaking/pronunciation practice and no employment/visa advice. Preserve those labels on its own route. Do not let the general site's speech text imply that this track offers oral assessment or guarantees work eligibility.

## 5. Verify phone and multiplayer behaviour

Check target/explanation language selection, English-only grammar notes, script rendering, hint-to-checkpoint transition and the 80% threshold. Test an actual room with two consenting adults before filming it. Mask names/codes, leave AI chats and account data out of replay, and show a useful error when a provider/room fails. Replace no real proof with no testimonial, rather than an invented endorsement.
