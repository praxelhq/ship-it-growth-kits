# Vernacular — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count a new non-test account or, for the working no-account demo, a meaningful first lesson session: the learner selects their languages and begins taught content. A landing view or empty onboarding load does not count. Deduplicate a demo learner who later signs up; explain anonymous/browser limits and do not fingerprint people to pretend certainty.

**Activation:** first taught-word checkpoint completed and one unlocked game attempted. Additional useful evidence: returning to another lesson or a real friend round. Points, 80% checkpoint thresholds and AI scores are product mechanics, not certified language proficiency or proof of A1 attainment.
