# Vernacular — Video scripts

Record a successful real lesson/game path on a phone. Use a word set you can check; do not perform an accent or claim a translation is authoritative. Keep the early-version limit visible.

## A. Teach before play — 40 seconds, homepage/session invite

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Word lesson before game. | “Meet the words before the game.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual lesson and guided hints. | “Vernacular starts by teaching a small set, then lets you practise with help.” |
| 14–27 s | Actual checkpoint and unlocked game. | “Check what you remember, then use those words in a game.” |
| 27–35 s | Real report-problem control and early label. | “This is an early version. Content can be wrong, and reports help me fix it.” |
| 35–40 s | Demo URL. | “Try one free lesson in the demo.” |

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual weekly-practice view. | “Miss a day? Come back.” |

Hold B if a two-device game has not worked in your own test. Hold C if the return view is not available. Show a real limitation rather than staging success.
