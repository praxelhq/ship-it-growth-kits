# Boomerang — Video scripts

Use your own writing or a permitted public source with a short excerpt. Hide account/private library details. Screen recording, voice and captions are enough. No fabricated sync or retrieval success.

## A. Save now, find later—60 seconds

**Where:** own profiles/project sessions. **Hook:** “I remembered the sentence, not the tab.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Actual permitted article/original link. | “I remembered the sentence, not the tab. I want a way back.” |
| 8–22 s | Actual save/passage selection and context note. | “I keep these original words and why they matter to this project.” |
| 22–35 s | Show actual mode/provider boundary. | “This is the current beta mode. Local and account storage are different promises.” |
| 35–48 s | Real later retrieval; label date. | “Now I find the passage and reopen its source for the work.” |
| 48–60 s | URL/free-beta line. | “Boomerang. Use non-sensitive material and read the current data limits.” |

Do not claim later retrieval until a later real test exists; an intended workflow can be labeled a demonstration instead.

## B. Quote versus summary—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “These are not the same words.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Short original passage and labeled summary. | “These are not the same words.” |
| 6–14 s | Original source link visible. | “A summary helps orient me. A quotation needs the original words and source.” |
| 14–20 s | Actual saved passage/URL. | “Keep the difference clear.” |

## C. Why did I save it?—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “The missing field was why.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | A real saved link without context, then your note. | “The missing field was why.” |
| 7–18 s | Your actual project question and relevant passage. | “One line of context tells me what I needed from the source when I return.” |
| 18–25 s | URL and beta disclosure. | “That is the Boomerang test—not another bookmark count.” |

## D. Saved where?—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “A local save is not a cloud backup.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Actual storage-mode label. | “A local save is not a cloud backup.” |
| 7–18 s | Real tested mode/import path, if available. | “Check the mode and explicit import before expecting a passage on another device.” |
| 18–25 s | Current support/export limits. | “Read the beta limits before relying on the library.” |
