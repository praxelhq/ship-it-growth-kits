# Boomerang — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.boomerangit.me/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/boomerang

**Do not promise a finished production vault.** The privacy disclosure describes a development build, configured services and incomplete in-app cloud export/deletion. Start with a clearly supported free beta and public material, not confidential research or a mass launch against unresolved data controls.

The [actual headline](https://www.boomerangit.me/) is “Good ideas deserve a way back.” Controls say “Start your library” and “Install Chrome extension.” The page demonstrates an original passage, optional summaries and topics. Public home/privacy were read 29 October 2026; account entry, extension installation, capture/sync and AI processing were not completed.

## Your first person

An adult student builder collecting sources for one live project, who remembers a useful sentence but not which tab it came from. They do not need another pile of links. They need the exact passage, its original source and the reason it matters when they return to the project.

Begin with a small public-source project. Avoid private employer documents, personal inboxes or material the person has no right to upload. The [privacy disclosure](https://www.boomerangit.me/privacy.html) says connected AI can send source text through providers; local storage is not an encrypted vault.

## Pitch

> Save the passage that matters, keep its source, and find it again when your project needs it.

Short version:

> Your useful passage, with a way back.

The wedge is retrieval after a day: save one permitted source, select the original passage, add context, leave, then find it for the actual project. Optional summaries are not replacements for original words. Activation is a real saved source/passage; retention is later retrieval/opening of the source for a genuine purpose.

## The weakness

The extension/local/account boundary is complicated enough to lose trust. Existing local saves upload only through explicit import; new configured account saves and provider processing have separate rules. Explain the exact release a tester is using. A “second brain” slogan cannot substitute for a working save/return/export contract.

Keep **1,000 signups** as the cohort target, with saved-source users and later retrievers beside it. Free-beta access capacity and unresolved launch gates may cap this month's feasible result; state that rather than count anonymous preview views.
