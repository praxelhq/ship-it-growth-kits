## Composition and gap

Three sessions, twenty invitations, six posts and one listing model **25–104 accounts before overlap**. They assume a usable beta and sufficient allowed access. The target remains 1,000, but the initial plan is not mathematically sufficient. At assumed 25% visit-to-account, you need 4,000 qualified visitors. Expansion depends on a retrieval demonstration people actually use and voluntary organizer introductions.

Your shared-circle window is Days 10–13, after Kept's personal-collection session and before Minutes' reading-completion exercise. Distinct contacts, actual dates and seven days between invitations to the same organizer. If export/deletion/access gates remain unresolved, keep testing small and report capacity honestly rather than broad-launching a development promise.
