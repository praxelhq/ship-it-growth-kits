# Boomerang — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Report **unique people saving a real permitted source/passage** and **independent later retrievers opening the original source** beside accounts. [Target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md).

Count one newly created usable beta account once. Exclude tests, bots, repeated sign-ins, extension reinstalls and preview clicks. Local saves or separate web/extension libraries are not automatically distinct people. Report guest/local trials separately if present. Activation is a genuine saved source/passage with context; retention is useful later-day retrieval, not saving fifty links once.

Private account/save/retrieval totals and allowed beta capacity were not supplied; unknown is not zero. Record actual values before promotion. The privacy disclosure says public Clarity is opt-in/masked and excluded from the extension/local development; preserve that. Never send saved passage bodies as analytics events.

Events: `account_created`, `source_saved`, `passage_saved`, `context_added`, `capture_failed`, `source_retrieved`, `original_opened`, `later_day_retrieval`, `export_requested`, `deletion_requested`. Keep actual storage modes separate. A summary completion is not proof of accuracy or an opened source. Use coarse source tags, no private library titles/contacts in public URLs.

## Capacity and forecast

The actual plan schedules three sessions, twenty invitations, six posts and one listing: **25–104 accounts before overlap**. It assumes the beta path/support capacity works. It is not sufficient evidence for 1,000. At assumed 25% visit-to-account, you need 4,000 qualified unique visitors; replace with actual data after fifty visits.

If access or export/deletion gates limit the pilot, state the capacity and unresolved work. Do not count test checkout sessions as paid users, revenue or product activation.

Visits without account entry suggest beta/trust friction. Accounts without saves suggest capture confusion. Saves without later retrieval mean context/retrieval is not solving the job. Misleading summaries need source-first correction, not more generated text. Persistence failures or exposed private content require repair before promotion. Your useful result is a source found again for actual work, not a larger library counter.
