# Patterlane — Positioning

**Live:** https://patterlane.in/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/patterlane · Checked 29 October 2026.

Your first evaluator is an experienced individual Mac email user who wants calmer conversation reading and understands an early beta. Start with the no-credential sample. This isn't ready to be sold as a supported replacement for an organization's mailbox or a mass public install campaign.

Actual H1 **“Less inbox. More conversation.”** **View plans & join** → `/join.html`; **Explore the live demo** → `/app.html`. The sample website doesn't connect to real mail, send mail or run a local AI model. Browser localStorage retains its sample messages/tasks/drafts until cleared. No native app, live provider, Google identity, invitation or model was operated in this audit.

## The useful promise

> Follow an email conversation, keep a next step beside it and review a reply before sending. Start with a sample, not your real inbox.

Bio: **A calmer conversation workspace, in English and Hindi.**

The homepage describes chat-like threads, to-dos/private notes/local calendar and optional local Ollama summary/draft assistance. Original email/task/note language stays unchanged when switching interface English/Hindi. No hosted AI fallback or Patterlane cloud mailbox is claimed; that doesn't make mail itself offline—reading/sending still contacts the provider.

## Current access and release limits

First **100 accepted email-bound invitations** receive ongoing free access; invitations expire after **seven days**. Later individual price is **$5 USD/person/month**, but **payments and paid activation aren't available**. No card/charge today. Google identity is separate from mailbox access and support says OAuth configuration remains a prerequisite. Don't promise an uninvited stranger immediate free access.

The native Mac ZIPs are **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-verified publisher signed or notarized**. Native Mac startup/live-provider/local-model tests remain pending. A monitored public support destination is also not configured. Automated checks/local TLS fixtures listed in release notes don't prove real Gmail/iCloud operation or supported delivery. These specific release gates precede broad installation or real-mail credential invitations.

Eligible Gmail/iCloud app passwords and compatible TLS IMAP/SMTP are described; **Outlook and provider-calendar sync aren't enabled**. Manual recent-mail sync, no outbound attachments, no offline send queue, no automatic updates/full-mailbox sync/system notifications/cloud-device sync. Don't promise a complete Outlook replacement based on privacy text describing future OAuth scopes.

## Alternatives and trust

An existing provider web inbox or built-in mail client may already be available at ₹0 extra specialist software cost; the provider/account itself can have separate costs. Patterlane must earn switching through conversation clarity and local review, not claim all other clients upload mail to AI.

[Privacy](https://patterlane.in/privacy.html) explains local encrypted vault/keychain, direct provider connections, plaintext exports, local activity logs that may contain addresses/task titles, and disconnect not revoking provider grants or deleting backups/exports. Google identity/invitation/session data is server-held. No claim of total anonymity, end-to-end security or no network. Use only synthetic/sample messages in public.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account/install target** as a future goal, with compatible verified first launch and meaningful conversation/to-do/review alongside. The current first-100 free-invitation offer and closed paid activation do not support promising 1,000 admitted users this month. Track sample evaluation separately and report the access cap/readiness gap without changing the board target.
