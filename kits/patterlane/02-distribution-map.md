Planning guesses: 20 warm invitations × 50–70% visit = 10–14 sample visitors; × 40–60% complete conversation→to-do/review exercise = 4–8 sample activations. Six posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 20–40% sample loop = 1–12. Accepted ten-person session × 60–80% attendance = 6–8; × 40–60% sample loop = 2–5. Remove overlap.

Actual first-100 invitations are owner-issued and finite; paid activation closed. Report readiness and the target gap honestly. No unaccepted editor/launch/community/directory contribution belongs in the base.
