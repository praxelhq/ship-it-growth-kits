# HAUS HOME — Distribution map

**Your buyer lives at a known street address.** PGs cluster around colleges, IT parks and hospitals, and they are all listed, with phone numbers, on free public platforms. This is a *findable* market, which makes it an outbound market.

Pick **one corridor** — one college area or one IT-park belt — and saturate it. PG owners in a corridor know each other, compete with each other, and copy each other. Twenty owners on one road is worth more than two hundred spread across India.

---

## Tier 1 — The listing platforms are a free, complete prospect database 🔴

Every PG in India is already advertising itself, with a phone number, on:

| Source | What you get | Volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Maps** — search `PG in [area]`, `hostel in [area]`, `ladies PG [area]` | Name, phone, photos, rating, bed count hints | Hundreds per corridor |
| **NoBroker, Magicbricks, 99acres, Housing.com** — PG/hostel sections | Owner contact, bed count, rent, amenities | Thousands per city |
| **Stanza Living / Colive / Zolo competitor listings** | Independent PGs listed alongside | Hundreds |
| **JustDial, Sulekha** | Phone numbers, direct | Thousands |
| **Facebook Marketplace + local PG groups** | Owners actively posting vacancies | Hundreds |
| **Instagram** — many PGs now have accounts | DM-reachable | Hundreds |
| **College notice boards and nearby shop boards** | The smallest, most under-digitised ones — often your best customers | Dozens |

**Build 800 qualified leads in two days.** Columns: name, area, owner phone, estimated beds, listed on which platform.

### WhatsApp outreach — your highest-converting channel

PG owners live on WhatsApp. They answer unknown numbers because every unknown number might be a tenant.

- **30 personalised messages per weekday.** Individually sent — never a broadcast list, which gets the number banned.
- Never a link in message one. Ask a question about *their* PG.
- **Realistic:** 600 messages → 30–40% reply (very high; they think you might be a prospective tenant, so be honest immediately) → **180–240 conversations → 50–90 signups.**
- Copy pack #2.

## Tier 2 — Walking the corridor 🔴

PGs have boards outside with phone numbers. You can map a street in an hour.

- **Visit 15 PGs a day, 11am–4pm** (before the evening rush, after the morning exodus).
- Ask for the owner or the warden. The warden is a real decision-influencer — they do the chasing.
- **Onboard on the spot.** Google sign-in, add the property, add five beds, show the bed map. Ten minutes.
- **Realistic:** 8 field days × 15 = 120 visits → 50 conversations → **20–35 signups**, and these are your highest-retention users because you set them up personally.

**Also worth doing:** leave a printed card at the chai shop, the laundry, and the mess kitchen on the same road. Those three businesses know every PG owner in the area.

## Tier 4 — Free tools and SEO 🟠

PG owners google their admin problems. Own those queries.

| Query | Free asset |
| --- | --- |
| `pg rent agreement format india` / `leave and licence agreement format` | **Free agreement template generator.** Highest intent of anything here — the owner needs one *today*. |
| `rent receipt format pdf` | Free receipt generator |
| `pg rules and regulations format` | Downloadable template |
| `how to collect rent on time from tenants` | Guide |
| `rent reminder message to tenant` | 15 ready messages, English + Hindi |
| `pg management software india` / `hostel management software` | Your product page, properly optimised |
| `how to start a pg business in [city]` | Long guide — catches owners at the moment they're setting up, which is the best time to win them |

The **free agreement generator** is the strongest acquisition asset available to you: every single PG owner needs agreements, most are using a photocopied template from 2014, and the person generating one is unambiguously your buyer. Build it, ungate it, and put a one-line offer after the download.

Realistic: small in month 1, **300–1,500 visits/month by month 4**, converting far above average.

**Pitch 15 in week 2.** Realistic: 2–4 productive, **15–50 signups.**

## Tier 6 — The tenant side 🟡 *(an underrated flanking move)*

Tenants outnumber owners 40:1 and they're on Reddit, Instagram and college groups.

- Publish something genuinely useful for **PG tenants**: *"Your rights as a PG tenant in India"*, *"What to check before paying a PG deposit"*, *"How to get your deposit back"*.
- It ranks, it gets shared in student groups, and a fraction of readers say to their owner: *"why don't you use something like this?"*
- Low direct conversion, good reach, and it builds a brand owners see from both sides.

---

## Channels to skip

- **Hacker News, Product Hunt, dev communities.** Zero PG owners. Do Peerlist once for credibility.
- **Cold email.** PG owners don't use email. WhatsApp or nothing.
- **Twitter/X.** No.

## Realistic month-1 totals

| Channel | Signups, low | Signups, high |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp outreach (600 messages) | 50 | 90 |
| Walking the corridor (120 visits) | 20 | 35 |
| Facebook + WhatsApp owner groups | 15 | 60 |
| Partners (2–4 productive) | 15 | 50 |
| Free agreement generator + SEO | 10 | 50 |
| Instagram DMs | 8 | 25 |
| LinkedIn (chains) | 3 | 15 |
| **Total** | **~120** | **~325** |

**Honest read:** 1,000 PG-owner signups in 30 days is not realistic — the addressable list you can build and personally work in a month is roughly 800–1,000 owners, and nobody converts a cold list at 100%. **120–325 signups, all real property owners with beds in them, is a strong month.**

If a four-figure number is required, the free agreement/receipt generators are the honest route to it — report them separately from owner signups.
