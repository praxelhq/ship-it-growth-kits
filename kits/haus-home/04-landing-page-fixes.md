# HAUS HOME — Landing page conversion fixes

Based on https://haushome.tech/ as it was on 28 Oct 2026.

**Your current above-the-fold:** `Run your PG, hostel, studio or rental like a pro. Not a diary.` / *"HAUS puts your tenants, rent agreements, WhatsApp rent reminders, repair tickets, staff and announcements in one place."* / `Let's get started, it's free →` · `See what it does`

**This is a strong page.** The before/after block, the WhatsApp Day −3 / Day 0 / Day +3 timeline, the per-bed pricing table and the FAQ are all genuinely good, and the pricing transparency puts you ahead of most of this category. **Almost every fix below is about moving something you already have higher up the page**, not writing something new.

One framing note: every single doorstep conversation ends with an owner opening this page on his own phone while you stand next to him. **This page is a closing tool, not a top-of-funnel page.** Optimise it for that job.

---

## Fix 1 — "Your tenants don't need another app" belongs in the hero 🔴 highest impact

It is currently a subheading two sections down.

This is **the single most persuasive sentence on your site** and the only one that answers the first objection every PG owner raises. He has tried to make 40 students do something before and it failed.

**Put it directly under the headline, in the hero:**

> # Run your PG from one dashboard. Not a notebook and a WhatsApp group.
> ### Rent reminders, UPI collection, agreements and complaints — all on WhatsApp.
> **Your tenants don't need to download anything.**

Note the headline change too: *"like a pro. Not a diary"* is a slogan. *"Not a notebook and a WhatsApp group"* is a description of his actual Tuesday. **Your own before/after block already proves you can write this way** — the hero just hasn't caught up with it.

---

## Fix 2 — The 5th-of-the-month line is your best copy and it's three screens down 🔴

From your "Before HAUS" block:

> *"Calling every tenant on the 5th to ask about rent."*

That is the whole product in nine words. An owner reads it and feels caught.

**Pull the before/after comparison up to directly below the hero** — it should be the first thing after the fold, before features. Five rows, two columns, emoji and all. It currently sits after the feature grid, which is the wrong order: **the problem has to land before the feature list means anything.**

---

## Fix 3 — Make the bed map the hero visual, and make it move 🔴

On the doorstep, the bed map is what makes owners sit up. It is the only screen that looks like *their building*. Dashboards and feature grids do not.

- **Hero visual = a live bed map**, floors and rooms, green/red/grey, with a real-looking occupancy number.
- Better: make it **clickable**. Let the visitor tap a red bed and see the reminder that would go out. Thirty seconds of interaction beats any amount of copy.
- Label the colours in the image itself: `green = paid · red = pending · grey = vacant`. Do not make him guess.

**This is the highest-return engineering on the page** and it doubles as the thing you screen-share in every WhatsApp follow-up.

---

## Fix 4 — The hero has four competing actions 🟠

Nav currently offers `Owner login`, `Tenant login`, `Create a free account` and `Register your PG`, and the hero adds `Let's get started, it's free →` and `See what it does`. That is six ways in, and two of them mean the same thing.

- **One primary CTA everywhere: `Register your PG — free up to 15 beds`.** Use the same words in the nav, the hero and the footer. Your final-CTA section already uses `Register your PG, free →`, which is the better phrasing — standardise on it.
- Collapse `Owner login` / `Tenant login` into a single `Log in` in the top-right.
- Keep `See what it does` but make it scroll to the bed map, not to a feature list.

---

## Fix 5 — Put the Aadhaar/KYC answer where the fear is 🔴

FAQ #6 — *"Is my tenants' data safe?"* — is collapsed at the bottom. An owner is being asked to upload **40 people's Aadhaar documents**. That is a real responsibility and he feels it before he feels anything else.

**Add a hero trust strip:**

> `Free up to 15 beds · Tenants need no app · KYC encrypted, access role-based · Sign in with Google, 5 minutes`

Four lines, four fears: cost, tenant adoption, data responsibility, setup effort. Those are the four things that stop this sale, in that order.

---

## Fix 6 — Hindi / Hinglish 🔴

An all-English page tells a large share of your actual buyers that this product is for someone else. This is not about comprehension — plenty of them read English fine — it is about belonging.

- Language toggle, at minimum covering the hero, the before/after block and the pricing table.
- **Show the WhatsApp reminder timeline in Hindi.** This is the highest-value translation on the page, because it is the thing *his tenants* will receive, and he will immediately imagine it arriving.
- Keep the English version as default; detect nothing, just offer the toggle visibly.

---

## Fix 7 — "Setup in an afternoon" undercuts "about 5 minutes" 🟠

Your onboarding section says **"Setup in an afternoon"**; the final CTA says **"Set up your first property in about 5 minutes."** Both are on the same page.

An afternoon is a barrier. Five minutes is a yes. **Pick five minutes, say it everywhere, and make it true for the first property.**

> **Three steps. About five minutes.**
> 1. Sign in with Google
> 2. Add your floors, rooms and beds
> 3. Invite tenants on WhatsApp

Add, below it: *"Add the rest of your beds whenever. You'll see the bed map after step 2."* — that removes the fear of having to enter 40 beds before seeing anything.

---

## Fix 8 — There is no proof on the page 🟠

No owner names, no property count, no photos, no number of beds managed. For a product handling someone's rent and his tenants' Aadhaar, that is the gap most likely to lose a careful buyer.

As soon as you honestly can:

- **`N properties · N beds managed · ₹N rent collected this month`** as a live-ish counter strip.
- **Two owner quotes with first name, area and bed count** — *"Rahul, 38 beds, Kalyan Nagar"*. A photo of the owner at his PG is worth more than a logo.
- **Local proof beats national proof in this market.** *"Used by 11 PGs in [area]"* is the single most persuasive sentence you will ever put on this page, and it directly supports the corridor-saturation strategy in `02`.

Until you have real proof, do not fake it. Use the before/after block to carry the weight instead.

---

## Fix 9 — Make "Call me back" prominent, not a footnote 🟠

It's currently a secondary option in the final CTA. For this buyer it may be the **highest-converting element on the page** — a 45-year-old PG owner is far more likely to leave a phone number than to complete a Google sign-in on his phone while standing in a corridor.

- Make it a persistent, visible second action in the hero: **`Prefer to talk? Get a WhatsApp message from us →`** (name + number, nothing else).
- Say "WhatsApp", not "call" — it's lower commitment and it's where you'll actually follow up.
- Then respond within the hour, from a human.

---

## Fix 10 — Put the ₹29 comparison on the page, not just in the table 🟡

Your pricing table is clear, which is already better than most. But the owner has to do the arithmetic himself.

**Add a one-line bed calculator next to the Pro plan:**

> `I have [ 40 ] beds → ₹1,160/month`
> *That's less than most owners lose to a single month of late rent.*

And keep **"Owners who join now keep launch pricing for life ✌️"** — it's working, it's honest, and it creates a reason to act today.

---

## Fix 11 — Add the free agreement generator as a page 🔴

Covered in `02` Tier 4 and `03` #8. It belongs here too, because it changes what this domain is for: right now the only reason to visit haushome.tech is to buy software. The generator gives every PG owner in India a reason to visit before he's ready to buy.

`haushome.tech/free/rent-agreement` — ungated, no signup, one soft offer underneath. Plus `/free/rent-receipt` and `/free/pg-rules`.

---

## Fix 12 — Mobile, on a mid-range Android, on 4G 🔴

Your buyer is opening this on a ₹12,000 phone in a building with one bar of signal, usually while you stand there.

- Hero, bed map and pricing must all work one-handed.
- **Test the Google sign-in flow on mobile specifically**, including the app-switch back from the Google screen. Every doorstep close depends on it; if it breaks, the field days in `06` are wasted.
- Under 3 seconds to first paint on 4G.
- Phone number fields: numeric keypad, no country-code fiddling.

## What to watch in Clarity

- **Mobile vs desktop split.** Should be overwhelmingly mobile. If it isn't, your field visitors aren't opening the page afterwards — which means you must onboard on *your* phone, in the room, every time.
- **Scroll depth to the before/after block.** If fewer than half reach it, Fix 2 is confirmed.
- **Which FAQ gets expanded most.** If it's *"Do my tenants need to download an app?"*, Fix 1 is costing you conversions and you can prove it. If it's *"Is my tenants' data safe?"*, Fix 5 is.
- **Recordings of sessions that clicked a CTA and abandoned during Google sign-in.** This is where B2B signups silently die. Watch five of them end to end.
- **Dead clicks on the bed map image.** If people are tapping a static image, they are telling you to build Fix 3.
- **Rage clicks in the property/bed setup flow** — if owners stall at "add 40 beds", add a bulk-add and a "finish later".
