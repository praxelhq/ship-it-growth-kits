# HAUS HOME — Copy pack

**Almost all of this is WhatsApp and spoken Hinglish.** Your buyer does not read English marketing pages and does not use email. Write and speak the way he talks to his tenants.

One rule for everything below: **never send a link in the first message.** A link in message one from an unknown number reads as a scam, and PG owners get scam messages daily.

---

## 1. WhatsApp cold message — message one 🔴 *(your highest-volume channel)*

Found him on Google Maps / NoBroker / JustDial. He answers because unknown numbers might be tenants — so **be honest in the first line** or you burn the relationship.

> Namaste Sir 🙏 Main [Name], [city] se.
>
> Main tenant nahi hoon — pehle hi bata deta hoon. Aapka [PG name] Google pe dekha, [area] mein.
>
> Ek chhota sa sawaal: abhi is mahine ka rent kitne beds ka pending hai? Aur aapko wo yaad hai ya register dekhna padega?

**Why this works:** it is honest in line two, it is short, and the question is one he cannot answer without feeling the pain. Most owners genuinely do not know and will say so.

**If he replies anything at all:**

> Yahi reason hai maine poocha. Maine PG owners ke liye ek cheez banayi hai — har tenant ko 3 din pehle, due date pe, aur late hone pe WhatsApp pe apne aap reminder jaata hai, saath mein UPI link. Jaise hi wo pay karte hain, receipt unko chali jaati hai aur aapke dashboard pe bed green ho jaata hai.
>
> **Aapke tenants ko koi app download nahi karni.** Sab WhatsApp pe.
>
> 15 beds tak bilkul free hai. Chahein toh 5 minute mein main aapke saamne set kar dun — aap bas Google se sign in kijiye.

**If he says "abhi busy hoon":**

> Bilkul Sir. Ek kaam karta hoon — aapko ek rent reminder ka sample bhej deta hoon jaisa aapke tenants ko jaata. Dekh lijiyega. Agar bakwaas lage toh number delete kar dijiye. 👍

Then send the actual sample message as a screenshot. **This converts a large share of the maybes**, and it costs you nothing.

### Rules that keep your number alive

- **30 individually-sent messages per weekday. Never a broadcast list** — broadcasts get numbers banned, and a ban ends this channel permanently.
- Business profile set up properly: display name HAUS, a photo, the website.
- Wait for a reply before sending anything further. Two unanswered messages is the limit, ever.
- 11am–2pm or 6pm–8pm. Not during the morning rush.

## 2. The doorstep script 🔴

You are standing outside a PG with a board and a phone number. Walk in.

**At the gate (usually a warden or a cook):**
> "Namaste — owner Sir/Madam hain? Ya unka number mil jayega? Main do minute lunga, kuch bechne nahi aaya jiske liye aaj paisa dena pade."

**The warden matters.** He is the person who actually chases rent and handles complaints. If the owner isn't there, pitch the warden properly — he will bring the owner.

**To the owner:**
> "Sir, aapke kitne beds hain? [40.] Aur abhi, is waqt, kitne khaali hain?"

*(He will either know instantly or go get a register. Either answer gives you the pitch.)*

> "Aur rent — aap 5 tarikh ko phone karte hain na, ek ek ko?"

*(Almost always yes. Let him complain about it; do not interrupt. This is the whole sale.)*

> "Bas yahi cheez maine banayi hai. Dekhiye —"

*(Open your phone. Show the live bed map first. **The bed map is your demo, not the dashboard** — he recognises his own building in it immediately.)*

> "Ye aapki building hogi. Floor, room, bed. Green matlab paid, red matlab pending, grey matlab khaali. Ek nazar mein pata chal jaata hai ki kaun baaki hai.
>
> Aur rent ke liye — 3 tarikh ko tenant ko WhatsApp jaata hai, 5 ko jaata hai, 8 ko late fee ke saath jaata hai. Har message mein UPI link. Wo pay karta hai, receipt usko jaati hai, yahan bed green ho jaata hai. Aapko phone nahi karna padta."

**The line that closes it:**
> "Aur sabse important — **aapke tenants ko koi app install nahi karni hai.** Sab WhatsApp pe hi hota hai. Aap 40 students se app download nahi karwa sakte, mujhe pata hai."

**The close:**
> "15 beds tak free hai, hamesha. Uske baad ₹29 per bed. Aapke 40 beds matlab ₹1,160 mahina — ek tenant ka ek mahina late ho jaye toh usse zyada nuksaan ho jaata hai.
>
> Abhi set kar dein? Google se sign in, property add, paanch bed daal ke dikhata hoon. Das minute."

**Set it up on the spot, on his phone, with his real beds.** An owner who leaves with a live bed map of his own building does not churn. An owner who takes your card does nothing.

**If he hesitates:**
> "Theek hai Sir. Ek kaam karein — aap mujhe apna WhatsApp dijiye, main aaj raat aapke PG ka setup bana ke bhej dunga, aap bas dekh lena. Pasand aaye toh login kar lena."

Groups punish long messages and punish links harder. Lead with a free thing, no signup.

> Namaste sab log 🙏 [Name] here, main [area] mein PG owners ke saath kaam karta hoon.
>
> Ek free cheez bana di hai — **rent agreement generator.** Naam, kiraya, deposit, period daaliye, leave-and-licence agreement PDF ready. Koi signup nahi, free: [link]

> Banaya isliye kyunki jo template ज्यादातर log use kar rahe hain wo 2014 ka photocopy hai aur usme deposit refund ki condition hi nahi likhi hoti — jo baad mein sabse zyada jhagda karwati hai.
>
> Kisi ko kuch aur chahiye (rent receipt format, PG rules ka format) toh bata dijiye, bana dunga.

**Do not pitch the product in your first group message.** Give twice before you ask once. The second or third time, someone will ask what you do.

## 4. Facebook group post — the finding 🔴

For "PG Owners [city]", "Hostel Owners India", "Landlords [city]". Framed as research, not a pitch. **Do the survey first — the number must be real.**

> **Maine [city] ke 60 PG owners se poocha: "abhi kitna rent pending hai?" 44 ko pata nahi tha.**
>
> Pichle mahine main [area] mein ghoom raha tha aur har PG owner se ek hi sawaal pooch raha tha. Jo mila wo ye hai:
>
> - **44 out of 60** turant nahi bata paaye ki kitna rent pending hai — register ya WhatsApp scroll karna pada
> - **51** abhi bhi 5–10 tarikh ko ek-ek tenant ko phone karte hain
> - **38** ke paas deposit ka koi proper record nahi hai — move-out ke waqt sabse zyada jhagda yahi karwata hai
> - Average **3–4 din** har mahine sirf rent chase karne mein jaate hain
>
> **Jo 16 log turant bata paaye, unme se 14 ne ek common cheez ki thi:** rent due date se pehle ek reminder bhejte the, baad mein nahi. Itni si baat. Pehle bhejo toh tenant ke paas paisa hota hai; 10 tarikh ko bhejo toh kharch ho chuka hota hai.
>
> **Teen cheezein jo kisi bhi PG mein aaj se kaam karti hain:**
> 1. Reminder **due date se 3 din pehle** bhejiye, baad mein nahi.
> 2. Reminder ke saath **UPI link** dijiye. "Rent bhej do" aur "yahan tap karke bhej do" mein 30–40% ka farak hai.
> 3. **Deposit ki receipt likh ke dijiye**, din ek se. Saal bhar baad ka jhagda abhi khatam ho jaata hai.
>
> Agreement banane ke liye ek free tool bhi bana diya hai, kisi ko chahiye toh: [link]
>
> *(Disclosure: main is field mein ek product banata hoon, toh main biased hoon. Upar ke numbers asli hain, method poochhein toh bata dunga.)*

Different buyer: co-living chains, hostel operators with managers. English, numbers, one post a week.

> Spent the last month walking PG corridors in [city] and talking to operators running 2 to 40 properties.
>
> The thing that surprised me: **the operators with the most properties have the worst visibility, not the best.**
>
> A single-property owner at least knows his 40 beds. An operator with 6 properties has 6 wardens, 6 WhatsApp groups, 6 registers, and a monthly number that arrives a week late and is wrong.

> 1. **Occupancy is reported, not observed.** The warden says 92%. Nobody can check without calling.

## 9. "15 rent reminder messages" — the giveaway that spreads

Post this in every owner group, on its own, as plain text. It is immediately useful, it costs nothing, and it gets forwarded.

> **Rent reminder messages jo actually kaam karte hain**
>
> **3 din pehle (sabse important — yahi wala sabse zyada kaam karta hai):**
> > Namaste [Name], [month] ka rent ₹[amount] [date] ko due hai. UPI se bhejna ho toh: [link]. Dhanyavaad 🙏
>
> **Due date pe:**
> > [Name] ji, aaj [month] ka rent due hai — ₹[amount]. Yahan se ek tap mein: [link]
>
> **3 din late:**
> > [Name] ji, [month] ka rent ₹[amount] abhi tak pending hai. [date] ke baad ₹[late fee] late fee lag jayegi. Koi dikkat ho toh batayiye, baat kar lenge.
>
> **Paid confirmation (ye bhejna mat bhooliye):**
> > Mil gaya [Name] ji ✅ ₹[amount], [month]. Receipt: [link]. Dhanyavaad!
>
> **Two rules:**
> 1. **Pehle bhejiye, baad mein nahi.** Due date se 3 din pehle tenant ke paas paisa hota hai. 10 tarikh ko nahi hota.
> 2. **Hamesha UPI link ke saath.** "Rent bhej do" aur "yahan tap karo" mein bada farak hai.

## 11. Tenant-side content *(the flanking move)*

Tenants outnumber owners 40:1 and they're on Reddit and in college groups. Publish for them:

- **"PG deposit wapas kaise milta hai — aur kya kya likhwa lena chahiye"**
- **"What to check before paying a PG deposit in [city]"**
- **"Your rights as a PG tenant in India"**

Each one ends with:

> *If your PG runs on a notebook, ask your owner for a written receipt for every payment — including the deposit. If they'd rather it happened automatically, send them this: [link].*

Low direct conversion, good reach, and a handful of owners each month will hear it from their own tenants — which is the most persuasive way to hear it.
