# HAUS HOME — Video scripts + shot lists

**Your videos have one job: survive being forwarded in a PG owners' WhatsApp group.**

That dictates everything. Vertical. Under 60 seconds. Under 10MB. Burned-in captions, because he's watching on mute in a corridor. Hinglish voiceover, because English halves your reach with this buyer.

Scripts and shot lists only — recording these is yours.

---

## Video A — The 40-second WhatsApp demo 🔴 *(your most useful asset by far)*

**Use:** WhatsApp follow-up after a visit, after every cold-message reply, owner groups, Instagram DMs.
**Goal:** the owner who said "kuch bhej do" actually watches it and understands the product without you there.

| Time | On screen | Voice (Hinglish) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | A phone, a call log with 11 outgoing calls to different names, all today. Date visible: **5**. | **"Har mahine, paanch tarikh. Ek-ek ko phone."** |
| 0:05–0:11 | Cut to the bed map. Floors, rooms, beds. Some green, some red, some grey. | **"Ab ye dekhiye. Ye aapki building hai. Hara matlab paid, laal matlab pending, grey matlab khaali."** |
| 0:11–0:18 | Tap a red bed → tenant card opens, shows ₹8,000 pending, 4 days late | **"Laal bed pe tap kijiye — kaun hai, kitna baaki hai, kitne din se."** |
| 0:18–0:28 | Split to a tenant's WhatsApp: the Day −3 reminder arrives with a UPI link. Tap it. Payment succeeds. | **"Aur ye tenant ke phone pe. Teen din pehle reminder, UPI link ke saath. Usne tap kiya, pay kiya."** |
| 0:28–0:34 | Back to the bed map — that bed turns green. Receipt lands in the tenant's chat. | **"Bed hara ho gaya. Receipt usko chali gayi. Aapne kuch nahi kiya."** |
| 0:34–0:40 | End card: **"Tenant ko koi app nahi chahiye"** / `15 beds tak free · haushome.tech` | **"Aur sabse badi baat — tenant ko koi app download nahi karni. Pandrah beds tak free hai. haushome.tech"** |

### Shot list
1. **The bed map is the star.** Real-looking: actual floor names, real room numbers, 30–40 beds. A four-bed demo looks like a toy.
2. **Show a real WhatsApp screen receiving the reminder**, not a mockup of one. Owners look at WhatsApp 200 times a day; they spot a fake instantly.
3. **The colour change from red to green is the money shot.** Hold on it for a full second. That is the emotional payoff of the entire product.
4. Phone screen recording, vertical, 9:16.
5. Burned-in Hindi captions. Also cut an English version for chains and metro operators.
6. **Under 10MB.** This matters more than resolution — WhatsApp compresses, and a video that fails to forward is worth nothing.

## Video D — The 90-second full demo (horizontal + vertical cut)

**Use:** the landing page, partner pitches, LinkedIn for multi-property operators, any owners' association meeting.

**0:00–0:15 — The problem, in his words**
> "Agar aap PG chalate hain, toh aapko do cheezein pata hain. Ek: paanch tarikh ko aapka din phone karne mein jaata hai. Do: agar main abhi poochhun ki kitna rent pending hai, toh aapko register dekhna padega."

*Shot: a real paper register, a WhatsApp group with 40 members, a call log. Three quick cuts, no narration over them.*

**0:15–0:35 — Setup**
> "Google se sign in. Property add kijiye — floor, room, bed. Paanch minute. Aur bas, aapke saamne aapki poori building hai."

*Shot: the actual sign-in, then floors/rooms/beds being added, then the bed map appearing. **Show the real elapsed time.** A fast-forward with a visible timer is more convincing than a claim.*

**0:35–0:55 — The rent loop**
> "Teen tarikh ko tenant ko reminder jaata hai. Paanch ko due date pe. Aath ko late fee ke saath. Har message mein UPI link. Jaise hi wo pay karta hai — receipt usko, aur yahan bed hara."

*Shot: the Day −3 / Day 0 / Day +3 timeline from your own site, animated, then the payment, then the bed turning green.*

**0:55–1:15 — Everything else, fast**
> "Agreement Aadhaar se e-sign. Tenant ka KYC ek jagah, encrypted. Complaint aaye toh ticket ban jaata hai aur staff ko assign ho jaata hai. Announcement sab beds pe ek saath. Aur mahine ke end mein, kitna aaya kitna baaki — ek screen pe."

*Shot: four quick cuts, 5 seconds each. Do not linger. This section exists to show depth, not to teach.*

**1:15–1:30 — Close**
> "Tenant ko kuch download nahi karna. Pandrah beds tak free, hamesha. Uske baad ₹29 per bed. haushome.tech"

### Shot list
- **Real data throughout.** Real-sounding tenant names, real rent amounts for your city, a real building layout.
- Never show a feature that doesn't work yet. You will meet these owners again next week — you're working one corridor.
- Phone-shaped framing even in the horizontal cut; everyone watches on a phone.
- Captions in both cuts.

## Video E — The warden version (0:25) *(nobody else is making this)*

The warden is your hidden decision-influencer. He is the one who actually chases rent, and if he likes it he will sell it to the owner for you.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | A warden's phone, 14 unread tenant complaints across different chats | "Aap warden hain? Toh shikayat aapke paas aati hai — paani nahi aaya, light chali gayi, WiFi band hai. Sab alag-alag messages mein." |
| 0:08–0:18 | Complaints arriving as tickets in one list, each assigned, each with a status | "Yahan har complaint ek ticket ban jaata hai. Kisko assign hua, kab tak karna hai, ho gaya ya nahi — sab ek list mein." |
| 0:18–0:25 | Owner's dashboard showing resolved count | "Aur Sir ko bhi dikh jaata hai ki aapne kitna kaam kiya. **Wo aapke liye sabse badi baat hai.**" |

That last line is the whole pitch to a warden. His work is invisible; this makes it visible.
