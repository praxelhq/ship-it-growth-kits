# HAUS HOME — 30-day execution calendar

**Pick one corridor on day 1 and do not leave it for 30 days.**

That is the single most important decision in this plan. PG owners in one corridor know each other, compete with each other and copy each other. Twenty owners on one road, all using HAUS, all mentioning it to each other, is a business. Two hundred scattered across India is a list.

**The weekly rhythm:**
- **Mon, Wed, Fri — field days.** 15 PG visits each, 11am–4pm.
- **Tue, Thu — build, WhatsApp outreach, partners, content.**
- **Sat — owner groups, Facebook, SEO.**
- **Sun — review, and plan next week's streets.**

**Every weekday, no exceptions: 30 individually-sent WhatsApp messages from your prospect list.** This runs underneath everything else and it is your highest-volume channel.

## The four things that decide this month

1. **Did you do 11 field days?** ~165 visits is the floor. Nothing online substitutes for it with this buyer.
2. **Did you send 600 WhatsApp messages?** 30 a weekday, individually. This is the channel that scales past your legs.
3. **Did you get into owner WhatsApp groups?** Getting into five is a month's work that pays for years. Ask every single owner you meet. This is the highest-leverage question in the kit.
4. **Are onboarded owners' tenants actually paying through the UPI link?** An owner who signed up and still calls on the 5th has churned and doesn't know it. **This is the number that matters**, not signups.

---

## The compounding play

Finish one corridor before you open another. When road 1 is done, your opening line on road 2 becomes:

> *"[Area] ke gyarah PG ismein hain. [Owner] ka bhi, do gali door."*

That sentence is worth more than any feature you could ship this month, and it only exists if you stay put.

---

## If you get into an owners' association or a big WhatsApp group

**Clear the calendar.** Speaking to 50 PG owners in one room, or getting a real endorsement in a 300-member owners' group, is worth more than two weeks of walking. Prepare properly: Video B (the survey — not the demo), the free generator link, and a signup sheet in the room.

Lead with the survey finding, not the product. Let them ask.
