# Late Brake — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Keep the player action primary

Actual heading **“LATE BRAKE”**, supporting line **“Six real-world circuits at true scale. Race your ghost, chase purple sectors, brake later than last lap.”**, CTA **“PLAY NOW”**. This link opened the real `/play.html` menu. Keep it prominent above advertising/blog links. A menu load does not prove a completed race; privately test a valid lap before wider promotion.

Supporting copy: **“Free browser time trials. Pick a circuit and try one clean lap.”** Show **“No install”** separately from data/privacy terms; do not promise no account/storage when guest identifiers and laps can be stored.

## 2. Label actual versus upcoming modes

The menu currently marks **“Online race — Coming soon”** and the supporter pack **“Coming soon”**. Preserve that distinction in previews, listings and video. Do not replace it with a live multiplayer invitation because terms mention online data. If cosmetics become purchasable, display the actual price/currency and entitlement before any payment CTA.

## 3. Explain a valid lap before the first failure

The homepage says leaving the track for more than half a second invalidates a lap. Put a short reminder in the actual first-race instructions: **“Stay on track for a valid lap. Use the racing line while you learn.”** Give the real invalid-lap reason visibly, rather than leaving the player to infer why a time did not count.

## 4. Make sharing/guest identity clear

Privacy says driver names, uploaded lap times and ghosts can be public. Use **“Choose a public driver nickname”** and explain what uploads and when. A guest's browser storage can be lost on clearing data; optional Google sign-in is a different choice. Verify current prompts and upload behaviour before claiming they are tested.

## 5. Phone, controls and trust

Test touch steering/pedals, landscape prompt, pause/resume, first circuit load, invalid-lap feedback and return to menu. Keep keyboard controls accessible and sound optional. Maintain circuit/audio credits and the independent-game notice. Existing Clarity is described on every page: inspect masking and exclude identity/payment screens, rather than adding another recorder. Do not add fake medals, lap records or official-series partner badges.
