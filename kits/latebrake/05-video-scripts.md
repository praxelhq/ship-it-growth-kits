# Late Brake — Video scripts

Use normal-speed footage from your own actual valid lap. No private driver data, altered times or fictitious online rivals. The race menu was inspected, but a full lap was not tested in this research; complete that gate before recording.

## A. One clean lap — 40 seconds, homepage/game invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Real corner approach. | “One corner. One cleaner lap.” |
| 3–13 s | Actual circuit menu and start. | “Open Late Brake, pick a circuit and begin a free browser time trial.” |
| 13–26 s | Actual racing-line/controls segment. | “Use the racing line while you learn. Stay on track if you want the lap to count.” |
| 26–34 s | Your real result, no fake record. | “Then try the same corner again against your own best.” |
| 34–40 s | URL/independent label. | “No install. Independent browser racing.” |

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |

Hold C until a real ghost is saved and displayed. No online racing claim or real-world driving-skill/safety promise follows from these clips.
