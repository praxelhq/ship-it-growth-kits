# DeClutterMac — Positioning

**Live:** https://decluttermac.com/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/decluttermac

**Earn trust with a copied test folder and an actual undo.** A low price and safety badges do not establish that a cleaner handles someone's valuable files correctly. Do not make a full-disk cleanup the first demonstration.

The [actual headline](https://decluttermac.com/) is “A Mac cleaner that doesn't feel like malware.” The primary CTA says “Download Free(Sort 2 Folders),” with “Buy Lifetime Pass—$3.99.” The installer URL returned HTTP 200 in a HEAD check; it was not downloaded or executed. Public page checked 29 October 2026. Native behaviour, safety claims and purchase were not tested.

## Your first person

An adult Mac-using student builder with a messy collection of downloaded PDFs, images and archives, who wants to organize a bounded folder before considering cleanup. They care more about understanding and undoing the change than a dramatic “GB freed” number.

Start with a copied disposable sample folder containing non-sensitive files. Keep originals separately. The first job is preview→explicit sort→inspect→undo, not granting broad permissions and hoping a cleaner knows what to remove.

## Pitch

> Preview a sort for one messy folder, see exactly what moved, and test undo before trusting it with more.

Short version:

> One folder. A visible change. A tested undo.

The page advertises two free folder sorts and $3.99 one-time lifetime unlimited use across personal Macs. It claims Intel/Apple-silicon support; AI topic sorting specifically requires macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, while other Macs use file types. Do not advertise AI topic sorting on every supported Mac.

## The weakness

The home makes strong claims: zero false positives, never deleting all duplicate copies, full undo and protected systems. These were not validated here. Narrow the marketing to behaviour tested in the current release and show its limits. A restored test file is useful evidence; it is not proof every future move is safe.

Keep **1,000 signups**, defined as valid new installs/first launches, with users completing a reviewed bounded sort beside it. Download clicks and the paid pass are separate. The initial small-Mac-circle plan does not honestly forecast a thousand.
