# Apna Catalog — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each wholesaler must satisfy [all four policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): real trading business; its own product data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known owner name and phone with permission. Keep this identifying register outside the public repository. Free-active merchants can count.

Activation: the owner publishes at least ten actual products and one intended buyer successfully submits an accurate item/quantity selection which the owner can review. Report this beside the customer count. A product listing, a WhatsApp forward, a PDF download or a buyer login is not a new merchant. Receiving a sheet is separate from accepting an order, receiving payment and fulfilling it.

Private dashboards were not accessed; the baseline is unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine wholesalers and latest independent use | Product records joined to private owner register; exclude test accounts and assisted-only sessions. |
| Owned products and catalogs published | Seller records; no stock images standing in for the seller's own range. |
| Buyer selection starts/completions and owner receipts | Aggregate status records; check accuracy with the merchant, not private buyer data copied into analytics. |
| PDF/phone-photo issues | Error/device category and permitted sample reproductions. |
| Paid plans/refunds | Actual billing data, separating ₹49 intro month from regular rate. |
| Traffic/course position | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/catalog-maker), timestamp and period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate. Mask/exclude buyer names/phones, negotiated prices, private catalogs, login/payment fields and order contents. Aggregate events: sellercreated, ownproductadded, catalogpublished, buyerorderstarted, buyerordersubmitted, ownerreceiptconfirmed, laterindependentuse. No third-party customer list is required to measure setup completion.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If owners like the appearance but cannot identify units or receive a buyer sheet, fix the path before promoting. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare conversations, own-range setup and actual buyer completion. More photos do not compensate for wrong item quantities. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Check independent recent sharing/updates and owner receipt. Separate a buyer's return from merchant use. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests, not just catalog creation. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying wholesalers against ten, activation, buyer completion, payments/refunds, hours and honest gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 wholesalers. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Three statuses and two introduction requests are upside only. Replace the rates after eight approaches and again after 20. If many owners only need one static catalog, report useful one-off setups separately from businesses meeting the last-seven-day rule. Do not prompt everyone to open the page just to inflate activity.

Do not promise revenue improvement from a cleaner image, count ₹49 as recurring monthly revenue, or claim AI scenes/tracking/videos are live. A real result is a seller and buyer agreeing which item and quantity the sheet represents, with fewer explainers needed in that observed test. Report the actual sample size and limits.
