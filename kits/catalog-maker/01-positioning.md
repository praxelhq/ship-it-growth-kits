# Catalog Maker / Apna Catalog — Positioning

Checked 29 October 2026: [apnacatalog.com](https://apnacatalog.com/) opens **“Welcome to Apna Catalog”** and says **“Take a photo, add a name and price.”** The actual seller CTA is **“I sell — make my catalog”**, leading to `/login?next=%2Fseller`; **“I buy — browse catalogs”** is separate, and **“Add another business”** uses `new=1`. A real-browser read also showed **“Try as demo seller →”**, not exercised here. Seller login, photography, order sheets, phone alerts and checkout were not exercised.

## Your first business

A small wholesale costume-jewellery seller with 15–25 repeat items and existing retail-shop buyers. Today the seller sends a pile of photos and later matches “the second gold one” to an order. Start with a stable small range, not a marketplace full of unknown sellers or a merchant needing live warehouse inventory.

**Pitch: Give repeat buyers one catalog link and a clearer order sheet instead of another pile of product photos.**

Short version: **Photo, name, wholesale price, one link to send.**

The first useful result is the seller entering its own ten products and an existing buyer choosing the intended items and quantities. A sent WhatsApp link is not an order; a saved order sheet is not payment, stock reservation or delivery.

## Public offer

[Plans](https://apnacatalog.com/plans), read 29 October 2026, list Free ₹0 forever: up to 25 products, own photos, catalog link/PDF, buyer order sheets, phone alerts and an Apna Catalog badge. Dukaan ₹199/month lists unlimited products, white-background studio photos and no badge. The page says the first month of any paid plan is ₹49. Pro ₹399/month and Business ₹799/month list extras, but **AI scene photos, catalog-open tracking and product videos are marked coming soon**. Never sell those as current features. Taxes, actual checkout and the exact photo-processing experience were not established here.

The landing language “make it look professional” must not imply the free tier includes white-background processing. Free uses the seller's own photos as taken. The paid white-background offer still needs a real test on reflective jewellery: altered colour, clasp, stone count or scale can misrepresent an item.

## The alternative

The zero-extra-tool workflow is a seller's existing phone photos plus a numbered PDF and a manual order sheet in [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/). [WhatsApp Business](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/business-app) is another official small-business entry point; do not invent comparative paid prices or claim WhatsApp cannot hold product information. Apna Catalog must earn adoption through less item confusion and a usable buyer order sheet.

Prove the ten-item catalog on a buyer's actual phone. Ask them to select three specific items without coaching; compare the resulting sheet with their intent. Let the owner inspect every generated photo before sharing. Keep competitor comparisons about this workflow, not unsupported claims that every seller will increase revenue.

**Target: 10 real supply-side business customers in 30 days.** Under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), each must be a real wholesaler, onboard its own actual products, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Buyers, orders and twenty-five catalog items are separate counts. No public personal contact data belongs in this kit.
