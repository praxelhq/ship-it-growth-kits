# Apna Catalog — Distribution map

Lead with wholesale jewellery sellers already sending product photos to shop buyers. Do not mix this with Parchi's WhatsApp-to-invoice workflow or a stock-management pitch.

| Route | Execution / permission | Planned quantity and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local wholesale jewellery businesses | Walk one public wholesale market or use existing supplier relationships. Ask the owner about repeat-item identification and current buyer ordering. No pressure during peak trading. | Ten blocks of four businesses =40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Existing retail buyers who know their supplier | Ask whether they would forward a catalog-demo invitation to a consenting supplier. Do not request the supplier's private customer list. | Two asks, Days 8 and 15; #4. No assumed extra customers. |
| Own WhatsApp Status | Show an owned sample catalog with fictional prices, no merchant contacts. Let viewers opt into a conversation. | Days 9,18,26; #3. Guess 20–100 views and 0–2 relevant replies/status, not a benchmark or base conversion. |
| One owner-authorized buyer test | The merchant invites one existing buyer to check item selection on their own device. | Day 12; #5. Activation support for one business, not a new seller. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-product setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 wholesalers. At 25% conversation the scenario is 4.9. These are assumptions to replace, not field research. The ten four-owner blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Status replies and warm forwards are upside, deduplicated.

Use a single market segment for the first month. Existing buyer relationships are valuable because the merchant can test order-sheet comprehension without acquiring consumers from scratch. Never create fake buyer demand, upload another shop's photos or submit an enquiry pretending to buy wholesale just to pitch software.

No trade-association endorsement, market WhatsApp-group posting permission or newsletter placement is established. Ask before any group post; one organizer receives one primary invitation per week across kits. Days 8–11 can be a small merchant demonstration only with agreement, not a mass founder workshop. Keep Muggy's campus kitchens and QLess's Gurugram café lists separate.

No paid listings, scraped buyer lists, bulk DMs or promised free paid-photo credits. One relevant follow-up after seven days; a decline ends contact. Report actual shop conversations, not the number of stores passed on the walk.
