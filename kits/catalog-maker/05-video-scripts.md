# Apna Catalog — Phone-demo scripts

Record an owned sample range, your phone screen and your voice. Label every sample price. Do not show customer contacts, private wholesale rates or supplier images used without permission.

## A. Catalog to buyer sheet — 65 seconds

0–3 s: a confusing pile of owned sample photos. **“Which item did the buyer mean?”**

3–18 s: photograph one sample item and enter a precise name, wholesale unit and price in the actual seller flow. “Give this product a name the buyer can recognize.”

18–32 s: open the real catalog link on a second phone. “Now the buyer can inspect the range in one place.” Leave the item selection uncut.

32–47 s: select three items/quantities and inspect the actual order sheet, if supported. “The seller still checks stock and confirms the order.” If receipt fails, fix it rather than editing in success.

47–57 s: show Free and Dukaan boundary. “Free uses your own photos, up to 25 products. Studio photos are a paid feature.”

57–65 s: regular/intro price and URL. “Check the current first-month offer and monthly plan at apnacatalog.com.”

## B. Identification hook — 20 seconds, Status

0–3 s: two visually similar sample pairs. **“The gold one is not an item code.”**

3–12 s: show clear product names beside photos. “Make the buyer's choice easier to identify.”

12–20 s: real catalog link and free allowance. “Your own-photo catalog starts free. Apna Catalog.” No claim of increased revenue.

## C. Photo honesty hook — 25 seconds, Short

0–3 s: original beside an actual processed owned image. **“Cleaner should not mean a different product.”**

3–15 s: inspect colour, stones, clasp and scale. “Check what the image changed before sending it to a buyer.”

15–25 s: owner approval and current plan boundary. “Free uses your photos. Studio-photo processing is in the paid plan. Review before sharing.”

If image processing has not passed a test, show the published plan boundary and explain that it remains to be tested. Do not fabricate a processed result. Capture actual catalog, PDF, order-sheet receipt, current prices and coming-soon labels; label cuts in waiting time.
