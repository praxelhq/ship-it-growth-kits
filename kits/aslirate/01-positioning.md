# AsliRate — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.aslirate.in/ · **Category:** Procurement comparison · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/aslirate

Your headline is **“The lowest quote isn’t always the lowest cost.”** **Try it with sample quotes** opens /demo; **Create a free account** opens /signup. [Pricing](https://www.aslirate.in/pricing) is ₹0 for 3 comparisons/month and ₹999/month plus GST for Pro. The sample demo says nothing is saved. No real supplier quote, account or payment was submitted.

Your first ten are small packaging buyers: D2C operators buying the same box from two to six suppliers, with different tax/freight/MOQ terms. They have an actual purchase this month and can check every extracted field. One SKU and quantity are enough; don't begin by replacing an enterprise procurement system.

**Pitch:** Put supplier quotes on the same quantity and cost basis, review the extracted numbers, then compare the whole order.
**Short version:** Compare the order cost, not just the printed unit rate.

The sample makes the point: a ₹17.20 box rate can become ₹36.20 per required box when the MOQ is twice what is needed and freight is added. That is a labelled sample, not a customer's savings case.

Your named free alternative is a manually reviewed [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/). It can calculate the same arithmetic without a new software purchase. Your value is quote extraction, explicit assumptions, review and repeatability.

**Tax assumptions are not entitlement.** The demo implies GST registration plus a proper invoice produces input credit. [CBIC's Section 16 entry](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter5/section16_v1.00.html) explicitly concerns eligibility/conditions. A comparison must use the buyer's confirmed credit assumption; the app is not a tax-advice or eligibility decision. Have the buyer/CA confirm it.

**Target: 10 real businesses**, with their own authorized quotes reviewed and independent recent use. Sample comparisons, quote views and a CA operating ten demo companies do not become ten customers.
