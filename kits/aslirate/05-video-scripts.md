# AsliRate — Video scripts

Use labelled sample quotes. Never expose negotiated supplier documents or imply a real saving from a demo.

## A.65-second demo — buyer follow-up
0–2: two unit rates; **“Cheapest rate. Bigger order.”**
2–15: show required quantity and a higher MOQ. “You may pay for more stock than you need.”
15–32: open actual sample quotes; review extracted specification, quantity and freight.
32–47: show payment versus assumed credit and cost per required unit. “The tax treatment is an assumption you confirm.”
47–58: compare fixed working with a manual calculation.
58–65: “Three comparisons/month free. aslirate.in.”

## B.20-second hook
0–2: **“Freight not mentioned isn't free.”**
2–12: highlight missing freight in a synthetic quote.
12–20: show the actual uncertainty flag, or label the flag as proposed if not yet implemented.

## C.25-second hook
0–2: three-ply/five-ply labels; **“Same size. Different box.”**
2–14: “A lower rate doesn't win when specifications differ.”
14–22: show the review decision before ranking.
22–25: full URL.

Phone mic, large captions and hard cuts. Show actual loading; don't fake extraction accuracy, tax eligibility or savings.
