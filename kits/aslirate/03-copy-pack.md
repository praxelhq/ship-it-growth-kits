## 3. LinkedIn checklist post
The lowest printed box rate can still produce the most expensive purchase.

Before comparing, write down:
The same material/specification.
The quantity actually needed.
The quantity the supplier makes you buy.
Freight and other charges.
The tax-credit assumption you have confirmed.
Delivery date and quote validity.

I am building AsliRate to make that review visible before ranking suppliers. The sample's savings are sample arithmetic, not a customer result. https://www.aslirate.in/
