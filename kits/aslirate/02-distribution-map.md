Planning assumptions: 50 approaches ×40% substantive conversation ×70% own-quote trial ×70% independent recent use ≈10 businesses. These are demanding guesses, not observed procurement benchmarks. At 20% response the scenario is about 5. Reforecast after the first ten approaches.

No verified procurement newsletter/editorial slot or trade-group promotion permission is included. Do not send sales pitches through suppliers' buyer-enquiry forms. Paid listings and a new acquisition tool purchase are outside budget.
