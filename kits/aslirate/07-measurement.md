# AsliRate — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is an account; activation is an authorized own-quote comparison, with extracted fields reviewed and working inspected.

All four tests apply: genuine business, actual purchasing data onboarded, independent use within the last seven days, privately known buyer/owner name and phone. Payment is not mandatory. Sample calculators, repeated corrections and multiple staff at one firm do not create new customers.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and mask documents, rates, GSTINs and contacts. Record actual business accounts, own-quote comparisons, fields corrected, completed/failed results, manual-check agreement and independently active firms. Private values were unavailable, not zero. Save course rank, reading and sync from [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/aslirate).

Events: account_created, own_quotes_added, fields_reviewed, comparison_completed, working_opened, correction_saved, independent_return. A completed result is use, not proof the extraction or tax treatment is correct.
