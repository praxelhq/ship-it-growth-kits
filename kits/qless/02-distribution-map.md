# QLess — Distribution map

**Café fieldwork first.** A broad city launch will expose an untested operational handoff. No ads, delivery discounts or paid ambassadors.

| Route | Action, rules and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · One walkable Gurugram café route | Select independent cafés with a real lunch queue and an owner who can approve a pilot. Visit outside the rush. Four first approaches on ten named days = 40. Assumed 30% substantive conversation × 60% controlled pilot × 50% independent continued use ≈ 3–4 cafés. No interview or filming permission is implied. | #1 |
| 2 · Café-owned public contact route | Check current menu/opening hours on the café's own site or linked business page; ask about pickup operations, not generic “growth.” Use the same 40-prospect log, not another duplicate list. One follow-up after seven days; stop after no. | #2 |
| 3 · Owner-approved counter sign / regulars | Only after order/payment/staff handoff is verified. The owner places a plain QR/link beside the normal order point and chooses whether to mention it to regulars. Scenario one café, 20 personally invited regulars → 5–10 visits → 0–5 real pickup orders. These are guesses and aren't new B2B customers. | #3 |
| 4 · Office/community organizer | With an actual café live, ask one nearby office organizer to share a useful pilot invitation. No employee list access, group additions or assumed association permission. Model 0–1 accepted invitation and 0–10 orders; no claim of ten new cafés. | #4 |
| 5 · Your own LinkedIn | Show the operational test at 09:00 IST Tuesday, with permission or synthetic data. Assumed 100–500 impressions, 0–5 relevant visits for a small account, possibly zero. No fake queue photo or café endorsement. | #5 |

[Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/) is a checked source for public business-presence tools; use the café's own official pages to verify facts. It doesn't authorize scraping or messaging through consumer review channels. Specific account worth following is the actual pilot café's own business account, with permission for co-posting; no existing partner is claimed here.

No newsletter, local WhatsApp group or public directory placement is booked. If an office organizer requests an opt-in newsletter blurb, #4 is suitable. Your base **3–4 independently active cafés** from 40 approaches is below the ten-business target. At day 14 use staff adoption, capacity and failed-pickup data to decide whether more similar cafés are justified. Don't count visitors or a busy queue as proof of merchant use.
