# QLess — Measurement

**Target: 10 real active café businesses.** Report **cafés whose own staff independently handle real pickup work** beside that number. Orders and consumer accounts are demand metrics; provisioned credentials alone are onboarding inputs.

## Day 0

From your own records count approved cafés, complete live menus, provisioned staff, controlled tests, real orders and independently active merchant days. The public page did not establish those totals or current merchant fees. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, with customer and payment fields masked. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/qless) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero.

Events: `merchant_provisioned`, `menu_approved`, `order_created`, `merchant_acknowledged`, `pickup_ready`, `pickup_completed`, `order_cancelled` and `merchant_returned`. An order-page visit isn't an order, and a submitted order isn't a fulfilled pickup. Exclude all controlled tests from real transaction totals. Do not log passwords, private phone numbers or payment payloads in public analytics.

## The four tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a real café, its actual menu/operations data onboarded, independent product use in the last seven days without your prompting, and a responsible person's name and callable contact held privately. Paying is optional. Multiple staff logins at one café aren't several customers. Your administrator running the queue on behalf of a café is assisted work, not independent adoption.

## Forecast

The calendar schedules 40 first approaches. Hypothetical 30% owner conversation × 60% controlled pilot × 50% independent continued use = **3–4 businesses**. A nearby-office invitation may bring diners; it doesn't create more merchants automatically. The base forecast is below ten. After the first 20 approaches, measure owner fit and staff adoption before expanding. Don't close the gap by relabelling diners as customers.
