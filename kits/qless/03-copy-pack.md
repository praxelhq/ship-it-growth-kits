# QLess — Copy pack

Only use the consumer blocks after a real café approves its menu, pickup terms and live order path. Fee and settlement terms must be confirmed with the owner before a real-money pilot.

## #1 — Owner in-person opener

“Do regulars ever call ahead and still wait while the counter works out their order? I'm testing QLess for order-ahead pickup in a small Gurugram café cluster. Could I come back at a quiet time and walk one order through your staff process? We'd start with your approved items and realistic pickup times. I won't send customers before you and your team agree the test works.”

Don't interrupt lunch service, claim an existing integration or ask staff to give you a private owner number.

## #2 — Café business email / DM

**Subject:** A controlled pickup-order test for your café

Hi — I'm building QLess in Gurugram. The first test isn't a city-wide launch: it's whether one café can receive a pre-order, acknowledge it and hand it over at an honest pickup time.

Would you be open to a short owner/staff walkthrough outside your rush? Staff access is set up by the Qless administrator, not a public merchant signup. Before any live orders, we'll confirm the actual fees, payment handling and cancellation process with you. Public site: https://q-less.app/

If it doesn't fit your kitchen, no problem. One follow-up only: “Would a quiet-time staff test be useful, or should I close this out?”

## #3 — Owner-approved counter message

Trying order-ahead pickup today? Ask our counter team whether QLess is available for your order. We'll confirm the menu and pickup time before you rely on it. It is pickup, not delivery, and preparation can still take time. If something goes wrong, speak to the counter team rather than placing the same order again.

The café adds its actual live link/QR through its own approved sign. Never print the general discovery URL as though it confirms a particular café is accepting orders.

## #4 — Office organizer invitation

Would your members like to try an owner-approved pickup pilot with a nearby café? The point is to order before walking over and see a realistic pickup status, not promise instant food. I'll share the confirmed café, menu, live link and terms only once the café has passed a controlled order test. No one needs to give me an employee list or join a new group. QLess overview: https://q-less.app/

This is a request for permission, not a ready claim that an unnamed café is live. An accepted organizer publishes the actual approved merchant link separately.

## #5 — Public operations post

“Skip the queue” is easy to write. A café still has a kitchen.

I'm testing QLess with a much more useful question: can the staff receive an order, acknowledge it and give a pickup time they can actually meet?

I'm starting in one Gurugram cluster, with owner-approved menus and controlled handoffs before any public push. The café should know what is coming, what it can prepare and what to tell the customer when something changes.

If you've run a café counter, which failure should I test first: sold-out items, late preparation or a customer placing the order twice?
