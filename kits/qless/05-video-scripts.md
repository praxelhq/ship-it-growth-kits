# QLess — Video scripts

Film outside service rush with owner permission. No customer faces, payment data or staff passwords. Screen recording plus your phone voice is enough; label controlled orders as tests.

## A · From order to counter · 60 seconds · owner demonstration

0–2s hook: **“Did the café actually receive it?”** Show a test order ID with no personal data. 2–15s: display an owner-approved item and pickup terms: “Start with a menu the café can serve.” 15–28s: place a controlled test order, text “Controlled test”; say “A customer confirmation isn't enough if the staff never sees it.” 28–42s: show the authorized staff acknowledgement: “Now the café checks the order and gives an honest next step.” 42–55s: show the actual pickup/status handoff: “The kitchen's capacity still matters. This isn't a delivery promise.” 55–60s: “Testing in one Gurugram cluster. Q-less dot app.”

If the staff path fails, don't edit around it. Fix it before filming a successful demonstration.

## B · A kitchen still takes time · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“Ordering ahead doesn't cook the food.”** 0–8s: show a clearly labelled preparation example: “A café must confirm what it can prepare and when.” 8–15s: show the real status language: “The useful promise is a clear handoff, not a stopwatch we can't control.” 15–20s: “That's what I'm testing with QLess.” No invented average wait saving.

## C · The sold-out test · 25 seconds · owner onboarding

Hook 0–2s: **“What happens when the last sandwich is gone?”** 2–10s: show a controlled sold-out item and the owner's actual availability control. 10–18s: “If the app still accepts it, stop the pilot and fix the flow.” 18–25s: show the corrected customer state: “A clear no beats a paid order the café can't fulfil.”

If availability control doesn't exist, label it a required test rather than staging a fake feature. The video should reveal operational readiness, not hide it.
