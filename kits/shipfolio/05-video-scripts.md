# Shipfolio — Video scripts

Use a fictional candidate and project. Do not expose real resumes, API keys, employer confidential work or personal portfolio links without permission.

## A. Demo — 60 seconds, LinkedIn and site

0–2s, resume bullet beside empty case-study page: **“Your project needs more than one line.”**

2–12s, upload a fictional resume. “This example has a real source for every fact I'm about to show.”

12–28s, generate the preview; label any cut in waiting time. “Shipfolio builds the first page. Now I check the role, numbers and contribution.”

28–43s, open one project, highlight evidence and delete an unsupported phrase. “The useful part is what a reader can inspect, not the size of the claim.”

43–52s, preview on a phone. “Check this as a recruiter would: can you explain the work in half a minute?”

52–60s, show actual pricing. “Preview free. Publishing/downloads are $9, with hosting renewal after year one. shipfolio.xyz.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel/Short

0–2s: “Would you click this project?” Show a vague resume line.

2–10s: show a project page with problem, contribution and evidence. “Give someone a question, your part and a result they can verify.”

10–15s: synthetic page on phone and URL. Text: **Fictional example · free preview**.

## C. Trust hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “A bigger number isn't a better portfolio.”

2–12s: highlight output metric, then its source. “If it isn't in your evidence, delete it. Your interview still has to stand behind this page.”

12–20s: show the edited truthful claim. “Review before you publish.” End URL, no hiring guarantee.

## D. Price explainer — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “What's free, and what isn't?”

2–12s: show the Free and Pro cards. “Generate and preview free. Pay $9 to publish or download, with the first hosting year included.”

12–20s: highlight renewal. “Hosting renews at $7.99/year afterward. Read the cancellation terms before buying.” Honest pricing is a better hook than an abandoned checkout.
