# Shipfolio — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.shipfolio.xyz/ · **Category:** Career portfolio tools · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/shipfolio

The live headline checked on 29 October is **“This portfolio builder gets you hired.”** The CTA is **“Create my portfolio”**, pointing to login with an onboarding redirect. That route exists; this kit did not create an account, upload a resume or pay to publish.

**The hiring promise is stronger than your evidence.** A portfolio can show work; it cannot guarantee employment. Your best first user is a business-school student with two genuine projects who wants a clickable proof-of-work page for a recruiter tomorrow.

## The offer to lead with

**Turn your resume and real project evidence into a portfolio you can preview, edit and publish.**

Short version: **Give your projects a link, not just a bullet.**

The page offers free generation and watermarked Studio preview. Publishing/downloads require **$9 tax included**, with one year of hosting, then **$7.99/year hosting renewal**. Cancelling hosting retains Pro/downloads according to the page. Do not say “free hosting forever” or hide the renewal behind “pay once.”

## Your wedge

A marketing, operations or consulting project that needs one clear case study: problem, personal contribution, evidence and outcome. The first test is whether a stranger can understand that contribution in 30 seconds. A fancy home page is secondary.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) offers a free professional profile, verified credentials and project showcase, including a profile on one's own domain according to its public page. It is a real $0 alternative. Shipfolio has to earn its price through resume-to-page speed, editing control, hosting/export convenience and the student's preferred presentation. Do not claim it is the only way to put work online.

## The uncomfortable demonstration

The page rewrites an unquantified task into four launches, $1.2M pipeline and 35% improvement while promising no invented facts. Unless those facts are shown in the source, the sample contradicts the promise. Fix that before promoting factual accuracy. Your marketing should demonstrate traceability from a fictional source resume to the output.

Existing public portfolios/testimonials are visible on the site, but do not reuse people's names or employment as an endorsement in this kit. Use a fictional profile for recording. The B2C target is 1,000 accounts; report reviewed previews and published portfolios beside it. A thousand empty accounts is not a thousand career outcomes.
