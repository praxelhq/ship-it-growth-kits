# Shipfolio — Landing page fixes

Based on [shipfolio.xyz](https://www.shipfolio.xyz/), checked 29 October. Public copy and login destinations were inspected; authenticated generation, publishing, checkout and file export were not tested.

## 1. Show where every improved fact came from

The displayed “before” contains no figures, while the “after” adds four launches, $1.2M and 35%. That undermines the strict fact-checking claim.

Replacement sample: show a fictional source with those exact figures, highlight the fields, then show the output. Caption: **“Fictional example. Every number is supplied in the source resume.”** If no source exists, remove the numbers. This is the most important trust fix.

## 2. Replace the hiring guarantee

Current headline: **“This portfolio builder gets you hired.”** Current CTA: **“Create my portfolio.”**

Replacement: **“Turn your real work into a portfolio people can inspect.”** Support: **“Start from your resume, review the claims, add project evidence and preview your page before publishing.”** Primary CTA: **“Build my free preview.”**

## 3. Put the full payment lifecycle beside the first CTA

The page says “Pay once” but also lists annual hosting renewal. Replacement strip: **“Preview free · Publish/download for $9, tax included · First year hosting included · Hosting renews at $7.99/year.”** Explain what cancelling hosting changes and what downloads/Pro access remain. Do not change the real offer merely to improve copy.

## 4. Explain what becomes public

The current privacy language is broad. Before publishing, show a clear review: name, contact links, location, employer/project details and images. Suggested button: **“Review what will be public.”** Then: **“Publish this version.”** Candidate resumes may contain information they never intended to post on the web.

## 5. Make proof inspectable without overclaiming

The site already includes real-portfolio links with permission. Keep a small representative set and describe them as examples, not employment guarantees. Add a synthetic sample case study the visitor can open without login. Do not imply a listed employer is a customer or partner.

## 6. Test the whole file and hosting handoff

On a fresh account with a fictional resume: inspect every generated fact, open the live preview on a phone, verify the paywall appears before publish/download, and test a downloadable HTML/zip after an authorized test payment. Test refund/cancellation instructions separately. Never spend a student's money to verify a marketing claim without authorization.

## 7. Match the link preview

Suggested title: **“Shipfolio — resume to editable portfolio.”** Description: **“Preview your project story, review every fact and publish when ready.”** Use fictional content in the preview image. At 390px, ensure the price and renewal fit without hiding them behind hover or small print. These are checks, not observed visual defects.
