# Shipfolio — Distribution map

**Lead with proof-of-work demonstrations among students already applying.** A recruiter-facing portfolio needs real projects, so recruit people with evidence before chasing broad reach.

| Route | Scheduled action and permission | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four placement/project WhatsApp groups | Ask admins; share one invitation each. Participants upload privately, never send resumes into the group. | 4 × 30 readers × 20–40% trial = 24–48 first previews. | #1 |
| Ten peer-project reviews | Ten willing classmates or alumni with a completed project. Each may invite one friend if useful; no referral pressure. | 10 initial trials plus 0–10 invited trials; not a guaranteed doubling. | #2 |
| Three LinkedIn posts | One case-study explanation, one factual check, one recruiter-view demonstration at 12:30 IST on Days 8, 16, 25. | 3 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 3–41 accounts. | #3 |
| Three Reels/Shorts | Synthetic resume-to-case-study proof, published Days 11, 19, 26. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Honest maker launch after generation/export tests; acknowledge it as an alternative. Check current Monday launch slot. No vote trading. | 0–40 accounts, uncertain upside. | #5 |
| [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) | Optional free suggestion after email verification. Review backlog is large; paid priority is out of budget. | Zero month-one forecast until accepted. | #5 adapted |

This base plan is closer to dozens or low hundreds than 1,000. At 30% visitor-to-account you need about 3,334 qualified visits. Do not claim the listed experiments already supply them. Expand only the channel with real preview completion and candidate satisfaction.

No unverified newsletter, Discord invite or subreddit permission is assumed. For a career-club newsletter, ask the organizer you know for one free project-review invitation; zero forecast until accepted. The appropriate accounts to reply to are candidates publicly asking how to present a real project; answer their question before mentioning your tool and never paste the same pitch under unrelated hiring posts.

Keep your review invitations in Days 17–20 and use different groups from OnePager's formatting clinic and Anvaya's human coaching. Do not sell a paid publication requirement as a free campus workshop.
