# PlayLead — Video scripts

Use an adult tester and fictional family details. No child photos, parent contacts or real enquiry screenshots. Record screen and voice; label sample and shortened waits.

## A. Parent question to reviewed next step — 65 seconds

0–3 s: fictional “Which batch suits us?” **“What does the academy need to know first?”**

3–18 s: actual sample collects age band, experience, centre and timing. “Get the context before suggesting a trial.”

18–32 s: inspect the answer against owner-confirmed batch information. “A helpful answer must match what the academy can offer.”

32–47 s: show requested slot and owner handoff. “Requested is not confirmed.” Use real controls only; stop at sample if live handoff isn't tested.

47–57 s: actual manual WhatsApp draft. “The team reviews and sends. Automation is planned.”

57–65 s: offer/URL. “Check the current trial and plan before onboarding. playlead.co.in.”

## B. Availability hook — 20 seconds

0–3 s: sample Saturday slot. **“Can the academy actually honor this time?”**

3–13 s: compare current batch information with the requested slot. “The owner confirms availability, not a confident sentence.”

13–20 s: pending-confirmation label and URL. No fake booking success.

## C. Handoff hook — 25 seconds

0–3 s: lead label beside its reason. **“Hot for which reason?”**

3–16 s: show actual context and the next human action. “Read the question, centre and timing before calling.”

16–25 s: manual draft/current boundary and URL. “A useful enquiry helps a person follow up.” No invented admissions or revenue.
