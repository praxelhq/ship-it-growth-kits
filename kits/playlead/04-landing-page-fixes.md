# PlayLead — Landing-page fixes

Read public page and sample academy, 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“The play that turns every enquiry into an admission.”** CTA **“Start free trial”**. Starter ₹999/300 conversations, Growth ₹2,499/1,000, Pro ₹4,999/3,000 monthly before GST. Account, actual parent flow and billing were not exercised.

1. **Remove guaranteed admissions.** Replace headline with **“Turn parent questions into a clearer academy follow-up.”** Support: **“Collect the context, show current batch information and let your team confirm the next step.”** An assistant cannot guarantee every enquiry becomes an admission.
2. **Label the sample and ROI.** Keep Velocity Sports Pune as a clearly identified demonstration. Replacement figure caption: **“Illustrative scenario, not measured customer results. Actual admission rates and fees vary.”** Do not present three additional admissions or lifetime value as actual revenue.
3. **Separate selected and confirmed slots.** Replacement: **“Requested trial: awaiting academy confirmation.”** Test unavailable slots, timezone/date, duplicate requests, cancellation and human handoff. Do not imply calendar integration or guaranteed coach availability without testing.
4. **Keep future features out of current value.** Replacement: **“Today: reviewed lead details and manual WhatsApp drafts. Planned: automated WhatsApp and online fee payments.”** Preserve this beside paid plans and phone screenshots.
5. **Explain conversation allowances and trial.** Publish actual trial duration, consumption/reset rule, failed-conversation behavior, price plus GST and chosen renewal terms. No duration was verified; don't invent seven or fourteen days. Show final payable amount before payment.
6. **Explain labels and data.** Current hot/warm/cold labels include reasons. Replacement helper: **“A follow-up aid from the information entered, not proof a family will enroll.”** Collect only necessary parent-controlled information and explain visibility/deletion. No child profiles in public analytics.
7. **Phone gate.** Fresh adult tester checks English/Hinglish, wrong centre, unknown answer and real human follow-up. Replacement fallback: **“I cannot confirm that. Please ask the academy.”** Do not hide failure behind a persuasive invented answer. Claims about academy-only data remain unverified security claims until tested.
