# PlayLead — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each needs a genuine academy business, own accurate centre/batch information onboarded, independent use within the last seven days and privately known owner name/phone. Count a business once; parents, coaches, branches, enquiries, trial requests and admissions are separate measures.

Signup is an academy account. Activation is a real academy's accurate information supporting a permitted enquiry and an owner-reviewed next action. A sample conversation or an assisted demo doesn't establish independent business use. Confirm actual availability before reporting a trial booking; report later attendance/admission/payment separately.

Private baseline unavailable, not zero: genuine academy accounts, own-data setups, permitted enquiries, owner follow-ups, confirmed trials, actual admissions, independent recent use and billing. Capture rank, reading, period and sync from [profile](https://shipped.money/p/playlead). Don't sum rolling readings.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude parent/child identity, conversation content, phones, schedule details, accounts and payment screens. Aggregate events: academy_created, own_batch_ready, enquiry_started, permitted_enquiry_completed, trial_requested, owner_confirmation_recorded, manual_followup_logged, independent_owner_return. Verify event meanings; no personal transcripts needed for a growth dashboard.

Inspect wrong answers, unavailable slots, weak handoff and consent issues weekly. The site's illustrative ROI doesn't prove incremental admissions or lifetime revenue. Record real outcomes only with permission, denominators, dates and attribution limits. No invented coach endorsement or children's testimonials.
