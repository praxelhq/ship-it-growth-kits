The public assistant describes English/Hinglish questions about age, level, centre and timing, trial-slot selection, reasons behind hot/warm/cold labels and manual WhatsApp drafts. Automated WhatsApp Business API messages and online fee payments are planned, not live. The owner must confirm actual availability; a selected slot is not automatically a confirmed admission or paid fee.

Verify actual renewal/allowance behavior before purchase. The page's enquiry-to-admission and lifetime-value arithmetic is illustrative, not measured academy ROI.

The free manual alternative is a [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) with parent permission, age band, requested batch, next action and confirmed trial. PlayLead must reduce missing context and handoff confusion, not claim every enquiry will convert. Check minor/parent privacy, human handoff and incorrect answers before live use. No medical, fitness-readiness or child-safety advice from the assistant.

**Target: 10 real academy businesses**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): own current academy data onboarded, independent recent use, real operation and privately known owner name/phone.
