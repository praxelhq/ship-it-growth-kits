# Play XI — Video scripts

Use your own/explicitly permissioned team; hide identifying details. No broadcast match footage, another manager's data, password, prizes, betting or guaranteed points. Current player/deadline facts require fresh official checks.

## A. Import-and-compare demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“Why this move for this squad?”**

2–12s: open the actual public Team ID field. “No FPL password. This is my team.” Hide the ID if sharing it isn't needed.

12–27s: import after owned-data flow verified, show actual Gameweek/data timestamp. “First check that the squad and data are current.” Label waiting cuts.

27–43s: open a genuine available idea and its factors. “Projection, form, fixtures, season returns and availability are reasons to compare—not certainty.”

43–53s: show one reason you might reject it. “The decision stays mine. Actual transfer happens in official FPL.” No real move required for the demo.

53–60s: URL, **3 ideas/GW free · extra unlock not open yet**. If import fails, fix before filming a successful result.

## B. Reason hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Would I still want this move without the top-line projection?”**

2–12s: show actual factor comparison with no real-time recommendation in the caption. “Read the assumptions and availability.”

12–18s: URL/model-limit note. No guaranteed rank improvement.

## C. No-password hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“An FPL Team ID isn't your password.”**

2–12s: show actual import explanation, no private ID entered. “The planner reads public data and cannot submit a transfer.”

12–20s: official-game boundary/URL. “Use your own team, and keep account credentials private.”

## D. Freshness hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A good explanation can still use old data.”**

2–12s: actual update/Gameweek field if implemented, otherwise labelled proposed display. “Check when this was updated and the current official news before acting.”

12–20s: URL and estimates note. No invented injury, fixture or deadline; don't use a fake timestamp to make a prototype seem live.
