# ContingencyPlan — Positioning

Live: https://www.contingencyme.app/ · Other / B2C · [Course profile](https://shipped.money/p/contingencyplan)

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, with a completed, usable backup plan reported beside accounts. The present public evidence supports an account form, not a demonstrated planning workflow. Do not spend the month promoting a mystery behind a login.

Inspected 29 October 2026: the live brand is ContingencyGenius. The login headline is **“Welcome back, stay ready.”** and the subhead is **“Your day changes. Your backup plan changes with it.”** “Enter my workspace” asks for email/password. “New here? Create an account” opens **“Build your safety net.”**, with name, email, password and “Create my account”. We opened both forms without submitting. No workspace, price, plan generation or privacy implementation was verified. The course profile supplies no additional product explanation.

Your proposed first audience is adults who already write ordinary day-to-day fallback arrangements: a postponed meeting, an unavailable venue or a cancelled activity. This is a research wedge, not a claim that your current app supports these cases. First establish what one real user can actually create and retrieve. Do not advertise emergency, medical, financial or disaster readiness on the strength of this homepage.

Working pitch after that test: “Write down what you will do if today's arrangement changes.” Bio: “A calmer place for your everyday Plan B.” Replace these if the actual workspace does something different; evidence decides the use case.

The genuine alternative is an existing note or [Notion Free, $0](https://www.notion.com/pricing). A new account is worthwhile only if ContingencyGenius makes a fallback easier to create, find or update than that note. You have not publicly demonstrated this advantage. Publish one fictional, complete example before inviting strangers. No public price was found; do not call the service permanently free.
