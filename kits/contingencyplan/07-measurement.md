# ContingencyPlan — Measurement

**Headline: 1,000 signups in 30 days. Activation beside it: people who complete and can reopen one usable backup arrangement.** Account creation alone does not prove readiness. If the workspace does not support this activation, establish its actual useful action on Day 1 and explicitly document the correction before inviting testers.

Day-0 baseline is unknown: record total real accounts, new accounts this month, completed plans, repeat users, current leaderboard position and analytics status. Do not convert the course profile's empty chart into zero users. [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is free, but setup and correct event capture were not verified. Exclude private plan bodies, names and form input from recordings; use aggregate events and consented process interviews. Never copy account emails into this public kit.

Measure invitation source → landing → account start → account success → first useful plan → reopen after a day. Count each genuine person once; exclude your tests, bots and duplicate accounts. Track errors without plan text. A research reply or waitlist email is not a signup. Distinguish first-month accounts from pre-existing accounts.

`02` schedules 20 invitations, two accepted circle posts and three owned posts. Its guessed 6–21 account attempts cannot substantiate 1,000 successful accounts. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified unique visits; that is a requirement, not predicted traffic. Compute the actual rate once you have observations. Do not inflate it with generic impression counts.

W1: if three people cannot explain the use without you, repair `04`. W2: if people understand it but abandon signup, test necessity, errors and price. W3: if accounts create nothing, shorten the first action; if plans are never reopened, ask whether an existing note solves the job. W4: expand only the source producing completed, reopened arrangements. Otherwise report the target gap and product uncertainty plainly. Do not purchase impressions or imply a successful safety outcome from a saved plan.
