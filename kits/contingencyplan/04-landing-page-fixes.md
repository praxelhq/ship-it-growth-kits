# ContingencyPlan — Landing page fixes

Observed 29 October 2026: **“Welcome back, stay ready.”** / **“Your day changes. Your backup plan changes with it.”**, followed by “Enter my workspace”. New-user form: “Build your safety net.” / “Create my account”. The public routes render; authenticated planning and pricing were not tested.

1. **Explain the product before the account gate.** Current welcome-back copy assumes prior knowledge. Proposed ordinary-planning wedge, subject to workspace verification: **“When today's arrangement changes, keep your next step ready.”** Body: **“See a fictional everyday backup arrangement before deciding whether to create an account.”** CTA: **“See an example”**, secondary **“Create an account”**. Link the example to a real accessible screen, not another login.
2. **Show one complete outcome.** Add a clearly labelled fictional scenario: **“Original arrangement: meet at the courtyard. If rain prevents it: use the agreed indoor room. Confirm the room before telling the group.”** This is explanatory copy, not evidence of an implemented field, reminder or automation. Adapt it to the actual working interface. Do not imply monitoring, automatic alerts or emergency protection.
3. **State price and boundaries.** No price was publicly verified. Add the actual price/free limits before signup. Replace unsubstantiated reassurance from “Private by design” with a verified statement of storage, providers, deletion and account recovery. Do not collect sensitive plans until this is established.
4. **Make the first action and failures clear.** After a fictional-data test, name the actual first action and whether it saves. Written error: **“Your change wasn't saved. Try again before closing this screen.”** Only show saved confirmation after persistence succeeds. Test refresh and another signed-in session rather than assuming success from a button click.
5. **Mobile and preview.** Test narrow-screen form labels, keyboard and error focus. Proposed title: **“ContingencyGenius — everyday backup arrangements”** only if that is the verified use. Preview image should show the fictional example; never a real schedule or address. No fabricated customer proof.
