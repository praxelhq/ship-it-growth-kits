# ContingencyPlan — Distribution map

**Discovery and small tests first; public launch only after the workspace is explained.** All ranges below are planning guesses, not historical results. The calendar budgets 20 personal invitations, two permissioned circle invitations and three owned posts. Nothing assumes a large existing following.

| Priority / channel | Permission and use | Expected result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. People who already use a backup note | Ask 20 known adults individually about a recent ordinary change. Do not request private plans or mass-message contacts. | 5–10 conversations, 3–8 account attempts; #1. |
| 2. One existing adult class or work-planning circle | Ask its admin first; publish only after acceptance, in two different circles/weeks. An invite is not permission to extract member contacts. | Combined 40–100 relevant views, 2–8 accounts; #2. |
| 3. Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) profile | Personal build explanation, disclose ownership; no unsolicited tagging or success claims. [Basic is free](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545596). | Three posts total: 60–240 impressions, 1–5 accounts; #3. |
| 4. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) | Optional free personal-account launch after a usable product exists; check an existing listing and current launch rules. No direct upvote requests. | 0–5 accounts, excluded from the base forecast; #4. |

Base: 6–21 account attempts before deduplication, perhaps 3–15 meaningful plan completions if the workflow works. These are hypotheses to replace with observed rates. They do not support 1,000 accounts. No paid newsletter, bought directory placement or unverified emergency community is in this plan. Coordinate campus circles with other planning products; a circle gets one product invitation that week.
