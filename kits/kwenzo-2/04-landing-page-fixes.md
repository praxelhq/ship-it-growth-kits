# Kwenzo — Landing page fixes

Live page and browser controls checked 29 October 2026. No order was placed.

1. **Make the promise local and concrete.** Current hero: “The one who does.” Replace the supporting heading with **“Order site materials in Hyderabad. Check your pincode first.”** Keep the existing brand line as a signature, not the only explanation.
2. **Stop the WhatsApp dead end.** The page says support is not available yet and the business number will be added before launch. Remove any promise to order by WhatsApp until a real supported channel exists. Replacement: **“Order on this website. For help, use the contact route below; WhatsApp ordering has not launched.”** Verify the email/support route actually delivers before outreach.
3. **Clarify delivery before the cart.** Existing **Check delivery** is the right first control. After a pincode, show **“Delivery available to this pincode. Confirm timing and unloading conditions before you place an order.”** Only show a specific time after stock and operations support it.
4. **Keep the list review explicit.** Current **Upload your list** opens a photo/text panel. Photo reading requires sign-in and consent to Google Gemini. Replacement helper: **“Type the list without uploading a photo, or sign in to use photo reading. Review every matched item; handwriting can be wrong.”** Never hide the data disclosure under a generic checkbox.
5. **Resolve price assumptions.** A 50 kg cement bag shows ₹395; free delivery is advertised above ₹5,000. Add next to the cart total: **“Check brand, pack size, tax, delivery and unloading charges before confirming.”** Show whether the threshold uses tax-inclusive or exclusive value. Do not claim a current stock price from a static sample.
6. **Prove fulfilment, not a render.** Add one permissioned order case showing confirmed list, delivery evidence and discrepancies. Until then label imagery illustrative and say **“Local pilot: availability confirmed per order.”**
7. **Mobile/preview check.** On a 360 px phone test pincode→variant→cart with slow data and a fresh session. Photograph the confirmed order summary for the user before payment. Ensure language switches preserve cart context. No visual defect was independently established, so test rather than claim one.

Traffic gate: a real serviceable order can be reviewed, confirmed, fulfilled and supported. A working catalog alone is insufficient.
