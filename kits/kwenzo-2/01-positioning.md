# Kwenzo — Positioning

**Live:** https://kwenzo.me/ · **Category:** E-commerce / construction procurement · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kwenzo-2

Your promise is “The one who does.” The page serves Hyderabad and offers cement, plumbing, paints and other site materials. A real browser on 29 October 2026 showed **Check delivery**, **Upload your list** and **Start shopping**. ACC Suraksha PPC cement, 50 kg, was displayed at ₹395; free delivery is advertised above ₹5,000 and pay-on-delivery is offered. These are the site's displayed terms, not a confirmed stock/fulfilment quote.

**Say the awkward thing first:** WhatsApp ordering support is explicitly “not available yet.” Do not tell contractors to send a list to a number that has not launched. Photo-list reading asks permission to send the photo to Google Gemini and warns not to upload personal documents. Checkout, availability and an actual delivery were not tested.

Your first ten are small Hyderabad contractors with a live job, a material list this week and permission to choose the supplier. Begin in one actually serviceable pincode. Do not pursue homeowners nationwide or claim citywide stock depth before you can deliver.

**Pitch:** Turn this week's material list into a reviewed Kwenzo order, check delivery to your Hyderabad site, and pay under the terms shown.
**Bio:** Site materials in Hyderabad, with a list you review before ordering.

The wedge is an urgent replenishment list for one live site: exact brand, grade, variant, quantity and promised delivery. [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/) and [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) expose existing suppliers; buying through a contractor's incumbent supplier may require no new platform fee, with materials quoted separately. Your advantage must be a completed correct delivery, not an unsupported “best price.”

The target is **10 real contractor businesses**, each with its own site/list onboarded and independently using the service in the final seven days. One contractor's five workers are one customer. A cart or delivery-check click alone is not a customer.
