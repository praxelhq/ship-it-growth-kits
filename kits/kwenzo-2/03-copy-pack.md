# Kwenzo — Copy pack

## 1. Contractor visit / phone opener
Hi, I am building Kwenzo for site-material orders in Hyderabad. Are you the person who decides where this site's smaller replenishment orders come from?

I would like to understand one recent list: what was needed, what arrived late or wrong, and how you checked the final bill. If our service covers your pincode, we can work through one real list and confirm items before you order. I am not promising a price or delivery time until it is checked.

If invited to demonstrate: “Here is Check delivery. Then we match the exact material, brand, variant and quantity. Please correct anything wrong before it enters the cart.”

## 2. Supplier introduction request
Do you know a contractor with a live Hyderabad site who regularly needs small material top-ups? I am testing a reviewed list-to-order flow. Please ask them first; I do not need your customer list. One introduction in an area we actually deliver to is enough.

## 3. Warm message
I am testing Kwenzo with Hyderabad contractors, one serviceable area at a time. If someone in your network has a real site-material list this week and chooses the supplier, could you ask whether they want a short conversation? No purchase needed to discuss the workflow. Please do not add them to a group or share their number without asking.

## 4. LinkedIn post
A construction list is not just “cement and pipes.”

It is the brand, grade, size, quantity, site location and the time the crew needs it. Getting one of those wrong can make a cheap quote useless.

I am building Kwenzo around reviewing that list before ordering. We start by checking the Hyderabad delivery pincode, then confirm the exact items. Pay-on-delivery and delivery charges follow the terms shown for the order.

I am looking for a few contractors willing to walk through an actual replenishment list. Not a nationwide launch: a small local test we can fulfil properly. https://kwenzo.me/

Use a redacted list with permission. Do not say “saved hours” until the contractor measures it.

## 5. After a real delivery
Did the exact brand, quantity and variant arrive at the agreed site and time? What did you still have to call us to clarify? Please judge the delivery against the confirmed order, not this message. If you do not have another material need this week, no problem — we will not invent one.

One follow-up after seven days to an unanswered approach, then stop. Never turn a trial customer's private site address into a public case study.
