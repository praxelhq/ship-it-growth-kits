# Kwenzo — Measurement

**Target: 10 real contractor-business customers**, not general website signups. See [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md).

An account/cart is a signup-stage metric. Activation is a contractor's real list reviewed into a confirmed serviceable order. Report deliveries completed correctly and independently returning contractors beside the headline.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag; verify public-page/pincode activity, masking addresses and order details. Record accounts, contractor businesses, confirmed orders, fulfilled orders, wrong/missing items, delivery promises kept, cancellations and revenue from actual records. These values are unknown to this kit, not assumed zero. Record your current course rank, saved reading and sync timestamp at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/kwenzo-2).

Events: delivery_checked, list_reviewed, order_confirmed, order_fulfilled, discrepancy_reported, repeat_order. Retain personal delivery/contact data privately.

## Customer test
Each counted business must be genuine; have its own site/material list onboarded; have independently used the service in the last seven days; and have a privately recorded decision-maker name/phone. One firm counts once. Payment is not mandatory under policy, but an unfulfilled shopping cart never counts as a fulfilled customer.

| Checkpoint | Goal / diagnosis |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 |8 approaches, 2 real lists; stop if you cannot fulfil accurately. |
| Day 14 |20 approaches, 5 trials; if conversations do not become lists, check zone and timing fit. |
| Day 21 |28 approaches, 4 qualifying recent users; investigate fulfilment before more content. |
| Day 30 |40 approaches and 10 qualifying businesses target; report actual result. |

The assumptions in 02 yield roughly 7 qualifying businesses, not 10. Do not make the gap disappear with duplicate workers, unconfirmed orders or consumer accounts. If accepted introductions do not close it, explain the shortfall.

Sunday sheet: approaches→conversations→lists→confirmed orders→correct deliveries→recent independent use. Add hours and acquisition source. A high repeat rate caused by fixing your own wrong delivery is a support failure, not retention. If wrong-item deliveries repeat, stop new outreach until the matching/review problem is corrected.
