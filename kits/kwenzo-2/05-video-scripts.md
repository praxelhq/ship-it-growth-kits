# Kwenzo — Video scripts

Use a synthetic material list, phone mic and screen recorder. Never film a site, worker or delivery address without permission.

## A.65-second demo — contractor follow-up
0–2: close-up of a list; text **“Which size did you order?”** Say “This is where a material order goes wrong.”
2–15: show Check delivery and explain “First confirm the site's pincode.”
15–35: type a small list, choose the exact brand/size/quantity; say “Each match needs your review. A photo reader can misread handwriting.”
35–50: show the order summary, charges and current payment terms. “Check the total and confirmed delivery conditions.”
50–65: full URL. “Kwenzo is starting locally in Hyderabad. Ask us about one real list we can fulfil.”

Keep the item review uncut. Do not complete checkout during filming unless it is a real authorized order.

## B.20-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: hold two differently sized pipe fittings, text **“Same name. Wrong size.”**
2–10: “A low price is no use if the item cannot be used on site.”
10–17: show exact variant confirmation in Kwenzo.
17–20: “Review the list before you order. kwenzo.me.”

## C.25-second hook — permission-based contractor message
0–2: text **“Delivery to which pincode?”**
2–9: show the serviceability control. “Before promising a truck, check the delivery area.”
9–20: show cart charges and material list; “Then confirm stock, quantity and timing.”
20–25: “One local order, done correctly, is the test.”

Use large captions and real UI at 150% zoom. These scripts demonstrate ordering clarity, not invented discounts or guaranteed delivery speed.
