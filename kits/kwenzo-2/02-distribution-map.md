# Kwenzo — Distribution map

**Fieldwork and supplier referrals first.** A Hyderabad site supervisor is more useful than an international launch directory. Stay in one delivery zone until fulfilment is proven.

| Channel / route | Execution and boundaries | Planned result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) contractor discovery | Manually find local civil/interior contractors; verify their own business site and operating pincode. Do not submit fake project enquiries or export phone lists. Ask for a short procurement conversation outside work rush. | 10 field blocks × 4 unique contractors =40 approaches; #1. |
| 2. Your existing hardware/plumbing supplier relationships | Ask two real merchants whether they will introduce one contractor with a current list. No commissions, credit promises or claim of stock you do not hold. | 0–4 introductions; #2. Not guaranteed orders. |
| 3. One campus/alumni contractor introduction | Ask a person you know; get permission before entering a project site or forwarding contacts. No posting in unjoined groups. | 0–3 conversations; #3. |
| 4. Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 8,18,26 at 09:00 IST: exact list→review→delivery story, with permission and measured facts only. | Guess 100–500 impressions/post, 0–3 relevant visits; #4. |

Working scenario, not a benchmark: 40 qualified approaches × 50% conversation × 60% real list trial × 60% independent recent use ≈7 businesses. The board still wants 10. Accepted introductions and better actual conversion must close the gap; do not manufacture it by counting unfulfilled orders.

Skip Product Hunt, paid directory plans and broad WhatsApp broadcasts. They do not solve serviceability. No verified relevant newsletter slot is included. Your own WhatsApp can support existing permission-based conversations, but the product's official WhatsApp channel is not yet available.

Qualify every prospect: serviceable site, exact material need, decision-maker, timing, ability to fulfil. Never visit an active construction area without permission and site safety instructions.
