No promotional permission in a financial subreddit/Telegram group, external newsletter slot or professional-trader partnership was verified. Don't pitch under a person's trading loss. If an introduced educational editor wants a free source/time worksheet, send #4 after permission; subscriber count isn't booked reach.

Base **8–39 accounts before overlap**, using twenty invitations, six posts, one optional session and one optional launch. Below 1,000. At day 14 inspect whether the learner returns and trusts the original source more, rather than treating a machine score as certainty. Do not use paid “real-time” language until actual tier latency/coverage are established.
