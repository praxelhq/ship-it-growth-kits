# SquawkWire — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **one original-source/timestamp review completed, followed by a later independent news-reading return**. Track first review and return separately. Scores, headlines, alerts delivered and terminal page views aren't distinct users or financial outcomes. No trading action is required or attributed to adoption.

## Day 0

The [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/squawkwire) had no eligible connected-source readings at research time. Missing isn't zero accounts. Record actual accounts, source opens, delay labels, independent return use, failed/stale sources and any real billing from owner records, with current rank/timestamp. Public login exists; private baseline/feed performance wasn't inspected.

Check actual consent/privacy and existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding tracking. The current policies are unfinished templates, so resolve their operating truth first. Mask account credentials, watch keywords, alert rules and private workspace details. Content-free proposed counts only if supported: `account_created`, `headline_viewed`, `original_source_opened`, `delay_viewed`, `interpretation_reviewed`, `independent_return`, `source_failed`, `subscription_confirmed`. A source-open event doesn't prove comprehension; use a small voluntary question without collecting financial details.

## Forecast from `06`

Assumed rates, not financial-news benchmarks:

- 20 invitations × 40% try × 70% account ≈ five to six accounts.
- Six posts × 100–500 impressions × 2% visits × 25% account ≈ 3–15 accounts.
- One accepted session: ten invited × 60% attend × 50% account ≈ three, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–100 visits × 15% account = 0–15 accounts.

Rounded **8–39 accounts before overlap**, far below 1,000. If half complete a first source review and later return, roughly 4–20 activate. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified visits; the calendar doesn't establish that scale. Don't increase the forecast with an assumed paid real-time tier or a promised professional-trader audience.
