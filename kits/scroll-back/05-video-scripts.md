# Scroll Back — Video scripts

Every file and answer in these recordings is synthetic. Use readable captions and real screen capture; never ask a friend to surrender a private export for marketing.

## A. Demo — 60 seconds, site and LinkedIn

0–2s, phone network toggles: **“This chat story doesn't need your chat uploaded.”**

2–12s, open Scrollback, then disconnect. “I loaded the page first. This is a made-up WhatsApp export.”

12–28s, select file and show actual parsing. “The file is read here on the device.” Don't edit a failed parse into success.

28–42s, read three free screens without exposing private content. “You see a preview before choosing whether to unlock.”

42–52s, show price cards. “Full story ₹49; gift edition ₹149. Payment needs internet.”

52–60s, show share warning and URL. “Check what the link contains before sending it. scrollback.in.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: airplane mode icon and **“Still works?”**

2–10s: select synthetic export and reveal story. “Loaded page. Network off. Story built.”

10–15s: URL and **3 screens free · synthetic demo**. Say: “Try the preview without giving me your chat.”

## C. Export tutorial — 25 seconds, Story/Short

0–2s: “The file picker isn't the first step.”

2–15s: demonstrate export on your own fake conversation: open chat settings, Export, Without media, save to Files. Name the phone platform on screen.

15–25s: return to Scrollback and select that file. “Never send the export to me. Use it on your device.” End with URL.

## D. Gift hook — 20 seconds, Reel

0–2s: fictional gift card: **“A birthday gift only your chat could make.”**

2–12s: show synthetic story highlights and personal note. “Use a conversation you're both comfortable turning into a keepsake.”

12–20s: show ₹149 gift price and the content-bearing link warning. “Private to send doesn't mean impossible to forward. Ask first.” No staged crying reaction or invented testimonial.
