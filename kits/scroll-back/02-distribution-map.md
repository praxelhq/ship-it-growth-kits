# Scroll Back — Distribution map

**Lead with the offline demonstration, then an existing friend-group occasion.** People will hesitate to upload intimate chats; prove the local flow before asking them to trust you.

| Route | Scheduled action / boundary | Assumed result, not observed performance | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing friends | Ask them to use synthetic or mutually agreed chat data on their own device. Never request their export by DM. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed previews if the export path works. | #1 |
| Three existing friend/alumni WhatsApp groups | Admin permission, one message each, product URL only. No group member data or raw chat in replies. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% start = 18–36 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels/Shorts | Offline proof, export tutorial, gift example and private-card explanation on Days 8, 13, 20, 26 at 20:00 IST. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–120 first sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | A technical trust demonstration and one lesson about private sharing, Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–30 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker-feedback launch after offline and paywall tests, current Monday eligibility/slot checked. No votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |
| [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) | Only a usable local demo, not a landing/signup page. Explain how it works; don't solicit votes or comments from friends. | Zero baseline; optional technical feedback, not promised birthday buyers. | #6 |

The base may reach tens or low hundreds. At 50% visit-to-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. Do not model every preview as a public share: most should remain private. Measure whether a voluntary share brings one new person before forecasting a loop.

No paid gifting pages, influencers, directory boosts or unverified newsletter placements. A creator can share a synthetic demo voluntarily; do not promise a free paid story or discount that does not exist. Keep chat-court products out of these same groups that week: Scrollback's wedge is a gift/story, not deciding who is wrong.
