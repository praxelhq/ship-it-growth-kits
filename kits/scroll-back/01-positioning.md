# Scroll Back — Positioning

**Live:** https://scrollback.in/ · **Category:** Private chat-story entertainment · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/scroll-back

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“Your chat, as a story.”** The CTA is **“Make my story”**, leading to the local upload section. The site offers three free screens, **₹49** for the full story and **₹149** for a gift edition, one payment rather than a subscription.

Your first user is someone approaching a friend's birthday who wants a personal, funny gift from a chat they both recognize. Start with consenting friends, not a tool for exposing an ex or monitoring a partner.

**Pitch:** Turn a WhatsApp export into a funny story on your own device; see three screens free before choosing whether to unlock more.

**Short version:** Your group chat's story, without uploading the chat.

## The wedge

The trust demonstration is the product: open the page, disconnect the network, load a synthetic export and show the story still being built. The page explicitly claims local/offline parsing, no account and no server storage. Those are claims to verify in your demonstration, not a reason to hide limitations. Payment and any generated gift link involve different sharing decisions; explain them separately.

[WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) offers free messaging/calling and its own [chat-export instructions](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1180414079177245). The $0 alternative is searching a chat and making your own screenshot collage. Scrollback earns its price by doing the structure and playful highlights while leaving raw messages on the device. Do not claim manual searching is impossible or invent a competitor's price.

## The uncomfortable bit

The page says a whole story can travel inside its link. A link that contains story content can be forwarded, copied or exposed even without a storage database. “No upload” does not mean “safe to post anywhere.” Share a card only after everyone represented is comfortable; never publish raw excerpts, private names or link contents without consent.

The live page and upload destination were read; no real export or purchase was submitted. Day 1 proves parsing, offline behaviour and unlock recovery with synthetic data. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful story sessions, with completed free previews beside it. A click or repeated parsing of the same export is not another user.
