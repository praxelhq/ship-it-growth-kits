# Fan Notch — Video scripts

Film the real beta with your own voice and captions. Use sample values only with a persistent sample label. Don't show tokens, private prices or financial account information.

## A. Menu access demo —45 seconds, horizontal

| Time | Shot / caption | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
|0–2|Pointer approaches the menu. “Can I still click this?”|“A ticker is useless if it steals your menu.”|
|2–12|Actual row runs behind the notch.|“Fan Notch keeps scores in the top row while I work.”|
|12–23|Move pointer up, strip yields, open a real menu; uncut.|“When I need the menu, the row moves out of the way.”|
|23–33|Expand sports, then a labelled market view with no keys visible.|“Sports are free. Markets can use your own supported provider key. Pro changes layout and movement.”|
|33–45|Download page plus beta/freshness labels.|“This beta isn't Apple-verified yet and sports data is delayed. Try free on a personal Mac if that fits. fanotch.me.”|

Use on the homepage and allowed Mac-community submission.

## B. The pointer trick —15 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Wait, the menu is underneath.”** Show the row.

2–8: Move pointer up; click the menu. **“So it gets out of the way.”**

8–12: Move away; row returns. **“Scores when you glance. Menus when you work.”**

12–15: **“Free Mac beta. Apple verification pending.”** URL on screen. Publish to LinkedIn/X during Days 11–14.

## C. What is actually free —25 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“You don't pay just to see a score.”**

2–10: Real free app sports setup. **“Sports, refresh rates and notifications are in the free app.”**

10–18: Open layout controls that accurately show the paid distinction. **“Pro is for pinning, placement and scroll speed.”**

18–25: Price page with current tier, not a frozen remaining-stock claim. **“Founding licences start at the listed tier while available. Test the beta first. fanotch.me.”**

## D. Sports first, keys later —30 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“You shouldn't need a market token to try a cricket score.”**

2–13: Record the supported sports-only first run without cuts; if it cannot be done, fix it before publishing this script.

13–22: Show freshness age. **“This is delayed score data, not an instant trading or ball-alert tool.”**

22–30: Point to later market settings with token fields blank. **“Market providers are optional setup after this. Start with the free view.”** Use in support and the installation area, not as financial advice.
