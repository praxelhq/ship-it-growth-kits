Your base plan supports roughly 6–83 first launches before overlap, not an evidenced thousand. Deduplicate sources, and replace these assumptions after the first week. Reach is not the same as app compatibility.

## Directories, newsletters and accounts

[Product Hunt's launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) supports a free maker launch; the calendar first checks whether your current version is already listed. No paid promotion. Treat it as 0–10 additional launches until measured, and use #3.

Do not budget a Mac-newsletter placement or name an editor whose submission route has not been verified. Read actual maker discussions in r/macapps and reply to questions about menu obstruction and stream freshness. Ask permission before privately sending a build. A useful response does not need a link.

Campus/workspace demonstrations lead on Days 11–14, between 12th Man's first-week cricket demos and Munchy Mates' later pet demos. Different developers still respect the community's own rules; a shared cohort is not permission to flood a feed.
