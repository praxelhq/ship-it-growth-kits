# Luna — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://luna.org.in/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Your period tracker grew up.”**; the action is **“Start free”**. Luna Free is $0. Luna+ is listed at $29/year, charged in US dollars through Dodo Payments; taxes may be added. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is an adult deciding whether to keep sensitive cycle notes in a new tool. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Lead with the actual job

**Current:** “Your period tracker grew up.” is broad; the basket can attract delivery expectations.

**Write this instead:**

> Your cycle notes, clearly kept. Log dates and symptoms, see estimates with a range, and prepare a history you can share yourself.

**Verify:** Keep real record-keeping first and sample commerce secondary.

## Fix 2 — Keep demo separate from account

**Current:** The page offers “Start free” and “Try the demo”.

**Write this instead:**

> Try a fictional demo without an account. Start your own private record only when you choose.

**Verify:** Test /demo and /login separately. Never prefill an actual user record with demo data.

## Fix 3 — Label commerce at the click

**Current:** Period Emergency uses sample items and simulated checkout.

**Write this instead:**

> Demo basket only. No order is placed and no delivery is promised.

**Verify:** Repeat the boundary before every basket checkout action, not only in the FAQ.

## Fix 4 — Keep predictions honest

**Current:** The sample says “In about 18 days · give or take 3”.

**Write this instead:**

> Estimate, still learning from your entries. Logged dates are your record; predicted dates can change.

**Verify:** Do not replace ranges with a precise-looking certainty badge or ovulation promise.

## Fix 5 — Make optional AI explicit

**Current:** The page says AI is off by default and sees anonymous totals.

**Write this instead:**

> AI insights are optional and off by default. Review what is sent before turning them on.

**Verify:** Verify that control in the actual account flow before claiming it as tested.

## Fix 6 — Show annual billing clearly

**Current:** Luna+ displays $29/year and a $2.42 monthly equivalent.

**Write this instead:**

> Luna+ is charged $29 for a year, in US dollars. Taxes may be added at checkout.

**Verify:** Do not present the monthly equivalent as a cancellable monthly charge; verify export/deletion with synthetic data.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
