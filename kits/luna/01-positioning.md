# Luna — Positioning

**Live:** https://luna.org.in/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/luna

## The thing to face first

Period Emergency is a sample catalogue and simulated checkout, not a delivery service. Lead with the actual tracking job. Do not turn a cycle estimate into a diagnosis, precise ovulation prediction or a promise that products will arrive.

## What is actually on your page

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Your period tracker grew up.”**. The next explanation is cycle and symptom logging with personal history, estimates and a doctor-ready summary. The action is **“Start free”**. Luna Free is $0. Luna+ is listed at $29/year, charged in US dollars through Dodo Payments; taxes may be added. These are observations from [your public product page](https://luna.org.in/), not a completed purchase or a promise that every authenticated flow works.

The free action points to /login and the no-account sample to /demo. The page labels the sample basket and its prices as illustrative. It says AI insights are optional/off by default and predictions have a range. Authentication, health-record permissions, paid checkout and deletion were not exercised in this research.

## Your first person

An adult who currently records period dates in scattered notes and wants one clear personal history for an ordinary appointment. Start with that record-keeping job, not an anxious person looking for a diagnosis or an urgent delivery.

Do not broaden this to everyone who might someday enjoy the category. Find twenty people with this exact situation, watch five use the product, then repeat the invitation that produced an unprompted return.

## The pitch

> Keep your cycle dates and symptoms together, with estimates clearly separated from what you logged.

For a bio or a short reply:

> Cycle notes and a readable personal history. Free tracking; optional yearly Plus.

## Your opening use case

Show a wholly fictional three-cycle history and the distinction between logged and estimated days. Then show a blank daily log and the summary route. Let the person decide privately whether to start; do not ask them to discuss dates or symptoms in a group.

## The real alternative

[Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) costs $0/year and can hold dated personal notes; a paper calendar is another manual option. Luna must earn use through clear cycle-specific logging and summaries, not by pretending every note app is paid. No current Flo or Clue regional subscription price was verified, so no unsupported cheaper-than claim belongs here.

## What you need to prove

Use synthetic data to test a summary, export and deletion end to end before public proof. A visible privacy promise is not proof access controls were audited. Describe the completed test accurately; do not publish real cycle histories or health outcomes as marketing evidence.

**Your board target stays 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Your product-specific counting rule is in `07`. The channel forecast in `02` is a planning scenario, not evidence that you will reach it. If your actual ceiling is lower, report the shortfall and the active people you did help; never reclassify visits or repeat sessions to fill the gap.
