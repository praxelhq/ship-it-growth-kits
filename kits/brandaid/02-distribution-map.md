# BrandAid — Distribution map

Lead with agencies already preparing a brief. Keep the list separate from Before's stationery founders and PitchPanel's founder clinic; agency research is a different buying job.

| Route | Action and permission | Quantity / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing independent strategy/design agencies | Manually qualify a live packaged-food brief, client-data permission and responsibility for research. Use the agency's own public business enquiry route or a consented introduction. | Ten blocks of five agencies = 50 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two warm strategist introductions | Ask a known professional to forward the invitation, not send their client/contact list. | Days 14 and 17; #4. No guaranteed additional customers. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Publish one sourced-matrix lesson, one sample-versus-evidence lesson and one reviewed-brief lesson. | Days 8, 18 and 26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One permission-based peer brief review | A willing agency reviews its own draft privately; use a fictional example for anyone else. | Day 19; #5. Existing-trial support, not another customer. |

Base assumptions: 50 approaches × 40% substantive conversation × 70% authorized own-brand setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 agencies. At 20% response, about 5. These are guesses, not observed agency funnel benchmarks. The ten outreach blocks are Days 4, 5, 9, 11, 14, 16, 17, 22, 24 and 27. Introductions and owned posts are upside only; deduplicate all sources.

Days 14–17 are reserved for the cross-lane agency invitation. Coordinate with [the founder note](../../shared/a2-founder-research.md): BrandAid does not re-invite PitchPanel's D2C clinic or NXT's technical-founders list. ArtificialInfluence owns direct brand-owner AI visibility; Threadly agency reporting is narrower answer-engine monitoring, so distinguish the brief and avoid the same strategist receiving both invitations that week.

No agency newsletter has agreed to feature the product. No Slack, WhatsApp or professional community promotion rule has been established. Ask the organizer you know before any group message; declining ends outreach. One relevant follow-up after seven days is enough. No purchased agency list, automated DM sequence, paid directories or pretending to be a prospective client to reach a salesperson.

A reply about a fictional sample measures interest. Count a real customer only after authorized own data and independent recent use. If no live sources can be inspected, present the current report as a hypothesis exercise and repair that gap before promoting evidence-backed intelligence.
