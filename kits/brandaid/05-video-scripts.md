# BrandAid — Phone-demo scripts

Record your screen and your own voice. Use a fictional brand and permitted public competitor materials. Do not expose client login, strategy documents or personal contacts. A source panel must be real; build it before demonstrating it as working.

## A. The reviewed brief — 65 seconds

0–3 s: three source tabs beside a draft brief. **“Can you challenge the recommendation?”**

3–15 s: enter one fictional brand and three clearly relevant comparison brands. “I am checking one positioning question, not asking software to run the whole strategy.”

15–30 s: show the actual analysis flow, leaving one interaction uncut. Label any shortened wait. “The first report is a draft to inspect.”

30–47 s: open one genuine source and compare its exact wording with the output. “Here is the observation. Here is the interpretation. If the source is missing, the claim stays unresolved.”

47–57 s: remove an unsupported recommendation in the real workflow, or annotate your own review sheet if editing is not supported. “A useful brief can show what we rejected.”

57–65 s: free allowance and URL. “One brand, three monthly analyses free. brandaid.live.”

Do not pretend a source was fetched or a PDF exported when only a landing-page illustration exists.

## B. Sample hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: fictional skincare card with its sample label. **“This number is not a market fact.”**

3–12 s: highlight the disclosure, then a real source/date. “A sample shows the format. Your client brief needs its own evidence.”

12–20 s: real entry route and URL. “Try the workflow. Keep the labels. BrandAid.” No made-up before/after growth.

## C. Positioning hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: three competitor pack claims. **“Different words, or a real difference?”**

3–15 s: compare the same product category and pack basis. “Match the product and context before declaring an empty space in the market.”

15–25 s: one clearly labelled hypothesis beside the sources. “Use the gap as a question for buyers, not proof that demand exists.” End URL.

Capture actual entry, Free allowance, sample disclosure, analysis state, source inspection and a reviewed output only when supported. Hold text long enough to read on a phone. No finished video or paid editor is required.
