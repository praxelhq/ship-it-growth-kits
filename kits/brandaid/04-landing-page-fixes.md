# BrandAid — Landing-page fixes

Read [brandaid.live](https://brandaid.live/) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“See what others miss.”** CTA: **“Try it now · no sign-up”**. Free ₹0, Pro ₹999/month plus GST, Team ₹2,499/month plus GST. Public destinations were observed; actual analyses, connected data, billing and shared workspaces were not tested.

## 1. Say what the first action produces

Current headline is memorable but does not define the first use.

Replacement: **“Build a clearer competitor-positioning brief.”** Support: **“Start with one brand and three relevant rivals. Inspect the evidence before deciding which opportunity belongs in your plan.”** CTA: **“Explore one brand.”**

Verify the entry really supports that workflow. Do not promise a no-account result if it immediately demands registration without explanation.

## 2. Keep fictional labels beside every figure

Current page marks the skincare examples and overall figures fictional. Repeated screenshots can lose the surrounding disclosure.

Replacement caption on every sample card: **“Fictional brand · illustrative numbers · not live market evidence.”**

Keep the label on downloadable screenshots, previews and clips. Never remove it to make a deck look credible.

## 3. Separate live sources from model suggestions

Current copy promises connected brand data, competitor activity and market signals. The sample lists source types rather than inspectable real sources.

Replacement panel: **“Evidence used: source URL, checked date and exact observation. Interpretation: what we think it may mean. Missing evidence: what still needs checking.”**

Only mark a source live when actually fetched. Make a source failure or stale date visible rather than filling the gap with an invented trend.

## 4. Explain the brand-health score

Current sample is 72/100, with visibility, engagement, sentiment and distinctiveness.

Replacement note: **“A product-defined score from the inputs shown, not an independent rating of your business.”**

Publish the real weighting, missing-data handling and comparison basis. If the methodology is not ready, label the score experimental; avoid an unexplained precision badge.

## 5. Put allowances near brand entry

Current Free has one brand/three analyses monthly/three competitors/twenty messages. Pro and Team add different brand/seat limits.

Replacement strip: **“Try one brand free: three analyses and three competitors per month. See plan limits before adding another brand.”**

Check which actions consume an analysis and disclose that actual behavior. Show GST and billing interval before payment. Do not imply Team's ten seats mean ten separate customer businesses.

## 6. Make exports and sharing safe to preview

Current paid plans promise PDF/slides and shared comments. Show a synthetic export, then test authorized accounts before claiming real collaboration.

Replacement pre-share note: **“Review client confidentiality, sources and unsupported claims before sharing this brief.”**

Check the recipient's actual permissions, revocation and export content. Never put a real client strategy in a public preview.

On a clean phone browser, verify brand entry, loading/failure states, signup handoff, allowance messages and source links. An actual source gap should pause an evidence-backed campaign. A fetch failing during research does not mean the public site is dead.
