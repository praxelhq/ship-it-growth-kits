# BrandAid — Positioning

Checked 29 October 2026 against [brandaid.live](https://brandaid.live/). Actual headline: **“See what others miss.”** Main CTA: **“Try it now · no sign-up”**, with brand-name entry and a dashboard destination. The public site also links to `/signup` and plan-specific signup routes. Entering a real client brand, running an analysis, account creation, exports, collaboration and checkout were not tested.

Your most important disclosure is **“All figures above are samples for a fictional brand.”** Preserve it. The skincare score, rising search percentages and three-step-kit recommendation are illustrations, not measured trends or customer results. A report without inspectable sources is a set of hypotheses, however polished its design.

## The first customer

A small brand-strategy agency preparing a real positioning brief for an authorized Indian packaged-food client. It needs a dated comparison of three relevant rivals' product promises and pack formats. The strategist has permission to use the client's information and can verify the product pages before presenting recommendations.

**Pitch: Build a competitor-positioning brief, then inspect the evidence behind each proposed opportunity.**

Short version: **A clearer brief starts with a source you can open.**

The opening use case is one brand, three comparable competitors and one decision: which claim is underused and credible for this client? Avoid a broad promise to predict the next trend or optimize every marketing channel. Agencies are buying a workflow for their business; this is B2B even if a solo consultant runs the analysis.

## Public offer and boundaries

[Pricing](https://brandaid.live/pricing) shows Free ₹0: one brand, three analyses/month, three competitors and twenty assistant messages/month. Pro ₹999/month plus GST lists five brands, unlimited analyses, five competitors and PDF/slide exports. Team ₹2,499/month plus GST lists unlimited brands, ten seats, shared reports/comments and priority support. These are website offers, not verified entitlements or billing outcomes. Do not call team collaboration operational until tested with authorized accounts.

The page promises brand health inputs, competitor intelligence, market trends and evidence/confidence for opportunities. The audit observed those claims and fictional demonstrations; it did not independently establish fresh search data, connected analytics, complete competitor coverage or a validated scoring methodology. Never convert a sample +38% into a real category statistic.

## Your real alternative

A strategist can build a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) matrix of competitor URLs, checked dates, exact claims, pack sizes and notes, with a free personal [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing) page for the brief. Do not compare paid AI/team capabilities as free. BrandAid must earn adoption through saved review effort and an inspectable workflow; a guessed brand-health number is not the advantage.

Prove one real authorized brand input, source verification and a reusable reviewed brief. Ask the strategist which recommendation they removed after checking it. A discarded unsupported claim can be useful feedback; it is not a marketing failure to conceal.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Count agencies, not each client brand or seat. Under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), each must onboard its own authorized data, return independently in the last seven days, and have a privately known owner name/phone. A no-account sample visitor is not a customer.
