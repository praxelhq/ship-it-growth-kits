# BrandAid — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), all four tests apply: genuine agency/consultancy; its own authorized brand/client data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; owner name and phone privately known. Store identifying details privately, not in this repository. Free-but-active agencies can count; paying-but-dormant ones cannot.

Activation: an agency completes one authorized brand comparison and reviews at least one proposed opportunity against its source. Record a reviewed brief beside the customer total. Ten client brands in one agency workspace are one customer business, not ten. An anonymous sample session cannot satisfy the business tests.

No private dashboard was accessed. Baseline totals, paid conversions and rank are unknown until recorded.

| Baseline | Source / meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Real agencies, total and recent independent users | Workspace records plus private owner register; exclude tests and assisted-only sessions. |
| Own-data setups and completed analyses | Actual brand setup/result events; sample pages are separate. |
| Source checks and reviewed opportunities | Status events or consented private review notes; not copied confidential strategy. |
| Export/share success | Real output and permission tests; button clicks alone are not successful delivery. |
| Paying agencies/refunds | Actual billing records, excluding internal tests. |
| Traffic and course position | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/brandaid), with date and period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a second script. Mask/exclude client names where confidential, strategy text, uploaded data, private reports, account/payment fields and access-bearing links. Aggregate events can record workspacecreated, ownbrandadded, analysiscompleted, sourceopened, opportunityreviewed, laterindependentuse. A source click is not proof the statement is correct.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Ten approaches scheduled. If conversations produce interest but no authorized setups, inspect trust, scope and permission. Stop if the product invents live sources. |
| Day 14 | 25 approaches scheduled. Check how many agencies can verify a recommendation. Fix evidence gaps before widening distribution. |
| Day 21 | 35 approaches scheduled. Check independent recent use and whether a reviewed brief was useful. Do not manufacture repeat use by messaging every agency first. |
| Day 28 | 50 approaches scheduled. Audit all four policy tests; separate seats, client brands and real agencies. |
| Day 30 | Report actual agencies against ten, activation, reviewed outputs, recent use, revenue/refunds and hours. State the gap. |

Base planning guesses: 50 × 40% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 agencies; at 20% conversation, 4.9. Introductions and three owned posts are upside only. Replace rates after the first ten approaches and again after 25. If one-off pitch work ends, the agency may have received value while failing the last-seven-day rule: record that honestly instead of claiming an ongoing habit.

No health score, predicted campaign outcome or fictional trend proves client revenue. Record real downstream campaign results only with the agency's permission, dates and attribution limits. Publish aggregate lessons, not client strategy or invented testimonials.
