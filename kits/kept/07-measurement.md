# Kept — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Report **unique people confirming a real save** and **independent later retrievers** beside accounts. [Target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md).

Count one new confirmed email account once. Exclude tests, bots, repeated sign-ins, screenshots, collection rows and extension interest. Activation is a confirmed permitted save after reviewing suggestions. Retention is a later-day retrieval/opening for an actual purpose. “Been there” is self-reported, not verified venue attendance.

Private accounts, saves, retrievals and subscriptions were not supplied. Record actual totals before promotion. Verify existing Clarity once and mask screenshots, notes, email and private collections. Use aggregate events; do not upload collection contents to analytics.

Events: `account_created`, `email_confirmed`, `save_started`, `suggestion_corrected`, `save_confirmed`, `search_used`, `save_retrieved`, `original_opened`, `later_day_retrieval`, `subscription_started`, `subscription_canceled`, `refund_recorded`. A suggested address is not verified accuracy; a completed purchase is not another user. Use coarse campaign labels, no private locations/contact names in URLs.

## Forecast and decisions

The actual plan schedules three sessions, twenty invitations, eight clips and one listing.02 models **25–117 accounts before overlap**, not enough evidence for 1,000. At assumed 25% visit-to-account, you need 4,000 qualified unique visitors. Replace the rate after actual data; do not count sample collection browsing as accounts.

Milestones are decisions, not predictions. Weekly rows: qualified visitors, new confirmed accounts, savers, later retrievers, failed confirmations, suggestion corrections, paid starts, refunds and hours. Show numerators/denominators.

Visits without account creation point to unclear value/trust. Accounts without confirmation point to delivery/return friction. Confirmed accounts without saves point to input/privacy confusion. Saves without retrieval mean the collection is not solving the later decision. Repair misleading place suggestions and preserve original sources before promoting more capture volume.
