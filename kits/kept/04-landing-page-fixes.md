# Kept — Landing page fixes

Home, pricing, privacy, terms and contact read 29 October 2026. Signup/email confirmation, save and subscription were not completed. No visual audit was performed; test rather than infer defects from a text fetch.

## 1. Show the retrieval job before authentication

Current headline: “Keep what you love.” The page presents “Sign in” and “Create account” with email/password and confirmation guidance.

Replacement supporting section:

> **Saved the place? Find it when you make the plan.**
>
> Keep permitted screenshots and links, check suggested details, and return to a small collection you can search.
>
> **Create my first collection**
>
> Free up to 30 saves. Confirm your email to start.

Add a labeled sample of three places and one retrieval before the form. Keep the calm direction; do not replace it with a generic feature grid.

## 2. Make email confirmation recoverable

The home explicitly asks new users to confirm email. Test three unfamiliar adults on mobile data: signup, receive/confirm email, return, save one item and find it again. Test expired confirmation, wrong password and opening email in another browser.

Copy:

> Check the email you used to create your account. Follow the confirmation link, then return to sign in. If it expired, request a new confirmation rather than making another account.

Only name a resend control after verifying it exists. If the required path fails, pause promotion until repaired.

## 3. Show suggestions as suggestions

Pricing/terms say AI suggests title, collection and details and can be wrong. Put review at save:

> Check the title, collection and place details before confirming. Kept's suggestion is not a verified address, current price or opening-hours guarantee.

Keep the original link visible so the user can verify later. “Been there” is the user's report, not verified footfall or a venue endorsement.

## 4. State screenshot/link processing accurately

The privacy page says Google services receive screenshot/text for suggestions, and a provider reads public Instagram details for shared links. Do not claim fully offline or no uploads.

Copy:

> Save only material you have the right to use. Your screenshot/link context is processed by the services described in our privacy policy. Use public, non-sensitive examples for the first test.

The extension captures on explicit click according to the policy; verify actual install/permissions before promoting it. A live store listing was not checked here.

## 5. Keep the price and renewal unmistakable

Current offer: 30 saves free; ₹99/month or ₹499/year plus tax for unlimited. Pro renews automatically. The annual price is not lifetime.

Replacement:

> **Free—30 saves.** Try a real collection first.
>
> **Pro—₹99/month or ₹499 paid yearly, plus applicable taxes.** Renews until canceled. Review the final total before payment.

Show the real Plans/cancel/refund path and test entitlement under owner-approved test conditions. Do not invent a free trial or guaranteed refund beyond the published policy.

## 6. Demonstrate actual return and deletion

Show save→correct→find with your own public item, then where a user can remove it. The privacy page says deleting a save removes the screenshot; test that behaviour before promising it. Account-data requests use the published contact route; do not replace manual support with an imaginary self-service export button.

Mask emails, screenshots, notes and private collections in recordings. Use aggregate save/retrieval events rather than their contents.

## 7. Phone/share check

Test 360 px, large text, keyboard use, slow uploads and confirmation return. Suggested title: “Kept—saved places, found when you plan.” Description: “A small searchable collection. Edit suggestions.30 saves free.” Preview privately; never expose someone's actual collection by default.
