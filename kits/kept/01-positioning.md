# Kept — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.keptapp.website/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kept

**Sell finding the cafe again, not unlimited saving.** Your competitor can already save unlimited links for free. Your useful first result is a small personal collection that helps someone make an actual weekend decision.

The [actual headline](https://www.keptapp.website/) is “Keep what you love.” The page shows Cafes, Outfits, Trips, Books and Movies, then “Sign in” and “Create account,” with email/password entry and email confirmation. The [pricing page](https://www.keptapp.website/pricing) explains screenshot/reel/link saves, AI suggestions, search and “Been there” tracking. Public pages were read 29 October 2026; signup, confirmation, save and subscription were not completed.

## Your first person

An adult who saves cafe/place ideas from friends or public posts, then cannot find the right one when a weekend plan arrives. Start with one city and one use case: three places they genuinely want to remember. Do not pitch five collections and a lifetime memory system on the first visit.

The person checks suggested title, collection and address before confirming. AI extraction is an assistant, not a verified location listing or an assurance a venue is still open. The [terms](https://www.keptapp.website/terms) explicitly say suggestions can be wrong.

## Pitch

> Keep the places you want to try, and find the right save when you make the plan.

Short version:

> Saved somewhere? Kept for the weekend.

Your wedge is save-three/retrieve-one: save three permitted public place ideas, correct suggestions, then find one later using search/filters. Activation is one confirmed real save; the more useful second number is someone retrieving it for an actual plan. “Been there” is a later self-report, not verified footfall.

## Actual costs and alternatives

Free includes up to 30 saves, AI suggestions, collections, search/filters and supported sharing entry points. Pro is ₹99/month or ₹499/year plus tax, auto-renewing, for unlimited saves. The actual final checkout total and cancellation controls were not tested; do not call ₹499 a one-time lifetime price.

## The weakness

The quiet home goes straight to authentication without showing the save→correct→find outcome clearly. A new person may not understand why to confirm an email before seeing the value. Show a labeled non-private sample collection and actual retrieval before asking for trust.

The [privacy page](https://www.keptapp.website/privacy) says screenshots/text can go to Google services and public reel details may be read through a provider. Do not claim fully offline or no uploads. Use permitted public examples, not private messages or content the person has no right to save.

Keep **1,000 signups** as the cohort target, with confirmed savers and later retrievers beside it. Anonymous sample browsing and extension interest do not count as new accounts.
