# Kept — Video scripts

Use your own permitted public item, not a private message. Hide account/location details that are not meant for sharing. Phone screen recording, voice and captions are enough. No fake venue partnership or visit.

## A. Save three, find one—60 seconds

**Where:**owned accounts/small-circle session.** Hook:**“I saved it. Now where is it?”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Three actual permitted place ideas. | “I saved it. Now where is it? I want a collection I can return to.” |
| 8–22 s | Actual save and suggested details. | “I add the item and check the title, category and details. The suggestion can be wrong.” |
| 22–35 s | Three-item collection. | “Three real ideas are enough for the first test. Not a thousand-item dump.” |
| 35–48 s | Real later search/retrieval. | “When I make the plan, I find the source and check current details.” |
| 48–60 s | URL and 30-save limit. | “Kept. Free up to 30 saves. Email confirmation required.” |

Do not claim the later test happened until it did. A collection is not a booking.

## B. Check the suggestion—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“AI found a place. Check it.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 6–14 s | Correct a real title/category/detail if needed. | “I keep the original link and edit the suggestion before confirming.” |
| 14–20 s | Saved item and URL. | “A useful collection keeps the source close.” |

## C. Retrieval beats collecting—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“The test is finding it again.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Actual search prompt. | “The test is finding it again, not saving another screenshot.” |
| 7–18 s | Real item retrieved for a genuine later decision. | “This is why I kept it. I still check whether the details are current.” |
| 18–25 s | URL, private collection hidden. | “Kept for the plan, not just the pile.” |

## D. Thirty before unlimited—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“You can test the job with thirty saves.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Free pricing card. | “You can test the job with thirty saves.” |
| 6–14 s | Actual Pro price/renewal text. | “Unlimited is ₹99 a month or ₹499 paid yearly, plus tax. Not lifetime.” |
| 14–20 s | Real small collection. | “Make one collection useful before buying more room.” |

Eight clips are scheduled. Change the true item/retrieval lesson, not just the music. Ask permission before showing another person's saves or feedback.
