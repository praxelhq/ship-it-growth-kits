# SecretAIry — Positioning

**Live:** https://secretairy.tech/ · **Category:** Small-organisation meeting administration · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/secretairy

Checked 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Less meeting admin. More time for what matters.”** CTAs **“Explore the workspace”**→`/login` and **“Try a meeting walkthrough”**→`/demo`. The page explicitly says **“Launching soon · Explore the interactive demo while new registrations are closed.”** No real workspace, transcript, approval or payment was created. No numeric price verified.

**You cannot treat walkthrough viewers as customers while new workspaces are unavailable.** Begin with design research and the release gate. The first business hypothesis is a small professional association with a 10–15-person executive team and a real secretary responsible for agendas, minutes and action follow-up. Its decision authority and governing rules remain human responsibilities.

**Pitch: Keep the meeting papers, reviewed minutes and approved actions connected to their source.** Short version: **Draft, review, approve—then follow the agreed action.**

The site describes pasted transcript→structured draft→explicit human approval→actions and approved-decision memory. It does not make governance decisions. Do not imply live recording, automatic meeting attendance, legal certification, valid quorum or a legally compliant board record. The displayed 30 October meeting is sample interface content, not an event invitation.

The free manual alternative is a shared document plus a [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) action log with source meeting, owner, due date and status. SecretAIry must improve traceability and review effort, not invent a need to replace the committee's approval process. Confirm transcript consent, processing/provider disclosures, access and deletion before any private organisational material.

Release proof must show a permitted organisation can actually create a workspace, review a harmless draft, approve the exact version and find the correct source decision across authorized roles. A walkthrough does not prove any of these in a live organisation. **Target:10 real business/organisation customers**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Own permitted data, independent last-seven-day use and privately known responsible name/phone are required. Members, meetings and demo viewers don't become businesses; if release remains closed, report zero live qualifying adoption.
