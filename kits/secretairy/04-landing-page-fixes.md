# SecretAIry — Landing-page fixes

Read public page 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Less meeting admin. More time for what matters.”** CTAs **“Explore the workspace”**→login and **“Try a meeting walkthrough”**→demo. New registrations explicitly closed. Numeric price unknown; live approval/storage untested.

1. **Make the available route primary.** Current workspace CTA competes with a signup-closed notice. Replacement primary **“Explore the sample meeting walkthrough.”** Secondary **“Existing workspace sign-in.”** Support **“New workspace registrations are currently closed.”** Never imply the walkthrough creates an organisational account.
2. **Keep sample dates labelled.** Current Executive Committee card shows 30 October. Replacement caption **“Fictional meeting workspace · demonstration only.”** No public event/date claim. Keep the caption on shared screenshots.
3. **Name the human approval boundary.** Current “Prepared for you. Approved by you.” is valuable. Add **“Generated minutes remain a draft until an authorised person reviews and explicitly approves this exact version.”** Test draft edits, stale approval, approval revocation and role access before release; don't claim legal signature/quorum certification.
4. **Explain transcript processing before paste.** Replacement **“Use only transcripts your organisation is authorised to process. Review where text is sent, who can access it and how it is deleted before pasting confidential material.”** Publish actual provider/retention terms; don't invent browser-only processing or zero retention.
5. **Make the source visible.** Replacement action panel **“Agreed action · source meeting · approved version · owner · due date · current status.”** Show only actual supported fields. A generated action suggestion must not silently become an approved organisational decision.
6. **State live offer only when ready.** No price verified. Add an accurate release/access statement and actual plan terms before accepting a pilot. Test phone walkthrough, live workspace creation, authorised sharing, source search and deletion with harmless records. A working demo does not prove a private organisation can onboard.

Do not acquire real transcripts to compensate for a closed registration route. Design feedback is useful, but it is not ten customers.
