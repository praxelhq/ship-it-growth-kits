# OneThread — Landing-page fixes

Read [onethread.tech](https://onethread.tech/) and rendered controls on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Your WhatsApp, answered.”** CTA: **“Get early access”**; Sign in points to the workspace app. Public FAQ says setup open, live WhatsApp phased for early users, no paid plans yet. Authentication, number connection, AI replies and handoff were not exercised.

## 1. Put rollout status beside the first CTA

Current page already discloses setup/live separation, but the hero can still read as an immediately live service.

Replacement hero note: **“Prepare your workspace today. Live WhatsApp replies are rolling out by invitation; connecting your number is not immediate or guaranteed.”**

Keep Get early access distinct from Sign in. A form submission is not active service.

## 2. Explain what setup lets an owner inspect

Current FAQ lists business profile, catalog, team and AI setup.

Replacement first-step summary: **“Review your business information and who should handle each request. Use a sample before adding actual customer data.”**

Verify actual setup, persistence, team permissions and deletion. Do not show a static request board as proof an order was received.

## 3. Keep illustrative labels with every conversation

Current site says product views are illustrative.

Replacement per-example label: **“Illustrative conversation and prices. Not an actual customer order.”**

Keep it attached to screenshots, video crops and previews. Never use staff/customer names from the mockup as proof of live usage or an endorsement.

## 4. Give unknown information a safe handoff

Current promise says answers come only from supplied information and the team takes over when needed.

Replacement expectation: **“If a price, stock item or deadline is not confirmed in your approved information, route it for human review.”**

Test unknown question, conflicting catalog, explicit person request, AI pause and return-to-AI with authorized sample chats. Do not guarantee hallucination-free replies from a claim alone.

## 5. Explain number eligibility and operational change

Current FAQ postpones number eligibility until a spot opens.

Replacement pre-connection note: **“Review whether your number is eligible and what the connection changes before proceeding. Keep your existing customer route until the new one is verified.”**

Never ask for OTPs, account credentials or a live number migration during a public demo. Verify team visibility and staff personal-number boundary in the actual release.

## 6. Keep commercial terms honest

Current FAQ says paid plans unavailable and notice before charging.

Replacement pricing note: **“No paid plan is open today. Early-access setup does not establish a permanent free live-messaging allowance. Review future terms before accepting any charge.”**

Link real privacy/deletion/support information before setup. Check removal, access revocation and which data/providers process customer messages before inviting real customers. Do not invent Meta affiliation, certification or an afternoon-to-live guarantee.
