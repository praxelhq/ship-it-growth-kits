# OneThread — Distribution map

Lead with print shops that already receive routine WhatsApp enquiries. Explain setup versus live rollout before asking for interest.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing local print-shop relationships | Qualify real enquiry volume, owner control of the business number/catalog and willingness to review handoffs. Use agreed shop visits or business enquiries honestly. | Ten blocks of four shops = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two design/print-supplier introductions | Ask a known business to forward the invitation; do not request private customer lists, chats or rate sheets. | Days 8 and 15; #4. Upside only. |
| Own WhatsApp Status | Show a fictional routine question and an explicitly illustrative human handoff. | Days 9,18,26; #3. Guess 20–100 views and 0–2 relevant replies/status, excluded from base. |
| One owner-controlled setup review | A shop verifies its own product information and who should take over custom jobs. | Day 12; #5. Existing-trial support, not additional reach. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% owned-catalog setup × 70% independent recent workspace use = 9.8, about 10 businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. This is a **setup-use scenario**, not a forecast of live WhatsApp connections. Live customer count is constrained by actual invitation, number eligibility and rollout capacity, currently unknown. It may be zero even if the setup target is met.

The ten four-shop blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts and introductions add zero guaranteed conversions. If the product only collects early-access details rather than allowing actual setup, count waitlist separately and reduce qualifying customers accordingly.

Use a print-shop list distinct from SalonQ's salons, Muggy's campus kitchens and local café-order tools. Apna Catalog owns wholesale jewellery catalogs/order sheets; OneThread owns routine print enquiries and human takeover. One primary invitation per shared market organizer/week; do not bundle every shop tool into one pitch.

No WhatsApp partnership, print association newsletter, trade-group permission or guaranteed API onboarding is established. Ask a known organizer before group posting. No bulk customer messages, scraping chats, automated DMs or a fake print order to reach the owner. One relevant follow-up after seven days; decline ends outreach. Do not connect or migrate a business number as a sales-demo shortcut.
