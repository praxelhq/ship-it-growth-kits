# OneThread — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each print shop must satisfy [all four policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine business; its own actual permitted catalog/workflow onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Store identifiers outside the public repo. Free-active shops may count, waitlist-only entries may not.

Activation has two separately named stages: **setup activation** is a reviewed own catalog/FAQ and human-handoff plan in the actual workspace; **live activation** is an eligible invited business completing a controlled actual customer-message/handoff workflow. Report both beside the customer total. Do not imply a setup-only customer receives the core live-answer benefit.

No private dashboard was accessed; actual setup totals, live rollout capacity and number eligibility are unknown.
