Do not invent competing API fees or claim manual replies are impossible. OneThread must earn trust through correct routine answers and a handoff a human can actually control.

Prove a synthetic workspace first. Test that the team can review supplied product information, remove it and independently update it. When the owner is invited into live rollout, verify a controlled consented conversation before any wider customer use. Existing setup can be useful while the core live-message benefit remains unavailable.
