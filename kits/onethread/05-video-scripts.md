# OneThread — Phone-demo scripts

Use a fictional print shop and owned synthetic catalog. No real customer chats, numbers, order details, staff contacts, OTPs or keys. Record actual workspace setup only when verified; label every illustrative conversation. A live demonstration requires the owner's specific approved rollout and controlled participants.

## A. Prepare the handoff — 65 seconds

0–3 s: fictional print enquiry. **“Who handles the custom quote?”**

3–16 s: actual approved workspace setup or public setup description labelled as such. “Start with product facts and the questions the shop needs answered.”

16–32 s: own synthetic product/FAQ review. “A custom price or urgent deadline needs a person when it is not confirmed.” Leave one real supported action uncut.

32–47 s: actual controlled handoff only if enabled, otherwise illustrative labelled diagram. “This is the handoff we need to verify, not proof the current shop is live.”

47–55 s: number eligibility and phased rollout notice. “Workspace setup and live WhatsApp connection are different stages.”

55–65 s: current no-paid-plan status and URL. “Review the actual early-access terms at onethread.tech.”

## B. Unknown-price hook — 20 seconds, Status

0–3 s: fictional question about a custom paper. **“Does the system know this price?”**

3–12 s: approved catalog versus unknown field. “If the owner has not confirmed it, do not turn a guess into a customer quote.”

12–20 s: actual or clearly illustrative human handoff. “A person needs the next action.” No fabricated sent reply.

## C. Rollout hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: current setup/live disclosure. **“Setup open is not messaging live.”**

3–15 s: actual workspace entry and invitation boundary. “Owners can prepare. Live replies depend on rollout and number eligibility.”

15–25 s: no-paid-plan FAQ and URL. “Know what is available before changing your business number.”

Capture actual early-access CTA, setup route, illustrative label, supplied-information boundary, person-request path and current commercial terms. If live messaging is unavailable, show that openly. Do not splice a website mockup into a claimed real customer conversation or invent reply-speed results.
