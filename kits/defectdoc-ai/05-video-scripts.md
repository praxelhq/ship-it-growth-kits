# DefectDoc AI — Video scripts

Use a synthetic closed complaint and no production/customer data. Don't demonstrate a live safety decision, claim certification or invent a successful private feature. Phone voice and screen recording are enough.

## A · The source behind the draft · 60 seconds · quality-lead call

0–2s hook: **“What evidence supports this sentence?”** Show a synthetic draft. 2–15s: open the actual source context: “These are observations, not a confirmed cause.” 15–30s: show the real supported draft process: “A draft can suggest wording; the engineer must inspect it.” 30–45s: correct an unsupported conclusion and mark the review: “A confident sentence without evidence stays unapproved.” 45–55s: show actual sign-off boundaries or explicitly state the current limit: “Your plant's review process remains responsible.” 55–60s: “A controlled walkthrough first. DefectDoc AI.”

If source context/approval is not functional, record that limitation and repair it before promoting the clip.

## B · A cause isn't a fact because AI wrote it · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“This is a hypothesis, not the root cause.”** 0–8s: show a synthetic proposed explanation labelled Hypothesis. 8–15s: “The physical investigation and evidence determine what can be concluded.” 15–20s: “Software should preserve that distinction, not erase it.”

## C · Invite does not mean certify · 25 seconds · access explanation

Hook 0–2s: **“An invited account isn't a compliance certificate.”** 2–12s: show the public organizational access explanation without credentials. 12–20s: “Access, data isolation, engineering review and standards compliance are separate questions.” 20–25s: “Ask for evidence of the actual scope before a live pilot.”

Keep a clear synthetic label and readable source text. No plant logo or professional quote without explicit authorization.
