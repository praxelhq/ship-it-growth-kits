# DefectDoc AI — Distribution map

**Permissioned quality-lead pilots first.** A broad maker launch won't establish trust with a plant. Keep real production and customer data out of the initial demonstration.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed outcome | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm manufacturing/quality introductions | Ask ten known professional contacts to request an introduction to a quality lead with an existing complaint process. No plant records or employee contacts transferred without permission. Assumed 3–5 conversations and 0–1 authorized active pilot. | #1 |
| 2 · Supplier-owned public business pages | Manually verify an actual component-supplier business and appropriate professional contact route. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. Hypothetical 20% reply × 50% authorized trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 2 companies. Don't pretend to be a purchasing customer or scrape staff data. | #2 |
| 3 · Quality professional's permitted internal session | Only after a responsible lead agrees; use a synthetic closed complaint and the plant's actual review expectations. No current event or external certification endorsement is assumed. A session isn't another business customer. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, explain fact versus proposed root cause using a synthetic example. No customer logo, plant photo or injury/safety claim. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 relevant visits, possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · [Trello](https://trello.com/pricing) / existing template comparison | A checked $0 manual task-tracking alternative. Compare administrative traceability, not a claim that free tools perform engineering validation. No promotional support-ticket or directory spam. | #4 |

Specific source to learn from is each prospective company's own quality/process documentation that it intentionally publishes. Don't infer private standards or use an “IATF-ready” phrase as proof of an approved workflow. No newsletter, professional association group access or free trade-publication placement was verified; their forecast contribution is zero.

Conservative **2–3 active businesses** is below ten. Procurement, data permissions and an existing QMS may make a 30-day live pilot difficult. At day 14 improve the controlled proof and narrow the complaint-administration job; don't bypass the plant's approval process or count interviewees as customers.
