# DefectDoc AI — Measurement

**Target: 10 real manufacturing businesses.** Activation beside it: **authorized quality teams independently maintaining a permitted real complaint record with accountable evidence review**. Staff accounts, demo plants and generated 8D paragraphs are separate counts.

## Day 0

Query actual companies, permitted real records, reviewed drafts, source gaps and independently returning businesses from your own records. The public auth page establishes no such totals. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding confidential plant/customer material and credentials. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/defectdoc-ai) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero. No private plan price or live billing was verified.

Proposed events, only where functions exist: `plant_scope_authorized`, `complaint_record_saved`, `source_reviewed`, `draft_corrected`, `engineer_review_recorded` and `business_returned`. A generated draft isn't an approved report; an approved report isn't proof the physical corrective action worked. Keep engineering outcomes separate and professionally substantiated.

## Four tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), a legitimate business has its own authorized actual workflow onboarded, independent use in the final seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Payment isn't required. Multiple users/plants inside one company don't automatically multiply customer businesses. If current access/features can't support the job, the target is gated; don't count a research conversation.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 approaches × hypothetical 20% reply × 50% permitted trial × 50% independent use = **2 companies**. Ten warm requests may add 0–1. **Base 2–3 is below ten**, with data approval/procurement a real constraint. These aren't measured quality-software conversion rates. After 20 approaches recompute from actual authorized trials, not flattering interest.
