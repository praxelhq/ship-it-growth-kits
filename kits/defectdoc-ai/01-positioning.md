# DefectDoc AI — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.8dcopilot.online/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/defectdoc-ai

**Your ten are legitimate manufacturing businesses, not ten operators or sample complaints.** Start with an automotive component supplier's quality lead managing a real customer complaint and existing 8D process. Don't pitch production-safety decisions or automatic root-cause conclusions.

The root initially served **“Opening plant workspace…”**; the accessible `/auth` page renders the actual headline **“Close complaints with evidence, not guesswork.”** It says the product drafts 8D reports from plant history and that the engineer reviews/signs off. The public client labels the actions **“Sign in”** and **“Create account”**, with instructions to use the work email invited by a plant's Quality Engineer. This is an invitation/access boundary, not proof the website is down. Public pricing and the private complaint-to-report workflow weren't verified.

## A narrow honest offer

> Keep one complaint's evidence and reviewed 8D draft together, with the quality engineer responsible for every conclusion.

Bio: **Evidence before the 8D sign-off.**

Use a synthetic closed complaint for the first walkthrough. Check whether the actual workspace can retain source context, separate facts from proposed causes and preserve engineer review. These are required pilot tests, not claims every private feature already passed. Never enter confidential customer drawings, process details or plant records without authorization.

## Alternatives

The company already has an 8D template, Excel and a customer-required approval process. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) costs $0 up to ten collaborators/workspace and can track corrective-action tasks, but isn't evidence validation or an 8D-quality system. Don't quote a QMS competitor's price or claim you replace the plant's existing certified process without evidence.

The product wins only if the quality lead can inspect the source and correct the draft with less administrative confusion. A well-written report is not proof the physical root cause was found or the corrective action worked.

## The trust gate

The auth page claims “Plant-isolated data” and “IATF-ready workflow.” This research did not audit tenant isolation or establish certification/compliance. Say what the operator can verify, link the actual control scope and remove implied certification if unsupported. Invited staff sign-up is not a tested new-plant onboarding flow.

Count actual companies with their own permitted real complaint workflow, independent final-week use and a callable responsible contact held privately. Multiple plants/staff inside one company aren't automatically separate business customers. If current access prevents that workflow, the ten-business target remains gated.
