# PerkEasy — Positioning

**Live:** https://perkeasy.app/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/perkeasy

**Your first user is a US employee, not an Indian student with no US benefits.** The real headline is **“Understand your benefits. Maximize your potential.”** Actions **“Get started free”** and **“Explore demo”** lead to signup and a fictional employee's demo. The product records user-entered benefits/paycheck details, source checks and deadlines. It does not connect to HR/payroll or determine public-assistance eligibility.

[Current pricing](https://perkeasy.app/pricing): **Free $0 for one benefit category; Plus $7.99/month or $59/year, 14-day trial, all eight categories.** A category can hold multiple benefits; health/dental/vision/life insurance count as one category, and deadlines don't count as categories. Current in-app deadline alerts are available; email reminders, receipt/claim tracking and employer plans are coming soon. No signup, stored record, AI explanation, payment or notification was tested here.

## Your first useful job

An adult US employee who already has a benefits document and one upcoming date they need to confirm. Start with one category and a deadline from the actual plan, not a promise to optimize their retirement or choose insurance.

> Put one benefit and its confirmed date in a clear record, with the official source beside it.

Bio: **Know the source. Keep the date. Review the next step.**

The wedge is one employee checking an entered detail against the document, marking its source and finding the date again later. Use the fictional demo first. Never treat the demo's November dates or $780 possible extra match as the person's actual deadline or money owed.

## Alternatives

The employee's official plan portal/documents and benefits team remain the authority for that plan; accessing what they already have needs no extra organizer subscription. A calendar/notes app can hold a manual date for no additional specialist fee if already available. For government assistance, [USA.gov's official benefit finder](https://www.usa.gov/benefit-finder) is a real authority route; PerkEasy links there instead of estimating eligibility. Don't confuse employer benefits, public programs and insurance enrollment rules.

Your product must earn the account through a clearer source/date record and controlled review, not claim exclusive access to employer data or government eligibility. The paid plan is optional for the first one-category test; no need to purchase it to enter a deadline.

## The honest limits

The headline statistics cite 2015 and 2022 source periods. They aren't current universal employee outcomes. Avoid using them as “you are losing this much” acquisition copy. All estimates depend on entered data and plan terms; don't instruct someone to change contributions, choose coverage or spend an FSA balance from an app example.

[Privacy](https://perkeasy.app/privacy) says no SSN, bank account, member ID or medical information is requested; don't enter it. Optional AI Explain sends the entered text to Anthropic after masking specified patterns, not every possible sensitive detail. Provider storage/backups mean “delete anytime” isn't proof every copy vanishes instantly. These are operator disclosures, not independent security verification.

Keep **1,000 individual accounts** as the target, with **one real permitted benefit/date record checked against its source and revisited** alongside. Employer-sponsored distribution is explicitly unbuilt, and campus friends without relevant US benefits won't make up the gap.
