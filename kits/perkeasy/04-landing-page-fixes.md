# PerkEasy — Landing-page fixes

Home, pricing/privacy and fictional-demo destinations read 29 October 2026. No account, benefits record, explanation request, payment or deadline notification was tested. Product features marked coming soon must stay out of current launch claims.

## 1. Lead with an entered record, not promised money

**Current headline:** “Understand your benefits. Maximize your potential.” Pricing says “Simple pricing. Real savings” and finding hidden money.

**Write:** **“Keep your US benefits, sources and deadlines in one place.”** Supporting line: **“You enter the details. Check them against the actual plan. Estimates are possibilities to review, not guaranteed money.”** Preserve the clear labels already attached to fictional demo values. Don't imply every user is underclaiming or owes an action now.

## 2. Keep old statistics and fictional dates in context

**Current:** homepage cites a 2015 match statistic and 2022 FSA data; demo shows November/December dates and illustrative extra match.

**Write beside statistics:** **“Historical source periods; not your expected outcome.”** Better first proof is **“Fictional demo. Enter and verify your own plan dates.”** Never reuse the demo's open-enrollment date as universal US guidance or calculate someone's entitlement from a landing-page sample.

## 3. Explain exactly what Free covers

**Current CTA:** “Get started free” → signup; Free covers one category, Plus all eight.

**Write helper:** **“Free: one benefit category, many records within it. Deadlines don't use a category.”** Keep insurance categories grouped as the actual pricing defines them. First action: **“Try the fictional demo”**, then **“Create my one-category account.”** Test signup, one record, source check and later retrieval before seeking a large audience.

## 4. Reconcile available features across pricing and home

**Current:** home clearly says in-app reminders only, receipts/claims and email reminders coming soon, no HR sync. Pricing's “comprehensive deadline and claim tracking” and “Premium” future wording can overstate/blur today's Plus scope.

**Write:** **“Available now: dates and deadlines you record, shown in-app. Receipt/claim submission, email alerts and employer integration are not included today.”** Make roadmap and plan names consistent. Don't advertise an email arriving or a claim being filed unless the actual feature works and was tested.

## 5. Make plan choice and billing plain

**Current:** Plus $7.99/month or $59/year, 14-day free trial, cancel anytime; Free doesn't need a card.

**Write before Plus action:** **“Review trial start/end, payment requirements, renewal amount and cancellation before checkout.”** Confirm actual trial/card/billing setup rather than assuming Plus is also card-free. Show annual charge as $59/year, not a cancellable monthly equivalent. Data remains on downgrade according to the page; verify actual access before demonstrating it.

## 6. Put Explain and data limits at input

**Current policy:** no SSN, bank numbers, member IDs or medical information; optional Explain text goes to Anthropic after emails/long-number masking. Saved records aren't sent by that feature.

**Write at Explain:** **“Use a generic term or redacted wording. Don't paste identifiers, private HR files or medical information. Pattern masking doesn't catch every sensitive detail.”** Keep built-in explanations distinct from optional AI. Show actual export/deletion controls with synthetic data; provider backups/logs can retain deleted data under their policies.

## 7. Phone, locale and preview

**Current:** broad benefits overview can look universal, but examples and functions are US-specific.

Preview: **“PerkEasy — a source-checked benefits organizer for US employees.”** Helper: **“Manual entry. Official terms decide eligibility and coverage.”** Test 360px, dates/time zones, missing inputs, errors and declined tracking. No measured mobile/load defect was established. Don't recruit Indian students into US benefit forms unless they actually have the relevant personal situation.
