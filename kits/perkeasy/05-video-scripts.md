# PerkEasy — Video scripts

Use the fictional demo or your own synthetic plan. No payslip, SSN, account/member identifier, medical detail or real employer document. No insurance/retirement instruction. Phone voice and screen capture suffice.

## A · One benefit, one checked date · 60 seconds · own feed / listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Synthetic deadline. **“Where did this date come from?”** | “A benefits deadline needs a source.” |
| 2–14s | Fictional demo notice. | “This employee and these dates are made up. They aren't your plan.” |
| 14–28s | Actual supported record/source field. | “I record one category and check the detail against the plan.” |
| 28–42s | Actual calendar/list view. | “The date stays visible when I return. Alerts are in-app today.” |
| 42–53s | Open actual official source route. | “Unclear coverage or eligibility still needs the responsible plan or agency.” |
| 53–60s | Free scope and URL. | “One category free. PerkEasy dot app, for individual US employees.” |

If a record/source control doesn't work, fix it or show that limit. Don't stage a real enrollment or imply the tool applies for benefits.

## B · The estimate isn't money owed · 20 seconds · short post

Hook: **“This isn't verified employer money.”** 0–8s, show the demo's illustrative estimate label. 8–15s: “It depends on entered numbers and the actual match rule. Check both.” 15–20s: “Keep the estimate and confirmed fact separate.” No recommendation to change a contribution.

## C · In-app, not email · 20 seconds · expectation help

Hook: **“Don't wait for an email that isn't built yet.”** 0–8s, show current home disclosure. 8–15s: “Today the reminders are in the app. Email is coming soon.” 15–20s: “Use a reminder method you can actually rely on for your own deadline.” No invented notification arrival.

## D · A term, not a private file · 25 seconds · Explain help

Hook: **“Ask about the term, not your whole payslip.”** 0–8s, use a generic benefits term. 8–18s: “Optional AI processes what you enter; pattern masking isn't a guarantee to catch everything.” 18–25s: “Keep identifiers, medical details and private HR files out.” Don't submit actual sensitive text for a demo or imply the answer chooses a plan.
