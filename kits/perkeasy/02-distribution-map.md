# PerkEasy — Distribution map

**Introduced US employees first, not generic campus promotion.** A small relevant pilot is more useful than a large unrelated signup count. No employer partnership, benefits-team mailing list or licensed-adviser role is assumed.

| Route | Action and boundary | Planning assumption / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known or permission-introduced adult US employees | Five invitations on days 5,10,15,20 = **20** maximum. Ask a known contact to request permission before an introduction. No payslips, HR files, SSNs or private plan records sent to you. If you cannot identify twenty legitimate contacts, reduce the volume and forecast. | 20 × 40% try × 70% account ≈ five to six accounts; half activate ≈ three. #1 |
| 2 · Own LinkedIn educational posts | Six on days 5,10,15,20,25,29, with a fictional benefit/date/source example and US scope. Follow [professional policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies); no personalized financial recommendations or automated DMs. | Six × 100–500 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup ≈ 3–15 accounts, possibly lower if audience isn't US employees. #2, #3 |
| 3 · One introduced US employee/alumni organizer | Ask day 8 about one free optional ten-person source/deadline-recording demo. Accepted session day 17, fictional data. No inferred employer endorsement or HR list. | 10 × 60% attend × 50% signup ≈ three accounts if accepted, otherwise zero. #4 |
| 4 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Check actual Monday Launchpad slot/eligibility after demo/free signup/data controls pass. One maker launch; describe US employee scope, not employer integration. No vote requests. | 0–100 visits × 15% signup = 0–15 accounts. #5 |
| 5 · Official source routes | [USA.gov benefit finder](https://www.usa.gov/benefit-finder) and each user's official plan documents help verify a source. They are not advertising boards or evidence of agency affiliation. | Zero acquisition forecast. #3 |

No current finance subreddit promotional rule, US HR newsletter placement or employer-sponsored benefit agreement was verified. Don't scrape employee lists or buy leads. An introduced editor can inspect a free source-check worksheet, but no publication is secured. Use #4 only after a genuine appropriate introduction.

Base **8–39 accounts before overlap**, conditional on real relevant contacts and optional session/launch. This is below 1,000. The first bottleneck may be access to actual US employees, not product virality. Report it honestly and earn permissioned introductions from people who found the one-category record useful. Never attract people with a false eligibility or guaranteed-savings promise.
