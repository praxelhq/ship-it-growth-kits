# PerkEasy — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **one permitted real benefit/date record checked against its official source and revisited**. Track source review and later return separately. Fictional demo accounts/values, employer leads and people without relevant US benefits don't establish the intended adoption. Don't require sensitive data to count the first useful record.

## Day 0

Record actual relevant accounts, saved category/date records, source checks, later returns, errors and real Plus purchases/cancellations. No private baseline was established here. Capture current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/perkeasy) rank, saved reading and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. The fictional employee's benefit value/match and old headline statistics are not user outcomes or current benchmarks.

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and actual consent/privacy before adding measurement. Mask profile, employer, pay, benefit, date and Explain inputs; no SSN, member ID or medical information should enter the product. Content-free internal events only if supported: `account_created`, `benefit_record_saved`, `source_checked`, `deadline_recorded`, `independent_return`, `estimate_reviewed`, `plus_purchase_confirmed`. A formula displayed doesn't prove an entitlement, and a marked-done deadline doesn't prove enrollment or reimbursement.

## Forecast matching the calendar

Assumptions, not benefits-product benchmarks:

- Up to 20 relevant invitations × 40% try × 70% account ≈ **five to six accounts**.
- Six posts × 100–500 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup ≈ **3–15 accounts**; reduce if the actual audience isn't US employees.
- One accepted session: ten invited × 60% attend × 50% signup ≈ **three**, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–100 visits × 15% signup = **0–15 accounts**.

Rounded base **8–39 before overlap**, lower if fewer contacts or weak US relevance. If half activate, roughly 4–20 source-checked return users. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 4,000 qualified visits; this calendar doesn't provide that reach. Employer plans are unbuilt, so no invented organization distribution enters the forecast.
