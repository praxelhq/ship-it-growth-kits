# Kahi Chalo — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real accounts. Report **circles with at least three willing real members and one jointly chosen plan** beside them. Keep actual outings and diary memories separate. One person in several circles remains one person.

## Day 0

Read latest date/traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/kahi-chalo). Record real accounts, circles, accepted invites, active three-person circles, chosen plans, actual outings and return use. This kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured**, not zero. A public marketing promise is not evidence of the private flow.

Verify the actual optional [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) choice and exclude/mask circle names, private dates/places, photos, notes and invite tokens. Do not collect contacts or location history merely to measure acquisition. Keep operational friend-circle information private.

## Funnel

`account_created → circle_started → invite_accepted → three_willing_members → plan_chosen → plan_happened → later_plan_wanted`.

An invite sent isn't a member, a suggestion isn't a chosen plan and a chosen plan isn't an outing. Use consented aggregate feedback rather than requiring photos/attendance evidence. Do not add a diary photo as another activation.

## Honest forecast

`02` schedules ten planners, two circles, three clips and one listing, suggesting roughly 12–71 accounts before overlap under guessed rates. At hypothetical three genuinely new accounts per activated circle, 1,000 accounts would require roughly 334 such circles; current distribution doesn't establish that. Report the gap without counting invite recipients who never join.
