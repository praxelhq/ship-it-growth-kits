# Kahi Chalo — Video scripts

Use fictional circles and locations, or specific permission from every person shown. Never display an active private invite token.

## A. Circle to plan — 40 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“One person signed up. That isn't a circle yet.”**

2–15: Show the actual supported create/invite/accept flow with test profiles after verifying it. “Start with people who already want to do something together.”

15–27: Show a real supported suggestion and group choice. “An idea is a starting point. Pick what fits your time and budget.”

27–40: “Keep the memory after it happens. kahichalo.me.” Label the demonstration; don't fake an outing.

## B. One small plan — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Not another saved place.”**

2–10: Show a fictional no-spend local hangout, date and willing circle. “One feasible plan is enough for the first test.”

10–15: “Your people. Kahi Chalo.” URL. First clip.

## C. Suggestion, not destiny — 20 seconds

0–2: **“The group still gets to say no.”**

2–13: Show an actual suggestion/sample label. “A tool doesn't know everyone's mood or guarantee the perfect trip. Choose together.”

13–20: “A private circle, not a command.” URL. Second clip.

## D. After the memory — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Don't upload the whole camera roll.”**

2–12: Show a fictional diary entry. “One memory, with the circle's agreement. Know who can see it.”

12–20: “The outing comes first. The diary follows.” URL. Third clip; no private photos/precise location.
