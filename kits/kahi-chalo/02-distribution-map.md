# Kahi Chalo — Distribution map

**Recruit existing friend circles through their willing planner.** Don't invite random people into a supposedly private circle or assume every group-chat member wants another app.

Forecasts are assumptions for the exact calendar, not measured referral benchmarks.

| Route | Execution / permission | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ten known adult circle planners | Ask individually about a real no-spend hangout. They decide whether to invite friends; no contact-list upload or group addition. | 10 × assumed 40–60% try × 2–4 genuinely joined members = 8–24 accounts, before deduplication. | #1 |
| 2. Two existing permissioned friend/alumni circles | Admin-approved one-time invitation, not an unsolicited forward. Let groups self-select. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–20% visit × 20–40% signup = 2–6 accounts, often overlapping route 1. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Existing-circle plan, no-repeat claim honesty, diary after the outing at 18:30 IST. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after circle/invite/plan tests; no vote exchanges or fake group activity. | Assume 5–20 visits and 1–5 accounts. | #4 |

Base range is roughly 12–71 accounts before overlap, with circle activation likely lower. A thousand requires evidence of a much larger working invitation loop. Counting invited people before they choose to join is not growth.

## Communities, newsletters and accounts

No city-community promo permission, travel newsletter or creator placement was verified. A private friend's group is not a public advertising channel. Use the organizer request #5 and respect a no.
