# Kahi Chalo — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://kahichalo.me/) and [supplied groups entry](https://kahichalo.me/groups) were opened 29 October. The latter shows account entry, not a missing application. No account, circle, invite or photo was submitted.

## 1. Explain the circle job before the account gate

Current H1: **“Kahi Chalo.”** CTA: **“Start a circle.”** The groups entry says **“Make an account, then your circle.”** Add **“For an existing group of friends. Create an account, invite only people who agree, then choose one feasible plan.”** A new user should know that another person's participation is needed before investing in setup.

## 2. Show an actual three-person journey

Add a labelled fictional demonstration: create circle, accepted invite, group suggestion, chosen plan. Don't present three sample profiles as real users or a generated suggestion as a booking. Test fresh accounts/invite expiry/decline and failed return links before launch.

## 3. Narrow broad personality and novelty promises

The page says ideas derive from shared temperament and never repeat. Use **“Suggestions informed by what your circle chooses to share. Review them together; the final choice is yours.”** Only claim no repeats if the actual history logic supports it, including edits and new members.

## 4. Make private invitation scope visible

Before sharing an invite, show who can use it, whether it expires and what they will see on joining. Do not promise perfect privacy from a long URL alone. Let the organizer control membership according to actual implemented features; don't invent approval controls.

## 5. Put diary consent after the first plan

Replacement: **“Add a memory only when your circle agrees. Photos and locations are visible to the people with access.”** Explain storage/deletion/export honestly. Don't collect private location history or contacts merely to produce an idea.

## 6. Respect the visible analytics choice

The groups page offers an optional Clarity explanation. Verify actual masking/exclusion of typed private details, photos and invite links. Don't turn optional analytics consent into a condition for planning or use it to claim a security audit.

## 7. Keep phone and shared cards clear

Title: **“Kahi Chalo — plans for your existing circle.”** Description: **“Choose a feasible hangout or trip idea together, then keep the memory.”** Private circle names, dates, places and photos must not leak through default previews. Test mobile sign-in return, text zoom and invite handoff.
