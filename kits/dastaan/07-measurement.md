# Dastaan — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Define a signup as one unique household submitting a genuine, consented start request with a reachable contact. Exclude duplicates, tests and form spam. Beside it report **households whose consenting storyteller sends the first real voice note**. A requested call, payment and finished book are separate stages.

## Day 0

Read the latest [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/dastaan), recording date, traffic reading and rank. Record existing form requests, calls completed, quotes accepted, paid households, first voice notes and second-week contributions. These private figures are not known to this kit; use **not measured**, not an assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on public pages and mask all family names, phone fields, recordings and dashboard content. Store the operational contact/callback sheet privately. Do not send story contents to traffic analytics.

## Funnel and capacity

`sample_opened → start_request_valid → call_completed → quote_accepted → storyteller_consented → first_voice_note → second_voice_note → chapter_reviewed`.

The homepage sample is illustrative; playing it is not a family activation. A paid household with an unwilling storyteller is not successful onboarding. Report second voice notes/continuation separately because the service requires a sustained habit over months.

## The target gap

`02` budgets roughly 3–17 genuine requests before overlap, plus 1–6 if the organizer session happens. These are guesses to learn from. At an illustrative 10% visit-to-request rate, 1,000 requests requires 10,000 qualified visits. Even if reach arrives, 1,000 calls × 15 minutes is 250 call hours before transcription, support or book work. Your current personal-call promise cannot quietly absorb that.

Keep the board number visible, but report the feasible delivery capacity and unmet target honestly. Do not count a voice note as a new signup every time the same parent replies.

## Weekly checks and decisions

| Review | What to record | What changes if behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Families who understand package, data handling, language and timeline. | If 2 of 5 misunderstand the deliverable, correct `04` before invitations. |
| Day 14 | Valid requests, reachable calls, quotes, consenting storytellers, first notes. | Requests without calls are a capacity failure; stop adding channels until caught up. |
| Day 21 | First-note households returning for another note and reviewing a chapter. | If most stop, ask whether prompts, WhatsApp setup or consent are the obstacle. |
| Day 28 | Support time per activated family, changes/corrections, referrals. | Keep the source yielding real participation, not the most emotional likes. |
| Day 30 | New households, activated/continuing storytellers, paid families and refunds. | Show target gap and the delivery obligations carried into next month. |

The thresholds above are chosen experiment rules, not industry standards. Each Sunday record hours and callback backlog alongside acquisition. A full hardcover is not a reasonable 30-day activation event when the published process takes four to six months.

Save course traffic readings with timestamps and never add overlapping 24-hour snapshots. A family-story post attracting views is not proof a parent felt comfortable recording or that you delivered a book.
