## #6 — Genealogy/oral-history reply, no product link

I would start with a place rather than “tell me your life story”: the kitchen they grew up in, the street outside school, or the first workplace. Ask one question, let silences happen, and offer to stop. Keep the original recording alongside any edited chapter, and let the storyteller correct names and dates. A polished narrative should never overwrite their meaning.

Disclose your professional interest if the thread asks about products. Don't slip a link into a no-promotion thread.
