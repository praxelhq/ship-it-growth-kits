# Dastaan — Video scripts

Use fictional examples or separate, specific permission for each family recording and public use. A sample must be labelled throughout. A family buying the service has not automatically consented to marketing.

## A. Voice to chapter — 60 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“Your parent's voice can become a chapter.”** Show a sample question.

2–15: “Dastaan sends one question on WhatsApp. They reply with a voice note, like they already do with family.” Show a labelled sample message and playable audio.

15–30: “That recording becomes a draft chapter, not just a transcript.” Move between the sample transcript and chapter, keeping the sample label visible.

30–42: “The family checks names and dates before anything is printed. The original voice stays linked to the story.” Show actual supported review/export behaviour; if unavailable, use an explicitly marked proposed walkthrough and fix the proof gap before launch.

42–52: “It grows over several months. They can skip questions or pause.” Show the FAQ.

52–60: “Ask about the package and price before paying. dastaanapp.in.” Use on homepage and LinkedIn.

## B. One question — 20 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Don't ask for a whole life story.”**

2–12: Hold a simple question card: **“What did your journey to school look like?”** Voice: “Ask about a place they remember. Then listen instead of rushing to the next question.”

12–20: “You can do this yourself today. Dastaan is the guided WhatsApp version if you want help keeping it going.” URL. No urgency based on age or loss.

## C. The sample is a sample — 25 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“This isn't a customer testimonial.”**

2–14: Show labelled sample voice/transcript/chapter. “It's a demonstration of what happens between a spoken memory and a written page.”

14–21: “The real story belongs to the storyteller. They get to correct it and decide who sees it.”

21–25: “See the process before starting: dastaanapp.in.” Use with `03` #3.

## D. What happens after the form — 30 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“The form doesn't buy a book.”**

2–12: Show the start page with no personal details entered. “You request a personal call. We explain the package and price before any payment.”

12–22: Show the real timeline and language information. “The full project takes several months, currently in Hindi or English.”

22–30: “Ask your storyteller whether they want to do it first. Then decide together.” End on the FAQ and policy links. Use to answer objections privately.
