# Dastaan — Landing-page fixes

Evidence: [home](https://www.dastaanapp.in/), [FAQ](https://www.dastaanapp.in/faq), [sample](https://www.dastaanapp.in/see-a-sample), [start form](https://www.dastaanapp.in/get-started) and [privacy](https://www.dastaanapp.in/privacy), opened 29 October. Form delivery, phone payment and private dashboard were not submitted/tested.

## 1. Make the package and retention promise agree

Current home/FAQ: printed hardcover and permanent recordings access. Current policy: ebook/subscription, with retention for a reasonable period after ending. Pick what you actually deliver and update all pages before collecting paid families.

If hardcover is the real offer, replacement summary: **“Your package includes the agreed printed book and downloadable chapters/recordings. Your quote states format, copies, delivery area and every extra cost before payment.”** Add a precise storage/export/deletion period you can honour. Do not copy “forever” unless you can operationally support it.

## 2. Tell people the call is the next step

Current headline: **“Some stories only they can tell you.”** Keep it. Current CTA: **“Get started.”**

Replace CTA with **“Ask about your family's book.”** Under it: **“A short personal call, package details and price before payment. No online checkout.”** If you promise a callback within one business day, publish current capacity and keep that promise.

## 3. Replace the sample's simulated audio with something usable

Current sample is labelled illustrative and includes text describing an audio player/photo rather than a complete real artifact. Keep illustrative content labelled; add a playable fictional/consented voice note with transcript and edited chapter. Label **“Sample workflow — not a customer story.”** Show what the family can correct and how the original is preserved.

## 4. Put the true timeline and language limit near the first CTA

Replacement: **“Stories grow over several months. Current languages: English and Hindi. Your storyteller can skip any question or pause.”** The FAQ's four-to-six-month expectation should not surprise someone after a call. Don't promise a birthday delivery next month without a distinct feasible package.

## 5. Explain the data journey in ordinary words

The policy names WhatsApp, transcription and AI processing providers. Add **“Voice notes are processed into text and chapters, stored for your family, and shown only to authorized family members. Review the processing, export and deletion details before starting.”** Link the actual policy. “Private” must not be shorthand for “never leaves the phone.”

## 6. Show what is and isn't included before payment

No public fixed price is currently available. Offer **“Get the current package quote”**, not a false ₹ figure. The written quote should state call/payment method, book format, extra copies, shipping, revision limits and cancellation terms. Avoid asking for card credentials in an informal call; use your verified payment-provider flow.

## 7. Make a shared link answer the buyer's question

Metadata title: **“Dastaan — your parent's stories, told through WhatsApp.”** Description: **“Guided family memoirs in English and Hindi. Personal call and package quote before payment. Sample chapter available.”** Test the actual WhatsApp card and start form on a phone. Keep the sample accessible before signup; don't load a giant decorative video ahead of the explanation.
