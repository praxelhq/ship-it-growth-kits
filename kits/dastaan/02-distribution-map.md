# Dastaan — Distribution map

**Start with permissioned adult-child circles and one real sample, not a broad app launch.** A family-trust service converts through a useful example and clear terms.

These forecasts are assumptions for the scheduled work, not audience-size claims or benchmarks.

The base plan is approximately 3–17 requests before overlap; a confirmed session can add 1–6. A thousand is a board target, not a credible forecast from this small network.

## Useful resources, directories and accounts

Share [StoryCorps' recording guidance](https://storycorps.org/participate/) as a genuinely useful resource, without implying affiliation or posting a sales pitch into its programs. Its stories provide a model for asking specific questions, not permission to reuse recordings in your ad.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is a verified free listing option but a weak first-month family channel; leave it out of the base forecast and do it only after the sample and terms are ready. No newsletter, genealogy influencer or campus association has promised coverage. Pitch an organizer with #5 rather than inventing a named group or member count.

Coordinate with Little Moments and Nimantran: your invitation is about preserving an elder's memory, theirs about gifts/weddings. Use different groups or different weeks. Never make the same family receive three cohort offers at once.
