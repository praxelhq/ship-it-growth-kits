# Atlaspong — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Free play needs no account, so count meaningful first game sessions under the existing policy: a new adult starts an actual round after entry. Homepage/gate views, reloads, your tests and repeat rounds do not count as new people. Use existing consented identifiers where available and disclose anonymous/browser dedup limits.

**Activation:** one valid place chain interaction plus a round played to its actual outcome. Report valid-answer and finished-round counts separately if helpful. A short loss is an honest outcome. Return play is another day/round, not a new signup. Separate free and Pro conditions and do not treat points as verified geography proficiency.

## Baseline

Record actual starters, valid-answer events, endings, return play, rejected-place reports, room failures and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/atlaspong. Public research did not establish private totals. Check existing Clarity/analytics before adding tools. Exclude age/parent flows, profiles, room codes and typed identifying data from replay; no birth-year collection in your growth sheet.
