# careVoyage — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from the public homepage, checklist, directory and visa guide. No patient data was entered, trip saved or authenticated flow tested. This is a product-claim audit, not validation of any medical/immigration requirement.

## 1. Make the promise match the evidence

**Current:** “Know every paper, every place, every person — before you fly” and “every step verified.” The checklist contains “Source being added”; directory hospital rows say “Unverified.”

Replace with:

> **Organize the questions before your treatment trip.**
>
> Track administrative paperwork, sources and what still needs confirmation. Check the requirements for your own case with the responsible authority and hospital.

Remove blanket “every rule verified” language until every displayed claim supports it. Put unverified/draft status above any “mandatory” label, and hide unsupported prescriptive requirements from the recommended checklist until reviewed. A source link alone does not validate the summary.

## 2. Narrow the first route

**Current:** the country/treatment/city selectors imply broad coverage; sample copy says the selection gives an “exact list.”

Use **“See a planning list for your selected route”** and **“Coverage and source status vary. Confirm what applies before acting.”** Publish which specific administrative route is maintained and its checked date. Do not present every dropdown option as validated coverage or repeat visa/stay details from an unverified note.

## 3. Explain the actual CTA and save step

**Current:** “Plan my treatment journey”; public “Save as my trip” requires a free account.

Try **“Inspect a sample checklist”** first, then **“Save my planning checklist — free account.”** Explain **“Saving organizes your questions; it does not submit documents, book treatment or apply for a visa.”** Test a fresh account saving a synthetic trip and returning to it before inviting real caregivers. This audit didn't establish that save works.

## 4. Remove any implied hospital endorsement

**Current:** the directory says listings aren't recommendations and hospital rows are unverified, while the homepage uses a broad verified-journey claim.

Keep the directory's limitation beside every entry: **“Listing only. Details not yet verified. Contact the institution through its official site.”** Do not use hospital logos as customer proof, infer accreditation or rank treatment quality. Publish the correction/report route and verify it delivers to someone who will act.

## 5. Make item-level sourcing inspectable

For every maintained statement show **responsible authority, exact source, date checked, scope and verification status**. Label a draft **“Question to confirm”**, not “Mandatory” with a tiny caveat. Proposed correction acknowledgement: **“Thanks. This item is flagged for review; please confirm with the responsible authority before relying on it.”** Only claim a response time you can actually meet.

## 6. Put the data boundary before saving

Explain the actual data stored for a trip and how deletion works. Start a test with synthetic country/city choices; don't ask for reports or passports merely to demonstrate a checklist. Mask all trip/account inputs from recordings and keep health details out of analytics events. Don't invent “never stored” or “fully local” assurances without checking implementation.

## 7. Preview and mobile

Proposed title: **“careVoyage — organize your trip paperwork questions.”** Description: **“A source-labelled administrative planning checklist. Confirm requirements with the responsible authority and your hospital.”** Test actual previews and country-selector usability on a phone. Keep source-status labels readable without opening several accordions. No measured load or clipping defect was established; verify on slow mobile data rather than invent one.

Three fresh reviewers should be able to identify which items are confirmed, which are questions and who is responsible for the final answer. Until that is true, don't scale distribution to patients.
