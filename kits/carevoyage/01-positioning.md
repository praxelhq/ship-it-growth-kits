# careVoyage — Positioning

**Live:** https://carevoyage.tech/ · **Category:** Consumer travel administration · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/carevoyage

**Your first fix is the verification promise.** The live headline is “Know every paper, every place, every person — before you fly,” followed by “Your medical journey to India, every step verified.” Yet the actual [checklist](https://carevoyage.tech/checklist) contains many “Source being added” or “Being verified” entries, and the [directory](https://carevoyage.tech/directory) labels hospital entries Unverified. Do not send caregivers a claim stronger than the underlying evidence.

The public CTA is “Plan my treatment journey.” A sample checklist is readable without signing in; “Save as my trip” says a free account is required. No paid software amount was visible in the checked flow. Creating an account, saving a trip and authenticated tracking were not tested by this writer.

## Your first user

An adult family caregiver who has already chosen a hospital with the patient's clinicians, is organizing travel to India and wants one place to track administrative questions. They are not asking your app to choose a treatment, certify a hospital or guarantee a visa. Start with one origin-country/city route you can actually maintain, and only source-verified administrative items. Don't infer a country's eligibility from a dropdown option.

## The pitch

> Organize the paperwork questions for a planned treatment trip to India. See the source and verification status, confirm requirements with the responsible authority, then track your own checklist.

Bio version: **A clearer trip checklist, with sources and limits visible.**

Your wedge is reducing scattered notes after a hospital is already involved. A caregiver should be able to see which item is confirmed, which needs checking and who to ask. “No source yet” must be obvious before they rely on a document requirement, not hidden behind a polished badge.

## The real alternatives

[India's official visa portal](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/) is the authority route for its own current information; reading a checklist does not replace an application or its fees. [Vaidam](https://www.vaidam.com/) advertises complimentary patient facilitation services on its own site, while treatment/travel costs are separate. A hospital's international desk and a manually maintained checklist are also real alternatives. Don't claim assistance is available only if someone buys your product, or copy a facilitator's treatment-price promises into your pitch.

Your distinction is an independently inspectable administrative organizer, not “better hospitals” or a cheaper medical package. If you accept referrals or commissions later, disclose them; don't undermine the current no-paid-ranking claim without telling users.

## The honest limit

Rules and hospital requirements can differ and change. This kit deliberately does not validate any individual visa rule, document deadline or treatment suitability. Your product needs item-level source, checked date, status and correction process before broader distribution. A directory listing is not a clinical recommendation, and an official link does not prove your summary is correct.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-signup target**, counted as new accounts created to save a trip, with a saved and meaningfully reviewed checklist alongside. A few carefully verified caregiver tests will not reach that number this month by themselves. Report the gap rather than trading accuracy for reach.
