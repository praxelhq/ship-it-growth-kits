# careVoyage — Video scripts

Use a fictional trip and generic administrative examples. No patient name, diagnosis, passport, bank statement or real treatment file. Phone microphone, captions and a plain screen recording are enough. Do not film a hospital as though it endorses the product.

## A. A checklist with unanswered questions — 65 seconds

**Where:** landing page and private organizer/desk introduction. **Hook:** “A confident checklist can still have unanswered questions.”

| Time | Shot | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Synthetic item with a visible status | A confident checklist can still have unanswered questions. |
| 2–14s | Public sample, no personal details | This is an administrative organizer for a fictional treatment trip. It doesn't choose treatment or grant a visa. |
| 14–30s | Open one actual maintained source/status | I want to see who is responsible, the source and when it was checked. |
| 30–43s | Show a draft item clearly as a question | If this isn't verified, it stays a question to confirm, not a guaranteed requirement. |
| 43–55s | Demonstrate an actual synthetic saved trip, only if tested | I can track what I have and what I still need to ask. Saving doesn't submit any application. |
| 55–65s | Sample URL and limits | Inspect the sample at carevoyage.tech. Confirm your own case with the responsible authority and hospital. |

If the corrected source-status or save behaviour isn't implemented, omit that shot and say what is still being built. Don't simulate it as live.

## B. The source column — 25 seconds

**Where:** LinkedIn educational post. **Hook:** “Who requires this?” Show a plain worksheet: item, responsible authority, source, date checked, open question. Say, “A saved link is useful only if its summary and scope are right. Keep the unanswered question visible.” End with the manual worksheet and founder disclosure. No individual visa rule is taught in this clip.

## C. A listing isn't a recommendation — 25 seconds

**Where:** directory introduction. **Hook:** “A hospital appearing here isn't an endorsement.” Show the actual listing/status explanation, then an official institution website entry point. Say, “Check details with the institution. A directory shouldn't imply treatment quality or a relationship it hasn't established.” Don't show logos as customers or stage a patient testimonial.

## D. Use a fictional trip first — 20 seconds

**Where:** onboarding/privacy explanation. **Hook:** “You don't need to share a medical file to test a checklist.” Show generic trip choices and no names/documents. Say, “Inspect the organizer with a fictional example. Understand what it stores before deciding what to enter.” End with the public sample and non-advice boundary.

Reusable clips: source status, exact source link, checked date, open question and a synthetic save/return flow after it is tested. The proof is clear scope and correction, not emotional travel footage.
