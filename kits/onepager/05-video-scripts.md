# OnePager — Video scripts

Use one fictional Master CV throughout. Show the source fields when an output includes a number. Captions and screen recording are enough; no paid editing.

## A. Source-to-export demo,60 seconds

0–2 s, three-page CV: **“One page. No new achievements.”**

2–12 s, upload fictional CV and pick a target role. Say: “I'm using a made-up profile so you can see every source fact.”

12–28 s, inspect the Master CV. “These are the dates, numbers and roles the rewrite is allowed to use.” Highlight one real input metric.

28–45 s, show generated page and compare that metric. “Now check the facts, the role focus and the line breaks.” Leave real processing time or label a cut.

45–60 s, open Word/PDF. “Edit what needs work before applying. First one-pager free; current extra-output pricing is on softlaunch.in.” Post LinkedIn and homepage.

## B. One-line fit hook,15 seconds

0–2 s: “Your strongest result shouldn't spill into tiny text.” Show a fictional overflowing bullet.

2–10 s: show the fitted version with the same facts. “Cut repetition, keep evidence, check the page.”

10–15 s: Word download and URL. Text: **Fictional example · review before applying**. Reel/Short.

## C. Fact-check hook,20-seconds

0–2 s: “Where did that number come from?”

2–12 s: highlight one output figure, jump back to the matching Master CV field. “If you can't trace it to your own work, it doesn't belong on your resume.”

12–20 s: delete an unsupported example claim. “Better a true scope than a fake percentage.” Product URL last. LinkedIn/Story.

## D. Phone handoff,20-seconds

0–2 s: “Before you send the PDF, open it.”

2–14 s: open the actual export on a phone, zoom, check one page and a long bullet. “The preview isn't the application file. Check the download too.”

14–20 s: show Word alternative. “You keep an editable version.” Caption states whether the recorded output is free or paid under the actual current offer.
