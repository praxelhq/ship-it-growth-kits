# OnePager — Landing page fixes

Live page checked 29 October 2026 at[softlaunch.in](https://softlaunch.in/). `/app.html` is the listed creation route; output and payment were not submitted during research.

## 1. Resolve the contradictory price

The same page says **“Free while in beta”**, **“Your first one-pager is free”** and **“$0.90 each.”** Choose the actual enforced offer. If first-free pricing is active, use everywhere:

**“Your first one-pager is free. Each additional role-specific one-pager costs $0.90. Review the final price before paying.”**

Remove the blanket free-beta statement. If all beta use is genuinely free instead, clearly label $0.90 as future pricing and do not collect payment yet. The product's actual behaviour decides.

## 2. Say what the app does in the headline

Current: **“Every CV starts out crumpled.”** CTA: **“Build my one-pager.”**

Replacement: **“Fit your own experience into one role-focused page.”** Support: **“Upload your CV or build a Master CV. Review every fact, then download Word or PDF.”** Keep the paper animation as supporting visual, not the only explanation.

## 3. Repair the example's evidence trail

The visible source sentence contains no numbers; its improved version includes 9%→ 3% and ₹40 Cr. Either show the source Master CV fields containing those facts or use a rewrite that contains no new facts. Caption: **“Fictional example. Every number below appears in the source CV.”** This matters more than the animation.

## 4. Remove the implied hiring guarantee

The page says one-page resumes “get shortlisted.” Replacement: **“A clear, editable resume for your next application.”** Keep factual output checks; do not imply format guarantees an interview.

## 5. Make upload/privacy expectations useful

Current: **“Only you”** can see saved CVs. Link the actual privacy controls and say how to delete the Master CV and exported versions. Suggested form note: **“Upload only information you want processed. Review and remove personal details before sharing an exported resume.”** Do not claim a retention period without checking implementation and policy.

## 6. Test the output, not only the hero

Use a fictional long CV, missing metrics, a non-Indian profile and a long institution name. Check one-page export, Word editability, no invented figures, second-output pricing and failed-payment recovery. On 390 px, ensure the upload and role fields remain reachable with the keyboard open. These are test requirements, not claimed defects.

Preview title: **“OnePager — your Master CV, fitted to one role”**. Preview image: a synthetic CV with personal details removed. No real student's phone, grades or email in public marketing.
