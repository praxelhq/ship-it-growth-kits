# OnePager — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count one confirmed account per real candidate. Report **first fact-checked resume download** beside it. A sample preview, second role, repeated export and your own test CV do not create new users.

The roster's down status is stale: the live page was read on 29 October. Private analytics were not accessed; baseline values are unknown until you record them.

| Day 0 metric | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts, total and last 7 days | App authentication records |
| Master CVs created | App records, excluding tests |
| First Word/PDF download | Download event or verified application record |
| Known incorrect facts and export failures | Private issue tracker, no personal CVs in public |
| Traffic/referrers, last 7 days | Existing consented analytics or [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) |
| Current course metric, rank, timestamp | [Project profile](https://shipped.money/p/onepager) |

Check Clarity is not already installed; mask CV text, files, contact details and payment fields. Capture aggregate `account_confirmed`, `master_cv_ready`, `resume_generated`, `fact_review_completed`, `first_download`, `additional_output_paid`. A checkbox is not proof of factual correctness: sample with willing testers and record confirmed errors separately.

## Weekly decisions

| Checkpoint | Planned learning | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | 20–50 accounts; can five willing testers get an accurate file? | Any invented date or number: pause promotion and fix guardrails. |
| Day 14 | 60–150 accounts; at least half of workshop participants export accurately? | Low export: watch upload/format friction before adding groups. |
| Day 21 | 150–300 accounts if the organizer route works | If nobody returns for another role, ask whether the Master CV actually saves work. |
| Day 30 | Report actual against 1,000; verified downloads beside it | Keep channels by accurate downloads per hour, not views. |

These are checkpoints for intervention, not claimed conversion benchmarks. The initial channels in `02` do not guarantee 1,000. At 30% visitor-to-account you need 3,334 distinct qualified visits; at 15% you need 6,667. Recalculate with observed rates on Day 14. Do not close the gap by counting exports as people.

Track paid additional resumes and refunds separately from free accounts. Record whether $0.90 actually applies after the first output; do not report future prices as revenue. Current course Clarity readings are overlapping 24-hour snapshots, not cumulative users or proof that a resume helped get an interview.
