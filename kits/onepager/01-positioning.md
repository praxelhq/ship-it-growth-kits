# OnePager — Positioning

**Live:** https://softlaunch.in/ · **Category:** Career tools · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/onepager

**Your site is live.** The roster's earlier down label is stale. The rendered page on 29 October shows **“Every CV starts out crumpled.”** and **“Build my one-pager”**, linked to `/app.html`. The offer is one free resume and **$0.90 each** afterward, but the page also says **“Free while in beta.”** Resolve that contradiction before inviting candidates; do not quote the old down status or promise everything is free.

Your first user is an Indian business-school student whose master CV is three pages and who needs a truthful one-page version for one target role. Their pain is fitting the evidence, not creating more adjectives.

**Pitch:** Turn your own Master CV into a role-focused, one-page resume, with editable Word and PDF output.

**Short version:** Your facts. One role. One clean page.

## What makes the first use useful

Upload a CV or fill the Master CV form, choose a target role, review facts and download the page. The live page promises real-page line fitting, one-line bullets and checks that numbers exist in the Master CV. Treat these as product claims to demonstrate, not independently proven accuracy. A candidate must check every date, role and number before applying.

Your wedge is **one-page fitting with factual constraints**, not “guaranteed shortlists.” The site mentions Indian template conventions, including ClassX/XII and score rules. Let the candidate select the required template; do not impose those details on every international or experienced-hire resume.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) offers a free professional profile for proof of work. An existing document editor and a manually edited one-page template cost no extra purchase. You win when the candidate wants fast role-focused fitting and a reusable Master CV; the free alternatives win when the student can already edit the document well. Do not invent another resume tool's price.

## The honest limit

The page's sample changes an unquantified sentence into a bullet with 9%→ 3% and ₹40 Cr. Those numbers must be visibly present in the example's input, otherwise the example undermines your factual promise. Your research did not upload a CV or verify generated output. Day 1 uses a fictional source CV and compares every output fact before any launch.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 accounts. Report first verified download beside it. A login without an accurate resume is not success.
