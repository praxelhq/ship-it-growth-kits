# OnePager — Distribution map

**Lead with campus resume workshops and before/after demonstrations.** You need people applying to a role now, not every person who dislikes their CV.

| Channel | Exact route and boundary | Scheduled forecast: assumptions, not benchmarks | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four existing placement/project groups | Privately ask admins; one useful message each, no member-list export or student CVs in chat. |4 groups × 30 relevant readers× 20–40% try = 24–48 accounts if signup is required. |#1|
| Ten willing peer organizers | Invite ten people who have asked for resume help; each may choose to run one 15-minute demo for their own group. Never claim their college endorses you. |10 organizers × 5–15 willing participants = 50–150 trials, minus overlap. |#2|
| Your LinkedIn | Three factual fitting demonstrations on Days 8, 16, 25 at 12:30 IST. |3 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits× 30% account = 3–27 accounts. |#3|
| Your Reels/Shorts | Three 20-second clips showing a real one-page export with fictional input. |3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits× 30% account = 1–36 accounts. |#4|
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Builder-feedback listing after upload/export tests. Homepage advertises Monday Launchpad; check current eligibility and available slot. No vote solicitation. |0–30 accounts as uncertain upside; not a target foundation. |#5|

The base experiments suggest around 100–250 trials before overlap, not 1,000. At 30% visitor-to-account, the target needs roughly 3,334 unique relevant visits. To expand, recruit more organizers only when at least half of workshop participants get a fact-checked download. Otherwise you amplify mistakes.

No cold CV collection. A workshop uses fictional documents; participants upload privately to the app if they choose. No unverified subreddit, Discord or newsletter placement is included. For career-club accounts, use the official contact page of the student's own institution and ask permission; a campus name is not permission.

Coordinate with Anvaya's human-coaching offer: OnePager serves self-editing students and a one-page export, not a free coaching call. Use Days 10–13 for your workshop invitations, with different groups from the other career projects. Free directory submissions can wait until the product proves useful; paid placement is out of scope.
