# GOAT Cards by streak — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** This tested entry needs no account, so count a meaningful first dealt-game session under the existing policy. A homepage view, your test or another match by the same person is not another signup. Use existing consented measurement and disclose anonymous/browser deduplication limits rather than fingerprinting.

**Activation:** first stat selected and its actual comparison viewed; report **completed match/outcome under the actual first-to-five or deck-exhaustion rule** beside this early action. A match ending by deck exhaustion follows the actual rules and should be labelled accurately. Return play is another day/session, not another new person. No multiplayer or leaderboard metric should be invented.

## Baseline

Record actual first deals, first stat results, completed matches, restarts, returns, errors, landing sources and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/goat-cards-by-streak. Public research did not establish private counts. Existing Clarity is present: inspect before adding tags and avoid identifying data in replay. Aggregate gameplay events are enough; do not collect fan names/contacts to create a reporting number.
