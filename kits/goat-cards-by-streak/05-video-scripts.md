# GOAT Cards by streak — Video scripts

Record your own actual duel on a phone. Keep the unofficial/game-rating notice on screen and preserve required credits if exporting assets. One first stat selection was verified; finish a real match yourself before using a completion clip.

## A. Call the stat — 35 seconds, homepage/game invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Your actual card. | “Which stat would you call?” |
| 3–13 s | Tap a real stat and show real comparison. | “Pick a stat on your top card. Higher number takes the cards and gets the next call.” |
| 13–24 s | Actual next-card sequence. | “GOAT Cards deals ten each from the deck. First to five rounds wins.” |
| 24–30 s | Actual outcome, no edited fake win. | “These are game ratings, not official football stats.” |
| 30–35 s | URL/unofficial label. | “Try the unofficial browser duel.” |

## B. Flip it — 15 seconds, Reel

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Card before flip. | “Confident in that call?” |
| 2–10 s | One uncut actual comparison. | “Choose your stat, then see what the rival has.” |
| 10–15 s | URL and game-rating label. | “First to five. Unofficial fan game.” |

## C. What a tie does — 20 seconds, optional Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual tie, if encountered. | “A tie is not the end.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual pot/next result, not fabricated. | “The tied cards wait in the pot. The next winner collects them too.” |
| 14–20 s | URL/unofficial label. | “Try a match at goatcards.club.” |

If you do not encounter a tie, replace C with an honest rule explanation over the public rules panel; do not animate an event as real gameplay. Never say a chosen game rating proves who is the real GOAT.
