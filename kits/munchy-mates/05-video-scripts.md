# Munchy Mates — Video scripts

Use your own app and a disposable file. No borrowed anime footage or private documents. Caption everything; phone viewers should understand the Mac-only requirement.

## A. Real product demo —45 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“My battery has a pet now.”** Show the real Dock and companion.

2–12: Low battery then charging, uncut where possible. **“She gets hungry when my battery drops and munches back when I plug in.”** Label any time jump; don't pretend a simulator toggle is hardware proof.

12–22: Choose/name a pet. **“Cat, pup or bunny, with their own personality.”**

22–32: Turn chatter off and show Focus settings. **“Company when I want it. Quiet when I don't.”**

32–40: Feed a disposable file; open Trash and restore. **“Files I feed her go to Trash. PDF tools make separate results.”**

40–45: **“Apple silicon, macOS 14+. Paid app; try the web preview before you decide. munchymatess.app.”** Use on homepage/Peerlist.

## B. Lunchbox —15 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“She's hungry because my laptop is.”** Close-up on pet and real battery.

2–8: Plug in and reaction. Caption **“Battery buddy.”**

8–12: Return to work. **“A tiny friend above the Dock.”**

12–15: **“Mac only · currently $5 launch price · web demo first.”** URL. First owned clip.

## C. The considerate pet —20 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Cute until you join a meeting?”**

2–10: Actual quiet setting and silent pet while a dummy call starts. **“I gave Munchy Mates a quiet side: calls, Focus, mute, or just turn chatter off.”** Only show behaviour the build supports.

10–16: Hide/show from menu. **“You can also hide her.”**

16–20: **“Your Mac, your amount of company.”** Supported hardware and URL. Second owned clip.

## D. Feed, then restore —25 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Don't feed her your only important file.”** Show a named disposable test file.

2–12: Drag to mouth, then show Trash. **“This action moves the file to Trash, like deleting it yourself.”**

12–20: Restore that file. **“You can restore it before you empty Trash. PDF tools are different: they keep originals and create results.”**

20–25: **“Fluffy doesn't mean the consequence should be hidden.”** URL and Mac requirement. Third owned clip; use as product education rather than a scary prank.
