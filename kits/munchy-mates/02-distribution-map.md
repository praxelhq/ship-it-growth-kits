**The product is visual. Lead with three short real-app clips and small Mac-owner tests, then one permitted Mac-community launch.** Don't buy shoutouts or message generic pet accounts whose followers cannot run it.

All forecasts are unmeasured assumptions for the exact volume in `06`; zero sales is possible.

## Community, newsletter and account work

No newsletter inclusion or influencer response is confirmed. Identify real Mac-desktop setup posts within r/macapps, answer a relevant question with no link, and disclose the relationship if asked for your app. Ask the creator before sending a build privately. Do not pretend a broad anime audience is qualified Mac demand.

The free demo is the safe link to share first; never send checkout directly to a phone user. Your warm-audience window is Days 21–24, after 12th Man and Fan Notch. Each developer still has their own 30-day promotional limit and must not coordinate votes.
