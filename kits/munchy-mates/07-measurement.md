# Munchy Mates — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** This no-account paid Mac app counts **unique first launches**, with **pet chosen/named and visible on the desktop** beside it as activation. Record purchases, successful licence activations, launches, adopted pets and return use separately. Website-demo use is not an installed user.

## Day 0 evidence

Read the current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/munchy-mates), recording date, latest traffic reading and rank. In your existing records log site visits 7 days, Mac-versus-phone traffic, paid orders, refunds, licence deliveries, successful activations and confirmed first launches. These private totals are not known to this kit; **not measured** is different from zero.

Verify public-site [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and mask checkout/licence/user fields. Don't record personal files or screenshots from the installed pet. Keep tester details private. Website analytics cannot prove what happened inside the app.

## Funnel and return use

`web_demo_used → buy_clicked → purchase_verified → licence_activated → first_launch → pet_adopted → used_on_other_day`.

A refunded order is still an order event but is excluded from net sales. A buyer activating twice is one person. Extra $3 pet purchases are not new app users. If no privacy-respecting unique-launch counter exists, report the verified test sample and state the limit.

## Arithmetic worth facing

The scheduled channels in `02` imply approximately 1–11 buyers before overlap and optional Product Hunt 0–3. That is a learning cohort, not a forecast of 1,000 paid installs. At a hypothetical 3% qualified-visit-to-purchase and 80% first-launch success,1,000 launches needs about 41,667 qualified visits. These are scenario assumptions, not benchmarks. The present calendar does not establish that reach; a clip going viral is upside, not a base plan.

## Weekly decisions

|Review|Inspect|Rule for this experiment|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|How many of 10 invitations are compatible, interested, paid and launched?|If most are incompatible, fix targeting/endcards before recording more clips.|
|Day 14|Paid order→licence→first launch; first-day hide/mute use.|Any missing paid delivery is a support priority ahead of promotion.|
|Day 21|Adopted pet→another-day use; reasons for hiding.|If most hide it, test calmer defaults before chasing views.|
|Day 28|Source-specific buyers/activations/returns and refunds.|Keep the channel producing pets people retain.|
|Day 30|Launches, activations, return users, net sales and target gap.|Say what actually happened; never count demo drags as signups.|

Each Sunday save these figures with build/version and hours spent. Also count file-confusion reports: a user surprised that feeding moves a file to Trash is a product-copy failure even if restoration is available. Fix that immediately.

Do not add overlapping 24-hour course traffic readings together. A pet clip's views and a rank spike are discovery signals; the product outcome is somebody choosing to keep a Munchy Mate on their actual Mac.
