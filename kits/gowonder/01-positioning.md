# GoWonder — Positioning

**Live:** https://gowonder.me/ · **Category:** Other / curiosity-led exploration · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/gowonder

**Your 110 people are curated profiles, not 110 users or mentors waiting for introductions.** The [live interface](https://gowonder.me/), inspected 29 October, connects subjects/questions to fields, public-source profiles and small activities. Don't turn “meet someone doing this” into a promised personal connection.

Actual headline: **“Discover things you never knew existed.”** Entry actions: **“Start with a subject”** and **“Start with a question.”** The Netflix-question route was exercised without an account: it explained recommendation systems, linked real source material and offered a 5–15-minute task—choose three films for a friend and explain why. No private save/persistence or personal contact was tested. No public paid price was visible.

## The first learner

An adult student who understands a classroom subject but can't picture interesting things people do with it. Start with “Why does Netflix know what I might like?” rather than “choose your career.” Let curiosity lead to one small action, not an aptitude label.

**Pitch:** Start with a question you already have, see the work behind it, and try a small piece yourself.

**Bio:** One familiar question. One unexpected thing to try.

The wedge is question→field→source→tiny activity. A person can browse widely, but your first useful result is trying something, not accumulating a large saved list. The optional interest check is a preview, not a validated career decision tool.

[Khan Academy](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/202260114-Does-it-cost-money-to-use-Khan-Academy) offers genuine free learning; a search engine and public expert articles are other no-additional-software-cost routes. Your difference is a short connected path and low-pressure action, not better certification or guaranteed career fit. A source profile does not imply its subject endorses GoWonder.

Target stays **1,000 signups**, defined for the observed no-account flow as unique genuine discovery sessions, with **a detail path opened and one small activity tried/self-reported** alongside it. Keep self-report distinct from observed completion. Cards, curated profiles and repeat visits aren't extra people.
