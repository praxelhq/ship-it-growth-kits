# GoWonder — Distribution map

**Lead with a familiar question and a tiny activity in adult student circles.** A generic directory launch is weaker than showing how mathematics connects to a recommendation choice.

All forecasts are planning assumptions for the calendar, not measured benchmarks.

| Route | Permission / action | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fifteen adult classmates | Individually offer a question relevant to something they study; no career recommendation or personal-data quiz required. | 15 × assumed 60–80% explore × 50–70% try an activity = 5–8 trials. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned subject/creative circles | Admin-approved 10-minute exercise, no compulsory result submission. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 15–30% explore × 30–60% try = 4–14 trials. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Netflix question, film-sound question, small-action boundary at 18:30 IST. Use actual source paths. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% explore × 30–60% try = 2–54 trials. | #3 |
| 4. One free peer session | Ask a known class/club organizer; use adult participants, no payment/member list. Count only after accepted. | If held, 5–15 explorers and 3–10 activity trials; likely overlaps the circles. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after the anonymous path works; no vote exchange. | Assume 5–20 visits and 2–8 activity trials. | #5 |

The base plan suggests roughly 13–84 trials before overlap. Discovery starts can be higher, but don't equate every card view with a thousand activated learners.

## Sources, accounts and directories

The actual [Netflix path](https://gowonder.me/#question/netflix) links public Netflix/Spotify material and identifies historical work as historical. Share those sources as context; don't mass-tag the people profiled or imply they have agreed to mentor users. The [credits page](https://gowonder.me/credits.html) is for attribution, not a roster of partners.

No specialist education-community promo permission, newsletter slot or institutional adoption was verified. Ask an organizer you know rather than inventing a named campus club. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional free discovery after usability, with zero base forecast until measured. Coordinate circles with the cohort's career/learning tools: GoWonder is exploration and doing, not placement preparation or a career assignment.
