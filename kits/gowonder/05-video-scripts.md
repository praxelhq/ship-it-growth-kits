# GoWonder — Video scripts

Record actual paths and public-source labels. No invented learner/mentor outcomes. Use your own activity example.

## A. Familiar question to action — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Why does Netflix know what you might like?”**

2–12: Open the actual question path. “A familiar question leads to recommendation systems.”

12–23: Show the real source links and historical profile label. “You can inspect public examples of the work. These aren't promised mentor introductions.”

23–35: Show the actual film-choice task. “Choose three films for a friend and explain your clues. A useful guess can still be wrong.”

35–45: “You don't have to pick a career. Try something small. gowonder.me.” Main demo.

## B. Who makes that sound? — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Who made those footsteps in a film?”**

2–13: Open the actual sound-related question and its sources. “One everyday curiosity can lead to a kind of work you hadn't noticed.”

13–20: “Follow a question, not a career label.” URL. First clip; don't use copyrighted film footage.

## C. Three films — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Make one tiny recommendation system.”**

2–13: Write three film suggestions for a fictional friend with known preferences. “Name the clues, explain the choices and admit where your guess could miss.”

13–20: “That's the small task at the end of this GoWonder path.” URL. Second clip; no coding or expert outcome implied.

## D. Save versus try — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Interesting isn't the same as tried.”**

2–10: Show Save and optional I tried this labels. “You can keep reading, save it, or try one small piece. No public submission needed.”

10–15: “Your curiosity, your pace.” URL. Third clip; a self-reported tick isn't observed learning proof.
