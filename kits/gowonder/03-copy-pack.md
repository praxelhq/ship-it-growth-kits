## #5 — Maker listing

**Tagline:** A question, a field, a real source and something small to try.

GoWonder connects school/college subjects and everyday questions to possibilities, curated public profiles and optional activities. The observed discovery path works without account creation. It is not a validated career assessment, mentor marketplace or certificate course. Saves/self-reported trials and source profiles are distinct from actual users.

**Maker comment:** I'd value feedback on whether a new explorer can follow one path and try one small task. Please don't treat profiled people as available mentors or assume they endorse the app.

## #6 — One consented follow-up

Did you try the small task, save it for later, or just read the path? All three answers help. If you stopped, was it unclear what to do, too much reading, or simply not interesting? No need to submit private work.

Repeat posts should use a genuinely different question and actual path. Never invent student outcomes, mentors, certificates or a user's future career fit.
