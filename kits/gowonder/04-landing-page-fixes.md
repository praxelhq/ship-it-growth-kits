# GoWonder — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://gowonder.me/) and the [Netflix question path](https://gowonder.me/#question/netflix) were inspected in the actual browser on 29 October. The path worked without signup. Save persistence, optional interest-check outcomes and private notes were not exercised.

## 1. Make the first journey smaller than the catalogue

Current H1: **“Discover things you never knew existed.”** Entry actions: **“Start with a subject”** and **“Start with a question.”**

Add **“Pick one question. See the field behind it. Try a five-minute piece.”** Make **“Follow one example”** the easiest first action; the existing Netflix route is a concrete starting point. Keep the wide catalogue secondary until a new user understands the loop.

## 2. Clarify what “meet” means

Current copy invites users to meet people behind possibilities. Replace with **“Read about real people through public sources.”** Add **“Profiles do not imply endorsement, availability or an introduction.”** Historical role dates should remain historical, as the inspected path already does.

## 3. Put the activity near the explanation

The Netflix route offers choosing films for a friend, after several sections. Add a short preview **“Try it: choose three films and explain your clues.”** near the top, with optional detail below. Don't require a public upload or checkmark to keep browsing.

## 4. Separate content counts from community proof

The homepage states 150 possibilities/110 real people. Label those as **curated content counts**, not active learners or partner mentors. Keep attribution/source links real and don't turn sample save/trial counts into social proof.

## 5. Explain saving and local/private state accurately

Test save/reload/device behaviour before promising persistence. Tell users where their saved discoveries/notes live and how to remove them. A visible Save button isn't proof of account sync. Keep private notes out of share previews and analytics.

## 6. Keep the interest check optional and limited

The page calls it an optional preview. Preserve that wording and add **“A starting suggestion, not a validated career assessment.”** Users should be able to follow a subject/question without handing over personal answers.

## 7. Test the actual mobile path and source exits

Title: **“GoWonder — follow a question, try something small.”** Description: **“Connect familiar subjects to fields, public sources and optional tiny activities.”** Test back navigation, external-source links, text zoom and slow images. No third-party video/source should trap users or imply an endorsement that wasn't granted.
