# askpragzy — Distribution map

**Find founders with interviews already booked.** Don't compete with the cohort's pitch-deck or market-report clinics. Your specific job is question quality before the conversation, not a generic founder workshop.

| Route | Action, rule and assumed outcome | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm founder introductions | Ask ten known builders whether they can introduce a real business owner preparing interviews. No private participant lists or automatic group addition. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 independently active businesses. | #1 |
| 2 · Public founder business posts/sites | Manually qualify a current research job before a relevant business enquiry. Four new first approaches on ten named days = 40. One follow-up after seven days, stop on no. Scenario 40 × 25% response × 60% real interview trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 businesses. | #2 |
| 3 · [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) organizer route | Network verified; no current free event or group permission assumed. Offer a narrow script-review session on days 28–30 only after organizer approval and avoiding the other founder clinics. Don't imply a reserved slot. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn / X | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show one synthetic leading question and a reviewed alternative. No invented interview result or founder quote. Assumed 100–500 impressions, 0–5 useful visits per small-account post; possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Official site advertises Monday Launchpad; check actual current eligibility and slot. A working paste/review flow can be demonstrated. Optional 0–100 visits, 0–2 relevant trials, not the main customer plan. | #5 |

[YC's interview guidance](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Iq-how-to-talk-to-users) is a checked primary learning route; no YC affiliation is implied. Specific source to follow: its actual user-research material, responding usefully from your own account rather than adding promotional comments. [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) can help identify legitimate founder organizations, not provide a bulk contact list or advertising permission.

Astitva's shared clinic days 4–7, PitchPanel days 10–13 and Adya days 24–27 stay clear. Your early work is private qualified outreach; a late organizer session is optional and contributes no guaranteed count. No unverified newsletter, Discord or Reddit rule is included. Base **3–5 businesses** is below ten; inspect real interview reuse at day 14 before increasing volume.
