# askpragzy — Measurement

**Target: 10 real businesses.** Activation beside it: **businesses independently reviewing a real interview script and using the reviewed questions in an actual customer conversation**. A student assignment, public example or anonymous review click isn't a business customer.

## Day 0

Query actual completed script reviews, answer reviews, errors and known real business pilots. The no-signup flow doesn't establish distinct businesses by itself. Use a consented private pilot register for business identity and actual interview use; keep raw scripts/transcripts out of public reports. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent with paste fields masked. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/askpragzy) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero.

Events: `script_review_started`, `questions_parsed`, `review_completed`, `question_revised`, `answer_review_completed` and `business_returned`. Track successful output rather than button clicks, without raw interview content. A changed question isn't proof of market validation; actual research learning is a separate qualitative record.

## Four business tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a legitimate business, its own permitted real script/context onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required; no paid plan was verified. One founder reviewing three scripts is one business. A generic product idea with no real business context doesn't automatically qualify.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 25% response × 60% real interview trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 businesses. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–5 is below ten.** These aren't measured research-tool rates. At 20 approaches, recompute from actual response, planned interview completion and repeat use; do not substitute anonymized session counts for companies.

Review ten permitted critiques: did they correctly flag leading questions, preserve business context and avoid inventing evidence? A confidently wrong interpretation is a more important failure than low launch traffic. Keep what a customer actually said distinct from what the model inferred.
