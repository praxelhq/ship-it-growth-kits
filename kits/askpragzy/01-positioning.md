# askpragzy — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.askpragzy.co.in/start · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/askpragzy

**Your ten are real businesses preparing customer interviews—not ten student questionnaires.** Start with a founder who has a working product or actual business hypothesis and already scheduled one-to-one interviews this week. Don't turn “validated by AI” into a result the product cannot establish.

The assigned live entry's headline is **“Review your interview questions.”** The main action is **“Review my questions.”** The [homepage](https://www.askpragzy.co.in/) says free to try with no signup. The current `/start` flow accepts up to 30 questions and marks Survey, file upload and starting from scratch as coming soon. `/analyze` accepts one customer's Q:/A: conversation up to 30 exchanges. No paid plan or price was established.

## Your one line

> Find leading or hypothetical questions before your next customer interview, then check what the answers actually support.

Bio: **A review step before you mistake politeness for demand.**

The wedge is **one interview script for a real interview already booked**. The founder enters the business context, pastes the questions, reviews the critique and keeps control of the rewrite. Later, with the interviewee's permission, they can review a redacted conversation. Don't promise survey analysis, automatic research participants or a prediction of willingness to pay.

## Why use it instead?

[ChatGPT's official pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) includes a Free plan. A founder can already ask a general assistant to critique a script. [YC's primary user-interview guidance](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Iq-how-to-talk-to-users) is another learning route, not a product endorsement or paid service quote. Your advantage must be the structured idea/audience/problem/payment-assumption context and a usable question-by-question review—not pretending generic AI cannot do criticism.

Your actual competitor is often “send the questions as written.” Show one leading question becoming a past-behaviour question, then observe whether the real interview becomes more informative. A nicer questionnaire still doesn't establish market demand.

## The weakness

The product needs no account and says results are retained for seven days. Anonymous requests cannot prove ten distinct businesses or independent return use. Keep a small consented private pilot log and aggregate events. Never paste private interview content without authority or claim the deletion promise was independently audited from a public page.
