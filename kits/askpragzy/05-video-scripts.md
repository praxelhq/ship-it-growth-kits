# askpragzy — Video scripts

Use synthetic business ideas and conversations, not private interview material. Record actual supported controls; label any shortened wait. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · Before the customer call · 60 seconds · founder walkthrough / LinkedIn

0–2s hook: **“This question is asking for a compliment.”** Show “Would you use my app?” labelled synthetic. 2–15s: enter the idea/audience/problem context: “The critique needs to know what you're assuming.” 15–30s: paste a small script and show the actual review: “Now inspect what is leading, hypothetical or too broad.” 30–45s: revise one question yourself: “I want a past event I can ask follow-ups about, not automatic approval from an AI.” 45–55s: show the corrected script: “The interview still has to happen. The tool doesn't prove demand.” 55–60s: “Free to try, up to 30 questions. Ask Pragzy.”

## B · Polite isn't evidence · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“‘Sounds interesting’ didn't validate the business.”** 0–8s: show a synthetic polite answer. 8–15s: ask what actual behaviour, cost or workaround the person described. 15–20s: “Keep the conclusion smaller than the evidence.” Don't pretend this example came from a customer.

## C · One conversation, correctly formatted · 25 seconds · onboarding reply

Hook 0–2s: **“Zero exchanges may mean the format is wrong.”** 2–10s: show a synthetic unformatted paste. 10–20s: add Q:/A: labels and inspect the actual parser/review: “One customer conversation at a time, with permission and private details removed.” 20–25s: “Check the input before believing the conclusion.”

If the parser still fails, show the limitation and fix it; don't edit around the failure or fabricate an analysis result.
