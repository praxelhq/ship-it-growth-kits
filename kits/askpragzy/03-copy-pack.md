## #4 — Public example post

“Would you use my app?” is easy to answer politely.

“Tell me about the last time you had this problem” gives you something different to inspect. It still doesn't guarantee demand, but it makes it harder to mistake encouragement for evidence.

I'm building Ask Pragzy as a review step before the interview: enter the business context, paste the script and challenge the questions. Afterward, you can review an authorized redacted conversation too.

The examples I'm sharing are synthetic. The real test is whether a founder changes a question and learns something useful in an actual conversation.

Current interview flow, free to try: https://www.askpragzy.co.in/start
