# Duvidha — Video scripts

Use a harmless sample choice and sample account, with no real dilemma or gift note. Only show a generated reflection/payment working after testing it. Use your own paraphrase when explaining a verse rather than copying an unverified translation.

## A. The first day — 45 seconds

**Use:** homepage and reading-circle invitation.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Sample choice. “A little room to think.” | “One choice, a little space.” |
| 3–15 s | Actual Day 1 entry. | “Duvidha begins with naming the choice you are stuck on. This example is harmless and invented.” |
| 15–30 s | Real verse/reflection/action flow, if tested. | “Each day gives a Gita verse, a reflection and one small action.” |
| 30–40 s | Free/paid boundary. | “Day 1 is free. The rest and your summary are ₹99 once. You still make the decision.” |
| 40–45 s | URL. | “Try the first day at duvidha.online.” |

## B. Not another command — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short/Reel.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Sample reflection. | “The choice stays yours.” |
| 3–13 s | Verse and question labels. | “A verse can open a question. It does not have to make the decision for you.” |
| 13–17 s | Day 1 free. | “Start with one quiet reflection.” |
| 17–20 s | URL. | “Day 1 is free.” |

## C. What ₹99 means — 25 seconds

**Use:** maker post/Peerlist.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | Free Gita section. | “Reading the Gita is free.” |
| 4–15 s | Journey price. | “The ₹99 choice is the rest of your personalized journey and summary after Day 1.” |
| 15–22 s | No-renewal wording. | “It is one payment, not a subscription.” |
| 22–25 s | URL. | “Decide after the first day.” |

Keep captions readable. If generation takes time, label any cut. Do not turn the sample into a testimonial or a promise of clarity for every person.
