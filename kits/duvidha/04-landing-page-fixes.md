# Duvidha — Landing-page fixes

Checked against the live page, `/auth`, `/gift` and privacy on 29 October. Current headline: **“Your 5-day Gita journey from confusion to clarity.”** Current main action: **“Begin your journey — Day 1 is free”**. Day 1₹0; Days 2–5/summary ₹99 once. No personalized response, purchase or gift was tested.

## 1. Remove certainty about Day 5

**Current:** “Clarity on Day 5” can sound like a guaranteed resolution.

**Write:**

> Five quiet days to examine your choice. On Day 5, keep a summary of what you noticed and the next step you choose.

Show a labelled sample, not a fabricated successful user decision.

## 2. Say what happens after the button

**Current:** the home action leads into the adult auth flow.

**Write:**

> Create your account to begin Day 1 free. You can decide later whether to continue for ₹99 once.

Test whether the dilemma entered before signup carries forward correctly, and what happens on back navigation. Do not claim this was verified just because the route loads.

## 3. Keep AI processing near personal input

**Current:** privacy explains that personal text is sent to AI providers to personalize the journey.

**Write:**

> AI helps write your reflection and can misunderstand. Read how your text is processed before sharing sensitive details; treat the response as something to consider, not instructions to follow.

Mask dilemma/reflection/summary/gift-note content from analytics and public demo recordings.

## 4. Keep scripture access separate from payment

**Current:** the full Gita is free, while the personalized journey has a ₹99 continuation.

**Write:**

> Read the Gita free. Pay ₹99 only if you choose the remaining personalized journey and summary after Day 1.

A visitor should never think the scripture itself is locked behind the purchase.

## 5. Explain a gift before its checkout

**Current:** `/gift` asks for an account and describes a private gift link; the page says refundable until opened.

**Write:**

> Create an account, write a private note and review the gift terms before paying. The recipient chooses their own dilemma.

Verify redemption, visibility and refund boundary with a harmless test. Do not promise automatic delivery or a refund state not reproduced.

## 6. Make a phone preview honest

Use: **Duvidha — a five-day Gita reflection for one choice. Day 1 free; continue for ₹99 once if you choose.** Check the preview on a phone and the actual auth/payment routes. Use readable verse/reflection labels, not a dramatic guaranteed-answer graphic.
