# Duvidha — Positioning

**Live:** https://duvidha.online/ · **Category:** Education · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/duvidha

## Lead with reflection, not a guaranteed answer

Your page promises clarity on Day 5. Be careful: five days can give someone a structure for thinking, but cannot guarantee that a difficult decision will be resolved. The choice stays theirs. The [privacy page](https://duvidha.online/privacy) also says their text is processed by AI providers; do not imply that it remains only on their device.

## The current product

Checked 29 October 2026: **“Your 5-day Gita journey from confusion to clarity.”** The main action is **“Begin your journey — Day 1 is free”**. The auth route is `/auth`, which states 18+ and shows the Day 1 offer. Day 1 costs ₹0; Days 2–5 and a personal summary cost ₹99 once, with no renewal. A ₹99 gift journey is a separate choice. The full Gita-reading section is free.

The site lays out a daily verse, reflection and small action, ending with a summary. Public auth/gift pages were read; no account, personalized response, payment or gift redemption was completed here. The offer is real visible copy, not proof every step works end to end.

## Your first person

An adult who already finds the Gita meaningful and is weighing one ordinary choice, such as two study/work directions. They want a reflective practice they can complete privately, not an oracle, a therapist or someone to make the decision for them.

**Pitch:** Think through one choice over five quiet days, using a Gita verse, reflection and small action each day.

**Bio:** A five-day Gita reflection. Day 1 free; continue only if you want to.

The first use is naming the two options and completing Day 1. Do not start with a sprawling crisis, private relationship disclosure or a promise that a verse will solve the problem. Use a harmless sample when demonstrating.

## The alternative is already free

The [Gita Supersite at Heritage IITKanpur](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/gitasupersite) offers public text/commentary resources; your own `/gita` section is also free. [Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) is$0/year for journaling. Duvidha must earn its ₹99 through the five-day structure and personally relevant reflection, not by pretending access to scripture requires payment.

Get five people through Day 1, then ask whether they chose to return and whether the interpretation respected their situation. Do not turn a polite answer into an effectiveness testimonial. Keep **1,000 consumer signups** as the assigned target, with first-day and five-day completions beside it.
