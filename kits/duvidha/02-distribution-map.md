# Duvidha — Distribution map

**Lead with people who already choose Gita-based reflection.** A broad spiritual promise attracts the wrong expectation; a clear Day 1 demonstration lets the person decide.

| Route | Entry and boundary | Planning result, assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing adult reading relationships | Ask twenty known, relevant adults over the month. No scraped religious-group lists. | 20×50% visit×50% account =5 signups. | #1 |
| Your short demonstrations | [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) or your own Reel account. Show a harmless sample and scope, not a promised life change. | 6 clips×500–2,000 views×1% visit×50% account =15–60 signups. | #2 |
| Your LinkedIn posts | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/). Explain the small practice and price; no advice under strangers’ distress posts. | 4 posts×300–1,000 views×1% visit×50% account =6–20 signups. | #3 |
| Existing adult Gita/reading circle | Ask its actual organizer privately. No institutional endorsement or open posting permission is assumed. | 2 accepted sessions×15 adults×50% lateraccount =15 signups. | #4 |
| Peerlist | [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/), if the working tool fits current submission rules/Monday slots. No vote swaps. | One optional listing:0–10 signups. | #5 |
| Opted-in Day 1 follow-up | Ask whether the person wants another message; never request their dilemma text. | Ten follow-ups: retention learning, not ten new users. | #6 |

These are guesses for a small account, not platform benchmarks. The calendar schedules 20 invitations,6 clips,4 posts,2 accepted sessions and 1 optional listing:41–110 accounts before overlap; use 30–85 distinct accounts as a working range. It does not support a guaranteed 1,000. An actual larger reading partner or stronger measured sharing is needed; do not invent one.

Prefer Days 10–13 for a shared reading organizer. The spiritual reflection audience also overlaps wellbeing products; coordinate actual dates and keep one broad invitation per organizer per week. The IIT reference is a source and alternative, not a place you can advertise or a partner that has endorsed Duvidha. No teacher, newsletter or spiritual community placement was verified. Ask permission before a private-group invitation, respect a decline and do not use faith-based pressure to solicit a purchase or gift.
