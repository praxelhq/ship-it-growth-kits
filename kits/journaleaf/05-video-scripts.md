# Journaleaf — Video scripts

Use a neutral synthetic sentence and your own recording. No person's real journal, feeling or account identity appears. No clinical outcome claim or paid effects.

## A. The one-minute loop — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “A garden that doesn't punish a missed day.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Actual headline and storage/trial explanation|“Journaleaf starts with one small typing session.”|
|10–25s|Choose a neutral demo feeling and sentence, labelled synthetic|“I'm using a made-up example; your words stay yours.”|
|25–42s|Type at a readable pace, no fake completion|“Letter by letter, without a race.”|
|42–52s|Actual flower result|“This records showing up, not a perfect streak.”|
|52–60s|URL and trial disclosure|“Try the loop. No automatic charge after the 3-day trial.”|

Publish only after a real test confirms the flow; no garden screenshot used as medical evidence.

## B. Nothing wilts — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “I missed yesterday. The garden is still here.” Show your test garden, the no-wilting explanation and today's voluntary first action. Say: “You can return without earning your way back.” Don't fake a lost/returned date sequence as a real user story.

## C. Local or signed in? — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “Where does your sentence live?” Show the actual privacy explanation, local browser state and sign-in choice. Say: “Before sign-in, this browser stores it. Account sync changes that. It isn't end-to-end encrypted, and the page explains the details.” No private text or security certification claim.

## D. Trial without surprise — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Trying a ritual shouldn't hide a charge.” Show the real 3-day trial explanation and current local plans, then the readable-after-trial statement. Say: “Nothing charges automatically when the trial ends. Choose a plan only if you want to keep adding.” Use the actual displayed region/price; don't dub an invented rupee amount.
