# Journaleaf — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). The public flow supports use before sign-in; use distinct new affirmation-session starts as the transparent no-account measure, labelled **new session starters**. Report optional registered accounts separately and deduplicate as far as privacy-preserving data permits. A refresh or abandoned demo replay is not a new person.

Always report **completed first flowers** beside starts, then later-day completed sessions. Paid plans, trial users and readable expired gardens are separate states. A 3-day trial is not a subscription sale; the site says nothing charges automatically at trial end.

## Day 0

Record actual existing starts, completions, accounts, returners and paid plans from your own system; private counts were unavailable to research. The [privacy page](https://journal-leaf.com/privacy) describes existing Vercel, internal aggregate events and masked Clarity. Confirm these work rather than installing a duplicate tracker. Never collect words, selected feelings, private affirmations or identifying data to improve acquisition reporting.

Record the actual [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/journaleaf) reading, period and rank. Keep any [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) use aligned with the real masking/consent design; the policy claim is not an independent audit. Minimum events are `session_started`, `sentence_completed`, `flower_created`, `sign_in_completed`, `later_day_session_completed` and authoritative plan confirmation, without sensitive payloads.

## Honest funnel

Ten private invitations, four owned-feed posts, six Shorts and an optional ten-person demo support tens of sessions under uncertain assumptions. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 starts need 2,000 qualified visits; at 1% view→visit, 200,000 views. This is a gap to show, not a reach promise. The target is unchanged; the observed funnel must earn any scale-up.
