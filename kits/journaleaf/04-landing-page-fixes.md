# Journaleaf — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026: home, pricing, affirmations, app and privacy. No private entry, account sync, notification or payment was exercised.

1. **Keep the actual concrete headline.** “Type a kind sentence. Grow a flower.” is already specific. Add **“Try one short session at your own pace. Miss a day and nothing wilts.”** Make the feeling→sentence→flower action visually primary, ahead of a pricing or installation prompt.

2. **Disclose the trial before people invest in a garden.** Current pricing says 3 days free, no automatic charge, then a plan to keep adding. Put **“3 days to try. Nothing charged automatically. Your existing garden stays readable afterward.”** near the first action. Display the actual local plan price; the fetched US view is not proof of the Indian rupee price.

3. **Explain local versus account storage at the right moment.** Current privacy page is clear but separate. Before first use: **“Before sign-in, this browser stores your garden. Other people using this browser can see it; clearing its data removes it.”** At sign-in: **“Signing in saves your garden to your account for other devices. Read the storage details first.”** Do not invent end-to-end encryption.

4. **Make the end of a session a calm choice.** Preserve the no-wilting promise. Result copy: **“You showed up today. Your flower is here. Come back when you want.”** Offer the next step without forcing a public share or paid upgrade before the person sees the earned flower. Match actual reward rules rather than inventing rare-bloom thresholds.

5. **Separate shipped features from Soon.** Pricing labels some keepsake/themes/founding-mark features as forthcoming. Keep them visibly **“Coming later”** and never put them in the primary demo as available now. The lifetime founding price is time-limited; recheck after 1 January 2027 rather than leaving an evergreen $39 promise.

6. **Be precise about getting the app.** `/app` describes one-tap access and reminders, but no store release was verified. Explain the actual install/home-screen steps for the current browser/device. Do not claim a native iOS/Android release from the words “Get the app.” Keep reminders optional and explain they contain no private sentence only after verifying behavior.

7. **Test preview and private-state masking.** Title **“Journaleaf — one sentence, one small flower”**; description **“A gentle typing ritual with a garden that doesn't wilt when you miss a day. Clear trial and storage choices.”** Check phone keyboard, first letter, finish animation and large text. Confirm private words/feelings are excluded from replay according to the real implementation; this kit has not audited masking or measured load performance.
