**Show the actual typing-to-flower moment.** It should feel like a small invitation, not an instruction to disclose feelings to a group.

Read [Day One's public blog](https://dayoneapp.com/blog/) for journaling questions, not as a place to recruit its users. Your own opt-in update or a permitted organizer bulletin can carry the simple invitation; no external newsletter placement is assumed. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after first-use and trial terms work, with a personal maker account and no upvote requests.

The calendar supports tens of sessions, not a proven 1,000. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 starts need 2,000 qualified visits. At 1% video-view→visit, that is 200,000 views. Treat those as inputs to test, not what six clips will inevitably achieve. A quiet person returning voluntarily is a better signal than a forced screenshot share.
