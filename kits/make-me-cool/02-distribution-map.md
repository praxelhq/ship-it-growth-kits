# Make Me Cool — Distribution map

**Give people a small taste, then let the actual story earn the visit.** Use owned cards with credit rather than reposting a full paid archive.

| Route | Calendar volume and permission | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Creator's own established channels | Ask permission once Day 22; founder/brand account only if actually controlled/accepted. | Zero baseline; displayed community totals do not guarantee reach. | #6 |

Gross planning range is about 28–213 sessions before overlap, not a 1,000 guarantee. At 60% visit→meaningful start, 1,000 requires roughly 1,667 relevant distinct visits. If clips turn only 1% of views into visits, that is about 166,700 views before overlap. Six clips don't promise those views.

No paid art newsletters, creator fees or invented book-club membership. Check current platform rules before publishing; no platform permission to copy stories or send unsolicited repeats is assumed. Use own channels, respect group rules and quote only permitted share material. Ask before any free newsletter mention; forecast zero until accepted. Keep campaign timing an organizer preference, not a confirmed date.
