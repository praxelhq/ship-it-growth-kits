# Make Me Cool — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, opened public reader, editorial/terms/refund pages checked 29 October. No quiz completion, account or payment. A live story preview differed between extraction and browser; current subject must be checked at publication.

1. **Keep the personality, explain the action.** Current H1: “knowing stuff is sexy.” CTA: “play today's story.” Add **“One short, human-picked story. Read the cards, try four questions and retell one connection.”** Proposed button **“Read today's free story.”** Keep “free, no sign-up needed” beside it, then verify that route remains true on a fresh phone.

2. **Reconcile effort with the reader.** Homepage says about five minutes/nine cards/four questions. Opened reader showed `1/8`. Use the actual story's card count and measure completion time. If story length varies, say **“A short daily read; length varies by story.”** Don't hide long onboarding behind a five-minute promise.

3. **Make evidence part of the free experience.** Editorial page says stories list sources and uncertainty; Plus advertises behind-the-story sources. Clarify what source context a free reader can inspect. Suggested note **“Here is the reference for this claim; this part remains uncertain.”** Don't use “rumour has it” to rescue a claim that cannot support the central story. Keep corrections visible.

4. **Separate pass from subscription.** Current **₹99 for 30 days**, seven free days when signing up, pay once/no auto-renew. Put **“Fixed pass. Ends on its own. No charge unless you buy.”** beside the action. Verify trial expiry, production payment/entitlement and refund recovery using authorized tests before selling the pass as proven.

5. **Make the score playful and bounded.** “A few percent cooler” and cool score are entertainment labels, not validated measures of intelligence, learning or attractiveness. Add **“A playful progress score, not an assessment.”** Let a missed day pass without guilt or a deceptive countdown.

6. **Don't let parent-brand numbers impersonate product traction.** Clearly label the Abstract Room community/event claims as parent-brand claims, with their basis/date if used. They aren't Make Me Cool active readers. Prefer an actual story and inspectable source over an unverified borrowed audience number.

7. **Check saved place and share accessibility.** Public reader says progress is saved; explain device/account limits after testing. At 390px, check next/previous, long card text, quiz recovery, closing/reopening and a credited share card. Preview **“Make Me Cool — one short curious story a day.”** No private email or unsupported story fact in preview images.
