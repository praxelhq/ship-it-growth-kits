# Make Me Cool — Video scripts

Record the actual current free story with permitted share material and credit. No invented trivia, fake quiz score, claimed memory improvement or full paid-archive reproduction.

## A. Read-to-retell demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Could I tell this story after closing the cards?”**

2–12s: open “play today's story,” show actual story title and progress. “One human-picked connection, told in short cards.”

12–25s: show a small permitted excerpt and the real quiz, without pretending every answer was correct. “Read, then check what you understood.”

25–38s: speak a faithful line in your own words. “The retell should keep the source's uncertainty.” Only name a factual claim after checking it.

38–50s: show free/Plus boundary. “Daily story free. Optional ₹99 fixed 30-day pass; no auto-renewal. makemecool.me.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: **“Close the tab. What would you say?”**

2–10s: own brief retell of a source-checked story. Label it your demonstration, not a user's testimonial.

10–15s: actual product card/URL. “Try one free story.”

## C. Source hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“The better version of the story includes ‘we're not sure.’”**

2–12s: show a real uncertainty note and source boundary. “A catchy line shouldn't erase what isn't established.”

12–20s: corrected retell/URL. If no appropriate example exists, show editorial-process page without inventing a false fact.

## D. Tomorrow hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Did I come back for the story or the streak?”**

2–12s: real second-day visit and new actual story title. “The useful habit has to earn tomorrow.” Don't fabricate consecutive days or stored progress.

12–20s: URL and daily-free note. “Read when you want. A missed day isn't a failure.”

For six calendar clips, reuse the best structure with a different verified story/editorial lesson. Don't claim a new format requires six completely new shoots or recycle the same uncredited excerpt.
