# BuskAlong — Distribution map

**A real singing demonstration is your main channel.** One guitarist seeing the page follow an actual performance is worth more than a directory listing that calls you another music app. Use original or verified public-domain material in public clips.

| Route | Action and boundary | Planning reach / basis | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Your own campus music-society organizer | Ask for one 20-minute rehearsal test, five guitarists, Thursday 18:00 IST. Permission first; no claim to access a national society network. | Five invited musicians × assumed 80% attendance × 75% completed practice = 3 activations. | #1, #4 |
| 2. Your own YouTube channel | Publish six original Shorts across the calendar, plus one complete 60-second demo. No commercial backing track or copied lyrics. Follow [YouTube's policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/). | Six Shorts × assumed 100–500 views × 1% visits = 6–30 visits. At 50% practice-start, 3–15 starts. Zero is possible. | #2, video hooks |
| 3. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Explain the actual microphone/scrolling problem and let people try the app. Confirm current slots; avoid vote trades. | One maker post: budget 0–10 useful testers, not an assured launch spike. | #3 |
| 4. [r/learnGuitar](https://www.reddit.com/r/learnGuitar/about/rules) | Rule endpoint resolved without readable rules. Get moderator permission before any promotional post; no unverified “promo Friday” claim. | Zero forecast until allowed. Use as question research meanwhile. | #5 |
| 5. Teacher or guitarist you already know | Send one personalized request to test with their own authorized chart. Five notes in total, not a broadcast to every student. | Five requests × assumed 40% response = 2 test conversations. Learners choose whether to join. | #4 |

Watch the verified [JustinGuitar channel](https://www.youtube.com/@justinguitar) for the practical difficulties of playing and singing together. An account worth learning from is not an invitation to advertise under its videos. Answer a genuine question with no product link unless requested and permitted.

Your directory experiment is Peerlist, then [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) only after you have a usable recorded demo and have checked for an existing listing. A music-society newsletter is useful only if its organizer agrees to include #1; no named newsletter or guaranteed inclusion was verified here. Do not buy a directory placement.

The initial forecast is tens of practices, not 1,000. The route to scale is a compelling performance that musicians share with another musician, backed by reliable following. Measure that share loop before multiplying a YouTube view count into an account count.
