# BuskAlong — Landing-page fixes

Public page and HTML checked 29 October 2026. The homepage uses buttons for app actions, so absence of an `href` is not proof of a broken CTA. Microphone following and private import were not exercised during research.

## 1. Name the hands-free job above the library

Current H1: “What do you want to play?” Replace with **“Sing and play. Let the chart follow.”** Supporting line: **“Choose a complete built-in public-domain song, or privately import a chart you are authorized to use.”** Keep the library immediately below; musicians came to play, not read a long brand story.

## 2. Make a working first song the primary action

Current card button: “Sing with Follow Me.” Add a top CTA **“Try a ready-made song”**, opening one supported chart already visible in the library. Secondary **“Import my authorized chart”**. Keep Community and Publish below first practice. Verify the selected chart opens on a fresh mobile browser and permission denial has a usable fallback.

## 3. Explain the eight familiar titles before people click

Current block: “Your eight songs” and “0 of 8 ready to sing.” Replace title with **“Add your own charts for these songs.”** Supporting copy: **“These titles are import targets, not bundled lyrics. Add the complete version you have permission to use; it stays on this device.”** Keep the real public-domain songs visually primary.

## 4. Explain the microphone request at the moment it matters

Current metadata says lyrics and chords follow singing, but the library does not make the microphone behavior clear in its lead. Add before the permission prompt: **“Follow Me needs microphone access to follow your singing. You can stop it at any time.”** Publish a precise processing/storage statement only after checking the implementation; do not invent “nothing is uploaded” from the word private. Provide manual navigation if permission is denied.

## 5. Prove following with an uncut rehearsal

Use a 30-second original performance with a pause and repeat, chart visible. Caption: **“Recorded on a real device with a supported chart; pace changes included.”** If it fails, fix or disclose the limitation rather than cutting out the failure. Do not use an artist's recording to imply a licensing partnership.

## 6. State access and price plainly

No paid plan was established on the fetched page. Add **“Start with the ready-made library”** and disclose any actual account or payment requirement before the first action. Do not write “free forever” unless that is the policy. Keep imports and publication permissions distinct so a private chart is never published accidentally.

## 7. Phone and link-preview checks

Use title **“BuskAlong — lyrics and chords for singing guitarists”** and description **“Try a complete public-domain chart or import your own authorized arrangement. Keep the song in view while you play.”** On your phone, test portrait/landscape, large text, screen sleep and a denied mic. Record the result; none of these visual behaviors was independently measured for this kit. Do not shrink chord text to fit decorative controls.
