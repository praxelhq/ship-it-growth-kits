# BuskAlong — Positioning

**Live:** https://buskalong.app/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/buskalong

**Your first demonstration must be a complete song, not a famous title that needs importing.** The live library headline is “What do you want to play?” It offers 51 built-in public-domain charts, while the eight familiar commercial-song targets say “Needs your chart.” That distinction belongs in your pitch. Do not promise that copyrighted lyrics are bundled.

Your first user is a college guitarist who already knows basic chords, sings while playing, and keeps interrupting the song to scroll. She is rehearsing alone or with one singer, not buying a full guitar course. Your wedge is finishing one supported song while keeping both hands on the instrument.

**Pitch:** Keep lyrics and chords in view while you sing and play, using a ready-made public-domain song or a chart you are authorized to import.

**Bio:** A singing guitarist's music stand. Start with one ready-to-play chart.

The song-card action is “Sing with Follow Me.” The page also offers community charts, private imports, setlists and a tuner. Lead with the supported-song action; do not make first-time users publish a chart. This research read the page and markup, but did not grant microphone permission or test live vocal following. Record an honest demonstration before making a reliability claim.

## The alternatives you really face

[JustinGuitar](https://www.justinguitar.com/) has free website lessons and song tutorials. [Ultimate Guitar](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/) offers a large public chart discovery surface and a paid Pro+ route; its [current Pro page](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/pro) showed a promotional offer without a reliable final checkout price, so do not invent one. A printed authorized chord sheet costs nothing beyond printing and never asks for microphone permission.

You win when following the song is easier than scrolling or printing. You lose when the mic misses the singer, a chart is incomplete, the screen sleeps, or the user cannot get their own allowed material into the app. Put those failures into your rehearsal tests, not behind a polished promo.

The public page did not establish a paid BuskAlong plan. Say **“Start with a built-in chart”**, not “free forever” or a guessed price. Keep the agreed B2C target of **1,000 signups**, with the no-account/session interpretation explicitly defined in `07`. A song-title click is not activation; a useful uninterrupted practice is.
