# BuskAlong — Video scripts

Use your own voice, guitar and authorized chart. An original song is safest for a public demo; built-in public-domain material may be used after checking the version and recording rights. No commercial lyrics are reproduced here.

## A. Hands stay on the guitar — 60-second demo

**First-two-second hook:** “My strumming hand shouldn't be a scroll wheel.”

| Time | Shot / text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Stop strumming to touch the phone | “This is the interruption I'm trying to remove.” |
| 8–18s | Open one complete built-in chart, show its public-domain label | “BuskAlong starts with charts that are ready to sing.” |
| 18–42s | One uncut performance segment, hands and readable chart both visible | “I sing, slow down, and repeat a section. Watch whether the chart stays with me.” |
| 42–52s | Show the actual recovery/manual action if needed | “When it loses the line, this is what I do.” |
| 52–60s | Hold the URL and chart name | “Try one song at buskalong.app. Tell me where it interrupts your practice.” |

Post to YouTube and the approved music-society invitation. Record the performance with a second phone if the test device's microphone is needed by the app.

## B. Pause test — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A song doesn't move at one fixed speed.” Shots: 0–5s play; 5–10s pause; 10–16s restart; 16–20s show the visible line and URL. Say: “A timer keeps moving. A musician pauses. I'm testing whether BuskAlong follows the singer rather than a clock.” Show the actual result. Use on your own Short feed.

## C. Ready-made versus imported — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “A famous song title isn't a complete chart.” Shots: 0–8s the “Needs your chart” label; 8–17s switch to a complete public-domain card; 17–25s open it. Say: “These familiar titles need an authorized chart you add privately. To try the app immediately, start here.” No artist lyrics on screen. Post with the onboarding fix.

## D. Rehearsal handoff — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “Try it without me in the room.” Show a guitarist opening a supported chart, propping the phone and beginning to play, five seconds each. Say: “Open a ready-made song. Put the phone where you can read it. Tell me if it helps a real practice.” Film another person only with permission; otherwise use your own hands. This is a tester reminder, not proof of retention.

Keep the guitar sound clear and captions large. Do not dub a perfect studio track over a broken live-follow test.
