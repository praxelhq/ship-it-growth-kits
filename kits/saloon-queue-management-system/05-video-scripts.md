# SalonQ — Video scripts

Use two phones and synthetic queue entries. No customer faces, phone numbers or owner PIN on screen.

## A.60-second owner demo — visits and homepage
0–2: three paper tokens; text **“Who is next?”** Say “A queue changes while you are cutting hair.”
2–15: owner phone: create a sample salon/services only in your authorized demo environment. “Set service names and approximate minutes.”
15–30: customer phone joins; show owner receiving the entry. “The customer sees a position.”
30–45: owner calls next, marks served and adds a walk-in. Keep the actual update uncut.
45–60: pause joins and show the customer state. “It works only if the owner updates it. Test one real queue before sharing. salonq.me.”

## B.20-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: empty chair, text **“They joined. Then left.”**
2–10: show a sample no-show; say “A live queue needs a way to move past this.”
10–17: owner marks no-show; next entry moves.
17–20: “Build the owner's action, not just the customer's wait screen.”

## C.25-second customer clip — owner-approved channel
0–2: phone with position, **“Join before you sit.”**
2–12: demonstrate join and position using sample data.
12–20: demonstrate leave; say “Changed your plan? Leave the queue.”
20–25: “Check arrival timing with your salon.”

Use captions and large text. Do not edit away failed updates or promise a fixed wait from illustrative service durations.
