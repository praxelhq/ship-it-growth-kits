# SalonQ — Copy pack

## 1. Owner conversation
Hi, are you the person managing the walk-in queue? I am building SalonQ nearby. Could I watch how you handle the line for five minutes at a quiet time, without recording your customers?

It lets a customer join by phone and see their position. You call next, mark served or no-show, pause new joins and add walk-ins. I want to know whether those controls are simpler than your current system.

If useful, we can set up your actual services and test a small queue before inviting real customers. I will confirm pilot terms first; the public screens do not state a price.

## 2. Warm introduction
Do you know a salon owner with a busy walk-in queue? Please ask whether they want to try a phone-based queue with three or four real entries. I do not need their customer list or a group addition. One introduction to someone who controls the queue is enough.

## 3. Owner-approved customer message
You can now check this salon's live queue from your phone. Open the salon's own SalonQ link, choose the service and join when you are ready. Keep an eye on your position; ask the salon about arrival timing. If you leave, remove yourself so the next customer is not waiting on you.

Use only after live joining works and insert that salon's actual verified queue link. The owner sends it to their existing permitted audience, not you to strangers. Do not promise an exact wait unless the product supports and verifies it.

## 5. Seven-day follow-up
You tried SalonQ last week. When did you choose to use it again without me asking, and when did you go back to the paper list? The second answer is especially useful. I am not asking you to open it just to improve a usage number.
