# SalonQ — Distribution map

**Walk the street before posting online.** Ten real salons require observing actual service flow, not viral consumer traffic.

| Priority | Route, permission and expected outcome | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Nearby salons found through [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) and a permitted street walk | Verify the shop yourself. Ask for five minutes at an owner-approved quiet time, suggested 11:00–12:00 on a weekday. Never interrupt a haircut. Ten blocks of three unique salons =30 approaches. | #1 |
| 2. Existing barber/beauty-supplier relationships | Ask two known contacts for one owner introduction each. No paid referral, contact list purchase or false customer booking. | #2 |
| 3. Salon's own customer channel | With the owner, send one message to existing opted-in customers or display a handwritten QR/link at that salon. Do not broadcast to unrelated groups. | #3 |
| 4. Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 10 and 24,09:00 IST: show the honest owner workflow and one permitted result. | #4 |

Forecast assumptions: 30 shops ×60% owner conversation ×70% real queue trial ×60% independent weekly use ≈8 customers. These rates are guesses and ambitious;10 needs stronger actual adoption or accepted introductions. Do not count a launch post's views as shops. For the two personal posts, budget 100–400 impressions and 0–2 relevant visits each as a guess; zero is possible.

No verified salon-owner community promotion slot or editorial newsletter route was established. Do not invent a “salon owners WhatsApp group.” Skip Product Hunt this month: the real bottleneck is an owner updating the queue while serving people.

Stay in one neighbourhood so you can troubleshoot on foot. Consumer distribution begins only after at least one owner's queue genuinely works.
