# SalonQ — Positioning

**Live:** https://salonq.me/ · **Category:** Salon operations · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/saloon-queue-management-system

The live headline is **“Skip the waiting room.”** The customer page says pick a salon, join by phone and watch your position. The **Salon owner** link opens /owner; **+ Register a new salon** opens name, area, 4–8-digit owner PIN and service-duration fields, ending with **Create salon**. No price was displayed on the inspected public screens. Registration and a live customer queue were not submitted/tested.

A fresh browser on 29 October 2026 showed the customer salon list stuck at “Loading...” while the owner registration form opened. That is an observed state, not proof the whole site is down. **Verify a salon can appear and accept a queue entry before asking customers to use it.**

Your first ten are owner-operated neighbourhood walk-in salons with two to four chairs, a visible peak-time queue and one person able to update it. Start on one street near campus. A premium appointment-only studio is the wrong first buyer.

**Pitch:** Let waiting customers join your salon's queue from their phone while your staff calls the next person and updates the line.
**Short version:** One live walk-in queue for your salon.

Your wedge is Saturday's waiting-room confusion: who is next, who left, and which service they need. Do not promise no waiting, guaranteed service time or more revenue. The queue only stays accurate if the owner updates it.

The named alternative is a manually shared [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/), available with a Google Account without buying Workspace, plus the existing paper/token queue. SalonQ must beat that on ease, not pretend the incumbent costs a software subscription.

**Target: 10 real salon businesses**, each with its own services/queue configured and independent use in the last seven days. Customer queue joins, demo salons and ten stylists at one location are not ten customers.
