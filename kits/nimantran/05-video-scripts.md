# Nimantran — Video scripts

Phone screen recording and your own voice only. All names/events fictional. Do not record another person's voice without consent; avoid showing real guest lists.

**A. Demo, 45 seconds; owned profile and accepted circle.** Hook 0–2s: “An invitation in your voice, not a voice clone.” Show the browser-local notice. 2–10s edit fictional wedding details. Say, “Start without an account.” 10–20s show one reviewed guest script, labelled example. 20–35s record your own short message and render using the real workflow. 35–45s download and play the file. Say, “Download before refreshing. You send it yourself.” End with demo URL. If export fails, fix it before recording a successful walkthrough.

**B. Approval hook, 20 seconds; own post.** “Would you send this script to your aunt?” Show a fictional over-formal sentence, then your actual edited warm sentence: “We'd love you to celebrate with us.” Say, “Review the words before recording them.” Show the real review screen. End: “One approved invitation before the whole list.” No claim the app automatically understands every family relationship.

**C. Honest limits, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** “This button does not send 300 WhatsApps.” Show the actual local studio. Say, “You record, download, check playback and choose where to send. Cloud batch and voice cloning aren't available.” On-screen: “Local studio today.” End on the download warning, not a fabricated sent confirmation.

**D. Two jobs, 25 seconds; own post.** “Invitation creation and guest planning are different jobs.” Shot 1: demo header. Shot 2: public planner page, with illustrative sample label. Say, “Use the studio for the file. The separate planner is for guest/travel coordination and asks for Google sign-in.” End: “Choose the job you need; don't enter a real guest list just to explore.”
