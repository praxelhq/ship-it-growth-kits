# Nimantran — Landing page fixes

Observed 29 October 2026: **“Every guest gets a personal invitation from you. Even the 300th.”** “Create an invitation” leads to a real studio: **“An invitation worth keeping.”** It visibly distinguishes local recording from unavailable voice cloning/cloud delivery. Rendering, microphone use and payment were not tested.

1. **Move today's scope beside the hero CTA.** Replacement: **“Record a wedding invitation in your own voice. Download it and send it yourself.”** Support: **“Try the no-login browser studio. AI voice cloning, cloud batch generation and WhatsApp sending are not available.”** Primary CTA: **“Try the browser studio”**. A 300-guest promise otherwise reads like current automation.
2. **Separate the two products.** Add two cards: **“Invitation studio — edit a script, record, download”** and **“Guest & Travel Planner — coordinate guest and travel details with Google sign-in.”** Link to the existing demo and [planner](https://ni-man-tran.app/planner/). Explain actual planner permissions after testing, not from a sample screenshot.
3. **Make media loss impossible to miss.** Before recording: **“Your recording stays in this tab. Download before refreshing or closing.”** Export success: **“Play your downloaded file before sending it to guests.”** Test denied microphone access, mobile recording, playback and export with test media. Do not promise device support you have not checked.
4. **Label packages and samples.** Beside ₹999 / ₹2,499 / ₹5,499 and the separate ₹1,999 planner: **“Payments are not enabled. Contact us to confirm what can currently be delivered before planning a paid order.”** Mark example families, guests and walkthroughs **“Illustrative sample data”**. A failed walkthrough video should have a working text walkthrough and explicit playback fallback.
5. **Consent, preview and mobile.** State actual storage/deletion and who can access a shared planner before real guest data is entered. No unsupported privacy guarantee. Proposed page title: **“Nimantran — record and download a personal wedding invitation”**. Use fictional names/venues in previews. Keep the local-media warning visible on a narrow screen; verify the generated file, not just a success animation.
