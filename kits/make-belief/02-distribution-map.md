# Make Belief — Distribution map

**Lead with small creative-writing/idea circles and a useful fictional-world prompt.** Do not recruit into faith-support communities with a claim that software knows someone's true religion.

All result ranges are assumptions for the actual scheduled volume, not measured benchmarks.

| Route | Rules and action | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fifteen adult creative peers | Individually ask whether they'd try one dilemma/world draft; don't request religious disclosures. | 15 × assumed 60–80% try × 60–80% finish = 5–10 completed experiments. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned writing/friend circles | Admin permission for one neutral creative exercise each, with optional product link. No copying real religious names or mocking faiths. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 15–30% start × 60–80% complete = 7–19 results/drafts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | One dilemma, three fictional rules, honest editorial labels at 18:30 IST. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% start × 60–80% finish = 4–72 completed plays. | #3 |
| 4. [r/worldbuilding](https://www.reddit.com/r/worldbuilding/) | Community existence/content verified; no current commercial permission confirmed. Read current rules and ask moderators before a product post. Use no-link original lore only if permitted. | Zero product visits in the base plan until permission exists. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after real quiz/result and publish tests; disclose sample worlds. No vote coordination. | Assume 5–20 visits and 3–12 completed plays. | #5 |

The base plan is roughly 19–113 completions before overlap; it doesn't establish 1,000 signups. A creator drafting a world is different from publishing it and attracting another real person.

## Resources and account work

[World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/) is a verified worldbuilding alternative, not an advertising channel for you. Comment on genuine fiction-design discussions with a useful question, not a religion score or product link. No newsletter or influencer has promised coverage. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional free discovery after usability, with zero forecast until observed; no direct upvote asks.

Read [your own community rules](https://makebelief.in/guidelines) before inviting creators. Fiction cannot impersonate real religious organizations; reports and moderation need to work as users arrive. Coordinate distinct circle/week choices with Ankiro, Karma Atlas and The Patterns.
