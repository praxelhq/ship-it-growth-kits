# Make Belief — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://makebelief.in/), [quiz](https://makebelief.in/quiz), [creator](https://makebelief.in/create), [Discover](https://makebelief.in/discover) and [guidelines](https://makebelief.in/guidelines) were opened 29 October. The kit did not complete a personal quiz, publish an account world or test moderation.

## 1. Put the non-identity boundary beside the action

Current H1: **“MAKEBELIEF.”** Current CTA: **“Find my religion match.”**

Replace primary action with **“Explore the values quiz.”** Add **“A reflection on selected teachings, not your religious identity or a validated assessment.”** Keep the same sentence on result/share screens. A percentage should not look like a diagnosis.

## 2. Give each path one clear job

Use two separate cards: **“Reflect privately — 15 dilemmas, no account needed”** and **“Create fiction — name a world and write three rules.”** Don't make quiz completion imply a user must join a fictional movement. Let people choose just one task.

## 3. Explain local drafts before the account step

The creator says drafts save on this device and sign-in comes at publishing. Put **“Draft here without signing in. Sign in to publish. Local drafts don't automatically follow you to another device.”** near the first field. Test save/reload/publish with your own fictional world and don't promise cloud persistence the local draft doesn't have.

## 4. Keep honest samples and zero states

Discover labels editorial starter worlds and showed zero real weekly votes at inspection. Preserve that distinction. Add **“Editorial example”** to every starter card and reserve member/vote counts for real recorded actions. “Community worlds loading” should lead to a meaningful empty/error state, not endless apparent activity.

## 5. Make the interpretation inspectable

Show the selected theme/source behind a result, what was simplified and how uncertainty was handled. Do not invent a scriptural citation or imply one score represents an entire tradition. Add **“You can disagree with this comparison.”** An interpretation should invite thought, not obedience.

## 6. Put community rules before publishing

The actual guidelines ban impersonating real religious organizations, harassment and harmful recruitment. Show a short reminder and working report route before a public world appears. Test with your own sample report; don't promise active moderation capacity you haven't arranged.

## 7. Make phone/share states discreet

Title: **“Make Belief — values reflection and fictional worlds.”** Description: **“Explore 15 dilemmas or invent a three-rule world. No religious identity assignment.”** A shared card should not reveal private quiz answers by default. Test text zoom, progress after reload and the publish handoff on a phone before launch.
