# Make Belief — Copy pack

## #1 — Personal creative invitation

If you could make one tiny fictional society, what would its three rules be?

I built Make Belief for values dilemmas and playful worldbuilding. The quiz compares selected teaching themes; it does not assign you a religion. The creation side is entirely fictional.

Would you try a draft and tell me where it feels confusing? No account is needed for the quiz, and a world draft saves on this device until you're ready to publish. https://makebelief.in/

## #2 — Permissioned circle exercise

Ten-minute prompt: a fictional world has one spare room. A quiet refuge, a workshop or a gathering space—which would you choose, and why?

Make Belief starts with questions like that, then lets you make an original fictional world with three rules. Nobody has to reveal their faith or publish a quiz result. Don't impersonate or mock a real religion.

If you'd like to try the tool after the exercise: https://makebelief.in/create

## #3 — Three clip captions

**Dilemma:** A percentage isn't your religious identity. Make Belief's quiz is a reflection on selected themes, with uncertainty welcome. Keep the result private if you prefer. https://makebelief.in/quiz

**World:** Name a fictional world. Give it three rules. Make the last rule the one your friends would debate. This is a creative game, not a real-faith replacement. https://makebelief.in/create

**Honesty:** The starter worlds are editorial examples. They don't have invented members or rankings. A new community can start at zero and still be worth making. https://makebelief.in/discover

## #4 — No-link worldbuilding contribution

My fictional society has one room everyone can use, so the conflict is what it becomes: refuge, workshop or gathering space. I would make its rules reveal the tradeoff—who gets quiet, who gets tools, and who decides when a group is too loud. The interesting lore is the disagreement, not just a list of virtues.

Use only where original lore is appropriate and follow current rules. Don't turn this into a disguised external-link post.

## #5 — Maker listing

**Tagline:** Explore your values. Invent a fictional world.

Make Belief combines a 15-dilemma reflection quiz with a short original-world creator. The quiz compares selected themes, not religious identity or a validated assessment. Local drafts work before sign-in; publishing uses an account. Editorial starter worlds are labelled, with no invented members/rankings. Community rules keep fiction distinct from real faiths.

**Maker comment:** I'd value feedback on whether people understand that boundary and can create a coherent three-rule world. Please don't share private belief details in comments.

## #6 — One consented feedback message

Did the exercise help you explain a value or make an interesting fictional rule? Or did the result feel like it was assigning you a label? An honest objection helps more than a high score. No need to send your answers.

For repeats, change the dilemma and fictional world, not the intensity of a religious claim. Don't publish a user's result or world as endorsement without permission.
