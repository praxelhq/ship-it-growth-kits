## Your first user

An adult who likes thoughtful dilemmas and playful worldbuilding, and can treat a result as a conversation starter rather than an identity assignment. Start with a fictional-world exercise: three rules for a society they'd want to inhabit. Religious comparisons are optional private reflection, not a party scoring contest.

**Pitch:** Explore what your choices value, then invent a fictional world with rules of your own.

**Bio:** Thoughtful dilemmas. Entirely fictional worlds. Your own judgment.

Your wedge is the small creative output: a world name, line to live by and three rules. The editorial worlds on [Discover](https://makebelief.in/discover) are labelled starter creations, with no invented members/rankings. Preserve that honesty rather than seeding fake followers.

[World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/pricing) has a genuine free worldbuilding tier; its Master plan is displayed at $54/year excluding applicable taxes. It is a richer writing toolkit. [16Personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/free-personality-test) offers a free personality quiz but doesn't do your same fictional-world job. Win on a short values-to-creation exercise, not a claim your comparisons scientifically establish belief.

The target remains **1,000 signups**. Count unique no-account quiz starts, with completed reflective results alongside them. Report world drafts and real publishing accounts separately; the same person doing both is not two acquired users. Don't count an editorial sample world as a real creator.
