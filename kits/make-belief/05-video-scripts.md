# Make Belief — Video scripts

Use your own fictional worlds and example choices. Don't display a real user's belief details or imitate a real religious organization.

## A. Two paths — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“This quiz doesn't tell you what religion you are.”**

2–15: Show one dilemma and the uncertainty wording. “It compares selected themes as a reflection, not a validated label.”

15–28: Open the creator, name an original world and write three rules. “The second path is entirely fictional. Make a world your friends could debate.”

28–36: Show local draft/sign-in explanation. “Draft first; sign in when you choose to publish.”

36–45: “Keep real faiths respected and fiction clearly fictional. makebelief.in.” Use as homepage/maker demo.

## B. The spare room — 20 seconds

0–2: **“One free room. Who gets it?”**

2–13: Show refuge/workshop/gathering choices. “Your reason is more interesting than winning the answer.”

13–20: “Try a dilemma, not an identity test.” URL. First clip.

## C. Three rules — 20 seconds

0–2: **“A world your friends would actually debate.”**

2–14: Type three original fictional rules, one with a real tradeoff. “The lore begins when two good intentions collide.”

14–20: “Create fiction. Don't impersonate a real faith.” URL. Second clip.

## D. Start at zero — 20 seconds

0–2: **“These aren't fake members.”**

2–12: Show editorial labels and the actual current community state. “Starter worlds are examples. Real votes and published worlds should be counted honestly.”

12–20: “Make the first real thing instead of inventing a crowd.” URL. Third clip; use current figures, not a frozen screenshot as a live claim.
