# Make Belief — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count unique genuine no-account quiz sessions, with **completed reflective results** beside them. Report publishing accounts and original published worlds separately; don't add both paths together as different people when identity is unknown.

## Day 0

Record the date, latest traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/make-belief). In your actual records log quiz starts/completions, local drafts where safely observable, publishing accounts, original community worlds, real votes and reports. The kit does not know private totals; use **not measured** rather than assumed zero. Editorial starter worlds are not user-generated traction.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only where it cannot expose belief answers or personal drafts. Use aggregate events without logging sensitive choices. A local draft on a browser is not a unique identified human; disclose that measurement limit.

## Two separate jobs

Quiz: `quiz_started → quiz_completed → result_viewed`.

Fiction: `world_draft_started → auth_completed → original_world_published → real_person_returned`.

Track community reports and moderation response as actual obligations, not engagement to celebrate. A repeated vote by the same person or a founder-created starter doesn't become a new user.

## Projection and decisions

The exact scheduled work in `02` suggests roughly 19–113 completed experiments before overlap. It is a guessed learning cohort, not a forecast of 1,000. At a hypothetical 70% quiz completion, 1,000 quiz starts produces 700 completed results. The current reach does not establish either number.
