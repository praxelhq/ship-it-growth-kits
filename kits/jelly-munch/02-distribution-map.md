# Jelly Munch — Distribution map

**A playable break and a clear touch-control clip beat a launch manifesto.** Keep your own friend circle separate from the other cohort's browser-game invitations.

| Route | Specific use / boundary | Planning result, not benchmark | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ten willing classmates | Two sets of five personal invitations, Days4/11; no compulsory group additions or public player names. | 10 invites × 60% start × 50% finish = 3 first-level completions. |#1|
| Your own YouTube channel | Six original Shorts and one full demo. Follow [YouTube policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/); no borrowed gameplay or music. | 6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits; zero is possible. |#2,videos|
| [itch.io](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started) | Free project page with original screenshots, accurate browser controls and external playable link/your own HTML5 build. Preview before making public. | 0–20 visits; listing does not ensure discovery. |#3|
| Existing campus games organizer | Ask for one optional ten-player break test in Days 10–13. Do not book the same organizer already hosting another c1 game. | 10 invited × 50% attend × 60% finish = 3 completions. |#4|
| [r/WebGames rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/WebGames/about/rules) | The endpoint resolved but rules were not extractable. Read current sidebar or ask moderators before a promotional link. |Zero until allowed. |#5|
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | A usable maker post, with demo-shop caveat. Check current slots, no reciprocal votes. |0–10 useful testers, upside only. |#6|

Neave's [creator page](https://neave.com/) is a verified example of small browser toys, useful for studying a fast first action. It is not permission to advertise on someone else's work. A permitted campus bulletin can carry #4; no external newsletter placement is assumed.

The initial plan supports tens of players. Reaching 1,000 at an assumed 50% visit→play rate needs 2,000 qualified visits; at 1% video-view→visit, that needs 200,000 relevant views. These are scenario inputs, not a promise that six Shorts achieve them. Measure whether players voluntarily send one friend a link before claiming a share loop.
