# Jelly Munch — Video scripts

Use your own screen and voice, no paid editor or borrowed soundtrack. Record real input and actual scores.

## A. One short run — 45 seconds

**Hook:** “This loss should be my fault, not the controls.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–8s|Open the first level on a phone|“Jelly Munch is a small Snake break.”|
|8–22s|A real run, fingers/swipes visible|“Eat fruit, grow, and leave room for the tail you're creating.”|
|22–32s|An honest win or crash|“Did that turn happen when I expected?”|
|32–38s|Star/goal explanation if actually reached|“A clear target makes one run enough to test.”|
|38–45s|Plain URL and demo-shop label|“Try one level. The shop is a demo, not a real purchase.”|

Post to your itch page and own channel. Do not edit a crash into a fabricated completed level.

## B. Own tail — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “I built the obstacle myself.” Show eating fruit, tail growing and a real collision, five seconds each. Say: “Every snack makes the next turn harder. That's still the joke.” End with the domain. This Short needs no claim about improving focus.

## C. Swipe versus pad — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Which one would you use?” Show ten seconds of actual swipe control and ten of on-screen pad. Say: “Same level, different controls. Tell me which felt predictable on your phone.” Use your real attempts, not a staged perfect comparison.

## D. Demo means demo — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “This shop doesn't charge you.” Show the visible demo notice, a demonstration unlock only if you choose to test it, and no fake receipt. Say: “Life packs and Plus are test interactions. No payment is collected here. I won't call these taps revenue.” If you haven't tested the unlock, show the notice only. Post as a candid maker update.
