# Jelly Munch — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the no-account policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Use distinct new player sessions for the immediately playable browser experience, clearly labelled **new player starts**. Report optional registered accounts separately. Refreshes and your tests do not count as new players.

Place **completed first levels** beside starts, then later-day returners. A skin preview, demo life pack or Plus unlock is neither activation nor revenue. If the current flow actually requires an account before play, use unique accounts instead and record that definition before outreach; do not switch mid-report to whichever number is bigger.

## Day 0

Record real existing starts/accounts, first-level completions, repeat days and last-seven-day visits from your own system; private values were not available in research. Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) receives visits without private account fields or unnecessary player input. Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/jelly-munch) reading, period and rank. A displayed sample score is not a baseline user count.

Instrument `game_started`, `level_completed`, `game_over`, `control_mode_selected`, `pause_used`, `signup_completed` and `demo_shop_used`. Keep demo events explicitly separate from any future authoritative real payment events. Broad device/control categories are enough; don't capture personal text or manufacture identities for anonymous users.

## Funnel and weekly decisions

Ten personal invitations, one optional ten-person test and six Shorts support tens of starts under `02` assumptions. At 50% visit→play, 1,000 starts require 2,000 relevant visits. At 1% view→visit, that needs 200,000 relevant views. These are planning inputs, not evidence that the calendar will reach them.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Starts, finishes, control complaints by device|If more than two of the first five testers describe unexpected turns, repair input before adding traffic.|
|Day 14|Completion share and later-day choice to return|If first runs are confusing, simplify goals; if people deliberately stop satisfied, don't call that a bug.|
|Day 21|Repeat players, progress retention and demo-shop confusion|Any belief that a demo charged real money needs immediate wording and support correction.|
|Day 30|New starts, completed levels, returners, actual reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000 and what players actually enjoyed. Demo revenue remains zero.|

Keep weekly actual rows for visits, unique starts, first-level finishes, returners, input failures, demo taps and hours. Don't sum optional accounts, local sessions and level attempts as though they were separate people.
