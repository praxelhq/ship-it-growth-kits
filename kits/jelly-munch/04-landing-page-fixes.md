# Jelly Munch — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from the rendered page. Extracted text includes multiple interface states; this does not prove every modal is visible at once. No level, sign-in or purchase was submitted. The actual current primary Play/Start label was not established in the capture; “Play the first level” below is a proposed replacement, not a quotation of a verified button.

1. **Explain the first goal before the first loss.** Current opening says “A quick, juicy break” and describes fruit/tail avoidance. Add **“Eat the level's fruit target. Avoid walls and your tail. Finish one short run.”** Primary action **“Play the first level”** should open actual gameplay, not a shop or account gate. Test a fresh phone; no completion was observed here.

2. **Show controls in context.** Current: arrow keys/WASD, swipe or pad. Put **“Swipe to turn. Prefer buttons? Use the pad.”** beside the phone play area, and **“Arrow keys or WASD”** on desktop. Show a brief reversible practice move before scoring. Do not display keyboard-only guidance on a phone.

3. **Keep simulated purchases unmistakable.** Current notices explicitly say packs add lives or unlock Plus without charging. Keep **“Demo only — no payment is collected”** above every shop action and confirmation. Replace ambiguous Buy wording with **“Try demo unlock”** until real payments are actually available. Demo balances are not receipts.

4. **State local versus account progress before sign-in.** Current page says signing out keeps progress on this device and sign-in syncs across devices. Use **“Play here now. Sign in only if you want progress on another device.”** Publish this only if that is the actual flow. Explain what happens after clearing browser storage and test a second device before claiming sync.

5. **Make star rewards understandable.** Current “Earn stars to unlock new flavours” needs the actual condition beside the level. Add **“Finish this level to earn its star; see the next skin requirement here.”** Match the real rules, not an invented reward schedule. Show your own earned star, not a seeded player result.

6. **Make a break feel finite.** Current “Touch-grass break? Your snake will wait” is a useful humane cue. Keep it at a natural stopping point with **“Save this device's progress and take a break.”** Do not add deceptive countdowns or pretend a demo life pack is scarce.

7. **Preview and mobile checks.** Title **“Jelly Munch — a tiny browser Snake break”**; description **“Eat fruit, earn stars and dodge your own tail. Keyboard or touch controls; shop unlocks are demo-only.”** Test browser zoom, safe areas and a pause/resume on a phone. A responsive-looking screenshot is not a tested control system.
