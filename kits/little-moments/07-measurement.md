# Little Moments — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, defined as unique real sender accounts created. Always report **senders who publish a gift that a recipient actually opens** beside it. Gift links, photos, messages and recipient opens are not new accounts. Exclude your test content and duplicate signups.

## Day 0 baseline

Read the current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/little-moments), noting latest reading date, traffic and rank. In actual product records log existing accounts, drafts, published gifts, recipient opens, disabled links, second gifts, premium purchases and refunds. These figures aren't known to this kit; record **not measured** until verified rather than zero.

Check public-page [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and mask all personal message/photo/contact content. Disable recording where it could expose a private editor or gift. A private recipient reading a letter should not become a marketing session replay.

## Events and definitions

`creator_started`, `signup_completed`, `gift_saved`, `gift_published`, `recipient_opened`, `gift_disabled`, `second_gift_published`, `premium_purchase_verified`.

Count recipient opening carefully: a sender preview or bot fetching a share card isn't a real recipient. Use an aggregate method consistent with the product's privacy explanation; don't promise exact unique humans if the mechanism can't distinguish them reliably. State the measurement limit.

## What the calendar can support

`02` estimates about 7–57 sender accounts before overlap from ten warm invitations, two groups, three owned clips, voluntary gift referrals and one Peerlist listing. These are assumptions, not benchmarks. At an illustrative 25% qualified-visit-to-signup rate, 1,000 accounts requires 4,000 qualified visits. At 50% publish-and-open activation that would be 500 real opened gifts. The current small-channel plan does not establish those volumes.

## Weekly decisions

| Review | Record | What you change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Starts, accounts, saves, publishes and opens from the first senders. | If most stop at signup, explain when/why the sender account is needed. |
| Day 14 | Publish → valid recipient open, phone browser and photo errors. | Fix an actual opening failure before chasing another group. |
| Day 21 | Opened gifts → optional new sender accounts, with duplicates excluded. | If recipients read but never create, don't call the sharing loop viral. |
| Day 28 | Second gifts, premium orders/refunds and disabled-link requests. | Keep the occasion/source producing intentional creation, not just preview clicks. |
| Day 30 | New accounts, activated senders, second gifts, revenue and unmet target. | Report results and next month's tested growth hypothesis. |

Use ten observed new-sender attempts to find repeated confusion, not to invent a universal conversion rate. A surprised recipient who cannot open the gift is a product problem. A sender surprised that a live link can be forwarded is a privacy-copy problem.

Save course traffic snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings aren't additive and can't substitute for accounts or gifts someone actually read.
