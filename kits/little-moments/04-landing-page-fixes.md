# Little Moments — Landing-page fixes

[Home](https://www.littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in/), [creator](https://www.littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in/create), [pricing](https://www.littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in/pricing), [example](https://www.littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in/example) and [privacy](https://www.littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in/privacy) were opened 29 October. The site is live. Saving/publishing and paid checkout were not completed by this kit.

## 1. Prove the complete free flow before posting

Current **“Create your gift”** opens the format picker. Test a free letter with a fresh sender account, open its link on another phone, edit it, then switch it off. If any required stage fails, pause traffic and fix that exact step. Do not call the site broken just because a login step wasn't tested.

Add: **“Start without an account. Sign up when you save or publish. The recipient never needs an account.”** That removes the surprise at the real conversion step.

## 2. Give the headline a concrete five-minute job

Current H1: **“Write it. Wrap it. Send the feeling.”** Keep it; replace the subhead with **“Write a personal letter or build a bouquet, then send one link they can open in WhatsApp. Free to start.”**

Primary action: **“Write a free letter.”** Secondary: **“Open a fictional example.”** A first-time sender should not have to choose between eight formats before understanding the simplest one.

## 3. Reconcile free styles with premium styles

The pricing page says free letters in every paper style, then lists premium templates/letter styles at ₹49. Say exactly which designs or animations are paid in the picker before someone writes a long message.

Replacement labels: **“Free — letter, bouquet, up to 3 photos”** and **“Optional premium design — ₹49 once.”** For ₹99 show its 20-photo allowance on the paid page, not as a free baseline. Don't hide a charge behind the final publish action.

## 4. Explain link privacy accurately

The page says private unguessable links; the policy correctly says anyone with the link can open a published gift. Replacement: **“Only share with people you choose. Anyone with the link can open or forward it; switch it off from your dashboard.”** Long links are not a promise that forwarding is impossible.

Check deletion across actual storage/reporting systems before claiming it is immediate and complete. Publish a finished, accurate data-handling explanation rather than a draft assurance the implementation has not been checked against.

## 5. Keep the sample labels

Current reactions and example are explicitly fictional. Don't remove that honesty for a conversion boost. Put **“Fictional example”** beside the preview action and add real permissioned reactions later, only with the sender and recipient comfortable about their public use.

## 6. Let a recipient finish the gift before another pitch

Add the optional referral from `03` #4 only after opening/reading. Don't require signup, a share or a premium purchase to reveal a message that the sender already published. Check this inside WhatsApp's browser and with reduced-motion enabled.

## 7. Test mobile readability and shared-card discretion

Use title **“Little Moments — a personal letter in one link”**, description **“Make a free letter or bouquet. Your recipient opens it without an app.”** Shared previews must not leak the private note or photos. Test keyboard, text zoom, upload errors and a slow network; keep the simple letter usable before loading decorative animations.
