# Little Moments — Video scripts

Use fictional words/photos or explicit sender-and-recipient permission. Never record a real private message just because it looks moving.

## A. Full journey — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“A better wrapper for one sentence only you can write.”** Show a fictional note.

2–12: Choose the free letter format; type a specific memory. **“Start with your words, not a speech about love.”**

12–22: Style and preview. **“Wrap it in a letter or bouquet. Free letters and three photos are enough for a real moment.”**

22–32: Show the actual save/signup/publish step with no personal details. **“You make the sender account when you save or publish.”**

32–40: Open the resulting test link on a second phone. **“They open it in the browser. No recipient account.”**

40–45: **“littlemomentsforyourlovedones.in — create a free letter.”** Use on the homepage and listing.

## B. The specific sentence — 15 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Not ‘you're amazing.’ Tell them why.”**

2–9: Type a fictional example: **“You saved me the window seat when I was having a bad day.”** Voice: “A detail beats a paragraph of generic compliments.”

9–15: Preview envelope opening. **“Little Moments wraps the words. You bring the memory.”** URL and free-letter label. First owned clip.

## C. No recipient app — 20 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Please don't make them download an app to read your note.”**

2–13: Screen recording of your own fictional example opening from a message in a phone browser. **“One link, tap the envelope, read the gift.”** Label fictional example.

13–20: **“The sender saves it. The recipient just opens it.”** URL. Second owned clip; show an actual test, not a mocked chat.

## D. Your link, your choice — 25 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“A personal gift needs an off switch.”**

2–12: Open your test gift's actual dashboard control and disable it. **“Switch the link off here.”**

12–20: Refresh on the recipient test phone. **“This is what a disabled gift looks like.”** Record only if the current feature works as described.

20–25: **“Anyone with a live link can forward it. Share thoughtfully.”** URL. Third owned clip; clear control is better proof than an absolute privacy slogan.
