# Little Moments — Distribution map

**Lead with sender-to-recipient sharing, supported by a real creation demonstration.** A generic startup launch has less emotional relevance than a gift someone actually wants to send.

The numbers are assumptions for the calendar, not observed conversion benchmarks. Deduplicate senders across routes.

| Route | Execution and rule | Forecast for scheduled volume | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ten warm senders | Ask individually if they have someone they want to thank this week. Don't ask them to invent an occasion or buy a premium design. | 10 × assumed 60% try × 50–80% save = 3–5 accounts; recipient opens reported separately. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned campus/friend circles | Ask the admin before one message per circle, about a real near-term occasion or just-because note. No unsolicited group additions. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–20% visit × 30–50% signup = 2–8 accounts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned Reels/Shorts | Show real creating, receiving and link-off control, at 18:30 IST. Use fictional contents or specific consent. Don't show private gifts as “user proof.” | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. Sender referrals | After a genuine recipient open, offer an unobtrusive “Make your own” link. Never auto-message the recipient or require sharing to unlock. | If 10 real opened gifts × assumed 10–30% recipients visit creation × 30–50% signup: 0–2 extra accounts. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday launch after the publish/open/revoke test. Show free versus paid precisely and check existing listing. | Assume 5–30 visits and 1–6 accounts; maker curiosity may not become gifts. | #5 |

This base plan supports roughly 7–57 sender accounts before overlap, including the optional Peerlist scenario. It does not prove a thousand. The loop has to earn repeat creation and voluntary referrals before reach is scaled.

## Where not to waste the month

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is a verified free directory option, but use it only after the actual sender flow works. No paid placement or upvote requests; forecast zero additional accounts until measured. No gifting newsletter, relationship influencer or campus society has promised coverage. Ask a real organizer you know using #6; don't put invented accounts or member counts in the plan.

Keep wedding invitations with Nimantran and elder memoirs with Dastaan. Your message is a five-minute note someone can send now. If you share an organizer, use a different week and ask permission again for that distinct offer.
