# Afterthoughts — Video scripts

Use your own deliberately non-sensitive invented night. Don't show a real diary, name, address, private conversation or inference about a person. Phone microphone and a screen recording are enough; never pretend a staged transcription was real output.

## A. Find the walk tomorrow —65 seconds

**Where:** homepage, Peerlist and organizer introduction. **Hook:** “Tomorrow, how will I find this minute?”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|A fictional entry labelled sample|Tomorrow, how will I find this minute?|
|2–14 s|Actual demo/account boundary and capture state|I'm using a non-sensitive example and the actual data rules for this mode.|
|14–28 s|Record a simple invented walk memory|A small detail is enough; it doesn't need to be a performance.|
|28–42 s|Inspect the actual transcription/source card|I check what it heard before trusting the label.|
|42–55 s|Search a word and reopen the actual entry|This is the payoff: the source comes back.|
|55–65 s|URL and privacy reminder|Inspect the demo and account terms at afterthoughts.app before recording your own night.|

If the actual capture/retrieval doesn't work, fix or show the limitation; don't animate a perfect result.

## B. A private note can stay private —20 seconds

**Where:** owned feed. **Hook:** “You don't have to post the memory.” Show an invented card and a private-kept label. Say, “A useful diary doesn't need your personal story as its advertisement.” End with a product demo link and founder disclosure, no public-share demand.

## C. Ask a question it can't answer — 30 seconds

**Where:** recall demonstration. **Hook:** “What if I never recorded that?” Ask an actual unanswerable question of the synthetic diary. Show its real response and source behaviour. Say, “I'd rather see ‘not in these entries’ than an invented memory.” If the product fabricates an answer, treat it as a defect to fix before launch, not footage to conceal.

## D. The word you remember —25 seconds

**Where:** creative-club session and social clip. **Hook:** “I won't remember the date. I'll remember the bench.” Show a fictional note, search the concrete word and retrieve the source. Say, “A small ordinary detail can be a useful handle later.” No diagnosis, mood-improvement claim or another person's real name.

Bank recording-state, transcript, keyword search, source reopening and actual data controls. Keep the browser-demo/local versus account distinction in the relevant caption.
