# Afterthoughts — Distribution map

**Lead with a privacy-clear demo and individual evening-use tests.** Don't ask people to expose diary content to acquire other users. All ranges are planning assumptions, not journaling benchmarks.

No current promotional permission in a named journaling subreddit/Discord was verified. Don't send a diary pitch under someone's distress or relationship post. Useful public conversation can focus on finding an old note, not extracting personal disclosure.

Twenty warm invitations, six owned posts, one conditional small session and uncertain directory discovery plausibly form **15–35 first retrievable-entry users**, with overlap removed. That isn't an evidenced route to 1,000 accounts. Earn voluntary recommendations after repeat use; count actual accepted referrals, not a share button or “tell a friend” request.

Keep the first month distinct from DecisionLog's forecast/outcome checks and Mimori's visual-reference library. One organizer receives one product invitation per week, and no group becomes a repository of private voice files.
