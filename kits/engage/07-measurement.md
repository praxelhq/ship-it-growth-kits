# Engage — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each tutor/cohort operator must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine paid teaching business; its own agenda/questions onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; owner name and phone privately known. Free-active operators may count. Keep identity/contact records outside this public repo.

Activation: a tutor runs its own lesson question with participants, reviews the group result and identifies whether to clarify or continue. Report this beside the business total. An attendee, demo session, poll response or meeting is not an extra business. Count one operator once even across multiple classes.

No private dashboard was accessed. Baseline totals are unknown until the owner records them.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine tutor businesses / latest independent session | Host/product records joined to private permitted owner register. |
| Own lessons/questions launched | Real operator records, excluding fictional demo questions. |
| Participant joins and response completion | Aggregate counts; no learner identity copied into marketing analysis. |
| Host-use outcome | Consented tutor description of the teaching decision, not a claimed learning-gain score. |
| Join/deletion/export failures | Status/device categories and synthetic reproductions. |
| Offer/payment status | Actual configured host offer; no invented free allowance or paid revenue. |
| Traffic/course baseline | Home analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/engage), timestamp and period. |

The [data notice](https://engage.neuronlab.in/privacy.html) says Clarity is home-only and never demo, host or meeting pages. Do not add it to those screens. Its published retention is 24 hours after end for no-account host meetings and 30 days for account meetings; host deletion is available. Measure business recurrence using only privacy-consistent host metadata and the owner's permitted register; if the records expire, say unknown rather than extending data collection silently. Avoid storing participant names, responses, notes, question text or access tokens in growth analytics.

Aggregate events: hostcreated, ownquestionlaunched, participantjoined, responsecompleted, groupresultviewed, meetingdeleted, laterindependenthostuse. A response timestamp is not attention, learning or attendance proof.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If the demo/join does not work or visibility is misunderstood, repair before broader invitations. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare owned-question setups with actual lesson use; investigate abandonment rather than recruiting attendees as customers. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Check independent recent host use and whether the result helped a teaching decision. Do not send usage reminders before measuring independence. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests against ten tutor businesses. |
| Day 30 | Report real qualifying businesses, activation, independent returns, actual offer, hours and shortfall. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two organizer asks are upside only. Replace guesses after eight approaches and again after 20. A meeting with fifty attendees is still one operator's use, not fifty customers. An assisted session alone fails the independent-use requirement.

Never claim increased retention, better grades, improved mental wellbeing or objective engagement accuracy from a single poll demonstration. Quote a tutor only with permission and state what was actually observed. The useful signal is an informed host using the tool again for a real lesson without pressure.
