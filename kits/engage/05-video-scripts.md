# Engage — Phone-demo scripts

Use a harmless adult skills-lesson question and fictional participant identity. Record both host and participant views with your own voice. Do not expose learner names, meeting links or real questions. No public video needs private class footage.

## A. One question, one teaching decision — 65 seconds

0–3 s: lesson slide with a single concept. **“Can I move on, or should I explain again?”**

3–15 s: actual host launches a two-option recall question. “I check one point, not the person's attention.” Leave the launch continuous.

15–30 s: show the participant disclosure, then a sample response. “Participants know what the host can see.”

30–45 s: inspect the actual group result and action timing. “The answer is not shown beside their name. Quiet only means no recent action inside Engage.” Verify before recording this claim.

45–55 s: return to the lesson and show another explanation you prepared. “This is the teaching decision I wanted to make.”

55–65 s: public demo/data notice and URL. “Try a harmless sample first. engage.neuronlab.in.” No invented free-plan promise.

## B. Quiet hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: empty reaction view. **“Quiet does not mean not listening.”**

3–12 s: a participant chooses to answer a simple recall check. “Ask a useful question. Inspect the response, not a guessed attention score.”

12–20 s: disclosure and URL. “Engage records its own interactions, not meeting audio or video.” Show current policy; no surveillance montage.

## C. Visibility hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: participant join notice. **“What can the host see?”**

3–15 s: actual named activity view beside grouped poll result. “Names and participation timing are visible. Poll answers are grouped. Questions are named.”

15–25 s: privacy link and lesson view. “Explain that before the class joins. Then test one question.” End readable URL.

Capture fresh demo state, expired-demo handling, participant disclosure, real launch/answer/group result, host activity limits and deletion route. If a fresh demo fails, fix or label the failure; never edit an ended session into a successful interaction. Label shortened waits.
