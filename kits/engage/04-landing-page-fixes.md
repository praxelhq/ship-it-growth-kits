# Engage — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://engage.neuronlab.in/), rendered controls, [/try](https://engage.neuronlab.in/try) and [privacy](https://engage.neuronlab.in/privacy.html) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“See who has gone quiet in your meeting, while it is still running.”** CTAs: **“Try the live demo”** and **“Start a meeting.”** No public price/allowance found. No real host account, meeting or poll was created in research.

## 1. Put the quiet-versus-attention boundary in the hero

Current headline centers names of quiet people; the privacy notice has the essential limitation.

Replacement: **“Ask a quick check before your lesson moves on.”** Support: **“See participation inside Engage. Quiet activity is not evidence of inattention.”**

Retain the useful live-participation view without implying surveillance or an objective attention score.

## 2. Show participant visibility before joining

Current privacy says named activity timing and public named questions, but anonymous-to-name group answers.

Replacement join note: **“The host sees your name and participation timing. Poll answers are grouped; questions are named to the room. Read the full data summary before joining.”**

Verify this actual behavior with two devices, including a recall-check “Missed that” answer. Do not claim anonymity for all interactions.

## 3. Make the two routes clear

Current home offers a Meet side panel and a browser form for other tools.

Replacement route labels: **“In Meet: open Activities and choose Engage, when available to your account.”** / **“Alongside another meeting: create a session and share its link.”**

Check actual Meet listing, host/participant permissions and account availability before describing the integration as universal. Platform trademarks are not partnerships.

## 4. Explain an ended demo without implying product failure

Current `/try` describes a throwaway host/participant meeting and can show “The demo meeting has ended.”

Replacement state: **“This temporary demo has ended. Open a fresh demo to try the host and participant views again.”** Provide a real restart control before advertising it.

Test fresh entry, expiration and refresh; do not fake a live host response or call a stale throwaway session a dead site.

## 5. State the current offer

Current public entry shows no price or usage limits.

Replacement offer block: **“Before creating a session, review the current host offer and any limits.”** Replace with the real free/paid allowance only after verifying it. Do not invent unlimited meetings because the public creation form exists.

## 6. Keep data retention understandable

Current policy says no-account host meetings delete 24 hours after end, host-account meetings retain 30 days, host deletion available, attendee notes local. CSV contains group results and question text, no names.

Replacement summary: **“Meeting records have a retention limit. Personal notes stay in this browser. Review export and deletion before using confidential material.”**

Test actual deletion and export, including whether named question text is inadvertently identifying. Never move meeting screens into session replay; the policy says Clarity may appear on home only, not demo/host/meeting pages. Check presenting-mode behavior when sharing an entire screen.
