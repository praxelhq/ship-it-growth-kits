Checked 29 October 2026 against [engage.neuronlab.in](https://engage.neuronlab.in/), its rendered controls and [data notice](https://engage.neuronlab.in/privacy.html). Actual headline: **“See who has gone quiet in your meeting, while it is still running.”** CTAs: **“Try the live demo”** to `/try` and **“Start a meeting”** to the creation form. **“Host sign in”** is separate. No public price or free-plan limit was shown; do not invent a subscription or a free-forever offer.

## The first paying business to serve

An independent tutor running an adult paid online skill cohort in Google Meet. A session has a short explanation followed by a poll or recall question. The tutor wants to know when to pause and clarify, without treating quiet learners as inattentive.

**Pitch: Put one quick recall check inside the lesson, then decide whether the room needs another explanation.**

Short version: **Ask, inspect the response, adjust the lesson.**

This is B2B because the tutor runs a paid teaching business. It is distinct from Abhyava's preparation/home practice and ChoreoStudio's dance-studio operations. Do not recruit a broad student audience to manufacture tutor customers.

## What the public product says

The page offers Google Meet side-panel use and a separate link beside Zoom, Teams and Zoho. The rendered home has meeting title, platform, optional expected attendance and agenda, plus Create meeting and Join with a code. No real meeting or attendee data was submitted. The `/try` description says a real throwaway meeting with a fictional participant, no signup/Meet call, deleted after 30 minutes; the fetched state included an ended-demo message. That is not proof a fresh demo or real meeting is broken. Verify a fresh session before a public walkthrough.

The [privacy summary](https://engage.neuronlab.in/privacy.html) says the host sees names, action times, whether someone responded and public questions, but not poll answers attached to a name. Questions are named to the host and room. Personal notes/action items stay in the browser. It says no audio, video, screen, transcript, face/voice or attention analysis. These are published disclosures, not an independent security audit.

Crucially, Engage cannot see listening, speaking or another window. **Quiet in Engage does not mean not paying attention.** Never use the host view as attendance proof, a grading score, an employee-performance rating or a diagnosis. Give participants the disclosure before asking them to join.

## The manual alternative

A tutor can ask a numbered question in its existing meeting chat and record group comprehension in a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) log. That loses some structured interaction but costs no extra tool purchase. Engage must earn adoption through a clearer check-and-adjust loop, not a blanket claim that every poll/Q&A tool lacks recall checks.

Prove one adult-consented two-device sample: launch a harmless question, answer it, inspect the host view and show exactly which named details appear. Test a missed response, presenting mode and deletion. Do not make health, learning-gain or engagement-uplift claims from a product demo.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each must be an operating paid tutor/cohort business, onboard its own agenda/questions, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Attendees, demo participants and meeting counts do not become extra businesses.
