## Specific sources and newsletters

[Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) is a real builder feed. Use one transparent demo and answer questions about import, resume and the trial. [Instapaper's comparison page](https://www.instapaper.com/premium) is a real alternative source, not a place to spam your product.

No reading subreddit/Discord promotion rules or newsletter editor route were verified for this product. Do not invent a book club or publisher partnership. If an actual organizer has an opted-in bulletin, ask permission for#2 and count no reach before publication. A reading-resource discussion is useful even when it permits no product links.

## Honest composition

Three sessions, twenty invitations, eight clips and one listing model **25–117 accounts before overlap**, after the essential route gate. That is not sufficient evidence for 1,000. At assumed 25% visit-to-account, the target needs 4,000 qualified unique visitors. Replace the assumptions after actual data.

Wider distribution should follow someone finishing/resuming a real piece and voluntarily introducing the exercise to another circle. Don't share a full copyrighted article as the acquisition asset or count every sample-card view as a reader.

Use Days 17–20 for the reading-completion exercise, distinct from Kept's personal collection and Boomerang's source retrieval. Separate actual contact lists and seven days between primary invitations to the same organizer. Keep a broad launch off the calendar while direct login remains 404.
