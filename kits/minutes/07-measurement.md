# Minutes — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Report **unique people importing permitted material and completing a first real reading moment**, plus **independent later resumers**, beside accounts. [Target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md).

Count a new usable individual account once. Exclude tests, bots, repeated logins, marketing-preview taps and trial-page views. Activation is actual import plus first completed reading moment. Retention is a later-day return to the saved place in the actual piece. Swiping sample cards or playing a demo voice is not a completed reading session.

## Day 0 baseline and gate

[Public profile](https://shipped.money/p/minutes), checked 29 October, showed latest saved Clarity 6 sessions/24 h for 28 October, sync 16:01 UTC. Four shipping-active days and $0 net revenue for 21–27 October; zero settled search clicks for 17–23 October. No rank was visible. These are period readings, not lifetime totals.

Home 200/direct login and trust routes 404 were independently checked. Private accounts/imports/reads/resumes were not supplied; unknown is not zero. Record actual totals after fixing the route gate. Verify existing Clarity once and mask reading text, notes, email and imported documents. Use aggregate events, not source contents.

Events: `account_created`, `import_started`, `import_succeeded`, `import_failed`, `first_moment_completed`, `position_saved`, `later_day_resumed`, `piece_completed`, `trial_ended`, `payment_succeeded`, `refund_recorded`. A save-position event must match actual later recovery; it is not proof by itself. Record source format and coarse campaign tags without private titles/content.

## Forecast and decisions

The actual plan schedules three sessions, twenty invitations, eight clips and one listing, after entry/product gates.02 models **25–117 accounts before overlap**, not enough evidence for 1,000. At assumed 25% visit-to-account, you need 4,000 qualified visitors. Replace assumptions with actual rates; preview traffic does not close the gap.

| Day | Account milestone | Paired decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 50 | Direct routes and three real import/read tests pass; otherwise repair before promotion. |
| 14 | 150 | Independent later resume and preserved text structure. |
| 21 | 500 | Actual sessions/permissions and voluntary introductions; reforecast shortfall if expansion is absent. |
| 28 | 850 | Later readers, trial-end behaviour, failed imports and support. |
| 30 | 1,000 | Accounts, first readers, resumers and actual paid/refund outcomes together. Report a miss plainly. |

Milestones are decisions, not predictions. Weekly rows: qualified visitors, new usable accounts, successful imports, first readers, later resumers, failed imports/position recovery, trial ends and hours. Show numerators/denominators.

Visits without a usable login are a route failure, not bad content. Accounts without imports point to unclear permissions/value. Imports without reading may mean broken extraction or too much setup. First reads without returns challenge the core job; inspect saved-position recovery before adding reminders. Do not claim improved comprehension, attention or reading speed from completion counters alone.
