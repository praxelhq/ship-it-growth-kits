## Your first person

An adult student or early-career reader with one long piece they genuinely want to finish, but who repeatedly saves it for a mythical free afternoon. They have five minutes over tea or between tasks. They do not need another content feed or an AI summary pretending to be reading.

Start with a permitted article or their own essay, not confidential employer PDFs or a commercial book they cannot lawfully upload. Your job is helping them continue the actual text, not promising better comprehension or cognitive benefits from an unvalidated segmentation claim.

## Pitch

> Bring one piece you want to read, and continue it in the small gaps your day actually has.

Short version:

> Your reading pile, one small pause at a time.

The wedge is import one/read one/return once: bring a source, read a first moment, leave, then resume at the saved position later. Activation is a real imported piece plus its first completed reading moment. Retention is a later-day resume, not flicking through the sample cards.

## The weakness

The page is elaborate enough to demonstrate a reading experience without proving the actual product path works. The sample excerpt, 38% progress and audio-ready state are not user evidence. Show a real empty-library import and a later resume after the route repair.

Keep **1,000 signups** as the cohort target, with first readers and independent resumers beside it. A marketing-preview interaction or waitlist email is not a usable reader account.
