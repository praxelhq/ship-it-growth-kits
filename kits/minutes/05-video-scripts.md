# Minutes — Video scripts

Record only after the entry/import path works. Use your own essay or permitted source, readable browser/phone recording and voice. No full copyrighted article dump, fake completed book or cognitive-effect claim.

## A. Import one, read one, resume once—60 seconds

**Where:**owned accounts/reading sessions.** Hook:**“Not another reading pile.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | One actual permitted unread piece. | “Not another reading pile. I want to continue this one.” |
| 8–22 s | Actual empty library and successful import. | “I bring my own material. The marketing sample is not my library.” |
| 22–35 s | Read a real short section with heading/context intact. | “A small pause is enough to start. I check that the section still makes sense.” |
| 35–48 s | Real later resume, date visible. | “Later, I return to my place. This is the useful test.” |
| 48–60 s | URL/three-day trial. | “Minutes. Your reading, in small pauses. Three-day trial, no card.” |

If later return hasn't happened, label it an intended demonstration rather than a result.

## B. Your library starts empty—20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“This is not a feed.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Actual empty library. | “This is not a feed. It starts empty.” |
| 6–14 s | Import your own permitted piece. | “I choose what I wanted to read, not what an algorithm wants me to scroll.” |
| 14–20 s | First moment/URL. | “One piece is enough for the test.” |

## C. Keep the idea intact—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“Shorter should not mean broken.”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Original heading and short permitted passage. | “Shorter should not mean broken.” |
| 7–18 s | Actual moment boundary and original context. | “I check whether the boundary preserves the idea. If it doesn't, that is an import problem to fix.” |
| 18–25 s | Source/URL. | “A reading tool should keep a way back to the actual text.” |

## D. The later pause—25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:**“Where did I leave off?”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Real later date and reopen. | “Where did I leave off?” |
| 7–18 s | Actual saved position and continued reading. | “This is what I need the product to remember. Not a claim I understood everything.” |
| 18–25 s | Trial/URL. | “Continue the piece. One small pause at a time.” |

Eight clips are scheduled; each needs a real import, boundary or return lesson. Keep another person's text/library private unless they explicitly permit its use.
