# Kindred — Distribution map

**Begin with adults who already like reflective journaling.** Do not recruit through fear, romantic certainty or a claim that a card can diagnose their life.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult journalers | Ask about the signup/sample flow, not their private entry. Explain actual price first once known. | 10 trials, 5–10 first reflections if onboarding works. | #1 |
| Two existing journaling/book-club groups | Admin-approved invitation, clear reflection-not-prediction wording. | 2 × 30 readers × 20–40% visits × 50% signup = 6–12 accounts. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | Fictional prompt/entry, privacy explanation and short return ritual on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 2–48 accounts. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Product-design story about leaving interpretation to the reader, Days 11/24. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 1–14 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited voluntary journal session | Existing club organizer, 20 minutes, fictional sample; no sharing of entries. | 8–15 attendees if accepted, 3–8 accounts assumed; zero without acceptance. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker feedback after a usable sample and actual offer are public; check current rules/slot. | 0–20 accounts, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No unverified spiritual subreddit, Discord or newsletter is included. Competitor communities are not an invitation to promote. If a club newsletter agrees to a free mention, count only the observed result. Paid astrologers/influencers and purchases of reach are out of budget.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 requires about 3,334 distinct relevant visits. These tests do not promise that. The first decision is whether a willing user understands the offer and returns without a fear-based notification.
