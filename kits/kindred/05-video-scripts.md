# Kindred — Video scripts

Use fictional mood/entry data and an adult tester who wants to participate. No prediction performance, distressed-user scene or private diary screenshot.

## A. First-reflection demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“A prompt doesn't get to decide what happens next.”**

2–12s, show actual entry/offer. “Here is what Kindred currently includes before you register.” Do not invent a free plan.

12–25s, show one symbolic prompt. “Read it as a question, not a prediction.”

25–38s, type a fictional reflection and save. “The useful part is what you choose to write.”

38–50s, reopen/delete the example and show privacy link. “Understand where entries live before adding personal details. kindreds.social.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “You can disagree with a prompt.”

2–10s: read a card, then write a different interpretation in a fictional journal. “It's a place to reflect, not an instruction.”

10–15s: product URL and **Symbolic reflection, not prediction**.

## C. Small ritual — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Not another twenty-minute routine.”

2–12s: actual first prompt and one short fictional entry. “Notice a mood. Read. Write one thing. Stop when you're done.”

12–20s: close the app. “Your reflection doesn't need an audience.” Don't promise a measured time saving you haven't tested.

## D. Privacy explainer — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Private needs an explanation.”

2–15s: show the actual storage/deletion policy and controls after verification. “This is where the entry goes and how to remove it.”

15–25s: delete a fictional example. “Don't use a real diary entry to demonstrate a diary.” If controls are missing, fix them before recording this claim.
