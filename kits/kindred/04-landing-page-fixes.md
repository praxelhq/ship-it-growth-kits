# Kindred — Landing page fixes

Based on [kindreds.social](https://kindreds.social/), checked 29 October. The public entry screen was read; signed-in readings and journal storage were not tested.

## 1. Show the actual offer before signup

The fetched screen shows **“Sign up”** and **“Log in”**, but no price/free allowance. Add the current price, trial limits and payment boundary before asking for an account. If a free preview exists, say exactly what it includes. Do not add “free” merely to improve conversion.

## 2. Explain the job in the heading

Current title: **“Kindred.”** Support: **“A short daily space to reflect with tarot and astrology, notice your mood, and journal privately.”**

Suggested heading: **“A daily prompt for your own reflection.”** Support: **“Notice your mood, explore a symbolic prompt and write what it brings up for you.”** CTA: **“See a sample reflection”**, only after an actual sample exists. Keep **“Not a prediction or professional care”** beside it.

## 3. Provide a fictional sample without collecting private input

Let a visitor see one prompt and a clearly fictional journal response before creating an account. Label **“Example entry, not a real user's private journal.”** Show how the person can disagree with or skip a prompt. Do not stage a seemingly accurate personal reading to manufacture trust.

## 4. Make privacy concrete

“Journal privately” needs an explanation: where entries are stored, what providers process them, how to delete/export and who can see them. Publish the actual policy. Do not claim device-only storage or end-to-end encryption unless verified. Suggested entry note: **“Write only what you are comfortable storing under the privacy policy.”**

## 5. Test the whole first-entry path

Fresh account → actual offer → first prompt → own entry → save → reopen → delete. Test a network failure and sign-out. If saving or access fails, stop promotion. A Sign up button in a rendered page does not prove the journal works.

## 6. Check a calm phone experience

At 390px, check keyboard overlap, long entries, low-contrast text, reduced motion and a clear way to skip. Preview title: **“Kindred — symbolic prompts for private reflection.”** No prediction or clinical outcome in the title or image. Never expose a real user's birth details, mood or journal in a screenshot.
