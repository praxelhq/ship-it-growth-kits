# Before — Positioning

Checked 29 October 2026 against [trybefore.me](https://trybefore.me/). The actual headline is **“Find out if people will actually buy it, before you make 5,000 of them.”** The visible CTAs include **“Start free”** and **“Start a test”**, linking to `/login?mode=signup`. These are observed destinations; account creation, test publication, payment and response collection were not exercised.

The strongest sentence is in your FAQ: people say yes more often than they pay. Preserve it. Before collects design preferences, stated buying intent, a price ladder and objections. Its Build/Modify/Retest/Drop verdict is a research aid, not a purchase order or a probability that inventory will sell.

## The first business

An operating small stationery brand choosing between two notebook-cover concepts for its next batch. It already has real customers it can ask, permission to contact them, and a decision on quantity and timing. This excludes students with an imagined brand and founders expecting Before to supply a ready audience.

Your pitch: **Compare two product concepts with people who might actually buy them, then decide what to test next.**

Short version: **Ask before you order the next batch.**

The wedge is an owner publishing its own two notebook concepts, recruiting relevant respondents and reading objections before ordering. Keep the product, audience and one changed variable comparable. If one image shows a finished product and the other a rough sketch, the result may measure presentation quality.

## The offer today

Curious is $0: one live test, two concepts, unlimited responses and the full report. Serious is $9/month: five live tests, four concepts and CSV export. Obsessed is $29/month: 25 live tests and four concepts. These are the public prices, not a verified checkout or a tax-inclusive promise. Link [current pricing](https://trybefore.me/pricing) before purchase.

The [FAQ](https://trybefore.me/) says the owner brings respondents. Its brand-new tester network starts empty. Respondents need no account according to the page. A verdict requires at least 20 responses; the product recommends 30–50 for a steadier reading. Neither a response threshold nor a displayed margin of error repairs biased convenience sampling. Do not imply a campus-friend sample represents all notebook buyers.

## The real alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) comparison plus direct customer interviews can record concept choices, objections and willingness to pay. Before needs to earn adoption through a simpler respondent flow and a clearer reviewed report, rather than claiming research cannot happen without software. Interview actual recent buyers about their last purchase before relying on hypothetical intent.

Prove the anonymous response route, the response denominator and the first report with a clearly labelled internal example. Then help an owner publish its own test. Do not fabricate twenty respondents to unlock a verdict, promise free respondent acquisition, or call screenshots of fictional concepts customer traction.

**The target stays 10 real business customers**, under [the target policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each must onboard its own business data, use independently in the last seven days, and be privately contactable by owner name and phone. Twenty survey respondents are research inputs for one brand, not twenty business customers.
