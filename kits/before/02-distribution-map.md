# Before — Distribution map

Lead with a batch decision and an existing customer audience. “Validate your idea” attracts people expecting certainty or respondents you do not have.

| Route | Execution and permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing stationery and gifting-brand relationships | Manually qualify an operating brand, two comparable concepts, a batch decision within a month and access to relevant consenting buyers. Each owner gets one personal invitation. | Ten blocks of five businesses = 50 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Packaging/print supplier introductions | Ask an existing supplier contact to forward an invitation to a brand that requested concept feedback. No customer-list disclosure or fake buying enquiry. | Two introduction requests, Days 8 and 11; #4. No assumed extra customers. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Demonstrate one comparison and why intent is not an order. Days 9, 18 and 26 at 09:00 IST are test times, not universal best times. | Three posts; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits per post; not counted in the base. |
| One owner-led review | Help one willing brand check recruitment and interpret objections privately. Other brands can inspect a fictional example; never expose respondents or confidential concepts. | Day 12, conditional; #5. Support for existing trial, not additional reach. |

Planning guesses: 50 approaches × 40% substantive conversations × 70% own-test publication × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, approximately 10 businesses. At 20% conversation the result is about 5. These are demanding assumptions, not measured conversion benchmarks. Lack of an audience can break the second step even when the owner likes the software. Reforecast after the first ten approaches.

The ten outreach blocks are Days 4, 5, 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, 22, 24 and 27. Three posts and two supplier asks are upside only, deduplicated against those businesses. Do not quietly add event attendees or tester registrations to the forecast.

Reserve Days 8–11 for this existing-customer-list founder wedge. NXT has technical complaint research, BrandAid agency briefs, PitchPanel the Days 10–13 founder clinic and ShelfTest pre-product founders. Before should not ask that same clinic to supply twenty respondents per brand. Consult [shared research](../../shared/a2-founder-research.md) and keep one primary invitation per organizer per week.

[Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) and [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) are organizer-discovery entry points, not approved mailing lists or confirmed workshops. No newsletter acceptance, subreddit promotion permission or paid listing is established. Zero-budget outreach means own accounts and consented relationships, not automated DMs or scraped customer lists. Stop after a decline; one follow-up after seven days is enough when relevant.
