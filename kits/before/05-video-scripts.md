# Before — Phone-demo scripts

Use your voice, phone microphone and screen recording. All designs and answers in public footage are fictional and labelled. Do not expose an unpublished brand concept or respondent identity. No finished media is required to use these scripts.

## A. Continuous demo — 65 seconds, owner introduction

0–3 s: two fictional notebook covers. **“Which design deserves the next batch?”**

3–15 s: create a real two-concept test using the same product, pack size and proposed price. “I keep the alternatives comparable. These are sample covers.”

15–30 s: open the actual respondent link on a phone, without an account. “The owner brings relevant customers. Before does not supply a ready-made panel.” Leave one response action uncut.

30–45 s: show the real count and threshold. “A verdict needs twenty responses. These demonstration responses are samples, not customer evidence.” If the report is unavailable, show its incomplete state; do not manufacture one.

45–57 s: inspect one objection and the price denominator in a properly labelled internal report. “The useful question is what to change and test next.”

57–65 s: price and URL. “One two-concept test is free. Intent is not an order. trybefore.me.”

## B. Problem hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: sample cover beside a production quantity. **“A compliment cannot buy your inventory.”**

3–12 s: show one design choice and one objection field. “Ask the target buyer what stops them, before committing to the batch.”

12–20 s: free allowance and URL. “Bring your own audience. One test, two concepts free. Before.” Do not imply the displayed objection came from a customer.

## C. Audience hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: blank respondent count. **“Where will your twenty answers come from?”**

3–15 s: owner writes a recruitment plan using anonymous categories, not real contacts. “Recent buyers and people who fit the product beat twenty friends being polite.”

15–25 s: actual share route and page FAQ. “The test organizes answers. You still own recruitment and the next real purchase experiment.” End with readable URL.

Capture: two comparable concepts, free allowance, publishing destination, respondent phone route, incomplete count, a labelled internal report only if verified, pricing and audience FAQ. If generation or loading is shortened, label the cut. A fictional report must never be presented as a customer result.
