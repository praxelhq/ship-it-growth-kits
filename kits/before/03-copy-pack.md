# Before — Copy pack

## 1. Brand-owner opener

Hi — are you choosing between two notebook designs before your next production batch?

I build Before. You put your concepts and proposed prices into one short test, then send the link to customers you already have permission to contact. It collects choices and objections before you order stock.

I am looking for an operating brand with a real decision this month. We do not supply a ready-made tester panel. Would a two-concept example be useful?

## 2. Trial email

**Subject: Two designs, one upcoming batch decision**

Before lets you compare two notebook concepts with your own audience. Respondents open one link without an account. You can review design preference, stated intent, price choices and objections together.

The free Curious plan supports one live test and two concepts, with unlimited responses and the full report. The verdict needs at least 20 responses; 30–50 gives more to inspect. Please choose people who fit the intended buyer, rather than asking friends for compliments.

Your respondents are not preorders. The useful next step is a change, another interview or a small real offer you can fulfil. Start here: https://trybefore.me/

If the decision is not relevant, say no and I will stop. No need to send me confidential design files.

## 3. LinkedIn post

“Looks nice” is an easy answer. Buying a notebook at the proposed price is a different decision.

Before puts two concepts in front of the audience a brand can already reach. Which do they choose? What price do they select? What would stop them buying?

The important part is reading the objections before placing the next batch order. A verdict is a direction for the next test, not proof that inventory will sell.

One live two-concept test is free. Bring your own relevant respondents: https://trybefore.me/

For a demonstration, I use fictional cover concepts and clearly label every response as a sample. Real customer results deserve their actual denominator and limitations.

## 4. Supplier introduction ask

Do you work with a stationery brand deciding between two designs for a real upcoming run? If they have asked for a way to collect customer feedback, would you forward this invitation? Please do not share their contact or concept files without permission. Before supplies the test flow; the brand brings its own respondents.

## 5. Report-review invitation

Would you like to review the first test together? We can check who answered, where they came from, which objections repeat and whether the alternatives were comparable. I will not treat twenty answers as twenty buyers. You keep control of the decision and of any follow-up to respondents.

## 6. Consented follow-up

Did you publish your own test and share it with an appropriate audience? What blocked the next step: setting the concepts, finding respondents, completing the form or understanding the report? A count and description are enough; please do not send respondent names or private answers into a public thread.

Send one relevant follow-up after seven days only if appropriate. Never promise an audience, an incentive or a discount that has not been authorized.
