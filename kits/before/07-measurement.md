# Before — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, unchanged under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). All four tests are required: operating business; its own concept/product data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; owner name and phone privately known with permission. Paying is not required. Keep identifying details outside this public repository.

Activation: an owner publishes its own comparable two-concept test and receives a genuine relevant response. Report separately the number reaching 20 responses, owners reviewing the report and businesses returning independently. Activation alone does not satisfy all customer tests. One brand with 50 respondents is one business, never 50 customers.

No private dashboard was accessed. The actual baseline is unknown until recorded.

| Day 0 measure | Source and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified businesses total / active last 7 days | Product records joined to private permitted owner register; exclude founder/test accounts. |
| Owned tests published | Actual publication records, not a draft or sample. |
| Genuine respondents and completion fraction | Test status and denominator; exclude internal examples, duplicates and known abuse. |
| Reports at 20+ responses | Report state; disclose recruitment source and bias. |
| Owner report review / independent return | Status events and timestamps; separate assisted sessions from independent visits. |
| Payments/refunds | Actual billing records; no assumed paid conversions. |
| Traffic and course position | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/before); timestamp/period, unknown is not zero. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate tag. Mask/exclude unpublished concepts, free-text responses, respondent identity, owner contacts and payment screens. Use aggregate events: accountcreated, ownedtestpublished, respondentstarted, responsecompleted, reportopened, laterowneruse. No raw respondent content is needed for acquisition measurement.

| Checkpoint | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Ten approaches scheduled. Count conversations and real owned tests; observe one setup. If owners expect supplied testers, fix 04 and the opener before more outreach. |
| Day 14 | 25 approaches scheduled. Compare owner-publication and relevant-response completion. If tests stall below 20, diagnose recruitment; do not invent answers or call an incomplete report a success. |
| Day 21 | 35 approaches scheduled. Inspect independent recent use and report comprehension. If owners read once and never return, ask whether the batch decision is finished or the product is unhelpful; do not force artificial weekly testing. |
| Day 28 | 50 approaches scheduled. Audit all four customer tests. A report viewed only during your call fails the independent-use test. |
| Day 30 | Report actual qualifying businesses against 10, activation, response thresholds, recent use, revenue and hours. Explain the shortfall honestly. |

The base guess is 50 × 40% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 qualifying businesses. At 20% conversation it becomes 4.9. No newsletter, workshop or respondent network is counted. Replace the assumptions after ten approaches and again after 25. The most vulnerable assumption is that a real business has both relevant respondents and a reason to return independently in the final week. A completed one-off test may be useful yet fail the recent-use target; record that distinction rather than messaging everyone to manufacture use.

A survey's purchase-intent score is not revenue, an order, product-market fit or a verified market-size estimate. Track any later small-batch/preorder experiment separately under terms the owner can fulfil. Public learning can report aggregate counts and limitations; quote an owner only with permission.
