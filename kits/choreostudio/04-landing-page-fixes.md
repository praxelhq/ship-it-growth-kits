**Current:** “Payment claims” explains that dancers report payment and the owner confirms against bank/UPI statements.

**Write near every claim action:** **“Claim submitted—not confirmed paid. Your studio checks the actual payment.”** Don't let a reassuring badge turn a claim into verified receipt. Test partial payment, duplicate claim and excess-credit confirmation with controlled records before a real pilot.

**Current headline:** “Less chasing. More dancing.” CTA: “Start free trial,” linked to registration mode.

**Keep the headline; write supporting copy:** **“Start with this month's fee list. Review who owes, send your own reminder and confirm what lands.”** Button: **“Set up my studio's free month.”** Test fresh signup → five controlled dancers → due list → manual reminder → owner confirmation before more outreach. Repair any failed required step first.

## 3. Separate actual messages from planned automation

**Current:** automated WhatsApp appears as coming soon on Studio+ while FAQs say nothing sends automatically today.

**Write on both plans:** **“Available now: reviewed reminders you send yourself. Automated WhatsApp is planned, not included as a live feature.”** Don't let the higher-tier price imply today's automation is working. Keep the actual >50-dancer capacity reason clear.

## 4. Make pricing and payment flow plain

**Current:** free first month, ₹249 up to 50 dancers and ₹479 above 50; dancer payments aren't processed.

**Write beside upgrade:** **“This subscription pays for your studio software. Dancers still pay you directly.”** Show the actual full charge, renewal and cancellation terms before purchase. Verify any taxes in checkout rather than inventing them. Preserve no-charge-until-plan-choice wording only while the real flow matches.

**Write:** **“Illustrative month. Your result depends on your records and follow-up.”** For early spots, show only a real remaining count or omit it. A prospective owner should not mistake sample collection or a static counter for customer proof.
