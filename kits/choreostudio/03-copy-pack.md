ChoreoStudio keeps that distinction: a dancer can submit a claim through a private link, and the owner confirms it. Reminders are drafts that open in the studio's own WhatsApp; nothing is sent automatically today.

The first test is whether a real owner can maintain one month's list without relying on me. The demo's perfect paid total isn't a customer result.
