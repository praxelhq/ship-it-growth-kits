# ChoreoStudio — Measurement

**Target: 10 real dance-studio businesses.** Activation beside it: **studios independently maintaining the month's actual fee/claim/reminder workflow**. Dancer accounts, row counts and sample fee totals are not business customers.

## Day 0

Record actual studio businesses, permitted dancer records, reviewed reminders, confirmed payments, partial-balance corrections and independent return use from your own records. The calculator and perfect paid demo establish no baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent with private student/fee fields masked. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/choreostudio) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero.

Events: `studio_onboarded`, `fee_list_reviewed`, `reminder_draft_opened`, `payment_claim_received`, `owner_payment_confirmed` and `studio_returned`. A claim isn't confirmed payment; a WhatsApp draft opening isn't a sent message. Software subscription receipts are distinct from dancers' money, which ChoreoStudio doesn't process.

## Four tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), a legitimate studio has its own permitted real data onboarded, uses the product independently in the final seven days without your prompt, and has a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required during the free first month. Fifty dancers or several staff at one studio are one customer. Your manually maintained demo doesn't count.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 30% owner conversation × 60% trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3–4 studios. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–6 is below ten.** These are planning assumptions, not measured studio-software rates. At 20 approaches inspect actual owner fit, setup and balance correctness before expanding.

Review the first ten permitted claim/partial-payment transitions against the owner's actual record. A wrong paid status is more important than a launch spike. Don't advertise money recovered or time saved until you have actual before/after evidence and the owner's permission.
