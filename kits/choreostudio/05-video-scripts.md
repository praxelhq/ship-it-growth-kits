# ChoreoStudio — Video scripts

Synthetic dancer records only. No real fee list, phone number or bank entry in a public video. Show actual supported controls and label demonstrations. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · One month's fee loop · 60 seconds · owner walkthrough

0–2s hook: **“Who still owes this month?”** Show a synthetic due list. 2–15s: add/check five controlled dancers and batch fees: “Start with the actual studio record, not a fake recovered-revenue number.” 15–28s: review a reminder and open the draft without sending to a real person: “Your studio reviews and sends from its own WhatsApp.” 28–43s: submit a synthetic payment claim: “This is a claim, not confirmed receipt.” 43–55s: show owner confirmation/partial balance: “Check the real bank record before marking paid.” 55–60s: “First month free. ChoreoStudio dot live.”

## B · Automation isn't here yet · 20 seconds · objection reply

Hook: **“Does it send WhatsApp automatically?”** 0–8s: show the current coming-soon label. 8–15s: “Today it opens a reviewed reminder and you press Send.” 15–20s: “Choose the tool for what it does now, not a roadmap promise.” No provider-approved automation claim.

## C · A part-payment stays partial · 25 seconds · owner onboarding

Hook 0–2s: **“Half paid shouldn't become paid.”** 2–12s: enter a synthetic partial amount in the actual flow. 12–20s: inspect the remaining balance and next reminder: “The owner needs the unpaid amount, not a green status from a rough guess.” 20–25s: “Test this before moving a whole studio list.”

If a partial-payment or private-link test fails, fix it before the next owner invitation. Don't edit around a failed control to make the video look complete.
