# Place a Brick — Video scripts

You can film public inspection now, with no payment. Film a completed placement only after a real authorised purchase/test succeeds and the owner clears publication. Never show payment details or another person's chosen name without consent.

## A. What the piece is — 35 seconds, homepage/creative invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Rotate actual virtual monument. | “This monument lives on a screen.” |
| 3–13 s | Actual piece types and price. | “Place a Brick lets people choose a digital piece, starting at one US dollar for a brick.” |
| 13–24 s | Public-name warning; no form submission. | “The name you choose is public. Inspect the monument before deciding whether to buy.” |
| 24–30 s | Digital-only terms. | “No physical construction, investment or charity donation.” |
| 30–35 s | URL. | “Have a look at placeabrick.com.” |

## B. Your public name — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Name-field warning, no real entry. | “Choose what you make public.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual piece preview and price. | “An etched display name is visible on the digital piece. Use a name you are comfortable showing.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Inspect first. Purchase is optional.” |

## C. One placed piece — 25 seconds, conditional demo

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Your actual authorised completed piece. | “Here is the piece I placed.” |
| 3–15 s | Actual piece selected and inspected. | “It is part of the virtual monument, with the chosen name visible on it.” |
| 15–22 s | Real total/terms, no receipt identifiers. | “The price was in US dollars. It buys a digital element, not a physical object.” |
| 22–25 s | URL. | “Read the current terms first.” |

Hold C until true. No invented purchase, fake live counter, lifetime-hosting claim or “donation” language.
