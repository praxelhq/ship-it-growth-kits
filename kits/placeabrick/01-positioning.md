# Place a Brick — Positioning

**Live:** https://placeabrick.com/ · **Category:** Digital collective artwork · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/placeabrick

## Say digital before asking anyone to pay

The 29 October 2026 homepage headline is **“Put your name in stone. One brick at a time.”** It offers virtual monument elements: **US$1 brick, $3 window, $5 pillar, $10 door, $25 capstone**. Actual Chrome inspection of the Masters' Union Arch showed quantities, a **“Name to etch”** field, a $1 total and **“Sign in to build”** linking to `/login?next=/monuments/masters-union-arch`. The login page offers account creation. No account, payment, refund or etched purchase was performed.

Terms explicitly say digital elements on a virtual monument: **not a physical object, investment or charitable donation**. Both terms and privacy currently describe themselves as drafts needing replacement before launch. Resolve those owner-facing publication gaps and verify purchase/delivery before broad paid invitations. The homepage's $58/15-element counters are site claims, not independently verified transactions. Do not use them as proof of sales.

Your first person is an adult campus community member who understands a tiny digital-art purchase and enjoys seeing a shared virtual structure take shape. The first ask is to inspect the monument and decide whether a public display name belongs there. No physical campus arch is being funded, and no university endorsement was established.

**Pitch:** Choose a digital monument piece and display your chosen name on the shared virtual build.

**Bio:** A shared virtual monument, one named digital piece at a time.

The [Million Dollar Homepage](https://milliondollarhomepage.com/) is a historical digital-space comparison; its metadata describes the original $1/pixel model, not a verified current purchase offer. Your difference is a three-dimensional monument with distinct elements and visible progress. A shared digital scrapbook or free community board is the zero-purchase alternative. Do not imply resale value, ownership of physical property or a financial return.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, with activation as a confirmed paid piece placed and inspected. Accounts and paid placements are separate. The word “forever” in the current preview needs a realistic service/durability explanation, not another lifetime guarantee. Public etched names are personal disclosure: suggest a chosen nickname and never etch someone else's identity without consent.
