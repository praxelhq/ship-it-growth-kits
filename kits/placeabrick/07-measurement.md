# Place a Brick — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one new non-test account. Browsers inspecting a monument, payment clicks, purchased elements and repeated orders are not additional accounts. **Activation:** a verified successful payment results in the correct named digital piece, then the buyer inspects it. Report paid buyers and pieces separately: one buyer can place several pieces.

## Baseline and data

Record actual accounts, verified payments, placed pieces, pending/failed placements, refunds, monument sources and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/placeabrick. Homepage counters are not an independently established baseline; confirm against actual records and remove tests. Unknown private values are not zero.

Current privacy mentions Clarity and stored emails/display names/contributions. Check the actual implementation before promotion; exclude name fields, identity/account and payment screens from replay. Public etched names do not authorise copying identities into your marketing log. Aggregate account/payment/placement/failure events are sufficient.

The plan assumes 16–32 accounts and perhaps 2–8 activations, far below 1,000; paid conversion is unproven. No physical property, financial return, charity benefit or institutional endorsement has been established. Keep the offer as a transparent digital-art choice.
