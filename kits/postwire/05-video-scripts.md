# Postwire — Video scripts

Choose and read a real original article before filming. The script explains the workflow without inventing the article's facts. Only record a completed draft/sample after it actually works; hide accounts, keys and confidential style samples.

## A. Source to draft — 45 seconds

**Use:** homepage and adult writing-session invitation.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Publisher link. “Read first.” | “Read first. Draft second.” |
| 3–15 s | Open the original article and return to Postwire. | “The headline is a starting point. I still open the original before deciding what I think.” |
| 15–30 s | Actual three draft options. | “Postwire gives me angles to work from. I choose one and edit anything I cannot support.” |
| 30–40 s | Actual copy/manual-publish step. | “Nothing goes out automatically. I review and publish it myself.” |
| 40–45 s | URL/trial boundary. | “See the sample at postwire.in.” |

## B. Your edit matters — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short/Reel.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Draft line highlighted. | “That is only a draft.” |
| 3–13 s | Make one real edit after checking the article. | “Keep the point you actually mean. Remove a claim the source does not support.” |
| 13–17 s | Original-source link. | “The source stays one click away.” |
| 17–20 s | URL. | “You publish, not the app.” |

## C. Choose the route — 25 seconds

**Use:** maker post/Peerlist.

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | Manual prompt route. | “You can bring your own chatbot.” |
| 4–15 s | Copy prompt, paste response, only if tested. | “Use the prompt in the service you choose, then bring the answer back to edit.” |
| 15–22 s | Privacy link. | “Read its data terms. Keep confidential examples out.” |
| 22–25 s | URL. | “The final words are yours.” |

Keep one edit uncut. Do not pretend a loading sample worked or imply a scheduled LinkedIn publication that the product does not perform.
