## The boundary that matters

A headline is not a researched opinion. Your user must read the publisher's original, check the draft and publish it themselves. Postwire does not read their LinkedIn account or post for them. Keep that manual-review boundary: it is a strength, not a missing automation to conceal.

## Your first person

An early-career individual trying to discuss one product/marketing news story thoughtfully on a personal LinkedIn profile. They already read the news but get stuck converting it into their own point. Start with this consumer adoption wedge, not agencies scheduling dozens of client accounts. Keep the assigned **1,000 consumer signups**; do not quietly change the target to businesses.

**Pitch:** Read a headline's original source, explore three draft angles, then edit and publish your own LinkedIn take.

**Bio:** News-led LinkedIn drafts. You read, edit and publish.

Your wedge is source-first commentary, not voice-note storytelling, relationship comments or automated publishing. [Postifyy](https://postifyy.in/) currently advertises three free posts and a voice-note workflow; [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is$0 for personal notes. Postwire must earn use through feed selection, source linkage and draft comparison. No paid competitor rate is needed to make an unsupported cheapest claim.

Prove one full headline→original→draft→human edit→copy path. Do not count a generated draft as a published post, assume the feed is correct, or use a publisher's image as your own. No account, prompt generation or publication was completed in this research.
