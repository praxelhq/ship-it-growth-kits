This is an assumption-based small-account forecast, not evidence of 1,000. A larger real writing partner or much stronger measured conversion is missing. Do not invent newsletter reach to fill it.

## Coordinate the actual people

The LinkedIn-workflow cluster reserves Lynkr Days 4–6, SignalPost Days 10–12, Postifyy Days 17–20 and TwoCents Days 24–26. Postwire's news-reading exercise uses **Days 24–27 only with different approved organizers/groups from TwoCents**. Confirm real dates with the coordinator. Same organization:at most one broad product invitation in a week. Its source-first junior-analyst wedge differs from Postifyy's voice-note project story and TwoCents' relationship replies.

No news publisher, newsletter editor or LinkedIn creator has agreed to feature Postwire. Source links are references, not promotion permission. Do not cold-message people just because they commented on a finance story, and do not offer investment advice. Handwritten, consented invitations only; stop after a decline.
