# Postwire — Measurement

**Target:1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** A signup is one new non-test account. **Activation is a first draft the person edits and copies after opening the original source.** A draft generated without review is not the same result. Report actual publication only when the person voluntarily confirms it; do not claim the app publishes or scrape their LinkedIn account.

## Day 0 baseline

Record current accounts, trial starts, activated people, saved drafts, landing sessions/referrers and the current cohort reading/position at https://shipped.money/p/postwire. Public research did not establish these private values; use actual counts and periods, not assumed zeroes. Separate free trial, paid subscription and test events.

Privacy already describes optional Clarity with masking andDNT/GPC handling. Check it rather than installing another tag. Keep passwords, keys, style samples, drafts and account content out of replay/public analytics. A headline/source identifier can be enough; do not store confidential personal text for growth analysis.

## Events

Account created; original opened; draft options ready; human edit made; draft copied; saved draft revisited; trial ended; subscription verified. Count one person once. Optional manual-chatbot use is not a failed activation if the actual edited/copied workflow succeeds.

| Checkpoint | Actual denominator | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | 5 invitations,1 clip,1 post; five observed attempts. | If nobody reaches signup, fix entry. If accounts stall, watch source→draft→edit→copy. |
| Day 14 | 10 invitations,3 clips,2 posts. | Replace 02 guesses with delivered visit/account/edited-draft rates. |
| Day 21 | 15 invitations,4 clips,3 posts and one optional listing. | Check later edited drafts, not generated-word volume. |
| Day 28 | 20 invitations,6 clips,3 posts and accepted-session attendance. | Deduplicate members; do not count a session attendee twice. |
| Day 30 | 4 posts total, eligible accounts, activations, returns, real subscriptions, hours. | State the gap to 1,000 and which source actually produced use. |

Do not add overlapping 24-hour snapshots or claim a copied draft was published. If users mistake AI output for verified news or automatic posting, correct the expectation before more acquisition. The small-network forecast is below target; an actual larger channel must be earned, not invented.
