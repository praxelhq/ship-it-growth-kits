Use https://postwire.in/sample for a first demonstration only after it loads reliably; otherwise send the main explanation. Test signup before sharing the trial link. Never invent a news event or personal reaction to one.

## 1. Personal invitation

> You mentioned wanting to write about product news. I’m building Postwire: choose a headline, read the original, compare three draft angles and edit the one that sounds like you.
>
> Want to inspect the sample? You publish yourself; it does not post to LinkedIn for you. New accounts have a seven-day, no-card trial. https://postwire.in/

## 2. LinkedIn post

> A headline gives you a topic. It does not give you an opinion.
>
> That is the boundary I want to keep in Postwire. It brings news links and draft angles together, but you still read the original, check the facts and choose what you actually think.
>
> There are three options to work from, not three things to publish unchanged. You edit and post yourself.
>
> If you want a less blank starting point for news-led posts, see the sample and trial here: https://postwire.in/

## 6. Source-checking post

> Before turning a story into a post, I want to do three things:
>
> Read beyond the headline. Separate the source's claim from my own interpretation. Remove anything the draft added without evidence.
>
> That is how I want Postwire to be used. A fluent paragraph is still only a draft. https://postwire.in/
