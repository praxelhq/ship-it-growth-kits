# PeakX — Video scripts

Use synthetic records and an authorized test space. No real diet advice, body transformation, lab report, form video, medical claim or private health analytics.

## A. Log-and-correct demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“Could I correct this estimate before trusting the summary?”**

2–12s: open actual logging flow after verified, label synthetic input. “This is demonstration data, not a nutrition recommendation.”

12–25s: show an actual estimate and correction control. “Check the item/portion; AI can misread.” No microphone upload needed unless owner deliberately uses a nonsensitive authorized test.

25–38s: show saved result/weekly-summary source only after verified. “The report depends on what is actually recorded.” If unavailable, label that rather than fake a report.

38–50s: URL/free-entry note. “General tracking, not diagnosis. Paid coaching has its own terms.”

## B. Placeholder hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“A starting target isn't personal advice.”**

2–12s: point to actual until-setup note without reading numbers as recommendations. “Know which values are defaults and which came from your inputs.”

12–18s: URL/scope limit. No claim the setup produces clinically validated targets.

## C. Record-quality hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“A missing log can change the story.”**

2–12s: synthetic complete/incomplete record example, labelled as an explanatory diagram if not actual product state. “A report should show what it knows and what it doesn't.”

12–20s: verified summary or proposed disclosure label. “Better records before bigger conclusions.”

## D. Offer-honesty hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Coach review isn't medical diagnosis.”**

2–12s: actual service/terms distinction, no uploaded video or lab. “Fitness coaches, target reply times and limits should be clear before purchase.”

12–20s: current regular price/selected renewal terms only after rechecked. Don't amplify an unverified remaining-slot counter.
