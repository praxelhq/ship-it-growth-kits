**Live:** https://www.peakx.live/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/peakx

**Start with consistent records, not a diagnosis of why someone's progress stalled.** Actual dashboard H1 checked 29 October is “Good morning,” not a conventional marketing hero. “Set up my plan” opens a setup route; “Log a meal” opens logging. The document title says “Stuck at the gym? Find out why.” Generic starting targets remain until setup; don't describe the initial displayed calorie/protein values as a personal prescription.

They need an understandable log and correction process, not a body-transformation promise. No profile, meal, weight, workout, video, lab report, device permission or payment was submitted in this research.
