## 4. LinkedIn
The apartment viewing shows the balcony. It rarely shows who takes calls at 1 am or what “clean kitchen” means to each person.

I am testing SpaceSync around those conversations: explicit habits, visible trade-offs and mutual choice before chat.

The first test is a small real moving cohort in one locality. No fake profiles to make a map look active, and no claim a compatibility percentage guarantees a good flatmate.

If you are actually moving soon and want to compare the questions, the core profile is free: https://www.thespacesync.in/

## 5. First-use guidance
Before publishing, check which answers are visible. Before accepting interest, read the actual answers and deal-breakers, not only the total score. Keep decisions about meeting and housing with the people involved; the app does not decide suitability for them.
