# SpaceSync — Video scripts

Use synthetic profiles clearly marked sample. No real addresses, contact details or private habits on screen.

## A.60-second demo — approved moving cohort
0–2: two notes, “Quiet after 11” / “Calls at 1”; text **“Talk about this before the lease.”**
2–15: create a sample compatibility card; “The useful part is making the routine explicit.”
15–30: show private preview and publish control. “Check what others can see before publishing.”
30–45: compare answers and deal-breaker explanation, not only score.
45–55: show a documented mutual-interest sample flow. “Both people choose before private chat.”
55–60: “Core is free. A score starts a conversation; it does not make the decision.”

## B.20-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: kitchen clock, **“Both said ‘clean’.”**
2–10: “One means wash immediately. One means tomorrow morning.”
10–17: show a specific question/answer comparison.
17–20: “Ask what the word means. thespacesync.in.”

## C.25-second hook — cohort follow-up
0–2: city map labels, **“Ten profiles. Three cities. No useful choice.”**
2–14: explain why the same locality and move-in window matter.
14–22: show honest sample cohort filter with no invented counts.
22–25: “Start with real availability, not a big number.”

Burn in captions and use a phone mic. Do not present staged profiles as genuine users or implied identity verification.
