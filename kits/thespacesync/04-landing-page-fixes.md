# SpaceSync — Landing page fixes

Public page inspected in a real browser 29 October 2026. Signup, matching, privacy enforcement and payment were not tested.

1. **Replace a guarantee with a specific job.** Current headline **“Somewhere out there is your perfect flatmate.”** Suggested supporting line: **“Compare everyday living habits before deciding who to talk to.”** Keep brand warmth while avoiding a promise that a score can guarantee a perfect relationship.
2. **Show actual local supply.** The page lists many city corridors and a polished live compatibility example. Label any illustrative pair clearly. Show real published-profile counts by chosen locality only when supported. If empty, say **“No suitable published profiles here yet. Your profile can remain a private draft.”**
3. **Make free versus paid consistent.** Core ₹0 is prominent; Premium ₹199/month appears lower down. Add next to CTA **“Core profile and mutual chat are free. Advanced filters are optional at ₹199/month.”** Verify actual plan rights before publishing.
4. **Qualify trust claims.** Current copy says zero fake profiles/bots and “verified boundary alignment.” A rules-based calculation does not verify identity or self-reported habits. Replace with **“Scores use the answers people provide. Review answers, report suspicious profiles and make your own decision.”** Publish specific verification claims only if enforced.
5. **Keep publication consent visible.** Current process says private draft until publish. Add **“Review the public preview before publishing. You control whether to publish your profile.”** Test unpublish, block/report and mutual-chat boundaries with authorized test accounts.
6. **Explain score limits.** Current 40/40/20 formula and 49% deal-breaker cap should show the underlying answers, weights and missing-answer treatment. Add **“A higher score is not proof someone is safe or suitable; read the breakdown.”**
7. **Phone check.** At 360 px, complete the 15-question flow, preview and return later without losing a draft. Keep one next action dominant. No mobile defect was independently reproduced by this read-only inspection.

Gate: two consenting users can publish, compare, mutually accept and communicate within the stated privacy boundaries. Do not send housing seekers to a decorative example.
