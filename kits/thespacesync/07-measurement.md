# SpaceSync — Measurement

**Assigned target: 10 supply-side customers in 30 days.** [Policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires genuine businesses for B2B, but this actual product serves individual flatmate seekers, not business/property suppliers. Keep the target visible and report the mismatch. **Do not certify ten individual profiles as ten qualifying businesses.**

Report two separate lines: genuine published/available profiles with independent recent use, and policy-qualifying businesses, if any can actually be established. The latter may be zero/unknown even when the product helps people. Course-owner interpretation is needed; this kit does not invent an exception.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Mask all profile answers, private messages and contact data. Record real accounts, completed drafts, published profiles by locality/timeframe, interest requests, mutual acceptance, independently returning users and reports. Private values were unavailable; do not assume zero. Save current rank, reading and sync at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/thespacesync).

Signup: account created. Product activation: a real person chooses to publish an accurate profile, inspects a relevant profile and can use the mutual-interest flow. A score view alone is not useful matching.

Policy's four tests remain: genuine business; actual data onboarded; unprompted use in last seven days; privately known contact name/phone. Apply them honestly rather than forcing consumers into the first test. Keep any contact data private.

The 02 assumptions yield about 6 active profiles, an honest gap to 10 before the policy issue. Do not repair either gap with fake availability, paid agents or duplicate accounts.

Sunday decisions: if completion is poor, simplify questions; if profiles do not overlap geographically, narrow recruitment; if people accept but cannot chat, fix flow; if someone finds a flatmate and leaves, record a voluntary successful outcome rather than treating every departure as failure. Do not infer a housing outcome from a button click.
