# SpaceSync — Distribution map

**One moving cohort, one locality.** You are building reciprocal availability, not collecting disconnected accounts.

| Route | Permission and action | Planned scale / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing campus/alumni/workplace network | Ask an actual organizer for permission to invite people moving within the next month. Do not scrape housing groups, request private member lists or publish addresses. Six blocks of three distinct eligible people =18 approaches. | #1/#2 |
| Participants' own referrals | After profile creation, ask if they know one genuinely moving person in the same locality. They ask first; no automatic contact upload. | #3; outcome not guaranteed. |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) own profile | Days 9 and 23 at 09:00 IST: explain the habit-conversation problem and geographic cohort honestly. No published personal profiles without permission. | #4; guess 100–500 impressions and 0–4 suitable visits/post. |
| Product's [community guidelines](https://www.thespacesync.in/guidelines) | Read current rules with participants before publishing. This is an internal conduct reference, not an external acquisition community. | #5 |

Assumptions: 18 suitable approaches ×70% genuine interest ×80% completed/published profiles ×60% independent recent use ≈6 active profiles. Ten is the assigned target, but this scenario does not clear it; accepted same-locality referrals or stronger actual conversion must close the profile gap. Even ten active profiles do not establish ten qualifying businesses.

No unverified Facebook/Telegram group names, member counts or posting permissions are included. Contact an organizer before posting anywhere. Do not buy or scrape “flatmate leads,” and do not imply the platform has verified every person merely because a score is deterministic.
