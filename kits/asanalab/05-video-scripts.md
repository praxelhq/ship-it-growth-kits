# AsanaLab — Video scripts

Film the interface on a phone, not an unqualified demonstration of someone else's form. A completed timer needs a real test first. No health details, before/after body claims or fake paid unlock.

## A. One pose slowly — 35 seconds, homepage/product invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual Mountain Pose page. | “Read one pose at your pace.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual steps, modifications and safety notes. | “AsanaLab brings the photo, instructions and easier options together on one page.” |
| 14–25 s | Actual timer adjustment; start only after verifying. | “Choose a hold time that suits your practice, then use the timer when you are ready.” |
| 25–31 s | Guest/free label. | “The free pose page needs no account.” |
| 31–35 s | URL. | “Try the lesson at asanalab.org.” |

## B. Pause the explanation — 20 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Scroll from photo to steps. | “No video to keep up with.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual steps/notes, no physical coaching. | “Read the next step and the modification at your own pace. This is educational content, not personal form assessment.” |
| 14–20 s | Direct lesson/URL. | “Start with a free AsanaLab pose.” |

## C. Guest first — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Guest label. | “Try the page before an account.” |
| 3–14 s | Actual free lesson and timer controls. | “You can inspect the free pose and its timer as a guest. Read its safety notes before practising.” |
| 14–20 s | Free-entry URL. | “Paid checkout is not open yet. Start free.” |

Keep the timer interaction uncut once. Do not imply that software feedback checks alignment or that completing a countdown proves a health benefit.
