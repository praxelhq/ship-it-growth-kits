# AsanaLab — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** For free guest practice, count meaningful first practice sessions under the existing accountless policy: a new person opens a pose lesson and starts its actual timer/practice flow. A homepage view or repeated timer is not another signup. Optional Google accounts are separate; deduplicate guest→account where consented measurement permits and disclose anonymous/browser limits.

**Activation:** one completed timer/lesson interaction, plus optional confirmation that the person used the educational steps. A countdown completion does not prove a pose was performed correctly, safely or at all. Return to another lesson/practice is a separate useful number. Paid progress entitlement is not activated until an actual working payment/saving path exists.
