# LaunchPad — Video scripts, recovery evidence

No product trailer is justified until the real action is known. These scripts use your own screen/voice, no private credentials or made-up interface.

**A. Fresh route, 50 seconds; private review first.** Hook: “Before a launch, the public link has to work.” 0–10s show the supplied hostname and dated observed state, no private dashboard. 10–25s show the owner-verified public replacement/recovery only after it works. Say, “This is the intended build, opened as a new visitor.” 25–40s record one uncut actual first action. 40–50s show real success or blocker: “This is what can be completed today.” If unresolved, stop at that fact; do not fill the remaining shots with a fictional product.

**B. Owner session isn't the test, 20 seconds.** Hook: “Works for me isn't a public-route check.” Show a fresh-browser attempt at the verified URL. Say, “A new person needs the intended page without my owner session. That's the route I'm checking.” Keep private hosting/account information hidden. Post only a factual current result.

**C. Name isn't the product, 20 seconds.** Hook: “LaunchPad doesn't tell you what it does.” Show the recovered real first-screen explanation when available. Say, “A visitor should be able to name the action before clicking. I ask first; I don't explain over the page.” Use a consenting tester's actual answer only with permission, never an invented quote.

**D. Count the completion, 20 seconds.** Hook: “Restoring a homepage isn't an activated user.” Show a clearly illustrative aggregate funnel, labelled, then the actual first-action definition after recovery. Say, “I separate a visit, a signup and a useful completion. Until the action is known, its count stays unknown.” No sample statistics as traction.
