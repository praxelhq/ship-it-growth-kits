A usable product, current slot/prior-listing checks, personal maker participation and no vote solicitation come first. No directory traffic is forecast or scheduled here.

Specialist communities, newsletters and campus groups must follow the actual recovered audience. Do not select startup or career communities merely from the name. After five interviews, verify two named destinations, read current promotion rules and request permission where required. Write a small exact invitation budget, then measure visits and useful completions. Paid placement is outside this plan.
