## The first business

A small owner-run hardware shop recording daily counter sales, an expense and a customer due in a notebook after the rush. It needs an accurate reviewed entry, not a voice assistant making financial decisions or an invoice/stock system the public demo cannot prove.

**Pitch: Speak the workday entry, inspect the extracted details and correct missing amounts before saving.**

Short version: **Your words, a checked entry, your confirmation.**

This is the intended actual-use job to verify. The homepage promises sales, expenses, dues and stock updates, with confirmation before saving. Its “so your books stay accurate” sentence is stronger than the evidence. User confirmation can catch errors; it does not certify accounts or guarantee every spoken amount is correct.

## What the demo really does

The actual demo says **no microphone, messaging app, database or AI service is contacted**. The voice note/transcript/figures are prewritten sample data; nothing persists beyond the page. A read-only click through the sample revealed four candidate events and a deliberately missing sales amount, which the demo says it will not guess. This usefully demonstrates the intended clarification pattern, not real speech recognition, extraction accuracy, messaging integration or saved business records.

No software price, free allowance, supported live languages, transcription provider, production storage or account entitlement was established. Do not invent unlimited free voice notes, WhatsApp delivery, guaranteed Hindi accuracy, GST accounting or offline processing. A demo that contacts no service does not prove the signed-in product has the same architecture.

## The alternative

The owner's current notebook plus a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) daily ledger can hold sale amount, expense, dues change and source note. A simple voice memo can preserve what the owner said but still needs human review/transcription. VocTrace must earn adoption through a reliable confirm/correct/save/return workflow, not a sample animation implying a working financial record.

Prove a synthetic actual voice entry under an authorized test account, including missing amount, correction, explicit confirmation, saved record, return and deletion. Do not enter real customer names, dues or worker attendance until data handling and permissions are reviewed. The owner retains its existing business records during evaluation.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each shop must onboard its own actual authorized data, use independently within seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Demo players, candidate events, customer-dues rows and fictional records are not business customers. If only the static sample is available, qualifying actual-use count may remain zero.
