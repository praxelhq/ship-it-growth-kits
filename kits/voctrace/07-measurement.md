# VocTrace — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each shop must satisfy [all four policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; own actual authorized business data onboarded; independent use within seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identities outside this public repo. Sample events, dues customers, staff and voice-note counts do not multiply businesses.

Activation: the owner reviews/corrects one actual entry, explicitly confirms it and verifies the saved record after returning. Report this beside customer totals. Static demo clarification is prototype understanding, not production activation; it uses no microphone/AI/database and persists nothing. If only that path exists, actual business-use count may remain zero.

No private workspace or voice processing was accessed; actual record totals, production availability and customer counts are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real shops / latest independent workday use | Actual product records plus private permitted owner register, excluding samples/tests. |
| Verified actual voice/confirmation/save path | Owner-authorized synthetic production test, not prewritten demo. |
| Own-data entries reviewed/saved/reopened | Status evidence; no raw ledger/customer data in growth logs. |
| Corrections and missing fields | Aggregate error categories for amount, quantity, date and type. |
| Access/save/deletion/processing failures | Synthetic reproductions and private issue categories. |
| Offer/payment and course baseline | Actual configured allowance if established, [profile](https://shipped.money/p/voctrace), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate. Exclude voice recordings/transcripts, customer names, dues, worker attendance, private amounts, ledger records and account/payment fields from replay. Minimal status events: actualentrystarted, clarificationneeded, entrycorrected, entryconfirmed, saveverified, laterindependentuse only where supported. Do not invent actual extraction or saved-record events from sample animation.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If owners mistake the demo for actual AI/voice storage, fix 04 and the invitation before more promotion. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare real owned setups/records with synthetic demonstration interest. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent reviewed entries and actual persistence, not microphone clicks. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four business tests and count genuine shops rather than entries/people in a ledger. |
| Day 30 | Qualifying businesses against ten, activation, independent use, current scope/offer, hours and honest gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three statuses/two introductions are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. The model assumes real authorized production entry; if that cannot be verified, qualifying conversion may be zero. A one-off assisted trial fails independence unless the owner returns without prompting.

A confirmed entry is not audited books, a dues balance is not received cash and a sample transcription is not measured language accuracy. Publish only permitted aggregate workflow observations, with dates and limits. No invented shop revenue, saved hours, compliance claim or testimonials.
