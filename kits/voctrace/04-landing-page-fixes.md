# VocTrace — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://voctrace.xyz/) and the actual [sample flow](https://voctrace.xyz/voice-demo) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“Turn voice notes into business records.”** CTAs **“Try the demo preview”** / **“Sign in to your account.”** No public price/allowance established. Demo is prewritten, page-local and contacts no microphone, messaging app, database or AI. No actual voice/account/save test completed.

## 1. Put the sample's architecture beside its CTA

Current home says interactive sample data; the demo explains no service is contacted.

Replacement button/help: **“Explore the prewritten demo. No microphone or AI service is used; nothing is saved.”**

Keep this on the opening and recorded clips, not only below the sample. A sample transcript must never look like fresh speech recognition.

## 2. Narrow the book-accuracy promise

Current copy says confirmation keeps books accurate.

Replacement support: **“Review and correct the extracted details before saving. Keep checking against your original records.”**

Confirmation reduces opportunity for error but does not certify accounts. Do not imply tax-ready bookkeeping, reconciled balances or guaranteed financial accuracy.

## 3. Preserve the missing-amount stop

Actual sample shows sales quantity without an amount and says it will not guess.

Replacement review note: **“Amount missing. Enter the correct value before confirming this record.”**

Verify the real product handles quantity versus money, missing date, ambiguous due and duplicate entry without inventing values. Do not let a sample's good behavior substitute for a real backend test.

## 4. Explain actual voice/data processing before use

Current public sample has no live service, while the homepage describes voice transcription/extraction.

Replacement pre-use block: **“For the actual account workflow: microphone requirement, supported language, processing provider, storage, access and deletion explained here.”** Fill actual verified terms, not assumptions that sample-local means live-local.

Test permission denial and network failure with synthetic inputs. No real customer/worker details needed for the first evaluation.

## 5. Verify explicit confirm and saved return

Current four stages end in approved searchable records; the sample state stays in this page only.

Replacement production-state labels must distinguish **“Candidate — not saved”**, **“Confirmed”** and **“Saved record”** only when true.

Test actual correction, final confirmation, save failure, refresh/sign-out, another device if supported and deletion. A confirmation animation without persistence is not a business record.

## 6. Publish the current offer and truthful records boundary

Current public entry shows no price/free allowance and links a View Records route from the sample.

Replacement offer note: **“Review the actual account allowance and any price before using real records.”** Label a records sample clearly; do not imply a demo event has been saved to a business ledger.

Check phone readability, local-language rendering and encoding: the observed demo contains garbled dash characters in some text. Correct those strings without changing figures/units. Keep contact/payment fields and all actual records out of replay. No live-language accuracy or setup-time claim was measured here.
