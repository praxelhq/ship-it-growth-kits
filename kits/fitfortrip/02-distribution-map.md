# FitForTrip — Distribution map

**Lead with travellers whose trip is already booked, not everyone who enjoys fashion.** Your strongest small test is planning one real suitcase.

Forecasts are assumptions for the calendar, not measured audience benchmarks.

| Route | Action / permission | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fifteen known adult travellers | Individually qualify for a real upcoming trip; don't ask for tickets/passports or private itinerary screenshots. | 15 × assumed 60% try × 50–80% account/first-trip completion = 5–7 activations. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned trip/friend circles | Ask the actual organizer before one message each. A circle without a booked trip isn't qualified just because it is large. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–25% visit × 20–40% signup = 2–8 accounts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | One short trip, outfit-to-packing relation and illustrative-versus-live shopping at 18:30 IST. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday launch after the saved-trip flow works; no vote trading or invented user outfits. | Assume 5–20 visits and 1–5 accounts. | #4 |

Base scenario is roughly 9–56 accounts before overlap. Actual trip activation is another conversion stage, not an automatic outcome. A thousand signups needs evidence of a larger qualified route.

## Communities, newsletters and creators

No current travel/fashion subreddit commercial permission, newsletter slot or creator collaboration was verified. Don't drop a product link under someone asking for destination safety or private travel help. Ask a real outfit/travel creator you already follow whether a no-purchase suitcase-planning demonstration is useful, using #5; forecast zero until they agree.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is an optional free listing after usability, with no direct upvote requests and zero forecast until observed. Whering is a comparison, not your promotional board. Coordinate with Closet Stylist and other fashion/travel tools: your first job is a booked trip's daily looks, not general daily styling.
