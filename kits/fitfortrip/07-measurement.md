# FitForTrip — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real accounts. Report **one actual trip saved with daily looks and a usable packing list** beside it. Waitlist emails, moodboard views and retailer clicks are separate events, not activation or extra people.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/fitfortrip) traffic/rank. Record actual current accounts, old waitlist, trips saved, lists generated/edited and return use. This kit doesn't know private totals or full app behaviour; mark **not measured** until verified.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and masking/exclusion of private dates, locations, account fields and uploaded photos. A useful aggregate funnel doesn't need tickets, passports or exact accommodation. Don't log the contents of someone's private itinerary as analytics.

## Funnel

`app_entry → account_created → real_trip_saved → daily_looks_chosen → packing_list_used → returned_before_departure`.

One user planning two cities remains one user. A list shown in a marketing mockup isn't an activated trip. Ask a small consented sample whether the list actually helped packing; a retailer click doesn't prove a useful plan or purchase.

## Projection and checkpoints

The exact calendar in `02` suggests roughly 9–56 accounts before overlap under guessed rates. At hypothetical 20% qualified-visit-to-signup, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visits. Activation after signup is another rate to establish. Current small-channel reach doesn't prove the board target.

| Review | Check | Decision |

| Day 7 | Five travellers explain the task and reach a real saved list. | If two cannot, fix the exact setup/save stage before promotion. |
| Day 14 | Accounts→trips→looks→lists; mobile errors. | Don't replace failed activation with more moodboard views. |

These are chosen experiment checks, not travel/style guarantees. Sample retail prices and temperatures aren't current data. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings aren't additive users.
