# FitForTrip — Video scripts

Use your own clothes/photos and a clearly labelled sample trip. No private ticket, passport or accommodation details.

## A. Trip to suitcase — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“You booked the trip. You packed seven unrelated tops.”**

2–12: Show the actual trip setup with sample city/dates. “Start with the days you'll actually spend there.”

12–25: Pick real supported daily looks. “Choose outfits, then see which pieces repeat.”

25–35: Show the actual saved packing list after testing it. If unavailable, label the preview honestly and don't present it as a completed user flow.

35–45: “Use what you own. Check the departure forecast. fitfortrip.in.” Use as the main homepage/listing demo.

## B. Tops versus outfits — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Seven tops. Still nothing to wear?”**

2–10: Arrange two real outfits reusing one item. “Plan the look, not just the number of pieces.”

10–15: “Your trip, daily looks, one list. FitForTrip.” URL. First clip.

## C. One piece twice — 20 seconds

0–2: **“This goes in once, not twice.”**

2–13: Show one piece reused across two sample days and the actual packing-list behaviour. “A useful plan connects the outfit idea to what goes in the bag.”

13–20: “Start with clothes you own.” URL. Second clip; no false app behaviour if duplicates aren't handled yet.

## D. Sample isn't forecast — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Paris on a mockup isn't your weather.”**

2–12: Show the illustrative weather/product labels. “Destination inspiration starts the plan. A current forecast and real stock/prices finish the check.”

12–20: “Plan thoughtfully, buy nothing for this demo.” URL. Third clip; no live-weather claim without a real current source.
