## The first traveller

An adult with an actual short trip booked who has saved outfit ideas but hasn't turned them into day-by-day choices. Start with one Goa weekend or one city break, using clothes they already own. They don't need to photograph their whole wardrobe or shop for a suitcase of new items to get value.

**Pitch:** Turn your next trip into daily outfit choices and one packing list.

**Bio:** Your trip is booked. Plan what you'll actually wear.

Your wedge is trip-first planning. A generic digital closet demands work before the user feels progress; you can start from destination/dates and a few looks. [Whering](https://whering.co/) is a real wardrobe/planning alternative with packing features, so don't say closet apps cannot plan trips. [Notion's personal Free plan](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 and can hold an itinerary/packing list. Win on less setup and a clearer day-to-look-to-item connection, not a false competitor claim.

The Paris temperatures and sample prices on the landing page are illustrations. Use a real departure forecast before packing; don't imply static destination guidance is a live weather guarantee.

Target stays **1,000 signups**: unique real accounts, beside **one actual trip saved with daily looks and a usable packing list**. A waitlist email, moodboard swipe or retailer click is not that activation.
