# Fintellix — Measurement

**Target: 10 real CA/accounting firms.** Activation beside it: **firms independently maintaining their own permitted real workflow in the actual supported workspace**. A signup, staff account or controlled synthetic record isn't a business customer.

## Day 0

Query actual firms, authorized real records, successful independent jobs, access errors and return use from your own records. Login/signup pages don't establish those totals. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding client records and credentials. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/fintellix) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero. No public price/live billing was established; don't invent subscription revenue.

Proposed events only where actual functions exist: `firm_created`, `permitted_workflow_onboarded`, `real_task_saved`, `record_reviewed`, `export_succeeded` and `firm_returned`. Public route metadata doesn't prove each function exists. Keep synthetic-test data separate and don't log raw client information in public analytics.

## Four customer tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), a real firm has its own permitted actual work onboarded, uses it independently in the final seven days without your prompt, and has a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required. Several staff users inside one firm are one business. Founder-operated work is assisted service; design interviews aren't independent software use.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × hypothetical 20% response × 50% authorized live trial × 50% independent reuse = **2 firms**. Ten warm requests may add 0–1. **Base 2–3 is below ten and may be zero if the capability/data gate fails.** These aren't observed CA-workspace acquisition rates. Recompute after 20 approaches from actual successful permitted jobs.
