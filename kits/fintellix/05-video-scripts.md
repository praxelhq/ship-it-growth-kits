# Fintellix — Video scripts

Synthetic firms/records only. No client identifiers, credentials or unsupported security guarantees. Film actual released features; if the private job isn't verified, the video remains a scoped pilot explanation.

## A · What can this firm actually do? · 60 seconds · owner walkthrough

0–2s hook: **“Before the client upload, show me the real workflow.”** 2–15s: show controlled firm-account creation, label Synthetic: “This is the actual account entry, not evidence of every private capability.” 15–30s: complete one actual supported small job: “The owner needs a saved result they can inspect.” 30–45s: demonstrate a verified access/export behaviour or state the current gap: “Don't promise a control from a button label.” 45–55s: return to the saved work: “Can the practice continue independently?” 55–60s: show current real terms and URL; don't quote an unpublished price.

If any required step fails, show the limitation and fix it before promoting live client use.

## B · A login isn't a security audit · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“Signing in doesn't answer who can see the records.”** 0–8s: show a neutral synthetic access example. 8–15s: “The firm needs actual permissions, recovery and record-handling scope.” 15–20s: “Check the controls before trusting the word ‘private’.”

## C · Open the right firm's record · 25 seconds · owner-control walkthrough

Hook 0–2s: **“Before you act, are you in the right firm record?”** Use a clearly synthetic workspace. 2–10s: show the actual firm identity and supported record context: “The owner should be able to tell whose work this is.” 10–20s: demonstrate a verified access or account-switching limit, or explicitly state the current gap: “Don't expose another firm's data just to make a demo convenient.” 20–25s: “Clear ownership before client use.” Never display credentials or pretend an unverified control passed.

Keep captions readable. Use the actual supported scope, not a security guarantee or a real firm's logo without permission.
