# Fintellix — Positioning

**Live:** https://fintellix.dhukaan.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/fintellix

**Your ten are CA/accounting firms, not staff logins.** The actual signup resolves the buyer clearly: firm name, responsible person's name, work email and password create a firm account. Start with a small practice owner who needs a controlled workspace, not a consumer tracking personal tax.

The root returns an opening shell and rendered retrieval requires login. The real `/login` headline is **“Welcome back.”** `/signup` says **“Your practice, in order.”** and **“Create your firm's private workspace.”** Its action is **“Create firm account.”** Both routes were read without authenticating. **The site is live, not proved down.** No public price, private feature set or security implementation was independently tested.

## A scoped pilot hypothesis

> Give your practice one controlled place for the work you can actually verify, with an owner responsible for access and records.

Bio: **A firm workspace to test on one small practice workflow.**

Before choosing a more specific sales promise, create a controlled account and confirm the actual client/task/document capabilities, permissions, export and deletion. The publicly served client describes tasks/checklists in route metadata, but that doesn't prove the private workflow works. Don't advertise statutory filing, automated reminders or document analysis from a product name.

## The real alternative

A practice can continue using its existing Excel/folders and a manual task board. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace; [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) supports creation with a Google account. Neither is automatically equivalent to a correctly controlled firm workspace, and neither proves Fintellix's security. Compare a real measured job rather than an unverified competitor price.

## The weak point

“Private” is a claim to verify before client use, not a feature established by the signup form. A controlled pilot should prove that one firm's records aren't visible to another and that staff permissions match the actual offer. This kit doesn't claim those checks passed.

Keep the buyer different from CA Deskk's filing-season chase, Audit extractor's first transcription pass and PBC's engagement support. Fintellix's early offer is a **firm-control pilot** until actual supported work is verified. Ten customers need their own permitted real workflow, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact privately. A research interview or synthetic firm isn't one of ten.
