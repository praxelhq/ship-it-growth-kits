# Kreator — Distribution map

Lead with active paid collaborations and the daily next-action review. Avoid promising a deal pipeline to creators who currently have none.

| Route | Execution / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing paid short-form creator relationships | Qualify two current brand jobs, ownership of the business workflow and permission to use commercial information. Use personal introductions or the creator's public business enquiry route honestly. | Ten blocks of four creator businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two editor/creator introductions | Ask a known collaborator to forward the invitation, not disclose a creator's rates, clients or contact list. | Days 16 and 19; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a fictional three-deal board and actual next-action review, not invented earnings growth. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One consented creator review | Owner reviews next action/deadline/payment status in its own permitted deals. | Day 20; #5. Activation support for the existing trial. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-deal setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 creator businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. The ten four-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts and introductions are upside only; a board with five brands is one creator customer.

Days 16–19 are the creator-administration reservation in [coordination](../../shared/a2-creator-operations.md). Cutroom handles an existing editor's version review Days 12–15; CreatorDeal scope/rights Days 20–23; booked UGC fulfilment Days 24–27. Do not send all tools to the same creator or broad organizer. Kreator should own deal next-action/date/payment tracking, not promise editing delivery or contractual rights management without evidence.

No creator newsletter, influencer group or professional-community promotion permission is established. Ask a known organizer before posting and allow one primary invitation per organizer/week. No purchased follower list, automated DMs, fake paid collaboration offer or promised sponsor introductions. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after a decline.

Do not forecast sales from AI pitches before a real creator reviews and separately authorizes sending them. Do not send subscription traffic until the free-versus ₹60/month boundary and actual payment route are clear. A `#` href alone is not proof checkout is broken.
