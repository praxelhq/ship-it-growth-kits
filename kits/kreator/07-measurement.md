# Kreator — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each creator business must meet [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine paid-work operation; own actual deal data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known owner name and phone. Keep identity/contact data outside this public repo. Paying for Kreator is not required to qualify, but real business work is.

Activation: the owner enters actual current deals, records a next action/date and uses that board to choose a work step. Report this beside the customer count. Brand rows, pitch drafts, invoice files and followers do not become extra businesses. Generated earnings graphs are not authoritative payment records.

No private dashboard was accessed; baseline values are unknown until recorded.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine creator businesses / latest independent use | Workspace records plus private permitted owner register; exclude synthetic demos. |
| Owned deals and clear next actions | Actual commercial records with permission; no public client details. |
| Calendar/reminder completion | Supported status and consented owner confirmation, not just a notification toggle. |
| Invoice drafts and recorded actual receipts | Distinguish generated/sent/due/paid; never infer cash from a PDF. |
| Kreator payments/refunds | Actual configured billing/provider records; advertised ₹60 is not revenue. |
| Traffic/course baseline | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/kreator), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before installing another tag. Exclude deal names where confidential, client contacts, negotiated fees, contracts, invoices, AI pitch inputs and account/payment screens from replay. Aggregate events: owndealadded, nextactionset, calendarreviewed, invoicedrafted, actualreceiptmarked, laterindependentuse. A reminder or assisted visit is separate from independent use.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If creators expect sponsors or a replacement manager, fix the invitation and first-screen promise. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare qualified paid creators, own-deal setups and actual next-action use. Fictional boards do not count. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent recent activity, date accuracy and actual reminder behavior. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests, counting creator businesses rather than brand rows. |
| Day 30 | Report actual qualifying businesses against ten, activation, independent return, real payments/refunds, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two introduction requests are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. If a creator's paid jobs finish and it does not independently return, record useful one-off setup separately from final-week customers. Do not prompt a login to make the metric look active.

A rate suggestion is not proof of commercial value. An invoice draft does not establish tax compliance or payment. Public illustrations are not client endorsements or user revenue. Report real outcomes only with the owner's permission, dates and limitations; never invent a creator case study.
