# Kreator — Copy pack

## 1. Creator opener

Hi — with your current brand collaborations, where do you keep the next action, delivery date and invoice/payment status?

I build Kreator. I am testing a small deal-board routine with creators who already have paid work, not promising to find sponsors or replace a manager.

Would a fictional three-deal walkthrough be useful? No need to send your brand contacts, rates or private contracts.

## 2. Trial email

**Subject: What does each collaboration need next?**

The first useful Kreator test is three real collaborations you are allowed to record: next action, due date, agreed fee and payment status. Then open the board on another workday and see whether it helps you choose what to do.

The page advertises one plan at ₹60/month and also says Start free. Please check the exact current free/trial allowance and payment terms before committing: https://www.thekreator.in/

Rate suggestions and AI pitches are drafts to review. An invoice draft is not a payment or a guarantee about tax treatment. We can start with fictional deals before deciding whether your own workflow fits.

If not relevant, say no and I will stop.

## 3. LinkedIn post

A brand collaboration can look active while the next action is still unclear.

Do I owe a draft? Does the brand owe approval? Is the invoice sent? Has the payment actually arrived?

Kreator puts those questions around one creator's deal board. The routine I am testing is simple: review the next action and date before starting the day's work.

The demonstration uses fictional deals. It does not show customer earnings or imply a brand endorsement. Current plan and entry details: https://www.thekreator.in/

If you manage your own paid collaborations, which status gets lost most often?

## 4. Introduction ask

Do you know a creator managing two or more active paid collaborations? Please ask whether a next-action board demo would help. Do not send me their rates, brand contacts or phone number without permission. I am not offering a sponsorship or collecting pitches for a brand.

## 5. Owner review invitation

Would you look at your next action, delivery date and payment status for each actual collaboration? You decide which business details can enter the product. We can keep client contracts and personal contacts out of the first setup. The test is whether the board helps on another day, not whether every feature gets clicked.

## 6. Consented follow-up

Did you open the board independently when choosing your next work task? Which status or date was missing/wrong? Did a reminder actually arrive where expected? Please describe the issue privately without brand contracts, invoices or client screenshots in public replies.

Never invent creator earnings, guaranteed sponsorship rates, GST compliance, sent-pitch outcomes or testimonials. Different posts should show different real workflow lessons with all private data removed.
