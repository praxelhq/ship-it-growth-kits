# Kreator — Landing-page fixes

Read [thekreator.in](https://www.thekreator.in/) in a real browser on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Your creator business, in one vibrant place.”** CTAs: **“Start free”**, **“Create your account →”** and **“Subscribe with Razorpay.”** One plan ₹60/month; no tax, trial, entitlement or checkout completion verified. The public subscription href is `#`, not evidence by itself that the interactive checkout fails.

## 1. Explain what Start free includes

Current entry says Start free while one paid plan is listed.

Replacement offer block: **“Before you register: see the current free/trial allowance, when payment is required and the ₹60/month plan terms.”** Fill the actual limits from the live product; do not invent a free week or unlimited guest access. Show renewal, tax and cancellation before payment.

## 2. Narrow the manager-replacement claim

Current copy says it replaces an expensive social media manager.

Replacement: **“Keep your own brand-deal admin together.”** Support: **“Track next actions, dates, fee and payment status. You remain in charge of the relationship and decisions.”**

Make one actual deal routine the first demonstration, rather than asking a creator to believe all business work disappears.

## 3. Make rate estimates visibly conditional

Current rate calculator promises exactly what to charge from followers/engagement.

Replacement: **“A starting estimate to review against deliverables, usage rights, workload and the actual buyer conversation.”**

Show actual inputs/limitations. Do not claim a fee is market-verified or guarantee a brand will accept it. Do not present a rate estimate as contract advice.

## 4. Separate invoice drafting and payment

Current invoice claim says professional GST invoices and get paid on time.

Replacement: **“Create a draft invoice from the details you provide. Review business identity, items, amount and tax treatment before sending. Track payment only when actually received.”**

Verify actual fields and output before advertising GST-specific capability. A generated PDF cannot prove payment or compliance. Do not send a real invoice to test delivery without the owner's consent.

## 5. Label illustrations and verify reminders

Current hero shows a named brand deal and monthly earnings card; calendar/reminders are advertised.

Replacement label: **“Illustrative deal and earnings, not a customer result.”** Reminder note: **“Choose the supported reminder route and confirm delivery before relying on it.”**

Test timezone, permission denial and a missed reminder with synthetic records. Do not promise never missing a posting day.

## 6. Make the paid route and data lifecycle inspectable

Test Start free/account creation, a synthetic deal, board update, calendar date, invoice draft, deletion, sign-out/return and actual Razorpay route under an authorized test. A `#` link may open JavaScript; inspect its real behavior before calling it broken.

Show privacy, export/deletion, support and cancellation information before asking for brand contacts or financial details. Mask client data and keys from recordings. Preserve the distinctive colourful direction while making the work-state hierarchy clearer; a decorative earnings graph is not baseline traction.
