# Kreator — Phone-demo scripts

Use a fictional three-deal board, synthetic invoice details and your own voice. No real brand endorsement, private rate, contract or contact data. All account/payment actions remain internal tests; never send a pitch or invoice as part of recording without consent.

## A. The next work action — 65 seconds

0–3 s: three fictional collaboration cards. **“What needs you next?”**

3–18 s: enter one real sample deal with deliverable/date/status. “Start with work you already have, not a promise of new sponsors.”

18–32 s: actual board review and next-action change. “This draft needs my action. This one is waiting on approval.” Leave a real status update uncut if supported.

32–47 s: actual calendar and invoice draft. “Check the date and amount. A draft invoice is not money received.”

47–55 s: show actual reminder/return behavior only after verification. “The board needs to help on another workday.” Do not fake a notification or historical return.

55–65 s: current plan/free boundary and URL. “The public plan is ₹60/month. Check what Start free includes. thekreator.in.”

## B. Status hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: fictional card marked active with no next action. **“Active is not an action.”**

3–12 s: actual next-step/date entry. “Name who needs to do what before the delivery date.”

12–20 s: real board and URL. “One clearer day of creator admin. Kreator.” No fake time-saved statistic.

## C. Invoice hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: synthetic invoice beside unpaid status. **“Sent is not paid.”**

3–15 s: review actual output and payment-status field. “Check the details. Mark payment only after money actually arrives.”

15–25 s: owner review and URL. “A useful board keeps the states different.” Do not imply payment processing if the product only records status.

Capture real entry/free boundary, synthetic deal fields, actual status/calendar, reminder permissions, invoice draft and advertised ₹60 plan. If the signed-in workflow is not ready, demonstrate public claims as claims and repair before a real-work campaign. Label shortened waits.
