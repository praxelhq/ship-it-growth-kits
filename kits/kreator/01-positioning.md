## Your first business

An independent short-form creator already managing two or three paid brand collaborations. The creator has actual deliverables, due dates, a quoted fee and payment status to track. Follower count alone is not qualification: a 10k–100k audience can still have no current business workflow.

**Pitch: Put the next action, deadline and payment status for your actual brand deals in one reviewed board.**

Short version: **Know what each paid collaboration needs next.**

The first useful result is entering three permitted real deals, naming the next action and checking which deliverable or invoice is due. Start there rather than promising to replace a human social media manager. Negotiation, strategy, relationships and client approval remain the creator's responsibility.

## What the public page says

Features include a brand-deal pipeline, rate calculator, calendar/reminders, earnings graphs, AI pitch templates and invoice generation. The page says “Know exactly what to charge” and “Professional GST invoices in seconds.” Treat a rate as an estimate, not an assured market price. Invoice output needs review of actual seller/buyer details and tax treatment; do not promise compliance or on-time payment from a generated document.

The displayed brand/deal/monthly income figures are marketing illustrations, not independently established paid customer results. Do not reuse the brand name as an endorsement or copy the ₹29,500 illustration into revenue proof. The current offer's tax treatment, free allowance, trial period, actual checkout and entitlement were not established. “Start free” beside ₹60/month needs a precise explanation before sending users to purchase.

## The alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) deal log can hold brand, deliverable, due date, quoted fee, invoice date and payment status. A personal [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) page can organize tasks, without pretending paid team/AI features are free. Kreator must earn adoption through an easier next-action routine, not the fiction that a low-cost CRM replaces every manager task.

Prove one synthetic deal from entry through deadline view, invoice draft and recorded payment status. Verify which reminders actually send, where data persists and how to delete a deal. Then onboard the creator's own permitted work. Do not send AI-generated pitches or client invoices during a demo without separate consent.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Paid-work creators are B2B. Each must onboard its own actual deals/data, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Brands inside a deal board, generated pitches and followers are not extra Kreator customers.
