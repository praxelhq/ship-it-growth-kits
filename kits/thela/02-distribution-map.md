# Thela — Distribution map

**A careful concierge-supply launch, not a viral buyer launch.** Spend the month finding ten creators whose existing demand can support a real first order. Only solicit as many applications as your setup capacity permits.

| Route | Rules and expected result, explicitly assumed | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm teacher/creator introductions | Ask 10 people you know for one relevant creator introduction. Verify they already sell a digital guide/course/call. Model 3–5 conversations,1–3 applications. Nobody is added to a WhatsApp group or given a stranger's number without permission. | #1 |
| Creator-owned public business pages | Manually inspect Instagram-linked websites for a current digital offer and a suitable business enquiry route. Four new approaches on ten named days=40. No automation, repeated promo comments or contact harvesting. Scenario 40 × 25% reply × 60% pilot × 60% independent use≈4 businesses. | #2 |
| Small local creator sessions | Your own campus/teacher circle only with organizer permission. Demonstrate one fictional order, then explain the manual steps and delivery responsibility. One accepted session might add 0–2 active businesses; no event is booked. | #3 |
| Your own LinkedIn / X | Show payment-check/delivery workflow, not screenshots of private transactions. Tuesday 09:00 IST or Thursday 18:00 IST chosen for your availability. New-account assumption 100–500 impressions/post,0–5 qualified visits,0–1 application. | #4 |
| [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) organizer route | Official network exists; no current free event/group access assumed. Offer a practical session for digital-product sellers after a current organizer agrees. No paid sponsorship. | #3 |

Use [Gumroad's pricing page](https://gumroad.com/pricing) as a factual comparison source, not a place to recruit through customer support or hijack comments. [WhatsApp Business](https://www.whatsapp.com/business/) distinguishes the small-business app from the programmatic platform; your current manual workflow is not an approved automatic-messaging integration.

No newsletter placement, public Discord or creator-group permission was verified. Their contribution is zero until an organizer accepts a free opt-in feature. You don't need a Reddit post to hand-build ten stores.

The calendar has 40 first approaches plus 10 warm asks. Do not add the same creator twice. Approximate base:4 from direct outreach plus 0–3 from warm/accepted sessions, **below ten in the conservative case**. Review actual applications, store launches and independent seller use at day 14. If stores don't sell because there is no demand, change qualification; don't buy traffic or promise creators an audience.
