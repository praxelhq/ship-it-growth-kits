# Thela — Measurement

**Target:10 real creator businesses.** Activation beside it: **approved stores whose owners independently handle a real order or maintain their real selling workflow**. One buyer is not a business customer; a fictional demo or a live page the owner never touches doesn't count.

## Day 0 and events

Record actual applications, accepted creators, approved live stores and independently active stores; none was established by this public research. Record available founding capacity from the actual private log, not the static ten-spots label. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection and mask buyer forms; configure it if missing. At the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/thela), record actual rank/latest reading/timestamp. Unknown isn't zero; don't add overlapping rolling readings.

Track `application_received`, `creator_accepted`, `store_approved`, `store_live`, `order_code_created`, `creator_delivery_recorded` and `returning_creator_active`. An order code is not payment confirmation. Bank-confirmed payment, delivery and refund are separate seller-recorded outcomes. Never put banking screenshots or buyer details in a public report.

## Four tests, all required

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), a customer must be a real business; have its actual approved products/prices/store data onboarded; use the workflow in the last seven days without your prompt; and have a responsible contact whose name and callable number you hold privately. Paying ₹299 is not required in month 1. Two products from one creator are one customer. A friend without a selling business doesn't qualify.

## Forecast versus target

40 direct approaches × assumed 25% reply × 60% pilot × 60% independent use≈4 businesses. Ten warm requests and an accepted session might contribute 0–3 more without duplicating those creators. **The conservative 4–7 range is below ten.** Those are assumptions, not measured creator conversion. At day 14 use actual rates, demand and setup capacity to choose more qualified referrals or less broad outreach; don't lower qualification to fill the spots.

| Day 28 | Eight qualifying businesses | Audit final-week unprompted use and real-business status. |
| Day 30 | Ten or the honest shortfall | Report approved stores, independently active owners, deliveries, complaints, fee renewals and hours separately. |

A delivery complaint unresolved beyond the store's actual promise is an operating failure, not a growth opportunity. Count and resolve it. If every order requires you to remind the creator, the active-customer number is weaker than the store count suggests. The month 2 test is willingness to keep using the manual workflow at ₹299, not merely accepting a free storefront.
