# Thela — Video scripts

Use the disclosed demo or your own controlled test store. Never record a real buyer's name, UPI details or bank transaction. Label every demonstration; no money moves in the fictional demo.

## A · The actual manual workflow ·60 seconds · creator demo / LinkedIn

0–2 s hook: **“The screenshot isn't the payment.”** Show a fictional screenshot with “Example” across it.2–12 s: open the demo store, text “Fictional. No real payment.” Say: “A creator's offers live behind one link.”12–25 s: choose a sample item and show the order code: “The buyer pays the creator directly and sends the code.”25–40 s: show a synthetic bank-check diagram, not your banking app: “The creator checks the actual bank entry. Thela doesn't verify it automatically.”40–50 s: show the saved delivery reply: “Then the creator sends the file or booking link.”50–60 s: founding price card: “Manual setup. First month free from store launch, then₹299/month. No Thela sales cut.”

## B · Automation honesty ·20 seconds · Reel / X

Hook: **“This store still needs its creator.”**0–5 s: show the manual bank-check and delivery labels.5–13 s: “If you need automatic payment verification and instant file delivery, those aren't in Thela today.”13–20 s: “If you already do that work by DM and want a clearer store link, see the demo.” Hold the URL and current boundaries. Don't hide those lines behind tiny captions.

## C · One offer before ten ·25 seconds · private creator onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“Can a buyer understand this one product?”**2–10 s: show one owned sample guide with what it contains and who it's for.10–18 s: show creator-approved price, delivery time and refund rule. Say: “Write these before adding a second product.”18–25 s: “The first test is a clear offer and a completed real delivery, not a pretty page full of products nobody asked for.”

Bank the approved product page, demo disclosure, unique code and saved reply. Captions plus hard cuts are enough. If you later show a real success, get creator permission and remove all buyer-identifying information.
