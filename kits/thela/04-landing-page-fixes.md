# Thela — Landing-page fixes

The public site and demo were read 29 October 2026. The application points to `#apply`; form submission, real UPI transfer and creator delivery were not performed. The demo clearly disables real payment. Don't describe it as broken for doing so.

## 1. Keep the manual-work boundary beside the hero

**Current headline:** “Stop chasing screenshots. Sell from one link.” The lower page explains manual checks and delivery.

**Write under it:** **“Your own UPI. You check the bank and send the product; we set up the store and saved replies.”** Keep this beside **“Apply as a founding creator”**, not hidden below multiple examples. A buyer-order code is not bank verification.

## 2. Make application status a real next step

**Current:** the CTA scrolls to an application and promises a WhatsApp reply within 2 days.

**Write:** **“Apply for a manually set-up store. We'll review your offer and confirm whether we can onboard you.”** After submission show a clear received state and the actual response window you can meet. Test the form with your own consented contact on a phone; if it doesn't persist/notify you, stop outreach and repair it. Don't collect applications you cannot find again.

## 3. Separate examples from proof at every entry

**Current:** made-up creator examples and a demo store are disclosed as fictional.

**Write on every demo share:** **“Fictional store. No real payment or delivery.”** Keep this attached when recording or screenshotting. Replace examples with real creator proof only after written permission, and don't publish names or buyer records in the kit.

## 4. Put delivery and refunds before the pay instruction

**Current:** demo says delivery follows a bank check and refunds come from the creator.

**Write for a real store:** **“Pay this creator directly. After they confirm the bank payment, expect delivery within the time shown here. Refunds follow this creator's displayed policy.”** Show the creator-approved actual delivery period and refund terms—not a generic 12-hour promise copied to every store. A UPI-ownership check is not product-quality endorsement.

## 5. Keep the flat-fee tradeoff explicit

**Current:** ₹299/month after the first month free, manually paid; paused stores go offline after the grace period.

**Write:** **“First month starts when your store goes live. Then₹299/month by UPI. No auto-debit. No Thela percentage cut.”** Keep the current nonpayment/grace terms linked and readable. Don't compare to a merchant-of-record checkout as if taxes, payment handling and delivery were identical.

## 6. Prioritize the one real order on mobile

**Current:** many creator categories and planned features compete with the actual founding workflow.

**Write above planned features:** **“Available now:store, order code, direct UPI and saved replies. Automation is planned, with no launch date.”** On a 360 px phone verify product→buyer details→code→creator contact without overlap. Test shared preview title **“Thela — a store link for your digital offers.”** No specific mobile or load failure was measured; don't invent one.
