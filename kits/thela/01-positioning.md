# Thela — Positioning

**Live:** https://thela.site/ · **Category:** E-commerce · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/thela

**Ten active creator businesses is the whole job.** Your current founding offer has ten spots. Don't advertise more stores than you can manually set up, and don't count their buyers as your customers.

The live headline is **“Stop chasing screenshots. Sell from one link.”** The CTA **“Apply as a founding creator”** points to the application section. The offer is a first month free from store launch, then **₹299/month**, manually paid by the creator's UPI; no auto-debit and no cut of creator sales. The founder sets stores up by hand. These are current public terms, not proof a live creator has already been onboarded.

## Your first buyer

Choose an independent Indian teacher selling a small existing digital guide or paid call through Instagram DMs. They already have an audience and a product people ask to buy. They are manually checking payments and sending a file or booking link. You are not supplying demand or creating their course.

> Put your digital offers on one page, let buyers pay your own UPI, then check the bank and deliver with a saved reply.

Bio: **A simple storefront for creators who sell by DM.**

The wedge is one small product and one clear delivery promise, not an automated creator business. Your live page explicitly says **payment verification and delivery are still manual**; AutoDM, WhatsApp delivery, GST invoices, card payments and AutoPay are planned. Never pitch those as included.

## Why choose this instead?

[Gumroad's own pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing), checked 29 October 2026, lists 10%+$0.50 for direct-link/profile sales and 30% for marketplace-discovered sales. It is a different checkout/merchant-of-record model, not an interchangeable fee comparator. Thela offers direct creator UPI and a flat monthly fee, but asks the creator to verify and deliver manually. Don't claim tax or refund handling is equivalent.

The cheapest real alternative is the creator's current DM, UPI and saved-reply process without another storefront subscription. Keep that if their volume is tiny. You win when a public offer page and order code reduce confusion enough to justify ₹299—not because you promise fake-payment prevention or guaranteed income.

## The trust boundary

The [demo store](https://thela.site/s/demo/) clearly says nothing is real and no payment goes anywhere. Preserve that disclosure. “Checked by Thela” on a real store should explain exactly what was checked; UPI ownership does not guarantee delivery or product quality. Buyers pay the creator, and refunds come from the creator.

Your ten must be real businesses with their own approved store/product data, independent recent use and a callable responsible contact. An application, a fictional store or a creator whose page you manage without their involvement is not yet one of ten.
