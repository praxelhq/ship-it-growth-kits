# CreatorForge — Landing page fixes

Public offer, samples, pricing and signup checked 29 October 2026. No account/order/payment was created.

1. **Bring delivery timing into the hero.** Current **“Fully Edited. Ready to Post.”** and **Get your first video free** can feel immediate. Add **“Delivered in 2–3 working days for Shorts/reels; 3–5 for long videos. Script approval time is separate.”**
2. **Reconcile revisions.** Homepage FAQ says one revision; Creator/Pro pack cards say two/three. Replace with one authoritative per-pack table and **“Your order shows the included revision rounds and the deadline before you confirm.”** Honor the actual purchased entitlement.
3. **Separate one-time packs from auto-refill.** Current pricing says credits never expire and optional auto-refill saves about 15%. Add **“One-time purchase by default. Auto-refill is optional and must be explicitly selected.”** Only publish this if checkout behaves that way; test it before acquisition.
4. **Prove the whole output.** [Samples](https://creatorforgeforu.tech/samples) lists finance, history and productivity examples. Show a permitted sample brief, approved script, full playable output and included assets. Label studio samples; do not present them as paying clients or endorse factual claims without checking.
5. **Make approval and rights concrete.** Current “Who owns the content?” says the customer does. Add a plain statement matching actual licences for generated visuals, music and voice, and the review responsibility. Do not promise guaranteed platform monetization or copyright clearance from ownership wording alone.
6. **Narrow the free trial.** Replacement helper: **“Choose one YouTube format and one Instagram format. Submit a topic and channel brief; confirm the script before production.”** Show how delivery status and corrections work, not only a signup field.
7. **Mobile and load test.** On a phone, play one sample with captions, open pricing, sign up and recover from an upload/order error. Keep the finished-output preview as the focal point. This inspection did not establish a rendering defect; test rather than claim one.

Gate: one permitted end-to-end real trial can be delivered to the agreed standard and turnaround. Do not scale free orders beyond capacity.
