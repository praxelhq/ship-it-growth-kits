# CreatorForge — Measurement

**Target: 10 real creator businesses in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is an account; activation is a real channel brief accepted and a usable delivered piece reviewed by its creator.

Each counted customer must be a genuine business/working creator, have its own topic/brand brief onboarded, use the workflow independently within the last seven days, and have a privately known contact name/phone. A free trial may qualify; a sample download or invented business does not. One creator business counts once.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/), verify a public page session, mask unpublished scripts and account/order details. Record accounts, real creator businesses, accepted briefs, approved scripts, pieces delivered, revision requests, on-time deliveries and independently returning customers. Private values were not available to this kit; do not assume zero. Record rank, reading and sync timestamp at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/creatorforge).

Events: signup_completed, real_brief_submitted, script_approved, delivery_ready, delivery_accepted, revision_requested, independent_return. Track actual working days including approval pauses separately.

| Review | Input and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 |No outreach yet; delivery gate must pass. |
| Day 14 |16 approaches, 4 suitable trials desired. Few trials: revise niche/brief; don't widen into unreviewable topics. |
| Day 21 |28 approaches, 4 qualifying active businesses desired. Late/poor files: pause new orders and fix fulfilment. |
| Day 30 |40 approaches, 10 qualifying customer target. Report usable deliveries, repeat use, paid packs and achieved count. |

The 02 scenario gives about 7 businesses; accepted referrals or stronger conversion must close the gap. Free pieces produced but never accepted are production cost, not activation.

Sunday review: delivery acceptance, factual corrections, revision burden, actual creator publishing (only when voluntarily shared), retained businesses and hours per finished piece. Do not promise views or monetization. A healthy month is ten creators willing to trust another brief, not forty unused generated files.
