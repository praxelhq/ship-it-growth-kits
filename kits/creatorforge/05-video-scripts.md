# CreatorForge — Video scripts

Record your own screen/voice. You are showing a production service, so include the actual time gap; do not imply a finished file appears instantly.

## A.75-second demo — homepage / LinkedIn
0–2: blank edit timeline; text **“The idea was ready. The video wasn't.”**
2–15: show a synthetic channel brief. Say “Topic, audience, tone and the facts to preserve.”
15–30: show the returned script and one real approval/correction. “The creator checks the story before production.”
30–50: show a short section of actual delivered video, captions and thumbnail. “The file arrives with the assets shown for this format.”
50–65: show delivery status with dates. “Shorts and reels take 2–3 working days; long videos 3–5, with approval time separate.”
65–75: “First piece per platform free to test. creatorforgeforu.tech.”

## B.20-second hook — creator outreach
0–2: note titled “Topic ideas”; text **“Twelve ideas. No upload.”**
2–10: “If editing is the bottleneck, another idea generator will not help.”
10–17: show a real brief and its finished sample.
17–20: “Test the finished work, not the promise.”

## C.25-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: caption with deliberately marked wrong proper name; text **“Ready to post? Check this first.”**
2–15: correct the name and script visibly. Say “AI voice and captions still need human review.”
15–22: play the corrected clip.
22–25: “CreatorForge delivers a file. You keep the final say.”

Burn in captions, hard cuts, no paid effects. Label any deliberately introduced error as a demonstration. Never publish client material without permission.
