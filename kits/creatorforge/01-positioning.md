# CreatorForge — Positioning

**Live:** https://creatorforgeforu.tech/ · **Category:** Creator services · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/creatorforge

Your headline is **“Fully Edited. Ready to Post.”** **Get your first video free** leads to a real signup page. The first YouTube piece and first Instagram piece are advertised free; [pricing](https://creatorforgeforu.tech/pricing) offers one-time packs at ₹499/10 credits, ₹999/55 credits and ₹2,499/150 credits. Optional auto-refill is separate. A post uses 1 credit, a Short/reel 2 and a long video 4.

**Do not sell instant generation.** Your FAQ says 2–3 working days for Shorts/reels/posts and 3–5 for long videos, excluding time awaiting script approval. Homepage and pricing copy also differ on revision entitlement; reconcile those terms before accepting work. Signup opens, but delivery, payment and content rights were not independently tested.

Your first ten are small educational creators who already publish under a business/monetized channel, have a genuine subject to explain, and can review factual accuracy. Start with one niche you can serve well, such as practical software tutorials. Finance/health topics need specialist review; do not scale them because samples happen to show them.

**Pitch:** Give us your topic and channel brief; review a script, then receive an edited explainer ready for your own final check and upload.
**Short version:** A finished explainer from a brief, with approval before production.

[DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) has a free editor; DIY can cost ₹0 in software but requires the creator's work. Your distinction is completing the production workflow, not claiming editing software is expensive. Named freelance alternatives must be quoted for the actual brief; your site's generic market ranges are not independently verified rates.

**Target: 10 real creator businesses**, each with its own brief onboarded and independent recent workflow use. Free downloads, sample viewers and one creator's multiple channels are not ten customers. A video must be suitable for the creator to use, not merely generated.
