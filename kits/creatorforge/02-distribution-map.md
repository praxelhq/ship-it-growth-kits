# CreatorForge — Distribution map

**Direct creator outreach first.** You need ten people who can review a delivered piece, not a launch-day audience that requests unlimited free work.

| Route | Use and permission | Scheduled input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing public creator business sites, found through their own YouTube/Instagram profiles | Choose educational channels with recent original uploads and a visible business contact. Read their work, then ask permission to discuss a single topic. Do not spam comments, scrape addresses or impersonate sponsors. | 10 blocks of 4 creators = 40 unique approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two warm professional introductions | Ask people you actually know for one introduction each to a commercially active creator. | 0–4 conversations; #3. |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 10, 18, 26 at 09:00 IST: brief→approved script→delivered file. Label samples, get permission for real work. | Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 suitable visits per post; #4. |
| [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Optional later, only if signup, delivery capacity and current slot/rules are verified. No vote requests. | No assumed customers; #5. |

Planning assumptions: 40 targeted approaches × 40% conversation × 75% first-piece trial × 60% independently active after delivery ≈ 7 businesses. Ten remains the target; referrals or better observed rates must close the gap. Delivery capacity may be the tighter limit. Do not accept 40 free production orders at once.

Your reserved creator-production window is Days 8–11. Agency brand-guide checks and creator CRM outreach use different lists. No verified relevant newsletter slot or group permission is included; paid coverage is out of budget.

Cap work in progress at the number you can actually deliver within the published turnaround. A queue you cannot fulfil is not distribution success.
