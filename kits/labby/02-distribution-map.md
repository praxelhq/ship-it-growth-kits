# Labby — Distribution map

**Authorized research-record pilots, not a campus signup contest.** The current personal-user offer makes the institutional target a validation/release question. No experiment design, technical protocol advice or claim of university approval belongs in acquisition.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed yield | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm lab/research administration introductions | Ask ten known academic/professional contacts to request an introduction to an authorized lead. Start with synthetic non-sensitive records; no unpublished data or private student lists. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–1 permitted institutional pilot if the product supports it. | #1 |
| 2 · Institutions' own public professional pages | Manually identify a responsible research-record/operations contact, not a demographic list of students. Four first approaches on ten days = 40. Hypothetical 20% response × 40% permitted institutional trial × 50% independent use ≈ 1–2 entities. No unsolicited credential/data requests. | #2 |
| 3 · Lead-approved record-provenance session | A small private synthetic-notebook walkthrough after permission; no institution-wide invitation or implied partnership. It is research feedback unless an actual permitted workflow is onboarded. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show an innocuous observation/source check, not a scientific breakthrough. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 relevant visits, possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Current Monday Launchpad eligibility must be checked. If using a personal-tool launch, keep those registrations separate from the institutional target. Optional 0–100 visits, 0–2 useful conversations, not guaranteed institutions. | #5 |

Specific sources worth listening to are the actual institution's public data/IP policies and responsible researchers' documentation concerns—not private protocols or requests for technical experiments. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) and [ChatGPT's pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) are verified alternative references, not prospect lists.

No free academic newsletter slot, lab-group access or institutional directory promotion right was verified. If a lead requests a free opt-in blurb, use #3 after approval. Base **1–3 institutions** is below ten and can be zero if only personal accounts are supported. Don't try to close the gap by counting each student or researcher at one institution separately.
