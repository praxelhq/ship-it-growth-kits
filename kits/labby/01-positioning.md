# Labby — Positioning

**Live:** https://labby.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/labby

**The assigned target is ten businesses/institutions, but the live offer currently addresses individual researchers.** Keep that mismatch visible. Ten PhD students creating personal accounts are not ten institutional customers. A lab/PI buyer is an outreach hypothesis, not an existing team feature or confirmed purchasing model.

The actual headline is **“Your experiment. One intelligent workspace.”** **“Start Researching Free”** leads to the app/home route. Public pricing is $0 for three active experiments, $12/month Researcher and $29/month Pro. It describes notebooks, calculations, planning, memory and report/poster tools; private behaviour and checkout weren't tested.

## A credible first job

> Keep a non-sensitive research record and its source context together, so the authorized researcher can find and review what was actually observed.

Bio: **Research notes with source context—not AI-generated evidence.**

For an institutional pilot, ask a responsible lab lead whether a small authorized, non-sensitive record-organization task is permitted under their policies. Do not infer shared tenancy, institutional ownership, team permissions or approved storage from personal plans. If the current product only supports individual accounts, report that the ten-institution target isn't supported yet rather than quietly relabelling users.

Start with an innocuous synthetic observation log, not confidential protocols, unpublished results or biological troubleshooting. AI interpretation and generated reports must remain draft material checked by the responsible researcher; the tool must not fabricate observations or citations.

## Alternatives

[Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) supports creation with a Google account; a researcher can maintain a manual observation table without paid AI features. [ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) is a real general-assistant option, so don't claim all competing assistants lack context or source capabilities. Labby's difference must be its actual linked record/notebook workflow, demonstrated on a controlled example. No institutional ELN competitor price was verified.

## The honest weakness

The page's sample research memory and scientific answers demonstrate an intended experience; they aren't proof the model always retrieves or interprets a source correctly. A generated poster isn't a research outcome. Verify record provenance, export and deletion with controlled data before any institutional use. Your ten need real authorized entities, permitted workflows, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact privately.
