# Shelf Test — Landing-page fixes

Read [shelftest.in](https://shelftest.in/) and the actual Test my idea handoff on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Know if your idea will sell before you spend a rupee.”** CTAs **“Test my idea”** / **“Sign up with Google.”** The former opens `/login?next=%2Fnew`, with Clerk development-mode sign-in. No quiz, report, account or payment completed; no public software price/allowance established.

## 1. Replace certainty with the actual first job

Current headline promises knowledge of sales before spending, based on a short quiz.

Replacement: **“Pressure-test the assumptions behind your first product.”** Support: **“Get a structured critique and choose what to investigate next. A quiz cannot prove demand.”**

Keep the approachable quiz entry without pretending the answer came from buyers or live market evidence.

## 2. Explain the authentication boundary

Current Test my idea opens login and development-mode provider UI.

Replacement entry note: **“An account is required for the current test route. Review access and how reports are saved before starting.”**

Test an unfamiliar authorized account, Google/email return, failed auth and `/new` redirect. If restricted, state the pilot eligibility; do not call every sign-in problem a down website.

## 3. Show a permitted sample before private input

Current home describes verdict/first fix/plan but the actual quiz/output are gated.

Replacement link only once built: **“See a fictional test and its limitations.”**

Label inputs/output sample and show which recommendation is an assumption. Never fabricate a working report or imply a static preview is a generated customer result.

## 4. Give the verdict a verification action

Current promise is a straight verdict and the one thing to fix first.

Replacement result note: **“Based on the answers you supplied. Check the assumptions, then choose a buyer conversation or small real test.”**

Verify actual report structure before naming its categories. Do not invent scoring dimensions, expert review, research sources or a profitability model absent from the real product.

## 5. Publish offer and data lifecycle

Current public entry has no software price/free allowance; it says tests/reports private to the account.

Replacement pre-test block: **“Current test allowance and any price. What is stored, who can access it and how to delete/export.”** Fill real current terms after testing; no invented free-forever promise or private-data guarantee.

Check account isolation and deletion with fictional records before confidential business plans.

## 6. Make saved tests and feedback usable

Current navigation includes My tests and Give feedback.

Test actual save/return, empty state, edited answers, failure recovery and feedback destination. Use **“Your tests start empty”** for a fresh account if that is true; no seeded result should look like past business work. Preview title: **“Shelf Test — a first product critique, followed by real evidence.”** No demand certainty or funding claim in the image.
