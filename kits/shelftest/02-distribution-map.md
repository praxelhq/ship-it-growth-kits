# Shelf Test — Distribution map

Lead with the next assumption to test, not “know it will sell.” First-time founders may be easy to find but harder to qualify as real businesses under the course policy.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing first-time D2C operator relationships | Qualify genuine owner-led business work, actual product assumptions and a small launch decision. Keep students exploring fictional concepts separate. | Ten blocks of four businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two founder introductions | Ask a known operator to forward one critique-demo invitation, not disclose private plans or contacts. | Days 21 and 24; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a fictional assumption→question process and the quiz-versus-demand boundary. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One accepted pre-product founder review | Willing real operators inspect a permitted sample and identify one next buyer test. Organizer acceptance required. | Day 23; #5. Existing-trial support, not assumed extra customers. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% actual-own-product setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Those rates are optimistic for first-time founders and assume genuine business qualification plus usable account access. Ten four-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts/intros/session add no guaranteed conversions.

Reserve **Days 21–24** for this pre-product assumptions invitation, under [founder research](../../shared/a2-founder-research.md). PitchPanel owns the Days 10–13 operating-brand pack/price/city clinic; Before needs brands with their own respondent audience; BrandAid is agency briefs; NXT technical complaint research. Do not reuse their same organizer audience or claim every student attendee is a qualifying business. One primary invitation per organizer/week.

[Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) and [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) are organizer-discovery entry points verified in the shared research, not booked workshops or approved mailing lists. No newsletter/community promotion permission is established. Ask the organizer before a group message; stop after decline. One relevant follow-up after seven days is enough.

No paid listings, purchased founder numbers, automated DMs, forced referrals or funding promises. Development-mode account access is a product gate to test, not evidence the entire site is down. If new owners cannot use/save their tests, pause acquisition and repair the exact route rather than collecting empty signups.
