# Shelf Test — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each genuine consumer-product business must meet [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): own actual permitted product data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; real operation; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identities outside this public repo. Student idea exploration and fictional examples remain separate, even if useful.

Activation: the owner completes a real own-product critique and identifies an assumption/next evidence-gathering action from the actual output. Report that beside customer totals. A verdict, quiz score, workshop attendance or account without own data is not evidence of demand or a qualifying business.

No private account records were accessed; actual business count, quiz availability and saved tests are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real businesses / latest independent use | Actual account/test records plus private permitted owner register. |
| Usable account access and current offer | Authorized unfamiliar-user test and configured allowance, not CTA inference. |
| Actual own-product tests completed/saved | Product records, excluding seeded/fictional samples. |
| Assumptions/next buyer questions | Consented aggregate feedback, not private launch plans in growth logs. |
| Auth/report/deletion failures | Synthetic reproductions and issue categories. |
| Price/payment and course reading | Actual configured offer if any, [profile](https://shipped.money/p/shelftest), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude quiz answers, private prices/costs, plans, reports, accounts and access tokens from replay. Minimal events can record ownteststarted, owntestcompleted, reportsaved, assumptionreviewed, laterindependentuse only where supported. A clicked test button is not a completed report or validated idea.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. Fix access or certainty confusion before recruiting a broad group. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare real own-data tests with student/fictional trials; no relabelling to fill the count. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent use and actual evidence-gathering questions, not model praise. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests and verify genuine owner-led businesses. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying businesses against ten, activation, independent use, access/offer scope, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts/two introductions/one conditional review are upside/support only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. Genuine-business qualification and development access may reduce conversion substantially, even to zero; record that limitation rather than count classmates with imaginary startups.

A one-off critique may be useful while failing final-week independent use. Report it separately without prompting an artificial login. No quiz proves sales, market demand, product-market fit, saved investment or investor readiness. Publish only permitted actual usability observations with dates and limitations; no invented founder testimonials.
