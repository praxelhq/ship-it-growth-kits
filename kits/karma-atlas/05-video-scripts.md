# Karma Atlas — Video scripts

Use labelled sample answers/cards. No genuine private answers, birth dates or invented sold-edition claims.

## A. Quiz to creature — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“This isn't your actual past life.”**

2–12: Show the fictional/sample quiz. “Karma Atlas turns your choices into an illustrated creature story.”

12–25: Show a sample reveal and its explanation. “The fun is whether the story feels like your instincts, not whether a tool can prove reincarnation.”

25–35: Show free/paid boundary. “The personal teaser is free; the fuller story and two cards are ₹99 for that attempt if you choose.”

35–45: “Your answers stay out of the download. Share a card only if you want to.” URL. Use on homepage and listing.

## B. The card — 15 seconds

0–2: **“A creature version of your group-chat energy.”**

2–9: Reveal a labelled fictional card. “Illustrated fiction, not a verdict about who you are.”

9–15: “Free teaser after 19 questions. Optional ₹99 full reading. mittaldivyang.site.” First short clip.

## C. What ₹99 means — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Paying again doesn't promise a different creature.”**

2–12: Show the actual repeat-attempt explanation. “A purchase unlocks one attempt's story and cards. Another attempt can match the same creature.”

12–20: “Read the terms, try the teaser first, choose for yourself.” URL. Second clip; no scarcity countdown.

## D. No confession contest — 20 seconds

0–2: **“You can skip that question.”**

2–12: Show a fictional question and skip option if actually supported. “This is a story game. A skipped question doesn't lower your worth; animals aren't punishments.”

12–20: “Share the card if you want. Keep the answers yours.” URL. Third clip; don't invent a working skip control—record the actual current flow.
