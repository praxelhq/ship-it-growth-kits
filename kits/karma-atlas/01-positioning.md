**Sell an illustrated fiction, not a revelation about reincarnation.** The [live FAQ](https://mittaldivyang.site/), checked 29 October, explicitly says the reading cannot determine past/next lives or spiritual worth. Keep that honesty above the purchase, not only at the bottom.

Meet that creature.”** CTA: **“Discover my other lives.”** Nineteen questions lead to a free creature teaser; **₹99 per attempt** unlocks the fuller reading and two downloadable cards. The kit opened the public page, not a personal quiz checkout. The FAQ says personal previews become labelled demos when checkout is unconfigured; don't assume payments are enabled from the page alone.

## The first player

An adult who enjoys a personality quiz and sending friends a beautifully odd creature card. They want “that is very me” entertainment, not religious authority or a score of moral value.

**Pitch:** Answer a strange little quiz and meet a fictional creature version of yourself.

**Bio:** Nineteen questions. One creature glimpse. Entirely imaginative.

Your wedge is the card reveal and the reasoning that fits the answers. The public owl/mammoth cards are samples, not evidence of actual paid inventory. The edition has a stated cap of 100 numbered copies per creature; availability is not guaranteed at checkout, and another paid attempt can return the same creature. Never imply investment value, resale income or a guaranteed rare card.

[16Personalities' free test](https://www.16personalities.com/free-personality-test) is a real free quiz alternative; it does a different assessment job, and you shouldn't borrow its scientific appearance. A friend inventing “which animal are you?” is another no-cost substitute. Your advantage is original illustrated fiction and a thoughtful share artifact, not predictive accuracy.

Target remains **1,000 signups**. For this no-account experience, count unique genuine quiz sessions, alongside people completing the questions and seeing a free personal teaser. Paid readings are a separate conversion, not extra users. Keep questionnaire answers out of social sharing.
