# Karma Atlas — Landing-page fixes

The [page and FAQ](https://mittaldivyang.site/) were opened 29 October. The public entry action reaches `#begin`; personal preview, payment and saved collection were not submitted/tested by this kit.

## 1. Put fiction beside the provocative headline

Current H1: **“You've been something else. Meet that creature.”** Add **“A fictional creature reading shaped by your answers — not a claim about reincarnation or spiritual worth.”** beside **“Discover my other lives.”** The FAQ already draws this boundary; don't make visitors scroll to find it.

## 2. Make the free/paid boundary clear before the quiz

Current page offers a free teaser and ₹99 full reading. Use **“Take the quiz → see a free creature glimpse → optionally unlock this attempt's full story and two cards for ₹99.”** Don't let “₹99 per attempt” imply even viewing the free teaser requires payment.

If checkout is unconfigured, label personal preview **“Demo preview — purchases unavailable”** and remove purchase language for that state. Test the actual configuration before distribution.

## 3. Explain repeat purchases and edition allocation plainly

The FAQ says each attempt is a separate ₹99 purchase, cards may repeat, and numbered copies aren't guaranteed. Put those facts beside paid confirmation. Replacement: **“This payment unlocks only these answers/cards. A different creature or numbered edition is not guaranteed.”** Never present remaining stock unless backed by actual verified-payment inventory.

## 4. Make private input expectations visible

The site says the birthday is for age checking and not saved, previews are processed without saving answers, and paid fulfilment can save name/answers/reading privately. Show that distinction before the relevant send action. Don't collapse it into “nothing ever leaves your phone.” Verify the implementation before repeating the promise.

## 5. Give the reveal a useful explanation, not only a card

Keep sample owl/mammoth clearly labelled. Show how one fictional answer leads to one story trait without exposing real people's confessions. Replacement label: **“A creative interpretation of your choices, not a diagnosis.”** Preserve skipping without a penalty; don't use moral pressure to reduce abandonment.

## 6. Protect the shared artifact

Downloaded/shared cards should omit answers and birthday by default. Give the user an actual preview of what leaves the page. Metadata: **“Karma Atlas — a fictional creature quiz”**, description **“Free teaser, optional ₹99 full reading per attempt. Illustrated entertainment, not a past-life claim.”** Test on a phone and slow connection; the quiz and result should survive an image failing to load.
