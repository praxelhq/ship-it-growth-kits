# Roamio — Distribution map

**Lead with people already planning a trip.** Inspiration viewers are less useful than ten travelers who need to reopen a folder tomorrow.

| Route | Scheduled action / boundary | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing trip planners | Ask about an upcoming plan; use fictional dates/destination for public feedback. No tickets/passports sent to you. | 10 trials, 5–10 reopened folders if useful. | #1 |
| Three existing alumni/friend travel groups | Admin-approved single invitation; no generic travel spam or booking promise. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account clips | Folder creation, return, storage limits and a real organization lesson on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–80 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | A practical planning/return-path lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–24 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback after create/save/reopen tests; current rules/slot checked, no votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No travel-agent commission, paid destination page, influencer or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. A free alumni newsletter mention is optional only after permission, zero forecast until accepted. The accounts worth replying to are people discussing organizing a trip; don't present an app link as current visa, safety or fare advice.

At 50% visitor-to-first-folder-session, 1,000 needs 2,000 relevant unique visits. Small tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Replace assumptions after three clips. Keep this separate from Magnetos' savings goals, NxtMood's sample experience story and Shotgun's roles/split; don't send several pitches to one friend group.
