# The Woof Chapter — Video scripts

Use your own pet-safe example or a clearly invented pet, and record the current prototype. Do not fake automatic photo matching, a finished purchase or an export you have not actually tested. Keep other people out of reference photos.

## A. One quirk, one draft — 50 seconds

**Use:** homepage and adult creative-session invitation.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Harmless pet example. Text: “The empty bowl is the plot.” | “Give the quirk a plot.” |
| 3–15s | Open /comic-studio and enter a short idea. | “In The Woof Chapter, I start with the quirk and give it a beginning, a twist and a punchline.” |
| 15–32s | Make real draft and manual character edits. | “These character controls are manual in the current prototype. I can work on the story before expecting a finished piece.” |
| 32–45s | Hold the prototype and test-checkout labels. | “Automatic photo artwork, films and avatars are still being built. Test checkout does not buy a finished comic.” |
| 45–50s | Studio URL. | “Try the current studio, and tell me what is unclear.” |

## C. What exists today — 25 seconds

**Use:** Peerlist and maker post.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Text: “Prototype, not a finished comic shop.” | “This is a prototype, not a finished comic shop.” |
| 4–16s | Actual draft editor and manual character controls. | “You can work on pet stories and manual character settings. Automatic photo artwork, films and avatars are still being built.” |
| 16–22s | Test-checkout wording. | “Checkout is test mode. It does not buy a finished comic.” |
| 22–25s | Studio URL. | “Try what is available today.” |

Use captions, hard cuts and normal-speed edits. If a supported preview fails, fix it rather than substituting a staged successful result. Do not include third-party AI processing in a privacy promise that says photos never leave the device.
