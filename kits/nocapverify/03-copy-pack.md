## #4 — Public methodology post

A creator price estimate isn't a platform API metric.

NoCap Verify needs to keep those labels clear: source data, a calculated ratio, a model estimate and an unknown are different things. A report ID or a polished score doesn't prove a creator is fraudulent or guarantee a campaign result.

I'm testing whether a brand buyer can inspect the evidence behind a shortlist, and correcting the product's own wording where it overstates certainty. The examples I share are synthetic or authorized.
