# Nocapverify — Video scripts

Use synthetic creator examples or explicit permission for public material. No unsubstantiated fraud accusation, private analytics, campaign budget or promised return. Show real supported labels, not staged verification.

## A · Which kind of number is this? ·60 seconds · brand-buyer call

0–2 s hook: **“Did the API say that—or is it an estimate?”** Show a synthetic report field. 2–15 s: inspect a real supported source/timestamp: “Observed data needs a source and time.”15–30 s: show the calculation/estimate separately: “A ratio or model projection is not another raw platform fact.”30–45 s: show missing information: “Unavailable data should stay unavailable.”45–55 s: review the shortlist without a fraud verdict: “The buyer still checks fit and the actual offer.”55–60 s: “Evidence and uncertainty before the campaign decision. NoCap Verify.”

## B · A score isn't a fraud finding ·20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“A low ratio doesn't prove bought followers.”**0–8 s: show a clearly synthetic score/ratio. 8–15 s: “Ask what was measured, what wasn't available and what other explanations exist.”15–20 s: “Don't turn a heuristic into a public accusation.”

## C · A price estimate isn't a quote ·25 seconds · onboarding

Hook 0–2 s: **“The creator didn't quote this number.”**2–12 s: show an estimated-rate label. 12–20 s: “Scope, rights, timing and the actual creator offer still need direct confirmation.”20–25 s: “Use estimates to frame a question, not claim a deal.” No invented market rate or ROI.

If live source verification doesn't work, show the limitation and fix it before promotion. Keep sample labels readable in every cropped frame.
