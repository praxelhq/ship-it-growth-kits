# Nocapverify — Positioning

**Live:** https://nocapverify.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/nocapverify

**Your ten are brand/agency businesses checking a real creator shortlist—not ten creators being scored.** Start with a small brand-side campaign buyer who already has two candidates and wants to inspect the evidence before committing. This is a diligence aid, not proof somebody bought followers or a guarantee of campaign return.

The actual headline is **“Discover Creators with AI.”** **“Plan Your Campaign”** leads to `/campaign`, where the page asks the user to sign in/create a free account. The homepage says everything is currently free, but [pricing](https://nocapverify.in/pricing) lists ₹0 Free and **₹999/month Pro** for planner/competitor tools. Resolve that entitlement/price conflict before inviting a paid workflow. No account/report/API response/checkout was independently tested.

## The useful narrow promise

> Put the source, timestamp, calculations and uncertainties behind two shortlisted creators where a brand buyer can review them.

Bio: **Inspect creator evidence before the campaign decision.**

The first job is a controlled comparison using permitted public information, not automatic discovery across every creator on two platforms. Don't publish a “fake follower” accusation from a heuristic or a sample trust score.

## The methodology contradiction

[How We Verify](https://nocapverify.in/how-we-verify) says every number is direct API data, no estimates and no proprietary trust scores. The homepage offers creator-price estimates, ROI projections and a 0–100 Trust Score. Those cannot all be described as raw API facts. Clearly separate observed API fields, derived ratios, model estimates and unknown data. A report ID doesn't independently validate its contents.

The manual alternative is the buyer's existing source check and a [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/); a general [ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) workflow can assist with permitted notes but isn't a verification authority. No competing influencer-platform price was verified. You win only if the reviewer can inspect the actual evidence more easily—not by claiming all other sources are fake.

## Keep the target honest

The sample report and zero-looking homepage counters don't establish actual customers or report quality. Ten customers require real businesses, their own permitted campaign-review workflow, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact privately. A creator profile and a campaign are data objects, not customers. If the method/entitlement gate isn't fixed, pause promotion rather than exaggerating trust.
