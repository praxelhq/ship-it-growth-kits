# Nocapverify — Measurement

**Target:10 real brand/agency businesses.** Activation beside it: **buyers independently reviewing source-labelled creator evidence for their own permitted campaign decision**. Creator profiles, report IDs and score generation aren't customer counts.

## Day 0

Query actual businesses, successful report/source reviews, corrected claims, missing-field rates and independent returns. Static sample/counters establish no baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, excluding private campaign/creator information. Capture actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/nocapverify) rank, saved metric and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Confirm actual entitlement/billing before reporting ₹999 plan receipts.

Events: `business_onboarded`, `report_completed`, `source_opened`, `estimate_label_reviewed`, `shortlist_reviewed` and `business_returned`. A generated trust score isn't proof of fraud; projectedROI isn't realised return; an estimated rate isn't a creator quote. Keep these separate in dashboards and customer stories.

## Four tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a real business, its own permitted actual campaign-review workflow onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible person's name/callable contact held privately. Paying isn't required. Several buyers at one agency are one business. A creator being analysed isn't automatically your customer.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × hypothetical 25% response ×50% permitted trial ×50% independent reuse ≈2–3 businesses. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 2–5 is below ten and can be zero until the method/entitlement gate passes.** These aren't measured influencer-tool acquisition rates. Recompute after 20 approaches from actual independent source review.
