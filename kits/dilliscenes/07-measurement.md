# DilliScenes — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real accounts. Report **genuine RSVPs that lead to actual attendance** beside them. A public listing, a “going” click, repeat RSVP or host test is not another acquired user or proof an event happened.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/dilliscenes) traffic/rank. Establish actual accounts, verified willing hosts, published real plans, RSVPs, withdrawals, completed events, attendance and reports. Public card counts alone don't establish these totals. This kit doesn't know private figures; use **not measured**, not assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and exclude/mask emails, private locations, guest lists and reports. Keep organizer/contact information privately. Attendance can be checked with a simple consented aggregate; no forced photo evidence or participant tracking.

## Funnel

`account_created → real_plan_viewed → genuine_RSVP → plan_updates_seen → event_attended → later_event_wanted`.

Track host-side event completion separately. A canceled event can be handled well, but doesn't count as attended. A women-only tag or a report button isn't evidence of safety or eligibility enforcement by itself.

## Honest scale

`02` schedules three host conversations, 20 individual invitees, two circles and two clips, suggesting roughly 13–38 accounts before overlap. At hypothetical 25% qualified-visit-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 4,000 qualified visits, plus enough real events to make activation useful. The current plan doesn't establish that supply or reach.

| Review | Check | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Real host, arrangements and working event/RSVP/change path. | Don't invite guests until the plan is real. |
| Day 14 | Accounts→RSVPs and clarity of cost/time/place. | Fix repeated confusion before a second circle. |
| Day 21 | Actual attendance, no-shows, cancellations and reports. | Improve host reliability and consent before adding reach. |
| Day 28 | Completed events and willingness to return. | Keep the source yielding real gatherings, not curiosity clicks. |
| Day 30 | New accounts, attended RSVPs, hosts and target gap separately. | Report capacity and next month's feasible experiment. |

These are selected product checks, not safety or friendship guarantees. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings and repeated event views aren't additive users.
