# Footholds — Distribution map

**Personal adult task tests and own-feed demonstrations first.** No additional shared campus habit clinic: the cohort already has separate deadline, anchor, humorous-start and campaign products. Your contacts should want a visible short focus session, not another pledge.

| Route | Exact action / boundary | Hypothetical result and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Known adult students/builders | Five opt-in invitations on days 4,7,10,13,16,19 = **30**. Ask them to choose a non-sensitive ordinary task and judge the smallest useful session. No disability/diagnosis targeting or pressure to reveal a task. | 30 × 50% try × 60% start usable session = nine first sessions; half complete an action ≈ four to five activations. #1 |
| 2 · Own LinkedIn/X feed | Four posts on days 5,12,19,26. Show your actual timer/task result or a clearly labelled demonstration. Follow [LinkedIn professional policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies), manual relevant replies only. | Four × 200–600 impressions × 2% visit × 40% usable session ≈ 6–19 sessions. #2, #3 |
| 3 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Official site advertises Monday Launchpad. Verify current slot/eligibility for a Claude-hosted artifact; show exactly what visitors can try without authentication. No vote exchange. | One optional launch, 0–100 visits × 40% usable session = 0–40 sessions. #4 |
| 4 · [Product Hunt guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) | Optional after persistence and platform-gate tests. Inspect existing listing first; a hosted artifact must be described accurately. Don't imply an independent native app or ask for upvotes. | Zero base forecast. #4 adapted |
| 5 · Returning tester introduction | Up to three requests on day 24, only after independent use. They ask a relevant adult friend whether the link is wanted; no member/contact list exchange. | Zero guaranteed contribution; report actual sessions. #5 |

[Loop's official site](https://loophabits.org/) is a comparison/learning source, not a place to promote under support requests. No specific productivity subreddit rule, newsletter placement or private Discord permission was verified; they are not scheduled. A useful answer can offer a manual five-minute task idea without linking your artifact or promising it will treat a condition.

Base **15–68 first usable sessions before overlap**, from thirty invitations, four posts and at most one optional launch. This doesn't reach 1,000. The next step is evidence that people return after the novelty and can access the actual required features, not more imaginary focus-room members or a paid plan that is still a demo.
