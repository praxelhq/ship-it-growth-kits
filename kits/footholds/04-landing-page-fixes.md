# Footholds — Landing-page fixes

Actual rendered Claude artifact inspected 29 October 2026, including Today, Level up, Focus and Buddy. No habit/timer/chat/upgrade was executed. The roster's generic Claude metadata isn't the product description; the artifact is inspectable and live.

## 1. Distinguish demo pricing from an actual sale

**Current:** Plus $4.99/month, “Try 7 days free,” then “Demo mode: no money changes hands.”

**Write on the action:** **“Preview Plus — demo only, no charge.”** Helper: **“This prototype does not start a paid subscription.”** Preserve the displayed future-price context if useful, but don't make a visitor hunt for the no-money line. Before real billing, verify price, renewal, trial, cancellation and entitlement separately. A demo unlock isn't revenue.

## 2. Give a new visitor a product headline

**Current:** “Hey, night owl” and “Let's start ridiculously small,” with “How's the brain battery?” choices.

**Write supporting first task:** **“Choose one tiny ordinary action for the next five minutes.”** Helper: **“Pick a preset or make your own. A small action counts; you can stop.”** Keep the greeting as personality, not the explanation. Show a non-medical example such as **“Put five things away.”** Don't lead with a medication preset or promise an outcome beyond the user's actual action.

## 3. Put platform gates beside the promised feature

**Current:** Free lists focus room and buddy chats, but Focus says the room is unavailable here and Buddy requires Claude sign-in.

**Write on the cards:** **“Focus timer: check current availability. Shared room and Claude buddy require supported signed-in access.”** Replace that helper with the exact tested distinction. Make **“Unavailable in this session”** explicit; don't populate synthetic participants as real people. Test a legitimate signed-in session before claiming a live room/chat works.

## 4. Explain persistence before a habit is added

**Current:** Claude's wrapper asks sign-in to see artifact data; the public interface doesn't establish which habit records survive reload or move across devices.

**Write while persistence remains unverified:** **“Try a disposable task first. Return-session storage and recovery are still being checked.”** After testing, replace that notice with the actual supported storage location and limits. Explain reset/export/deletion and access limits that actually exist. Until known, invite a disposable non-sensitive test, not a month of private habits. Do not market browser-local or cloud-sync certainty from the wrapper alone.

## 5. Keep attention language practical

**Current Focus:** “Time blindness is real” and body-doubling language can sound like a demonstrated clinical intervention.

**Write:** **“See how much time remains in this short session.”** Room helper, if working: **“Optional shared focus space. Choose whether your task is visible.”** No clinical or guaranteed productivity result. Missed-day passes and streaks are game rules, not evidence the user made progress or recovered a condition.

## 6. Verify first-use and phone recovery

**Current:** “Start focusing” offers five/ten/25-minute durations, but a completed run was not tested here.

**Write failure copy:** **“This session didn't start. Your task isn't marked complete.”** Match actual state behaviour. Test start/pause/end, reload, backgrounding, keyboard focus and 360px inside the artifact shell. Suggested preview: **“Footholds — a tiny action and a short focus session.”** No measured load or mobile defect is claimed; don't hide Claude-hosted access behind a standalone-app label.
