# Picture Abhi Baaki Hai — Video scripts

Record a real successful recommendation on a phone before using these. Hide account notifications; no film clips or copyrighted montage required. If a provider result is wrong, fix or explain it instead of editing around the failure.

## A. Tonight's pick — 40 seconds, homepage/film-society invite

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Your empty watch decision. “Still browsing?” | “Still browsing instead of watching?” |
| 3–14 s | Open onboarding; select actual services. | “Start with the OTTs you already use, then the mood you are in.” |
| 14–27 s | Show a real result and its explanation. | “Picture Abhi Baaki Hai aims to find a film outside your usual watchlist.” |
| 27–35 s | Open actual provider destination. | “Check where it is available in your region before you settle in.” |
| 35–40 s | URL and free/no-signup text. | “Try it free. No signup needed.” |
