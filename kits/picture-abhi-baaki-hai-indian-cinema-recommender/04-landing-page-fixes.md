# Picture Abhi Baaki Hai — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Prove the result, not just the doorway

Current headline **“Discover Indian cinema you’ve never heard of.”** and CTA **“Begin my film education”** lead to `/onboarding`. That route rendered **“First, a short audition.”** One detail route stayed at **“Adjusting the aspect ratio of your soul…”** in extraction. Test onboarding→pick→provider on a clean phone before publicity. Do not infer a universal outage from that extraction.

If result loading fails, keep traffic private until repaired. Use **“We couldn’t load this pick. Try again or browse by language.”** with working retry/browse actions, not an endless joke loader.

## 2. Make the evening task clearer

Keep the current headline if people understand it. Add **“Choose your mood and streaming services. Find one film outside your usual watchlist.”** Test a button **“Find tonight’s film”** against the current playful one, using completed picks rather than clicks alone. Keep **“Free · no signup”** visible throughout if true.

## 3. Explain why and where

On every actual result, add **“Why this pick fits”**, the real mood/service rationale, language and subtitle information only where verified. Show **“Availability checked for your region on the date shown. Confirm on the provider.”** Use real dates and region values; do not print this as a claim if no check exists.

## 4. Separate support from watching

A Razorpay support link exists. Label it **“Support this project”** if that is its actual purpose; explain that it is optional and does not buy streaming rights. Avoid making the provider or donation link look like the recommender's signup.

## 5. Mobile, previews and truth

The existing title/description clearly describe Indian-film discovery. Keep that, rather than unverified film-count claims. Check OTT multi-select, back navigation, result reload, long titles and provider links on a narrow phone. Use your own product captures or authorised assets. Do not add fictitious quotes or streaming partner badges.
