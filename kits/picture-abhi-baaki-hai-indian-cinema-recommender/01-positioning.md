## Your product does not currently ask for signup

On 29 October 2026 the actual headline is **“Discover Indian cinema you’ve never heard of.”** The button is **“Begin my film education”**, followed by **“Free · no sign-up · no tote bag required”**. It goes to `/onboarding`, which opens **“First, a short audition.”** and asks for OTTs. Genre, language, director and actor browsing are also linked. One public film-detail link remained at a loading message in extraction; that is not proof it fails in a real browser, and not evidence that a recommendation was completed.

Keep the assigned **1,000 consumer signups**. The existing target policy counts a started session when a product needs no account. Here count one meaningful first cinema-discovery session: a new person starts onboarding and selects a real preference, not merely opens the homepage. Report activation beside it: completed onboarding plus a recommendation and source/provider destination inspected. Deduplicate repeat attempts where consented measurement permits, disclose anonymous limits and exclude your own tests. No forced login is needed.

## Start with a specific evening

Your first user is an adult who wants one unfamiliar Indian film for tonight, is open to subtitles and already knows which streaming services they have. Start with “one film outside your usual language”, not every cinema fan or a promise to know all Indian cinema.

**Pitch:** Tell us your mood and OTTs; find an Indian film outside your usual watchlist.

**Bio:** A different Indian film for tonight. Free, no signup.

[Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/about/) is a real film diary/list/discovery alternative; its public About page does not establish the current paid price, so do not quote one. The user's existing streaming search has no extra discovery-tool charge, though its subscription price varies. Your wedge is narrowing the choice with mood, services and Indian-language discovery. Prove a real useful pick and verify its availability; do not claim exclusive streaming rights, guaranteed platform accuracy or that the film is free to watch.

Homepage industry counts and film stills are not independent evidence for market size or permission to reuse imagery in ads. Avoid repeating those counts. A recommendation also is not a full film stream. Your honest weakness is availability changing by country and time; show the date/region and send people to the actual provider to confirm.
