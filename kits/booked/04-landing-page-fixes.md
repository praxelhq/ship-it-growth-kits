# booked — Landing-page fixes

The public sign-in/create-account surface was read 29 October 2026. Rendered retrieval required login; direct HTML confirmed the entry. No authenticated functionality or price was established. Don't call the site down.

## 1. Show the actual first job before the account gate

**Current:** “Your brand work, sorted.” with sign-in/create-account actions and three feature bullets.

**Write:** **“Track one paid job's deliverables, revisions and payment status.”** Show a labelled synthetic walkthrough of only features that work today. A prospective creator should understand the job before making an account. If those features aren't functional, state a design-partner pilot rather than a released product.

## 2. Explain account creation and current terms

**Current CTA:** “Create account” is visible; public pricing and private-data terms weren't established by this research.

**Write beside it:** **“See the current plan and data terms before adding client work.”** Publish the actual price, trial limit and renewal/cancellation rules once verified. Don't invent free-forever access or a subscription amount. Test fresh account → synthetic job → revision/status update → return on another device only if supported. A login form alone doesn't prove the loop.

## 3. Make recorded payment status different from collecting money

**Current:** “Know exactly who still owes you money.”

**Write:** **“Track the payment status you confirm.”** Unless a live payment/accounting integration is actually verified, don't imply automatic bank reconciliation, debt collection or money movement. Mark example amounts fictional and keep private client financial records out of demos.

## 4. Show revision scope rather than a vague counter

**Current:** “Revision rounds tracked, no more lost feedback.”

**Write:** **“Record the requested change, round and next due date.”** Use a real supported record and explain whether feedback is entered manually. Don't imply clients collaborate inside the product or approve legally binding terms if that hasn't been tested.

## 5. Clarify ownership and data handling

**Current:** the public sign-in surface gives little evidence of storage/access controls.

**Write:** **“Use only client material you're authorized to process. Read who can access your records and how export/deletion work.”** Publish actual controls after verification; don't assert local-only storage, encryption or tenant isolation from a login screen. Begin demos with synthetic data.

## 6. Make the phone task obvious

**Current:** the entry makes account access the first decision.

**Write the main demonstration around:** **“What's due next?”** Show one readable deliverable, revision and status at 360px. Test keyboard, large text, errors and actual share preview. Suggested preview: **“booked — the fulfilment record for paid creator work.”** No clipping/load failure was measured. This is evidence work, not a request for a speculative visual redesign.
