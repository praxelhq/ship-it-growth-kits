# booked — Positioning

**Live:** https://booked.social/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/booked

**Your target is ten real solo creator businesses.** Start with a paid UGC creator handling several deliverables for an existing brand, not someone seeking their first follower or first sponsorship. Their current job is fulfilment after the deal—not CreatorDeal's usage-rights review or Kreator's pre-invoice pipeline.

The actual accessible entry says **“Your brand work, sorted.”** It offers **“Sign in”** and **“Create account”**, then claims: every deliverable/deadline in one place, revision rounds tracked, and visibility into money owed. Rendered fetches required login; direct public HTML confirmed the sign-in/create-account surface. **That is an access boundary, not proof the site is down.** No current public price, authenticated workflow, team sharing or payment processing was verified.

## The first offer

> Keep one real UGC job's deliverables, revision rounds and payment status in a record you can check before the next brand reply.

Bio: **The fulfilment checklist behind paid creator work.**

Treat this as a pilot hypothesis to verify after signup. Don't promise automatic brand reminders, invoice generation, accounting or multi-user approvals from three login-page bullets. The first controlled test should use a synthetic brand job; then an authorized creator can decide whether to enter real data under the actual terms.

## The alternative already works

[Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) has a $0 individual tier with limits, and [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace. A creator can track deadlines and revisions manually there. Your product wins only if the creator's next job is easier to maintain without configuring a general database. Do not invent a creator-CRM competitor's price or pretend a free checklist doesn't exist.

## The honest weakness

The public page doesn't let a buyer inspect the product's real fulfilment flow or price before account creation. Fix that evidence gap before sending broad traffic. Private access is not necessarily broken, but it prevents this research from claiming the features passed a working test.

Count a creator only if they are an actual business, onboard their own permitted real job, independently use it during the final seven days and have a responsible callable contact held privately. One creator with ten deliverables is one business. If the current app can't support the core job, keep acquisition gated and say the target isn't supported yet.
