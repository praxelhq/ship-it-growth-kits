## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. For the observed no-account reading flow, report eligible first reading sessions separately from optional accounts. Do not create an artificial account wall just to call readers signups. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Prefer Days 10–13 for the first shared-campus reading invitation. Narra is a transformation tool; Footnotes is a curated reading experience. Coordinate with Infolio and C 1’s reading products so the organizer chooses one clear session, not several similar broadcasts.
