# Footnotes — Video scripts

Use your own screen recording and phone microphone. Keep the prose readable, show only a short excerpt with the correct edition attribution, and leave the reading pace natural. Do not invent a saved-progress or upload demonstration.

## A. First episode — 45 seconds

**Use:** homepage, reading-circle invitation, Peerlist.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Phone opens the direct White Nights episode. Text: “One episode before another scroll?” | “Try one episode first.” |
| 3–15s | Hold the title and a readable opening excerpt. | “This is White Nights in Footnotes. It opens in your browser, free, without an account for the sample.” |
| 15–30s | Scroll slowly through part of the actual episode. Text: “Read at your pace.” | “I wanted a smaller way into a book I would otherwise keep saving for later.” |
| 30–40s | Hold the real next-episode action. | “At the end, you choose whether you want the next part. One episode is not a whole book finished.” |
| 40–45s | Hold the direct sample URL or a tested short on-screen label. | “Start with the first episode at footnotesapp.in.” |

## B. The continuation — 20 seconds

**Use:** Short or Reel.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Library card, then cut directly into the episode. | “Start the book.” |
| 3–12s | A legible section at normal scroll speed. | “Footnotes puts classics into short episodes you can read on your phone.” |
| 12–17s | Actual next-episode link. | “Want another? That is your choice.” |
| 17–20s | URL and “Free public sample”. | “Try White Nights, free.” |

## C. What you count — 25 seconds

**Use:** LinkedIn maker post.

| Time | Picture / on-screen text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Episode label. Text: “An episode, not a whole book.” | “This is one episode, not a whole novel.” |
| 4–14s | Readable text, then the continuation action. | “For Footnotes, I care whether someone finishes a passage and chooses another.” |
| 14–22s | First-episode route, no invented dashboard. | “A catalogue view does not tell me that. The actual reading does.” |
| 22–25s | URL. | “Try the first episode.” |

Capture the title, a short readable passage, the episode label and continuation link once. Use hard cuts and captions. Do not speed up the text to imply a measured reading time.
