# Footnotes — Landing-page fixes

Read against [the live page](https://footnotesapp.in/) on 29 October 2026. The current headline is **“Read books the way you scroll reels.”**; the action is **“One more chapter (we both know it won’t be one) →”**. The current page metadata says the classic-story episodes are free to read. No paid plan or subscription price was shown in the checked reading flow. This audit records visible copy and inspected routes; it does not claim a signed-in workflow, payment or device test was completed.

Your visitor is a late-night phone reader deciding whether to enter one story. Give that person one obvious next action and a real example of the result. Keep the product's own visual character; fix the task hierarchy before adding decorative sections.

## Fix 1 — Give a clear first read

**Current:** The library opens with story hooks, including the short line “They have four nights.”

**Write this instead:**

> Start White Nights · Episode 1 · read it now, no account needed in this public sample.

**Verify:** Use a real direct episode route, not a generic signup detour.

## Fix 2 — Use the time claim carefully

**Current:** Metadata describes sixty-second episodes.

**Write this instead:**

> Short episodes for the time you have. Read at your own pace.

**Verify:** Measure actual reading time before publishing a typical duration; avoid forcing timed scrolling.

## Fix 3 — Make continuation clear

**Current:** The reader has a next-episode action and Episode 1/78 label.

**Write this instead:**

> Continue to Episode 2, or return to the book page.

**Verify:** Test the next route and back navigation on a narrow phone screen.

## Fix 4 — Label the edition

**Current:** The product presents classic text and author names.

**Write this instead:**

> Source edition and translation: open the details before reading or downloading.

**Verify:** Supply actual edition/translation attribution and rights information, not a blanket public-domain claim.

## Fix 5 — Do not oversell upload

**Current:** The library includes an upload route, untested in this research.

**Write this instead:**

> Want to try your own file? Review supported formats, limits and permissions before uploading.

**Verify:** Inspect the real upload flow and only then state its exact capabilities.

## Fix 6 — Preview the reading job

**Current:** The social description says “Read books the way you scroll reels.”

**Write this instead:**

> Footnotes — start a classic in short readable episodes. Try a public first episode.

**Verify:** Use legible text and a story-specific preview, not an unsupported claim of finishing books faster.

## Release check before the first invitation

Open the public URL in a clean phone browser on mobile data. Have someone who has not seen it explain what will happen after the main action, then try it. Check permission denial, a slow connection, back navigation and the narrow-screen keyboard. Log the exact route and device if it fails; a research fetch failing is not a dead site. Do not send traffic into a route you have reproduced as broken.

Make one change at a time after the baseline. Keep a dated screenshot and the old wording so a better conversion number can be tied to the change. Do not call a copy change an improvement until people complete the intended activity more often.
