# Postifyy — Distribution map

**Campus demonstrations and student-made proof come first.** A generic “AI LinkedIn writer” launch puts you beside dozens of alternatives. A student showing the original voice note and the honest project post is recognizable and shareable.

All ranges below are planning assumptions, not measured benchmarks. Zero is possible. Record actual reach, qualified visits, account creation and approved drafts separately after each activity.

## Specific accounts and newsletters

Follow [Peerlist's own project feed](https://peerlist.io/scroll) and [Buffer's LinkedIn resource page](https://buffer.com/linkedin) to find actual discussions about writing and publishing. Reply only when you can add a concrete project-story example; disclose your product if relevant. Do not fill comments with a link or expect either organization to endorse you.

No newsletter acceptance or editorial contact has been verified for your specific product, so **newsletter traffic is zero in the base plan**. Ask clinic organizers whether they run a student email bulletin, then offer copy #2 only after permission. Do not replace an unknown editor with a made-up email address.

## The month-one gap

A conservative base is 30–60 clinic signups, 6–66 from the six personal posts scheduled on Days 5, 8, 11, 16, 25 and 29, 30 referrals, 5–75 Peerlist and 2–20 guide traffic: **73–251 accounts before overlap**. The referral scenario requires 100 activated students and may not occur at the low end, so the low total is not a floor. Deduplicate people who saw several links. These assumptions do not honestly support a promise of 1,000.

To reach 1,000 at 25% visitor-to-signup requires 4,000 unique qualified visitors. At 2% impression-to-visit that needs about 200,000 relevant impressions. The plausible route is partner expansion after proof, not 200,000 cold messages. Days 14 and 21 explicitly decide whether a larger campus network is materializing; report a shortfall plainly if it is not.

## Coordination and working limits

Reserve Days 17–20 for your primary student clinic invitations. Coordinate actual dates with the cohort so Astitva, PitchPanel, Lynkr and the other content tools are not pitching the same organizer. Send at most five personalized organizer approaches in a working day, one follow-up seven days later, then stop. This is a workload limit, not a platform-approved sending quota.

Do not schedule Reddit or Discord promotions: no specific community's current rules have been verified for this student-first plan. Your useful owned posts and permissioned clinics give you a cleaner first test.
