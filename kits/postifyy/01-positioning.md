# Postifyy — Positioning

**Live:** https://postifyy.in/ · **Category:** AI · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/postifyy

**Your first month should sell one honest project post, not a job offer.** The live page promises a voice-note-to-LinkedIn workflow. It does not establish that posting gets someone shortlisted. Remove that leap from your outreach and prove the smaller outcome on screen.

## The person you start with

You want a final-year student or recent graduate who has a working project, an internship lesson or a club responsibility to describe, but whose LinkedIn activity is mostly congratulations. They can explain the work to a friend. They freeze when the empty posting box appears.

Begin with classmates outside your own project team. Qualify with three questions: what did you actually make, what was unexpectedly difficult, and who should understand that work? A person with no experience to describe needs help finding a true story, not an AI-generated accomplishment.

Your [current homepage](https://postifyy.in/) says “Talk for two minutes. Post on LinkedIn all week.” Its main CTA is “Turn a voice note into posts,” leading to `/signin`; the alternative public demo is at `#try`. The sign-in page offers LinkedIn or an email link. Those destinations were checked, but completing authentication, generation and publication was not tested in this review.

## The pitch

> Talk through one project. Turn your own words into a LinkedIn post you can edit, approve and publish.

Bio/DM version:

> Your project story, from voice note to first LinkedIn post.

“Your own words” needs proof. Keep the original audio or transcript beside the edited draft in the demo. Point out which sentence was retained and which claim you removed. That is stronger than an automated “Excellent” score.

## The first use case

The student records: what I built, what broke, what I changed, what I still cannot do. Postifyy offers draft choices; they choose one, correct it, then copy or approve the exact wording. **Activation is an approved first draft exported or published**, not a hook-checker visit.

Do not start with daily posting. Someone who has never published needs one useful post and the confidence to do it again next week. Sell the weekly batch after that first success.

## What the live offer actually costs

The [public pricing](https://postifyy.in/pricing) advertises three free posts and one free scheduled post without a card. Growth is displayed at $11/month, or $110/year; Pro at $15/month, or $150/year. The separately linked sprint is ₹999 once, including 30 days of Growth. Recheck billing currency and tax in checkout before quoting a payable Indian total. Do not call the free allowance three posts per month: it is three in total.

| [Buffer Free](https://buffer.com/pricing) | $0; three channels, ten queued posts per channel, AI assistance | Your entry point is a spoken project story and a visible quality-review flow. Scheduling alone is not the difference. |
| [The Steady Post](https://thesteadypost.app/) | Trial: 14 days or five drafts; ₹499/month afterwards | It starts from trends and a steady habit. You start from one real project spoken aloud. |
| [Creative Studio](https://studio.neuronlab.in/) | Free while in beta; story bank and weekly planning; explicitly manual posting | You demonstrate voice input and optional approved scheduling. Do not claim Creative Studio has autoposting. |

Prices above were checked on 29 October 2026. The other two are cohort products; do not disparage them or send simultaneous pitches into the same group.

## The uncomfortable part

Your page divides attention between founders, job seekers, a hammer discount game, a hook checker and an extension that is still coming soon. A student can spend five minutes playing and leave without making a draft. Give this month's campaign a student-specific path and put the game below the first successful action.

Keep founders as a secondary segment. Astitva and PitchPanel already have founder outreach windows in the shared plan. Your campus clinic is Days 17–20, with organizer permission and a distinct contact list. This is coordination, not a restriction on answering genuine inbound requests.
