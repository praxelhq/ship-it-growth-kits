# WhoDunnit — Distribution map

**Recruit hosts with a willing group already in mind.** Do not broadcast a private room code or promise a party works before a multi-phone test.

| Route | Calendar quantity / consent | Assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Six willing adult hosts | Three Day 5, three Day 10; each already has 4–6 consenting friends, roles may overlap. | 6 × 4–6 = 24–36 potential players; ×50–80% actually start = 12–29 sessions before overlap. | #1 |
| Two existing game/social groups | Admin request Day 9, one approved product invitation Days 12/13. Private room links stay private. | 2 × 40 readers × 20–40% visits × 50% start = 8–16 sessions. | #2 |
| Four original short demos | Days 8/15/20/26, owned footage with fictional names/quirks. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–80 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST; actual coordination/trust lessons. | 2 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 3–30 sessions. | #4 |
| One invited demonstration | Ask one existing organizer Day 10; optional six adults Day 13, not counted twice if already host-wave players. | Up to 6 starts if accepted; zero otherwise, deduplicate. | #5 |
| Maker feedback | Optional [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) Day 22 after current rules/slot check. | Zero baseline; builders aren't guaranteed party hosts. | #6 |

Roughly 26–155 gross sessions before optional demo/overlap. At 50% qualified visit→meaningful play, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits, plus enough groups and working room capacity. A visitor wanting to host later isn't an activated guest now. Replace assumptions after two clips and observed host attendance.

No paid party venues, creator fees, prizes or sponsored newsletters. An existing organizer's free newsletter mention requires permission, zero forecast until accepted. No group additions without consent or scraped host contacts. A mystery game isn't an excuse to publish another person's quirks.

Use different organizers from Bad Alibi/Vault Break and c2 games. c1 preferred Days 10–13, c2 Days 17–20, actual dates subject to approval. No second product pitch in one group within seven days. Don't imply a room/date exists in the invitation; agree it with the host first.
