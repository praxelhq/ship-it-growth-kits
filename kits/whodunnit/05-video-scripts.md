# WhoDunnit — Video scripts

Use owned test phones, fictional names/quirks and consenting adults. Keep room codes, private clues and solutions out of public footage where they spoil or expose a real group. No hidden recordings or real grievances.

## A. Host-to-clue demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“A role card is only the start.”**

2–12s: actual host screen/player limit and chosen world, after own flow verified. “Check the current group size before planning.”

12–27s: create/join real test room with fictional identities, codes obscured. “Each willing player joins on their own phone.”

27–42s: show enough actual role/clue delivery to explain privacy without revealing a real game's answer. “The right player should see the right information.” Label cuts.

42–54s: demonstrate verified group progress/reveal only with permission and no spoiler, or state that this clip ends at setup. Never stage a complete successful game.

54–60s: URL, **Current Free Game · paid options coming later**. If room delivery fails, repair before recording success.

## B. Fiction hook — 18 seconds

0–2s: **“Real friends. Fictional secrets.”**

2–12s: choose harmless fictional quirks in an authorized test. “You don't need to expose a private story to play a character.”

12–18s: public product URL, not a room code. No psychological accuracy claim.

## C. Join hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Can everyone actually get into the room?”**

2–12s: actual second-phone join and role receipt with identities/code hidden. “Setup has to work before the mystery starts.”

12–20s: current player/access limits. If only a proposed flow exists, label it instead of faking a successful join.

## D. Reveal hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Did the ending explain the clues?”**

2–12s: discuss one genuine own-test consistency finding without spoilers or guest quotes. “A surprising answer still needs to make sense.”

12–20s: actual fix/process or honest not-yet-tested note. URL. No invented completed-party footage or laughter track as proof.
