# Muggy — Positioning

**Live:** https://muggy1.me/ · **Category:** Campus food marketplace · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/muggy

Your auth headline is **“Lunch that fits between classes.”** It is explicitly exclusive to Masters' Union, Gurugram, and asks visitors to verify a student email. The public offer says order ahead, pay by UPI/card and collect using a 4-digit code. Four counters are named across Capital Building, CDS Corporate Tower and WoCo. One visible menu item, Indori Poha, is ₹79. These are displayed offers; no food order or payment was placed.

**The awkward constraint: your target is 10 supply-side business customers, not 1,000 student orders.** The existing campus may not permit ten independent kitchens. The page does not establish how many kitchens exist, and the public signup is student-focused. Do not invent ten suppliers or expand beyond the stated campus to manufacture the count.

Your first customers are campus-authorized kitchens that can deliver the right dishes to an approved counter in a real pickup window. Start with one dependable lunch window and the students whose next lecture is nearby.

**Pitch:** Let students pre-order from your campus-approved kitchen and collect the right food near their next lecture.
**Short version:** Campus lunch, pre-ordered and picked up by code.

The alternative is the kitchen's existing direct order counter or permitted WhatsApp/phone ordering: no new platform fee, with food priced by the kitchen. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can support a manual order manifest without a paid Workspace plan. Muggy must improve accurate preparation/pickup, not simply add another queue.

The page says both “no platform fee right now” and ₹5/platform fee per order; payment charge is described as about 2.4%. Resolve what a student actually pays before marketing. A monthly kitchen subscription is agreed directly with the kitchen; do not describe it as a Muggy subscription.

Board target remains **10 real supply-side kitchens/businesses**. Treat an undersized authorized supplier pool as an honest target gap, not a reason to count classmates as sellers.
