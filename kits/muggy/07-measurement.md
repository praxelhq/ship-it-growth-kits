# Muggy — Measurement

**Target: 10 supply-side business customers in 30 days**, per the marketplace rule in [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Retain this target while clearly reporting any campus-pool mismatch.

Signup: a student verifies an account, or a kitchen begins onboarding; report separately. Supply activation: a real authorized kitchen's actual menu/prices/window is onboarded and it successfully uses the order/collection workflow.

Count a kitchen only if it is a genuine business, its real menu/operations are onboarded, it independently used Muggy in the last seven days, and you privately know the operator's name/phone. Payment is not required. Several counters from one business are one customer. Student purchases, waitlists and test kitchens do not substitute.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a tag; mask codes, emails, orders and payment data. Record eligible kitchens, approved kitchens, active kitchens, verified students, placed/fulfilled/collected orders, wrong/missing items, late pickups and refunds. These private values are unknown to this kit, not zero. Save current course rank, reading and sync time from [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/muggy).

Events: student_verified, menu_published, order_paid, kitchen_confirmed, pickup_completed, pickup_missed, refund_resolved. Use operational records for payment/collection truth, not a frontend click.

| Review | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 |At most two supplier approaches; prove one real window. Any safety/fulfilment concern stops expansion. |
| Day 14 |At most six approaches; quantify actual authorized supplier ceiling. |
| Day 21 |At most eight approaches; report independently active suppliers and pickup reliability. |
| Day 30 |At most ten approaches; target 10 suppliers, actual count honestly beside fulfilled student orders. |

The 02 example yields about 4 active businesses from ten prospects. If only three eligible kitchens exist, the ceiling is three until the authorized scope changes. Do not assert a new target yourself or count students as suppliers.

Sunday review includes charge disputes, order accuracy, collection rate and supplier independent return. An app with many student clicks and nobody able to prepare lunch has not activated a marketplace.
