# Muggy — Video scripts

Film only with campus/kitchen permission. Use sample order screens and hide every live pickup code.

## A.60-second walkthrough — authorized campus group
0–2: clock and lecture timetable; **“Lunch before the next class?”**
2–12: show student-email entry without typing personal data. “Muggy is for the Masters' Union campus.”
12–28: sample menu/counter/window. “Choose food that is actually available at a counter you can reach.”
28–40: show full charge breakdown. “Check the total before paying.”
40–53: masked sample code at a permitted counter. “Collect during your window.”
53–60: “Current menus live in the app, not this video. muggy 1.me.”

## B.20-second kitchen hook
0–2: labelled sample order manifest; **“How many portions?”**
2–10: show quantities grouped by a single pickup window.
10–17: say “A useful pre-order app helps the kitchen prepare the right number.”
17–20: “Test one approved window first.”

## C.25-second operations hook — LinkedIn
0–2: two building/floor signs; **“Right dish. Wrong counter.”**
2–14: show one sample order's counter/window emphasis.
14–22: “The pickup location is part of the product, not a footnote.”
22–25: full URL.

Use captions and existing phone footage, no paid production. Never show students without consent or claim a fast queue from staged footage.
