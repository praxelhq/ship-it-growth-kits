# AI Hunt — Distribution map

**Lead with campus builder circles and a useful release briefing.** Directory-to-directory promotion is secondary. You need readers who tried a tool, not twenty makers trading votes.

All ranges below are planning assumptions, not measured benchmarks. Replace them with your first week's results. No membership counts are used.

| 1. Your own campus project group, reached through its existing administrator | Ask for permission to share a three-release briefing on Tuesday at 18:00 IST. Do not add people to a group or claim access to other campuses. | Invite 40 people you actually know; assume 20 read, 8 visit, 3 create an account. Test the assumptions. | #1, #4 |

Watch the [Futurepedia YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC_RovKmk0OCbuZjA8f08opw) for the questions viewers ask about actual workflows. Reply only when you can answer the question; do not paste your URL under videos. A verified account is a research source, not permission to advertise.

Your newsletter channel is initially your own weekly briefing, offered only to opt-ins after its unsubscribe works. Product Hunt has a daily newsletter, but submission does not buy a place in it. Do not spend money on directory placements or forecast editorial coverage you have not secured.

The initial plan supports tens of accounts, not a guaranteed four-figure month. Reaching 1,000 needs repeat briefing distribution through willing organizers or a much stronger observed share loop. Do not hide that gap with fabricated impression estimates.
