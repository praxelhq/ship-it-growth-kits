# AI Hunt — Video scripts

Use your own screen recording and voice. No finished video is supplied. Show current verified entries, not starter examples presented as real releases. Record the source page on the day you record.

## A. One task, one release — 60-second demo

**Hook, first two seconds:** “This launch is useful only if you can actually open it.”

| Time | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Your real task written on a note: summarize a research paper | “I have a task, not a desire to collect fifty AI logos.” |
| 8–20s | AI Hunt categories and the relevant verified entry | “I find a release, then check what it is: a model, a feature, or an app.” |
| 20–38s | Open its official source; highlight date and access | “Here's the original announcement. This is who can use it today and what the first task costs.” |
| 38–50s | Run the allowed first action or clearly show access limitation | “If it is a waitlist, I say so. An announcement isn't a working product.” |
| 50–60s | Return to index, source and label visible | “Try one release for your task at aihunt.co.in. Tell me what you couldn't verify.” |

Post to your LinkedIn and Peerlist product page. If the tool cannot be tried, keep the limitation in the recording rather than editing it away.

## B. Three meanings of launched — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A launch can still be a waitlist.” Shots: 0–5s write “announcement”; 5–10s write “API access”; 10–15s write “working app”; 15–20s show one verified access label. Say: “These are different. Before spending your evening, check which one you have. That's what I'm making clearer in AI Hunt.” Post as an original Short and LinkedIn clip. Use no provider footage.

## C. The vote-count honesty test — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “This number is not a user.” Shots: 0–7s point at a clearly marked demo card; 7–17s show the disclosure and then a real editorial source; 17–25s show corrected labels. Say: “Starter examples are useful for testing a layout. They are not community demand. Here's how the page tells you the difference.” Record after Fix 1, and say “this is the change I'm making” if it is still in progress. Post to Peerlist and your own feed.

## D. A reader, not a launch collector — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “Close the directory after one useful tool.” Show one task, one release and the source opening, five seconds each. Say: “Pick the work. Find a release. Check the source. Then go do the work.” End with the plain URL. This clip is the campus invitation attachment, not an ad under somebody else's video.

Keep captions large enough to read on a phone. Crop around the date/access fields; record one continuous source-opening sequence so the evidence remains clear.
