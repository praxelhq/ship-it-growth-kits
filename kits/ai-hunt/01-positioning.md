# AI Hunt — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.aihunt.co.in/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/ai-hunt

**Your first job is credibility, not more listings.** The homepage promises timely AI discovery, but [How it works](https://www.aihunt.co.in/how-it-works) says five starter launches have illustrative descriptions and seeded votes. The current index contains more editorial entries. Reconcile those two experiences before asking anybody to trust the ranking. A demo vote is not community demand.

## The first person to win

Start with an Indian student builder who tests one AI app for a real project each week and is tired of launch threads that never say whether the product is usable. She wants a working link, access status, price and one concrete reason to try it. She is a reader first; makers supplying listings are the supporting side of the same product.

Your page currently says “Meet AI Hunt.” The title promises “Every new AI. The day it ships.” Keep the useful distinction between **models, features and apps**, but stop implying completeness or same-day coverage until your editorial process supports it.

**Pitch:** Find a newly shipped AI model, feature or app, see what it does, and check the maker's original release before you try it.

**Bio:** New AI releases, with a source and a clear access label.

The wedge is a weekly three-release shortlist for one task: building a student demo, finding research papers, or producing an original clip. Each entry must explain who can actually use it today. A list of thirty names is less useful than three products someone can open.

## Why choose this instead

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) is free to browse and already covers daily launches across categories. [There's An AI For That](https://theresanaiforthat.com/) provides free discovery/search entry points. [Futurepedia](https://www.futurepedia.io/) offers role-based AI discovery and workflows; its public page did not establish a comparable paid tier, so do not quote one. Your own public browse and submission pages did not show a payment requirement. Confirm account creation is free before publishing that claim.

You cannot win by saying you have more tools. Win with independently checked official links, clear release dates, no hidden waitlists and a short explanation of the actual workflow. Do not call yourself an official partner of any model provider.

## What counts this month

Keep the B2C target: **1,000 signups**. Count unique real accounts; report readers who open a verified maker link and return in week two beside that number. Makers submitting products are not a substitute for readers using the index. If only makers join to upvote themselves, you have collected a queue, not a discovery habit.
