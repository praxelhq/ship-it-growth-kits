# Fan Wars India — Video scripts

These are status/recovery scripts until a real replacement exists. No rendered promo, fake refund proof or invented gameplay.

## A. Current-state walkthrough — 35 seconds

0–2s: **“The previous experiment is withdrawn.”**

2–12s, show current official notice. “Payments are closed. The site carries the current refund-processing statement.”

12–25s, show dated status and genuine support route only after added/verified. “Specific payment questions belong in the official private channel, not public comments.”

25–35s: “There is no replacement purchase or signup open yet. We will publish a dated change when that is true.” URL. Don't imply all refunds completed.

## B. Clarity clip — 15 seconds

0–2s: “A future promise isn't a live product.”

2–10s: current notice/no purchase. “We're separating recovery from any later experiment.”

10–15s: official status URL. No comeback countdown.

## C. Privacy clip — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Please don't post payment details in comments.”

2–12s: show the actual private support path after it exists, with all personal data hidden. “Use the official route for a specific issue.”

12–20s: current status note. No dummy card/bank details that could be mistaken for instructions.

## D. Conditional relaunch demo — 45 seconds

Only after a real replacement passes checks: open its actual page, explain the current user action, perform that action with owned non-sensitive test data, show the actual result/limits and state the real offer. Keep the earlier withdrawal/recovery status separate. If no replacement exists, don't record a fictional promo to satisfy a schedule.
