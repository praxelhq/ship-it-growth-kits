# Fan Wars India — Landing page fixes

Based on the live withdrawal notice checked 29 October. No payment records, refund or replacement were accessed.

## 1. Keep acquisition closed while the offer is withdrawn

Current headline: **“We heard you.”** Current state: payments removed/refunds promised; no signup or product CTA. Do not add a purchase or launch button just to make the page convert. Primary action should be **“Read the current status”** and a real private support route once the owner provides it.

## 2. Date the processing statement

The notice mentions 5–7 days. Add when the notice was issued, what the window refers to and the actual next update time the team can honour. Do not reset the estimate silently or claim refunds are complete without records. Replacement wording: **“Refund processing status—last updated on the date shown here. Contact the official private support route for a specific payment.”**

## 3. Explain what is and isn't live

Suggested support: **“The former fictional fan experiment is withdrawn. No replacement product, new payment or signup is open on this page.”** A future promise should not look like an active offer or a countdown.

## 4. Give affected people a genuine support path

Use the owner's real support process, with only the minimum necessary information requested privately. No public form asking for card details, banking credentials or screenshots posted to a feed. Test acknowledgement and delivery before linking it. This kit supplies no invented contact address.

## 5. Define relaunch readiness before changing the page

A new version needs an actual product/action, current eligibility and offer, privacy/support information, conversion test and clear relation to the withdrawn version. A payment return isn't proof of fulfilment. Do not reuse old payment access or ask for repeat payment to resolve an earlier transaction.

## 6. Check phone and link previews

The withdrawal notice must be readable on a phone and visible in current previews. Suggested title: **“FanWarsIndia — current experiment status.”** Remove obsolete launch/price promises from shared metadata where the owner controls them. No private refund or payer details in public screenshots.
