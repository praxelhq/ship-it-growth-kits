# NetFixLab — Video scripts

Record your own comic panels on a phone. Verify actual taps before filming; no authentication or paid unlock was completed in this research. Use a staged home-device count, not network details.

## A. Meet the router — 40 seconds, homepage/session invite

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Router comic character. | “Your router gets a speaking part.” |
| 3–14 s | Tap actual free lesson entry and open comic. | “NetFixLab starts with a simple question: what is a network, really?” |
| 14–26 s | Actual devices/router panels. | “The devices talk through the idea, one panel at a time. No networking background needed.” |
| 26–34 s | Show real takeaway and quiz. | “Then try a small task and check what you understood.” |
| 34–40 s | Free entry/URL. | “First three lessons free. Start at netfixlab.tech.” |

## B. Count them — 20 seconds, Reel

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Phone, laptop, TV icons. | “How many devices use your network?” |
| 3–14 s | Lesson 1 task and your staged count. | “Phones are easy to remember. The TV and little smart devices count too. That is the first lesson's task.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Try the free comic at NetFixLab.” |

## C. Try before paying — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Picture/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | First three lesson cards. | “Read three before you decide.” |
| 3–14 s | Free/full distinction and actual price. | “The first three lessons need no account to read. The full twelve-lesson course is a separate one-time purchase.” |
| 14–20 s | URL and terms pointer. | “Start free. Check the current checkout terms.” |

Keep at least one panel-to-quiz path uncut. Do not film an invented internet repair or fake payment success.
