# NetFixLab — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Preserve the real free entry

Current headline: **“Your home runs on a network. Learn it from a comic.”** The delivered page includes **“Read lesson 1, free”**, wired to its in-page lesson opener. Rendered extraction missed that button, so a missing extraction is not proof of a missing live CTA. Test it on a clean phone: cover→**“Open the comic”**→panels→quiz→next lesson. Pause wider sharing if this actual path fails.

Keep **“Read lesson 1, free”**, with **“No account needed for lessons 1–3”** beside it. The first lesson title is **“What is a network, really?”**; use that as a preview rather than an abstract course promise.

## 2. Make the amount resilient

The price container is filled by script from a current **$6** configuration. Article extraction showed only “one-time”. Render the actual amount as accessible initial content too: **“US$6 one-time for all 12 lessons”**, if still current. Show the actual final checkout total/currency and any required tax before confirmation. Do not promise a ₹ amount or currency conversion you have not checked.

## 3. Explain free versus account versus purchase

Replacement sequence: **“Read three lessons free. Create an account when you want the full course. Pay once to unlock lessons 4–12.”** Verify entitlement and progress in a controlled test before claiming cross-device purchase success. A live-host payment URL alone is not a transaction test.

## 4. Keep the troubleshooting promise bounded

Replace broad repair certainty with **“Learn common checks and what to tell your provider when the fault is outside your home.”** Do not encourage factory resets, disabling security or sharing credentials as growth exercises. The first lesson's device-count task needs no private screenshots.

## 5. Mobile and confidence

Check comic text size, panel navigation, quiz focus/error feedback, back button and saved progress. Keep refund/terms available: the public policy says free lessons let people try first, with payment/access failures handled separately. Do not rewrite that as an unconditional money-back trial. Existing Clarity is present in delivered HTML; exclude account/payment and typed inputs before any user recording.
