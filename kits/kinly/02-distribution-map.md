# Kinly — Distribution map

**Lead with a small “one person, one plan” exercise.** This is a private utility, not a social network that needs public posting or contact imports to work.

All forecasts are assumptions for this exact calendar, not channel benchmarks.

| Route | Permission / execution | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fifteen adult peers | Ask individually to try a fictional person first; let them decide whether to keep a real note privately. | 15 × assumed 60% try × 60–80% save a useful plan = 5–7 activations. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned friend/alumni circles | One admin-approved activity each. Never ask people to post their notes or friends' details in the group. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–25% try × 50–80% activate = 4–16 activations. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Before-meeting context, local backup, optional AI boundary at 18:30 IST. Use only fictional data. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% try × 50–80% activate = 3–72 activations. | #3 |
| 4. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after draft/backup flow checks. Show examples as examples; no vote trading. | Assume 5–20 visits and 2–10 useful workspace sessions. | #4 |

This base plan suggests roughly 14–105 activations before overlap, not an evidenced thousand people. Don't make the privacy-sensitive task bigger just to raise a headline count.
