# Kinly — Video scripts

Use fictional people, plans and notes. Never turn on optional AI or send real context just to make a clip.

## A. Before a catch-up — 40 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“You remembered the time. What about the person?”**

2–15: Show one fictional person and upcoming plan. “Kinly keeps the little context beside the next meeting.”

15–25: Show a short fictional question/note. “What did they mention last time? What do you actually want to ask?”

25–33: Open the real local-storage explanation. “Current records stay in this browser. Keep a backup.”

33–40: “One person, one plan. kinly.in.” Use as the main demo; label examples throughout.

## B. The small note — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Not a contact database. One useful memory.”**

2–10: Show a fictional note about asking how a project went. “A detail you choose to remember, not a score of the friendship.”

10–15: **“Kinly. Remember the person.”** URL. First short clip.

## C. Saved where? — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Saved doesn't mean synced.”**

2–12: Open actual Settings local-storage/backup text. “These records live in this browser/device. Clearing data or changing devices won't bring them along.”

12–20: “Export a backup before relying on it.” URL. Second clip; show the option, not somebody's downloaded personal data.

## D. Optional means optional — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Your notes shouldn't quietly become an AI prompt.”**

2–13: Show the actual off-state/explanation for Deeper answers, without toggling. “The deeper action sends relevant context to the named provider. Read and choose; the local workflow stays available.”

13–20: “That boundary should be clear before you ask.” URL. Third clip; no fake data-flow certification.
