# Kinly — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** With no observed account requirement, count unique genuine users beginning their own meaningful workspace session, alongside **one real person and plan saved privately by that user**. Opening examples or reloading a browser isn't activation. Don't count each person in a contact list as an acquired user.

## Day 0

Record latest date/traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/kinly). Establish actual meaningful starts, non-example saves, return days and support issues where measurable. The public example counters are not your baseline. This kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured** until established.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on safe surfaces; exclude/mask names, plans, feelings, questions and private notes. Don't instrument optional deeper answers by logging their contents. Browser-local records do not establish globally unique people; state that measurement limit rather than adding invasive identification.

## Funnel

`own_space_started → person_saved → plan_saved → context_used_before_plan → returned_later`.

Use aggregate events consistent with the local/privacy promises. If there is no reliable safe unique-user count, report observed test cohorts separately. Export availability doesn't prove a user successfully restored a backup; verify with disposable data.

## Projection and decisions

The actual calendar in `02` supports roughly 14–105 activations before overlap under guessed rates. It does not establish 1,000 new users. At hypothetical 50% start-to-person/plan activation, 1,000 starts would mean 500 useful workspaces; current reach doesn't prove either number.

These are chosen product checks, not a measure of relationship health. A “drifting” label or more stored notes doesn't prove a better friendship. Save course snapshots with timestamps and never sum overlapping 24-hour readings.
