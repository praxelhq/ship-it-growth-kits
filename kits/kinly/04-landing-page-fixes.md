# Kinly — Landing-page fixes

The [public workspace](https://www.kinly.in/) and Settings were inspected in a browser on 29 October. No records were added/deleted, no backup was imported and no deeper AI request was sent.

## 1. Label the initial data as examples

Current H1: **“Your people. A more intentional tomorrow.”** The initial workspace already shows people, plans and a reflection. Put **“Example space — explore with fictional data”** above it, with a clear **“Start my own space”** action whose consequences are explained. Don't silently overwrite someone's existing local records or imply examples are live customer activity.

## 2. Give the first action one small job

Current visible CTA: **“Add a person.”** Pair it with **“Start with one person and your next plan. No contact import needed.”** Keep the familiar private-notebook feel rather than adding public feeds or engagement counters.

## 3. Bring local-storage truth out of Settings

Settings says records are only in this browser/device and clearing browser data loses them. Add a persistent calm line **“Saved on this device. Export a backup to keep a copy.”** Make Export discoverable after the first meaningful save. Don't promise cross-device continuity until it actually exists.

## 4. Keep the deeper-answer exception explicit

Current explanation says a deeper request sends one question with relevant notes/plans/reflections to Claude. Keep it off by default and show the exact included context before each send. Replacement summary: **“Local answers stay here. Choosing a deeper answer sends the displayed context to the named provider.”** Don't make a blanket “never sent anywhere” claim.

## 5. Make relationship labels descriptive, not authoritative

“Might be drifting” can sound like the app knows the relationship. Use **“No recent time recorded”** or explain the actual rule. Let users disagree or hide the label. A missing note is not evidence of another person's feelings.

## 6. Test backup and recovery with disposable examples

Import says it replaces current data. Show a clear preview/warning and offer export before replacement. Test this yourself with fictional records, including invalid backup/error states. Don't ask a tester to clear personal data as part of a casual demo.

## 7. Make mobile/private shares safe

Title: **“Kinly — private notes for people and plans.”** Description: **“One person, one plan, more thoughtful context. Browser-local workspace with export backup.”** Shared preview must never include actual names or reflection text. Test text zoom, navigation and note editing on a phone; keep analytics off private content.
