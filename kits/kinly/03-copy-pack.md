## #5 — Useful no-link note structure

Before a catch-up, write three short lines: what they mentioned last time, what you genuinely want to ask, and what you can offer. Afterward, record only what you'll want to remember. Don't turn a missed entry in an app into a verdict that the friendship is drifting.
