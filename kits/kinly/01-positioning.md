# Kinly — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.kinly.in/ · **Category:** Other / personal relationship planning · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/kinly

**The public workspace contains examples, not proof of your users' relationships.** The [live interface](https://www.kinly.in/), inspected 29 October, opens with example people, plans and a reflection. Don't market that seeded activity as traction or make a new visitor wonder whose private life they are seeing.

Current headline: **“Your people. A more intentional tomorrow.”** Visible action: **“Add a person.”** The public app has People, Plans, Reflections, Goals and Settings. No published paid price was visible. The kit inspected navigation/storage explanations, not a real person's saved record or an AI request.

## Your first user

An adult who has a few relationships they care about, makes plans in several places and forgets the small context that makes a meeting thoughtful. Start with one person and one plan, not a demand to import a whole social network.

**Pitch:** Keep one person's context beside your next plan, so showing up feels more intentional.

**Bio:** Remember the person, not just the appointment.

Your wedge is the before-meeting note: what they mentioned last time and one thing you want to ask. It is not a score of who deserves your time. Labels such as “might be drifting” should describe missing recorded contact, not pretend to diagnose a relationship.

Settings says people/plans/goals/reflections are saved **only in this browser on this device**. It offers export/import, and warns clearing storage or changing devices loses access without a backup. Optional **Deeper answers** is off by default; its explanation says the chosen question and relevant person context are sent to Claude only when that action is used. “Private by design” must not erase that exception. This kit did not turn it on or send data.

[Notion's personal Free plan](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 and can organize notes/plans; a notebook is another no-additional-software-cost alternative. Kinly wins if the person→plan→reflection connection is easier without building a system. Don't invent an AI feature advantage or a cloud-sync promise.

Target stays **1,000 signups**: unique people beginning their own meaningful workspace session, beside **one real person and plan saved by that user**. Opening examples is not activation. Local storage limits unique-person measurement; say so rather than calling every reload a signup.
