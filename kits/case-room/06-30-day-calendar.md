# Case Room — 30-day execution calendar

**Days 1–3 are page work and nothing else.** You have no landing page, so every channel you spend before Fix 1 ships is a channel wasted — and in most of these communities you only get one good first post.

It will feel like losing a tenth of the month. You are buying the other twenty-seven days.

After that, the shape of your month is simple: **enter competitions yourself, and get organisers to hand you their teams.**

Open this file every morning.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **The landing page shipping on day 1** (`04` Fix 1). Every channel is worthless until it exists, and three days of page work is the cheapest thing in this kit.
2. **Sixty organiser DMs in week 2, twenty more in week 4.** Each yes is 25–100 teams with one shared deadline. Four yeses is most of your target.
3. **Entering competitions yourself.** It gives you credibility nobody can question, access to every other team, and real feedback under real pressure. It is also the only way you'll find out whether your product survives day six.
4. **Under 60 seconds to usefulness, and teammates reading without an account** (`04` Fix 4). Your product's adoption moment is one person pasting a link into a group chat mid-crisis. Every extra step kills it.
5. **Measuring teams, not signups.** A thousand solo accounts is a failure. 120 teams of three is a product.

## What you have that nobody else in this cluster does

**No cliff.** Case competitions run continuously all year — [21,000+ live listings on Unstop](https://unstop.com/competitions) at any time. Skill Prep's audience vanishes on 29 November; the placement-season kits go quiet in December. **Your audience is the same size in February as in November.**

That means the organiser relationships you build this month compound rather than expire — and it's why day 28 has you pitching December and January competitions before the month is even over.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 18), **Peerlist** (day 13 — builder audience, not teams), **Reddit** (day 16).

**Never drop:** days 1–3 (the page), days 8/10/12 (the sixty DMs), day 4 (entering competitions), day 15 (converting the yeses).
