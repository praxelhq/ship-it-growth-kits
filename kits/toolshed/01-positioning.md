# Toolshed — Positioning

**Live:** https://tool-shed.app/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/toolshed · Checked 29 October 2026.

Your first user is an individual builder who saves useful tools and later forgets which solved a particular job. Start with finding one public tool by use case, then saving a link with a non-sensitive reason. This is a personal retrieval problem, not an agency procurement platform or a guarantee that curated legal/consulting prompts are professionally validated.

Actual H1 **“You keep finding good tools. Then you lose them.”** Banner **“Not launched yet — building in the open.”** **Browse the directory** → `/discover`, currently labelled **84 tools**. **Start your shed — free** goes to `/register`; it offers email/password or Google sign-in. Library promise: **free, unlimited saved tools, no card**. No account, metadata fetch, private note, export, payment or deletion was exercised in this audit.

## The pitch, with a necessary boundary

> Find the tool by the job you saved it for. Check fetched details, put it in the right use cases and keep a non-sensitive reason beside it.

Bio: **A personal tool shelf organized by what you do.**

The page says URL metadata fills name/description/logo, but **you check and save**. A tool can belong to several categories. Don't claim automatic recommendations are verified, all prices are current or pasted URLs can safely include credentials. Save canonical public product links, never secret dashboard/invite/token URLs.

## Privacy isn't an absolute private-note promise

[The actual policy](https://tool-shed.app/privacy) says Clarity records page content **including notes and email**, on library/account pages as well as public pages. It currently offers no in-product consent/refusal. People with Clarity project access can watch recordings. Account deletion **does not delete those recordings** and the operator says it can't identify one by email. No-admin-library screen doesn't remove that exception.

Therefore lead with the public directory and non-sensitive sample notes. Gate a private/confidential notebook campaign on owner correction of recording/masking/consent and clear updated policy. Don't call all notes inaccessible to operators or promise immediate erasure of every copy. This audit isn't legal advice or a security audit; it is a concrete product-trust boundary stated by the site's own policy.

## Alternatives and target

An existing browser bookmark folder plus local note costs ₹0 extra specialist software; [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can hold a manual tool/use-case table. Toolshed must earn adoption by making a saved link findable for a job, not by asserting everyone needs another directory. Eighty-four listings aren't eighty-four customers or quality endorsements.

Role-specific paid kits/all-access/advertising are mentioned in policy/navigation, but no current price was verified here. Don't invent a paid offer or make kit revenue the account target. Sponsors on a public directory aren't proof of a vendor/customer relationship unless actually evidenced.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**, alongside first non-sensitive link saved, assigned to useful categories and later retrieved. Public-directory visitors and tool-card clicks are separate from accounts. The initial audience is the individual builder, even though role-kit examples include professional work.
