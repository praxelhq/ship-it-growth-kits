# Listings For Me — Measurement

**Target:10 real business customers.** Show **businesses independently creating and reviewing destination-specific drafts for their own products** beside it. Free title checks, photo uploads, marketplace selections and copied fields are separate counts.

## Day0

Record your actual current businesses and product generations from your own records; this research did not establish them. Check an existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) connection or configure it if absent. Record sessions, sources, upload starts, output success and mobile share. At the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/listings-for-me), capture the actual current rank, saved reading and timestamp; unknown is not zero. Never add overlapping24-hour snapshots together.

Because the tool needs no account and keeps no listing history, instrument aggregate success events and run a **consented private pilot register** for distinct businesses. Don't fabricate an account-based retention measure. Don't collect raw product photos or seller contact details in public analytics or this repository.

## Customer definition

All four [policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): real selling business; its own actual products and facts onboarded; independently used within the final seven days without your prompt; a responsible person known and callable privately. One seller choosing four destinations is one business. Repeated browser sessions and the default sample are excluded. Paid plans are coming soon, so never report simulated or unavailable subscriptions as revenue.

Use `product_upload_started`, `draft_completed`, `fact_corrected`, `field_copied` and `repeat_business_job` without storing private photos or full listing content. A copy event is an intent signal, not proof a marketplace accepted the listing. Seller-reported publication is a separate, clearly labelled outcome.
