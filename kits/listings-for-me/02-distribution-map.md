# Listings For Me — Distribution map

**Manually recruited multi-channel sellers first.** Your free checker is an opener, not the customer metric. Never use marketplace buyer messaging to advertise an outside seller tool.

| Channel | Exact route, rules and planning range | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local makers / craft sellers | Your own campus bazaar or neighbourhood craft event only after organizer permission. Ask a seller with two destinations to bring one owned photo. Ten warm asks might yield3–5 conversations and1–2 trials; these are assumptions, not measured event conversion. | #1 |
| Public seller business websites | Start from a maker's own site and publicly linked business enquiry route. Qualify two destinations and a current product-release job before writing. Four first approaches on ten scheduled days=40. One follow-up after seven days, stop on no. Scenario40×25% replies×60% trials×50% independent use≈3customers. | #2 |
| [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/feed/) | Feed is real; discussions require login and current rules were not visible publicly. Read rules while signed in before participating. Use a factual, link-free answer only where allowed; no tool pitch or unsolicited private messages. Model0–3 conversations, no guaranteed traffic. | #3 |
| [Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/145b6d0f-999c-4555-896c-c694bda2e470) | Read the official announcement and distinguish non-media scope. This is a source and learning channel, not assumed permission to advertise. Don't drop your domain into the announcement thread. | #3 |
| Your own LinkedIn / X | Show one owned product with two different destination drafts. Tuesday09:00IST, stay available for replies. Assumed100–500 impressions/post and0–5visits; zero is possible. Don't ask sellers to reveal private sales data. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | The official site advertises Monday Launchpad. Check today's rules/slots; use a real working upload flow and #5. Optional0–100visits,0–2seller trials, not the main acquisition plan. | #5 |

Specific official account worth listening to: the **News_Amazon** author on the linked title announcement. Watch actual policy updates rather than repeating secondary screenshots. For newsletters, ask a maker-event organizer for a free opt-in educational blurb only after permission; use #1 shortened. No newsletter placement or directory membership is assumed.

Base case: about3 independently active businesses from direct approaches plus0–2 from warm demonstrations. This is below10. At day14, keep the best-performing seller category and ask active sellers for one permissioned introduction. Don't count the same business again because it selected four destinations.
