# Listings For Me — Positioning

**Live:** https://listingsfor.me/ · **Category:** E-commerce · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/listings-for-me

**Your target is ten selling businesses, not title-checker visitors.** Choose a handmade home-goods seller with at least two actual destinations—Etsy and an independent store, for example—and a small batch of new products to list this month. A seller who only uses Amazon's own tool has less reason to switch.

The real headline is **“Your listing broke a rule. Nobody told you.”** The action **“Fix it with one photo, free”** opens `/app`, which currently offers photo upload for one product and marketplace choices. [Pricing](https://listingsfor.me/pricing) says three products free in beta. The $5/five-credit pack and $19/month Unlimited plan are **coming soon**, not available purchases. Don't sell subscriptions that aren't open.

## The promise to make

> Turn one accurate product brief and photo into marketplace-specific listing drafts, then review each field before copying it yourself.

Bio: **One product. Different listing drafts for each store.**

The photo cannot establish material composition, dimensions, certification or origin. Ask the seller for those facts. Your one-minute claim is a current marketing claim, not a timed independent result. Time a real upload-to-reviewed-copy session before quoting a saving.

## Different from the tools already there

[Amazon's official title update](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/145b6d0f-999c-4555-896c-c694bda2e470) confirms a75-character title rule from27July2026 for non-media categories on that announcement's marketplace, and describes native AI title/highlight suggestions. Don't say Amazon lacks its own checker. [Etsy's handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517) also describes a native optional AI title tool for eligible English listings. Their tool access is part of the seller workflow described; this research established no separate subscription price. Marketplace selling fees still apply.

Your difference is **several destinations from the same corrected product facts**, not “AI that no platform has.” Compare against the seller's current copy-and-edit process, which costs no additional listing-tool subscription but takes work.

## The uncomfortable bit

The headline's fear is broader than the evidence. Amazon's announcement says affected listings remain active during its title changes. Etsy allows **up to**13 tags; using fewer is not the same as a rejected listing. Rewrite this distinction before promotion. A length check is not full policy compliance, search ranking assurance or proof that sales improve.

Count a business only after its own products are onboarded, it returns independently in the final seven days, and you have a responsible callable contact privately. The no-account flow means you need a consented business pilot log; anonymous browser uses cannot automatically prove ten distinct customers.
