## #3 — Useful forum answer, no promotion

Check the exact marketplace and category before changing every title. Amazon's July2026 announcement covers non-media titles and explains how Item Highlights fits beside them; it also says listings stay active during the change. Etsy's guidance separates clear titles from accurate, varied tags. A character counter won't tell you whether a guessed material is true. Keep the seller-verified product facts beside the draft, then check the platform's current form before publishing.

Link only the official source if the current forum rules permit it. Don't add your product domain or pretend to be a neutral customer.
