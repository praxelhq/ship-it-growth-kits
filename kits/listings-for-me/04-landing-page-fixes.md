# Listings For Me — Landing-page fixes

Public page, pricing and `/app` upload route read29October2026. No photo generation or seller-account publication was performed. The route is accessible; that isn't proof the full output is correct.

## 1. Replace blanket fear with the actual supported check

**Current headline:** “Your listing broke a rule. Nobody told you.”

**Write:** **“One product. Listing drafts shaped for each marketplace.”** Subhead: **“Add a photo and verified product details. Review the counts and wording before copying anything.”** A listing can have policy issues a character checker cannot detect; don't imply full compliance or lost sales from an unverified cause.

## 2. Put rule scope and sources beside the checker

**Current:** “Amazon cuts titles at75characters” and Etsy “exactly13tags” read as universal penalties.

**Write:** **“Amazon title guidance: check your marketplace and category. Etsy: up to13tags, up to20characters each.”** Add dated official links. [Amazon's announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/145b6d0f-999c-4555-896c-c694bda2e470) excludes media and says listings stay active. [Etsy's help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336307-How-to-Use-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search) says up to13tags. Fewer tags can be an opportunity, not proof of rejection.

## 3. Clarify the real conversion action

**Current CTA:** “Fix it with one photo, free” opens `/app`.

**Write:** **“Draft my first product free.”** Supporting line: **“Three products in beta. You review and copy; nothing publishes automatically.”** Test photo→details→destination→draft→copy on a phone. If generation fails, repair it before outreach. Don't call a counter interaction a customer signup.

## 4. Keep future pricing separate

**Current pricing:** $5for five credits and $19/month are marked coming soon, with waitlist actions.

**Write above all cards:** **“Beta now:3products free. Paid plans aren't available to buy yet.”** Keep future cards secondary. Do not route a button labelled Buy to a waitlist. The site's browser-based credit/recovery explanation should be visible before any eventual purchase.

## 5. Correct the photo-confidence promise

**Current:** “One photo in. Four listings out. Every rule checked.”

**Write:** **“One product brief. Four destination drafts. Check facts and remaining issues before you publish.”** Ask for material, size and brand where the image cannot prove them. Keep source photo and editable facts together; a green count should not visually approve the factual content.

## 6. Prove the manual handoff and mobile result

**Current:** the sample includes a corrected running-shoe title and field counts.

**Write beside it:** **“See what changed, then copy the reviewed field.”** Show one full readable field on mobile and an unresolved fact that remains flagged. Test360px width, clipboard confirmation, keyboard access, link preview and actual load on mobile data. No performance failure was measured; these are verification tasks, not invented bugs.
