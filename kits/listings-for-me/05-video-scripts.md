# Listings For Me — Video scripts

Use your own product photo; never scrape another seller's image for a public demo. No paid editing software needed. Keep output legible and label shortened generation waits.

## A · One product, two destinations ·55seconds · LinkedIn / seller call

0–2s hook: **“Would you paste the same listing into both?”** Show two destination columns.2–12s: upload an owned photo, text “Seller-owned image”; say “The photo is only the start. I supply the material and dimensions I actually know.”12–28s: select two destinations and show the real draft process; say “One product uses one credit, however many destinations I choose.”28–43s: inspect counts and correct an unsupported material; say “Passing a count isn't the same as getting the product right.”43–55s: copy one reviewed field; say “Three products free in beta. You publish it yourself. Listings For Me.”

## B · The missing product fact ·20seconds · Reel / X

Hook0–2s: **“That photo doesn't prove it's leather.”** Show a clearly labelled example draft.2–8s: highlight the material field: “An AI guess can become a false product claim if you copy it.”8–15s: replace it with your verified material and regenerate.15–20s: “Review the facts, not just the character count.” Text: “Photo→verified details→draft.” End with URL, no sales-uplift promise.

## C · Thirteen opportunities, not thirteen guarantees ·25seconds · seller education

Hook0–2s: **“Thirteen tags don't guarantee a sale.”**2–10s: show [Etsy's official tag guidance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336307-How-to-Use-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search): “The limit is up to13, each up to20characters.”10–20s: compare a repeated generic tag set with varied accurate phrases for your owned sample.20–25s: “Use relevant terms, then measure actual shop results. A checklist is not a ranking guarantee.”

Capture: one upload, verified-fact entry, two destination outputs, one error correction, copy confirmation. Don't show a fake live marketplace publication or imply the tool connects to seller accounts.
