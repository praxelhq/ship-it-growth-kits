## Alternatives you must beat honestly

[Shazam](https://www.shazam.com/) and its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shazam-find-music-concerts/id284993459) offer a free app for identifying playing music. [Google's primary description of hum-to-search](https://blog.google/products/search/hum-to-search/) explains melody-based discovery; it is a 2020 feature article, not proof of every current device/language combination. Don't claim the category is unsolved or that either service is paid. Your potential niche is a short remembered-word flow with no extra install, if you can demonstrate it.
