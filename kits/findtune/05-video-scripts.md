# findtune — Video scripts

Record only after the working app is restored. Use original, licensed or public-domain material and no private recording. Don't reproduce full lyrics or imply rights to a commercial song clip. Phone screen recording and your voice are enough.

## A. From remembered words to a checked candidate —60 seconds

**Where:** restored homepage and Peerlist. **Hook:** “I remember the words, not the title.”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|A permitted short input, no commercial lyric excerpt|I remember the words, not the title.|
|2–14 s|Actual restored first screen|This build supports this input; it isn't every recognition mode.|
|14–30 s|Enter the permitted example at normal speed|I'm looking for candidates I can check, not a guaranteed answer.|
|30–43 s|Actual result/no-match state|Here's what it returned. Ambiguity stays visible.|
|43–53 s|Check a candidate through the actual supported route|I still listen/check whether it's the right one.|
|53–60 s|URL and actual access terms|Try the supported flow at findtune.shop. Failures are useful feedback too.|

No simulated successful result if the restored app doesn't produce one. Label any shortened waiting time.

## B. Three input problems —20 seconds

**Where:** own social feed. **Hook:** “A melody isn't a lyric.” Show cards “playing audio,” “remembered words,” “remembered melody.” Say, “Before choosing a tool, check which input it actually supports.” End with your actual supported mode, not an invented capability.

## C. The no-match case —25 seconds

**Where:** help and a follow-up post. **Hook:** “This search didn't work.” Show a real permitted failure and a clear next step. Say, “I'd rather show no reliable match than present a random result as certain.” No fake accuracy score or claim you solved every language.

## D. Recovery proof — 30 seconds

**Where:** launch preparation. **Hook:** “A title tag isn't a working app.” Show the restored app loading in a fresh phone browser and one genuine end-to-end permitted test. Explain the previously missing mount plainly without calling the whole domain dead. End with actual supported scope and no paid artist-exposure promise.

Bank restored load, input, candidate/no-match and check action. Keep the demonstration about the product's work, not a famous song doing the marketing for it.
