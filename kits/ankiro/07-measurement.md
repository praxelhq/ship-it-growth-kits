# Ankiro — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** For this no-account free insight, use unique consumer sessions beginning a genuine Moolank calculation, beside **completed explained results**. Report consultation requests, confirmed quotes, paid/attended sessions and return use separately. Free calculator use is not a human-consultation customer.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/ankiro) traffic/rank. Record current public visits, free-insight starts/results, valid received requests, scheduled/attended sessions, payments/refunds and support capacity. This kit does not know private totals; mark **not measured** rather than assuming zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on public pages and masking/exclusion of all birth details, private questions, request fields and UPI references. Don't log a date of birth to count a result. Use aggregate privacy-consistent events; if unique people cannot be measured reliably, state that limitation rather than fingerprinting visitors.

## Two funnels

Free: `insight_started → calculation_completed → working_viewed`.

Human: `request_prepared → request_received → amount_time_confirmed → payment_verified → session_attended`.

A WhatsApp draft opening is not a request received. A payment reference typed by a user is not verified payment. A scheduled slot is not an attended consultation.

## Honest arithmetic

`02` forecasts roughly 18–98 completed insights before overlap from 15 invitations, two circles and three owned clips. A confirmed session may add 3–10. At hypothetical 70% result completion, 1,000 result-producing sessions requires about 1,429 genuine starts. Those are planning rates, not evidence that the current channels provide the reach. Keep the gap visible; don't turn repeated calculations by the founder into new users.
