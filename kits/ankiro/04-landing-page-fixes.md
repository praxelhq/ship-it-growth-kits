# Ankiro — Landing-page fixes

The [home](https://ankiro.in/), [free insight](https://ankiro.in/free-insight), [request](https://ankiro.in/consultation/request), [expert](https://ankiro.in/experts) and [consultation](https://ankiro.in/consultation) pages were read 29 October. A consultation request and UPI payment were not submitted.

## 1. Put the exact service behind the broad headline

Current headline: **“Your questions. Deeper answers. A brighter you.”**

Replacement subhead: **“Try a free, explained Moolank calculation. For detailed traditional guidance, request a scheduled human consultation.”** Keep **“Start a consultation request”** and add **“Price and time agreed before payment; experts are not on call.”** Don't promise instant availability.

## 2. Explain the request handoff before the final action

The four-step page promises review before data leaves the browser. Show **“Review what you will send”**, then **“Send my request through the chosen channel”**. Identify WhatsApp/email clearly. Test your own sample request, cancel/back actions and success/error state before claiming delivery. A prepared message is not a request received by the team.

## 3. Close the expert trust gap before payment

Current expert page says the team chooses and connects the expert, without an advance list. Offer a factual named introduction, relevant experience and session scope before taking money. Replacement: **“Before you pay, we tell you who will speak with you, what the session covers, its duration and the agreed amount.”** Don't invent certification or clinical credentials.

## 4. Keep free input minimal

Moolank uses day only; don't make a full birth date feel necessary if the tool doesn't need it. Show **“Day of month, 1–31. Month and year are not used.”** Preserve the visible working and reflection disclaimer. The current local-processing promise should be verified against analytics before it is repeated as an absolute assurance.

## 5. Keep consultation price honest

There is no fixed public price. Use **“Request the current price before booking”**, not a made-up “from” amount. The quote should state service, duration, mode, scheduling and cancellation/refund terms. Show payment verification through the real process; a user sending a UPI reference is not itself confirmed payment.

## 6. Give mobile shares a clear boundary

Title: **“Ankiro — explained numerology and scheduled human guidance.”** Description: **“Free Moolank introduction. Traditional reflection, not prediction. Consultation amount/time agreed before payment.”** Test the actual shared card and form on a phone. Keep font sizes readable and privacy text adjacent to inputs, not below a decorative service grid.
