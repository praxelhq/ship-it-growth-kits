# Ankiro — Video scripts

Use fictional birth details. No dramatic prediction, fear imagery or claims that a remedy cures a condition.

## A. Transparent introduction — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“You should be able to see the working.”**

2–15: Enter an example day, 29. Show 2+9=11 and 1+1=2. “Ankiro's Moolank introduction uses the day only.”

15–25: Show the result's traditional themes. “The arithmetic is a calculation. These meanings are traditional reflections, not proof of a future event.”

25–35: Open consultation page. “Detailed guidance is from a scheduled human expert, not this calculator.”

35–45: Show price-before-payment explanation. “Try free, or request the amount and time before deciding. ankiro.in.” Use on the homepage and first owned post.

## B. Day only — 15 seconds

0–2: **“The month and year aren't needed here.”**

2–10: Show example 22→4 with the working. “This introductory Moolank reduces the digits of the day.”

10–15: **“Traditional reflection, not prediction. Free insight at ankiro.in.”** First short clip; don't show someone's real birthday.

## C. Human, scheduled — 20 seconds

0–2: **“This isn't a person waiting on call.”**

2–12: Show actual consultation process. “The team reviews the request, connects an expert and agrees a time. Sessions are described as about 20 minutes.”

12–20: “Know the person, scope and price before paying.” URL. Second clip; no fake expert call.

## D. Review before send — 25 seconds

0–2: **“A request should not be a surprise charge.”**

2–15: Walk through the actual form with fictional details, stop at review. “Check what goes out and where. Ask the team to confirm amount and time.”

15–25: “You decide whether to continue. The free insight doesn't require a consultation.” URL. Third clip; don't record a real person's question or payment reference.
