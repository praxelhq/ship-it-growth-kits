# Ankiro — Positioning

**Live:** https://ankiro.in/ · **Category:** Other / human spiritual guidance · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/ankiro

**The website calculates a free number; a person delivers the consultation.** Don't market Ankiro as an instant AI astrologer. The [live page](https://ankiro.in/), checked 29 October, says detailed guidance is from human experts and sessions are scheduled personally, not on demand.

Current headline: **“Your questions. Deeper answers. A brighter you.”** CTA: **“Start a consultation request”**, leading to `/consultation/request`. The [four-step form](https://ankiro.in/consultation/request) says users review before anything leaves the browser. The team confirms price/time before UPI payment. There is no published fixed consultation price; [sessions](https://ankiro.in/consultation) are described as about 20 minutes. This kit did not send a request or test the payment/session.

## Find one person, not everyone seeking answers

An adult already curious about traditional numerology who wants the calculation explained and the choice of a scheduled human conversation. Start with the transparent Moolank example, not fear about an uncertain future or a promise that a gemstone solves a health/money problem.

**Pitch:** Understand your Moolank for free, then request a clearly arranged human consultation if you want to explore further.

**Bio:** A traditional reflection, explained. A human conversation if you choose.

The [free insight](https://ankiro.in/free-insight) uses only the birth day, reduces digits to 1–9 and shows the working. It says the calculation happens in-browser and the date is not sent/stored. Treat that as the product's stated behaviour, not an independent security audit. Don't request full names or complete birth dates in public feedback.

[AstroSage's free reports](https://www.astrosage.com/free/) are a real $0 alternative, although their predictive language differs. Doing the displayed digit arithmetic yourself also costs no additional software purchase. Your wedge is a narrow transparent introduction and clearly scheduled human help, not more certainty than another service.

The expert page does not publish an advance roster. This is a trust gap to address before payment, not permission to invent credentials. Offer a real introduction and terms before someone commits.

The board target remains **1,000 signups**. For the free no-account tool define unique consumer insight sessions and completed number results; report human requests, confirmed sessions and repeat use separately. A calculator visitor is not automatically a consultation customer.
