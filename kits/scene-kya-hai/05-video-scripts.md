# Scene Kya Hai? — Video scripts

Use built-in/fictional mood input and a broad public area. No home coordinates, private answers or fake venue confirmation.

## A. Complete-flow demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“Can the result be as good as the opening line?”**

2–15s, show actual step count and non-sensitive input. “This is the current flow, not a one-tap claim.”

15–30s, manual broad area and actual remaining questions after verified. “You can choose what to share.”

30–45s, show the real result/reason. “Does this fit, and what is its source?”

45–55s, check current venue information without a purchase. “A suggestion isn't a booking.”

55–60s: URL and **Your call, always**. Don't edit an absent result into success.

## B. Mood hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “'Do something' isn't a plan.”

2–10s: actual mood → result clip after it works. “Make one choice worth checking.”

10–15s: URL and **Example input · verify current details**.

## C. Location hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Location permission shouldn't be a surprise.”

2–12s: actual manual area alternative and explanation. “Tell people what you need before asking for it.”

12–20s: broad demo area, no permission grant required for the video.

## D. Relevance hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Why this scene?”

2–12s: actual reason tied to a fictional preference and verified result. “A clear reason beats a random list.”

12–20s: edit/reject option if implemented. “The product suggests. You choose.”
