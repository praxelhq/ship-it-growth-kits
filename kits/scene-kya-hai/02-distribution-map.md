# Scene Kya Hai? — Distribution map

**Start in one supported city with people who actually have time for a plan.** Don't invite a national audience before results are reliable locally.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult local testers | One broad city/area and an actual free-evening use case; no home coordinates or private moods sent to you. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed useful results if the flow works. | #1 |
| Two existing local alumni/friend groups | Admin-approved one-time invitation, no venue/booking guarantee. | 2 × 40 relevant readers × 20–40% try = 16–32 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | Actual mood-to-result demonstration, step count and venue check on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 40% start = 2–96 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Choice-relevance and location-trust lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 40% start = 2–24 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback only after a completed usable result, current rules/slot checked. No votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No paid city page, influencer, venue subsidy or newsletter sponsorship is assumed. No unverified city subreddit permission. A free local alumni newsletter mention can be requested after a real organizer agrees, with zero baseline forecast. Don't post a private starting point or precise location in public examples.

At 40% visitor-to-start, 1,000 needs 2,500 relevant unique visits. The initial tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Replace rates after three clips; a visually fun first step doesn't prove the eight-step result retains people.

Use Days 24–27 for your local discovery push and separate groups from Adda's Bengaluru fairness tests, Shotgun's roles/split and Crewup's hosted board-game/study plans. Scene's job is an individual's mood-to-choice, not the whole group's coordination.
