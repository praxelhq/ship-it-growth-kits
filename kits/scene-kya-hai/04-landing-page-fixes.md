# Scene Kya Hai? — Landing page fixes

Based on actual browser entry and first two curation screens checked 29 October. No location permission, personal location, full result or payment was submitted.

## 1. Explain location and offer before the eight-step commitment

The second screen asks an area/city or location detection. Before permission/input, state what is sent, why it is needed, retention and the manual alternative. Publish the actual free/paid allowance early. Don't ask someone for several steps of input just to reveal an unexpected price.

## 2. Keep the voice, clarify the job

Current headline: **“SceneKyaHai?”** CTA: **“Let's make questionable plans.”**

Suggested support: **“Tell us the mood and broad starting area. Get a scene to inspect, with the final choice yours.”** Button: **“Find a scene to check.”** Keep the playful brand line as personality; don't let it imply reckless or unwanted action.

## 3. Show honest progress and a sample result

The flow displays 1/8. Give a non-sensitive example result before the form and explain actual effort without an unmeasured time claim. Label any sample venue and cost. Don't imply an example is a currently verified listing.

## 4. Verify the whole result and its reasons

Test a supported city, broad manual location, incompatible request, empty result and slow network. For a real venue, show source/freshness and a reason it fits. Copy: **“Check current opening hours, price and availability before travelling. This is not a reservation.”** Missing live facts should stay visibly missing.

## 5. Preserve user control

The footer says **“Your call, always.”** Let users edit, reject, skip where possible and start again without pressure. Don't use the “bad decisions” tone to push a choice outside their stated comfort/budget.

## 6. Test phone/permission failures

At 390px, check textarea/keyboard, denied location permission, manual fallback, back navigation and result readability. Mask moods, locations and answers from replay. Preview: **“Scene Kya Hai — a mood-led idea, your choice.”** No precise starting point, fake partnership or unverified booking in the image.
