# Bloomie Mental Health — Distribution map

**Lead with private, opt-in adult usability tests after the trust gate passes.** Do not sell into distress, enter support groups to pitch, or ask people to reveal why they want a self-care app.

|Route|Specific action / boundary|Planning result, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Adults you already know|Ten private invitations across Days 5/12, only after accurate privacy/support wording and tests. Synthetic entries allowed for UX feedback.|10 × 40% interest × 50% account = 2 accounts. No health improvement inferred.|#1|
|Your own LinkedIn feed|Four factual maker posts Days 8/15/22/29 at 09:00 IST. No private user stories or treatment claims.|4 × assumed 200 impressions × 2% visits × 20% signup = about 3 accounts; zero possible.|#2|
|Existing adult campus wellbeing/reading organizer|Ask for one optional ten-person navigation demonstration in Days 24–27. No therapy positioning or contact list.|10 invites × 50% attend × 40% signup = 2 accounts; zero until permission.|#3|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Usable product/transparent limitations, current slot confirmed. No vote exchange or medical endorsement.|0–10 testers, optional upside.|#4|
|[r/Journaling rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/Journaling/about/rules)|Endpoint resolved but rule text wasn't extractable. Read current sidebar/ask moderators before any promotional development post.|Zero until allowed. No unsolicited DMs to vulnerable posters.|#5|

The official [Daylio account linked from its own site](https://www.instagram.com/hellodaylio/) and [Medito's public material](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app) are research sources for clear product explanations, not audiences to solicit. Do not post a competing app link under someone's personal disclosure.

A permitted organizer bulletin can carry #3 once. No external newsletter placement is verified, and paid health partnerships are outside budget. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is a possible maker directory after the trust gate, using a personal account and no upvote requests; inspect existing listings first. Don't launch to hide unfinished data handling.

This plan supports a small number of voluntary testers, not a proven 1,000 accounts. At an assumed 20% visit→signup rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified visits. No evidence says the calendar reaches that. Expand only if actual users understand the privacy terms and return by choice; never use sensitive-content sharing as a referral mechanism.
