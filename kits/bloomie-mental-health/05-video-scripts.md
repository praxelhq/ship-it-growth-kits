# Bloomie Mental Health — Video scripts

Use synthetic entries and your own recording. Never show a real journal, mood history or another person's account. These are product demonstrations, not treatment testimonials.

## A. One quiet first action — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “You don't need to share anything personal to test this.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Public page and actual data explanation|“Bloomie is a self-care check-in, not a clinical service.”|
|10–25s|Your test account, labelled synthetic content|“I'm using a made-up entry to show the flow.”|
|25–40s|Actual first check-in and save, if working|“A short action should be understandable without a long tutorial.”|
|40–52s|Data/export/delete controls, only after tested|“The choices need to be easy to find before you enter real material.”|
|52–60s|Plain URL and voluntary-test invitation|“Try the navigation only if the data explanation works for you.”|

Publish only after `04`'s trust gate. If a control isn't implemented, show the limitation rather than editing in a false success.

## B. Calm isn't privacy — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A calm screen isn't a privacy explanation.” Show the landing page, then the actual completed data/support page. Say: “Who stores entries? What does optional AI send? How do I delete? These need real answers.” Do not claim certification or reveal account information. Use as a factual maker update.

## C. Made-up entry — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “Please don't show me your journal.” Type a neutral synthetic line, label it clearly and show only navigation. Say: “A usability test can use made-up content. I want to see where the interface confuses you, not your personal life.” Post on your own feed.

## D. Return by choice — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Opening it again should be your choice.” Show the reminder setting, then a quiet exit. Say: “A small reflection doesn't need public streak pressure. Choose the reminder that suits you, or none.” Only demonstrate settings that actually exist; no invented toggle. This is design intent, not proof that mood improved.
