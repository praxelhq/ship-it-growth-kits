# Bloomie Mental Health — Positioning

**Live:** https://bloomie.world/ · **Category:** Health and fitness · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/bloomie-mental-health

**Finish the privacy and support explanation before inviting sensitive journal entries.** The current [privacy page](https://www.bloomie.world/privacy) identifies itself as a template and leaves hosting-region, backup-retention and contact details unresolved. The [help page](https://www.bloomie.world/help) also has unfinished support information. Do not repeat “Only you can read it” as an independently verified security guarantee.

The real current headline is “Small moments of care, every day.” The main action is “Create a free account” → `/signup`. It describes mood/energy/body check-ins, a journal, short guided sessions and personal patterns. The older roster promise that it will solve people's issues is not the current product and should not become your marketing.

Your first user is an adult student who wants a brief, private end-of-day reflection, not diagnosis, therapy or a crisis response. The current policy says 18+. Start with **one optional minute of noticing the day**, not a promise of feeling better or a public mood streak.

**Pitch:** Take a quiet minute to check in, write a reflection and notice your own patterns over time.

**Bio:** A small daily self-care check-in, at your pace.

The wedge is the first private check-in followed by a voluntary return, with no requirement to share the content. A product tester can judge navigation without entering a real personal disclosure. Do not ask classmates to demonstrate vulnerable journal material to prove adoption.

[Medito](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app) is free forever, with no account/paywall for its meditation content. [Daylio](https://daylio.net/) is an established mood/activity log; its applicable premium price was not established from the official page, so do not guess one. Your potential distinction is a calm integrated browser check-in/reflection experience—not claiming all alternatives charge or that a new app provides clinical care.

The honest weakness is trust before habit. A free signup isn't useful if people cannot understand who holds their entries, what optional AI does, or how deletion/support works. Complete those facts and test the implementation before traffic.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**, with completed first check-ins and later-day returners beside it. This measures use, not improved health. No therapeutic outcome or safety monitoring is implied.
