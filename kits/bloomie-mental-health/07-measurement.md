# Bloomie Mental Health — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real adult accounts, excluding synthetic tests and duplicate registrations. Report **completed first check-ins** and later-day returns beside that headline. These are product-use metrics, not measures of improved mental health.

## Day 0 baseline and privacy boundary

Record existing accounts and aggregate first-check-in/return counts from your own system; these private numbers were unavailable in research. Note unfinished privacy/support items as blockers, not zero-valued marketing metrics. Use [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on public acquisition pages only where appropriate; keep journal, health-input and private account screens out of session replay. Do not collect feelings, entry text, sensitive tags or AI prompts in analytics labels.

Record the actual [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/bloomie-mental-health) reading, reporting period and rank. A public traffic reading is neither an account count nor evidence of wellbeing. Optional internal product events should be minimum aggregate `signup_completed`, `first_checkin_completed`, `later_day_returned` and support issue counts; no mood score is needed for acquisition measurement.

## Honest funnel

The calendar has ten private adult invitations, four owned-feed posts and one optional ten-person demo. It supports a small tester cohort under uncertain assumptions, not a proven 1,000 accounts. At an assumed 20% visit→signup rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified visits. Do not close the gap by targeting people in distress or importing sensitive-group contact lists.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Trust gate completion, actual first actions, questions about data|Any unresolved/incorrect privacy or support promise takes priority over promotion.|
|Day 14|Accounts→first check-ins and voluntary later returns|If people cannot find the first action or controls, fix navigation; don't request private content as proof.|
|Day 21|Aggregate returns and unresolved account/data issues|Unserved deletion/export/support issues pause expansion.|
|Day 30|Accounts, first check-ins, returners, actual reach, hours and remaining blockers|State the gap to 1,000 without implying a clinical outcome.|

Keep weekly aggregate rows only: visits, accounts, first check-ins, later returns, outstanding support/control issues, source and hours. A staged entry in a video is a test, not user traction. An optional AI response is not a diagnosis or verified therapeutic benefit.
