# Noteorious — Video scripts

Use an agreed non-sensitive test conversation and actual compatible Mac. No hidden recording, fabricated transcript or untested language claim. Screen recording, voice and captions suffice.

**Where:**owned clips/project-circle demo.** Hook:**“Who actually agreed to this?”

| Time | Shot/text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Participants' visible agreement/test script. | “Who actually agreed to this? We are recording a test call and checking the notes together.” |
| 8–22 s | Actual recording indicator on compatible Mac. | “This is the current release, with the actual permissions and mode.” |
| 22–35 s | Real summary/action output. | “The app gives us a draft. We check the decision, owner and stated date.” |
| 35–48 s | Actual correction against original words. | “This needed correcting. A plausible answer is not an agreed action.” |
| 48–60 s | Price/compatibility/model note/URL. | “Noteorious. $5 once, Apple silicon, macOS 14.4+. Read setup and privacy details first.” |
