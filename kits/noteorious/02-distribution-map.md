## Honest composition

Twenty personal invitations, two demos, eight clips and one listing model **8–81 valid first launches before overlap**. That is not sufficient evidence for 1,000. At assumed 10% qualified-visitor-to-install, the target needs 10,000 qualified visitors. Purchases without model-ready first launch do not close the gap.

Use the early Mac-project-call window Days 4–7; DeClutterMac has a later copied-folder exercise and SoundBuddy a pre-call microphone demo. Separate contacts and actual dates; one primary invitation per organizer each seven days. Do not create a referral loop around sharing private transcripts.
