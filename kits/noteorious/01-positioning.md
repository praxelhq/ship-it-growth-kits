# Noteorious — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.noteorious.works/ · **Category:** Productivity · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/noteorious

**“No bot joins” is not permission to record people without telling them.** Your first demo should be an agreed test conversation, with the participants checking the resulting decisions and owners. That is stronger proof than a privacy slogan.

The [actual headline](https://www.noteorious.works/) is “You talk. It writes.” The CTA says “Pay & Download for Mac.” The offer is $5 once for up to 2 Macs, Apple silicon M1+ and macOS 14.4 or later. Public home/privacy checked 29 October 2026; purchase, installation, recording and generated notes were not tested.

## Your first person

An adult student builder or individual professional with an Apple-silicon Mac, who attends a small English/Hindi/Hinglish project call and then has to reconstruct who agreed to do what. They want a local note-taking workflow, not a team procurement system or an invisible meeting guest.

Start with non-sensitive consented project discussions. The page advertises summaries, decisions, owners/dates when mentioned, searchable transcript and imports. It also advertises automatic meeting start and language switching; demonstrate the exact current release rather than treating every claim as tested.

## Pitch

> Turn an agreed project conversation on your Mac into decisions and action items you can check.

Short version:

> One call. Checked decisions. Clear next actions.

Your wedge is a three-minute test call with two actual decisions and one next action, then manual verification of the output. Activation is a completed usable note/transcript after a consented test. Retention is another independently initiated recording/import on a later day.

## The weakness

The paid-only first entry and 3.9 GB setup mean a casual visitor cannot instantly judge the result. A stranger may buy, download, and still not reach a first note because of permissions/models. Show that setup honestly and collect actual failure reasons before pushing a large launch.

Keep **1,000 signups**, defined as valid new installs/first launches, with checked-note users beside it. Purchases/downloads are separate. The initial niche plan does not honestly forecast a thousand; `02` shows the gap.
