# Cassify — 30-day execution calendar

**Week 1 is a build week. There is no marketing in it.**

That is unusual in this cluster and it's the right call for you: your site saves nothing, so every visitor you send it today is a visitor you lose — and most of them are on your campus or in your society, which are the channels that matter for the next two years.

**Days 1–7: make two levels real. Days 8–30: three weeks of distribution into a market nobody else is serving.**

Open this file every morning.

## The target, and what to tell the board in week 1

**You lose a week to the build, so 1,000 signups is unlikely.** A fair expectation is **300–700**, and that would be a good month from where you're starting.

**Say this in week 1, not week 4.** The alternative — launching on day 2 against a product that saves nothing — produces a worse number *and* burns your own campus and your societies, which are channels you need for years.

What you should be judged on, and what `07` reports first:

| | Target |
| --- | --- |
| **Two levels working with saved progress** | **by day 7** |
| **Level 01 completions** | **400** |
| **Level 01 → Level 02 rate** | **35%** |
| **Societies that shared it** | **5** |
| Signups | 300–700 |

## The five things that decide this month

1. **The build landing by day 7.** Nothing else on this list exists without it.
2. **Not launching early.** The single most tempting and most expensive mistake available to you.
3. **The twelve Instagram reels, posted daily.** Your audience is undergraduates on Instagram and your text-card design is better than anyone's talking head. One breakout reel does 20,000 views on its own.
4. **65 society DMs with the story graphics attached.** The graphics are what turn a DM into a post, and societies are how you reach campuses you have no connection to.
5. **Unstop, every week.** It's the only place your user is identifiable *at the moment she needs you*, and it refreshes constantly.

## What you have that nobody else in this cohort does

**An audience with no competitor.** Of 307 Ship It projects, you and Case Room are the only two aimed at case competitions, and Case Room does teams while you do individuals. Nine other case-prep projects are fighting over the MBA consulting candidate.

**You also have no cliff.** Competitions run all year — [21,000+ live listings on Unstop](https://unstop.com/competitions) at any time. Skill Prep's audience vanishes on 29 November; the placement kits go quiet in December. **January looks exactly like November for you**, which means a week spent building in week 1 costs you much less than it would cost anyone else here.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 18 — compounds into next term), **Peerlist** (day 12 — builder audience, not undergraduates), **campus subreddits** (day 16).

**Never drop:** days 1–7 (the build), the daily reel, day 9/11/13 (the society DMs with graphics), day 10 and 17 (Unstop), day 15 (converting the yeses).
