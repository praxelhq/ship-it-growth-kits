**Read this first: nothing here works until the page is visible** (`04` Fix 1–2).

Your site is 1,024 bytes with no meta description and no `og:image`, and your deep links 404. Every channel below is either a search result (you have none), a shared link preview (yours renders as bare text), or a direct URL (which 404s if it points anywhere but the homepage). **Posting before day 3 spends every channel at a fraction of its value, permanently** — a Reddit thread and a Peerlist listing stay up for years.

Then two things make your plan unlike anyone else's in this cluster:

1. **You're internationally positioned.** NYC, London, Berlin, Oxford/LSE/Ivy League benchmarks. **Your ten cohort-mates are all competing for Indian campuses. You can go where none of them are**, into a larger market with higher willingness to pay.
2. **Your Datasheet is the most SEO-shaped asset in the cluster.** Benchmark figures are searched constantly, year-round, by exactly your audience — and you have them curated already.

**Your window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is "after the page renders."** Take **days 8–12**.

1. **You lose the first two or three days to making the product visible at all**, so you have less channel time than your cohort-mates. That's unavoidable and it's still the right trade — **a fortnight of posting into a product with no previews and 404ing links is worth less than ten days of posting into a visible one.**

The ranked levers: **make it visible** (`04`), **publish the Datasheet as pages** (nobody else can), and **go international**, where ten cohort-mates aren't.
