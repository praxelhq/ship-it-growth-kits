# Ballpark — 30-day execution calendar

**The target is 1,000 account signups in 30 days** (`TARGET-POLICY.md`). The free Datasheet can attract someone without an account; report those readers separately, never as signups. Budget: **zero**. One student, a phone, the product, and its existing case library.

**Do not publish a link until the three `04` launch checks pass:** the homepage returns readable HTML, a shared URL shows a useful preview, and `/app` opens directly and survives refresh. Days 1–2 prepare named assets for named channels; day 3 is the first possible public post. If the checks fail, move the public rows forward and spend that day's slot repairing them. Never send people to a broken page to keep a calendar date.

Your public launch window in the [case-prep cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is **days 8–12**. Reserve **one r/MBA post**, and check the community's current rules before posting or adding a link. `02` ranks search first for long-term value; for signups *this month*, lead with international consulting communities, clubs, your own campus, and LinkedIn. Search pages start their indexing clock now, but do not count on November search traffic to deliver 1,000.

Use the numbered assets in `03-copy-pack.md` and the lettered formats in `05-video-scripts.md`. A “benchmark page” below means **`03` #7**: verified number, source and date, case use, one worked line, and a Datasheet link. A “walkthrough” means **`03` #5** and **`05` Format A**, with a forgotten branch, sanity check, and interviewer follow-up. Publish a page only after checking its number against the underlying source; a plausible invented benchmark would break the whole pitch.

## What gets cut when time runs short

Cut the lowest-yield experiments first: a second Quora answer, a YouTube comment, then extra video cross-posts. Keep the **launch checks**, the **Datasheet hub and verified benchmark pages**, the **single r/MBA post in its slot**, and the **personalised club outreach**. If a community removes a post, do not repost a disguised ad. Put that hour into the next club note or an existing page with a proven search impression.

**One rule throughout:** never turn a reach estimate, a video view, a Datasheet reader, or a club DM into a “signup.” `07` counts accounts from Ballpark itself and reports first-case activation beside them.
