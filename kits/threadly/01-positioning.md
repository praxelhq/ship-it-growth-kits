# Threadly — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.gothreadly.live/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/threadly

Checked 29 October 2026 against [gothreadly.live](https://www.gothreadly.live/). Actual headline: **“When buyers ask AI, does it mention you?”** CTAs: **“Start tracking your brand”** to `/portal` and **“See the live demo”** to `/demo`. The demo uses sample data. Account, paid checkout, scan, API keys and team permissions were not exercised.

## The first business

A small agency responsible for a permitted recurring brand-visibility report. It has an actual client mandate, ten stable buyer questions and someone who can review original answers/sources. Start with one authorized brand and a consistent question set, not a promise to improve every client's AI rankings.

**Pitch: Track the same buyer questions over time and show the actual answers behind the visibility report.**

Short version: **A repeatable answer sample, with the evidence attached.**

The useful job is comparing like-for-like responses: same question, provider/configuration, date and included competitors. A mention is not a recommendation, a citation is not traffic and an early position is not evidence of sales. API responses may differ from what an individual sees in a consumer assistant; do not claim this measures every buyer's experience.

## Actual paid terms and contradictions

The public offer is a **$7 one-time seven-day trial with one full scan**, ten questions and one seat. Pro shows **$39/month billed annually**, 50 questions/five seats; Growth **$119/month billed annually**, 150 questions/25 seats. Annual checkout links are explicit; the displayed monthly equivalent is not a monthly purchase price. The FAQ says monthly/annual terms are one-time purchases through Razorpay and do not renew automatically. Final total, tax, currency and entitlement were not tested.

The page also says “no credit card” near the free-looking entry and “unlimited manual scans” in a general FAQ, while the paid trial comparison says one total. Reconcile those before paid promotion. Do not call the trial free or unlimited. Pro+ bring-your-own keys use the customer's accounts/quota, potentially adding provider cost beyond the software purchase. Do not request keys in outreach or public demos.

The product promises ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini scans with web/search grounding, archives, mentions, citation share and share of voice. Those are website claims, not tested coverage or a privacy audit. Inspect original answers and failed scans before treating a percentage as comparable. Do not imply providers endorse Threadly.

## The alternative

An agency can record a small permitted manual answer sample in free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/), keeping question, provider, date, answer excerpt, exact sources and errors. Threadly must earn adoption through repeatability, history and review convenience; it cannot guarantee a brand will be included or that a recommended content change causes future visibility.

Prove a synthetic sample, paid-trial allowance, one authorized own-brand scan, answer archive and source denominator before a client report. The brand owner controls what may be processed/shared. Never publish client strategy, prompts or API keys as product proof.

**Target: 10 real agency businesses in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each needs its actual authorized brand/question data onboarded, independent last-seven-day use and a privately known owner name/phone. Client brands, seats, questions and demo visits are separate counts, not extra agency customers.
