# Threadly — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each agency must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine operation; actual authorized own/client brand-question data onboarded; independent use within the last seven days; privately known owner name/phone. Keep identifiers outside this public repo. Paid-inactive agencies fail; sample-only viewers fail.

Activation: an agency completes an actual authorized scan, inspects original answers/sources and can explain the metric's denominator/failures for a real reporting task. Report that beside customers. Seats, client brands, questions, generated answers and scans are separate activity counts, not additional agency businesses.

No private scan or billing data was accessed; actual accounts, scan completion and client outcomes are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Genuine agencies / latest independent reporting use | Workspace records plus private permitted owner register; exclude seeded demos. |
| Authorized own-brand setups / successful scans | Actual scan status and question/provider/date configuration. |
| Reviewed answers/sources | Owner review status or consented aggregate feedback, not client prompts in growth logs. |
| Denominator/failure handling | Successful-provider count and explicit failed/partial results, not silently mixed data. |
| Actual term/payment/provider cost | Billing/entitlement and owner-authorized provider records, separately reported. |
| Traffic/course baseline | Existing consented analytics and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/threadly), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate. Exclude client strategy, prompts, reports, API keys, answer archives where private, account/payment and invite links from replay. Minimal events: ownbrandconfigured, scancompleted, scanfailed, answerreviewed, sourcereviewed, laterindependentreportuse. A source click is not a verified correct claim; a mention is not traffic or a sale.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If trial/annual price is misunderstood, fix 04 before asking for payment. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare actual own scans/reviews with demo/empty-account interest; inspect expiry and one-scan allowance. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Check independent reporting use and consistent questions/providers, not unrelated score changes. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests and count agencies, not their client brands/seats. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying agencies against ten, activation, independent use, true paid/provider costs, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts/two intros are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. Trial expiry and real agency mandate may lower onboarding/use; show useful one-off reports separately from final-week active customers, without a reminder manufactured for measurement.

Displayed $39/$119 monthly equivalents are not the annual amount due or verified checkout totals. A paid trial is not a free/unlimited scan plan, own keys can create separate provider cost, and prepaid terms do not renew automatically according to the FAQ. No visibility growth, client revenue or causal content result is proven by sample scans. Publish only permitted actual observations and limitations.
