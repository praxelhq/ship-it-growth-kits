# Threadly — Landing-page fixes

Read [gothreadly.live](https://www.gothreadly.live/) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline **“When buyers ask AI, does it mention you?”** CTAs **“Start tracking your brand”** / **“See the live demo.”** Trial $7/seven days/one scan; Pro $39/month and Growth $119/month displayed as annually billed. FAQ says prepaid monthly/annual terms, no automatic renewal. No account, checkout, keys or scan exercised.

## 1. Reconcile free-looking entry with a paid trial

Current hero says no credit card; pricing says seven days for $7 one time.

Replacement CTA note: **“Inspect the sample demo free. The actual trial costs $7 once for seven days and one full scan.”** Use only if the sample really remains available without payment.

Do not call Start tracking a free trial or imply ongoing no-cost tracking. Distinguish signup, sample access and purchase.

## 2. Reconcile one scan with general unlimited-manual-scan FAQ

Current comparison gives Trial one total, while a general FAQ promises daily plus unlimited manual runs.

Replacement allowance note: **“Trial: one full scan total. Other plan scan schedules/limits are listed separately.”**

Verify actual counters, retries and failures before claiming unlimited paid scans. A failed provider should not silently appear as an absent mention.

## 3. Show the real annual total before checkout

Current Pro/Growth cards show monthly equivalents with annual checkout links.

Replacement price layout: **“Annual term: show the full amount due. Monthly equivalent shown only as a comparison. Review taxes and prepaid/no-auto-renewal terms.”**

Calculate the total from the real checkout/configuration, not a guessed discount. Do not say $39 buys one month when the selected route is annual.

## 4. Keep provider charges separate

Current Pro+ BYOK says scans run on customer accounts/quota and keys encrypted.

Replacement connection note: **“Optional own-key scans may create provider charges beyond Threadly's price. Review permissions, storage and revocation before connecting.”**

Test scope/roles with authorized synthetic workspaces before actual client data. Never ask for keys in a sales conversation or imply encryption wording is an independent audit.

## 5. Make metrics inspectable and comparable

Current definitions include mention rate, citation share, share of voice and position.

Replacement report footer: **“Question set, provider/configuration, scan date, successful-answer count and failures shown here. Inspect the original answers and exact cited sources.”**

Verify denominators and failed-provider handling using samples. A linked domain appearing in an answer is not a click, endorsement or sale. Consumer assistant results may vary from API responses.

## 6. Separate suggestions from promised growth

Current visibility-gap recommendations can help a report but do not prove a content change causes a result.

Replacement recommendation label: **“A hypothesis to review, not a placement guarantee. Record actual changes and comparable later scans before drawing conclusions.”**

Test account save/history, paid-trial expiry, export, seat permissions, deletion and billing with owned sample data. Keep sample labels attached and never seed a real workspace with illustrative gains presented as actual results.
