# Threadly — Phone-demo scripts

Use a fictional brand and sample questions, your screen recorder and voice. No real client strategy, report, account email, API key or paid scan submitted as a demonstration without owner consent. Sample dashboards remain labelled.

## A. The evidence behind the rate — 65 seconds

0–3 s: sample mention percentage beside its question. **“What is this number counting?”**

3–17 s: actual supported brand/question setup, or labelled demo. “Keep the question set and provider configuration clear.”

17–32 s: actual saved answer and exact source. “Read what the model actually said before treating a mention as a recommendation.” Leave one supported archive/source interaction continuous.

32–47 s: inspect successful-answer denominator and failed providers where shown. “A failed scan is not a clean no-mention result.” If denominator/error detail is missing, say so and label the gap.

47–55 s: comparable later result only if a real test exists. “Compare like with like, not two unrelated prompts.” Do not fake a trend.

55–65 s: trial and annual-price boundary. “Seven-day trial, $7 once, one scan. Review the selected term before paying. gothreadly.live.”

## B. Citation hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: sample source and brand mention. **“Named is not the same as cited.”**

3–12 s: actual answer/source inspection. “Check whether the domain is a source, not just a word in the reply.”

12–20 s: sample label and URL. “A better report keeps the evidence attached.” No traffic or revenue claim.

## C. Price hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: annual-billed price card. **“Monthly equivalent is not the amount due.”**

3–15 s: actual selected-term disclosure, no payment submitted. “Check the full period, trial scan limit and any own-key provider cost.”

15–25 s: actual paid-trial versus demo boundary and URL. “Know the allowance before the first client report.”

Capture exact headline/CTA, sample disclosure, trial one-scan limit, annual billing, answer archive, source/denominator and failure state. Label wait cuts. Do not invent scans, customer improvement or API-key security tests.
