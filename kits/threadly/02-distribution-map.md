# Threadly — Distribution map

Lead with agency reporting, not a generic “rank in AI” promise. ArtificialInfluence already owns direct brand-owner visibility diagnostics; keep the buyer list distinct.

| Route | Action / permission | Planned input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small SEO/content agencies | Qualify actual recurring reporting responsibility, one authorized brand and ten stable questions. Use genuine public business enquiries or consented professional introductions. | Ten blocks of four agencies = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two agency-operator introductions | Ask a known strategist to forward one evidence-review invitation; no client lists, prompts or private contacts. | Days 16 and 19; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Show a labelled sample answer/denominator and failure boundary, not an invented mention-rate lift. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One owner-controlled report review | Agency reviews original answers, exact questions, source links and failed-scan handling after actual scope/paid terms verified. | Day 17; #5. Existing-trial support. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% authorized own-brand setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 agencies. At 25% conversation, 4.9. Ten four-business blocks occur Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. Posts/intros add no guaranteed conversions. A seven-day trial with one scan may not support a month of recurring use; do not assume a paid upgrade or count repeated sample views as retention.

Reserve **Days 16–19** for agency recurring-visibility reporting under [AI-visibility coordination](../../shared/a2-ai-visibility.md). ArtificialInfluence direct brand owners use Days 4–7. BrandAid agency positioning briefs are a separate job; if the same strategist overlaps, pick one immediate need and leave at least a week between primary organizer invitations. Client-brand authorization is required even when an agency owner likes the tool.

No GEO newsletter, Slack group, SEO community or provider partnership has agreed to distribute Threadly. Ask the known organizer before any post. No paid listings, bulk DMs, scraped emails, vote requests or invented AI-ranking case study. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after decline.

The [docs](https://www.gothreadly.live/docs) and [security page](https://www.gothreadly.live/security) are product references to inspect before client data or keys, not independent certification. Resolve trial/scan/billing contradictions before asking a buyer to pay. A demo can establish format, not actual current brand coverage.
