# WhatNxt — Landing-page and selection fixes

Homepage through equipment→area→beginner filter→actual result tiles inspected in browser 29 October with fictional choices. No physical exercise, form video, account or analytics permission. No clinical suitability assessment.

1. **Make speed subordinate to inspection.** Current H1 “Bored mid-workout?” support “Get your next set in 10 seconds,” CTA “START” opens equipment. Proposed support **“Filter movement options by equipment, area and level. Inspect the cues before deciding whether one fits your plan.”** Button **“Explore movement options.”** Measure real selection time, including an understandable first-run disclaimer, before repeating ten seconds as tested fact.

2. **Don't use a level label as safety clearance.** Current Beginner description “Simple moves, low risk”; Any level default mixes everything. Use **“Less complex in this library—not a guarantee of suitability or safety.”** Explain level criteria and let newcomers deliberately choose a filter. No algorithm knows injury, skill or limits just from three inputs. Don't infer safe from beginner.

3. **Keep a result consistent with available equipment.** Observed Bodyweight→Chest→Beginner produced three tiles including a bench-named movement. Check whether required support/equipment is clear and truly available, not just a broad category tag. Show actual required setup on each tile and a reject/back option. Don't manufacture variety by including unavailable kit or changing the selected level silently.

4. **Make information/cues inspectable before action.** Result says tap for sets, form cues and video. Verify accurate source/instructor provenance, accessible captions and limits before presenting technical instructions. This research didn't inspect or endorse video quality. Don't turn a generic set/rep suggestion into a personal prescription; show unavailable/unsupported guidance honestly.

5. **Clarify phone/install status.** “Open on phone” links `/install`, while desktop also works. Explain actual browser/PWA installation and offline limits if supported. Don't imply released native apps from QR/install wording. Repeated mobile prompts shouldn't block a valid desktop flow or obscure back/result controls.

6. **Keep analytics and preferences private.** Banner says optional Google Analytics off until allowed, no exercise selections/account details sent. Verify actual implementation before claiming that privacy scope. Don't add extra replay or personal health fields for growth. A general information flow doesn't need body measurements, health conditions or a public workout history.

7. **Check mobile flow states.** At 390px, test no equipment selection, Back retaining choices, empty/three-match result, long cue/video and Shuffle respecting filters. Preview **“WhatNxt — filter movement information by equipment and level.”** No body transformation, implied trainer approval or unsafe challenge. A polite rejected result is useful feedback, not failed activation to hide.
