# Create or Die — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.createordie.in/ · **Category:** Content and media · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/create-or-die

Your first user is a student creator with three unfinished posts who wants to publish twice next week. **Do not sell fear of failure to somebody who needs a manageable first commitment.** Your pirate metaphor is memorable; a useful publishing habit must survive the joke.

Current headline: “CREATE OR DIE.” The page says to choose one publishing quota and deadline, log each live link, and survive the voyage. “BOARD THE SHIP” goes to `/auth`; the auth page exists, but account creation and a completed voyage were not tested. No price was visible on the checked pages; do not promise a permanent free tier without confirming it.

**Pitch:** Pick a small publishing target, log the work you actually publish, and finish one voyage with other creators.

**Bio:** Less draft collecting. One publishing commitment, with live links as proof.

The wedge is a **seven-day, two-post voyage** for people already making original work. A draft, copied AI output or fake URL is not a shipped piece. Make the first commitment small enough to finish, then ask whether the creator starts another voyage without you.

[Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) costs $0 per member/month and can hold a publishing log. [Beeminder](https://www.beeminder.com/) tracks quantifiable goals and charges when users cross their commitment line; its exact applicable pledge/premium cost varies, so don't quote a guessed monthly comparison. Your advantage is the publishing-specific proof and one clear finite challenge, not “the only accountability app.”

The weakness is shame without durable motivation. The current line “Miss the quota. Lose the voyage. Re-board and try again” can be playful, but a missed week must not erase a creator's real published work. Make recovery explicit and keep public metrics opt-in, consistent with the auth page's privacy statement.

Keep the B2C target **1,000 signups**. Report creators who publish a first valid piece and those who complete a voyage beside it. Account registrations with no work are not the success story.
