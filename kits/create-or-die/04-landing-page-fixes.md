# Create or Die — Landing-page fixes

Read 29 October 2026: homepage and `/auth`. No account or voyage was created. The auth route is real; its existence is not proof that every signup state works.

1. **Explain the job beneath the theme.** Current H1: “CREATE OR DIE.” Keep the brand and replace the supporting line with **“Publish two original pieces in seven days. Log the live links. Finish your first voyage.”** Offer the example as a choice, not a mandatory product limit. The human is a nervous first-time publisher; make the next action clearer than the punishment.

2. **Make the CTA describe what follows.** Current: “BOARD THE SHIP” → `/auth`. Replace primary label with **“Choose my first voyage”** and show **“Create an account, choose one quota and deadline, then log published work.”** Test account creation, return routing and a failed submit on a fresh phone before driving traffic.

3. **Show one real voyage.** Current “CHOOSE YOUR VOYAGE / LOG YOUR PUBLISHED WORK / SURVIVE THE VOYAGE” describes the process but not the first screen. Add an example **“2 pieces · 7 days · 1 live link logged”**, clearly labelled illustrative until it is yours. Show title, platform and live link; never invent a creator's result.

4. **Explain what failure preserves.** Current: “Miss the quota. Lose the voyage. Re-board and try again.” Replacement: **“Missed this deadline? Your published work stays in your record. Choose a smaller next voyage.”** Ship this behavior before claiming it. A missed target should not imply the creator failed as a person.

5. **Bring the privacy promise forward.** The auth page says accounts/missions are private and public profiles show selected discipline metrics. Repeat near the CTA: **“Private by default. Share only the progress metrics you choose.”** Verify the default and profile preview; don't infer that private drafts are public proof.

6. **State price and access before registration.** No price appeared in the checked pages. Publish the actual current access policy beside the CTA. If it is a free pilot, say **“Free during this pilot; any future paid terms will be shown before purchase.”** Use only if true, not as an invented offer.

7. **Preserve readability on a phone.** Preview title **“Create or Die — finish one publishing voyage”** and description **“Choose a realistic quota, log published work and build a repeatable creative habit.”** Test the auth form and long link input on mobile data. No visual load score was measured here. Keep the form focus and error visible; the pirate decoration should not obscure them.
