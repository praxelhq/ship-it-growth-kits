# Create or Die — Copy pack

## 1. Seven-day creator invitation

> Eight people. Seven days. Two original posts each.
>
> I'm testing Create or Die, a publishing accountability game. You choose a small quota, publish on your normal platform, and log the live links. A draft doesn't count. You can keep your account and missions private; only share discipline metrics if you want to. If you miss, your published work still matters and you can begin another voyage. Want to try a small commitment rather than another content plan?

Send to the approved group with the actual accepted date; never promise a recovery behavior before it is implemented.

## 2. LinkedIn post

> I don't need another list of post ideas. I need to finish one piece and press publish.
>
> So I'm trying a smaller rule: two original posts in seven days. Live links, not draft screenshots.
>
> That's the loop in Create or Die: choose a voyage, ship the work, log the evidence. The pirate theme is the joke. Actually publishing is the point.
>
> What's the smallest publishing promise you could keep next week?

Attach only a piece you really published; no fabricated streak.

## 3. Peerlist launch

**Tagline:** One publishing commitment, with live links as proof.

> Create or Die is a small accountability game for creators who keep accumulating drafts. You choose one quota and deadline, then log work already published on your normal platform. I'm testing whether a finite voyage helps people finish without turning a missed week into public shame. Please try a small target and tell me which part helps you ship—or gets in the way.

## 4. Permission-first community question

**Title:** Does a small publishing deadline help, or just create another guilt dashboard?

> I'm building a publishing accountability game and testing a two-post week. My concern is that a leaderboard can reward activity while making the actual work worse. If you've kept a writing habit, what counted as useful accountability: a private log, a friend, or a deadline? I build Create or Die, so this is a disclosed product-design question, not an independent recommendation.

Only post if current rules/moderators allow it. No concealed promotional link.

## 5. Creator DM

> I liked the work you've been publishing. Would a small two-post, seven-day challenge be useful, or would it just add pressure? I built Create or Die to log actual live links, and I'm looking for candid feedback. No need to post publicly about the app or join a leaderboard. Can I send it over?

Personalize with the actual piece before sending. One note; no chase after silence.

## 6. Organizer email

**Subject:** An eight-person publishing sprint, with work as the proof

> Could I offer a free, small publishing sprint to your creator group? Participants choose a realistic target, publish wherever they already work, and log live links in Create or Die. There is no requirement to reveal private drafts or make a public profile. I'd bring a clear missed-deadline recovery path and ask for feedback, not testimonials. If this fits, we can agree a date; otherwise I'll leave it here.

## 7. End-of-voyage check-in

> What did you actually publish this week? One link is enough to start the conversation. If the target was too large, let's reduce the next voyage rather than pretend the drafts shipped. Would you start another week without me reminding you?

Send only to consenting testers. Change the example and opening between public posts; don't repeat the same invitation across groups.
