# Create or Die — Distribution map

**Lead with a small creator cohort, not a mass productivity launch.** The challenge needs enough support to get real work published. Start with eight people, then let completed voyages provide evidence.

| Route | Exact action / boundary | Planning result, not benchmark | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Campus creator club, through your existing organizer | Ask permission for one eight-person writing sprint on Day 5 at 18:00 IST. No forced public profiles. | 8 invited × 75% join × 50% publish = 3 first pieces. | #1 |
| Your LinkedIn feed | Four posts, Days 6/13/20/27 at 09:00, showing your own live work or an honest lesson. | 4 × assumed 200 views × 3% visits × 20% signup = about 5 accounts. Zero possible. | #2 |
| [Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) / [Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Present a usable voyage, not a landing-page render. Confirm current eligibility/slots; no vote trades. | One post: 0–15 signups, budget 3 until observed. | #3 |
| [r/GetDisciplined rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/GetDisciplined/about/rules) | Rules endpoint resolved but rule text wasn't extractable. Ask moderators before a tool/development post. No link until permitted. | Zero forecast while permission unknown. | #4 |
| Existing creators you genuinely know | Ten personal invitations across Days 9 and 16. No automation or scraped audience list. | 10 × assumed 40% interest × 50% signup = 2 accounts. | #5 |

Read [Beeminder's public blog](https://blog.beeminder.com/) for thoughtful discussion of commitment design. A useful account/source to engage with is not permission to pitch in its forum. Your club's own opt-in newsletter can carry the invitation if its organizer agrees; no named external newsletter placement is assumed.

[Product Hunt's guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is a verified directory route after the first real cohort. Personal maker account, no upvote requests, inspect an existing listing first. Treat it as optional feedback, not the core month forecast.

Initial expected result is roughly 10–30 signups under uncertain assumptions. It does not support an invented 1,000-user promise. The share loop is a genuine finished piece plus an opt-in invitation to another creator, not tagging reluctant friends into a leaderboard.
