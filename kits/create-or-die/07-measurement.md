# Create or Die — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real accounts, excluding tests and duplicates. Report **first valid published links** beside signups, then completed voyages and a second voyage started without your reminder.

## Day 0

Record existing accounts, active voyages, real logged links and the last seven days of sessions from your own system. These private values were not visible to the researcher; unknown is not zero. Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is receiving a test visit with private mission text masked. Record the current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/create-or-die) reading, period and rank instead of copying a stale leaderboard number.

Instrument `signup_completed`, `voyage_created`, `published_link_logged`, `voyage_completed`, `voyage_missed` and `next_voyage_started`. A logged URL must be a real accessible published piece, not a local draft, repeated URL or your own seeded example. Inspect a small consented sample; do not scrape private drafts or publish creator identities.

## Arithmetic and decisions

The initial calendar has eight cohort invitations, ten personal requests and four owned-feed posts. Its assumptions produce tens of signups, not 1,000. At an assumed 25% visit→signup rate, 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified visits. At 3% impression→visit, that is about 133,000 relevant impressions. These are scenario assumptions; replace them with your own observed conversion before expanding.

| Review | Record | Change if unhealthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Accounts, voyages and first valid links | If fewer than half of new voyages have a first piece, shrink the suggested commitment and improve logging. |
| Day 14 | First-cohort completions and missed targets | If people miss because the quota is unrealistic, revise the default rather than adding punishment. |
| Day 21 | Second voyages started without reminders | Low unprompted return means the theme attracted attention but the habit did not stick. Interview five people. |
| Day 30 | New accounts, first links, completed voyages, later returns, actual reach and hours | Explain the gap to 1,000 and the channel that produced actual work. |

Keep one weekly row: visits, accounts, voyages, first links, completed voyages, missed voyages, unprompted second voyages, best referrer and hours. Report the signup number and activation number on the same line. A leaderboard place is not evidence that the original work was useful; ask participants what they were proud to publish.
