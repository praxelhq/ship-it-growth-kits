# Create or Die — 30-day calendar

Every day: 15 minutes answering participants and 10 minutes logging actual work. Never replace creation time with promotional volume. Times IST.

| Day | One main task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Record baseline; test signup, quota and a valid live-link log. | 2h |
| 2 | Ship clear first-voyage CTA and process explanation (`04` 1–2). | 2h |
| 3 | Verify recovery/privacy behavior and publish accurate wording. | 2h |
| 4 | Ask the campus organizer for an eight-person sprint (#6). | 1h |
| 5 | After permission, invite up to eight creators (#1) at 18:00 and help them choose small targets. | 2h |
| 6 | Publish LinkedIn #2 at 09:00, using your own live work. | 1h |
| 7 | Checkpoint: how many people logged a real link? | 1h |
| 8 | Record demo A and hook B; create your own next piece. | 2h |
| 9 | Send five personalized creator notes #5. | 1h |
| 10 | Repair the biggest quota/logging obstacle reported. | 2h |
| 11 | Prepare Peerlist #3; confirm current slot and eligibility. | 1h |
| 12 | Close the first seven-day sprint; ask #7 privately. | 1h |
| 13 | LinkedIn 09:00: publish the actual lesson, not invented cohort results. | 1h |
| 14 | Checkpoint: first-link rate and completed voyages; revise target size. | 1h |
| 15 | Publish the usable project to eligible Launchpad/Scroll. | 2h |
| 16 | Send the remaining five personal creator notes #5. | 1h |
| 17 | Help willing returners start their own second voyage. | 1h |
| 18 | Record hook C after testing recovery, or describe the unfinished change honestly. | 1h |
| 19 | Read current community rules; request permission for #4 if uncertain. | 1h |
| 20 | LinkedIn 09:00: show the missed-week recovery lesson with your own example. | 1h |
| 21 | Checkpoint: count second voyages begun without prompting. | 1h |
| 22 | Fix the one behavior that made returners leave. | 2h |
| 23 | Publish hook D; no engagement pod or mass tags. | 1h |
| 24 | Ask consenting completers whether they'd invite one creator. | 1h |
| 25 | Optional Product Hunt prep only if the loop genuinely works. | 1h |
| 26 | Work on your own promised publication; log the real link. | 2h |
| 27 | LinkedIn 09:00: share the piece and what the small quota changed. | 1h |
| 28 | Interview three people who missed or left; do not shame them. | 1h |
| 29 | Choose the best source of actual publishing creators; drop empty traffic. | 1h |
| 30 | Report signups, first links, completed voyages and unprompted returns. | 2h |

If participants sign up but publish nothing, halve the suggested quota and help them finish one genuine piece. More invitations won't fix an unusable commitment loop.
