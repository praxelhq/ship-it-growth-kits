# Create or Die — Video scripts

Film your own screen and voice; no rendered video or paid editing. Use work you actually published and obscure personal drafts.

## A. From draft to evidence — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “A draft screenshot isn't a published piece.”

| Time | Shot / text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Three unfinished draft titles, no private text | “These are intentions. None is a shipped piece.” |
| 8–20s | Create a small voyage on your test account | “I choose two posts in seven days, not a heroic daily promise.” |
| 20–36s | Open your genuinely published post | “The work lives on my normal platform.” |
| 36–48s | Log its title, platform and live link | “The voyage records evidence, not a box I ticked.” |
| 48–60s | Show progress and plain URL | “Try one small commitment at createordie.in. Tell me if it helps you finish.” |

Post on Peerlist and your own LinkedIn feed. Do not demonstrate a fictional completed voyage as customer proof.

## B. Smaller promise — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Thirty posts is how I avoid publishing one.” Show a huge calendar, cross it out, write “two this week,” then open the quota screen. Say: “Make the commitment small enough to test. Publish it. Then choose the next one.” Use as an original Short with large captions.

## C. Missed week — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “You missed a deadline. Your work still exists.” Show your actual missed test voyage, preserved published links and the next smaller target. Say: “This should be a restart, not a public punishment. A missed quota doesn't delete a real piece.” Record after the recovery behavior is confirmed; otherwise explain it as a planned change. Post as a candid design update.

## D. One link — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “Show the link, not the streak.” Show your post opening, copy its URL, log it. Say: “Pick one piece you can finish. Publish it somewhere real. Keep the evidence.” End with the URL. Use for the approved club invitation; no manufactured leaderboard or borrowed creator work.
