## Channels gated on the recovered product

[Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) is a real maker-project surface; check current slots and show usable work, not an unidentified placeholder. [Product Hunt's guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) permits personal maker submissions but prohibits direct upvote requests. Neither is a substitute for identifying the audience. [itch.io's creator guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started) is relevant **only if** the recovered project is actually a game or other supported creative work. Do not list there merely because the roster category says Games.

Reddit communities, specialist newsletters, particular creator accounts and offline clubs cannot be selected responsibly without the use case. They are intentionally not fabricated here. After recovery, verify two places the actual user already visits, read the current rules and request organizer permission before scheduling outreach.

## How to build the real map

Interview five actual prospective users after establishing the job. Ask where they last found the current alternative, not where they generally spend time. Verify the resulting channel links, cost and promotion rules. Then run one small test with a stated assumption: invited people, visits, first action and repeat use. Replace guesses with actual results. Do not fill a four-figure target gap with another community's total membership.
