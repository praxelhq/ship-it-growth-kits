# Anonymous project — Video scripts, provisional recovery kit

No product identity, live page, price or CTA is verified. **Do not record a fictional gameplay trailer.** These scripts document recovery and testing. A product demo becomes publishable only after the real first action is known and works.

## A. Evidence walkthrough — 60 seconds, private review first

**Hook:** “Before I promote it, a stranger needs to know what this project is.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|The anonymous public entry, no personal details|“This record doesn't identify the usable product.”|
|10–25s|Your owner-verified production page, only after recovery|“This is the actual public deployment I verified.”|
|25–45s|One uncut attempt at the real primary action|“I'm checking what a fresh visitor can complete, not showing a mock animation.”|
|45–55s|Actual success or honest blocker|“This is the result; anything still blocked stays visible.”|
|55–60s|Factual status, no fabricated feature slogan|“The next step is to test this with three people before promoting it.”|

Keep private until the identity and any sensitive content are cleared. If no deployment is found, stop at the record and explain the unresolved state; don't fake the remaining shots.

## B. Can you explain it? — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “What do you think this page lets you do?” Show a consenting tester opening the recovered page, then their actual explanation. Say: “I won't explain first. The page has to do that work.” Use the real answer with permission; no invented user quote. Publish only after a usable page exists.

## C. Follow the button — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A button isn't the same as a working first action.” Show the actual CTA, an uncut click and the resulting state. Say: “This route needs to end in what the page promised. A signup screen or a loading state isn't proof of success.” Keep the actual outcome, even if it fails.

## D. Count the right thing — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A page visit isn't an activated user.” Show a private aggregate measurement sheet with invented example numbers clearly labelled illustrative, then the real event definition once established. Say: “I'll report the first completed action beside visits or signups. Until the action is known, I won't make up the activation number.” Do not present sample metrics as traction.

Film with your own phone/screen recorder, readable captions and no paid effects. These are useful verification artifacts; they are not a replacement for a real product-specific demo after recovery.
