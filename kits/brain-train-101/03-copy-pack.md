A preview hostname or screenshot alone won't establish that a stranger can use it. Let's test the actual public route before sharing it.

Keep personal contact details private and don't infer features from the old slug. The growth-kit target should stay explicitly provisional until the actual buyer is confirmed.

Would you open the page I send, tell me what you think it does, and try the first action without my instructions? Please don't pay, upload anything sensitive or connect an external account just for this test. A point of confusion is more useful than a compliment.

Send a verified public URL privately after consent. This is a real usability request, not a substitute for a feature pitch.
