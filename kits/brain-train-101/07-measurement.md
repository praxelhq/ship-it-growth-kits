## Recovery baseline

|Field|Evidence required before acquisition|
|---|---|
|Product identity and current URL|Owner's source/deployment record plus publicly accessible page|
|Actual buyer and use case|Observed first action and interviews with relevant people|
|Real signup definition|The implemented account/install/session event appropriate to that product|
|Activation definition|The demonstrated first useful action, not a decorative CTA click|
|Current sessions/referrers|Connected analytics with a dated reporting window|
|Existing users and payments|Private authoritative records, excluding tests/demo data|
|Course reading/rank|Current profile/board with date and eligibility noted|

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) is receiving a test visit only after the real domain is identified, with sensitive inputs masked. Never attach another project's analytics merely to fill a blank panel. Account, install and no-account session definitions are alternatives, not numbers to add together.

## Weekly gates

|Review|Evidence to record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Correct identity, public URL, actual headline/price/CTA and fresh-phone first action|If absent, acquisition remains blocked; continue recovery.|
|Day 14|Three actual user attempts and an explicit activation definition|If users cannot explain/complete the first action, fix it before distribution.|
|Day 21|Confirmed audience, verified alternatives and two permitted channels|Only now can a product-specific reach forecast be written honestly.|
|Day 30|Real invitations, visits, signups, activations, returners and remaining gaps|Report recovery separately from growth. Do not declare 1,000 reached through profile views.|

The first acquisition experiment should show its own arithmetic: actual invitations, visits, first completions and later use. No conversion rate is asserted here because no product or audience was observed. A dated, unresolved source gap is a valid report; a fabricated game funnel is not.
