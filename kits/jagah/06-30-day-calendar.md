# Jagah — 30-day execution calendar

**This month is 60 Reels and one unblocking fix.**

Day 1 has exactly one job: make the buttons on jagah.me do something (`04` Fix 1). Until that ships, every other activity in this calendar converts to zero, so **do not post a single Reel before it's done.**

**The daily rhythm, every single day, no exceptions:**
- **Morning (1h):** shoot and edit 2 Reels.
- **Post at ~1pm and ~8pm.** Same files to YouTube Shorts.
- **First hour after each post (30 min):** reply to every comment. Comment velocity is the one algorithmic lever you control.
- **Evening (15 min):** log the numbers in `07`.

Everything else slots around that.

## The four things that decide this month

1. **Did Fix 1 ship on day 1?** Everything else is multiplied by this. A perfect month of content against dead buttons produces zero users and no learning.
2. **Did you post 60 Reels?** Not 20 good ones — 60. Reach on short-form is a lottery and you need tickets. Two a day is the floor, and the two-a-day habit matters more than any individual video.
3. **Did you reply in the first hour, every time?** It is the only algorithmic input you control directly, and it costs 30 minutes.
4. **What's your install → completed-clean rate?** This is the number that separates a real product from a viral page. An install that never completes a clean is a download, not a user.

---

## The thing most likely to go wrong

**You will get installs and they will not come back.**

A storage cleaner is used once and forgotten — that's inherent to the category, not a failure of your execution. The fix isn't more Reels; it's the weekly auto-clean already described on your site, and a notification that earns its place:

> *"1.4 GB cleaned while your phone charged ☕ — 38 duplicate selfies and 16 stale forwards. Kept your sharpest 4 shots."*

**Ship that notification in week 2, not month 3.** It is the only thing standing between "1,000 installs" and "1,000 users", and it's the difference the board will ask about.

---

## What to do if a Reel breaks out

It will happen, probably in week 2 or 3. When one goes past 100k:

1. **Make three more of that exact format within 24 hours**, while the algorithm is still favouring you.
2. **Pin the airplane-mode Reel to your profile** — a breakout brings sceptics, and that video answers them without you typing.
3. **Be in the comments continuously.** A breakout Reel's comments are the best free user research you will ever get, and the most common comment usually tells you your next landing-page fix.
4. **Check the site can take it.** A breakout is the only day the ₹0 infrastructure question becomes real.
