# Jagah — Video scripts + shot lists

**For Jagah, video is not a supporting asset. It is the entire distribution plan.** `02` puts ~70% of the month into Reels and Shorts, which means roughly 60 videos in 30 days.

You cannot script 60 videos in advance and you shouldn't try. What follows is **six repeatable formats** plus the clips to bank once. Each format carries 8–12 videos by swapping the phone, the person, the junk and the number.

Scripts and shot lists only — recording is yours.

---

## The rules that apply to all 60

1. **First 1.5 seconds: a full storage bar or a "Storage Full" system alert.** Never your logo, never your face, never a title card. This one rule matters more than everything else here.
2. **Vertical 9:16, burned-in captions, 15–25 seconds.** Longer only when the content genuinely earns it.
3. **Hinglish voice, Hindi captions.** English-only halves your reach.
4. **End on the GB number**, held a full second, `jagah.me` on screen.
5. **Real galleries only.** Your own phone, your family's, your friends' — with permission. A mocked-up gallery is spotted instantly by people who look at their own gallery forty times a day.
6. **Same file to YouTube Shorts, same day.** Free second distribution, different algorithm, older and more Hindi-speaking audience.
7. **Under 10MB** where you want it forwarded on WhatsApp.

## Format A — "Mummy/Papa ke phone" 🔴 *(highest breakout potential — make 10 of these)*

Warm, not mocking. The humour has to be affectionate or it curdles.

**Full script, version 1:**

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:03 | Parent's phone, storage alert, camera refusing to open | **"Mummy ka phone. Storage full. Camera khulta hi nahi."** |
| 0:03–0:09 | Fast gallery scroll: flowers, GOOD MORNING, temple images, birthday graphics, cake GIFs | **"Dekho isme kya hai."** |
| 0:09–0:13 | Still scrolling. Still forwards. Deadpan. | **"Abhi tak koi asli photo nahi aayi."** |
| 0:13–0:16 | One real family photo, buried | **"Yahan ek hai. Baaki sab forward."** |
| 0:16–0:21 | Scan running. Counter: WhatsApp forwards 3,841 | **"Scan chalaya. Teen hazaar aath sau ikyalis forwards."** |
| 0:21–0:25 | Review screen — **visibly show real family photos NOT selected** | **"Aur dekho — asli photos select nahi hui. Sirf forwards."** |
| 0:25–0:30 | Delete. **11.4 GB.** Camera opens. | **"Gyarah point chaar GB. Camera chal gaya. Mummy khush."** |

**The 0:21–0:25 beat is doing the real work.** It proves the hardest-to-believe claim — that it can tell a forward from a photo of your family — and it's the shot every sceptical commenter is waiting for.

**Variations:** papa's phone · nani's phone (devotional forwards) · *"meri behen ka phone"* (12 selfies) · a friend's phone, live, unrehearsed · *"maine 5 logon ka phone saaf kiya"* compilation.

## Format B — The airplane-mode proof 🔴 *(pin this one; make 4)*

No jokes. This is the video you drop into any comment thread that turns suspicious.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Settings. Airplane mode ON. Wi-Fi toggled off separately, visibly. Both shown. | **"Wi-Fi band. Data band. Airplane mode on."** |
| 0:05–0:10 | Open Jagah. Start scan. It runs normally. | **"Ab scan chalao. Chal raha hai."** |
| 0:10–0:15 | Results: 8,412 photos analysed, 6.2 GB found | **"Aath hazaar photos analyse ho gayi. Internet ke bina."** |
| 0:15–0:20 | Hold on the result | **"Matlab photos kahin upload nahi ho rahi. Ho hi nahi sakti — internet hai hi nahi."** |
| 0:20–0:25 | Text card: **"Kisi bhi app ko aise test karo"** | **"Koi bhi app kahe ki 'private hai' — usko aise test karo. Das second lagta hai."** |

**The last line is the most valuable second in your whole content plan.** It teaches a test worth knowing even to someone who never installs Jagah — which is exactly why it gets saved and shared — and it happens to be a test you pass and most of your competitors fail.

### Shot list
- **Show both toggles.** A sceptic will claim you only turned off Wi-Fi.
- One continuous take, no cuts. A cut destroys the proof.
- Do a version on an iPhone too; the control centre is recognisable and the audience is more privacy-anxious.

## Format C — The scroll of shame *(make 12 — cheapest to produce)*

Thirty seconds of work each. Pure recognition. Post these on days when you have nothing else.

**The pattern:** one hook line → 10 seconds of scrolling a real, specific category of junk → the count → the GB.

| Variant | The scroll | The payoff line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good Morning forwards | 200 flower images | "Teen sau chhalis. Ek bhi kabhi khola nahi." |
| The same selfie, 12 times | burst grid | "Baarah. Ek chahiye tha." |
| Dead screenshots | payment confirmations, OTPs, boarding passes | "Ye ticket March ka hai. Aaj October hai." |
| Festival graphics | Diwali, Holi, New Year, all years | "2022 ki Diwali ki wishes abhi tak phone mein hain." |
| Blurry night photos | 40 failed concert photos | "Chalis photos. Ek bhi clear nahi." |
| Wedding invite JPEGs | 15 invitation cards | "Shaadi ho chuki. Card abhi bhi hai." |

**Why these work:** no production, no script, and the viewer's reaction is *"this is literally my phone"* — which is the only reaction that matters.

## Format D — The money angle *(make 8)*

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Google One billing screen, ₹130/month, 24 months of history | **"₹130 har mahine. Do saal se. Teen hazaar rupaye."** |
| 0:04–0:12 | Scroll the gallery: forwards, duplicates, dead screenshots | **"Aur ye hai wo cheez jiske liye main pay kar rahi thi."** |
| 0:12–0:20 | Run the scan. 16.8 GB freed. Storage drops below the free tier. | **"Saaf kiya. Solah point aath GB. Ab free tier mein aa gayi."** |
| 0:20–0:26 | Cancel the subscription on screen | **"Subscription cancel. Saal ke pandrah sau bach gaye. Flowers store karne ke liye de rahi thi."** |

**Cancelling the subscription on camera is the strongest ending you can film.** It's a real, irreversible-looking action, and it's the proof that the product did something economically meaningful.

**Be careful here:** only say "cancel your cloud storage" if the cleanup genuinely gets a typical user under the free tier. Overpromising on this one gets you angry comments and app store reviews, which cost more than the Reel earns.

## Format E — The satisfying clean *(make 10 — no voice needed)*

No narration. Music, captions, and a number going up. These travel furthest internationally and are the easiest to batch.

**0:00–0:02** — storage bar at 127.3 / 128 GB, red
**0:02–0:06** — scan running, photo thumbnails flying past, counter climbing
**0:06–0:14** — duplicate groups appearing in grids, the sharpest one highlighted in each
**0:14–0:18** — one tap, the grid clearing
**0:18–0:22** — storage bar animating down to 110.5 / 128 GB
**0:22–0:25** — big text: **16.8 GB** · `jagah.me`

**The bar moving is the payoff.** Make it a real recording, not an animation; the system storage screen is instantly recognisable and a fake one isn't.

## Format F — "Do it manually" *(make 6 — these build trust and rank in search)*

Teach the viewer to solve the problem **without your app.** Counter-intuitive, and the highest-trust content you can make.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and Data → Manage Storage | **"App ki zaroorat nahi. Ye khud kar sakte ho."** |
| 0:05–0:14 | Tap "Larger than 5MB", then "Forwarded many times". Select. Delete. | **"Ye do option. 5MB se bade, aur jo bahut baar forward hue. Select karo, delete."** |
| 0:14–0:20 | Storage freed: 4.2 GB | **"Chaar point do GB. Das minute mein. Bilkul free."** |
| 0:20–0:26 | Turn off media auto-download for groups | **"Aur ye band kar do — group ka auto-download. Problem dobara nahi hogi."** |
| 0:26–0:30 | *(quiet, no hard sell)* | **"Agar ye roz nahi karna chahte, toh Jagah hai. Lekin ye tareeka free hai aur kaam karta hai."** |

**This will get saved more than anything else you post**, and saves are a strong ranking signal on both platforms. It also makes you the person who told the truth, which is worth more than one install.

## The one WhatsApp-forward video 🔴

Separate from the Reels. **Designed to be forwarded**, which has different constraints: no link in the video, nothing that looks like an ad, under 10MB, and it must make sense with no caption.

**30 seconds:** parent's full phone (0:00–0:06) → scroll of forwards (0:06–0:14) → scan and the forwards count (0:14–0:20) → deletion, real photos untouched (0:20–0:26) → GB number and `jagah.me` as plain text (0:26–0:30).

Seed it into family groups, college groups, society groups. Ask 20 friends to forward it to three groups each. A product about WhatsApp junk, spread by WhatsApp forward, is a joke the audience will get and share for you.

## Three long-form videos for YouTube search *(one afternoon each, they rank for years)*

1. **"Phone storage full — 7 tareeke, bina photos delete kiye"** (8 min). Six genuinely free methods, Jagah seventh and briefly.
2. **"Google One ka paisa bachao — kya aapko really chahiye?"** (6 min). High commercial intent, very little good Hindi content exists.
3. **"Maine 30 phones scan kiye — Indian phones mein kya bhara hai"** (10 min). The `03` #4 study as video. Your most linkable asset, and it feeds the Reddit post, the LinkedIn post and the landing-page sourcing fix in one piece of work.
