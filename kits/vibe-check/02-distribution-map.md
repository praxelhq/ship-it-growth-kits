[JustWatch's India guide](https://www.justwatch.com/in) is an availability/comparison source, not an advertising forum. Don't pretend its catalogue is your licensed integration or copy another service's editorial recommendations wholesale. No film subreddit promotion rule, newsletter slot or streaming-provider affiliation was verified. A real organizer can approve a small experiment; group existence alone isn't permission.

Base **46–191 first usable sessions before overlap**, from forty invitations, six posts and optional group/launch. Below 1,000. At day 14 check whether the suggestion is relevant enough that someone stops browsing. More re-spins may mean indecision continues, not higher value. Don't compensate with fabricated restaurant bookings, copied community activity or a promise to improve mood.
