# Vibe Check — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups**, using the no-account equivalent **distinct first usable decision sessions**. A mood tap without a working result is a funnel start, not completed adoption. Report **one relevant suggestion reviewed/chosen** beside sessions, then later independent return. Choice can be a useful informed rejection; track that separately from a selection. Re-spins, recommendation cards and community entries aren't distinct users.

## Forecast matching `06`

Assumptions, not lifestyle-app benchmarks:

- 40 invitations × 60% usable start = 24 sessions.
- Six posts × 300–1,200 impressions × 2% visit × 60% start ≈ 22–86 sessions.
- One accepted group: 20 invited × 50% attend × 60% start = six, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–150 visits × 50% start = 0–75 sessions.

Rounded base **46–191 before overlap**, below 1,000. If half reach a useful reviewed choice, roughly 23–96 activate. At an assumed 60% qualified-visitor-to-usable-session rate, 1,000 needs about 1,667 qualified visits. That rate is optimistic until proven; failed generation, permission friction and repeat people lower the total. Recompute after 20 invitations, and keep impressions separate from visitors.
