# Vibe Check — Video scripts

Use a neutral fictional preference and only records you are authorized to use. Hide city/location, private inventory/history and payment/account details. Record actual output and label demo/pre-filled sources. No serotonin, mental-health outcome or fabricated provider booking.

## A · One suggestion, checked ·60 seconds · own feed/listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
|0–2 s|Your own watchlist, no private account. **“One choice, not everything.”**|“Tonight I only want something to watch.”|
|2–14 s|Actual supported check-in with neutral fictional preference.|“This is an ordinary demonstration choice, not a mental-health assessment.”|
|14–29 s|Actual generation/result, waits labelled.|“Here is the real suggestion. I can reject it.”|
|29–43 s|Inspect actual reason and current service source.|“I check whether it fits the time and service I already have.”|
|43–53 s|Choose/skip using actual supported control.|“The point is a useful decision, not endless re-spins.”|
|53–60 s|Free limits/URL.|“One daily ritual free. Vibecheckmood dot me. Your choice stays yours.”|

If generation fails or the reason/provider isn't available, show that limit and repair it. Don't stitch a result from another service into an apparently successful Vibe Check flow.

## B · Another re-spin? ·20 seconds · short clip

Hook: **“Am I choosing or just scrolling differently?”** 0–8 s, show your controlled demo result.8–15 s: “A useful suggestion earns a yes or a clear no. More spins don't automatically help.”15–20 s: “Measure the decision, not button taps.” No claim the person is cured of fatigue.

## C · A label isn't an integration ·25 seconds · expectation help

Hook: **“Where does this button actually take me?”** 0–8 s, show an actual provider link/route with permission.8–18 s: “A curated card, external link and confirmed in-app booking are different things.”18–25 s: “Check the real destination and receipt before promising a transaction.” Don't click a real booking/payment action for footage or imply a partner relationship from a logo.

## D · Demo inventory isn't mine ·20 seconds · trust update

Hook: **“Who entered these twelve items?”** 0–8 s, use a controlled sample inventory and clearly label it Demo.8–15 s: “If the app shows a prefill, its source needs to be obvious. It isn't proof of synced personal records.”15–20 s: “Explain before generating.” If the actual count's provenance is unknown, say it is under investigation rather than asserting it is demo data.
