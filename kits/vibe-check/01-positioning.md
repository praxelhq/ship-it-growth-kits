## Your first person and sentence

An adult who already has streaming access and wants one plausible thing to watch tonight instead of another scroll. They can reject the suggestion. Start with available service, time and ordinary preference, without claiming to measure mental state or treat fatigue.

> Get one watch suggestion to inspect, check where it is actually available, and choose whether it fits tonight.

Bio: **One suggestion. Your choice. Less browsing.**

The first wedge doesn't need shopping, dinner reservations, precise location or a fridge upload. If the existing flow outputs all three categories, test the watch card first and keep the other offers clearly bounded. Don't invent a watch-only interface before it exists; propose it as a simpler next improvement.

Subscription/rental charges for a title are separate; no current streaming-plan amount is invented here. Vibe Check must earn its place by narrowing one choice with a reason, not claiming nobody can find where a film streams.
