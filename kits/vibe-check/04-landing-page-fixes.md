# Vibe Check — Landing-page fixes

Home/pricing/check-in read 29 October 2026; browser verified monthly/lifetime amounts and a neutral demonstration choice opening the generation stage. No final generation, photo/inventory upload, location permission, booking, purchase or payment was completed. A city/weather label and prefilled item count don't independently prove data provenance or sensor sync.

## 1. Remove or substantiate provider, activity and booking claims

**Current:** “Connected with Zara, H&M, Myntra, Zomato & Netflix,” “380+ live” community, instant in-app restaurant reservations and retail checkouts.

**Write for unverified curated/link-only routes:** **“Suggestions and links to check with the provider. Availability and transactions are confirmed by the actual service.”** If a real approved integration exists, show its exact tested scope rather than borrowing a logo as endorsement. Label sample/community entries and count periods honestly. Don't invite paid bookings or shopping until the end-to-end operator/receipt/refund path is tested.

## 2. Replace clinical-sounding and absolute promises

**Current headline:** “KILLS DECISION FATIGUE.” Check-in says “We'll decide everything else”; paid plan lists “Personalized Serotonin Tracker.”

**Write headline:** **“One suggestion for a smaller everyday decision.”** Helper: **“Tell us an ordinary preference. Inspect the result and choose what fits.”** Replace serotonin language with **“Your self-reported check-in history”** only if that real history exists. A tap doesn't measure a neurotransmitter or establish treatment benefit. Keep rejection/skip available and don't imply the app controls the person's choices.

## 3. Explain location and inventory before generation

**Current:** GPS radar/device sensors/location-synced labels. After a neutral choice, this research saw city/weather and 12 closet/8 fridge items without entering them.

**Write on each field:** **“Source: your entry / demo / permitted device lookup”**, using the actual one. Label sample records **“Demo inventory, not yours.”** Offer a manual city or location-free path for a watch decision, and request only needed permissions after explanation. Publish actual storage, provider, history/deletion and support terms before private data intake. Don't claim the observed counts are seeded if you haven't established their source; investigate first.

## 4. Prove the ten-second free result

**Current CTA:** “START 10-SEC VIBE CHECK” → mood choices → “VIBE ME NOW.” Final generation wasn't executed here.

Test a fresh free session from first tap to a readable usable recommendation, including slow/failure states and declined location. **Write while timing unproven:** **“Start a quick choice check.”** Failure helper: **“No suggestion is ready yet. Try again or use your own list.”** Match actual state/quota behaviour; don't let a failed request silently consume the only daily attempt. No edited speed video as proof.

## 5. Make one narrow decision inspectable

**Current:** wear/eat/watch and stores/community/wardrobe/history all compete with the first action.

**Propose a watch-first path:** **“Tonight: one watch suggestion to inspect.”** Show a real reason, runtime/service context if supported and a source link to check availability. Implement before advertising it as a separate mode. Keep wardrobe, restaurant and social branches secondary until their actual data/transaction scope is verified. No dietary suitability, allergy or fit guarantee from an ordinary mood choice.

## 6. Clarify paid period, entitlement and provider choices

**Current pricing:**₹199/month, lifetime tab₹999 one-time; Dodo/Cashfree/Razorpay buttons, instant activation and broad unlimited features.

**Write before purchase:** **“Choose the exact billing period and review renewal, supported features, activation and refund terms before paying.”** Keep monthly recurring versus lifetime distinct. Verify each offered checkout's amount/currency/entitlement handling rather than assume three buttons work. Don't sell exclusive checkout access, “instant GPT-4o” or no-tracking guarantees without the implemented proof. First experiment stays Free.

## 7. Phone and previews

Preview: **“Vibe Check — one suggestion, your choice.”** Description: **“A small everyday watch-choice experiment. Check actual availability, free limits and data terms.”** Test 360 px, readable check-in choices, keyboard, location decline, loading/quota errors and history privacy. No measured mobile defect is alleged. Keep private location/history out of shared screenshots, and don't use an unverified testimonial as proof the product removes fatigue.
