# Chaturanga — Video scripts

Record these yourself with a phone microphone and your device's screen recorder. No finished video, paid editing tool or generated testimonial is required. Use invented sample material labelled **sample**, not another person's private information. Keep captions large enough to read on a phone; hard cuts are enough.

## A — the continuous demo · 50seconds · club screen and homepage

**First two seconds:** “One capture changes this whole corner.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–10s: actual Atomic position labelled sample. | Here is the move in ordinary chess. |
| Middle | 10–30s: make the Atomic capture; hold board. | In this mode the capture changes the surrounding squares too. Watch which pieces disappear. |
| Payoff | 30–45s: reset and invite a move, then mode entry. | Try the example, then choose a game. No login is required. |
| Close | Hold `https://chaturangachess.games/` legibly. | Start with one mode at chaturangachess.games. |

Verify the demonstration against the current rules; never edit the board to manufacture a legal move.

## B — the problem hook · 20seconds · Shorts/Reels

**First two seconds:** “You can’t see the whole board.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–5s: Fog of War board. | Only some squares are visible. |
| Middle | 5–14s: make one actual move and reveal squares. | Your next move changes what you can see. |
| Payoff | 14–18s: mode label and URL. | Fog of War is one of the modes in Chaturanga. |
| Close | Hold `https://chaturangachess.games/` legibly. | Try a move, not a new account. |

Keep the board readable and do not imply this matches another platform’s exact rules.

## C — the honest comparison · 25seconds · Peerlist and club follow-up

**First two seconds:** “Free chess already exists. Here’s why I made this.”

| Cut | Shot / on-screen text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–7s: regular board, then mode picker. | I’m not replacing serious chess study. |
| Middle | 7–18s: one variant and private-room entry. | I wanted an easy way to try a different rule with friends. |
| Payoff | 18–22s: device-local stamp note. | The stamp collection stays on this device. |
| Close | Hold `https://chaturangachess.games/` legibly. | Tell me which first rule is unclear. |

A private-room join should be recorded on two real devices before claiming it works end to end.

## Record once, reuse carefully

Capture mode choice, one legal Atomic move, a Fog reveal, the private-room entry screen and the local-stamp disclosure. Do not show private room codes from a real group.

Leave the product interaction at normal speed for at least one continuous section. If a generation step is shortened, label the cut; do not imply instant output. Record voice in a quiet room, close unused tabs and notifications, and listen once before posting. The clip succeeds when someone understands the next action, not when it looks like an advertisement.

Post one hook first. If it gets views but no qualified starts, check whether your caption and destination promise the same activity. If people start but abandon, stop adding clips and fix the point of abandonment.
