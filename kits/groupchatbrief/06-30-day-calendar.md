## The three things that decide this month

1. **Do you know your install funnel numbers?** Click → download → connect. Without them you cannot tell whether a weak month was the marketing or the installation, and they are different problems with different fixes.
2. **Did the Web Store listing move?** It is worth more than every post in this calendar combined.
3. **How big is the "email me when it's one click" list?** That list is your relaunch, and it's the most valuable output of a constrained month.

## The day the Chrome Web Store approves

Have this ready in advance:

1. Email the entire waitlist within the hour.
2. Change every CTA on the site to a real `Add to Chrome`.
3. Shoot and post the Hinglish Reels you already wrote.
4. Re-post to every community you posted to this month, with the honest update: *"It's one click now."* **A re-post with a genuine change is welcome in almost every community** — that's a free second launch.
5. Switch to the Tier 5 mass-consumer plan in `02`.
