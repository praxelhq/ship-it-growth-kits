No newsletter pickup, Discord access or subreddit permission was verified; don't invent those channels. A free class newsletter mention can be requested from a real organizer, zero forecast until accepted. Never post Pro license keys or developer contact data in a public kit.

At 20% qualified visit-to-install, 1,000 needs 5,000 relevant unique visits. The base is likely dozens, not thousands. Replace assumptions after three posts and compare store installs with useful voluntary retrieval tests. Do not inflate with update checks or uninstall/reinstall tests.
