# neverloseawebsite — Video scripts

Use public sample pages and fictional notes. No private browsing tabs, URLs, API keys or Pro licenses in the recording.

## A. Retrieval demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“You remember why you liked it, not what it was called.”**

2–15s, save a public page and write a useful note. “Keep the reason with the address.”

15–28s, save two more references, close the original tab. “Three saves fit inside the free limit.”

28–40s, retrieve by the note/tag. “This is the actual search, not an invented accuracy result.”

40–50s: store link/limit/storage note. “Ten saves free, local to this Chrome device. neverloseawebsite.me.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “'Useful website' tells future you nothing.”

2–10s: write a specific reason, then find by that phrase. “Save why.”

10–15s: URL and **10 saves free · local storage**.

## C. Permissions clip — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Why does this extension ask for that?”

2–15s: show actual permission explanations. “Metadata is read on click for local saving/tag rules. Pro key checks are separate.”

15–25s: privacy link and official store. Don't show or activate a real key publicly.

## D. Honest-limit clip — 20 seconds

0–2s: “Local isn't the same as backed up.”

2–12s: show actual policy warning and library. “Know what happens before removing the extension.”

12–20s: free/pro boundary. “Choose based on the actual tradeoff.” No untested export/sync claim.
