# neverloseawebsite — Landing page fixes

Based on homepage, privacy policy and official store listing checked 29 October. Installation/paid activation were not performed.

## 1. Prove save-note-retrieve in a fresh Chrome profile

Current headline: **“never lose a website again.”** CTA: **“add to chrome - it's free.”** Test store install, permissions, three public saves with notes, retrieval, restart and free-limit behaviour. If the core fails, pause acquisition before asking more people to grant permissions.

## 2. Put the ten-save limit beside every free CTA

Suggested button: **“Add to Chrome — 10 saves free.”** Support: **“Save the link and why you cared. Search your note, tag or folder later.”** Don't let the hero imply unlimited free use while pricing says ten.

## 3. Make local storage's tradeoff explicit

The policy says removing the extension deletes data. Add **“Stored on this Chrome device. Not cloud sync or a backup. Removing the extension deletes your saved library.”** If export exists, show it; if not, don't invent it in copy. Users need this before trusting important references.

## 4. Explain permissions and Pro exception plainly

The policy lists storage/activeTab/scripting for on-click metadata and optional Dodo permission for key activation/rechecks. Put a short explanation beside installation/upgrade. Saved websites aren't sent in those license checks. Do not use “never contacts a server” as an absolute claim.

## 5. Preserve the illustrative-match caveat

The homepage says the example's match score isn't overall accuracy. Keep **“Illustrative search example, not an accuracy benchmark”** next to it and in clips. Local keyword rules are not evidence of an AI reading service.

## 6. Show actual plan/renewal terms

Listed Pro options: $5/month, $12/3 months, $25/year. State the exact billing/renewal/cancellation terms enforced at checkout before purchase. Test a valid/invalid key and unavailable check without exposing a real key in screenshots.

## 7. Make phone-to-desktop handoff useful

The extension installs on Chrome desktop; a phone viewer should see **“Open this official store link on your Chrome computer.”** Test popup text at normal laptop scaling and keyboard search. Preview: **“neverloseawebsite — the link plus why you saved it.”** No private URLs, notes or license keys in images.
