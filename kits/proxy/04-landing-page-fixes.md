# Proxy — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from rendered public controls and current public assets/terms. The page is live; an obsolete roster access-key description must not be used as its pitch or copied publicly. No signup, practice generation or payment was submitted.

## 1. Finish the paid-service promises

**Current:** public terms/refund pages call themselves drafts and leave operator decisions incomplete. Pricing describes$9.99 monthly/$39.99 annual Pro, but that is not proof a live checkout is ready.

Finalize and publish the actual service/refund/cancellation commitments and confirm the real billing mode before promoting Pro. Until then use **“Try the free Work practice. Paid access is available only when checkout and final terms are confirmed.”** Don't promise the draft's suggested refund window as an established policy. This is a product-owner decision, not an invitation to invent legal terms.

## 2. Tell visitors what they will do

**Current:** “The conversation can wait. Your courage can't.”

Try:

> **Practise your next work conversation, one sentence at a time.**
>
> Choose a Work scenario, try your opening and respond to pushback. Get practice feedback, then decide what you'd say differently. One text practice a day, free.

Keep the existing emotional line as supporting copy if you want. The first screen should make text rehearsal and the free boundary obvious.

## 3. Make the primary CTA name the first result

**Current:** “Start practicing” opens an auth action; scenario cards have “Practice” and non-Work cards show Pro.

Use **“Try a free Work rehearsal”** and helper **“Create an account, choose a Work situation, then review the feedback. Text only today.”** Test a fresh account through its first scorecard before posting. A JavaScript auth button isn't automatically broken because it has no link; verify the actual action, back navigation and error state.

## 4. Explain the score before people believe it

**Current:** basic/full scorecards and XP/streaks are part of the offer.

Add **“Practice feedback, not a measure of your personality or a prediction of a real conversation.”** Show a synthetic example where one specific sentence improves after a retry. Avoid “confidence increased 70%” unless a valid measurement actually supports it. Don't push users to share intimate scorecards publicly.

## 5. Put privacy at the point of entry

**Current:** the app has “Privacy & safety”; source shows consent-based Clarity and strict-masking claims.

Use **“Start with a fictional example. Don't enter names, private messages or sensitive personal details. AI replies can be wrong.”** Explain actual processing/storage in the real policy. Verify masking on a test account and keep conversation content out of event properties. Don't add a second analytics script or bypass the existing consent choice.

## 6. Label free and paid scenarios together

Work cards are free under the daily allowance; Dating/Home/Life cards show Pro. Add **“Free: one Work scenario per day. Pro: additional packs and the limits shown in the plan.”** Recheck actual entitlements before publishing “unlimited,” particularly the 12-turn limit described in the current client. Don't market future voice as a current benefit.

## 7. Preview and mobile checks

Proposed preview title: **“Proxy — rehearse a clearer work conversation.”** Description: **“One free text Work practice per day. Try an opening, respond to pushback and review feedback. Use fictional examples first.”** Test actual previews rather than assert one is broken. On phones, make the scenario/free status, input and send button readable above the keyboard; test slow-network failure preserves the draft. No measured mobile/load defect was established in this audit.
