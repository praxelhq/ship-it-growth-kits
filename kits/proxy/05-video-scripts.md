# Proxy — Video scripts

Use invented adult work situations and your own screen recording. Never show a real manager's messages, relationship transcript or private scorecard. Phone microphone, hard cuts and captions are enough. Current product is text, so don't imply a voice feature.

## A. Practise the next sentence —60 seconds

**Use:** landing page, LinkedIn and approved practice-circle invitation. **Hook:** “What do you say after ‘I can't do both’?”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|Synthetic request with two competing tasks|What do you say after “I can't do both”?|
|2–12 s|Choose actual “Say no to extra work” scenario|I'm rehearsing a fictional ordinary work request, not asking the app to handle a dispute.|
|12–27 s|Type an opening, show actual pushback|I name the constraint and ask which task matters more.|
|27–40 s|Read actual feedback, reject anything that doesn't fit|This is practice feedback, not a prediction of my manager.|
|40–52 s|Retry one sentence with a clearer alternative|The useful part is this change, not a flattering score.|
|52–60 s|Free status and URL|One free text Work practice a day. Start with a made-up example at proxy.velixir.run.|

Don't simulate successful feedback. If the flow fails, fix it and reshoot after it genuinely works.

## B. Request, constraint, alternative —20 seconds

**Where:** LinkedIn native video. **Hook:** “You don't need five apologies.” Show three text lines: “What I'm asking,” “What I can manage,” “One alternative.” Read a synthetic example: “I can finish the deck or the report today. Which is the priority?” End: “Practise it first; the other person's response still isn't yours to control.” Small founder disclosure/URL.

## C. Don't share the real chat —25 seconds

**Where:** first-use/privacy explanation. **Hook:** “Use a made-up version.” Show a fake message with invented names crossed out, then a generic situation. Say, “You can rehearse the shape of the conversation without pasting someone's private words.” Show the actual privacy entry point and text-only status. End on “Practice, not professional advice.” No real transcript even blurred.

## D. Score versus sentence —25 seconds

**Where:** Peerlist gallery and session follow-up. **Hook:** “A higher score isn't the whole point.” Show a clearly labelled test scorecard, then the one wording change you accept and one suggestion you reject. Say, “Did the second attempt sound clearer and still like me? That's the test I care about.” End with the free Work allowance, not a promise of a raise or repaired relationship.

Bank scenario selection, a generic typed opening, pushback, feedback and retry clips. No artificial conversation success notification after the demo.
