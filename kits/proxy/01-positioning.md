## Start with a conversation that happens this week

Your first user is a new employee or intern who wants to decline one extra task without sounding hostile. They can describe a low-stakes situation in generic terms and rehearse two or three sentences. They do not need a therapy app, a persuasion weapon or a score claiming to measure courage.

Start with **“Say no to extra work”**, one of the real free Work scenarios. “Ask for the raise” and “Give difficult feedback” are adjacent, but negotiating a serious employment dispute is a different level of stakes. The product provides practice feedback, not professional HR/legal advice or a prediction of another person's reaction.

## The pitch

> Rehearse a work conversation before you have it. Try your opening, respond to pushback and leave with words you'd actually say.

Short version: **Practice the awkward sentence first.**

The wedge is a calmer first sentence plus one response to a reasonable objection. Show how a vague apology becomes a clear request without pretending the app guarantees the outcome. No one should need to share a private conversation, manager's name or employer's internal details to get value.

## Real alternatives

[ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) is a free general role-play option. A friend or a written rehearsal in a notes app costs nothing. Proxy's advantage must be a short scenario, believable pushback and specific feedback that makes the second attempt better. Don't claim general AI can't role-play; demonstrate why your guided three-minute loop is easier to start.

The actual free Work pack is a better acquisition wedge than immediately selling the Dating/Home/Life packs marked Pro. Keep the price visible and avoid implying all scenario cards are free. Don't market “unlimited voice practice” when the current version is text.

The target remains **1,000 new consumer accounts**, with completed first rehearsals and voluntary repeat practice reported alongside. The first month succeeds as learning only if people can explain what they'd say differently; a high AI score without that is decoration.
