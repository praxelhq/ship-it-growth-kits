# NxtMood — Distribution map

**Lead with a disclosed interactive prototype, not a fake travel launch.** Recruit people who can critique the mood-to-plan idea and builders who notice trust gaps.

| Route | Scheduled action / boundary | Assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing outing-planners | Ask about relevance of sample choices and story; no claim of imported personal saves. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed flows if interaction is clear. | #1 |
| Two existing design/project groups | Admin-approved prototype-feedback invitation. No fake live-event or venue offer. | 2 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 12–24 sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account clips | Mood choices, sample-card label, estimated bill and story rationale on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–80 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Prototype/reality boundary and choice-relevance lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. | 2 × 300–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 3–30 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Disclosed prototype feedback after actual local flow test; check current rules/slot. No votes requested. | 0–30 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No paid city page, travel influencer, newsletter sponsorship, unverified subreddit or booking partner is assumed. A free student-design newsletter mention is optional after acceptance, zero until then. The accounts worth replying to are people discussing prototype choices or saved-place friction; answer their actual question rather than claiming a real integration.

At 50% visitor-to-meaningful-demo, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. Small tests may yield tens or low hundreds. Don't count a fictional budget as sales, root Instagram links as actual saved posts, or screenshots as working trips. Keep contacts separate from Adda's real Bengaluru group-zone tests and Shotgun's existing-crew roles.
