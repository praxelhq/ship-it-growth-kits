# NxtMood — Video scripts

Every shown save/story is a sample unless clearly replaced with owned verified material. No live venue, imported account or booking claim.

## A. Prototype demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“This is a prototype, not your Instagram feed.”**

2–12s, choose category/vibes. “I'm testing whether these inputs help express the kind of experience you want.”

12–25s, adjust dials and select sample cards. “The saves are examples. Nothing has been read from an account.”

25–38s, build actual story and explain changes. “Does the reasoning match the choices?”

38–50s, show sample-estimate receipt label. “Not a booking or quote. Try the concept at nxtmoodd.shop.”

## B. Choice hook — 15 seconds

0–2s: “What changed when I changed the vibe?”

2–10s: two actual tested combinations and the resulting difference. “A choice should have a visible reason.”

10–15s: URL and **Local prototype · sample saves**.

## C. Trust hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “A beautiful bill isn't a verified price.”

2–12s: point to sample estimate labels. “The interface needs to say what it knows and what it is inventing for the concept.”

12–20s: prototype disclosure. “Useful feedback starts with honest scope.”

## D. Saved-idea hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: “You saved it. Would you ever choose it?”

2–12s: sample board to story. “That's the concept test—not an imported history.”

12–20s: ask one real question about relevance. No fake personal account or social proof.
