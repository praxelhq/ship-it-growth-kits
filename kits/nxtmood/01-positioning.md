# NxtMood — Positioning

**Live:** https://nxtmoodd.shop/ · **Category:** Experience-planning prototype · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/nxtmood

**Your site is live, but it is explicitly a prototype.** The browser-visible headline checked 29 October is **“What's the vibe?”** The action button is **“BUILD MY MOOD.”** Visitors can choose a category, vibes, dials, sample saves and a story. The footer says the page runs in the browser, nothing is connected, no Instagram account/feed/Reel was read, and every save is a sample.

Move that truth before the first interaction. Don't market imported saved Reels, real venue availability or an integrated travel service. The displayed mood bill is an estimate/sample, not a payment or verified quote. No live paid offer was visible.

Your first tester is a person who has too many saved outing ideas and wants to see whether choosing a mood helps them narrow a plan. They are evaluating a concept, not booking the example restaurant.

**Pitch:** Try a mood-to-story planning prototype and see whether the choices explain an experience you would want to refine.

**Short version:** A mood board that becomes a sample evening—not a booking.

## The wedge

One category, two vibes, a few sample choices and a generated story whose reasoning is understandable. Ask the person what they would keep, change or verify. The first useful signal is relevance and comprehension, not money “spent” on a fictional receipt.

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/about/) already offers public place discovery, saved lists and group reactions. Consumer browsing is a no-charge alternative. NxtMood must earn attention through the mood/choice explanation; it doesn't replace actual route, hours, booking or price checks.

## The uncomfortable bit

The sample cards link to Instagram's root rather than proof of specific imported Reels. A realistic interface can mislead if the boundary is buried after the story. State samples early and do not ask for Instagram access that isn't implemented.

The public page was inspected in a browser; no personal preference or plan was submitted. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful prototype sessions, with completed choice-to-story flows beside it. Those aren't 1,000 live trips, connected Instagram users or reservations.
