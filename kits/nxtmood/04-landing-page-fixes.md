# NxtMood — Landing page fixes

Based on the actual browser page checked 29 October. No personal choices or booking were submitted.

## 1. Move the prototype disclosure before the first pick

The footer says **“Nothing is connected”** and every save is a sample. Put **“Interactive prototype: sample saves and stories, no Instagram connection or live booking”** beneath the opening text. A disclosure after several immersive screens is too late to shape expectations.

## 2. Connect the headline to the actual prototype job

Current headline: **“What's the vibe?”** CTA: **“BUILD MY MOOD.”**

Suggested support: **“Try a mood-to-experience concept. Choose sample ideas and see how they shape a story.”** Button: **“Build a sample experience.”** Preserve the editorial mood-board direction; clarity doesn't require redesigning the whole page.

## 3. Stop generic Instagram links looking like real evidence

Sample cards say they open the saved Reel, but observed links point to Instagram's root. Either use an owned real demonstration post with permission and clear sample status, or remove/disable that action with **“Sample card—no original Reel connected.”** Don't make a dead-end feel like an integration.

## 4. Label the receipt as a sample estimate everywhere

The receipt already says estimates/sample order. Keep that next to each total and in screenshots: **“Illustrative budget only. Not a venue quote, tax calculation or reservation.”** Decorative ticket amounts are not product prices. Never imply the app can collect money or confirm availability.

## 5. Test choice-to-story coherence

Use several actual combinations, change city, clear picks, change party size and inspect the rationale. Does a calm at-home choice still yield the sample night-out plan? If so, repair the logic or label its fixed limits before claiming personalization.

## 6. Make the sideways story usable on a phone

At 390px, test keyboard/screen-reader choice labels, sliders, long cards, horizontal story navigation and reduced motion. The sample boundary must remain visible after cropping to a share clip. Preview: **“NXT MOOD — a mood-to-experience prototype.”** No user's real saves, account or private location in the image.
