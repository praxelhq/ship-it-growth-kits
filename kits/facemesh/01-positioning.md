# FaceMesh — Positioning

**Live:** https://facemesh.tech/ · **Category:** Photo measurement / presentation feedback · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/facemesh

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“A real read on your face.”** The public buttons include **“Scan your face.”** The free preview shows facial landmarks/canthal tilt; a **one-time $1.99** report adds six trait scores and presentation tips. The site requires **18+ and the user's own photo**. This research did not upload a face or purchase a report.

Your first user is a consenting adult curious about how a photo's geometry is measured. Do not recruit minors, people seeking medical assessment or someone looking for an objective verdict on their worth.

**Pitch:** Inspect a private photo's measured landmarks and understand the limits of a rubric-based report.

**Short version:** Photo measurements with clear limits, not a verdict on you.

## The wedge

Make the free measurement understandable before offering a paid grade. Distinguish measured geometry from an AI-written interpretation and from subjective aesthetic scoring. Camera angle, pose and image conditions can change a photo's reading; a score is not a diagnosis, objective beauty truth or prediction of social success.

[Photofeeler](https://www.photofeeler.com/) is a real alternative for photo feedback from people; its publisher offers free tests in exchange for voting, with paid credits optional. FaceMesh is a different job: automated geometry/rubric feedback rather than human-photo votes. Do not claim one is scientifically superior without evidence.

## The uncomfortable privacy detail

The [privacy page](https://facemesh.tech/privacy) says raw landmarks are computed locally, but high-level ratios are sent to the server. Paid images go to AI providers and may be temporarily retained for fulfilment, with a stated deletion schedule. **Do not advertise “nothing leaves your device.”** The policy claims are not an independent implementation audit.

The page also mixes FaceMesh and Facemetric naming. Keep one public brand. Before growth, verify consent, upload, preview, payment/report recovery and deletion. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful preview sessions, with completed previews and report satisfaction beside it. No public face leaderboard, shaming hook or invented transformation belongs in this plan.
