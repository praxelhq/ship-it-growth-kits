# FaceMesh — Distribution map

**Lead with a privacy-and-method demonstration for consenting adults.** Curiosity is enough; don't manufacture insecurity to get a scan.

| Route | Scheduled action / boundary | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing adult testers | Own photos only, direct informed choice, no files sent to you. Ask about consent/measurement clarity. | 10 trials, 5–10 completed previews if the flow works. | #1 |
| Two existing adult photography/design groups | Admin-approved method demo using your own photo or a consenting adult, no public ranking. | 2 × 30 relevant readers × 10–25% try = 6–15 first sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account short clips | Free mesh, geometry-versus-grade and processing explanation on Days 8, 13, 20, 26. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% start = 2–48 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | Product-method/privacy lesson on Days 11/24 at 12:30 IST. No body-transformation claim. | 2 × 200–800 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% start = 1–14 sessions. | #4 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker feedback after consent and fulfilment tests, current rules checked. Never require a real face for public feedback. | 0–20 sessions, uncertain upside. | #5 |

No appearance-rating subreddit, underage community, paid influencer, newsletter sponsorship or scraped-photo distribution is assumed. A photography-club newsletter can mention a voluntary adult method test only after permission; zero baseline forecast. Don't post other people's faces or use publicly available photos as permission.

At 30% visit-to-preview-start, 1,000 needs roughly 3,334 qualified unique visits. The small scheduled tests may yield only dozens. If willing adults don't understand the processing/score limits, fix the explanation before increasing reach. A high share rate from insulting grades is not a quality signal.
