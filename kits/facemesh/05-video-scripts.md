# FaceMesh — Video scripts

Use your own adult photo or explicit consent. No minors, public-photo scraping, shame, rankings or fabricated before/after transformations.

## A. Method demo — 50 seconds

0–2s: **“What is measured here, and what is interpretation?”**

2–12s, show adult/own-photo consent and processing note. “Understand the stages before choosing a photo.”

12–25s, actual free mesh preview from an owned image. “These are landmarks and calculated ratios.”

25–38s, show labels distinguishing optional report/AI tips. “A rubric score isn't an objective verdict about a person.”

38–50s, price and policy links. “Free preview; $1.99 optional report. Read what is processed. facemesh.tech.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Short

0–2s: “This number isn't your worth.”

2–10s: show the method/limit label on an owned example. “A photo and a rubric produce a reading with limits.”

10–15s: URL, **18+ · own photo only**.

## C. Processing hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “Local landmarks doesn't mean every step is local.”

2–12s: show actual policy stages: raw landmarks, summary ratios, paid report. “Explain each stage before asking for trust.”

12–20s: consent/notice link, no upload required to watch. No compliance certification claim.

## D. Better-demo clip — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “You don't need a stranger's face for a demo.”

2–12s: use your own test image and label it. “Public examples should be deliberate, not scraped.”

12–20s: hide/delete the test data through verified controls. “Keep private results private.” If deletion isn't verified, don't fake that footage.
