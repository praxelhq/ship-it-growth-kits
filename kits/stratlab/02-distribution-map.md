[QuantConnect's official platform material](https://www.quantconnect.com/pricing/) is a comparison source, not a lead list or advertising forum. No current quant subreddit promotional rule, professional newsletter placement or academic endorsement was verified. A known organizer may approve a free worksheet; that permission must exist before any group invitation.

Base **8–39 accounts before overlap**, from twenty invitations, six posts, one optional session and launch. That doesn't reach 1,000. At day 14 inspect whether the first learner understands costs and evidence better and returns without pressure. Do not solve a reach problem by advertising a strategy's highest historical return or giving a bonus for placing real trades.
