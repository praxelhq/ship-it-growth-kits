## Your first learner

An adult learning to inspect a backtest who is willing to stay entirely on paper, with no real portfolio upload or financial action. Start with the public example where costs erase the apparent idea, then test whether the learner can explain assumptions and limits.

> Inspect a trading idea's past-data test after costs, review the uncertainty checks and keep the result separate from a forecast.

Bio: **Past-data evidence, visible costs, paper only.**

Your wedge is the difference between an attractive gross curve and a limited after-cost conclusion. The public [20/50 currency-pair example](https://stratlab.studio/library/seed-ema-20-50-ls-cds) reports a loss after costs and explains that passed checks don't establish an edge when the tested result loses. That is a product example, not independently reproduced performance or a strategy recommendation.

## Real alternatives

[QuantConnect's official pricing](https://www.quantconnect.com/pricing/) includes a Free plan with unlimited backtesting and several asset/data classes; paid tools/compute differ. Don't claim free backtesting doesn't exist or quote an unverified paid node cost. A spreadsheet and an already available dataset can also support a small manual cost exercise without another software subscription, but data quality and assumptions still matter.

Your possible difference is a plain-language rule workflow, visible market costs and the four stated checks: unseen periods, nearby settings, reshuffled drawdowns and trade count. Their thresholds are product heuristics, not universal statistical proof. No passed badge establishes future returns or suitability for a person's money.

## Data and scope limits

The [terms](https://stratlab.studio/terms) say 18+, education/research, delayed/incomplete data possible and no registered investment-adviser/research-analyst claim. Privacy describes AI idea text going to configured providers, including some free-tier terms that may retain/use it for training. Don't type proprietary strategies or sensitive broker records into a growth demonstration. Paper-only does not mean data-free.

Keep **1,000 individual accounts** as the target, with **one backtest/report reviewed for rules, costs and missing coverage** alongside, then independent later use. Company-page views, seeded strategies and hypothetical returns aren't users or earnings. The Money/tax branch is outside this first acquisition test; no need to upload real holdings or claim a filing-ready tax result.
