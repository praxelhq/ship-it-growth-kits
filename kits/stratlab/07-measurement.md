# StratLab — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **one report reviewed for rules, costs and missing coverage**, then independent later use. Record first review and return separately. Public company-page sessions, seeded strategies, paper trade count and hypothetical gains are not unique accounts or earnings.

## Day 0

Record actual accounts, free backtests requested/succeeded/failed, useful report reviews, later independent use and real subscriptions/refunds from owner records. No private baseline was established here. Capture fresh [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/stratlab) rank, reading and timestamp; unknown isn't zero. Rotating samples and the 43 public strategy count observed in rendering aren't active users or independently validated performance.

Follow the actual [privacy](https://stratlab.studio/privacy) boundaries: optional Clarity/text hidden, protected Money/tax/holdings/account/settings/payment exclusions, DNT/GPC handling and limited usage analytics. Don't install another tracker or record private ideas/holdings. Proposed content-free counts only if supported: `account_created`, `public_report_opened`, `backtest_completed`, `costs_reviewed`, `coverage_reviewed`, `independent_return`, `backtest_failed`, `subscription_confirmed`. A source-open event doesn't prove comprehension; use a voluntary small workflow question without financial details.

## Forecast matching `06`

Assumptions, not quant-tool benchmarks:

- 20 invitations × 40% try × 70% account ≈ five to six accounts.
- Six posts × 100–500 impressions × 2% visits × 25% account ≈ 3–15 accounts.
- One accepted session: ten invited × 60% attend × 50% account ≈ three, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–100 visits × 15% account = 0–15 accounts.

Rounded base **8–39 before overlap**, below 1,000. Half activating yields roughly 4–20 meaningful reviewers, not profitable traders. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 needs 4,000 qualified visits; the calendar doesn't provide that reach. Replace guesses after 15 invitations and don't raise the forecast with a cherry-picked historical return.
