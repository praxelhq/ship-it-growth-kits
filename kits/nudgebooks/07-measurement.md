# NudgeBooks — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each CA/bookkeeping practice must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): genuine business; own actual permitted collection data onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known owner name and phone. Keep identifying details outside the public repo. The practice's client businesses do not become NudgeBooks customers merely by sending documents.

Activation: the practice can review one actual authorized recurring cycle and distinguish requested, received and outstanding items through the product's real supported workflow. This definition is conditional on verifying that capability; a synthetic checklist or sign-in alone does not qualify. Report actual receipt/status use and independent returns beside the customer total.

No private workspace was accessed. Product readiness, totals and paid terms remain unknown until recorded by the owner.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real practices / latest independent use | Product records plus private permitted owner register, excluding tests. |
| Verified current feature/offer scope | Actual release demonstration and configured terms; no automation inferred from metadata. |
| Own authorized cycles onboarded | Practice-owned actual data, with safe minimal content chosen by owner. |
| Actual receipt/outstanding reviews | Status metadata, not copied client financial documents. |
| Access/deletion/messaging failures | Synthetic reproductions and private issue categories. |
| Payments/refunds and course reading | Actual billing if present and [profile](https://shipped.money/p/nudgebooks), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding another tag. Exclude client identities, financial files, document contents, account details, inbox/message text and access-bearing links from replay. Use minimal aggregate events only for verified functions, such as owncyclecreated, receiptconfirmed, outstandingreviewed, laterindependentuse. Do not invent automation events or copy documents into a marketing dashboard.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If the authenticated workflow cannot be demonstrated, pause real-data onboarding and repair; count sample feedback separately. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare qualified practices with actual permitted setups and receipt/outstanding clarity. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent recent use, missing-item errors and actual follow-up route. A reminder sent is not a document received. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests, counting practices rather than staff/client firms. |
| Day 30 | Actual qualifying practices against ten, activation, independent returns, current scope/terms, hours and honest gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts and two introduction requests are upside only. Replace rates after eight approaches and again after 20. Product/data gates may reduce actual onboarding to zero; do not report a hypothetical conversion as achieved traction.

An assisted review can be useful while failing independence. A monthly cycle may finish and not require final-week use; report useful one-off cycles separately from the recent-use customer count. Do not ask an owner to log in solely to make the metric active. No claim of fewer filing errors, guaranteed deadline compliance, zero chasing or saved hours follows from the metadata tagline.
