## Your first business

A small CA practice collecting recurring monthly bookkeeping documents from existing business clients. The owner already has a permitted client relationship and a real missing-document list. The useful first job is one recurring client cycle: what was requested, what arrived and what still needs follow-up.

**Pitch: Keep one recurring client's outstanding document list clear before the next follow-up.**

Short version: **Know what is still missing before you nudge.**

This is an intended wedge to verify against the authenticated release, not a claim every request/reminder feature is shipped. If the real product cannot support a reviewed request/received/outstanding state, repair that first. Do not solve a CA's missing bank statement by requesting confidential client files in a product-sales chat.

## What would make it useful

Start with a synthetic client and three clearly fictional document requests, then inspect the actual creation, receipt/status update and next-action workflow. Verify whether the client submits through a link, a message or a manual owner update; market only the route that exists. Check what gets sent, to whom and with whose approval before enabling any follow-up.

After reviewing access/data handling, the practice can choose one authorized real monthly cycle with minimally identifying records. It retains its existing process until receipt and permissions are proven. A repeated status open does not mean documents were actually received or a compliance task completed.

## The alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) checklist with request, due date, received status and next follow-up can organize the practice's existing authorized communication. NudgeBooks must earn adoption by reducing uncertainty about outstanding items, not by claiming all manual follow-up disappears. Its exact storage, automation and commercial terms need verification before real client records.

Separate this recurring chase from PBC Tracker's one audit-engagement request/support matching. CA Deskk owns filing-season WhatsApp-to-folder handling; Audit extractor handles invoice/order extraction. NudgeBooks should not market every adjacent function just because the same practice might buy it.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count CA practices, not their client firms, document requests or staff accounts. Each must onboard its own permitted actual workflow/data, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. A signed-in friend using synthetic data does not qualify.
