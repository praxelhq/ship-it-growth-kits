# NudgeBooks — Distribution map

Lead with recurring monthly collection, after the actual request/receipt workflow is verified. A generic “CA automation” pitch hides the specific missing-document problem and promises more than the public product establishes.

| Route | Execution / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small CA-practice relationships | Qualify a real monthly collection cycle, an owner who controls the process and authority to evaluate client-data handling. Use genuine professional introductions/public business enquiries. | Ten blocks of four practices = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two bookkeeping/CA introductions | Ask a known professional to forward one invitation, not disclose its clients, contacts or account documents. | Days 16 and 19; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Demonstrate a fictional outstanding list and an actual supported status/follow-up step. Do not imply unverified automated messages. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One owner-controlled collection review | Practice reviews one permitted cycle after synthetic access and receipt tests. | Day 20; #5. Existing-trial support, not another business. |

Base planning guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% authorized own-cycle setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 practices. At 25% conversation, 4.9. The ten four-practice blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. These assumptions may fail if the authenticated product or data handling cannot support real onboarding. Posts/introductions add no guaranteed customer conversions.

The shared CA invitation window is **Days 16–19**. See [CA/audit coordination](../../shared/a1-ca-audit-operations.md): CA Deskk Days 4–7, Audit extractor Days 10–13, NudgeBooks Days 16–19, PBC Tracker Days 22–25; Fintellix requires a distinct invited firm. Use different owner/contact lists first and one primary invitation per organizer/week. Do not ask the same CA group to try four document tools in a month without an organizer choosing one clear job.

No professional newsletter, institute endorsement, CA WhatsApp group or association promotion permission is established. Ask the known organizer before any post. No bulk scraped practice numbers, automated DMs, paid directories or pretending to need accounting services just to sell the tool. One relevant follow-up after seven days; a decline ends contact.

Do not upload client documents or send reminders merely to demonstrate a feature. A synthetic account proves a workflow at most. Actual-business-data permission and independent return remain necessary to count a customer.
