# Couples Connect — Distribution map

**Begin with willing pairs and private invitations.** This product should make one conversation easier, not create pressure to share private answers publicly.

| Route | Action / permission | Forecast for scheduled volume, explicitly assumed | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing friend/family pairs | Ask people you know whether they want to try one prompt. Do not contact their partner or parent without consent. | 10 pairs × 2 people = at most 20 first sessions, fewer after overlap; 5–10 completed reveals if the flow fits. | #1 |
| Three existing family/alumni groups | Admin-approved message with the free Radio entry, not a demand for confessions. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% try = 18–36 sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels | A fictional two-phone question/reveal and an honest free-versus-paid explanation. Publish Days 8, 13, 20, 26 at 20:00 IST. | 4 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–80 sessions. | #3 |
| Two LinkedIn posts | A thoughtful project story about making a conversation easier, Days 11 and 24 at 12:30 IST. No private-user quotes. | 2 × 200–1,000 impressions × 1–3% visits × 50% start = 2–30 sessions. | #4 |
| One invited campus reading/story club | Ask an organizer for a voluntary 20-minute demonstration with fictional responses. No recording of participants. | 10–20 people if accepted, zero otherwise; 5–10 meaningful trials assumed. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional builder-feedback listing after the two-phone test; current Monday slot/eligibility checked before submit. No votes requested. | 0–20 relevant sessions, uncertain upside. | #6 |

The first-month low case is tens of users, not 1,000. At 50% visit-to-start, 1,000 requires 2,000 distinct relevant visits. A public clip needs to show the interaction clearly enough that viewers try it with someone, rather than just like the sentiment. Replace assumptions after the first three clips.

No paid influencers, newsletter sponsorships or unverified subreddit permission. A free campus newsletter inclusion is optional only after an organizer agrees; zero forecast until then. Keep your Days 10–13 story-club invitation separate from the cohort's other family-gift products. Always give a way to decline a question or end the session.
