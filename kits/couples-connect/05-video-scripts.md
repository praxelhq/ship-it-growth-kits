# Couples Connect — Video scripts

Use two of your own phones and fictional answers. No stranger's recording or private family story is marketing material without explicit permission.

## A. Demo — 50 seconds, site and LinkedIn

0–2s, two phones face down: **“Ask the question before another visit ends.”**

2–12s, show the same prompt on both phones. “Two Chairs gives both of you one question.”

12–25s, type clearly fictional short answers separately. “Neither sees the answer first. You can keep the real answer between you.”

25–36s, show the actual simultaneous reveal. “Then turn the cards over together.” Do not fake a working reveal if the product fails.

36–45s, show Radio/free entry. “You can listen first or join an invited table free.”

45–50s, show membership price. “Starting tables is ₹149/year. notyetsaid.com.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: “What did sixteen feel like for your mum?” Text on a fictional card.

2–10s: two people choose to answer, then look up from phones. “One question can be enough to begin.”

10–15s: Two Chairs label and URL. **Fictional scene · no need to share your answer publicly**.

## C. Hook — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “No likes. No audience. Two chairs.”

2–12s: close-up of the private answer states. “Answer separately. Reveal together. Skip anything you don't want to answer.”

12–20s: a calm wide shot, not a staged emotional reaction. “Try one question with someone who wants to sit with you.” URL and free/paid boundary.

## D. Radio intro — 20 seconds, Reel

0–2s: radio dial and age marker. “A memory from a life that isn't yours.”

2–12s: play only an owned recording or use a fictional narrated sample, labelled. “The Radio is a listen-first way into Not Yet Said.”

12–20s: stop playback and show listen CTA. “Three memories free. Take a moment; you don't owe the internet a confession.”
