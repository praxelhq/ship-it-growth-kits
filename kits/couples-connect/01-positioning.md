# Couples Connect — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.notyetsaid.com/ · **Category:** Conversation / memory experience · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/couples-connect

**Your live product is Not Yet Said, not a generic couples app.** The course listing still says Couples Connect. Explain the difference once and ask for the listing to match; the kit stays under the submitted slug.

The actual homepage says **“Some things are easier to say to a stranger.”** The CTA is **“Pick something up”**. Two open experiences are [The Radio](https://www.notyetsaid.com/radio) and [Two Chairs](https://www.notyetsaid.com/chairs). Four other objects are labelled soon; do not market them as usable.

Your first user is a student visiting home who wants to hear a parent's story but does not know how to start. A partner or old friend is another valid pair, but “people who love each other” is too broad for the first demonstration.

**Pitch:** One question, two phones, a conversation you would otherwise put off.

**Short version:** A small prompt for the thing you haven't said yet.

## The first useful moment

Two Chairs gives both people the same card, keeps answers hidden until both respond, then reveals them together. Starting a table requires a **₹149/year membership that renews annually until cancelled**, as explained on the [membership page](https://www.notyetsaid.com/join); the invited person joins free. The homepage also offers three Radio memories and joining a friend's table free. Be clear which part a newcomer can try without paying.

Your wedge is a specific shared question, not a promise to improve relationships. The Radio is a different entry: hearing a short memory can make a person curious enough to try a table. Do not turn strangers' confessions into marketing content.

The real $0 alternative is asking a question directly on a normal call, or sending one question by WhatsApp. You earn ₹149 when the shared timing and simultaneous reveal make starting easier. You do not need to pretend an ordinary conversation requires a paid tool. No therapy, diagnosis or guaranteed intimacy claim belongs here.

## The uncomfortable bit

The homepage's memory snippets say “from our notebook.” They are editorial examples, not proof of real customer submissions. Likewise, physical city objects have dates “soon”; do not send someone to a park expecting a live installation.

The public Radio/Chairs pages were fetched, but no membership, payment or two-phone answer flow was completed. Prove the invitation and reveal before promotion. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful sessions, with completed two-person reveals reported beside them. Private answers never need to become your analytics.
