# Couples Connect — Landing page fixes

Checked [Not Yet Said](https://www.notyetsaid.com/) and its Radio/Chairs pages on 29 October. No authenticated table or payment was submitted.

## 1. Test the two-phone invitation before acquisition

The homepage routes to Radio, Chairs and Join. Open a table on one phone, invite a second willing tester, verify neither sees the other's answer early, and test leaving/rejoining. If this fails, stop promotion until fixed. A visible route is not a successful shared experience.

## 2. Give Two Chairs its own clear entry

Current headline: **“Some things are easier to say to a stranger.”** Current CTA: **“Pick something up.”** That fits Radio better than a conversation with a parent.

Suggested split: **“Hear a stranger's memory. Or ask someone you love one honest question.”** Buttons: **“Listen to The Radio free”** and **“See how Two Chairs works.”** Keep the quiet visual language; let the visitor choose the job.

## 3. Put the payment boundary beside the table button

The site says **“₹149 opens everything, for a year.”** Make the first-time path explicit: **“Listen to 3 memories or join a friend's table free. Membership is ₹149/year, renews annually until cancelled, and lets you start tables.”** Keep renewal and cancellation visible before checkout, matching the [membership page](https://www.notyetsaid.com/join). Do not label the start-table flow free if it requires membership.

## 4. Keep examples honest

Notebook memories are not necessarily user submissions. Keep **“Editorial example from our notebook”** beside them. For a table preview, use **“Fictional answers shown only to explain the reveal.”** Do not attach stock names/ages as though they are verified participants.

## 5. Make privacy and consent visible before answering

Suggested text: **“Only answer what you want to share. You can skip a question or leave the table. Ask before sharing someone else's response.”** Link the actual policy and explain retention/deletion truthfully; do not promise end-to-end encryption or no storage unless verified.

## 6. Separate “soon” from usable objects

The Mirror, Flower Bush, Unsent Letters and Time Capsule are labelled soon. Keep them behind an upcoming section, with **“Not available yet.”** City installations also have no confirmed dates; don't make a map imply an open location.

## 7. Check mobile listening and preview

Test audio controls, muted phone, slow network, second-phone join, long answers and reduced motion. No autoplay should surprise a user. Suggested preview: **“Not Yet Said — one question, two phones, no audience.”** Use fictional text in the image and never a private table link.
