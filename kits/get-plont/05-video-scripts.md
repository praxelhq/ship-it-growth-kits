# Get Plont — Video scripts

Record your own plant and phone screen. No paid effects, stock rescue story or accelerated fake recovery. Hide addresses, photos of other people and sign-in codes. Caption the action at readable size.

## A. One plant, one next check — 60 seconds

**Where:** owned video, nursery demonstration, plant-circle session. **First two seconds:** “When did I last check this plant?”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Your actual plant by its window. | “When did I last check this plant? I usually remember the plant, not the date.” |
| 8–20s | Add/choose the plant in the real app; confirm the suggested name. | “I add it and check the suggested match. A photo suggestion is not certainty.” |
| 20–35s | Enter actual room/conditions and a note. | “I record where it lives and what I noticed today. No made-up ideal routine.” |
| 35–48s | Set a real next-check reminder, show permission choice. | “This reminds me to look again, not water automatically.” |
| 48–60s | Saved record, then URL and two-plant allowance. | “My first two plants and their care history, free at getplont.app.” |

Only show steps that genuinely complete. If the reminder has not arrived in a real test, call it configured rather than delivered.

## B. The reminder distinction — 20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “A reminder is not a watering order.” **Where:** owned short video.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Reminder beside the actual pot. | “A reminder is not a watering order.” |
| 5–13s | Look at plant/conditions, record an observation. | “I check what is happening first. The record helps me remember what I did last time.” |
| 13–20s | Saved check and URL. | “That is the routine I am building with Plont. Two plants free.” |

Do not demonstrate a universal soil test or treatment unless you have verified the advice for that plant.

## C. The second visit — 25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “The useful part is coming back.” **Where:** a later owned post after a real later-day check.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Two actual dates in the record. | “The useful part is coming back.” |
| 6–17s | Add today's observation, compare notes without claiming recovery. | “I can see what I noticed last time and what changed. This is my plant, not a before-and-after promise.” |
| 17–25s | Record and next check. | “One small record makes the next decision less forgetful. getplont.app.” |

## D. No exact name yet — 25 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Check the suggested plant name.” **Where:** onboarding help clip.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Your plant photo and suggested match. | “Check the suggested plant name. Photo identification can be wrong.” |
| 6–17s | Show the actual review/change choice if supported. | “Compare it with what you know or ask someone knowledgeable before relying on the care information.” |
| 17–25s | Plant record, URL. | “Plont helps you keep the record. It does not remove the need to check.” |

Bank clips of the plant, adding it, conditions, saved note and later check. Eight original clips are scheduled this month: change the observation rather than recycling the same claim. Do not show another person's home or plant record without consent.
