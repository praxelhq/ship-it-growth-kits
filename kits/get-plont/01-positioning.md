# Get Plont — Positioning

**Live:** https://getplont.app/ · **Category:** Mobile app · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/get-plont

**Lead with remembering to check two real plants, not promising to keep them alive.** Your public terms correctly say identification can be wrong and plant care depends on conditions. The same truth belongs in the acquisition copy.

The [live headline](https://getplont.app/) is “Life grows better with a little green.” The offer underneath is two plants free, with care reminders. “Find your green” leads into the welcome/login path; the [signup page](https://www.getplont.app/login?mode=signup) offers an emailed code rather than a password. Those pages were read on 29 October 2026; email delivery, plant creation and reminder receipt were not completed in this review.

## Your first person

An adult renter or early-career professional who owns one or two indoor plants, does not remember their names or last care check, and sometimes responds to a yellow leaf by watering again. They want a calm record and a reminder to inspect the plant, not a complicated gardening dashboard.

Start with a person who already owns the plant. “Find your green” can sound like shopping; the stronger first job is adding the plant on the windowsill today. Do not recruit schoolchildren: your current [privacy](https://www.getplont.app/privacy) and [terms](https://www.getplont.app/terms) say 18+.

## Pitch and wedge

> Keep a simple record for your first two plants and remember the next care check.

Short version:

> Your two plants, their history, the next check.

The wedge is the weekly look-and-record routine: identify or choose a plant, confirm the match, record where it lives, add the next care check, then return and log what you observed. A reminder should prompt a check, not an automatic instruction to water regardless of soil and conditions.

## The weakness to fix before promotion

Your privacy and terms pages still call themselves a working draft pending review, and the terms mention a password despite the actual email-code sign-in. Finalize the owner-approved operational statements and remove contradictory instructions before asking people to trust plant photos, location or recurring payments. This is a product-trust fix; the kit is not legal advice or a substitute for reviewing those terms.

Keep **1,000 signups in 30 days** as the cohort target, with people who saved a real plant and later logged a care check alongside it. A thousand reminder permissions without a stored plant is not adoption.
