# Get Plont — Copy pack

Use real plants and an honest timeline. `[name]` and `[group]` are recipient fill-ins. No script claims a plant was rescued or a photo identification was certain.

## 1. Adult plant-circle organizer invitation

> Hi [name], I built Plont for people with one or two plants who forget what they last checked or did.
>
> Could I show [group] a free 20-minute routine? We add one real plant, check its suggested name, record its conditions and set the next care check. Nobody needs to buy anything; the public offer includes two plants and reminders free.
>
> It is not a diagnosis session or a promise to keep a plant alive. It is a way to remember what you observed. Happy to show you first, and I will not add members to a list.

## 2. Permissioned group/bulletin message

> Own one plant but cannot remember its last care check?
>
> Bring a photo to a short Plont demonstration. We will make a simple plant record and a next-check reminder. The first two plants are free. A reminder means check the plant and its conditions, not automatically water it.
>
> I made the app. Adults only under its current terms, and you choose whether to share location or photos. https://getplont.app/
>
> Session details from the organizer, once agreed.

Do not forward this into unrelated resident groups without permission.

## 3. Nursery conversation and QR caption

> I am testing a two-plant care record for beginners. Could you try the add-plant and next-check flow and tell me where it could mislead someone? I am not asking for your customer list or claiming your endorsement.

If the owner agrees to a card:

> **Remember the next care check.** Add your first two plants free at getplont.app. Check identification and care suggestions against your actual plant and conditions. Made by an independent student builder; not a nursery diagnosis.

## 4. First short-video caption

> The plant is still on the windowsill. The last care check is somewhere in my memory.
>
> I built Plont to keep the plant, its conditions and the next check in one place. Two plants free, reminders included. The video shows my actual plant record, not a guaranteed watering schedule.
>
> What do you always forget about your first plant? https://getplont.app/

## 5. Useful follow-up caption

> A reminder to water and a reminder to check are different things.
>
> Today I am recording what I see before deciding what to do. I want the app to help me remember, not pretend every home has the same light, soil and routine.
>
> Plont keeps that history for your first two plants free. The identification is a suggestion you should confirm.

Show your actual check. Do not turn this into horticultural advice you have not verified.

## 6. Peerlist / optional directory description

**Tagline:** Your first two plants and their next care check.

> Plont is a mobile-friendly plant-care companion. Add a plant, confirm the suggested identification, record its conditions and return to log care. Two plants and care reminders are included in the public free offer. Email-code sign-in; optional location and photos as explained in the privacy policy.
>
> I would value feedback from beginner adult plant owners: does the reminder help you check the plant, or does it feel like an instruction you should follow blindly?

## 7. Non-promotional community question

> For your first two houseplants, what did you find hardest to remember: the last watering, where the plant did well, or the advice someone gave you? I am interested in the actual routine, not a universal watering schedule.

Use only where the rules permit research questions. Disclose product-building if asked or if the rules require it. Do not use a question to hide a link promotion.

## 8. One returning-user check-in

> Has the next-check reminder been useful, or did it arrive at the wrong time? I would like to know whether you opened the plant record and wrote what you observed. No need to say it helped if it did not.

Send only to someone who agreed to hear from you, with an easy stop option. Do not publish the reply or home photo without permission.
