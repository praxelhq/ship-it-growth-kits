## Resources, accounts and newsletters

[RHS houseplant guidance](https://www.rhs.org.uk/houseplants) provides a real source to learn about conditions and care. It is a reference, not a place to drop your product link. Use a specific guide only after reading it; do not invent watering intervals or universal fixes.

[Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) is a verified builder conversation feed. Show your actual work once; do not reply to unrelated projects with a plant-app pitch. No gardening newsletter submission or editor contact has been verified. If the nursery has an actual opted-in bulletin, ask the owner whether copy #2 would be useful; count no reach until it runs.

Optional [AlternativeTo submission](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) needs verified email and review. Use accurate free-plan limits and ownership disclosure. Its backlog means zero guaranteed traffic this month; do not pay for priority.

## The honest total and next decision

Three sessions, four nursery visits, eight original clips and one listing model **22–126 accounts before overlap**. That is a focused first test, not a sufficient forecast for 1,000. At an assumed 30% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 needs roughly 3,334 qualified unique visitors. The expansion must come from users voluntarily showing their plant records or accepted organizer introductions, not inflated video views.

Get Plont uses the earlier routine-care window, Days 4–10; PlantPal uses Days 17–23 for symptom-photo checks and distinct nursery contacts. Coordinate actual dates and leave seven days between invitations to the same organization. A shared buyer does not justify six simultaneous app pitches.
