# JobLossByAI — Video scripts

Use the public writer example and fictional work context. No worker ranking, salary, employer, private report email or invented layoff outcome.

## A. Task-map demo — 60 seconds

0–2s, score beside method label: **“This is not your probability of losing a job.”**

2–12s, homepage job selector/public writer example. “A title hides several kinds of work.”

12–27s, show writing, team communication and other task groups. “These are model weights based on task importance, not a time diary.”

27–42s, open methodology and actual occupation mapping. “It joins one assistant's India-use data to a US occupational model. Check whether the default fits.”

42–52s, public “With email” boundary. “The sample is public; a fuller report asks for email. Course emails are a separate choice.”

52–60s, source/date/URL. “Use it to ask a better task question, not predict a layoff. joblossbyai.online.”

## B. Hook — 18 seconds, LinkedIn/Short

0–3s: **“Thirty-two percent of what?”**

3–12s: show writer exposure definition beside its actual breakdown. “A weighted task mix in this model—not 32% of people losing work.” Label the figure as the app's estimate, not independently reproduced.

12–18s: open methodology. “Read the denominator before sharing the chart.” URL last.

## C. Fit hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“Same title. Different week.”**

2–12s: show two fictional written task lists, labelled examples, not generated reports. “One writer drafts; another spends much more time interviewing and checking sources.”

12–20s: show actual mapping/mix controls only after tested. “Inspect the defaults before treating the profile as yours.” No fabricated output.

## D. Data-limit hook — 20 seconds

0–2s: **“No measured use doesn't mean impossible.”**

2–12s: show the public “No measured use” category and source period. “This dataset measures use of Claude in this period. It doesn't observe every tool or workplace.”

12–20s: source link/URL. “A useful map tells you where its view ends.” Don't imply the absence of a measured task proves permanent job protection.
