## #3 — LinkedIn post

An exposure percentage isn't a redundancy probability.

It may describe a modelled task mix, measured use of one assistant and assumptions about which occupation a title resembles. Those are useful things to inspect. They aren't a verdict about a person's future.

That's the distinction I'm trying to make with JobLossByAI. Open a role, inspect the task breakdown and check the methodology before sharing the score.
