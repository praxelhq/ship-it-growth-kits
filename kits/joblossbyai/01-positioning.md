# JobLossByAI — Positioning

**Live:** https://joblossbyai.online/ · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/joblossbyai

**Sell a task conversation, not fear of losing a livelihood.** Actual headline checked 29 October: “Every job is 31 kinds of work.” CTA: “See your exposure profile,” leading to the job/optional-industry form at `#start`. The form's visible note says Claude only, India May 2026, estimates of use rather than layoffs. No price was shown. Public job samples are open; fuller material is labelled “With email.” No report email was requested in research.

Your first user is an Indian early-career content writer who wants to distinguish drafting from research, judgment and working with a team. Their question is which task to inspect and practise, not whether a score can decide their career.

**Pitch:** Explore the tasks in your role, see where one AI assistant is being used, and decide which assumption about your work to check next.

**Bio:** A task-level AI-use map, with its limits visible.

The [methodology](https://joblossbyai.online/methodology) matches 50 titles to US O*NET occupations, weights tasks by importance rather than hours, and joins published India usage groups. Some job matches are explicitly low-fit. Industry does not change the numbers. “No measured use” is not proof AI cannot do a task, and a relative risk score is not a probability of redundancy.

The [official Anthropic dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/EconomicIndex) confirms a June 26 release and CC-BY data; the [Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index) is about Claude adoption. We did not independently reproduce the app's India joins, weights or scores. Attribute exact numbers to the app's model rather than saying Anthropic predicts a particular person's job loss.

The $0 alternative is inspecting that public dataset and the [O*NET database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html), or writing a task inventory in a notebook. Your advantage is an accessible joined view; your weakness is hiding the model's assumptions behind a dramatic number. A useful reader should be able to correct the default work mix and explain a limit.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**: real requested/confirmed report recipients as defined in `07`, with inspected task profiles and useful next-step reflections beside it. Don't count unrelated course-email consent, predictions or course sales as activation.
