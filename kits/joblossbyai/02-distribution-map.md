These overlapping experiments imply perhaps 15–110 gross signups before deduplication, not 1,000. At 30% relevant visit→report request, 1,000 requires roughly 3,334 distinct visits. Replace assumptions with actual rates after two posts; an impressive exposure graphic doesn't establish a report audience.

The primary data pages above are sources, not distribution partnerships. A free alumni newsletter mention is optional only after acceptance, zero forecast. No paid ads, fear-based lead lists, HR surveillance pitch or unverified subreddit rules. Explain the model openly under your own relevant posts instead of inserting links under a stranger's layoff announcement.

Use different organizers from resume clinics and Devils Advocate's career-reflection session; coordinate Days 17–20 only after actual acceptance. Don't reuse the same learning group within seven days or make a proposed workshop sound booked.
