# JobLossByAI — Landing-page fixes

Checked homepage in a browser, plus methodology/privacy/public writer route, 29 October. No report request, email confirmation or deletion was submitted. The task calculation wasn't independently reproduced.

1. **Make the limit travel with the headline.** Current H1: “Every job is 31 kinds of work.” CTA: “See your exposure profile.” The form already explains one assistant and no layoff prediction. Move a concise version to the hero: **“Explore measured Claude use across a modelled task mix. India, May 2026—not a forecast of job loss.”** Proposed button: **“Explore my role's task mix.”**

2. **Defuse probability confusion at the result.** The public writer page leads with “53/100” and “Risk score · High, relative to these 50 jobs.” Use **“Relative model score: compared with these 50 mapped roles, not a 53% chance of losing a job.”** Keep exposure distinct from that relative score. Don't convert importance weights into hours or jobs eliminated.

3. **Show the title match and editable assumptions.** Methodology maps Product Manager to Marketing Managers with low fit. Display the actual matched occupation and fit label before the result, with **“This default may not describe your role. Inspect the tasks before interpreting the score.”** Test whether mix changes really update the displayed result. Don't promise state-level automation data: methodology says states have use shares only.

4. **Make “With email” an explicit exchange.** Public sample sections show this gate. Explain exactly what will be sent, whether it costs anything and which fields are necessary before submission. No visible paid offer was established; don't invent a subscription. Keep course/upskilling emails unchecked and separate from getting the report, matching the [privacy notice](https://joblossbyai.online/privacy).

5. **Link evidence with scope, not authority theatre.** Keep primary dataset/version, source period, pull date, weighting method and attribution. Use **“Based on published Claude data; our role mapping and score are our calculation.”** The official dataset confirms a release exists; that isn't independent validation of your join or an endorsement.

6. **Verify report, consent and deletion.** Use an authorized test email to check request→delivery→optional confirmation→delete. Confirm privacy's 24-month retention and immediate deletion claim match implementation. Mask job/email/work-mix fields in Clarity; don't assume policy wording proves masking. Never put email tokens in marketing clips.

7. **Make charts usable on phones.** At 390px, check 31 labels, chart text alternatives, job search, industry choice and error return. “No measured use” needs a plain explanation, not “AI-proof.” Preview: **“JobLossByAI — inspect the task assumptions behind AI-use estimates.”** Avoid an alarming job-loss percentage in the share image.
