# lowercase — 30-day execution calendar

**Days 1–3 have one job: open Foundations free.** It's a product change, not a copy change, and until it ships every channel in `02` sends beginners into a ₹3,499 wall.

After that, your month has an unusual and pleasant shape: **you win by teaching in public.** Thirty answers to beginners' questions, ten concept explainers, eight concept pages. Every one of them is useful with no product attached, which is why they're welcome in communities that remove promotion — and why they keep working in January.

**Your public window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 17–21**, the last slot. **This costs you nothing.** Beginners aren't seasonal, and nine cohort-mates will have exhausted the placement-panic audience by then anyway — which was never yours.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **Opening Foundations free, by day 2.** The target is arithmetically unreachable otherwise. Nothing else on this list is close.
2. **"One plan covers both of you."** Your referral loop is already built and currently invisible. It's the cheapest 1,000 signups available to anyone in this cluster.
3. **Thirty minutes a day answering beginners' questions.** Teaching in public is welcome where promotion is removed, and you out-answer everyone because you have an actual curriculum. It compounds all month.
4. **Twelve concept pages.** They rank, they qualify exactly the right visitor, and they're the only thing here still working in February.
5. **Four or five clubs adopting the 6-week path.** A syllabus is what a club needs and can't write, and you're the only one with one.

## The honest tension in your month

**Signups and revenue pull in opposite directions for you**, in a way they don't for your cohort-mates. Opening Foundations free will raise signups a lot and lower revenue per visitor.

**That is the right trade for a month whose target is signups**, and `07` reports both numbers so the trade is visible rather than argued about in retrospect. Say so to the board in week 1 rather than week 4.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Peerlist** (day 19 — builder audience, not beginners), **YouTube comments** (day 20), **Format D** (day 13).

**Never drop:** days 1–2 (Foundations free), the daily beginner answering, the concept pages, day 8/11 (club DMs), day 17 (r/MBA — one shot).
