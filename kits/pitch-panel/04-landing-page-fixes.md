# PitchPanel — Landing-page fixes

Read [the rendered home and full sample](https://www.pitchpanel.in/) on 29 October 2026. Actual headline: **“Know your market. Then face the panel.”** CTA: **“START RESEARCH”**; full sample opens through **“OPEN THE FULL SAMPLE REPORT.”** No public software price/allowance was established. No new search, pitch, account or payment was submitted.

## 1. Put no-live-web beside the search

Current method section clearly discloses no live data, while the hero can sound like a fresh market search.

Replacement search note: **“AI-generated first view from model knowledge, not live web research. Verify product listings and numbers before deciding.”**

Keep the useful category/price/pack/city entry. Do not replace honest method disclosure with a generic real-time badge.

## 2. Distinguish product links from checked sources

Current sample “Website” can open a Google search; retail links can be search results.

Replacement link labels: **“Find the official site”** / **“Search current listings.”** Add a separate checked-source field only after a real exact URL/date is recorded.

Do not call a search query evidence that the price is current or the product is available. Test broken/ambiguous listings and unit mismatches.

## 3. Explain what a price-map gap means

Current sample says open bands have no listed products, excludes other units and starts its axis at zero.

Replacement note: **“Gap in this listed sample, not proof of unmet demand. Products in other units may be excluded. Compare like pack/quantity bases and verify current offers.”**

Keep per-pack/per-unit controls. Check unit normalization and excluded-item counts before recommending a band.

## 4. Keep estimates and rules of thumb attached

Current sample labels market size/growth/ratings estimates, and metrics unsourced rules of thumb.

Replacement card footer: **“Model estimate, no cited source. Do not quote as market fact.”** Metric footer: **“Illustrative operating assumption; replace with your own evidence.”**

Keep labels in print/PDF and cropped share images. A score out of 100 is not a probability or independently measured demand.

## 5. Make the fictional panel unmistakable

Current panel names are original AI characters, explicitly not real people or investment advice.

Replacement action label: **“Rehearse with fictional AI viewpoints.”** Supporting line: **“Questions to prepare, not investor interest, funding access or a professional recommendation.”**

Do not use real investor portraits/names to imply endorsement. Keep actual generated feedback distinct from a planned question or landing example.

## 6. Explain account/allowance and save behavior

Current public page promises saved reports and offers sign-in inside the report, but no software price/allowance is visible.

Replacement pre-search note: **“Review what this search includes, any limits and whether an account is required to save.”** Fill real terms only after verification.

Test new report, failed generation, account save/return, deletion, pitch privacy and print/PDF using permitted synthetic input. 30–60 seconds is an advertised duration, not a timing measured here. A working illustrative report is not proof the authenticated generation pipeline works.
