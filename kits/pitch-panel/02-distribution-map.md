# PitchPanel — Distribution map

Lead with an operating brand's specific pack/price/city decision, not a promise to validate any startup. Coordinate the founder session already reserved in [founder-readiness research](../../shared/founder-readiness-cluster.md).

| Route | Execution / permission | Planned input and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing D2C snack-brand owner relationships | Qualify actual sales, a defined pack/price/city decision and permission to process product information. Use genuine public business contact or consented introductions. | Ten blocks of four businesses = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| One accepted founder session | Days 10–13 invitation window. Ask an existing organizer to choose operating consumer-product owners and one verification exercise. No assumed member list, event booking or endorsement. | One conditional session Day 12; #4/#5. Existing-trial support, no guaranteed extra customers. |
| Two founder introductions | Ask a known operator to forward one invitation to a consenting brand, without confidential plans/contact disclosure. | Days 10 and 13; #4. Upside only. |
| Own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Demonstrate sample estimate versus checked source and one unanswered buyer question. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 qualified visits/post; excluded from base. |

Base guesses: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-product report setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about 10 businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. The ten four-business blocks are Days 4,5,8,10,11,15,17,22,24,27. A session attendee already approached is not a second acquisition; an unaccepted session contributes zero reach.

PitchPanel's shared founder invitation is **Days 10–13**. Astitva uses Days 4–7, Postifyy 17–20 and Adya 24–27 in the existing founder sequence. [A2 research](../../shared/a2-founder-research.md) reserves Before for brands with their own respondents, BrandAid agency briefs, NXT technical complaint research and ShelfTest pre-product assumptions. Use sector/stage-specific owner lists and one primary invitation per organizer/week. Do not claim the same workshop supplies every tool's pipeline.

[Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) and [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) are verified organizer-discovery entry points in the shared notes, not confirmed distribution partners or scraped mailing lists. No newsletter or community-promotion acceptance is established. Ask the organizer you actually know before a post. No paid launches, automated DMs, purchased founder numbers or requests for votes.

One relevant follow-up after seven days, then stop after decline. A skeptical model response is not a real investor meeting; no one should be recruited by a promise of funding or introductions. If reports cannot be saved or source links cannot support verification, fix that before a broad launch.
