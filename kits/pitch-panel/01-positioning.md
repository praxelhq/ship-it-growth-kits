## The first business

An operating Indian D2C snack brand reviewing a specific pack/price in one city before its next small launch. The owner already has a product and real buyers to interview. Start with its actual pack size, intended price and city, not a student inventing a market to impress fictional investors.

**Pitch: Structure a first market view, verify the competitor details yourself, then rehearse the questions your plan still cannot answer.**

Short version: **A research starting point and a practice panel, with the limits visible.**

The useful first result is not the model saying “good idea.” It is a corrected comparison and one important assumption the owner takes to real buyers. The panel's five viewpoints are fictional AI characters, not real investors, endorsements, funding access or investment advice.

## What the sample actually establishes

The page explicitly says **no live web data yet**. It uses model general knowledge; market size, growth and 0–100 scores are uncited estimates. Trends link to news searches for the user to check. The full sample says metrics are rules of thumb, not sourced benchmarks. Do not quote its tea-market size, growth, margins or prices as researched current facts.

The sample includes pack/unit price views and notes that other-unit products can be excluded. “Open band” means no listed sample product in that range, not proven unmet demand or absence of competitors. Some “Website” links open Google searches, and retailer links open searches rather than an exact checked SKU. The operator must open the real source and confirm product, quantity, date, tax/delivery basis and availability.

Public copy says reports save to accounts and pitches are not shown to other users; neither authenticated persistence nor privacy was tested. A reported 30–60-second generation time is a product promise, not measured in this audit. Do not invent a paid plan, unlimited free report allowance or guaranteed saved-work recovery.

## The alternative

A free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) matrix can hold exact product URL, pack size, observed price/date and unresolved questions. The founder can rehearse with a willing peer and real customer interviews. PitchPanel must earn its place through a useful decision structure and sharper questions, not a claim the AI has checked today's whole market.

Prove a new own-product report, account save/return and one manually checked competitor before promoting it as real-work support. Keep confidential plans out of the first public example. The existing sample is an illustration, not the founder's report or evidence of business demand.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each must be an operating brand, onboard its own actual permitted product data, use independently within seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Students in a workshop, sample readers and fictional panel characters are not business customers.
