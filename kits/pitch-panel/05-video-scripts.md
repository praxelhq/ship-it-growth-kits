# PitchPanel — Phone-demo scripts

Use a fictional snack brand and non-confidential product details. Record your screen and voice. No real business secrets, buyer contacts or investor impersonation. The existing tea sample must stay labelled illustrative; do not repeat its numeric estimates as facts.

## A. Report, source, question — 65 seconds

0–3 s: pack/price/city input beside the no-live-web notice. **“What needs checking before the pitch?”**

3–17 s: actual supported new report, or clearly labelled full sample. “This is a first structure from model knowledge. It has not browsed today's market.”

17–32 s: inspect one actual product link and its destination. “A search result is not the same as an exact checked SKU.” Open the real official listing only when verified; leave the source review legible.

32–47 s: compare pack/unit basis and mark one correction on a separate labelled review sheet if the product cannot edit the report. “Correct the comparison before interpreting a gap.”

47–55 s: actual panel description and one verified generated question only if available. “These are fictional viewpoints, not investor interest.”

55–65 s: next buyer question and URL. “Take the unresolved assumption to real buyers. pitchpanel.in.”

## B. Gap hook — 20 seconds, Short

0–3 s: illustrative open band. **“No dot here does not mean no competitor.”**

3–12 s: listed brands/excluded units notice. “The chart only contains the products in this report.”

12–20 s: source-check note and URL. “Verify the list, then ask whether buyers need the offer.” No invented market opportunity.

## C. Panel hook — 25 seconds, LinkedIn

0–3 s: fictional-panel disclosure. **“This is practice, not a funding meeting.”**

3–15 s: numbers/market/product/brand/skeptic lenses without presenting fictional names as real experts. “Five viewpoints can help you prepare a question. They cannot approve your business.”

15–25 s: one clearly labelled unresolved assumption and URL. “Use the challenge to improve your next real conversation.”

Capture actual headline/CTA, sample label, method limits, search-link destination, price normalization, estimate footers and panel fiction. Label waiting cuts and generation untested. Never fabricate a successful new report or funding reaction.
