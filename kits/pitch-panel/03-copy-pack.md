# PitchPanel — Copy pack

## 1. Brand-owner opener

Hi — are you deciding the pack size and price for a real snack product in one city?

I build PitchPanel. It structures a first market view, then gives fictional AI-panel questions to challenge the plan. It does not browse the live web or tell you whether buyers will pay.

Would a sample-plus-source-check demonstration help? We can start without your confidential launch plan.

## 2. Own-product trial email

**Subject: Check one pack/price comparison before trusting the report**

For the first PitchPanel test, use one actual product, its pack size, proposed price and first city. Then choose a competitor and verify the real listing manually.

The report's market sizes, growth and scores are AI estimates, not sourced market data. Even its product links need checking: some lead to searches, not the exact current SKU. The useful output is a corrected comparison and a clearer question for real buyers.

Public sample: https://www.pitchpanel.in/

The panel characters are fictional, not investors you are pitching for funding. Please inspect the actual allowance/account terms before relying on saved reports; no software price was visible in the public entry. If not relevant, say no and I will stop.

## 4. Organizer/introduction request

Could we offer a short verification exercise to willing operating consumer-product owners? Each brings a non-confidential pack/price/city question. We inspect the sample, verify one actual product listing and name one buyer question the report cannot answer.

The tool uses model knowledge, not live browsing. The panel is fictional and no funding, endorsement or investor introduction is offered. Please choose a suitable owner group rather than sending several overlapping startup-tool invitations that week. I will agree the actual date and copy with you first.

## 5. Approved-session invitation

A short market-report check, not an AI verdict on your business.

Bring one product, pack size, proposed price and first city you are comfortable discussing. We will inspect a PitchPanel report, compare one real listing and separate facts from assumptions before the practice panel.

No confidential plan, purchase or public pitch recording required. The organizer will confirm the actual session time; please do not enter private business data in a shared screen.

## 6. Consented follow-up

Which product or price did you correct after opening the real listing? Which panel question led to a genuine buyer question? Did you independently return to your own report? Please share a general lesson, not confidential plans or private buyer contacts. I will ask separately before quoting you.

Never invent investors, funding interest, customer demand, market sizes, brand prices or founder testimonials. Keep every illustrative sample and estimate labelled when reusing a clip.
