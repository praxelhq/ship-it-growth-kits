# PitchPanel — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days.** Each operating brand must satisfy [all four tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): own actual permitted product/market inputs onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; genuine business; privately known owner name and phone. Keep identifiers outside the public repo. Students with fictional ideas, sample viewers and panel conversations do not qualify.

Activation: the owner generates/uses its own product report, checks at least one real listing and records an unresolved buyer question or corrected comparison. Report that beside customer totals. A report score is not demand, an estimate is not a sourced fact and fictional feedback is not investor validation. A workshop attendee is not automatically a brand customer.

No private account data was accessed; actual saved-report/brand totals are unknown.

| Baseline | Source / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Real brand businesses / latest independent use | Account/report records plus private permitted owner register; exclude test/sample use. |
| Own-product reports and successful saves | Actual product records; landing sample opened separately. |
| Checked exact sources/corrections | Owner-controlled verification notes, no confidential strategy in growth analytics. |
| Panel use / next buyer question | Actual generated feedback/status and consented aggregate description. |
| Generation/save/privacy failures | Synthetic reproductions and private issue categories. |
| Offer/price and course baseline | Actual configured allowance if established, [profile](https://shipped.money/p/pitch-panel), timestamp/period. |

Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate. Exclude confidential product plans, price/margin inputs where private, pitch text, saved reports, account fields and access links from replay. Minimal events: ownreportcreated, reportsaved, sourcechecked only when actually known, panelquestiongenerated, laterindependentuse. A clicked search link does not prove the owner verified a price.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Eight approaches scheduled. If owners interpret estimates as facts or fictional panel as real funding access, fix 04 and copy first. |
| Day 14 | 20 approaches. Compare real own reports, source corrections and the accepted session's actual outcome; no double counting. |
| Day 21 | 28 approaches. Inspect independent use and whether a next buyer question was clearer; do not claim validated demand. |
| Day 28 | 40 approaches. Audit all four tests and identify operating brands rather than sample audiences. |
| Day 30 | Qualifying businesses against ten, activation, corrected comparisons, return use, actual offer, hours and gap. |

Base guesses: 40 × 50% × 70% × 70% = 9.8 businesses; at 25% conversation, 4.9. Three posts, two introductions and one conditional session are upside/support only. Replace assumptions after eight approaches and again after 20. One-off research may help a decision while failing final-week independent-use requirements; report that separately rather than prompting a login for the metric.

No software revenue is inferred from sample product prices. No funding, investor interest, market size or consumer demand is proven by a generated score. Publish only permitted aggregate product observations, with actual source dates and limitations; never invent founder outcomes or use fictional panel names as endorsements.
