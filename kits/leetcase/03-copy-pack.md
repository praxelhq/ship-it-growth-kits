**Title:** `Nobody tracks case prep, and I think that's why most people plateau`

> Observation from my own prep, and from asking about twenty people: **almost nobody has any record of their case preparation.**

>
> **This matters more than it sounds.** Improvement in cases comes from noticing a pattern in your own mistakes, and you cannot notice a pattern you haven't recorded. Each case feels like a fresh event, so the same error survives for months.

> 3. **A fixed sheet to work through**, instead of picking cases at random from a 400-page casebook. Random selection means you avoid the uncomfortable ones without realising.
