# LeetCase — 30-day execution calendar

**Day 1 has two jobs and both are cheap: buy a domain, and turn on uptime monitoring.**

Everything in `02` is a link someone pastes or types, and `leetcase.rambhardwaj.in` taxes all of it. And you went down during placement season, which for a product calling itself an operating system is the worst possible thing to leave unmonitored.

**Your window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 19–23.** That's the most comfortable position in this cluster — **nearly three weeks to get the page, the proof and the content right before anyone looks.** Use it rather than launching early.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **The domain, on day 1.** It taxes every channel simultaneously and costs a few hundred rupees. There is no cheaper high-impact fix in this entire cluster.
2. **Uptime monitoring, on day 1.** You went down during placement season. A product called an operating system cannot do that twice.
3. **"LeetCode for cases" as the first four words, everywhere.** You have the shortest pitch in this market and an audience of ex-engineers who need no explanation. **That's structural and nobody can copy it.**
4. **The r/developersIndia post on day 15.** Your best single post of the month, in the one place your pitch is assumed knowledge.
5. **Day-7 return above 20%.** You sell a tracking habit. If people don't come back, the streak, the log and the readiness score are all decoration — and no traffic fixes that.

## The thing you can do that nobody else here can

**Charge money and have students pay it.**

At ₹100/month you are five times cheaper than the nearest competitor in your own cohort. That makes **paying users a realistic month-1 metric for you and a fantasy for everyone else here.** `07` targets 80, and it also makes the club purchase real — ₹4,000 for a 40-member society is a sum a society actually has.

**Don't raise the price this month.** Signups are the target, and cheap is the weapon.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **Quora** (day 18 — compounds into next year), **r/consulting** (day 20 — credibility, not volume), **Peerlist** (day 22 — builders, not users).

**Never drop:** day 1 (domain + monitoring), day 13 (testimonials), day 15 (r/developersIndia), days 9 and 11 (club DMs), the guesstimate pages.
