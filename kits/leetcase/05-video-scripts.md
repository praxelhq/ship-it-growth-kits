# LeetCase — Video scripts and shot lists

Scripts and shot lists only — **you record these on your phone.**

You have an advantage no other project in this cluster has: **your audience already has a shared reference, and it's visual.** A LeetCode dashboard — the streak grid, the solved count, the topic breakdown — is an image a large share of Indian MBA students recognise instantly and feel something about.

**So your signature shot is a side-by-side: a LeetCode dashboard next to yours.** That comparison explains your entire product in two seconds with no words.

All vertical 9:16, captions burned in. **Use the new domain in every end card** (`04` Fix 1).

## Video 1 — The hook (your most important asset)

**Length:** 20–28 seconds · **Where:** LinkedIn, Instagram, r/developersIndia, WhatsApp status, the landing page

### Script

| Time | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | **A LeetCode dashboard. Streak grid, solved count, topic breakdown.** | "This is how we prepped for coding placements." |
| 4–8s | Your face | "Sheets. Streaks. A problem count. You knew exactly where you stood." |
| 8–14s | **Cut to: a 400-page casebook PDF, scrolling** | "This is how we prep for case interviews." |
| 14–19s | Still scrolling, then stop. Hold the blank feeling. | "No sheet. No streak. No count. Fourteen cases in, I couldn't tell you what I kept getting wrong." |
| 19–25s | **Your dashboard — streak, topic completion, error log** | "So I built the missing layer." |
| 25–28s | End card | "LeetCode for cases. leetcase.[domain]" |

### Shot list

1. **The LeetCode dashboard, 0–4s — open on it, not on your face.** Your own, or a generic one. **The streak grid is the single most recognisable image you can show this audience** and it does the positioning before you speak.
2. **Talking head, 4–8s.** Propped phone, eye level, window light, quiet room.
3. **The casebook PDF scroll, 8–19s.** Scroll fast through a real 400-page casebook. The length is the point — it should feel exhausting.
4. **Your dashboard, 19–25s.** Streak, topic-wise completion, and the error log. Readable on a phone.
5. **End card with the new domain as text.**

### Rules

- **Open on the LeetCode dashboard, not your face.** It's the hook and it's free.
- **The cut at 8 seconds is the whole video** — from a structured system to an unstructured PDF. Make it abrupt.
- **Say "LeetCode for cases" exactly once**, at the end. Any more and it's a slogan rather than a realisation.
- **Caption the URL, never speak it.** Especially true for you until Fix 1 ships.
- No logo intro, no music under speech.

## Video 2 — The error log video (your most persuasive)

**Length:** 35–45 seconds · **Where:** LinkedIn (pinned), Reddit comments, Instagram, the landing page

Your error log is the one feature nobody else in this cluster built, and the story sells it better than the feature does.

> **(0–5s, your face)** "I made the same mistake in case interviews eleven times before I noticed."
>
> **(5–14s, screen: your error log, real entries, scrolling slowly)** "Here's my actual log. 'Decided the answer before checking the data.' Again. Again. Again."
>
> **(14–24s, your face)** "Each case felt like a fresh event. I'd finish, feel vaguely okay, close the laptop. Nothing carried over. So I kept doing it for two months."
>
> **(24–34s, the log, highlighting the repetition)** "The fix wasn't more cases. It was writing one line down after each one. By the eighth, the pattern is obvious and slightly humiliating — and that's the exact moment you stop."
>
> **(34–42s, the product: topic completion, score trend)** "That's what this is. Sheets, streaks, topic-wise tracking, the error log, and rubric scores so the trend means something."
>
> **(42–45s)** "leetcase.[domain]. Free tier, ₹100 a month."

**Why this is your best video:** it's a confession, which is the one register no competitor uses, and **"slightly humiliating" is the word that makes it true.** Everyone in your audience is currently repeating a mistake they haven't noticed. **Pin this.**

**Use your real log, with real repeated entries.** A fabricated one will read as fabricated.

## Video 3 — The guesstimate tree (make 10 — your volume and SEO asset)

**Length:** 25–35 seconds · **No face, no voice needed** · **Where:** Instagram, Shorts, your guesstimate pages, Reddit comments

Your multi-step trees with unit-economics checks are the most filmable thing you have, and they double as the content for the 12 SEO pages in `03` #7 — **same afternoon produces both.**

### Template

| Time | On screen |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4s | **"How many ATMs are there in Mumbai?"** |
| 4–7s | **"20 seconds. Go."** |
| 7–9s | *(beat — nothing moves)* |
| 9–20s | The tree building: population → households → bank accounts → transactions/month → transactions per ATM/day |
| 20–27s | The number, then **"Sanity check: that's 1 ATM per ~4,000 people. Plausible."** |
| 27–32s | **"The sanity check is the part interviewers actually watch for."** |
| 32–35s | **"leetcase.[domain]"** |

### The ten

1. How many ATMs in Mumbai
2. How many cars sold in India per year
3. How many cups of tea India drinks daily
4. Market size of gyms in India
5. How many people order food online in Bengaluru daily
6. How many weddings happen in India per year
7. How many litres of petrol one city uses daily
8. How many school uniforms are sold in India annually
9. How many smartphones are replaced in India each year
10. How many Swiggy delivery partners are active at 8pm

**The rule that makes these work: always end on the sanity check**, not the number. It's the step everyone skips, it's genuinely what interviewers watch for, and it's the part of your product that's distinctive.

### Shot list

1. **Text cards and a tree drawn on paper, overhead.** Daylight, black marker, white unlined paper. Speed up writing 1.5–2×.
2. **Hold the final sanity check for two full seconds.** That's the screenshot.
3. **Batch ten in one afternoon** — no face, no voice, so it's the cheapest content in your kit.

## Video 4 — The 60-second product tour

**Length:** 55–70 seconds · **Where:** landing page (you already have a demo video — this replaces or supplements it), Peerlist, "how does it work" replies

Your product is a dashboard, which is hard to convey in text and easy on film.

> **(0–6s)** "Sixty seconds, the whole thing, nothing cut."
> **(6–18s, the tracker)** "Topic-wise completion — market entry, profitability, M&A. I've done eleven profitability cases and two market entries, which tells me I've been drilling the one I'm already good at."
> **(18–30s, a case running, voice dictation)** "Do a case. Type it, or dictate it out loud, which is closer to the real round."
> **(30–44s, the rubric)** "Scored on MECE structure, math and synthesis, step by step. 74 on this one — lost it on synthesis."
> **(44–54s, the error log)** "Then the log. One line. And three weeks later you see the pattern."
> **(54–64s, the readiness score)** "And a readiness score against your actual interview date, which is the number that stops you feeling vaguely prepared."
> **(64–70s)** "150+ questions free. ₹100 a month for unlimited. leetcase.[domain]"

**Show a real middling score.** A 74 proves the rubric is honest; a 94 proves you can game your own product.
