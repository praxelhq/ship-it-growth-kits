# LeetCase — Distribution map

**Read this first: fix the URL before you spend a single channel** (`04` Fix 1).

`leetcase.rambhardwaj.in` cannot be said out loud, typed from memory, or pasted without looking like a side project. **Every channel below is a link someone has to share, remember or say**, so you are paying a tax on all of them simultaneously. A domain costs a few hundred rupees and it is the highest-return purchase available to you this month.

Two things then make your plan different from the rest of the cluster:

1. **Your audience has a shared prior.** A large share of Indian MBA students are ex-engineers who ground LeetCode for placements. **"LeetCode for cases" needs no explanation to them** — which means your copy can be four words long and your channels can be the places engineers-turned-MBAs congregate.
2. **₹100/month is five times cheaper than anything else in this market.** That makes paid conversion your easiest win in the cluster, so unlike your cohort-mates you can talk about the paid tier without flinching.

**Your window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is "after the site is back"** — it's back, so take **days 19–23**, after the others. You lose little: the grinder isn't a four-week-panic buyer.

## Tier 1 — Where the grinder is 🔴

### 1. The ex-engineer MBA, who already understands your product

| Where | Scale | Reach | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **r/developersIndia** | Very large and extremely active | 1,000–8,000 views | **Full of ex-engineers now in or heading to MBA programmes, and they all know LeetCode.** *"I built LeetCode for case interviews"* is a one-line pitch that lands here and nowhere else. Disclose you're the founder. |
| **r/MBA** | Several hundred thousand | 2,000–15,000 views | Self-promo removed on sight. **Days 19–23, one post.** Your angle: the tracking/error-log insight, which is genuinely novel in that sub. Link in a comment. |

**The one-line pitch that works in all of these:** *"LeetCode for case interviews — sheets, streaks, an error log and a readiness score. Free tier, ₹100/month."* **Four words of concept, then the proof.** No other project in this cluster has a pitch this short.

**At ₹100/month you can also say something nobody else can:** *"If your club wants paid access for everyone, it's ₹100 a head and I'll do a club rate."* **Forty members is ₹4,000 — a real club actually has that**, unlike a ₹499 or $19 product. **Nobody else in this cluster can make that offer**, and it's worth testing on two clubs this month.

### 3. Guesstimate SEO — your best compounding asset

Your **multi-step guesstimate trees with exhibits and unit economics** are the most content-shaped feature in the cluster, and *"how many X in India"* queries are searched constantly, year-round, by exactly your audience.

| What | Reach | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **12 worked guesstimate pages** | 100–3,000/month each, compounding | Full tree, stated assumptions, the sanity check, and *"the follow-up a real interviewer would ask"*. **Give away the complete answer.** |
| **"Guesstimate questions with answers" hub page** | 500–5,000/month eventually | The query everyone types. Be the best page for it. |
| **Case-type pages** — market entry, profitability, M&A | 100–1,500/month | You already organise by these topics; make them indexable |

**This won't move November much and it's the thing that makes next October work without you posting anything.** Write 12.

**The honest read.** 1,000 signups needs roughly 8,000–9,000 visitors, so the low end misses and the high end clears it. Three things decide where you land, and none is "post more":

1. **The domain** (`04` Fix 1). It taxes every single channel above, and it's the cheapest fix in this entire cluster.
2. **"LeetCode for cases" as the lead line.** You have the shortest pitch in the market and a built-in audience of ex-engineers who need no explanation. **That's a structural advantage — use it in the first four words, everywhere.**
3. **Three or four clubs turning on a leaderboard.** Each is 30–150 people, and a cohort makes your ranks mean something.

**One thing you can report that nobody else in this cluster can:** at ₹100/month, **paid conversion is a realistic month-1 metric for you, not a month-6 one.** Everyone else's paid tier is priced out of a student month. `07` targets 80 paying users — a number your cohort-mates cannot credibly aim at, and worth showing the board as the upside of the price you chose.
