## 1. Put the three-company scope before signup

Suggested support under H1:

> Research Resend, Sarvam AI or PostHog, inspect published contact evidence and prepare a contribution-focused introduction. Broader company discovery is coming later.

CTA **Explore a supported company** only if that destination genuinely exists; otherwise **Open my personal workspace** with account requirement stated. Don't collect a profile before revealing the user's chosen startup isn't supported. No claim of a broad verified founder directory or automated applications.

## 2. Show evidence limits at the decision point

Keep official source, excerpt/date, relationship and routing separate. Suggested contact card:

> Published on this official page on the checked date. Relationship: team mailbox/person as actually evidenced. Domain routing found does not prove mailbox existence or identity. Verify contact purpose and relevance before using it; outdated/unavailable evidence is labelled.

No guessed email patterns or inferred founder identity. Don't use a support/sales address for unrelated job pitches solely because it is public. Owner tests stale/unavailable/source-changed states; no fabricated green verification badge.

## 3. Make exact-version approval visible

Suggested pre-send screen:

> Review recipient, subject and full body. Approve this exact version only. Editing recipient or content requires fresh approval. No send until Gmail is connected and you explicitly approve.

Owner tests edited-after-approval, changed recipient, duplicate click/retry, ambiguous failure and revoked Gmail access with controlled addresses/accounts. A successful draft generation isn't a send; a success URL/toast alone doesn't establish real delivery. Don't claim this audit verified those controls or send real job emails for testing.

## 4. Separate manual contact from automation

Suggested WhatsApp help:

> Manual FirstDoor contact is available. Automated replies/updates are not active; Meta setup remains open.

Gmail help:

> Sending and reply sync require the actual Gmail connection/setup. You may review a draft before connecting, if implemented. Read the exact permissions and revoke them when you no longer want access.

Do not imply WhatsApp bot support, email sending or reply sync works simply because a link exists. Don't publish the operator's personal phone number in reusable public copy; link the product's actual contact route.

## 5. Explain data and price before profile entry

No price/policy link was visible in the inspected landing/workspace. Owner publishes actual current access/limits, profile/resume storage, contact evidence retention, Gmail token handling, deletion/export and analytics masking. Don't invent free forever or no-data storage.

Suggested first-use note:

> Use a fictional profile for evaluation. Don't paste private CV/contact information until you've reviewed the actual data/permission terms. No guaranteed reply, interview or job.

Test auth/account separation and private-data masking with owned samples; no student names/emails/CVs in recordings/events. A personal workspace label isn't independent privacy proof.

## 6. Lead with relevance over volume

Suggested final prompt:

> What have you actually built or learned that is relevant to this company? Start with one honest reason, not a hundred generic messages.

Keep supported-startup logos/names as research subjects, not partnerships. Measure first well-evidenced reviewed draft and user control, not total sent emails. The 1,000 target is product accounts, never emails to three companies.
