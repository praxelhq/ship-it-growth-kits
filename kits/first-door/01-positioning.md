# FirstDoor — Positioning

**Live:** https://firstdoor.co.in/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/first-door · Checked 29 October 2026.

Your first user is an individual student/job seeker who can name a startup they care about and one contribution they could credibly make. Help them research the company, inspect a published contact and review a thoughtful introduction. Don't position this as mass job applications, guessed founder-email hunting or a shortcut around hiring consent.

Actual H1 **“Don’t wait for an opening. Start a conversation.”** **Build your first outreach/Open your workspace** → `/workspace`; that page says **“Good work deserves an open door.”** and requires email sign-in for the personal workspace. The public WhatsApp route is manual contact; automated replies/updates await Meta setup. Gmail setup is required for sending/reply sync. No account, Gmail authorization, send, reply sync or WhatsApp automation was tested here. No paid amount is visible.

## The pitch

> Start with a company you understand. Check the contact's source, write a relevant first message and approve exactly what gets sent.

Bio: **Evidence before outreach. Your words before every send.**

Current company research is explicitly limited to **Resend, Sarvam AI and PostHog**. Broader discovery is planned. Don't promise any startup, a verified founder directory or an auto-application engine based on the older roster idea. Public evidence separates official source, contact relationship and domain routing; routing doesn't prove a person's identity or mailbox existence. Team inbox stays labelled team inbox. No guessed email patterns.

The product's strongest differentiation is control: review recipient, subject and full exact-version email before approving. That must survive edits/retries; a changed draft needs fresh approval. A drafted message isn't a sent email, a send isn't a reply, and a reply isn't an interview or job.

## Alternatives and a real contribution

Manual company research plus a carefully written email in an existing mail account costs ₹0 extra specialist software. A local tracker or [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) can record source/date/status. FirstDoor must reduce confusion while preserving evidence and user control, not claim thoughtful outreach is impossible without AI.

Named-company subject matter is real: [Resend's documentation](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/introduction) covers transactional email, domain verification and delivery tooling; [PostHog docs](https://posthog.com/docs) include analytics/replay/flags; [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/) describes Indic AI/APIs. These are **not** evidence of vacancies, hiring interest, a contact's consent or a relationship with FirstDoor. A contribution example must reflect the user's actual work, never fabricated experience or metrics.

## Trust and target

Keep resumes/profile data, contact evidence and OAuth permissions private and minimal. Don't publish student names/contacts, personal recipient addresses or access-bearing Gmail/WhatsApp links in kits/reports. An official source link isn't permission to bulk-message everyone. Respect refusals and relevant contact-purpose rules; don't use support inboxes for unrelated job pitches.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**, alongside first supported-company research and a genuinely reviewed draft. The target measures users of FirstDoor, **not** 1,000 emails sent to the three companies. Contact volume isn't acquisition or placement success. No advertised price means don't invent a free-forever plan, subscription or guaranteed outcome.
