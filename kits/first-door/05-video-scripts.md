# FirstDoor — Video scripts

Use a fictional profile, no private CV, personal contact address, OAuth token or real email thread. Do not actually send job outreach for a demo. Official company sources are context, not vacancies or endorsements. Authenticated screens require owner test recording first.

## A. Main demonstration — 60 seconds, landing/clinic

**0–8s:** Fictional learner and one supported company. “Start with a company and a real reason, not a list of guessed emails.” Label fictional profile.

**8–21s:** Actual official source and product evidence view if implemented. “Where was this published, when was it checked, and is it a team inbox or a person?” Hide any personal address; use generic labels.

**21–31s:** Routing label. “Routing doesn't prove this mailbox exists or a person's identity.” No false “verified founder” badge.

**31–43s:** Actual draft review with hypothetical contribution and full text. “Only describe work you've really done. Read recipient, subject and every word.” No invented portfolio metrics.

**43–52s:** Actual approval control if owner-tested; stop before send. “This demonstration sends nothing. Actual Gmail sending needs setup and exact-version approval.” Don't fabricate a delivered email.

**52–60s:** Three supported companies, account/privacy/current limits, URL. No guaranteed reply/interview/job or WhatsApp bot claim.

## B. Hook — 23 seconds, Day 10

**0–5s:** Source/relationship/routing labels. “Three checks that aren't the same thing.”

**5–16s:** Official page versus domain routing graphic, clearly illustrative. “A public team mailbox isn't a verified founder. Routing isn't mailbox verification.”

**16–23s:** “Evidence before outreach.” URL/current three-company limit. No personal email address shown.

## C. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 16

**0–6s:** Draft with generic recipient/subject. “Approving a draft isn't approving every later edit.”

**6–17s:** Owner-tested exact-version reapproval after harmless change, if implemented. Otherwise show requirement as a proposed checklist, not live control proof.

**17–25s:** “Your words, your final say. No send in this demo.” URL/Gmail setup note. Don't imply testing a control grants permission to send actual outreach.

## D. Hook — 25 seconds, Day 24

**0–6s:** Generic “I love your company” draft. “What is the actual contribution?”

**6–17s:** A fictional proposed learning project tied to [Resend's documented transactional-email workflow](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/introduction). “A specific question is better than an invented achievement.” Label hypothetical, not completed work.

**17–25s:** “Use what you've really done. Review the appropriate contact route.” URL. No claim Resend is hiring or endorsed the tool.

Four assets plus two honest process posts fill the six-post calendar. Avoid fake inbox replies or celebratory job-offer screenshots as product proof.
