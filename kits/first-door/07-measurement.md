# FirstDoor — Measurement

## Baseline

[Public profile](https://shipped.money/p/first-door), checked 29 October 2026, showed **0 authored-source active days** for 21–27 October UTC, last sync 28 October 06:14 UTC. Clarity, Search clicks, net revenue and PostHog had **no eligible readings**, not zero. Actual accounts, drafts, sends, replies and retention are **not known**. No current-source activity doesn't prove no work elsewhere or no existing customers.

Keep the **1,000 B2C account target**. It measures users of FirstDoor, **not emails sent**, supported companies, published contacts or guessed founders. No target becomes a quota to message Resend, Sarvam AI or PostHog.

## Milestones and controls

Activation: a verified account researches one actually supported company, understands source/relationship/routing limits and reviews a relevant complete draft. A draft can activate learning without sending. Actual approved exact-version send, reply sync, real recipient reply, interview and job are distinct later outcomes and require independent evidence. No guaranteed hiring result follows from product use.

Track coarse aggregate signup, supported company selected, evidence inspected, draft reviewed, exact approval success/failure and voluntary return only under actual policy/consent. Sending metrics are optional operational data, never required for growth. No resumes/profile narratives, personal recipient addresses, email subjects/bodies/threads, Gmail tokens, WhatsApp numbers, private links or contact excerpts tied to people in growth events/replays. Owner tests controlled accounts only, never probe strangers' mailboxes to “verify” them.

Private ledger: date, product acquisition source, visits, verified accounts, first supported research/reviewed draft, evidence/approval errors, volunteered later use, opt-outs, hours/spend. Source tags `warm-career-research`, `owned-evidence`, `organizer-draft`, `peerlist`. Remove known overlap without fingerprinting. Don't combine Clarity sessions/PostHog views or publish student names/contact lists as receipts.

## Weekly decisions

**Day 7:** Can five voluntary testers explain official source versus relationship versus routing and review one draft? Five is a learning gate. If users expect any startup/automatic applications, fix scope before more invitations. If draft approval is ambiguous, repair control rather than celebrate generated-email count.

**Day 14:** Split account, research/source freshness, draft quality and exact-version approval failures. A Gmail connection isn't an approved send. If edits keep stale approval or retries can duplicate messages in controlled tests, pause sending promotion until fixed. Don't send real job email to diagnose it.

**Day 21:** Look for voluntary return to supported-company research. If no one sends, that isn't necessarily failure: the contact purpose or user's contribution may not be appropriate. Honor refusals; never optimize for messages at the expense of relevance. A team inbox isn't a founder endorsement.

**Day 28:** Review actual Gmail/Meta setup, privacy/deletion/permission terms and complaints. WhatsApp automation pending means zero automated-service success claims. Product acquisition and employer outreach remain separate; no CV or private-thread collection to strengthen a growth report.

**Day 30:** Report verified new accounts, researched/reviewed drafts, exact-control failures, eligible voluntary return, actual source contribution after overlap, hours/spend and gap to 1,000 accounts. 02 guesses only 3–14 activations without a clinic or 5–19 with it before overlap; no unaccepted launch/editor/directory traffic. If genuine later send/reply outcomes are supplied with consent, label separately with denominators; don't call a reply an interview or job. No fabricated placement rate or hiring partnership.
