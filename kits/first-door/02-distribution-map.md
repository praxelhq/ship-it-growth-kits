# FirstDoor — Distribution map

Separate two kinds of outreach: **acquiring users for FirstDoor** versus **users choosing to contact companies**. This calendar authorizes drafting growth materials, not sending job emails. Never turn the 1,000-account target into a quota of emails to Resend/Sarvam/PostHog.

## Ranked acquisition routes

**Twenty warm student/job-seeker invitations**, five each Days 5,12,19,26. Qualify people interested in at least one actually supported company and a credible contribution. Offer research/draft critique without connecting Gmail or sending anything, using 03 #1. No CV collection. One follow-up maximum.

**Six owned process posts**, Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Show fictional profile/source/relationship/routing distinctions and a user-reviewed draft. Use 03 #2/#3 and 05. Manual [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) posting only; no scraping candidates/founder emails, bulk DMs, automated job-message commenting or fake engagement. Product use doesn't grant permission to modify LinkedIn behavior.

**One conditional twelve-person supported-company research clinic**, Day 15. Ask a known career/project organizer Day 8 with 03 #4; use different actual participants from MyWorkFolio's adult document-pack session and Postifyy's voice-note post clinic. One organizer gets one primary product offer per week. No real CV, email send, Gmail OAuth or private contact disclosure required. Twelve is a proposed cap, not secured reach. If declined, individual draft tests and zero event contribution.

**Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad. Inspect current eligibility/slot/existing listing Day 18; optional Day 23 only after auth and exact-approval behavior owner-tested. No assumed slot/traffic or vote asks. Use 03 #6 with three-company limit explicit.

**Product Hunt**, https://www.producthunt.com/launch, optional Day 25 after working-product/existing-listing review. Real maker, no paid hunter/upvotes/unchanged duplicate launch. Don't promise interviews or employer endorsement.

**One introduced career-writing editor**, Day 22 with 03 #7, original evidence-before-outreach exercise. No guessed contacts/subscriber totals. Zero base traffic until accepted/published. Don't imply the supported startups sponsor this exercise.

**AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional verified-email factual platform/license entry. Free reviews may be indefinite, paid priority outside budget; zero traffic until accepted. No false application-automation category if the current workflow is reviewed conversation outreach.

## Forecast

Planning guesses: 20 warm invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12; × 25–50% account/first researched draft = 2–6 activations. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 10–25% account/first draft = about 1–8. Accepted twelve-person clinic × 60–80% attendance = 7–10; × 30–50% later account/first draft = about 2–5. Remove overlap.

Rough base 3–14 activations without the clinic, 5–19 with it before deduplication. Auth/approval failures reduce this; unaccepted editor/launch/directory traffic is zero in base. The 1,000 accounts target remains far away. No volume of job emails is assumed or required. Seek larger accepted learning partners only after evidence review and control work; don't “scale” by sending many job pitches to the same three companies.
