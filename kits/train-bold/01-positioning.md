# Train Bold — Positioning

**Live:** https://trainbold.fit/ · **Category:** Personal race-goal training log · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/train-bold

## Your preview is not an athlete's result

Actual 29 October 2026 homepage headline: **“YOUR RACE IS ONE DAY. YOUR TRAINING IS EVERY DAY.”** It describes race goals, training sessions and progress. The public `#app` route opened in Chrome with a prefilled HYROX campaign, sessions and milestones. **“LOG IN”** opened an **“ATHLETE ACCESS”** dialog with email/password, **“CREATE ACCOUNT”** and **“LAUNCH INSTANT ATHLETE DEMO MODE”**. No account, actual race, real training session, integration, export or payment was created/tested.

The landing terminal is labelled **“ILLUSTRATIVE TRAINING DATA”**. Its 68% progress, predicted finish, PRs and nutrition numbers are examples, not evidence of a real athlete improving. The actual app also opens with a prefilled campaign; do not count it as a user-created race. No numeric paid price was exposed, so do not invent a subscription or claim free lifetime access.

Start with an adult recreational endurance/hybrid athlete who already has a real event goal and an independent training plan, but wants the week's completed work organised around that event. The first use is entering their actual race date/goal and logging one real past session. Do not ask them to follow a new workload, chase a prediction, change fuel intake or train through a rest day to make a product demo.

**Pitch:** Put your race goal and actual sessions into one campaign, then review the work behind it.

**Bio:** Your next race. Your real sessions. A campaign you can revisit.

[Strava's US pricing](https://www.strava.com/pricing) lists US$11.99/month or $79.99/year plus taxes for its paid activity features. [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for a personal log. Train Bold needs to earn use through event-anchored organisation and useful milestones, not a claim of automatic Strava/Garmin sync or accurate finish prediction. Those integrations/accuracy were not established.

Keep **1,000 consumer signups**, with first real campaign plus one real logged session and return review beside it. PeakX's general workout logging and WhatNxt's next-movement choice are different use cases; use distinct contacts. Your honest weakness is trust: a convincing dashboard can make estimates look like validated coaching. Keep sample, user-entered facts and calculated suggestions visibly separate.
