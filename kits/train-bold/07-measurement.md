# Train Bold — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Count one genuine new non-test account. Public/default campaign views, demo launches, your tests, repeated logs and race-page visitors are not new accounts. If a real accountless personal mode is actually supported, apply the existing meaningful-session policy separately and deduplicate later sign-in; do not treat the illustrative demo as real adoption.

**Activation:** a real event goal created plus one real past session saved and reopened. Return review on another day is the next useful number. A predicted finish, fuel value or sample achievement is not a verified performance improvement. User-entered real facts, illustrative data and calculations need distinct labels in every report.

## Baseline

Record actual accounts, user-created campaigns, saved/reopened real sessions, returns, errors, actual sources and cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/train-bold. Public research did not establish private totals; unknown is not zero. Check existing Clarity/analytics before adding tags. Exclude health/body data, exact routes, private training notes, identities and account screens from replay/public logs. Aggregate events can show where saving or returning fails.

No finish-time accuracy, automatic third-party sync, race affiliation, training effectiveness or nutrition outcome was established. Reporting truthful organisation/use is enough; do not turn a sample dashboard into an athlete-success claim.
