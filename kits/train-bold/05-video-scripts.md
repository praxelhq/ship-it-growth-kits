# Train Bold — Video scripts

Record the actual interface on a phone. Public example metrics must retain an “Illustrative” label. Record a real personal campaign/session only after persistence works and only with content you choose to show; no private routes or body/health data.

## A. Connect the session — 40 seconds, homepage/group invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual goal/campaign view. | “Give the log a destination.” |
| 3–15 s | Actual verified goal entry or labelled example. | “Train Bold anchors the campaign to an event goal, then brings the sessions behind it into view.” |
| 15–28 s | Actual saved-session/review path, only after testing. | “Use training you already did. The first test is whether you can log it and find it again.” |
| 28–35 s | Example/prediction caveat. | “Sample metrics are examples, and estimates are not performance promises.” |
| 35–40 s | URL. | “Explore the workflow at trainbold.fit.” |

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
