# Train Bold — Landing-page fixes

## 1. Make actual adoption separate from the terminal

Current headline **“YOUR RACE IS ONE DAY. YOUR TRAINING IS EVERY DAY.”** Public `#app` opens a prefilled campaign; actual **“LOG IN”** opens account and demo options. Keep separate actions **“Explore an example campaign”** and **“Create your own race goal”**, wired to the real available paths. Privately verify account creation, a real goal, session save and reload before broad acquisition. An attractive public terminal is not proof of persistence.

## 2. Keep example labels everywhere

The landing says **“ILLUSTRATIVE TRAINING DATA”**. Preserve that on every metric, screenshot and export made from the example. Default app campaigns also need **“Example campaign — replace with your own event”**. The preview/app show differing example race dates: do not imply an official event date without checking the organiser. Let real users enter their confirmed event date.

## 3. Bound predictions and calculators

The page claims live finish predictions, fuel targets and automated benchmarks. Replace broad certainty with **“Calculated estimates from the information you enter. Review assumptions before using them.”** Explain required inputs, missing data and limitations. Do not imply validated finish accuracy, correct individual nutrition or personalised coaching from a sample. Rest/recovery days must not look like failed adoption or pressure to log extra work.

## 4. State real imports, exports and price

Strava/Garmin references describe fragmented data, not a verified integration. Show actual manual entry/import options and only claim connections that work in a real test. Check a real achievement-card export before demonstrating one. No numeric price was exposed in public research: publish the actual access/price terms if applicable instead of letting a login button imply a paid or permanent-free plan.

## 5. Phone and data

Check race-date/target inputs, unit/time formats, session edit/delete, rest-day display, save/reload and returning to the right campaign on a phone. Use harmless demo data for recordings; hide health/body details, private training locations and identity. Inspect existing Clarity/analytics and exclude account and sensitive athlete input from replay. No fake athlete testimonial or official race-series badge is needed.
