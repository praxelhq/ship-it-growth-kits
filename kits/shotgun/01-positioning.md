# shotgun — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.shotguns.in/ · **Category:** Existing-friend group planning · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/shotgun

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“Get your next plan out of the group chat.”** The CTAs are **“Create a Crew”** and **“Join a Crew.”** The creation route says **“Just your name. No login needed”** and offers a separate demo. No paid plan was visible in the inspected public pages; don't invent a subscription or free-forever promise.

Your first user is the friend who always chooses the venue, assigns jobs and reconstructs the bill for a group that already knows each other. Shotgun is not a stranger-discovery network, travel agent or payment processor.

**Pitch:** Put the place to a vote, share the jobs and keep the bill split in one crew plan.

**Short version:** Your existing friends. A decision, some roles, one clear split.

## The wedge

One real outing with four friends: at least two people vote, roles are assigned, and one actual bill is recorded afterward. Don't lead with a broad “all your memories forever” promise before the practical plan works. The live sample crew and ₹6,000 dinner are demonstrations, not traction.

[WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) offers free group messaging/polls, while [Splitwise](https://www.splitwise.com/) publicly describes a free tool for shared expenses. Those are real alternatives. Shotgun earns attention if it reduces the handoff between deciding, doing and splitting, not by pretending those tools do not exist.

## The uncomfortable bit

The public Create/Join routes were inspected, but no real crew, invite, vote or expense was submitted during research. A no-login flow needs a clear explanation of who can access a private crew link and how a person resumes their identity on another browser. Don't imply authentication or privacy controls not tested.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 first meaningful crew sessions, with activated real crews beside it. A crew creator refreshing five times isn't five people, and four people in one crew aren't four groups. Keep contacts separate from Crewup's board-game/study plans and Adda's Bengaluru-zone tests.
