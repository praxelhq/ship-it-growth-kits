# shotgun — Landing page fixes

Based on homepage and `/create-crew`, checked 29 October. No real crew or financial record was created during research.

## 1. Test the organizer-to-friend path

Current headline: **“Get your next plan out of the group chat.”** CTAs: **“Create a Crew”** and **“Join a Crew.”** Test fresh creation with fictional data, open the invite on a second phone, vote, assign a role, add a fictional bill and reopen. If the shared state fails, stop acquisition and repair it before a public clip.

## 2. Explain no-login identity and access

Creation says **“Just your name. No login needed.”** State who can see a crew link, what is stored, how a returning person is recognized and what clearing/switching browsers changes. Suggested share note: **“Send this crew link only to people you want in the plan.”** Don't imply account security that doesn't exist.

## 3. Make the practical three-part value immediate

Suggested support beneath the current headline: **“Vote on the place, share the jobs and keep the expense split in one plan.”** Primary CTA: **“Make a plan with my crew.”** Secondary: **“Try the fictional demo.”** Keep reputation titles secondary until the basic workflow works.

## 4. Label the sample crew and bill

The page already labels a demo crew. Carry **“Fictional sample—no real outing or transaction”** beside the example bill and in share previews. A ₹6,000 example isn't revenue, savings or a real expense processed by Shotgun.

## 5. Clarify split versus payment

Use **“Shotgun calculates who owes whom. It does not pay or collect the money.”** unless actual verified payment capability exists. Test uneven participation, a changed payer and rounding. Let users correct mistakes; don't overwrite an unexplained balance.

## 6. Check phone use during a plan

At 390px, test long venue names, keyboard overlap, vote change, role labels and a bill edit. Private names/bills should be excluded from replay. Preview: **“Shotgun — decide together, share roles, split clearly.”** Use synthetic names and no private invitation link.
