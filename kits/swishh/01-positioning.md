## The pitch

> Fix a short message where you're writing. Review the change; bring your original words back if it isn't right.

Bio: **A small fix, a different tone, your original words still yours.**

Free is **$0**, unlimited on-device fixes without an account. Free sign-in adds **10,000 AI characters/day**: rewrites count each character, AI fixes count one fifth. Roughly 50,000 AI-fix characters is the stated conversion, not 50,000 extra rewrite characters. The pricing FAQ still mentions “5 free writes”; reconcile that with the character-based promise before quota outreach.

Proposed paid plans show Plus **$6/month**, Pro **$14/month**, Max **$39/month**, and a planned **$5/500-credit** pack, but **paid plans/packs aren't open**. They are interest lists, not purchasable subscriptions. Don't forecast revenue or imply a student discount exists. Sponsored lines are labelled/planned or samples; don't present a sample sponsor as a customer/endorsement.

## Local and AI are different choices

Accessibility reads/replaces the invoked text. Optional Screen Recording helps position the visual effect, not a prerequisite to basic fixing. The [privacy policy](https://swishhit.com/privacy) says AI text goes through Swish/OpenRouter to Google/OpenAI and isn't retained/trained under their instructions. Operator claims aren't an independent audit. Turn **Smart grammar off** to keep Fix local; rewrites/translation are AI requests, not guaranteed offline.

The policy describes a non-content key-count for an optional reminder, random device hash, aggregate usage, account/credit data and ad events. “Never records keystrokes” doesn't mean no key-count or usage metadata exists. Advertisers don't receive the writing per policy, but the ad system still has placement/language/coarse-category/device records. Keep that boundary clear.

## Alternatives, release trust and target

[Grammarly's own plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) list a Free $0 tier; [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) has a Free tier for manual rewriting. Neither comparison proves identical permissions, in-place workflow or privacy. Existing manual review costs ₹0 extra software. Swish earns adoption with controlled in-place change and reliable undo, not a universal grammar-superiority claim.

The site says **Apple Developer ID signed and notarized**. Don't label it unsigned just because nearby Mac products are; owner verifies the actual build normally before inviting installs. Keep the **1,000 B2C install/account target**, with successful first reviewed fix and exact undo test alongside. Count free first launches separately from optional AI accounts; taps, rewritten characters and devices aren't distinct people.
