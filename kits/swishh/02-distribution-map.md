# Swish — Distribution map

Separate the audience from SnipText's image OCR and KnockKnock's window hiding. Swish is an in-place bilingual writing/review tool. Use different actual adult contact lists and organizers; one organization gets one primary product offer per week. No need to recruit everyone in the same Mac forum at once.

## Ranked routes

**Twenty qualified warm adult Mac invitations**, five each Days 5,12,19,26. Confirm macOS 13+ and offer a harmless short-message fix/undo test. Use 03 #1, no private work text or Accessibility-grant pressure. One follow-up maximum. Signing/notarization is site-claimed, still owner-verify actual delivery/permission/undo before public scale.

**Six owned demonstrations**, Days 6,10,16,20,24,29. Show original→reviewed fix→undo, then a separate opt-in AI/tone example. Use 03 #2/#3 and 05 clips. Manual [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) or maker-owned account; no scraping/bulk DMs/fake engagement. The quota and privacy boundary belong in the caption, not only the footer.

**One conditional ten-person bilingual writing exercise**, Day 15, with a known adult organizer asked Day 8 via 03 #4. Use fictional Hindi/English messages and maker's machine. No account, private text or install required to attend. Ten is a proposed cap, not secured reach. If declined, individual usability tests and zero event contribution.

**r/macapps**, https://www.reddit.com/r/macapps/comments/1ryaeex/rmacapps_mods_went_too_far_whats_changing_phase_3/. Inspect current pinned/sidebar rules, actual trust/transparency tier and developer 30-day history Day 18. Day 23 one submission only if permitted, correct main-feed/App Pile destination, maker disclosure and Problem/Comparison/Pricing. Notarization alone doesn't prove Tier 1; real portfolio/contact/Privacy/Terms and current moderation apply. Removed posts still count for developer frequency. No vote requests/alternate-account evasion.

**Peerlist**, https://peerlist.io/launchpad, optional Day 25 after current eligibility/slot/existing listing and working-flow review. No assumed slot/traffic. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is an optional later genuine working launch/iteration with maker account and no paid hunter/upvote asks/unchanged duplicate launch.

**One introduced bilingual-writing creator**, Day 22 with 03 #7, original harmless demo for critical consideration. No guessed contacts, audience totals, sponsor relationship or promised coverage. Zero forecast until accepted/published.

**AlternativeTo**, https://alternativeto.net/faq/, optional correct-platform/licence factual entry after verified email. Free review may be indefinite, paid priority outside budget; zero month-one traffic until accepted.

## Arithmetic

Planning assumptions only: 20 qualified invitations × 40–60% visit = 8–12; × 25–50% first install/reviewed fix = 2–6 activations. Six small-account posts × 100–500 impressions = 600–3,000; × 1% visit = 6–30; × 10–25% first install/fix = about 1–8. Accepted ten-person exercise × 60–80% attendance = 6–8; × 20–40% later install/fix = about 1–3. Remove overlap and don't equate optional AI accounts with all installs.

Rough base 3–14 activated installs without the event, 4–17 with it before deduplication. Unaccepted community/launch/creator/directory reach is zero in the base. Paid plans are closed, so paid conversion/revenue forecast is zero. Keep the 1,000 target and report the gap. A larger accepted workflow partner is worth testing after in-place edit/undo and consent boundaries work; repeated forum promotion isn't a reliable substitute.
