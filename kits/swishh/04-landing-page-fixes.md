# Swish — Landing-page fixes

Actual H1 **“just swish it.”**, support **“fix and rewrite, right where you write.”**, **download for mac** → `/downloads/Swish.dmg`; macOS 13+. Free $0/local fixes; 10,000 AI characters/day after sign-in; paid plans closed. No native edit/undo/install/account/AI call tested here. Ranked recommendations before expansion.

## 1. Make review and undo the first useful demonstration

Suggested support copy under the actual H1:

> Fix a short message where you're writing. Review what changed. Four taps restore your original if it isn't right.

Show a real harmless original→fix→exact undo in one continuous native recording. Owner tests punctuation, selection, line breaks, rich text, concurrent edits and unsupported app behavior. Don't promise universal application compatibility or exact restoration from a web animation. If a failed edit risks text loss, repair before more invitations.

## 2. Reconcile the allowance wording

Homepage/pricing headline says 10,000 AI characters/day, AI fixes count one fifth. Pricing FAQ still mentions “5 free writes.” Replace the stale unit with the actual implemented rule after owner verifies it:

> After free sign-in: 10,000 rewrite-character equivalents per day, reset at 00:00 UTC. Rewrites count every character; AI fixes count one fifth. Local fixes remain unlimited and don't need an account.

Use this only if exactly implemented. Show remaining allowance and actual reset, no invented rollover. Don't conflate daily free usage with future monthly paid credits or promise 50,000 rewrite characters.

## 3. Put processing choice beside the control

Suggested local/AI explanation:

> **Local Fix:** turn Smart grammar off to keep fixes on your Mac. **AI Fix/Rewrite/translation:** selected text is sent to our service/providers to return a result. Read the policy before invoking it on private writing.

The [policy](https://swishhit.com/privacy) names OpenRouter/Google/OpenAI and says processing text isn't retained/trained under instructions. Don't turn this into an independently audited guarantee or “no text leaves your Mac” for every mode. A local fix doesn't mean account/usage/ad metadata never exists.

## 4. Explain permissions without pressure

Suggested first-run copy:

> Accessibility lets Swish read and replace text when you invoke it. Screen Recording is optional for positioning the glow; you can skip it. Revoke permissions in System Settings anytime. Test with a harmless sentence first.

Site claims no password-field reading and no recorded keystrokes; policy also describes a non-content count for an optional reminder. Explain that number-only feature plainly rather than saying no keyboard-related signal exists. Test denial/revocation and actual supported fields; don't ask users to override security or approve everything blindly.

## 5. Keep upcoming prices out of checkout language

Pricing lists Plus $6/month, Pro $14/month, Max $39/month and planned $5/500-credit pack but says not open. Suggested card label:

> **Planned — not available to purchase.** Leave an email only if you want the one-time opening notice. Current Free is not a trial; on-device fixes remain free.

Don't forecast paid revenue or say cancellation/refund is tested when no checkout exists. Clarify current versus sample/planned sponsored line; no invented advertiser/customer endorsement or student discount.

## 6. Prove actual release and consent boundaries

The site says Developer ID signed/notarized; owner verifies the delivered build/publisher/version normally. Don't copy another kit's not-notarized warning. The policy says public GA/Clarity only after analytics acceptance, strict masking, no trackers on sign-in/account/payment/admin/sponsor pages. Verify accept/decline/no-action and no private demo text in events/replays; don't add duplicate scripts.

Suggested privacy summary:

> Your writing isn't given to advertisers. Usage/credit/device and sponsored-line records still exist under the policy. You can choose local fixing, switch off reminders and remove permissions.

Success is a first reviewed safe fix/undo and correct understanding, not maximal invoked characters or forced sign-ins.
