# Scoreyard — Positioning

Live: https://scoreyard.app/ · Other / B2C · [Course profile](https://shipped.money/p/scoreyard)

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique genuine creator/scorer accounts. Report accounts that score a real match and have its link viewed by an intended spectator beside signups. Anonymous spectators, players listed by an organizer and page refreshes are not registered creators. This small match-organizer plan does not substantiate 1,000 accounts.

Checked 29 October 2026: **“YOUR TOURNAMENT. ONE LINK.”** The homepage offers football quick matches, football tournaments and gully cricket. Spectators can watch without an account, but the actual [football quick-match route](https://scoreyard.app/quick-match?sport=football) says **“Sign in to continue”** so the match is saved and counts towards players' stats. No account, match, score update or spectator synchronization was tested. No public price was established; don't assume every feature is free forever.

First user: an adult organizer of an already agreed small amateur football fixture who currently relays score/fixture updates through chat. Start with one real match and one consenting scorer. This is distinct from finding players or organizing a citywide tournament: the game and participants already exist.

Pitch: “Run the match's score and fixtures through one link your group can follow.” Bio: “One real match. One scorer. One link.” The first useful result is a correct score visible on a second device at the right time, not a beautiful empty tournament.

[Challonge Standard](https://challonge.com/pricing) is $0, ad-supported, with tournaments up to 256 participants in the checked plan. Paid registration has separate fees; this kit recommends no paid event. An existing chat/score sheet is the practical low-friction alternative. Scoreyard must win through football/cricket-specific scoring and understandable spectator updates, if those work; it is not the first tournament tool.

The weakness is density and correctness: one wrong score shared widely damages trust faster than extra views help. Test corrections, accidental taps, interrupted connection and second-device viewing with fictional teams before a real match. Do not imply officiating authority, automatic verified player statistics or betting facilities. Use adult fixtures first and keep player/contact privacy explicit.
