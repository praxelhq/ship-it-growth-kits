# Scoreyard — Video scripts

Use fictional adult teams and your own devices. No unconsented player photos, contact details or paid editor.

**A. Two devices, one result, 45 seconds; owned profile.** Hook: “The score has to be right on the other phone.” Show creator sign-in boundary, then your authorized test account making a synthetic match. Record an actual score update and second-device view. Deliberately correct a fictional mistake and show the actual result. Say, “A correction should reach the spectator without another message.” End with URL and synthetic label. If it fails, show the failure and fix before real-match invitations.

**B. Watch without signup, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “The spectator shouldn't need another account.” Show actual public spectator path only after testing it, with no private fixture. Say, “The organizer saves the match. The group follows the link.” End: “Views and creator accounts are different numbers.”

**C. The wrong tap, 20 seconds; accepted club bulletin.** Hook: “What if the scorer taps twice?” Show a synthetic scoring error and actual correction flow. Say, “I test mistakes before a real match, not only the perfect demo.” Keep the true outcome; don't animate a recovery that isn't built.

**D. No fixture invention, 15 seconds; owned post.** Hook: “A tournament tool doesn't supply a tournament.” Show a clearly synthetic fixture card, then the existing organizer invitation text. Say, “Start with an actual agreed match and a willing scorer. The software is one part of running it.” No fabricated club badge or partnership.
