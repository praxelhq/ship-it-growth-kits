# Scoreyard — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, defined as unique genuine creator/scorer accounts. Activation beside it: **an account scores a real match and the correct link is viewed by an intended spectator**. Anonymous spectator use is important but separate. Do not relabel views, rostered players or match count as registered creators to bridge the target gap.

Day-0 actual accounts, scored matches, spectator sessions, leaderboard and analytics are unknown. Empty on-this-device history is not a platform baseline. Record authorized aggregate counts with dates. [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) setup and privacy handling were not audited; keep account details, player/contact records and private fixture information out of replay/events. No public list of student players belongs in this kit.

Track invitation source→creator account→match created→real score saved→spectator link opened→correction acknowledged→next voluntary fixture. Separate synthetic tests and real matches. A spectator page load does not prove the score was current; test last-update/correction visibility. Deduplicate creator retries and accounts where possible without collecting unnecessary player data.

`06` schedules ten organizer notes, two accepted club bulletins and three owned posts. `02` guesses 5–16 creator accounts before overlap, perhaps 2–7 real-match activations. It does not support 1,000. At a hypothetical 20% qualified-visit→creator-account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 qualified unique organizer visits. Player/fan reach cannot be substituted into that creator conversion assumption. Optional launch results remain zero until observed.

W1: scoring/synchronization errors pause real-fixture promotion. W2: if accounts make only test matches, ask whether actual organizers need the tool and support one agreed fixture. W3: if every scorer needs you, simplify correction/resume before expanding. W4: grow the source producing correct real-match use, or report the gap. No payments, player-stat verification or officiating result is inferred from account creation or a published score.
