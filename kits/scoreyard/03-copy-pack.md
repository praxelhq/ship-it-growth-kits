“Do you already have an adult football fixture arranged? I built Scoreyard to keep scores and fixtures on a link the group can follow. No player list or club endorsement needed. Can I send the public page?”

“May I share a small scoring-tool test in your adult sports circle? It is for an existing fixture, not player recruitment or a paid competition. I built it and will post once only if approved.”

First check a fictional score and correction on two devices. Actual product limits are on https://scoreyard.app/. I'm the builder. No need to publish player contact details or sign up just to watch.”

**3. Owned post**

“A tournament page isn't useful if the score on the spectator's phone is wrong. I'm testing Scoreyard around one small loop: create a match, score it, correct a mistake, then check what a second device sees. Real amateur fixtures already have enough coordination work. The tool should reduce score questions, not create another group everyone has to join. https://scoreyard.app/”

**4. Maker listing**

“Scoreyard gives amateur football/gully-cricket organizers a match/tournament link. Creators sign in so work is saved; spectators watch without accounts. I made it and want feedback on scoring, corrections and actual spectator visibility. Use fictional teams when testing. No verified competition, automatic officiating, betting or player-performance certification is claimed.”
