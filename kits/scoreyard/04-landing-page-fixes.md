# Scoreyard — Landing page fixes

Observed 29 October 2026: **“YOUR TOURNAMENT. ONE LINK.”** Football quick-match creation opens **“Sign in to continue”**, with account saving/player-stat explanation. No match, signup, synchronization or payment was tested; price was not established.

1. **Explain both sides before auth.** Supporting replacement **“One organizer signs in to create and score. Your group follows the match link without an account.”** CTA **“Create a football match”** with **“Sign-in required to save”** beside it. Do not let a spectator think they must register merely to watch.
2. **Show a synthetic complete match.** Add **“See an example score link”** leading to an actual accessible synthetic result, labelled throughout. Show fixture, score and last update, not fake live teams or attendance. Only claim live synchronization after the two-device test succeeds.
3. **Make mistakes recoverable.** Scorer copy **“Check the teams and score before publishing.”** Correction confirmation **“Corrected score saved”** only after persistence. Offline/failure copy **“This update hasn't saved. The shared score may be out of date.”** Test accidental tap, correction, refresh and connection loss before a real fixture.
4. **Clarify player records and permission.** Current auth explanation mentions player stats. State who enters them and whether they are self/organizer reported. Never imply independently verified ability. Let organizers avoid unnecessary identifiers; establish visibility and correction/removal routes before publishing real player names. No private phone numbers in match previews.
5. **State actual access and cost.** No price was publicly verified. Put any true free/trial/payment limits before creating an event. A real future charge needs a billing period and terms; don't invent a permanent free plan. No paid tournament or prize pool is part of this acquisition plan.
6. **Mobile and preview.** Title **“Scoreyard — one score link for your amateur match”**. Description **“Organizers create and score; spectators follow without an account.”** Test glare/readability, one-handed score entry, large controls and browser resume. Never put an unverified result or private roster in shared-link metadata. A fresh-device empty match history is not zero platform usage.
