# Scoreyard — Distribution map

**Existing match organizers first.** No fixture or audience is invented for the calendar. Estimates are hypotheses for ten organizer invitations, two permissioned club bulletins and three owned posts.

| Route | Rule and action | Assumed result / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ten known adult fixture organizers/scorers | Five personal notes Days 6/10. Ask about an already scheduled match, not permission to recruit its players. | 3–6 accounts, 1–3 real scored matches; #1. |
| Two existing adult sports circles | Organizer approves one bulletin each in different weeks/groups. No scraped phone lists, unsolicited player additions or public room codes. | Combined 40–120 relevant views, 2–6 creator accounts; #2. |
| Your [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) profile | Three original demos with synthetic teams. [Basic is free](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a545596). Ownership disclosed; no invented club endorsement. | Combined 60–240 impressions, 0–4 creator accounts; #3. |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Optional free usable-product submission after actual scorer→spectator test. Check current slot/prior listing; no vote requests. | 0–3 creator accounts, excluded from base; #4. |

Base: 5–16 creator accounts before overlap, perhaps 2–7 actually scored matches. Spectator views are a separate distribution/usefulness metric, never an assumption that every viewer signs up. This plan cannot support 1,000 creators from one small fixture.

[Challonge](https://challonge.com/pricing) is a verified alternative, not an advertising venue. No public sports community, federation partnership or newsletter slot was verified. A club's own bulletin can carry #2 after permission. Coordinate fixtures with the separate Mezzala/fan-tool lanes: different organizers or weeks; no multiple app pitches to one team. If a fixture is cancelled, support existing users instead of fabricating an event.
