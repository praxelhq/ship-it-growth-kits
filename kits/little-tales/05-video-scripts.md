# Little Tales — Video scripts

Record on a phone with staged content. The first script is usable before entry verification; the other two require a real demonstrated save/return path. Do not film private memories, email addresses or another person's face.

## A. The promise — 30 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | A cup on a table. “No audience needed.” | “This did not need an audience.” |
| 3–13 s | Little Tales homepage. | “I’m building a private scrapbook for ordinary moments. No feed and no followers.” |
| 13–23 s | Point to the actual entry after checking it. | “I want the first step to feel simple. I’m testing whether it is clear where to begin.” |
| 23–30 s | URL; no invented editor. | “Try the doorway at littletales.me. Keep your memories to yourself.” |

## B. One little moment — 20 seconds, Reel

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Staged example stamp. | “A small moment still counts.” |
| 3–13 s | Actual verified create/save steps. | “Start with one thing you want to remember. It does not have to become a public post.” |
| 13–20 s | Finished harmless stamp and URL. | “That is the idea behind Little Tales. A private scrapbook.” |

## C. Tomorrow — 20 seconds, return-use clip

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Return to a real saved example. | “Would you come back tomorrow?” |
| 3–14 s | Reopen the example at normal speed. | “Saving a moment is one step. I want it to be easy to find again, too.” |
| 14–20 s | URL and honest trial pointer. | “Try Little Tales. Read the current trial terms first.” |

Hold B/C if those functions are unavailable. A screen recording of a real limitation is more useful to the build than a staged success.
