## The first user

An adult who wants to keep one ordinary personal moment without posting it to followers. They already take notes or photos but dislike turning every memory into a public update. Start with a single day's memory, not a complete life archive or therapeutic journaling promise.

**Pitch:** A private scrapbook for little everyday moments, with no social feed.

**Bio:** Little moments. A private scrapbook. No followers.

Keep your assigned **1,000 consumer signups**. A page visitor or a promise to try later is not an account. Activation should be a first saved memory reopened later, once you verify that the product supports that path.

[Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) offers a Basic plan at $0/year. [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing) has a $0 personal Free plan. A paper notebook is also a real alternative. Your postage-stamp presentation and lack of feed are the reasons to try Little Tales; a claimed security advantage or cheaper paid plan is not yet established. “Private” needs a readable explanation of storage, access and deletion before people trust personal memories to it.
