# Little Tales — Copy pack

Use the research invitation now. Use the product invitations only after a real start/save path is verified. Never ask someone to send their private memory back to you.

## 1. Personal invitation

> I’m working on Little Tales, a private scrapbook for small everyday moments. No feed and no followers.
>
> Would you try opening it on your phone and tell me whether it is clear how to begin? You can use an invented moment; I only need feedback on the doorway. https://littletales.me/

After the doorway works, change the middle sentence to: “Would you keep one ordinary moment in it and see whether you want to return tomorrow?” Describe the verified trial terms before inviting signup.

## 3. Maker post

> Not every memory needs a post.
>
> I’m building Little Tales around that idea: a private scrapbook, with each day presented like a little postage stamp.
>
> I want the first step to be as simple as the promise. If you try it, tell me where the page feels unclear. Please keep your actual memories private. https://littletales.me/

## 4. Organizer request

> Could I offer a short private-memory exercise to adults in your creative group? I’m building Little Tales and would bring a harmless example, then let people decide whether to try it themselves.
>
> Nobody would need to share a personal entry, sign up during the session or post a result. I’d like your permission before sending any invitation.

## 5. Launch listing

**Tagline:** A private scrapbook for little everyday moments.

Little Tales gives everyday memories a postage-stamp scrapbook presentation, without a social feed or follower count. Start with an ordinary moment. Check the current trial and storage terms before adding anything personal. https://littletales.me/

Use the listing only when its actual entry works. For repeats, choose another harmless moment and show a different verified interaction; do not reissue the same pitch to the same people.
