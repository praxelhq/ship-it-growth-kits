# Little Tales — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days.** Keep this assigned target. Record what the actual product calls an account before counting. Anonymous visits, research volunteers and a private saved memory are different events. Activation: first memory saved and later reopened, conditional on verified product support. Do not add a forced account wall just to make the target easier to count.

## Day 0

Read current account counts, landing sessions, existing saved/reopened-memory events and the cohort standing at https://shipped.money/p/little-tales. These private values were not visible in public research; do not assume zero. Record the period and exclude your tests.

Check whether Clarity or another analytics tool already exists. Keep scrapbook content, photos, identity and account screens out of replay. Prefer aggregate entry-click, account-created, first-save and return counts. Masking is not permission to collect someone's private memory. If storage is local-only, explain the cross-device measurement limits instead of fingerprinting.

| Day 30 | 4 maker posts total plus any real listing. | Report unique signups, activated people, returns and gap to target. |

Expected accounts from this small plan are below 1,000. No launch slot or group attendance is guaranteed. A page click is not consent to store memories, and a signup is not proof that the private scrapbook is useful. Report those distinctions plainly.
