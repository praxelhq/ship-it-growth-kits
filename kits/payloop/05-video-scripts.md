# PayLoop — Video scripts

Use fictional invoices, your own voice and screen recording. No real client names, account numbers, messages or bank images. Label sample money and shortened waits.

## A. The balance before the reminder — 65 seconds

0–3 s: fictional invoice beside a payment. **“Are you reminding them for the right amount?”**

3–18 s: actual sample/verified internal record with ₹10,000 original and ₹4,000 payment. “Check the payment in your own records first.”

18–32 s: show the correct ₹6,000 balance and previous contact. “A part-payment changes the conversation.” Leave the actual correction uncut if supported.

32–47 s: generate or open a real draft; inspect amount, date and tone. “The draft is yours to edit.” If generation isn't verified, label the public sample instead.

47–57 s: manual copy/handoff, not fake delivery. “PayLoop does not message the client automatically.”

57–65 s: free allowance and URL. “Ten unpaid invoices, five AI drafts a month free. payloop.co.in.”

## B. Context hook — 20 seconds

0–3 s: sample last-contact log. **“They replied yesterday. Does your next reminder know?”**

3–13 s: inspect a permitted fictional contact note and draft. “Read the history before choosing the next message.”

13–20 s: edit and URL. “Keep the follow-up human.” No invented client reply in a claimed case study.

## C. Manual-control hook — 25 seconds

0–3 s: unsent draft. **“Drafted is not sent.”**

3–16 s: actual review/edit/copy controls. “Check the balance, agreed terms and tone. You choose the channel and timing.”

16–25 s: current manual-sending disclosure and URL. “No automatic client messages.” Never stage delivery/read receipts.
