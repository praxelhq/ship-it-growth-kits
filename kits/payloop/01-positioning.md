# PayLoop — Positioning

**Live:** https://payloop.co.in/ · **Category:** Invoice follow-up · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/payloop

Checked 29 October 2026: [home](https://payloop.co.in/) uses **“PayLoop — Get paid on time, without chasing clients”** in its actual page heading. Public links include **“Try the demo”** to `/demo` and **“Sign up free”** to `/login`. [Pricing](https://payloop.co.in/pricing) confirms Free: up to ten unpaid invoices, five AI reminder drafts/month; Pro ₹299/month or ₹2,999/year in India, $6/month or $60/year elsewhere, 100 AI drafts/month and unlimited invoices. Public offer, not a tested account or checkout.

Your first ten are small independent design-service businesses with three to ten currently unpaid client invoices. The owner knows the client and original agreed terms. Start with one invoice, a part-payment and the next appropriate reminder. Do not target people seeking debt enforcement or automatic bulk collection.

**Pitch: See what is actually unpaid, review the next reminder and send it yourself.**

Short version: **The balance and the last follow-up, together.**

PayLoop describes part-payments, ageing, follow-up logs, manual WhatsApp/email drafts and CSV export. It explicitly never messages clients itself. Keep that manual control. A draft's Friendly/Professional/Firm/Final tone is a writing choice, not proof a debt is enforceable or that a final warning is appropriate. No legal threats, invented penalties or recovery guarantee.

The zero-extra-tool alternative is an owner's existing invoice file plus a free [Google Sheet](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) with original amount, verified payments, remaining balance and last contact. PayLoop must improve reconciliation and next-action clarity. It is not accounting software, GST invoice creation, a payment processor or a substitute for the owner's ledger.

Before real use, test a fictional invoice, partial payment, corrected payment, draft editing and export. Confirm balances against the owner's actual records; do not infer bank receipt from a reminder click or payment screenshot. No live client contact, account or payment was submitted during research.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count the service business using PayLoop, not its debtors, invoices or recovered amounts. Each must onboard its own permitted records, use independently within seven days and be privately contactable by responsible owner name/phone. Free-active businesses can count.
