# PayLoop — Landing-page fixes

Read [home](https://payloop.co.in/) and [pricing](https://payloop.co.in/pricing), 29 October 2026. Actual heading **“PayLoop — Get paid on time, without chasing clients”**; links **“Try the demo”** and **“Sign up free.”** Free ten unpaid invoices/five AI drafts monthly; Pro ₹299/month or ₹2,999/year India, $6/month or $60/year elsewhere. Authenticated record handling and billing remain untested.

1. **Replace the outcome guarantee with the controllable job.** Current headline can imply on-time payment. Replacement: **“Keep unpaid balances and the next follow-up clear.”** Support: **“Review recorded payments, last contact and a reminder draft. You decide when to send.”** No guaranteed collection or time saved.
2. **Keep manual sending beside the CTA.** Current page says it never messages clients. Replacement strip: **“PayLoop drafts. You review and send through your own WhatsApp or email.”** Verify buttons and draft states don't imply automated delivery or tracking.
3. **Show part-payment working.** Add a labelled fictional example: **“Invoice ₹10,000 · verified payment ₹4,000 · remaining ₹6,000.”** Test correction/reversal, rounding and export. A recorded payment is entered data, not independent bank verification.
4. **Make the allowance precise.** Replacement: **“Free: up to ten currently unpaid invoices, five AI reminder drafts per month. Templates, reports and CSV export included.”** Confirm when paid invoices stop consuming the limit, reset timing and failed drafts. Do not invent unlimited AI or monthly invoice deletion.
5. **Explain tone responsibly.** Current urgency levels include Final. Replacement helper: **“Choose a tone appropriate to your agreed terms and relationship. Review every amount, date and claim before sending.”** No invented legal action, fees or entitlement. Preserve an editable draft before copying.
6. **Put actual billing terms at upgrade.** Public pricing says cancel anytime, paid-period access remains, new subscriptions refundable within seven days. Show the actual currency, period, taxes and refund terms at checkout. Do not turn ₹2,999/year into a ₹299 monthly charge or invent GST treatment.
7. **Protect private finance on phones.** Test account→fictional invoice→payment→draft→manual handoff→CSV on a fresh phone. Check deletion, access isolation and failed generation. Mask client identity, balances, free text and payment screens in replay. The demo does not prove signed-in storage or sending.
