# PayLoop — Distribution map

Start with design-service owners already reconciling invoices. Keep this manual post-invoice follow-up distinct from FirmQuote's pre-work scope acceptance and Booked's creator-deliverable planning. Use different owners, not repeated invitations to one freelance organizer.

| Route | Action and permission | Input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing small design-service businesses | Qualify current unpaid invoices and an owner able to check the underlying records. Use an actual business contact honestly. | Ten blocks of four owners = 40 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two accountant/owner introductions | Ask a known contact to forward a demo invitation; no debtor list, client invoices or contact details without permission. | Days 5,16; #4. Upside only. |
| Own LinkedIn | Show a fictional part-payment and a reviewed reminder. Do not expose a real debtor or celebrate pressure tactics. | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST; #3. Assumed 100–400 impressions and 0–2 relevant visits/post, excluded from base. |
| One private reconciliation review | Owner compares an authorized record with its ledger and edits the reminder before manual sending. | Day 12, conditional; #5. Supports one existing trial. |

Base assumptions: 40 approaches × 50% substantive conversations × 70% own-record setup × 70% independent recent use = 9.8, about ten businesses. At 25% conversation, 4.9. These are demanding guesses, not observed recovery benchmarks. Blocks: Days 4,5,8,10,12,16,18,23,25,29. Posts/intros add no guaranteed customers; deduplicate firms.

No freelancer newsletter feature or professional-group permission is established. Ask a known organizer before any group invitation. A public accountant directory is not permission to send client files. No automated DMs, purchased debt lists, paid listings or contacting a debtor to acquire its supplier as a software customer. One relevant follow-up after seven days; stop after a decline.

Confirm current allowance, actual billing currency and cancellation before an owner upgrades. The free ten-invoice limit is unpaid invoices, not a reason to delete accurate payment history or split one firm into fake accounts.
