# Family Sub savee — Video scripts

Fictional services, amounts and households only. Don't show a bank statement, account password, real family member or payment reference. Record the actual tested product; label proposed sample features rather than pretending they're live.

## A. Before the next renewal —60 seconds

**Use:** public sample page and organizer introduction. **Hook:** “Who is paying for this one?”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|Fictional service row with payer unknown|Who is paying for this one?|
|2–14 s|Three fictional inventory items|A renewal list needs the payer, billing period and a person to review it.|
|14–30 s|Actual tested manual entry/save|I'm using made-up amounts, not a bank statement.|
|30–43 s|Review date and actual household access explanation|The tool organizes a conversation; it doesn't decide what every family member values.|
|43–52 s|A potential-review item clearly labelled|Potential saving isn't money saved. We still choose what to do.|
|52–60 s|URL and actual access terms|Inspect the sample and data terms at familysubsaver.live before entering real records.|

If the inventory/save loop isn't working, make this a labelled manual worksheet lesson, not a false live demo.

## B. Annual isn't monthly —20 seconds

**Where:** owned feed. **Hook:** “These two amounts aren't comparable yet.” Show fictional₹1,200/year versus₹120/month with clear annual/monthly normalization. Say, “Use the same period before comparing, then check what the household actually needs.” No real provider-price recommendation or guaranteed savings claim.

## C. Review doesn't mean cancel —25 seconds

**Where:** group session and social post. **Hook:** “Two subscriptions doesn't automatically mean waste.” Show two fictional household members with different uses, no names. Say, “Ask who uses the service and what would change if it ended. Put a review owner next to the renewal.” End with the manual worksheet and founder disclosure.

## D. What will your household member see? — 30 seconds

**Where:** permission explanation. **Hook:** “Check access before sending an invitation.” Use authorized test accounts and synthetic records to demonstrate actual permission boundaries. If not tested, say that feature is still being verified and don't invite public use. End with the implemented data controls, not a vague security slogan.

Bank fictional entry, period normalization, review-date edit and actual permission confirmation clips. No staged rupee-savings testimonial or live account import is required.
