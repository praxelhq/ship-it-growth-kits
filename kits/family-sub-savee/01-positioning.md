# Family Sub savee — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.familysubsaver.live/ · **Category:** Consumer household organization · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/family-sub-savee

**Your public acquisition page currently asks people to log in before explaining the value.** A normal request returns 200 and redirects to `/login`; it is not a dead site. The actual H1 is “Welcome back,” the text says “Sign in to manage your household subscriptions securely,” and the controls are “Sign in” and “Create an account.” Public navigation exposes Subscriptions, Savings, Import and Household, but those labels do not prove the authenticated workflows work. No public software price was readable and none is invented here.

The site's metadata describes an Indian family subscription organizer. This kit uses that direction provisionally and makes the first-week authenticated product check explicit. No account, bank connection, import, cancellation or savings action was performed by the writer.

## Your first user

An adult coordinating a household's recurring entertainment, software and membership bills who wants one inventory before the next renewal. They may not know which family member pays for each item. The first job is to record a service, payer, cost period and next renewal, then decide what to review. It is not account-password sharing or a way around service household restrictions.

## The pitch

> Put your household's subscriptions and renewal questions in one place. See who pays, when each item renews and what you want to review next.

Bio version: **A shared bill inventory before another renewal surprises you.**

Only use this as a live promise after testing the corresponding fields and household permissions. If the current app can't support it, fix the narrow loop rather than advertise an entire automated finance platform from navigation labels.

## Alternatives

A paper list or local spreadsheet costs₹0 in extra subscription-manager software. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) is a real collaborative spreadsheet alternative. [Rocket Money](https://www.rocketmoney.com/) publicly describes a free plan and paid premium features; the checked homepage doesn't publish a current premium amount or establish Indian-bank support. Don't invent a local price or claim its automatic cancellation features are yours.

Your difference should be an understandable household inventory and renewal review with clear access rules. You must beat “I'll ask everyone in the family chat” without making people upload financial statements just to inspect the tool.

## The honest weakness

“Savings” is not money saved. A duplicate-looking service may be deliberately used by different people, and stopping a renewal can have consequences. Present review suggestions, not guaranteed savings or automatic cancellations unless those actions truly exist and are authorized. A subscription's terms, allowed household use and current price must come from its provider.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-account target**. Activation is one actual household inventory with at least three non-sensitive items and a renewal reviewed by the user. A login, import button or suggested saving isn't the same outcome. The current login-first page needs to earn trust before a large account push makes sense.
