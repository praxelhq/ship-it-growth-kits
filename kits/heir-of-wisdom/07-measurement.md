# Heir of Wisdom — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real **parent accounts**, excluding tests. Child profiles aren't additional signups/customers; the product says children never create email accounts.

Report **families completing a first real guided lesson** beside parent accounts, then later voluntary practice. Adult demo solves are useful usability evidence but not a child's learning activity or family activation. No score, percentile, intelligence or admission prediction is inferred.

## Day 0

Record actual existing parent accounts, consented real-family first lessons and later-session counts from your own system; private numbers were unavailable in research. Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/heir-of-wisdom) reading, date window and rank. A public demo's zero progress isn't the real-user baseline.

The [privacy policy](https://www.hierofwisdom.me/privacy) restricts [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) to logged-out public marketing pages and excludes demo, Studio, signup and parent areas. Verify this exclusion; do not install session replay on child tasks for growth analysis. Use only minimum private aggregate parent/lesson counts under the actual consent design, never child names, profiles, reflections or answers in public reporting.

## Honest funnel and decisions

Ten private parent invitations, one eight-parent demo, four feed posts and six Shorts support a small cohort. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→parent-account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 visits. The calendar doesn't prove that reach. A free early-access account is not a paid Family subscriber; the $14 tier is currently coming later.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Adult demo understanding, real parent accounts, control issues|Unclear parent consent/access stops expansion before any child-data intake.|
|Day 14|Real first guided lessons versus adult demo attempts|Never merge the two into a flattering activation number.|
|Day 21|Later family practice, hints/repeats and parent questions|Report what was observed; don't extrapolate a learning or admissions outcome.|
|Day 30|Parent accounts, first real lessons, returners, actual reach and unresolved limits|State the gap to 1,000 without fear-based claims or public child comparisons.|

Keep weekly aggregate actual rows with source and hours. One household with several child profiles is one parent account; a correctly turned demo flag is one tested interface action, not proof the curriculum is validated.
