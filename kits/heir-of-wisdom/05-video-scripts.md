# Heir of Wisdom — Video scripts

Adults operate the public demo. No child face, voice, name, profile or private work. Use original product shapes, not proprietary test questions. No paid effects.

## A. The rule is the point — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “Don't just pick the answer. Show what changed.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Public flag goal and original shape|“This is a no-signup demo, operated by an adult.”|
|10–25s|One actual turn and explanatory match|“The flag turns 90° clockwise. The explanation should name the move.”|
|25–40s|Undo/replay or another real original puzzle only after tested|“The next question is whether the rule works somewhere new.”|
|40–52s|Actual coverage/parent-control page|“This is a limited visual curriculum, not an admission prediction.”|
|52–60s|URL and free-early-access terms|“Try the demo before choosing a parent account.”|

Only show working actions. The single tested demo turn is not evidence of child learning or the full quest's reliability.

## B. Hint isn't independent success — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Getting help and doing it alone are different evidence.” Show an original labelled example of a hinted move and an untouched new puzzle. Say: “Parents need to see the difference, not a flattering score.” Use synthetic examples; no child's error exposed.

## C. What isn't covered — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A visual puzzle isn't the whole test.” Show actual curriculum exclusions. Say: “The product teaches visual-rule foundations, not every verbal or quantitative task, official timing or scoring.” Keep test-publisher independence visible. No outcome guarantee.

## D. Parent first — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Children don't need to create email accounts here.” Show the public demo and parent-signup/data explanation, without submitting a form. Say: “A parent chooses whether to save progress. Test with the demo before sharing any child information.” Do not claim the kit independently audited the controls.
