# Heir of Wisdom — Distribution map

**Demonstrate to parents, with original shapes and no child data.** Don't advertise directly to children, request school contact lists or promise admissions results.

|Route|Action and boundary|Planning outcome, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Ten parents who voluntarily express interest|Five private permission-first invitations Days 4/11. Offer public demo before parent account; no child's name/photo/score requested.|10 × 50% demo × 40% parent account = 2 accounts.|#1|
|Existing parent/community organizer|One optional eight-parent demo on Day 18 at 18:00 IST. Adults can try the shapes; children need not attend or be filmed.|8 invites × 60% attend × 40% account = about 2 accounts; zero until permission.|#2|
|Your LinkedIn feed|Four original posts Days 8/15/22/29 at 09:00, explaining the shape rule, scope and evidence limits.|4 × assumed 200 impressions × 2% visits × 20% account = about 3 accounts; zero possible.|#3|
|Your own YouTube channel|Six original adult-operated puzzle clips plus one demo, under [YouTube policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/). No child faces/data or copied test items.|6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits.|#4,videos|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Parent-focused working demo, original items and clear free-early-access terms. Current slot checked; no votes or academic endorsement requested.|0–10 adult testers, optional upside.|#5|

[Testing Mom's official page](https://www.testingmom.com/buy-now/) is a verified alternative and source of parent questions, not a place to solicit its customers. A willing organizer bulletin can carry #2, but no school endorsement, parent mailing list or external newsletter placement is assumed. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after parent controls and first lesson work; personal maker account and no upvote requests.

The initial plan supports a small family cohort. At an assumed 20% qualified-visit→parent-account rate, 1,000 needs 5,000 visits. The calendar does not establish that reach. Scale a useful parent explanation and voluntary referral after real use, not a child's public score or fear of missing admission.
