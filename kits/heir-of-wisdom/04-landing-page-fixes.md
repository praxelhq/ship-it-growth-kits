# Heir of Wisdom — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026: home, public shape demo, demo Studio, pricing, curriculum and privacy. One adult shape turn matched the goal with explanatory feedback. No child profile, parent signup, PIN, private report or payment was tested.

1. **Lead with the rule, not superiority.** Current H1 “Teach your child to see what others miss” can become **“Turn the shape. Discover the rule. Use it on a new puzzle.”** Supporting line **“Original visual-reasoning lessons for ages 8–10, under a parent's account.”** Keep **“Try a lesson, no sign-up”** primary, then **“Create a free parent account.”** The working interactive board is the focal proof.

2. **Keep early-access pricing unmistakable.** Current pricing is $0 now, Family $14/month coming soon. Add **“Every current lesson is free during early access. A paid family plan will require your choice later.”** Do not put a future feature/price in a current checkout or imply a parent will be auto-charged.

3. **Describe scope before test names.** Curriculum has 13 lessons, 10 quest puzzles and 31 original practice items across four skill families, with substantial exclusions. Link **“See exactly what is covered”** near the test-prep mention. Preserve no affiliation/official-score/admission disclaimer. Don't imply a visual-rule game covers verbal or quantitative batteries.

4. **Make evidence in the report modest.** Show original rule, new-item attempt, hints and number of observations. Copy **“A provisional picture from the work completed—not an official score or prediction.”** A solved demo shape or prompted repeat is not proof a child will generalize. No fake parent testimonial or comparative child ranking.

5. **Explain parent control before registration.** Privacy says parent-owned accounts, no child email, minimal profile fields, no child chat/public profile and parent deletion controls. Link that explanation before any child data. Test actual consent/PIN/access/deletion behavior with authorized synthetic profiles; a policy statement is not a security audit. No child's full name, birthday, image, voice or location is needed for a marketing test.

6. **Keep demo and real progress separate.** Public demo says nothing is saved/collected; account mode saves learning activity with parent consent. Label **“Demo — no saved child progress”** and **“Parent account — read the data choices before saving.”** Do not treat demo actions as family activation or silently record child data through an analytics tool.

7. **Verify accessibility and preview.** Title **“Heir of Wisdom — discover visual rules with original shapes”**; description **“For ages 8–10 under parent control. Try a no-signup demo; current lessons free during early access.”** Test keyboard turn/flip, visible goal, touch targets, undo and readable feedback. Keep analytics out of child/demo/signup/parent surfaces according to the stated design and verify implementation before claiming compliance.
