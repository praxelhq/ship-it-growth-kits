# CaseCrack — Distribution map

**Read this first:** you are selling a **daily habit**, not a tool, and that changes which channels work. Habit products are won in places where someone shows up repeatedly — Discord servers, a streak you can post, a community that notices you were there yesterday — and lost in places you post once and leave.

It also means your single best growth mechanic isn't a channel at all. It's **a public daily streak**, and `03` #2 is built on it.

**Your launch window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 13–17.** Nine cohort-mates are posting into these same communities this month; the stagger exists so none of you gets the category banned. **Don't jump the queue in r/MBA.**

Reach estimates state their basis. **Verify member counts before planning around them.**

## Tier 1 — Where a habit gets built 🔴

### 1. Discord servers — your best channel, and the cluster's most underused

A Discord server is the only place on this list where **showing up every day is the native behaviour**. That makes it the natural home for a daily-practice product, and nine other projects in your cohort will be posting on Reddit and LinkedIn instead.

| Where | Scale | Reach | How |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[MBA Raasta](https://discord.do/mba-raasta/)** | ~3,000 members, longest-running public Indian MBA server | 100–600 | Be a member for a week first. **Then offer the server something: a daily case prompt, posted by you, every day at a fixed time.** Not a link — a contribution. |
| **[CAT Preparation server](https://discord.me/catpreparation)** and other MBA servers | Active doubt-solving | 100–500 | Same rule. Small and tight; a drive-by is noticed and resented. |
| **Consulting / case-prep servers** (search Disboard for "case interview", "consulting prep") | 500–5,000 each | 100–800 | Several exist with real practice channels. **Join 6.** |
| **Campus Discords** (B-school and large engineering colleges) | 200–2,000 | 50–400 | One post each, written for that campus |

**The play that actually works here:** ask a moderator if you can run a **daily guesstimate thread** in the server. You post one prompt a day, people answer in replies, you give feedback. Within two weeks you are a fixture rather than a visitor, and your product is the obvious next step for anyone who wants the full version.

**That is the highest-leverage thing in this kit** and it costs twenty minutes a day.

### 2. The public streak — your best mechanic, not a channel

**Do the thing you're selling, in public, every day, for thirty days.** One case a day, posted as a short LinkedIn or Instagram update: the prompt, your structure, your score, and the one thing the follow-up caught you on.

Why this works better for you than for anyone else in the cluster:

- **It demonstrates the product thirty times** instead of announcing it once.
- **It proves the fifteen-minute claim** — the thing a visitor doubts most.
- **It compounds.** Day 1 gets twelve views; day 22 gets two thousand, because people have started watching.
- **It is the only honest way to sell a habit product.** A founder who sells daily practice and doesn't practise daily is transparent to this audience.

Expect **1,500–12,000 cumulative impressions** across thirty posts, with most of it in the last ten days. See `03` #2 and `05` Format A.

## Tier 3 — Don't 🔵

- **Product Hunt, Hacker News.** Wrong continent, wrong audience.
- **X/Twitter.** Negligible for Indian MBA aspirants.
- **Coaching-owned Telegram groups** (iQuanta, Cracku, CATKing). A competitor's funnel, and the audience is CAT aspirants rather than case candidates.
- **Any channel you post to once and leave.** For a habit product this is a strategic rule, not a preference — one-shot channels give you signups with no retention attached.
- **Paid anything.** Budget is zero.

| **Total addressable visitors** | **~1,950** | **~15,800** |

**The honest read.** 1,000 signups needs roughly 8,000–10,000 visitors, so the low end misses and the high end clears it. Three things close the gap, and none is "post more":

1. **The `04` fixes.** A page with no proof and no stated price converts at 2–3%; with three named testimonials, a published price and a visible scorecard it converts at 9–12%. That difference is worth more than every Tier 2 channel combined.
2. **The daily Discord thread and the public streak.** Both compound across the month instead of spiking once. By week 4 they're producing most of your traffic.
3. **Two or three consulting clubs distributing.** Each is 30–150 people who are all exactly your user, right now.

**One number you should care about more than signups:** `02` can deliver a thousand accounts, but you are selling a habit. **If day-7 retention is under 15%, this entire plan is filling a bucket with a hole in it** — `07` makes that the stop-everything threshold, and it's the most likely thing to go wrong.
