# CaseCrack — 30-day execution calendar

**Two things run every single day of this month, starting on day 1:**

1. **Your own case.** Fifteen minutes. You are selling a daily habit and you cannot sell it without having one.
2. **The streak post.** Ninety seconds (`03` #2 / `05` Format A).

Everything else is layered on top. **Your public launch window in the [cluster schedule](../../shared/case-prep-cluster.md) is days 13–17**, which is later than you'd like and it's an advantage: it buys you twelve days to fix the page, build proof and get a streak going before anyone looks.

Open this file every morning.

## The five things that decide this month

1. **Not missing a day of your own case.** You're selling a habit. A founder who skips four days has no product and the audience can tell.
2. **The daily Discord guesstimate thread.** Twenty minutes a day, compounds all month, and it's the one channel in this kit where showing up repeatedly is the native behaviour. By week 3 it's a fixture; by week 4 it's your best source.
3. **Three named testimonials by day 11.** Everything downstream converts at double rate with them.
4. **Publishing a price** (`04` Fix 2). Silence about cost costs you signups invisibly, every single day you leave it.
5. **Day-7 retention above 15%.** If people sign up and use it once, you don't have a growth problem and no amount of traffic will fix it.

## What to drop if you're short on time

Lowest value first: **guesstimate SEO pages** (days 17–18 — compound into February, won't help now), **YouTube comments** (day 19), **Peerlist** (day 13).

**Never drop:** the daily case, the daily streak post, the Discord thread, day 11 (testimonials), day 15 (r/MBA — you get one shot at it).

## One thing to be disciplined about

Nine cohort-mates are selling into the same communities this month. **Your slot in r/MBA is days 13–17 and you get one post.** Posting early, or twice, gets the whole category removed and hurts nine other students as well as you. The stagger is the only reason any of you gets a hearing.
