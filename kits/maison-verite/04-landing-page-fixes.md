# Maison Vérité — Landing-page fixes

Checked 29 October 2026 from rendered copy/buttons and public pricing. “Start a red-flag check,” “Run Scan with Preset” and “Create free account” are real JavaScript controls. No preset generation, real photo upload, account or checkout was submitted. Do not label controls broken simply because they have no href.

## 1. Keep the limit attached to every result

**Current:** the homepage says “Not a certificate of authenticity,” and its scope section is clear. A shared report could still lose that context.

Put on every result/export: **“Photo-based red-flag prompts only. This does not establish that an item is genuine or fake.”** Add **“Insufficient photo evidence”** as a prominent outcome where appropriate. Test the actual result and shared screenshot; don't assume a homepage disclaimer follows them.

## 2. Tell visitors the specific task

**Current:** “Check Before You Buy.”

Try:

> **See which resale-photo details need another look.**
>
> Inspect visible red flags, unclear areas and the next photo to request. A first look from images, not professional authentication or a buying guarantee.

Keep the editorial visual style; don't add fake trust seals to make the page look official. The important hierarchy is evidence → uncertainty → next question.

## 3. Put the sample before private upload

**Current:** “Start a red-flag check” and three “Run Scan with Preset” examples.

Make the first action **“Inspect a sample red-flag report”**, then **“Start my own check — three with a free account.”** Verify what each sample does and whether it consumes allowance. Use only licensed/owned sample photos. Label brand/model names as examples, never affiliation.

## 4. Explain photo scope and consent

**Current:** the page asks for logo, stitching and serial-area photos.

Use **“Upload only photos you own or have permission to use. Avoid faces, addresses and private seller information.”** Explain actual upload processing, retention and deletion before entry. Do not invent a local-only or instant-deletion promise. State supported item categories from tested capability, not from the variety of sample cards.

## 5. Make price and limits operational

**Current:** Free $0, three checks; Pro $19/month, unlimited checks, cancel anytime.

Explain whether sample/failed checks count, file limits and any fair-use restrictions according to the actual implementation. Keep **“$19/month, recurring”** visible before checkout. Test cancellation/access behaviour on authorized test data before demonstrating it. A low monthly price doesn't make the result equivalent to a professional certificate.

## 6. Show a rejection or unclear outcome

Add a short actual demonstration where the user requests a better photo rather than receives a confident answer. Caption **“This is a sample; no conclusion about a real seller's item.”** Don't publish an accuracy percentage without a valid evaluation, and don't make the model's own confidence its proof of correctness.

## 7. Phone and preview

Proposed title: **“Maison Vérité — a first look at resale-photo red flags.”** Description: **“Three checks with a free account. Photo prompts and uncertainty, not authenticity certification.”** Test actual link previews and the upload/photo-quality feedback on phones. This audit did not establish a mobile or load-time defect; verify before making one up. Keep the limitation readable before the first upload and in the result screenshot.
