# Maison Vérité — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.maisonverite.in/ · **Category:** Consumer resale research · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/maison-verite

**You are a red-flag assistant, not an authenticator. Keep that distinction on page one.** The real headline is “Check Before You Buy.” The primary action is “Start a red-flag check”; sample buttons say “Run Scan with Preset.” The page explicitly says it cannot certify or guarantee authenticity. Free accounts get three checks; Pro is $19/month. Sample use, account creation, uploaded-photo analysis and checkout were not submitted for this audit.

## Your first user

An adult considering one second-hand designer bag who has seller permission to inspect the listing photos and wants to know which close-ups to request next. They are not a reseller asking for an official certificate or a buyer looking for an AI blessing to transfer money immediately.

Start with one supported bag category and a clear photo-quality checklist. The homepage includes bag and watch presets, but those examples alone do not establish reliable coverage of every brand, year, material or model. A system that says “photo too unclear” at the right moment is more useful than a confident answer to every upload.

## The pitch

> Inspect the seller's photos for visible details worth checking, then ask better questions before paying. A first look, not proof an item is genuine.

Bio version: **Better questions from resale photos. No authenticity certificate.**

Your wedge is the next photo request: logo, stitching, serial-area image or another detail the model identifies as unclear. Don't teach counterfeiters how to evade detection, issue a “safe to buy” seal or market the report as evidence in a payment dispute.

## Real alternatives and prices

[LegitApp's own pricing page](https://legitapp.com/) publishes luxury authentication from $10 for its four-hour option and sneaker authentication from $3 for its thirty-minute option, with different faster-turnaround prices. It describes expert-plus-AI review and certificates. Those are its service claims, not a guarantee independently audited by this kit. Your $19/month photo-red-flag tool is **not the same service** and should not imply equivalent certification for less money.

[Entrupy](https://www.entrupy.com/pricing/) offers a business-oriented token subscription, with its displayed entry plan at $139/month. Don't present that as the price every individual buyer must pay to get an item reviewed. A buyer can also request clearer photos and consult an appropriately qualified service without buying your subscription.

## The honest weakness

An ordinary photo cannot reveal every material, provenance or physical feature. “No red flags found” must not become “authentic.” Keep uncertainty, unsupported-category and insufficient-photo outcomes visible. Brand names/presets are examples, not evidence of brand affiliation.

Keep the **1,000-consumer-account target**, with completed, correctly understood red-flag reports alongside. If users misread the output as a certificate, more distribution is harmful. Your first proof is that five new readers can explain the limitation and choose an appropriate next check, not that the result looks luxurious.
