# Maison Vérité — Video scripts

Owned/permitted sample photos only. No accusations about a seller, brand endorsement or implied professional certificate. Screen-record real output, use your phone microphone and large captions; no rendered video needed.

## A. Ask for a better photo — 65 seconds

**Where:** homepage, Peerlist and organizer invitation. **Hook:** “This photo isn't enough.”

| Time | Shot | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Owned sample close-up marked unclear | This photo isn't enough. |
| 2–13s | Actual preset/sample path | I'm using a sample to inspect visible details, not authenticate an item. |
| 13–30s | Actual red-flag/unclear result | What can I see, what can't I see and what should I ask to photograph again? |
| 30–43s | Read the limitation beside the output | No red flags found would still not mean genuine. |
| 43–54s | Show a clearer permitted photo or next-question list | The useful next step is a better question, not a guarantee to pay. |
| 54–65s | Price/free limits and URL | Three checks with a free account. Pro $19/month. Read the scope at maisonverite.in. |

If the product doesn't currently return an unclear outcome, label that as a product change to make; don't stage a live result it didn't produce.

## B. A screenshot isn't a certificate — 20 seconds

**Where:** owned social feed. **Hook:** “Don't turn this into proof it's real.” Show a labelled sample report with the limitation visible, then a fake certificate shape crossed out. Say, “This is a photo-red-flag prompt, not an authenticity service. Keep that boundary when you share.” End with the sample route, not a seller's listing.

## C. What is outside the frame? — 25 seconds

**Where:** education session and LinkedIn. **Hook:** “A photograph leaves things out.” Show a permitted sample and list “material, physical inspection, provenance, unclear detail” as questions rather than conclusions. Say, “The photo can help ask what to check next. It can't supply evidence it doesn't contain.” No claim that one visual cue proves counterfeit status.

## D. The result I would reject — 30 seconds

**Where:** launch gallery. **Hook:** “This wording is too certain.” Show an actual or clearly labelled proposed overconfident line, change it to a limited observation, and explain the missing evidence. Say, “A luxurious-looking report should not make a weak conclusion feel stronger.” End with the uncertainty label and founder disclosure.

Bank sample selection, photo-quality feedback, next-question output, visible limitation and result sharing. Don't film a real purchase or manufacture an expert endorsement.
