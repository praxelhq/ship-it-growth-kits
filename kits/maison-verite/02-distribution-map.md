No current promotional permission in a named luxury/resale subreddit or private group was verified. Do not post under a real buyer's authenticity dispute to sell them a subscription. A useful answer can say what a photo cannot establish without naming a product at all.

Twenty warm invitations, eight small-account posts, one conditional session and a directory listing plausibly form a **10–30 correctly understood first-report experiment**, with overlaps removed. That's not an evidenced route to 1,000 accounts. Earn an accepted organizer introduction or actual voluntary sharing before increasing the forecast. A screenshot forwarded as a certificate is not a successful referral.
