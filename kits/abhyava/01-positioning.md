# Abhyava — Positioning

**Live:** https://abhyava.space/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/abhyava

**Your ten are teachers running real teaching businesses, not ten students.** Start with an independent yoga teacher's small recurring adult group. The free limit of ten students is capacity inside one customer, not the acquisition target.

The actual headline is **“Every class, looped into the next.”** The page specifically introduces yoga teachers, then demonstrates other disciplines. **“Start free”** leads to `/signup`; the page offers all features free for up to ten students. Main pricing shows **₹2,990/year** for Pro, described as about ₹249/month billed annually. A lower strip says Pro from ₹299/month. Reconcile that discrepancy before any paid offer; this research didn't complete checkout.

## The specific job

> Turn the end of this week's class into a small home-practice task, then walk into the next class knowing who needs help.

Bio: **The practice loop between two classes.**

Start with a teacher who already sends home-practice messages and forgets which students replied. One adult group, one teacher-approved task, one next-class brief is enough. Don't pitch sixteen disciplines at once or ask the teacher to replace their fee system. ChoreoStudio's wedge is fees; yours is preparation, practice and feedback.

## Why use it instead of the existing process?

The teacher can keep sending WhatsApp messages and tracking responses in a spreadsheet. [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators/workspace and can hold a manual lesson/practice board, but students need a different participation process. Abhyava's claimed advantage is private browser links, no student app and a connected preparation/practice brief. Test whether the teacher and students can actually complete that loop unaided.

The AI drafts; the instructor remains responsible for safe, appropriate practice. Do not advertise health improvements, injury prevention or automatic expertise from generated yoga plans. Your first adult cohort avoids needing to demonstrate child/parent permissions before you understand the workflow; it doesn't waive consent or privacy.

## The honest weakness

A teacher may like a generated lesson and never send practice to a student. That is a one-off AI use, not the connected loop you sell. Measure one real assignment, one consenting student's response and the teacher's next-class review. Public demonstration names, streaks and practice counts are examples, not customer outcomes. The video's stated 90-day deletion and private-by-default claims are site claims; verify real handling before collecting sensitive student recordings.
