# Abhyava — Distribution map

**Independent adult-group yoga teachers first.** Don't pitch every dance studio and every music school in the same week. Use the shared teacher-operations rotation: Abhyava's organizer invitation is days 4–7, ChoreoStudio fees days 11–14, DancePass workshop supply days 18–21, PlayLead sports enquiries days 25–28. Student rehearsal tools use different organizers.

| Route | How, limits and assumed result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Warm teacher introductions | Ask ten people you know to request an introduction to a teacher with a recurring paid adult group. No student contact lists or unsolicited group additions. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 active businesses. | #1 |
| 2 · [Yoga Alliance directory](https://www.yogaalliance.org/Directory) | The real directory exposes teacher/school search. Manually verify the teacher's own current business page, then use an appropriate business route. Directory presence isn't endorsement or marketing permission. Four first approaches on ten days = 40; assumed 25% reply × 60% real-class trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 businesses. | #2 |
| 3 · [The Yoga Institute](https://www.theyogainstitute.org/) official resources | A verified source of its own programmes, useful for understanding teacher language. No free newsletter slot, group access or institutional partnership established. Ask a current organizer only if the narrow session fits. | #3 |
| 4 · Teacher-approved group invitation | The teacher chooses whether to invite consenting adult students into one practice loop. You don't contact students independently. One accepted teacher session may create useful participation; it isn't more business customers. | #4 |
| 5 · Your own LinkedIn / short demonstration | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show a synthetic teacher→practice→brief loop. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 useful visits from a small account, possibly zero. | #5 |

Specific source worth following: the teacher's own public class/programme posts, with relevant practical replies instead of a product advertisement under every post. [Trello's pricing](https://trello.com/pricing) is a verified manual-alternative reference, not a teacher prospect list. No Reddit promotion rule or paid directory placement is needed for this plan.

Forty new approaches plus ten warm requests can conservatively support **3–5 active teachers**, below ten. At day 14 inspect whether the whole loop is used independently. A thousand students in demo screenshots cannot close a business-customer gap. If an organizer requests a free opt-in newsletter blurb, use #3 after approval and don't claim a placement in advance.
