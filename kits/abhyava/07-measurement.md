# Abhyava — Measurement

**Target: 10 real teaching businesses.** Activation beside it: **teachers independently completing a real approved-practice → student response → next-class review loop**. Ten student portals inside one teacher account count as one business.

## Day 0

Record actual teacher businesses, real adult classes, approved assignments, student responses and independent teacher returns from your own records. The homepage's streaks and class participation are illustrative, not a baseline. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent; mask student/recording content. Record actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/abhyava) rank, saved metric and timestamp. Unknown isn't zero.

Events: `teacher_class_created`, `practice_approved`, `private_invite_accepted`, `student_response_received`, `next_class_brief_reviewed` and `teacher_returned`. Log aggregate events, not raw voice notes or student video. A generated lesson isn't a completed class or a health outcome. Verify the actual current annual/monthly plan before counting real paid upgrades.

## Four customer tests

The [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md) requires a real paid-teaching business, its own actual class/workflow data onboarded, independent use in the last seven days without your prompt, and a responsible teacher's name/callable number held privately. Payment to Abhyava isn't required. One teacher with three disciplines is one business; free example accounts are excluded.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × hypothetical 25% response × 60% real-class trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3 businesses. Ten warm requests may add 0–2. **Base 3–5 is below ten.** These are assumptions, not observed yoga-teacher conversion rates. After 20 approaches, replace them with actual counts and distinguish no interest, broken setup and no useful next-class job.
