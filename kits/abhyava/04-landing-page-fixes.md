# Abhyava — Landing-page fixes

Public page and CTA destinations read 29 October 2026. No authenticated teacher/student flow or actual deletion behaviour was tested. The app isn't labelled broken from that limit.

## 1. Resolve the price discrepancy before an upgrade

**Current:** the main Pro card says ₹2,990/year, about ₹249/month billed annually; a lower strip says Pro from ₹299/month.

**Write after checking checkout:** **“Pro: exact charge and billing period shown before payment.”** Then make the card, footer and checkout use the same actual annual/monthly configuration. Don't call ₹249 a cancellable monthly price when the charge is annual. Free scope can remain **“Up to 10 students, no card.”**

## 2. Make the first complete loop explicit

**Current headline:** “Every class, looped into the next.” CTA: “Start free.”

**Write:** **“Send one teacher-approved practice. Prepare for the next class.”** Button: **“Set up my first class free.”** Subtext: **“Free up to 10 students. Students open a private browser link.”** Test teacher signup → approved task → consenting adult student view → response → teacher brief before outreach. If that loop fails, repair it before recruiting more teachers.

## 3. Separate sample participation from real proof

**Current:** example student streaks, practice counts and feedback illustrate the loop.

**Write on every demo:** **“Illustrative class and students.”** Keep it attached to screenshots and videos. A “9 of 12 practised” example isn't evidence that customers increased participation. Replace with a real aggregate only after permission and a clear period/sample.

## 4. Keep the instructor's responsibility visible

**Current:** “Fresh classes that never repeat” and discipline-aware planning can sound authoritative.

**Write:** **“AI drafts your plan. You check suitability and approve what students receive.”** Avoid health-benefit or safety claims not demonstrated. A generated movement plan should not publish without the teacher's review.

## 5. Explain private links and recording retention before collecting video

**Current:** “Private by default,” no student app and videos deleting after 90 days are stated.

**Write:** **“Read who can open this link, who sees recordings and how deletion works before sharing.”** Verify access, revocation and the actual retention process. Don't infer those controls from marketing copy. Start with consenting adults; don't use child examples to skip parent permission.

## 6. Show the next-class brief on a phone

**Current:** sixteen disciplines and many features compete with the core loop.

**Write the first view around:** **“What to carry into tonight's class.”** Keep the practice task and next-step brief readable at 360px, enlarged text and keyboard focus. Suggested preview: **“Abhyava — the practice loop between two classes.”** Test slow-network recovery and private-link behaviour; no specific clipping or load defect was measured.
