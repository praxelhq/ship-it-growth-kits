## Where to learn and whom to follow

The [RHS houseplant hub](https://www.rhs.org.uk/houseplants) links real horticultural guidance and videos. It is a reference source, not an advertising surface. If you discuss a plant-care claim, open the relevant source first and distinguish it from the model's guess.

[Peerlist Scroll](https://peerlist.io/scroll) gives a real builder feed for one transparent demonstration and replies. Do not post plant advice beneath unrelated launches. No newsletter's editorial route was verified for this product; ask a real organizer or nursery whether their opted-in bulletin accepts the exercise, and count zero until publication. Copy #2 is ready only for a permitted bulletin.

Optional [AlternativeTo submission](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) requires verified email, accurate app details and review. No guaranteed approval date; paid priority is out of budget. Do not count a submitted entry as traffic.

## Composition and coordination

Three sessions, three nursery visits, eight owned clips and one listing model **22–126 accounts before overlap**. The target is still 1,000; these are first-test activities, not evidence the target is achievable from this route alone. At an assumed 25% visit-to-account rate, reaching it requires 4,000 qualified unique visitors.

Ask satisfied participants whether they want to share the useful exercise with one other owner. Do not require sharing to reveal a result or create a fake urgency loop. Wider distribution must come from real permissioned introductions or a measured demonstration that travels.

Get Plont uses the earlier routine-care window. Your Days 17–23 symptom-check window uses different nursery contacts and circles. Coordinate actual dates privately and leave seven days between invitations to the same organization. Do not imply the two products are one company or referral partners without agreement.
