# PlantPal — Video scripts

Record your own plant, phone and actual result. Use hard cuts, readable captions and your voice. No paid assets, fabricated diagnosis or staged recovery.

## A. Photo, context, next observation — 60 seconds

**Where:** owned account, nursery/plant-circle demonstration. **Hook in first two seconds:** “This photo is not the whole story.”

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Your actual plant and close-up symptom. | “This photo is not the whole story. I want a clearer first question, not a certain answer.” |
| 8–20s | Real first-scan flow; show upload disclosure. | “I submit the image and read what PlantPal suggests. The photo is uploaded for analysis.” |
| 20–35s | Actual result, enlarge an uncertainty/context sentence. | “This is the possible explanation. This is the context I still need to check.” |
| 35–48s | Save/find the actual result in My Garden. | “I keep the check so I can compare what I observe later. I do not need to act on every suggestion.” |
| 48–60s | URL and first-free-scan limit. | “Try one scan free after sign-in at theplantpal.in. Confirm important decisions with knowledgeable help.” |

If a result is too certain, film the correction you are making to the product rather than presenting it as safe advice.

## B. Yellow leaf question — 20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “A yellow leaf is a question.” **Where:** owned short video.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Your leaf, no recovery comparison. | “A yellow leaf is a question, not a complete diagnosis.” |
| 5–13s | Photo plus notes about actual conditions, without prescribing treatment. | “A photo helps start the conversation. The conditions and history matter too.” |
| 13–20s | Result/uncertainty and URL. | “PlantPal is a first check. Read what it cannot know before acting.” |

## C. Same plant, real later date — 30 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “Compare observations, not miracle pictures.” **Where:** later follow-up, only after time has passed.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7s | Original check with actual date. | “Compare observations, not miracle pictures.” |
| 7–20s | Same plant today, dated note. | “This is what I saw then. This is what I see today. I am not claiming a model suggestion caused a recovery.” |
| 20–30s | My Garden history and URL. | “Keeping the record helps me ask a better next question. That is the useful part.” |

## D. Packs without pressure — 20 seconds, vertical

**Hook:** “You don't need a pack to try the first scan.” **Where:** pricing question response.

| Time | Shot / text | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | First-free-scan line. | “You don't need a pack to try the first scan.” |
| 6–14s | Accurate shop cards, after “Best value” corrected. | “If it is useful, five scans are ₹49 and fifteen are ₹149. They do not expire. Check the final total before paying.” |
| 14–20s | Result and URL. | “Buy only if you have a real use for more checks.” |

Bank clips of the actual plant, photo selection, upload disclosure, result uncertainty and saved garden check. Eight original clips are scheduled; use a new true observation for each. Do not show a participant's home/photo without permission or ask them to praise a result publicly.
