# PlantPal — Positioning

**Live:** https://theplantpal.in/ · **Category:** Mobile app · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/plantpal

**Sell a clearer next observation, not a certain diagnosis.** A yellow leaf in a photo does not explain a plant's entire environment. Your useful first result is a person understanding what to check and when to ask for knowledgeable help.

The [actual headline](https://theplantpal.in/) is “Hi! I'm PlantPal 🌸”. No card needed.” The real navigation includes Scan, My Garden, Next Plant and Scans; `/garden` and `/shop` were read on 29 October 2026. Authentication, photo diagnosis and paid checkout were not completed in this review.

## Your first person

An adult with one or two balcony/indoor plants, who notices spots or drooping and is about to search several vague explanations. They can supply a photo and context, and they need a calm, limited first pass. They do not need the product to pretend it knows a disease with certainty.

Start with beginners already asking a nursery or a friend about a visible change. Do not manufacture a sick plant or create urgency about harmless variation. Get Plont serves routine records/reminders; your wedge is the uncertain symptom and the documented follow-up.

## Pitch

> Show the plant, read the possible explanation, and know what to check next.

Short version:

> A first plant check, with uncertainty made clear.

The best demo is not “before and after recovery.” It is the photo, the context, what the tool suggested, what you could verify and what remained uncertain. Store that check in My Garden and return on a later date only when a recheck is useful.

## Actual prices and alternatives

[Scan packs](https://theplantpal.in/shop) offer the first scan free after sign-in, five scans for ₹49 and fifteen for ₹149; scans never expire. The Next Plant finder is advertised as free. Confirm the final payable tax/currency total in checkout before quoting it as an all-in amount.

The fifteen-scan pack is marked “Best value,” but ₹149/15 is about ₹9.93 per scan versus ₹49/5 = ₹9.80. At displayed prices it is not cheaper per scan. Fix that claim before using it in promotion.

## The weakness you should say aloud

The shop offers “Ask an expert,” but the public inspection did not establish the person's qualifications, response time or scope. Do not call it a botanist consultation or promise instant help. Explain the actual service before promoting it.

The [privacy page](https://www.theplantpal.in/privacy) says photos and answers are stored in My Garden and that Gemini reads the photo. Do not claim “fully offline” or “nothing uploaded.” Its contact address appeared incomplete in the fetched text; verify the actual visible contact and deletion route rather than copying a possibly truncated address into outreach.

Your target remains **1,000 signups**, with people who complete and read a real first check alongside it. A free Next Plant browse or scan-pack page visit is not a new account.
