The core plan supports perhaps 20–60 joined players under uncertain assumptions. At an average five players per host, 1,000 joined players require 200 real hosted groups. At a 30% host-invite conversion, that requires roughly 667 qualified host invitations. You cannot manually support that many in this calendar. Improve unprompted host referrals before claiming the board target is covered.

Coordinate broad campus invitations in Days 10–13; WhoDunnit's larger-group offer should use a different organizer or later session. A second game pitch into the same group is not another audience.
