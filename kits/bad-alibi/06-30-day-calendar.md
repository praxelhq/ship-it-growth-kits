# Bad Alibi — 30-day calendar

Daily: 20 minutes answering hosts/players and 10 minutes recording join→start→reveal. Do not fund other people's games from a zero-budget plan; a host decides whether to pay after seeing terms. Times IST.

| Day | Main action | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Record baseline; test setup, invitation and current payment terms without charging strangers. |2h|
| 2 | Ship logistics/pay-at-start clarity (`04` 1–2). |2h|
| 3 | Test a complete authorized session and drop-out recovery; fix blockers before outreach. |3h|
| 4 | Send five host invitations #1, permission first. |1h|
| 5 | Record demo A and hook B with a consenting staged group. |2h|
| 6 | Publish first Short; ask organizer about a Day 10 small session. |1h|
| 7 | Checkpoint: actual host setups, players ready and completed reveals. |1h|
| 8 | Prepare itch page #5, accurate price link and original screenshots. |2h|
| 9 | Publish the page after preview and second Short, hook C. |1h|
| 10 | Run one approved six-person session at 19:00; no surprise recording. |2h|
| 11 | Send remaining five host invitations #1. |1h|
| 12 | Fix the most common dossier/readiness confusion. |2h|
| 13 | Publish third Short showing non-spoiler setup. |1h|
| 14 | Checkpoint: joined players→started games→reveal completion. |1h|
| 15 | Interview two hosts about effort, cost clarity and pacing. |1h|
| 16 | Record hook D with the actual current checkout terms. |1h|
| 17 | Publish fourth Short, price terms without an expiring voiceover quote. |1h|
| 18 | Improve one dropped-player/reconnect explanation. |2h|
| 19 | Ask consenting completed-game players #4 if they'd host another group. |1h|
| 20 | Publish fifth Short answering a real organizer question. |1h|
| 21 | Checkpoint: unprompted new hosts, refunds and support time. |1h|
| 22 | Check current promotion expiry and all host-price labels. |1h|
| 23 | Read current community rules; ask permission before #6. |1h|
| 24 | Publish sixth Short using an original staged reaction, no private names. |1h|
| 25 | Optional Product Hunt preparation only if real groups reliably finish. |1h|
| 26 | Spend time supporting requested nights rather than sending more invites. |2h|
| 27 | Ask three guests whether they would organize without your help. |1h|
| 28 | Audit paid games, failed starts and refund requests against delivery. |2h|
| 29 | Repeat the source that brought real hosts; stop empty traffic posts. |1h|
| 30 | Report unique joined players, reveal completion, repeat hosts and actual revenue. |2h|

If rooms fill but games don't start, repair readiness and price clarity. If games start but people don't finish, fix pacing before recruiting the next group. A hundred unfinished rooms are not a hundred successful game nights.
