# Bad Alibi — Video scripts

Record your own staged group, with permission. Use fake first names and harmless quirks. Do not show real private dossiers, active game codes or the culprit/solution.

## A. The organizer can play — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “The person who organized tonight doesn't know who did it.”

| Time | Shot / text | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10s | Friends deciding what to do, then one phone opens setup | “Instead of choosing a film for half an hour, we're trying a short mystery.” |
| 10–24s | Duration/tone fields and invite preview, code obscured | “One person sets it up. Friends join and answer harmless questions.” |
| 24–40s | Separate staged role screens, no spoiler text | “Everybody gets a private part. Even the host gets to play.” |
| 40–52s | A staged accusation and timer, not a revealed answer | “The group builds alibis, reads evidence and votes.” |
| 52–60s | Current live terms on screen | “3–10 people. 35 or 75 minutes. Check the host price; friends join free.” |

Post with #2 to the approved organizer group and on your own channel. Show real gameplay only after a complete test; don't imply a staged clip is a customer night.

## B. Always late — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “You're five minutes away. Where were you at 11:52?” Show a staged late-arrival text, a fake role label, then an amused reaction. Say: “Your group's harmless running joke becomes part of the case. Nobody needs to share a real secret.” End with the URL, no solution. Use an original Short.

## C. Thirty-five minutes — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “A game night that fits before the next plan.” Show the actual Quick choice, phones joining and the clock starting. Say: “Quick mode is 35 minutes. Gather the group first; setup isn't the same as everybody being ready.” No sped-up footage implying instant completion.

## D. One host pays — 25 seconds

**Hook:** “Your friends don't each need to buy a ticket.” Show setup, the current final host price, and a guest join screen with private code obscured. Say: “Inviting is free. One host pays when starting. Guests join free. Read the current terms before you press Start.” Avoid putting the temporary $2 price into an evergreen voiceover.
