# SwitchBoard — Video scripts

Use a fictional résumé and a public job description. No real applicant contact details, private work or employer documents. Record actual behaviour; don't animate a successful export the product didn't produce. Phone microphone and captions are sufficient.

## A. The change you reject —65 seconds

**Where:** homepage and clinic invitation. **Hook:** “This keyword doesn't belong on your résumé.”

|Time|Shot|Voice/text|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 s|An unsupported skill highlighted in a synthetic CV|This keyword doesn't belong on your résumé.|
|2–14 s|Your toy résumé and a public target role|The role asks for it, but that isn't proof I have it.|
|14–30 s|Actual section-level additions/removals|SwitchBoard makes the edits visible so I can inspect what changed.|
|30–44 s|Reject the unsupported addition; accept a supported clarification|I keep the true improvement and reject the invented qualification.|
|44–56 s|Resolve each decision; show actual export behaviour|Every change gets a decision before export.|
|56–65 s|Canonical URL and actual access terms|Start with a safe example at switchboardapp.studio. This isn't an interview guarantee.|

If export or decisions fail, fix and reshoot. Don't put a real jobseeker's identity in the demo.

## B. Team work versus my work —25 seconds

**Where:** LinkedIn. **Hook:** “Did you lead it, or did the team?” Show a synthetic team achievement, then separate personal contribution. Say, “A clearer résumé doesn't need a bigger job title. It needs an accurate account of your part.” Show a supported wording change and rationale. End with the productURLand founder disclosure.

## C. Before you upload — 30 seconds

**Where:** onboarding/privacy explanation. **Hook:** “Try the review process with a sample first.” Show the actual public sample if implemented, or your labelled demonstration clip if not. Explain where users can read implemented document-handling terms. Say, “Don't upload confidential material to test whether you like the interface.” Never claim local-only handling without evidence.

## D. The delta, not the score —25 seconds

**Where:** Peerlist gallery. **Hook:** “I want to see what changed.” Show the original bullet, proposed change, rationale and decision controls at normal speed. Say, “An opaque score can't tell me whether the new claim is true. I still need to check every addition.” End with an actual reviewed export, if it works, and no ATS/job claim.

Bank four clips: original section, proposed edit, rejection and final decision/export. One continuous review interaction is stronger than a montage of résumé scores.
