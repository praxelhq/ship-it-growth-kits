# SwitchBoard — Distribution map

**Lead with a résumé-diff clinic, not a mass-application promise.** Your first users need to trust the edits and the document handling. All reach ranges are explicitly uncertain planning assumptions, not channel benchmarks.

Useful replies go under actual discussions of describing projects, changing roles and keeping résumés accurate. Use people whose work you already read: a known alumni organizer or former colleague discussing their field. This kit does not invent a recruiter list, named influencer route or private group access. Don't pitch under another person's layoff or request their résumé in a public comment.

Coordinate actual clinic dates with other career-tool founders. Lynkr teaches a project-to-message job; SwitchBoard teaches inspectable résumé edits. Use a different adult role-switcher group, not the same student circle receiving another app link. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week.

Two accepted clinics, 20 warm invitations, eight posts and a directory listing might produce **15–40 reviewed first résumés**, with overlap removed. That's a useful first experiment, not an evidenced path to 1,000 accounts. Expand only through accepted organizer introductions or a channel that produces actual completed reviews.
