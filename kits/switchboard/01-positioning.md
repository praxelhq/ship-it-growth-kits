## Start with a truthful role switch

Your first user is an early-career professional applying to one adjacent role with relevant experience, who wants to change emphasis without adding skills they don't possess. They already have a résumé and a real job description. They do not want an opaque rewrite, a fabricated achievement or a claim that an ATS score guarantees an interview.

The visible product differentiator is **the delta**: additions/removals grouped by section, an explanation and a decision for every suggestion before export. The homepage says export remains locked until all changes receive a decision. That's the workflow to demonstrate, not “automated job applications” inferred from the old roster description.

## The pitch

> Tailor one résumé to one role, with every addition and removal visible. Accept only the changes that stay true to your experience, then export.

Bio version: **A résumé edit you can inspect, not a mystery rewrite.**

The wedge is taking an existing truthful bullet and making its relevance clearer. “Helped with product work” can become a more precise statement only when the user supports it. A keyword in the job description is not evidence the applicant has that skill.

## Alternatives

[ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) offers general rewriting at no extra writing-tool purchase. [Grammarly Free](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) is$0/month for grammar and writing assistance. A document editor's tracked changes plus a mentor is a credible manual alternative. Don't claim generic writers can't tailor a résumé. Show why section-level decisions and visible rationale make this process easier to review.

Your weakness is the login wall before anyone sees their own delta, plus an unreadable public price. A clearly labelled synthetic example and accurate access/pricing explanation can reduce that uncertainty without exposing somebody's real CV. Keep the actual final approval; don't quietly add automatic export to increase completion.

The target remains **1,000 consumer accounts**, with a fully reviewed first tailored résumé reported alongside. Export counts are not job offers. If users reject most additions because they aren't true, the product is failing even when the text sounds polished.
