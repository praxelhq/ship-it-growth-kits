# SwitchBoard — Measurement

**B2C target: 1,000 new consumer accounts in 30 days.** Always report **first fully reviewed tailored résumés** alongside. Activation means the user has reviewed every proposed change and reached a truthful final document; export is a separate, stronger step. A website visit, CV upload or AI score is not the same as completed review.

Tag links by channel, not by person.

## Arithmetic

At an assumed 20% visitor-to-account rate, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 qualified visitors. If half complete a review, that is 500 activations. These rates are hypotheses, not supplied benchmarks. Two accepted 15-seat clinics, 20 warm invitations, eight owned posts and uncertain directory discovery plausibly form a **15–40 reviewed-document experiment**. They do not establish 5,000 visits.

Do not borrow an ATS conversion rate to plug the gap. There is no verified interview causality in this kit. Track which accepted channels produce users who finish review, and seek voluntary organizer introductions after something works.

## Weekly decisions

| Day | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | Three fresh users must reach the working apex and understand actual access/price. If URL or pricing confusion persists, fix it before more invitations. |
| 14 | Fewer than half of the first ten willing testers finish review: watch the decision step. Any fabricated qualification requires product correction, not just a warning. |
| 21 | Who returned for another role without a nudge? Which edits did they reject? No repeat value: narrow the role/use case before adding more output formats. |
| 30 | Report accounts, completed reviews, exports, repeat use, paid conversions/refunds, hours and target gap. Do not present exports as applications, interviews or offers. |

These are experiment decisions, not industry norms. A user rejecting an unsupported addition is a sign the review step is doing its job; the better product reduces bad additions rather than making rejection harder.
