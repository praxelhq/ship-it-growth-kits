# TuckedIn — Copy pack

Use real-family invitations only after `04`'s access/support gate passes. For earlier feedback use synthetic data and say so.

**1. Parent invitation**

“Do you already have a sitter you trust and an agreed shift? I built TuckedIn for a clearer care handoff and recap. Could you try the navigation with a fictional profile first? Please don't send me your child's details, home address, medication instructions or WiFi password. I'll explain actual access, storage and support before you decide about real use. Can I send the public example?”

**2. Admin request and accepted bulletin**

“May I share one optional synthetic-data care-handoff demo in your adult-parent circle? I built the tool. This is not medical advice, sitter vetting or an invitation to post child details. I'll share once only if you approve.”

Accepted: “A sitter handoff should be easy to read without making family details public. TuckedIn is my project for a parent-authored care brief and shift recap. Explore with fictional data first and read the actual privacy/access explanation: https://tuckedin.me/privacy. Please don't post care instructions or child photos in replies. Real use is only for a sitter you already know, after checking the controls.”

**3. Owned post**

“A care app needs more than a reassuring PIN label. I'm testing TuckedIn with synthetic profiles: does the intended sitter see only the right shift, does access end, and can a parent reach real support? The useful promise is a clearer handoff and factual recap, not safer care proven by a nice screen. No family should enter private details just to help me test navigation. https://tuckedin.me/”

**4. Resource caption**

“Before a shift, review the care brief with the parent who knows the child. TuckedIn's instruction/checklist pages are organizational aids, not personalized medical or emergency advice. Confirm all actual routines, contacts and instructions for your household and location. Please don't share a filled care plan publicly. https://tuckedin.me/babysitter-checklist”

**5. Sitter research email**

Subject: A synthetic care-brief navigation test

“Could you look at a fictional TuckedIn briefing and tell me whether routine information and shift updates are easy to find? No client records, child photos, medical decisions or endorsement requested. I'd like feedback on the interface and access explanation. We can use only made-up content.”

**6. Consented follow-up**

“Did the intended sitter find the agreed information and could the parent read the actual recap? Please describe navigation/access problems without forwarding the brief, child details or photos. Did access stop when expected?”

Vary the organizational question between posts. Never borrow a family's story as proof of safety or imply a sitter is professionally vetted by using the app.
