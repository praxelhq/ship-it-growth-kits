# TuckedIn — Landing page fixes

Browser checked 29 October 2026: **“Babysitter instructions and shift reports, unified.”** Parent Dashboard and empty care-plan form render. `/privacy` is a real policy route, not broken merely because fetch repeated the homepage. No family data, shift, PIN, expiry, SMS, photo, deletion or account control was tested.

1. **Complete actual support and access facts before real family intake.** Policy contact email/mailto is blank. Provide a working responsible contact and actual storage/operator/provider/retention facts. Test parent identity, family separation, sitter PIN attempts, link expiry/revocation and what happens after closing a shift with authorized synthetic accounts. Four digits alone are not proof of security. Pause real child-data acquisition until these pass; don't just remove the blank contact label.
2. **Replace absolute protection with verified scope.** Current “secure PIN link” and ephemeral/immediate-purge claims need implementation evidence. Replacement **“Share a shift brief with the sitter you choose. Read exactly who can access it and when access ends before using real family information.”** Say what testing actually established; no independent security audit or guaranteed immediate backup purge is claimed.
3. **Keep the concrete job, narrow the outcome.** Supporting replacement **“Prepare a parent-checked care brief for a sitter you already know, and review the shift recap.”** CTA **“Explore with a fictional profile”** before real setup. Sample children/shifts must remain visibly **“Synthetic demo — not a real family or care outcome.”** No safety or medical guarantee.
4. **Reduce sensitive fields before value.** The form asks medication/dosing, address and WiFi credentials. Explain necessity, optionality, visibility and deletion before these fields. Copy **“Enter only instructions supplied and checked by the parent or appropriate professional. Do not use this app to generate medication or care advice.”** Avoid requiring a real child identity just for a navigation test.
5. **Localize actual emergency contacts.** Resource pages can contain US-specific references. Add **“Confirm the correct emergency contacts for this household and location with the parent.”** Do not present a country-specific number as universal. The kit provides no dosing, safe-sleep or emergency-treatment instruction.
6. **Fix dead-end navigation and previews.** Pricing button produced no visible state change in the inspection; establish a real pricing/access explanation, not a guessed plan. Preview title **“TuckedIn — a clearer known-sitter handoff”**. Keep names, medical data, shift links and PINs out of previews/replay. Test phone readability, error focus, actual share expiry and recap behavior before claiming real-time delivery.
