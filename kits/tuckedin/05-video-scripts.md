# TuckedIn — Video scripts

All profiles, shifts and photos synthetic. No real child, home address, health detail, WiFi password or access PIN. Phone recording and your voice only.

**A. The handoff, 45 seconds; owned profile after trust gate.** Hook: “The sitter needs the right brief, not the whole family account.” Show synthetic label and the actual care-plan flow after testing. Show a second authorized device viewing only the intended shift. Record one neutral update and parent recap. Say, “The test is correct access and a readable recap.” End on expiry/access explanation, not a claimed safety outcome. If sharing/expiry fails, publish a build update instead.

**B. Four digits aren't the explanation, 20 seconds; LinkedIn.** Hook: “A PIN label isn't a privacy audit.” Show policy and actual tested access boundary, without a PIN. Say, “Who sees it? When does access end? Who answers a deletion question? These need concrete answers before real family details.” No claim of certification.

**C. Fictional first, 20 seconds; accepted parent bulletin.** Hook: “Please don't use your child's details to test my form.” Show a synthetic draft and optional fields. Say, “Navigation feedback can use made-up content. Private care information belongs only in a verified, understood handoff.” End on the public example, not a real shift URL.

**D. Your location, 20 seconds; owned resource post.** Hook: “A checklist can't guess your household.” Show blank fictional contact/routine sections, not medical recommendations. Say, “Confirm actual routines, instructions and local contacts with the parent. An organizational aid isn't personalized care advice.” End: “Review before the real shift.”
