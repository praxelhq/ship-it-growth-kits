# Devils Advocate — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.devils-advocate.app/ · **Category:** Decision reflection · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/devils-advocate

The public HTML headline checked 29 October is **“Good ideas deserve better questions.”** The CTA is **“Start your first conversation.”** The page offers business validation and philosophical reflection, with a **scripted sample** explicitly labelled as demonstration rather than live AI. Current access is free while subscriptions are prepared. **₹2,000/month is the planned base subscription; live subscriptions are not open.**

Your first user this month is an MBA student choosing between two career directions who wants to inspect assumptions and leave with one reversible next step. Founder validation is another audience, but the cohort already has several founder-research products. Don't compete for the same pitch-clinic invitations.

**Pitch:** Examine the strengths and tradeoffs in a decision, then choose a small next step yourself.

**Short version:** Better questions before a bigger commitment.

## The wedge

One concrete decision, not “solve my life.” State what is known, answer up to a few focused questions, inspect assumptions/counterpoints, and save a decision card with open questions. The user can correct context and disagree. This is text-only today; don't market a voice coach or an omniscient judge.

[Day One Basic](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) offers a $0/year private journal with prompts. A notebook with a pro/con list is also a no-extra-purchase alternative. Devils Advocate earns attention through a structured conversation and explicit uncertainty, if it actually helps the person formulate a next test. Don't claim journaling cannot surface tradeoffs.

## The uncomfortable bit

The page's sample is scripted. A polished demo does not establish the live conversation's accuracy, evidence coverage or helpfulness. The curated-source safeguards are site claims to test; missing evidence must stay visible. Do not present fictional business examples or philosophical paraphrases as primary research or exact quotations.

This research read the public page but did not create an account, submit a personal decision or test billing. Keep the B2C target at 1,000 accounts, with a completed decision card and voluntary follow-up beside it. The product doesn't decide for the user and should not promise a correct career choice or professional advice.
