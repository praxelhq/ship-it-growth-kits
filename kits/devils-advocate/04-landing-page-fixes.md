# Devils Advocate — Landing page fixes

Based on [the public page](https://www.devils-advocate.app/), checked 29 October. The first headline was confirmed in public HTML; signed-in conversations and billing were not tested.

## 1. Keep free-now and planned-price states consistent

The page shows a planned **₹2,000/month** base subscription and a converted USD estimate, but also says live subscriptions aren't open and current free access continues. Put **“Current free access. Paid subscriptions are not open yet.”** beside every subscribe/plan action. Don't advertise a converted estimate as a live checkout quote or test-mode transaction as revenue.

## 2. Make the current headline concrete

Current headline: **“Good ideas deserve better questions.”** CTA: **“Start your first conversation.”**

Suggested support: **“Bring one work, study or personal choice. Examine assumptions and tradeoffs, then choose a small next step yourself.”** Primary CTA: **“Try the labelled sample.”** Secondary: **“Start a real conversation”**, only where the actual account flow supports it.

## 3. Do not let the scripted sample impersonate a live result

The page already says **“Scripted demonstration, not live AI or researched advice.”** Keep that visible during the interaction, not only before it. Mark fictional business examples and philosophical paraphrases. Suggested sample note: **“This illustrates the flow. Live responses and evidence coverage can differ.”**

## 4. Test the actual decision-card journey

Fresh account → selected perspective → context → clarification → counterpoints/source → corrected context → saved/downloaded card. Use a fictional decision. If the source is absent, show the limit; if the user disagrees, preserve that context. A confident final verdict should not replace the user's decision.

## 5. Make evidence boundaries inspectable

The page describes a curated collection, not live comprehensive research. Add **“Sources cover a limited reviewed collection. Missing evidence is shown as a limit, not filled with invented examples.”** Test an unsupported query before promoting this claim. Quote/paraphrase labels must survive into exported cards.

## 6. Check privacy and phone reading

Mask personal decision/history fields from replay. Explain actual storage/deletion controls. At 390px, check long text, correcting context, source/back navigation and Markdown download. Preview: **“Devils Advocate — inspect a decision, choose your next step.”** No promise of a correct choice, treatment or professional recommendation.
