# Devils Advocate — Distribution map

**Lead with a specific, non-sensitive decision demonstration.** Invite people who already want to think through a choice; do not target distress or promise certainty.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Planning assumptions | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing students/early professionals | Ask about a reversible study/work choice, using fictional input if preferred. No private decision transcript collected. | 10 trials, 5–10 useful decision cards if the flow works. | #1 |
| Three existing career/learning groups | Ask admins for one reflection-test invitation, not a founder pitch-clinic blast. | 3 × 30 readers × 20–40% visits × 50% signup = 9–18 accounts. | #2 |
| Three LinkedIn posts | Assumption check, counterpoint and next-step lesson on Days 8, 16, 25 at 12:30 IST. | 3 × 300–1,500 impressions × 1–3% visits × 30% signup = 3–41 accounts. | #3 |
| Three short demos | Actual sample/live distinction and decision-card flow on Days 11, 19, 26. | 3 × 300–2,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 30% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #4 |
| One invited campus learning session | Existing organizer, 20 minutes, fictional work/study example and no required personal disclosure. | 8–15 attendees if accepted, 3–8 accounts assumed. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Maker feedback after a real usable conversation/card exists; current rules/slot checked. No votes requested. | 0–30 accounts, uncertain upside. | #6 |

No unverified psychology/philosophy subreddit, Discord or newsletter acceptance is assumed. A free campus newsletter mention is optional after organizer acceptance, with zero baseline forecast. Don't advertise in a support community as though this is professional care.

At 30% visitor-to-account, 1,000 needs about 3,334 distinct relevant visits. These tests likely yield dozens or low hundreds. By Day 14, use the actual completion rate and channel result rather than a generic virality claim. Keep the career-reflection invitation in Days 17–20, distinct from Astitva/PitchPanel's founder events.
