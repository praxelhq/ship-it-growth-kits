# Devils Advocate — Video scripts

Use a fictional study/work choice. Don't turn a private life decision, crisis or professional-advice situation into marketing content.

## A. Decision-card demo — 60 seconds

0–2s: **“A counterargument should give you a better test, not just an argument.”**

2–12s, state a fictional choice and known facts. “I'm deciding what to test before committing more time.”

12–28s, show actual clarification and context correction. “The app needs the right context, and I can push back.” Label scripted versus live footage honestly.

28–43s, show one supported counterpoint and one open question. “This is evidence; this is uncertainty.”

43–55s, save a real decision card. “I choose the reversible next step.”

55–60s: URL and **Current free access · subscriptions not live**.

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Short

0–2s: “What would change your mind?”

2–10s: show one assumption and an actual proposed test. “Make the unknown small enough to learn from.”

10–15s: card and URL. Fictional example label visible.

## C. Context hook — 20 seconds, LinkedIn

0–2s: “The advice changed when the context changed.”

2–12s: correct a fictional missing fact and show the actual response. “A useful tool should let you correct it.”

12–20s: compare open questions, not a staged success. “You still decide.” URL last.

## D. Sample honesty — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “This demo is scripted.”

2–12s: show the public sample label. “It explains the experience; it doesn't prove the live answer is correct.”

12–20s: show where to start the real flow and the current free/paid state. Trust comes from explaining the difference.
