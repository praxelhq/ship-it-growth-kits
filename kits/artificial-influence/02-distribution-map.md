# Artificial Influence — Distribution map

**Direct brand-owner evidence audits first.** You need customers who can assess an answer about their own business, not a generic AI-tool audience.

| Route | Action and boundary | Scheduled input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public B2B company websites via your existing founder network and [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) resources | Manually qualify one category and use the company's advertised business contact honestly. No scraped customer lists or implication of government endorsement. |10 blocks ×6 firms =60 approaches; #1/#2. |
| Two known founder introductions | Ask permission for an introduction to a business with an actual category question. | #3; no guaranteed conversions. |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 9,18,26 at 09:00 IST: show a permissioned or labelled sample answer audit and uncertainty. | #4; guess 100–500 impressions and 0–3 relevant visits/post. |
| [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | Optional only after free onboarding, scans and current slot/rules are verified. No coordinated votes. | #5; not in base forecast. |

Planning assumptions: 60 approaches ×40% substantive conversation ×60% own-brand trial ×70% independently active ≈10 businesses. That last retention assumption is demanding, not measured. At half the reply rate, the scenario is about 5; reforecast after the first twelve approaches.

Days 4–7 are direct brand-owner outreach, not another founder workshop. Threadly uses agency owners Days 16–19 with a different list. One organizer receives one primary offer per week.

No verified GEO newsletter slot or community promotional permission is included. Don't post claims of “we made ChatGPT recommend us” without a defensible test. Paid directory placements and API spending solely to create marketing content are outside this acquisition plan.
