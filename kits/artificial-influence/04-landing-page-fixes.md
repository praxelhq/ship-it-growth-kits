# Artificial Influence — Landing page fixes

Public home and pricing inspected 29 October 2026. No account, scan, API-key submission or purchase was made.

1. **Make the promise measurable.** Current headline **“Be the answer AI gives.”** Add **“Measure what selected engines say for the questions you choose. Inspect raw answers and quality status.”** Do not imply the measurement itself causes a recommendation.
2. **Keep the proof boundary visible.** Current “Live Example coming soon” is honest. Retain it until a real permissioned scan is published; meanwhile label all demonstrations illustrative. Do not fabricate customer metrics to fill the section.
3. **Name the free scope beside Start free.** Add **“One brand, five questions, one engine, weekly checks. No card required.”** Verify actual entitlement before promising all four advertised engines on the free plan.
4. **Explain sample size and status.** Your page advertises confidence ranges and Verified/Provisional/Insufficient gates. Show the raw run count, dates, question text and what each label means. Replacement helper: **“Insufficient evidence is a result; we do not convert missing data into a score.”**
5. **Surface usage limits before paid choice.** Pricing says checks pause when included runs are exhausted. Add **“No overages. Checks pause after your allowance; see the next reset date.”** Test actual billing/allowance behaviour before claiming it passed.
6. **Make the action plan traceable.** Link each proposed correction to a specific answer/source and your confirmed business facts. AI-generated drafts need review; do not label an unverified generated claim a factual correction.
7. **Phone and privacy check.** Fresh flow: brand→five questions→scan status→raw answer→source→export. Mask client questions, keys and account details in analytics. Test interrupted scans and provider failure. No authenticated success was established by the public-page read.
