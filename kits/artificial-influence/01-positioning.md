# Artificial Influence — Positioning

**Live:** https://artificialinfluence.space/ · **Category:** AI visibility / B2B · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/artificial-influence

Your headline is **“Be the answer AI gives.”** **Start free** leads to /signup. [Pricing](https://www.artificialinfluence.space/pricing) lists Free: one brand, five questions, weekly checks and one engine; Pro $49/month and Agency $199/month. Paid plans have engine-run allowances and pause at the limit rather than charge overages. These are public offers, not a completed scan or billing test.

**Your live example is not live proof yet.** The page explicitly says it is coming soon and will use a brand's written permission. Do not turn an illustrative dashboard or an unverified scan into a customer result.

Your first ten are owner-operated B2B brands with a real website, a named category and five specific questions prospective buyers ask. Start with one category you understand. An agency managing fifteen brands can come later; Threadly takes a distinct agency-reporting outreach list.

**Pitch:** Check whether one answer engine mentions your brand for five real buyer questions, then inspect the answers and cited evidence.
**Short version:** Know what AI answers actually say about your brand.

The wedge is one evidence audit: chosen question, engine/date, raw answer, mention/citation and quality status. A score without those details is not a result a business can act on.

The manual alternative is a small permitted answer sample reviewed in [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/), available without a paid Workspace plan. Provider access may have its own cost/limits; do not call every API free. [Threadly](https://www.gothreadly.live/) has a $7 one-time trial with one scan; it is a real cohort alternative, not a reason to send both pitches to the same buyer.

Do not promise rankings, recommended status or revenue. Sampling, wording and provider behaviour change the answers. **Target: 10 real businesses**, their own brand/questions onboarded and independent recent use; free-scan visitors and ten brands owned by one firm are not automatically ten customers.
