# Artificial Influence — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup is an account; activation is the business's own brand/five buyer questions onboarded and a real audit reviewed with raw evidence.

All four customer tests apply: genuine business; actual brand/questions onboarded; independent use in the last seven days; privately known decision-maker name/phone. Payment is not required. Exclude demo brands, free-scan curiosity and multiple staff counted as firms.

## Day 0
Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and mask questions, keys, raw answers and account data. Record actual business accounts, configured brands, completed/failed audits, raw-answer reviews, quality statuses and independently active firms. Private values were unavailable, not zero. Save course rank, reading and sync at [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/artificial-influence).

Events: business_account_created, own_brand_ready, question_set_ready, scan_completed, scan_failed, raw_answer_opened, independent_return. A completed scan is not proof its estimate is reliable; retain status and denominator.

Sunday review covers source alignment, provider failures, allowance pauses and user decisions actually supported. Mention/citation changes depend on the question set and engine/date; do not attribute revenue or ranking improvement to the product without an appropriate separate study.
