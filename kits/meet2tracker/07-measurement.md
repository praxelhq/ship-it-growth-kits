# Meet2Tracker — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 distinct individual accounts.** Activation beside it: **one permitted meeting tracker reviewed/saved, followed by an independent later task-status update**. Track initial review and later update separately. Named action owners, tasks, transcript lines and sample rows are not users. One person's project tracker doesn't become a business customer just because several colleagues are mentioned.

## Day 0

Record actual accounts, successful review saves, corrected suggestions, later status updates, CSV success and independent returns from owner records. Sample 12 actions/75% completion/three due soon aren't a baseline. Capture current [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/meet2tracker) rank, saved metric and timestamp. No private account baseline was established here; unknown isn't zero.

The current [policy](https://meet2tracker.app/privacy) says Clarity runs on private workspace and text may be recorded unless masked. **Verify or remove private-content recording before real notes are onboarded.** Don't add a second script. Use synthetic transcripts to check actual recording protection, with transcript, task, owner, notes, source excerpt and account fields excluded. Internal aggregate events should never carry that content.

Proposed content-free counts only if supported: `account_created`, `review_started`, `suggestion_corrected`, `tracker_saved`, `task_status_updated`, `csv_export_succeeded`, `independent_return`, `pro_purchase_confirmed`. A click on export isn't proof of a usable file; a suggested owner isn't an agreed assignment. Count genuine Pro payment/access separately from checkout open and public ₹499 offer.

## Arithmetic linked to `06`

Assumptions, not meeting-tool benchmarks:

- 30 invitations × 50% try × 70% account ≈ **10–11 accounts**.
- Six posts × 200–700 impressions × 2% visits × 25% signup = **6–21 accounts**.
- One accepted session: 15 invited × 60% attend × 50% signup ≈ **four**, otherwise zero.
- One optional launch: 0–150 visits × 20% signup = **0–30 accounts**.

Rounded total **16–66 before overlap**, below 1,000. Half reaching review/later-update activation would be roughly 8–33 people. At an assumed 25% visitor-to-account rate, the target needs 4,000 qualified visits; this calendar doesn't provide that reach. Recompute after 15 invitations and don't call a private-club permission request secured distribution.
