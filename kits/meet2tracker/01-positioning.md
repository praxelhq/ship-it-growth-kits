**Your current extraction is rule-based, not AI. Sell the reviewed follow-up, not an imaginary meeting agent.** The real headline is **“Turn meetings into action, automatically.”** Supporting copy describes notes/transcripts becoming action items with owners, dates and status. Sign in and **“Sign up for free”** are public controls. The sample workspace's 12 actions, 75% completion and three due soon are explicitly sample data, not customer results.

It removes the tracker/monthly-character limits, not the per-file 5 MB limit. No account, parsing, export or payment was completed here.

## Your first person

An adult student coordinating their own small project, who already has permitted meeting notes and loses the follow-ups in a chat. They want to review the next action, owner and actual agreed date, then find its status before the next meeting. This is individual adoption, not a firm-wide procurement rollout.

> Paste permitted project notes, review the suggested actions and keep the agreed follow-ups in one tracker.

Bio: **The next action after the meeting, checked by you.**

Start with a short synthetic note, two explicit actions and one deliberately ambiguous sentence. The user reviews what the rules suggest, corrects the owner/date and saves only what the meeting actually agreed. Don't invent a deadline when the transcript didn't contain one. A later independent status update is more valuable than generating a long list once.

## Alternatives

[Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to ten collaborators per workspace and can hold manually entered tasks. A shared spreadsheet or the notes app someone already uses may require no new specialist subscription. Meet2Tracker must earn an account with a useful note-to-reviewed-task/source workflow and CSV, not claim teams can't track actions elsewhere.

Noteorious is a separate native Mac recording/local-processing product. You don't need to record a call for this first test, and your saved account workspace isn't a local-only transcript vault. Neither tool's sample numbers are evidence of your real adoption.

## The privacy gate

The rendered [policy](https://meet2tracker.app/privacy) says full saved transcripts and tasks are stored with the account. It also says Clarity runs on all pages, including signed-in workspace, and displayed text may be recorded unless masked. **Verify masking or exclude private content before recruiting real meeting uploads.** Use synthetic data until that is proven. Task deletion is in-app, but tracker/account deletion is request-mediated; don't promise a self-service wipe.

Keep **1,000 individual accounts** as the consumer target, with **one reviewed saved tracker and a later independent task-status update** alongside. Five colleagues named in one transcript are not five users, and a founder manually updating everyone's tracker isn't independent activation.
