# Meet2Tracker — Video scripts

Use invented project notes, owners and dates, labelled Synthetic. No real meeting, private participant, recording, account email or client decision. Screen capture and phone narration are enough. Demonstrate actual supported controls, not AI animation.

## A · From note to checked follow-up · 60 seconds · own feed / listing

| Time | Shot / text | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Synthetic ambiguous sentence. **“Who actually agreed?”** | “This sentence isn't a checked task yet.” |
| 2–14s | Paste a short synthetic note in actual flow. | “Meet2Tracker uses browser rules today, not AI extraction.” |
| 14–30s | Inspect actual suggested actions beside source. | “I compare each suggestion with what the note really says.” |
| 30–43s | Correct owner/date or leave unresolved. | “I don't invent an agreement. I confirm or leave it open.” |
| 43–53s | Actual saved tracker and later status update. | “The useful result is finding and updating this before the next meeting.” |
| 53–60s | Free quota/URL. | “Five trackers free, with a monthly character limit. Meet2Tracker dot app.” |

If parsing, saving or return fails, show and repair the failure. Don't present a perfect sample workspace as a completed user run.

## B · Three words that need review · 20 seconds · short clip

Hook: **“Action. Owner. Date.”** 0–8s, show those fields on a synthetic task. 8–15s: “The meeting might have agreed all three, or only one.” 15–20s: “Check the source before filling the blanks.” No automatic certainty or fabricated deadline.

## C · Rules, honestly · 25 seconds · product explanation

Hook: **“This isn't AI extraction today.”** 0–8s, show actual pricing disclosure. 8–18s, demonstrate the supported suggestion and review: “Rules suggest. I decide what to save.” 18–25s: “Judge the useful task loop, not a label.” Don't claim exact rule internals you haven't inspected.

## D · Thirty days, no renewal · 20 seconds · pricing help

Hook: **“Pro doesn't renew itself.”** 0–8s, show actual ₹499/30-day card. 8–15s: “This is a one-time period, not a monthly subscription. Final taxes appear at checkout.” 15–20s: “The 5 MB file limit still applies.” No purchase is needed for the clip, and no simulated receipt becomes revenue.
