# Thread — Distribution map

**Lead with a make-along, not an AI screenshot.** A small completed swatch earns more trust than a gallery of imagined garments.

| Route | Scheduled action / permission | Assumed reach and result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ten willing beginner makers | Ask people with yarn/tools already; observe one small project. No requirement to buy supplies for your test. | 10 trials, 3–7 attempted swatches; an assumption to validate. | #1 |
| Two existing craft/campus groups | Ask admins for one free make-along invitation each. No mass member DMs. | 2 × 30 relevant readers × 20–40% try = 12–24 sessions. | #2 |
| Four own-account Reels/Shorts | Photo → actual instructions → real stitches on Days 8, 13, 20, 26 at 19:30 IST. | 4 × 300–3,000 views × 0.5–2% visits × 50% start = 3–120 sessions. | #3 |
| Four [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/) pins | Your own process photos, honest labels and links to the free generator; no copied creator patterns. Days 9, 15, 22, 27. | 0–40 month-one visits, 0–20 sessions. Search distribution is slow and uncertain. | #4 |
| [r/crochet rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/crochet/about/rules/) / [r/knitting rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/knitting/about/rules/) | Rule-page fetches did not expose the rules. Ask moderators whether a tested beginner-pattern experiment is permitted. Do not assume AI/promotion is accepted. | Zero baseline; no unapproved public post. | #5 |
| [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Optional maker-feedback listing after a usable demonstration and current Monday-slot check. No vote requests. | 0–20 sessions, mostly builder feedback rather than committed makers. | #6 |

No newsletter acceptance, Discord invite or paid creator collaboration is assumed. The useful accounts to reply to are makers discussing a stitch problem you can genuinely answer; don't hijack their work with a link.

These tests may produce tens or low hundreds. At 50% visitor-to-generator-start, 1,000 needs 2,000 distinct relevant visits. More important: if fewer than half of observed beginners can follow the first rows, fix the pattern before widening reach. A visually persuasive output that wastes yarn is a product problem.
