# Thread — Video scripts

Use your own inspiration photo and materials you already own. Do not fake a finished object or hide corrections needed to follow the instructions.

## A. Real making demo — 60 seconds, site and Reel

0–2s, yarn beside output: **“Can I actually make the first rows?”**

2–12s, upload photo and select craft/skill/yarn. “I'm starting with a small beginner project.”

12–25s, read the actual generated instructions. “These are the counts and steps I need to check.”

25–45s, film the real first rows, with time compression labelled. “If I correct anything, it goes on screen.”

45–55s, show the swatch and any mismatch. “This is what worked, and this is what needs fixing.”

55–60s, URL. “Free generator. Pattern-pack checkout is test-only.”

## B. Hook — 15 seconds, Reel

0–2s: **“A pretty pattern isn't a tested pattern.”**

2–10s: compare an instruction with your actual stitches. “Start small. Count the row. Show the correction.”

10–15s: swatch and threadknits.in. No claim the pattern worked unless shown.

## C. Beginner hook — 20 seconds, Story

0–2s: “Don't know the yarn weight? Start there.”

2–12s: show the uncertainty option and actual yarn label. “The tool can suggest a starting point, but a swatch tells you what your hands make.”

12–20s: first few rows and URL. Text: **Use materials you already have · free draft**.

## D. Correction clip — 25 seconds, Pinterest/Reel

0–2s: “This row didn't add up.”

2–15s: show the exact instruction and stitch-count problem from a real test. “Here is where the beginner gets stuck.”

15–25s: show corrected instruction and result. “Making is the test.” If no genuine error occurred, use a different actual lesson; don't invent a bug for a hook.
