# Thread — Positioning

**Live:** https://threadknits.in/ · **Category:** Knitting/crochet tools · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/thread

The actual headline checked 29 October is **“See it. Make it.”** The CTA is **“Create My Pattern”**, pointing to `#/create`. The page calls the generator **“Free to use · No account needed.”** A separate $5 pattern pack is explicitly in **test checkout**, with no real charge. Do not report test purchases as customers or revenue.

Your first user is a beginner who owns yarn and a hook or needles but cannot turn an inspiration photo into a manageable first project. Narrow the initial use to a simple scarf, square or small flat accessory. A complicated fitted garment is a bad first proof of an untested pattern generator.

**Pitch:** Turn an inspiration photo and the yarn you have into a beginner-readable pattern you can check and try.

**Short version:** A first pattern for the thing you want to make.

## Your wedge

The value is not a convincing image. It is someone completing a small swatch from the actual instructions. Record the yarn, tool size, gauge and corrections. An AI pattern can sound precise and still have impossible stitch counts. Don't claim a photo can yield an exact replica, perfect fit or guaranteed result.

[Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/) is a free pattern/community database with free and paid patterns; its [free knitting bundle](https://www.ravelry.com/bundles/free-knitting-patterns-4) is a real $0 route. It wins on existing, community-used patterns. Thread earns attention through adapting an idea to the beginner's inputs, if the instructions survive a real making test. You don't need to claim every established pattern is hard or expensive.

## The honest weakness

The public creation route was fetched, but this research did not generate or knit a pattern. Do not use a finished object photo as “made with Thread” unless someone actually followed that output. Use your own inspiration photo or an image you have permission to demonstrate.

Keep the B2C target at 1,000 meaningful generator sessions. Report completed patterns and first swatches beside it. The proposed $5 pack remains a test-mode offer until production checkout and fulfilment are verified. Your first month should prove instructions, not celebrate fake payments.
