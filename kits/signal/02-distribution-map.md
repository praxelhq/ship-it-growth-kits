# Signal — Distribution map

**Lead with safe, fictional examples until the upload promise is verified; then a small adult pilot.** Do not turn a privacy-copy problem into a larger data-collection experiment.

These are scenario assumptions, not historical results. The calendar budgets 10 adult testers, two permissioned groups, three owned clips and one optional maker update.

| Route | Rule and execution | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ten consented adult testers | Start with your own fictional/redacted material. Real uploads only after actual storage/provider/deletion behaviour is clear. | 10 × assumed 60% try × 50–80% completion = 3–5 completed tests; call them fictional tests until real use occurs. | #1 |
| 2. Two existing adult peer groups | Admin permission for one post each. No public profile rating contest or screenshots in the group. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–20% visit × 20–40% audit start = 2–6 starts, conditional on readiness. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned short clips | Show actionable bio edits, sample labels and data handling at 19:00 IST. Never rank strangers' faces. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 10–30% audit start = 1–27 starts if live. | #3 |
| 4. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) build update | Say what is sample and what is tested. A progress post is not proof the private upload flow works. | Assume 0–3 real audit starts after readiness; zero before. | #4 |

Even after launch, this supports tens of starts, not an evidenced thousand. Don't replace missing adoption with sample-page traffic.
