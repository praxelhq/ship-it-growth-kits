## #5 — Useful no-link checklist

Does the profile give someone one concrete thing to ask about? Does each photo add a different piece of context? Does the suggested rewrite still sound like the person? Those questions are more useful than treating one score as a verdict. Don't share someone else's chats just to get a better rating.
