# Signal — Video scripts

All examples are fictional. Never publicly rate a stranger's face or show a private chat. Keep the sample label on screen.

## A. Explain one change — 40 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“A number isn't a reason to change your profile.”**

2–12: Show fictional generic bio, then a specific rewrite. “This line gives someone an actual question to ask.”

12–23: Point to the explanation, not the sample score. “You should be able to understand and disagree with the suggestion.”

23–32: Show illustrative label. “This is a sample, not proof a real person got more matches.”

32–40: “Signal's upload/privacy flow is being checked before real-profile recruitment. See the current example at signalapp.me.” Use as the main preview recording.

## B. Specific beats generic — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Travel and food. What would you ask?”**

2–10: Replace with fictional “I plan a trip around one meal.” Voice: “Now there is a conversation starter. Not a guarantee, just clearer context.”

10–15: **“Fictional Signal example. Keep your own voice.”** URL. First owned clip.

## C. Sample is not proof — 20 seconds

0–2: **“This 84 isn't a customer's result.”**

2–12: Show the actual illustrative before/after label. “A sample can show the format. It cannot prove the product improved someone's dating life.”

12–20: “I'd rather show the reason behind one edit.” Return to the text suggestion. URL. Second clip.

## D. Before the upload — 25 seconds

0–2: **“Where does your screenshot go?”**

2–14: Show a simple actual data-flow explanation only after verified; otherwise show the sample-only page. “Before collecting real profiles, the product needs a clear processing, storage and deletion answer.”

14–25: “Use fictional examples while testing. Don't share another person's private conversation for a score.” URL. Third clip. No fabricated security badge or staged data deletion.
