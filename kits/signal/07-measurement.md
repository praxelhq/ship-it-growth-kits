# Signal — Measurement

**Board target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** For a real no-account audit product, count unique people starting their own functioning audit session after readiness passes. Report **completed audits with at least one recommendation the user understands** alongside it. A sample open or fictional test is not a product signup/activation.

## Day 0

Read the latest [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/signal), recording date, reading and rank. Record actual preview visits, test audits, real audit starts/completions, paid orders/refunds and whether uploads are enabled. This kit does not know those private totals; use **not measured** or **not available**, not assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on safe public pages. Mask or exclude uploads, images, message text, private scores and payment fields. Don't send screenshot content to analytics or make public examples from operational data.

## Separate preview from product

Preview: `sample_opened → sample_reviewed → feedback_given`.

Real product when ready: `audit_started → upload_accepted → audit_completed → recommendation_understood → later_change_reviewed`.

Use privacy-consistent aggregate measurement, not invasive fingerprinting. If you cannot identify unique sessions reliably, disclose that limitation. A higher repeat AI score is not proof of more matches or a better relationship outcome.

## Honest projection

The calendar's ten testers, two groups, three clips and one maker update support roughly 6–41 assumed starts/tests before overlap and readiness loss. They do not prove 1,000 real users. At hypothetical 25% qualified-visit-to-start and 70% completion, 1,000 starts needs 4,000 qualified visits and yields 700 completed audits. Those rates are guesses to test, not benchmarks or current evidence.

These are chosen learning rules, not universal performance standards. Save course snapshots with timestamps and never add overlapping 24-hour readings. Your most important early result may be preventing a misleading privacy promise, even if that means delaying acquisition.
