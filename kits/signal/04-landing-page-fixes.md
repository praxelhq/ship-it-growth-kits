# Signal — Landing-page fixes

Evidence: [live page](https://signalapp.me/) rendered 29 October, including upload, privacy and pricing sections. A separate raw request timed out, which is not proof the site is down. No private images were uploaded and no checkout was tested.

## 1. Finish and verify input handling before recruiting uploads

Current upload copy says screenshots are sent for analysis and not saved. Current privacy cards still instruct the builder to describe encryption and confirm retention/deletion. Those cannot remain side by side.

Until checked, replace upload CTA with **“Explore a fictional sample audit”** and disable real-image collection. After actual review, publish the factual provider/storage/retention/deletion explanation. Don't fill it with imagined encryption or a “never stored” promise the implementation hasn't established.

## 2. Replace score certainty with a useful task

Current H1: **“Your dating profile has a score. We tell you how to raise it.”**

Replacement: **“One clearer dating profile. Every suggestion explained.”**

Subhead: **“Review feedback on photos and written prompts. Choose what feels accurate and keep your own voice.”** The word “objective” overstates what subjective AI feedback can establish; use **“An outside perspective”** instead.

## 3. Fix the draft price offers

Current page labels prices as draft yet displays $0, $29 per audit and $79/month with purchase language. Remove paid CTAs until actual price, delivery and cancellation/refund terms are settled. Interim copy: **“Pricing is not final. Preview the sample; no purchase is being offered here.”** If payment is already enabled, check the actual product and reconcile the text before advertising it.

## 4. Keep illustrative scores labelled at the point of viewing

The sample and 61→84 before/after are not customer results. Add **“Illustrative example — not a measured improvement or match guarantee”** directly beside the numbers. Use one written recommendation as the main proof, not a larger score animation.

## 5. Make upload limits and redaction understandable

Current limits: six images, 5 MB each, 12 MB total. When enabled, explain total-size errors before submission and offer **“Use the sample instead.”** Ask users to remove other people's names/messages. Don't say no data leaves the device; the page itself says screenshots are sent for analysis.

## 6. Replace the empty FAQ with answers to real trust questions

Answer who sees inputs, provider processing, retention, deletion, what the score means, actual payment terms and failure/refund handling. Write verified answers, not aspirational claims. If an answer isn't known, don't ask for private material yet.

## 7. Test phone and link-preview privacy

Metadata: **“Signal — dating-profile feedback, with reasons.”** Description should say sample/preview until launch readiness changes. A shared audit link must not leak someone's photos or score without their choice. Test keyboard, upload errors and text zoom on a phone; avoid collecting private data in analytics or showing it in a public demo.
