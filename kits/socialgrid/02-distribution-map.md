# SocialGrid — Distribution map

**Offline merchant visits first, after the contact gate.** You need ten owners who send a real brief and return for the next one.

| Route | Execution and permission | Input / copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) plus local shop walk | Manually find clothing/gifting shops, then visit at an owner-approved quiet time. Never pose as a shopper to obtain an owner's number. Eight blocks × four distinct shops = 32 approaches. | #1 |
| Two known merchant introductions | Ask an existing contact to obtain permission for one introduction. No customer-list sharing or paid referral. | #2; 0–4 accepted conversations possible. |
| Owner's existing [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/) account | The owner publishes the approved post. You request separate permission before showing it as your portfolio. No forced tagging, password request or engagement exchange. | #3 |
| Your own [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) | Days 10 and 24 at 09:00 IST: show one permitted brief→post example. | #4; guessed 100–400 impressions and 0–2 merchant visits/post. |

Forecast assumptions: 32 approaches × 50% owner conversation × 75% suitable real brief × 65% independent return ≈ 8 businesses. The 10 target still needs stronger observed conversion or accepted introductions. No hypothetical group or newsletter audience is added.

The Days 4–7 local-retail window differs from BrandEcho's agency-editor list. MiloStudio targets café-owner brand voice. Do not send both offers to the same shop in the same week.

No broad directory launch is needed until an unknown visitor can contact you and receive a clear quote. Paid placements, designer hiring and boosted posts are outside this zero-acquisition-budget plan.
