# SocialGrid — Landing page fixes

Public page and #talk inspected 29 October 2026. Authenticated design delivery was not tested.

1. **Connect the main action.** Current headline **“Just say it. We'll create it.”** and CTA **Talk to us on WhatsApp** lead to a section saying the number is coming soon. Add a working owned business-number link or a tested request form. Until then replace CTA with **“Request a post through our working contact form”** only when that form exists. Drive no unknown traffic into “ask whoever shared this.”
2. **Publish clear scope before a brief.** Current pricing is only “depends on how many posts.” Add **“Tell us your monthly post count. We confirm the price, formats, delivery time and included changes before starting.”** A real numeric plan can replace this once the operator sets one; do not invent₹ pricing in the kit.
3. **Make timing conditional.** Current FAQ says usually same day. Replace with **“Each accepted request shows an agreed ready time. Urgent work is confirmed before we accept it.”** Show actual capacity rather than imply an unlimited design team.
4. **Explain the approval boundary.** Current “You share it” is good. Add **“Nothing posts to Instagram automatically. Check price, dates and spelling, then download and share.”** Only claim this if actual workflow matches it.
5. **Prove a merchant-specific result.** Current sample designs are not evidence of real clients. Show one permitted brief/photo→finished post with its approved caption. Label illustrations; don't turn sample names into testimonials.
6. **Phone and language check.** Test message/voice-note intake and revision instructions in Hindi and English on a fresh 360 px phone. Ensure status, quote and download action remain clear. Do not claim an observed mobile bug from a text fetch.
7. **Share preview and trust.** The fetched metadata did not establish a usable preview image. Add one actual sample design, a short description of human production and the working contact action. Explain storage/deletion of business photos and voice notes in terms matching actual practice.

Gate: a stranger can send a brief, receive a quote/status, request a correction and obtain an approved file without knowing the founder personally.
