# SocialGrid — Positioning

**Live:** https://socialgrid.dev/ · **Category:** Merchant content service · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/socialgrid

Your headline is **“Just say it. We'll create it.”** The main CTA is **Talk to us on WhatsApp**. But that link lands at #talk, where the page explicitly says the WhatsApp number is coming soon and asks visitors to contact whoever shared the link. **A stranger cannot follow your main promise into a public WhatsApp conversation today. Fix that before promotion.** This is a conversion gap, not a down domain.

The offer is human-designed Instagram posts/captions from Hindi or English messages/voice notes. Pricing is quoted according to post volume; no numeric plan or free trial is published. “Usually the same day” is a delivery claim to confirm operationally, not a promise to repeat for every request.

Your first ten are nearby owner-operated clothing/gifting shops with real phone photos and a weekly offer, whose owner can use WhatsApp but will not learn a design tool. Pick one category and walking radius. Avoid creators asking for an AI video engine; CreatorForge serves a different job.

**Pitch:** Send your shop's offer and photos by message; review a finished Instagram post and caption from a person.
**Short version:** Your shop's next post, from a Hindi or English voice note.

[Canva Free](https://www.canva.com/education/students/) is a real free design tier; its official page verifies the tier exists, not free access to every premium asset. The merchant can also edit a photo in their existing phone workflow. Your edge is doing the work, understanding the offer and making corrections. Do not claim all templates or DIY tools are paid.

**Target: 10 real businesses**, with their own brief/photos onboarded and independent recent use. Ten sample likes or one merchant's ten posts are not ten customers. Capacity and actual delivery quality are the constraints.
