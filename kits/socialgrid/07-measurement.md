# SocialGrid — Measurement

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, per [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Signup/request means a merchant reaches intake; activation means its own photo/offer brief results in a reviewed usable post/caption.

Count only genuine businesses, their real materials onboarded, independent use in the last seven days, and a privately known owner name/phone. Payment is not required, but likes, sample downloads and the founder inventing a brief on the merchant's behalf do not count.

## Day 0
Verify existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) before adding a duplicate tag. Check public contact clicks; mask photos, voice notes, contact details and private briefs. Record enquiries received, qualified businesses, accepted briefs, delivered/approved files, independently returning merchants and paid agreements. Values were not available to this kit; record actuals rather than zero. Capture course rank, saved reading and sync from [your profile](https://shipped.money/p/socialgrid).

Events: contact_opened, real_brief_received, quote_agreed, design_delivered, revision_requested, design_approved, independent_new_brief. Count received only after real team receipt.

| Review | Input / decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 7 |8 approaches; prove working intake and at least one accepted delivery. |
| Day 14 |16 approaches;4 real briefs desired. Enquiries but no brief: clarify scope/price. |
| Day 21 |24 approaches;5 qualifying recent businesses desired. No returns: investigate quality or actual posting frequency. |
| Day 30 |32 approaches;10-business target retained. Report delivered/approved/repeat/paid beside count. |

The 02 assumptions yield about 8 active businesses, leaving a real gap to 10. Accepted introductions or improved actual conversion may close it. Never manufacture a second brief to turn a dormant merchant into a customer.

Sunday review: actual delivery hours, corrections per file, approval rate, owner independent return and acquisition source. A rising backlog with no usable delivered posts is not growth.
