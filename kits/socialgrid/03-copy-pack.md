# SocialGrid — Copy pack

## 1. Owner opener
Namaste — who decides what this shop posts on Instagram? I am building SocialGrid nearby. Can I ask what you wanted to post this week but did not have time to make?

You can explain the offer in Hindi or English and use your own phone photos. We prepare a post and caption for you to review; you choose whether to publish it. Before any work, we confirm the post count, price, delivery time and included changes.

If relevant, discuss one current offer. Do not promise a free piece or same-day delivery unless you can actually honor it.

## 2. Introduction ask
Do you know a shop owner who uses WhatsApp comfortably but keeps postponing Instagram posts? Please ask if they want to discuss one real offer/photo brief. I do not need access to their Instagram account or customer list.

## 3. Brief confirmation
Here is what I understood: the product/offer in your message, the price you confirmed, the offer dates and the photos you permitted us to use. Please correct anything before we design. We will not add a discount, testimonial, stock claim or customer photo you did not approve.

Before publishing: “Please check spelling, price, date and availability. Approving a design does not make an incorrect offer correct.”

## 4. LinkedIn
Some shop owners do not need another design dashboard.

They need to say, in their own language, “This is the new stock. This is the offer. These are the photos.” Then see a finished post they can correct and share.

That is the job I am building SocialGrid for. A person makes the design and caption; the owner keeps the final say. I am testing it with nearby merchants, one real brief at a time.

If you run a shop and have a post you keep postponing, I would like to understand what gets in the way. https://socialgrid.dev/

Publish this only after the public contact route works.

## 5. Repeat-use interview
What made you send the second brief? What was still easier to do yourself? Please do not make up an offer just to keep our app active. I want to know whether this fits your actual business week.

One unanswered approach gets one follow-up after seven days, then stop. Never reuse the merchant's private voice note as public marketing without separate permission.
