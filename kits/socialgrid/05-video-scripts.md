# SocialGrid — Video scripts

Use a synthetic shop offer and your own photos. Do not pretend an invented shop is a customer.

## A.60-second demo — merchant visits / homepage
0–2: phone voice note; text **“Say the offer. Don't design it.”**
2–15: say “Here is a sample shop brief: new cotton shirts, the actual price and the dates.” Show the photo/brief.
15–30: show the real accepted request status, then the completed design after the genuine production gap. “A person prepares this, under the agreed ready time.”
30–45: ask for one text correction and show the new version.
45–60: download and show manual share, without posting. “The owner checks the details and publishes. socialgrid.dev.”

## B.20-second hook — owner follow-up
0–2: blank post composer; **“The photos are ready. The post isn't.”**
2–10: “You already know the product. Explain the offer in Hindi or English.”
10–17: show the labelled sample post/caption.
17–20: “Review it before sharing.”

## C.25-second hook — LinkedIn
0–2: wrong offer date marked **“One digit matters.”**
2–13: “A pretty design with the wrong offer is still wrong.”
13–22: demonstrate correcting the date from the actual approved brief.
22–25: “The owner keeps the final check.”

Use captions, hard cuts and real status screens. Never promise increased sales, fake a response time or show a client's voice note without permission.
