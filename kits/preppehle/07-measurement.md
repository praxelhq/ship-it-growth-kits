# PrepPehle — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days** under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Count unique real accounts if that is the implemented flow. If actual analysis is usable without an account, the permitted alternate is distinct new meaningful analysis-session starts, clearly labelled and not added to the same people again. Sample/homepage views and upgrade clicks are neither.

Activation is **a completed analysis the learner checked against the source**, followed by a real practice attempt. The initial three-paper sample, 89% prediction card and sample question never count as another user's work. No grade/rank improvement or exam accuracy claim is inferred.

Keep real payment confirmations authoritative to the provider/server, not a button click.

## Honest funnel and reviews

Ten private invitations, one six-person session, four feed posts and six Shorts support tens of starts under uncertain assumptions. At an assumed 50% visit→meaningful-start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. No evidence says the calendar reaches that. A source-check success rate is a quality metric, not a future-exam prediction probability.
