# PrepPehle — Video scripts

Use material you may lawfully demonstrate, not private/unreleased papers or copied paid solution sets. Record a real extraction and keep mistakes visible. No paid editing.

## A. Check the evidence — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “A wrong extraction makes a confident revision list useless.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Authorized source paper and sample-status label|“This is real released material I'm allowed to use—not the dashboard sample.”|
|10–25s|Actual upload and extraction result|“First I compare the extracted question with its source.”|
|25–40s|One correction or verified topic match|“Equations, diagrams and repeated questions need checking.”|
|40–52s|Actual frequency list with counts/dates|“This says what appeared in these papers, not what must appear next.”|
|52–60s|URL and access limitation|“Try the checked analysis flow. No test checkout is a real subscription.”|

Don't fake a completed upload or hide an extraction failure behind a cut.

## B. Sample isn't yours — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “You haven't uploaded three papers yet.” Show the actual sample notice and empty recent-upload state. Say: “A sample explains the layout. It isn't your analysis or proof of traction.” Use a clear maker lesson, no ridicule of users.

## C. Frequency isn't prophecy — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Often before doesn't mean certain next.” Show an original count table with dates, labelled illustrative if synthetic. Say: “Use past questions to choose practice, not to believe an unexplained percentage.” Don't invent an actual exam's topic frequency.

## D. Test means test — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “This hostname is not a live purchase.” Show the current test-domain label without sensitive identifiers and the corrected disabled payment notice. Say: “Before inviting a buyer, state whether payment and access are actually available. A clicked upgrade isn't revenue.” Do not submit a payment for the video.
