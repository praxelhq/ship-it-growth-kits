# PrepPehle — Distribution map

**Lead with a verifiable worked analysis of released papers.** Students need to see the evidence and mistakes, not a claim you can predict their exam.

|Route|Specific use and boundary|Planning outcome, not benchmark|Copy|
|---|---|---|---|
|Ten adult classmates preparing the same actual subject|Five private notes Days 4/11, after sample/test-checkout labels are fixed. Use papers they may lawfully use; no private exam material.|10 × 50% start × 50% verified analysis = 2–3 activations.|#1|
|Existing subject-study organizer|One permitted six-person paper-review session on Day 18 at 18:00 IST. Different organizer/week from other learning offers.|6 invites × 80% attend × 50% actual analysis = 2–3 activations.|#2|
|Your LinkedIn feed|Four posts Days 8/15/22/29 at 09:00, original frequency-versus-prediction lesson and checked demonstration.|4 × assumed 200 impressions × 3% visits × 30% start = about 7 starts; zero possible.|#3|
|Your own YouTube channel|Six original demonstrations and one full example, under [YouTube policies](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/). No unreleased papers or copied commercial solution sets.|6 × assumed 200–800 views × 1% visits = 12–48 visits.|#4,videos|
|[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)|Working analysis/practice, explicit sample status and no test checkout marketed as paid access. Check current slot; no vote trades.|0–10 testers, optional upside.|#5|

[NTA's official site](https://nta.ac.in/) is a place to verify official notices/releases for relevant exams, not a promotional channel or endorsement. [ExamSIDE](https://www.examside.com/) is a verified free alternative; don't solicit its users in comments. A study-group bulletin can share #2 only after organizer permission. No coaching partnership or external newsletter placement is assumed.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after a real verified analysis works: personal maker account, check prior listing, no direct upvote requests. General exam communities aren't acquisition forecasts without verified current promotion permission.

Initial reach supports tens of starts. At an assumed 50% visit→meaningful-start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. Don't fill that gap with an exam's candidate count. Scale a worked example that actual learners can audit and voluntarily share with another legitimate study circle.
