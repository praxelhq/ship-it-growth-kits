## A month that adds up

The calendar schedules six clips (Days 4,8,12,18,23,27), three accepted sessions, four maker posts (Days 6,13,20,29), twenty opt-in invitations and one optional Peerlist listing. These scenarios give 61–140 first starts before overlap. Use 45–110 distinct people as a working range until measured. The privacy page says no account is needed to ride. Eligible first route starts can be the consumer headline; optional accounts, demo Pass unlocks, tips and affiliate clicks must remain distinct measures. This does not honestly support 1,000. The missing reach must come from an actual larger partner or stronger observed response, not a presumed viral multiplier.

Do not add views to signups. Deduplicate people who come through a clip, a friend and a live session. A forwarded link is an invitation, not an activation. If the forecast misses 1,000, the missing distribution must come from a measured partner or stronger observed response, not a new label for the same traffic.

## Contact and posting rules

Use your own public accounts. Reply where the discussion actually concerns this use case; disclose that you made the product. Do not paste links under unrelated popular posts, send automated DMs or add people to groups. For private campus or WhatsApp groups, ask the administrator first and send one invitation. Five individual opt-in invitations in a work session is a workload cap, not a platform-approved quota. Stop when someone declines; one follow-up after seven days is enough for a relevant organizer.

Prefer Days 17–20 for a shared adult leisure/creative invitation, coordinated with other browser experiences. A train-window break is a different job from racing or party games; still ask an organizer to choose one activity rather than sending repeated broadcasts.

## Directories, letters and accounts

Peerlist is the verified optional builder directory. WindowSwap is an alternative, not a promotion partner. No travel newsletter, railway fan group or official rail account has agreed to feature Window Safar. Do not imply an Indian Railways or IRCTC association. No newsletter placement, editorial acceptance or external community promotion permission has been established; do not count one in the forecast.

Paid listings, sponsorships, prizes and promoted posts are outside your zero budget. An editor accepting a story is upside, not a scheduled conversion event. When a page's posting rules cannot be read, do not infer permission from another creator's post.
