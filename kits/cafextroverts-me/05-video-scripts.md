# Café Xtroverts — Video scripts

Use test profiles and an authorized demonstration venue. Don't film real guests or publish a working presence QR to a broad audience.

## A. Two choices in one café — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Coffee isn't consent to socialize.”**

2–12: Show the actual mode choice with test data. “Order quietly, or opt into the social layer.”

12–25: Demonstrate email/presence steps without exposing a reusable code. “This is for people physically in a participating café.”

25–35: Show an actual test invitation/decline if the flow has been verified. “An invitation is a choice. A no or no reply ends it.”

35–45: “One café, one real experience first. cafextroverts.me.” Use in owner demos and approved venue material.

## B. Quiet is valid — 20 seconds

0–2: **“You can just order your coffee.”**

2–13: Real Introvert-mode walkthrough with test order, stopping before a purchase. “The social part is optional, not a price of using the menu.”

13–20: “Your café, your mode.” URL. First owned clip; no fake fulfilled order.

## C. Here, not everywhere — 20 seconds

0–2: **“A hundred scattered signups aren't a café community.”**

2–12: Show the venue-presence explanation without revealing the active QR. “The useful test is willing people in the same place at the same time.”

12–20: “Start with one participating café.” URL. Second clip; do not claim unconfirmed partners.

## D. Owner workflow — 30 seconds, horizontal

0–2: **“Your staff still run the café.”**

2–15: Show the actual ordering/control demonstration, labelled test. “The software supports ordering and optional social features; the café handles fulfilment and food payment.”

15–25: Show the real ₹2,000/month/location terms and current renewal statement. “A demo isn't a promise of sales lift.”

25–30: “Let's check fit before setup.” Operator-page URL. Send only to an owner who agreed to see it.
