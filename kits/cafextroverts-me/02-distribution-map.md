# Café Xtroverts — Distribution map

**Venue first, guest density second, broad reach last.** Do not send a public audience to an unconfigured café QR or imply a business is participating without permission.

Forecasts are untested scenarios for the calendar, not café traffic benchmarks.

| Route | Action / boundary | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Five café-owner conversations | Approach five cafés you can reach without paid travel; ask the manager for a 10-minute software demonstration, outside peak service. Show the actual ₹2,000/month terms. | Five × assumed 40–60% demo acceptance × 30–50% venue acceptance = 0–2 partners. Not guaranteed. | #1 |
| 2. One agreed in-venue QR display | Only after owner setup/consent, staff understand the experience and presence verification works. No table/ordering disruption. | If 100 actual eligible guests see the display over the experiment × assumed 20–40% signup = 20–40 accounts. Record actual footfall; don't assume it. | #2 |
| 3. Twenty invited adult friends/classmates | Invite only people genuinely able to visit the same agreed slot. No group additions or forced social mode. | 20 × assumed 40–60% attend × 50–80% signup = 4–10 accounts, possibly overlapping venue traffic. | #3 |
| 4. Two owned clips | Quiet/social choice and real venue entry at 18:30 IST; don't identify guests or advertise an unconfirmed location. | Two × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–2% qualified visit = 4–40 website visits; assume 0–4 actual in-venue accounts. | #4 |

This supports a small venue trial, not a thousand guests. At 40% signup, 1,000 accounts requires 2,500 genuinely exposed eligible café visitors. Your first partner and attendance need to be established before that becomes a plan.

## Communities and directories

Use the participating café's own approved noticeboard/social profile with #2 only after consent. A restaurant review page or city group isn't permission to advertise a venue event. No city-community promotional rule, café newsletter slot or influencer collaboration was verified, so none is in the forecast.

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) can document the software later but is not an alternative to venue presence. Skip broad launch directories in month one. Keep operator/guest contacts private; don't collect a café's customer list.

Your local warm-audience window is Days 4–7 after conversion/setup passes. DilliScenes, Kahi Chalo and ORBIT have separate host/friend-circle/building jobs and later windows. Coordinate actual dates; one organizer gets one primary offer per week.
