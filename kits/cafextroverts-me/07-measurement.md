# Café Xtroverts — Measurement

**Unchanged target: 1,000 consumer signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real guest accounts. Report **QR-verified presence plus the guest's intended completed order or mutually wanted social action** beside it. Keep ordering and social activation separate; choosing quiet mode isn't a failed social conversion.

The live product also sells a ₹2,000/month/location operator licence. Track actual participating/licensed cafés separately; that doesn't replace or inflate the consumer target.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/cafextroverts-me). Record actual partner cafés, eligible venue visits, guest accounts, valid presence passes, chosen modes, test/real orders, mutually accepted invites, reports and return visits. This kit doesn't know private totals; use **not measured**, not assumed zero.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) and exclude/mask email codes, profiles, table presence, chats and orders. Don't post guest/contact logs in this public repo. A valid presence pass is a system event, not proof of an interaction's quality.

## Funnel

`eligible_guest_exposed → account_created → venue_presence_verified → mode_chosen → intended_action_completed → returned`.

An order draft isn't a fulfilled order. An invite sent isn't an accepted conversation, and acceptance isn't proof of friendship. Staff/test accounts and repeated visits by the same guest don't create new signups.

## Honest scale

`02` budgets five owner approaches, one possible partner/display, 20 guest invitations and two clips. Its guest scenario produces tens of accounts before overlap. At hypothetical 40% signup, 1,000 accounts requires 2,500 actual eligible exposed visitors. Neither a confirmed venue nor that footfall is established by a public login page. Report the target gap instead of buying attendance or inventing a café network.
