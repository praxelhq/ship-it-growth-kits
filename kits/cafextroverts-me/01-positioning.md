## First guest and first enabling partner

Your consumer is an adult already sitting in one participating café, who wants either quiet ordering or an optional respectful table invitation. The enabling partner is that café's owner/manager, who must consent to the QR setup and guest experience. Don't treat every café listing as a partner.

**Pitch:** Order quietly, or opt into meeting someone already in this café.

**Bio:** Same café. Your choice to connect.

The wedge is one agreed two-hour slot at one café, with enough willing adults present together. Introvert mode must remain a real choice. A person buying coffee has not consented to a social invitation or public profile.

The alternative is existing café ordering and a polite in-person introduction, with no additional social-software purchase for the guest. [Notion's personal Free plan](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for a simple event/signup page, but cannot by itself deliver your presence-gated chat/order workflow. Win on real venue density and user control, not claims that stranger discovery automatically creates friendships or café revenue.

The roster's **1,000 consumer signups** target remains unchanged, beside **QR-verified guests who complete an intended order or accept a mutually wanted interaction**. Track licensed operators separately. The live B2B price is a second side of this product, not permission to count café payments as consumer users.
