# Farm profit — Positioning

**Live:** https://farmprofit.online/ · **Category:** Other · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/farm-profit

**Your ten are actual farm businesses using a reviewed planning workflow.** Start in one district where you can speak to growers and a qualified local adviser. Don't recruit anonymous consumers or promise a profitable crop to meet the business target.

The current headline is **“Know Your Potential Profit Before You Plant.”** The main button says **“Create My Farm Plan.”** The page asks for location, land size, soil, irrigation and budget, then describes crop-cost/revenue comparisons. No public subscription price was established. Its tomato/chilli figures are labelled demo estimates, its testimonials illustrative, and its what-if scenarios **coming soon**. Keep all three boundaries in the pitch.

## First buyer and offer

Choose a commercial grower already keeping input receipts and comparing two crops for their own land, or an authorized FPO planner working with consenting members. For the first cohort choose one of those buyer types, not both; an FPO with ten member examples is one business customer, not ten.

> Put your own crop-cost assumptions in one comparison, check them with local evidence, and see what still needs an expert's judgment.

Bio: **A transparent first comparison for a farm's next planning conversation.**

The first job is making assumptions visible: units, season, local input costs, expected yield range, selling-price source/date and costs left out. An AI “best match” isn't a planting instruction. Don't tell a farmer to change crop, borrow money or buy inputs from an unreviewed model output.

## Alternatives

[Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) supports creation with a Google account and can hold the farmer's manual cost model without purchasing a specialist planning subscription; paid Gemini features aren't needed for arithmetic. The grower's existing ledger and local adviser may be more useful than a glossy estimate. [ICAR](https://icar.gov.in/) is an official agricultural research source, not an endorsement of FarmProfit or a guaranteed free consulting appointment. No competing farm-software price was verified; don't invent one.

You win only if the comparison is easier to check and correct than the current ledger. The weak spot is consequential uncertainty: output can look precise while its price/yield assumptions are wrong. Keep a plain record of what is observed, estimated and missing. If the current app cannot expose or correct those assumptions, fix that before asking growers to rely on it.
