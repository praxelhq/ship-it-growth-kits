# Farm profit — 30-day calendar

Begin after a controlled private comparison works and a qualified reviewer has checked the method. Daily: 20 minutes replies, ten minutes private consent/source log. Four first business approaches on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 = **40 total**. Ten warm requests on day 4. One follow-up after seven days; stop on no. Visits use the farmer's chosen time.

| 4 | Four first approaches #2; ten warm requests #1. | 3h |
