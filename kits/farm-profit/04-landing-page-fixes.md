# Farm profit — Landing-page fixes

Public page read 29 October 2026. No private farm record or AI plan was submitted. The main action is a button; its complete save/share flow wasn't independently tested. No price was established from the public page.

## 1. Replace certainty with a checkable planning job

**Current headline:** “Know Your Potential Profit Before You Plant.”

**Write:** **“Compare crop costs and assumptions before your next planning conversation.”** Supporting line: **“Estimates aren't a planting recommendation or guaranteed return. Review local conditions and sources with a qualified adviser.”** Put this beside the first action, not just under a sample result.

## 2. Verify the actual first-plan path

**Current CTA:** “Create My Farm Plan” appears several times.

**Write:** **“Build a comparison I can review.”** Show what information is needed, where it goes and what can be corrected before saving. Test farm inputs → output → assumption edit → save/share using a non-sensitive controlled example. If any required step fails, stop real-user acquisition until repaired. A visible button doesn't prove a usable private plan.

## 3. Put assumptions next to every total

**Current:** sample investment ₹42,000, revenue ₹1.85L and potential profit ₹1.43L with a demo label.

**Write:** **“Demo only. Check area, season, yield, price date/location and included costs.”** On a real comparison show those underlying inputs and missing-cost warnings, not a standalone “best match.” Don't copy sample returns into another district or present them as observed farm outcomes.

## 4. Keep future scenarios clearly unavailable

**Current:** “What if market prices fall 20%?” is marked coming soon, with scenario figures.

**Write:** **“Planned scenario preview—not a current planning result.”** If an action is disabled, say why. Don't let a button make a farmer believe sensitivity analysis already ran on their own data. Retain the boundary in videos and outreach.

## 5. Replace illustrative voices with honest proof

**Current:** Farmer/FPO/adviser remarks are labelled “Pilot feedback · illustrative.”

**Write:** **“Illustrative concept feedback, not verified customer results.”** Keep that attached or remove the cards until real permissioned evidence exists. Useful proof is a reviewed input correction, not an unsupported “profit improved” quote.

## 6. Make units and sharing readable on a phone

**Current:** the workflow describes area, soil, irrigation and a shareable plan.

**Write near each input:** **“Unit and source required”**, with the actual supported unit, not an ambiguous bare number. Test 360px, large text, local language needs, slow-network recovery and a preview titled **“FarmProfit — a crop-cost comparison to review.”** No mobile or load defect was measured; test it. Explain real pricing before any paid action rather than implying a permanently free offer.
