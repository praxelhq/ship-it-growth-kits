# Farm profit — Video scripts

Use synthetic farm data, label it throughout and never film private farm finances or identifiable farmers without permission. These videos demonstrate assumption review, not crop or investment advice.

## A · The number behind the number · 60 seconds · grower/adviser walkthrough

0–2s hook: **“What assumptions made this profit number?”** Show a labelled synthetic total. 2–15s: zoom into area/unit and input costs: “A figure without these details can't describe a real farm.” 15–30s: show price source/date and yield assumption: “These need local evidence and qualified review, not a confident AI label.” 30–43s: correct one synthetic input in the actual flow: “Changing an assumption should visibly change the comparison.” 43–55s: show missing-cost/uncertainty notes: “This is a planning starting point, not a recommendation to plant or borrow.” 55–60s: “FarmProfit dot online. Check the assumptions first.”

If the current app cannot edit or expose an input, say that limitation rather than staging a fake control.

## B · Demo is not your farm · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“This tomato number isn't your expected income.”** 0–8s: enlarge the homepage demo label. 8–15s: “Different land, season, yield and selling price can change the result. The example isn't evidence about your farm.” 15–20s: “I'm testing whether the tool makes those differences visible.”

## C · Coming soon means not yet · 25 seconds · onboarding explanation

Hook 0–2s: **“Did this scenario actually run?”** 2–10s: show the what-if preview with “Coming soon” visible. 10–18s: “A screenshot of a lower-price scenario isn't a real sensitivity analysis for your inputs.” 18–25s: “Ask what the product supports today, then review the actual assumptions with a qualified person.”

Use captions and a readable crop of one field at a time. Don't film a farmer accepting a crop recommendation as a promotional success.
