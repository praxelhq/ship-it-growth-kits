# Farm profit — Measurement

**Target: 10 real farm businesses.** Activation beside it: **businesses independently reviewing a comparison built from their own permitted, source-labelled inputs**. A demo plan, a consumer account or ten farmers represented in one FPO-managed example isn't automatically ten customers.

## Day 0

Count real business pilots, reviewed comparisons, source completeness, critical input errors and independent returning businesses from your own records. The illustrative homepage numbers and quotes establish none of those baselines. Check existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) collection or configure it if absent, masking farm/financial inputs. Record actual [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/farm-profit) rank, saved metric and timestamp; unknown isn't zero.

Events: `comparison_started`, `inputs_reviewed`, `source_added`, `assumption_corrected`, `reviewed_comparison_saved` and `business_returned`. Don't collect private land documents, borrowing details or raw farmer data in public analytics. A saved prediction isn't proof an agronomic or financial decision was sound.

## Four business tests

Under the [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md), the customer is a real operating farm/FPO business, its own authorized actual planning inputs are onboarded, it used the product during the last seven days without your prompt, and an authorized responsible person's name/callable contact is held privately. Payment isn't required. An FPO and its ten represented members are not eleven customers without independently verified business adoption.

## Forecast and checkpoints

40 first approaches × assumed 30% conversation × 60% reviewed trial × 50% independent reuse ≈ 3–4 businesses. Ten warm requests might add 0–2. **Base 3–6 is below ten.** These are assumptions, not measured agriculture-tool conversion rates. Recompute after 20 approaches; trust and input reliability may matter more than message volume.

Review the first ten comparisons for area unit, yield unit, price date/location and omitted costs. Any critical mismatch is a reason to pause promotion and correct the method. Don't use a predicted profit increase as realised customer revenue, and don't claim a harvest outcome that hasn't happened.
