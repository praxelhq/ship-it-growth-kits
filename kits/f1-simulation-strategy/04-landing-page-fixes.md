# F1 StratSim — Landing-page fixes

Homepage, guide, scenarios and Dry Race entry inspected 29 October. No class name/account, decision, simulation or payment submitted. The rendered scenario gate matters more than raw extraction alone.

1. **Explain the actual model beside the hero.** Current H1 “Think like an F1 strategist.” CTA “START RACE” opens scenarios. Proposed support **“A simplified rules-based race model. Read the situation, make one call and inspect five simulated laps.”** Keep “Live Session”/“telemetry” from implying official live data. Proposed button **“Choose a modelled scenario.”**

2. **Disclose shared-name visibility before the gate.** Dry Race asks “Enter your name” and says calls use that name on a shared class leaderboard, despite header account sign-in. Explain guest/class identity versus authenticated accounts and what is published before input. Offer a permitted non-identifying display name if supported. Don't imply a secure private account from a name field; verify persistence and leave/reset controls.

3. **Reconcile base play, waitlist and Weekend Pass.** The site offers scenario links, a waitlist and premium-looking weekend buttons. Publish which runs are available now, what requires account/credits and the actual price/expiry/refund terms of any paid pass. No pass price was established here. Until verified, use **“Check current access”**, not “free unlimited” or a fabricated ₹99 offer.

4. **Make score arithmetic inspectable.** Guide lists five inputs out of ten but describes Strategy IQ out of 100. Explain weighting/normalization, randomness and limitations. Use **“Model/game score—not a validated assessment of intelligence or real race expertise.”** A replay should not hide changed conditions and make an identical call look arbitrarily right/wrong.

5. **Verify calendars without making them proof.** The homepage displays a next-race countdown and official calendar link. Check the primary schedule immediately before any dated promotion, then state zone. A calendar link isn't proof the simulation uses official current telemetry. If not verified, market the scenario without an event date or real-race prediction.

6. **Demonstrate a consequence honestly.** Authorized test: entry→read conditions→call→five laps→explanation→repeat/return. Show actual failure/error if it occurs. Check no-account progress versus cloud sync before claims; no fabricated run or leaderboard population. Builder availability needs its own test rather than inference from a nav link.

7. **Check the pit wall on phones.** At 390px, test readable units, compound labels, touch calls, simulation progress and back navigation. Mask identity/auth/payment and private class details from replay. Preview **“F1 StratSim — a modelled pit-wall decision and explanation.”** No official badge, real-driver image or invented result.
