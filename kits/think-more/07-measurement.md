# Think More — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, using **distinct new meaningful practice starts** for the no-account path under [the policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). A subject-page visit is not a start; count beginning a real answer task. Report optional registered/synced accounts separately and don't add them to the same people again.

Activation is **a genuine first answer submitted with feedback returned**, followed by an optional revised reason/application. A 20-character non-answer is not evidence of useful practice. Later voluntary practice is separate. These are usage measures, not proof of improved intelligence or learning outcomes.

## Day 0

Record actual starts, submitted attempts, returned feedback and later-day practice from your own system; private values were unavailable in research. Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) only on appropriate public surfaces with private answers/account input excluded. Record current [Shipped profile](https://shipped.money/p/think-more) reading, reporting period and rank.

Minimum aggregate events: `exercise_started`, `answer_submitted`, `feedback_returned`, `reason_revised`, `hint_requested`, `later_practice_started` and optional `account_created`. No answer text, voice transcription or identifying input in analytics labels. Keep written anonymous coaching and any actual signed-in AI mode separate; neither is assumed validated assessment.

## Honest funnel

Ten private invitations, one six-person session, four feed posts and six Shorts support tens of starts under uncertain inputs. At an assumed 50% visit→start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. At an assumed 60% start→genuine-submission rate, 1,000 starts produce 600 attempts, not 600 proven learning gains. Replace assumptions with your observed first cohort.

|Review|Record|Decision|
|---|---|---|
|Day 7|Starts, actual attempts, returned feedback, error/abandon step|If learners can't understand the question or receive feedback, repair before more invites.|
|Day 14|Later voluntary exercises and revision usefulness|If responses are generic, ask what part failed; don't market the gate as mastery.|
|Day 21|Feedback complaints, hint use, local/sync problems|Wrong or overconfident coaching takes priority over a launch spike.|
|Day 30|New starters, genuine submitted attempts, later practice, actual reach and hours|State the gap to 1,000 without a fabricated cognitive-improvement claim.|

Keep weekly aggregate actual rows with source and hours. Ellie clicks, difficulty changes, typed characters and a completed page view are not separate users or independently verified learning.
