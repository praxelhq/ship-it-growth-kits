# Think More — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.thinkmore.in/ · **Category:** Education · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/think-more

Your first user is a student who reaches for an AI answer before making one attempt of her own. **Sell the first reasoned answer, not a lecture about being less dependent on AI.**

Current page says “Think More,” “Less scrolling. More thinking,” and “AI can do the thinking. You should still do some.” “Try a thinking exercise” opens public practice choices. A browser check followed Logic→a real exercise: a 600-character answer field, optional hints and a disabled Submit until at least 20 characters. No answer or voice was submitted. The anonymous mode explicitly says it uses written coaching notes and keeps the answer in this browser; don't market that as a verified live AI tutor.

**Pitch:** Make your own short answer first, check its assumptions, then improve the reason behind it.

**Bio:** One attempt before the answer. A small rep in thinking for yourself.

The wedge is **one everyday-logic question with a concrete example**, not a broad six-subject tour. The checked prompt asks what evidence would test a causal claim about a study app. A learner can try it without a long reading assignment or an account.

The practice screen separates difficulty from feedback mode: Tutor, Timepass, Intense and Work affect how feedback feels; Easy/Medium/Challenging change the exercise. That is useful control, but too many choices can delay the first attempt. Give the first learner a simple default, then let her change it.

[Khan Academy's core platform](https://www.khanacademy.org/about) and [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) provide free learning resources. Your advantage is a short first-attempt-before-help interaction and an explicit reasoning step, not claiming to replace a course or prove intelligence gains. No paid Think More plan was visible in the checked flow; say “free to try,” as the page does, not “free forever.”

Your weakness is mistaking activity for learning. A twenty-character submission can be nonsense. Keep feedback modest and ask whether the learner can revise the reason or apply it in a new case. Don't equate a generated score with validated reasoning ability.

Keep B2C **1,000 signups**, transparently counted as distinct new meaningful practice starts for the no-account path, with first submitted attempts and later voluntary practice beside them. Optional synced accounts are separate.
