# Think More — Video scripts

Use your own answer and original scenario. No learner voice, answer or score without consent. No paid editing or measured-learning claims.

## A. First thought — 60 seconds

**Hook:** “Don't open the answer yet.”

|Time|Shot/text|Spoken line|
|---|---|---|
|0–10s|Actual question and anonymous mode|“Think More asks for one attempt first.”|
|10–28s|Type your original reason and example|“I'm giving the app something to respond to, not copying a polished answer.”|
|28–42s|Actual submitted coaching only after tested|“Anonymous practice uses written notes. I judge whether this helps me examine the reason.”|
|42–52s|Your own revision or application|“The useful moment is changing the reasoning, not collecting a score.”|
|52–60s|URL and optional-hint note|“Try one question. Type if you prefer.”|

If feedback fails or is generic, show that honestly rather than splicing a fabricated tutor response.

## B. Twenty characters isn't understanding — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “A long enough answer can still say nothing.” Show a synthetic non-answer labelled demo, then your concise actual reason. Say: “The gate creates an attempt. The test is whether the reason makes sense.” Don't publish a real learner's weak answer for ridicule.

## C. Difficulty versus style — 20 seconds

**Hook:** “Harder question. Different feedback. Not the same setting.” Show actual difficulty and mode controls. Say: “Difficulty changes the exercise; mode changes the kind of coaching. Pick what helps you practise.” No clinical or intelligence claim.

## D. One hint — 15 seconds

**Hook:** “A nudge shouldn't do the whole rep.” Show the actual hint affordance and your own attempt, only after verifying its behavior. Say: “Try your reason first. Use a hint when stuck. Then say what you think.” Never claim the app prevented all AI help outside the page.
