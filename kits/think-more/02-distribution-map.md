**Use an original question that invites an attempt before a product pitch.** Your proof is the reasoning loop, not a promise to fix attention spans.

Read [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/about) and [MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/) as real alternatives, not communities where you can advertise without permission. Your organizer's own bulletin may carry #3 once; no external newsletter placement or academic endorsement is assumed.

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is optional after a complete first-attempt/feedback path works: personal maker account, inspect prior listing and no upvote requests. No specialist subreddit is included as a promotional route because current permission was not verified.

Initial reach supports tens of starts. At an assumed 50% visit→meaningful-practice-start rate, 1,000 needs 2,000 qualified visits. Nothing here proves the calendar reaches that. Scale a question format that produces actual attempts and voluntary later practice; do not convert an educational community's membership into an impression forecast.
