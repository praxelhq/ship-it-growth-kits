# Think More — Copy pack

## 1. Private first-attempt invitation

> Before opening an AI answer, would you try one short reasoning question? I made Think More: your own attempt first, then hints/coaching and a chance to examine the reason. You can type without an account. Anonymous practice uses written coaching notes and keeps the answer in this browser; I won't call it a live AI session. Can I send a question for five minutes of candid feedback?

After consent send https://www.thinkmore.in/practice . No follow-up after silence.

## 2. LinkedIn question post

> A group uses a new study method and its marks improve. What would you need to know before saying the method caused the improvement?
>
> Make one attempt before opening somebody else's explanation. Give a reason and one concrete check, not just “correlation isn't causation.”
>
> That's the small loop I'm testing in Think More: attempt, examine the assumption, improve the reason. I'm interested in how you think it through, not a public intelligence score.

Use an original scenario when repeating this format; don't invent a real study or cite results that don't exist.

## 3. Organizer invitation

> Could I run a short, optional six-person reasoning session? Everyone makes a private first attempt on one everyday question, then we discuss what evidence could change an answer. Think More is a practice app, not a graded assessment. No microphone, public ranking or contact list is needed. I'd like a third-week slot so we don't repeat the cohort's other learning invitations.

## 5. Peerlist note

I'm testing the first-attempt gate and whether the response helps a learner improve a reason, not treating a minimum-length answer as proof of learning. Please try the anonymous typed path and report unclear feedback or controls.

## 7. Later-practice check-in

> Did you choose another exercise later, without my reminder? You don't need to send your answer or score. Was there a point where coaching helped you revise your reason, or did it just feel generic? That distinction is the feedback I need.

Send only to consenting testers. Ask separately before quoting an answer; a private learner response is not launch material.
