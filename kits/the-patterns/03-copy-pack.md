## #5 — Useful no-link response

Try separating the feeling from the story around it: “I feel worried” is different from “this must mean something bad will happen.” Name what you actually know and one small choice within your control. A reflective framework can suggest questions; it doesn't need to decide your future.

Use only where appropriate; don't insert a product link into somebody's vulnerable thread.
