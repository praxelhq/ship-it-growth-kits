## Your first user

An adult already interested in Vedic astrology who wants a calmer way to reflect on a recurring feeling, rather than another daily forecast. The narrow first job is putting one feeling into words and considering one practical next step.

**Pitch:** Start with what you're feeling, use an astrology-themed reflection, and decide your own next step.

**Bio:** Feeling first. Reflection, never prediction.

Your wedge is the feeling-first entry, not claiming a generic horoscope is secretly personalized. The page links four classical-text explainers; those references help a reader inspect vocabulary, but don't independently validate every generated interpretation or claim about personality.

[Ankiro](https://ankiro.in/free-insight) offers a free explained Moolank introduction and separately quoted human consultations. [AstroSage's free reports](https://www.astrosage.com/free/) are another $0 alternative, though their predictive framing differs. An ordinary private journal is also a no-additional-software-cost substitute. Win on an inspectable, calm reflection workflow, not stronger destiny claims.

Target remains **1,000 signups**: unique real users beginning a reflection session, with **completed reflections and a user-chosen next step** beside the headline. A homepage feeling typed without a delivered result isn't proof of activation. Birth details and private feelings are not marketing material.
