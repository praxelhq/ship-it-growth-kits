# The Patterns — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days.** Count unique real users starting a functioning reflection session, with **a completed reflection and an optional user-chosen next step** beside it. A typed feeling with no delivered result, sample article open or repeated session by an existing person isn't a new activated user.

## Day 0

Read the dated [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/the-patterns) traffic/rank. Record actual current users, session starts/results, errors, any paid access and return use. This kit doesn't know private totals or whether the whole reading flow is working; mark **not measured** until verified.

Verify [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) on safe public surfaces only. Exclude/mask birth details, feelings, relationship inputs and private readings. Use aggregate event counts, not the contents of a vulnerable reflection. If unique users cannot be measured reliably, state that rather than silently counting every page visit.

## Funnel

`reflection_started → required_information_understood → result_delivered → next_step_chosen → session_on_later_day`.

Record whether the user understood they could disagree and whether anything sounded like a prediction. A high return rate caused by repeated reassurance-seeking isn't automatically a good product outcome; look for understandable, voluntary use without stronger dependency claims.

## The gap

`02` schedules 15 invitations, two circles, three clips and one builder post, suggesting roughly 14–98 completed sessions before overlap under guessed rates. At hypothetical 70% result delivery, 1,000 starts would yield 700 reflections; current reach/readiness doesn't establish either. Report the board target honestly rather than renaming source-page visitors as users.

These are chosen experiment checks, not validated wellbeing measures. Do not measure success by a prediction “coming true” or a relationship score. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings are not additive acquisition.
