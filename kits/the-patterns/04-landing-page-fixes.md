# The Patterns — Landing-page fixes

The [public page](https://thepatterns.app/) and its source links/interface labels were read 29 October. Personal input submission, chart calculation and private result flows were not tested. No absence of a reading in a fetch establishes that the app is broken.

## 1. Make every feature honour the headline boundary

Current H1: **“Understand your patterns.”** Current promise: **“Reflection, never prediction.”** Yet the feature summary calls **“I'm Stuck”** simple yes/no questions for clarity.

Replace that summary with **“Explore the question behind a decision. Consider a next step; the choice stays yours.”** For compatibility use **“Reflect on connection dynamics”**, not a hidden relationship score. Test generated language as well as marketing copy before claiming consistency.

## 2. Label the arrow as a meaningful action

Current feeling entry uses an arrow with accessible label **“Explore feeling.”** Add a visible button **“Explore this feeling”** and short expectation: **“See what information is needed before a personalized reflection.”** Don't let a first typed feeling unexpectedly trigger birth-data collection or payment without explanation.

## 3. Explain birth inputs and processing before asking

The page claims exact birth time/place and sidereal positions. Say which fields are required, what happens when time is unknown, what is sent to providers and what is saved/deletable. Don't invent a local-only guarantee. A calculation claim should be checked against a named method before “astronomical precision” becomes proof in your pitch.

## 4. Make references inspectable

Four book explainers are linked. Connect a generated interpretation to the relevant concept/passage where actually supportable, and distinguish traditional vocabulary from factual validation. Replacement: **“Traditional themes offered as questions for reflection. You can disagree with the interpretation.”** Don't fabricate chapter citations.

## 5. Publish the real access and price boundary

No dependable public price was visible in the inspected entry page. Explain account requirements, free allowance if any, and the actual charge before the step that needs it. Until verified, don't say unlimited free reflections or list a made-up subscription.

## 6. Give failures a calm recovery

Test missing/uncertain birth time, unavailable result provider, interrupted session and back navigation with your own fictional inputs. A clear **“Your reflection couldn't be completed; try again or leave this here”** is better than pretending a stale generic message is a personal result. Preserve drafts only according to the stated data policy.

## 7. Keep mobile/shared previews discreet

Title: **“The Patterns — feeling-first reflection, never prediction.”** Description: **“Explore a traditional astrology-themed perspective and choose your own next step.”** Do not put private feelings or chart details into shared metadata. Test keyboard, text zoom, readable contrast and actual entry behaviour on a phone.
