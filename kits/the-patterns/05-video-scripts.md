# The Patterns — Video scripts

Use fictional feelings/birth inputs. Don't fabricate personalized-result behaviour; if a flow isn't ready, label its walkthrough a proposal rather than a live demo.

## A. Feeling-first journey — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“Start with a feeling, not a forecast.”**

2–12: Show actual entry with a non-sensitive example. “The Patterns asks what you're experiencing before offering a reflective perspective.”

12–25: Show required information/explanation in the real flow, without private data. “You should know what is needed and where it goes before submitting.”

25–35: Show a real supported reflection with sample label. “This is a prompt to think with, not a prediction to obey.” If the result isn't usable, record the entry/source overview only and say so.

35–45: “What is one next step you choose? thepatterns.app.” Use as the homepage/owned demo.

## B. Feeling versus forecast — 15 seconds

0–2: **“Worried doesn't mean doomed.”**

2–10: Split a fictional note into feeling, known fact and chosen next step. “A reflection can help you separate them.”

10–15: **“The Patterns: reflection, never prediction.”** URL. First clip.

## C. The yes/no test — 20 seconds

0–2: **“An app shouldn't decide this for you.”**

2–12: Show the actual “I'm Stuck” description and its revised reflective wording after it ships. “The useful output is a question or next step, not certainty about your future.”

12–20: “I'm checking that every feature honours the same boundary.” URL. Second clip; no staged relationship prediction.

## D. A source isn't a guarantee — 20 seconds

0–2: **“A book title isn't proof of every sentence.”**

2–12: Open an actual source explainer. “The vocabulary has a tradition. The interpretation should remain inspectable and optional.”

12–20: “You can question it and choose differently.” URL. Third clip; don't imply a text endorses the app.
