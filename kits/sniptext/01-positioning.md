## Privacy needs its full sentence

Recognition is local, but MLX setup downloads model/runtime and Pro activation/periodic checks contact Dodo. No-account doesn't mean no license code or network. Pixels/results are memory-only **by default**; optional history/export can persist them by choice. “Never written to disk” must not contradict those explicit options. Screen Recording is needed for screen capture; image import/practice needs no screen permission and copy-only doesn't require Accessibility per the page.

The footer's privacy destination is Microsoft's statement, not a clear own app/account/license policy. The project link returned 404 in this check. Both need owner correction/verification; neither means the whole site is dead. Don't describe a missing repository as proof the app is malicious or open source.

## Alternatives and target

Manual retyping or existing built-in image-text selection where available costs ₹0 extra software and may be sufficient. Don't claim every Mac/app supports the same native capture workflow or that OCR is unique. SnipText earns adoption through selection precision, editable review and clear engine/network/persistence behavior.

Keep the **1,000 B2C install/license target**, with first successful local OCR→edited/corrected copy and voluntary later use alongside. No account is required for free, so don't invent signup counts. Download clicks, website practice interactions and repeated captures aren't distinct installed people. Paid Pro activations remain separate from free installs and actual accuracy.
