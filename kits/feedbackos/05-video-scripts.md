# FeedbackOS — Video scripts

Use the actual sample demo and keep “Sample data—not customer results” visible. Never upload private support tickets to make the clip realistic. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · The issue still needs an owner · 60 seconds · design-partner call

0–2 s hook: **“Who actually fixes this complaint?”** Show a sample issue. 2–15 s: inspect the evidence/theme: “The AI grouping is a suggestion to review.”15–30 s: choose a responsible owner in the actual demo: “A named action is more useful than another summary.”30–45 s: show due/status/metric: “The result needs a defined period and denominator.”45–55 s: show sample comparison: “These numbers are illustrative. We haven't proved a real customer's improvement.”55–60 s: “Demo first. FeedbackOS dot org.”

Don't edit the sample into a fake live customer workspace or promise a feature the current demo doesn't support.

## B · Count versus rate · 20 seconds · own feed

Hook: **“Fewer complaints can still be a worse rate.”**0–8 s: show clearly synthetic orders/complaints for two periods. 8–15 s: “Keep the denominator and period visible before claiming improvement.”15–20 s: “A good dashboard should help you question the result, not just celebrate it.” No invented customer numbers.

## C · Demo isn't signup · 25 seconds · onboarding explanation

Hook 0–2 s: **“This is sample data, not your workspace.”**2–12 s: show the demo disclosure and proposed-plan labels. 12–20 s: “We're asking operators to critique the workflow. Self-serve onboarding isn't open.”20–25 s: “Know what you can use today before sharing business data.”

Capture readable source examples, owner and period controls. If a proposed loop isn't actually interactive, label the gap rather than staging a completed action.
