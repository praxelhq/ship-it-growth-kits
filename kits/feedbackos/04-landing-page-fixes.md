# FeedbackOS — Landing-page fixes

Homepage and demo read 29 October 2026. The product explicitly says signup isn't available and plans open a sample demo. No real-data workspace, analysis or payment was tested.

## 1. Make the release state the first decision

**Current:** “Analyse My Feedback” competes with “Try the Demo,” while the lower page says self-serve signup isn't available.

**Write:** **“Explore the sample demo.”** Secondary: **“Request a scoped design-partner conversation”** only once a real intake exists. Supporting line: **“Sample data—not your customer workspace.”** Don't drive a live-upload pitch into a demo. Test the actual intake and real private workflow before counting customers.

## 2. Narrow the outcome promise

**Current headline:** “Turn customer feedback into action.”

**Write supporting copy:** **“Review the problem, choose an owner and inspect what changes. Current interactive example uses sample data.”** Keep the useful loop without promising every AI priority or recommended owner is correct. The human operator must review the evidence.

## 3. Label every sample impact number

**Current:** sample complaints, growth percentages and a before/after 58% change illustrate the workflow.

**Write on every chart/card:** **“Illustrative sample—not a customer outcome.”** For real analysis show the reporting period, denominator and issue definition, and distinguish correlation from proof the action caused the change. Don't make a screenshot lose its sample label.

## 4. Keep proposed paid plans out of a purchase funnel

**Current:** ₹0 Starter, ₹2,499 Growth and custom Enterprise with many coming-soon features; buttons open the demo.

**Write:** **“Proposed pricing. Demo access only; listed future features aren't available to buy.”** A button labelled Choose/Analyse should not imply a real subscription or private workspace. Publish actual live plan terms only after release and billing verification.

## 5. Make evidence and ownership inspectable

**Current:** AI groups feedback, scores priority and routes suggested teams.

**Write:** **“Suggested theme and owner—review the underlying examples before assigning.”** Missing/uncertain classifications need a review state, not a polished priority badge. Don't claim direct integrations or automated intake that remains coming soon.

## 6. Verify real-data handling and mobile review before release

**Current:** public demo work doesn't establish a secure business workspace.

**Write for future onboarding:** **“Use only authorized feedback. Read what is uploaded, stored, shared and deleted.”** Verify the actual controls before client data. At 360 px test source review, owner change, period/denominator visibility and error recovery. Preview title: **“FeedbackOS — a sample feedback-to-action workflow.”** No specific clipping/load defect was measured.
