# FeedbackOS — Distribution map

**Design-partner conversations first, real-data acquisition only after the release gate.** A public demo can recruit feedback; it cannot silently become a live customer workspace.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed outcome | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · D2C businesses' own public sites | Manually qualify a business with a real support/operations owner and recurring complaint review. Four first approaches on ten named days = 40. No support-ticket scraping or fake customer complaints to reach the founder. Hypothetical 25% response × 50% authorized live pilot × 50% independent reuse ≈ 2–3 businesses **only after onboarding works**. | #1 |
| 2 · Warm operator introductions | Ask ten known contacts to request an introduction. No raw customer tickets, private phone numbers or review exports sent without permission. Assumed 3–5 conversations, 0–2 live active pilots if the gate passes. | #2 |
| 3 · [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) organizer route | Verified network, not a booked event or free group access. Offer a synthetic complaint-to-action critique, not a sales pitch for future Enterprise features. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, show why a complaint-rate denominator matters. Synthetic data only; no claimed 58% customer result. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 useful visits for a small account, possibly zero. | #4 |
| 5 · [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) | Check current Monday Launchpad rules. If publishing the demo, label it a demo/design-partner request and don't market self-serve signup. Optional 0–100 visits, 0–2 research conversations—not customers. | #5 |

Specific sources worth learning from are actual operator posts about packaging/service workflows and the prospect's own intentionally public policies. Don't turn public customer complaints into an unsolicited sales tactic or publish their identities. [Trello's official price](https://trello.com/pricing) is a checked alternative source, not a prospect list.

No newsletter placement, WhatsApp community permission or paid directory inclusion is assumed. If a relevant organizer asks for a free opt-in blurb, use #3 with the demo boundary. Base **2–5 live businesses** is below ten and may be **zero** if the release remains demo-only. At day 14 choose between a real scoped pilot release and more honest design research; don't relabel research participants.
