# FeedbackOS — Positioning

**Live:** https://feedbackos.org/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/feedbackos

**You cannot count ten live businesses from the current sample demo.** The page explicitly says self-serve signup isn't available and Starter/Growth open the interactive demo. Your first growth job is a truthful design-partner intake and a usable private workflow, not driving more traffic into sample data.

The actual headline is **“Turn customer feedback into action.”** The actions include **“Analyse My Feedback”** and **“Try the Demo.”** The [demo](https://feedbackos.org/demo) says it uses sample data, not real results. Pricing is proposed: ₹0 Starter, ₹2,499/month Growth and custom Enterprise, with many features coming soon. Don't sell those future features as available subscriptions.

## The narrow buyer hypothesis

Choose a small D2C operator with a regular batch of consented reviews/support feedback and an operations owner who can actually fix one issue. Start with **one repeated packaging complaint**, not every company and every feedback source.

> Review one recurring customer problem, assign a responsible owner and compare the complaint rate after the change—with the original evidence in view.

Bio: **From a reviewed complaint theme to an accountable next action.**

That connected workflow is a pilot hypothesis until the live product supports real authorized data. A demo task or founder manually analysing a spreadsheet doesn't establish independent business adoption.

## The actual alternative

[Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 up to ten collaborators/workspace and can track issue owners/due dates. [ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) can assist with a manually supplied analysis; don't falsely claim general assistants can't retain context or support structured work. FeedbackOS needs to prove its own evidence-to-owner-to-comparison loop is easier to maintain.

## The uncomfortable claim

The homepage's 1,284 items, product complaint counts and 58% reduction are sample figures, not demonstrated customer impact. A lower raw complaint count can also reflect fewer orders or a different observation window. Require denominators and period definitions; don't promise causation from a before/after chart.

Keep ten real businesses as the assigned target, but state that real onboarding is gated. Customers require their own permitted workflow, independent final-week use and a responsible callable contact held privately. Ten demo sessions are not ten customers.
