# DancePass — Copy pack

Use only owner-approved live classes. Never post a fictional date, unconfirmed price or “partner studio” claim. Do not say booking fees are zero when no current fee was verified.

## #1 — Instructor / studio business message

Hi — I'm building DancePass for workshop discovery and booking. Your studio appears in our public directory, which is clearly marked unclaimed; that does not mean you're a partner. Would you be willing to check the public information and discuss one upcoming workshop you actually control? I'd like to test a clear class page and booking handoff with you, not claim a partnership before you agree. We'll confirm current fees and cancellation terms before anything goes live. https://dancepass.in/

**Email subject:** Check your studio entry and one real workshop

Use the directory sentence only after checking that exact entry. One follow-up: “Closing the loop on the listing correction and workshop test. Happy to leave the entry unclaimed; factual corrections are still welcome.”

## #2 — Warm / in-person opener

“Do you run a workshop with places still available in the next two weeks? I'm testing DancePass with instructors in one local scene. Could we check whether a new dancer can understand the level, venue, price and cancellation rule, then complete a real booking? You approve the details. I won't film your class or contact your students without permission.”

Don't interrupt teaching. The instructor can decline the product while still asking for a factual directory correction.

## #3 — Instructor-owned announcement

My next workshop is listed on DancePass with the level, date, venue and booking details in one place. Please check those details and the cancellation terms before booking. If you aren't sure the class fits your level, ask me first. Reply if you want the confirmed class details and its booking link; I'll send them directly.

The instructor sends this only after their own class page is approved and booking works. They answer with the actual generated class link. It is not a generic DancePass endorsement, and no session is announced until its real details exist.
