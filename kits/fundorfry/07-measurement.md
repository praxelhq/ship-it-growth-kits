# Fund or Fry — Measurement

**Target: 1,000 signups in 30 days**, counted as unique real contributor accounts. Report **an original idea or constructive comment followed by a real reply** beside it. Digest-only email opt-ins, paid unlocks, founder test accounts and sample votes are separate.

## Day 0

Record latest date/traffic/rank on the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/fundorfry). Establish actual accounts, original ideas, non-self comments, replied-to ideas, reports, return days and any paid orders separately. The loading-state/zero-looking public counter is not proof of database totals. This kit doesn't know private figures; use **not measured** until checked.

The [privacy notice](https://www.fundorfry.live/privacy.html) says Clarity is inactive until configured and masks text/fields when enabled. Verify actual configuration before treating it as running. Don't log private email/contact-unlock data or unpublished drafts. Keep subscriber consent distinct from account creation.

## Funnel and service health

`account_created → first_real_contribution → non_self_reply_received → next_action_identified → later_day_returned`.

Track median wait to first useful reply, unanswered ideas, reports and moderator workload. Likes/votes alone don't establish validation; ask a small consented sample what test they can now run. Fund votes are not investment commitments or money raised.

## Projection and decisions

`02` schedules ten initial peers, two circles, three clips and one listing: roughly 9–57 guessed accounts before overlap. A confirmed session may overlap those users. At hypothetical 20% qualified-visit-to-signup, 1,000 accounts needs 5,000 visits—but also enough real reply supply to activate them. Current reach/reply capacity doesn't establish that.

| Review | Check | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | Initial real ideas receiving specific replies. | If most are unanswered, solve response supply before promotion. |
| Day 14 | Draft/publish/feed failures and reply usefulness. | Fix actual failures, not just a louder roast headline. |
| Day 21 | Independent replies, returns, reports and personal attacks. | Prioritize moderation over another acquisition channel. |
| Day 28 | Real contributor cohorts, unanswered queue and digest consent. | Keep the route yielding reciprocal participation, not lurkers alone. |
| Day 30 | New accounts, activated contributors, useful replies and target gap. | State actual capacity and next month's sustainable experiment. |

These are selected experiment rules, not a business-idea validity score. Save course snapshots with timestamps; overlapping 24-hour readings and repeated votes aren't additive users.
