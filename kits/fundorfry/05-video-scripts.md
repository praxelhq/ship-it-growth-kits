# Fund or Fry — Video scripts

Use your own labelled sample idea. No fake comments, votes, investors or public roasting of a person.

## A. One useful reply — 45 seconds, vertical

0–2: **“A roast should leave you a next test.”**

2–12: Show a clearly fictional/sample idea. “One sentence is enough to name the assumption.”

12–25: Show an original constructive critique, labelled demonstration unless actually posted. “Not ‘bad idea.’ Tell me what could fail and how to check it.”

25–35: Show actual rules. “Fund means encouragement, not money. Critique the idea, respect the person.”

35–45: “Free contribution after sign-in. fundorfry.live.” Record a live post/reply only after verifying it works with consenting testers.

## B. Better criticism — 15 seconds

0–2: **“‘Bad idea’ gives me nothing.”**

2–10: Replace with a specific assumption/test about a fictional idea. “Now I know what to do tomorrow.”

10–15: “Fund or Fry. Roast ideas, not people.” URL. First clip.

## C. Not money — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Fund is not a payment.”**

2–12: Show the actual rule and vote meaning. “It means someone supports an idea. It doesn't make them an investor.”

12–20: “No funding promise. Real feedback is the job.” URL. Second clip.

## D. Real first activity — 20 seconds

0–2: **“Don't invent a crowd.”**

2–12: Show current real state or labelled sample, accurately. “One original idea and one thoughtful response are a better start than a fake live counter.”

12–20: “I'm testing a small reciprocal cohort first.” URL. Third clip; never freeze sample figures as live evidence.
