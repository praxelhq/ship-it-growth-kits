## Your first contributor

An adult with a small project or everyday idea who wants a concrete objection and one next test. Start with five idea authors who will also give thoughtful feedback, not a large crowd waiting to be roasted.

**Pitch:** Put one idea in front of people who will challenge the assumption and suggest a next test.

**Bio:** Roast the idea. Respect the person.

The wedge is a closed, facilitated first discussion: one short idea, two specific objections, one next experiment. A vote without reasoning isn't validation. A laugh at the creator's expense isn't constructive feedback.

[r/SideProject](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) is a real existing feedback alternative; follow its current rules rather than assuming launch permission. [Notion's personal Free plan](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 and can publish a simple idea page. Your reason to win is real reciprocal conversation, not a claim that the internet lacks noticeboards.

Target stays **1,000 signups**: unique real contributor accounts, with **an original idea or constructive comment plus a real reply** alongside it. Fake seed votes, author self-comments, email-digest subscribers and paid contact unlocks are not additional activated contributors.
