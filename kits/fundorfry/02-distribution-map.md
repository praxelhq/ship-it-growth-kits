# Fund or Fry — Distribution map

**Build a small real feedback circle before promoting a broad feed.** Every new idea needs someone who will respond. Empty reach doesn't solve that obligation.

Forecasts are assumptions for the actual calendar, not established community benchmarks.

| Route | Rules / execution | Expected result | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ten adult makers/creative peers | Individually ask five to bring ideas and five to give constructive feedback, with roles allowed to overlap. No fake accounts or votes. | 10 × assumed 60–80% join = 6–8 accounts; activation depends on real replies. | #1 |
| 2. Two permissioned class/creative circles | One admin-approved feedback activity each. No confidential ideas or personal roast contest. | Two × assumed 40 readers × 10–25% visit × 20–40% signup = 2–8 accounts. | #2 |
| 3. Three owned clips | Assumption critique, Fund-not-money, honest cold-start demo at 18:30 IST. | Three × assumed 200–1,000 views × 1–3% visit × 20–40% signup = 1–36 accounts. | #3 |
| 4. One free facilitated feedback session | Ask a known organizer; count only after agreement. Five to ten participants, no event spend or member-list collection. | If held, 3–8 accounts, likely overlapping the initial cohort. | #4 |
| 5. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) | One eligible Monday listing after posting/reply/report flows work. No coordinated votes. | Assume 5–20 visits and 1–5 accounts. | #5 |

Base range is roughly 9–57 accounts before overlap; the session doesn't automatically add fresh people. A thousand requires a distribution and reply-supply engine not yet evidenced by this plan.

## Communities and owned digest

No newsletter/influencer placement is confirmed.

Your own Roast of the day email is separate opt-in under the [privacy notice](https://www.fundorfry.live/privacy.html). Don't add every account automatically or quote authors without the stated permission. Forecast zero until actual signup/configuration and delivery work. Optional [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) listing costs no ad budget, but don't buy Spotlight/Expert Fry or claim votes are backing.

Coordinate maker-circle weeks with founder tools and Make Belief. This offer is reciprocal idea criticism, not investor access or fictional-world membership.
