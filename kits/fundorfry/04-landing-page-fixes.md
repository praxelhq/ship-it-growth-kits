# Fund or Fry — Landing-page fixes

The [page](https://www.fundorfry.live/), rule modal, [terms](https://www.fundorfry.live/terms.html) and [privacy](https://www.fundorfry.live/privacy.html) were inspected 29 October. No idea, vote, email subscription or paid order was submitted. The feed remained in a loading state during the check; verify the real path before a launch rather than declaring a dead site.

## 1. Prove posting and replies before sending a crowd

Current H1: **“Great idea. Terrible idea. Let's find out.”** CTA: **“POST IDEA.”** Test your own labelled sample through sign-in, publish, another consenting person's reply, report and removal. If the feed doesn't resolve, show a real retry/error/empty state and fix the exact failure before promotion. Don't invent seed votes to hide it.

## 2. Explain Fund beside the button, not only in terms

The rule is clear: encouragement, no financial commitment. Put **“Fund = I support the idea. No money changes hands.”** beside voting and before any backing/contact purchase. Add **“A contact buyer is not a verified investor.”** wherever that optional feature appears.

## 3. Replace “no filters” with constructive boundaries

The current banner says no filters while rules ban harassment. Use **“Candid feedback on ideas. Respect for people.”** Prompt comments with **“Which assumption breaks, and what would you test next?”** rather than rewarding the sharpest insult.

## 4. Separate free participation from paid extras

Terms make basic contribution free and list one-time paid features. Add **“You don't need Spotlight or Expert Fry to post or receive community feedback.”** Keep actual price/duration and manual delivery limits beside any paid choice. No paid feature is part of this zero-budget kit.

## 5. Keep email consent separate

The page has Roast of the day and was checking signup state. Test configuration, confirmation and unsubscribe using your own address before advertising delivery. Explain **“Optional moderator-curated email; separate from your community account.”** Ask authors separately before quoting ideas/replies in mail, as the policy states.

## 6. Make drafts/publication consequences clear

The form says drafts save automatically. Add **“Draft stays in this browser. Publishing makes your handle and idea public; others may copy it.”** Don't collect confidential information or sell “private validation” from a public post. Test sign-in without losing the draft.

## 7. Use an honest shared preview

Title: **“Fund or Fry — constructive feedback for public ideas.”** Description: **“Free community participation. Fund means encouragement, not investment.”** Test phone keyboard, character limits and actual feed load; keep core post/reply controls usable before banners/animations.
