# Fund or Fry — Copy pack

## #1 — Reciprocal feedback invitation

Would you bring one small idea and give someone else one useful objection?

I built Fund or Fry for that exchange. “Fund” is encouragement, not money; “Fry” should challenge the idea, not insult the person. Browsing and contributing are free, with sign-in to post/vote/comment.

No confidential business details needed. A good response names an assumption and suggests a next test: https://www.fundorfry.live/

## #2 — Permissioned circle message

Bring an idea in one sentence. Leave with one test you can actually run.

We're trying a small Fund or Fry feedback circle. A Fund vote isn't investment, and a Fry isn't permission for personal insults. Give the kind of specific critique you would want to receive.

Free participation: https://www.fundorfry.live/

Please don't post secrets, private contact details or screenshots of someone else's work. Nobody needs to buy promotion or a review to join this exercise.

**Critique:** “This is bad” isn't feedback. “The idea assumes people will return daily—how would you test that this week?” gives the creator something to do. Fund or Fry: roast ideas, respect people. https://www.fundorfry.live/

**Fund:** A Fund vote means encouragement. No money, no investment commitment, no verified investor status. That distinction should be clearer than the button's emoji. https://www.fundorfry.live/

**Cold start:** The first community result is a real idea getting a real thoughtful reply. Not a fake vote counter. I'm starting Fund or Fry with a small reciprocal group before chasing a broad launch. https://www.fundorfry.live/

## #6 — One consented follow-up

Did your idea get a reply that helped you decide a next test? If not, was it no response, vague criticism, a posting problem or something else? You don't need to buy a review; that failure is useful product feedback.

Repeat posts should show a new original example and real learning, not invented crowd totals. Quote a real author/reply only with the appropriate permission, especially in the email digest.
