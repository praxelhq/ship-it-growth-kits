# Lynkr — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.lynkr.cloud/ · **Category:** AI / career writing · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/lynkr

**Fix the access promise before inviting anyone.** On 29 October the rendered page has “Start free” buttons, while its pricing section says there is no free tier. The [terms](https://www.lynkr.cloud/terms) describe a free interview, Story Vault, headline and first referral draft. Those cannot all describe the same offer. Test a fresh account and make the page match what actually happens. This kit does not assume payment or generation was tested.

## Your first user

A final-year commerce student who built a real dashboard, ran a society event or completed an internship, and now wants to write one sensible message to a person in their target role. They have evidence; they lack a way to explain it without “hardworking and passionate.” Start with students you can watch complete the flow. A recruiter, ghostwriting agency and established creator have different jobs and should not share this first pitch.

The visible hero is “Your LinkedIn co-pilot.” The supporting line, “Write to one person. Sound like you,” is more useful. The site demonstrates a sponsor dashboard fact, a fact-confirmation step, unknown details left blank, and a reason-to-contact check. Those are your product, not generic caption generation.

## The pitch

> Turn one real project into a factual LinkedIn profile and a message to one relevant person. You check the facts and send it yourself.

Bio version: **Your real work, explained clearly. No invented achievements or automated outreach.**

The wedge is the first conversation after a project ships. Interview the student about what they personally did, confirm three facts, and produce a headline plus one message with one clear question. Do not open with a referral request to a stranger. “I built this and have one question about your approach” is a better first job than “please get me hired.”

## Alternatives you have to beat

| Alternative | Checked price / what it does | Why someone would choose Lynkr |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ChatGPT Free](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) | Free tier exists; generic drafting is available without buying another writing subscription. | A reusable confirmed fact vault and explicit unknowns reduce repeated briefing. Demonstrate that difference using the same source facts, not an intentionally bad competitor prompt. |
| [Grammarly Free](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) | $0/month; spelling, grammar and writing-tone features. | Your problem starts before sentence polishing: what did the person actually do, and why contact this recipient? |
| A friend reviewing the message | ₹0 if a friend volunteers. | A repeatable workflow when that friend is unavailable. A human mentor can still offer better career judgment; do not pretend otherwise. |

Lynkr's published paid plan is **₹499/month including taxes, cancel any time**. Say the price plainly. A free first result, if the tested product supports it, earns a trial; a payment surprise loses it.

## The weakness to show rather than hide

Your drafts can be factually correct and still irrelevant to the recipient. A verified project fact is not a reason to message every analyst. Teach the user to read one public piece of work, contribute one relevant connection and ask one answerable question. Keep that decision visible before the copy button.

Lynkr says it does not scrape profiles, request LinkedIn passwords or send posts/messages. Preserve that manual boundary. The [privacy policy](https://www.lynkr.cloud/privacy) says account context is stored and draft text is sent to an AI provider; “nothing leaves your device” would be false. Use synthetic project examples in public demos and leave real résumés and private messages out of recordings.

The target remains **1,000 consumer signups**. A paid-only funnel would make that very difficult on zero budget; the first week's actual free-access and activation numbers decide the forecast, not an impressive reach claim.
