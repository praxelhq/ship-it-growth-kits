## The comment work

Follow posts from your own college's actual career office and alumni whose roles you want to understand. Choose people manually from relationships you can name; this kit does not invent accounts or guarantee a response. Add one specific observation and one relevant question. Do not turn another student's placement announcement into an advertisement.

On a post about project portfolios, for example, explain how to separate personal contribution from team contribution. The answer must be useful with your link removed. If the author asks what you built, disclose Lynkr once and let them decide. No engagement pods with classmates.

## Keep your month distinct

Your shared-campus invitation window is **Days 4–6**. SignalPost works on topic-to-post planning in Days 10–12; Postifyy's student clinic is Days 17–20; TwoCents has Days 24–26. If start dates differ, coordinate actual dates with the organizer. One organization gets one primary product invitation per week. You can support existing users any day.

Do not add Reddit or Discord to fill this table. No relevant community's current promotional permission was verified for this kit. A missing channel is better than telling a student to spam a real one.

## What the forecast really says

Three accepted clinics at seven activations each, five owned-feed posts at 0–5 visits, 30 warm invitations at 5–11 activations, and an optional directory launch might yield **roughly 25–60 activated users**, with overlap removed. That does not establish a route to the full target. Ask successful organizers for introductions to other organizers after the first clinic works; count those sessions only when accepted. If a network of organizers does not materialize, report the shortfall and what you learned rather than relabeling impressions as users.
