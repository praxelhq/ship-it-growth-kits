## The first business

A small independent design studio with one paid client job and an existing review process. The owner needs to know which artwork version the client reviewed and whether a requested change is still open. It is not looking for more clients, invoicing or an editing marketplace.

**Pitch: Review one client deliverable, its requested changes and the exact version awaiting approval.**

Short version: **One review. The right version. A clear next decision.**

That is the job to verify, not a claim the current release already delivers cross-device client approvals. Resolve the demo-versus-shared-workspace contract before asking a studio to send actual client work. A browser-local preview cannot deliver a real client invitation merely because it looks like a portal.

## The current offer is not established

No public price, free allowance, billing term or actual account entitlement was found. Do not invent a paid plan or a free-forever workspace. The public metadata's no-email/no-invitation disclaimer and the rendered shared-workspace sign-in require an exact release explanation: what saves locally, what saves to an account, what a client can actually open and what sends.

The studio should first use an owned synthetic deliverable. Verify save/return on the same browser and another permitted device, separate studio/client access, exact version association, comment/change states, actual approval and revocation. If there is only a local demonstration, market it as a design-partner evaluation and keep real client communication in the existing process.

## The alternative

A studio can use a free [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) log beside its existing approved file-sharing route: deliverable/version, feedback, next owner and approved state/date. A personal [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) page can organize a review checklist, without claiming paid sharing/team features are free. PortalFlow must earn adoption through clearer version decisions, not a mock dashboard that hides local-only storage.

Do not promise signed acceptance, payment, automatic reminders, secure client isolation or unlimited storage without evidence. A sample design, fictional client and preview approval are not a delivered paid project or a testimonial.

**Target: 10 real business customers in 30 days**, under [policy](../../TARGET-POLICY.md). Each studio must be a real operation, onboard its own permitted actual work/data, use independently in the last seven days and have a privately known owner name/phone. Report local-only owner use separately from verified shared client use. Sample evaluators, projects and clients inside a studio are not extra businesses.
