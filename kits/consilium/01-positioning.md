## The first buyer

Start with an independent India-focused MBA admissions consultancy already listed in your directory. Its owner can verify programme coverage, service scope, current pricing links and the person students should contact. Don't begin by selling a premium homepage slot to a global firm on the strength of traffic you haven't measured.

> Help applicants compare your actual services, then let them contact your firm from a profile you keep current.

Bio: **A source-linked admissions-consultant profile, without paid fit rankings.**

Your useful first offer is **check the free public listing and request a manual source correction**. Free directory inclusion does not establish free ownership or editing controls. The current Verified plan lists owning/editing the profile as paid benefits, and the published claim flow includes choosing a plan. Confirm actual entitlement and the billing period before offering an owner-control pilot. A factual correction request must not be used to imply the firm bought verification or sponsorship.

## Alternatives and the real difference

[Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/) is explicitly free and lets eligible businesses manage their public presence; it is not an admissions-fit comparison engine. A firm's own website is the primary source and may already answer everything. Consilium earns attention by making programme, services and published prices comparable across firms, with source links and honest gaps. Don't claim a particular consultant is good, affordable or successful from its self-reported description.

Your directory itself says results and fees have not been independently verified. A paid Verified badge checks business details, **not coaching quality**. Sponsored visibility must stay separate from fit ranking and review treatment. Never offer to suppress a negative review or improve a fit score for payment.

## What could prevent ten

Profiles may be a once-a-quarter job, not weekly software use. If a firm claims a listing on day5 and doesn't return during the final seven days, it doesn't meet the current policy's active-customer definition. Report claimed profiles and active suppliers separately instead of relabelling ten historical entries as ten customers. Show the target gap openly in `07`.
