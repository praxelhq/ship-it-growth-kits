# Consilium — Landing-page fixes

Public homepage, About, consultant directory, consultant plans and partner sign-in read 29 October 2026. Google authentication and payment were not completed. Public directory entries are not proved partners.

## 1. Make the business action distinct from student matching

**Current:** homepage title “Consilium” and the student-facing admissions sentence; the supply-side “Claim your listing” appears later.

**Write for the business entry:** **“Run an admissions consultancy? Check your public listing, then review the owner plans.”** Supporting line: **“Public inclusion is free. Verified ownership/editing is a listed paid benefit; verification is not a quality endorsement.”** Keep the `/partner` sign-in destination for the actual plan flow, and give manual factual corrections a separate clear route. Test real entitlement, unauthorized-claim rejection and a correction before promising self-service. Do not describe paid or unverified owner controls as free.

## 2. Resolve the billing-period contradiction

**Current:** the three plan cards each say $15/year; the lower paragraph says billed every three months or yearly.

**Write after confirming real checkout:** **“Your checkout shows the exact charge, currency and billing period before payment.”** Then make every card and term match that actual configuration. Do not invent a quarterly amount, promote a nonexistent discount or charge annual when the card promises a quarter. This is a conversion gate, not optional polish.

## 3. Put sponsorship separation beside each paid action

**Current:** “Rankings are never for sale” and separate sponsored slots are explained below the plans.

**Write beside purchase:** **“Sponsored visibility only. Your payment does not change applicant-fit ranking, review treatment or verification standards.”** Keep the Sponsored label on the actual placement, not only in the FAQ.

## 4. Keep unknown price and outcome data unknown

**Current:** directory cards include “Price on request” and some approximate prices; the directory says results and fees haven't been independently verified.

**Write:** **“Published price, source and checked date—or ‘Price not confirmed’.”** Link directly to the current official price where available. Distinguish a firm's claimed result from independent corroboration. Don't make an unknown price look cheaper through a score or a blank field.

## 5. Demonstrate a correction, not a prestige photograph

**Current:** campus imagery and comparison cards introduce the product.

**Write over a small walkthrough:** **“See a firm correct a source, and see what Verified does not mean.”** Use a labelled synthetic example. Show the review state before publication. A claim request isn't automatically approved just because a Google account signed in.

## 6. Check the phone comparison and share preview

**Current:** the page compares several consultants and displays a multi-factor matching explanation.

**Write on mobile:** **“Compare the services you need. Check each source before your first call.”** Ensure one profile's evidence, sponsorship label and contact action remain readable at 360px and enlarged text. Preview title: **“Consilium — admissions consultants, compared with sources.”** No mobile overlap or load-speed failure was measured; test instead of asserting one.
