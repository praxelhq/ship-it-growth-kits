# Consilium — Measurement

**Target: 10 real admissions-consultancy businesses.** Activation beside the headline: **firms that verify/correct their own profile and independently use the actual partner workflow**. Students, directory entries and external firm websites are not your customers by themselves.

## Day 0

From your own records, count unclaimed listings, ownership requests, approved real claims, corrected profiles and active partner firms. Public listing count is not an onboarded-business baseline. Check an existing [Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) connection or configure it if missing, with partner inputs masked. Record sessions, partner starts/completions, firm-to-student contact clicks and sources. At the [course profile](https://shipped.money/p/consilium), record the actual rank/latest metric/timestamp; this research didn't establish those values.

Events: `claim_started`, `claim_submitted`, `ownership_verified`, `profile_correction_approved`, `partner_returned`, `outbound_firm_click` and real `subscription_payment_succeeded`. A test payment or a click on a checkout button is not revenue. Keep student contact/application information out of public reports.

## What counts

Free public inclusion and a manual source-correction request do not establish free ownership/edit controls. The current Verified plan lists those as paid benefits. Confirm the actual owner entitlement before counting independent partner use; do not turn a correction email into a self-serve activation. If owner access requires a purchase, disclose that acquisition constraint even though the cohort policy does not require customers to be paying.

All four [policy tests](../../TARGET-POLICY.md): legitimate consultancy; its own actual profile data onboarded by an authorized responsible person; product use in the last seven days without your prompt; and that person's name and callable contact held privately. Payment is optional. Multiple consultants employed by one firm are one business. A paid badge does not waive the four tests.

**Retention risk is structural:** claiming a profile can be one-off. A day-5 claim alone doesn't qualify on day 30. Report claims and active firms separately; if the existing product gives no useful repeat job, acknowledge that the active-customer target isn't yet supported.

## Forecast tied to work

The calendar schedules 40 first firm approaches plus ten warm introduction requests. Under hypothetical 30% reply × 70% claim × 30% final-week independent use, direct outreach produces about 2–3 active firms. Warm introductions might add 0–1. **Base 2–4 is below ten** even if more profiles are claimed. These assumptions are not measured admissions-directory benchmarks. Replace them after the first 20 approaches.
