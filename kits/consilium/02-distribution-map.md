# Consilium — Distribution map

**Supply outreach first, demand education second.** Use your existing directory to find legitimate firms and their official business sites. A public listing is not a partnership or contact permission.

| Route | Action, boundary and assumed yield | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · [Your consultant directory](https://consilliumapp.app/consultants) | Manually choose firms with an MBA/India fit and an identifiable owner. Open each linked official site and use an appropriate business enquiry route. Four first approaches on ten scheduled days = 40. One follow-up after seven days, then stop. At assumed 30% reply × 70% claim × 30% final-week independent return, 40 approaches yield about 2–3 qualifying businesses. | #1 |
| 2 · Existing warm admissions contacts | Ask ten peers/alumni whether they can request an introduction to the consultant they actually used. Don't ask for private applicant files or promise a positive profile. Assumed 3–5 introductions, 1–3 claims, 0–1 final-week active firm. | #2 |
| 3 · Your own LinkedIn | Tuesday 09:00 IST, publish the difference between business verification and outcome endorsement. Use a synthetic profile example, not a public takedown. Assumed 100–500 impressions and 0–5 useful visits from a small account; zero is possible. | #3 |
| 4 · Your own campus admissions community | Only after organizer permission, offer a comparison checklist to students. It is demand research, not a consultancy customer count. One accepted session might yield 5–15 feedback conversations, an uncertain planning scenario rather than a booked audience. | #4 |
| 5 · Relevant professional follow-up | Firms already responding can receive a private walkthrough of free public inclusion/manual correction versus the paid or still-unverified ownership/edit controls, then sponsored separation. Ask them which qualified enquiries they actually saw. Don't send repeated email newsletters to all directory entries. | #5 |

[Google's Business Profile site](https://business.google.com/) is a verified source about its own free listing product, not a forum where you can advertise. The existing [Consilium directory](https://consilliumapp.app/consultants) names actual firms; verify their official pages before treating a description or approximate price as current.

No free newsletter placement, admissions Facebook-group rules or directory-submission rights were verified. Those contribute zero to the base plan. Don't pitch students through a school's private directory. Specific source worth following: Consilium's own linked firm updates and official programme pages, because those are the evidence your comparison depends on—not generic startup accounts.

**Entitlement gate:** free public inclusion and manual correction requests do not prove free owner controls. The claim forecast below assumes firms choose and can use the actual owner plan after its cost and period are confirmed. A manual correction request alone is not an active partner customer.

**Base risk:** 40 approaches can produce claims but only about 2–4 policy-qualified active businesses under the assumptions above. Ten remains the target. Improve legitimate return value—current enquiry information and corrections—rather than asking firms to log in solely for the activity metric.
