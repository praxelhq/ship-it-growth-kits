# Consilium — Copy pack

Write to firms using their actual public details. Don't imply partnership, quality endorsement, paid ranking or applicant outcomes. Change the first sentence and specific correction between approaches.

## #1 — Firm owner email / business DM

**Subject:** Check your firm's public Consilium listing

Hello — Consilium has a profile for your admissions business built from public sources. I'd like the responsible person to check whether the programme coverage, services and current links are accurate.

The public listing is free. If something is wrong, please send the current official source for a manual correction review. Free inclusion does not mean free ownership or profile-editing controls: the current Verified plan lists those as paid benefits, and we should confirm exact terms before you choose it. Payment never buys a better applicant-fit score, and business verification is not an endorsement of coaching outcomes.

The partner sign-in and exact plans are here: https://consilliumapp.app/for-consultants . Would a short walkthrough help? If this isn't relevant, reply no and I won't keep contacting you.

Only send “has a profile” after checking that exact firm exists. One follow-up: “Closing the loop on the public listing accuracy check. Happy to keep it unclaimed if you don't want to manage it; a factual correction can still be reviewed.”

## #2 — Warm introduction request

Did you work with an admissions consultant whose public information could be clearer? I'm building Consilium, a source-linked comparison directory. Could you ask the firm whether it wants to check its own listing? I don't need your application documents or private experience, and an introduction doesn't promise a recommendation or better ranking.

## #3 — LinkedIn educational post

“Verified” can mean very different things on an admissions directory.

On Consilium it should mean we checked the business details. It must not imply we audited acceptance rates or decided the consultant is right for every applicant.

The other boundary matters too: paid visibility is labelled Sponsored and stays separate from the fit ranking. A firm can't buy a better match score or pay away a negative review.

I'm looking for consultancy owners willing to correct their public profile and applicants willing to point out what the comparison still fails to explain. Current directory: https://consilliumapp.app/

## #4 — Organizer-approved student comparison message

Before choosing an admissions consultant, compare the exact programme, services, published price, assigned coach and what the contract excludes. A review score isn't proof of an admissions result. Consilium links its sources and labels missing prices rather than guessing. Would a short free comparison session be useful? No application documents are needed. https://consilliumapp.app/

Confirm a real session date with the organizer before announcing it. This is useful education, not a guarantee of a “perfect match.”

## #5 — Claimed-firm walkthrough / optional sponsored offer

Before choosing owner controls, distinguish the free public listing/manual correction route from paid Verified ownership and editing. Confirm the actual entitlement and billing period first. Your profile should be accurate regardless of any purchase. Let's check the public service links, what Verified means, and whether you can see the enquiry/click information actually available in the product. If sponsored visibility interests you, it will be labelled and won't change fit ranking. Before any payment, we'll confirm the exact charge, billing period and cancellation terms shown at checkout. No promised number of leads.

Use only the analytics the current account really exposes; do not invent a live click report to sell a plan.
