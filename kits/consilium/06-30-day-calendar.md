# Consilium — 30-day calendar

Begin after real ownership/correction handling works, paid owner entitlements are verified, and billing terms are consistent. A free manual source-correction request is not proof of free owner controls or an active partner customer. Daily: 20 minutes of replies plus a private firm/contact log. Four new first approaches on days 4,5,9,10,12,16,18,23,25,29 = **40 total**; ten warm introduction requests on day 4. One follow-up after seven days; stop on refusal.
