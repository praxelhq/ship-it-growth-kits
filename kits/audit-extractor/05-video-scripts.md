# Audit extractor — Video scripts

Use synthetic invoices/orders and no client identifiers. Show real output and corrections; label shortened processing waits. Phone voice plus screen recording is enough.

## A · Original, extraction, correction · 60 seconds · practitioner call / LinkedIn

0–2s hook: **“Would you trust this total without checking?”** Show a synthetic original and output. 2–15s: choose a text PDF: “This first workflow is for text-based invoices and orders, not scans.” 15–30s: run the actual extraction: “It gives me a first table, not an audit conclusion.” 30–45s: compare currency, date and total; correct one real observed error if present: “I check the fields that matter against the source.” 45–55s: export and open the actual workbook: “The professional owns the final review.” 55–60s: “Trial limits and test-payment status are on the site. Audit extractor.”

If there is no error in the sample, say so without claiming perfect accuracy; don't invent a failure to dramatize the clip.

## B · A scan isn't a text PDF · 20 seconds · onboarding

Hook: **“This looks like a PDF, but the text may be an image.”** 0–8s: show two synthetic files with the difference labelled. 8–15s: “Check the supported input before believing an empty table means no data.” 15–20s: show the actual unsupported-file state. Don't fake OCR capability.

## C · Confidence isn't review · 25 seconds · own feed

Hook 0–2s: **“A high score can still hide the wrong currency.”** 2–12s: show a clearly synthetic confidence example and currency field. 12–20s: “One critical field can matter more than the average score. Keep the original and correction visible.” 20–25s: “Extraction helps the first pass. It doesn't sign off your work.”

Record legible cells rather than shrinking twenty columns onto a vertical screen. Never show a real tax identifier or private invoice in a public video.
