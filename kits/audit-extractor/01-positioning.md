# Audit extractor — Positioning

**Live:** https://www.auditextractor.me/ · **Category:** SaaS · **Profile:** https://shipped.money/p/audit-extractor

**Your first ten are actual firms/teams using a reviewed extraction workflow, not ten PDFs or ten staff accounts.** Start with a small audit/advisory firm that regularly rekeys text-based invoices or purchase/sales orders into Excel. Choose one repeated document format before promising every client document.

The actual headline is **“Turn PDF paperwork into clean, reviewable data.”** **“Try it free”** takes the visitor toward the on-page tool. The page offers a ten-day free trial with five documents/day, $10/month Pro, $60/year Pro and $99/month Firm—but explicitly says **test mode, no real payment taken**. These are displayed offers, not evidence of live subscription revenue.

## Say this

> Make a first extraction pass on text-based invoices and orders, review every important field, then export the checked table to Excel.

Bio: **Reviewable PDF-to-Excel extraction, not an audit opinion.**

The useful first job is five authorized, redacted text PDFs of one type. The professional checks document number, dates, parties, currency, tax and totals against the original before using the workbook. A confidence score doesn't replace that review. Scanned/image-only PDFs aren't part of the stated supported scope.

## The honest alternative

[Tabula](https://tabula.technology/) is free/open source, extracts text-PDF tables to spreadsheets and explicitly doesn't support scanned documents. Don't pretend PDF extraction is an empty market. Your potential difference is structured invoice/order fields and an editable review table in a browser, if a real sample proves it needs less correction. The firm's existing manual Excel process is also valid; don't invent a paid competitor's price or claim audited time savings.

## The weakness to face first

“Audit-ready” can sound like evidence quality is guaranteed. It isn't. The site says processing is local, but this research didn't independently verify network behaviour or file handling. Start with synthetic/redacted documents and have the owner confirm suitability before client data is used. “Firm” doesn't establish tested multi-user permissions; don't sell team security from a pricing bullet.

This is not CA Deskk's filing chase or PBC Tracker's engagement-support matching. Your first buyer wants transcription they can inspect, not another document-request inbox. Ten customers need their own permitted documents onboarded, independent recent use and a responsible contact you can call privately.
