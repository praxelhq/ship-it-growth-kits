## #1 — Permissioned cricket-group invitation

I made a small Mac app for the score-tab habit. It keeps one cricket match beside the notch while you work, so you can leave your document open.

It's free to try: https://12thman.live/

Two honest limits: this build isn't Apple-verified yet, and the score feed can lag by up to 15 minutes. Please don't install it on a company Mac against your IT policy. If you try it on a personal Mac, tell me where you got stuck and whether you followed a whole session. No need to buy Pro.
