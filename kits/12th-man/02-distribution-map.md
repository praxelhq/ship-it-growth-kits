## Earned channels without invented access

[Product Hunt's guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) is the verified directory route. Submit only if you can support the build and have not already launched the same version. A launch costs no advertising budget; paid extras are excluded. Expect 0–20 launches until you have account-specific evidence. Reuse #3 with the precise free/Pro distinction.

No newsletter placement or specific influencer response is confirmed. Start with the actual maker posts in r/macapps: answer a notch/score question thoughtfully, disclose your relationship when relevant, and don't drop a competing link under another developer's launch. That is a specific account-discovery route, not a fabricated endorsement.

Coordinate with Fan Notch and Munchy Mates: your campus/demo invitation window is Days 4–7. A community's rules still outrank the cohort schedule. Do not ask the same organizer to promote all three apps in one week.
