# 12th Man — Landing-page fixes

Evidence: [public page](https://12thman.live/) and [install guide](https://12thman.live/install), opened 29 October. The download CTA resolves to `/install?download=1`; the guide links to `/download`. The binary was not installed or audited. Do not label the path broken merely because installation has not been tested.

## 1. Put the real tradeoff beside the primary action

Current: **Download for Mac (free)**, with Apple-verification and refresh disclosures further down.

Keep the CTA and add: **“Early Mac build. Apple verification pending. Scores may be up to 15 minutes behind. Check compatibility before buying Pro.”** Link **“Installation and build details”** to the existing guide. Do not claim the warning is proof of safety; signing/notarization and a verifiable build are the durable fix.

Watch five first launches on personal Macs. If any cannot complete the supported install path, pause outreach and fix/document that exact case. Never instruct someone to defeat company device controls.

## 2. Make the first screen the job, not the whole cricket category

Current H1: **“12th Man”**. The description explains a free Mac app showing one score beside the notch.

Headline replacement: **“Keep the score. Keep your document open.”**

Subhead: **“Follow one cricket match at the top of your Mac screen. Free to try, with optional alerts and styles in Pro.”**

Place a short real screen recording beside it: document, notch score, a match selection. Preserve the web demo's **not real match events** label. No simulated score movement presented as live proof.

## 3. Separate demo, download and paid upgrade

The page currently offers a match-picker demo plus Pro. Label the picker **“Try the website preview”**, the app action **“Download the free Mac app”**, and the upgrade **“See Pro coverage and price”**. Publish the exact current amount, licence duration and refund terms before enabling a paid pitch; do not write an amount the reader cannot verify.

## 4. Show freshness, not just a score

Add **“Last updated at 18:42 IST · delayed feed”** using the real update timestamp, not this illustrative time. When stale, show the age clearly. This helps a fan judge whether another source is needed. Do not use a green “live” indicator to imply instant data.

## 5. Shorten first-launch help without weakening trust

The guide has seven detailed steps. Add a visible three-stage overview: **“Download → move to Applications → review macOS's first-open prompt.”** Keep the complete instructions below. Add tested OS/architecture requirements from the actual build; the kit cannot infer them. Ensure the published checksum link returns an actual digest, not only instructions to find one.

## 6. Make a phone visitor useful without pretending it is an iPhone app

Show **“This is a Mac app. Copy the link and open it on your Mac.”** Offer a copy-link action without collecting an email unnecessarily. Test it in WhatsApp's phone browser; don't assert a mobile rendering defect without seeing one.

## 7. Check the preview and load path

The current metadata names live cricket and has an image. Use title **“12th Man — a free cricket score beside your Mac's notch”** and description **“Follow one match while you work. Mac app; delayed score feed. Try free before Pro.”** Test the actual shared card and compress the demo, keeping download/install text usable before video loads.
