# Solo Deleveling — Video scripts

Screen-record your own real attempts after verifying controls. Do not use the existing browser's saved progress as your own achievement. A phone camera can film a laptop session if touch play is not verified; label keyboard play.

## A. The obvious jump — 30 seconds, homepage/game invite

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Actual opening-room setup. | “That looks like the obvious jump.” |
| 3–13 s | One normal-speed actual attempt, outcome unedited. | “Solo Deleveling puts an exit in the room and gives you reasons to rethink the route.” |
| 13–23 s | Actual restart and second attempt. | “Try it, see what fooled you and go again.” |
| 23–30 s | URL/verified controls. | “Thirty rooms. Start with one browser attempt.” |

## B. Retry — 15 seconds, Short

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Your actual failed attempt. | “One more try.” |
| 2–10 s | Actual quick restart/retry. | “Same room. A different decision.” |
| 10–15 s | URL. | “Try Solo Deleveling.” |

## C. Fair or confusing? — 20 seconds, maker post

| Time | Shot/text | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Your actual input/room. | “Was that surprise fair?” |
| 3–14 s | Actual confusing moment and restart. | “I want a failed attempt to teach the room, not leave you guessing what the controls did.” |
| 14–20 s | URL. | “Try the opening and tell me where it gets unclear.” |

Show actual losses without exaggerating distress. No fabricated completion, rage reaction, edited speedrun record or untested touch demonstration.
