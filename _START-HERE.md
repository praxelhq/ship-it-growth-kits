# START HERE — how to use your Growth Kit

Find your folder in the [complete project index](README.md). Read the readiness gates in your own 01/04 before starting outreach; some products need recovery first.

You shipped a product. Almost nobody has used it. This kit is the thirty days that fixes that.

Zero budget. Organic only. No ads, no influencer payments, no tools you have to buy.

Your target depends on who you sell to:

- **Selling to consumers → 1,000 signups in 30 days.**
- **Selling to businesses → 10 real customers in 30 days.**

Ten is not a typo, and it is not easy. Read [`TARGET-POLICY.md`](TARGET-POLICY.md) once before you start — it defines which one you are and what counts. Your `07-measurement.md` already has your number in it.

---

## The one idea behind every kit

You do not need the whole internet to like your product. You need to find the people who already have the exact problem you solved, in the places they already hang out, and not waste a single post on anyone else. For a consumer app that's a few hundred of them; for a business product it can be twenty phone numbers.

That is why every kit starts with **positioning** (who, exactly) and only then goes to **channels** (where they are) and **copy** (what to say there). Skip the first two and the third is just noise.

## The rules that make this work

**1. Fix the landing page before you drive traffic.**
1,000 visitors at 2% conversion is 20 signups. The same 1,000 at 12% is 120. The conversion fixes in `04` are worth more than any extra channel, and they take an afternoon. Every kit front-loads them in week 1 for this reason.

**2. Post as a person, not as a product.**
Reddit, Discord and most communities will remove a post that reads like an ad, and some will ban the domain so you can never post it again. Every piece of copy in `03` is written to survive that. Read the posting rules column in `02` before you post anywhere — "no self-promo outside the Saturday thread" is a real rule and breaking it costs you the channel permanently.

**3. One channel per day, properly.**
Ten half-posts across ten communities beats nothing, but one good post in the right place plus two hours of replying to every comment beats all ten. The calendar in `06` is deliberately one or two things a day.

**4. Reply to everything for the first four hours.**
On Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt and Peerlist, early comment velocity decides whether the post is seen by 200 people or 20,000. Post when you can sit with it. Each kit tells you the hour to post in.

**5. Change one thing at a time.**
If you rewrite your headline, change your CTA and post in three places on the same day, you learn nothing. `07` tells you what to check and when.

## Before day 1, do these four things

1. **Install Microsoft Clarity** on your live site (clarity.microsoft.com, free, one script tag). Without it you are guessing. Everything in `07` assumes it is running.
2. **Write down today's numbers** — total signups, sessions in the last 7 days, where they came from. That is your baseline. You cannot show growth from an unknown starting point.
3. **Make sure signup actually works** on a phone, on mobile data, from a browser you have never logged in on. Ask someone who has never seen your product to do it while you watch. Most of the drop-off this kit will find for you is here.
4. **Do the top three fixes in `04`.**

## About the copy in `03`

It is written in a plain, specific, first-person voice and it names your real feature set, because that is what gets upvoted and what survives moderation. It is a strong draft, not scripture. **Change the details only you know** — the actual number of users you have, the thing a real user said to you, the bug you fixed at 2am. Those are the lines people reply to. Do not add hype; the copy is deliberately under-sold.

Never post the same text to two communities. Rewrite the first two sentences each time.

---

## What this kit does not do

- **It does not include finished videos.** `05` gives you scripts and shot lists. You record them on your phone. A rough, real video from the founder outperforms a polished one.
- **It does not include paid acquisition.** Budget is zero by design.
- **It does not guarantee your number.** Your distribution map shows the planned outreach, estimated results and assumptions for your product. Some initial plans fall well short of the target; they say so and tell you which evidence would justify expanding them. Replace estimates with your results, report activation beside signups, and do not count impressions as users.

## If your site is down

`roster/dead-links.md` is a dated snapshot from 28 October 2026, not proof your site is down today. Several sites have since recovered. Read the dated live-page findings in your own kit and check the URL from a normal browser before doing recovery work. If the real conversion path is unavailable, fix it before promoting; if it works, continue with the kit rather than repairing a stale status label.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Every morning:** open `06`, do the one thing for that day.
- **Every evening:** reply to every comment and DM you got.
- **Every Sunday:** fill in the checkpoint table in `07`. If a channel produced nothing two weeks running, stop using it and double the one that worked.
