# Wave 1 — the 10 projects, and why these 10

Wave 1 exists to get board feedback on **depth and usefulness**, not to cover the most students. So the 10 were picked to stress-test the kit format against as many different growth situations as possible. If the format holds for all ten, it holds for the other 303.

## Selection rules

1. **Live site, verified.** Every one returned HTTP 200 on 28 Oct 2026. No point writing a distribution plan for a dead domain.
2. **Enough public surface to be specific.** The project had to have a real page I could read and critique, so the landing-page fixes are about *that* page and not generic advice.
3. **Maximum spread across growth situations** — see the table. Different ICPs need genuinely different channel mixes, and that is the thing most likely to break a one-size template.

## What each one deliberately tests about the kit format

- **PasteMotion** — can the kit produce a programmatic-SEO plan, not just posts?
- **CartPing** — can it produce a cold outbound motion that is not spam?
- **FeeSathi** — can it work when the ICP has no online community? (This is the hardest one and the most common failure of generic growth advice.)
- **Keplar** — can it handle a market where the buyer and the user are different people?
- **DeskPet** — can it exploit a product whose best asset is that it is cute?
- **Mezzala** — what does the kit say when there is nothing to critique because there is no page?
- **GroupChatBrief** — can it be honest that the biggest growth lever is removing install friction, not posting more?
- **Dine Sight** — can it write a plan with real offline fieldwork in it?
- **HAUS HOME** — can it find a buyer who is clustered in physical locations?
- **Jagah** — can it do a mass-consumer play without a budget?

## What I want board feedback on

1. **Depth per section.** Is `02-distribution-map.md` specific enough to act on without further research, or does the student still have to go find the communities themselves?
2. **Copy quality.** Is `03-copy-pack.md` postable as-is, or does it still read as templated? This is the single thing most likely to need rework across 313 kits.
3. **Reach estimates.** I have given a realistic number per channel with its basis stated. Tell me if you want these tightened, removed, or sourced differently.
4. **The hard cases.** FeeSathi, Dine Sight and HAUS HOME required offline plans. Those take the most time to write and are the least reusable. Worth it, or should those students get a different, shorter kit?
5. **Length.** Each kit is ~7 documents. Too much for a student to actually read? I would rather cut than have them unread.

Waves 2+ carry whatever comes back from this.
