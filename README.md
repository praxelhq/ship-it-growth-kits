Find your project below, then read [START HERE](_START-HERE.md) and your seven documents in order. Your folder includes positioning, distribution, ready-to-use copy, landing-page fixes, video scripts, a 30-day calendar and measurement.

[Final handoff](FINAL-HANDOFF.md) records verification and unresolved source/target/release issues. [Target policy](TARGET-POLICY.md) defines the assigned 1,000 consumer signups or ten real business customers. A completed kit is a plan, not evidence those results have happened. Launch and recovery gates inside each kit take precedence over its posting calendar.
