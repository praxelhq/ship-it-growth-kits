## Outreach forecasts are assumptions

Use a small, manually qualified prospect list. For an early B2B pilot, a planning scenario is 100 individual approaches × 25% substantive response × 60% trial × 40% independently active after onboarding = 6 qualifying businesses. These are **assumptions, not measured conversion rates**. Ten requires better observed rates, more qualified introductions, or a second cohort. Checkpoint after the first 20 approaches.

For a new personal LinkedIn account, budget 100–500 impressions and 0–5 qualified visits per useful post as a conservative, explicitly uncertain planning range. Zero is possible. Replace this with the account's own first three posts. A partner invitation can beat a public post, but only count an event after an organizer accepts it.

Do not sum audiences across products, double-count the same founder across channels, claim access to campus groups, or imply that sending a connection request guarantees delivery. Daily volumes are self-imposed workload limits, not platform-approved quotas.
