## Shared channel evidence and cost boundaries

- [itch.io creator documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started): free creator entry/project pages, private by default and public only after author publication. Publishing is not guaranteed discovery. A project must use an appropriate supported browser/external-link setup.
- [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/): real maker network/launch surface; use a current available slot with a usable product. No vote solicitation, endorsement or promised placement. An invite-only or waitlist-only product may not fit; the relevant kits do not budget a directory audience in that state.
- [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): something people can actually try, no signup-only/fundraiser or requested votes/comments. Not a substitute for fixing an access gate.
- [r/Cricket rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cricket/wiki/rules/) prohibit AI-generated text/images and require moderation care for own content/stats. Do not paste generated kit copy there. StatsCourt's own-channel route avoids that conflict.

- [Duolingo's own explanation](https://blog.duolingo.com/is-duolingo-free/) confirms free courses; no current paid subscription rate is used in C2 comparisons.
- [Top Trumps football gift set](https://toptrumps.com/products/world-football-stars-top-trumps-card-game-football-gift-set): £19.99 UK includes a size-five football plus physical deck. Not a deck-only, Indian or digital-game price.

- [Strava pricing](https://www.strava.com/pricing) displayed US $11.99 monthly/$79.99 annual plus tax; do not convert that into Indian pricing or a claim of equivalent features.
- [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) Basic $0/year and [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing) personal Free $0 are real alternatives. No superiority, medical or security inference follows from comparing prices.

## Outreach coordination and collision rule

These are proposed **relative windows**, not booked events, free distribution rights or a single campus list promised to every student. A founder must choose actual dates and an actual consenting organizer. **At most one broad product invitation to a shared organization/group in any seven-day period.** If distinct adult groups cannot be found, move/omit a session and reduce the forecast. Do not stack all these products into the same two rooms.
