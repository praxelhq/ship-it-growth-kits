# Research — beginner plant owners

Checked 29 October 2026. Two tools share a buyer but solve different moments. Keep the kits standalone and their targets at 1,000 individual signups.

| Product | Verified offer | Wedge / outreach window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Get Plont / Plont](https://getplont.app/) | Two free plants with care reminders. Email-code sign-in. Public premium price not established. Privacy/terms still label themselves pending review; terms and privacy say 18+. Photos for identification go to a provider; no blanket offline claim. | Adult first-time owner maintaining a two-plant care-check routine. Small nursery/office plant-circle sessions Days 4–10. |
| [PlantPal](https://theplantpal.in/) | One first scan free; ₹49 for five scans or ₹149 for fifteen, nonexpiring. Free Next Plant finder. Garden and scan routes exist; actual auth, paid checkout and result accuracy untested. | Adult owner with an uncertain leaf symptom who needs a clear next observation, not a certain diagnosis. Sessions Days 17–23, distinct nursery contacts. |

[RHS houseplant guidance](https://www.rhs.org.uk/houseplants) is a real care resource, not an advertising channel. [iNaturalist's official cost FAQ](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000189329-is-inaturalist-free-are-there-any-subscription-costs-to-use-it-) confirms iNaturalist and Seek are free. Compare basic identification honestly; neither kit should claim that identification alone justifies payment or that an AI photo establishes pet safety.

[r/houseplants](https://www.reddit.com/r/houseplants/) rendered real community posts, but its rules endpoint did not expose the actual rules. Therefore neither kit assumes permission to promote. Use it to listen and obtain moderator guidance; until that guidance is read, no commercial post, product link or DM recruitment is scheduled. Do not label a suggested restriction as a verified subreddit rule.

Use owned short videos and permissioned in-person demonstrations for the primary test. Three accepted sessions of fifteen attendees do not imply a thousand signups. Forecasts in each kit are assumptions, with actual scheduled quantities and explicit gaps. No member counts, nursery partnerships, expert credentials or accuracy rates were verified.

One shared nursery/office organization receives one invitation in a seven-day period. Get Plont teaches recording conditions and checking care; PlantPal teaches photographing a symptom, reading uncertainty and following up. Never stage a before/after recovery or ask a nursery to diagnose a customer plant from a sales pitch. Do not use private home/location imagery without consent.
