# Shared consumer research — d1 coordination

Checked 29 October 2026. These notes coordinate writers; every kit repeats what its reader needs.

## Mac apps: three different jobs

[12th Man](https://12thman.live/) is a free, single-cricket-match notch score display. Its site discloses refreshes can lag play by up to 15 minutes and the app is not yet Apple-verified. Its wedge is a quiet glance while working, not fastest ball alerts. [Fan Notch](https://fanotch.me/) is the multi-sport/market ticker: free sports, paid layout customisation, founding Pro licence from $5 lifetime, three Macs. Its beta also lacks Apple verification. [Munchy Mates](https://munchymatess.app/) is now live despite the roster's old down label: $5 launch price, Apple silicon/macOS14+, battery-reactive desktop pet and file/PDF tools. It must not be pitched as a free app or Windows product.

[r/macapps moderator announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/macapps/comments/1ryaeex/rmacapps_mods_went_too_far_whats_changing_phase_3/) describes trust tiers, an App Pile megathread for unestablished developers, and once-per-developer-in-30-days promotion. A removed post still counts. It also requires disclosing the developer relationship; promotional comments need community participation. Read the current pinned instructions before posting. No writer may promise main-feed eligibility, and three cohort apps do not imply one developer may post three promotions. No engagement exchange.

12th Man leads cricket-working demos Days4–7. Fan Notch leads pointer/menu-access demos Days11–14. Munchy Mates leads pet/battery demos Days21–24. These are outreach windows, not reserved platform slots. [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) requires a usable, personally made product, not a waitlist; no requests for votes or comments. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) permits free maker launches but no direct upvote solicitation; check existing listings. [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) describes weekly Monday Launchpad launches; confirm current eligibility.

## Local social: density before breadth

Café Xtroverts is physically present café QR admission; test one café and one consented time slot. DilliScenes is Delhi host-led gatherings; use one small event, not invented citywide supply. Kahi Chalo is an existing friend circle making a plan. ORBIT is reciprocal introductions inside one building. Coordinate with d2's Adda (Bengaluru fair meeting zones), Kitchen Commune (cooking pairs), Shotgun (roles/expenses), and Scene Kya Hai (experience discovery). Never assume access to an organizer's private group. Keep invitations to one product per group per week, log real dates privately, and do not add strangers to WhatsApp groups.

## Family and reflection

Dastaan captures elders' voice memories into a memoir; Nimantran's current usable wedge is browser-local invitation creation, with AI cloning/cloud batch and payments unavailable; Little Moments makes a shareable letter/bouquet and is now live. Their creators should use separate occasions and circles. TuckedIn targets parents handing off a real sitter shift, not wedding guests or gift recipients.

Ankiro offers human spiritual consultations, not AI clinical advice. Karma Atlas explicitly fictional creature readings and Make Belief values comparisons/fictional worlds are entertainment and reflection. The Patterns explicitly says reflection, never prediction. No cure, destiny, scientific validation, religious ranking or guaranteed relationship outcome belongs in their copy.

## Evidence and forecasts

Live rendered pages were opened for every d1 project with a URL. A failed render alone does not establish downtime. Forecasts in kits are clearly stated scenarios, not measured channel benchmarks. Private founder contact details and sensitive user content stay outside this public repository.
