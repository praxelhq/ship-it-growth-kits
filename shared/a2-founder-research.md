# Shared research — five ways to investigate an idea

Checked 29 October 2026. Preserve the audience policy and 10-customer B2B target for these products; an interesting report does not itself establish a customer.

| Product | Live-page evidence | Wedge / audience list | Outreach reservation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [NXT](https://nxt-ai.me/) | Shows zero problem signals and says nothing has been ingested. Metadata's 18,800+ discussion claim is not the displayed dataset. | Technical founder willing to audit recurring complaints from a small permitted corpus. No broad launch before evidence exists. | Days 4–7: private technical founder design partners, conditional on real evidence. |
| [Before](https://trybefore.me/) | Free one live test/two concepts; Serious $9/month, Obsessed $29/month. Founders bring respondents; platform tester network starts empty. Twenty responses needed before verdict. Stated intent is not purchase. | Physical-product founder with an existing reachable customer list and two packaging/design alternatives. | Days 8–11: founders already able to recruit 20 relevant respondents. |
| [BrandAid](https://brandaid.live/) | Free one brand/three analyses monthly; Pro ₹999 plusGST; Team ₹2,499 plusGST. All showcased figures explicitly fictional examples. | Small brand agency needing a sourced rival-positioning brief before a client pitch. | Days 14–17: agency strategists, not same D2C founder clinic. |
| [Shelf Test](https://shelftest.in/) | Short D2C idea quiz and verdict. Public copy says know if idea will sell; a quiz cannot prove demand. `/tests` and `/feedback` exist. | First-time founder who has not yet spoken to buyers; turn result into an interview plan. | Days 21–24: pre-product campus founders, not funded/incubated brand operators. |
| [PitchPanel](https://pitchpanel.in/) | JavaScript-rendered page says Indian consumer-market research, no live web data, AI estimates and five fictional investor personas. | D2C operator comparing price/pack size in a specific city; manually verify competitor links. | Days 10–13: founder clinic reserved by shared/founder-readiness-cluster.md. |

Do not merge these promises into “validate your startup.” NXT observes complaints; Before collects responses; Shelf Test structures assumptions; BrandAid briefs brand strategy; PitchPanel structures a market view. None independently proves product-market fit.

Use the [Headstart](https://www.headstart.in/) and [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) official entry points documented in the founder-readiness note to find current organizers. Do not claim approval, event dates or member reach until an organizer agrees. The same organization receives one primary invitation per week. Unaccepted workshop slots contribute zero forecast customers.

The free manual alternative is [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) plus source links and actual customer conversations. [Notion Free](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is $0 for an individual; do not compare advanced team/AI features as free. Both official pages were read. Use actual screenshots of the product's real output and label fictional examples. No made-up market sizes, competitor prices or interviews in ready-to-post copy.

For each product, the 10 customers must be real businesses with their own product/data onboarded, unprompted use in the last 7 days, and a privately recorded owner name/phone. Students clicking a sample report are not ten businesses. Keep this private data out of the public kit repository.
