# Research — starting, repeating and reporting small tasks

Checked 29 October 2026. These are four distinct jobs, all retaining the roster's B2C 1,000-person target.

| Product | Actual public evidence | Distinct first job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Terms Apply](https://termsapply.me/) | Free; a specific task/deadline/self-chosen manageable pledge, optional email reminders, self-reported result. `/new` and `/auth` exist. Does not enforce consequences. | Finish one ordinary task in the next day or two, then report honestly. |
| [MakeHabit](https://makehabit.me/) | Anchor/new-action demo, Google sign-in or local guest mode. Rendered controls advertise ₹199 lifetime; checkout, exact entitlement and tax were not tested. See the dated MakeHabit source-correction note. | Attach one small action to an existing daily routine, then log it again tomorrow. |
| [Ughfix](https://ughhfix.in/) | Hinglish/English/Hindi task input, several comedic nag styles, short-start prompt, Pro page says entitlement stays on that phone/browser. A full-body pricing fetch advertises ₹99/month or ₹799/year, but payments are explicitly coming soon; see the Ughfix source-correction note. | Turn one avoided ordinary task into a tiny first action, with user-chosen tone. No therapeutic or ADHD-treatment claims. |
| [HabitForge](https://www.habitforgetracker.in/) | Home says private/authorized Google accounts, pricing says free tracker for everyone, ₹149 lifetime insights. Tracker exposes local-browser ledger text plus conflicting sync notices; dashboard has a locked overlay. | A private adult study/project cohort's 90-day ledger, only after public access/storage promises are reconciled. No broad launch against a restricted gate. |

[Loop Habit Tracker](https://loophabits.org/) is genuinely free/open source and offers reminders/charts. [Beeminder pricing](https://www.beeminder.com/premium) describes monetary pledges as a core revenue mechanism, so do not call the whole service cost-free or say Terms Apply collects stakes. No habit-science claims, efficacy rates or therapeutic outcomes were verified for these student products.

Shared distribution uses owned demonstrations, personal opt-in testers and existing organizer relationships. Do not invent productivity Discords, subreddit permissions, campus endorsements or safe messaging quotas. Proposed organizer windows: Terms Apply Days4–7 (one deadline); MakeHabit Days10–13 (one anchor); Ughfix Days17–20 (one starting step); HabitForge Days24–27 after access is resolved (private cohort). Different contact lists first; one primary invitation per organization in seven days. Count actual permission, not a proposed window, as access.

Forecasts must show the actual scheduled sessions/posts. Missed outcomes remain missed or unreported, not automatically successful. Do not make a stunt, financial deprivation, sleep loss or public humiliation the acquisition hook. The products' useful results are ordinary actions and honest records.
