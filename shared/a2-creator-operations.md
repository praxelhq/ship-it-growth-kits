## Shared channels and forecast discipline

Use your own LinkedIn profile and manually qualified public business websites. One personal approach per prospect, one follow-up after seven days, stop after no response. Daily caps in kits are workload limits, not claims about LinkedIn/Instagram permissions. Do not scrape or buy lists, invent group names, or DM commenters automatically.

[Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/) was opened and lists salons, caterers, photographers and other local businesses. It is a manual discovery source: visit the business's own page and use its advertised business contact, honestly as a seller. Never submit a fake customer enquiry or harvest contacts in bulk. [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/) was opened and lists manufacturers/suppliers. It is not a software-promotion forum.

[Notion's pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) explicitly has a $0 individual Free plan, with limits for teams and attachments. [Zoho Invoice's official pricing](https://www.zoho.com/us/invoice/pricing/) is $0 and includes invoices/quotes; do not pretend invoicing itself has no free competitor. [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) says anyone with a Google Account can create a sheet; paid Gemini features are not part of the free manual alternative. [Grammarly's official plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) has a $0 Free tier; enterprise style guides are separately offered. These are real alternatives, not proof of feature parity.

Expected results in kits are planning assumptions. For 120 qualified approaches, a scenario of 30 substantive replies,18 trials and 10 independently active businesses requires 25% reply,60% trial and 56% retained activation. These are not observed benchmarks and are demanding. At half those rates the target will be missed. Replace them after the first 20 approaches. A personal post may yield zero; don't add unaccepted newsletter or community placements to totals.

Cross-cohort organizer rule: one primary offer to the same group per week. Keep a private shared organizer log; never publish names or phone numbers here. No paid placements, engagement pods or repeated identical launches. Founder workshops use the separately agreed founder-readiness schedule.
