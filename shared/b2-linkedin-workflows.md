## Verified common sources

- [LinkedIn Professional Community Policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies): fetched successfully, locale was German. Use real identities, avoid spam/deception and unwanted repetitive messages. The work limits in the kits are conservative workload choices, not LinkedIn-approved quotas.
- [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing): free plan includes three channels, ten queued posts per channel, AI assistance and basic analytics. Essentials was displayed at $5/channel/month **billed $60 yearly**. Do not present that as a cancellable monthly quote. Scheduling is not a unique paid advantage for these products.
- [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) describes free profiles, project sharing and Monday Launchpad launches. Check current eligibility and available slots before choosing an actual launch date. No promised impressions or guaranteed placement.
- [Product Hunt launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch): free maker submissions, personal accounts, no requests for upvotes, meaningful product changes for repeat launches. Launches are optional proof channels, not the core Indian student acquisition plan.
- [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): real usable maker work, ideally without signup barriers; no landing pages or requests for friends to upvote/comment. Postifyy's public hook checker could support a demonstration, but its main campus plan does not depend on HN.

## Forecast discipline

No member counts, placement dates or creator prices were inferred. The kits show planning arithmetic and replace assumptions after the first three posts or first clinic. A small personal account may get zero qualified clicks. Ten partners agreeing to share is an ambition, not evidence that ten groups are accessible. Unique people are deduplicated across clinics, links and referrals.

The following is a proposed coordination rule, not a platform rule: only one invitation from this cluster reaches a given organizer in any seven-day period. Track organizer permission and actual dates privately. Do not commit personal contact lists, use engagement pods, imply cross-product endorsements or add people to groups without asking.
