## Platform boundaries are part of the product story

[LinkedIn's actual prohibited-software policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387) prohibits scraping, unauthorized automated engagement and extensions that modify its site. Human approval and an asserted official API do not independently establish approval of an extension's entire workflow. TwoCents' own FAQ acknowledges remaining account risk. Do not write “LinkedIn approved,” “ban-proof” or a guaranteed safe daily quota. Never suggest evading warnings. A standalone manual drafting flow would give risk-sensitive prospects a different adoption path, but do not claim it exists until implemented.

Lynkr's landing page says there is no profile scraping, no LinkedIn password, and nothing is sent for the user. Its terms say the user copies and sends drafts manually. That is a stronger relevant distinction than vague “AI-powered” copy. SignalPost's checked public demo is a form plus generate instruction; a successful generation was not submitted or independently tested.

- Personal LinkedIn feed: publish original useful material on your own account and respond manually. Do not infer group permission from the ability to join. With no supplied account history, assume 100–500 impressions and 0–5 qualified visits per post; zero is possible. Replace this with the first three posts' results.
- Campus clubs and alumni introductions: contact your own organizer privately and obtain permission for one free working session. No named club, membership size or invitation access has been independently supplied. A 20-seat accepted session × 60% attendance × 50% first action would produce six activations; all three inputs are assumptions until the organizer confirms the actual event.
- [ChatGPT's published Free tier](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) and [Grammarly Free at $0/month](https://www.grammarly.com/plans) are real alternatives. Generic drafting is available free. Sell fact checking, context or workflow, never “the only way to write with AI.” No unverified paid competitor price is needed.
