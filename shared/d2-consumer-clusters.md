## Chat and relationship products

- Couples Connect now presents as [Not Yet Said](https://www.notyetsaid.com/): Radio listening plus Two Chairs shared question cards. ₹149/year to start tables; invited partner joins free. Notebook snippets are explicitly editorial, not user testimonials. Focus on one two-person conversation, not therapy or couple outcomes.
- [Judge My Chat](https://www.judgemychat.live/) offers a comic court verdict and routes to `/court`. Entertainment only; do not imply legal validity or post another person's raw messages.
- [Kalesh Judge](https://kaleshjudge.app/) rendered only a file-opening status twice. This is a render limitation, not proof the site is down. Verify the app before claiming a feature works.
- [Scrollback](https://scrollback.in/) explicitly says local/offline chat parsing, three free screens, ₹49 full story and ₹149 gift. Show a synthetic chat, then demonstrate airplane mode. Gift link content requires consent even if no server stores it.
- [Kuchu Puchu](https://kuchupuchu.me/) is an Apple Silicon/macOS14+ paired companion beta, ₹100 tax-inclusive one time. Both apps must run; Intel/phones unsupported. Public notice says not Apple-notarized, and chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Focus on long-distance pairs; do not market as an invisible workplace messenger.

## Verified shared channel sources

[Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/) describes a builder network and Monday Launchpad. [Product Hunt's guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) forbids asking directly for votes and recommends personal maker accounts. [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) requires usable personally made work, not a signup page, and forbids soliciting votes/comments. Use these only for a demonstrable product, not an unrecovered URL.

Reddit rule-page fetches for Bangalore, knitting, crochet and Mac apps returned only the rules shell, not the rules themselves. **No permission to post is established by that fetch.** Reddit can be a mod-permission experiment, not the baseline forecast. Do not invent a weekly thread or member count.

Owned Instagram/LinkedIn posts, existing WhatsApp groups with admin permission, and invited campus demonstrations need no fictional public community. Each kit names exactly who should receive an invitation. Limit manual new conversations to 5–10 daily, stop after a refusal and send at most one follow-up after seven days. This is a workload choice, not an asserted platform limit.

All numerical reach in the kits is a planning assumption. One useful clip from an unproven account may receive zero distribution. Backsolve the 1,000 target honestly, show the gap from the low case, and replace assumptions after the first 3 posts/20 invitations. Do not add overlapping audience totals.
