## Verified research and manual alternatives

[ICAI's official website](https://www.icai.org/) exposes an official-directory route, events and contact information. Use it to identify legitimate organizations and current official resources. It does not give advertising permission, professional endorsement, access to member groups or a reusable email list. Verify any firm's own business site and use its relevant enquiry route; no scraped personal member details.

[Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing) is $0 for up to 10 collaborators/workspace and can hold a manual request checklist. It does not prove secure document handling or replace a reviewed extraction tool. Existing Excel or a firm's existing software licence can be the actual alternative without an additional subscription; don't fabricate the cost of Tally, Dext or another competitor.

## Rotation and handling constraints

Days 4–7: CA Deskk checklist walkthrough. Days 10–13: Audit extractor text-PDF comparison. Days 16–19: NudgeBooks recurring chase. Days 22–25: PBC engagement support matching. Fintellix waits for feature/authentication scope confirmation and uses a separate invited firm rather than a duplicate public-group pitch. Adjust actual dates if start dates differ; no organization receives repeated invitations inside seven days.

Every first demonstration uses redacted or synthetic documents. Never upload a client's PAN, bank statement or invoice to an unknown service just to make a demo convincing. The professional must authorize any real workflow and retain review responsibility. Don't advertise filing deadlines or statutory correctness from sample text. Test-mode payment receipts are not sales.

Forecasts are explicitly hypothetical:40 first approaches × 25% reply × 60% trial × 50% independent use≈3 businesses. Ten requires better observed rates, warm introductions or a reallocated channel mix. Ten staff inside one CA firm is one business, not ten.
