## Verified shared distribution entry points

- [itch.io creator guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started): free account and project page; games/comics/books supported, HTML5 embeds or external links allowed. New pages default private. Publish only one's own working build and correct asset licences. A listing is not a guarantee of discovery.
- [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/): real builder network; homepage describes Monday Launchpad. Use personal proof of work and substantive replies. Slots and account requirements must be checked before launch; do not buy ads or trade votes.
- [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): only personally made work people can try; no signup-only landing pages or fundraisers; do not solicit votes or comments. An interactive demonstration is appropriate only if usable.
- [r/Cricket rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cricket/wiki/rules/): ask moderators before posting own content; no AI-generated text/images, no daily voting games, no trivial narrowly-filtered stats. **Do not paste AI-written kit copy there.** For Statscourt this is research/reading unless the student writes their own substantive analysis and receives permission.
- [r/SideProject rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/about/rules/) and [r/languagelearning rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/languagelearning/about/rules/): pages loaded but detailed rules were not extractable. No blanket promotion permission is verified. Require a fresh rules/moderator check; budget zero reach until permitted.
- [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/about/): actual film diaries, reviews and lists; suitable for useful curated Indian-cinema lists, not unsolicited external-link replies to strangers. No claim of a permitted ad placement or organizer access.
- [Yoga With Adriene](https://yogawithadriene.com/): real free-video alternative and weekly letter. Reply usefully to relevant public discussions without product links. No editorial acceptance or newsletter placement was verified; do not include one in a forecast.
- [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org/): real ball-by-ball source and contact route for corrections. Attribution and a reproducible method matter more than declaring the product's numbers correct.

Campus and WhatsApp contacts refer to the student's existing, administrator-approved relationships. No named group, member count, open invite or affiliation is invented. Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn mean the maker's own account and the specific relevant clip/post, not mass DMs or bought lists.

## Forecast discipline

No kit may treat an audience-size claim as expected results. Where no account history exists, label every view/click/signup number a planning assumption. An illustrative small-account session: 5 administrator-approved sessions × 20 attendees × 50% starting the activity = 50 starts. Twelve short clips × 300–1,000 views × 1% click-through × 50% start = 18–60 starts. These are scenarios, not platform averages, and show why 1,000 requires a much larger partner or breakout; they do not pretend the target is guaranteed. Deduplicate people across channels, separate account creation from activation, and replace assumptions after the first measured cohort.

Do not put personal health information, private journal text, credentials, unredacted medical documents or student contact data into screenshots, public analytics or this repository. Use explicit sample data for demos and label it.
