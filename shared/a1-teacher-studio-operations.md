## Checked sources and boundaries

- [Yoga Alliance directory](https://www.yogaalliance.org/Directory) provides teacher/school search. It is prospect research, not permission to scrape contacts, assert endorsement or advertise to members. Manually verify the teacher's own business page and use a suitable business enquiry route.
- [Attakkalari](https://www.attakkalari.org/) is a real movement-arts organization. The fetched homepage contains historical event entries; do not announce a current event, registration date or partnership from those entries. Its own public educational content can inform a relevant, non-promotional reply.
- [The Yoga Institute](https://www.theyogainstitute.org/) is a real source for its own programmes. No member-group access, free newsletter inclusion or promotion permission was established.
- DancePass's own unclaimed directory is a starting point for manually checking public studio sites, not proof those organizations use DancePass, ChoreoStudio or Abhyava.
- [Trello Free](https://trello.com/pricing),$0 up to 10 collaborators/workspace, is an honest manual planning alternative. It does not provide the verified specialised functions above. Don't invent a competitor's local subscription price.

## Coordinated outreach windows

Each student's private prospect list must have a different primary job. One shared organization gets at most one introductory invitation in a seven-day span, after the organizer agrees. Suggested windows: Abhyava days 4–7, ChoreoStudio 11–14, DancePass 18–21, PlayLead 25–28. These are coordination choices, not reserved events or platform slots. Engage addresses a distinct online cohort segment; no reuse of the same studio group.

Keep the organizer/owner log private. Ask permission before sharing a referral or a teacher's recordings. For demos use adult volunteers or synthetic examples; do not publish student videos, names, fee records or parent contacts.

## Forecast discipline

Forty individually qualified first approaches over ten named outreach days at assumed 25% reply × 60% trial × 50% independent continued use yields about 3 customers. Warm referrals or an accepted demonstration may add 0–2. These are planning assumptions, not observed teacher conversion rates. Expose the gap to the 10-business target and revise after the first 20 approaches. A directory list, teacher registration, trial booking or parent chat is not automatically a qualifying business customer.
